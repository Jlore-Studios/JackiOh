## Steps

**Budget rules.** These come from probing `tiers.test.ts`'s pipeline at the base commit:
- A Mythic Spell's cast entry is 1400 ms: the 1000 ms hold plus the 400 ms cast.
- In that entry the shared takeover ends at 2100 ms, the shared rays at 2000 and the shared burst at 2140. The ceiling is 1950.
- A burst counts 900 ms and a ring 500. The `handGlint` row bursts.

So every recipe keeps to these limits:
- **Mythic Spells:** bursts only in the cast entry, at a delay of at most `holdMs` (`entry.setPiece?.holdMs ?? 0`) + 50.
- **Legendary Spells:** bursts only in the cast entry, at a delay of at most 300.
- **Units:** bursts in the entrance, and at delay 0 in the first text-beat entry.
- **Later entries:** only `figure`, `ghost` and `sheen` cues, each ending by D. Scratch Pad's tear ends by 0.8·D.
- **Rows:** every beat a recipe draws returns `row: "replace"`.
- **Shakes:** at most 1 on a Spell, at most 0.8 on a Unit.
