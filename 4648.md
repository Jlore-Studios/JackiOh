## Goal

At each decision's root, the practice AI evolves every readable card that can still evolve, on a sandbox copy:
- every unit on either field;
- every Unit in its own hand, placed on the field first;
- never anything in the enemy's hand.

For each card it records what the evolution changes in its controller's evaluation, as the price of one run of the Evolve text. `unit_worth` then adds `AI_EVAL.evolve` × that price × the number of runs the text would take now (R3536003, so High Evolutionary counts where it acts) × how close the card is to evolving. - the share of its controller's unit zones that are filled;
- the share of the needed (0)-cost cards held (the AI's own cards only);
