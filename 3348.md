# Plan: #3348 a living arena

## Goal
Every tile (`zone-<side>-<row>-<lane>`, either side) answers a press with a press-in, a ripple in its mat's material, that material's sound and, on touch, a short haptic. A legal tile answers harder, and an occupied one ripples around its card. Hovering a tile on desktop lights its edge. Taps keep their meaning.

Each of the ten playmats gets two corner props that animate, sound, escalate after 5–7 repeated taps and roll a rare outcome at 1 in 1,000. Four mats hide a tap sequence. Corner play is local to the viewer: it is never sent, never logged and never blocks the game. The settings are honoured.
