## Goal

In the private lobby, at all seven `MENU_SIZES` and in 1v1, co-op and 2v2, the player's Ready and the host's Start (with Leave) are in view without scrolling. They sit above the phone tab bar and the bottom safe area, and the seats scroll beneath them. The check mark in "✓ Ready to queue" gets a space after it.

**Choice, with the reason (ruling R3954000):** one sticky action bar at the foot of the lobby, not compact seat cards.
