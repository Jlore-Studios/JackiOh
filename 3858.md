# Plan (sonnet, medium)

## Goal

`face_value` (`crates/ai/src/reply.rs`) should count a Lifesteal attacker's heal when the attacker hits the enemy hero, as `trade_value` already does for a hit on a unit (SPEC §4.4 step 8: Lifesteal heals by what each hit deals, on a hero too).

- Normally the term is `heal_worth` of the damage the hit deals through the hero's Armor and caps.
- Under a heal converter (R413) it is minus `reply_face_value` of that damage on the attacker's own hero.
- The lethal shortcut stays first, and a lethal hit still scores exactly `AI_EVAL.win`.
- No new weight and no new ruling: this is AI valuation, and §4.4 already states the rule.
