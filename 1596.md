## Goal

When a finished 1v1 match's game record fails to write after its result commits, the retention loop files it again before the purge deletes the match's log. Each pass retries matches that ended in the last day (past a grace), plus a last try for every log the purge is about to delete. A refiled record is folded into the totals as written (R1044000) and names no build (R1367000). Matches that already have a record, and ceiling draws, are left alone. The new store query is covered by the contract on both stores. Ruling R1596000 records the decision and amends R376.
