## The table (R3888000)

**The principle:** a change is live where it can still change what the card does, from the zone it is in or from one it reaches in the normal course. A deck card reaches a hand. A field card reaches a graveyard, unless it is a unit token. A bounce, a resurrect or a "Trigger a Cry" is not the normal course.

**Footnotes:**
1. Not on a card that casts itself on draw (`draw::casts_on_draw`): it never reaches a hand.
2. Only while its draw casts it (`cast_on_draw_now::is_cast_on_draw`).
3. Only while `graveyard_play::playable_from_graveyard`.
4. Only while `combat::is_sick` (it entered this turn).
5. Only while `card.awakened != Some(true)` (R3039000).
6. Only while `card.evolved != Some(true)` (R3536000).
7. Not on a unit token (`zones::is_unit_token`).

A number whose `readOn` names one face counts as `Lasting` on the other face. Each row keeps its own conditions: types, bounds, R65, Vanilla and Immutable.
