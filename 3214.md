   2. Call `authenticate_upgrade(app, token.clone(), SOCIAL_PATH)`.

   5. Send a `social_frame`. On error: log it, refuse with 1011, `remove` the socket, and return `None`.
   6. Call `watch_session(app, socket, token, id)` and return `Some(id)`.
7. **`live.rs`, `handlers(app, socket, id) -> SocketHandlers`.**

     - once the budget is spent, close with `CLOSE_POLICY_VIOLATED` and "too many malformed frames".
   - `close` is a no-op.
8. **`live.rs`, the session check.**
