## Goal

End Times' record of its cuts (the "bank") moves out of the card's memory and into its tuning, next to the cut it records. Then a use always gives back every cut, including after the card is bounced and replayed, copied or Flickered: the issue's reproduction costs (10) after the use. New ruling **R4828000** settles the open question: **the card remembers**. A card that comes back keeps its cut price, because R78 keeps the tuning and R443 lets a copy carry it. Its next use gives every cut back. Done also means: replays of v0.6.0–v0.6.13i still fold, through a kept script; the bounce and copy cases are tested in `c098_heroic_power.rs`; and there is a pending patch fragment claiming `core-098`.
