# Plan (opus, strong)

**Plan for #4848: each question for the player not on turn gets its own 30-second prompt clock**

## Goal

The 1v1 match clock arms a fresh 30-second (`PROMPT_CLOCK_SECONDS`) prompt clock for every prompt held by the player not on turn. It tells prompts apart by the open prompt's id, not only by who holds it. So a question that follows a timed-out one for the same seat is timed (the bug), and a question that follows one answered in time gets a full 30 s (T8). This settles T8 by a new ruling, R4848000.

These stay as they are:
- §9.5 and R79's pause of the turn clock.
- R776000: a prompt whose clock ran out is re-armed only by the retry while that same prompt stays open.

Also in scope:
- The audit's two tests move into `crates/shared/tests/`.
- The host fuzz gains a liveness property: with every seat silent, a clock timer other than the ceiling is always armed.
- A check that the clients' countdown shows each question's clock. No client change is needed: `Clock.tsx` `readClock` reads `promptDeadline` from the last `clock` frame, and `after_change` sends that frame to both seats after every change.
