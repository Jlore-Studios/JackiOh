audit): R186 and R390's two passes over the 17 Meditative cards `SHADOW_BAN` held, on the AI of generation
13, the learned evaluation of #1477 (`AI_EVAL_KIND = Learned`), swept alone. Every one had been banned for

## What the timeouts measure

A `timeout` is one AI decision over 2 s at `AI_GATE_BUDGET` (or a game past 600 actions) in a game the card
was forced into, counted only when R977000's re-timer, the perf gate's yardstick, finds it slow again. In the
1,088 games the sweep's clock found 11 decisions over 2 s, all in pass 2, and the re-timer kept one:

- meditative-069 The Maestro, `hard`: 8 raw, 1 counted, all in game `sweep2:hard:meditative-069:8`;
- meditative-030 Fickle E-Kitten, `hard`: 2 raw, none counted;
- meditative-085 Playtester, `easy`: 1 raw, none counted.

No game reached 600 actions, so every count is a decision. To see the slow states, pass 2 of those three
cards ran again, three lanes at once as the record did, on a build that also prints each decision the
re-timer times (`still_slow`'s `slow decision:` line, #1675). These runs are diagnostics, not the record;
they reproduced The Maestro's verdict and Fickle E-Kitten's two raw timeouts, not Playtester's one.

- **The Maestro, `wide state`.** Game `sweep2:hard:meditative-069:8`, the AI on p2: seven decisions over 2 s
  on the clock, turns 12 to 16. The one still slow is on turn 16: 38 legal actions, 424 nodes, 2,080 ms on
  the development machine's scale (4,082 ms raw on this one). The six before it had 45 to 3,252 legal
  actions (1,793 ms re-timed at most). Through all of them p2's hand holds three copies of one fused card of
  nine ingredients (`t-1:core-086+core-031+meditative-069+classic-080+classicplus-014+classic-065+core-026+
  classicplus-t-ai-03+meditative-048`), and by turn 16 one of them is on p2's field: every ingredient's text
  on one card (R77's fused definition), The Maestro's among them. No My Pawn (`core-096`) is on either field,
  and the game did not reach 600 actions. Of the groups #1675 sorts by (a game past 600 actions; a simulated step
  that plays out a whole turn through My Pawn; legal actions past `AI_GATE_BUDGET.root_branching`, 20;
  anything else), the first that matches is the third: a wide state, made wide by a many-ingredient fusion.
- **Fickle E-Kitten**: two decisions of `hard` games 2 and 8, each on turn 10 (41 and 23 legal actions),
  2,375 and 2,091 ms raw and 780 and 682 ms re-timed: slow only on the clock of a loaded machine.
