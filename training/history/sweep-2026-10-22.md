# Shadow-ban sweep of record, 2026-10-22

The sweep that measures the 17 shadow bans again under generation 13 (#1675, part of the plan in #682's
audit): R186 and R390's two passes over the 17 Meditative cards `SHADOW_BAN` held, on the AI of generation
13, the learned evaluation of #1477 (`AI_EVAL_KIND = Learned`), swept alone. Every one had been banned for
`timeout` on the hand evaluation, in the 2026-10-12 sweep and the reruns after it. The sweep covered those 17
cards only, so every other card keeps the row the earlier reports gave it (R1186000); a card this report
clears, swept, neither banned nor watched and affordable at some tier, has no row in either table (R1675000).

**The verdict: none of the 17 banned.** 6 are watched (meditative-011 Double Header, meditative-025
Blue-Eyes White Felinor, meditative-069 The Maestro, meditative-075 Ever Growing Tree, meditative-078
Occidentless Mandate and meditative-099 Paranoia, each at risk by its own numbers at half strength, R600,
and none meeting a flag in full), 11 are cleared (meditative-012, -017, -029, -030, -031, -049, -058, -085,
-087, -091 and -096), and none is unswept. No game of either pass had an `error` or a `timeout`, and the
clock found no decision over 2 s at all: every row's raw timeouts are 0. The four pass-1 `neverPlayed` flags
(Blue-Eyes White Felinor and Occidentless Mandate at `hard`, The Maestro and Ever Growing Tree at `easy`)
all cleared in pass 2.

## How it ran

- **Tree**: `bot/issue-1675` on `main` at `c4bf61d`. The games of both passes ran on a copy of
  `target/release/jackioh` built once with `cargo build --release -p jackioh-tools` from `main` at `c4bf61d`
  with nothing changed; the report was printed by one built from the same tree with this branch's
  `crates/tools/src/sweep.rs` change (the `Cleared ids:` list, R1675000), which plays no game, before
  `sweep --write` touched `crates/ai/src`. `crates/ai/src` was `main`'s for both. `SHADOW_BAN` held the 17,
  kept out of the AI's decks as filler (each banned for `timeout`, so pass 2 deals none of them as filler
  either); `SHADOW_WATCH` the earlier sweeps' 88 rows, counted at risk from the start (105 at risk).
- **Machine**: 4 cores (AMD EPYC 9V45), with no build or test running beside either pass. Each pass ran as
  three lanes, one card per process, the ids dealt to the lanes in turn:
  - pass 1: `jackioh sweep --json meditative-<n>` over the 17 ids, 21:42 to 21:46 UTC;
  - pass 2: `jackioh sweep --pass2 <the 17 pass-1 files> meditative-<n>` over the 17 at-risk ids, 21:46 to
    21:56 UTC;
  - the report: `jackioh sweep --report <the 17 pass-1 files> <the 17 pass-2 files>`.
- **Seeds**: pass 1 `sweep:<tier>:<id>:<n>`, n = 1 to 8, for the 17 ids at `easy` and `hard` (272 games);
  pass 2 `sweep2:<tier>:<id>:<n>`, n = 1 to 24, for the 17 at-risk ids at both tiers (816 games).
- **Budget**: `AI_GATE_BUDGET` {"nodes":600,"lethalNodes":150,"determinizations":3,"beamWidth":4,
  "rootBranching":20,"branching":6,"maxDepth":8,"finalists":3}; `AI_SWEEP.decision_ms` 2000, `max_actions`
  600.

## What the timeouts measure

A `timeout` is one AI decision over 2 s at `AI_GATE_BUDGET` (or a game past 600 actions) in a game the card
was forced into, counted only when R977000's re-timer, the perf gate's yardstick, finds it slow again. None
was counted, and none needed the re-timer: in the 1,088 games no decision reached 2 s on the sweep's own
clock and no game reached 600 actions. So no card is left to sort by cause. The slow states the 2026-10-12
notes looked at (hands filled with cheap random spells and tokens, a simulated step that played out a whole
turn through My Pawn) were measured on the hand evaluation; on generation 13 these 17 cards' games stay under
the bar at both tiers.
