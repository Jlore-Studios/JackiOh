# Shadow-ban sweep of record, 2026-10-25

The sweep that measures The Maestro again (#1889, part of the plan in #682's audit): R186 and R390's two passes
over `meditative-069`, the one card `SHADOW_BAN` held, on the AI of generation 13, swept alone, so every other
card keeps the row the earlier reports gave it (R1186000). The 2026-10-22 sweep banned it at `hard` for
`timeout`: in game `sweep2:hard:meditative-069:8`, turn 16, one decision of 38 legal actions was still over
2 s timed again (2,080 ms), its state made wide by a fused card of nine ingredients.

**The verdict: none banned.** meditative-069 The Maestro is watched: at risk by its own numbers at half
strength (R600) and meeting no flag in full, so `sweep --write` swaps its ban row for a watch row (R1186000).

found no suspect line.

## Where the time went

#1889's plan took the slow state of the 2026-10-22 sweep again, by replaying game
`sweep2:hard:meditative-069:8` on `71b594b` (turn 16, the AI on p2, 38 legal actions, 424 nodes, 2,422 ms
raw), and profiled the decision on that state on `main`'s build. The time was the engine's, not the search's:
77% in `each_legal_action`, 52% of everything under `reduce::end_due_turns` → `auto_end_due`, which lists the
seat's actions again after every simulated step, and 51% in `play_choices::targeting_decls_of`, reached from
`priced_play_actions` through `targeting_discards_required`. That listing checked whether each choice
combination's targets' discard cost (B5 E5, R450, R682) could be paid once per price × Tribute set × zone ×
combination, though neither the Tribute set nor the zone changes the answer, and the fused card's many
ingredients make many combinations, sets and zones.

The change lists each price's payable combinations once, the first time the price offers a zone, and reads
R1200's `targets_random` once per listing: the same actions in the same order (R90). #1889's plan timed the
decision on that state at about 1 s raw on `main`'s build (987 and 1,029 ms) and 391 ms with the change, with
the same action, nodes and lines. `main` had already moved since `71b594b`, whose build took 2,422 ms.

On this machine, this run:
