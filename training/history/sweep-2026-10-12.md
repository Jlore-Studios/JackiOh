# Shadow-ban sweep of record, 2026-10-12

The sweep that brought the Meditative set into the AI's decks (#732, which superseded #616): R186 and R390's
two passes over the set's 102 non-token cards, on the AI of generation 8 with R1390's gate lifted
(`AI_DECK_GATED_SETS` empty). It swept the Meditative set only. R732000 keeps every card of an earlier set
(Core, Classic, Classic+) out of `SHADOW_BAN`, so their rows would never be copied, and the unban lane
already taught the AI to play every one it had banned (generations 1 to 8). `SHADOW_WATCH` keeps the 2026-10-07
sweep's rows for them.

**The verdict: 31 of the 102 banned, every one for `timeout`, so the AI deals itself the other 71 (70% of
the set; R732000 allows at most 51 bans).** 10 Meditative cards are watched (at risk and cleared), 1 is
unswept (meditative-037 CN in a bottle, never affordable), and no game had an `error`. No ban is for
`neverPlayed`, `selfHarm` or `selfKill`: the nine pass-1 `neverPlayed` flags and the at-risk cards all cleared
in pass 2, or were banned for a timeout of their own.

## How it ran

- **Tree**: `main` at `bf0f8e8` with `AI_DECK_GATED_SETS` emptied (this branch's `716cc6b`, whose
  `crates/ai/src` differs from that build only in comments), built once with
  `cargo build --release -p jackioh-tools` and run as a copy of `target/release/jackioh`. `SHADOW_BAN` was
  empty and `SHADOW_WATCH` held the 2026-10-07 sweep's 67 cards of the earlier sets, which R390 counts at
  risk from the start, so they were dealt more often as pass-2 filler. They were never forced and have no
  verdict.
- **Machine**: 4 cores (Intel Xeon, 2.1 GHz), one thread each. Each pass ran as three lanes, each pinned to its
  own core (`taskset`), one card per process:
  - pass 1: `jackioh sweep --json <id>` over the 102 ids dealt to the lanes in turn, 13:48 to 14:23 UTC. A
    release build ran on the fourth core for part of it.
  - pass 2: `jackioh sweep --pass2 p1-final.jsonl <id>` over the 22 at-risk Meditative ids from a shared
    queue, from 14:51 UTC, with nothing else running.
  - the report: `jackioh sweep --report p1-final.jsonl <the pass-2 files>`.
- **The timeouts run again alone.** Pass 1 flagged 24 cards `timeout`. Each was swept again with the machine
  to itself (14:26 to 14:51 UTC) and its two lines replaced, as the 2026-10-07 sweep did. The games are
  seeded and identical, and only the clock differs. Four cleared: meditative-001, -002, -022 and -047 (-047 was banned all the same, for a timeout in its own
  pass-2 games). Most
  of the rest kept fewer slow decisions than in the shared run (meditative-012 Fear Mongerer, easy: 17 to
  11). Pass 2's own timeouts were run again alone the same way (15:29 to 16:05 UTC, one other sweep process on
  another core): 12 cards, and the counts matched the shared run to within one decision. One cleared,
  meditative-098 Showdown, which is watched instead. meditative-091 Windfast was not run again: its pass-2
  `hard` games were stopped after 59 minutes in one game that never ended (the state passed 900 MB, as My
  Pawn's random play-out of a turn, core-096, fired inside the beam's simulations of a Windfast attack),
  and its `easy` games' timeout bans it anyway. Its `hard` pass-2 line is missing, so its `hard` numbers
  are pass 1's.
- **Seeds**: pass 1 `sweep:<tier>:<id>:<n>`, n = 1 to 8, for the 102 ids at `easy` and `hard` (1,632 games);
  pass 2 `sweep2:<tier>:<id>:<n>`, n = 1 to 24, for the 22 at-risk ids at both tiers (1,056 games).
- **Budget**: `AI_GATE_BUDGET` {"nodes":600,"lethalNodes":150,"determinizations":3,"beamWidth":4,
  "rootBranching":20,"branching":6,"maxDepth":8,"finalists":3}; `AI_SWEEP.decision_ms` 2000, `max_actions`
  600.

## What the timeouts measure

A `timeout` is one AI decision over 2 s at `AI_GATE_BUDGET` (or a game past 600 actions) in a
game the card was forced into. Two of the slow games were looked at closely, not all of them:

- `sweep:easy:meditative-012:4` (Fear Mongerer), replayed with a scratch harness around `play_match` and the
  sweep's own setup: ten decisions on turns 16 and 18 took 1.6 to 3.8 s, and the AI's whole game took 32 s.
  The AI's hand held four Auspicious Rocks (0-cost random spells) beside Spiritually 中国 and Touched by KY's
  bigger hand, and the boards had changed sides, so each of the beam's simulated plays cost several
  milliseconds and the 600 nodes added up.
- Windfast's pass-2 `hard` game above, caught in a debugger: the beam simulating an attack that Windfast
  redirects sets off My Pawn (core-096), whose "the rest of their turn is played at random" plays out a whole
  turn inside each simulated step.

So a ban says the card leads the AI into a slow game, not necessarily that the card itself is hard to think
about. Making the search fast enough in those states is the unban lane's work (`training/unban.md`).
