# Shadow-ban sweep of record, 2026-10-18

The sweep that brings the Meditative+ set into the AI's decks (#1186, part of #1173): R186 and R390's two
passes over the set's 26 non-token cards, on the AI of generation 12, with every set previewed (R1420) as
`fuzz` previews them, since the set had not shipped on the tree it ran on. It swept the Meditative+ set only.
R1186000 holds the shadow ban to Meditative and Meditative+ cards, each at most half its set, so the
2026-10-12 sweep's rows stand for the Meditative set and the 2026-10-07 sweep's watch rows for the earlier
sets; `sweep --write` reads the three reports oldest first, a card taking the row of the latest that lists it.

**The verdict: none of the 26 banned, so the AI deals itself the whole set (R1186000 allows at most 13
bans).** 11 Meditative+ cards are watched (at risk in pass 1 and cleared in pass 2), 1 is unswept
(meditativeplus-018 Cognitive Offloading, which is cast when drawn and so is never affordable from a hand),
and no game had an `error` or a `timeout`. The five pass-1 `neverPlayed` flags (NA Tech, Supply Drop,
Mountaintop Garden and Enormous Elderberry at `hard`, Bloodbloom at `easy`) all cleared in pass 2.

## How it ran

- **Tree**: `claude/mplus-1186-1187` at `e1f7728` (`main` at `13e527a` with Meditative+ batches 01 and 03
  merged from `bot/issue-1173`, and the sweep's preview of every set), built once with
  `cargo build --release -p jackioh-tools` and run as a copy of `target/release/jackioh`. Meditative+ had not
  shipped on that tree; the preview opens exactly what shipping opens (R1420), so the games are those of the
  shipped set. `SHADOW_BAN` held the 17 Meditative cards the 2026-10-12 sweep and the unban lane left, kept
  out of the AI's decks as filler; `SHADOW_WATCH` the earlier sweeps' 77 rows, counted at risk from the start
  (105 at risk with this sweep's).
- **Machine**: 4 cores (Intel Xeon, 2.1 GHz), one thread each, shared with other builds for the whole run
  (Rust and web builds of the same release in other worktrees). Each pass ran as three lanes, one card per
  process, the ids dealt to the lanes in turn:
  - pass 1: `jackioh sweep --json <id>` over the 26 ids, 20:51 to 21:11 UTC;
  - pass 2: `jackioh sweep --pass2 p1-0.jsonl,p1-1.jsonl,p1-2.jsonl <id>` over the 11 at-risk Meditative+
    ids, 21:11 to 21:35 UTC;
  - the report: `jackioh sweep --report <the three pass-1 files> <the three pass-2 files>`.
- **No timeout to run again.** No game of either pass had a decision R977000's re-timer still found slow, so,
  unlike the 2026-10-12 sweep, no card was swept again alone. The shared machine shows in the raw counts:
  14 rows hold a raw timeout that the re-timer cleared.
- **Seeds**: pass 1 `sweep:<tier>:<id>:<n>`, n = 1 to 8, for the 26 ids at `easy` and `hard` (416 games);
  pass 2 `sweep2:<tier>:<id>:<n>`, n = 1 to 24, for the 11 at-risk ids at both tiers (528 games).
- **Budget**: `AI_GATE_BUDGET` {"nodes":600,"lethalNodes":150,"determinizations":3,"beamWidth":4,
  "rootBranching":20,"branching":6,"maxDepth":8,"finalists":3}; `AI_SWEEP.decision_ms` 2000, `max_actions`
  600.

## What the timeouts measure

A `timeout` is one AI decision over 2 s at `AI_GATE_BUDGET` (or a game past 600 actions) in a game the card
was forced into, counted only when R977000's re-timer, the perf gate's yardstick, finds it slow again. None
was. The Meditative+ cards that pause or play for a seat answer at once: KY's Competition's questions are
worked out from their statement (R1184002), the crafters answer with their seeded presets (R1182000), and
Cognitive Offloading's autopilot is the engine's, never the search's (R1183002).
