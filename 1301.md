# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

The server buys a card pack with Jlore (`POST /api/packs/buy`), opens one (`POST /api/packs/open`) and reads the pity counters and odds back (`GET /api/packs`). Opening rolls each slot's rarity through the pity math, draws a card that leans toward unowned ones, then runs the dupe chain: an unowned card unlocks, a duplicate becomes a cosmetic on its own rarity scale, or the slot adds bar points. An opening rolls from a fresh seed that is logged in its ledger rows, so it replays exactly. Buying and opening each commit whole or not at all. Seeding `public.cosmetics` needs no work: #1299's `write_catalog` (`cli/seed_catalog.rs`) already does it, and `release` runs it at boot.
