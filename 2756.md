# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

On `/stats`, a full-art card's breakdown is the drill-down dialog opened from the Cards table or grid (for example Gary the Gambler, `core-004`). It gets a choice of the art its faces print: **No full art** (the normal face), **Epic**, **Legendary** or **Mythic**, which are the three full-art tiers of R2655000. The face prints the pressed choice, and so does the Radiant face when Show Radiant is on.

- It starts on what the face prints today: Epic while the Full-art cards setting is on, No full art while it is off.
- It stays picked from card to card, as Show Radiant does.
- It writes no card pick.
- A card without full art does not get the choice.

A new ruling, R2756000, records this.

   /**

   function artsOf(defId: string): DrillDownArt[] {
