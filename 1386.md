**Risks.**
- **Determinism and replay:** probes never touch `policy` or `order`, never enter `log` and never replace `state`. Games, hashes and heaviest lists stay as they were. The golden traces don't use `play_game`.
- **False failures:**
  - The 1v1 wrong seat is skipped while it still owes its mulligan: under R265 a mulligan with `keep: []` is legal for it.
  - The policy never picks concede, draw or emote (`AI_SKIPPED_ACTIONS`), so nothing it chose is legal for the other seat.
  - Duos only picks seats that `duos_seats_to_act` left out.
  - A probe failure in a 200-seed run is a real finding: report it with its seed. Never raise the stride, skip the probe or loosen `judge_probe` to hide it.
- **Cost:** two state clones and two compares per probe; hashes are computed only on failure. Step 15 measures the cost.
- **README contract:** update the `fuzz` row in the same change.
