## Risks

- **Closing a live client's socket.** The web client reconnects on close 1001 (`apps/web/src/game/net.ts`, `onclose`). Evicting with a socket open would bring the client straight back to rebuild, every reaper tick. The `presence` condition rules that out, so eviction never closes a socket.
- **Evicting a rebuilt actor before its socket attaches.** `Registry::attach` inserts the rebuilt actor and only then attaches the socket, and the reaper runs on another worker thread. Anchoring a rebuilt actor's window at `now_ms()` (step 2) makes every actor at least `REMATCH_OFFER_TTL_MS` old before it can be evicted.
- **A residual race.** A socket could attach to an evictable actor between `actor_for`'s map read and `actor.attach` (no `.await` in between). It is the same narrow race the ceiling reaper's `stop` already has, and it is accepted.
- **Lock order.** `reap_finished` locks each actor's core while holding the registry map's lock. No code takes the registry lock while holding a core lock: `forget` is called after `on_void`'s and `let_go_unrecorded`'s core locks are released. Keep it that way, and never `.await` inside the map's lock block.
- **Replay and determinism are untouched.** Nothing reads `finished_at` but the reaper. The log, `fold` and `telemetry::replay` don't change.

## Departures from the earlier (agy) plan, and decisions

- **No `JoinHandle` in `Shared`, no `abort` in `stop()`, no `is_queue_finished` or `is_stopped`.** `Shared` owns the inbox's only sender, and the queue, socket handlers, timers and clock all hold the actor weakly. So dropping the last `MatchActor` already ends `run_queue`. An abort would change `stop()` for every caller, and the test would still hold the actor, so it would prove nothing about memory. Instead the test uses `WeakMatchActor` plus tokio's `num_alive_tasks` (prototyped: exactly one fewer).
- **The no-open-socket condition is added.** Rejected alternative: evict on time alone (both plans' reading of "once its rematch window passes"). That closes sockets, which a client can see. The rejected "stricter variant" in the issue (evict at once when there is no socket and no offer) would make the registry read `api/rematch.rs`'s offers and fold again on every quick reconnect.
- **A rebuilt finished actor's window starts at the rebuild.** Rejected alternative: the row's `finished_at` (agy's plan). See Risks: that actor would be evictable at once. The restart that forces a rebuild also drops the offers, so their window starts again too.
- **`now` is a parameter of `reap_finished`.** That lets the tests hit the exact boundary without advancing the paused clock.
- **The backfill test lives in `tests/actor/telemetry.rs`**, which already has the backfill harness, not in `match_actor.rs`.
