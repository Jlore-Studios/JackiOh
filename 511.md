# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

A dev-only `/dev/replays` page lists the practice games kept on this device (part 2). When signed in, it also lists the account's online replays (part 3). It opens any of them in a new player.

The player reads only a `ReplaySource`. It draws `Game` with the step's view and no legal actions:
- No glows, no drag and no prompts; clicks change nothing. Inspect and hover still work.
- The notice slot holds seven controls, a scrubber and a "Step k of n · Turn t" readout.
- The arrow keys move a step; shift+arrow moves a turn.
- Play runs at the effects speed (R201).
- A step forward animates. Any other move remounts the board, so the log reports no gap.
- The last step shows both hands (R434).
- Pages are prefetched, each failure shows its message, and Back calls `onLeave`.

Production serves no replay route and ships no replay chunk. No production screen changes, and live `Game` behaves as before.
