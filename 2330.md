## Risks

- **The field must not ask the board for more height.**
  - A fixed cap on the field's track (`grid-template-rows: minmax(auto, <cap>)`) was tried in Chrome. It made the field's intrinsic height equal to the cap, and the board overflowed by 12 px at 1440×900.
  - Step 4's `height: 100%` plus `max-height` on the lanes left the board's height unchanged at every size measured.
  - Do not move the cap onto the field.
- **Never make `.zone`, `.lane` or `.field` a size or inline-size container.**
  - `--card-h` is an unregistered custom property holding `100cqh`/`100cqw`, so it resolves wherever it is used. A container in between would change every card height inside it.
  - That is why widths use `aspect-ratio`. It was checked on face-down backs (`container-type: size`) and on unrevealed traps (`inline-size`): 85×115 in a 220×117 zone at 1440×900.
- **Measured in Chrome with steps 4 and 5 injected on `/dev/hotseat`** (rows / lane width):
  - 1440×900: 117.1 / 220, with DEF at 114×98 and 7 px of mat, which is the padding plus the border.
  - 1280×720: 80.8 / 188.
  - 768×1024: 113.1 / 138.
  - 1920×1080: 157.7 / 316.
  - 820×1180 and 1024×1366 are capped at 135.6, with their lanes centred.
  - Phones are unchanged.

  Every card stayed in its zone except in near-square landscape windows 970-990 px wide, where a DEF footprint is up to 1.4 px wider than its lane. Report that edge in the PR; do not tune it.
- **Units get smaller where the field had no spare height.**
  - At 1280×720 a unit row goes from 102 px to 81 px, and the backrow from 68 to 81. That is what equal rows mean.
  - It can push a long minion name to its ellipsis in `meditative-faces.cy.tsx`'s board check, which allows that for minion plates.
  - If a component spec goes red, report it by name. Do not loosen it.
- **Animation.** positionSwitched plays over the view from before the switch. A CSS `scale` property on `[data-position="DEF"]` would therefore jump at the end of the turn on a phone. One inline transform shared with the keyframes avoids that.
