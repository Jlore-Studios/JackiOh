## Risks

- Double-counting with the static `cleave: 1.0` body weight: mitigated by design, since the static weight prices the body and `cleave_share` prices only board-dependent kills and pops; setting `cleave_share` to `1.0` counts each neighbour once, and tuning can lower it without code changes.
- `damage_past_taunts` is shared by `evaluate` under both `AI_EVAL` and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL`, and `simulate_reply` is shared by every simulated line: both sides of every comparison get the same smarter model, so only relative ordering moves; the gate floors confirm no regression.
- Splash amount deliberately differs between the two models: `trade_value` splashes `Exchange.splash` (bonus included, unmultiplied, as the engine deals), while `damage_past_taunts` splashes the raw attack figure it already carries (combat-only bonuses and lane strikes stay out of the threat term, still #1094's scope, built as #1106).
- Trample excess reaching the hero is counted for splashes in `damage_past_taunts` (symmetric with the main hit) but not in `trade_value` (symmetric with its main target, which prices no face excess).
- `cleaved_neighbours` mirrors the engine's `struck_by` (top card of each adjacent slot); a card dormant under a Stack pile is treated as struck, exactly as coarse as the engine's own projection.

## Open questions

None. Every design choice above follows the issue's proposal, the engine's `combat.rs`/`lethal.rs` reads, or the `pierce.rs` test precedent; the only judgment call (including Trample splash excess in the threat term) is spelled out in the steps.
