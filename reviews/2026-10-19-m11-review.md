# JackiOh review — 2026-10-19 — `8390d6f` (branch `claude/mplus-1186-1187`), SPEC at 989 ruling notes

**Scope:** Part A covers the whole spec against the five designer lists, `JackiOh_Mechanics.md` and `ARCHITECTURE-CCG.md`. Every set got a mechanical check; §8.9, §7's Meditative+ rows and the M11 additions to §2–§7, §10 and §11 were read clause by clause. Part B covers milestone M11, the Meditative+ release (issues #1186 and #1187, tracker #1173).

**Verdict: FAIL** (2 BLOCKER, 1 MAJOR, 10 MINOR)

**Result in brief.**
- **The spec and data are close to exact.** §8.9 matches the designer's list in every name, cost, stat, rarity, type and clause. The only differences are the recorded decisions (Dogpile, Bob is a Unit, Scratch Pad is Mythic) and two Radiant numbers that the designer's later ×2 rule lowered (A-1).
- **`catalog.json` matches §8.9 field for field,** declared numbers included.
- **The release tools are green:** catalog, patches, spec, golden (240/240), fuzz (1,000 plain, 1,000 handicapped, 200 Duos, the regression seed), sweep, the AI smoke gates, eslint, gen-music, and `cargo fmt --check` at 8390d6f.
- **All 30 Meditative+ scripts conform to their §8.9 rows.**

The release fails on two things:
- **Tests (B-1).** The workspace tests cannot pass at 8390d6f. Several tests still assert that Meditative+ is held, while `SHIPPED_SETS` lists it. Another session was committing the fixes, unfinished, while I reviewed.
- **Voice files (B-2).** None are rendered for the set, though R1420 says they are owed with the patch that ships it. `gen-voice --check` exits 1.
