# JackiOh review — 2026-10-11 — `d307681a` (branch `claude/vigilant-johnson-vh3bi5`), SPEC at R1–R1445 (785 notes)

**Scope:** Part A, limited to what M10 added: §8.8 (102 cards and 30 tokens), §7's Meditative rows, the Meditative additions to §2–§6 and §10, and the rulings R800–R1145, R1220–R1225, R1300–R1303, R1380–R1390, R1410–R1412 and R1420–R1445, plus the release's edits to older notes. Part B, milestone M10: the Meditative release (issue #553, patch v0.3.5). Its acceptance is BUILD.md's M10 rows and its end state is `docs/meditative-set.md` M10.

**Verdict: FAIL** (3 BLOCKER, one of them provisional; 5 MAJOR; 13 MINOR)

**Result in brief.** The spec port of the designer's list is very close to exact. A mechanical diff of all 133 source blocks (132 entries plus the doubled #27) against §8.8 finds every entry exactly once. Every cost, stat, rarity and tag matches, apart from departures that are recorded. `catalog.json` matches §8.8 field for field. All four release tools I ran are green: catalog check, patches check, spec check and golden check (240 of 240). `fuzz --seeds 200` is also green, with 370 deck-legal cards and 0 excluded.

The release fails on three things:
- **Voice lines (B-1).** The voice lines that R1420 says the shipping patch owes are missing. `gen-voice --check` exits 1 with 396 problems.
- **Economic Anxiety vs Blue-Eyes White Felinor (B-2).** One card behaviour contradicts §8.8: Economic Anxiety's +3 still applies against Blue-Eyes White Felinor.
- **Seed-pinned card tests (B-3, provisional).** Another session was re-pinning seed-pinned card tests while I worked. This strongly suggests some of those tests fail at the reviewed commit.
