# Issue #3941: the card text pass

2026-11-15, the deliverable [docs/issues-and-patches.md](../docs/issues-and-patches.md) (A task over every card) asks of a pass over every card: a row per card or group, its verdict and the reason. Pull request #3946.

## The measure

- **Terms and wording** (R3941000, `crates/cards/tests/cross/card_text.rs`): every English face of the catalog (978 entries, 1,956 faces) swept for a term not spelled as its label, a lower-case line start, a cost spliced onto an added card, "its owner's turn", a non-canonical hand or deck trigger opening, "Evolve: When", a lower-case stat and the retired wordings. On main before the pass: 35 faces failed; after: none. The Stinkyscape+ set, the newest and the source of most failures, was also read face by face (every base and Radiant face), which found the broken text the sweep cannot (Graphite, Scorch, Vela, Corvus, PIP, The Enigmata and the rest below).

- **Length** (R1017000's `FACE_TEXT_LIMIT`, 6 lines of about 28 characters): before, 135 faces clamped (their whole text cut by the rules box with "…"); after, 5 do, and 128 print words written for them (R3941001). Arc's base and Radiant faces fit after the wording pass, so they take no short form.

- **Keywords**: cards grouped by the flags and verbs their scripts set, and each group checked for a hand-built version of a §6.1 keyword. None collapses exactly; see *Keywords considered*.

- **Fused faces** (R3941002): every fusion's keyword lines print as one, by rule, so no per-card row.
