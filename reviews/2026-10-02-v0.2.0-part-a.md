# JackiOh review — 2026-10-02 — SPEC.md at patch v0.2.0 (`e762937`), §11 at R1–R602
Scope: Part A, patch v0.2.0 only: §8.6 Classic, §8.7 Classic+, §6's new keywords (Animated, Activate, Brittle, Degrade and Upgrade), §11 R380–R602 and the Core card patches of issue #40
Verdict: **PASS** (no BLOCKER, no MAJOR; 10 MINOR, 8 of them fixed in this pass, 2 listed under "Needs a decision")

Issue #67 (M9-T13). REVIEW.md Part A, run against `SPEC.md`, the designer's list `JackiOh_Classic_Cards.md`, its reading card by card `docs/classic-sets.md` (the brief), `JackiOh_Mechanics.md`, `JackiOh_Core_Cards.md` and `JackiOh_Tokens.md`, with issue #40 as the source of the Core card patches. Unlike the earlier Part A runs, this pass was asked to make the plain fixes in SPEC.md in the same change and to list, not decide, anything that needs a decision. Each finding says which it is. No §11 row was added, renumbered or removed, and no card behaviour or catalog data changed.

**Result.** v0.2.0's port of the brief into SPEC is close to exact. The mechanical diff of all 196 source entries against §8.6 and §8.7 finds every card and token once. Every cost, stat and rarity matches the source except where §5.3 or a §11 row records the departure. Every §-reference and R-reference resolves, and every count adds up. What the pass found is drift at the edges. One §8 Engine cell still described the trigger timing that R548 replaced, a ruling cited the brief's system number E11 where SPEC has no such thing, §5.3 under-reported the source's typos and numbering, and §10.6 did not say which prompt a cast's X uses. Two card readings follow the brief's default where the designer's words allow another reading, and the brief never put them to the designer.
