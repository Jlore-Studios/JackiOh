# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Every play's record keeps the outcomes it rolled:
- a Call to Chaos or pick-N list's entries, by table index;
- how many coins in a batch landed heads;
- a die's face, or whether it landed on the faces its card checks for.

A Recursion's rolls go to the play that started the chain. The totals add them up by face: each seat counts once per outcome per game, with roll counts and an expected count kept for the share. `GET /api/stats/cards/:id` returns each outcome with its label, Expected, Share, games, adjusted rate, range and tier, read against the card's other outcomes. The Extended stats panel shows them in Outputs under "Rolled effects", "Coin flips" or "Dice". The change adds rulings R4707000–R4707002 and edits §9.11.
