## Goal

The Duos gate (#936 B32) also fails when any game has an action that `duos_reduce` refused, on either team, even if every game was won. Zero refusals pass, as they do today. Three changes:

- `DuosGateReport` adds up the games' `rejected` counts, and `DuosGateReport::passes` needs that sum to be 0.
- Each `DuosGateGame` counts how many of its refusals came from the AI team (`ai_rejected`).
- The CLI verdict (`cargo jackioh gate --duos`) and the B32 crate test fail on a refusal. Their messages name each refused game's seed, the AI's team and the counts, so `duos_gate_game(n, series)` replays that game.
