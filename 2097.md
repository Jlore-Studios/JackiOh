# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

In the deck workshop (`/decks`), a deck's row in the list and its open editor show a badge when two things hold: its latest save came before the newest shipped patch, and it holds cards that patch changed. The badge reads "2 cards changed in v0.4.13" (or "1 card changed in …"). Pressing the badge lists exactly those cards, each with what changed, through the patch history's `diff.ts` and `ChangeList.tsx`. A flavor-only change does not count (R1437000), and a deck with no changed card shows nothing. Once the deck is saved under the newest patch, so that it carries that catalog version, the badge goes. The plan adds ruling R2097000, and Vitest covers every acceptance item.
