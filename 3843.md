## Goal

The server sends at most `DAILY_PLAYER_EMAILS_PER_DAY` (50) daily-game "your move" emails per UTC day. The count is shared by the sweep and the after-move queue (`NotifyQueue`) and is kept in the store, so a restart does not reset it. Past the cap, email is skipped for the rest of that UTC day and push still goes out. A player whose email was held back gets it the next day if a game still waits on them (the normal gap rule). The skip is logged once per day as `daily.email.capped`, not once per player.
