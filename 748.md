# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

The ladder bots' seed ratings are set from measurement instead of by hand; today they are hand-set in `BOT_STRENGTHS`. The strongest strength is the reference bot.

- **Offsets:** `jackioh-server bot-calibrate` plays every pair of strengths, 30+ games each, and fits each strength's Elo offset from the reference.
- **Anchor:** `bot-calibrate-live` measures the reference's own rating from its rated games against people.
- **Seeds** are the anchor plus each offset.
- **Re-calibration:** after an AI promotion, `bot-calibrate` shifts the anchor by the later promotions' `vsParent` Elo and plays the games again.
- **Ceiling gate:** a player rated above every bot never gets one. A test proves it, and the docs name it as the gate.

The protocol, the measured results and ruling R748000 are checked in. Live R610 ratings keep correcting between calibrations.
