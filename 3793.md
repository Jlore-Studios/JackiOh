An enemy or own unit whose Brittle tick destroys it at the start of its controller's next turn, before it can swing, counts as no attack. It adds 0 to `face_threat`, and `could_attack_next_turn` rejects it, so `stands_down` and `wall_switches` offer no switch on its account.
