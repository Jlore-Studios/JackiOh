# Plan (opus, strong)

**Goal.** In Duos, an AI seat passes a card its teammate could play now only when the pass is worth it: the teammate's gain from the card must beat the card's worth in the seat's own hand plus a margin for the pass cost. Done when:
1. A seat whose card the teammate can't use better than a play it already has ends the turn without passing.
2. A clearly profitable pass (cheap pass cost, big teammate follow-up) still goes.
3. `cargo jackioh fuzz --seeds 200 --duos` and `cargo jackioh gate --duos` stay green.
4. §13.7 is amended by ruling R1723000.

The issue's literal "pass_score > keep value" can't work, because `pass_score` is an absolute `evaluate` of the teammate's whole position, not a gain. So the gate compares the teammate's value *with* the card against its own best play *without* it.
