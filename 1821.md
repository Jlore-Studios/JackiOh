# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Today the practice AI's move order (`in_move_order` in `crates/ai/src/candidates.rs`) counts an attack as a killing even trade only when its target dies. After this change it also counts one when its Cleave splash kills a neighbour. Such an attack then gets the #1779 spot (each attacker's best even trade, ahead of the plays) instead of sitting with the other attacks.

Done when:
1. A Cleave attack whose only kills are splashed neighbours passes `even_trade`.
2. An ordering test puts such an attack ahead of the plays.
3. `cargo jackioh gate` (smoke size) passes with no regression.

The gate stays a kill gate: a chip attack that kills nothing is still not an even trade. The issue rules out relaxing it to `value > 0`.
