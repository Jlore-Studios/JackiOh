## Risks

- Floor drift between SQL and Rust: avoided by passing the floor as a bind computed from `tier_bottom(GrapeTier::Mythic as i32)`; the index names no tier and no number.
- Bot rows: the subset query copies the full read's `coalesce(bot_ratings, profiles)` join and `bot` flag, so a ladder bot's row keeps its own rating; Jlorious still filters bots in Rust, unchanged.
- Pool planner (`bots/pool.rs`) needs every person's rating for grind targets, so it deliberately stays on the full read; changing it would corrupt grind planning.
- The gain is proportional to season size and small while the ladder is small; the index adds one write-time cost per rank upsert.
- `ranked_standings_subset` as a name is a planner choice; the alternative `ranked_subset` was rejected as less discoverable beside `ranked_standings`.
