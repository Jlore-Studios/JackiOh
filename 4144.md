## Goal

Add a per-PR economy-bands test to `crates/tools/src/run_sim.rs`, beside the tiers test and built on the same harness. It fails with a named band break when:

- an ordinary winning run cannot afford the cheapest shop card by its first shop;
- the coins or tickets a run earns, or the Remove, Radiant and buff prices, leave their bands;
- the score or the Embers a run pays leave their bands.

## Risks

- **Flakiness.** All inputs are seeded: the run seed, the policy's own `Rng` stream and the AI at `TEST_BUDGET`. Stub-won runs involve no AI. The bands are 2× wide, and the measured figures sit in their doc comments.
- **Shop reachability.** The random policy may dodge shops in some runs. Hence `ECONOMY_RUNS = 24` and the `SHOP_RUNS_MIN_PERCENT` band. If fewer than half of the 24 runs reach a shop at the pinned seeds, raise `ECONOMY_RUNS` (the runs are cheap), not the policy.
- **Winning stub versus the AI.** Coins and tickets depend on the fight kinds won and on relic passives, not on how the fight was played, so an all-won stub run measures what an ordinary winning run earns. The score and Embers need real reports, so they come from AI runs. A header comment on the bands says the AI plays better or worse than a person, so the bands guide tuning and do not set targets.
- **Test time.** The AI whole runs may be slower than the tiers test; see step 8 for the cap.
- **The pure-crate rule.** `crates/run` stays untouched, and the tools crate may use the disk and clock, though this code uses neither.
