**Decisions taken, with the alternatives rejected:**
- **The deal.** Banning the card from both deals and replacing one seeded slot, rather than the sweep's `include`. That alternative would re-deal the holder's deck when the card's cost changes, and pairing would then compare different deals.
- **Where the deal lives.** In `dev_run.rs`, rather than a copy of the deal and the record in tools.
- **`--before`/`--after` flags,** rather than two positional lists that clap cannot split.
- **`--card` read literally,** rather than resolving tokens: `resolve_forced`'s errors say `--force`.
- **Games where neither or both seats hold the card are left out,** rather than counting both seats: a game where both hold it is 50% by construction.
- **Default series `dev-force-<id>`,** rather than `dev`, whose ids would collide in `stats-import`.
