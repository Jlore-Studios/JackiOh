## Risks

- **Timing is behaviour.** The three clock steps are different:
  - plain `tokio::time::advance`;
  - advance plus one yield (`recovery`, `series_recovery`);
  - sleep plus 8 yields (`aim`, `clock`, `match_actor`).

  Do not merge them. Do not route them through `support::socket::settle` (64 yields) or the `settle` in `chat` and `duos` (256). `SLEEP_SETTLE_YIELDS` must stay 8.
- **`fake_ref` is not `fake`.** With an owned `Arc`, `let g = fake(&app).lock().await;` borrows a temporary and does not compile. Keep the 5 `&Arc` files on `fake_ref`.
- **The order of the `store!` arms matters** (arrow, closure, `Db`): a later arm is tried only after the earlier ones fail to match a token.
