# Plan (muse-spark-1.3-contributor, medium)

## Goal

While a `Game` is mounted, the document never scrolls or overscrolls on touch: no vertical/horizontal page scroll from a board swipe, no rubber-band bounce, no pull-to-refresh, and no movement at all during a drag or aim. Taps, long-press inspect, card drags and drag-to-attack keep working. Each selection menu (Discover/card pickers, mulligan, `LanePick`, `MarketPrompt`/`Marketplace`, pile browser, match menu, `Dialog`, settings) keeps scrolling inside itself, as does the game log. Unmounting restores normal page scrolling. The lock stays on the page while a game is hosted and every menu scrolls in its own scroller (the simpler of the two options the issue allows).
