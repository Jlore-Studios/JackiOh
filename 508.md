# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

`replay_open` in `crates/engine/src/replay.rs` folds a finished game once. It refuses with `earlier_patch` (another catalog version) or `rules_changed` (a final-hash mismatch), and otherwise returns the step count and checkpoints. `replay_page` returns steps `[from, from+count)` as `{ step, turn, view_for(state_k, seat) }`, capped at `REPLAY_PAGE_STEPS`, and reports at most `REPLAY_CHECKPOINT_EVERY` `reduce` calls. Both are exposed through WASM and the TS wrapper, the two constants are exported, and R768 records the ruling. `GameState`, `fold`'s behaviour, `reduce`, `view_for`, hashes and golden traces do not change.
