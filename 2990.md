# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Watchers on `/ws/watch` can send emoji emotes. This works for a 1v1 and for a co-op game. A watcher's emote is relayed live as `spectatorEmote {watcher, emote}` to every person's seat socket and to every other watcher, and never back to the sender. It never reaches `reduce`, the log, the hash, a record, telemetry, a hearing card (R1127) or a bot persona (R645).

- **Hand:** each watcher is dealt a hand of emoji only, from the match seed and the watcher's account.
- **Rate limit:** watchers have their own, stricter limit from new `crates/server/src/config.rs` constants. The client's menu greys out from the same numbers, read from `serverConfig.ts`.
- **Malformed and dropped emotes:** an id outside the pool is answered `malformed`. An emote outside the hand, or past the limit, is dropped silently. A block between the watcher and any recipient keeps the emote from that recipient.
- **Client:** a sideline strip on `Game.tsx` shows each watcher's newest emote by name. It has a per-match mute and a device setting, `muteSpectatorEmotes`. It shows in watched, live and co-op games and works on the phone layout.
