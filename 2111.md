# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Each of the backrow's three moments gets its own deliberate motion. All of it is client-side.

- **Set:** whatever lands in a backrow zone is laid down: a short drop that lands flat and heavy, with no bounce and no flash. This plays on the board's silhouette and on the effects layer's stand-in, and the card lands where its existing thud already sounds.
- **Wait:** every face-down card breathes one slow breath. That covers a back on either board and your own face-down trap under its veil. One rule draws it with no per-card value, so nothing about a card can be read from its motion.
- **Flip:** `trapFired` opens with a beat of anticipation. The card, still face-down, sinks and dims, then turns, and the effects burst moves to the new reveal.

Reduced motion stops all three: the media query, the settings panel and the effects setting. Ruling R2111000 records the change in §10.10.
