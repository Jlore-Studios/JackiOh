**Goal.** A player can replace a held daily quest up to `QUEST_REPLACES_PER_DAY` times per UTC day. This is a new constant, set to 1.

- **The route.** `POST /api/quests/:questId/replace` works for an active account while `ECONOMY_LIVE` is on. It runs one transaction under the account's economy lock and writes two rows: the held quest's `Removed` row, then an `Arrived` row for the replacement.
- **The draw.** The replacement is drawn the way `top_up` draws an arrival: uniformly among the pool entries the account does not hold, with the replaced quest excluded and a fresh seed logged in `ref`.
- **The rhythm.** The arrival rhythm does not change. The replaced quest's progress is lost.
- **The answer.** The route answers the quest book plus `replacesLeft`. `GET /api/quests` answers `replacesLeft` too.
- **Refusals.** These are refused and write nothing: a second replace on the same day, a quest the account does not hold, a guest, and any call while the economy is off. When two replaces arrive at once, at most one lands, on both stores.
- **The panel.** The quests panel (on `/collection` and in the landing's bookmark) shows Replace on each quest while a replace is left, behind a confirm. It redraws from the server's answer.
- **The ruling.** R2210000 records all of this.
