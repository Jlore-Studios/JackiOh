# Plan (sonnet, medium)

## Goal

Every `cardPlayed` the viewer can read gets a supplementary `playImpact` sound under its voice line and sting. That means a Unit, Spell or Field Spell, but not a Trap, a hidden card or a card cast as it is drawn. The impact is sized by `costPaid` in four tiers (0–2 light, 3–5 medium, 6–8 heavy, 9+ massive) and coloured by type. A Trap's first firing gets a `trapSnap`. An opponent's play is heard a step quieter.

A heavy play shakes the board and a massive one shakes it hard. The shake goes through `FxShakeCue`, scales with the intensity setting, and is off under reduced motion. A phone also buzzes by tier. `/dev/fx` plays every tier and type side by side. The summon thud stays the stats tier (`UNIT_SLAM`). The change is web-only, with no engine, crate, wire or server change.
