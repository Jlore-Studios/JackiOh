## Goal

The invariant monitor used by the fuzz and the scenario tests gets a new check, **I7, no orphaned work**. It runs after every action, setup's included. When the game is live and no prompt is open, `state.work` must be empty and `state.work_cursor` must be 0. "No prompt open" means `state.pending` is `None` and the mulligans (`state.mulligan`) are closed. Any other state is reported as an `I7 orphaned work` finding.
