## Goal

Ensure that a Unit's Trample keyword only inflicts excess damage on a hero when that Unit is attacking, bringing Trample in line with its Magic: The Gathering origins. A Unit being attacked that strikes back in combat deals its full attack to the attacking Unit and deals zero Trample damage to the attacking hero; a Unit attacking an allied Unit (such as through Traitorous Blood) deals Trample excess to its own hero; and Spells printing Trample (or effects explicitly stating `trample: true`) continue to trample.
