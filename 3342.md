# Plan (sonnet, medium)

## Goal

Every face-up Legendary or Mythic permanent on the board gets a tile aura under its card, in the colours of its own picture. Legendary gets a soft edge glow and slow motes. Mythic gets a richer pattern chosen by its set's look: runes, starfield, roots, circuitry or ink. A card's own table `aura` (signature) replaces the default. A touch on the card or its tile sends a ripple, plays a soft bell in the aura's colour, and buzzes on a phone. The aura brightens while the card is picked as a target, flickers on damage, dims while Dormant, Frozen or Silenced, and fades out with its `destroyed` or `exiled` row. It stays under the particle budgets, is still under reduced motion, and is off at fx intensity off. It is presentation only: no engine, wire or catalog change.

Design choice: the aura is a DOM element inside each `.zone`, not another canvas layer. `AuraLayer` is a fixed overlay above the cards and cannot sit under them. `.card` is `position: relative`, so an absolutely positioned first child of `.zone` paints below it.
