# Plan (sonnet, medium)

## Goal

An Embiggened Wall's bonus is worked out from the card's printed face and not from `face_of`, and it is none while a Set stats has replaced its stat line. These are the results:
- A Wall set to new stats stands at exactly those stats, on both faces: Harmony Spirit's 3/3 gives 3/3, and Dino-size's 10/10 gives 10/10.
- A stat Buff or Nerf (`tuning.attack` and `tuning.health`) moves the Wall once, through layer 1, including one the card took in hand before it was played.
- An unmodified Embiggened Wall is still 0/32 (0/64 Radiant) and GIGA, a Vanilla still returns it to its printed stats and keeps its size, and Phase Wall still refuses a set.

The change is written up as ruling R3935000 (amending R3555001) and shipped as a card patch. The old script is kept for replays of older versions (R767000).
