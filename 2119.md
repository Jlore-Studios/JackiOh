## Goal

Deleting a deck, trio or custom card, and removing a friend, take the item off the screen at once. The three confirms and Remove's inline question go. Undo restores the item and sends nothing. A failed request restores the item and shows the server's words. A held deck is never saved and no trio shows it; its trio slots come back on Undo. Concede and Block keep their confirms. New ruling R2119000.
