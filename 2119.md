# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Deleting a deck, trio or custom card, and removing a friend, take the item off the screen at once. The three confirms and Remove's inline question go. In their place a `role="status"` toast shows for `UNDO_WINDOW_MS`, saying "Deck deleted · Undo", "Trio deleted · Undo", "Card deleted · Undo" or "Friend removed · Undo".

The request waits for the window to end. It goes at once in three cases:
- the page hides (sent with `keepalive`);
- the player leaves the screen;
- a second delete comes, which also replaces the toast.

Undo restores the item and sends nothing. A failed request restores the item and shows the server's words. A held deck is never saved and no trio shows it; its trio slots come back on Undo. Concede and Block keep their confirms. New ruling R2119000.
