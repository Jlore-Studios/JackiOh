# Plan (sonnet, medium)

## Goal

Four things in a dungeon run:

1. **Shop prices.** Each shop service shows its price in the shop's own terms: the remove price after relic discounts, the Radiant and buff Ticket prices, and each relic's next-level price (none at its top level). The stock shows what Buy will charge, discount included. A service button is disabled when the run cannot pay, and the run still decides.
2. **Campfire count.** The campfire titles the removal "Remove {count} cards" and ignores picks past that count. Its button is enabled only at exactly that count.

4. **End screen.** The run's end screen shows its time and its turns beside the score.

All prices and counts reach the web through the WebAssembly module (CLAUDE.md rule 9).
