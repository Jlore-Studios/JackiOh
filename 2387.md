# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

`cargo jackioh fuzz --cards <id,…|set>` deals the named cards into both decks of every seed of the gate's wave and of the handicapped wave (`--handicap`). Forcing goes through `golden.rs`'s shared helpers (`resolve_force`, `force_into`): a forced card takes a first slot in place of a card from the end, the deck keeps its size and holds no card twice, a token goes in through the card whose `refs` name it, an unknown id is refused with a message, and R2195000's one-of-a-pair rule holds. The summary, `--json` and every failure name the forced cards. A failure's `reproduce:` line (`--from N --seeds 1 [--handicap] --cards …`) replays its seed. With no `--cards`, every seed is dealt and played exactly as before. `docs/ADDING_CARDS.md` recommends the flag for card work.
