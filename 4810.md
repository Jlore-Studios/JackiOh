The opponent's imagined reply (`simulate_reply_with`, `crates/ai/src/reply.rs`) must keep steps for its attacks and always end its turn:

- Known plays and visible Activates together take at most a new `AI_REPLY.play_steps` (6) of the reply's `AI_REPLY.max_steps` (12) steps.
