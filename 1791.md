# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Right now, if an attack is in flight (stage `"attacking"`) and the player clicks a different unit that `legal` lists as an attacker, nothing happens. After this change, that click switches the attack to the new unit: `{ stage: "attacking", attackerId: <new>, candidates: <its attacks> }`. If R1200 applies (the unit's only attack is a random-target one), the attack is sent at once instead. Four things stay as they are:
- A click on a target of the picked attacker still sends that attack, and the target reading wins over the switch.
- A second click on the picked attacker still drops it.
- A click on a unit with no legal attack leaves the interaction unchanged.
- Legality still comes only from `legal` (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
