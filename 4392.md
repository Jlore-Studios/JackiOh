## Risks

- **Out-of-contract events.** Feeding a timer or a new action while a write is held would give false failures. Keep to Step 7's allowed list.
- **Rebuild shape.** The rebuild must mirror `match_actor.rs` and `duos/actor.rs`. In particular `finished` comes from the store, not the engine, and `Voided` leaves the row unfinished. That is why Step 7 ends the match on `Voided` instead of rebuilding.
- **Id collisions across a restart.** Timer ids restart at a rebuild, so `Timers::clear()` must run at every rebuild, or a stale id would match a live one.
