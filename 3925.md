# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

One engine rule ends every endless loop (§10.3, **R3925000**). An interaction (an action, a timeout's answer, an autopilot or AI action, a turn the engine ends) emits at most `RESOLUTION_EVENT_CAP` events, every `GameEvent` counted. At the cap:
- the trigger queue and every event not yet offered are dropped;
- a public `loopCapped` event is emitted;
- a last state check collects the dead without Death, Reborn or a replacement, and checks the heroes;
- card text stays off until the next interaction.

The cap's value is measured on the fuzz (plain, handicap, duos), golden, perf and gate corpora. Play can no longer reach `STATE_CHECK_PASS_CAP` or `MAX_WORK_STEPS`. The spec, BUILD §2 and M5-T4, REVIEW A3.6 and R2197004 are updated. `view_for` carries the event, and the turn banner reads "Loop stopped".
