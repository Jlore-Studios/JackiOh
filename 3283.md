## Goal

`cargo jackioh fuzz`, in both 1v1 waves, starts a share of its games the way a dungeon fight does (`run_fight_setup`, R2702000, R2922000, R2922001). Those games get deck marks (some cards dealt Radiant or Buffed), a drawn hero health and Armor, extra opening cards and crystals, and decks of 10 to 60 cards. They go through every existing check, and the fold and checkpoint take the same inputs.

- **Share and stability:** `RUN_START_SHARE = 0.15` of seeds start this way. Every other seed starts exactly as before.
- **Counts and replay:** the summary's `starts:` line and `--json`'s `starts` count the new start as `run`. A failure prints its handicaps and marks. `--from N --seeds 1` replays the seed unchanged, because everything is drawn from the seed.
