## Goal

When a Spell or Field Spell is cast at random (or driven with `targetEnemies`), any `target` prompt opened for its caster during resolution filters options through `targetable_options` before narrowing (`friends_only`, `prefer_enemies`, `prefer_friends`) or drawing a random answer.
