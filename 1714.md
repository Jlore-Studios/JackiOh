**Goal.** `scorer::concealed_copy` is the scratch state that the §10.7 dry run, `dry_run_base` (Zephyrs, C+ #27, pareto) and the autopilot's trials play on. Today it still carries hidden records. After this change it carries none of them: no `opening` decklists, no Glitch boards, no last board of the other seat, no unrevealed choice in the other seat's secrets, no `__answerKey` anywhere and no secret modes in the other seat's in-flight play. Its stand-ins also drop every field the AI's placeholder drops, and Rollback's snapshots hide the other seat's face-down traps. Done means:
- a guess tried on the copy judges nothing;
- an answer trial is not ranked by the real key;
