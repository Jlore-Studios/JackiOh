# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Online matches leave play telemetry for the ladder bots (#636). Three new server-only tables hold it: `action_timings`, `emote_events` and `match_signals`, keyed by match id and seat, with no profile id and no client grant. The match actor records think times, emotes and end-of-game signals in memory. It writes them in one batch right after the result commits, and a failed write is logged and never costs the result.

Four more pieces come with it:

- `jackioh-server timing-backfill` folds the `match_actions` logs still held into the same `action_timings` rows the live path writes.
- `jackioh-server timing-fit` prints log-normal parameters per bucket, leaving out `pilot = 'ai'` rows.
- The rows are covered by the retention purge and by account deletion.
- Ruling R1442 records the decisions, and the privacy policy names the telemetry.
