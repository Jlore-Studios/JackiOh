# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

The animated rarity sheen (the sweep on every Mythic face and every Radiant face, `data-foil="animated"`) stops drawing a dark triangle at a face's corner each time the band enters or leaves it, and keeps its cost on a full hand and field: one layer per face, moved by `transform` alone, with no extra work per frame. Done means the sweep's moving layer is composited without a blend mode, its band keeps about the brightness it has now, the still foil is unchanged, and `apps/web/src/cards/foil.test.ts` holds all of it.

**What the planner found (headless Chrome 141, ANGLE/SwiftShader GL, a copy of the sweep's CSS in `/tmp`, not in the repo):**
- The triangle shows only when a face is **rotated**, as every card in the home screen's fan is (`apps/web/src/routes/landing.css`, `.landing-fan-slot`, `rotate(±11deg, ±22deg)`). That is why it is "most evident on the home screen". It shows as a dark wedge past the rounded corner (top-left as the band comes in, bottom-right as it leaves, which is where the loop restarts) and a dark strip along the face's leading edge.
- It comes from `mix-blend-mode: overlay` on the moving layer. The same page with `mix-blend-mode: normal` draws nothing outside the clip and no dark strip. `screen` has the same wedges as `overlay`. Moving the sweep into `.cf-scale` behind a `clip-path` cut the band with hard straight lines.
- Cost: 40 animated faces at 1920×1080, frames drawn in 5 s (two runs each): no sweep 299/301; current overlay sweep 136/144; the same sweep with normal blending 172/180. Dropping the blend adds about 25% more frames, so the "< 5% degradation" acceptance holds with room to spare. (SwiftShader runs on the CPU, so these figures show the direction of the change, not a GPU's frame times.)
