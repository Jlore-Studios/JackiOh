## Risks

- **Replay placement.** The pack, token or bar is gone on a retry, and the account is already active. The lookup must sit right after the lock, before every check, or the retry answers 409.

- **Keyless path.** It gets no new lock and no new store call. `codes.rs` counts store calls for R107 timing.
- **R107.** Every redemption outcome, the replay and the 422 included, stays a padded `RedeemOutcome`.
