# Plan (sonnet, medium)

## Goal

`face_threat_with` should count an attack as able to reach the hero only when `attack_restriction` lets its Unit hit it. While The Gauntlet (S #157, R1849005) acts, a Unit in lanes 2 to 4 then adds no face damage. This applies to the AI's board and the enemy's, and The Gauntlet may sit in either seat's backrow. The read sits behind a new `EvalWeights` flag, on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`. The flag is the issue's own proposal, and it keeps the greedy yardstick frozen. Without a Gauntlet on the field, `face_threat` is unchanged.

Acceptance criteria covered:
- Gauntlet up with the enemy's Units in lanes 2 to 4: `face_threat` is 0 and `wall_switches` offers no stand-down.
- A Unit in lane 1 or 5: its attack counts.
- No Gauntlet: no change.
- `GREEDY_EVAL`'s numbers are unchanged, and `cargo jackioh gate` passes at smoke size.
