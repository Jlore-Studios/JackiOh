## Goal

At each decision's root, every Death the seat can read is priced once: each unit on either field with a live Death and each Unit in the AI's own hand with one. The probe runs on a sandbox copy of decide's probe world. The price is the controller's evaluation after the sandbox's destroy (Death run) less after its remove (no Death, R923003).
