# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

The six economy spend routes each take one of the account's in-memory spend slots before they touch the store: `POST /api/packs/buy`, `/api/packs/open`, `/api/tokens/redeem-card`, `/api/tokens/redeem-cosmetic`, `/api/cosmetic-packs/open` and `/api/cosmetic-packs/chase`. A spend sent while all `ECONOMY_SPENDS_IN_FLIGHT_MAX` slots are held gets an immediate 429 `rate_limited` that spends nothing, so a burst can no longer fill the pool. The client keeps a press's key on a 429, and its pack and Buy buttons wait while any spend of the tab is out; a refusal shows as the server's words. Seeding packs at purchase (item 2) is rejected: no migration, and §14.11's fresh-seed sentence stays.

**The reproduction comes from reading the code, not from a run.**
- The pool holds 10 connections (`db/pg.rs` `POOL_MAX_DEFAULT`) with a 10 s checkout timeout.
- Each opening's `app.db.begin` takes one, then waits in `pg_advisory_xact_lock` (`economy/store.rs` `lock_account`).
- Twenty concurrent opens hold all 10 on one lock.
- Every other request then times out waiting for a connection (`PoolTimedOut`), which becomes `ApiError::internal`: a 500 logged as `handler.threw`.
