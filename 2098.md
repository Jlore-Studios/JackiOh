# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Every glossary term that `RulesText` draws as `strong.cf-term` shows its glossary line (`GLOSSARY[id].label` and `.rule`, word for word) in a tooltip. That holds on the board and in the hand (`game/Card.tsx`), in the Almanac's and the deck editor's pool (`PoolGrid`), and in the inspect views (detail view, touch sheet, a pile's dialog).
- **Mouse or pen:** hovering a term shows the tooltip and leaving it hides it.
- **Touch:** a long press shows it. An ordinary tap still plays, aims, selects or opens the card. The term's long press takes over from the card's.
- **Tooltip:** it takes no pointer events, so it never blocks play.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** where the surface allows a control (detail view, sheet, pile dialog), the term is a tab stop. Focus opens the tooltip, which is `role="tooltip"` and linked to the term by `aria-describedby`.
- **Tests:** Vitest covers hover, long press and an ordinary tap. The change gets ruling R2098000.
