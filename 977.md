## Risks

- **Determinism and replay.** The hook clones the rng only when a timing hook is set, and re-timing works on that clone and a borrowed state. The game, its log and its hash do not change. b26's test still compares hashes with and without the hook.
- **A real re-timer under `catch_unwind`.** A panic in re-timing would count as a controller error. It would need the same deterministic `decide` that just succeeded to fail, so this is not expected.
- **Run time.** Each flagged decision costs up to 3 more runs of `decide` plus 3 yardsticks (2 random games each). A decision that runs for minutes (meditative-091 Windfast) costs three times as long again. That is the method the issue asks for; record it in the PR.
- **Older slices.** `#[serde(default)]` keeps the 2026-10-12 lines readable.
- **JSON shape.** The shape changes by one key. Only the round-trip test pins it, and step 5 updates its helper.
- **Lifetimes.** If a closure fails with "not general enough", write it inline in the `SweepOptions` literal. Keep the `&SlowDecision<'_>` annotation.
