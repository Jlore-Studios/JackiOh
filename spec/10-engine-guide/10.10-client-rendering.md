### 10.10 Client rendering

The client renders `viewFor` and animates from the event stream (each event type patch v0.2.0 added has its BUILD M5-T4 row too): `summoned` → card flies to zone; `damage` → number pops, shake; `damageAbsorbed` → a steel glint and a shield ([[R1363]]); `armorGained` → the hero's Armor badge ticks under a full shield ([[R3857000]]); `armorWorn` → the hero's Armor badge ticks, no effect of its own, the hit that wore it carrying its own ([[R5183000]]); `damageNullified` → the pool's card pulses and a mote carries the soak to the hit's target, where a small shield blooms with the nullified number ([[R5045000]]); `destroyed` → dissolve; `radiantSet` → glow; `attackDeclared` → lunge; `promptOpened` → modal; `controlChanged` → slide across the center line; `positionSwitched` → rotate 90°; `fatigue` → the deck pile (the library, [[R373]]) comes up empty under a "Fatigue N" badge before the hit lands; `burned` → the card burns over the full hand under "Hand full"; `libraryOverflow` → "Deck full" on the pile ("Ceased to exist" for a `ceased` card, [[R4821002]]) as the refused card fizzles or drops to the graveyard ([[R318]]); `drawLimited` → "Draw limit" on that Deck pile, `attackCancelled` → "Canceled" on the attacker, `countered` → "Countered" on the held card or the pile it went to, and `turnCutShort` and `autopilotEngaged` → the turn banner's words, all drawn by the board with the effects on or off ([[R3910000]]); `loopCapped` → the turn banner's "Loop stopped" while its entry plays, drawn the same way ([[R3925000]]). Keep a table `eventType → animation` so each animation has an acceptance criterion.
