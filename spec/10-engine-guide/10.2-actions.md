### 10.2 Actions

`reduce(state, action, rng) → { state, events, error? }`. Action types: `mulligan {keep[]}`, `play {instanceId, zone?, x?, embiggen?, equip?, tributes[], targets[], modes[], faceDown?}` ([[R81]], its choices validated per [[R90]]; `zone` is `{row, lane, player?}`, `player` the side it is on, absent the player's own, another side accepted only for a card that plays on either side ([[R5238002]]); `equip` plays an Equip card onto one of its controller's Units ([[R1086]]), and `faceDown`, `'cloaked'`, sets a card with Cloaked (printed, or lent by M #45's, M #45.1's or M #99's Aura) face-down in the backrow as a Trap that reveals only by its Activate: Reveal ([[R783004]], [[R783006]], [[R1040]], [[R1044]]); the wire's three timings `'endOfThisTurn'|'startOfNextTurn'|'endOfNextTurn'` were ME-ALTPLAY's, offered by nothing since patch v0.3.7; it may also carry a graveyard card under a play-from-graveyard permission and the Plague Counters that pay part of C #74's price; C #89's targeting cost discards at random as it is paid, so no action carries its discards, [[R682]]), `attack {attackerId, targetId}`, `switchPosition {instanceId}`, `activate {instanceId, ability?, targets[], modes[], tributes[]}` ([[R384]]; `activatePower {instanceId, targets[]}` is kept as its alias so old logs replay, [[R43]]), `answer {choiceId, selection}`, `offerDraw`, `answerDraw {accept}`, `emote {emote}` (an action only while the other seat controls a card that hears emotes, M #71's Radiant face; online the server mints it from a relayed emote and never takes one on the action channel, [[R1127]]), `concede`, `endTurn`, `trade {instanceId}` (a Tradable hand card into its owner's deck at random for a draw, at no mana, one a turn, [[R1838000]]), `marketBuy {offer}` (a copy of the lot at that index of the lots offered to the sender bought, [[R2250002]], for Credits and no mana, in the sender's own main phase, [[R1853003]]), `marketSell {instanceId}` (kept so older logs read, always refused: nothing sells, [[R4410000]]), `setAutoEndTurn {enabled}` (the sender's own [[R82]] preference: accepted from either seat at any moment of a live game, it emits nothing and `legalActions` never offers it, [[R345]]), plus server-only `timeout` (answers the open prompt of the player whose clock expired with the AI policy — a night market's Leave and a question sequence's Stop excepted, [[R1002]], [[R1184001]] — and ends the turn only when that is the active player; while the mulligans are open it answers that player's own mulligan by keeping the whole hand, [[R268]]), `disconnectExpired` (that player loses) and `ceilingReached` (the match ends in a draw), all per [[R79]]. Every action carries `playerId` and `nonce`. `legalActions(state, playerId)` is exported and is what both the client UI and the My Pawn AI consume; it lists `activate` from the same check that refuses one ([[R384]]); every play `reduce` accepts is listed, the zone and the choices read by the same checks on both sides ([[R90]]). And every action it lists `reduce` accepts: the fuzz sends the listed actions, a spread capped by `I14_ACTION_CAP` of a long list, to `reduce` on a copy of the state, fails the seed on a refusal (#3062), and checks the resulting state and events with the stateless invariants (I5, I7, I10, I11, I16 and I17; #4057), and I19 and I21 against the state it was reduced from (#4454, #4508).
