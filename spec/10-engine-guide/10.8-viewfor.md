### 10.8 `viewFor(state, playerId)`

Returns the full public board, the viewer's hand and pending options (of the other seat's prompt, only that it is open and a card picker's count, [[R2739000]]; while the mulligans are open, the viewer's own mulligan until it answers, then only whether each player has answered and what the viewer kept, [[R266]]), the standing draw offer ([[R269]]), the viewer's own draw-offer block while a declined offer keeps them from offering, as `drawOfferBlockedTurns`, the number of their turns it still covers (the one under way when it is theirs, never the turn of the decline), and never the opponent's ([[R36]]), the viewer's own automatic-turn-end preference when they have turned it off ([[R345]]), counts for the opponent's hand and both libraries, the viewer's own library as a list without order ([[R310]]: each definition and face with a count, sorted by printed cost, then name, then id, never an instance id or a position; cards the viewer was never shown only as a count of unknown cards, [[R311]], [[R312]]), face-down markers for the opponent's backrow (traps show as unknown but carry their cost, the number the controller's own view shows, a deliberate reveal, [[R351]]; Field Spells are public), the viewer's own face-down traps marked `unrevealed` ([[R351]], [[R371]]), both graveyards and exile piles in full, mana, health, armor, clocks and the last N events for animation. A trap that changes control becomes visible to its new controller only ([[R33]]); a Field Trap that has fired is public. A fired trap's `trapFired` event names it to each player who may read it where it is now: in a graveyard, an exile pile or face-up on the field to both, in a library to neither, in a hand to that hand's player ([[R154]], [[R763]], [[R2734000]]). The events up to a Glitch's reset or boards name no card the state no longer holds ([[R764]]). A card revealed out of a library (KY's Private Tutor) is revealed only as an option of the prompt that reveals it: the chooser sees it in full, the opponent sees only that a prompt is open ([[§10.6]]), and the rest of the library stays hidden from the opponent, and from the chooser beyond what their own list already says ([[R310]]). An open Black Hole is public on both seats as `blackHole` (its lane, what it has swallowed, its capacity) and a slash as `slashedLane`, both absent otherwise; a chained Unit carries its `sharedChain` or `splitChain`, and a line damage its public `lineDrawn` (positions only) ([[R3554000]], [[R3554002]], [[R3554005]], [[R3554007]]).

The viewer's own cards also carry Hearthstone's yellow glow as `conditionActive: true`: a card in the viewer's hand during the viewer's own main phase with no prompt open, and a unit or backrow card the viewer controls, whenever the card's script `conditionMet` holds ([[§10.9]], [[R195]]), and a hand card also when a condition another card grants it holds ([[R662]]). The key is absent otherwise, never `false`, and never set on the opponent's cards. The viewer's own hand cards also carry `counteredOnPlay: true` when a card on the field the viewer may read would counter them at every price they could be played at now (C #87 Plague Chalice, [[R667]]), with the same three rules. They also carry `choicesTruncated: true` while `legalActions` lists plays of them whose choice combinations [[R90]]'s bound cut, so a client can tell the slice it is offered from the whole list, with the same three rules (#992). While their player is shown the Pareto hint, they carry `paretoOptimal: true` when ME-PARETO's judge would call a play of them optimal now ([[R1125]], [[R1844001]]), with the same three rules. A viewer's own hand card with an embiggen price ([[§2.3]]) carries `embiggenCost` beside its `cost`: what a play of it at the embiggen price costs now, every discount and surcharge applied, read by the same price function its play is charged by ([[R65]], [[R81]]). It is the normal price plus the Embiggen premium ([[R5109000]]), and still the client shows that price and never works one out, since a Flashback floor, a Garden or a kept override makes it differ from `cost` plus the printed premium. Under Mountaintop Garden it is the dearer choice's price, the normal price plus the Garden's premium, and a Radiant card, whose normal price is then its only one, carries none ([[R1181002]]); a price for the Embiggened version alone moves it too ([[R1181003]]), and a card with no Embiggen premium shows its normal price there ([[R4350000]]). The card face shows it as the second badge beside the gem, named "Embiggened (N)", whatever the gem shows, a discounted or a surcharged normal price included ([[R5109001]]). It is absent on every other card, and never on the opponent's cards. A viewer's own hand card whose play asks a Tribute ([[§6.3]]) carries `tribute`, how many Tributes a play of it asks now, read by the function its play is held to: the printed X as a Nerf or a Buff left it, less what its owner's Floats took ([[R386]], [[R1841003]]), never what the units that could pay it are worth ([[§3.2]]). The client shows it as a small Tribute pip on the card and works out none itself. It is absent at 0, on every other card and on the opponent's cards ([[R2268000]]). A viewer's own hand card that `trade::is_tradable` passes carries `tradable: true`, whether by its printed face, a granted keyword or an aura over its controller's hand (The Syndicate, The Trade Union, [[R2574000]]). The client draws the Tradable pip from `card.tradable` alone and works out no aura itself (CLAUDE.md rule 7); the card's keyword list still shows only what the card is made of ([[R1864002]]). The key is absent otherwise, never `false`, and never set on the opponent's cards or post-game hands ([[R434]]).

A card whose text computes a number from the board, or turns on something it or the game remembers (the Unit it ate, the deaths it has counted, the card held beneath it, [[R2476000]]), carries what that number or thing comes to now as `preview`, a list of `{ label, value }` its script's `preview` hook returns ([[§10.9]], [[R280]]), a value carrying `display` ([[R372]]) or `ids`, the cards it counts (C+ #44, #45, which mark the permanents they would exile), or, on C+ #44 and C+ #45's Radiant faces, `{ label, ids }`, the permanents they would exile now, never one the viewer may not read ([[R177]]; the count still counts it, [[R1447]]): on the viewer's own hand cards, on a unit on top of its pile and on a face-up backrow card of either seat, and on a face-down card only for its controller. It is never on an opponent's hand card, a library card, a card beneath a Stack, or a card in a graveyard, exile or the resolving zone, and it is absent when the hook returns nothing.

Patch v0.2.0's cards add to the view, each under the same rules of who may read what ([[§9.1]], [[R97]], [[R177]]):

- **The cards' own state.** A card view carries its Brittle count (on the field to both players, except on a face-down card, whose count only its controller reads until it is revealed, C+ #74; in a hand to its owner, [[R385]]), its current `params` values filled into its `{key}` text and its `tuning` wherever the viewer may read the card ([[R386]]; a change made inside a deck is seen when the card leaves it, [[R311]]), its `loc` (public, [[§5]]), its Activate uses this turn, whether it may be activated now (exactly when `legalActions` lists an `activate` of it) and, when it may not, why ([[R384]], [[R3061000]]), and while an ability cools down, the turns left (`cooldownTurnsLeft`, [[R3550003]]), whether it is animated and the backrow zone it returns to ([[R383]]), and C #90's open quests with their progress and the rewards on offer ([[R404]]); a staged quest's line adds its stages, each with its name, whether it is done, open now or still to come, its goals (the one drawn once it has opened), its reward and, while the line waits to hear back with it, the turns left, and a "play a card that costs …" quest the cost it asks for, all public on both seats as the card is ([[R1854000]], [[R1854002]], [[R1854004]]), and from them a Quest card's face prints only the stage it stands at, In Too Deep's its open quests ([[R2450000]]); never its hidden Nerfs, to anyone, its owner included ([[R1845000]]).
- **Prompts that show cards.** A prompt that shows the opponent's hand (C #11; Stinkyscape+ #31 Soul Read's secret pick, [[R3550008]]), deck cards (T-AI-1, C #78's Radiant fuse) or a last board's cards (C+ #29) reaches the chooser only, as #51's reveal does; C+ #42's options reach the chooser and its answer key reaches nobody, being in the prompt's `resume` data, which no view sends ([[R420]]). `state.lastBoards` and `state.boardHistory` never reach a view ([[R417]], [[R419]]). A Discover of code blocks (Meditative+ #25) sends the chooser each option as its code alone, marked `code` and naming no definition, the cards behind the options staying in the prompt's resume data until the pick ([[R1183005]]). A prompt's asking card reaches its chooser as `sourceDefId` and `sourceRadiant` only while the chooser may read it where it now is, by `sourceId`'s test ([[R1088]], [[R2646000]]); a prompt no card opened, or whose card the state no longer holds, is hidden from the chooser or has since changed definition, names none, and the other seat's view never does. They are view fields, not the prompt's: no state hash or replay moves.
- **A dealt deck** (All Random, [[R258]]; practice's fresh random deck) is listed to its owner, like a built one, only as far as its owner has been shown its cards: the rest are unknown cards ([[R433]]).
- **The game's end** reveals both hands to both players once `result` is set ([[R434]]).
- **A revealed hand.** While an active permanent with `reveals_opponents_hand` acts on the field (IA #191 Ground Truth), its controller reads the opponent's hand as revealed: the opponent's hand cards are sent in full rather than redacted to card backs, testkit's `hidden_instance_ids` excludes them from hidden information, and AI observation leaves them unredacted ([[R4592003]]).
- **Call to Chaos's rolls.** The effects #95, C+ #73 or Meditative #95 rolled are named in the event stream both players read, since the rules box reads "???" in play ([[R436]]).
- **Coin flips and dice.** A card's `rolled` batch, every face it drew and the one it kept, reaches both players, its card named as the card itself is, except one its card made face-down in a backrow: that reaches its controller alone, unless that trap's own `trapFired` follows in the same action, as a Secret is seen only once it fires; the other player's window is taken without it, so it is as full as ever ([[R2652000]], [[R2652001]]).
- **Hook markers.** A card's `hookResolving` marker reaches a seat only if that seat could read the card where it stood as the hook began, unless that trap's own `trapFired` follows in the same action; one it reaches names the card as the card itself is named, and `hiddenFrom` never travels. A hook that runs with no card as its `self`, a Cry out of a graveyard or an Activate whose cost took its card, is judged and named by the card it belongs to ([[R3182000]]). A marker takes no room in [[R168]]'s floor, and `via` goes with a hidden card's identity ([[R2783000]], [[R2783001]]).
- **A marked card** carries its mark in both views: #50 K-Pop Fanatic's pending steal on its target, for as long as the delayed steal waits ([[R437]]); TG #15 In a hurry's Rush Tokens wear the `endOfTurn` mark while the destroy at the end of this turn waits on them ([[R2321001]]); a Unit a kill deadline watches wears the `deadline` mark (crimson) while it waits ([[R3550006]]). A card of a waiting meeting pair wears the `meet` mark (gold) in its owner's hand view alone, never in `marks_on`, so the opponent's marked-card count leaks nothing of it ([[R3558002]]). A damage pool's `preview` says what it has left this turn ([[R3550004]]). Neither memory key reaches any view or the AI: a secret pick's chosen card and cards seen (`@secretPick`, [[R3550008]]) and a side snapshot (`@sideSnapshot`, [[R3550007]]) are instance memory, which no view sends and the opponent's `redact` drops.
- **A count kept on a Unit** rides on that Unit as `tallies`, each `{ defId, value, goal }`, the card keeping it by its id: TG #31 Level Up!'s attacks toward its Radiant. It is asked of a card's `tallies` hook, a pure read like `preview`, on every card acting on the field that the viewer may read, so a face-down card's count reaches its controller alone, and it is absent when none counts ([[R2321000]]).
- **Each seat's Jade Counter** shows beside its hero on both seats once it has risen above 0, and the view carries no key before that ([[R961]]).
- **Each seat's Woozy counter** shows as a stein beside its hero on both seats while it is above 0, and the view carries no key at none (the key is `tipsy`, and a card's blur `tipsyBlur`, the keyword's old name, [[R5181000]]); a stamped card may blur one of its words on its owner's view alone, the same blur on a reload and a replay, and never on the opponent's view ([[R3548000]], [[R3548001]]).
- **Infinite mana** shows as infinity in the mana tray on both seats while a card giving it acts on that seat's field, and the view carries no key otherwise ([[R3548004]]).
- **Burn and Toxic** are public on both seats: a Unit carries them in its `counters` (a Unit standing in the backrow in its `BackrowCounters`), a hero as `burn` and `toxic` beside its `armor`, each absent at none; neither ever rides a face-down card, which carries neither ([[R3540000]], [[R3540005]]).
- **Resources and Credits.** A Resource is a hand card, in the hand's view like any other ([[R2250000]]); each seat carries its Credits as `marketCredits`, public on both seats, absent until first gained ([[R1840005]]).
- **Each seat's starscape map** shows on both seats once its player has first traveled, every node with its reward and the node they stand on, and the view carries no key before that ([[R1847000]]).
- **A Chinese card** carries `chinese: true` on every view of it the viewer may read — a hand card, a unit, a backrow card, a pile's card and a prompt's option — and on no view they may not read ([[R1301]]). `translated` reports a translation only where both players read the card; a change in a hand, a deck or on a face-down trap is silent ([[R440]]).
- **A card with a granted tag** carries `tags` on every view of it the viewer may read, where they differ from its definition's — and on no view they may not read ([[R923]]). So does a card in Chinese whose definition lacks CN: its `tags` hold CN ([[R1176000]]).
- **A Created card** carries `created: true` on every view of it the viewer may read — a hand card, a unit, a backrow card, a pile's card and a prompt's option — and on no view they may not read; the owner's library list marks the entries the owner knows ([[R943]]). Its owner's views of it name the card that generated it, `createdBy`, on a hand card, a unit, a public backrow card and a pile's card; no other player's, and no prompt's option or library entry, ever do ([[R1956001]]).
- **An Evolved card** carries `evolved: true` on every view of it the viewer may read — a hand card, a unit, a public backrow card and a pile's card — and on no view they may not read: evolved, or marked to enter the field evolved. Its evolving emits `evolved`, named as the card itself is ([[R3536005]]).
- **A GIGA permanent** carries `giga: true` at its anchor — a unit or a public backrow card — and on a face-down card too, while the three zones it covers read empty. The size is stamped as the card enters the field and fixed there ([[R3546000]], [[R3546002]], [[R3546004]]).
- **A set hand size** is public on both seats as `handCap`: Meditative #79's rest-of-game setting, absent until one is set ([[R1143]]).
- **A marked hand** shows its owner each mark on its card and the other player only the count, `handMarked`, never which ([[R1141]]).
- **A credit line** is public on its seat as `credit`: what more its controller can borrow (`available`, while a line acts), the instalment each coming refresh owes (`owed`, next first), the instalment the last refresh took (`locked`), and, while a lapsing face acts, whether the line was used this turn (`used`). Absent with no line, no debt and no locked instalment (M #89, [[R1223]]–[[R1225]]).
- **Lost refreshes, extra turns and held wins.** Each side carries its lost-refresh badge ("Your next N refreshes give 0 mana") while a loss covers a future refresh and its owed-turns badge ("Extra turns owed: N") while any is owed, and its `extraTurns` count and Rift flag, all public on both seats ([[R844]], [[R846]], [[R847]]); each held alternative win shows its progress ([[R848]]).
- **Countdowns.** An effect made "in N turns" (M+ #4 Jlockeed's Supply Drop) is a badge on its owner's side, on both seats, while it waits: its caption and the turns left ("Jlockeed's Supply Drop: 4 damage to each enemy in 2 turns", "… in 1 turn"), gone as it resolves ([[R1181000]]).
- **The Marketplace.** Once it is open, both seats carry `marketplace { offers, sales }`, absent before ([[R1853000]]): every lot offered to the viewer, their rolled offers and the lots an effect added for them ([[R1853005]], [[R2250002]]), with its index among those, `defId`, face, `price`, the Buffs or Nerfs it carries (`buffs`, `nerfs`, [[R3047002]]), the copies left to the viewer (`left`) and, while they may act and `legalActions` lists no `marketBuy` of it, the reducer's refusal as `reason`; and `sales`, always empty since nothing sells, kept so every view reads as it did ([[R4410000]], [[R1861000]]'s sales before it). A player's offers are their own, and what the opponent bought is told to them only as a card back arriving ([[R1853003]]).
- **Trades.** The viewer's own hand card that the Trade rule passes carries `tradable: true` ([[R2574000]]) and, while they may act and `legalActions` lists no `trade` of it, `tradeReason`, the reducer's refusal in its words; the viewer carries their own `trades { cost, left }` while their hand holds a Tradable card, never the opponent's; each reads the function the list and the refusal read ([[R1838000]]). A Trade is told to the opponent as a public `traded` and a card back leaving and one arriving ([[R1838001]]).
- **Secrets.** Each side's view carries the secrets it holds as `secrets: { id; choice? }[]`, the choice only for its owner or once revealed ([[R860]], [[R864]]), absent while none are held. The opponent's entries list the id alone, and no event or log line of theirs carries the choice before it is revealed.

Two marks since v0.1.1 name what a client would otherwise have to work out. A backrow Trap or Field Trap that has not flipped face-up carries `unrevealed: true` on its controller's own view of it, exactly where the other player sees a face-down marker ([[R33]], [[R351]], [[R371]]); the key is absent otherwise. A backrow card with a grade counter carries the letter it stands for beside it, `counters.gradeLetter` (#93, E to S), and a `preview` value may carry `display`, the word it prints as, such as that letter ([[R372]]).

Two fields on a `UnitView` since #849 spare a client the same work ([[R849000]]). `sick: true` is on a unit of either seat while it is summoning sick ([[R83]], [[R171]]) with neither Rush nor Charge, so the board can draw Hearthstone's zzz, and the key is absent otherwise. `attackReason` is on the viewer's own unit that may act now and cannot declare an attack: the refusal [[§4.2]] step 1 gives it, as an ability's `reason` is ([[R384]]); it is never on the opponent's units, and which attacks are offered stays `legalActions`' answer. Since #3821 the viewer's own unit also carries `switchReason` while its controller may act (their turn, the main phase, no prompt open), `canAct` or not: the refusal its own switch to the other position meets (`combat::why_cannot_switch_in`, the check `legalActions` offers a `switchPosition` by), in the engine's words (Dormant, Frozen, already acted this turn, or cannot be in Defense Position, [[§4.1]]). It is absent when that switch is legal and never on the opponent's units. A click on that unit when `legalActions` offers it no attack and the view gives no `attackReason` shows it and sends nothing ([[R3821000]]). Since #1174 a Frozen unit carries `frozen: true` on both seats, and its controller's `attackReason` says it is Frozen in its main phase though `canAct` is false ([[R1174000]]). Since #1839 a Dormant card carries `slumber` on both seats, `{ turns }` with the turns its countdown has left and `{}` for one that waits for an effect, which the board's Dormant badge reads; its `canAct` is false and no action names it ([[R1839000]], [[R1839001]]). Since #1852 a Starship carries `starship` on both seats, `{ pieces, name?, launched? }`: the Starship Pieces fused into it, oldest first, and once it has launched the name it took, which the board's comet badge and the card's face read; a Dormant Starship's one activation is its launch, its label naming its price ([[R1852003]], [[R1852005]]).

One field on a `CardView` since #979 spares it the same work ([[R979000]]). `playReason` is on the viewer's own hand card while they may act (their turn, the main phase, no prompt open) and `legalActions` lists no play of it: the first refusal in a fixed order — a play ban, no price it can pay, no open zone, no legal Tribute set, no legal choice for a required declaration or a play gate that does not hold ([[R1851000]]), or no choice [[§10.5]] step 1 accepts, its targets' discards or its Tribute pick refused ([[R2975000]]) — in the engine's words, as `attackReason` is. It is absent when a play is listed, on every other card, and on the viewer's own hand on anyone else's turn, and no refusal in the order can come from a hidden fact alone ([[§9.1]]); which plays are offered stays `legalActions`' answer. The fuzz monitor holds every such card to a reason ([[R2975000]]). It holds every Activate ability a view shows to `legalActions` the same way ([[R3061000]]). The card stays greyed out, and a click on it shows the reason and sends nothing. Since #1571 a card in the viewer's own graveyard that a permission admits carries it too, with the price asked of the permissions ([[R1571000]]).

One field on the `PlayerView` since #3777 names where a hidden target lies ([[R3777001]]). `hiddenTargets` lists each card the viewer may not read (an enemy face-down backrow card) that one of their own listed plays, activations or Heroic Powers names as a declared target, as `{ instanceId, zone }`: the bare id `legalActions` already offers it by ([[R177]], [[R227]]) and the zone its back lies in, which both players see, as a prompt option offering a face-down card names its zone ([[R821000]]). It is absent when there is none and while a prompt is open, and which targets are offered stays `legalActions`' answer. The client lights that zone while a play is built and takes a click or a drop on it as the pick of that id.

One field on an `ActivationView` since #3291 names where an ability comes from ([[R3291000]]). `printedOn` is on an ability of a fused card (a Starship's Weapon piece, an Equipped Unit's Weapon, any Fuse): the ingredient whose text prints it, `{ defId, radiant? }`, the innermost card of a Fuse of a Fuse, on the face the fusion runs it on. It is absent on a card's own ability and on the engine's (the Dormant Starship's launch, a Cloaked card's Reveal). It names a card already face-up on the field, so it carries no hidden information, and the client's Activate picker only wears its art.
