### 10.5 Playing a card

1. Validate: legal zone, cost ≤ current mana after all modifiers, Tribute available, X or embiggen chosen, targets legal — every choice the play carried is checked against what the card declared and what the board allows ([[R90]]). The player's mana as this step begins is recorded for the card's Cry (`ctx.manaBeforePlay`, C #22). While a Mayor acts, the declared `target` picks are drawn at random here, before anything is paid ([[R1200]]).
2. Pay: mana, Tributes (sacrifice), and consume the next-spell discount if used.
3. A live `replacePlays` (C #23 Devil's Pact) replaces the play or cast by a new card here, and a tag rule makes a play carrying that tag Radiant (C+ #68), read at step 1 as [[R214]] reads Gifted Program ([[R449]]). Gifted Program may set `radiant` now: each one on the playing player's side is a static flag the engine reads against the turn log's `costsPaid` ([[R213]]). Step 1 has already asked the same question, to know the face the play resolves with ([[R214]]). Each Feng Shui on the field judges the play by its element against its player's last ([[R981]]–[[R984]]): an auspicious play is made Radiant, an inauspicious one is given Brittle 2 there, each with its public `fengShui` event ([[R983]]). The card's hidden Nerfs then land, each a Nerf that spares its cost ([[R1845001]]). Then any `onPlayHook` runs ([[R153]]).
3a. Announce: once the price is paid and before the card moves, emit `cardAnnounced { instanceId, player, type, costPaid, targets }` and open a window in which counters answer it, as traps answer any event (traps first, then other triggers, [[§10.3]]). A card set face-down is announced to the other player by its zone, its cost and the type "Trap" only ([[R448]]). The first counter to resolve cancels the play ([[§6.3]] Counter): it stops here, so step 4 never places the card or emits `cardPlayed`, nothing that answers `cardPlayed` (Sheepish, [[R17]]) sees it, and its Echo repeats never happen. The window can open a prompt, so steps 4–8 park on `state.work` while it is open ([[R113]]). A cast is announced too ([[R70]]).
4. Move the card to the field (Units, Field Spells, Traps) or to a resolving state (Spells), a card set face-down taking a fresh id ([[R227]]) — from the hand, or from a graveyard while a permission is active (C #28 Second Wind, C #74 Corpse Plantation, C #90's reward L): cost, choices and counting as from hand, its Cry fires ([[R1]]), and [[R65]]'s player discounts apply, since they are prices for a play; a Flexible Unit (Flying has it innately) played into a backrow zone stands there as a Unit ([[R1081000]], [[R3543000]]), while no other card reads Flexible, so a Field Spell takes its backrow ([[R3828000]]); a GIGA takes only a fitting anchor — all four zones free, its own Tribute's counting — and a cast or replaced GIGA the leftmost fit ([[R3546000]]), the play holding the other three zones meanwhile ([[R210]], [[R3546003]]); a Resource played this way pays its player its Credits right after `cardPlayed` ([[R2250001]]); emit `cardPlayed`; increment `turnLog.cardsPlayed`, its plays by type, the game `played` counter, `gameLog.playedByTag` and the instance's `timesPlayed` ([[R429]]); each player's last face-up play's element is recorded beside them ([[R982]]). A card cast on draw is no play: its `cardPlayed` carries `onDraw`, wakes no trigger, and nothing here is counted or recorded for it; its `cardResolved` at step 7 carries `onDraw` too, which a card answering a play passes by ([[R2179002]]). A Unit played onto a Stack base tops its pile, the base beneath it ([[§3.2]]), and one played onto a Radiant Empty Plot (M #97.1) is Buffed once as it is placed, before its Cry ([[R1281]]). A card with Flashback is its own graveyard permission, once: played from its owner's graveyard by it, it loses the keyword for good ([[R783012]], [[R783013]], upheld by [[R3039001]]), an embiggen card at both its prices ([[R3138000]]), and that play costs at least (1) after every discount, at each price, where `legalActions`, the payment and `viewFor` all read it ([[R4285000]]); a graveyard play a field permission covers (C #74 Corpse Plantation's, whose Plague Counters may pay part of the price, the `play` action carrying how many ([[R454]])) or a card's own `graveyardPrice` (S+ #120 Crawler's through v0.6.13h, priced N there any number of times ([[R3560001]]); Flashback since issue #4783, [[R4783022]]) takes no such floor. A Unit, Spell or Field Spell with Cloaked in its controller's hand may also be played face-down into the backrow as a Trap (`faceDown: 'cloaked'`) at its own price, counted as a Trap play ([[R783004]], [[R783005]]). A card carrying a win (`effects::WIN_ON_PLAY_KEY` in its memory, put there by an `add_to_hand` `memory` rider: Stinkyscape #4 Olivian Expanse's Radiant pick) takes the key off as it is played, a cast too, and sets its player's won flag, held at the state check that closes the play, a hero at 0 losing first; a copy, a Transform or a burned card carries none ([[R1870000]]).
5. Resolve Combo checks, Quickstriker, /fullsend's Combo draw, then the card's own Cry or spell script (targets already chosen). A Spell resolving on a slashed board is cast on one half, chosen here: the run's answer, its target's half, or a random cast's draw, else a `mode` prompt ([[R3554004]]). A permanent's Cry runs extra times with the same targets and modes under Double Counting, and a Cry an effect triggers reuses its answers; a Spell's resolution is no Cry and runs once ([[R822]], [[R823]]).
6. Echo: repeat step 5 with fresh prompts N times (a card that keeps its targets asks no target prompt and takes the first resolution's picks, [[R3048002]]).
7. Spells go to the GY or exile (or back to hand under C+ #14's enchantment, or where a "would go to a graveyard" replacement sends them); Unstable Clone Machine and Bear Honeypot fire after resolution (Bear Honeypot only while its controller has an open unit zone, [[R430]]); Sheepish fires after a played Unit resolves, its Cry included ([[R427]]), and in the same window a captor may capture a Unit its controller's opponent played ([[§3.2]], [[R1284]]); Unlicensed Experimentation fires after a played permanent's Cry ([[R61]]); a play a Feng Shui punished takes its hit on its player's hero once it has resolved ([[R983]]); an Equip play's card then fuses into its host ([[R1086]]).
8. Run the resolution loop.
