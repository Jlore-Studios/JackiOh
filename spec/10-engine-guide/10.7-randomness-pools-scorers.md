### 10.7 Randomness, pools, scorers

- One PRNG (xoshiro128\*\* or mulberry32) seeded per match; `rng.next()` advances `rngCursor`, which is stored so replay resumes mid-log.
- `rng.pick(list)`, `rng.shuffle(list)`, `rng.coin()`, `rng.chance(p)`, `rng.lucky(x, roll, better)`; a negative `x` (a net luck an Unlucky took below 0) rolls |x| extra times and keeps the worst ([[R5236020]]).
- `catalog.query` (5.1) is the single source of random pools, over every set unless the card names one ([[R380]]); it never returns tokens, bar the Grapes in a Fruit pool and C+ #23's every-token pool ([[R382]]), nor the requesting def, by id, unless the effect names its pool ([[R387]]).
- Unique random cards ([[R3563000]]): every card an effect generates at random is drawn unique within the effect's run through the run's `draw_tally`, `pick_unique` for a plain pick and `pick_generated_unique` for a generated one, until its pool runs out and then evenly from the whole pool again; behind `UNIQUE_RANDOM_DRAWS`, on since the Stinkyscape+ release (#3573).
- Card metrics ([[R1846000]]): `crates/cards/build.rs` counts each catalog script's code lines on every build and compiles in the counts and two lists, BUSTED (over `BUSTED_CODE_LINES`) and complicated (over `COMPLICATED_CODE_LINES`), each sorted by id, which `register_all` hands the engine beside the catalog (`register_card_metrics`) and the query reads (`busted`, `complicated`); `everySet` adds the single-player sets to a pool that names no set ([[R1846001]]). A fused or crafted card has no count, and a fold reads its version's own lists, kept by `patches ship` once a card that reads them ships, or the build's for a version with none ([[R1846003]], [[R2173000]]); `classify` counts a move across a threshold as a change to the readers' play ([[R2173001]]).
- Copy semantics: `cloneInstance(inst)` for "copy of this unit" keeps radiant, buffs, granted keywords, vanilla, `statsOverride` and `tuning`; resets damage, exertion, counters (a Brittle count included), the times-played count and summonedTurn ([[R57]], [[R429]]).
- Zephrys scorer: for each candidate, simulate a dry-run score: lethal available → max; can clear the enemy board → high; hero below 10 and card heals → high; otherwise stats-per-mana plus draw value. Deterministic, ranks every non-token card of every set that ships except Zephrys itself ([[R1446]]), Discover offers the top 3. The weights are engine constants, tested against fixed states. C+ #27 Zephrys Zealotism uses the same scorer over the same pool but itself to choose a whole hand ([[R416]]). Its dry run plays on the ranking player's concealed copy: each card hidden from them a stand-in with no text ([[R222]]), and no record they may not read ([[R1714000]]).
- AI policy (My Pawn, "Targets chosen randomly"), described here once and only pointed at from [[R44]] and [[R84]]: draw uniformly from `legalActions` minus the three action types the policy never takes — `concede`, `offerDraw` and `answerDraw`, held as one named constant ([[R84]]) — ending the turn when nothing else is left on that set and otherwise with probability 0.1 (`AI_END_TURN_PROBABILITY`); with a prompt open that set is only that prompt's answers (`legalActions` adds nothing to them but `concede`, [[R211]]), which is what answering prompts uniformly means ([[R44]]). A ladder bot's skill miss is not this policy: it weighs every move, endTurn included, by a one-ply score ([[R2214000]]).
- Autopilot (Meditative+ #18 Cognitive Offloading, [[R1183002]], [[R1183003]]): the rest of a player's turn played by the scorer, never `crates/ai`. Each step tries its candidates on that player's concealed copy of the board ([[R222]], [[R1714000]]) on the dry run's own seed and ranks what each leaves: a won game, then the scorer's three priorities, then the board's balance (each side's hero life and Armor plus its Units' Attack and Health, theirs taken from yours). A prompt of theirs is answered by the best of its first `AUTOPILOT_ANSWERS_TRIED` answers; else their playable cards in the scorer's order, the first with a harmless play played at its best; else the best attack that meets a priority or raises the balance; else the turn ends. At most `AUTOPILOT_MAX_ACTIONS` actions, past which the rest is a timeout's ([[R79]]). An autopilot aura (Stinkyscape #46, #47) engages it as each of its controller's turns begins and reads the aura again before every step, playing a count of [[R456]]'s actions, after which the turn is handed back, or the whole turn ([[R1844000]]).
- The Marketplace ([[R1853001]], [[R1853002]], [[R1853005]], [[R2250002]], [[R3047002]]) draws every roll and every price move from the match rng, in one fixed order: for each player's roll (as it opens, the player whose turn it is then the other; at each player's turn start, theirs), `MARKETPLACE_OFFERS_PER_TURN` (4, [[R3047000]]) times while a lot has a card left: the lot by the lots' counts, the card of it, whether it is Radiant (`MARKETPLACE_RADIANT_CHANCE`), its Buffs (`MARKETPLACE_BUFF_CHANCE`, one draw a roll and another while one lands, none past `MARKETPLACE_TUNE_MAX` Buffs), only when none landed its Nerfs (`MARKETPLACE_NERF_CHANCE`, the same) and its price move (no draw while a tenth of its base rounds to 0); then, as it opens and at every turn start, each Resource's two sell quotes, its base face then its Radiant face, in sorted id order, then each `paysCredits` card's two the same way ([[R1861000]], [[R4410000]]); a lot an effect adds draws its card from its pool once; and a buy of a Buffed or Nerfed lot draws one row of the card's menu for each Buff or Nerf it lands ([[R3047003]]). A closed Marketplace draws nothing, so a game without one keeps every draw it had.
