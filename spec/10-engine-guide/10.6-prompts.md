### 10.6 Prompts (`state.pending`)

`PendingChoice = { id, playerId, kind: 'discover'|'target'|'mode'|'mulligan'|'hand'|'zone'|'tribute'|'direction'|'x'|'embiggen'|'number'|'answer'|'cell'|'reward'|'pick'|'craft'|'market', options, min, max, budget, craftMode, streak, resume }`. Patch v0.2.0's kinds: `number`, a number from a fixed range (C #18's 0 to 10, declared with the play); `answer`, one of a multiple-choice problem's options (C+ #42, whose key stays in `resume` and never leaves the engine, [[R420]]; asked again after each right answer as a question sequence, Meditative+ #20, its `streak` the right answers so far, which its chooser sees, and Stop offered after the lettered answers, [[R1184000]]); `cell`, a board cell (C+ #62, one cell a prompt, [[R422]]); `reward`, a completed quest's reward (C #90, [[R404]]); and `pick`, a pick of one card or several, budgeted by count or by cost, from a pile, a hand or cards across zones (C #44, C #56, C #78's Radiant face). C #11 opens a `hand` prompt over the opponent's hand, and its Radiant face a `mode` prompt over the costs there. An opponent-held prompt (`by: "enemy"`) never opens while its holder is not the active player ([[R1279000]]): C #8 asks its mode questions, C #9 its keep and C #90's reward G its discard at the start of the holder's next turn, each running on the holder's own turn clock with a timeout answered by the AI policy ([[R79]]). A prompt held by the card's controller still opens on either player's turn, as C #90's `reward` choice does. `resume` names the script continuation and its captured data, so the reducer is re-entrant: `answer` re-invokes the script with the selection. Multi-step effects (Private Tutor, Craft a Card, Masochism Mask radiant) chain prompts. The opponent's view shows only that a prompt is open, never its options; of a card picker whose count hides nothing, also how many cards it offers (`optionCount`, [[R2739000]]). The chooser's view names the card asking (`sourceDefId`, `sourceRadiant`) when it may read that card, so the client can title the prompt and explain each option ([[R2646000]]). The mulligan is the one exception to one prompt at a time: both players' mulligan prompts are open together, outside `state.pending`, as one sealed-bid step, and while it is open nothing moves but a mulligan and the actions that end a game ([[§2.1]], [[R265]], [[R266]]). A card's own play choices (zone, X, embiggen, Tribute, declared targets and modes) are not prompts; they travel in the `play` action, and the client builds them with the same pickers ([[R81]]). No card opens an `x`, `embiggen`, `zone`, `tribute` or `direction` prompt, since all five are play choices; the kinds stay for later sets. `hand` is reachable: an Echo repeat of Glowy Jelly Bean reopens its hand pick as a prompt, and a discard "of your choice" ("of their choice") asks its discarder while it resolves ([[R682]]; C #90's reward G does, on the discarder's turn under [[R1279000]]). `craft` is Meditative #17's answer (M6 #17): a recipe the block editor sends, validated against the point and line budgets of the cost the `number` prompt chose ([[R880]]); its options are the four seeded presets ([[R881]]); its `craftMode`, absent for #17, is `potion` or `hugePotion` for Meditative+ #16's and #16.1's fixed potion frame, whose answer must carry that frame and whose options are seeded Potions ([[R1182000]], [[R1182001]]), or `scratch` for Meditative+ #26's empty canvas, which changes only what the client shows ([[R1182002]]). `market` is Meditative #42's night market (ME-MARKET): held by the caster alone, its `budget` the yuan left, its options each lot the yuan covers, the Radiant face's barters and Leave, one deal an answer, reopening through `resume` until Leave or nothing is left to buy or barter ([[R1000]], [[R1001]]). `number` is reachable the same way: C #18 declares its number with the play (in `modes`), an Echo repeat reopens the pick as a `number` prompt, and a cast of an X card that is not random asks its caster for X as a `number` prompt, never an `x` one ([[R453]], [[R520]]; Zao Gao's discard was one too, until patch v0.1.1 made it random, [[R354]]). A `target` prompt that opens while a Mayor acts is answered at once at random ([[R1200]]). A `target` prompt that offers cards or heroes is answered on the board as well as in its list: a click on an offered card or hero, or on the zone of an offered face-down card, picks that option — at once for a prompt of one pick, into the picker's selection for a prompt of several ([[R4781000]]).
