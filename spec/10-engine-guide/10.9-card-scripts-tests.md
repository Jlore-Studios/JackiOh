### 10.9 Card scripts and tests

Each card is one file exporting `{ def, base: Script, radiant: Script }` where `Script = { cost?, cry?, death?, startOfGame?, entersHand?, resume?, delayed?, setStat?, startOfTurn?, endOfTurn?, aura?, triggers?, activate?, onPlayHook?, handTriggers?, staticFlags?, targets?, modes?, conditionMet?, preview? }` (patch v0.2.0 adds: `activations`, a card's Activate abilities, [[R384]]; `deckTriggers` and `graveyardTriggers` beside `handTriggers`, [[R464]]; `replacements`, the card's replacements as data, [[§6.2]], [[R460]]; `afterAttack`, run for the attacker after the state check closing each declared or forced combat it attacked in, only while it is still on the field on the stay it attacked from, with `{ targetId, destroyedIds, survived, forced }`, C+ #73.1, [[R2285001]], or, with `afterAttackEvenIfGone` ("Whenever this attacks"), whatever became of it, as the snapshot it fought with, #32, [[R426]], [[R2396000]]; `whenAttacks`, run for the attacker before any damage of each attack it makes, with `{ targetId, forced }`, C #13, C #45, C+ #73.1, S #75, [[R2285000]]; `afterAttacked`, the defender's mirror, run for the target of each declared or forced combat after that combat's state check — never for a Cleave splash nor an attack on the hero — on its snapshot when it died, with `{ attackerId, forced, attackerSurvived }`, M #49.3, [[R1026]]; `allyAttacked`, run for each card acting on the attacked Unit's side once a declared attack on it has come through [[§4.2]] step 4's trap window, before its combat, with `{ attackerId, defenderId }`, Stinkyscape #14, [[R1849008]]; `allyAttack`, run for each card acting on the attacker's side after the state check closing each declared or forced combat of one of its Units, after the combatants' own hooks, with `{ attackerId, targetId, forced, survived }`, TG #31, [[R2199000]]; `tallies`, a pure read like `preview`: the counts the card keeps on Units of the field, which `viewFor` surfaces on each as `tallies`, a fusion's being its ingredients' in order, TG #31, [[R2321000]]; `evolve`, what evolving the Unit does, run by the `evolve` verb alone, once per instance, Stinkyscape #91, [[R1849001]], [[R3536000]]; `evolvesWhen`, the standing "Evolves when" condition the settled state check reads, and `evolveExtra`, N extra runs of its controller's Evolve texts while it acts, the highest holding, Stinkyscape+, [[R3536001]], [[R3536003]]; `tradesPerTurn`, a pure read like `aura`: the Trades a turn its controller has while it acts, the most of several holding, Stinkyscape #165, [[R1864004]]; `grants`, the Death abilities the card grants, by `<defId>#<key>`, merged across a Fuse's ingredients, [[R1107]]; `costAura`, `graveyardPlay`, `drawLimit`, `heroGuard`, `conditionalKeywords` and `plagueMultiplier`, pure reads like `aura`, [[R455]], [[R454]], [[R457]], [[R463]], [[R471]]; `targetingDiscards`, C #89, [[R450]]; `recordsPlayAs`, C #57, [[R451]]; `wouldCounter`, the static half of a counter trigger, C #87, [[R667]]; `targetChecks`, a filter's named predicate, asked of the card as its play declares it, its X stamped ([[R1868000]]); and `ctx.manaBeforePlay`, the player's mana as [[§10.5]] step 1 began, C #22; `handAura`, what the card lends its hand neighbours (`HandAura { to, radiant, cost }`, [[§6.2]]; [[R3558001]]) and `castFromDeckWhen`, the standing condition that casts the card from its owner's deck at the settled state check ([[§4.5]], [[§6.2]]; [[R3558007]]); a script reads a tunable number through `param(ctx, key)`, never a literal, [[R386]]) (`cost` is Ceaseless Void's computed cost, [[R55]]; `startOfGame` is Heroic Power's roll, [[R43]]; `entersHand` runs on each hand arrival, after `startOfGame`, [[R925]]) (`resume` is the named-continuation step table a prompt answer or a delayed effect re-enters, [[§10.6]], [[R81]], [[R113]]; `delayed` is the hook a scheduled delayed effect lands on unless the card names another key, resolved at its [[R62]] point, and [[R126]] is the rule that one reader resolves either spelling; `setStat` is [[§10.4]] layer 2's hook, returning a delta added to the printed face, [[R116]], summed across a Fuse's ingredients, [[R102]]) (`targets` and `modes` declare the card's play-time choices, [[R81]]; `secretModes` keeps those modes secret — filed as a secret record, never carried on an event, and blanked from an in-flight play the other seat reads, [[R865]]; `playAllowed` is the card's play gate, a pure read of what its player may see for a card whose play needs something on the board though it declares no target it needs: `legalActions`, the refusal and `playReason` read it off the face the play resolves, a cast never asks it, and a fusion holds every ingredient's, [[R1851000]], [[R1851001]], [[R1851002]]) (`conditionMet` is [[R195]]'s read-only predicate: no writes, no random draws. It answers whether the card's printed condition holds now for its controller, asked with `zone: 'hand'` as if the card were played now and with `zone: 'field'` as the card on the field reads it, and `viewFor` surfaces it as `conditionActive`, [[§10.8]]; a fusion's hook is its ingredients' hooks or-ed, [[R196]]) (`preview` is [[R280]]'s read-only hook: no writes, no random draws; it returns the labeled numbers the card's formula comes to if it alone resolved now, asked with the same context `conditionMet` is, and `viewFor` surfaces them as `preview`, [[§10.8]]; a fusion's list is its ingredients' lists in order) (`liveFaceDown`: a kept script's lasting text works face-down, [[R767000]]; every other sleeps there, [[R1487000]]) and every hook returns `Effect[]` built from the primitives in section 6. A card never mutates state directly. Tests per card: one fixture per listed behavior in section 8 (base and radiant), plus a replay test that folds the recorded log and compares the final state hash; a card with `conditionMet` also tests both answers of it against the branch its own resolution takes ([[R195]]). Property test: 1,000 random-policy games per seed set must never throw, never desync between two folds, and always terminate within the cap.

Catalog test: exactly 800 cards and 178 tokens — per set 100 and 11 (Core), 90 and 1 (Classic), 78 and 38 (Classic+), 102 and 30 (Meditative), 26 and 7 (Meditative+), 178 and 52 (Stinkyscape), 60 and 1 (Training Grounds), 166 and 38 (Stinkyscape+) — with the indices, costs, types, tags, stats and rarities in section 8 and each set's rarity counts. End-to-end (Cypress) scope: a hotseat game to completion; each prompt kind exercised once; a trap firing on the opponent's turn; a reconnect mid-game restoring the same view; a room-code match between two browsers; a My Pawn turn played by the AI; the turn cap ending a game as a draw; an Animated trap springing; an Activate; a Counter on the opponent's turn; a Tribute onto a full board.
