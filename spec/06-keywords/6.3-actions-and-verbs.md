### 6.3 Actions and verbs

| Verb | Rule | Engine semantics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summon | Put onto the field from anywhere else | `summon(card, controller, zone?)`; with no zone named, the leftmost empty unlocked zone of its row ([[R64]]), else the leftmost empty Locked one, since a Lock refuses only plays ([[R688]]); fails if the row has no empty zone, a Flying Unit's unit row and backrow both ([[R2248000]]); no Cry, except a summon that names the card it summons ([[R1]], [[R411]]). A summon may name the X the card arrives with (`x`), as if it were played for it: its X stats and every read of its X follow it (Meditative+ #20.1 Lil' Kenny, [[R1184003]]). A fresh summon may also seed the card's memory (`memory`), which a later hook reads to tell that body from one played (TG #25 Trigor's chain, [[R2206000]]). A fresh summon may also name Dormant N (`dormantTurns`): the card it makes enters Dormant for N of its controller's turns though its own text prints none, and absent or 0 it enters awake (S+ #28 Zombie-Box, [[R3541002]]); so may a summon from a random pool, for the card it draws (`summonRandomWith`'s `dormantTurns`, FvH #97, [[R5239001]]). A fresh summon may also set a Unit face-down by its Cloaked (`setCloaked`): stamped as a Cloaked play is, into its side's leftmost open backrow zone, and face-up where it would go otherwise when none is open; no Unit is summoned by it, nor by its reveal (S+ #76, [[R4327000]], [[R4327001]]). Variants: `fillBoardRandom`, one pick per open zone from a pool (M #51, [[R1080]]); `summonRandomFromHand`, one random Unit card from the hand (M #63, [[R1083]]); `summonPile`, several Units summoned into one unit zone as a Stack pile, first listed at the bottom, each its own summon, none when the zone is not free ([[§3.2]], [[R1842000]]) |
| Destroy | Field to GY | Marks destroyed; state check moves it; Indestructible ignores. While a destroy-to-exile aura acts, the state check exiles what it would have moved instead, still destroyed ([[§4.5]] step 1, [[R1849002]], [[R4207000]]). "At the end of this turn, destroy them" is a `delay` per card at [[§2.2]]'s end-of-turn delayed-effect point of the turn running, whoever's ([[R350]]), under the engine's destroy hook and watching the card, so one that left the field is forgotten ([[R174]]) and one that changed control is not (TG #15, [[R2198002]]) |
| Sacrifice | Your own card (or an enemy unit a Tribute allows, #55), field to GY, bypasses Indestructible | `sacrifice(instance)`; counts as a death. A Sacrifice of an Untributable card does nothing, alone or together with other cards ([[R1220]]) |
| Exile | To the exile pile from anywhere | Increments the game exile counter; no Death trigger |
| Bounce | Return to controller's hand | Tokens vanish; hand cap applies; the instance resets per [[R78]] (buffs and damage included). **Ruling:** since balance patch 1 "Bounce" is the printed keyword for a permanent's return from the field to its controller's hand ([[R692]], [[R747]]); a card returned from the graveyard or exile, or a Spell after it resolves, says "Return … to hand" ([[R746]]) |
| Discard | Hand to GY, except a unit-token card, which ceases to exist instead ([[§3.2]], [[R11]]) | **Ruling:** since balance patch 1 the discard is random cards from the discarder's hand ([[R682]]); only "of your choice" ("of their choice") on the card opens a hand prompt the discarder holds (C #90 reward G does, [[R16]]). A card that crumbles from Brittle ([[§6.1]]) is destroyed on the field and is not discarded; a Temporary card ([[§6.1]], [[R637]]) is discarded at the end of its owner's turn, as a discard. A discard guard stops an effect's discard from a guarded player's hand while it is not their turn ([[R800]]). A Resource discarded from a hand pays its player its Credits as it reaches the graveyard ([[R2250001]]). A delayed discard at the end of a turn (`discardHandAtTurnEnd`, C #37 Last Hurrah) takes the whole hand, or with a count that many random cards, all there are of a smaller hand, each a discard ([[R3134000]]) "Discard your deck" (C #28 Second Wind's Radiant face, `discard_library`) is no discard: every card of the library goes to the graveyard, top first, a unit token ceasing to exist, with no draw, no `discarded` and nothing that answers a discard ([[R3951001]]) |
| Counter | Cancel a card being played or cast | In [[§10.5]]'s announce window (step 3a), before the card moves: it never resolves or enters the field and goes to its owner's graveyard, or to exile when the text says so. It is treated as never played: no Cry, no Death, no `cardPlayed` and no `cardResolved`, and it counts for nothing that counts plays (`turnLog.cardsPlayed`, the game's `played` counter, Combo, Quickstriker, Ceaseless Void); the mana and Tributes paid stay spent (Hearthstone). The first counter to resolve cancels the play and the rest find no card and stay set; a countered card's Echo repeats never happen. Event `countered`. A counter also answers declared Ranged attacks and `activate` actions (Stinkyscape+ #43 Segment): the attack deals no damage, cancelled before [[§4.2]] step 4's window (`attackCancelled`), the Activate does nothing, and the Activate's use and costs stay spent; one budget a turn shared by both, announced by `trapFired`, and a forced attack is never countered ([[R121]]). No trap window opens for an Activate ([[R3550005]]) |
| Kill deadline | "They have 1 turn to get a kill otherwise they are destroyed" | `kill_deadline { target, coinsOnFail? }` (Stinkyscape+ #94 PIP): the named Unit is destroyed at the end of its controller's next turn, unless it destroys a Unit first — any kill credited to it ([[R42]], [[R412]]) meets it at once. A destroy aimed at that stay (`watch`, [[R76]], [[R83]]); the watched Unit wears the `deadline` mark (crimson) while it waits ([[R437]]). A Unit that left the field fails nothing ([[R174]]). The Radiant face adds `coinsOnFail` The Coins to its owner's hand when it fails ([[R3550006]]) |
| Side snapshot | "Take a snapshot of all your other permanents" / "Reset your board to that state" | `snapshot_side` (Stinkyscape+ #84 Bogos Binted's Cry) keeps the controller's side without the running card on its memory; `restore_side_snapshot { radiant? }` (its Death) is [[R419]]'s restore on that side alone: extras go to hand, the snapshot's cards return to their slots, no Cry, announced by `rolledBack`. The Radiant face makes the rebuilt side Radiant. Instance memory, so it reaches no view and no AI ([[R3550007]]) |
| Secret pick | "Look at your opponent's hand and choose a card secretly" | `secret_pick { step }` (Stinkyscape+ #31 Soul Read's "When played face down:") opens the enemy hand to its owner alone, who secretly picks one; `keepSecretPick` keeps the chosen id and every id seen on the card's memory. Its trigger answers the opponent's announced play (`query::secret_pick_hit`): the chosen card is countered into its owner's hand ([[R448]]), another seen card tributes the base trap; the Radiant face answers the chosen card alone. | Steal | Take control | Moves the card to the stealer's side, same lane if free else first free zone (ruling, [[R15]]); one with no destination (no free zone in its row, or no fit for a GIGA, [[R3546003]]) is destroyed instead, and nothing prevents it ([[R4205000]]): not Indestructible, not Untributable, no "would die" replacement, though a destroy-to-exile aura still exiles it; its Death and Reborn apply on its own side and it goes to its controller's graveyard. Event `stolen`, hidden per zone ([[R97]]) **Give control** ([[R1423]]): the mirror, a card on the field going from its controller to their opponent by the same placement and entry (Meditative #6, #30), except that a card the receiver has no room for stays where it is ([[R803]]) **Take into your hand** ([[R1185003]], M+ #24): a Unit on the field leaves it for the taker's hand as the taker's card, as a Bounce takes it ([[R747]]) — `stolen` from the field, [[R78]]'s reset, no Death, the pile beneath resuming — a unit token ceasing to exist on the way and a full hand burning it into the taker's graveyard. A random enemy permanent (Stinkyscape+ #25 Snom Bunny's Death) is a steal ([[R15]], [[R33]]) of a card drawn by the game's Rng ([[R3550009]]). IA #135's set-count steal counts from the capped maximum, the victim's own first ([[R4590002]]); IA #138's swap moves both Units at once by [[R15]] ([[R4590003]]); IA #144 reads costs as it resolves and steals in the scope's walk ([[R4590005]]) |
| Transform | Replace a card with another in place | New instance in the same zone and the same battle position ([[R691]]), no Cry, without the old card's `tuning` ([[§6.3]] Nerf); blocked by Immutable. "After this attacks, summon a copy of this, then evolve" (Stinkyscape #91 The Propagation's Wasp, #92 The Propagation's Hornet) summons the copy first, so the copy lacks what the evolve gives ([[R1849001]]) |

**Where a Buff or a Nerf is live** ([[R3888000]]). A Nerf or a Buff draws only a change that can still change what the card does in the game, from the zone it is in or one it reaches in the normal course: a deck card reaches a hand, and a card on the field a graveyard, unless it is a unit token, which ceases to exist there ([[R11]]). A bounce, a resurrect or a "Trigger a Cry" is not the normal course, as [[R1748000]] reasoned about the cost: Hearthstone never reprices a minion in play, and its Battlecry never runs again. The engine reads the table from one place (`effects::tune_live`), every row of the menu and a hidden Nerf alike; each row keeps its own conditions besides (types, bounds, [[R65]], Vanilla, Immutable). The columns are where the card is: a deck; a hand; being played, at [[§10.5]] step 3, its price and Tribute paid and its Cry still to come, where hidden Nerfs land ([[R1845001]]); the field face-up; the field face-down (a set Trap, or a card set by its Cloaked and not yet revealed, read as the card it is, [[R783005]]); Dormant ([[§6.1]]); a graveyard; an exile pile.
