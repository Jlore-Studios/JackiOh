### 6.2 Triggers and timing words

| Keyword | Rule | Engine semantics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cry | When a card enters the field for the first time | **Ruling:** fires only when played from hand (or by Cast on draw / Echo / Call to Chaos casting), and since patch v0.2.0 also when played from a graveyard under a permission ([[§10.5]]) or cast by any other effect ([[§6.3]] Cast) ([[R1]]). Copies, Recruit, Reborn, tokens and Transform results do not fire it, nor does a Flicker, with one exception: a summon that names the card it summons fires its Cry (C+ #19 League of Losers' Mid Loser, [[R411]]). Otherwise Duplicating Felinors fills the board for 2 mana. "Trigger a Cry" ([[§6.3]]) runs one by effect. Targets are chosen at play time. |
| Death | When it dies: sent from the field to the GY, or to exile when a replacement sends it there instead | Fires on destroy, sacrifice or combat death, a Brittle crumble on the field included, wherever a replacement then sends the card ([[R4207000]]); not on bounce, exile, transform, Flicker or steal, nor when an Animated card moves between its zones. A backrow card that prints Death fires it as a Unit does ([[§4.5]]). A token with Death still fires it after vanishing. |
| Cry and Death | Both hooks share one script | #22; TG #38 Tutorial Master prints them on one line, "Cry & Death:" (issue #3139) |
| When this attacks | "When this attacks": before any damage of each attack it makes, declared or forced | The attacker's `whenAttacks` hook: a declared attack's once [[§4.2]] step 4's trap window has closed on an attack that still stands, before "When one of your Units is attacked"; a forced one's ([[R53]]) just before its combat. For the attacker's controller, a whole effect followed by its state check, parkable ([[R113]]), with `{targetId, forced}`. An attack called off in its window runs none ([[R44]]), nor does defending. The attack is then fought only if it still stands ([[R220]]), at the Unit that took its target's place when the hook transformed it ([[R2285002]]). [[R2285000]] |
| Whenever this attacks | "Whenever this attacks" and "Whenever this destroys a Unit": after the attack or kill, once its combat's state check has run; survival not required | `afterAttack` with `afterAttackEvenIfGone`, or a kill trigger with `evenIfGone`, run as the snapshot it fought with when it is gone, `survived` false, and once: a Reborn body ([[R83]]) answers nothing more. For its controller then ([[R212]]); declared and forced attacks ([[R53]]), never an attack called off ([[R44]]) nor defending, though a defending kill is a kill; parkable ([[R113]]). [[R2396000]] |
| After this attacks | "After this attacks" and "After this destroys a Unit": after each attack it makes, declared or forced, once that combat's state check has run, or after each kill, and only if it is still alive | The attacker's `afterAttack` hook, only while the attacker is still on the field on the stay it attacked from: one that died in the combat, or came back through Reborn ([[R83]]), runs nothing. With the combat's facts `{targetId, destroyedIds, survived, forced}`, `survived` true; parkable ([[R113]]). A kill trigger answers only while its card stands on the field as the `destroyed` is dispatched ([[R1245]]). [[R2285001]] |
| When this is Frozen | "When this is Frozen": every freeze that lands on it, a re-freeze included | A trigger on its own `marked` adding the Frozen mark (`query::is_frozen_event`), answered as any field trigger is, in [[R68]]'s order. A freeze after the state check: the trigger answers the event, not the status ([[R3550002]]). No card prints it since issue #4783 took it off S+ #90 Meltdown, whose replays through v0.6.13h still run it ([[R4783023]]) |
| After this is attacked | Once per combat it was the target of, after that combat's state check | The defender's hook, M #49.3 AI Girlfriend's: a declared attack's or a forced one's, never a Cleave splash (no attack on it) nor an attack on the hero. The attacker's `afterAttack` runs first. On the snapshot it fought with when it died ([[R78]], [[R89]]), with the combat's facts `{attackerId, forced, attackerSurvived}`; parkable like any hook ([[R113]]). [[R1026]] |
| When one of your Units is attacked | Before the combat of a declared attack on a Unit of yours | A card's `allyAttacked` hook (Stinkyscape #14 Arm of the Jempire, whose text names a Taunt Unit and checks it): once [[§4.2]] step 4's trap window closes on a declared attack that still stands on a Unit, each card acting on that Unit's side with the hook runs it, the attacked Unit's own included, in [[R68]]'s order, each a whole effect followed by its state check, and the combat follows. `ctx.data` holds `{attackerId, defenderId}`. Never for an attack on a hero, nor a forced one ([[R121]]); once the attack no longer stands no further hook starts ([[R220]]); a question in one pauses the rest and the combat until it is answered ([[R113]], [[R1849008]]) |
| After one of your Units attacks | After the combat of each attack a Unit of yours makes | A card's `allyAttack` hook (TG #31 Level Up!): once the state check that closes a combat has run, a declared attack's or a forced one's ([[R53]]), and after the attacker's `afterAttack` and the defender's `afterAttacked`, each card acting on the attacker's side with the hook runs it, the attacker's own included, in [[R68]]'s order, each a whole effect followed by its state check. `ctx.data` holds `{attackerId, targetId, forced, survived}`, and `destroyedEnemy: true` when the combat's closing check destroyed a Unit of the attacker's opponent with the attacker its killer ([[R42]], [[R3951000]]). Never for an attack called off before it fought ([[R44]]) nor for the other side's Units; a question in one pauses the rest until it is answered ([[R113]], [[R2199000]]) |
| Chance to reveal | "30% chance to Reveal when …": a Trap's condition reveals it only on a lucky roll | A face-down trap's static `revealChance`, a percent (its declared number `chance` where it declares it): each firing its triggers admit rolls the match rng once, its controller's Luck and its Lucky rolling again, any hit enough ([[R987]], [[R1440]]); a miss reveals nothing, emits nothing and leaves it set for its next chance. A trap without one, or a face-up Field Trap firing again, rolls nothing (Stinkyscape #174 Aerelret, #22 Gatinsir, [[R1849009]]) |
| When this awakens | When this Dormant card awakens | A trigger on the card's own `awakened` and no other card's (`when_this_awakens`), answered as any field trigger is, in [[R68]]'s order, once the card is awake: at its countdown's end at the start of its controller's turn, before the start-of-turn triggers, or when an effect awakens it ([[§6.1]] Dormant, [[R1839002]]) |
| When launched | When the Starship this is fused into launches | A trigger a Starship Piece prints, answering the `starshipLaunched` of the Starship it is fused into and no other card's (`when_launched`), so it runs only as part of a Starship: queued as the launch is reported and run once the Starship's fused Cries are done, in [[R68]]'s order, in the order the pieces fused ([[§6.3]] Launch, [[R1852003]]) |
| Evolves when | "Evolves when …": a condition that evolves the card | A standing condition (`evolvesWhen`) read at every settled state check ([[§4.5]], [[R68]]'s order): your hand holding at least N cards that cost (0), all your unit zones full, this below half health (current health times two below max), this Stacked on something (`zones::beneath_at` non-empty). Every other condition is the card's own trigger evolving itself: your opponent plays a Unit (`cardPlayed`), this moves from Defense to Attack Position (`positionSwitched` to Atk), this survives damage (`damage` on itself), a friendly Unit dies (`destroyed`), this destroys a Unit (`destroyed` with `killerId` itself), you launch a Starship (`starshipLaunched`), Start of Turn (`startOfTurn`), this discarded to Temporary (`discarded` with `byTemporary`, in its graveyard). A face-down or Dormant card keeps its mark until its text acts. ([[R3536001]]) |
| Endless Evolve | "Endless Evolve": this evolves again each time its condition is met | `StaticFlags.endless_evolve`, read by `evolution::can_evolve`, lifts [[R3536000]]'s once-only rule: each time the card's own trigger evolves it, it evolves again, one evolution each, its Evolve text runs, and the evolution multiplier applies as ever ([[R3536003]]). A standing "Evolves when" is met once while it holds, so it evolves the card once a stay ([[R5236030]]) |
| Start of turn | Controller's turn start, before the draw | After the mana refresh, the Brittle tick (and the Dormant countdown, [[R1839001]]) and the "Animated on your turn" step; delayed effects first (K-Pop Fanatic's steal), then the trigger queue in [[R68]] order ([[§2.2]], [[R62]]). A card whose script sets this hook, or End of turn's, is in the pool the query's `turnHook` names (M #9 Joint Filing's Cry, [[R3134001]]) |
| In N turns | At the start of its owner's Nth turn from now | A delayed effect counted in the turns its owner starts (`delay`'s `turns`), an extra turn counting and one made on the other player's turn counting from the owner's next; it resolves with the start-of-turn delayed effects, before the draw, whatever became of the card that made it, and while it waits a countdown badge (`label`) stands on its owner's side in both views (M+ #4, [[R1181000]]) |
| End of turn | Controller's turn end, before cleanup | Same queue; "add this back to hand" spells and Combo-Index resolve here; then the end-of-turn trap window (Bread and Butter, Intern Stimmy), then end-of-turn delayed effects ([[§2.2]], [[R62]]); an "Animated on your turn" card is still a Unit for all of it and returns at the end of cleanup ([[R383]]) |
| Float | A player Floats when their turn ends with unspent mana; the Float is that much mana | The `turnEnded` event's `unspentMana` ([[§2.2]]'s end step, the engine's `float_of`); a turn that spends everything is no Float ([[R1563000]]). "Reveals when any player Floats" is a trap trigger in the end-of-turn trap window (#18, [[R62]]). "Float reduces cost" is a hand trigger ([[R464]]) on its owner's Float, never the opponent's, and only while the card is in that hand, never in the deck (a deck trigger too through v0.5.9f, [[R2657002]]): a `costMod` of minus the Float on the card, kept in hand and deck until a graveyard or exile ([[R766]]), the price never below 0 (`float_reduces_cost`). "Float reduces Tribute requirement by 1" is a hand trigger on the same Float, the same way: 1 off the card's Tribute X per Float, whatever its size, kept in hand and deck (`memory.tributeLowered`), never below 0, and gone with a graveyard, an exile or the field's reset, a bounce included ([[R78]], [[R1841003]], `float_lowers_tribute`). C #90's quests "Float 3 or more mana" and "Float 5 or more mana" count a Float of at least that much |
| Trigger multiplier | Your hooks run extra times | The highest acting multiplier holds, never the sum ([[R820]]): each extra start- or end-of-turn run is its own queue entry ([[R821]]), and each extra Cry or Death reruns the play or its snapshot ([[R822]], [[R823]]) |
| Start of Game | After mulligan, before turn 1 | No card prints it since Heroic Power's Cry took its place ([[R3134002]]); the hook stays engine vocabulary, run at [[§2.1]] step 4 and as a card arrives in a hand, a library or on the field, a Transform's new card included ([[R151]], [[R4816000]]). Heroic Power wires it to the roll of a copy on the field with no Cry, and through v0.5.11r to the roll of every copy |
| Once per Turn | Activated ability limit | An Activate counts its uses per turn on the instance (`memory.activations`, below), Heroic Power's powers among them since [[R752]] ([[R139]]) |
| Activate / Activate X / Activate ♾️ | Once per turn on your turn, click the card to do an effect; "Activate X" up to X times per turn, "Activate ♾️" any number of times ([[R384]], [[R1751000]]) | The card's controller activates it in their own main phase (or the other seat, for an ability whose cost opens it to either player: the activator pays and runs it, [[R1105]]), with no prompt open and the game not over, while the card is on the field and acting (the top of its pile, or a face-up backrow card) and not Frozen ([[R2246000]]); summoning sickness and exertion do not apply. A card set face-down by its Cloaked has one ability while it is set, its Activate: Reveal, offered only while the card can come out whole and spending none of its own abilities ([[R783006]]). Uses are counted per ability per turn on the instance (`memory.activations = { turn, count, abilities: { <id>: <count> } }`, [[R1751000]]) and reset when it leaves the field ([[R78]]), so a bounced card or a copy starts fresh; multiple activate abilities on a card track uses per ability separately ([[R1751000]]). An ability that "has an extra turn cooldown" (`cost.extraTurnCooldown`) can't be used on its user's next turn after a use, an extra turn counting as that turn: kept in `memory.cooldowns`, so it too is cleared when the card leaves the field ([[R1841000]]). An ability with an N-turn cooldown (`cost.cooldownTurns`, Stinkyscape+ #125 Omura) is usable once, then not again for N of its controller's turns: a cooldown of N sits out N−1 of its user's turns, and the view shows the turns left (`cooldownTurnsLeft`); Refresh leaves it ([[R3550003]]). ♾️ is bounded by `ACTIVATE_UNLIMITED_CAP` (100 per ability per turn), and so is an X a Buff or KY's Constant took past it ([[R884000]]). A cost written into the ability ("Tribute a Unit", "Discard a random card", "Spend (X)") is paid as it is activated, and an ability whose cost cannot be paid cannot be activated. Its declared targets and modes travel in the `activate` action ([[§10.2]], [[R81]]); choices made during resolution are prompts. Activating is not a play: nothing that counts or answers plays sees it (Combo, Quickstriker, "whenever you play", Ceaseless Void), while what the effect plays or casts counts as usual ([[R70]]). One check is both `legalActions`' list and the reducer's refusal, as for Heroic Power ([[R43]]). A Nerf and a Buff move X by 1, never below 1, and a Buff turns "Activate" into "Activate 2"; neither touches ♾️ ([[R386]]). Event `activated` |
| Hand and deck triggers | "While this is in your hand / deck: when … , summon this" | A trigger answered from its owner's hand or deck, like Corpse Eater's hand trigger; "summon this from your hand/deck" moves the card straight to the field (a summon, no Cry, [[R1]]). A face whose script answers from where the card waits, its hand, deck or graveyard, prints that trigger as "While this is in your <zone>: …", naming exactly those zones, and its written words keep that line whole; only the Float term, a discard and a Spell's turn hook name their moment without the zone ([[R5038000]]). A card in a deck is hidden, so the summon is the first either player sees of it ([[R97]]). A library card answers only its deck triggers, after the hand's and before the graveyard's in instance creation order, never by library position ([[R464]]). "After a Spell of yours that targeted an enemy resolves, cast this at it" (M+ #17 Dogpile): the enemies a Spell picks as it resolves are kept with it, and its `cardResolved` names the one card that answers it — the leftmost in the hand, else the topmost in the deck — and the enemy, the first still there; that card's trigger casts it there, aimed, and every other copy does nothing ([[R1180005]]). "End of Turn: fuse a different card of a kind into this" (Stinkyscape+ #6.1 Fine Fermented Grape): a hand trigger on its controller's turn end fusing another card of the named kind into it ([[R3558005]]) |
| Hand aura | "The card to the right/left of this …" | A hand card's aura on its neighbours ([[§4.5]]): the card to its left takes what its `handAura` names for the left, the card to its right what it names for the right — Radiance while the lender sits beside it, a price move either way. A lent card is Radiant only while the pair holds; one that leaves the hand keeps its Radiance, and one made Radiant on its own keeps it too, its lend forgotten ([[R3558001]]) |
| When they meet | "When they meet both become Radiant" | Two cards of one hand, added to its two ends, become Radiant together the moment they sit side by side, however they got there — added that way, or passed by every card between them leaving. A card a full hand burns never meets its partner. The meeting takes the named price when it names one, and the waiting pair wears the `meet` mark (gold) in its owner's hand view alone ([[R3558002]]) |
| Cast from the deck | "When your exile has 5 cards cast this" | A standing condition on a library card, read at every settled state check ([[§4.5]]): while it holds, the card is cast from its owner's deck, its owner answering for it, at most `DECK_CAST_ACTION_CAP` casts an action. A card that leaves the deck casts nothing further ([[R3558007]]). The cast is a cast and no play, as a cast on draw is ([[R3920001]]) |
| Replacement ("would … instead") | An effect changes an event before it happens | A replacement acts at a fixed point and nowhere else: *would take lethal damage* ([[§4.4]] step 4a, heroes and Units, [[R1204]]; in a Stack pile only the top's, [[R1842001]]), *would be healed* (inside Heal, [[§6.3]]), *would die* ([[§4.5]] step 1, before cards move), *would go to a graveyard* (every zone move into a graveyard; "exiled instead" or "put on the bottom of your deck instead"), and *a friendly unit is targeted* ([[§4.2]] step 2 for an attack; [[§10.5]] step 1 and every target prompt for an effect). A trap that watches one fires there; an aura that watches one applies there. My Pawn's attack cancel ([[R44]]) was the first. Several replacements of one event apply one at a time in [[R68]]'s order, each card at most once, each re-checking the changed event ([[R460]]); a card a replacement exiles as it dies has still died: its Death fires, Reborn returns it and `destroyed` reports it ([[R4207000]], superseding [[R461]]); an aura that says "anytime something would be Destroyed, it is Exiled instead" (Stinkyscape #105 Disintegrex; its Radiant face, an enemy permanent) applies at the same *would die* point, after the would-die replacements, to every card the collection still holds, Units and backrow cards alike, which still die ([[R1849002]]); would-die is [[§4.5]] step 1's collection only, never a Sacrifice ([[R462]]), and a trap there that answers a Unit dying in combat (a hit killed it and its last hit was combat damage) may resolve its follow-up at once, before any card moves, the check then starting over (Stinkyscape #138 Pocket Peptides, [[R1875006]]); targeting means a declared `target` pick or a `target` prompt's answer ([[R450]]) |
| Aura | "Aura: …": standing text that works while its card is face-up and acting on the field (the top of its pile, [[§3]]) and reaches beyond the card: other cards on either side, a player's rules or a hero | Static modifier layer, recomputed on every state change ([[§10.4]]). Every such line prints the label, whatever runs it (a layer, a static flag, a replacement, the engine, or a field trigger when the line names no moment: C #87, M #11, S #37, S+ #74), and a face prints one "Aura:" line, its standing sentences together. Not an aura, so bare or under its own label: hand, deck or graveyard text (Hand aura, Hand and deck triggers), a lasting effect a Spell or a Trap leaves ("this turn", "from now on"), a Trap's reveal condition and firing, a keyword, a trigger or timing word (Cry, Start of turn, "When …", "Whenever …", "Once per turn, when …", "Reveals when …", Activate), an Embiggened line, a "While …:" condition, and the card's text about itself alone: its stats and keywords, its attacks, the damage it deals or takes, how it is played, stacked or targeted ("Has +1 Attack for each other friendly Unit", "This can't go Berserk"), which reads as a keyword does ([[R5039000]]) |
| Combo X | Extra effect if X or more cards were played earlier this turn | `turnLog.cardsPlayed` counter checked at play time; X defaults to 1 |
| Echo X | Recast this card X more times | Play resolves, then the same instance re-resolves X times with fresh mode/target prompts (a card that keeps its targets asks no target prompt: each repeat takes the first resolution's picks, and fizzles once they are gone, [[R3048002]]); a repeat is no play, so the card is played once ([[R2179002]]); Twinspell grants Echo +1 to the next spell.
