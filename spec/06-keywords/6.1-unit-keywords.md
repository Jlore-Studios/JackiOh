### 6.1 Unit keywords (computed as a set per unit)

Animated, Brittle and Temporary are printed on, or given to, cards that are not Units as well; their rows say how. Which cards and tokens print each keyword, and on which face, is [[CARDS#Keywords]], written from the catalog every day (#3804), so no row here lists them; a row names a card only for what the catalog's keywords do not hold: a card that gives, lends or sets the keyword, or one that draws from it.

**Ruling (random keyword pool):** the combat keyword pool ([[R21]], [[R781005]], [[R1025000]], [[R1615000]]) is Taunt, Rush, First Strike, Flying, Ranged, Poisonous, Reborn, Divine Shield, Windfury, Cleave, Deft, Lifesteal, Pierce, Trample and Frenzy, in that order, which picks what a seeded draw returns. Pierce joined in patch v0.1.1 ([[R346]]), Windfury by [[R636]] and Deft in patch v0.2.4 ([[R49]]), and S01 appends Frenzy ([[R3551004]]); patch v0.3.7 made it the designer's combat keyword pool, adding Flying, Ranged, Indestructible and Spell Damage +1 ([[R781005]]), patch v0.3.Y took Armor 1 and Spell Damage +1 out again, so no random grant gives either ([[R1025000]]), and issue #1615 took Charge and Indestructible out as too swingy, so no random grant gives those either ([[R1615000]]). Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao, the Conjure Rush Tokens, a Buff's keyword row and Keymaster Keenus's Aura draw from it. Immutable, Untributable ([[R1220]]), Stack, Lucky, Equip (M #65, M #67, [[R1086]]), Cloaked, Crater and Flashback stay out as meaningless or too swingy on a token, and so does Temporary, which does nothing on the field ([[R637]]), and Tradable, which does nothing out of a hand ([[R1838000]]). A unit never gets a keyword it already has, and an Indestructible one, Indestructible by its own text or another card's, is never offered Taunt, which it could not have ([[R347]], [[R781004]]).

**Ruling (keyword line order):** a card's keyword line ([[§5.4]], [[R366]]) prints its items in one fixed order, in three tiers: **economy** (cost, card flow and how or where a card is played), then **powerful effects and drawbacks**, then **combat** (in the order a fight reads: when it attacks, where it reaches, what its hits do, how it survives) ([[R4747000]]). In full: Tribute X and Echo X first, then Tradable, Flashback, Temporary X, Cloaked, Equip, Stack, Flexible, GIGA, Woozy X, Spell Damage +X, Lucky X, Indestructible, Immutable, Immune to Spells, Untributable, Evasive, Can't attack, Can't Defend, Brittle X, Crater, Animated, Animated on your turn, Starship Piece, Starship, Charge, Rush, Windfury, Frenzy, Deft, Taunt, Flying, Ranged, First Strike, Pierce, Trample, Cleave, Poisonous, Toxic, Burn, Lifesteal, Divine Shield, Armor X and Reborn. Evolve never prints as a bare word on the keyword line, only as "Evolve:" ability text, so it has no place, and the retired Immune to tribal tag based hate is left out too; repeats of a numbered keyword stay together at their kind's place. A display call only: the engine's keyword set keeps first-seen order, so no game plays differently.
