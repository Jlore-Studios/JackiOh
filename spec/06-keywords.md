## 6. Keyword glossary

Every keyword below maps to one engine primitive; a card script only ever composes these primitives.

Players read two of this spec's words differently (v0.1.1, [[R373]]): the rules' library is shown to players as the Deck, and Sacrifice as Tribute. The rules, the engine's identifiers and this spec keep "library" and "Sacrifice".

### 6.1 Unit keywords (computed as a set per unit)

Animated, Brittle and Temporary are printed on, or given to, cards that are not Units as well; their rows say how. The Cards column names Core cards as #N and the new sets' as C #N and C+ #N; for a keyword Core already had, it lists the Core cards, and [[§8.6]] and [[§8.7]] show which Classic and Classic+ cards print it.

| Brittle X | At the start of your turn, after a full turn cycle, the count drops by 1; at 0 the card is destroyed | A count on the instance, kept in every zone the card passes through (like `radiant`, [[R78]], [[R766]]) but ticking on the field only ([[R638]]): a card in a hand or a deck holds the count it has. A count given by an effect ("Give Brittle N" sets it to N; "gain +N Brittle" adds N) is held until the card enters the field, and a printed Brittle starts when the card enters the field; either way the count's turn cycle starts at that arrival (a move from one field zone to another is no arrival). The tick is [[§2.2]]'s step after the mana refresh, at the start of the turn of the card's controller, for the cards they control on the field: a count started on turn t first ticks at the first such start numbered t + 2 or later (`BRITTLE_FIRST_TICK_TURNS`), then at each one after. At 0 it is an ordinary destroy, so Indestructible ignores it ([[R46]]) and the count stays 0, checked again at each tick. Degrade lowers it by 1 and Upgrade raises it by 1 ([[§6.3]]); a Vanilla removes a printed Brittle and keeps a given one, as [[§10.4]] keeps every granted keyword; a copy never inherits a count ([[R57]]). Public on the field, except on a face-down card, whose count only its controller reads until it is revealed (C+ #74); the owner's in hand; `counterChanged` carries the kind `brittle`, and the destroy is reported by `crumbled` | C+ #23, C+ #74, T-AI-3 |
| Windfury | Can attack twice each turn | A Unit with Windfury, read through the layers ([[§10.4]]), may declare up to `WINDFURY_ATTACKS` (2) attacks in a turn, each a full attack ([[§4.2]]). The first spends the exertion a position switch needs ([[§4.1]], [[R6]]), so it cannot switch afterwards; a Unit that declared one attack before gaining Windfury may attack once more, and one that loses it after its first attack may not attack again ([[R636]]). `legalActions` and the reducer's refusal read the same count (`combat.hasExertion`). An effect's attack spends no exertion ([[R20]], [[R53]]) and is not counted. It is in [[R21]]'s random keyword pool, so Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao and an Upgrade can reach it | M #95.1 CN Golem's Radiant face ([[R1245]]) |
| Temporary | Discarded from its owner's hand at the end of their turn | A card keyword, in the set of a card in a hand (`cardKeywords`): printed on its running face or given by an effect ("Add … to your hand. They are Temporary"). Cleanup ([[§2.2]]), after every end-of-turn step, discards each Temporary card in the ending player's hand, in hand order, with no choice and no rng draw; it is a discard ([[§6.3]]), so "whenever you discard" sees each one and a unit-token card ceases to exist ([[R11]]). It does nothing on the field, in a deck or in a graveyard, and a card played before the end of the turn escapes it; a Vanilla loses a printed Temporary and keeps a given one ([[§10.4]]). Not temporary mana ([[§2.3]]), a different thing that only shares the word. [[R637]] | C+ #54's Books (its base and Radiant faces both) |
| Deft | Can attack and switch position in the same turn | A Unit with Deft, read through the layers ([[§10.4]]), may attack and switch position in the same turn ([[§4.1]], [[R6]], [[R49]]): one attack and one switch, each spending only its own exertion, in either order. `legalActions` and the reducer's refusal read the same function (`combat.hasExertion`). It is in [[R21]]'s random keyword pool, so Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao and an Upgrade can reach it | #45 |

**Ruling (random keyword pool):** Plastic Surgery and Zao Gao draw from Taunt, Armor 1, Rush, Charge, First Strike, Poisonous, Lifesteal, Reborn, Divine Shield, Trample, Cleave, Pierce (added by patch v0.1.1, [[R346]]), Windfury ([[R636]]), Deft ([[R49]], patch v0.2.4). Indestructible, Immutable, Stack and Lucky are excluded as too swingy or meaningless on a token, and so is Temporary, which does nothing on the field ([[R637]]). A unit never gets a keyword it already has.

### 6.2 Triggers and timing words

| Keyword | Rule | Engine semantics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cry | When a card enters the field for the first time | **Ruling:** fires only when played from hand (or by Cast on draw / Echo / Call to Chaos casting), and since patch v0.2.0 also when played from a graveyard under a permission ([[§6.3]] Play) or cast by any other effect ([[§6.3]] Cast) ([[R1]]). Copies, Recruit, Reborn, tokens and Transform results do not fire it, nor does a Flicker, with one exception: a summon that names the card it summons fires its Cry (C+ #19 League of Losers' Mid Loser, [[R411]]). Otherwise Duplicating Felinors fills the board for 2 mana. "Trigger a Cry" ([[§6.3]]) runs one by effect. Targets are chosen at play time. |
| Death | When sent from the field to the GY | Fires on destroy, sacrifice or combat death, a Brittle crumble on the field included; not on bounce, exile, transform, Flicker or steal, nor when an Animated card moves between its zones. A backrow card that prints Death fires it as a Unit does ([[§4.5]]). A token with Death still fires it after vanishing. |
| Cry and Death | Both hooks share one script | #22 |
| Start of turn | Controller's turn start, before the draw | After the mana refresh, the Brittle tick and the "Animated on your turn" step; delayed effects first (K-Pop Fanatic's steal), then the trigger queue in [[R68]] order ([[§2.2]], [[R62]]) |
| End of turn | Controller's turn end, before cleanup | Same queue; "add this back to hand" spells and Combo-Index resolve here; then the end-of-turn trap window (Bread and Butter, Intern Stimmy), then end-of-turn delayed effects ([[§2.2]], [[R62]]); an "Animated on your turn" card is still a Unit for all of it and returns at the end of cleanup ([[R383]]) |
| Start of Game | After mulligan, before turn 1 | Only Heroic Power |
| Once per Turn | Activated ability limit | An Activate counts its uses per turn on the instance (`memory.activations`, below), Heroic Power's powers among them since [[R752]] ([[R139]]) |
| Activate / Activate X / Activate ♾️ | Once per turn on your turn, click the card to do an effect; "Activate X" up to X times per turn, "Activate ♾️" any number of times ([[R384]]) | The card's controller activates it in their own main phase, with no prompt open and the game not over, while the card is on the field and acting (the top of its pile, or a face-up backrow card); summoning sickness and exertion do not apply. Uses are counted per card per turn on the instance (`memory.activations = { turn, count }`) and reset when it leaves the field ([[R78]]), so a bounced card or a copy starts fresh. ♾️ is bounded by `ACTIVATE_UNLIMITED_CAP` (100 per card per turn). A cost written into the ability ("Tribute a Unit", "Discard a random card", "Spend (X)") is paid as it is activated, and an ability whose cost cannot be paid cannot be activated. Its declared targets and modes travel in the `activate` action ([[§10.2]], [[R81]]); choices made during resolution are prompts. Activating is not a play: nothing that counts or answers plays sees it (Combo, Quickstriker, "whenever you play", Ceaseless Void), while what the effect plays or casts counts as usual ([[R70]]). One check is both `legalActions`' list and the reducer's refusal, as for Heroic Power ([[R43]]). Degrade and Upgrade move X by 1, never below 1, and Upgrade turns "Activate" into "Activate 2"; neither touches ♾️ ([[R386]]). Event `activated` |
| Hand and deck triggers | "While this is in your hand / deck: when … , summon this" | A trigger answered from its owner's hand or deck, like Corpse Eater's hand trigger; "summon this from your hand/deck" moves the card straight to the field (a summon, no Cry, [[R1]]). A card in a deck is hidden, so the summon is the first either player sees of it ([[R97]]). A library card answers only its deck triggers, after the hand's and before the graveyard's in instance creation order, never by library position ([[R464]]) |
| Replacement ("would … instead") | An effect changes an event before it happens | A replacement acts at a fixed point and nowhere else: *would take lethal damage* ([[§4.4]] step 4a), *would be healed* (inside Heal, [[§6.3]]), *would die* ([[§4.5]] step 1, before cards move), *would go to a graveyard* (every zone move into a graveyard; "exiled instead" or "put on the bottom of your deck instead"), and *a friendly unit is targeted* ([[§4.2]] step 2 for an attack; [[§10.5]] step 1 and every target prompt for an effect). A trap that watches one fires there; an aura that watches one applies there. My Pawn's attack cancel ([[R44]]) was the first. Several replacements of one event apply one at a time in [[R68]]'s order, each card at most once, each re-checking the changed event ([[R460]]); a unit exiled instead of dying has no Death, no Reborn and no `destroyed` ([[R461]]); would-die is [[§4.5]] step 1's collection only, never a Sacrifice ([[R462]]); targeting means a declared `target` pick or a `target` prompt's answer ([[R450]]) |
| Aura | Effect while in play | Static modifier layer, recomputed on every state change (section 10.4) |
| Combo X | Extra effect if X or more cards were played earlier this turn | `turnLog.cardsPlayed` counter checked at play time; X defaults to 1 |
| Echo X | Recast this card X more times | Play resolves, then the same instance re-resolves X times with fresh mode/target prompts; Twinspell grants Echo +1 to the next spell. The repeats outstanding live in `state.echoQueue` and resolve one at a time in the resolution loop, so a prompt inside one repeat pauses the rest until it is answered ([[§10.5]] step 6) |
| Cast on draw | Plays itself on draw, then draw again | Draw pipeline hook; a Cast ([[§6.3]]): costs nothing, counts as played ([[R40]], [[R70]]); chains capped by [[R58]]. Not dealt in setup: set aside through the opening draw and the mulligan, then shuffled into the deck ([[R635]]) |
| Targets chosen randomly | The effect picks its own target | Random target from the legal set (used by AI turns and My Pawn) |
| Quickdraw | Starts in your opening hand instead of a draw | Setup step 2: replaces one of the opening draws, so at most as many as the hand holds are dealt and the rest stay in the deck ([[R225]], [[R640]]) |
| Fatigue | Drawing from an empty library | Nth fatigue draw deals N damage (ruling) |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Play | Pay cost and cast from hand | `play(instance, X?, embiggen?, targets, modes)`; increments play counters; fires Cry. | Destroy | Field to GY | Marks destroyed; state check moves it; Indestructible ignores |
| Sacrifice | Your own card (or an enemy unit a Tribute allows, #55), field to GY, bypasses Indestructible | `sacrifice(instance)`; counts as a death |
| Exile | To the exile pile from anywhere | Increments the game exile counter; no Death trigger |
| Bounce | Return to controller's hand | Tokens vanish; hand cap applies; the instance resets per [[R78]] (buffs and damage included). **Ruling:** since balance patch 1 "Bounce" is the printed keyword for a permanent's return from the field to its controller's hand ([[R692]], [[R747]]); a card returned from the graveyard or exile, or a Spell after it resolves, says "Return … to hand" ([[R746]]) |

| Counter | Cancel a card being played or cast | In [[§10.5]]'s announce window (step 3a), before the card moves: it never resolves or enters the field and goes to its owner's graveyard, or to exile when the text says so.
