# Pending paragraphs for issue #3555 (Stinkyscape+ S08, the GIGA cards)

- **[[R3555000]] a permanent (a Unit) in your hand is a declared hand pick, its grants kept onto the field (issue #3555, 2026-11-11)**: "a permanent / a Unit in your hand" names no random, so it is a pick declared with the play ([[R81]]) from the player's own hand: a Unit, Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap for S+ #23, a Unit for S+ #100. GIGA, Indestructible and the +N/+N are given in the hand and kept onto the field ([[R1878003]]), where the card enters GIGA ([[R3546002]]). An Immutable hand card may be picked: [[R781001]] bars a Spell's aim only on the field, and a grant or a stat buff is none of [[R23]]'s. Radiant #23 picks two, and fewer resolve on what there is ([[R90]]).
- **[[R3555001]] an Embiggened Wall is GIGA at four times its stats, a text a Vanilla takes (issue #3555, 2026-11-11)**: GIGA is lent by the text to the card stamped embiggened ([[R1878001]], [[R3546002]]), and the stats are `EMBIGGENED_SCALE` times its face at [[§10.4]] layer 2. A Vanilla returns the Wall to its printed stats ([[R115]]) and keeps its size ([[R3546002]]). Immutable never stops its own text.
- **[[R3555002]] Big Time Recruits draws the N dearest permanents, a tie broken at random (issue #3555, 2026-11-11)**: the library's non-Spell cards by [[R24]]'s costs, a tie at the cut broken by the match rng, which draws nothing when there is no choice ([[R129]]). Fewer permanents draw what there is. Each is its own named draw, dearest first ([[R135]]): the hand cap burns ([[R4]]), cast on draw casts ([[R58]]). Each one that reaches the hand is given GIGA there by `drawFromLibrary`'s `keywords` rider, and the Radiant face also makes it Radiant ([[R1061]]).
- **[[R3555003]] Foreshadow loses its mana once per enemy summon, whether or not the attack is made (issue #3555, 2026-11-11)**: one trigger's two effects, each doing what it can (Hearthstone's order). An enemy that can't be attacked (Evasive, [[R3543002]]), or one gone before the trigger resolves, still costs the mana. Several summons stack.
