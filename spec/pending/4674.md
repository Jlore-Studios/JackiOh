**[[R4674000]] The patch bar's left arrow steps to the newer patch and its right arrow to the older patch (issue #4674, 2026-11-24)**: the arrows follow the tabs as drawn, newest first: the left arrow steps to the newer patch, toward the current patch, and is disabled on the newest; the right arrow steps to the older patch, toward the older tabs, and is disabled on the oldest the page lists; each names its patch (`Newer patch (v)`, `Older patch (v)`), and `patchPrevious` stays the older patch with `patchNext` the newer one.
