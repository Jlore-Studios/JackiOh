**[[R2881000]] lets a friend watch a live co-op game (issue #2881, 2026-11-03)**: a friend could watch a friend's live 1v1 match but not their co-op game, which [[§13]] left out of scope. The issue asked for the same watch of one co-op seat: friends only, under `SPECTATORS_PER_MATCH`, `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` behind, with no action offered and nothing a watcher sends reaching the game, the count told to both people and the socket closed after the result's view. The watcher sees the friend's seat's [[§13.6]] view and never a ping, and the friend's own teammate is refused. As that view holds the teammate's duel, a block between the watcher and the friend's teammate refuses a watch and ends one running, as a block with the friend does ([[§9.4]]). It takes R2881000, the first number of issue #2881's block. It edits [[§9.5]], [[§9.8]], [[§13.6]], [[§13.9]], [[§13.10]] and [[R2615000]].
