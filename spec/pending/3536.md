**[[R3536000]] A card evolves once; leaving the field, a copy or a Transform makes a card that may evolve again (issue #3536, 2026-11-10)**: the `evolve` verb marks the instance evolved before it runs anything, so a second evolve of the same permanent runs nothing, counts nothing and emits nothing; [[R78]]'s reset takes the mark off with the card leaving the field, and a copy or a Transform's new card may evolve. S #91 and S #92 keep their printed text and evolve once too. Rejected: keeping the mark in every zone like `awakened`, since Hearthstone resets a returned minion's once-only abilities.

**[[R3536001]] 'Evolves when' is read at the settled state check, or is the card's own trigger evolving itself (issue #3536, 2026-11-10)**: a standing condition is read at the state check once the board has settled, in [[R68]]'s order; an event condition is the card's own trigger evolving itself, and a Temporary discard is marked on its event so the card can evolve in its graveyard. Rejected: reading a standing condition before deaths, which would let a dying Unit evolve and count; and an engine enum of conditions, since ordinary triggers already say each event one.

**[[R3536002]] Evolve all cards in your deck: marked cards evolve at the state check after they enter the field (issue #3536, 2026-11-10)**: "Evolve all cards in your deck" marks each library card that prints an Evolve text, and a marked card evolves at the state check after it enters, so after its Cry. Marking is silent, since the library is hidden. Rejected: evolving inside the play pipeline, before the card's Cry.

**[[R3536003]] Your evolutions trigger twice or thrice: extra runs of the Evolve text, the highest multiplier holding (issue #3536, 2026-11-10)**: the highest acting multiplier holds, never the sum; the once-only rule counts evolvings, not repeats. The verb's first run is inline and the extra runs queue behind it, so a prompt in the text pauses the queue rather than dropping the rest. Rejected: running the extra runs inline, where a prompt would drop them.

**[[R3536004]] Each player counts the cards of theirs that evolved this game (issue #3536, 2026-11-10)**: an evolution counts for the evolved card's controller, and a mark counts nothing until the card evolves. Absent until the first, so a game without one hashes as before.

**[[R3536005]] An evolved card is marked on its views, reported by `evolved`, and wears a badge and glow (issue #3536, 2026-11-10)**: both seats see the mark and the event on a card they may read, and the face draws the Evolved badge with its inspect line while the client glows the card. Rejected: a mark only its controller sees, since the evolved card stands face-up on the field.
