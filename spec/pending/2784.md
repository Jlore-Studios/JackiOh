**[[R2784000]] gives every card its signature animation as an entry in its set's effects table: none, a bespoke recipe, or a shared motif per hook (issue #2784, 2026-11-02)**: the foundation of issue #2782's card animations. One JSON5 table per set (Stinkyscape's split in two at 091) holds every catalog card in catalog order as `"none: <reason>"`, `{ bespoke }` or a motif on its `cast` or `cry`, keyed by the definition of the readable play resolving now, so a hidden play keys none ([[R202]]). The first ten motifs, the damage and removal family, are pure planners over the existing cues in four beats inside the entry ([[R200]]), scaled by the effects speed ([[R201]]) and drawn not at all with the effects off or under reduced motion; twelve cards across the sets wear them as examples, and a dev-only `/dev/fx` gallery plays every motif and entry. It takes R2784000 and amends [[R502]] and [[R670]], whose one `CARD_FX` table becomes the per-set tables.
