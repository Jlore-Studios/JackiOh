**Open questions, rejected alternatives.** These are the builder's defaults, for a person to overrule:

- A fifth hardpoint, the spine behind the bow's guns, carries the defenses, the Ultralight Chassis and Salvage; the issue names four. Putting the defenses on the flanks was rejected: the flanks already hold every launcher and pod.
- At most 16 modules are drawn, the oldest pieces'; a Starship holding more draws the same picture whatever its pieces past the 16th. Drawing every one was rejected to keep two Starships' pictures within the 48 KiB each `STARSHIP_ART_MAX_BYTES` allows. Issue #3331's `ART_DETAIL_MAX_BYTES` is not on `main` yet, so 48 KiB stands in for it until it lands.
- A row of running lights along the keel, one for each module in its colors, oldest at the bow, shows the order of the pieces: without it, two pieces on different hardpoints drew the same launched ship in either order. Leaving the order invisible was rejected, since the issue asks that any two different piece lists differ.
- An escort is a frigate in silhouette (its prow, stripe and burn), not a full hull: at a quarter scale its windows and emblem cannot be seen, and dropping them keeps the biggest picture within its budget.
- A Radiant Starship gilds every module, so on a Radiant hull a piece's own Radiant face does not show; on a base hull it does.
- A name no design has gets one of the seven designs by its hash. Every name a Starship takes today is one of the seven (`STARSHIP_NAMES`, exported to the web so a test holds the designs to it).
- A kind on no ladder (every piece but the Beams, Repeaters, Cannons, Flak and Drills) draws at v3's size with no chevrons; the chevrons mark only a ladder's tier.
- Each class is drawn nearer in its window the smaller it is (a frigate at 1.15, a dreadnought at 0.8), so a frigate fills its window while each class still stands longer than the last. A module on a hull is drawn at 0.5 to 0.9 of its size; the plan's 0.28 to 0.55 drew them too small to see.
- A piece's card draws its module at 2.8 times on a slim deck section, not the plan's 2.4, so the module fills the card's window.
- The three Jlockeed pieces keep their blueprint grid as their bay, as the Jlockeed family draws.
- The collection's Dormant Starship, out of play, keeps the picture [[R503]] pins; only a Starship in play is built from its pieces.
- The hull plates grew from 8.5 by 5.5 to 10.5 by 7, so an icon can be made out on a board card.
