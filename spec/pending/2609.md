**[[R2609000]] A watcher's socket keeps a seat's limits on its later hellos and its junk frames (issue #2609, 2026-10-31)**: a watcher's socket had every later hello answered with its last view under the actor's lock, and its other frames went uncounted, while a player's socket coalesces its hellos ([[R835001]]) and is closed with 1008 past `MALFORMED_FRAMES_PER_WINDOW` malformed frames ([[R835002]]). A watcher now keeps a `SeatGuard` of its own: at most one later hello per `WATCH_HELLO_INTERVAL_MS` (1 s) is answered and sooner ones are dropped, and its frames that are not a hello, still unanswered, count against `MALFORMED_FRAMES_PER_WINDOW`; past it the socket is closed with 1008 and the players hear the new count. It takes R2609000, the first number of issue #2609's block, amends [[R1103000]] and [[R835002]] and edits [[§9.5]] and [[§9.8]].
