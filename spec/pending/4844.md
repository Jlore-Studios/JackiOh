**[[R4844000]] A fragment a kept script is keyed by claims that script's change (issue #4844, 2026-11-25)**: issue #4844 declared a helping aim on eight buff Spells' targets (Fortify, Hopeful Jelly Bean, Divine Spirit, Power Word Shield, Surge Alloy, Plastanium, Metaglass, Graphite), which changes only a stolen or random cast. `classify`'s forced games against three successive bases showed none of them playing differently, so its fragment could claim no `plays`, and `patches check` refused a fragment that claimed nothing, though its kept scripts need the version it ships as. The ruling lets that fragment ship; it edits no section and takes R4844000.
