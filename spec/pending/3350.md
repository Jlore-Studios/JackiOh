**[[R3350000]] A Mythic's normal picture, and a token printed Mythic's, draws a diorama of its own where a face draws depth planes: five to eight depth slices cut from its painter's still picture, some bent, two or more on an idle loop written for the card inside the motion slots, turned up to DIORAMA_TURN_MAX_DEG where the player turns it to look at it, drawn whole on the board while it moves, flown through by its takeover and drawn in the full-screen view, and flat and still under reduced motion (issue #3350, 2026-11-10; amends [[R3340000]], [[R3344000]], [[R3345000]])**: the owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to look into "quasi 3D models, giving them excess animations for these super, super rare ones", and this issue, past #3344's depth planes by role, gave each of the 30 Mythic cards and the 20 tokens printed Mythic a diorama of its own composed from its new picture (#3331, #3392 and the sets' picture passes). The plan cut each picture into keyed pieces (a scene's shapes by layer and part, a Stinkyscape painter's elements), had one file per card pick them into slices with depths, bends and loops, drew each slice as a picture of its own from the card's id and face alone, put them where a face draws its planes, on the board while it moves, in the full-screen view and in the takeover's flight, turned them to 25 degrees where the player turns the card to look at it, and held the loops inside the motion slots and the reduced-motion gate. Where those rulings would split or lift the picture, the diorama holds together: it never breaks the frame ([[R3344000]]'s near plane alone would lift), a board Mythic draws its slices in place of [[R3340000]]'s live layers, its window swelling on a hover and pressing in on a press and its slices over the farthest swinging on an attack, and the full-screen view holds the loops still while a tap pauses it and scales a turned diorama as little as keeps the screen covered. Rejected: slices by role alone, new drawing per slice or echo silhouettes, true 3D, loops outside the slots, a bent farthest slice, bends in the takeover, spinning Zephrys's cyclone whole, the frame-break on a diorama's slices, the live layers over or in place of a board diorama, dropping a board diorama's answer to an attack, and a full-screen turn at the pictures' own size.
