**[[R3767000]] A seat's replaced socket closes with SEAT_TAKEN_CLOSE_CODE, and its client stops in elsewhere, offering Play here (issue #3767, 2026-11-12; amends [[R1441]])**: a match opened in a second tab or on another device had the two clients fight over the seat: the server closed the older socket with 1000, every client reconnects on 1000 after 250 ms, and each reconnect took the seat back, for as long as both stayed open. Now the older socket of a seat, 1v1 and co-op, is closed with 4412, no grace starts ([[R744]]), and its client goes to `elsewhere` with no backoff and no socket however long it waits: the page says the game is open in another tab or on another device and offers Play here, whose press takes the seat back and puts the other tab there instead. A tab's own replacement of a stale socket never reaches that state, and a watcher's replacement stays 1000. Rejected: keeping 1000 and spotting the loop on the client; closing the new socket instead; a server-side grace; reusing 4410/4411, which mean the match is gone.
