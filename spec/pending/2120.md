**[[R2120000]] shows a chat message at once while it sends (issue #2120, part of #2036, 2026-10-28)**: a message the player sends shows at the end of the conversation marked as sending, the draft clears, and sends go to the server one at a time in the order sent, so the server stores them in that order. The server's copy replaces the pending row when it answers, and while a send is open a read takes only the friend's messages, so a message shows once even when a poll lands before the answer. A refused or failed send is marked failed in the server's words, with Retry (the same body again, in its place) and Discard. Hearthstone's whispers say nothing of a send in flight, so this follows the common messaging pattern; parallel sends (the server may store them out of order) and matching a read's message to a pending one by its body (the word filter changes it, and two equal sends are ambiguous) are rejected.
