**[[R4810000]] The opponent's imagined reply keeps steps for its attacks and ends its turn when its steps run out (issue #4810, 2026-11-25)**: an audit of the AI crate found that the AI's model of its opponent's turn ([[§9.9]]), which takes at most `AI_REPLY.max_steps` (12) steps, spent them beside S #66 Miner v5 on its Activate and the Resources each use gains, so the reply stopped mid-turn with a lethal unswung and the line read as survived. The ruling caps the reply's known plays and visible Activates at `AI_REPLY.play_steps` (6) and has a reply whose steps run out end its turn in up to `AI_REPLY.close_steps` (3) more; it adds one sentence to [[§9.9]]'s "The opponent's reply" and takes those two constants in `crates/ai/src/config.rs`.
