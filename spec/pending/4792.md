**[[R4792000]] The AI prices an attack whose target is drawn at the mean of its targets (issue #4792, 2026-11-26)**: the audit of issue #4787 found that while a Mayor acts ([[R1200]]) the AI priced an attack naming `"random"` at nothing, so the opponent it models never attacked, its own attacks fell behind every play, and `ready_gap` counted each as a hit on the hero. The AI now prices such an attack at the mean of a hit on each target it may draw, in the reply and the move order, and counts it in `ready_gap` only when the hero is its only target. It edits [[§9.9]]'s The opponent's reply and adds the switch `AI_REPLY.drawn_attacks`.
