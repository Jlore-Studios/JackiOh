**[[R3138000]] has a Flashback play of an embiggen card offered at both of its prices (issue #3138, part 4 of 7 of #3128, 2026-11-05)**: the owner's Stinkyscape Balance Changes IV (issue #3128) gives M+ #5 Spirit Lash, "(0) embiggen (1)", Flashback on both faces. [[R783012]] says a card with Flashback is played from the graveyard "at its current cost (X chosen at the play)" and [[R3039001]] keeps the keyword through each play, but neither says which price an embiggen card pays from there. A decision the spec does not make follows Hearthstone semantics, where a card with more than one way to be played offers each of them wherever it is played from, so the card is offered at (0) and Embiggened at (1), the choice made again with each play and nothing left on the card. The engine's price list already does this, so no code changes; the ruling records it and Spirit Lash's tests prove it. It edits §6.1's rows for Flashback, Tradable and Divine Shield (the cards that print them) and §6.3's Play row, and §8's rows and the Radiant audit follow the other eleven cards' costs, stats and keywords, none of which needs a ruling.
