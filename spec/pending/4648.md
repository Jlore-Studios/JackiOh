**[[R4648000]] The AI prices an unevolved card's coming evolution (issue #4648, 2026-11-24)**: the evaluation read an Evolve card ([[§6.3]]) at its body alone, so a Lone Drifter a zone from becoming a 12/12 counted as a 4/4. Once a decision, on a sandbox copy, the AI now evolves each card that can still evolve on either field and each such Unit in its own hand, placed first, and prices one run of its text by the change in its controller's evaluation. The same root reads how the card stands to evolve from its own "Evolves when" on throwaway copies: by its side's unit zones filled, by the (0) Cost cards the AI's own hand holds of those it needs, by its health lost, or at a fixed share when it waits on an event or on a hand the AI cannot see. `unit_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.evolve` of the price, once for each time the text would run ([[R3536003]]), times that closeness. A card its own turn or attack text evolves counts through those prices ([[R2477000]], [[R2688000]]), the enemy's hand is never probed, and `GREEDY_EVAL` reads none of it. So the AI fills its last zone for its Drifters and Polymorphs the enemy's Drifter before it grows.
