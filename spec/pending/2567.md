**[[R2567000]] the first player's win share counts only decided games (issue #2567, 2026-10-31)**: the Game figures panel and `stats report --games` took the first seat's share and its Wilson interval over every game, so a draw sat in the denominator as a first-seat loss and the first player could look weaker than they are: with even seats and 4% draws it read 48%. The share and its interval are now over `games - draws`, and the share is null (unknown) when every game was drawn, with the interval [0, 1]; `games`, `draws`, `firstWins` and the stored totals do not change, so there is no migration. The owner approved it without the draw figures, so the panel reads "First player wins X% of decided games (±m)" and shows no draw share. It takes R2567000, the first number of issue #2567's block, amends [[R1994000]] and edits [[§9.11]].
