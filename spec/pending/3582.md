**[[R3582000]] A handicap's `fewerOpeningCards` and `drawEvery` (issue #3582, 2026-11-10)**: a dungeon run's early enemies must be weaker than Easy (issue #3581), so a handicap takes two optional fields: `fewerOpeningCards` takes cards off a seat's opening draw ([[§2.1]]'s entry plus `extraOpeningCards`), never below one card, and the mulligan works on that hand; `drawEvery` n has the seat take its start-of-turn draws ([[R183]]) only on its own turns whose count is a multiple of n (its 2nd, 4th and so on with 2), a skipped turn drawing nothing, taking no fatigue step and casting nothing on draw, while a card's own "draw" still draws. A negative `fewerOpeningCards` and a `drawEvery` below 1 are refused; absent, 0 and 1 change nothing and are not stored, so every other game hashes and replays as before, and no tier, lesson, Duos seat or ladder bot sets either. Rejected: drawing on the seat's 1st, (n+1)th and so on turns, which draws before any skip, and a floor of 0 cards, which leaves a mulligan with nothing to offer. It takes R3582000, the first number of #3582's block.
