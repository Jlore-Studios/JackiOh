**[[R3838000]] The Almanac and the Statistics page have a Show card text button that turns their full art off (issue #3838, 2026-11-13)**: a full-art card prints no words, so a reader browsing the Almanac or the Statistics page could not read it. Both pages' headers hold a "Show card text" on/off button in the `Segmented` look; while it is pressed every face on the page prints its normal face with its words (`FullArtAllowed` false, as a lesson's), whatever the Full-art cards setting or a pick says. The device keeps the choice, one for both pages, and it never writes the setting, so games are unchanged. Flipping the setting from the page, the setting alone (an owned pick keeps its full art) and a choice per page were rejected. It takes R3838000 and edits [[§9.11]] and [[§10.10]].

**[[R3838001]] On the Almanac, a mouse resting on any card with words opens the hover preview with its whole text beside it (issue #3838, 2026-11-13)**: the card grid opened a preview only on a face that prints less than its whole text (#1017), and a small grid card's fitted words can be too small to read. Below the Almanac's `AlwaysFullText`, every card with words opens the hover preview, and its Full text panel carries the card's whole text at reading size; the deck builder and the boards keep #1017's rule. Opening a preview on every card everywhere was rejected. It takes R3838001 and edits [[§10.10]].

**[[R3838002]] A reference's tooltip carries its card's whole text beside a face that prints less, and on the Almanac beside any face (issue #3838, 2026-11-13)**: a reference's tooltip ([[R279]]) showed only the named card's face, so a full-art card's was its art alone. The tooltip now carries the Full text panel beside a face that prints less, everywhere, and beside any face with words below an `AlwaysFullText`. The face stays `REF_TOOLTIP_HEIGHT_PX` tall; the panel stands on its left at the hover preview's width, or on a phone stacks above it, `PREVIEW_EXTENDED_STACKED_MAX_PX` tall at most, the face shrinking to fit (`refTooltipSize`). The panel on the Almanac alone was rejected. It takes R3838002 and edits [[§10.10]].
