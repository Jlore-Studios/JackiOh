**[[R3822000]] The AI prices the Burn and Toxic still to come on Units and heroes (issue #3822, 2026-11-13)**: the AI's evaluation read neither counter, though both are public ([[R3540005]]). Over `AI_AFFLICTION_TICKS` (4) of the controller's turn starts, each tick met as [[R3540001]] meets it (a Divine Shield takes the first hit, Armor meets Burn and Toxic pierces it, the unit's own cap holds both, an Indestructible unit takes none), a Unit loses `unit_affliction` of a health point for each point the ticks will deal it, and one the nth tick kills keeps `1 − unit_doomed / n` of itself, or its Brittle share when that is smaller. A hero's coming ticks, read through `projected_hero_damage` and held to all but 1 of its life, cost its side `hero_affliction` of their price on the hero curve, and the enemy's also count at the race's flat rate. `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps all three at 0, the learned features still leave both counters out, and no game rule changes.
