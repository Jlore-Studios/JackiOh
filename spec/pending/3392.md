The plan put Core's 110 scenes in three recipe files in Core's family, gave the figure kit the poses, heads, headgear, outfits and items the briefs' people need and the scene kit their places, creatures, props and weather, let a recipe stand more figures than one (`cast`), drew beasts and objects that do not stand like a person as creature parts, kept KY in one look, redrew the two Core proofs from their new briefs, moved the full-art Core cards' props beat to their near layer, and tightened the short list's cap to 453. Rejected: a figure body for beasts and objects, keeping the two proof scenes, keeping motif glyphs in scenes, and leaving the cap at 561.
