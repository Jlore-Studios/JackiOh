**[[R2259000]] A Dormant card that awakens is summoning sick (issue #2259, 2026-10-28)**: the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set. [[R1839002]] had an awakened card not summoning sick, as Hearthstone's awakened minions are not, so a Dormant Starship launched for (3) by its Activate, or an Imprisoned Unit whose countdown ended at its controller's turn start, attacked at once. Now an awakening, however it comes, makes the card summoning sick for the rest of that turn, its `summonedTurn` the turn it awoke in: Rush and Charge let it attack as they let any sick Unit, and the invariant monitor's shadow counts the awakening as an entry. It takes R2259000, the first number of issue #2259's block, and edits [[§6.3]] Awaken and [[§8.10]] #17.
