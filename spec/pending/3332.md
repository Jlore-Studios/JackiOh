**[[R3332000]] A card's frame in its effects table: an entrance beside any entry of a Unit or a Field Spell, or alone; a takeover look beside a Mythic's; and the signature tier, held to loudness budgets by rarity and type, with a ratchet of every Legendary and Mythic still without one (issue #3332, 2026-11-07; amends [[R670]], [[R2722001]], [[R2784000]] and [[R2794000]])**: the owner asked, in issue #3330, that every Legendary and Mythic get an animation of its own, as in Hearthstone, instead of a few shared motifs; 86 of the 111 non-token ones played one. Issue #3332 is the foundation of #3330's per-set passes: it asked to fold the four marquee entrances and the four takeover looks into the table, unchanged, to add a signature tier with budgets by rarity and type inside the existing caps, and a ratchet listing every Legendary and Mythic without one, which only shrinks. The plan read "permanent" as a Unit or a Field Spell, the cards that land face up as they are played, a Trap's entrance being its firing, and made the tier an explicit flag. Rejected: a separate entrance or takeover table; a Trap's entrance at its reveal; and deriving the tier from an entrance.
