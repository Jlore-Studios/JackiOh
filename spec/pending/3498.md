**[[R3498000]] A decision a host's clock cuts short stops exploring at `AI_WRAP_UP_SHARE` of the time and still scores its finalists after the reply on every sampled world (issue #3498, 2026-11-09)**: the issue found that the search's reserve for its finalists' replies and their other sampled worlds was counted in nodes, not time, so a decision whose host clock (the browser's `PRACTICE_AI_CLOCK_MS`, the ladder bots' and the Duos AI team's `BOT_DECISION_DEADLINE_MS`) fired while its beam was still exploring replied to no finalist and judged each on one world, on exactly the slow devices and busy boards where that matters, while no gate, which all count nodes, saw it. The spec made no rule on it, so the builder followed the plan: a second callback, the wrap-up, fired by every host with a clock at a share of its limit, a named constant (0.6) that no screen has tested, stops the beam alone, its open lines end their turn and the replies and other worlds run on to the hard stop, with `SearchStats.replied` and `worldsScored` to report a decision that still fell short; with no clock every decision is as before. Rejected: the share passed from TypeScript, a stop reason of its own, no floor before the wrap-up is polled, one callback told the phase, failing the gate on a short decision, and closing the open lines under a budget cap too.
