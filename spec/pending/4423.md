Core #89 and TG #56 Corpse Eater, TG #35 Corpse Recycler, S+ #93 Recessive Replicators, M #60 Eschews, S #7 The Greatest Heist's Stage 2 and C #14 Shadowstep's base face read it, superseding in part [[R3542002]] and [[R4395000]].
