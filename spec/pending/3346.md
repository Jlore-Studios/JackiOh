**[[R3346000]] Plays that land with weight: an impact under every readable play sized by the mana it cost and coloured by its type, a trap's snap, a quieter impact for the other seat, and a board shake and a buzz for a heavy or massive play (issue #3346, 2026-11-07; beside [[R669]] and [[R702]])**: the owner asked, in issue #3330, for sounds beside the voice lines, "sort of like how when you play an expensive card, it feels the hit". Cost changed nothing about what the client played: `cardPlayed.costPaid` is public and no cue read it. The impact is sized by it in four tiers, 0 to 2, 3 to 5, 6 to 8 and 9 or more (`PLAY_IMPACT_TIERS`, tuned by ear later), coloured by the card's type, and sits on the effects bus so the voice line ducks it, as it does every effect. A card the viewer cannot name (the sentinel, [[R203]]) gets no impact, so it cannot be told apart from any other face-down card; a Trap's set keeps its click, a card cast as it is drawn keeps its sting, and a firing Trap gets a snap beside its sting. The other seat's impact is turned down by a cue trim of its own, `SfxParams.gain`, read by the engine beside the effect's gain; the existing `quiet` flag turns a cue down to 0.35, which is too low for "a step quieter". A heavy play shakes the board and a massive one shakes it harder, through the effects layer's shake, which already scales by the intensity setting and draws nothing under Reduce Motion ([[R2789000]]), and buzzes a phone by tier behind the Vibration switch. The issue says the shake holds "still under reduced motion" and its acceptance says it is "off under reduced motion"; the acceptance wins, as the effects layer's one rule for reduced motion. The unit's landing thud keeps its stats tier, so a cheap huge Unit and an expensive small one each read right, the impact by cost and the thud by stats 220 ms later. Rejected: sizing the impact by the card's rarity, which the sting already does; a new rarity-and-cost mix, which would make the tiers unreadable by ear; reusing `quiet` for the other seat, which is nearly silent; shaking or buzzing for a card the viewer cannot read, which would colour a face-down card as a landing when [[R203]] gives every one of them the plain whoosh; and a snap for a Field Trap's later firings, which the plain spell sound already answers.
