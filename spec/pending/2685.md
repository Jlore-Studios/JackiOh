**[[R2685000]] a 1v1 rematch offer is taken only within `REMATCH_OFFER_TTL_MS` of the finished match's end (issue #2685, 2026-11-01)**: the server kept one clock per finished match, not one per offer, and every offer from either seat restarted it, so a player who offered and closed the tab could be held to that offer for hours: the other player sent a mismatched offer every few minutes to keep the entry alive and then the matching one, and a game began with the first player absent, who could lose it by disconnect, in ranked with their rating. An offer is now refused once the window from the match's `finishedAt` has closed, as a co-op Play again's already is ([[R1398000]]): it lapses silently, records nothing and makes no game, while an entry that made one inside the window still answers its id. It amends [[R672]]'s offer window and edits [[§9.5]], and is the first number of issue #2685's block. A clock per seat's offer was rejected: more state, and the 1v1 and co-op windows would be counted differently.
