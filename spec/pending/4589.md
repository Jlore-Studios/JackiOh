**[[R4589000]] The recursion log (issue #4589, 2026-11-24)**: each side keeps a `RecursionLog` of this game — `played`, every face-up play as its `FaceUpRecord` (casts count, [[R70]]; countered plays never do, [[R448]]; face-down plays never do, [[R451]]), and `deaths`, the Units that died under that player, tokens included, each with the face it died on and the player-turn it died on. The log is kept once the game holds an Infinite Assault card; always on would move recorded hashes ([[R768]]), and `set_is_open` never opens for a single-player set.

**[[R4589001]] Time and copies (issue #4589, 2026-11-24)**: "this turn" is this player-turn, "since your last turn" this one and the one before. A random pick weighs each death record once ([[R60]]); IA #99, #110 and #116 each pick one record and copy it once or twice. A copy is fresh, on the face the card died with ([[R4577002]]), and two copies come from the one pick.

**[[R4589002]] What the recursion cards read (issue #4589, 2026-11-24)**: IA #105 takes the newest logged Spell, never a Field Spell; IA #113 takes the last N Spells but itself, oldest first, each cast new with every choice random ([[R452]]); IA #120 shuffles every logged play but itself, with its discount and face, up to the library cap ([[R80]]); IA #107 counts only the earlier Recursive Calls; IA #114's graveyard includes itself. With nothing to read each does nothing ([[R129]]).

**[[R4589003]] The Recursionist (issue #4589, 2026-11-24)**: IA #118 adds one Death run — the highest multiplier holds ([[R820]]), so beside Double Counting a Death runs twice — and a Reborn under it keeps Reborn once, so the Unit comes back twice; a Recursionist dying in the same pass doubles nothing ([[R823]]). Rejected: two bodies at once, for the one reserved zone ([[R64]]).
