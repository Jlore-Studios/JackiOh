**[[R2122000]] gives every control on a phone a 44x44 px tap area (issue #2122, part of #2036, 2026-10-28)**: under `(pointer: coarse), (max-width: 600px)`, a control smaller than `--hit-min` takes the tap through a transparent centred `::before`, a small input or select through its own height, and a checkbox or radio through its labels, while neighbours split the gap so no hit area covers another control. Inline links, cards and controls under an open modal are exempt, and spec 47 measures the board and every menu of [[R1581000]] at 390x844 on every pull request. It takes R2122000, the first number of issue #2122's block.
