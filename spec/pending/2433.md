**[[R2433000]] is the tutorial opponent playing only Core cards (issue #2433, part of #2429, 2026-10-30)**: the owner asked that the tutorial's opponent never play a card that isn't a Core card, even one added to its hand. A handicap's `coreOnly`, which `AI_TUTORIAL` sets, bars the seat from playing, setting or casting a card whose set is not Core, however it reached the hand: `legal_actions` lists no such play and `reduce` refuses one, so the AI, the random policy and the timeout all comply; such a card drawn is not cast on draw, and an effect casts or summons none out of that hand. A card the seat holds on the field by other means is left alone, Training Grounds' snapshots of Core cards are not Core, and the field reaches no other game. It amends [[R290]] and [[R1012003]] and edits [[§9.10]]; the remade tutorial (issue #2434) deals its opponent Core decks on it.
