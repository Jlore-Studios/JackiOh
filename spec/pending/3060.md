**[[R3060000]] One press is sent once per view (issue #3060, 2026-11-04)**: the board's moves stay live until the next view arrives and `net.ts` gives every send a fresh nonce, so a double click sent End turn twice, and a double tap sent a one-tap choice or the mulligan's Ready twice. Usually the second was refused and left "it is not your turn" on the board, but after a card that gave an extra turn ([[R846]]) the same player was still active, so the second End turn was accepted and ended the extra turn unseen. `Game.tsx`'s `dispatch` now drops an `endTurn`, `answer` or `mulligan` equal to the last one sent against the same view; a new view, or an error that gains text, releases it. Nothing the engine decides changed.
