**[[R2982001]] Claim all is one spend: one request claims every news entry the account has not claimed, in one transaction whose statements do not grow with the entries, each entry decided and paid as its own claim would be, and its one answer moves the list and the count (issue #2982, 2026-11-05)**: the owner found Claim all laggy. [[R2982000]] had sent it as one claim per entry, one after another, each its own request and transaction, so about fifty live entries cost fifty requests and about a thousand database statements. `POST /api/news/claim-all` is one spend in the account's turn, under its lock and an optional `Idempotency-Key` of its own route; it takes no list, and claims every entry live now and every dev post standing now that the account has not claimed, the test one claim puts to one entry. The claims go in one statement, whose key decides what is paid, and the rewards in one statement per ledger, each entry still its own ledger row with the reason `news`. The client sends one request and its answer replaces the list. Claims sent side by side, an id list from the client and all or nothing were rejected. It takes R2982001, the next number of issue #2982's block, amends [[R2982000]] and edits [[§14.13]].
