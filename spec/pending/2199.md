**[[R2199000]] to [[R2199007]] are Training Grounds' backrow part (issue #2199, part 6 of the tracker #2193, 2026-10-28)**: the scripts of the set's Traps, Field Spells and Field Trap, TG #13, #14, #19, #20, #24, #31 and #35, each proved in its own card file, and the one engine piece they lacked, proved in the engine's rules tests too. "After one of your Units attacks" is a new hook, `allyAttack`, run for the attacker's side once the combat's state check has closed, after the combatants' own hooks ([[R2199000]]). A Trap's printed Pierce and Trample are stated on its own hits ([[R2199001]]). Explosive Runes springs on an enemy Unit's declared attack on your hero and hits every enemy, so an attacker it kills fights no combat ([[R2199002]]); Explosive Trap hits a played Unit still on the field after it resolves ([[R2199003]]). Recruitment Posters never adds itself ([[R2199004]]). Goliath Situation fires on every Unit the opponent plays, at the cost paid ([[R2199005]]). Corpse Recycler answers both sides' non-Token Units ([[R2199006]]), and Level Up! counts each Unit's attacks apart from its arrival ([[R2199007]]). Where the designer said nothing, Hearthstone's semantics decide; each default is the brief's (`docs/training-grounds-set.md` TG6).
