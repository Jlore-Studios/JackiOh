# Pending paragraphs for issue #3548 (Stinkyscape+ E5)

- **[[R3548000]] Tipsy X (issue #3548, 2026-11-11)**: Tipsy X is a player counter like Jade or
  Credits, shown as a stein beside the player's hero. Playing a card with Tipsy X adds X to its
  controller's counter, which drops by 1 at the start of its owner's turn and is gone at 0.
- **[[R3548001]] the Tipsy blur (issue #3548, 2026-11-11)**: while the counter is up, a card the
  player newly plays, summons or draws may have one number, keyword or word of its name blurred on
  its owner's view alone, with a chance that grows with the counter to a cap of fifty; at 0 every
  blur clears, and a reload or a replay shows the same blur.
- **[[R3548002]] the Warhero Token's worth (issue #3548, 2026-11-11)**: every Warhero Token is worth
  one game-wide value in Credits — 0 once the first Token has been printed, growing by a random 0
  to 10 at the end of every turn after one has, doubled on the Radiant face — paid by playing,
  discarding or selling the Token.
- **[[R3548003]] Refresh (issue #3548, 2026-11-11)**: Refresh for Activates gives back this turn's
  uses of every Activate on the controller's permanents, except Activates whose effect is a Refresh,
  which stay spent; extra-turn cooldowns are untouched.
- **[[R3548004]] infinite mana (issue #3548, 2026-11-11)**: while a card giving infinite mana acts on
  its controller's field, that player's mana is not spent by paying costs, and the view shows
  infinity; it ends when the card leaves the field.
- **Replenish (issue #3548, 2026-11-11)**: the spec's word for the mana verb is now Replenish — older
  "Refresh N mana" texts keep that wording until S01 (#3551) rewords them, and no card prints both.
