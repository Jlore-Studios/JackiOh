**[[R2396000]] Three attack timings: "When" before, "Whenever" after even if gone, "After" only if still there (issue #2396, follows #2285, 2026-10-29)**: the owner split the timing [[R2285000]] had made one. "When this attacks" resolves before any damage of the attack; "Whenever this attacks" and "Whenever this destroys a Unit" resolve after the attack or the kill, once its combat's state check has run, whether or not the card is still on the field, as the snapshot it fought with and once, a Reborn body answering nothing more; "After" keeps [[R2285001]]'s survival. #32 Prem Panther now draws when it trades, Classic #13 Boots on the Ground moves to "When" and draws or Recruits before the damage, and Meditative #95.1 CN Golem shuffles its CN-Virus though it dies in the combat that killed ([[R1245]]); Badger, Honey Badger, Windfurious Prime and Nature Titan now print "When", Bot Loser and Ravager "After", and play as before. The engine runs "Whenever this attacks" as an `afterAttack` flagged `afterAttackEvenIfGone`, and a gone killer's kill trigger, flagged `evenIfGone`, from the snapshot the state check keeps for that kill. It amends [[R426]] and [[R212]], edits [[§4.2]] and [[§6.2]], and takes R2396000, the first number of issue #2396's block.
