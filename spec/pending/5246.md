**[[R5246000]] The AI counts the Nerfs it puts on the opponent's hand and the misfire it sets on their next Spell (issue #5246, 2026-11-28)**: asked by issue #5246. The Assault sweep had the Jailbreak enemy cast IA #137 Patch Tuesday Exploit on 3.9% and IA #130 Prompt Injection on 3.2% of the turns it could, against 18% for the median Assault card, because the AI priced the opponent's hand by its size alone and never read the misfire. The ruling edits [[§9.9]] (Evaluation) and takes R5246000, `AI_EVAL.enemy_hand_nerf` and `AI_EVAL.random_targets`.
