**[[R3393000]] Meditative+ and Training Grounds drawn as scenes: each entry a recipe of its own from its brief in its family's look, every Unit a figure-kit character, the kits grown by the briefs' parts, crowd figures and fades (issue #3393, 2026-11-08; amends [[R2202000]] and [[R3331000]])**: the owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) that every card's picture be more its own and richer, simpler for small cards and elaborate for Mythics, and this issue gave each Meditative+ and Training Grounds entry a brief. The plan gave each of the 74 entries of the two sets' own a recipe in its family's look, Meditative+'s ink-wash peaks under one red seal and Training Grounds' carved yard, built every Unit from the figure kit, grew the kits by the parts the briefs named, and added crowd figures and fades so a scene can draw more than one character, a ghost or a copy; the yard painter draws only a card with no recipe. Rejected: a painter per card, kit files per set, keeping the yard painter for the cards already over their floor, and drawing extra characters as scenery parts.
