**[[R2100000]] keeps a saved deck's earlier versions, and restores one (issue #2100, 2026-10-27)**: a player who tweaked a deck and regretted it had no way back. Each save now keeps a version of the deck's name and cards on the account, a burst of saves within `DECK_VERSION_DEBOUNCE_MS` (60 s) of the newest being one version and a save that changes neither keeping none, at most `DECK_VERSIONS_KEPT` (20) per deck. The deck builder's History panel lists them newest first with the cards in and out of each, and a restore saves one as the deck's, checked as a save is, and as a new version, so the one it replaced stays. Hearthstone keeps no deck history, so the numbers are the issue's planner's, each one constant. It takes R2100000, the first number of issue #2100's block, and edits [[§9.4]].
