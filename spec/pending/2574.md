**[[R2574000]] The view marks every hand card of the viewer's that the Trade rule passes as Tradable, the aura's included (issue #2574, 2026-10-31)**: an aura over a hand ("Cards in your hand have Tradable", S #164 The Syndicate, S #165 The Trade Union, [[R1864002]]) made cards Tradable for the reducer and the Trade bar, but the Tradable pip in hand read only `face.keywords`, so aura-lent Tradable cards lacked the pip and failed to dim when the turn's Trades were spent. The engine now marks every hand card of the viewer's that `trade::is_tradable` passes with `tradable: true` in `CardView` ([[§10.8]]). The client's Tradable pip reads `card.tradable`, sparing the client from working out field auras (CLAUDE.md rule 7); `keywords` still shows what the card is made of ([[R1864002]]). It edits [[§10.8]] and [[§10.10]].
