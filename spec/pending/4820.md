**[[R4820000]] An exile aura's exile of a Unit Immune to Spells inside a Spell's run is the aura's card's, and I21 allows it (issue #4820, 2026-11-24)**: the state check's exile aura ([[R1850002]]) exiles with no marker of its own, so an exile S #115 Scornboi made after S #150 Call to Chaos summoned it landed in the Spell's run, and the fuzz monitor's I21 ([[R4453000]]) named the Spell for S+ #67 Phase Wall's. The aura is its card's standing text, which Immune to Spells does not stop, so I21 now passes an `exiled` of an immune Unit while an exile aura acting on the field after the step reaches the side the Unit stood on (`exiled_by_an_aura`); with none standing, the exile is still the Spell's.
