**[[R5274000]] The server keeps the card statistics six hours, a dev account's Fetch live loads them now, and the gather lane files 250 games a batch, so a small database keeps its Disk IO and egress (issue #5274, 2026-11-28)**: Supabase warned that JackiOh-Staging was running through its Disk IO budget, with its egress climbing. Its `pg_stat_statements` put most of the IO in the card statistics' snapshot reloading `card_totals` every five minutes, and the rest in the gather lane's batches, 50 games each since #5176. The owner asked for the figures to be kept six hours, with a dev button that fetches them live. The ruling amends [[R978000]], [[R3067000]], [[R5174000]] and [[R5245000]], edits §9.11 (Public statistics and the publication gate) and takes R5274000.
