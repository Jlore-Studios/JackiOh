([[R640001]]). The owner chose to keep the bots as they are and disclose them as Hearthstone (patch 24.4), Fortnite and Apex Legends disclose theirs, in public text with no mark on the seat. The Terms' "Ranked play" section becomes "AI opponents": when ranked may pair a player with one and why, at any rank and in every queue mode; that it has a username, portrait, cosmetics, rank and leaderboard place as a player does, never in Jlorious; that it plays at a person's pace and emotes, concedes, answers draws, times out and rematches; that nothing in the game marks it; that it sees only what its seat could see and its games are rated like any; and that a friend's challenge is always that friend. `TERMS_VERSION` moves, so every account agrees again. It amends [[R640001]] and [[R1452]], and edits [[§9.5]] and [[§9.12]].
