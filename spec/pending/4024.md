**[[R4024000]] The AI prices each side's copy of a card's Death and turn hooks on that side's board (issue #4024, 2026-11-16; amends [[R2424000]], [[R2477000]], [[R2565000]], [[R2687000]])**: the issue found that the Death and turn-hook prices a decision's root probes ([[§9.9]]) were keyed by the card alone, so one side's copy read the price the probes set for the other's: the enemy's Core #13 Jlockeed Shredder-10, facing only the AI's Shredder, read the 11.0 the AI's copy earned against four units where its own firing is worth 5.0, and a Training Grounds #30 Floodgates in the AI's hand beside two Vanillas read the 0 of the enemy's copy alone on its board where its Death costs the AI 21.6. Both prices now hold the controller, as [[R2564000]]'s activation prices do, and a card reads only its own controller's entry, so each card is probed once for each controller on that side's board. A hand card is the seat's and shares the price of the seat's copy on the board, and a copy that changes sides reads no price until the next root probes it there. The probes' order, the probe world ([[R185]]) and both 24-node caps ([[R186]]) are unchanged, so a card both sides hold spends two probes from the same budget.
