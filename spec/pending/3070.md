**[[R3070000]] Every card's adjusted win rate and tier, against the average deck where it has no never-drawn games (issue #3070, 2026-11-04)**: the owner balances every card by its tier ([[R3022000]]), yet the 15 Quickdraw cards, which start in the opening hand ([[R640]]) and so are never left undrawn, and the Dormant Starship, which no deck holds ([[R1857001]]), had no adjusted rate and no tier. The field now holds a baseline, the average card's in-deck win rate over the report. A card with fewer never-drawn games than `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` is read against it: 50% plus τ²/(τ² + SE²) times its seen win rate less the baseline, SE² the seen side's alone, with [[R2895000]]'s range and [[R3022000]]'s tier. The Dormant Starship's row reads its Created games, the seats that made one ([[R2089000]]), against the same baseline. `GET /api/stats/cards` answers `tiers.baseline`, and the tiers note says what these cards are read against. An even game as the baseline, a test by the Quickdraw tag, and a new catalog class for the starship were rejected. It takes R3070000, the first number of issue #3070's block, and edits [[§9.11]].
