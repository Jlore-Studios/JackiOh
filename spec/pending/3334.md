**Open questions, rejected alternatives.** These are the builder's defaults, for a person to overrule:

- The launch's 3-2-1 countdown is gone. Keeping it inside the cinematic was rejected as clutter over the camera's pass.
- A War Doctrine is announced with its English card name, as the cards print it. A new translated string for each was rejected: the name is the card's own text, which the client shows as the catalog gives it.
- The odd weapons fire as their nearest kind: AP and Vamp Rounds (S #100, #122) as repeaters, the RR-5000's rail and the Yamato Cannon (S #107, #128) as cannons, the Disintegrex and Trila 3 (S #105, #108) as beams; the Missile Pod (S #129) lobs arrows. Giving each its own motion was rejected as more than the five kinds the issue names.
- "Prow first" is a turn of `FX_STARSHIP_RAM_TURN_DEG` (30 degrees) toward the target, with sparks at the prow. A full turn to face the target was rejected as unreadable on a card.
- A cannon's recoil is drawn as an overlay of its module kicking back, since the picture is one image and cannot move a part of itself.
- The shipyard idle's numbers live in `cards/constants.ts`, since a card face must not import the effects layer (`fx/`). The idle sits under the Dormant seal's veil, so it is greyed with the rest of the face; lifting it over the veil was rejected, as the seal must read as one sleeping card.
- The picture is drawn at least as wide and as tall as the screen, and the camera looks only where the zoomed picture still fills it (`cameraPoint`), so a small ship's pan from stern to bow is shorter than its hull. Letting the camera reach the stern and bow themselves was rejected: the board showed beside the picture, which the issue asks to fill the screen.
- The cinematic plays only at Big moments "full": "short" shortens the hold, and a camera pass squeezed into it was rejected for the title card the issue names.
- A War Doctrine is announced however it is cast, Dropshipping's (C+ #23) included, over its caster's launched Starship, else the board's middle. Announcing only the two Legendaries' casts was rejected: the play carries no link to the card that cast it.
- The director sends a fuse run's docks as its entry starts, each delayed to its landing, and the sound engine's retrigger guard now reads a dock (`SCHEDULED_SFX`) at the time it sounds: otherwise only a run's first dock was heard. Every other sound's guard is unchanged (B6), and timers in the director were rejected: it schedules every other delayed sound the same way.
- The wreck reads the ship as the board shows it before the death, Dormant or launched; a Starship that dies inside a wipe keeps the board clear's set piece ([[R2800000]]).
- Pictures in the effects layer's DOM cues are `<img>` elements: B27 forbids a `url(` in `fx/*.ts`.
