**[[R4552000]] A record carries one row per turn of each seat, read through a calibrated win probability (issue #4552, 2026-11-23)**: each seat of a game record written since carries `swingTurns`, one row per turn it took: the turn counter, the AI's evaluation of the seat at its turn's start less its opponent's, in thousandths, read off the true, whole state, and the cards acting on each side's field (a face-down Trap left out). The AI's fold (`jackioh_ai::swing_turns`) writes them, replaying the log from the engine's own start and steps (`recorded_start`, `read_steps`), under the record's catalog pin on the server and in the development runs. The record keeps the raw evaluation, so a refit rewrites no record; older records have no rows and count toward nothing new. The win probability is `σ(β₀ + β₁·eval + β₂·turn + β₃·first)`, its weights named constants fit by `cargo jackioh stats calibrate` over development records against the AI generation `WIN_PROBABILITY_AI_GENERATION` names; a test holds them to the fit of a fixture of those rows and checks their reliability and Brier score over it.

**[[R4552001]] A card's Decisiveness, shown as ±X% on the Cards tab; its Swing and Board swing stay internal (issue #4552, 2026-11-23)**: a turn's change is the seat's win probability at its next turn's start less at this one's (the last turn's at the result), tallied in whole thousandths per card under its key: the turns it was played on, acted on its field and acted on the other. An ordinary turn's gain `α` is read per own turn of each seat. A card's play term and Board swing are the means of what `α` leaves of those turns, shrunk as [[R2777000]] shrinks a lift, the spread read off the field as [[R3022000]] reads it (a diagonal ridge, not the full regularized plus-minus, whose matrix the totals cannot hold); Swing adds, per play, the Board swing of the turns it and the cards it made then act. Decisiveness is the root mean square of its play turns' unexplained change plus its play term, shrunk toward the typical card's by its delta-method sampling noise (games clustering the turns). Only Decisiveness shows: a column after Adjusted and before Win rate, `±14.2%`, sortable, "not enough games" below [[R654]]'s floor, with a tooltip naming the typical card's value; the rest only in `stats report --swing` and `stats-cards --swing`.

**[[R4552002]] A Created card an effect put onto the field credits its arrival to its maker (issue #4552, 2026-11-23)**: the fold names each Created card's maker, the card whose effect summoned it or whose play or trigger was resolving as it appeared, unless a hand held it first. One a hand held and played has its own play term; one an effect put onto the field or cast has none, its arrival in its maker's play turn, and reads Board swing and Decisiveness off the turns it acts; its maker's Swing adds those turns' Board swing. Created cards are read against their own field, the cards a rule makes against the deck cards' ([[R4082000]]).
