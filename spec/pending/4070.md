**[[R4070000]] The learned evaluation's training rows carry the priced hand eval the search corrects (issue #4070, 2026-11-16; amends [[R1006004]], [[R1006006]])**: the issue found that `cargo jackioh positions` scored every row with `evaluate` and simulated a reply row's reply with no price, while the search corrects `evaluate_priced`, the hand eval priced with the Death, turn-hook, Activate, attack-hook and awakening prices of the decision's root ([[R2424000]], [[R2477000]], [[R2564000]], [[R2688000]], [[R3064000]]), after a reply simulated with them ([[R1012001]]). A network fitted on those rows learned the priced values as residuals on a card's features, Core #37 Gravedigger's return to hand at the start of its owner's turn among them, and the search then counted them twice. A turn or decision row is now priced as a decision at its own state would price it (`decision_prices_at`, on the decision's probe world, [[R185]]), and a reply row by the decision that ended the turn, whose prices also play the reply. A row whose root prices nothing keeps its score. The features, `FEATURES_VERSION`, the fit and the shipped network are unchanged, so the gain arrives with the learn lane's next refit.
