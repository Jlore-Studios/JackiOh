**[[R2422000]] settles a practice claim once, after a restart or a failed pay (issue #2422, 2026-10-31)**: a practice claim burned its seed and then ran its check and pay in a task of its own, so its pay was lost three ways, each leaving the seed burned and a later claim answering `already_claimed`: a shutdown's drain closed the store under a settle whose caller had gone, a crash took the settle, or all three tries at the pay failed ([[R1688000]]). The claim is now kept with its seed until it settles, the pay marks the seed paid in its own transaction and only where it has no mark, a refusal marks it refused, the drain waits for every settle running, and the retention loop checks again and settles any claim still unsettled `PRACTICE_SETTLE_GRACE_SECONDS` after it was made, paying it once with no board (migration 0071). It takes R2422000, the first number of issue #2422's block, amends [[R1688000]] and [[R1303001]], and edits [[§14.8]].
