**[[R1882000]] to [[R1882009]] are the two Calls to Chaos, Stinkyscape's S25 (issue #1882, part of #1856, 2026-10-27)**: Wildar, Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition), its Stinkyscape Golem and Call to Chaos (Scape Edition), built on `main` behind [[R1420]]'s gate. Wildar's two Calls are independent picks of the editions that ship, the opponent's at (0) and hidden ([[R1882000]]). The two editions are tables of Core #95's subsystem, as C+ #73's and M #95's are. The Stinky Edition's Quests and Wincons are the Field Spells with either tag from every set ([[R1882001]]); its Acclaimed Unit refreshes its own cost once it is in the hand ([[R1882002]]); its "all enemies" are the enemy Units, switched to Attack Position and then Frozen ([[R1882003]]); and its rest-of-game Acclaimed Death is a death watcher on its caster, answering either player's Acclaimed deaths for the dead card's controller, at most three a player ([[R1882004]]). The Scape Edition's board swap draws from all ten Anomaly boards ([[R1882005]]); its two Systems are different ones cast in the order drawn ([[R1882006]]); "they have stack" tops each unit zone with a Drone Interceptor ([[R1882007]]); its launch fuses four pieces into its caster's Dormant Starship, a new one if none, and launches it for nothing ([[R1882008]]); and its Marketplace lot is its caster's alone, a Call to Chaos at a fixed 20 Credits ([[R1882009]]). They take R1882000 to R1882009, the first numbers of issue #1882's block, and edit [[§8.10]].
