**[[R3342000]] Every face-up Legendary and Mythic permanent marks its tile with an aura in its picture's colors and set's pattern that answers touch, damage and targets (issue #3342, 2026-11-07)**: standing auras existed only for cards whose table entry gave them an `aura` ([[R2787000]]), mostly Field Spells and Field Traps drawn by `AuraLayer.tsx` over the board. A Legendary or Mythic Unit had no tile feedback of its own once its entrance completed. Every face-up Legendary and Mythic permanent on the board now marks its tile with a DOM aura painting beneath its card: a soft edge glow and slow motes for Legendary, and a richer set pattern (circuitry, runes, ink, starfield, roots) for Mythic, tinted by its picture's painter palette with an id-hashed hue spread so distinct Legendaries differ in look. A card's table aura replaces the default. The aura brightens when selected as a target, flickers on damage, dims while Dormant, Frozen or Silenced, and fades out with its card's destroyed or exiled row. Touching or hovering sends a ripple, plays a bell in its hue (`auraTouch`) and buzzes on phones (`touch` haptic, [[R669]]). Motes stay within `TILE_PARTICLE_SHARE` of the particle cap and the phone frame budget ([[R2803000]]), freeze under reduced motion ([[R438]]), and disable at intensity `off`. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no engine, wire or catalog change. Rejected: a canvas overlay (which paints above cards), a view field, and reading the opponent's aim arrow.
