**[[R2990000]] A watcher throws emotes from the sidelines, relayed live to both seats and the other watchers and never to its sender (issue #2990, 2026-11-04)**: friends watching a live game over `/ws/watch` ([[§9.5]], [[R2881000]]) could only look; they now react with the very `emote` frame a seat sends. It stays out of `ActionBody`, so no reducer, log, hash, record, telemetry, card that hears emotes ([[R1127]]) or bot persona ([[R645]]) sees it; an id outside the pool is `malformed`. The actor relays it at once as `spectatorEmote` with the watcher's `name#tag` to each person's socket and every other watcher, never the sender. A watcher sees only the friend's seat, `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` late, so what an emote can carry to the opponent beyond that is accepted as the feature's cost. It takes R2990000, the first number of issue #2990's block, and amends [[R1103000]], [[R2609000]] and [[R2881000]].
**[[R2990001]] A watcher's emote hand is emoji only, dealt from the match seed and its own account, and sent to it alone (issue #2990, 2026-11-04)**: a watcher holds `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_HAND_SIZE` (5) distinct emoji and no voice line, which would sound like a player speaking, dealt on the stream `{seed}:spectator-emotes:{profile id}` as [[R1341]] deals a seat's, and sent to it alone as `spectatorHand` after the loadouts. An emote it does not hold is dropped silently, before the limit, as [[R1342]] drops a seat's.
**[[R2990002]] A watcher's emotes have a limit of their own, stricter than a seat's, which the server enforces and the menu greys out from (issue #2990, 2026-11-04)**: [[R643]]'s one gate now takes the limit it applies; a watcher's is `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_COOLDOWN_MS` (3 s), `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_WINDOW_MAX` (3) and `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_WINDOW_MS` (30 s), in the server's config and generated for the web, so the menu and the actor agree. A drop is silent and no refused action, and the actor keeps each watching account's sends across its sockets.
