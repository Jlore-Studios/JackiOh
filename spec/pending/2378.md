**[[R2378000]] runs one spend at a time per account (issue #2378, 2026-10-29)**: a player spam-clicking the pack panel saw the server fail, since every spend took a database connection and then waited on its account's economy lock ([[R1301003]]) and every press was a new key ([[R2124000]]), so a burst held the pool's connections on one lock until every other checkout timed out. Each of the six economy spends now claims its account's turn before it reads the store and holds it until it answers: `ECONOMY_SPENDS_RUNNING_MAX` run at once, the next ones wait their turn in the server holding no connection and then run, and one sent while `ECONOMY_SPENDS_WAITING_MAX` already wait is refused at once with 429, stating `SPEND_BUSY_RETRY_AFTER_MS` and spending nothing. The owner asked for a wait over an error, so a second tab or a racing retry waits instead of being refused, and the client, keeping a 429's key, holds Buy, Open, Open all, the sealed pack, Reveal all and Next pack while one of its spends is out, Buy and Open turning a loading wheel. Seeding each pack at purchase was rejected: the openings already commit one at a time from fresh seeds, so it would change no result for a migration and a backfill. It takes R2378000, the first of issue #2378's block, edits [[§14.11]] and amends [[R1301003]] and [[R2124000]].
