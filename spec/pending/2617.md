**[[R2617000]] makes a touch held on a card its hover, on either turn and on every screen (issue #2617, follows #165, 2026-10-31)**: on a touch screen, a finger held on a card, a face-down card or a pile opens its hover preview while it stays down, on either turn and on every screen. The lift closes the preview and sends no click, and a drift past the slop closes it and still drags. #165 had kept the modal inspect sheet on the viewer's own turn and off the board. The user asked for the hold to be the hover everywhere. With Hover previews off, a hold opens the sheet as before, and a hold with its own use (the hero portrait's view, the card detail of the deck builder's pool and the Almanac) keeps it. Presentation only. It takes R2617000, the first number of issue #2617's block, and edits [[§10.10]].
