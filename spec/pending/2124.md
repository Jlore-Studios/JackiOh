**[[R2124000]] runs a retried spend once (issue #2124, part of #2036, 2026-10-28)**: a spend the server finished after the client gave up on it (`API_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`) ran again when the player pressed again. Every spend now takes an optional `Idempotency-Key`, a UUID the client mints for a press and sends again on that press's retries; under the account's economy lock the same key with the same route and body replays the answer it committed, another route or body is 422 `idempotency_key_reused`, and an unknown key's answer is written with its grant, kept `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_TTL_HOURS` and swept. It takes R2124000, the first of issue #2124's block, and edits [[§14.11]].
