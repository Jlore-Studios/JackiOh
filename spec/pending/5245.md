**[[R5245000]] The card statistics are served from what is held while they refresh, and a kept snapshot lasts from when its load finished (issue #5245, 2026-11-28)**: `/stats` did not load. Staging's deploy check timed out at 75 s on the card statistics twice, because a read past the cache's time waited for a whole reload, and a load longer than the time finished already expired and started again (#5207). The ruling amends [[R978000]], [[R1583000]], [[R3067000]] and [[R5186000]], edits §9.11 (Public statistics and the publication gate) and takes R5245000.
