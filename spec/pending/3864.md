**[[R3864000]] A choice of one board zone is answered on the board alone (issue #3864, 2026-11-13)**: amends [[R514]]. A play's placement zone, a `cell` prompt and a declared zone target, each of one zone, opened a list of zone buttons beside the lit board, which on a phone covered the field. They now draw no list: the zones glow, a tap, Enter or Space on one answers, and the modal is the hint and a play's Cancel, with any option that names no cell ("Done") still a button; a `cell` prompt opens with its first lit zone focused, and a replay still shows the title. A `max` above 1 or `min` 0 zone choice, a target of cards or heroes, Discover-style cell choices ([[R831003]]) and lane picks are unchanged. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Rejected: keeping the list for small screens only, and a menu fallback for the keyboard.
