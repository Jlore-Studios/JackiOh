**[[R2176000]] and [[R2176001]] are the M12 audit's Stinkyscape fixes (issue #2176, part of #1856, 2026-10-28)**: the follow-ups of REVIEW Parts A and B on the held set ([[R1420]]). S #22 Gatinsir summons the Phalanxes by name, so each runs its Cry, as [[R411]] reads a summon that names its card; the set's other summons by name that reach a card with a Cry (S #6's Radiant S #7, the FO board's S #82 and S #83) already fire it ([[R2176000]]). S #150's death watch ([[R1882004]]) gets a stated terminator: it casts at most `CHAOS_STINKY_DEATH_WATCH_CASTS_PER_TURN` (5) Calls to Chaos for a player in a turn, each a chain with [[R28]]'s cap of its own ([[R2176001]]). The same issue corrected rulings it found reading otherwise than the engine plays: Shadow Word Death and Pain check their limit with the play and not again as they resolve, as No-No Words does ([[R1867005]], [[R1868000]]), and Jade Launcher's roll takes its controller's Luck ([[R1862005]], [[R987]]). They take R2176000 and R2176001, the first numbers of issue #2176's block, and edit [[§6.3]], [[§7]], [[§8.10]] and [[§10.1]].
