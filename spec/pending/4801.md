**[[R4801000]] A host's wrap-up is first polled once the beam has tried every move at its root (issue #4801, 2026-11-25; amends [[R3498000]])**: an audit of the AI crate found that the floor [[R3498000]] held the wrap-up off the beam with counted nodes, not moves, so a wrap-up that fired before the beam refused a first root move whose surcharge passed it (a play of #97 Zephrys takes 100) and the decision fell back to End turn, and a root that cost more than the floor never tried its own End turn. The beam now tells its slice when it has tried every move at its root (`NodeCounter::end_root`) and only then is the wrap-up polled; the hard stop is unchanged, and with no clock or a wrap-up that never fires a decision is node for node what it was. It edits [[§9.9]]'s Budgets and takes R4801000 from issue #4801's block.
