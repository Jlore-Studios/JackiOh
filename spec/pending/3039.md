**[[R3039000]] and [[R3039001]] make an awakening for good and keep a Flashback spent (issue #3039, part 2 of #3000, 2026-11-04)**: the owner's pass over the Stinkyscape set ends the "once" of a Dormant card. An awakened card is never Dormant again: every awakening marks it (`CardInstance.awakened`, kept in every zone and through [[R78]]'s and [[R766]]'s resets), so played again from its graveyard, Reborn, returned to a hand and replayed or summoned again it enters awake, while a card that left the field still Dormant is Dormant again; an awakened Dormant Starship that returns stands awake with a fresh, empty memory, unlaunched, and the next piece to die fuses into a Dormant Starship, a new one if there is none, rather than launching it again with nothing fused ([[R3039000]], amending [[R1839000]]). The Flashback half was undone on the owner's word (2026-11-05), once a (0) Flashback card made a free board wipe repeatable in one turn: a play by the card's own Flashback still spends it, so a card is flashed back once, and [[R783013]] stands ([[R3039001]]). It takes R3039000 and R3039001, the first numbers of issue #3039's block, and edits [[§6.1]] Dormant and Flashback and [[§6.3]] Play.
