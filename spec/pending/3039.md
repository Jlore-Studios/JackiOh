**[[R3039000]] and [[R3039001]] make an awakening and a Flashback for good (issue #3039, part 2 of #3000, 2026-11-04)**: the owner's pass over the Stinkyscape set ends the two "once"s. An awakened card is never Dormant again: every awakening marks it (`CardInstance.awakened`, kept in every zone and through [[R78]]'s and [[R766]]'s resets), so played again from its graveyard, Reborn, returned to a hand and replayed or summoned again it enters awake, while a card that left the field still Dormant is Dormant again; an awakened Dormant Starship that returns stands awake with a fresh, empty memory, unlaunched, and the next piece to die fuses into a Dormant Starship, a new one if there is none, rather than launching it again with nothing fused ([[R3039000]], amending [[R1839000]]). A play by the card's own Flashback spends nothing, so the card keeps the keyword, printed or lent by Sanctum's aura, and is offered from its graveyard again each time it lies there; `flashbackSpent` is retired, and [[R783013]] is superseded ([[R3039001]], amending [[R1867007]] and [[R1869003]]). It takes R3039000 and R3039001, the first numbers of issue #3039's block, and edits [[§6.1]] Dormant and Flashback and [[§6.3]] Play.
