**[[R2657000]] Can't Defend is a keyword (issue #2657, 2026-11-01)**: the owner's second Stinkyscape balance pass asked for "Can't Defend", the short successor to "Can't be in Defense Position". It became a keyword the cards print, which the combat validator and the state check read, and which a Buff may take off. The static flag it replaced stays for the scripts kept for older versions.

**[[R2657001]] A Field Trap reveals once (issue #2657, 2026-11-01)**: the request had a Field Trap's later firings count as normal triggers, without the long reveal animation. `trapFired` marks them `again`, so the client and "when one of your Traps reveals" tell a reveal from a later firing.

**[[R2657002]] Float works from the hand alone (issue #2657, 2026-11-01)**: the request had "Float reduces cost" trigger only while the card is in its owner's hand. The six cards with a Float trigger lose their deck trigger.

**[[R2657003]] A Trade never draws its own card back while the deck holds another (issue #2657, 2026-11-01)**: the request had a traded card redraw itself only when it is the only option. It goes in under the top card.

**[[R2657004]] Summoning sickness lasts its whole turn (issue #2657, 2026-11-01)**: the request named a Chaos Golem whose refreshed attack could hit the hero on the turn it was summoned. Every "may attack again" now keeps the sickness, as R2521002 did for Tmack Trav alone.

**[[R2657005]] The Buff's and the Nerf's new keywords (issue #2657, 2026-11-01)**: the request named "Reminisce" (a card; the owner chose Flashback) for Buffs, Crater in a hand for Nerfs, and Can't attack and Can't Defend both ways. The owner settled the last: a Buff removes either, and a Nerf adds neither.

**[[R2657006]] KY's Constant's further numbers (issue #2657, 2026-11-01)**: the request asked KY's Constant for more valid targets. The owner chose more numbers on every card: a Temporary card's count and its Plague Counters, and the amounts the Units and Traps printed, now declared.
