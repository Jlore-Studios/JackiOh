**[[R2701000]] a dungeon run's deck holds at most the library cap (issue #2701, 2026-11-02)**: a run deck grows after every fight and from shops and events, and each fight's setup gives the human's handicap the deck's length as its deck size, which the engine refuses past `LIBRARY_CAP` ([[R184]]), so a long run could reach a fight it cannot start. Following Hearthstone, whose full deck gains no more cards ([[R80]]), a run deck holds at most `RUN_DECK_MAX`, the library cap: a pack's keep count is also at most the room left, so a full deck keeps none; a shop refuses to sell a card to a full deck; and an event adds no card to one, its other changes paid. Dealing a fight only the deck's first `LIBRARY_CAP` cards, and refusing the pack outright, were rejected: the first drops cards the player chose without saying so, and the second leaves a full deck no way past a fight's rewards. It takes R2701000, the first number of issue #2701's block, and edits [[§15.7]] and [[§15.8]].
