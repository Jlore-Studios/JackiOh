**[[R4334000]] An ingredient fused on its Radiant face reads and shows its numbers on that face (issue #4334, 2026-11-20)**: an ingredient that went into a fusion on its Radiant face ([[R469]]) now reads its declared numbers ([[R386]]) on that face in every text it runs in its place (Activate, trigger, Cry, Death, end or start of turn, When launched), whatever the kept card's face, and so does an ingredient fused inside it. A fused card's own numbers, the first of each key ([[R102]]), are each read on the face its ingredient went in on, so `CardView.params` and a read that names no place (an aura's) show and use the number its text uses. A Radiant Starship Piece's copy fuses Radiant ([[R1852000]]): a Radiant Beam v3 in a base Starship deals and shows 8, a base one 4. Eighteen pieces read the base number in a Starship before; [[R1859001]]'s per-card `piece_number`, which five pieces called, is retired. The kept card's one tuning record and the Nerf and Buff draws, read off the kept card's face, are unchanged. Rejected: `piece_number` in the other eighteen pieces, an aura's read on the kept card's face, and the tune draws on each ingredient's face. It takes R4334000 and edits [[§5.2]], [[§6.1]], [[§6.3]], [[§8.12]] and [[R1859001]].
