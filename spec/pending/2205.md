**[[R2205000]] to [[R2205003]] are the tutorial remade from Training Grounds (issue #2205, part 12 of #2193, 2026-10-29)**: the owner asked for a tutorial dealt from the Training Grounds set that cannot be lost and lets a player discover the mechanics themselves, with the set's Legendary and Mythic cards only in the last lesson, and chose five lessons. Each lesson's coach is built around moments. A moment poses a question and is done once the human's view shows the mechanic found; the coach names the rule in a discovery bubble, at most two between the player's moves, and its hints escalate only while the player stalls or asks. A moment waiting for its board lets a later one show first, and a moment never holds the AI ([[R2205000]]). Every lesson is proved won under every way to play it, a reckless player who attacks into everything and guards nothing included ([[R2205001]]). The mechanics found fill a Field Guide kept on the device, where a lesson won counts its mechanics met ([[R2205002]]). The five lessons keep the Core tutorial's four ids and add `finale`; both decks are Training Grounds cards, lessons 1 to 4 hold no Legendary or Mythic card and set the human's pool hold, and lesson 5's human deck holds the set's four ([[R2205003]]). They amend [[R291]], [[R292]], [[R293]] and [[R314]], rewrite [[§9.10]], and take R2205000 to R2205003 from issue #2205's block.
