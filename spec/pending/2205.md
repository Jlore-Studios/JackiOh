**[[R2205000]], [[R2205001]] and [[R2205002]] are the discovery coach of the remade tutorial (issue #2205, part 12 of #2193, 2026-10-28)**: the owner asked that the tutorial let a player discover the mechanics themselves. A lesson's coach is built around moments: it poses a question, recognises from the human's view alone when the player has found the mechanic, and names the rule in a discovery bubble, with hints that escalate only while the player stalls or asks ([[R2205000]]). Every lesson is proved won under every way to play it, a reckless player who attacks into everything and guards nothing included ([[R2205001]]). The mechanics found fill a Field Guide kept on the device, where a lesson won counts its mechanics met ([[R2205002]]). They follow [[R292]], [[R293]], [[R294]] and [[R314]] and take R2205000 to R2205002 from issue #2205's block.
