**[[R2436000]] lets a Buff give a Field Spell Immutable (issue #2436, 2026-10-30)**: the owner asked that every Field Spell may gain Immutable from a Buff. A Field Spell's keyword row, Indestructible alone since issue #1748 ([[R1748001]]), now offers Immutable after it, so a Field Spell that deals damage draws among four keywords. A Field Spell made Immutable takes no later Buff or Nerf, and while it is face-up on the field no Spell, Field Spell or Field Trap targets it ([[R23]], [[R781001]]); Units keep [[R21]]'s pool, which leaves Immutable out. It takes R2436000, the first number of issue #2436's block, edits [[§6.1]] and [[§6.3]] and amends [[R386]] and [[R1748001]].
