**[[R2783000]] A card's hook is marked as it resolves, and only a seat that could read the card sees the marker (issue #2783, 2026-11-02)**: issue #2782's keyword and card animations had nothing to anchor a Death or a turn hook on. The engine now emits `hookResolving` (`sourceId`, `defId`, `controller`, `hook`) once, just before the effects of each first run of a Cry, a Death, a start-of-turn or end-of-turn hook, a Whenever trigger whose condition holds and an Activate; a hook that fizzles is marked too, a continuation after a prompt is not, nor a card set by its Cloaked whose text is inert ([[R1040]]), a Trap keeps its `trapFired` alone, and a Cry run with no card names its definition with `sourceId` `""`. Nothing answers it: it is withheld from the trigger loop as [[R1361]]'s report is, takes no number in [[R68]]'s order and moves no hash. A seat that could not read the card where it stood as the hook began never sees it, unless that trap's `trapFired` follows in the same action ([[R2652000]]'s rule); otherwise it names its card as the card itself is named ([[R97]]). It takes no room in [[R168]]'s floor, and the golden texts leave it out. It rejects the issue's suggestion of redacting the card as [[R154]] does a firing Trap, since even a card back would tell the other seat that a hidden card's hook answered. It takes R2783000, the first number of issue #2783's block, and edits [[§10.3]] and [[§10.8]].

**[[R2783001]] `via`: the keyword behind a hit, a heal, a death or a summon, and First Strike's unanswered strike (issue #2783, 2026-11-02)**: the client guessed Poisonous from the view and could not tell a Cleave hit, a Trample excess, a Lifesteal heal or a Reborn return from any other. `damage`, `damageAbsorbed`, `healed`, `destroyed` and `summoned` now carry an optional `via`, set by the engine where the keyword applies, never by a card, and on the wire only when set: Cleave on a neighbour hit, Trample on an excess, First Strike on the strike [[§4.3]] moves ahead when exactly one side has it, else Pierce on a hit that skipped Armor its target had; Lifesteal on step 8's heal; Poisonous on the death of a unit a Poisonous hit marked, else First Strike on the death of a unit that fell to that strike and so struck nothing back; Reborn on §4.5 step 4's return. One keyword per event, the hit's route first; the trigger loop holds every event without it and a view hides it with its card. It rejects the numbered `Keyword`, a list per event and a `combatStep` field, from which the client would still have to infer the missing strike back. It takes R2783001, the next number of issue #2783's block, and edits [[§10.3]].
