**[[R3205000]] A hidden tab's polls pause and read once when the tab shows again, except the ones that hold a place the server expires (issue #3205, 2026-11-05)**: tracker #3201 asks for less compute and faster traffic, and a hidden tab was polling on its timers whether or not anyone could see it. One helper, `pollWhileVisible` beside `pageWake`, runs a poll whose timer sends nothing while `document.visibilityState` is `hidden`; when the tab shows again, or the network comes back, it reads once at once and the pace starts over. The social poll (`SOCIAL_POLL_MS`), the friends list (`FRIENDS_POLL_MS`), an open chat (`CHAT_POLL_MS`), the live-game banner, the rematch status, the series banner and co-op Play again (`SERIES_POLL_SECONDS`) use it. The queue wait on `/play` and the queue follow keep running hidden, since their reads keep the ticket alive ([[R897000]]), and so do the series pick ([[R333]]), the co-op host's room wait, the presence heartbeat and the social poll while the tab has a challenge of its own open (a room that expires, and the follow that takes the challenger to the board rides that poll). Pausing the queue reads, pausing the pick and the room wait, and pausing the social poll with a challenge open were rejected. It takes R3205000, the first number of issue #3205's block, and edits [[§9.4]].
