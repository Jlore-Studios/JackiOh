**[[R4592000]] Play history records each face-up play in order, never a set trap, and records nothing while Infinite Assault is held (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: while the Infinite Assault set is held ([[R4577000]]), play history records nothing unless a card of the set is open in the match. When recorded, every face-up play is appended to the game log's play history in play order. Setting a Trap or Field Trap is face-down and never recorded in play history. `add_play_copies` takes the newest N face-up plays in chronological order (or plays from last turn), adding fresh copies to hand.

**[[R4592001]] Fingerprinter adds a copy of a random Spell from the opponent's hand, or a chosen card on Radiant (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: Fingerprinter's Cry on its base face picks a random Spell in the opponent's hand and adds a copy of it to its controller's hand; if the opponent holds no Spell, nothing is added. On Radiant, it opens a prompt of the opponent's hand shown to the chooser alone ([[§10.8]], [[R81]]), and adds a copy of the chosen card to the controller's hand.

**[[R4592002]] Deck origin tracks where a card started, surviving swaps and steals, but never riding a copy (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: a card that begins the game in a player's library has its origin noted (`started_in`). Stealing a card from a deck or swapping libraries transfers ownership while preserving that the card started in the other player's deck. A copy made of a card in a deck, hand or graveyard is a fresh card created in hand or deck and never counts as having started in the opponent's deck ([[R57]]).

**[[R4592003]] Ground Truth reveals the opponent's hand to its controller, visible in the view and unhidden from AI (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: while Ground Truth stands active on the field, its controller reads the opponent's hand as revealed. The view presents the opponent's hand cards openly with no `hand_marked` redaction, the testkit invariant `hidden_from` does not flag them as private, and the AI's observation does not redact them from its search ([[§10.8]], [[R865]]).

**[[R4592004]] Pattern Recognition looks at top three of opponent's deck, copying one and exiling another (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: Pattern Recognition reveals the top three cards of the opponent's library. On base, the player chooses one to add as a copy to their hand, then chooses another among the remaining cards to exile; the second pick excludes the first pick (`except`). On Radiant, the player chooses two to copy, and if three cards were offered, the remaining card is exiled automatically without a second prompt.

**[[R4592005]] Discovering or copying from the opponent's deck leaves the original in place and applies cost riders (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: discovering or copying cards from the opponent's deck leaves the original card in the opponent's library. Fresh copies added to hand carry cost modifications such as discount riders on the card in hand ([[R65]]).

**[[R4592006]] Mimic Unit copies printed and granted keywords from enemy units, ignoring Brittle and spent shields (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: Mimic Unit's Cry gains the keywords of enemy units on the field: base gains keywords from one random enemy unit, Radiant from every enemy unit in lane order. Granted and printed keywords are copied, while temporary keywords, Brittle, spent Divine Shield and spent Reborn are not gained.

**[[R4592007]] Style Transfer and Copycat Drone transform units into copies keeping R57 riders, refused by Immutable (issue #4592, 2026-11-24)**: transforming a friendly unit into a copy of an enemy unit (Style Transfer) or copying the last played unit (Copycat Drone) applies standard transform rules ([[§6.3]], [[R23]], [[R57]]): stats, keywords, and face are replaced, R57 riders (such as Radiant or Chinese) ride the transformation, and an Immutable unit refuses the transform.
