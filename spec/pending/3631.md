**[[R3631000]] A micro past `z` takes two letters, `aa`, `ab`, …, and keeps its line (issue #3631, 2026-11-10; supersedes [[R3471000]], amends [[R1366000]])**: [[R3471000]] had a micro after `vA.B.Cz` open the next line, `vA.B.(C+1)`, wearing a patch's name. The owner asked for the letters to go on instead, as a spreadsheet's columns do: after `z` come `aa` … `az`, `ba` … `zz`, then `aaa`, so a line never runs out and only a patch moves `C`. A taken name's revision ([[R646]]) counts the same way, and every reader of a version (`patches ship`, the scanner a replay's renames go through and the bot's title checks) takes a run of letters. v0.5.13 keeps its name and micro tier, since a shipped version never reopens. Rejected: a retcon renaming the v0.5.13 line, which bumps the versions scheme and renames every row that names it, for a name no reader keys off.
