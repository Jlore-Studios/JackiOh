**[[R2214000]] A ladder bot's missed decision is a beginner's move, not a uniform one (issue #2214, 2026-10-28)**: a decision a ladder bot's skill does not give to the AI ([[R747000]]) is no longer [[§10.7]]'s uniform random move but a softmax draw, on the bot's own stream, over the greedy baseline's one-ply scores of its legal moves on one world dealt from what it sees, so a weak bot ends a turn with a card it could play, or makes an attack that loses it value, far less often. The strengths were calibrated again with it ([[R748000]]): they still stand apart, from 1517 for the weakest to 1832, and only new bots take the new seeds.
