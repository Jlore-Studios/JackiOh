**[[R3502000]] A dungeon run's time is the time it was played, not the calendar's (issue #3502, 2026-11-09; amends [[§15.10]]):** a run's time counts only while a Dungeon page is open and the browser page is shown, kept as the time played and added to on each save, when the page hides or closes and when the Dungeon page is left; a reload resumes from the kept total and a run kept before this starts counting when it loads; the recorded time and the on-screen timer are that total, floored at zero per stretch.
