**[[R2728000]] The cosmetic token cycle gains a Legendary step and a bar size per step (issue #2728, 2026-11-01)**: the card and cosmetic token bars were both 200 points and took the same points from every slot, so they filled on the same openings ([[R1953000]]), and no token reached above Epic ([[R1305003]]). The owner's numbers in #2728 make `COSMETIC_TOKEN_CYCLE` Rare, Epic, Legendary, Rare, Epic, Cosmetic Pack, each step at a size of its own (`COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_RARE` 50, `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_EPIC` 75, `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_LEGENDARY` 100 and `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_PACK` 100, a cycle of 450 points), with one target per step in place of `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS` (6, 9, 12 and 12 card packs). The cosmetic bar settles step by step, its overflow compared with each next step's size, so one gain can pay several steps in order, and a bar the old size left part filled settles whole at its next opening; nothing is migrated. An opening answers `bars.cosmeticSizes`, the size of each step its sweep crosses, and the cosmetic bar's `size` is the step it stands at, there and in `GET /api/tokens`, which answers `legendary` as a next step and `cosmeticLegendary` among the tokens held. A Legendary token buys any unowned cosmetic up to Legendary, a Legendary chase included, and no token a Mythic; the dev fill fills the cosmetic bar to its current step's size. The card token bar is unchanged. It rejects one size for the whole cycle, the client working out the step sizes itself (CLAUDE.md rule 7), resetting existing bars and a Mythic token. It takes R2728000, the first number of issue #2728's block, amends [[R1305001]], [[R1305003]], [[R1305004]], [[R1310003]], [[R1333000]], [[R1953000]], [[R1953001]] and [[R1999000]], and edits [[§14.2]], [[§14.5]] and [[§14.10]].
