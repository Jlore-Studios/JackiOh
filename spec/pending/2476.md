**[[R2476000]] shows what a card remembers in its text (issue #2476, 2026-10-30)**: the owner asked that every card that records or remembers some state show it in its text, as #31 KY's Math Equation shows its damage in braces. A pass over the whole catalog held each card to one measure (its text turns on something recorded before now, and the view shows it nowhere on the card) and gave the 33 cards that meet it an [[R280]] `preview`: a count after the words that name it, or the remembered card's name as its `display`, a dash before anything is remembered. A fused card's ingredients each preview what their own text remembered ([[R102]]). It takes R2476000, the first of issue #2476's block, and edits [[§10.8]] and [[R280]].
