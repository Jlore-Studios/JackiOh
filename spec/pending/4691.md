**[[R4691000]] The hourly cron wakes a sleeping server for the daily sweep only once a sweep has something due (issue #4691, 2026-11-24)**: the server answers `GET /daily-next` with the first time a sweep has anything to do (a running game's deadline, a waiting account's next notification by `next_notify_at`, the next invite lapse, the oldest feed row's keep), the Worker keeps that answer as the container goes to sleep and forgets it whenever one starts, and the hourly cron sweeps a running server, or a sleeping one whose kept time has come or that left none, and leaves any other asleep; every loss, notification and purge lands at the same cron as before.
