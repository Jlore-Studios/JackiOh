**[[R2777000]] A card's adjusted win rate (issue #2777, 2026-11-02)**: the owner found `/stats`' win rates misleading: a card with a few dozen games topped the table on noise, and a card in a strong deck showed the deck's strength. Each card now has an adjusted win rate, 50% plus its drawn delta ([[R2427000]]) kept in the share τ²/(τ² + SE²), where SE² is the delta's sampling variance at its widest and τ, `ADJUSTED_WIN_RATE_PRIOR_SPREAD`, five points: the posterior mean of its lift, so a small sample's swing is mostly discounted and a large one's mostly kept. `GET /api/stats/cards` answers it and lists the cards by it, and the Cards tab shows an Adjusted column and sorts by it by default. A Beta-shrunk win rate, which keeps the deck's strength, and the played delta were rejected. It takes R2777000, the first number of issue #2777's block, and edits [[§9.11]].

**[[R2777001]] Constructed apart from All Random (issue #2777, 2026-11-02)**: `?mode=constructed` reads Best of 1 and Conquest together and no All Random record, on the cards, the drill-down and the game figures, the gate still counting every mode's live games; `/stats` opens on Constructed, offers All Random, Best of 1 and Conquest beside it, and no longer offers "All modes". It takes R2777001 and edits [[§9.11]] ([[R377]]).

**[[R2777002]] All Random counts a card once seen (issue #2777, 2026-11-02)**: under `?mode=random` a card's games, win rate, play rate, sample floor and per-patch history count only the decks that drew it; a deck dealt it and never drawing it is the never-drawn baseline, not a game of the card's. Constructed keeps every deck that held the card. It takes R2777002 and edits [[§9.11]].

**[[R2777003]] Only Easy and ladder games (issue #2777, 2026-11-02)**: the player's own statistics ([[R639]]) count a practice game only against Easy, `PRACTICE_TALLY_DIFFICULTY`, since Medium and Hard hand the AI resources ([[R180]]); the public card figures already counted no handicapped game (live games, development runs on a person's resources, and no practice game, [[R187]]), which a test now holds the development run to. Filing Easy practice games as records was rejected. It takes R2777003 and edits [[§9.11]].
