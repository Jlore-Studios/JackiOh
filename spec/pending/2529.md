**[[R2529000]] gives a change to how cards look a micro and a system the owner names a patch (issue #2529, 2026-10-30)**: a change under `apps/web/src/cards/` (`CARD_VISUALS`, tests aside) is a Cards micro of no family, claimed by a fragment's `visual` (`patches --visual`). The owner names a system in `SYSTEM_RELEASES` by its tracker issue, and a fragment claiming it (`patches --system <issue>`) ships as a patch, recorded `system: true` when the system alone made it one. Card statistics read such a patch as a micro over the families it plays. It takes R2529000, the first number of issue #2529's block, and amends [[R1365000]], [[R1366000]], [[R1367000]] and [[R1370000]].
