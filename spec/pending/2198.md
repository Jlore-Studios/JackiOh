**[[R2198000]] to [[R2198004]] are the Training Grounds spells (issue #2198, part 5 of the tracker #2193, 2026-10-28)**: the systems four of the set's Spells are written with, each following Hearthstone where the spec was silent (`docs/training-grounds-set.md` TG4, which proposed them as TG-P5-1 to TG-P5-4). Dino-size's set is [[R1185002]]'s on one Unit (`set_stats`, [[R2198000]]); Seance's exact copy is [[R57]]'s copy of a card on the field with its price, a stated price replacing it (`add_to_hand`'s `exact`, [[R2198001]]); In a hurry's tokens are destroyed at the end of the turn they arrive, whoever's, a stolen one included, and print no Brittle ([[R2198002]]); and a Spell's Cleave on a board pick reaches the permanents beside it in its row and side, the board twin of [[R1563001]] ([[R2198003]]). Fortify keeps its name: "Fortify Mind" joins [[R961]]'s rules phrases, still naming M #22.1 itself ([[R2198004]]). They edit [[§6.3]] (Set stats, Add to hand, Destroy) and [[§8.11]] (#15, #26, #27, #34, #37).
