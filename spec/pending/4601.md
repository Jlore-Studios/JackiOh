**[[R4601000]] A standing exile aura's exile at the state check is the board's, not a Spell's, and I21 allows it (issue #4601, 2026-11-24)**: S #115 Scornboi's exile aura exiles at the state check with no marker of its own ([[R1850002]]), so a Spell that summons it, as S #150 Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition) may, read to I21 as reaching an immune Unit Scornboi exiled ([[R4577008]]). The exile is the board's, as the state check's other rules are ([[R4508005]]): I21 allows an `exiled` in a Spell's run while a card laying an exile aura acts on the field after the step. Rejected: a marker event for the aura's exile, which would move every replay's events and hash.
