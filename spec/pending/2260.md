**[[R2260000]] A Dormant card hosts no Equip play (issue #2260, 2026-10-28)**: the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set. A Dormant card is out of play and nothing reaches it ([[R1839000]]), yet `equip_host_at` took any Unit its controller acts with on top of a zone, so an Equip card could be played onto a Dormant card, the Dormant Starship among them, and fuse into it. Now a Dormant host is no host: no Equip play onto its zone is offered or accepted until it awakens, while a Stack play onto it stays as [[R1839003]] and [[R1852007]] have it. It takes R2260000, the first number of issue #2260's block, and edits [[§6.1]] Equip.
