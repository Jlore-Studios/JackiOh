**[[R2095000]] is the Both boards view of the Duos co-op board (issue #2095, 2026-10-27)**: on a screen wide enough for two boards, the player may show the teammate's duel beside their own, live, instead of flipping with the portal. The rail's "Both boards" toggle (`duos-both`, `aria-pressed`) keeps its choice per device in `localStorage`, starting off; the second board is the portal's read-only board (`legal` empty, a no-op `onAction`, `replay`) plus silence, so it feeds no audio; a hand card dragged onto it passes when `legal` lists that pass, as a drop on the portal does; the portal hides while both boards show and returns when the toggle is off, and below the phone breakpoint the toggle hides and the board falls back to one. It takes R2095000, the first number of issue #2095's block, and edits [[§13.6]].
