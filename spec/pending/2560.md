**[[R2560000]] folds a practice claim to its result and no longer re-runs its AI moves (issue #2560, 2026-10-31)**: the owner asked to remove the server's re-run of a practice claim's AI moves ([[R1303002]]) for three reasons: it refused games the player really played, when the browser's clock cut a move short or its AI drifted from the server's, which [[R2208000]]'s long cap only narrowed; it ran the AI at the full `AI_BUDGET` on the blocking pool for every claim; and practice pay is already held to the day's Jlore cap ([[R1483000]]), so a forged AI side earns no more than an honest game. A claim still pays only when its log folds from its seed's setup, under the worker's nonces and seats, to a finished, unvoided game with the claimed result: an action the engine refuses, one under the wrong nonce or seat, an unfinished log, a voided game and a wrong result are refused as before, and no refusal is `ai_mismatch`. A legal move of the AI's seat the AI would not make, a concession among them, now pays. The browser drops what existed for the re-run: `PRACTICE_EARNING_AI_CLOCK_MS`, so a game on a server seed decides under `PRACTICE_AI_CLOCK_MS`, the `clockCut` mark and its "ran out of time" wording. Keeping the long clock was rejected, since slow devices still failed; refusing what [[R188]] keeps from the AI as a cheap guard was rejected, since the cap bounds what it could earn. `PRACTICE_VERIFY_CONCURRENCY` stays, since the fold still runs on the blocking pool. It takes R2560000, the first number of issue #2560's block, supersedes [[R1303002]] and [[R2208000]], and edits [[§9.9]] and [[§14.8]].
