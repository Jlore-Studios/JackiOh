**[[R3012000]] The Patch notes page nests micro patches under their patch, and a legend on the side jumps to any entry (issue #3012, 2026-11-04)**: amends [[R724000]], [[R507]] and [[R1369000]]. The owner asked for the patch notes to be streamlined by tier: a major and a patch are entries, and a micro patch (`v0.Z.Xb`) is appended under the nearest major or patch before it in `patches.json`'s order, oldest first, with its version, date and notes, and no art. A sticky legend (a `<nav>` that becomes a collapsible bar on a phone) lists the lines, majors and patches with micros nested; following a link, or an address hash such as `/patch-notes#v0.5.3`, opens the line, loads the entry's cards as [[R507]] does for an entry opened by hand, scrolls to it and marks it `aria-current`. The tier is the record's own (never a parsed version, [[R105]]); a micro with nothing before it in its line stays an entry of its own, and the static `/patch-notes` HTML keeps listing every version flat.

**[[R3012001]] Release art follows the tier, a still poster for a major, art for a patch and none for a micro patch (issue #3012, 2026-11-04)**: amends [[R724000]] and [[R1369000]] where #2681's art gave every patch a picture and animated a major's. Nothing moves any more; a major's poster is richer (the line's name large, headline cards by rarity, a frame), a patch's art is the same style at its scale, and a micro has none. Old `/release-art/<version>.svg` links are kept (CLAUDE.md rule 13): a major's `-still.svg` serves the poster too, and a micro up to `RELEASE_ART_LEGACY_MICRO_LAST` (the last version of `patches.json` when this shipped) keeps its file. A micro's GitHub release carries no image. Decided here: the old micros keep their files, as the issue's default asks, rather than being dropped.
