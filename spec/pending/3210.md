**[[R3210000]] One social read for the challenges and the unread counts, and every signed-in request counts as presence (issue #3210, 2026-11-05)**: tracker #3203 asks for less compute, better security and faster traffic, and a signed-in tab's social poll was two requests every `SOCIAL_POLL_MS`, beside a presence heartbeat every `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` that the server, which already counted every signed-in request as queue presence ([[R897000]]), did not need from a busy tab. `GET /api/social` now answers the open challenges and the unread counts, what `GET /api/challenges` and `GET /api/chat/unread` answer, by the same reads in one transaction for the caller alone, and the social poll makes it in place of the two, which stay for older tabs and other callers. Every request of an active account that passes auth, the rate limit and the gate refreshes its presence to friends, written at most once a `PRESENCE_TOUCH_EVERY_MS` (30 s) and never while the account owes the terms ([[R970002]]); agreeing to them clears the mark. The client sends `POST /api/presence` only once `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` has passed with no signed-in request, so two writes are at most 75 s apart, under `PRESENCE_TTL_MS`. A write on every request, a period of a whole heartbeat, counting pending or banned callers, a beat at mount and a client fallback to the two reads were rejected. It takes R3210000, the first number of issue #3210's block, and edits [[§9.4]].
