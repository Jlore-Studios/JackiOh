**[[R4327000]] A summon may set its fresh Unit face-down by its Cloaked (issue #4327, 2026-11-20)**: the summon verb's `setCloaked` stamps a fresh Unit as a Cloaked play stamps it and sets it in its side's leftmost open backrow zone, where it stands as a card set from a hand does: hidden, inert, a backrow card and no Unit, revealed only by its Activate: Reveal and summoning sick on the turn it is revealed. With no backrow zone open it lands face-up where it would without the option; its `summoned` is hidden from the opponent, it takes no second id, and the summon's `keywords` rider lands on it unannounced.

**[[R4327001]] A summon set face-down is no Unit summoned, and its reveal no summon (issue #4327, 2026-11-20)**: the set card's `summoned` names a backrow card that does not stand, so nothing answering "a Unit is summoned" (C #5 Tesla among them) answers it. Its reveal is an animation, as every Cloaked reveal is, and none of them answers that either.

**[[R4327002]] Ayys LMAO sets all three summons face-down (issue #4327, 2026-11-20)**: S+ #76's Ayys and Tiny Asteroids alike are set face-down in the backrow by their Cloaked, and with no backrow zone open each is summoned face-up into the unit row as before; the Radiant face and the picks are unchanged. It supersedes [[R3557003]]'s placement, which games of v0.6.8b and before keep. It takes R4327000–R4327002, edits [[§6.1]], [[§6.3]], [[§8.12]] and [[R3557003]].
