**[[R5268001]] A win-rate table or a version's card lists that did not change is kept once (issue #5268, 2026-11-28)**: the same audit found the 90 files under `crates/cards/patches/win_rates/` held 2 tables and the 224 under `card_metrics/` 26 lists, each copied to every version that kept it. It amends [[R1329000]] and [[R2173000]] on how those files are kept and takes R5268001.
