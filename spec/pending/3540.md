**[[R3540000]] Burn and Toxic are counters on a Unit or a hero, and a new one adds to the one there (issue #3540, 2026-11-10)**: the designer's Burns [int] and Toxic [int] are counts a Unit or a hero carries, so a new one adds to the count already there (the default of SP3), reported by `counterChanged` with the amount it added; [[R78]]'s reset takes a Unit's off as it leaves the field. Rejected: storing them as `Keyword::Burn` and `Keyword::Toxic` in the layers, where a Nerf would move them and a hero has none to hold.

**[[R3540001]] Burn then Toxic tick at the start of their controller's turn, in the Brittle stage, before the draw (issue #3540, 2026-11-10)**: "at the start of their owner's turn" is read as the Brittle tick's stage, so a Unit the tick kills dies before the draw; the hero ticks first, then the Units in [[R68]]'s order, each tick a sourceless [[§4.4]] hit, Burn's unpierced and then down by 3, Toxic's piercing Armor only and lasting, with one state check after the stage ([[R59]]). Burn drops whatever its hit did, a hit Armor or a shield took whole included. Rejected: ticking among the start-of-turn triggers, where a prompt could pause it; and letting Toxic pass a Divine Shield, which Pierce never does.

**[[R3540002]] 'Damage also Burns N' and 'also applies Toxic N' add N after each hit their card deals that its target survives (issue #3540, 2026-11-10)**: every hit counts, at [[§4.4]] step 7, and a hit that dealt nothing or killed its target adds nothing; N is the card's declared number where it declares one ([[R386]]), and an Equip adds its N to its host's. Rejected: adding to a target the hit killed, which no tick would reach, or after a hit that dealt nothing ([[R63]]), which would let a shield be burned through.

**[[R3540003]] 'Damage dealt Burns' and 'is Toxic' turn a hit into its target's counter after Armor, and it deals nothing (issue #3540, 2026-11-10)**: the target gains the counter equal to the damage it would have taken, at [[§4.4]] step 3a, so a Divine Shield and Armor have met the hit and nothing of steps 4 to 9 follows; an Indestructible Unit gains none and a source with both Burns only. Rejected: converting before Divine Shield and Armor, which would let both be burned through.

**[[R3540004]] A Vanilla clears Burn and Toxic; a Nerf leaves them (issue #3540, 2026-11-10)**: a Vanilla takes both off with the text, as it removes Frozen ([[R1174000]]); a Nerf does not, since they are counters and not keywords it could remove, and Degrade's menu holds neither. Rejected: keeping them through a Vanilla, as it keeps damage.

**[[R3540005]] Both seats see Burn and Toxic on Units and heroes, and the client badges them (issue #3540, 2026-11-10)**: the counters ride the Unit's and the hero's view on both seats, absent at none, and the client draws a badge for each, ember and green, beside the Plague Counters and on the hero's portrait. Rejected: keyword chips, since neither is a keyword.
