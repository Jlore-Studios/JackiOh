**[[R2647000]] takes the Token and Created marks off the card faces and keeps them in the hover preview (issue #2647, 2026-11-01)**: a card's Token and Created status almost never matters in combat, yet a small face, a full-art face and the board minion wore a token mark ([[R1177001]]) and every face in play a "Created" pill ([[R943]]), cluttering the board, the hand and the backrow. The faces now wear neither; a token's full face still prints "Token Unit", which the hover preview shows, and the preview's state notes still say a card was Created. Presentation only. It takes R2647000, the first number of issue #2647's block, amends [[R943]] and [[R1177001]], and edits [[§7]], [[§8]] and [[§10.10]].
