**[[R3937000]] The AI's count of the damage past its Taunts takes the opponent's better order of attackers and keeps the ones a Taunt would stop whole (issue #3937, 2026-11-15; amends [[R2976000]])**: the issue found that `damage_past_taunts_with`, behind `face_threat` ([[§9.9]]), sent the opponent's attackers at the AI's Taunts smallest first and spent an attacker a Taunt stopped whole, though [[§4.2]] step 3 lets the attacking player choose the order: a 3-health Defense guard against 3, 3 and 4 counted 4 where the 4 breaks it and both 3s go face for 6, and at 6 life `wall_switches` offered no second guard. The count is now the larger of two orders, smallest first and each Taunt broken first by the smallest attacker that destroys it alone, and an attacker the Taunt's Armor ([[R1361]]) or First Strike ([[§4.3]]) would stop whole waits until the Taunt falls instead of being spent. The flag `best_attack_order_past_taunts` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick keeps the old walk.
