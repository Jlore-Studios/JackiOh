**[[R4581000]] An open Infinite Assault attempt continues on the UTC day it started (issue #4581, 2026-11-23)**: the device folds an open attempt on the UTC date and catalog version in its file, so midnight never redeals an encounter already in progress; the current day's deal is offered after it ends.

**[[R4581001]] Malformed Infinite Assault device data is ignored one key at a time (issue #4581, 2026-11-23)**: a malformed or differently versioned attempt or records value is removed and read as absent without losing the other key, and valid records are trimmed to the newest `history_days` UTC dates.
