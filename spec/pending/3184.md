**[[R3184000]] /stats' Cards tab shows no When drawn, Drawn vs not drawn, Radiant or Normal (issue #3184, 2026-11-05)**: the adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]) is the page's headline read of a card's strength, so the drawn win rate, the drawn delta and the face rates beside it had become noise. The Cards tab's table drops the four columns with their show/hide toggles and sorts, the grid its Radiant and Normal figures, and the row's "not enough data" goes with the drawn delta; an address that sorts by one of them opens on the default sort, as [[R2446000]]'s old sorts do. `GET /api/stats/cards`, the client's wire types, the card's stats block, the reports and the drill-down's Show Radiant face are unchanged. Presentation only. It takes R3184000, the first number of issue #3184's block, amends [[R1147000]] and [[R2427000]] and edits [[§9.11]].

**[[R3184001]] A card's tier fit on /stats: how much of its Adjusted range lies inside its tier (issue #3184, 2026-11-05)**: a tier ([[R3022000]]) said where a card's adjusted rate sits, not how firmly. With Show 95% ranges on, each card's tier now shows beneath it the share of its Adjusted 95% range ([[R2895000]]) inside its tier's band, to a whole percent: S from the band's top up, F from its bottom down, A to D its quarters from the top, a range of no width wholly in or out, and none below the floor or without a tier, a range or a band. Sorting by tier orders a tier's cards by it, the higher first descending and a card with none after, then by the adjusted rate, whether or not the ranges show. The client computes it from the server's figures, interval arithmetic and no statistic; the probability inside the band read off a normal was rejected as a statistic the client should not compute. It takes R3184001, the second number of issue #3184's block, amends [[R2895000]] and [[R3022000]] and edits [[§9.11]].

**[[R3184002]] /stats' Created cards table sorts by any of its columns (issue #3184, 2026-11-05)**: the Created cards table ([[R2089000]]) kept the server's order alone. Each of its headers now sorts it, the first press descending (the card's name ascending) and the next flipping it, with an arrow and `aria-sort` on the sorted header; a figure the row does not show, below [[R654]]'s floor or withheld, sorts after the rows with one either way, ties keep the server's order, and the table keeps the server's order until a header is pressed. The sort is not kept in the address. Presentation only. It takes R3184002, the third number of issue #3184's block, amends [[R2089000]] and edits [[§9.11]].
