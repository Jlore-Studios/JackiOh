**[[R3945000]] A card's play rate is how often it was played when seen (2026-11-15)**: `/stats`' Play rate was a card's counted games over every seat counted, how common the card was. It is now the card's played games over its seen games (opening hand or drawn, played or not), in every mode, so a balance pass can tell a card passed over in hand from one cast at once; a card only a rule makes reads its used Created games over its made ones, and a card never seen reads 0. Amends [[R2777002]] and [[R3185000]]; the Pocket Pick's rates ([[R1583000]]) are unchanged.
