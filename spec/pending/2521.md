**[[R2521000]] A dead Starship Piece fuses its base version (issue #2521, 2026-10-30)**: the owner's first Stinkyscape balance pass asked that "only the base version" of a dead piece fuse into the Starship, its fused cards and its Buffs left behind. The copy [[R1852000]] fuses is now a fresh card of the piece's base definition: for a fused card, the card the others were fused into, its last ingredient (a fusion keeps its kept card last, [[R1852001]]); its tuning is not carried, so no Buff or Nerf goes with it; a Radiant piece still fuses Radiant, as the owner chose, and a token keeps its X/X. Hearthstone brings a minion back without its enchantments or Magnetic upgrades. Carrying the whole fusion, as before, was rejected. It takes R2521000, the first number of issue #2521's block, and edits [[§6.1]] Starship Piece.

**[[R2521001]] Flak's random damage: one roll for each enemy (issue #2521, 2026-10-30)**: Flak v3 to v5 now deal "a random 1 to 3 damage to each enemy" (Radiant 2 to 4, Lucky 1), weighted 60, 30 and 10. The spec did not say whether the roll is one for the sweep or one for each target; this follows Hearthstone's Lightning Storm, whose minions each take their own roll: each target rolls just before its hit, in the sweep's order, a round of v4 or v5 rolling anew, and Lucky repeats each roll and keeps the highest. `damage_all` takes the odds and the Lucky count; without them it draws nothing, so every other sweep plays as before.

**[[R2521002]] Tmack Trav's extra attack keeps its summoning sickness (issue #2521, 2026-10-30)**: a Tmack Trav played with Rush that destroyed a Unit was offered the enemy hero the same turn, because `may_attack_again` lifted its sickness for the turn. The owner reported it; now its extra attack keeps the sickness (`keepSick`), so on its summon turn Rush lets it attack again a Unit only, and on a later turn the extra attack may go face. C+ #73.1 Classic Golem still lifts it ([[R424]]).

**[[R2521003]] A University Activates at most once a turn (issue #2521, 2026-10-30)**: ZolarKeth's "Activate your Universities" ran each one's Activate spending none of its uses ([[R1876001]]), so two ZolarKeths, or a University's own use beside one, ran a University twice. Now the run spends one of the University's uses and passes by one with none left this turn (`spendUse`), whoever spent it, and ZolarKeth's Cry summons a University on its base face. The refusal of the summoned University's own Activate stands ([[R1876000]]).

**[[R2521004]] Swear Loyalities exiles only another faction's cards, from its caster's hand and deck (issue #2521, 2026-10-30)**: its new text reads "exile every card in your hand and deck that doesn't belong to that faction", and its example exiles a card of another faction. The owner chose the narrower reading: a faction is its Faction card and the cards that card adds, a card of no faction is never exiled, and Tylannt, of ETHICS and of Vanguard Coalition, stays when either is chosen. The literal reading, which would exile nearly the whole deck, was rejected. It narrows [[R1880001]]: the graveyard is no longer read.

**[[R2521005]] Pestilent Slime spreads its Plague Counters (issue #2521, 2026-10-30)**: "Death: Spread its Plague Counters among your permanents." places each counter it died with, one placement each, on a random permanent its controller has on the field, face-down cards included, so a Pestilent Slime there multiplies each; with none there, they are lost. Hearthstone has no counterpart; one placement per counter keeps [[R689]]'s count of placements.

**[[R2521006]] Fruit Launcher's pool states its tokens (issue #2521, 2026-10-30)**: asked to confirm that Fruit Launcher launches Fruit tokens, its Fruit pool already held the Grapes ([[R382]]); its query now states them, as Dropshipping's does, and no draw changes.
