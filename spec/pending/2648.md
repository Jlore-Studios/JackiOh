**[[R2648000]] Your table's stats name the right kind of card (issue #2648, 2026-11-01)**: a Spell was named Unbreakable, since a Spell never sends `destroyed` and so is never destroyed, and the player's own Tributes, Sacrifices and Brittle crumbles counted as losses, since a Unit given up that way does send `destroyed`. Now `destroyed` and `defeated` count a death only when the other seat caused it (a `killerId` on that side, or that side's last play, ability, Trap or attack before it in the batch), never after a `crumbled` of the same card, and not when the card's own side acted last with no killer of the other's; totals already stored keep their counts. Unbreakable, Glass Cannon and Workhorse name only a Unit, Unbreakable one that prints no Indestructible and has at least 5 plays; Medic credits a Lifesteal hit's heal to the attacker; Hot Streak names the card with the most wins played in, The Record the card played in the most games, and Ride or Die reads `survived` per win played in, which is the only game `survived` counts in, with Survivor's line saying a win; a lunge plays a new stage animation `cast` (the board's `cardPlayed`) on a Spell or Field Spell and `trap` on a Trap or Field Trap; and Comeback Kid is Lucky Charm. A death with no killer and no actor counting for nobody, the cause being the seat across from the card's owner, any other `damage` clearing a heal's source, and The Record being left out while no card has a game played in were chosen, against counting it for the seat whose turn it is, the controller, keeping the source, and falling back to the top `seen` card. It takes R2648000, the first number of issue #2648's block, amends [[R1582000]], [[R1584000]] and [[R1743000]], and edits [[§9.11]].
