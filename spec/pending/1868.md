**[[R1868000]] and [[R1868001]] are two of the spells that bend a rule, Stinkyscape's S14 (issue #1868, part of #1856, 2026-10-27)**: built on `main` behind [[R1420]]'s gate. Secure System's "set all Units' Attack to 0" and Unsecure System's "double it" are Buffs of each Unit's Attack as it stands when the Spell resolves, since the spec has no set of Attack alone ([[R1868001]]). They take R1868000 and R1868001, the first numbers of issue #1868's block, and edit [[§8.10]]; their paragraph was written by issue #2176 (the M12 audit's B-8), since #2064 merged without one.
