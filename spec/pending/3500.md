**[[R3500000]] Uncached replays fold behind `REPLAY_FOLDS_RUNNING_MAX`, concurrent opens of a match share one fold, and an open past `REPLAY_FOLD_WAIT_MS` is a 429 stating `retryAfterMs` (issue #3500, 2026-11-09)**: the game server is one `basic` container (a quarter of a processor), and an opening of a replay it does not hold in memory folds a whole game, about 200 ms of a full core for 256 actions. The per-account allowance ([[R109]]) let one account with more than `REPLAY_CACHE_MATCHES` finished matches start sixty folds a minute, two requests for one match folded it twice, and the ladder bots, which think against a clock, played badly while they ran. The 1v1 and co-op replays now take a permit from one pool for the server (`REPLAY_FOLDS_RUNNING_MAX`, 1), held until the fold's job ends; an opening takes its match's turn first, so concurrent openings of one match fold it once, and the two waits together are bounded by `REPLAY_FOLD_WAIT_MS` (5 s), past which the request is 429 `rate_limited` with `retryAfterMs` (`REPLAY_FOLD_RETRY_AFTER_MS`, 2 s, [[R192]]). Cached pages wait for nothing. A request dropped mid-fold leaves its fold to finish and its replay unkept. The bots' and the co-op AI's searches may share the cap later. Rejected: two folds at a time, a shared future, a cap on how many wait, and a change to the client.

**[[R3500001]] A practice claim still being checked keeps its place among `PRACTICE_SEEDS_OPEN_MAX`, and the sweep skips a claim its server is still settling (issue #3500, 2026-11-09)**: [[R1483000]] counted only unclaimed seeds and noted that a claim waiting for its check freed its place at once, "left out" because the burn was the only record of a claim. A claim burns its seed before its check waits for one of `PRACTICE_VERIFY_CONCURRENCY`'s two permits, so an account could take a new seed and claim again as fast as it liked while the queue of folds grew. The claim is kept until it settles since [[R2422000]], so the count now reads it: a seed issued within `PRACTICE_SEED_TTL_SECONDS` counts while it is unclaimed or claimed with its claim kept and no settle mark, in both stores, and stops counting when its seed lapses, so a claim whose pay failed every try cannot hold a place for ever. The retention loop's sweep skips a claim a settle of the same process still runs, so a live claim waiting for a permit past `PRACTICE_SETTLE_GRACE_SECONDS` is not folded a second time. Amends [[R1483000]] (the claim frees its place when it settles) and [[R2422000]]. Rejected: a new column or counter, counting past the lifetime, a limit on the claims waiting in the process, and a lease column for the sweep's skip.
