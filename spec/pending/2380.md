**[[R2380000]] has the AI price a Dormant card by the unit it wakes as (issue #2380, 2026-10-29)**: [[R1839000]] left a Dormant card out of the AI's evaluation, so the AI almost never played Stinkyscape's Imprisoned Units. It takes R2380000, the first number of issue #2380's block, amends [[R1839000]] and edits [[§6.1]] and [[§9.9]].
