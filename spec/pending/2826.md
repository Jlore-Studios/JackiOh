**[[R2826000]] the practice AI samples each side's hidden cards against that side's shown cards (issue #2826, 2026-11-02)**: the AI's determinization sampled every hidden card against one set, the cards the opponent had shown, so a dealt deck's library ([[R433]]) could hold a second copy of a card the seat already showed, against [[§2.6]], and could never take a card only the opponent had shown. Each hidden card is now sampled against what its own side has shown and what the determinization has given that side, with a card of the seat's library minted for the opponent's deck ([[R73]]) on the opponent's side, and every draw in the same order. Rejected: one shared set, the old sampler; every hidden card of the seat's library against the seat's set, which lets a card swapped in from the opponent's deck repeat one the opponent shows; and every placeholder by the deck it was minted for, which moves the worlds of every game with a steal or a board swap. It takes R2826000, the first number of issue #2826's block, and edits [[§9.9]].
