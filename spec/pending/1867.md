**[[R1867000]] to [[R1867010]] are the spells on existing primitives, Stinkyscape's S13 (issue #1867, part of #1856, 2026-10-27)**: built on `main` behind [[R1420]]'s gate, each card's reading of the designer's text on verbs the engine already had. Freeze Ray Freezes the target and its neighbours in its row on its side, whichever side it stands on ([[R1867000]]). Otherworldly Endurance's Radiant face adds all three of its spells Radiant, as #11's and #12's do ([[R1867001]]). Inner Fire sets Attack as a buff of the difference, never below 0 ([[R1867002]]), and Divine Spirit doubles the Health a Unit has left ([[R1867003]]). Frostbolt hits a Unit or a hero, and its Radiant hit on the neighbours is the Spell's own damage, not Cleave ([[R1867004]]). Shadow Word Death and Pain read Attack through the layers as the target is chosen, and their Radiant faces take the neighbours whatever their Attack ([[R1867005]]). Psychic Scream shuffles every permanent on the field into the opponent's deck as their card ([[R1867006]]). Moonberry's random Other-Origins card is any of the tag's tokens in the sets that ship ([[R1867007]]). Fortify Flesh rolls its hero's Armor uniformly, Lucky keeping the higher roll ([[R1867008]]). Flash Freeze Freezes every Unit, and the side its Radiant face spares keeps its attacks ([[R1867009]]). Catalyst Comet adds a random Catalyst card, then shuffles itself into its owner's deck ([[R1867010]]). They take R1867000 to R1867010, the first numbers of issue #1867's block, and edit [[§8.10]]; their paragraph was written by issue #2176 (the M12 audit's B-8), since #2044 merged without one.
