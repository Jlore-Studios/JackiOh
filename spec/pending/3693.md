**[[R3693000]] A Twice Forward card an earlier trigger of its own firing took off the field is not revealed by its later trigger (issue #3693, 2026-11-12)**: the fuzz monitor's I16 (the card's recorded place against the pile holding it) found, on handicapped seed 1152, a fused Twice Forward whose Warp Disruptor ingredient's trigger forced an attack that killed it, after which its own trigger, the last of its firing, ran on the graveyard card and set `faceUp` there, against [[R78]]'s reset. The firing's later triggers keep running, as Hearthstone resolves a trigger that has begun though its source has died; only the reveal, which needs a card on the field, is skipped. Rejected: stopping the firing's remaining triggers once the card leaves the field, which changes what every fused trap does; and an I16 exception for `faceUp`, which would hide the leftover.
