**[[R4817000]] A card's face swapped where it stands starts the Brittle its new face prints, and ends a count its old face's printed Brittle started (issue #4817, 2026-11-25)**: an audit of the engine's continuous effects found that a base S #135.1 Sack made Radiant on the field showed its Radiant face's Brittle 1 and never crumbled, and its comment found the mirror, a base S #9 Ticketed made Radiant crumbling from a Brittle 3 its Radiant face does not print. Make Radiant and De-Radiant now refit the count to the new face. Edits [[§5.2]]'s "On the field" bullet, [[§6.1]]'s Brittle X row and [[§6.3]]'s De-Radiant row. Takes R4817000.

**[[R4817001]] A Radiant face that newly prints Reborn gives a used Reborn back, as it does a spent Divine Shield (issue #4817, 2026-11-25)**: the same audit found that a base C+ #6 Wrong-House Attacker back through a granted Reborn and then made Radiant lacked the Reborn its Radiant face prints, where a spent Divine Shield came back in the same case. Edits [[§5.2]]'s "On the field" bullet. Takes R4817001.
