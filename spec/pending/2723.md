**[[R2723000]] A full-art card in your own hand draws its art alone (issue #2723, 2026-11-02)**: a full-art card ([[R1017001]], at any tier of [[R2655000]]) in its owner's hand printed its name banner across the foot of the art, with its rarity gem, its set mark and its crest, and a Mythic signed its name across the art too, though the player who holds it knows what it is. On a board the viewer plays, the full-art faces in their own hand now draw only their art, their cost gem with its live cost and tone and a Unit's attack and health, with the hand's states, the scrim, the tier's motion ([[R2655003]]) and any finish, border or foil kept; they draw no name banner, signature, rarity gem, set mark or crest, and the name stays in the face's words, visually hidden, so a screen reader, the tooltip with hover previews off and the by-name finders still find it. Every other view is unchanged: the hover preview, the sheet and the detail view show the whole card with its name, and so do the board, a mulligan's and a Discover's options, the deck builder, the Collection, the Almanac, an opponent's revealed hand and a watched or replayed game, whose hand nobody wears ([[R1295000]]). It rejects hiding `.card-name` by CSS alone, which jsdom cannot see and so no unit test could prove, and a new `aria-label` on the card's generic root, which a `div` without a role does not announce. It takes R2723000, the first number of issue #2723's block, amends [[R1017001]] and edits [[§10.10]].
