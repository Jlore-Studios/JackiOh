**[[R2727000]] A Buff in a hand may give Cloaked, and a Nerf there takes it off (issue #2727, 2026-11-01)**: the owner asked that in a hand a Buff and a Nerf move a card's Cloaked and Crater in opposite directions. A Buff's keyword row now offers Cloaked, last, to a Unit, a Spell or a Field Spell in a hand that lacks it of its own (`TUNE_BUFF_HAND_KEYWORDS`), never to a Trap, a Field Trap or a Vanilla card; a Nerf's row already took off a Cloaked the card had of its own, never a lent one, and is now proven to.

**[[R2727001]] A Buff in a hand may take Crater off (issue #2727, 2026-11-01)**: the other half of the request. In a hand a Buff's row also removes a Crater the card has of its own, printed, given or added by a Nerf (`TUNE_BUFF_HAND_REMOVES`), from a card of any type; in a deck or on the field a Buff leaves it, and a Nerf never removes it.
