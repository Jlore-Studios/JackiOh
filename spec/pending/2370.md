**[[R2370000]] adds a Tokens toggle to /stats (issue #2370, 2026-10-29)**: presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The Cards tab's Show tokens toggle, off by default and carried in the address as `tokens=1`, shows the tokens among `GET /api/stats/cards`' `created` in a section of their own, each with its games, its win rate (the seats that made it, used or not) and its Used and Not used figures, best first. The game record, the totals and the response are unchanged: [[R2089000]] already counts every Created card, tokens among them. It takes R2370000, the first number of issue #2370's block, and amends [[R2089000]].
