**[[R3792000]] A queued or room-waiting player is told when the server cannot be reached (issue #3792, 2026-11-12)**: amends [[R897000]]. When `LOBBY_UNREACHABLE_AFTER_READS` (3, about 6 s, inside the server's ticket timeout) account reads of a queue or room wait in a row fail as unreachable (`ApiUnreachableError`), `/play` shows one `role="status"` notice, "Can't reach the server. Still trying…", beside the status line, which stays. Any read the server answered, a refusal among them, clears the notice and resets the count; fewer failures than the constant show nothing. The ticket logic (R897000's ended-reads count, the pairing navigation) is unchanged, and the notice is presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
