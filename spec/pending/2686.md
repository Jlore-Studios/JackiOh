**[[R2686000]] A player's later hellos on one socket are answered at most once per interval, and a new socket's first at once (issue #2686, 2026-11-01)**: a seat's hellos coalesced only while one was queued ([[R835001]]), and the flag clears as the task starts, so a client that sent again as each answer arrived kept one queued for ever and had the actor rebuild and send the whole view, the clock, the portraits and the cards' picks each time under the lock both players' actions wait on; a watcher's socket had been given one answer per `WATCH_HELLO_INTERVAL_MS` for this ([[R2609000]]). A seat's socket, on `/ws/match` and on `/ws/duos`, now holds the same interval through its `SeatGuard`, which keeps the socket generation with the time of its last answered hello: a hello of the same generation sooner than the interval is dropped unanswered, the socket stays open, and the first hello of a new generation, a reattach, is always answered and starts its own interval. An action is never held back by it. It rejects closing the socket on a hello flood, which would end an honest page's game on a reconnect storm. It takes R2686000, the first number of issue #2686's block, amends [[R835001]] and edits [[§9.5]], [[§9.8]] and [[§13.9]].
