**[[R2208000]] lets the AI of a practice game that earns finish each move (issue #2208, 2026-10-28)**: the browser cut every AI decision at `PRACTICE_AI_CLOCK_MS`, earning games included, while the server re-runs each AI move of a claim at the full budget with no clock ([[R1303002]]), so on a slow phone, or in a long game on any device, a won game could pay nothing, and its dialog blamed the AI's moves rather than the device. A game on a server seed now decides under `PRACTICE_EARNING_AI_CLOCK_MS`, many times that cap and only a backstop for a stuck worker, while the node budget stays the real bound; a free game without a server seed, a lesson and a sandbox game keep the short one. A decision that long cap still cuts marks the game, in its save too ([[R668]]), and the worker's claim answer carries the mark beside the claim, never in it. The claim is still sent, and a marked game's `ai_mismatch` refusal reads "this device ran out of time on one of the AI's moves"; every other refusal reads as before. Rejected: not claiming a marked game, since a cut move often matches the full one, and no cap at all, since a stuck worker would hold the game. It takes R2208000, the first number of issue #2208's block, and edits [[§9.9]], [[§14.8]] and [[R1303002]].
