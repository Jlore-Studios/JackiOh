**[[R2718000]] prints each set's watermark behind a card's rules text and gives Meditative+ a set mark (issue #2718, 2026-11-01)**: the owner's reference was Hearthstone, whose cards carry their expansion's symbol, large and faint, behind their text. Every face that prints a rules box, a Radiant, a Chinese and a blank box included, now carries its set's mark in one ink, centred in the box under the words, in a shade of the box's own colour whose contrast with the box's inks the tests hold; Meditative+, which had no mark and wore the fallback diamond, has one of its own, the ensō with a plus. A full-art card, which prints no box, wears its watermark behind its Full text and detail rules; Glitch, a face too small for its box, a card back and a set with no symbol (Boss, Boss-X, Custom) wear none. A **Set watermarks** switch in the Visuals settings, on by default and kept on the device, takes them all off. Presentation only. It takes R2718000, the first number of issue #2718's block, and edits [[§9.1]] and [[§10.10]].
