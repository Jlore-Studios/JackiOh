**[[R2194000]] to [[R2194002]] are the Training Grounds foundation (issue #2194, part 1 of the tracker #2193, 2026-10-28)**: the owner asked, on 2026-10-28, for a new set, Training Grounds, in the normal game, and for the tutorial to be remade from it. The set is built on `main` behind [[R1420]]'s gate as the fourth held set, after Stinkyscape in the catalog order, until its release (#2204) ships it as the newest set ([[R2194000]]). The designer's "Training-grounds" and "Tutorial-Grounds" are the set, and a pool that names it works while it is held ([[R2194001]]). Its data: #1–#40 and the token #39.1 by the designer's numbers and rarity bands, the twenty Core snapshots #41–#60 at their Core rarities, #28 and #29 read as Units, and the designer's names as written ([[R2194002]]). `docs/training-grounds-set.md` reads every card against the engine and plans the parts that build them.
