**[[R4065000]] The AI's face threat and stand-downs leave out a unit the first tick of its own Burn and Toxic destroys before it can attack (issue #4065, 2026-11-16; amends [[R3793000]] and [[R3822000]])**: the issue found that `face_threat` and `could_attack_next_turn` ([[§9.9]]) left out a unit its own Brittle tick destroys but still counted one its Burn or Toxic destroys at the start of its controller's next turn ([[R3540001]]), so an enemy 4/4 on Burn 5 beside a 3/3 read as 7 against a hero at 7 when only 3 can come. Such a unit now counts no attack, its tick read as the evaluation's `afflicted` reads it ([[R3822000]]: Armor meets Burn, Toxic pierces, an unspent Divine Shield or Indestructible keeps it), with [[R3793000]]'s timing, and an unspent Reborn does not keep it counted, its body coming back summoning sick ([[R83]]), unless that body has Charge, or Rush where only a Unit is the target. This drops [[R3822000]]'s rejection of a doomed unit's face threat, as [[R3793000]] did for Brittle, and [[R3822000]]'s weights at 0 now read no counter only with the new flag off too; the flag `affliction_kills_before_swing` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`.

**[[R4065001]] The AI reads a hit that exiles on damage as a removal, as it reads a Poisonous one, and prices what it exiles whole (issue #4065, 2026-11-16)**: the issue found that the AI read Meditative #72 The Banisher's hits ([[R1124]]) as plain damage, so a 5/5 attacking it read as a free kill and an enemy Banisher only chipped the AI's Taunt in the count of the damage past it. A hit from a source that exiles what it damages now removes the Unit it deals 1 or more to wherever the AI reads a Poisonous hit (`blow`, `kills_and_survives`, the Taunt walk and its Cleave splash, `breaks_alone`, `falls_to_first_strike`), a hit that deals nothing exiling nothing, and `trade_value` prices what it exiles at its whole `unit_worth`, with no Death and no Reborn body left; the flag `exile_on_damage_read` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`.
