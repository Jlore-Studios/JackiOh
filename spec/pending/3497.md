**[[R3497000]] Hidden tabs drain the animation queue and flushed bursts keep the turn-start cue (issue #3497, 2026-11-09)**: Hearthstone has no hidden-tab client, so the spec did not decide it. On an online board, while the page is hidden each new view drains the animation queue, and a tab that turns visible with steps still queued drains them then, so coming back shows the live board with its moves ready at once while the game log still lists every event; practice, dungeon and opened-replay boards never drain, and a visible tab behaves exactly as before. A flushed burst still sends the viewer's own turn-start cue, as it keeps the game-over sting. Rejected: always animating the backlog while the turn clock runs, and dropping the turn-start cue.
