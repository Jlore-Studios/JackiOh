**[[R5271000]] The card statistics' read of the card totals lets the server's other requests in as it goes (issue #5271, 2026-11-28)**: staging's deploy check still timed out on the card statistics with [[R5225000]] in place, and its ask of the load's progress timed out too, because the read of the card totals held the server's one worker for its whole length. The ruling amends [[R5225000]], edits §9.11 (Public statistics and the publication gate) and takes R5271000.

**[[R5271001]] The snapshot's load cuts the window every first read asks for, so a read that gave up leaves the next none to wait on (issue #5271, 2026-11-28)**: once the server answered during the load, staging's deploy check followed it to its end, but its next read still timed out, because the window's cut began only in a read that waited on the snapshot. The ruling amends [[R5225000]], edits §9.11 (Public statistics and the publication gate) and takes R5271001.
