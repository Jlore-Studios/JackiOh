**[[R4331000]] A spent Quest goes to the graveyard (issue #4331, 2026-11-20):** a Quest card whose final reward is single-use, not an Aura, goes to its graveyard once that reward is given (`end_quest_line`, a plain move as [[R3044000]]'s), unless a quest of its line is still open or waiting or it holds a reward's aura. The Greatest Heist goes after Stage 3, and In Too Deep's base face after J or K. L and M are Auras and keep it, its Radiant face always ends holding them, and Job Application never ends its line. The scripts of v0.6.9 are kept for their replays.

**[[R4331001]] Stolen mana crystals stand past the cap (issue #4331, 2026-11-20):** amending [[R1854003]], a thief keeps every crystal it steals on top of the cap (`stolenManaCrystals`), and a steal from a thief takes those too, for good. The Greatest Heist's Stage 3 reward prints it: "Steal your opponent's mana crystals, even past 4." Its script of v0.6.9 is kept, its steal stopping at the cap.
