**[[R3935000]] An Embiggened Wall's bonus is three times its printed face, none once a Set stats has replaced its stat line (issue #3935, 2026-11-15)**: [[R3555001]] worked the Walls' bonus out from `face_of`, so a Set stats (Harmony Spirit's 3/3, Dino-size's 10/10, [[R1185002]]) was multiplied by four and a Buff or Nerf counted four times. The bonus is now `EMBIGGENED_SCALE - 1` times the printed face of the face the card wears, read off its definition, and none once a Set stats has replaced the stat line: the Wall stands at exactly the stats set, as every Unit does (Phase Wall's Immutable still refuses the set). A stat Buff or Nerf counts once, at layer 1, a Buff taken in hand before the play included: a set replaces the stat line, and an enchantment after it adds its own amount once. GIGA, the stamp and the Vanilla reading stay. Through v0.6.4d the bonus was three times `face_of`, which the kept script keeps ([[R767000]]).
