A stat Buff or Nerf counts once, at layer 1, a Buff taken in hand before the play included: a set replaces the stat line, and an enchantment after it adds its own amount once. GIGA, the stamp and the Vanilla reading stay. Through v0.6.5b the bonus was three times `face_of`, which the kept script keeps ([[R767000]]).
