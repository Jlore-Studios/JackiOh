**[[R3791000]] The result screen says when the opponent has left (issue #3791, 2026-11-12; amends [[R3213000]], [[R1452]] and [[R672]])**: once a finished match's opponent was gone, the rematch offers and the waiting line vanished with no word, so a seat that had offered could not tell whether its offer stood. While the match socket is open and the seat's rematch status says `opponentHere` is false, the result panel now shows one status line, "Your opponent has left.", in place of the offers, the waiting line, the lean picker and a refused offer's alert; a person who left and a ladder bot whose linger ended read the same, so it tells no bot from a person. It shows nothing when the result's own reason is the opponent's disconnect, which says "Your opponent left the match." already, nor on a closed socket or before any status. Presentation only: it reads the status the server already sent this seat. Rejected: a line in the result text itself, a separate line per bot or person, keeping a refused offer's alert beside the line, saying it a second time when the game ended by their leaving.
