**[[R3547000]] A Mineral's or Cryofluid's "Give a Unit …" names one Unit on either side, never a hero (issue #3547, 2026-11-10)**: S+ #22 Cryofluid and S+ #44–#50, the Minerals, print "Give a Unit …" and no side. The plan read it as one declared Unit on the field on either side ([[R81]]), as Training Grounds' Fortify and Stinkyscape's Meteor declare theirs: a player may give a Mineral to their own Unit or their opponent's, never to a hero, and a Spell cannot pick a Unit that is Immutable ([[R781001]]) or Immune to Spells. Rejected: enemy-only picks, and the hero as a pick.

**[[R3547001]] Blast Compound's "lock the tile it is on" locks the zone the chosen Unit stands in (issue #3547, 2026-11-10)**: S+ #44's "tile" is the zone the chosen Unit stands in as the Spell resolves, locked ([[§6.3]]) after the Unit has its attack and Cleave: its unit zone, or its backrow zone when a Flexible Unit stands there ([[R3543000]]). Only that zone is Locked; the Unit is unaffected, and the Lock stays when it leaves. Rejected: locking the zone across the board from it, and every zone of its row.

**[[R3547002]] Phase Fabric gives its keywords together, so Immune to Spells does not turn away the rest of the Spell (issue #3547, 2026-11-10)**: S+ #46 gives Immutable and Immune to Spells, and Evasive on its Radiant face ([[R3543002]]). A Unit Immune to Spells is not affected by a Spell's effects, so a grant of it that came first turned the Spell's own later grant away: the Radiant face landed no Evasive. The Spell now gives Immune to Spells last, which shows only in the end state, every keyword the face lists. Rejected: the printed order, and a verb that grants a list at once.
