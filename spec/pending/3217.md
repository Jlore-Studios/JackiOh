**[[R3217000]] Google's and Roblox's sign-in buttons are one size and share one centered row, Google first, wrapping only when too narrow; one rule sizes both and Google Identity Services is drawn at its width (issue #3217, 2026-11-05)**: the issue asked that "Continue with Roblox" match Google's button, side by side on one row, in Roblox's branding with its logo. The spec had no rule for how the providers sit, so this one fixes their size (200 by 40 px, the width GIS is given and the height its large button has), their row and their states; the colors are the palette's nearest to Roblox's black and white, and the logo is left to a person because the repository holds no Roblox asset and the logo must not be redrawn. It amends [[R2983000]] and [[R3147000]].
