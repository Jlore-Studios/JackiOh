**[[R2210000]] is the daily quest replace (issue #2210, 2026-10-28)**: a quest the player did not want held its slot until they finished it, and the full slots skipped every arrival, so each held quest may now be replaced `QUEST_REPLACES_PER_DAY` times a UTC day, through `POST /api/quests/:questId/replace`, by a quest drawn as an arrival is, neither held nor the one replaced, with its progress lost and the arrival rhythm unmoved; `GET /api/quests` and the replace answer `replacesLeft`, and the quests panel shows Replace on each quest while one is left, behind a confirm. It takes R2210000, the first of issue #2210's block, adds `QUEST_REPLACES_PER_DAY` to `crates/server/src/config.rs` and `serverConfig.ts`, and edits [[§14.9]] and [[§14.11]].
