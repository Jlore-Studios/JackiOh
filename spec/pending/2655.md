**[[R2655000]] Every full-art card has an Epic, a Legendary (animated) and a Mythic (signed) full art, and a card's pick chooses which owned one it wears (issue #2655, 2026-11-01)**: the owner asked for two chase tiers over every full art. Each full-art card's one Epic full art ([[R1313000]]) gains a Legendary, `full-art-<slug>-animated`, and a Mythic, `full-art-<slug>-signed`, both chases, none granted to any account; they drop by the chase routes and no token buys them. The registry's check refuses two full arts of one rarity on a card. A card's pick is `false`, `true` (its Epic) or a full art's id, held to ownership by the server, kept in migration 0072's `full_art_id` and frozen into a match's loadout as the tier worn. Amends [[R1313000]], [[R1333000]], [[R1655000]] and [[R1834001]].

**[[R2655001]] The cosmetics registry, not the portrait roster, is the list of full-art cards, and fifteen more cards join it (issue #2655, 2026-11-01)**: [[R1017001]] made the hero portraits' cards the full-art cards. A card now prints full art when the registry has a `fullArt` entry for it, and fifteen iconic cards of every type join the six, each with three full arts. Amends [[R1017001]] and [[R1313000]].

**[[R2655002]] A listed token prints its parent card's full art, at the tier its parent's copy would wear (issue #2655, 2026-11-01)**: the tokens of five of the new full-art cards print their parent's full art, behind the parent's pick, ownership, setting and lesson gates, through a data map of parent to tokens rather than the generator, which an opponent's copy never carries. Amends [[R1956001]].

**[[R2655003]] The animated and signed full arts move by transform and opacity alone, hold still under reduced motion, and sign the card's English name in a self-hosted Dancing Script (issue #2655, 2026-11-01)**: the issue left the motion's gate, the signature's face and its language to the builder.
