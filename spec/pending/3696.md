**[[R3696000]] The search keeps one place among the lines it replies to for the best line with a payoff still to come (issue #3696, 2026-11-12)**: the issue found that only the best six lines of the beam, ranked by a static score that prices almost none of the seat's own delayed effects, were scored after the opponent's reply, so a line whose value was a countdown (Supply Drop) or a next-turn reward (Mind Games) ranked on its cost alone and was cut before the AI weighed it, and the sweep of 2026-10-28 saw both cards cast on a few turns in a hundred they were affordable. The spec made no rule on it, so the builder followed the issue's plan: when none of the lines `shortlist_lines` took hands the turn over with an entry of the seat's own waiting that the root did not hold, the best line that does is added last, past the count and the per-action cap, which is at most one more reply a decision. Rejected: the per-action cap for the extra line, putting it first, a larger reserve, and pricing delayed entries statically.

**[[R3696001]] The opponent's reply judges its plays after its turn ends while it holds a card that end would discard, and guesses a secret of the AI's without reading it (issue #3696, 2026-11-12)**: the issue found that once Mind Games reached the reply, the imagined opponent never played the Fortify Mind it was handed, since the reply compared each play with the position as it stood, where a winning guess changes nothing the evaluation reads, so the card was kept and discarded into all three penalties and the AI would overrate Mind Games. The spec made no rule on it, so the builder followed the issue's plan: while the opponent holds a card the seat watched arrive that its turn's cleanup discards, `known_play` judges each play, and standing still, after its turn ends, and a card guessing an unrevealed secret of the seat's is tried with one guess, drawn on its own stream (`AI_GUESS_SEED`) before anything is weighed, so it never depends on the secret. Rejected: weighing all three guesses, averaging them, drawing the guess from the decision's stream, and judging every reply step after the turn's end.
