**[[R2202000]] and [[R2202001]] are the Training Grounds set's look and sound (issue #2202, part 9 of the tracker #2193, 2026-10-28)**: as Stinkyscape's set had its own art ([[R2170000]]) and its mark and intros ([[R1883000]]) before its release, the held Training Grounds set ([[R2194000]]) draws its own art, a training yard under one subject per family, its snapshots #41–#60 keeping their Core cards' pictures ([[R2202000]]), and wears a mark of its own, a straw target over crossed wooden swords, with intros for its five Legendaries, Mythic and token printed Legendary ([[R2202001]]). Presentation only; they take the first two numbers of issue #2202's block.
