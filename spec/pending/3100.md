**[[R3100000]] The coach names its attacks by the engine's forecast of them, which reads only the board (issue #3100, 2026-11-05)**: `apps/web/src/tutorial/advice.ts` did combat maths of its own (an attack less Armor, nothing through a Divine Shield), which was a rule in the client and which a Roblox port (#2898) would have had to translate. The owner's aim for the tracker (#2956) is that a second client rewrites as little as possible, so the engine answers it: `combat_forecast` (`crates/engine/src/query.rs`) projects, for each attack a seat may declare, the damage it deals and takes, whether it kills, whether the attacker survives and whether it breaks a Divine Shield, and whether the attackers that reach the enemy hero finish it, all from the board's stats through the damage pipeline's own readers. The practice worker asks it of a lesson's game and the coach only picks among the numbers.
