**[[R3526000]] A patch's games for the statistics are its own and its micros', and the newest patch keeps its tier (issue #3526, 2026-11-09; amends [[R1973001]])**: `/stats` named its newest patch v0.5.10 while the game was on v0.5.13e, and offered no tab for v0.5.11 or v0.5.12. Each had shipped in one commit with the micro after it, so no build ran it and no game was filed under it, and [[R1973001]] read both as micros. A patch now counts the games of its run, it and the micros after it up to the next patch or major, and the newest patch keeps its tier as the newest version did. Shipping each patch alone, filing records under the newest patch and counting only the first micro were rejected. It takes R3526000, the first number of issue #3526's block, amends [[R1973001]] and edits [[§9.11]].
