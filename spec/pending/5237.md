**[[R5237000]] "For the rest of the game, whenever you summon a Unit": the summon watcher (issue #5237, 2026-11-28)**: the Felinors vs Humans set (`docs/felinors-vs-humans-set.md`, FV2 and FV3 E2, wave 1 of tracker #5220) needs the engine to keep lasting effects, watchers, grants and auras that no shipped card had asked for, and part E2 builds them. It takes R5237000 to R5237013, the first numbers in issue #5237's block: the rest-of-game summon watcher (FvH #4), grants that last for a turn or two (#7, #8, [[R5237001]]), the Float last turn (#16.2, [[R5237002]]), a raised mana cap (#18, [[R5237003]]), a Unit that stays summoning-sick (#37, [[R5237004]]), the hand place a played card left from (#60.1, #60.2, [[R5237005]]), a granted End of turn (#79, [[R5237006]]), a stat lend to a player's tagged Units (#73, [[R5237007]]), a keyword lent to every card a player controls (#95, [[R5237008]]), harm that passes a tag by (#88, [[R5237009]]), immunity to a tag (#100.1, #100.2, [[R5237010]]), "log 2 utility" and its Radiant reach (#92, [[R5237011]], [[R5237012]]), and the recursion log kept for the Plagiarized reprints ([[R5237013]]). It edits [[§6.3]] (the Grant an ability row, and new rows Grant for turns, Units have stats, Cards have keyword, Summon watch, Immune to a tag, Harm passes a tag by, Log 2 utility, Raise the mana cap and Stay summoning-sick), [[§10.1]] (`manaCapBonus`, `lastFloat`, `recursion`), [[§10.3]] (`cardPlayed.fromHand` and the summon watch) and [[§10.4]] (the grants for N turns, `unitsStats`, `cardsHaveKeyword` and "log 2 utility" in the layers). The Float last turn and the hand place are recorded on every game's turns and plays, so, as the coordinator of the build decided, both are kept only in a game holding a Felinors vs Humans card (`recursion_log::fvh_kept`, `FVH_KEPT_SET`), and no other game's hash moves.
