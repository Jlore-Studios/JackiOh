**[[R2499000]] counts reprinted cards apart from the cards an engine change reaches (issue #2499, 2026-10-30)**: [[R2455000]]'s single bar of 50 families made Meditative balance changes IV, which reprints 19 families, a micro. A change is now a patch when `PATCH_MIN_REPRINTS` (15) families are reprinted (their catalog entry changed and they play differently), when `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` (50) play differently by any route, or when a rule every game meets changed. It takes R2499000, the first number of issue #2499's block, and amends [[R2455000]].
