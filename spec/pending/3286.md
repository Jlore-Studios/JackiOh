**[[R3286000]] A boss whose drop is already held offers another relic, a mini-boss's of Gold or better (issue #3286, 2026-11-06)**: a dungeon run's two mini-bosses each pick their drop from the same few top relics when the map is made, so a run can be dealt one relic at both, and a shop or a chest can sell a drop before its boss; a player who held it beat the boss and was offered nothing, though [[§15.7]] says a held drop is replaced. [[§15.7]] gave the replacement no tier, so this decides it: a held drop rolls like a node with no drop, at `RUN_ARTIFACT_PERCENT` of its kind, from the artifacts not held, and a mini-boss's replacement is of `RUN_MINI_BOSS_DROP_TIER` or better, as its dealt drop is; none left means no artifact, as before. It draws from the random stream only for a held drop, so every other run folds as before. Making the two mini-bosses' drops distinct when the map is dealt (it moves every seed's map, the daily run's included, and misses a shop's sale), no floor (a Bronze relic from a mini-boss) and the held relic's own tier as the floor were rejected. It takes R3286000, the first number of issue #3286's block, and edits [[§15.4]] and [[§15.7]].
