**[[R2618000]] sends a card that dies to the graveyard of the player who controlled it, and a played card to its player's (issue #2618, 2026-10-31)**: issue #2606 asked for a badge to warn that a stolen card on your side went back to the other player's graveyard if it died ([[R12]]), and the designer rejected the badge and changed the rule. A card that leaves the field for a graveyard goes to its controller's, and a card that leaves the resolving zone for one goes to the graveyard of the player who played or cast it; it lands as that player's own card, as a Bounce makes a card its controller's for the hand ([[R747]]), so a replacement of the landing ([[R460]]) sends it to that player's exile pile or library. An Exile or a shuffle from the field still goes to the owner's pile. It takes R2618000, the first number of issue #2618's block, amends [[R12]], [[R172]], [[R360]], [[R453]], [[R747]], [[R803]], [[R1282]], [[R1852002]] (a stolen Starship Piece now builds the thief's ship) and [[R1859012]], and edits [[§3.2]], [[§4.5]] and [[§6.3]].
