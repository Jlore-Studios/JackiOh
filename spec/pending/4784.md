**[[R4784000]] A random deck that leans on no set keeps its theme's floor and its units' together (issue #4784, 2026-11-25)**: an audit of the AI crate found that a deck leaning on no set lost the units' floor whenever its theme held few or no Units, since the units' narrowing never counted the theme cards still owed: about 3% of the AI's own decks, some of the quality gates' among them. A pick that would leave both floors out of reach is now drawn again from the same roll, so only the decks that missed the floor are dealt again (16 in the `gate:v3` full run) and no rng cursor moves. It amends [[R1370]]'s last paragraph and edits [[§9.9]].
