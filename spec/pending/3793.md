**[[R3793000]] The AI's face threat and stand-downs leave out a unit its own Brittle tick destroys before it can attack (issue #3793, 2026-11-13; amends [[R2021000]] and [[R2976000]])**: the issue found that `face_threat` counted a unit whose Brittle count ([[R385]]) destroys it at the start of its controller's next turn, before that turn's attacks: a grown Corpse Eater (#89, Rush, Brittle 1) at 8 attack read as lethal and fed the walls and the stand-downs. The spec made no rule for the AI's reading, so the builder followed the issue's plan. A unit now counts no attack when its count in force is at most one tick, that tick is due at its controller's next start (`brittle_due_by`, the engine's own test) and it is not Indestructible ([[R46]]); the next start is the next turn for the player not on turn, and for the player on turn the turn after the other player's, or the next one when it owes an extra turn ([[R845]], [[R846]]). A side the evaluation reads as swinging first on its own turn keeps its units. It applies to both sides, through `face_threat` (threat, pressure, `wall_switches`, the learned features) and `could_attack_next_turn` (`stands_down`); `unit_worth` and `can_swing` are unchanged. The flag `brittle_crumbles_before_swing` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`. Not modelled: an extra turn owed to the other player, a unit that comes back after it crumbles, a card a Stack uncovers, a defender's Taunt crumbling first. Rejected: the test inside `can_swing` (it would discount `unit_worth` twice), a `stands_down` reading the flag ignores, and a weights parameter on `stands_down` and `wall_switches`. It takes R3793000, the first number of issue #3793's block.
