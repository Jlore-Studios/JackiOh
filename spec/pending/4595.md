**[[R4595000]] A game's setup may name, per seat, the sets its deck is dealt from; they are public, and determinization resamples that seat's unseen cards from them (issue #4595, 2026-11-24)**: `createGame` takes `deckSets`, per seat, the sets its deck is dealt from; every card of that deck must be of one of them. The state keeps them (`PlayerState.deckSets`), the view shows them on both seats, a replay and a Glitch reset keep them, and a seat that names none stores nothing, so every other game hashes as before. The AI's and the lethal hint's determinization resamples a naming seat's hand, library and face-down cards from those sets' non-token cards alone, its Traps and Cloaked cards among them, still by the cost shown and what it has not shown; a seat that names none samples as [[R185]] says. `assault_fight_setup` names `Infinite Assault` for the enemy when its deck is that set's cards (a day of the shipped stand-ins names none), so the lethal hint against an Assault deck samples Infinite Assault cards.

**[[R4595001]] A decision whose beam spent nodes and closed no line takes the candidate whose one unanswered step evaluates best, a search of one ply, not a fallback (issue #4595, 2026-11-25)**: when a chain of expensive casts drains the beam's slice before its first child stands, each candidate is stepped once on the searched determinization, its prompts left open and no node charged, and the best `evaluate_priced` position is taken (the first on a tie), with reason `search`; a candidate the engine refuses is skipped, all refused falls back, and a beam given no node at all still falls back (B15).
