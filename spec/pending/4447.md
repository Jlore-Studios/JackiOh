**[[R4447000]] A finalist replayed on another sampled world counts the steps that world does not offer, and past one can finish its turn greedily (issue #4447, 2026-11-22)**: the issue found that `decide`, which runs its beam on the first sampled world and replays each finalist's line on the others ([[§9.9]]), ended a replay's turn at the first step its world does not offer, the seat's own prompt aside (issue #1636), and that a line's own draw or random roll is what most often makes the step go missing: each world shuffles the seat's own library and rolls on its own seed, so a line that draws and then plays what it drew was scored on every other world as the draw and nothing after it, its mana unspent. `SearchStats.replaysCut` now counts those cut replays over the worlds every finalist was scored on, and `cargo jackioh think` prints it. With `AI_SEARCH.replay_fill_steps` above 0, a cut replay plays that world's turn on greedily, each step the best by `static_score` of the first `branching` candidates that are not endTurn, taken only when it beats standing still and never leaving the seat's own prompt open, on a slice of `steps × (branching + 1)` nodes that `decide` keeps back per world; the switch ships at 0, its fork screen ([[R4397000]]) recorded in its comment, so every decision is node for node what it was. Rejected: playing the line's later steps past the missing one, one library shuffle shared by every world, the beam on every world, endTurn as a candidate of each fill step, answering an own prompt the fill opens, and the beam's extra stand-downs in the fill.
