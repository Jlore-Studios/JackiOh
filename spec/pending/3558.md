# #3558: Stinkyscape+ E8 — the hand, deck and fuse verbs

Date: 2026-11-11. Nine rulings, none shipping on a card yet: the verbs are proved on fixture
scripts in `crates/engine/tests/rules/hand_deck_fuse.rs`, and the cards that use them come in
S15, S19, S20 and S22.

- **R3558000** (hand positions, §3, §6.3): index 0 is the leftmost card; an added card takes the
  right end unless its effect names the left end, and no player reorders the hand.
- **R3558001** (hand aura, §4.5, §6.2): a hand card lends its neighbours Radiance and cost while it
  sits beside them; a card that leaves the hand, or is made Radiant on its own, keeps its Radiance.
- **R3558002** (when they meet, §4.5, §6.2, §6.3, §10.8): the two cards become Radiant together the
  moment they sit side by side, at the meeting's price when it names one; the waiting pair wears
  the `meet` mark (gold) in its owner's hand view alone.
- **R3558003** (fill once, §6.3): a Smoothie fuses two random Fruits into itself as it arrives, as
  one fusion; an already-fused card is never filled again.
- **R3558004** (fuse until a cost, §6.3): Dingleberry Droat fuses until the uncapped sum of printed
  costs reaches 5 — R77 caps a fused card's cost at 4, so the sum must be uncapped.
- **R3558005** (End of Turn hand fuse, §6.2, §6.3): Fine Fermented Grape fuses a different card whose
  English name holds "Grape" (`nameContains`) into itself; each fuse narrows the next pool.
- **R3558006** (bottom starters, §2.1, §6.2): after the opening shuffle the flagged cards sink below
  every other card, keeping the shuffle's order, in random order among themselves.
- **R3558007** (deck cast, §4.5, §6.2): Animosity casts itself from its deck whenever its condition
  holds at a settled check, its owner answering for it; a fusion drops the hook.
- **R3558008** (cast N times, §6.3): Shadiris's "Cast Shadiris Visions twice" is two separate casts,
  each asking its own targets.
