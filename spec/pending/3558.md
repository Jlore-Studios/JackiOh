**[[R3558000]] to [[R3558008]] are the hand, deck and fuse verbs of Stinkyscape+ E8 (issue #3558, part of #3596, 2026-11-11)**: proved on fixture scripts in `crates/engine/tests/rules/hand_deck_fuse.rs` and used by the set's cards of S15, S19, S20 and S22 since its release. Hand positions run from index 0, the leftmost card: an added card takes the right end unless its effect names the left, and no player reorders the hand ([[R3558000]]). A hand card lends its neighbours Radiance and cost while it sits beside them, and a card that leaves the hand, or is made Radiant on its own, keeps its Radiance ([[R3558001]]). Two cards that meet become Radiant together the moment they sit side by side, at the meeting's price when it names one, and the waiting pair wears the `meet` mark (gold) in its owner's hand view alone ([[R3558002]]). A Smoothie fuses two random Fruits into itself as it arrives, as one fusion, and an already-fused card is never filled again ([[R3558003]]). Dingleberry Droat fuses until the uncapped sum of printed costs reaches 5, since R77 caps a fused card's cost at 4 ([[R3558004]]). Fine Fermented Grape fuses at End of Turn a different card whose English name holds "Grape" (`nameContains`), and each fuse narrows the next pool ([[R3558005]]). After the opening shuffle the bottom-starting cards sink below every other card, keeping the shuffle's order, in random order among themselves ([[R3558006]]). Animosity casts itself from its deck whenever its condition holds at a settled check, its owner answering for it, and a fusion drops the hook ([[R3558007]]). Shadiris's "Cast Shadiris Visions twice" is two separate casts, each asking its own targets ([[R3558008]]). They take the first numbers of issue #3558's block.
