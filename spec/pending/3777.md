**[[R3777000]] Any face-down backrow card is a backrow card, a set Cloaked one included; only a Unit standing in the backrow is not (issue #3777, 2026-11-12)**: a face-down card in a backrow zone is a backrow card to every effect that targets, counts or hits backrow cards, whatever it prints, so #36 Magic Jammed may target it and C #51 Back Breaker and C+ #5 Guy Att destroy it. A card set by its Cloaked ([[R783004]]) is one of them and no Unit, whether it prints a Unit, a Spell or a Field Spell: destroyed unrevealed it reaches the graveyard as its printed card ([[R783008]]), stolen it stays set. A Unit standing in the backrow ([[R1081000]]) and a Cloaked Unit revealed into a unit zone ([[R783006]]) are no backrow card. The engine already read a set card as its Trap; the bug was the client's ([[R3777001]]). Rejected: passing a set Cloaked Unit by as a Unit, which would tell the other player what it is ([[R33]]).

**[[R3777001]] A face-down card one of the viewer's listed actions targets is named in their view with the zone its back lies in, `hiddenTargets` (issue #3777, 2026-11-12; amends [[R821000]])**: `legalActions` offers an enemy face-down backrow card as a declared target by its id alone ([[R177]]), and the view draws it as a back with no id, so the client lit nothing for it and a click on its zone matched no play. The `PlayerView` carries `hiddenTargets`, each such card's `instanceId` (the one `legalActions` already sends, taken fresh as it was set, [[R227]]) and its public `zone`, and nothing else of it; absent when there is none, and empty while a prompt is open. The client lights that zone while a play is built, takes a click or a drop on it as the pick of that id, and lists it as "Face-down card". I6 allows the id there only while the listing offers it ([[R97]]). Rejected: an `instanceId` on every face-down back; a `zone` selection for a backrow target, which would change the protocol and old logs.
