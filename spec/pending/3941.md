**[[R3941000]] The printed-text conventions every English face is held to (issue #3941, 2026-11-15)**: a term prints as its glossary label past its first letter, so it bolds and shows its rule ([[R910000]]); a line opens with a capital; a lasting turn effect is "For the rest of the game." over a Start of turn line; a cost set on an added card is a sentence of its own outside a Call to Chaos list ([[R436]]); a hand or deck trigger opens "While this is in your …"; a stat gained is "+N Attack" or "+N Health". [[§5.4]] holds them, and `card_text.rs` sweeps the catalog for them.
