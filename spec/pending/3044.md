**[[R3044000]] makes Twinspell an Activate whose grant is its player's for the turn, and amends [[R30]] and [[R209]] (issue #3044, part 7 of #3000, 2026-11-05)**: Twinspell was a lasting grant, an `echoNextSpell` rider its permanent installed standing on the field, which waited past cleanup and buried its Twinspell when a Spell took it. It is now "Activate: The next Spell you play this turn gains Echo +1. Then this goes to the graveyard.", and its Radiant face the same without the graveyard, so it serves once each turn; `echoGain` is 1 on both faces (Radiant 2 before). The Activate is once a turn ([[R384]]) and puts a rider with no source on its controller, ending at this turn's cleanup. The issue left the verb of "goes to the graveyard" to be ruled: no verb moved a card from the field to the graveyard without a death, so the engine gains one, `send_to_graveyard`, a plain move that is no death and that Indestructible does not hold. It takes R3044000, the first number of issue #3044's block. The engine keeps the lasting grant for the replays of v0.5.11p and before, which fold under the kept `script_v0_5_11o` that `KEPT_SCRIPTS` names in the reconcile ([[R767000]]); [[R30]] is retitled as that grant and [[R209]] names Twinspell as its example through v0.5.11p. It edits §8.3's row 79.
