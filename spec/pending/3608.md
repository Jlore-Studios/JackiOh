**[[R3608000]] No infinite animation sets a paint property the guard lists (issue #3608, 2026-11-11)**: part of #3604. No looping animation in the web client sets `box-shadow`, `filter`, `backdrop-filter`, `background-position`, `stroke-dashoffset`, `clip-path` or `text-shadow` any more; the loops that did move only `transform` and `opacity`, so the compositor runs them and nothing repaints per frame: the hand's glow, the face-down breath and veil, a Mythic's hand shimmer, End turn's, the match clock's, the lane picker's, the Duos portal's and the coach's pulses, the skeleton's sheen and the two dashed arrows each draw their glow, tint or sheen once on a pseudo-element, or as four stepped copies of the path, and fade or slide that layer. A test reads every stylesheet and fails on a loop over `box-shadow`, `filter`, `backdrop-filter`, `background-position`, `stroke-dashoffset`, `clip-path` or `text-shadow` outside an empty allow-list. Rest states, reduced motion and `--anim-scale` are unchanged.
