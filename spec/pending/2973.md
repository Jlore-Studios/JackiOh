**[[R2973000]] Leaving a live co-op game asks first, and says the teammate plays on and the AI takes the seat (issue #2973, 2026-11-04)**: amends [[R2479000]] for co-op games. While a co-op board shows a view with no result on a socket neither closed nor refused, every way off it is held by the page's leave guard ([[R1754000]]) and asks "Leave the match?" plus the co-op line, naming the teammate playing on and the AI taking the seat after `DISCONNECT_GRACE_SECONDS` ([[§9.5]], [[§13.8]]). Stay (focus, Escape, Back per [[R2127000]], click outside) sends nothing; Leave makes the held move; a result, a closed-for-good socket or a refused one releases the guard and closes an open question; a forced `navigate` is never held.
