**[[R4353000]] Every Indestructible Unit card is Immutable innately (issue #4353, 2026-11-21):** Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V: "Indestructible -> innately have immutable and can't defend and can't have taunt." Can't Defend ([[R2179000]]) and no Taunt ([[R347]]) were so already; Immutable is new, had as Flying has Flexible ([[R3543000]]), never added to the keywords (`layers::is_immutable`, `layers::immutable_by`), and only on a Unit card: an Indestructible Field Spell (C+ #33 Ivory Tower, Core #98 Heroic Power) keeps the fusing and tuning its own text works by. Whatever Immutable stops ([[R23]], [[R386]]) stops on an Indestructible Unit card, so no Nerf takes its Indestructible away, and a Unit Indestructible for a while is Immutable for as long.

**[[R4353001]] Immutable bars no targeting (issue #4353, 2026-11-21):** "Immutable -> spells can target this unit." Its targeting half ([[R781001]]) and the Field Trap reading ([[R781002]]) are gone: Spells, Field Spells and Field Traps may aim at an Immutable card in play, and its text half still leaves it as it is while damage and the rest land. Classic #5 Tesla damages it and C+ #74 Twice Forward, One Step Backwards fuses in a copy of it.

**[[R4353002]] Evasive: Spells and Field Spells can't target it (issue #4353, 2026-11-21):** "Evasive -> no longer can be targeted by spells or field spells." Hearthstone's Elusive beside Evasive's attack half ([[R3543002]]): no declaration or `target` prompt of a Spell or a Field Spell offers an Evasive card face-up on the field, its controller's own included (`restrictions::evasive_bars`), the source read as [[R781001]] read it; Units, Traps, Field Traps, random picks and "all" effects still reach it.

**[[R4353003]] Supernova's Alnilam still takes the board in (issue #4353, 2026-11-21):** Alnilam is an Indestructible Unit and so Immutable since [[R4353000]], but T-Supernova's Fuse into the Alnilam it has just summoned is how the card is made, not a change to a card in play: a Fuse whose kept card the same effect made for it (`FuseArgs::fresh`) is not refused by its Immutable. Every other Fuse onto an Immutable card is ([[R23]]).
