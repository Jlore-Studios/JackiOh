**[[R3213000]] While a finished match's socket is open, its rematch status and its series' view come over it, and the screen polls only once it is gone (issue #3213, 2026-11-06)**: tracker #3204 asks for less compute and faster traffic, and after a result the match screen polled `GET /api/matches/:id/rematch` or `GET /api/matches/:id/series` every `SERIES_POLL_SECONDS` ([[R3207002]], [[R3205000]]) over the socket that was still open. The match socket now sends each seat a `rematch` frame (its own offer, the opponent's, whether the opponent is here, the game equal offers made and the rematch's mode) or, in a Conquest game, a `series` frame (its own `SeriesView`), each exactly what that seat's own read answers, so no lean and no sealed pick rides it ([[R336]]), and never to a watcher. Each rides every greeting and goes again when the rematch book, a seat's presence or the series changes; a ladder bot's linger ending is a presence change of its own (`botLeaves`, `presenceChanged`), so the buttons leave a bot's death screen as they leave a person's ([[R639000]], [[R1452]]), and a series frame older than one already sent is dropped. The client sends neither read while its open socket has pushed a frame, skips the status read after an offer, and polls as before once the socket drops or if it never pushed; the series screen keeps its poll. Frames carry `v`, the server's `POST_GAME_FRAME_VERSION`: a client reads only the version it knows, and an older one ignores both types. Frames sent only after the result, a capability frame, a bot's absence on the reaper's tick, ts-rs in the server crate and the frames inside `ServerMessage` were rejected. It takes R3213000, the first number of issue #3213's block, amends [[R3207002]] and edits [[§9.5]].
