**[[R4826000]] Galaxia's attack buff is spent on the first Unit played that turn even when it leaves the field during its play (issue #4826, 2026-11-25)**: S+ #74 Galaxia recorded `grantedTurn` only when `played_unit` found the played Unit still standing face-up on the field, so a first Unit dying to its own Cry or leaving during resolution left the offer unrecorded and gave the Attack to the second Unit played that turn; it now records `grantedTurn` on any resolved Unit play of her controller's, and buffs only if the Unit still stands. Edits [[§8.12]]'s row 74 and takes R4826000 from issue #4826's block.
