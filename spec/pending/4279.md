**[[R4279000]] A card cast on its draw, or cast or summoned from its deck or hand by an effect, went into play (pull request #4279, 2026-11-19)**: a game's record gains each seat's `castOrSummoned`, the dealt cards an effect cast or summoned from the seat's own deck or hand rather than its play from the hand (cast on its draw or from the deck, or summoned from the deck or the hand: C+ #26 Tommy Tempo, #21 Hinder, M #87 Tatches the Totem, a Recruit), each time with its turn and face, and the statistics count each as played. Before it a card cast on its draw, which never reaches the hand, and a card summoned from the deck were in neither `drawn` nor `played`, so their games fell among the never-drawn: the drawn delta and the adjusted win rate read the games the card shaped as games it had no part in, a card that always casts on draw almost never had a rate or a tier, and its play rate read close to 0. Now they are seen games, split by face and counted at their turn in the drill-down, and the drawn delta, the adjusted win rate and tier, All Random's own figures and the play rate read them there. No migration: the stored totals already hold `played`, and records filed before keep their counts. Rejected: a breakdown of its own, counting them as drawn but not played, and leaving out the summons from the hand. It takes R4279000 and edits [[§9.11]], [[R377]], [[R2427000]], [[R2777000]] and [[R3950000]].
