**[[R2117000]] cross-fades a move between two menu screens in the browser's view transition (issue #2117, part of #2036, 2026-10-28)**: menus swapped in a single frame. Now a link, a redirect or Back/Forward between two menus runs inside `document.startViewTransition` with the default fade. A board's route never fades, entered or left, and neither does a board on screen (a replay in place among them). A change of query alone does not fade, nor does any move under Reduce motion or without the API, and a move the leave guard holds ([[R1754000]]) waits until it goes. Knowing a board by its route alone was rejected. It takes R2117000, the first number of issue #2117's block, and edits [[§10.10]].
