**[[R2021000]] has a stand-down count only an enemy unit that could attack the unit on its next turn (issue #2021, 2026-10-27)**: [[R1759000]]'s stand-down asked only whether an enemy unit would kill the unit and survive the fight. So an enemy in Defense Position without Deft, a Frozen one, one that cannot attack, or a ground unit facing a Flying unit in Attack Position still made one, and each took one of the beam's two kept slots. An enemy now counts only when it is in Attack Position or has Deft in Defense Position ([[R49]]), may swing (`can_swing`, [[R1174000]]), and no attack restriction bars it from the unit ([[R783002]], [[R1839000]]). No constant or weight moved. It takes R2021000, the first number of issue #2021's block, and amends [[R1759000]].
