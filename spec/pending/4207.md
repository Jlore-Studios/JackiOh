**[[R4207000]] A card that dies is dead wherever a replacement sends it (issue #4207, 2026-11-18)**: supersedes [[R461]]; amends [[R1849002]], [[R398]], [[R1852000]] and [[R2564000]]. A card the state check collects, or a Sacrifice takes, that C #50 Voidwalker's or Stinkyscape #105 Disintegrex's aura exiles instead of sending to its graveyard has still died: its Death fires in [[R68]]'s order off its last-known state ([[R78]]), Reborn returns it from that exile pile at 1 health ([[R64]]), `destroyed` reports it and the destroyed counter counts it ([[R55]]), and a Starship Piece fuses ([[R1852000]]); otherwise it stays in exile. A card C #50 exiles on any other way to a graveyard (a discard, a resolved Spell, a burn, a spent Trap, a mill) still fires nothing, and so does a plain exile ([[R1124]]). It edits [[§4.5]], [[§6.1]], [[§6.2]], [[§6.3]], [[§8.6]], [[§8.8]] and [[§8.10]].
