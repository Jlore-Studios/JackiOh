**[[R2724000]] redraws each Training Grounds Legacy card in the set's yard (issue #2724, 2026-11-01)**: the owner asked that the twenty Legacy cards, #41–#60, which drew exactly their Core cards' pictures, each get one of its own that still reads as the card it freezes. Each draws the yard under its Core card's subject, beside a touch no other Legacy card shares; no other picture moves. Presentation only; it takes the first number of issue #2724's block, amends [[R2202000]] and [[R2511000]] and ships as a micro ([[R2529000]]).
