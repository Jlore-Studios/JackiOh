**[[R2200000]] to [[R2200004]] are Training Grounds #39 Call to Chaos (Training-Grounds Edition) (issue #2200, part 7 of the tracker #2193, 2026-10-28)**: the set's Legendary Call to Chaos, held with its set ([[R1420]]), is built on Core #95's roller with a table of its own. Its "Training Grounds cards" and "permanents" are the set's non-token cards ([[R2200000]]). Its first entry's Taunt and Divine Shield reach the tokens it summons, and its "refresh 4 mana" is §6.3 Refresh ([[R2200001]]). Its "every other turn" is a new rest-of-game effect: one Call to Chaos now, then one at the start of every second turn of the caster's, capped at 3 a player ([[R2200002]]). Its Rush, Trample and Pierce are three player modifiers ([[R2200003]]). It joins "a random Call to Chaos" and The Big Crunch only when its set ships ([[R2200004]]). The designer's "every other turn" and "a Call to Chaos" take the brief's defaults (TG6 row 25).
