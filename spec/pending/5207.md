**[[R5207000]] The statistics' kept snapshot is dated from when its load finished, and every read joins a load under way however long it has run (issue #5207, 2026-11-28)**: staging's deploys and production's of 2026-11-27 failed their statistics check, since a load longer than `CARD_STATS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS` finished into a cell already replaced, and each read began it again. The ruling amends [[R3067000]] and [[R5186000]], edits §9.11 (Public statistics and the publication gate) and takes R5207000.
