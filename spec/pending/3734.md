**[[R3734000]] The Collection is four tabs over the whole window, Cards, Decks, Cosmetics and Progress, and Cards is one searchable grid of the whole catalog (issue #3734, 2026-11-12; amends [[R1579000]])**: the Collection was a hub of three doors inside a 960 px frame, its Cards an accordion with no search, filter or sort, and the wallet, tracks and quests under it. It now fills the window in four tabs by `?section=` (Cards when none or an unknown one), the route holding each tab's filters across a switch. Cards is one virtual grid of every collectible card with the deck builder's search, filters and sort reused (a search always on show, chips for cost, set, type, rarity, keyword and All, Owned or Missing, the sorts Set and Newest added), the filters a sidebar over 1024 px and a sheet under it, an owned count and a bar for each set. Progress is a tab of its own for the wallet, tracks, Fleet track and quests; Decks are tiles with the validator's verdict and modes, each opening its deck at `/decks?deck=<id>`; Cosmetics gets the same search and filters and keeps its previews. Rejected: a Progress strip, the issue's 0 to 7+ cost row (it would change the mana curve and saved filters), three type chips, and a filter bar apart from the deck builder's.
