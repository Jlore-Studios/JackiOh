**[[R3563000]] Random cards are unique within one effect until their pool runs out (issue #3563, 2026-11-10)**: the owner's words of 2026-11-09 made every randomly generated card aim for unique, with repeats only when the pool runs out. The rule draws every random card through the run's tally (`draw_tally` on the `EffectContext`), unique by catalog id until the pool is used up, then evenly from the whole pool again; Grape and Glitch come after the tally, Resources keep their rarity odds over the least-drawn, and a prompt that pauses a run starts its continuation's tally afresh. It ships behind `UNIQUE_RANDOM_DRAWS`, off until the Stinkyscape+ release turns it on (2026-11-10), so no game, golden trace or pinned seed moves; till then [[R60]]'s repeats stand. It takes R3563000, the first number of issue #3563's block, amends [[R60]] and edits [[§6.3]] and [[§10.7]].
