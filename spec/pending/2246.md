**[[R2246000]] A Frozen card can't use its Activate (issue #2246, 2026-10-28)**: the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set. Frozen ([[§6.1]], [[R1174000]]) stopped a Unit's attack and its switch, and an Activate, which no exertion binds ([[R384]]), still fired, so a Unit Frozen forever by Stinkyscape #2 Absolute Zero kept its weapon. Now the Activate refusal reads the status: a Frozen card's abilities are neither listed nor accepted until it thaws, its uses untouched, while another card's effect may still run them ([[R1876001]]). It takes R2246000, the first number of issue #2246's block, and edits [[§6.1]] Frozen and [[§6.2]] Activate.
