**[[R4672000]] The game record keeps each play's variant: its Choose One options and other card-fixed answers, its X and its Embiggen (issue #4672, 2026-11-24)**: each play from the hand (`playedVariants`), cast (`castOrSummoned`) and Created play (`createdUsed`, `createdPlayed`) whose card offered a choice keeps it: the options declared with the play ([[R81]]) and the card-fixed `mode` and `number` prompts its own script asks while it resolves, the X paid ([[R65]]) and whether it was Embiggened. Board choices (targets, zones, tributes, cells, a hand card, a half), an Echo repeat's prompts and random or open-ended prompts are left out. `cardPlayed` carries the declared options as `modes`, which no rule reads, no view forwards and the golden text leaves out.

**[[R4672001]] Each Created card in the game record names the card whose effect made it, none for a rule's (issue #4672, 2026-11-24)**: a `createdUsed` entry's `source` and `createdUnusedSources` name the catalog id and face of the card whose effect minted each Created card, `None` for The Coin and every card a rule mints. The engine keeps them in `GameState.makers`, written as the minting verbs record [[R1956001]]'s generators, left out of the hash, sent to no view and dropped by the AI's redaction.

**[[R4672002]] A card's Discover-like choice is a pick in the game record: what it offered and what was picked (issue #4672, 2026-11-24)**: each `discover` or `pick` prompt a card's script asks is a `picks` entry of the seat it continues, naming the asking card and its face, the catalog ids offered and those picked; an offer of indices or code blocks names no card and is no pick.

**[[R4672003]] The card totals keep each card's variants, outputs and offers, a seat once a game by face, inside its stats (issue #4672, 2026-11-24)**: each card's totals row holds `variants`, `outputs` and `offers` in its `card_totals.stats` JSON, each a seat once a game split by face, folded in the same transaction with no migration; an older total reads as none and an older record adds none. `stats-cards --card` and `stats report --card` print a variants table and an outputs table after their other tables and `--json` writes them as `choices`; no public endpoint or page changes, and nothing is back-filled.
