**[[R2119000]] makes four deletions undoable instead of asked (issue #2119, part of #2036, 2026-10-28)**: deleting a deck, a trio or a custom card, and removing a friend, take the item off the screen at once behind an undo toast for `UNDO_WINDOW_MS` (8 seconds), and the request waits for the window to close; it goes at once when another deletion takes the toast, when the page hides (as a `keepalive` request) or when the player leaves the screen, and in the workshop before anything that needs the item gone. Undo puts the item back and sends nothing; a held deck or trio is never saved meanwhile and no trio shows a held deck, whose slots come back on Undo. A request the server refuses puts the item back in its words; a deck or trio whose request could not reach the server stays deleted and is tried again ([[R256]]). Block and Concede still ask. SPEC §10 decided neither the window nor the held request, and Hearthstone has no undo here, so they follow common practice. It takes R2119000, the first number of issue #2119's block, and edits [[§10.10]].
