**[[R2577000]] A unit on the field draws only the keywords that can still act there or as it leaves (issue #2577, 2026-10-31)**: the issue asked that the board minion stop drawing keywords that do nothing on the field, since their chips and treatments took room from live ones. Equip, Tradable, Cloaked, Crater, Flashback, Stack and Temporary X are marked `onField: false` in `KEYWORD_VISUALS`: no treatment and no chip on the field, their badges hidden in the DOM; every other keyword, Starship Piece included, stays, and the hover preview and the inspect sheet still list them all. Hearthstone draws no board mark for Magnetic or Tradeable either. It amends [[R438]].
