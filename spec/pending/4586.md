**[[R4586000]] Figment: a fresh face-down summon with a random set cost that reveals to nothing (issue #4586, 2026-11-24)**: setting a Figment is a fresh summon, face-down, into the leftmost open backrow zone; with no open zone, nothing happens. Its `startOfGame` clause rolls `costOverride` uniformly from its declared `low` to `high` as it arrives — 1 to 4, 2 to 4 on a Radiant Figment — through the match rng, one draw per arrival. It reveals on a declared enemy attack (a forced attack declares nothing, [[R53]]), does nothing ([[R61]]), then ceases to exist ([[R11]]).

**[[R4586001]] A face-down card is is_face_down: a Cloaked set counts, a Revealed one does not (issue #4586, 2026-11-24)**: "face-down card" is `preview::is_face_down`: a backrow Trap or Field Trap neither flipped face-up nor revealed. A Unit set by its Cloaked counts (it is the Trap it was set as); a Revealed one does not. Shell Game's draw counts its own Figment, which lands before the count ([[R61]]). The Lie Detector sets one Figment per card its destroy marked, as many as fit.

**[[R4586002]] When this is revealed answers the card's own Cloaked reveal firing (issue #4586, 2026-11-24)**: a Cloaked Unit's reveal fires as a trap firing, so "when this is revealed" is a trigger on its own `trapFired`, answered after the reveal resolves; a face-up play never fires it. The target is a resolution prompt, and with no target nothing happens ([[R61]]).

**[[R4586003]] Setting a card face-down arrives face-down in the backrow (issue #4586, 2026-11-24)**: a face-down play and a summon landing face-down both place the card in its controller's backrow still face-down ([[§10.5]] step 4 places every arrival, [[R227]]), emitting the one arrival event — so one trigger on it answers each set exactly once. Face-up arrivals never answer it ([[R61]]).

**[[R4586004]] Paranoia Engine's ward passes the opponent's destroy, destroyAll and reveal over face-down cards (issue #4586, 2026-11-24)**: while the card acts, `destroy`, `destroyAll` and `reveal` run by an effect the opponent controls pass over its controller's face-down cards.
