**[[R2660000]] ladder bots concede only facing an unstoppable lethal (issue #2660, 2026-11-01)**: the owner asked that ladder bots concede only when a lethal shows on the board and they have no way to stop it, and never where there is none. A bot whose temper concedes at all (`BOT_CONCEDE_FIT.share`, still fitted) now concedes at its turn start only when `jackioh_ai::unstoppable_lethal` proves it: on the bot's redaction, its opponent's hand and face-down cards taken out, every line of its turn is played through the engine and each loses, by its own turn or to the lethal solver's line on the opponent's board alone, played again. A cut walk, a failed step, a roll of the game's random numbers, a card moved in a library the bot cannot see into, a prompt the bot would answer in the opponent's turn and a panic all play on; a turn that ends with the Marketplace open ends without it. The deficit threshold and `min_turn` are no longer read. Summing the board's attack, counting the opponent's hand, keeping the guessed face-down cards, trusting the worlds' rolls and draws as samples, and having every bot concede whatever its temper were rejected. It takes R2660000, the first number of issue #2660's block, and edits [[§9.12]], [[R639000]], [[R1452]] and [[R188]].

**[[R2660001]] ladder bots may offer a draw (issue #2660, 2026-11-01)**: the owner allowed that a bot may offer a draw when it thinks it is losing, which [[R639000]] had forbidden. At the start of its own turn, when it does not concede and [[R36]] lets it offer, a bot that trails its opponent by at least `BOT_DRAW_OFFER.min_behind` (10, on the standing's even-centered scale) offers with the chance `BOT_DRAW_OFFER.chance_per_turn` (0.05) on its turn's own stream, both hand-set since `timing-fit` fits no offer rate; the person answers it as a person's ([[R269]]), and [[R188]] still holds for `decide` and the practice AI. Fitting the rate now and offering later in the turn were rejected. It takes R2660001, the next number of issue #2660's block, and edits [[§9.12]], [[R639000]], [[R1452]] and [[R188]].
