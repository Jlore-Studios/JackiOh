**[[R3568000]] Galaxia buffs the first Unit played while she stands (issue #3568, 2026-11-11)**: S+ #74's "Aura: The first Unit you play each turn gets +3 Attack" is a standing answer to its controller's plays, not a layer: while Galaxia stands, the first Unit her controller plays each turn gets a permanent +3 (Radiant +6) once its play resolves, its Cry included. "First" is the first Unit play she hears, so the first Unit played after her on the turn she arrives counts as that turn's first (the designer's note), whether she was played or summoned; she never answers her own play ([[R119]]). A face-down card is no Unit, a cast on draw no play. Rejected: a layer-5 aura on the turn's first Unit, counting the Units played before her arrival, and a count of Units played this turn.

**[[R3568001]] Cosmic Bard draws the topmost Units, the cheapest drawn is the deck's price (issue #3568, 2026-11-11)**: S+ #102's "Combo: Draw 2 Units" takes the two topmost Units of the deck, top first, with no random number ([[R801]]); the Radiant face sets every drawn Unit still in the hand to the least price they had in the deck ([[R65]], [[R66]]: an X-cost Unit's is 0, no discount applies), a carried cost change taken off the override. A Unit the hand cap burned or a cast on draw took counts toward the least and gets nothing. Rejected: random picks as [[R1061]] draws them, the price read in the hand, and the printed cost.
