**[[R4798000]] The AI reads a Reborn unit's body as the engine makes it: an X/X token keeps its X/X, and a stolen unit comes back to the thief (issue #4798, 2026-11-26)**: an audit of the AI crate (issue #4787) found `reborn_return` building a Reborn unit's body by resetting the unit itself, so a unit token summoned X/X came back at its printed stats and a stolen unit on its first owner's side. The ruling adds the engine's read ahead of its own return, `reborn_body_of`, which the AI's body is with the flag `reborn_body_read` (on in `AI_EVAL`, off in `GREEDY_EVAL`), and the sentence of [[§9.9]]'s Evaluation that names it; it takes R4798000 of the issue's block.
