**[[R4395000]] Shadowstep's base face answers only your Units that die (issue #4395, 2026-11-21):** the trigger keeps a `destroyed` only when its card is a Unit, read by its definition's type on the face it died on, as M #60 Eschews counts a death ([[R172]]'s one firing still covers every such Unit of the pass). A Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap of yours destroyed alone leaves it set, and one destroyed in the same pass as your Units stays in the graveyard while they return at cost 0. A Unit standing in the backrow still counts ([[R1081000]]); an Animated Field Spell or Trap that died standing as a Unit still answers it ([[R383]], [[R4395001]]). The Radiant face is unchanged. The script of v0.6.9g is kept for its replays ([[R767000]]). It takes R4395000, the first number of issue #4395's block, and edits the row of #14 in [[§8.6]].

**[[R4395001]] A `destroyed` event says an Animated card died standing as a Unit (issue #4395, 2026-11-21):** a Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap that dies in a unit zone ([[R383]]) carries `animated: true` on its `destroyed` event, since its definition's type alone does not tell a card that died as a Unit from one destroyed in the backrow. The field is absent on every other death, a Unit's included, is left out of what the trigger loop holds, as `via` is, so no state hash moves (Shadowstep reads it off the action's own events), and, as `radiant` is, goes with a hidden card's identity ([[R97]]). Shadowstep's base face reads it ([[R4395000]]). It takes R4395001 and adds a payload to `destroyed` in [[§10.3]].
