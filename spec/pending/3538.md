**[[R3538000]] The winrate of record and its 50% default (issue #3538, 2026-11-10)**: a card's winrate of record is the newest per-version table row at or before the version its id names, past patches included, among rows with at least `WIN_RATE_RECORD_MIN_GAMES` (20) games, read from the frozen tables the cards crate compiles in; it is the same under a fold, because the tables and snapshots never change ([[R3049000]]). A card with no recorded winrate counts as 50% (the owner, 2026-11-09), and so does KPIs itself when it has none. Rejected: the game's own snapshot, which keeps 8 rows and is not handed to practice, AI or fuzz games.

**[[R3538001]] The tier of record is R3022000's tier read off the record (issue #3538, 2026-11-10)**: S, A, D and F are the card metrics' tiers of record — [[R3022000]]'s tier read off the winrate of record, the exact rate against the band and its quarters and the S/F certainty against the Wilson bounds, all in integers, with no record meaning no tier. "Or A tier if none exist" means an empty pool, "fill your hand" adds until `HAND_LIMIT`, and repeats are allowed until E9. Rejected: thresholds of our own.

**[[R3538002]] Past versions: runs, ids, scripts, minting and the past pool (issue #3538, 2026-11-10)**: each card's carried definitions from `BROWSER_CARRIED_FROM` on group into play-alike runs, each run one past version with id `<card>@<version>`, running the script its game version kept ([[R767000]]) or its card's own, minted into the state's `transient_defs` so the view ships it ([[R243]]). A pool reads them through `past` (`also` or `only`); a `defId` card id names that card and its past ids, a past id only itself, so [[R387]] keeps a card's past versions out of its own pool. Rejected: one past version per shipped version, which would offer the same play many times over.

**[[R3538003]] True Conservative: yours become their strongest past version (issue #3538, 2026-11-10)**: "strongest" is chosen among a card's past versions only — the past Unit definition with the highest attack + health on the face the unit wears, then the highest cost, then the latest; a past unit's Past is its own earlier versions, tokens are included, a card with none is untouched, Immutable refuses ([[R23]]) and no Cry runs ([[R1]]). Rejected: weighing the current version too, which "from their Past" excludes.

**[[R3538004]] Neoconservative draws from the past Jlockeed definitions of the sets that ship (issue #3538, 2026-11-10)**: one uniform draw from `{ tags: ["Jlockeed"], past: "only" }`, Radiant on the Radiant face, hidden from the opponent ([[R440]]). Rejected: weighing recent versions more.
