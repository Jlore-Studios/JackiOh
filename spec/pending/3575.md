**[[R3575603]] A decision answers an expensive step it already simulated from the same state without running the engine again (issue #3575, 2026-11-13)**: Plastanium's sweep timeout came from a Jogg's Box in its deck. A random cast of Zephrys Zealotism ran the perfect-hand scorer for up to 2 s inside one step, and the decision ran that same step up to four times (39 s raw). The root counter now keeps the steps it charged more than a node for (`SimMemo`, at most `AI_SEARCH.memo_steps`) and answers the same step from a state equal but for its nonce record from the memo, its nonce record rebuilt as the engine builds it and its nodes charged as before, so the decision is unchanged and only faster (20 s raw, 1711 ms re-timed). Rejected: loosening `AI_SWEEP.decision_ms`, and skipping random casts in the simulation.
