**[[R3575800]] The AI counts each draw a side's coming turn will burn as a card fewer in that side's hand (issue #3575, 2026-11-13)**: Hard draws two cards a turn ([[R180]]), and a card drawn into a full hand is burned ([[§2.4]], [[R4]]). The evaluation priced every card in hand and never read the hand cap, so the beam never weighed a burn its line would cause. Now `coming_burns` counts the draws the side's coming start of turn takes past its hand cap. Those are DRAWS_PER_TURN plus its handicap's `extraDrawsPerTurn`, read off the state ([[R183]], [[R3582000]]), and no more than its library holds. Each costs `AI_EVAL.burned_draw_share`, 1, of an unseen hand card's price. Only the side whose turn is not running is counted, since the other may still play its hand down. `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. Rejected: a discount per hand card past some size, and a weight tuned to the sweep.
