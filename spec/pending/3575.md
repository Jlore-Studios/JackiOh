**[[R3575400]] The AI prices the Activate of a card in its own hand (issue #3575, 2026-11-13)**: at a decision's root, after [[R2564000]]'s two turns and within `AI_HAND_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES` of its own, each Unit and Field Spell in the seat's hand with an ability is spawned where a play would put it ([[R923001]]), the two turns are played out and its uses alone are priced at the start of the seat's next turn, keyed by the card as the seat holds it and never by what the probe's turns made of it; the enemy's hand is never probed. **[[R3575401]] A standing Unit's backrow lane is a lane variant, not a position (issue #3575, 2026-11-13)**: `search_signature` leaves a Unit standing in the backrow ([[R1081000]]) out of its backrow text, since it is already there among the active units, and says in its place how many of that side's Units stand there and never in which lanes, which is what the rest of the position turns on; `unit_lanes` says that unit's row beside its lane. Rejected: taking the standing Unit out of `unit_lanes` instead, reading the backrow slot without its lane, and saying nothing in its place.
