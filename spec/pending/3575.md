**[[R3575300]] The AI's evaluation prices a Burn or Toxic count as damage on its way (issue #3575, 2026-11-13)**: a count on a Unit or a hero is walked tick by tick as [[R3540001]] deals them, Burn's down by `BURN_DECAY` past Armor, Toxic's lasting, until they have dealt the carrier's life, each tick at `AI_EVAL.affliction_turn_share` (0.5, as `slumber_turn_share`'s turn) to the power of the owner's turns until it lands; a Unit loses `health` a point and, at the tick that kills it, the rest of what its death would take, and a hero loses the ticks off `hero_value`'s curve and, for the enemy, the race. Before it a Fire Beam's or a Scourge Mortar's Activate had no price ([[R2564000]]) and a hit Volcanic or Noxious Rounds converted ([[R3540003]]) read as no hit. Rejected: a flat weight per counter point, which would price Toxic 1 on a 30-health Unit as Toxic 1 on a 1-health one.
