- **[[R3575400]] The AI prices the Activate of a card in its own hand (issue #3575, 2026-11-13)**: at a decision's root, after [[R2564000]]'s two turns and within `AI_HAND_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES` of its own, each Unit and Field Spell in the seat's hand with an ability is spawned where a play would put it ([[R923001]]), the two turns are played out and its uses alone are priced at the start of the seat's next turn, keyed by the card as the seat holds it and never by what the probe's turns made of it; the enemy's hand is never probed.
