**[[R5252000]] Every Scandalous Top Hat a Unit wears is a grant of its own, so it adds one Hat for each as it dies (issue #5252, 2026-11-28)**: slice S02 of the Felinors vs Humans set's stage B (`docs/felinors-vs-humans-set.md`, FV2, tracker #5220) builds FvH #7 Whimsy!, #8 Joy!, #9 Whimsy and Joy!, #21 Scandalous Top Hat, #22 The Mad Hatter, #38 Short Term Loan, #51 Scavengers Ingenuity, #55 Adorable Infestation, #56 Pressure Plate, #61 Devolve, #77 Anaphylaxis and #104 Dark Skies. The Top Hat's §8.14 row makes each Hat a grant of its own, which the engine's one copy of a repeated grant would undo for a Unit wearing two, so the build numbers each Hat's grant. It takes R5252000, the first number in issue #5252's block, and edits [[§8.14]] row 21.
