**[[R2721000]] takes the rarity foil off Legendary and Mythic faces by default and adds a Rarity foil setting that brings the Mythic's back (issue #2721, 2026-11-01)**: with the default settings a Mythic face carries `data-foil="none"`, no rainbow sheen, no rainbow edge and no Mythic portrait ring; its edge and ring take its rarity colour as every rarity's do, and a Legendary face is unchanged, so the two are told apart by the crest at the top (the Mythic's tinted), the rarity gem and the intro music ([[R1350]]). Rarity foil is the viewer's own switch in the card settings store beside Hover previews, off by default and synced by the account's `cards` group as they are ([[R633]]); on, the Mythic wears today's look exactly, Animated foil deciding whether the sheen moves. A Radiant face keeps its foil ([[R277]]) whatever the setting says, and a Mythic wearing no rarity foil wears its card's cosmetic foil, none with it on. It takes R2721000, the first number of issue #2721's block, amends [[R1295000]] and edits [[§10.10]].
