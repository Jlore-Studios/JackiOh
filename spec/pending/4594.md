# Pending: the Commanders, IA #217–#240 (issue #4594)

C10's §11 paragraphs for the held Infinite Assault set's Commanders (IA12.10), 2026-11-24.

**[[R4594000]] Echo-1 answers its own Figment's reveal after combat, once, on a standing attacker (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: A Figment of Echo-1's revealing is an ordinary trigger after combat: this answers its `trapFired` and hits only an attacker still on the field.

**[[R4594001]] Thousand Faces guards face-down cards from destroy, either side, and caps the set draw per turn (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: A destroy from either side is stopped at §4.5 step 1 beside Indestructible, but reveal, Exile, bounce, steal and transform are not; the draw cap is per turn on either player's turn.

**[[R4594002]] Last Lap's lends reach later arrivals and expire at cleanup; a Spell's lend passes Immune to Spells by (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: "This turn" player-level modifiers read through the layers for later arrivals until §2.2's cleanup; a Spell's lend passes Immune to Spells by.

**[[R4594003]] The Hivemaster's aura and Swarm Uplink's buff name the Drone, IA #4.1 (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: The aura reads its controller's IA #4.1 Drones in the unit row or standing; the buff reaches the new Drones, passing Immune to Spells by.

**[[R4594004]] Loopback gains on its first three Reborn returns a game, on the reset body (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: Only a Reborn return gains — a kept Reborn counts — +N/+N with Reborn again on the reset 1-health body, three times per instance.

**[[R4594005]] Rollback Point reads the recursion log's deaths by printed cost (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: One record per death under its controller, tokens in, discards out ([[R4589000]]'s log, kept only in a game holding an Infinite Assault card); stable-sorted by printed cost with the cut tie drawn.

**[[R4594006]] The Radiant Root Kit steals the highest current Attack, breaking ties at random (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: The Radiant Cry steals the reachable enemy Unit with the highest current Attack, ties drawn, a lone highest taken silently.

**[[R4594007]] Bastion.exe caps its controller's hero damage at 0 while it stands (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: A hero guard with cap 0 stops hits, attacks and fatigue; lose-health and Set health land; the guard follows control.

**[[R4594008]] Firewall Rules reads the attacker's Attack through the layers at declaration (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: The cancel lands only at the declared threshold through the layers; forced attacks never fire; it fires every time.

**[[R4594009]] The Harvester and Everything You've Ever Played read the Units the opponent played (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: Every resolved Unit play feeds the recursion log (R4589000); `fill` picks unique per zone, otherwise one pick summoned `copies` times; nothing played draws nothing.

**[[R4594010]] The Black Box exiles any enemy permanent and always gains the Health (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: Any unit- or backrow-zone card, face-down in and Dormant out, exiled at random; +5 Health regardless.

**[[R4594011]] Spaghettification exiles a Unit and its neighbours, and the Radiant face locks their zones (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: Same row, lanes ±1; Immune neighbours passed by; the Radiant face locks the exiled zones first.

**[[R4594013]] Critical Temperature grows per turn seen, once a turn, and resets on leaving (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: First start deals `damage`, later starts add `growth` per turn seen on `{turn, seen}` memory; leaving resets.

**[[R4594014]] Zero-Day steals by current cost in lane order, and randomizes the opponent's Spell targets (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: Steals reachable enemy Units at or under the cap now, destroying overflow; the aura is The Jailbreaker's, drawing the declared targets of the opponent's face-up Spell plays at random (R4590001).

**[[R4594015]] Unpatched counters an opponent's Spell and casts it with free aim (issue #4594, 2026-11-24)**: Counter to the thief's hand, cast with no fence; a full hand burns yet the cast resolves; the Radiant copy keeps the face.
