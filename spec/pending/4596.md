**[[R4596000]] The Infinite Assault set's look and sound, while it is held and after (issue #4596, 2026-11-24)**: presentation only. A face of the set wears a glitch accent over its frame (a red and cyan split name, scanlines over its window). Its eight doctrines and Commander are Almanac chips, never the deck builder's. Its twelve Commanders boot onto the board with entrances of their own, and its eight doctrine Mythics have casts of their own (each on its own play alone, within a Mythic Spell's ceiling). Its Mythics' tiles wear circuitry. A single-player set takes intros as any set does: its 44 Legendaries and Mythics have theirs (the ai family, a digital bell for a glint), and the music budget is 54 MiB. Its voice files are the release's.
