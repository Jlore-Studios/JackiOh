**[[R2615000]] and [[R2615001]] make watching and online play's data part of playing, with no opt-out (issue #2615, 2026-10-31)**: the owner decided that friends may always watch a player's live games, the way out being to remove or block the friend, and that what online play records is always collected, since it is usage data about the game and how the cards are balanced. [[R2615000]] removes the "Let friends watch my games" switch (`allowSpectators`): the server stops reading `profiles.allow_spectators` (the column stays until a later migration, so a server on the old code reads it safely while a deploy overlaps), `GET` and `PUT /api/settings` answer the groups alone, and Settings and Play together lose the switch, Play together's Watching panel saying instead how to stop someone watching. [[R2615001]] changes nothing that is collected and holds it there: game records, play telemetry, the card statistics and the player statistics have no switch, and none is to be added. The Privacy Policy now says both, and lists the public player statistics it had left out, practice games' counts among them ([[R639]], [[R2000000]]). Both ship with [[R2614000]] in one change, so `TERMS_VERSION` moves once. They edit [[§9.5]] and [[§9.11]].
