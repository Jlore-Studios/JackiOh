**[[R2455000]] raises the bar for a patch (issue #2455, 2026-10-30)**: a patch restarts every card's statistics ([[R1367000]]), so it waits for a change broad enough to move them. `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` goes from 10 to 50 families. Divergences that name no card are a rule every game meets, and make a patch, only once they hold `RULE_MIN_GAMES` (a quarter) of `classify`'s 200 unforced games, or when the base build refuses the head's decks ([[R1705004]]). Fewer count as no card, and the change is tiered by the families it plays. Shipped versions keep their names and tiers. It takes R2455000, the first number of issue #2455's block, and amends [[R1365000]], [[R1366000]], [[R1367000]], [[R1369000]] and [[R1370000]].
