# Pending paragraphs for issue #3550 (Stinkyscape+ E6)

- **[[R3550000]] attack an enemy as it is summoned (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: when an enemy Unit
  is summoned — onto the enemy side, or a played one once it resolves — this attacks it at once, a
  forced attack that exerts nothing, ignores Can't attack and opens no window, though the target
  restrictions still hold.
- **[[R3550001]] N attacks a turn, only across (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: "can attack N times per
  turn" gives N attacks, the larger of N and Windfury's two; "can only attack the unit directly
  across from this" attacks only the enemy Unit across, never a hero, declared or forced.
- **[[R3550002]] the Frozen trigger and the freeze rider (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: "when this is
  Frozen" answers every freeze that lands, a re-freeze included; "Freeze any Unit damaged by this"
  Freezes a Unit dealt 1 or more, never a hero.
- **[[R3550003]] Activate cooldowns (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: an Activate usable once, then not
  again for N of its controller's turns — a cooldown of N sits out N−1 of its user's turns, the
  extra-turn cooldown sitting out exactly one, Refresh leaving it — and the view shows the turns
  left.
- **[[R3550004]] a per-turn damage pool (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: the first damage allies would
  take per turn is nullified after Armor, on the controller's Units and hero, never spent on an
  Indestructible Unit, pools acting in side order and refilling at the start of the controller's
  turn, the view previewing what is left.
- **[[R3550005]] counter a Ranged attack or an Activate (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: a declared
  Ranged attack is cancelled before the trap window and an `activate` action does nothing, the
  Activate's use and costs staying spent; one budget a turn shared by both, and a forced attack is
  never countered.
- **[[R3550006]] a kill deadline (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: the target must destroy a Unit before
  the end of its controller's next turn, or it is destroyed — any kill credited to it meeting it at
  once — and the Radiant face adds 3 The Coins to the owner's hand when it fails.
- **[[R3550007]] a side snapshot and its restore (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: a Cry stores the
  controller's side without the running card, and its Death rebuilds that side from the snapshot —
  extras to hand, no Cry, Radiant rebuilt Radiant — reaching no view and no AI.
- **[[R3550008]] a secret pick from the opponent's hand (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: the trap's Cry
  shows its owner the opponent's hand for a secret pick of one card; the trap reveals when the
  opponent plays the chosen card before any other card seen, taking control of it instead, and the
  Radiant face answers the chosen card whenever it is played. The pick is in no view and no event.
- **[[R3550009]] take control of a random enemy permanent (issue #3550, 2026-11-11)**: a random
  enemy permanent comes under the controller's control, a steal of a card drawn by the game's Rng.
