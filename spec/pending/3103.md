**[[R3103000]] Whether a ladder bot passes for a person is a setting of its seat, and a bot's decisions are the match host's machine's (issue #3103, 2026-11-05; amends [[R1452]], [[R1453]] and [[R639000]])**: the owner asked that a Roblox server get ladder bots without porting any bot code, and that what exists only to hide a bot be a switch the server turns on; a bot is a seat of the machine whose moves, pace, aim, emotes, habits and rematches come out as its timers and actions, `hidden` gates what only disguises it (the pace, the aim, the emotes, the concessions and draw answers, the idle turns, the rematch and the fallback time), and a bot that is not hidden plays as the practice AI does.
