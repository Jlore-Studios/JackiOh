**[[R3911000]] A banned account drops off the public Players list, as it already drops off the ranked leaderboard (issue #3911, 2026-11-15)**: `moderate ban` sets `profiles.status` to `banned` ([[§9.4]]). While it is banned, the account is excluded from the public Players tab aggregates and search (`GET /api/stats/players`, [[§9.12]]), as it is from the ranked leaderboard ([[R3290000]]). The player's stored statistics ([[R1142000]], [[R1771000]]) are kept, and lifting the ban restores the account's row to the public list immediately.
