**[[R3022000]] A card's balance tier, and its adjusted win rate read against the field (issue #3022, 2026-11-04)**: the owner asked for one figure on `/stats` to balance every card into a band of 45% to 55%. [[R2777000]]'s adjusted rate read 50% as a drawn delta of 0 rather than as the average card, assumed real cards differ by five points, and shrank a small sample into the band. The cards a page reads now give a field, every deckable card of the report with the sample floor's games on both sides of its drawn delta, from which DerSimonian and Laird's random-effects estimate reads the mean delta and the spread τ of real card lifts (`stats_field`; below 30 such cards, R2777000's centre of 0 and τ of 5 points). The adjusted rate is 50% plus τ²/(τ² + SE²) times the delta less the field's mean, its 95% range [[R2895000]]'s at that τ. Each card with enough games gets a tier: S when its rate is above 55% and its range wholly above 50% (a nerf candidate), F when below 45% and its range wholly below 50% (a buff candidate), and otherwise A, B, C or D by the band's quarters from the top. `GET /api/stats/cards` answers `tier` and the field, and the Cards tab shows a sortable Tier column and a line giving the band and the measured spread. Quantile tiers, a fixed τ, the band's edges as S's and F's test, and a field read from the filtered cards alone were rejected. It takes R3022000, the first number of issue #3022's block, and edits [[§9.11]].
