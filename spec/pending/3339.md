**[[R3339000]] Art-first cards in play: full-art looks and every Legendary and Mythic drop their name, type line, and rules text on the board, showing edge-to-edge art and icon marks, with names shown on hover or long press (issue #3339, 2026-11-07; amends R3046000)**: on the board across Unit rows and the backrow for both seats, cards wearing a full-art-shaped level (Full art, Alt full art rank >= 2, Sketch's full art rank >= 2, Extended art, Out of frame, Immersive, Prismatic, Set showcase, or any animated or signed tier of them) or of Legendary or Mythic rarity render art-first. Art-first faces drop their static name, type line, and rules text, showing edge-to-edge art and vitals (attack, health, armor, and counters); unit keywords and states render as icon marks without words (`keywordMarks.ts`, `KeywordFx.tsx`). On desktop, hover or keyboard focus reveals a slim name plate over the bottom of the art within `--motion-fast`, followed by the full hover preview after its delay; on a coarse pointer (phone), a long press opens inspect. Common to Epic normal units keep a smaller translucent name plate over the art's bottom edge, expanding the art window, with keyword icon marks. Card settings (`cards/settings.ts`) gain "Names on the board" (`boardNames`: `"artFirst"` default, `"always"`, `"never"`). This amends [[R3046000]] regarding the permanent name band below art. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
