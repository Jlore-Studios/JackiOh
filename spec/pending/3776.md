**[[R3776000]] A unit's art wears no mark for the health it is missing (issue #3776, 2026-11-12; amends R3340000)**: the damage cracks of [[R3340000]] (`ART_WEAR_LEVELS`, one more for each quarter of health missing) are gone, since the health number already says a unit is damaged and the cracks covered its art. A Unit's art wears the buffed glow and the frozen frost, and a whole or damaged unit with neither wears nothing (no `.cf-art-wear`, no `data-art-damage`). Brittle's keyword cracks ([[R385]]), the Starship keel's cracks, the freeze FX's `cracks` cue, the canvas `crack` cue ([[R502]]) and the Cracked ice foil stay. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Rejected: the cracks behind a setting, which leaves dead code and a constant; fewer cracks, which still cover the art.
