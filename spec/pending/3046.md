**[[R3046000]] prints full art on the board and amends [[R1017001]] (issue #3046, part 9 of #3000, 2026-11-05)**: the owner's pass asked that a card which prints full art in a hand look full-art on the board too, its name below the art. [[R1017001]] had kept "a compact face and a board minion" to their windows, an oval portrait with the name plate over its rim and a framed window. A Unit's `MinionFace` and a face-up backrow card's compact `CardFace` now ask the full face's own decision (`boardLookOf`: the Full-art cards setting, ownership [[R1313000]], the tiers [[R2655000]], a listed token's parent [[R2655002]], the Full art and Alt full art versions [[R2725000]] [[R2940001]], a lesson's board [[R1108000]]) and, where it says full art, lay the art across the face, still, with the name in a band below it: the Unit's plate at the foot of the panel with the attack and health gems on the art's lower corners, a backrow card's band 24% of the face. The tiers' motion and the augments stay on the full faces ([[R2715000]]). The art covers what stood behind the oval, so Taunt wears a steel ring over the art's edge and the Stack keeps its chip, while the veils fill the art and Divine Shield's bubble glows along its edge. The compact face has a type line no longer when it is full art, as no full-art face does. The issue left open whether a prop should limit it to the board; the compact face is drawn the same wherever it is, so none does. It edits [[R1017001]]'s sentence on the board and the comment on `showsFullArt`.
