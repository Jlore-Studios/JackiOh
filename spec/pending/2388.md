**[[R2388000]] shows a ranked match's rank move on its result (issue #2388, 2026-10-29)**: a ranked result showed nothing of the rank it moved, and the bar kept the old rank. `GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks` now carries each seat's `rankBefore` and `rankAfter` off the rated record ([[R611]]), a ranked series' last game the series' ([[R604]]), visible ranks only ([[R612]]); the client re-reads it when the result arrives, retrying briefly, and shows each seat's move in the result panel and the new rank on the bar. It takes R2388000, the first number of issue #2388's block, and edits [[§9.12]] and [[R612]].
