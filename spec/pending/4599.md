**[[R4599000]] Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition)'s switch to Attack passes an enemy Unit Immune to Spells by (issue #4599, 2026-11-23)**: S #150's fifth entry switches every enemy Unit to Attack Position and Freezes them ([[R1882003]]); its Freeze passed an immune Unit by and its switch did not. Since a Spell passes an immune field Unit by whatever its wording ([[R4453000]]), the switch does too: the immune Unit keeps its position and is not Frozen.

**[[R4599001]] A Unit Immune to Spells that dies to a hit with a killer, or after an affliction's tick, inside a Spell's run dies to that hit, and I21 allows it (issue #4599, 2026-11-23)**: the state check's death runs inside a Spell's run with no marker of its own. A `destroyed` that names a killer had its lethal hit credited ([[R42]]; a destroy effect clears the credit), and that hit is judged where it landed; one that follows a Burn or Toxic tick on the Unit in the same step is the tick's ([[R4508005]]). The fuzz monitor's I21 allows both; a death with no killer and no tick is still a reach.

**[[R4599002]] A card announced face-down in a step runs as a Trap for I21 though it leaves the field as it resolves (issue #4599, 2026-11-23)**: under M #99 Paranoia a set Spell resolves as it is set, as a Trap ([[R3806000]]), and may be gone from the backrow by the step's end. I21 reads a card whose `cardAnnounced` says `faceDown`, and the `cardPlayed` that follows it under its id or as its `formerId`, as a Trap's run.

**[[R4599003]] I21 ends a Spell's run at its own cardResolved, the run an answer continued included (issue #4599, 2026-11-23)**: a run the step began inside (the answer a prompt continues) was ended by nothing, and when the prompt was the Spell's own (a cast on draw's target, asked before its play), the nested play's `cardResolved` handed the walk back that same run, so a later card's hit was named as C #3 Book of Heal's or M #30.1 Love Bomb's. The running card's own `cardResolved` now ends its run; another card's leaves it as it was.

**[[R4599004]] An exile aura's exile reaches a Unit Immune to Spells inside a Spell's run, and I21 allows it (issue #4599, 2026-11-23)**: an exile aura ([[R1850002]]) exiles at the state check, which runs inside a Spell's run with no marker of its own. It is the board's rule, not the Spell's effect, so I21 allows an immune Unit's `exiled` while one stands as the step begins or ends, or once a card with an exile aura has arrived during the step; with none, a Spell's exile is still a reach. The Black Hole's pull at a turn's end is [[R4577008]]'s, and a Spell's exile elsewhere while the hole stands is still a reach.

**[[R4599005]] A Unit Immune to Spells does not take a Spell's lethal hit on itself; the hit stays on its target (issue #4599, 2026-11-23)**: M #92 Unan's lethal guard ([[R1204]]) and the Shield Layer's do not answer a lethal hit whose source is a Spell while they are Immune to Spells ([[R4453000]]): the hit lands on the hero or Unit it was dealt to, and the next guard may still answer it. A Unit's or a Trap's hit is caught as before.

**[[R4599006]] A Spell's hit on a chained Unit passes a member Immune to Spells by: no echo reaches it, and a split is divided among the others (issue #4599, 2026-11-23)**: an echo or a share of a chain ([[R3554007]]) is a hit from the first hit's source, so a Spell's echo skips a member Immune to Spells, and a Spell's split hit is divided among the members it may reach, the target among them. Any other source's hit reaches every member as before.
