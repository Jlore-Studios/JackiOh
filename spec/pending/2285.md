**[[R2285000]] to [[R2285002]] are the two attack timings and the Classic Golem they bring back to its design (issue #2285, 2026-10-28)**: the owner asked, on 2026-10-28, to codify that "When this attacks" resolves before the attack happens, before any damage, and "After this attacks" after it, only if the attacker is still alive. [[R2285000]] makes "When" and "Whenever this attacks" the attacker's `whenAttacks` hook, run after a declared attack's trap window and before a forced one's combat, so Classic #45 Nature Titan draws and heals and Stinkyscape #75 Badger and #76 Honey Badger gain before the damage, where they had resolved after it. [[R2285001]] runs `afterAttack` only for an attacker still on the stay it attacked from, so #32 Prem Panther (reversing balance patch 1's [[R426]] reading, which the owner confirmed), Classic #13 Boots on the Ground and Stinkyscape #91 and #92 do nothing after an attack they died in. [[R2285002]] has Classic+ #73.1 Classic Golem transform the Unit it attacks, before the damage, as the designer's note says, the base Golem into a Radiant Unit and the Radiant Golem into a base-face one, the attack going on against the new Unit, and lets a Golem that destroys it attack again; it replaces [[R424]]. They take R2285000 to R2285002, the first numbers of issue #2285's block.
