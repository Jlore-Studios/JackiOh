**[[R3778000]] "Aura: Has X random combat keywords" follows its number: a Buff, a Nerf or a Radiant flip adds or takes off keywords and never rerolls them (issue #3778, 2026-11-12; refines [[R781004]])**: Keymaster Keenus, Shockwave447, the Drone Interceptor and the Drone Corvette drew their keywords once as they arrived and kept them whatever their number did, so a Buffed Keenus read "Has 6" and had 5. A Buff's Number row, a number set higher or the card made Radiant on the field draws only the rest, as that change resolves, by the arrival's rules ([[R21]]'s pool, none the card holds from any source or has listed, no Taunt beside Indestructible, [[R347]], every one left when fewer remain); a lower number (a Nerf, De-Radiant) takes the newest off first, and the number back returns the same ones. A listed keyword the card also gains from another source still counts, with no replacement drawn, as Hearthstone's sources stand apart. Leaving the field clears the list ([[R78]]); a Fuse's kept card draws only what its number asks beyond any list it holds. Games through v0.6.0b fold under the kept scripts ([[R767000]]).
