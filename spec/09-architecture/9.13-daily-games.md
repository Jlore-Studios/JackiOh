### 9.13 Daily games

A daily game is a 1v1 game played by correspondence, as chess.com's daily chess is: each move has days, not seconds, and a player makes it whenever they come back (issue #3727). It plays by a live game's rules ([[§2]] to [[§10]]), with [[§9.5]]'s clocks, grace and ceiling replaced by one clock per move, and it is unranked. Its clock (`jackioh-shared`'s `host::daily`) and its notifications' decision (`notify`) are pure; its numbers are `crates/shared/src/config.rs`'s, the sweep's the server's.

- **Modes.** Best of 1, one saved deck, checked and frozen as a room's is ([[§9.4]], [[R264]]), or All Random, both decks dealt from the match seed, each seat's leaning on its own "More cards from" pick ([[R258]], [[R1372]], [[R924000]]). There is no Conquest: a series of games at days a move would last months.
- **Time per move.** One of `DAILY_TIME_PER_MOVE_DAYS`: 1, 3, 7 or 14 days, named by whoever starts the game, the challenger or the seeker. Any other is refused.
- **A challenge.** A player challenges any friend, online or not and in a game or not, naming the mode and the time per move; it makes no room and reserves nothing. The friend accepts it, with their own deck or "More cards from" pick, which starts the game at once, or declines it; the challenger may cancel it while it is open, and it lapses `DAILY_CHALLENGE_TTL_DAYS` (7) days after it was sent. A second open daily challenge to the same friend is refused (`already_challenged`), and one to a player who is not a friend, or between two either of whom blocks the other, gets the 404 a challenge to a non-friend gets ([[R1689000]]). It counts toward the live challenges' `CHALLENGES_PER_HOUR` (30) ([[§9.5]]). A challenge or a seek is kept no longer than its lapse: once it has passed, the sweep deletes it, whatever became of it, and the game an accepted challenge or a paired seek started is not touched ([[R4390000]]).
- **The daily queue.** A player seeks a game of a mode and a time per move. The seek pairs at once with the oldest open seek of the same mode and time from another player, or else waits for one up to `DAILY_SEEK_TTL_DAYS` (7) days, and its player may cancel it; a player holds one open seek per mode and time (`already_seeking`). Daily games are unranked, so the queue has no rating window and pairs by age alone. It never seats a ladder bot: [[R1452]]'s fallback is the live queue's, for a person who would otherwise wait seconds. Unlike the live queue ([[§9.4]]), it never pairs two players either of whom blocks the other. A banned account's open seeks pair with no one and its open challenges cannot be accepted, returning on unban before they lapse ([[R4019000]]).
- **Seats.** Either way the two are seated by [[R1989000]]'s coin on the match seed, and Player 1 takes the first turn. Each request's account plays the seat [[R677]]'s swap gives it, and only that seat's `view_for`, legal actions and the forecast of its attacks ([[R3855000]]) reach it ([[§9.1]]).
- **The move.** The game waits on whoever holds the move, and each of their actions is one request:
  - in the mulligan ([[R265]]), each seat still owing its mulligan, each with its own window; the first turn starts once both have answered ([[R3727002]]);
  - while a prompt is open, the player it asks, whoever's turn it is, so a prompt for the other player hands them the move and its answer hands it back ([[R3727001]]);
  - while the active player's draw offer stands, the player it was made to ([[R3727006]]);
  - otherwise the active player, for the whole turn: its plays, attacks, their own prompts and End turn are one move.

  Only the player holding the move is offered or may send any action but concede, which either player may send at any time; anything else from the other player is refused (`not_your_move`) and logs nothing, so they can never make the holder's move stale. An action is sent after the log's last row; of two sent after the same row, whatever their nonces, only one is logged and only its clock stored, and the other is refused (`stale`), or answered as the game stands when the logged one carried its nonce ([[§9.3]]).
- **The clock.** Each time the move passes to a player, they get the whole time per move again from that moment, and time a move did not use is not carried to the next ([[R3727001]]). A hold keeps its deadline while the same seat holds the same move, the mulligan or one player's turn, so the actions of a turn share one deadline. The clock is stored beside the match and never enters `GameState`, so the fold and the replay hash are a live game's ([[§9.3]]).
- **Time runs out.** A move whose deadline passes loses the game for the player who held it ([[R3727000]]). The server logs `disconnectExpired` for them under its own nonce, `srv-daily-<seq>` ([[R270]]), so the result's reason is `disconnect`, which the daily board shows as a loss on time; it never sends [[R79]]'s `timeout`. Of two deadlines past at once the earlier loses, and of two at the same instant the first seat's. An action sent after the deadline is refused (`time_up`).
- **What stays as live.** The rest of a live game holds. Either player may concede at any time, whoever holds the move. The turn cap (`TURN_CAP_PLAYER_TURNS`, 60) ends the game in a draw ([[§2.5]]). A player may offer a draw on their own turn within [[R36]]'s limits; while it stands it holds the move for the other player, who answers it or loses on time, and its offerer may only concede, so it never lapses at the turn's end as [[R269]]'s does ([[R3727006]]). There is no turn clock, prompt clock, mulligan clock, disconnect grace or hard match ceiling (`MATCH_CEILING_MINUTES`): the reaper never draws a daily game. A daily game has no emotes, aim or choice hover, which are a live match socket's messages, no watcher and no live rematch ([[§9.5]]): its result offers Rematch instead, a new daily challenge to the opponent at the game's mode and time per move, with a deck or lean picked again, which the server takes as any other challenge, so only to a friend.
- **KY's Competition.** A question sequence (Meditative+ #20) arms no `KY_COMPETITION_SECONDS` window in a daily game: its questions keep the one hold of their player's move, whose deadline is the sequence's window ([[R3727003]], amending [[R1563006]] there).
- **Catalog version.** A daily game folds under the catalog version it started on for its whole life, however many card patches ship while it runs, and its replay hashes on that version ([[R767000]]). Its read names the version and says when it is older than the server's (`olderCatalog`), which the board tells its players ([[R3727005]]).
- **Many games at once.** An account holds at most `DAILY_MAX_ACTIVE_GAMES` (20) daily games running, open seeks and open challenges it sent, together, and a challenge, a seek or an acceptance past that is refused (`daily_full`), however many arrive at once. A daily game holds no seat of [[R1523000]]'s ledger and sets no in-match flag ([[R3727004]]): its players may queue, join a room, take a live challenge and play co-op while it runs, a friend's status ([[§9.4]]) and the live-game banner ([[R765]]) do not count it, and no live game waits on it.
- **Unranked, and it counts.** A daily game moves no rating ([[R604]]). It pays Jlore and XP, moves quests and counts proficiency as an unranked game, a friend's room game, does ([[§14.8]], [[§14.9]], [[§14.14]]), and leaves its game record under its mode for the card statistics ([[§9.11]]). It leaves no play telemetry ([[R1442]]): a think time of days is no pace for a ladder bot to copy. A daily game a Glitch voids never happened, as a live one ([[R679]]): it writes no result and pays nothing, and the server forgets its match, its log and its daily game, so it leaves both players' lists and holds neither account from deletion.
- **Blocks.** Blocking a player, or ending a friendship, cancels every open daily challenge between the two, either way; a daily game already running between them goes on to its end, as a live match does ([[R3727007]], [[R1351000]]).
- **Deleting an account.** An account playing a running daily game is not deleted ([[§9.4]]): "Delete my account" first ends on time each of its games whose move has run out, then answers 409, deleting nothing, while any still runs, so its player finishes or concedes them first, as in a live match ([[R3727008]], [[R1497000]]). Once a daily game has ended, a deleted account's seat on it empties and the game stays in the other player's list and opens as before, naming that opponent "a deleted account", with no Rematch ([[R4621000]]). Its open challenges and seeks, its notification settings and its push subscriptions go with it.
- **Notifications.** The in-game notification feed ([[§9.4]], [[R4039000]]) is the channel that is always on: a game waiting on the player, its end and every daily challenge and answer reach the inbox, a toast and, if the player turned them on, the browser's notifications, with no key the server must be given. A row counts as seen once its owner opens or answers what it is about, and a dead lobby invite counts nowhere ([[R4233000]]). Email and push are extras, each on only while the server holds its keys, and the notification settings offer the email toggle only while the server sends email at all (`emailAvailable`), saying otherwise that JackiOh tells the player in the bell and on the device ([[R4039001]]). A player is told "your move" by email and by push. The decision is `decide_notify`, pure, in `crates/shared/src/notify.rs`, and the server only sends what it answers:
  - A channel is due when a game began waiting on the account since that channel last sent, or a waiting game's reminder, `DAILY_REMINDER_HOURS` (12) hours before its deadline, came due since then. Each message lists every game waiting on the account, the nearest deadline first, each linking its board; nothing is sent while none waits.
  - An email goes when it is due, the account's email setting is on (the default) and the account has an address, at most one per `DAILY_EMAIL_MIN_GAP_HOURS` (6) hours; one the gap holds back goes at the first look after it, listing every game then waiting, so the gap loses none. An account with no address, as a Roblox sign-up starts ([[R3147000]]), gets none. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058), `List-Unsubscribe` naming the account's own unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, so the mail client's button turns the setting off with no sign-in, and the link opens a page whose button does; opening the link changes nothing, so a mail scanner that follows every link turns nobody's emails off. At most `DAILY_PLAYER_EMAILS_PER_DAY` (50) of these emails go out in a UTC day across every account, counted in the store so a restart starts none afresh: they share the mail provider's daily allowance with the sign-in, password-reset and alert mail, and the cap keeps half of it for those. Past it no email goes for the rest of that day while push still does, and one it held back goes at the first look of the next day, the gap allowing. The Daily screen turns it on and off.
  - A push (Web Push) goes when it is due, with no gap, to each device of the account that turned it on. A subscription its push service answers 404 or 410 is gone, and one it answers 403, as it does every subscription made under VAPID keys the server no longer holds, can no longer be pushed to; both are deleted, at once and not retried, and signing out deletes the device's own first. The server keeps a subscription only when its endpoint is at a browser's push service, an https URL on the default port at one of `PUSH_SERVICE_HOSTS` or a subdomain of one, and a push follows no redirect, so a player can aim the server's requests at nothing else ([[§9.8]]).
  - The server decides after each move, for both players, and at each sweep. The decision after a move runs once the move has been answered and never holds it: a slow or failing mail or push service delays only the notifications queued behind it, and a profile the server did not reach is decided for at the next sweep. A channel whose keys the server was not given is off.
- **Storage.** Nothing of a daily game stays in the server's memory between moves, and it never has a match actor ([[§9.2]]). Each request loads the match and folds `(seed, decks, log)`, from its checkpoint when one passes ([[§9.3]]); an action is reduced, appended to the log and the clock stored beside it in the same request, and every `MATCH_CHECKPOINT_MIN_ACTIONS` (32) rows of its log a checkpoint is stored, so the next request folds only the rows after it. An action names the seq of the log it was made on: one made on an older one is refused as stale, and one whose nonce the log holds is answered as it was ([[R776000]]). The server sweeps every `DAILY_SWEEP_MINUTES` (60), and the site's hourly cron sweeps a sleeping server too, waking it only once a sweep has something due: the time the server gave as it went to sleep, of a deadline, a notification, a lapse or a feed row's keep, has come, or it gave none ([[R4691000]]). A sweep expires the lapsed challenges and seeks, deletes every one past its lapse, applies every loss on time that is due and sends the notifications that are due. Any read of a game past a deadline applies its loss then, so a late sweep changes when a loss lands, never whether. A finished game's log goes with the retention purge `MATCH_ACTION_RETENTION_DAYS` (90) days after it ended ([[§9.5]]), though the Daily screen lists its newest finished games however old, and a finished game with no log left is never folded, since an empty log folds to a new game at the mulligan. Its read answers `expired`: its list row (the turn, both heroes' life, the result and when it ended, from the stored board) with no view, legal actions or forecast, and an action sent to it is refused `over` (`time_up` when it was lost on time) and logs nothing ([[R3934000]]).
- **The client.** The Daily screen (`/daily`) lists the account's games, those waiting on it first with the least time left first, then those waiting on the opponent, the challenges and seeks, and the newest `DAILY_RECENT_FINISHED` (10) finished ones. A board (`/daily/<matchId>`) shows the game's view with its clock in days and hours, in the urgent colour under `DAILY_URGENT_HOURS` (12) hours left, and Next game opens the next game waiting on the player. The board names the opponent on its hero, lists the modifiers in play in its rail, draws an aimed attack's outcomes from the forecast the read carries, and a finished game's result offers Watch the replay beside Rematch ([[R3855000]]). The Play hub's Daily entry and the screen's title count the games waiting on the player. An open board and the Daily screen also read the server again on a slow period while the tab shows, and when it shows again, so a game that ended on the opponent's move, or while the move was the player's, shows as ended without a reload ([[R3840000]]). A finished game whose moves are no longer kept opens as its result, with both heroes' life, when it ended, a line saying so and a way back to the list, and offers no replay ([[R3934000]]). A board that shows a finished game that is not voided tells the page it paid, once, as the match screen does, so the Jlore balance, the quests and the card proficiency the game paid show without a reload ([[R5231000]]).
