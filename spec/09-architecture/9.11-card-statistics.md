### 9.11 Card statistics

Every finished game leaves a record, and card win rates are read off the records, folded as each is written into totals that every figure reads, so balance decisions rest on data: the live games, and the AI's development runs played on a build before its patch ships ([[R376]]–[[R378]], [[R1044000]]). What an online game records, here and in the play telemetry and the player statistics below, has no opt-out ([[R2615001]]).

- **The record.** A game's record holds its match type ([[§9.5]]'s mode), its patch (the newest patch in [[R388]]'s `patches.json` of the build that played it, which is also its catalog version), each seat's pilot (human or AI), which seat took the first turn, each seat's decklist, opening hand, the cards it drew into its hand and the cards it played from it with whether each was Radiant as played ([[R1147000]]), the dealt cards it held Radiant in its hand at some point ([[R5054000]]), all of them dealt cards; apart from them, the Created cards ([[R943]]) the seat made, the controller of the card or effect that minted each, that were used (played, cast, or summoned or set by an effect, by either seat, each instance once at its first use, with its turn and face), those it made that were never used, and those it played from its hand ([[R2089000]]); the dealt cards an effect cast or summoned from the seat's own deck or hand rather than its play from the hand, each time with its turn and face: cast on its draw or from the deck, or summoned from the deck or the hand (M #87 Tatches the Totem, a Recruit) ([[R4279000]]); what was chosen for each of those plays and casts whose card offered a choice, its Choose One options and the `mode` and `number` prompts its own script asked as it resolved that the seat which played it answered, the X paid and whether it was Embiggened (`playedVariants` beside `played`, and a use's `variant`) ([[R4672000]]); the card whose effect made each Created card, with that card's face, none for one a rule made such as The Coin (a use's `source`, and `createdUnusedSources` beside `createdUnused`) ([[R4672001]]); each Discover-like choice its cards asked of it, the cards offered and those picked (`picks`) ([[R4672002]]); what each of those plays rolled, a Call to Chaos's or another pick-N list's entries by their places in its table, a batch of coins' heads and a die by the faces its card checks for, a Recursion's casts' rolls on the play that began the chain (a variant's `rolled`) ([[R4707000]]); and per-seat counts of its fatigue draws and damage, the Traps it set and that fired, its units' position switches and the cards an effect made Radiant ([[R1256000]]), and the result. The engine reads it off `(seed, decks, log)` (`summarizeGame`), so the game state and the log are unchanged. The server files a live match under its match id once the result has committed; the record names no account and never costs a result ([[R376]]); one whose filing failed is filed by the retention loop before the purge takes its log ([[R1596000]]).
- **The totals.** The server folds each record it writes into totals in the same transaction, and only when the write is new: records counted by patch, source, mode and both seats' pilots; each card's breakdowns below, and its games by the turn it was played on and with each card that shared its deck, by patch, source, mode and seat pilot; and each patch's decklists people took into live Best-of-1 games, for the ladder bots. Each card's row also keeps, under the same key and each a seat once a game split by face, its variants (the seats that played it with each), its outputs (the seats whose copy of it made each card) and its offers (the seat-games its Discover-like choices offered and picked each card in), which `stats-cards --card` and `stats report --card` print and no public figure reads yet; a total or record written before them reads and adds none ([[R4672003]]). It also keeps what the card's plays rolled, by table: each outcome's seats, how many times it came up and how many its table expected, over the table's rolls ([[R4707001]]). Every figure below reads the totals, by the same counting a record file is read with, and is byte for byte what the records gave. A record's detail is kept `GAME_RECORD_RAW_RETENTION_DAYS` (7) after it was written, once folded, for `stats-export` and the analysis notebooks; its id, and so its counts, outlive it and the log's retention ([[R1044000]], [[R1044001]]). A record written before the totals whose detail does not read is set aside when it is folded: marked folded with no counts and its detail emptied, with an alert naming it, so it never holds back the records behind it ([[R1715000]]). A development run's file may instead be filed as totals only, `stats-import --totals-only`: its records are folded and no row is written for them, and the file is claimed once by the SHA-256 of its contents, so the same file never counts twice and each game is read off the file ([[R1976000]]). Each record folded also folds its whole game under its records key: who went first and won, how it ended, how many turns it ran and [[R1256000]]'s counts, so the game figures outlive the detail too; a record folded before they were kept counts in the records and not in them, with no back-fill ([[R1994000]]). A practice game records nothing and sends nothing ([[R187]]), so it leaves no record.
- **Sources.** A record is live, a match the server ran, or development, a game of an internal AI run (`cargo jackioh stats`): AI-against-AI All Random games, both decks dealt by [[R258]]'s deal and both seats on this spec's resources at the browser's budget, filed under the patch the run says it tests. A development record enters the database only through `jackioh-server stats-import`, which refuses any other kind, and no figure counts one unless the query asks for development data ([[R378]]). A game against a ladder bot ([[§9.12]]) is a live game: its human side counts like any live game's, and its bot side, filed with the AI pilot, counts only when a query asks for the AI's seats ([[R1451]]). A run with a card forced into one seat's deck (`cargo jackioh stats --force`) files records of the same shape under a series of its own, for `cargo jackioh stats compare` to read beside another build's run of the same seeds, never for `stats-import` ([[R1284000]]). The self-play exports ([[R1006001]]) of the learn lane, retired with the learned evaluation ([[R4159000]]), became development records too until #4702 removed the command that filed them ([[R4702000]]): each game that still reached its hash under the catalog it was played on, finished and clean, filed under `dev:<patch>:selfplay:<seed>`, which no `stats` run may take ([[R1676000]]), since both databases still hold them. Production and staging each have a database, and every development record is imported into both, so the publication gate below pads production's figures as it does staging's ([[R1933001]], which replaced [[R1933000]]).
- **The breakdowns.** A card's figures come from the decks that held it, each deck once per game: in deck, in the opening hand, going first, going second, and played against its baseline, drawn but not played. A card an effect cast or summoned from its deck or hand counts as played, since it went into play: a card cast on its draw, which never reaches the hand, or summoned from the deck is seen and never among the never-drawn ([[R4279000]]). Each is a win rate, wins over games with a draw a game not won, with its number of games beside it. The played delta is the played win rate minus the drawn-but-not-played win rate, in percentage points ([[R377]]). Never drawn, the decks that held the card and never had it in hand, is in deck less played less drawn but not played, for games, wins and draws alike; the drawn delta, the drawn win rate (played and drawn but not played together) minus the never-drawn win rate in percentage points, is the card's delta wherever its figures are shown, in the played delta's place ([[R2427000]]). The played decks are further split by face: decks that played the card Radiant at least once, and decks that played it only on its normal face ([[R1147000]]). The Cards tab of `/stats`, which leads with the adjusted win rate below, shows neither the drawn win rate, the drawn delta nor the face split; the card's stats block and the reports still do ([[R3184000]]). A Created card is no deck's card and has figures of its own, kept apart from the deck's under the same key and read by its catalog id over the seats that made it, each seat once a game: used, made but never used (its baseline), the used split by face at first use, and the Created delta between the first two ([[R2089000]]). A card's adjusted win rate is 50% plus its drawn delta's distance from the field's mean delta kept in the share τ²/(τ² + SE²), SE² the delta's sampling variance at its widest over its seen and never-drawn games and τ the spread of real card lifts: the posterior mean of its lift, so a few games' swing is mostly discounted and many games' mostly kept, an average card reads 50%, and it is the public page's headline figure and order ([[R2777000]]). The field is every deckable card of the report, whatever the page filters, with the sample floor's games on both sides of its drawn delta; its mean delta and τ are DerSimonian and Laird's random-effects estimate over them, τ at least `ADJUSTED_FIELD_SPREAD_FLOOR`, and below `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` such cards the mean is 0 and τ `ADJUSTED_WIN_RATE_PRIOR_SPREAD` (5 points) ([[R3022000]]). A card with enough games has a balance tier: S when its adjusted rate is above `TIER_BAND_HIGH` (55%) and its 95% range wholly above 50% (a nerf candidate), F when below `TIER_BAND_LOW` (45%) and its range wholly below 50% (a buff candidate), and otherwise A, B, C or D by the band's quarters from the top ([[R3022000]]). With its ranges shown, the Cards tab puts under each tier the share of the card's adjusted range inside that tier's band (S from the band's top up, F from its bottom down, A to D its quarters), and sorting by tier orders a tier's cards by it, then by the adjusted rate ([[R3184001]]). A card with fewer never-drawn games than the sample floor, every Quickdraw card among them, is read against the average deck instead: 50% plus the same share of its seen win rate less the average card's in-deck win rate, its noise the seen side's alone; and the Dormant Starship, which no deck holds, is read the same way from its Created games, the seats that made one, so every card has an adjusted win rate and a tier from the floor ([[R3070000]]). Each card's win rate and adjusted win rate come with their 95% ranges, the win rate's Wilson's over the games it counts and the adjusted rate's its posterior's, the rate ± `STATS_INTERVAL_Z` · √(weight · SE²), which the Cards tab shows under them behind a button, off by default ([[R2895000]]). Under All Random, whose decks are dealt, a card's own games, win rate, play rate and sample floor count only the decks that saw it, its drawn games; a deck dealt it and never drawing it is its never-drawn baseline and no game of its ([[R2777002]]). A card's Decisiveness is how far a play of it typically moves its player's chance of winning, either way, shown as its plain value, `±14.2%`, beside the typical card's: each record since it carries one row per turn of each seat, the AI's evaluation of the seat less its opponent's once the turn's start has resolved (a start that asks is read at the first action past it, [[R4785000]]) and the cards acting on each side's field, read through a win probability calibrated on development records ([[R4552000]]); a turn's change runs from its start to the seat's next turn's (the last to the result), and a card's Decisiveness is the root mean square of its play turns' change less what an ordinary turn and its own mean explain plus its play term, shrunk toward the typical card's by its sampling noise as the adjusted win rate is shrunk, while its play term, Board swing and Swing are kept for the reports alone ([[R4552001]]). A Created card an effect put onto the field or cast credits its arrival to the card that made it, and reads the turns it acts; one a hand held is read by its own plays ([[R4552002]]).
- **Play telemetry.** A live match also leaves a record of how it was played, kept by match and seat and naming no account, for the ladder bots to copy a person's pace: each move a person made (a ladder bot's moves are not a person's and make none, [[R1452]]) with its think time (from the push of the view that made it the seat's move), the clock left and the legal choices, each emote relayed before the result and what it followed, and each seat's concede, draw offers, rematch offer and timeouts. The server writes it with the result and never at the result's expense, keeps it `PLAY_TELEMETRY_RETENTION_DAYS` (365) after the match, deletes a seat's rows with its account, and fits from it, the AI's seats left out, the human think times and the rest of the habits a ladder bot copies (concessions, draw answers, timeouts, rematches, emote delays and personas), to paste into config ([[R1442]], [[R639000]]).
- **Player statistics.** The player's own tally ([[R639]]) is kept on the device, not in these records: games finished and how they ended, and for each card how many games it was seen in, how often the player played it, how often the opponent played it where the player could read it, how often a copy of the player's own was destroyed, how often one of the opponent's was (since [[R2648000]], only a death the other seat caused), and ([[R1582000]]) how often the opponent's Allure took a copy of the player's, dealt the lethal blow of a game the player won, fired as the player's Trap and stood on the player's side when a game they won ended, and ([[R1743000]]) the damage a copy of the player's dealt and its biggest hit and most overkill, its attacks, the healing it caused, its draws, its Radiant changes and the games the player won and lost with it played, with the win streak, its best and the longest game's turns and plays, and ([[R2649000]]) how each card fared with the game's own mechanics (Temporary, Brittle, Feng Shui, Trades, Created copies, combat and its keywords, freezes, Flashback, mana saved, counters, burns, bounces, Starships). It counts the player's online matches and their practice games against Easy alone, the tier on a person's resources, since Medium and Hard hand the AI more ([[R2777003]]), and not a tutorial lesson or a hotseat game. It is read off the views the client already holds, so it can name only a card the player was shown ([[§9.1]], [[R97]]): a trap the opponent sets face-down is not counted until it is revealed. It sits in the browser's `localStorage`, makes no request and leaves no trace on the server, and no figure above reads it. Its Pocket Pick, the card the player plays far more often than the human seats of live games do, is read off it against the player base's play rates (`GET /api/stats/play-rates`), a request that sends nothing of the tally ([[R1583000]]).
- **Queries.** `jackioh-server stats-cards` reads any combination of match type, patch and pilot (human, AI, or both together, "unified"), over live data, development data or both, for every card or one, and prints the Created cards' table after the deck's, as `cargo jackioh stats report` does, `GET /api/stats/cards` answers it as `created`, each row only its id, name, cost, rarity, set, games, whether they reach the floor, its adjusted win rate and range, its tier and its window ([[R4083000]]), and `/stats` shows it on a Tokens tab of its own beside the Cards tab, under every one of the Cards tab's filters, the keyword among them ([[R2089000]], [[R4279001]], [[R4083000]]), as two sections that share no row: the Tokens, and the Other created cards. Each card has an adjusted win rate and a tier, read against the other Created cards rather than the deck cards: a seat makes one only after playing what makes it, so the Created cards' own field ([[R3022000]]'s estimate over their made lifts) takes out the selection they share, and an average Created card reads 50%. The Coin, Glitch and the Dormant Starship, which a rule makes, are read against the average deck ([[R3070000]]). Those two are the only figures the sections show ([[R4082000]], [[R4083000]]): their columns are Card, Name, Tier, Adjusted and Decisiveness ([[R4552004]]), with Games, the seats that made one, used or not ([[R2370000]]), behind the Columns toggle as in the deck table ([[R4155000]]), and a row opens the card's drill-down as a deck row does. The tab shares the Cards tab's sort, kept in the address, which every header of either section sets ([[R3184002]]), its Table and Grid views and its Show 95% ranges ([[R4083000]]). Tier sorts S first, then by tier fit within a tier ([[R3184001]]), then by Adjusted; each section opens on Adjusted, best first, a row below the floor last; and a Win rate or Play rate sort, which the sections lack, reads as Adjusted. In the Grid a Created card shows its face, its tier chip, its Adjusted and its games, as a deck card does; with the ranges shown, its Adjusted range shows under its Adjusted and its tier fit under its tier. A card no deck holds shows its games, tier and adjusted win rate alone in its Statistics panel ([[R4082000]], [[R4083000]]). The public routes also read Constructed, Best of 1 and Conquest together and no All Random game (`?mode=constructed`), and the gate still counts every mode's live games ([[R2777001]]). `/stats` opens on every mode together, offering Constructed, All Random, Best of 1 and Conquest beside it ([[R2777004]]). Naming a patch reads that patch's games alone, so its pre-release run and its live games compare as two queries of the same patch, one with `--source=dev` and one without; no one query sets the two side by side ([[R377]], [[R378]]). A query that names no pilot counts the human seats when it reads live data alone and every seat when it reads development data, so a ladder bot's side is read only when a query names the AI's seats or every seat, or reads both sources (`--source=all`), which counts every seat of both unless a pilot is named ([[R1451]]).
- **Public statistics and the publication gate.** Public card win-rate data is accessible without an account on `/stats` and inside the card detail view (`CardDetail`). `/stats` has five tabs, each a raised button showing its name alone, in a row that never shrinks out of sight: Cards, Tokens (the Created cards), Sets, Players and Experimental ([[R4279001]], [[R4335000]], [[R4678000]]). The Experimental tab holds figures that span patches, in a Segmented of its views, Major patch running, Historical, Versions and the Hall of Fame; on it the patch bar, the games tile and the Face control are hidden and one line names the view shown ([[R4678000]], [[R4680000]], [[R5040000]]). Its Historical view lists every card the Cards tab's filters list, on its live play version, with that version's figures over every patch since it last changed, its tier, Adjusted, Decisiveness ([[R5036000]]), Win rate, Play rate (behind the Columns toggle, with games) and Since, the version it began at and the patches it reaches, Win rate and Play rate the Cards tab's summed over the span ([[R5037000]]), under the Cards tab's sort, view and ranges, a row opening the card's drill-down on All and the newest patch ([[R4678001]]). Its Major patch running view, the first, is the same over the newest major's line alone: each row from the later of its version's start and the major, read against those rows' own field, so every row starts again at each major ([[R5040000]]). Its Versions view lists every play version of those cards as its own row, its Card as its own catalog printed it and its span in Since, "v0.6.10 – live" marked for the live one, on the same scale and under the same filters, sort, view and ranges, with one Past versions only button; each old version's catalog is loaded only once a row on screen needs it, from the patch history the browser carries or, for a version it lacks, `GET /api/catalog/:version` ([[R4680000]]). All three show each row's Iteration right of Since, as a person names the version: `v1` its card's first play version, a new number where its face changed and a new letter where only its play did (`v1b`, `v2`), never sorted ([[R5035000]]). A version's row opens its own drill-down, `?card=<id>&version=<first>`: its faces from its own catalog, its figures, and the versions before and after it, each with its face and the change between them ([[R4680001]]). The Cards and Tokens tabs' Face control, All, Normal or Radiant, kept in the address, reads every figure they and the Sets tab show but the play rate and Decisiveness on one face: All every seat, Normal the seats that saw the card, played or not, and never on its Radiant face, Radiant those that saw it Radiant (held in the hand, played, cast or summoned so), so a win-more card's plays are not its whole face ([[R5054000]]), each face against a field and a band of its own, and the card drill-down on the same face, with the card's Normal and Radiant adjusted rates and the Radiant gap between them ([[R4673000]], [[R4673001]]). The card drill-down's actions row holds an Extended stats toggle on every card, which swaps its details for the Extended stats panel, kept in the address as `extended`: a Segmented of the sections the card has, Choices (each Choose One option, X paid and Embiggen of the card, `GET /api/stats/cards/:id`'s `variants`, with its share, tier and adjusted win rate, each read against a field of every card's choices on the page's face and patch), Outputs (each card the card made or its Discover-like choices offered, `outputs`, with its share, games, tier and adjusted win rate read against the card's other outputs, a pick's offers and pick rate, a row opening that card's drill-down, and under it each outcome the card rolled, `rolled`, under Rolled effects, Coin flips or Dice, with its expected share beside its share, its games, tier and adjusted win rate read against the card's other outcomes) and Radiant vs normal (the two faces' figures and the gap) ([[R4676000]], [[R4676001]], [[R4679000]], [[R4679001]], [[R4707001]], [[R4707002]]). To prevent misleading statistics on freshly shipped patches, AI development games pad the card statistics until the active patch has logged `PUBLIC_STATS_MIN_LIVE_GAMES` (1000) live games (ranked and unranked combined, tutorial games excluded). Below 1000 live games, public card stats combine AI development runs and live games, which the response labels "AI games + live games (provisional)"; the page says only that its figures are provisional, and shows neither the source nor the gate's progress ([[R661]]). At and above 1000 live games on the patch, the gate flips: public statistics use live games only, and AI development games are ignored with no toggle to bring them back. Each card is read over its own window: from the later of its X's start and the last micro that played it, the gate counting every live game on the window's versions; a short window shows the card's previous window when that one clears, and only when both are short do AI games pad it ([[R1367000]]). Each game record names the build that played it, its commit and the AI generation of a seat the AI played ([[R1367000]]). Public card statistics count the human seats of live games, never a ladder bot's, and every seat of development games; the gate counts every live game on the patch, a game against a bot included ([[R1451]]). None of them is a game against Medium or Hard: a ladder bot and a development seat play on a person's resources, and practice records nothing ([[R2777003]]). A card with fewer than `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` (20) games displays "not enough games" rather than a percentage, and no tier or adjusted win rate ([[R654]], [[R3022000]]). Outside the Cards tab, the card detail view shows a card's games, tier, adjusted win rate, Decisiveness, win rate and play rate and nothing else; a card no deck holds, its games, tier, adjusted win rate and Decisiveness alone ([[R4083000]], [[R4552003]]). A card's play rate, there and on the Cards tab, is how often it was played when seen: its played games over the games it reached its owner's hand ([[R3950000]]) or went into play from the deck ([[R4279000]]). `GET /api/stats/cards` sends no breakdown or delta that no client reads ([[R3185000]]), but a face's figures under `face=` ([[R4673001]]), nor a Created card's used and unused figures ([[R4083000]]), and of [[R4552001]]'s figures only each card's `decisiveness` and each field's typical value ([[R4552003]]). The card aggregates endpoint (`GET /api/stats/cards`) is public and cacheable with `CARD_STATS_MAX_AGE_SECONDS` (300 s, [[R5274000]]). So is the game figures endpoint (`GET /api/stats/games`), over the window every card shares at the patch under the same gate, every game read whole and split by both seats' pilots, which the Cards tab's Game figures panel shows for the picked patch (the first player's share of the games somebody won, unknown when every game was drawn, and no draw figure, [[R2567000]]; the median game's turns; and the share of games that reach fatigue, [[R2446000]]), "not enough games" below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` ([[R1994000]]). The server computes each patch's card figures once for each source and pilot, off the totals, and keeps them that long, so a figure on `GET /api/stats/cards` or the card drill-down (`GET /api/stats/cards/:id`) may be up to `CARD_STATS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS` (six hours, [[R5274000]]) old ([[R978000]], [[R1044000]]). Every window and history is cut from one snapshot of every patch's totals, kept as long and loaded once however many requests miss it, and a patch the server does not ship reads only its own totals and is never kept ([[R3067000]]). The snapshot the cards, the game figures, the drill-down and the win-rate snapshot read loads the card totals of the versions it keeps as filed alone ([[R4965000]]). Only the play versions' table and the Hall of Fame read the older versions' totals, folded, and only once one of them is asked for ([[R5174000]]). The snapshot loads on a task of its own, so a read that gives up leaves the load to finish for the next. `GET /api/stats/loading` answers what that load is doing, which `/stats` draws as a progress bar over its skeleton while its first read waits, and a read that finds no answer in time while a load is under way asks again ([[R5186000]]). Its CPU-bound work runs off the server's async threads, and a window's figures are cut on a task of their own, the load's last step ([[R5225000]]). Its read of the card totals hands the server's async threads back to their other requests as it goes, so the server answers them, the load's progress among them, while it runs ([[R5271000]]). The load itself cuts the window the page's first read, its patch list and every match's win rates ask for, so the next read after it answers without a cut ([[R5271001]]). The bar fills on the page's own clock on a curve that slows and never stops, and never fills before the statistics load ([[R5219000]]). A snapshot is kept from when its load finished, and a load under way is joined however long it runs; once figures are held, a read past their time answers with them at once while one refresh runs beside it, the snapshot past its time let go as its next loads so one is held at a time; only a read with nothing held waits, the first after a boot among them ([[R5245000]]). A dev account's Fetch live on `/stats` (`POST /api/dev/stats-refresh`) lets go of every figure kept and the snapshot, as a boot does, and reloads the page, whose reads wait for figures loaded then ([[R5274000]]). The drill-down reads every shipped patch's history off that snapshot, kept once for each pilot and match type and shared by every card's page ([[R2491000]], [[R3067000]]). Player statistics from [[R639]] are synced to the account (`GET /api/stats/player`, `PUT /api/stats/player`), pruned to the public summary's inputs and bounded by `PLAYER_STATS_BYTES_MAX` ([[R1142000]]), and the server keeps only those inputs, as whole counts and for cards its catalog holds ([[R1771000]]); the public players tab reads aggregates from `GET /api/stats/players` (cached `PLAYER_STATS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS`, 60 s), excluding a player whose row is private ([[R654]]) or whose account is banned ([[R3911000]]); Settings offers no opt-out, so a new row is public and a row made private before stays private ([[R2000000]]). Each of its rows carries the player's `profileId` and `username`, and its search matches usernames by NFKC case fold ([[R1436]]).
- **Patches on `/stats`.** `GET /api/stats/cards` lists every patch the page can show, newest first: each window the cards share, with its versions, its games and its live games. Above the tabs, a row of patch tabs offers the newest patch, the `STATS_PATCHES_OFFERED` (5) newest patches with `STATS_PATCH_MIN_GAMES` (1,000) games or more, and the patch shown. The patch arrows sit at either end of the row and follow the tabs: the left arrow steps to the newer patch ([[R4674000]]) and the right arrow to the older patch, so the page steps by patch, never by micro ([[R1973002]]). How far back a caller reads is the server's: anyone but a dev account ([[§14.10]]), signed in or not, reads the longer of two newest-first runs, every patch of the newest patch's line (its `major.minor`) and the `PUBLIC_STATS_PATCHES_LINE_BEFORE` (2) newest of the line before, or the `PUBLIC_STATS_PATCHES_NEWEST` (5) newest, and a dev account every patch back to v0.1.0. `GET /api/stats/cards?patch=` and `GET /api/stats/games?patch=` answer a patch past the caller's 403 `patch_dev_only`; the list, the patch before and the card drill-down's Win rate by patch stop at the caller's patches; no patch, the current one, stays open to everyone; and the page sends the signed-in token with these reads, a dev account's answer kept by no cache ([[R4677000]]). A search button at the bar's end, Find a patch, opens a dialog of the caller's patches, newest first and grouped by line with their games, filtered by version, micros included; picking one shows it as a tab does, and for anyone but a dev account it ends with a line saying older patches are for dev accounts, as does an address naming a refused patch, which opens on the current patch ([[R4677001]]). While the newest patch has fewer than `STATS_PATCH_MIN_GAMES` games it is collecting. Its tab says so, and its Cards, Tokens and Sets tabs say its data is still being collected, with no figures and no count, offering the patch before ([[R3324000]]). The header names the current patch, the newest version listed ([[R3324001]]). A card's figures name the patch they were collected on, the patch before while a newer one gathers its games: in the card's Statistics panel, and in `/stats`' games tile ([[R3447000]]). The page's on/off controls wear the shared `Segmented` look, pressed in its selected skin ([[R3324003]]). Its header's Show card text, the Almanac's, prints every face on the page as its normal face with its words, no full art, the device keeping the choice for both pages and never in the Full-art cards setting ([[R3838000]]). The card breakdown, which covers the header, holds the button too, below View in Almanac ([[R3939000]]). The Cards tab's Plain text button prints every card the tab lists, and a breakdown's reference cards (token or not), as plain text rather than a face, the device keeping it apart from Show card text ([[R3887000]]). For the statistics, a patch that a later patch or major replaced before any game was played on it or on the micros after it is read as a micro over the cards it changed, so it restarts no other card's figures; a major and the newest patch keep their tier ([[R1973001]], [[R3526000]]).
- **The sets.** `/stats` has a Sets tab beside the cards. For the patch and match type the page shows, it reads every card of every set from `GET /api/stats/cards`, whatever the Cards tab filters, and the previous patch's, and sums them in the client: each set with games, highest pooled win rate first, with its cards (those with `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games and all), its card-games, its pooled win rate (its cards' wins over their card-games) with one standard error, the mean and median of its cards' win rates over those with the sample floor's games, its change since the previous patch, its best and worst card, and how many of its cards are `SET_STATS_OUTLIER_Z` (3) or more standard errors over 50% and under it. Tiles name the set with the highest pooled rate, mean card and median card, and below the table the strongest and weakest cards, `SET_STATS_OUTLIER_Z` or more standard errors from 50%, at most `SET_STATS_CARDS_LISTED` (10) a side, open their details. Decks mix sets ([[R380]]), so a set's rate is how its cards fare in mixed decks ([[R1966000]]).
- **The win rates of record.** The cards that read a card's win rate (the tier pools and KPIs, [[R3538000]], [[R3538001]]) read it off a table frozen with each version, so every game of a version, server, practice or AI, reads the same figures and its fold reads them again. Once per patch, `.github/workflows/winrates-refresh.yml` reads production's windowed figures above and `cargo jackioh winrates --refresh` writes them into a fragment, which `patches ship` freezes as `crates/cards/patches/records/<version>.json`, a micro ([[R3882000]]). M #50 CN Tech and M+ #2 NA Tech read each game's own snapshot instead ([[R3049000]]).
- **A card's play versions.** Each card's play versions are the runs of shipped versions over which it plays the same, read at compile time off `patches.json` (`jackioh_cards::play_versions`): a new one starts where a change line changes a field that plays, any but its wording and `loc`, or the version's own `plays` claim its family, and no major, patch or rule every game meets starts one. Each has an id, `<card>~<first version>`, its versions, its name and whether it is live ([[R4675000]]). Each also has its iteration, counted over its card's whole shipped history, which every row of `GET /api/stats/versions` sends as its label ([[R5035000]]). Its figures are its card's totals summed over the versions it spans, live games once its span clears the gate above and live and development games together below it, read against a field of every play version's; one wholly before the records has none, and the frozen win rates of record fill none. `GET /api/stats/versions` sends them, figures only, cached as the cards' figures are and cut from the same snapshot, and `jackioh-server stats-cards --versions` and `cargo jackioh stats report --versions` print the same table ([[R4675001]]). Each play version also counts the patches its versions reach, the windows every card shares, which the Experimental tab's running views show ([[R4678001]]). `GET /api/stats/versions?scope=major` sends the live play versions alone, each cut to the newest major's line, the last `major` step of the shipped history through the newest, its `first`, `patches` and figures read off the cut span against the cut rows' own field, kept once per scope ([[R5040000]]). Each also sends its Decisiveness, read as a card's over its span against one baseline of every record the table counts, none below the floor ([[R5036000]]).
- **The Hall of Fame.** The most busted cards JackiOh ever shipped, snapshotted as they were, with the version live today beside them: Most broken (the highest Adjusted of all time), Most nerfed and Most buffed (the biggest falls and rises from one version of a card to the next), and Most changed (the most versions, ties by fields changed), read off every play version's figures and ranked by the bound of the range ([[R4681000]]). `GET /api/stats/hall-of-fame?mode=` answers the four lists, public and cached as the versions are, and the Experimental tab shows them on its Hall of Fame view, after Versions, each entry its before/after faces, versions, dates, Adjusted, tiers and balance line, opening the before version's drill-down ([[R4681001]]).
- **The daily run.** `GET /api/stats/ci` (public) lists the last `CI_RUNS_LISTED` (14) runs of the daily super run (`.github/workflows/super.yml`) on `main`, newest first, each with when it started, its commit, whether it passed, failed or is still running, and a finished run's jobs that did not pass; the server reads them from GitHub's Actions API and keeps what it answered `CI_RUNS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS` (120 s), and a read that fails answers `unavailable` ([[R1935000]]). `/stats` no longer shows them: [[R1973000]] took its Daily runs tab off the page, and the route stays.
