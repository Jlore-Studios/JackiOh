### 9.3 Engine requirements (binding on section 10)

- `reduce(state, action, rng)` is pure: no I/O, no clock reads, no framework. Timestamps arrive as action data.
- Seeded RNG only, `Math.random` banned by lint. `(seed, log)` reconstructs any match.
- Mid-action choices are state, not callbacks: a choice made during resolution (Discover, mulligan, chained steps, Echo repeats, triggered effects) sets `state.pending` and returns; the answer is another action. The choices a card declares for its own play (zone, X, embiggen, Tribute, targets, modes) travel in the `play` action instead ([[R81]]). This is what makes prompts identical in live play, replays and tests.
- Every action carries a client nonce, deduped server-side: the log answers a nonce it already holds at the same seq with that row ([[R776000]]).
- `reduce` refuses illegal actions itself and returns the reason; client greying-out is UX only.
- Append-only action log per match. An actor stores one checkpoint of its state after the log's newest row, every `MATCH_CHECKPOINT_MIN_ACTIONS` (32) rows of its log while it is played and when it is stopped for a restart; the next rebuild starts from the last one stored and folds only the rows after it when every check passes (the same rules build, catalog and checkpoint version, its row still logged, its integrity hash), and otherwise folds the whole log, so a crashed actor's rebuild too folds only the rows since. A match that ends drops its checkpoint, and no replay reads one. A co-op or 2v2 match keeps one the same way, held to the same checks ([[§13.9]]). A replay shows a seat each step exactly as `view_for` did, folded under the catalog version it was played on, and is refused on a version the build neither is nor carries or on a final-hash mismatch ([[R768]], [[R767000]]). A number a carried definition never declared reads off the build's definition there, and `cargo jackioh carried` plays every card's era on every carried version and folds it back ([[R4382000]]). Its board draws that seat's own open prompt as it was offered, folded, greyed and sending nothing, and the other seat's as the waiting panel ([[R2211000]]). A clip of one is shared as a GIF drawn from its steps' views alone, with no account name ([[R513000]]).
