Issue #3996 asked to "nerf the strongest ones by ~15%" of S+ #142's ten entries ([[R3570000]]). The strongest are the two that win the board the turn they resolve and ask nothing more of their caster: entry 4, which puts five random Jlockeed cards into play at once, each Spell cast and each permanent summoned for nothing ([[R3570003]]), the most cards and the most mana of any entry; and entry 7, which gives every enemy Unit and their hero Toxic 3, three piercing damage each turn for the rest of the game, and Burn 3 on top ([[R3540000]]). The entries that add cards to the hand still cost their caster a later play (the Terminus cards their full price), the GIGA entry seats at most one Unit on a board with four open zones, and the Golems, the launch watch and the recursion carry no number to cut.

The cuts are the declared numbers' (catalog data, read with `param`), the same on both faces, the nearest whole numbers to 15%:

| Entry | Through v0.6.5b | Now | Cut |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4: summon or cast random Jlockeed cards (`card3`) | 5 | 4 | 20% |
| 7: Toxic and Burn all enemies (`toxic`, `burn`) | Toxic 3, Burn 3 | Toxic 3, Burn 2 | 17% of the counters it puts on each enemy, and of the first turn's damage |

The Burn goes and the Toxic stays: Burn ticks once (its count drops by 3, [[§6.1]]), so a point of it is a point of damage, while a point of Toxic is a point every turn, and Toxic 3 → 2 would take a third off every later tick, about twice the asked size over a game. A count of 3 (the Paperclip, Evolved Swarm and Terminus counts) has no 15% cut: 3 → 2 is a third. The clauses `chaosRolled` announces ([[R436]]) are the engine table's printed words (`CHAOS_MS_EFFECTS`), so entries 4 and 7 now announce "Summon or cast 4 random Jlockeed cards" and "Toxic 3 and Burn 2 all enemies". A replay of v0.6.5b or before resolves under its own catalog's numbers ([[R767000]]), so no script is kept; only its announcement's words are today's, and an event changes no state hash.

Since issue #4154 the counts of three or more and the Burn are a step lower again ([[R4154303]]).
