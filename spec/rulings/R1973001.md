[[R1367000]] opens a new statistics window for every card at each major and patch. Patches shipped back to back, before anyone played one, each opened a window and closed it empty, so a card's figures restarted at every one of them although none had been played.

- **The rule.** A patch that a later version replaced before any game, live or development, was filed under it is read as a micro over the families it plays (`collapse_gameless`): it opens a window only for the cards it changed, and every other card's window runs on across it. Its version keeps its name and its tier everywhere else (the patch notes, the classifier, replays): only the statistics read it this way.
- **What stays a patch.** A major, whatever its games, and the newest version, which has had no time for games yet: it opens its window as before, and the gate's previous window ([[R1367000]]) covers it until its games come in.
- **Where it holds.** Every reader of public card figures: `GET /api/stats/cards` and the stats page, `cargo jackioh stats report --window` and `cargo jackioh winrates` (`windowed_card_stats`), and the window of a card no counted deck held.
- **Micros.** As [[R1367000]] has it: a micro restarts the figures of the cards it changed alone, and every other card keeps all its games. A game in which a changed card stood is not taken out of the other cards' figures: the totals the figures are read from ([[R1044000]]) hold each card's counts per patch, not which cards shared a game, and a record's detail is kept a week.
- **Rejected.** Renaming the versions themselves (a second retcon, [[R1371000]]): it would rewrite every snapshot, record and the database's catalog versions to tidy what only the statistics read. The owner later had the v0.5 line renamed by today's tiers ([[R2532000]]).
