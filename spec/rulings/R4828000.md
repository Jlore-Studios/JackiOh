End Times wrote its cut into the card's tuning ([[R3551002]]), which leaving the field keeps ([[R78]]) and a copy carries ([[R443]]), but it kept the record of its cuts in the card's memory, which both drop. A Heroic Power bounced after some cuts came back cut and with nothing to give back, so its next use left it cut and it went on falling from there. What does a returning or copied End Times cost, and what does its next use give back?

The card remembers. The record is the card's tuning too (`Tuning.taken`, by the declared number's key, `endTimes`), so it goes wherever the cut goes: off the field and back, into a copy ([[R443]]), and into a Fuse with the ingredient whose set price the Fuse keeps ([[R102]]). Once End Times is the card's power again, it is still cut, as a power's numbers keep their tuning ([[R1430]]). Its next use gives back every cut since its last use, wherever each was taken, so the use after it costs (10), moved only by its Buffs and Nerfs ([[R386]]). Each card's record is its own: a copy's use gives back the copy's and leaves its source cut. A cut is recorded only as far as it moved the price, so an Immutable End Times, whose number nothing changes, records nothing and its later use gives back only what moved the price. Through v0.6.14 the record was the card's memory (`script_v0_6_14`, kept, keyed by the patch's fragment, [[R4779000]], [[R767000]]).

Rejected:
- Back at (10) on its return, the cut held in memory alone beside the declared price: the price, the `{endTimes}` text and KY's Constant all read the declared number, so each would need End Times' memory taken off it.
- Giving the cuts back as the card leaves the field: a copy, a Fuse and every other path that keeps tuning but not memory would each need the same.
