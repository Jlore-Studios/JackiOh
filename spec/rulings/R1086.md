Equip (MD-D24) is a play option onto one of your acting, non-Immutable Units ([[R23]]), chosen in the `play` action beside `zone` ([[R81]]; a play with `equip: false` is a plain play, and the host must not be tributed by the same play). A card with Stack too has both options in `legal_actions`. The card stands on top of that Unit while its own play resolves, its Cry included, the host dormant beneath it meanwhile ([[R13]]), as Ivory Tower's machinery does with a Unit host ([[R653]]'s reading of Hearthstone's Mech fusion). On its `cardResolved` the card on top is fused into the host ([[R77]], [[R102]]), never as a trigger: the host is the kept instance (zone, damage, exertion, the turn it arrived), stats and buffs summed, keywords and tags united, texts joined, the cost the lesser of the sum and 4, and the Equip card ceases to exist with no Death. If either card left first, nothing is fused, and the top card, if still there, stays the pile's top. It counts as a play (Combo, [[R70]]'s counters). Core #56 Jilliax is Equip only when played at its embiggen price, (3) (patch v0.3.10, [[R831013]]).
