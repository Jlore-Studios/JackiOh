The fuzz monitor's I19 (issue #4454) found the turn's ticks reaching Dormant cards. A Unit that turns Dormant while it carries a count (Stinkyscape+ #51 Chains Bind Me makes any Unit Dormant, [[R3557002]]) kept it, and:
- its Brittle count ([[R385]]) still dropped at each of its controller's turn starts, and at 0 it "crumbled", reported by `crumbled`, though the destroy that follows passes a Dormant card by ([[R1839000]]), so it crumbled again every turn and stayed;
- its Burn ([[R4154000]]) was removed at the turn's end, the hit it should have dealt passing it by, and its Toxic ([[R3540001]]) was sent at it at each of its owner's turn starts, to the same end.
**Each count waits.** A Dormant card's text is off and no damage reaches it ([[R1839000]], [[R3539003]]), so each tick passes it by as it passes a card beneath a Stack by: no `counterChanged`, no crumble, no hit, and the count stays as it was. Once it awakens each ticks again from where it stopped, at the next turn start or turn end that ticks it, as the Dormant countdown waits beneath a Stack and counts again on top ([[R1839003]]). A card that awakens at its controller's turn start has already been passed by that start's Brittle tick, which comes first ([[R62]], [[R1839001]]).
A replay this change plays differently is refused as `rules_changed` rather than shown wrong ([[R767000]]).
Rejected: letting the ticks reach it, which would have a destroy, a hit or a count's loss reach a card nothing else may.
