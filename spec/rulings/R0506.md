A sound may answer the readable play an event happens inside: the innermost `cardPlayed` whose `cardResolved` has not come, forgotten when a turn starts, never a play whose card is the sentinel. #21 Hinder's rider landing on its victim's next refresh (`modifierChanged` "nextTurnMana", whether it adds a rider or cancels one) is a glassy mana crack in place of the plain notice, on both seats, since that is where the victim's mana visibly changes. A card #27 Blood Ridden Glowy Jelly Bean turns Radiant is a blood drain and then a gold burst in place of the plain glint, the same on the opponent's seat, where the `radiantSet` carries the sentinel, because the sound reads the public cast and never the card it hit. A readable `drawn` directly followed by that card's `cardPlayed` is a card cast as it is drawn, and stings in place of the play whoosh, except a Trap's. A token's printed rarity ([[§5]]'s `printedRarity`) gives its summon the Legendary or Mythic sting as a card's rarity does. Call to Chaos's roll ([[R436]]) spins like a slot machine and dings once for each effect it names, at most three. A mark ([[R437]]) brands its card as it lands and lets go softly as it lifts, whatever the mark, its colour or the card. Book, Pancake and AI are sound families like the other tags, following the card art's theme ([[R503]]). The last 30 seconds of the viewer's own running turn clock ([[R439]]) beat once a second through the effects bus, a heartbeat and then, in the last 10, a tick sharper each second; never the other seat's clock, and nothing while muted, at master or effects volume 0, or before the first gesture. None of this is a rule, and [[R203]] bounds all of it
