Once a finished match's opponent was gone, the rematch offers and the "You offered a rematch. Waiting for your opponent…" line vanished with no word ([[R672]], [[R3213000]]), so a seat that had offered could not tell whether its offer still stood.

**The line.** While the match socket is open and the seat's rematch status, pushed over the socket ([[R3213000]]) or read ([[R672]]), says `opponentHere` is false, the result panel shows one status line, "Your opponent has left.", where the offers, the incoming and waiting lines, the Double-or-nothing note, the lean picker and a refused offer's alert were. It is `role="status"`, drawn below the panel's row as the waiting line was. If the opponent comes back (`opponentHere` true again), the offers return as they were.

**Once only.** When the result's own reason is the opponent's disconnect, which says "Your opponent left the match." already (`Result.tsx`'s `resultReason`), the rematch panel shows nothing for the absence, so the screen never says it twice. Every other ending that leaves the opponent gone (a concession, a hero's death, a turn cap, the viewer's own disconnect) gets the line.

**Nothing.** A closed socket ([[R3694000]]) or no status yet shows nothing, as before.

**A bot reads as a person.** A person who left and a ladder bot whose linger ended produce the same `opponentHere: false` ([[R3213000]], [[R639000]]), so the one line tells no bot from a person ([[R1452]]).

**Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).** The line reads the status the server already sent this seat; no rule runs in the client and nothing hidden is shown.

**The primary hands on.** The left line is no button, so the panel's next way on (Watch the replay) takes the primary look that the rematch block's first button has while the offers show (`buttons.css`).

Hearthstone semantics: the other side leaving shows the moment it happens, as [[R3213000]] says of the frames.

Rejected: a line in the result text itself (it would repeat the disconnect's own reason, and the result does not know of a bot's linger ending); a separate line for a bot and for a person (it would tell them apart, [[R1452]]); keeping a refused offer's alert beside the line (the offer can no longer be retried); saying it a second time when the game ended by their leaving.
