Stinkyscape #18.1 MELCHIOR votes ([[R1843000]]) at its controller's start of turn when their opponent has strictly more Units than they do, counted as every rule counts a player's Units (`active_units_of`): a carried Unit and one standing in a backrow zone count ([[R446]]), a Dormant card does not ([[R1839000]]). Its Activate ([[R384]], once a turn) is a Discover ([[§6.3]]) of three different combat keywords drawn from [[R21]]'s pool ([[R781005]]), offered to its controller alone as a `mode` prompt; the pick is granted to each Unit its controller has when the answer resolves. With no Unit nothing is asked, as a Discover with nothing to give, and the turn's use is spent all the same, as a Hearthstone Hero Power that does nothing is. Its Radiant face asks nothing: each of its controller's Units draws its own `keywords` (3) random combat keywords, as [[R21]]'s grant draws them, never one the Unit has. Rejected: one draw shared by every Unit, which "Give each of your Units random combat keywords" does not say.
