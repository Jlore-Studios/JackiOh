Stinkyscape #18.1 MELCHIOR votes ([[R1843000]]) at its controller's start of turn when their opponent has strictly more Units than they do, counted as every rule counts a player's Units (`active_units_of`): a carried Unit and one standing in a backrow zone count ([[R446]]), a Dormant card does not ([[R1839000]]). Its Activate ([[R384]], once a turn) gives each Unit its controller has random combat keywords, each Unit drawing its own from [[R21]]'s pool, never one it has: 3 on its Radiant face and, since #3142, 1 on its base face ([[R3142001]]). Through v0.5.11p its base face was a Discover ([[§6.3]]) of three different combat keywords drawn from [[R21]]'s pool ([[R781005]]), offered to its controller alone as a `mode` prompt, the pick granted to each Unit its controller had when the answer resolved; the script kept for those replays still asks it. With no Unit nothing is given, or asked, and the turn's use is spent all the same, as a Hearthstone Hero Power that does nothing is. Rejected: one draw shared by every Unit, which "Give each of your Units random combat keywords" does not say.
