Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: Crawler, "Has Flashback instead of 'Can be played from your Graveyard for (1)'". Both faces of S+ #120 print Flashback, a catalog keyword ([[§6.1]]), and their script no longer carries the `graveyardPrice` of [[R3560001]]. From its owner's graveyard it is offered as Flashback offers any card ([[§10.5]], [[R783012]]): once, at its own cost, which a discount takes no lower than (1) ([[R4285000]]), so a Crawler at its printed (1) still costs (1) there and one a Nerf raised costs more; the play spends the keyword for good ([[R783013]]), so once it dies again it stays in the graveyard. The keyword is printed, so every Crawler carries it, S+ #134 Basket of Crawlers', S+ #135 Crawler Swarm's and S+ #136 Crawler Assembly Line's included. Replays of the versions before this patch fold under `script_v0_6_13h` (and through v0.6.6h under `script_v0_6_6h`), each priced at (1) in the graveyard any number of times as they were ([[R767000]]).
