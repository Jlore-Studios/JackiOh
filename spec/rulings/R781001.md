Immutable keeps what it did ([[R23]], [[R386]]: no Transform, Vanilla, Fuse-onto, Nerf or Buff reaches the card itself, and Radiant still does) and adds a targeting half (patch v0.3.X, the designer's): "While in play, Spells, Field Spells and Field Traps can't target it." Targeting is [[R450]]'s choosing, so no `target` declaration of a Spell, a Field Spell or a Field Trap offers an Immutable card in play, and no `target` prompt they open lists it; `legal_actions` and the reducer's refusal ask one function (`restrictions::immutable_bars`, §10.2), a card whose only would-be target is Immutable has no legal target, and a Mayor's random draw of a declared target ([[R1200]]) draws from the legal ones, so never it.

- **Whose card it is.** The source is read by its type where it stands: anything in a unit zone is a Unit (an Animated card there included); any other card is its running face's printed type, so a Spell set face-down (ME-ALTPLAY) is still a Spell as it reveals, and a Field Trap firing from the backrow is a Field Trap.
- **Friend and foe.** Its controller's own Spells, Field Spells and Field Traps can't target it either, as with Hearthstone's Elusive.
- **What still reaches it.** A declared or a forced attack (it is no restriction on attacks, [[§4.2]]); anything a Unit does (a Cry target, a Death, a trigger, an Activate); a Trap that is not a Field Trap (the designer: the rule is for Field Traps), whose effects still act on it, while its text half still stops what it stops (Sheepish fires and is consumed with no effect, [[R17]]); and every effect that does not choose it: "all" and "every" effects and the other board and card scopes, Cleave's splash, an effect on the Units beside a target, and a random pick, which in Hearthstone is no target either (the designer: yes).
- **Where.** In play only: on the field and face-up, as Immune to Spells and the tribal-hate immunity protect ([[R940]]). A card in a hand, a deck or a graveyard may still be picked; its text half holds there ([[R386]]). A face-down card's Immutable is dormant, and reading it would tell its owner's opponent what the card is.
- **Beside Immune to Spells.** The two stay separate keywords. Immune to Spells stops every effect of a Spell, aimed or not, and nothing from a Field Spell or a Trap; Immutable stops the aim of Spells, Field Spells and Field Traps, and no area effect.
- **The cards.** Midrange Menace's and The Rock's and Blue-Eyes White Felinor's Radiant faces, Jade Beauty, Aluneth (a Field Spell: a Spell, Field Spell or Field Trap that targets a permanent can't pick it) and The Great Wall keep printing it, with no change to their data; Keymaster Keenus loses it with its rewrite. A Spell that Immutable already left with nothing to do (Silence's Vanilla, a Transform, a Nerf) can no longer be aimed at an Immutable card in play at all. It stays out of the random keyword pool ([[R21]]).
