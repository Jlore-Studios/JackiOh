FvH #21 Scandalous Top Hat gives a Unit +1/+1 (Radiant +3/+3) and "Death: Add a Scandalous Top Hat to your hand." A Unit may wear several: two Hats cast at it, or The Mad Hatter's Hat on a Unit that already wears one. A card keeps one copy of a grant it is given twice ([[R1107]]: the same name, face and numbers are the same grant), so each Hat's grant carries its own number, one past the Hats the Unit already wears, and is a grant of its own: the Unit shows each quoted Death ([[R1445]]), and as it dies, after its own Death, it adds one Hat for each it wore, to the hand of the controller it dies under, each on the face of the Hat that gave it. The number is no tuned value and changes nothing else the Hat does.
