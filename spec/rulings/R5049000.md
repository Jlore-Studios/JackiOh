A Smoothie in hand printed the Fruits' text it gained ([[R4440006]]) with every number left a placeholder ("Deal {amount} damage … Heal your hero {heal}."). A fused definition declares no numbers of its own (`params`, [[R77]]): its text joins its ingredients' templates, and its numbers are its ingredients' ([[R102]]), each read on the face it went in on ([[R4334000]]). The face filled a fused text only with keys its definition declares, so it filled nothing; and the view's numbers for a gained text (`CardView.copies.params`, [[§10.8]]) are read off the Smoothie, which declares none, so they carry nothing either. They stay as they are: the face reads each Fruit's own declaration, and a view that carries the same game differently would read to the classifier as cards that play differently.

- **Each ingredient prints its own.** A fused text's face fills each ingredient's `{key}`s with that ingredient's own numbers, on its own face ([[R469]]: one that went in Radiant, or inside a fusion that did, prints its Radiant numbers on both forms). The first ingredient to declare a key prints the view's number for it, on the face the card stands on, since the view keeps each key's first declaration ([[R4334000]]); a later one prints its own declaration as the card's tuning moves it, by the formula the view's numbers come from (`params::tuned_value`). Two ingredients that declare one key print each their own number, as their texts read them: a Smoothie holding Fig of Life and All Purpose Apple heals 20 and 2, not 20 twice. A fusion of fusions fills each leaf, an inner definition the view does not carry read from the id it spells ([[R179]], [[R243]]). Where an ingredient cannot be found, or a face's text is not its ingredients' joined, the view's numbers fill it by key as before.

Presentation only: nothing in the engine changes but `params::tuned_value` made public, and no card plays differently.
