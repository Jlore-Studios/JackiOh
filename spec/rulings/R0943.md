ME-CREATED (MD-B6): every instance minted after the decks are built carries `created: true` — effect-made cards, tokens, The Coin in shipped games — while dealt deck cards (and a Glitch reset's new deal) carry none. A transform's new card is Created; a copy keeps the flag only as its instance does. It persists through [[R78]]'s and [[R766]]'s resets and every zone move, and survives a JSON round trip. It is public wherever the viewer may read the card (beside `chinese` in every view and prompt option), and nowhere else: placeholders drop it, and the owner's library list marks only cards the owner knows ([[R312]]). The client shows it as a small "Created" mark.
