A fused card carries its ingredients' declarations in order, every needed pick among them ([[R77]], [[R102]], [[R703]]), so it is played only while each can be answered. Its play gate ([[R1851000]]) is held the same way: every ingredient's gate must hold, as a fusion allows a mode option only when every ingredient's check allows it ([[R831008]]). A copier (Classic #57 Echo, [[R399]]) declares the choices of the Spell whose text it has, on the face that Spell was played on, and its gate is that face's too: Echo holding the text of a Prestige played on its Radiant face is played only while a Radiant enemy card its player can see stands on the field. Its own face adds only its Echo.
