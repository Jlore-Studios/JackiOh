Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView`, the log or a replay. Hearthstone semantics do not reach composited layers.

[[R3351000]]'s shed ladder and [[R3674000]]'s moment read frame time alone. A phone's browser kills the tab for memory within a frame or two of a big mount, before any slow window trips. So the effects director also keeps a memory budget, `FX_PHONE_LAYER_BUDGET_MB` (200 MB), for the layers its mounted DOM cues hold. A layer holds its element's box at its largest keyframe scale, squared, times the device's pixel ratio squared and `FX_LAYER_BYTES_PER_PX` (4) bytes. The budget applies on a phone (its window's narrower side under `FX_MOBILE_WIDTH`, [[R4493000]]) and on any device reporting `navigator.deviceMemory` of `FX_LOW_DEVICE_MEMORY_GB` (4) or less, at any width. Elsewhere, and in a director given no device (the stats, packs and gallery pages'), nothing changes. At 390x844 and 3x the budget is about 17.7 screens' worth.

**What each cue holds** is a table in `fx/layers.ts`, in screens' worth at 390x844. A takeover's entry is its look's dark, root and sweep, its rays and wave, its art ([[R3400000]]) and its picture ([[R3407000]]), and its stage: the stage and each slice or plane at its largest scale. A set piece's entry is its shape's, and every other kind's is its largest case's. A fan, a sheen or a figure landed on the whole board counts its board entry, every other anchor being a card's size. `layerBudget.test.ts` holds every entry to the estimate from `fx.css` (never under it, within 0.05 over), and every cue's largest case at 3x to the budget, so a cue that grows fails there before it ships.

**The order.** Before a frame fires a takeover, the director reads the device and counts what is held besides it: every mounted effect still up, the frame's other DOM cues (an earlier takeover in the form it took) and the pending ones due before this takeover ends. A takeover still to come counts at the least it will mount, since it takes its own form when it fires. It then mounts the fullest form that keeps the held layers and its own within the budget, in a fixed order:

1. its stage merged into `FX_PHONE_STAGE_LAYERS` pictures, as a phone always draws it ([[R4493000]]; a low-memory desktop tries its full stage first);
2. without its look's own art and its picture;
3. without its stage: the dark, the sweep, the rays and the wave, as a takeover without a set piece plays;

and failing all of them, its dark and its sweep alone. A takeover always mounts, and no other cue is ever reduced or dropped: a set piece's main beats, a launch, a rift and a title card mount as they are, as [[R3351000]] holds them, and the hold stays the takeover's ([[R2785000]]). In the real browser, the Drone Battleship's arrival plays three takeovers at once, one for each layer it summons: it held 207 MB at its fullest frame before this order's last step, and holds within the budget with it.

This extends [[R3351000]] and [[R3674000]] with a budget of memory beside their budget of frame time. Neither's ladder, window or numbers change, and the frame-time shed never touches a takeover's form.

Rejected: frame time alone, which never sees the spike; reducing or delaying any cue but a takeover, which would hide what the game just did; counting a takeover still to come at its fullest, which leaves the first of several overlapping takeovers the darkest while the later ones take the room; reading real layer memory at run time, which no browser reports (the tables are held to the estimate instead); and a smaller budget, under which every phone's takeover would lose its stage.
