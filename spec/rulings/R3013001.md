The issue asked that each level unlock a stamp, a small marker the player may wear on the card, at
most one per card, and that the server check every pick. This rules how.

**Which stamp a level unlocks.** There are `PROFICIENCY_LEVELS` stamps, one per level, the same set
for every card ([[R3013002]] draws them): reaching level n unlocks stamp n, so a card at level n may
wear any of stamps 1 to n, and a card never played, at level 0, none.

**Where the pick is kept.** On the card's own proficiency row ([[R3013000]]), its `stamp` column,
NULL for none. Migration 0081's check holds it to `1..level` where it is written, so no row wears a
stamp its level has not reached; a pick is no holding and has no ledger ([[§14.11]]).

**The route.** `PUT /api/proficiency/stamp` with `{ cardId, stamp }`, `stamp` a whole number from 1
to `PROFICIENCY_LEVELS` or `null` for none, sets the card's one stamp and answers `{ cards }`, the
account's rows as `GET /api/proficiency` answers them. It takes the account's turn ([[R2378000]]) and
its economy lock, as a card pick does ([[R1834000]]), and reads the card's level under it. A stamp
above that level, a card never played among them, is a 400 (`"stamp" needs level n of this card`)
and writes nothing; so is a card that is no card of the catalog, a token, and a stamp outside the
range. `null` on a card with no row writes nothing and answers 200.

**The dev fill.** A dev account's fill ([[§14.10]]) sets every collectable card to the top level,
its plays raised to the last threshold where they were fewer, its stamp kept, so every stamp is
unlocked for testing. It writes no ledger row, a level being no holding; every other part of the
fill is still marked `dev` ([[R1300000]]).

**Bots.** A ladder bot has no proficiency and wears no stamp ([[R1454]]): its wardrobe holds
cosmetics alone ([[R1834005]]).

Hearthstone semantics: a reward a player earns by playing is theirs to show or not, and the server
decides what they have earned.

Rejected: keeping the pick in `card_cosmetic_picks` beside the card's other picks, which the
cosmetics registry's rework is rebuilding and which this must not collide with; and a stamp
unlocked as a ledger grant, which would be a second record of what the level already says.
