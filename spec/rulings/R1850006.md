A face-up Field Spell with Crater Locks the backrow zone it enters, any way it enters — played, summoned or flickered — before its Cry, through the call a Crater Unit's arrival makes (`crater::lock_on_arrival`, [[R783010]]). The Lock is [[R688]]'s: it refuses plays into the zone and outlasts the card. Until #1850 Crater did nothing on a Field Spell ([[R783011]]); on a Spell it still does nothing.
