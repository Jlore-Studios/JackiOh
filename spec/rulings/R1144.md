## R1144 — Only an Embiggened play rolls, once per copy, before the copy goes in

Built (Meditative #81). "Embiggened (2)" ("Paid (2)" until issue #1175, [[R1175000]]) is its
Embiggened version, the only text that names it: played as its normal version, no copy rolls.
Embiggened, each copy whose card is not already Radiant rolls its chance once,
on the match rng, before the copy goes in, so it goes in on the face it rolled; a Radiant card's
copy is Radiant without a roll ([[R129]]). "This" is copied last, on the face it was played with,
and a copy is not generation ([[R387]]).
