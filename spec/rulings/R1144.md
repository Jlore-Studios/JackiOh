## R1144 — Only a paid play rolls, once per copy, before the copy goes in

Built (Meditative #81). "Paid (2)" is the embiggen price, the only text that names it: played
unpaid, no copy rolls. Paid, each copy whose card is not already Radiant rolls its chance once,
on the match rng, before the copy goes in, so it goes in on the face it rolled; a Radiant card's
copy is Radiant without a roll ([[R129]]). "This" is copied last, on the face it was played with,
and a copy is not generation ([[R387]]).
