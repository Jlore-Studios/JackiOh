Later cards read the ship: #133 Ultra Capacitor counts the pieces launched, #137 Firststrike Capability launches the ship "if you are building a ship", and #137.4 Space Doctrine fills your hand "with the Starship Pieces that are part of the launched Starship".

**The memory.** A Starship's memory is `CardInstance.starship`, started empty as the Dormant Starship's hull enters the field (`starship::start_starship_on_field`): each piece whose copy fuses into it is recorded, oldest first, by its definition and its Radiant face ([[R1852000]]); as it launches the memory takes the name it took and the order it launched in ([[R1852003]]). A card reads it through the prelude: `starship_pieces_of` for a Starship's pieces, and `launched_starship_of` for a player's launched Starship on the field, the one launched last when they have several. It is a status, so [[R78]]'s reset takes it off with the card leaving the field; absent on every other card, so a game with no Starship serializes, hashes and replays exactly as before.

**Building a ship.** `building_a_ship(player)` is whether that player has a Dormant Starship (`dormant_starship_of`): true from the first piece's death until the launch, and false again until the next piece dies.

**Seen by both players.** `UnitView.starship` is on the card for both seats: its pieces and, once launched, its name.
