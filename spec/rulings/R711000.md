Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The client's reader language is a device-only setting stored locally that sets `<html lang>` and governs the language the entire web interface is rendered in. Changing the reader language never affects the game engine, protocol wire format, serialisation, replay or server state.

When the reader's language is English (the default), every card and interface string renders as printed in English. When the reader's language is Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`), every card face, card name (in matches, the deck builder, and the Card Almanac), and frame terms (types, tags, rarities, keywords) are drawn from the Chinese card tables ([[R1303]]).

A card flagged Chinese by the Meditative mechanic (`chinese: true`, [[R1300]], [[R1301]]) is drawn in Chinese for every reader, regardless of whether their interface is in English or Chinese. When a reader who is already reading in Simplified Chinese inspects or hovers over a card that was flagged Chinese, the card's inspect view and hover preview display the easter egg line where the flavour line sits: "本来要把它翻译成中文的——可它已经是中文了！" ("This would be translated to Chinese, but it's already in Chinese!"). It is presentation only and plays no sound.

The engine's and the server's English (prompt titles and options, refusals, validator issues, modifier badges, API errors) stays English on the wire and is looked up where the client displays it, by the same English-keyed dictionary. Such a sentence arrives with its values filled in, so a dictionary key holding `{name}` tokens matches it whole when the key has at least two words of its own, or holds each token in parentheses (`({cost}) Cost`); each value it captures is looked up in turn. A key with fewer words of its own (`{n} cards`) answers only its own calls, so it never leaves an unentered sentence half translated. A sentence the dictionary lacks stays English. The game log's lines keep the language they were first read in, as its history keeps them ([[R745]]).
