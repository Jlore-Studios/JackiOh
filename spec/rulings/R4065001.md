M #72 The Banisher, a 1-mana 2/1 (Radiant 4/2 with Cleave), exiles each Unit it damages: a Unit it deals 1 or more is marked, and the next state check exiles it ahead of deaths, so no Death fires and Reborn does not bring it back ([[R1124]], [[§4.5]] step 1). The AI read its hits as plain damage: the move order ranked an attack of a 5/5 into it as a kill the 5/5 survives, the opponent model's static trader priced that trade as losing nothing, and the count of the damage past the AI's Taunts ([[§9.9]]) let an enemy Banisher chip a 0/8 Taunt for 2 instead of removing it.

A hit from a source with `exiles_on_damage` (`exiles_what_it_damages`, the flag `damage.rs` reads at [[§4.4]] step 7) now reads as a Poisonous one everywhere the AI reads a hit:

- `blow`, through its `Side`'s `exiles`, so `fight`'s two blows and the Cleave splash of `trade_value` ([[R63]]): any damage dealt removes the Unit;
- `kills_and_survives`, both ways: the attacker's hit, and a strike back that exiles the attacker;
- the Taunt walk of `damage_past_taunts_with` ([[R3937000]]): an exiling hit or splash removes the Taunt, with no Reborn body back, `breaks_alone` counts the attacker that does as breaking it alone, and `falls_to_first_strike` counts a First Strike Taunt's exiling strike back as stopping the attacker whole ([[§4.3]] step 1).

A hit that deals nothing exiles nothing: an unspent Divine Shield takes it whole, Indestructible takes it, Armor that takes all of it leaves no damage instance ([[R1361]]), and neither does a hit the side's damage pools soak whole, which the AI reads with [[R4064000]]'s flag; the unit's own cap still lets 1 through ([[R2179005]]). An Indestructible unit never has Taunt ([[R347]]), so the walk never meets one.

`trade_value` prices what an exiling hit removes at its whole `unit_worth`: no Death payout is taken off and no Reborn body is credited back, where a kill removes `kill_worth`. That holds both ways: a target the attacker exiles, and an attacker the target's strike back exiles. A neighbour the splash removes is already priced at its `unit_worth`.

The flag `exile_on_damage_read` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick's evaluation keeps the old walk. `side_of`, `kills_and_survives` and the reply's trader take no weights and read `AI_EVAL`'s flag, as `stands_down` does for [[R3793000]], so the move order the greedy baseline takes its candidates from (`candidate_actions`) reads it too, as it reads [[R3793000]]'s; the Taunt walk reads its own weights' flag.

Rejected: a `poisonous`-style parameter on `blow` or a field on `Exchange`, since the exile is the source's own flag and the `Side` already carries the source; a keyword weight, since the exile is no keyword. Not modelled: the engine's own combat forecast (`combat_forecast`) and the lethal subsystem's Taunt walk (`subsystems::lethal`), which also read the hit as plain damage; that is the engine's and the client's side, and outside the AI.

**Gate.** The smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`, fixed seeds), with both of issue #4065's flags (`affliction_kills_before_swing`, `exile_on_damage_read`) on: ai-vs-random 19 of 20 (17 needed), ai-vs-greedy 16 (10), hard-vs-easy 16 (16), with no search panic, on base `f94a31821f` and again merged onto `699914740f` ([[R4064000]]'s pools read); with both off on the first base, and on `699914740f` itself, the same three counts.

It reads only public state (a face-up unit's script flags, which both players see), and it adds no rule of the game.
