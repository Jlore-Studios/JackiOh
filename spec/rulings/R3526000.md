**Why.** [[R1973001]] read a patch as a micro when no game was filed under its own version. Every game is filed under its build's newest version, so a patch shipped in one commit with the micro after it (v0.5.11 with v0.5.11b, v0.5.12 with v0.5.12b) never had a build of its own, and never a game. Both were read as micros, and on `/stats` the window every card shares ran from v0.5.10 to the newest version under v0.5.10's name, with no tab for v0.5.11 or v0.5.12 (#3526).

**The rule.** A patch's **run** is its version and the micros after it, up to the next patch or major. A patch is read as a micro over the families it plays only when no game, live or development, was filed under any version of its run, and a later patch or major replaced it. The newest patch, whose run holds the newest version, keeps its tier whatever its games, as the newest version did: it has had no time for games, and `/stats` shows it collecting ([[R3324000]]). A major keeps its tier, as before. Every reader of [[R1973001]] reads it this way: `GET /api/stats/cards` and its patch list ([[R1973002]]), the game figures, `cargo jackioh stats report --window` and `cargo jackioh winrates`. One function computes it, `collapse_gameless`.

**Unchanged.** A micro opens a window only for the families it plays ([[R1367000]]). A micro that wears a patch-form name, as v0.5.13 did ([[R3471000]]) until the v0.5.13 retcon named it v0.5.12aa ([[R3631001]]), is a micro: it never opens a tab. No version is renamed.

Hearthstone semantics: a stats site's patch is every build played on it, whatever its hotfixes are called.

Rejected:

- Shipping a patch alone in its own commit, so that a build files games under it: `patches ship` takes every pending fragment at once, and the statistics should not depend on how a release was batched.
- Filing a record under the newest patch, not the build's version: the version a game was played on is what a replay folds under.
- Counting only the micro straight after the patch: a patch whose first micro was replaced as fast would fold all the same.
