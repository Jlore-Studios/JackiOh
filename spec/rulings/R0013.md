Cards under a Stack are not on the field except for Felinor Fiender's count, in a backrow pile as in a unit pile ([[§3.2]]): only the top acts, so a face-down trap beneath cannot fire and an aura beneath is off. A carrier is no pile: the carrier acts beneath the Unit standing on it ([[R446]]), as C+ #33 Ivory Tower does while the Unit stacked onto it resolves its play, before it is fused in ([[R418]], [[R653]])
