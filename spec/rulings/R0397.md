C #48's base Cry may target any permanent, and at resolution destroys it only if its card's `loc` ([[§5]]) is greater than Hired Shrimp's, so a wrong guess fizzles: the joke is kept as a rule. `loc` is public, printed in the inspect overlay, so the guess can be informed. The Radiant face offers only the permanents that qualify, which is its text's "valid targets are highlighted", except a face-down card its chooser may not read, which is always offered and judged at resolution as on the base face, so the offer never gives away that card's `loc` ([[R177]], [[§10.8]]). A played Unit's Cry is chosen with the card still in hand, so Hired Shrimp never offers itself ([[R70]], [[R90]])
