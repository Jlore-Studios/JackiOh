title: A First Strike unit strikes once

[[§4.3]]'s step 1 is when its strike happens, not an extra one: each unit deals its damage once per combat, and First Strike only moves **that unit's** strike earlier — on whichever side of the combat it is, since [[§6.1]] says "deals damage before non-First-Strike units" without naming a side. A defender with First Strike therefore hits a plain attacker first and takes nothing back if that attacker falls: #20 Pointmaster, a 7/2, kills a 3/3 that attacks into it and survives. Two First Strikers trade in step 1
