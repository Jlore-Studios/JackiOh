TG #39's seventh entry summons five base Rush Tokens, then gives its caster three `unitsHaveKeyword` modifiers ([[R1859007]], the War Doctrines' verb), one each for Rush, Trample and Pierce: every Unit its caster controls on the field has the three, those that arrive later too, for the rest of the game, kept when the card is gone. They are the player's, so the opponent's Units never have them, and each shows as a badge on both seats ([[R169]]).
