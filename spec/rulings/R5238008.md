Felinors vs Humans #79 grants an End of turn that Allures. `allure_enemy_units` ([[R963]]) Allures every enemy Unit; a single Allure was a script's own delay and steal step, which a granted hook cannot carry.

**The rule.** `allure_unit { instanceId?, randomEnemy? }` Allures one Unit with the very entry `allure_enemy_units` makes for each ([[§6.3]] Allure): stolen at the start of the Allurer's next turn, or destroyed with no open zone, the `allure` mark on it while it waits ([[R437]]), dropped if it leaves the field first ([[R174]]), landing even after the Allurer has gone ([[R76]]). The Allurer is the effect's controller. `instanceId` names the Unit, which must be one `allure_enemy_units` would mark now: an enemy Unit on the field, a pile's top or one standing in the backrow, not Dormant, and not passed by as Immune to a Spell's Allure ([[R4453000]]); any other is not Allured. With no `instanceId`, `randomEnemy` draws one of those Units from the match rng; with none there, nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). With neither, nothing happens.
