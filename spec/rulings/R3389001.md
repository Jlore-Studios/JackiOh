Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The owner asked for every Legendary and Mythic to have an animation of its own (issue #3330). Classic's eleven, of which seven played a shared motif and four a recipe of their own ([[R670]], [[R2794000]]), are signatures now ([[R3332000]], [[R3389000]]), each a `{ bespoke }` recipe in `fx/cards/classic/`, and leave the ratchet:

- **C #4 Palantir** (Field Spell): a black seeing-orb drops onto its zone and its iris opens in cyan, a scan sweeping the other side's unit row; under its steal's marker its base face's Tribute cracks the orb and an eye-beam locks onto the other hand, and the Spell it counters into its owner's hand reels across as a card back; a slow cyan iris (`seeingIris`).
- **C #7 InfiniScepter** (Field Spell): a crystal sceptre spins down and plants, its locket opening; its Cry draws the Spell it exiles into the locket, and each Activate of its own pulses it and sends a spectral copy out; the held Spell turning in its loop (`sceptreLoop`).
- **C #9 Income Tax** (Trap): as it fires, a strongbox lid flies open, a chain and a crimson seal close on the other hand, and each card it takes is yanked across, a card back; no aura, a face-down Trap showing nothing ([[R202]]).
- **C #28 Second Wind** (Field Spell): a gale bursts and it lands in a whirl of wind; its Cry blows the deck into exile and the hand into the graveyard, back by back, beside each pile's one wave when the whole pile goes; a breeze (`graveBreeze`).
- **C #33 Joro** (Unit): nothing while it waits in a hand; a thread drops and it swings down into its zone, and a web snaps from the Spell's aim to it as it takes the target; a faint web (`silkWeb`).
- **C #44 Back from the GY** (Spell): a lantern flare over the graveyard and cracks along the row, each Unit it summons clawing up through its zone, the Spell crumbling into exile; no aura.
- **C #45 Nature Titan** (Unit): its `titanBloom` grown, roots cracking, a moss boulder heaving up, wildflowers round its foot; its Cry draws on a leaf and a seed of light heals its hero; moss (`mossCreep`).
- **C #56 Spell Tyrant** (Unit): its `tyrantSigil` grown, a crown-sigil slamming and cracking the stone; each Spell its Cry casts rises from the graveyard to its crown, casts, then burns into exile; a crown-sigil (`crownSigil`).
- **C #80 BOOM! Big Max** (Unit): its `bigBoom` grown, a fuse burning along its row, the drop and a blast down its lane; its Crater cracks and clamps its zone; crater smoke (`craterSmoke`).
- **C #85 King Wagtoggle** (Unit): a velvet cloak sweeps in and he tips his crown to the far side; the two decks cross under a crown, and its Radiant Recruit leaps from its new deck; a crimson shimmer (`cloakShimmer`).
- **C #90 In Too Deep** (Mythic Field Spell): its own takeover, `abyss`, then water bursting over its zone as it surfaces; each quest step of its own lights a shrine lower down a spiral on the card, and a quest completed bursts it open, its rewards never drawn; a whirlpool (`abyssWhirl`).

A concept is the target, not a script, so a beat moved where the budgets or the view's events asked: the draw limit's shutter, Nature Titan's beats as it attacks, Big Max's blows and Trample, Second Wind's later graveyard plays and Income Tax's deferred keep ([[R1279000]]) open no play or hook of their card ([[R2797000]]) and play the shared rows; Palantir's beam locks at the crack, Income Tax's chain at the firing and the Tyrant's ash is a card back, so each ends inside its budget. Each card's sound bed is its own. Every signature plays in the `/dev/fx` gallery, the component specs capture each at 1280x720 and 390x844, and In Too Deep and BOOM! Big Max, the heaviest, hold `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS`. Rejected: keeping the four recipes the cards had as their whole signatures, which would leave their text's beat or their aura to the shared motifs.
