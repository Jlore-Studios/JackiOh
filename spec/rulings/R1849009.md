A Trap that prints "30% chance to Reveal when an opponent attacks" (`staticFlags.revealChance`, a percent, or the card's declared number `chance` where it declares it, [[R386]]) fires only on a lucky roll. Each time its triggers admit an event while it is face-down, after their `when`, which never rolls ([[R99]]), the match rng rolls once against the chance out of `PERCENT` (100); its controller's Luck and the trap's own Lucky roll it again, any hit enough ([[R987]], [[R1440]]). A hit fires it as any trap fires. A miss reveals nothing, emits nothing and leaves it set in its zone for its next chance; the view carries no rng cursor, so the opponent learns nothing of it ([[R33]]). A trap without a chance, a face-up Field Trap firing again and a set card's own reveal draw nothing, so no other firing moves the rng. Rejected: one roll for the trap's whole stay, which would make it a trap 30% of the time rather than a 30% chance at each attack; and rolling inside `when`, which [[R99]] keeps free of draws.
