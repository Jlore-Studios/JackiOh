[[R643]]'s limit is one rule with one config, enforced at both ends. A watcher's emotes ([[R2990000]]) are held to the same rule under numbers of their own, the server's config's, so the sidelines never drown out the players: at least `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_COOLDOWN_MS` (3 s) between two of one watcher's emotes and at most `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_WINDOW_MAX` (3) inside a rolling `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_WINDOW_MS` (30 s), where a seat has 1.5 s, five and 20 s.

- **One rule.** The engine's gate takes the limit it applies (`emote_gate_within`); a seat's is `SEAT_EMOTE_LIMITS`, the engine config's three numbers, and a watcher's is the server config's three. The web reads those generated into `serverConfig.ts` and runs the same gate, so the watcher's menu greys an emote out exactly when the actor would drop it.
- **A drop is silent.** An emote past the limit is dropped unanswered and recorded nowhere; it is no refused action and counts toward no alert or malformed budget, as a seat's is ([[R643]]). Only an emote that is let through is counted toward the limit, so one outside the hand spends none of it ([[R2990001]]).
- **Kept by account.** The actor keeps each watching account's recent sends for its life, so a new socket of the account, a replacement or a reconnect, starts from where the last one stopped.

Rejected: a seat's own numbers, which with eight watchers would let the sidelines send many times what the players do; and a limit per match shared by every watcher, which would let one watcher spend the others' turn.
