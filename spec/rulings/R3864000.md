A choice of one board zone (a play's placement zone, a `cell` prompt of `min` 1 and `max` 1, a declared zone target of `min` 1 and `max` 1 whose selections are all zones) draws no list, at any width. The zones it offers glow on the board; a tap on one, or Enter or Space on a focused one, or a tap on what stands in it ([[R446]]), answers, and the modal is the hint "Tap a highlighted zone on the board." and a play's Cancel, as a lane pick's is (#2757). The lit zones are focusable, so the keyboard reaches them with Tab, and a `cell` prompt opens with its first lit zone focused. An option that names no cell ("Done", "Stop") stays a button, since the board has nowhere to tap for it. A replay or the other seat's view still shows the title. It amends [[R514]], whose list "for the keyboard and small screens" is gone.

A zone choice of more than one (`max` above 1) or one that may answer with none (`min` 0) keeps its list and Confirm, a target that offers cards or heroes, alone or beside zones, keeps its list, and Discover-style choices of cells ([[R831003]]), lane picks (#2757) and Plague Counter placement ([[R689]], [[R821000]]) are unchanged. Dragging a card to a zone still plays there. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no engine, `PendingChoice` or action changes.

Rejected: keeping the list on small screens only (the issue asks for every width), and a menu fallback for the keyboard (the lit zones are already focusable and answer Enter and Space).
