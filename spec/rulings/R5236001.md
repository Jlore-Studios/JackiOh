Once a card's Durability left is 0 ([[R5236000]]), every Activate of its text is refused with "No
Durability left": `why_ability_unusable` refuses it ahead of the per-turn uses, so the reducer, the
`legal_actions` list (which offers no `activate` of it) and the AI agree ([[R43]], [[R384]],
[[R3061000]]). `run_activation` with `spend_use` runs nothing on such a card either, as it runs nothing
on one whose use this turn is spent.

Each `ActivationView` of the card carries `durability_left`, the count every Activate of it shares:
`Some(0)` once it is spent, with `usable` false and that refusal as its `reason`; absent on a card
without Durability and on the engine's own abilities.

A **Unit** at 0 lives on: it attacks, defends and keeps its other text, and has no Activate it may use.
Only a card that is no Unit leaves at 0 ([[R5236002]]), as a Hearthstone weapon at 0 is destroyed but no
minion has Durability.
