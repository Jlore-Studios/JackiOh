Curses raised a run's score ([[§15.9]]) and changed no fight, and Combat artifacts did nothing in
one, so taking every curse was free score. Now each curse and each Combat artifact may carry a fight
effect, and `run_fight_setup` folds them into the two handicaps ([[R180]]) it hands the game, after
Presence has set the enemy's tier ([[§15.6]]):

- Each curse taken changes the enemy's handicap once: more hero health, more cards in its opening
  hand ([[R182]]), or more mana crystals every turn.
- Each Combat artifact the human holds changes the human's handicap once per level, as an Economic
  artifact's map effect counts ([[§15.4]]): more cards in the opening hand ([[R182]]), or Armor the
  hero starts the fight with ([[R2922000]]).

An extra crystal raises the enemy's `manaBonus` and its `manaCap` together, because under [[R181]] a
bonus alone stops at the cap, and an Easy enemy's cap is already the one a human plays to: one more
crystal every turn means one more at the top too. A negative amount counts as none, so a content's
mistake can make no setup the engine refuses. An unknown curse and a held artifact of another kind
change nothing, and an enemy's own artifacts are read only for its Presence: the final boss's relic
helps nobody in its own fight. With no curse and no Combat artifact, the setup is the one a run got
before, field for field.

The placeholders' effects and amounts are strawmen until the designer's lists land ([[§15.13]]):
the curses give 5 enemy health, 1 opening card and 1 crystal; Thick Skin 1 Armor a level, Quick
Start 1 opening card a level, the Crown 2 Armor a level and the final relic 2 opening cards a level.
Armor cuts every hit and is never spent, which is why the issue's suggested 3 Armor for Thick Skin
(9 at its highest level) was cut to 1.
