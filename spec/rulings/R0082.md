A turn ends by itself when the active player's only legal actions are ending the turn, conceding and offering a draw; the engine emits `turnAutoEnded` and ends the turn. A draw offer does not hold the turn open, so a player with nothing else to do cannot offer on that turn ([[R36]]). A player may turn this off for themselves ([[R345]])
