The fuzz monitor's I19 (issue #4454) found the state check's switch out of Defense Position ([[§4.5]] step 1) reaching a Dormant card: a Unit made Dormant in Defense Position (Stinkyscape+ #51 Chains Bind Me, [[R3557002]]) was switched to Attack Position, `positionSwitched` naming it, when an aura barred Defense ([[R1849006]]) or it was Indestructible, had Can't Defend or was Evasive ([[R2179000]], [[R2657000]], [[R3543002]]).
**It keeps its position.** A Dormant card is no Unit for any rule that reads the Units, no aura reaches it and nothing switches it ([[R1839000]]), so the switch passes it by and it stays in Defense Position while it sleeps; it cannot defend meanwhile, since nothing attacks it. The first state check after it awakens switches it as it would any Unit. The fuzz monitor's I17 holds a settled board to the same rule.
A replay this change plays differently is refused as `rules_changed` rather than shown wrong ([[R767000]]).
Rejected: switching it, which has a rule reach a card nothing else may, and an aura reach it besides.
