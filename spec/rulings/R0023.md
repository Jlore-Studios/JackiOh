Blocks Vanilla, Transform (Transmogulate on the board included), Fuse-onto, Degrade and Upgrade ([[R386]]) on the Immutable card itself; Radiant still allowed; Prejudiced Postdoc may copy an Immutable unit because the Vanilla applies to the copy, and its Cry, a Unit's, may target one ([[R781001]]). Since patch v0.3.7 Immutable has a targeting half too: while in play, Spells, Field Spells and Field Traps can't target it ([[R781001]]), so a Spell that would do one of these to it can no longer be aimed at it in play
