Since issue #1704 the card Meditative #37 CN in a Bottle's base face adds costs (1) more, up to (4). That is a `costMod` of +1 that never takes the card's own cost ([[R65]]: its printed or overridden cost and its `costMod`, with no player discount) past (4), as a Nerf's cost row stops at (4) ([[§6.3]]). A card at (3) or less costs (1) more, a card at (4) or more costs what it did, and an X-cost card, whose X is no cost a number may move, is unchanged.

The rider is `add_random_from_catalog`'s `costModUpTo`, applied once the card is in the hand, so a card the full hand burns takes no price ([[R215]]). The Radiant face's card still costs (0). Through v0.4.0f the base face's card took no raise; `script_v0_4_0f` keeps that for those versions' replays ([[R767000]]).
