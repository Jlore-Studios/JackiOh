Amending [[R1751000]]'s picker, which drew each ability's choice card ([[R2646000]]) with a picture seeded from the card's id and the ability's, so nothing on it said where the ability came from. A fused card's abilities are its ingredients' ([[R102]]): a launched Starship's are its Weapon pieces' ([[R1852000]], [[R1852003]]) and an Equipped Unit's include its Weapon's ([[R1086]]), and two weapons' abilities can read alike.

- **The engine names the card.** Each `ActivationView` of a fused card carries `printedOn`, the ingredient whose text prints the ability, `{ defId, radiant? }` ([[§10.8]]). A Fuse of a Fuse names the innermost card; `radiant` is the face the fusion runs that text on ([[R469]]). The list follows the composed script's (`fuse.rs` keeps one source per ability beside `Script.activations`), so two copies of one card name it twice and an ability `has` leaves out takes its source with it. It is absent on a card's own ability and on the engine's: the Dormant Starship's launch and a Cloaked card's Reveal.
- **The picker wears its art.** In the Activate picker each choice card's picture is the `printedOn` card's art, or the activated card's own when there is no `printedOn`, read as a card face reads it (its name, tags and type from the catalog). Its name and text are unchanged: the ability's words and its uses this turn. A longer list keeps its plain rows.
- **A launched Starship always asks.** Pressing the Activate control of a Starship that has launched (`UnitView.starship.launched`, in a unit zone or standing in the backrow, [[R2249000]]) opens the picker even with one ability, so the player sees which weapon fires before it does; the pick sends the `activate` naming it. A Dormant Starship's one ability is its launch, no weapon, and still sends on the press, as any other card with one ability does. A drag from the control to a target is unchanged: the drop picks the shot.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): `legalActions`, the refusal and every rule are unchanged, and no card plays differently. So the golden traces' view text leaves `printedOn` out (`testkit::golden::view_texts`), as it leaves out a prompt's asking card ([[R2646000]]): a trace recorded on either side of this change replays on the other. It names a card the viewer reads through the fused card, whose definition keeps its `ingredients` ([[R468]]), so the fuzz's hidden-information invariant (I6) reads each ingredient an ability is printed on as no secret, as it reads a Starship's pieces, whatever copy of it waits in a library or a hand. Rejected: the client working out each ability's card from the fused id and the catalog, which needs the engine's `has` filters and ids made unique (`power#2`), and a full card face per option, which would draw one face several times on a card that prints several abilities itself.
