The evaluation ([[§9.9]]) counts two harms to the opponent's coming plays, each at a weight of its own in `AI_EVAL` and 0 in `GREEDY_EVAL`.

Each open Nerf ([[R386]]) on a card in the opponent's hand counts against that player at `enemy_hand_nerf` (0.5, a crystal's `hand_cost_delta`, which is what the seat's own hand already takes off its own dearer card, [[R65]]). A Nerf is read off what was written on the card, a step each: a crystal its `costMod` made it dearer (any price hike written on the card alike); a `TUNE_STAT_TOTAL` (4) of stats its attack and health together stand below its face, or part of one; a keyword it lost that is not harmful, or a harmful one it gained (Crater, [[R2657005]]); and a step a numbered keyword, its X or a declared number moved the worse way (more is worse for Tribute and better for every other X; a declared number goes by its `better`). What an Upgrade wrote counts nothing, and an Upgrade and a Nerf on the same number cancel. A secret Nerf ([[R1845000]]) never counts, since no seat reads it. `redact` drops a hidden card's changes ([[R185]]), so only the Nerfs the line itself puts on the hand and those on a card the seat may read count. While a delayed discard of that hand waits, the Nerfs keep the hand's own `doomed_hand_discount` share, as the cards do. The term reads the hand, not what wrote on it, so every Nerf there counts alike: IA #137 Patch Tuesday Exploit's, the Radiant Cries of IA #121 Packet Sniffer and IA #142 The Jailbreaker, and IA #233 Model Collapse's start-of-turn ones.

Each live `RandomTargetsNextSpell` ([[R4590001]], IA #130 Prompt Injection) counts against its side at `random_targets` (1.0). The price is flat, since the seat cannot see the Spells that player holds. It counts while the modifier waits and nothing once a Spell spends it. It sits in `material`, not in `coming_refreshes` as [[R5122000]]'s price hike does, so a reply that spends one keeps no credit for it: that play already drew its random targets.

Not counted: IA #127 Bad Prompt's transform, since whether it harms hangs on the card it replaced, which the seat cannot see, and the random targets of IA #142 The Jailbreaker's aura, a flag on a Unit and no modifier. This follows Hearthstone: a card made worse in the opponent's hand is worth something to the player who did it.
