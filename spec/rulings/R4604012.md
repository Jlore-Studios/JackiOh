In the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 3928, Meditative #49.1 YileGPT Prime's Cry Nerfed every card, a Unit's ability and no Spell's. Among its Nerfs was Infinite Assault #239's Immune to Spells, taken away (`degraded`, the keyword removed). A Spell played later in the same step exiled that Unit, which was then no longer immune. I21 ([[R4453000]]) judged the exile against the immunity the Unit held as the step began. As a Unit that leaves the field is free for the rest of the step ([[R4599015]]), a Unit whose Immune to Spells a Nerf takes away now is too. The Nerf itself is judged first, so a Spell's own Nerf of an immune Unit is still a finding.

Hearthstone semantics: a minion that lost a keyword no longer has what it gave.
