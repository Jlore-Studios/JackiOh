**Why.** The owner balances what a card gives: The Mother Pancake (C+ #12) adds a random one of eight Pancakes each end of turn, Jlockeed's Evil Blueprints (M #97) Discovers one of nine buildings, and every output should be about as good as its siblings, so that no player feels bad about the one they got or picked. [[R4672003]] totals, under each source card, the seats whose copy of it made each card ([[R4672001]]) and the seat-games each card was offered and picked in by its Discover-like choices ([[R4672002]]), each by the source's face; this note says how a page reads them. The question each row answers is "is this output better than its siblings", not "is it better than a Created card" ([[R4082000]]).

**The rows** (`output_figures`). One row per card the source made or offered on the face asked, each a catalog card: on All both faces of the source, on Normal its normal copies', on Radiant its Radiant copies' ([[R4673001]]'s `face`). A card neither made nor offered on that face has no row.

**A row's figures**:

- **games**: the seats whose source made it (`seen`).
- **share**: its games over every output's games on the face: how often the source gives it. A card only offered has 0.
- **adjusted**: `50% + τ²/(τ² + SE²) · (delta − center)` with [[R2895000]]'s 95% range, as [[R4676000]] reads a variant, its delta its seats' win rate less its **siblings'**: the seats whose source made one of its other outputs, every output's seats added less its own (`output_sides`). With fewer sibling games than the floor it has no rate, so a source with one output has none.
- **the field** (`output_field`): [[R3022000]]'s random-effects estimate over every output of every source of the report the catalog holds on the same face, whatever the page filters, from the outputs with `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games on both sides. Its centre is how far a typical output reads from its siblings, so one source's outputs sit on one scale. Below `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` such outputs it is `StatsField::PRIOR`'s.
- **tier**: in the face's band (`StatsFace::band`); none, and "not enough games", below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games of its own ([[R654]]).
- **offered** and **pick rate**, for a source that offered any card: the seat-games the card was offered in on the face, and those it was picked in over them, to three places ([[R1994000]]'s `round_share`). Pick rate is how players rate it, so it is shown at any count, below the floor too. A source that offered nothing sends neither.

**The order.** Best adjusted first; a row below the floor or with no rate after every row with one, then by games, then by id.

**The API.** `GET /api/stats/cards/:id` answers `outputs`, the rows in that order, each `key` (the output's catalog id), `label`, `cost`, `rarity`, `games`, `share`, `adjustedWinRate`, `adjustedLow`, `adjustedHigh`, `tier` and `hasEnoughGames`, and `offered` and `pickRate` where they apply. It reads the page's `patch=` over the window that patch's table reads and the `face=` asked, as `variants` does, so a patch past the caller's is 403 `patch_dev_only` before anything is read ([[R4677000]]). A card the catalog lacks is left out, so every row's drill-down opens. A card that made and offered nothing answers an empty list; an older client ignores the field.

**What it still measures.**

- **Seats overlap.** A seat that made two outputs in a game counts in both, so its games sit on both sides of each and shrink the deltas: The Mother Pancake makes one every turn, and a seat makes several.
- **The noise is read as if the two sides were independent**, so a range is wider than it would be, never narrower.
- **Call to Chaos's effects** (#95) are no cards, so [[R4672003]] records none and they have no row; the cards an effect makes (its Chaos Golem, its Rush Tokens, its random Units) are its rows.
- **A card given to the other seat** counts on the seat that made it ([[R2089000]]), as one kept does: Wildar (S #149) adds a random Call to Chaos to the opponent's hand and a Radiant one to its own, so an edition's row holds both, read by the games of Wildar's seat.
- **A source with one output** has share 1 and no rate: it has no sibling.

Amended by [[R4707001]] (issue #4707): Call to Chaos's effects, and every other outcome a card rolls from a known list, are read against their siblings in `rolled`, beside `outputs`.

**Rejected.**

- **The Created field** ([[R4082000]]): it says whether an output is better than an average made card, not whether it is better than the outputs it was rolled or picked beside.
- **The source's plays as the baseline**: a source whose every output is strong would read every output above an even game, and the source's own figures already say that.
- **A share over the source's plays**: one play can make several outputs, and the shares would sum past 1.
- **"Not enough games" on a pick rate**: a pick rate is no win rate, and players' choices are the figure this asks for at any count.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
