## R1081000 — Flying units may stand in the backrow

Built (#1081). A Unit with Flying in its controller's hand is offered empty, unlocked, unreserved
backrow zones of its side in addition to its unit-row zones: [[R64]], [[R391]] and [[R688]] apply as
written, with `freed_by_tribute` read on either row, and a Locked or reserved backrow zone is
neither offered nor accepted. A Flying Unit with Stack is additionally offered occupied zones of
either row via `accepts_stack_card`, as a Stack card is ([[§6.2]]), but never stands on a carrier: a
carrier takes a Unit only as its Unit ([[R446]]), Flying or not, so one that has taken its one Unit
takes no Flying Stack Unit either ([[R653]]). A Unit that cannot fly still goes in the units row,
except onto a carrier ([[R446]]).

A Flying Unit standing in the backrow is a Unit for every rule: it attacks and is attacked under
[[R783002]] and [[R783003]], counts among its controller's Units, and is hit by "all Units" effects.
It switches position and readies again at its controller's turn start, and the state check collects
it at 0 health, as for any Unit. It is public to both players like any Unit, and keeps damage, buffs,
counters, memory and granted keywords wherever it stands. In Defense Position it has landed
([[R783003]]): anything may attack it and its Taunt binds every attacker. Nothing else about Flying
changes.

A Unit stands in the backrow only because it was played there as a Flying Unit, read where it was in
hand: from then on it is a standing Unit whatever its text says, so a Silence, a Vanilla or an aura
lifted mid-swap never unseats it. It stays standing when it is stolen, swapped, rotated or restored by a
Rollback, lands in the open backrow zone the move names, and returns standing from Reborn. A card played
onto it with Stack leaves it dormant beneath ([[R13]]), and a move that sets the zone down whole (a
swap, a rotation, a Rollback) sets it down beneath its top again. No other
arrival puts a Unit in a backrow zone no carrier holds: a summon, or a move out of a carrier's zone, goes
as before. It holds its triggers once, is replaced by a Transform as a Unit, by a Unit, and a Tribute
empties its zone only when nothing lies beneath it ([[R13]]). Beside it, "adjacent" (Cleave, "adjacent"
effects, a redirect) means another Unit standing in the neighbouring backrow zones, never a Trap or a
Field Spell, which is no Unit. An Immutable fuser takes no Unit in, a Flying Stack Unit included
([[R653]], [[R23]]).
