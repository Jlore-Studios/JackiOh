## R1081000 — Flying units may stand in the backrow

Built (#1081). A Unit with Flying in its controller's hand is offered empty, unlocked, unreserved
backrow zones of its side in addition to its unit-row zones: [[R64]], [[R391]] and [[R688]] apply as
written, with `freed_by_tribute` read on either row, and a Locked or reserved backrow zone is
neither offered nor accepted. A Flying Unit with Stack is additionally offered occupied zones of
either row via `accepts_stack_card`, as a Stack card is ([[§6.2]]), but never stands on a carrier: a
carrier takes a Unit only as its Unit ([[R446]]), Flying or not, so one that has taken its one Unit
takes no Flying Stack Unit either ([[R653]]). A Unit that cannot fly still goes in the units row,
except onto a carrier ([[R446]]).

A Flying Unit standing in the backrow is a Unit for every rule: it attacks and is attacked under
[[R783002]] and [[R783003]], counts among its controller's Units, and is hit by "all Units" effects.
It switches position and readies again at its controller's turn start, and the state check collects
it at 0 health, as for any Unit. It is public to both players like any Unit, and keeps damage, buffs,
counters, memory and granted keywords wherever it stands. In Defense Position it has landed
([[R783003]]): anything may attack it and its Taunt binds every attacker. Nothing else about Flying
changes.

Every read of "a Unit on the field" or "your Units" takes it as it takes one in a unit zone: an aura
on your Units reaches it, and so does "your Units on the field" in a card scope (C+ #40
Appropriations), a Vanilla keeps it alive at 1 health, a captor captures it ([[R1284]]) and one standing
there captures beneath itself, its Cry may be triggered, it is the Unit that destroyed what its hit
doomed, it goes Berserk and may attack again, Allure and a would-die replacement take it, a Spell's
pick of it summons an interposer from hand, an Equip card may be played onto it ([[R1086]]), and its
numbers read through every layer. Its return from Reborn, standing, is a Unit summoned (C #5 Tesla
answers it). A count of permanents counts it once. Like an animated card, it is no Field Spell, Trap or backrow card
to an effect that counts or hits those ([[§6.1]] Animated): "every backrow card" (C #51 Back Breaker,
C+ #5 Guy Att) and "target backrow card" (#36 Magic Jammed) pass it by, while "every card" or "each
permanent" of both rows reaches it once. Its lane is its column: from its backrow zone it is in midlane
in the midlane's, "every other card in this lane" is the lane's other three zones, "the Unit to the
right of this" is the one standing in the next backrow zone, it is across from the Units in the
opposing lane (C+ #19.2 Jungle Loser's Bot Loser), and M #97.5 Bunker strikes it in its lane. A Crater
Unit standing there Locks that backrow zone as it arrives ([[R783010]]).

A Unit stands in the backrow only because it was played there as a Flying Unit, read where it was in
hand: from then on it is a standing Unit whatever its text says, so a Silence, a Vanilla or an aura
lifted mid-swap never unseats it. It stays standing when it is stolen, swapped, rotated or restored by a
Rollback, lands in the open backrow zone the move names, and returns standing from Reborn. A card played
onto it with Stack leaves it dormant beneath ([[R13]]), where #92 Felinor Fiender still counts it, and a
move that sets the zone down whole (a swap, a rotation, a Rollback) sets it down beneath its top again.
No other arrival puts a Unit in a backrow zone no carrier holds: a summon, or a move out of a carrier's
zone, goes as before, so a Flying #55 Lava Golem played into the backrow and summoned for the opponent
([[R360]]) goes into their unit zone of that lane. It holds its triggers once, is replaced by a
Transform as a Unit, by a Unit, and a Tribute empties its zone only when nothing lies beneath it
([[R13]]). Beside it, "adjacent" (Cleave, "adjacent"
effects, a redirect) means another Unit standing in the neighbouring backrow zones, never a Trap or a
Field Spell, which is no Unit. An Immutable fuser takes no Unit in, a Flying Stack Unit included
([[R653]], [[R23]]).
