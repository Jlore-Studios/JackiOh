## R783004 — Cloaked: a card that is not a Trap may be set as one

Built (#783). Any card that is not a Trap may carry Cloaked: a Unit, a Spell or a Field Spell, never a
Trap or a Field Trap, which `legal_actions` offers no face-down play of whatever it prints. A Cloaked
card in its controller's hand, its keyword read through the layers ([[§10.4]]) — printed, given, or
lent by an aura ([[R783007]]) — is offered, beside its ordinary plays, a play face-down into each empty,
unlocked backrow zone at its normal price (mana, X and Tribute), declaring no targets or modes: the
`play` action's `faceDown` is `cloaked`, a timing that is none. It is announced and placed as a Trap with
a fresh id ([[R448]], [[R227]]), counts as a Field Trap play for a set Unit or Field Spell and a Trap
play for a set Spell, hides like any set card ([[R1046]]: the opponent sees a face-down zone and its
cost, and only its controller its `revealAt` and its Reveal), and its own text is dormant
([[R1040]]). A card in a graveyard is never set by its Cloaked: a play from there is the card as
printed. Knowledge Breaker's, its Prime's and Paranoia's face-down permissions (`face_down_play`) are
unchanged; a card both may set has both plays.
