Small Time Recruits draws three different random cards ([[R60]], the match rng) among its controller's deck's Units whose cost as it stands in the deck is 1 ([[R65]], [[R66]]; X-cost cards excluded), fewer when fewer exist, each a named [[§2.4]] draw (the hand cap, cast on draw, the draw counter), the set read once ([[R113]]). The Radiant face makes each drawn card still in its holder's hand Radiant once it arrives there; one the hand cap burned or cast on draw stays as it was. The draws are hidden from the opponent as any draw is. The brief's MD-D17.
