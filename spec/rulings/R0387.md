A card never Discovers or makes a random copy of itself unless its text names a pool that holds it. The exclusion is by definition id, not index, since an index repeats across sets ([[§5]]), and it holds on every path by which a card makes a card from a pool: added to a hand, shuffled in, summoned, Discovered, cast (C+ #47 Jogg's Box, C+ #38.1 Solarius Prime), transformed into (C+ #73.1), fused in (C+ #31 Fusion Lab, C+ #43 AI Slop, C+ #73's deck fusion) or put in another card's place; a fused card excludes every ingredient's definition. Only the Call to Chaos cards (every edition: #95, C+ #73 and Meditative #95, [[R1240]]) name such a pool — "cast a random Call to Chaos" ([[R28]]) and C+ #73's "replace your deck with random Call to Chaos" — so every other generator leaves itself out: C+ #54 Book of Books makes no Book of Books, C+ #58 Fruit Basket no Fruit Basket, C+ #23 Dropshipping no Dropshipping ([[R382]]), C+ #47 Jogg's Box never casts itself, and C+ #27 Zephrys Zealotism never offers itself. Copies are not generation: "summon a copy of this" (#12), "fill your board with copies of this" (C+ #46.1), Echo repeats and C+ #14 Forever&'s return are unaffected. A test sweeps every generating script with a seeded rng and asserts that its own id never comes out
