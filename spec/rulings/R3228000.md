`/decks` waited on a plain page. Its loading screens (the account's read, then the decks') were a bare `.app-shell`
with a bar that had no gear (`showSettings` off, so a settings dialog opened there could not close when the decks
arrived, #1524), and when the decks landed the workshop replaced it with the tavern's frame: another background,
another bar (`header.db-header`, the gear, the workshop's own extras) and the title redrawn. The bar's look and
contents jumped, on a wait the skeleton (R2115000) was meant to make calm. Two other waits were still a bare
sentence ("Loading…" in the account's linked sign-in methods, "Checking…" in its two-step panel), and the workshop's
empty rail was a `ws-hint` paragraph, where every other empty menu panel draws `EmptyState` (R2108000).

Every state of `/decks` now draws one frame, `game/deckbuilder/WorkshopFrame.tsx`: the tavern `app-shell` (the same
class string, so the same background), the `db-header` bar of R3218000 (Back left, the Jlore pill, Friends,
language and the gear right) and the `PageTitle` "Decks" of R3223000. The route draws it around the skeleton while
the account is read and while the decks are, around the account's error, the banned notice and the decks' error;
the loaded workshop draws it around its editor, with its history, cosmetics and save line in the bar's extras. The
gear is always drawn. Both loading returns have the same shape, so one `Skeleton` stays mounted from the account's
read to the decks', and only the body changes when the decks land. `SKELETON_DELAY_MS` still applies (R2108000): a
read that answers sooner shows no blocks, and a slow one shows them in a frame that was already standing.

`LinkedAccounts` waits with a `Skeleton` of `rows` and `TwoStepSettings` with a `panel`, each keeping its old
sentence (`Loading…`, `Checking…`) as the visually hidden `role="status"` line. The workshop's rail, with no deck,
draws `EmptyState` with its words and a "New deck" action (testids `deck-list-empty` and `deck-list-empty-new`; the
main pane's `workshop-empty` and its own action are untouched). The Friends panel's "No friends yet" and the
cosmetic shard bar's "Nothing to buy yet" draw `EmptyState` too, with the testids they had; the existing tests
`FriendsButton.test.tsx` and `Shop.test.tsx` assert the empty state around them. What stays text, and why, is the
list in `apps/web/README.md` beside R2115000's paragraph: a button's or field's busy word, the deck history
popover (which sizes itself to its words), one line inside a panel that already has content (the leaderboard's
among them, which spec 46 measures as it is), an option's text and a card face's.

The cost, taken: a settings dialog opened from the loading bar's gear closes when the decks land, because the
loading frame and the loaded workshop are different trees (what #1524's guard had prevented). The wait is short
or skipped when the last visit's decks are kept (R2125000), and the issue asks for the gear on the loading bar.

Rejected: lifting one bar above both states, which would need the workshop's extras portalled into a shared bar and
would keep the dialog open, at the price of a second owner of the bar; keeping the gear off while loading, which
the issue rules out; and fixing only the loading shell, which would leave the error, Retry, loading path going from
the plain page to the tavern one.

Hearthstone semantics do not reach a page's chrome. **Proof.** `routes/decks.test.tsx` renders `/decks` while the
account is read, while the decks are read and once they land, and asserts the same frame (the tavern workshop
`app-shell`), the same bar (Back first, the gear last) and the same `h1` "Decks" in all three, and, with
`SKELETON_DELAY_MS`, that a read which answers at once never shows the skeleton's blocks while a held one shows
them with the bar already drawn. `game/deckbuilder/DeckWorkshop.test.tsx` asserts that the workshop with no deck
draws the rail's `EmptyState`, whose "New deck" makes and opens one, and none with a deck kept.
`auth/LinkedAccounts.test.tsx` and `auth/TwoStepSettings.test.tsx` assert the `rows` and `panel` skeletons whose
sentences are `role="status"` lines, and the panels that replace them.
