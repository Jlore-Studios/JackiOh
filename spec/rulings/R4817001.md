[[§10.4]] takes a used Reborn off a card only until it gains Reborn again, as it does a spent Divine Shield, and a grant of either puts it up again. A card made Radiant on the field takes its new face's keywords at once ([[§5.2]]), so a Radiant face that prints a Reborn its base face lacks gives the card that Reborn, a used one included, as it already gave back a spent Divine Shield. C+ #6 Wrong-House Attacker that came back through a Reborn an effect gave it ([[R21]]'s pool holds Reborn; the grant went with [[R78]]'s reset, and the Reborn stayed used on the body, [[§4.5]]) and is then made Radiant by C+ #65.4 Golden Grape has its Radiant face's Reborn, and so do Core #25 4-mana 7/7 and Core #56 Jilliax, among the cards that print Reborn on the Radiant face only.

A Reborn or a Divine Shield printed on both faces is not newly gained, so it stays used. A Vanilla card prints neither. De-Radiant has no mirror: no card's base face prints a Divine Shield or a Reborn its Radiant face lacks.

Rejected: a used Reborn kept through the face change, which would have the Radiant face print a Reborn the card cannot have, against §5.2 and the shield's own rule.
