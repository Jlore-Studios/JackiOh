Temporary is a card keyword: a card is Temporary while the set of keywords of a card in a hand (printed on its running face, or granted) holds it. At the end of its owner's turn, in cleanup after every end-of-turn trigger, trap and delayed effect ([[R62]]), each Temporary card in their hand is discarded, in hand order, with no choice and no rng draw; it is a discard ([[§6.3]]), so "whenever you discard" sees each one and a unit-token card ceases to exist ([[R11]]). A Temporary card in the other player's hand waits for that player's own turn end. It does nothing on the field, in a deck or in a graveyard; [[R215]] takes a granted Temporary off a card that reaches a graveyard. A Vanilla card loses a printed Temporary and keeps a given one ([[§10.4]]). The keyword is not in [[R21]]'s random pool, and it is not temporary mana ([[§2.3]]): the engine names the card keyword `Temporary` and the mana `gainMana`, so the two cannot be confused. C+ #54 Book of Books gives each Book it adds, in both its faces, a granted Temporary once the Book is in the hand (a Book the full hand burns carries none); the card's printed text names it (#480)
