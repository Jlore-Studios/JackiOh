S #77 Yuma's Radiant face prints Lucky 1 beside the Cry that makes a trade offer ([[R1845002]]). The roll its own text makes is the opponent's Discover: which of their other hand cards are offered ([[R50]], [[R60]]). Lucky ([[§6.1]]) rolls that again and keeps the better, so it needs a "better" (ST-S18-3).

- **The Lucky carried.** The offer records the Lucky its card has as the offer is made, printed plus given (`lucky_on`, [[R1438]]), in the offer's own data, so it holds whatever becomes of the card before the offer comes due ([[R76]]). A card with no Lucky records nothing, so every offer made before this ruling, and Yuma's base face, draws as it always did.
- **The better set.** With Lucky X, the Discover's options are drawn 1 + X times, each draw as the plain offer's, and the set kept is the one whose cheapest card costs most, the cost [[R65]]'s outside play (`effective_cost`); on a tie the earlier draw stays. The opponent picks the card to give, and will give the worst one offered, so the offerer gains by raising the worst option.
- **No idle draws.** A hand with no more cards than the options (`DISCOVER_OPTIONS`) is offered whole, so it is drawn once, Lucky or not.

Rejected: keeping the set with the highest total cost, which an offer of one dear card and two cheap ones wins although the opponent gives a cheap one; and letting the offerer see the draws, which no Discover does.
