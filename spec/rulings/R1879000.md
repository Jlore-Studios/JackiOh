Anomaly Found! replaces its caster's side with a random Anomaly's board ([[R1855000]]). The board is drawn from the match rng as the Spell resolves, each equally likely: on the base face one of the seven boards the designer's list leaves out of the Radiant three (WH Wormhole, BF Battlefields, AL Asteroid Field: Large, AT Asteroid Field: Small, SP Wrecked Spice Platform, MT Monument, DH Drone Hideouts), on the Radiant face one of the three rich ones (AN Asteroid Field: Narcour, AA Asteroid Field: Axnit, FO Frontier Outpost) (ST-S20-2). The card's text names no board, since "Wormhole" or "Asteroid Field" would name a card ([[R279]]).

- **The boards.** Each is the designer's list, zone by zone, an unnamed zone left empty and a "Radiant" card laid Radiant. The list's "Small Asteroid" is S #169 Tiny Asteroid (ST-S20-3).
- **The pools.** An asteroid field's "only gives Narcor and red Narcor" (AN) and "only gives Axnit" (AA) are a `resourcePool` rider on each card the board lays ([[R1855002]]), so its asteroids mine only those Resources.
- **SP's backrow.** Its three Drone Interceptors stand in their backrow zones by their Flying ([[R1879001]]).
- **BF's Drone Battleship.** Its three layers are one pile in unit zone 2, Meltdown at the bottom, then Propagation, the Shield on top, as S #36's layered summon lays it ([[R1842000]], [[R1879002]]).
