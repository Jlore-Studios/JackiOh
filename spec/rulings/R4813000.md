Both seats of a round act at once ([[§13.3]]), so the server asks every AI seat that owes a move on the same state, and plays each answer as it arrives whenever the engine still accepts it. A pass crosses the duels ([[§13.5]]), so two answers decided on one state can each stay legal after the other and undo each other:

- a pass, judged on the teammate's duel ([[§13.7]], [[R1723000]]), lands after the teammate's End turn, so the card goes to a seat that has ended and the passer has paid for it;
- the teammate's End turn, decided before the pass arrived, lands after it and ends the turn with the card just passed to it unplayed.

Hearthstone has no second duel to follow. So this follows what [[§13.8]] does with a hold that changed and what [[R1452]] does with a refused move on a moved-on state: a move whose premise moved under it is dropped, and the seat is decided for again.

- **What is dropped.** A move decided at a log seq the game has since moved past, when it is a pass, or an End turn while the seat's hand (its cards' instance ids, in order) differs from the hand it held when it was asked. This holds for the search's move and the job's fallback alike.
- **What is kept.** Every other move, a play or an attack in the seat's own duel among them, which the teammate's duel reaches only through the pool the search already prices ([[R4188000]]). Dropping every stale move would starve one seat while the other plays, since each action of one would void the other's decision in flight.
- **What follows.** The seat pauses (`DUOS_AI_STEP_DELAY_MS`) and is asked again on the state as it stands, its AI stream moving on as after a refused move. A pass decided while the teammate keeps acting may be dropped more than once; once the teammate has ended, the seat's fresh decision no longer passes to it.

Nothing reaches the log or a replay but the actions written, so a co-op game still folds from its seed and its log.
