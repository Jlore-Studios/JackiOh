The beam expands the first `root_branching` (at the root) or `branching` of `candidate_actions` that are
not End Turn, plus End Turn ([[R1171001]]), and `candidate_actions` ends in the position switches, the
last tier before End Turn. A board or a hand with that many earlier moves (below the root, `branching` is
6) therefore cut every switch, so a line never stood a unit down that the opponent's reply would stand
down on its own side (`AI_REPLY.defense_switches`, [[§9.9]]): its beam tried the stand-down only where
fewer than six earlier moves stood in front of it.

This note keeps the first `AI_SEARCH.position_switches` (2) **stand-downs** in `candidate_actions`' order
(a switch of a unit in Attack Position to Defense Position, [[§4.1]]) that the cut left out, after the
prefix and before End Turn. A stand-down the prefix already holds is not added twice, so a level's moves
number at most the width, plus that constant, plus End Turn. A switch back to Attack Position is not kept:
it stays where the prefix leaves it, as before. The kept moves are read from the seat's own units' public
positions, so no hidden card is read. The policy prior ([[R1171001]]) re-sorts the prefix only; the
stand-downs are chosen from the heuristic's order and added after it, with the prior on or off, and the
prior itself still never adds, drops or changes a move.

It adds no rule of the game and no evaluation weight (`AI_EVAL.position_grants` stays 0, so Defense's Taunt
and Armor are still read only through the damage they stop, [[§10.4]]); it widens the lines the beam
compares by at most two children per expanded line.
