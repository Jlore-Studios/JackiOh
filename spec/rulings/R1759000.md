The beam expands the first `root_branching` (at the root) or `branching` of `candidate_actions` that are
not End Turn, plus End Turn ([[R1171001]]), and `candidate_actions` ends in the position switches, the
last tier before End Turn. A board or a hand with that many earlier moves (below the root, `branching` is
6) therefore cut every switch, so a line never stood a unit down that the opponent's reply would stand
down on its own side (`AI_REPLY.defense_switches`, [[§9.9]]): its beam tried the stand-down only where
fewer than six earlier moves stood in front of it.

This note keeps the first `AI_SEARCH.position_switches` (2) **stand-downs** in `candidate_actions`' order that
the cut left out, after the prefix and before End Turn. A stand-down is a switch of a unit in Attack Position
to Defense Position ([[§4.1]]) where a unit of the opponent's would kill it and survive the fight (the AI's own
`kills_and_survives`): a unit that cannot win its next fight. A stand-down the prefix already holds is not added
twice, so a level's moves number at most the width, plus that constant, plus End Turn. A switch back to Attack
Position, and a switch of a unit no enemy unit would beat, are not kept: they stay where the prefix leaves them,
as before. The kept moves are read from the board's public positions, attacks and health, so no hidden card is
read. The policy prior ([[R1171001]]) re-sorts the prefix only; the stand-downs are chosen from the heuristic's
order and added after it, with the prior on or off, and the prior itself still never adds, drops or changes a
move.

It adds no rule of the game and no evaluation weight (`AI_EVAL.position_grants` stays 0, so Defense's Taunt
and Armor are still read only through the damage they stop, [[§10.4]]); it widens the lines the beam
compares by at most two children per expanded line.

[[R2021000]] counts an enemy unit in a stand-down only when it could attack the unit on its next turn: in Attack Position or with Deft in Defense Position, able to swing, and barred from the unit by no attack restriction.
