A card evolves once ([[R3536000]]): its first evolve marks it `evolved`, and a later one does nothing.
**Endless Evolve** is an Evolve that may happen any number of times: a card whose text has it
(`StaticFlags.endless_evolve`, which `evolution::can_evolve` reads) evolves every time its condition is
met. Each evolution is one, as ever: it counts one for its controller (`evolutions`), reports `evolved`,
runs its Evolve text, and the evolution multiplier's extra runs apply to it as to any first evolution
([[R3536003]]).

Its condition is the card's own trigger evolving itself ([[R3536001]]): each time that trigger fires, the
card evolves again. A standing "Evolves when" ([[R3536001]]) is met once while it holds, since it is read
at every settled state check ([[§4.5]]) and would otherwise evolve the card at each one: it evolves an
Endless Evolve card once a stay, as any card. A number only the Evolve text reads stays live for a Buff
or a Nerf after the card evolves, since it will be read again ([[R3888000]]).
