Each Terminus Starship comes with a fixed loadout worth 5–12 mana. Placement follows
`open_starship_zone`, and summons repeat no ship until all ten are out. They are Dormant
Starships, so dying pieces fuse into the first one.

**The loadouts.** Each of the ten Terminus Starships is a token hull holding a fixed list of
Starship Pieces (Ark of Ashes 5 mana of pieces to Vacuum Psalm's 12), fused into it oldest first as
it is summoned. The lists are the cards' own data (`TERMINUS_STARSHIPS`, each token's `PIECES`),
summing each piece's printed cost (`query_cost`, [[R65]]); every piece is a non-token Stinkyscape
card printing Starship Piece ([[R1878000]]). Rejected: the foundation text's random pieces worth
5–12 mana, which would leave the ten names cosmetic and need a budget algorithm.

**The summons.** A made Starship lands in its controller's first open backrow zone, the middle
first, then in their unit row the same way (`open_starship_zone`, [[R2249000]]'s order without its
stacking: never a pile or a Locked zone), standing in the backrow as a Flying Unit does
([[R1081000]]). With no open zone the summon and the rest are not summoned. The picks come in a
shuffled round, so no blueprint repeats until every one is out; nothing is drawn when there is no
blueprint or no zone ([[R129]]). A made Starship is Dormant with an empty memory until its pieces
fuse, so no state check reads it at 0/0; its summon fires no Cry ([[R1]]).

**They are Dormant Starships.** A made hull is a Dormant Starship like any other: a piece that dies
fuses its copy into the first one in lane order ([[R1852000]], [[R1852001]], [[R1852005]]), and its
awakening is its launch ([[R1852003]]).
