[[R2477000]] and [[R2565000]] priced a card's start-of-turn and end-of-turn hooks by one firing at the decision's root, on the board as it stands. A heal reads nothing there when nothing is damaged: Stinkyscape+ #41 Mend Projector ("End of turn: Heal all Units to full"), #40 Mender and #123 Oct ("End of turn: Heal all allies 8") counted no more than their bodies, though what a heal is worth is the damage it undoes, and that damage comes in the opponent's turn, after the seat's turn ends and before the hook fires again. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** Where a finalist is scored after the opponent's reply (`reply_score`, and again for a countdown it fires), each of the seat's own cards with a start-of-turn or an end-of-turn hook on its field or in its backrow is fired once more on a sandbox copy ([[R923000]], automatic turn end off, [[R345]]) of the board the reply left (`replied_turn_hook_prices`, `turn_hook_probe`), and that price replaces the root's for the card, its start-of-the-opponent's-turn part staying the root's since the reply has run that turn ([[R2689000]]). Those hooks fire next on the seat's coming turn, on that board, so a heal counts the damage the reply dealt and an enemy unit it would also heal counts against it. The multiplier of [[R821]] reads the board scored, as before. The probe takes at most `AI_TURN_HOOK_REPLY_PROBE_NODES` nodes of the decision's ([[R185]]: it reads only the determinized world the reply ran on), and a card it does not reach keeps the root's price.

**What is not priced again.** The enemy's hooks, which fired in the reply; a card whose key (definition and face) the enemy also holds with such a hook, since both copies share one price; a line scored statically, never replied to; every card while a prompt is open or once the game is over.

**Rejected.** A flat premium for a heal, which cannot tell a 4/16 that will be hit from an empty board; pricing the heal against the enemy's threat at the root, which the reply already plays out.
