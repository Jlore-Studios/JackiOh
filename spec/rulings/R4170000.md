The engine refuses any Frozen attacker (`why_cannot_declare`), and only its controller's cleanup thaws it ([[R1174000]]), so a Unit Frozen to thaw at the cleanup of the turn under way attacks nothing on that turn. A Unit that entered the field this turn is summoning sick (`is_sick`: a summon, a Reborn body, [[R83]], a change of control, [[R171]], a card a Stack uncovers, [[R1829000]]). It declares no attack without Rush or Charge ([[§4.2]] step 1, [[§4.1]]), and with Rush alone it attacks only Units ([[§6.1]]). This is Hearthstone's rule: a frozen minion misses the attack it would have made, and a minion summoned this turn attacks a hero only with Charge.

The evaluation reads a position with one side swinging first (`NextSwing`, [[R3793000]]). When that side is the player on turn, its attacks are this turn's ([[R4064000]]). But the face damage it counted (`face_threat_from`, [[§9.9]]) asked only `can_swing`, whose freeze test, `frozen_next_turn`, answers for the end of a turn: a Unit that thaws at this turn's cleanup reads as one that swings next turn, right at the turn's end and wrong at its start. Sickness went unread there, since no Unit is still sick on a side's next turn, the turn that read looks to. So these Units still counted at the hero:

- S+ #27 Gargantuar, which freezes itself after it attacks ([[§6.3]] Freeze): its 12 still counted at the AI's next turn start, after the opponent's reply, so the attack read as free;
- a Unit S+ #22 Cryofluid froze after it attacked;
- a Unit the opponent's S+ #91 Tsunami or S #175 Frost Module's Activate froze in the reply;
- a Unit S #146 Flash Freeze froze on the AI's own draw;
- an enemy Unit the AI froze on its own turn, read on the enemy's turn in a line the AI has passed;
- a body summoned at its controller's turn start: Core #58 Rush Token Farm's Rush Token, S+ #26 Jlockeed's Forever War's Conscript, Core #65 Masochism Mask's Spikey Pillow.

For a side whose attacks the evaluation reads as this turn's (the side on turn is the one `NextSwing` names), the face damage it counts now applies the engine's refusals of this turn:

- a Unit with any freeze stamp counts no attack, whatever cleanup thaws it, and a Frozen Windfury Unit neither of its two ([[R636]]);
- a Unit summoning sick without Rush or Charge counts no attack;
- a Unit summoning sick with Rush and no Charge counts an attack that lands nothing on the hero, read as an attack The Gauntlet bars from the hero ([[R1849005]]): a Taunt still binds it.

It applies to both sides: an enemy Unit adds no threat and the seat's own adds no pressure. It reaches only `evaluate`'s threat and pressure terms. `face_threat`, which reads the end of the turn under way, and with it `wall_switches` and the learned features, does not change, nor do `could_attack_next_turn`, `can_swing`, the engine's `frozen_next_turn` and `unit_worth`, which price the Unit as one that swings on a later turn. The Brittle and affliction reads ([[R3793000]], [[R4065000]]) keep their frames. The flag `frozen_sick_before_swing` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick is unchanged.

Not modelled, each of which can only leave a Unit counted, as before:

- a Unit that already attacked this turn, which a read in the middle of the turn still counts;
- an effect still to come that thaws a Unit or grants it Charge.

Rejected: the test inside `can_swing`, since `unit_worth` and `could_attack_next_turn` read it for later turns, where a Unit that thaws at this turn's cleanup does swing; changing the engine's `frozen_next_turn`, which is right at a turn's end, the frame every other reader of it asks.

It reads only public state (the freeze stamp and the turn a Unit entered the field, its keywords and the player on turn, which the view shows), and it adds no rule of the game.
