Meditative+ #16 Jazakus's Cry ("Craft a custom Potion. It costs (1).") opens Meditative #17's `craft` prompt ([[R880]]) in its **potion mode**, with no cost prompt before it: the prompt carries `craftMode: "potion"`, its budget is the fixed cost `CRAFT_POTION_COST` (1), and its answer must be a recipe in the **potion frame** (`frame: "potion"`), which the recipe itself carries, so the definition it compiles to is rebuilt from the recipe alone in any process and its digest names it ([[R882]]). A recipe in the frame is a Unit at the frame's cost whose one hat is `activate`, "Activate: Tribute this.", holding at least one effect; its stats and keywords are the player's, as any crafted Unit's; its points are the crafter's budget at cost (1), `2 + 5 × 1` = 7, and its lines, effects and numbers are the crafter's (24, 8, 1 to 10, [[R883]]). The Activate costs 4 lines, as a reveal hat's trigger, and no type takes it outside the frame. Any other answer is refused with the state unchanged and the prompt open: a recipe with no frame ("this prompt crafts a Potion"), another frame, another type, cost or hat, or one over the points; and Meditative #17's and Scratch Pad's prompts refuse a framed recipe ("this prompt crafts no Potion").

A crafted Potion is a transient definition as [[R882]] makes every crafted card, with two differences: it is of the **Meditative+ set** and tagged **Potion**, so it meets every rule a Potion card meets (M+ #11 to #13) and, being transient, is in no pool ([[R77]]); and it is named "<words> Potion" ("Pure Closure Potion"). It is Mythic and not a token, as every crafted card. Its Activate compiles to one ability ([[R384]]) whose cost is the card itself (`tribute_self`, Classic #84's), once per turn, labelled "Tribute this": the controller activates it in their main phase while it acts on the field, summoning sickness not applying; the Potion is tributed first and the effects run after, from its graveyard, on the face it was activated with. A targeted block under the Activate declares its target in the `activate` action, as a Cry's does in the play ([[R81]]), and never aims at the Potion, which pays for it. Its Radiant face doubles every number and stat ([[R882]]) and still tributes itself: the Radiant Living Potions drop their Tribute, but a crafted card's Radiant face is [[R882]]'s doubling, already twice the base.

The prompt's options are `CRAFT_PRESETS` (4) Potions rolled from the match rng, so the AI, the random policy and a timeout answer with one ([[R881]]): each takes its own verb, drawn without repeat from those that aim at nothing and take a number (damage to the enemy hero, to a random enemy or to each enemy, heal your side, gain Armor, draw, give your units +N/+N) and that fit half the budget at N = 1, at the largest N that fits that half, and spends all the rest of the points in stats (attack half, health the rest above 1), so every preset is valid and spends the whole budget. Since #1563 the frame is a Spell with no stats under one When cast, on 4 points, and its blocks are the prompt's offer ([[R1563008]], [[R1563007]]).
