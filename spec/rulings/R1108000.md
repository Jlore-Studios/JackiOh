A tutorial lesson ([[§9.10]]) teaches the cards by their words, so on a lesson's board no card prints full art ([[R1017001]]), whatever the Full-art cards setting says: the heroes' cards print their art window, type line, rules box and tags like any card, in hand, in a prompt, in the hover preview and in the touch sheet, and those carry the Full text beside a face only when it prints less than its whole text, as for any card ([[R1017000]]). The setting itself is untouched: it reads as the player left it, and a practice game and every other screen follow it as before. Presentation only: no rule reads it.
