A trigger's condition (`TriggerDef.when`) is the card's printed condition, whatever the card is. [[R99]] keeps it apart from the trigger's effects so a trap can decline an event without being spent, and the traps' dispatch reads a trap's before it reveals it. Every other trigger is queued and resolves in turn ([[R68]]), and its condition is read as it resolves, once, with the context its effects are built from: when it declines, the trigger does nothing, as a trap that declines is not spent ([[R61]]). A trap on the field is never queued ([[R383]]), so a trap's condition is still read once, by the dispatch.

Until this ruling the queue never read a condition, so a card that is no trap and printed one in its condition alone answered every event its trigger names: Meditative #97.8 Prison ("After your opponent plays a Unit") could capture its own controller's Units. A fusion reads each ingredient's condition in that ingredient's place ([[R102]]), on a fused trap as on any other fusion.
