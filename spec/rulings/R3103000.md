A ladder bot is a kind of seat in the match host's machine ([[R1452]]), not code a host runs beside it. The host tells the machine who the bot is (its strength, its speed, its persona and one more setting, below) and answers its `decide` outputs with what the AI found; the machine does the rest, as timers and actions: the think time ([[R1452]]), the aim shown a beat before a targeted move and taken down after it ([[R738]], [[R639000]]), the persona's emotes and replies ([[R645]]), the concession, the draw offer and the draw answer ([[R639000]], [[R2660000]], [[R2660001]]), the turn clock it lets run out now and then ([[R639000]]), the fallback for a move the engine refuses ([[R188]], [[R1452]]) and its stay for a rematch ([[R639000]]). The streams it draws them on are the ones [[R1452]] names, each seeded from the match seed and the seat the bot began in, so a match with a bot replays from `(seed, log)` as before. A host runs a bot the way it runs a person, with no bot code of its own, and the server's actor is that host.

**Hidden.** What exists only so that a bot passes for a person is one setting of its seat, `hidden`: [[R1452]]'s pace, aim and emotes, [[R639000]]'s concessions, draw offers and answers, idle turns and rematch, and [[R1453]]'s fallback time (a ticket waits 15 to 40 seconds before it may pair with a bot, so that a pairing does not come the moment it is queued). The server turns it on, as every opponent on the web is a person to the player, so nothing the player sees changes. A host whose players see every account (a Roblox server) will label its bots and leave it off; a bot that is not hidden plays as the practice AI does ([[R188]] holds for its seat as well as its search): it plays the move as soon as it has decided it, shows no aim, sends no emote, never concedes, offers or accepts a draw, never lets a clock run out, stays on no finished game, and a ticket is due for a bot the moment it is queued. Its strength is the same, and so is its search.

Hearthstone has no equivalent. Where the spec is silent, this follows the practice AI: the smallest bot is the AI at a strength, and every habit of a person's is what a host adds to it.
