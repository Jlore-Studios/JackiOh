A `cell` prompt (Classic+ #62, one cell a prompt, [[R422]]) is answered on the board: the zones its options name glow green on both sides, filled or empty, as a play's zones do, and wear a crosshair while it is open; a click or a tap on one or on what stands in it, or Enter or Space on a focused one, sends that option. The picker lists the same cells, grouped by side and row and named by lane, with any option that names no cell ("Done") among them, for the keyboard and small screens. Which zones answer is read off the options alone; a zone not offered answers nothing. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
