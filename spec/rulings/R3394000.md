Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to "go harder on alt art": keep the theme but move
it to another style or place, so each Alt art is clearly its own. [[R3406000]] did so for Stinkyscape's later cards;
until this issue an early Stinkyscape card's Alt art redrew its own picture re-seeded ([[R2940001]], [[R3391000]]):
its scene's parts, or its own painter's picture, from another seed, which changed a median 20% of the picture's
pixels and at most 34%, the least 10% (Drone Core VII).

**An alt recipe per card.** Each of the 48 cards of #3394's table, every Stinkyscape card from #1 to #90 offered Alt
art (`version-alt-art`) and the Dormant Starship, which #3406's table had left out, draws an alt recipe of its own
(`stinkyscapeAltRecipes1.ts` to `stinkyscapeAltRecipes3.ts`, `STINKYSCAPE_ALT_RECIPES`, spread into
`ALT_SCENE_RECIPES` after #3406's), written from #3394's brief for it: the same subject and idea in another place,
time or story moment, in Stinkyscape's family. `sceneRecipeOf` reads it under the card's Alt art seed (`<id>#alt`)
alone, as [[R3396000]] set; the card's id, every repaint's seed ([[R3017002]]) and any other seed keep the normal
picture. Every level of Alt art draws the one alt picture ([[R3015002]]), base and Radiant.

**A piece of its own painter.** Fourteen of the 48 have no normal scene recipe: Olivian Expanse, whose painter draws
its violet expanse ([[R3338001]]); the twelve early Starship Pieces, whose normal face draws its module in its bay
([[R3333001]]); and the Dormant Starship, drawn in its shipyard ([[R3333000]]). Their normal face, and every seed but
the Alt art one, keeps that painter's picture byte for byte; only their Alt art seed finds the alt recipe, through the
scene the Stinkyscape painter tries first (svg.ts `sceneArtSvg`). A Starship in play draws the ship its pieces build
and never reads the Alt art seed, so the Dormant Starship's alt picture shows only out of play, as its Alt art did
before. Each stands its piece in the alt scene as a part of the scene kit, never the module's own markup, under a sky
that is neither black nor slate-900, the deep space their normal pictures share (Olivian Expanse's not violet or
purple either, its expanse's colours, but the brief's olive-grey nothing): a survey ship's cockpit and console, the
flank pod on hull plates and the great cannon on a barge ([[R3406000]]'s parts), a drill on a pebble, a break, a comet
or an asteroid, and the seven parts the pass added (below).

**The same subject.** A figure both pictures draw is the normal one's person, its head, outfit and skin unchanged
(only its cloth, hair or held item may change): Tylannt, the Jempire's enforcer, Tnack Trav, KY, ZolarKeth, the Core
Researcher, the Drone Commander, Z'Zoth and the two Sivas. A subject that is a part of the kit stays in the subject
layer: the ray gun and its frost, the prayer-lights, the frost shard, the three cores, the moonberry vines, the drones
and corvettes, the crystal, the helper drone and its core, the paperclip planet's cores, the fighters, the miner, the
trader and its crate, Radix's frame, the gunship, Halo's ring and Prototype-X2's fused hull. Masochism Unmasked's
mask, cracked in its normal picture, waits whole on a steel table before the hit, a chrome mask the pass added. Halo's
normal picture seats its pilot in the ring; its character is its craft ([[R3391000]]), so its alt picture draws the
ring alone from far above.

**Measurably elsewhere.** Each of the 34 alt recipes with a normal one shares no sky colour with it, and its subject
moves at least 8 units, or grows or shrinks by at least a quarter, or a figure comes or goes: a close-up, a wide or
distant view or one from above. In a real browser each Alt art window changes at least `ALT_PICTURE_CHANGE_MIN`
(0.6) of the normal window's points sampled on card-versions.cy.tsx's grid, counted with its threshold for a point
that differs, base and Radiant.

**What still holds.** Each alt picture clears its rarity's detail floor and the byte ceiling ([[R3331001]]), on both
faces, and an Epic or rarer one has far, mid, subject and near layers; prints no text; draws one geometry on both
faces, the Radiant one gilded under its rays, with no base colour in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]); is apart
from every other picture ([[R503]]); stands its emblem small inside the band every face shows; and keeps props out of
its subject layer. A Radiant face keeps its marks at every Alt art level. Seven parts join the kit in a module of
their own (`sceneKitStinkyEarlyAlt.ts`, spread into `SCENE_KIT` after every other), so that no brief's subject is a
stand-in: a chrome face mask, a bare chassis frame, a beam emitter, a repeater gun, an orca-shaped mining hull, a pair
of solar panels and a dormant hull with no flame. A minor detail the kit lacks is left out or stood for and named in
the recipe's comment: the last star a small glare high in Olivian Expanse's canopy for want of a rear-view mirror,
Masochism Unmasked's mask on a steel table for want of a tray, Tylannt's lockpick a key and his vault hologram a faint
vault door, the enforcer's mechanic holding a flask for an oil can, Tnack Trav's stolen heroes faint ghost figures
among the mirrors, ZolarKeth's medals small discs and his harness left off, the cleaner drones' spray standing for
brushes, the Core Researcher's goggles a spark, the quartermaster arguing with a point, the unread manual a notebook,
the heavy shell a meteor, the bots' flasks beside them, the Falcon crooked by where it stands, Z'Zoth kneeling for
want of a curled pose, Badger's miner resting rather than curled, Yuma's rival's hatch already open, Phalanx's quills
standing, Halo's pilot too small to see, Prototype-X2's cockpits along its spine, Siva's leash a ribbon of light, and
Siva 2's crystal glass a teacup.

Rejected, beyond [[R3396000]]'s and [[R3406000]]'s lists: near-copies of #3406's hull plate, flank pod and great
cannon for Healing Capsule and Cannon v4, which [[R3391000]]'s rule against two parts drawing one thing forbids, so
those three are reused; the kit's `starship` for the Dormant Starship, whose engines burn where the brief's hull is
dormant, so `dormant-hull` draws one unlit; #3391's cracked mask for Masochism Unmasked, where the brief is the trap
before the hit, so `chrome-mask` draws it whole; Halo's pilot kept in its alt picture, which a view from far above
could not show; leaving the Dormant Starship out again, as [[R3406000]] had, where #3394's table names it; and
splitting the 48 cards into smaller pull requests, which the issue allowed at about 40 cards a part and the three
recipe files map onto.
