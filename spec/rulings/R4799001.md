The AI tries one lane of each placement of a play, every placement at the search's root (`collapse_zones`, [[§9.9]]): two lanes whose placement keys match are the same placement and only one is tried ([[R2203001]] keeps the midlane's). The key (`placement_key`) read each zone through `card_at`, which sees only the zone's own pile, so a zone a GIGA covers ([[R3546000]]) read as open, and it read nothing of a slashed board ([[R3554003]]). So with an enemy GIGA anchored in lane 2 and the seat's row empty, lanes 3 and 4 shared a key, and lane 4, the only inner lane not across the GIGA, was never tried; and on a board slashed at lane 3, lanes 2 and 4, each beside the void ([[R3554002]]) with nothing else near, shared one, though a Unit in lane 2 attacks only the Left half's Units and one in lane 4 only the Right's.

**The rule.** The AI decides this; no game changes. A placement reads each zone's occupant as the engine's lane reads meet it (`occupant_at`, [[R3546001]]): the zone's own top, else a GIGA covering it from its own row. And it reads the half of a slashed board the play's lane is on (`half_of_lane`). So on the GIGA board lanes 2 and 3, which both face the GIGA, are now one placement, its midlane lane 3 tried where lane 2 was tried alone before, and lane 4 is a placement of its own; on the slashed board lanes 2 and 4 are two. On a board with no GIGA and no slash every key is what it was, so the lanes tried are the same.
