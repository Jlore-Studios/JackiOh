"When they meet" is the moment the two cards become adjacent in the hand, however they got there — added to the two ends of an empty hand, or passed by every card between them leaving. A card a full hand burns never meets its partner; the meeting takes the named price when it names one. The waiting pair wears the `meet` mark (gold) in its owner's hand view alone. Rejected: meeting only on arrival, which would miss the cards-between-leaving case the card names.
