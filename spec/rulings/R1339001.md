title: "Pity forces a tier on top of a card pack's rolled slots (issue #1339)"

[[§14.4]]'s pity keeps its counters and thresholds (`PITY_EPIC_PACKS`, `PITY_LEGENDARY_PACKS`,
`PITY_MYTHIC_PACKS`, one set per account, [[R1298000]]) and sits on top of the tiered roll
([[R1339000]]): the table is exactly what one roll does, and a counter only forces a tier when it
runs out. `apply_pity` (`crates/server/src/economy/math.rs`) takes the counters and the slots'
rolled rarities:

- **A force.** A pack in which a counter would reach its threshold, and none of whose slots rolled
  its rarity, forces one slot to it: Mythic first, then Legendary, then Epic. A force takes the
  first slot, in slot order from the floored slot (`PACK_FLOORED_SLOT`), that no earlier force
  took, that is below its rarity, and whose loss takes no tracked rarity from the pack (a Common or
  a Rare, or a rarity another slot also holds), so the force raises it; with none, the first whose
  rarity another slot also holds. Either way the pack keeps every Epic, Legendary and Mythic it
  rolled, and with `PACK_SLOTS` at least the three tracked rarities one of the two always exists.
  So a pack of a floored Rare and four Commons that forces all three opens Mythic, Legendary, Epic,
  Common, Common, and a floored Legendary keeps its slot while a forced Mythic takes the next.
- **The counters.** After the forces, each counter resets when the pack holds its rarity and adds
  one when it does not, so a Mythic leaves the Epic and Legendary counters running, and a Legendary
  the Epic one. No counter ever reaches its threshold after a pack, so none passes it.
- **The rates.** Before pity a pack holds a rarity of weight w in 1 − (1 − w/`TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL`)^
  `PACK_SLOTS` of packs: an Epic in 30.4%, a Legendary in 9.6% and a Mythic in 4.9%. Pity adds a
  little on top. A seeded simulation of 200,000 packs reports both (about 31.3%, 11.1% and 6.3% with
  pity), checks that each rate with pity is at least the rate before it and that no counter reaches
  its threshold, and nothing is tuned to it: `PACK_BASE_RATES`, `PACK_TARGET_RATES` and
  `PACK_RATE_TOLERANCE` are gone. The bars' points per duplicate stay [[§14.5]]'s, derived for a
  collection whose every slot is spent, whatever its rarity.
- **The draws.** Pity takes none: it reads the slots' tier draws, the opening's first
  ([[R1339002]]), and no cosmetic roll touches it ([[R1333000]]). A slot pity forced keeps its
  forced rarity when its card falls to another ([[R1301001]]).

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's pity guarantees a rarity within a set number of packs without
changing the odds of a pack in which it does not fire; this forces the tier the same way, on top of
the published table.

Rejected: forcing the floored slot whatever it rolled, the plain reading of "the floored slot
first", which would take a rolled Epic or Legendary away for a forced Mythic and leave that
counter running, so pity would take from a pack; filling the pack's lowest slot first, which would
move the forced rarity to wherever the Commons fell; and tuning the table so the rates with pity
land on the owner's targets, which the owner dropped.
