Issue #4154 took the line "It costs ({setCost})." off both faces of M+ #2 NA Tech, "so it would be the cost of the fused together". The fused card it adds to its caster's hand now carries no set price: it costs what [[R77]] gives any fusion, the printed costs of its two ingredients summed ([[R65]]'s reading of a cost) and capped at `FUSE_COST_CAP` (4), on both faces, a Radiant face's fusion summing the same printed costs. In the engine that is `fuse_generated`'s `fused` hand price ([[R352]]) in place of [[R1183001]]'s `costOverride`; the `setCost` number goes, so nothing tunes the price. Through v0.6.6h the card cost the declared `setCost`, (2) and Radiant (0) (`script_v0_6_6h`, [[R767000]]). Rejected: the bare sum with no cap, which no other fusion's card pays; and the free price `fuse_generated` gives a fused card by default, which the issue's "the cost of the fused together" rules out.
