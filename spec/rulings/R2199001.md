The engine reads a source's Trample only for an attacking Unit's hit or a Spell's ([[R1750000]]), and a Trap is neither, so a Trap that prints Pierce or Trample (TG #13's Radiant face, TG #14) carries it on the hits of its own text: the effect states `ignoreArmor` ([[R346]]) and `trample`. A trampling hit's excess past the Unit's Health, after its Armor, goes to that Unit's controller's hero ([[§4.4]] step 9); a Divine Shield or Indestructible that stops the hit leaves nothing to trample, and a Divine Shield still stops a pierced hit. The keyword on the face is the reminder of what its hits do, read by nothing else.
