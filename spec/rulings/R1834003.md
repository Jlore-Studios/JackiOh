[[R1295000]] and [[R1313000]] left showing another player's cosmetics to "a `Cosmetics` frame and a
ruling of its own". These are the frames that carry [[R1834002]]'s frozen loadouts, and the rule
that keeps them from saying more about a deck than the views do ([[§10.8]]). It amends
[[R1295000]] (*Whose cards wear them*, its last sentence) and [[R1313000]] (*The gate*, its
sentence on showing the opponent's).

**`loadouts`.** `{ "type": "loadouts", "p1": SeatLoadout, "p2": SeatLoadout }`, each half
`{ table, field, cardBack, foil, finish, border }`, one registry id each: a seat's deck slots and
card slots as the match froze them, none naming a card. Each match socket is sent it as it joins
(its `hello`) and again as it reconnects or attaches, before the view, as `portraits` rides
([[R642]]), and a watcher as it attaches. Every receiver gets the same frame.

**`cardLoadouts`.** `{ "type": "cardLoadouts", "seat": PlayerId, "cards": [{ cardId, foil?, finish?,
border?, fullArt? }] }`: the own picks of cards of `seat`'s deck. A kind an entry leaves out wears
the seat's, and a card with nothing of its own is never listed.

**The send rule.** A card's entry goes to a socket after the first view sent to it that shows a card
dealt from that seat's deck, still as the card it was dealt as, and never again on that socket
until its next `hello` or attach, which starts it afresh. A card counts by the deal: each card the
decks dealt as the game began, by instance id, with the seat whose deck dealt it and the card it was
dealt as (`actor::engine::dealt_cards`), read against the cards a view shows face up, on either side
(its units, carried units and face-up backrow, its hand where the view lists it, its graveyard, its
exile and what is resolving; `loadout::shown_defs`). A card made after the deal (a token, a copy, a
last board's card) and one transformed into another card count for no deck, and a face-down card, a
hand shown as a count, a library, an event and a prompt show none. So no frame names a card its
receiver has not been shown, and no decklist can be read from the frames. A watcher's frames follow
the delayed view it watches ([[R1107000]]), never sooner. A card its view shows is still worn in its
kinds' defaults until its entry arrives.

**Replay pages.** Each page of `GET /api/replays/:matchId` ([[R768]]) carries `loadouts`, both
seats' seat-level halves (`{ p1, p2 }`, every kind's default for a match from before
[[R1834002]]), and `cardLoadouts`, a list of `{ seat, cards }`, the entries of the cards that page's
steps show, by the same rule over the deal read from step 0's state. A step is still exactly its
seat's view ([[R97]]).

Nothing here is a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7): neither frame is part of `PlayerView`, an action, the log
or a replay hash.

Hearthstone semantics: a player sees an opponent's card back from the start and a card's version
once that card is revealed, and nothing of a deck before it is.

Rejected: sending each seat's card picks whole at the start, which would hand the other seat a list
of the cards of its deck that wear something; and counting a card by the owner field of the view,
which moves when a card changes hands (an opponent's card taken into the receiver's hand), so a
seat's entry for a card could go out though no card its deck dealt had been shown, telling that its
deck holds that card. The deal does not move: a card counts for the deck that dealt it, and only
while it shows as what that deck dealt.
