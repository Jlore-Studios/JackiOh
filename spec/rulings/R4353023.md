S+ #96 Open Enrollment since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V costs (0) embiggen (2) ([[R1175000]]) and prints "Embiggened (2): They cost (1) less." (Radiant: "They cost (0)."). "They" are every card the play fills the hand with, F tier or the D tier fallback ([[R3538001]]), and the change is made on each as it is added, as any add-to-hand rider is: the base face's is a `costMod` of minus 1 on each card, which other cost changes stack with and which never takes a price below (0) ([[R65]]); the Radiant face's is a `costOverride` of 0, the card's price set, as "costs (0)" adds are everywhere. A card it adds that has an Embiggened price of its own keeps that price ([[R1181003]]). Each is kept until the card goes to a graveyard or exile, as every price on a card is. At its base price, (0), neither face touches the cards' costs. The numbers are prices, printed, declared nowhere ([[R1748003]]). Through v0.6.8b the card had no Embiggened price, so no game of those versions plays it embiggened and its script needs no keeping ([[R767000]]).
