Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Classic+'s pass fills its effects table ([[R2784000]]): every Spell, Field Spell, Trap and Field Trap of its 78 cards names its `cast`'s motif, a set piece ([[R2800000]]) or a bespoke recipe; every Unit whose hook changes the board names a motif on that hook; and every Field Spell given an entry, the set's ten among them, wears an aura ([[R2787000]]). At least 55% of the 78 have an entry other than `none`: a card with no hook, a vanilla Unit and a Unit's hook whose only event no motif reads stay `none`, with the reason. A token takes an entry only where it earns one: its hook ran in at least three of the census's windows and a motif reads its own event. A motif is picked from `cargo jackioh fx census`, never by comparing card texts, and overridden with a reason where the census's suggestion predates the motifs of issues #2786, #2793 and #2796 or comes from the turn's events in its window; a card whose name or art sits squarely in a sub-look's line takes it (the Felinors, the Books, KY's cards, the AI cards). An entry whose play makes no event its motif reads (a Trap's `cast`, which opens no play, and every `death`, `trigger` and `activate`, which plan nothing until issue #2783's marker reaches them) draws nothing in a game yet and plays in the gallery.

No motif joins the vocabulary. The set's cards that make cards take issue #2793's `send` (27 of its entries: the hand's `addedToHand` and the library's `shuffledIn`, a card back on both seats, [[R202]]); the three whose hook makes only a fuse (#30 Felinor Fuser, #31 Fusion Lab, #74 Twice Forward One Step Backwards) take `transmute`, which reads `fused` (issue #2802); #28 Nuestro hogar, nuestras tumbas's heal and #19.4 Support Loser's take `mend`, and #65.4 Golden Grape's Radiant takes `radiance` (issue #2793); #14 Forever&, #52 Jlockeed's Permanent Defense Contract and the token Alignment Tax, whose plays only lay a player modifier (`modifierChanged`), take issue #2796's `seal`. Issue #2796's `deal`, which reads `send`'s events and `fused`, came after this table took `send` and `transmute`, which say the same. The pass's per-card table is `fx/cards/classicplus.md`, one row per card and token, held to the table by its test.

Three Flourishes join Crushing Walls, each a card recipe in `fx/cards/classicplus/` built from the motif primitives (the phases, the look, the card's scale by its rarity and cost) for a card whose one event no motif reads: #35 Rollback, at its first `rolledBack`, runs an arcane ring back from lane 5 to lane 1 through the unit zones of every side that event names, `FX_ROLLBACK_STEP` of the entry apart, then glints those heroes; #41 KY's Constant stamps each `numberChanged` in chalk on the card this seat's view draws, and none on a change hidden from this seat; #56 Book of Pain tears each card it discards out in ink, its first tear with a ring and a shake (`FX_INK_TEAR_TRAUMA`). Each keys on its readable play alone, so a hidden play keys none ([[R202]]), keeps its events' rows (and Rollback the rewind that issue #2802 draws for `rolledBack`), starts every cue inside its entry and ends it within the existing tails ([[R200]], [[R201]]), and draws nothing with the effects off.

The `/dev/fx` gallery plays every Classic+ entry other than `none` as issue #2793 made it play every entry (`galleryPlayOf`): a `cast` or `cry` motif on the board, a card recipe on its fixture (the three Flourishes join `RECIPE_FIXTURES`), a set piece with its hook's marker, so it holds as its card's does, a `death`, `trigger` or `activate` hook as its motif through the gallery's own director, and a Field Spell or Field Trap standing face-up in the viewer's first backrow zone, wearing its aura.

[[R2722001]] is unchanged: the pass restyles no Legendary's entrance and no Mythic's takeover, since a table entry has no field for one and the shared looks are issue #2803's to rework. Rejected: a motif of the set's own for the cards it makes or the modifiers it lays, since `send`, `transmute` and `seal` read those events and the three-card rule draws a motif only where three cards fit none; and motifs for `rolledBack`, `numberChanged` and `discarded`, one card each, which took Flourishes instead.
