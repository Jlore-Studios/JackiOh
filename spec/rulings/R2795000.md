Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Classic+'s pass fills its effects table ([[R2784000]]): every Spell, Field Spell, Trap and Field Trap of its 78 cards names its `cast`'s motif, a set piece ([[R2800000]]) or a bespoke recipe; every Unit whose hook changes the board names a motif on that hook; and every Field Spell given an entry, the set's ten among them, wears an aura ([[R2787000]]). At least 55% of the 78 have an entry other than `none`: a card with no hook, a vanilla Unit and a hook whose only event no motif reads stay `none`, with the reason. A token takes an entry only where it earns one: its hook ran in at least three of the census's windows and a motif reads its own event. A motif is picked from `cargo jackioh fx census`, never by comparing card texts, and overridden with a reason where the census's suggestion predates the motifs of issue #2786 or comes from the turn's events in its window; a card whose name or art sits squarely in a sub-look's line takes it (the Felinors, the Books, KY's cards, the AI cards). An entry whose play makes no event its motif reads (a Trap's `cast`, which opens no play, and every `death`, `trigger` and `activate`, which plan nothing until issue #2783's marker reaches them) draws nothing in a game yet and plays in the gallery.

`deal` joins the motifs by the three-card rule, since at least three of the set's cards (28 of them) make cards that no motif read: a gather at the resolving play's hero (else at the hero the card goes to), once a play, then a card back flying from it to the hand, the library or the card a fuse merges into (`addedToHand`, `shuffledIn`, `fused`), landing on the Land phase with a sparkle, at most `FX_MOTIF_DEAL_MAX` an entry, with no shake. It reads the event's pile or card as its row does and never the card dealt, so it draws the same backs on both seats ([[R202]]), and every row it draws over still plays.

Three Flourishes join Crushing Walls, each a card recipe in `fx/cards/classicplus/` built from the motif primitives (the phases, the look, the card's scale by its rarity and cost) for a card whose one event no motif reads: #35 Rollback, at its first `rolledBack`, runs an arcane ring back from lane 5 to lane 1 through the unit zones of every side that event names, `FX_ROLLBACK_STEP` of the entry apart, then glints those heroes; #41 KY's Constant stamps each `numberChanged` in chalk on the card this seat's view draws, and none on a change hidden from this seat; #56 Book of Pain tears each card it discards out in ink, its first tear with a ring and a shake (`FX_INK_TEAR_TRAUMA`). Each keys on its readable play alone, so a hidden play keys none ([[R202]]), keeps its events' rows, starts every cue inside its entry and ends it within the existing tails ([[R200]], [[R201]]), and draws nothing with the effects off.

The `/dev/fx` gallery plays every entry but `none`, an aura alone and a bespoke entrance: a card recipe on a fixture of its own, a set piece with its hook's marker, so it holds as its card's does, a `death`, `trigger` or `activate` hook as its motif through the gallery's own director, and a Field Spell or Field Trap standing face-up in the viewer's first backrow zone, wearing its aura.

[[R2722001]] is unchanged: the pass restyles no Legendary's entrance and no Mythic's takeover, since a table entry has no field for one and the shared looks are issue #2803's to rework. Rejected: a motif for `modifierChanged`, which two of the set's cards' plays alone make (Forever&, Jlockeed's Permanent Defense Contract); motifs for `rolledBack`, `numberChanged` and `discarded`, one card each, which took Flourishes instead; and a heal motif for Nuestro hogar, nuestras tumbas, one card with one token beside it, which stays `none`.
