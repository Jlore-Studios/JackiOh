**Why.** A card that Discovers ([[§6.3]]) or picks among cards offers a choice of N cards whose result no record kept, so no figure could say which of its offers are taken.

**The record.** Each seat's record gains `picks`, absent when empty and from every record written before it: one entry per `discover` or `pick` prompt a card's own script asked (§10.6), in order, with its `source` (the asking card and its face), the catalog ids it `offered` in the order offered, and the ones `picked`, wherever the cards came from: the catalog, a library or a graveyard. It is the seat's whose sequence the answer continues (`prompts::prompt_owner_of`), the asking card's controller. An offer that names no card, a Discover of §5 indices ([[R247]]) or of code blocks ([[R1183005]]), or any option that is not a card, is no pick. The pick's output is also the source's output when the card picked is Created ([[R4672001]]). The record is read off the whole game after it ends, so the hidden options a pick showed its chooser alone (§10.8) reach no player.

Rejected: recording only the card picked, which leaves no baseline for the cards passed over; and recording a `mode` prompt as a pick, which is a variant of the play ([[R4672000]]).
