Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event or response changes.

**What opens it.** A Versions row ([[R4680000]]), Enter or Space on it, or its grid card opens the drill-down on that play version ([[R4675000]]), not the card's: the modal the card's drill-down is ([[R3221000]], [[R2127000]]), titled with the version's name.

**What it shows.**
- Its base and Radiant faces side by side, drawn from its own definition, its catalog's at its last version ([[R4675001]]), with the patch notes' face (`PatchFace`), not today's; an old printing's references are not interactive ([[R279]]). On a phone the two stay side by side, each half the column.
- Its figures from `GET /api/stats/versions`: its tier with its fit on the versions' band, Adjusted with its 95% range, its games, and Since, its span and the patches it reaches ([[R4678001]]). It reads no patch's details: the card's win rate by patch and turn and its co-played cards are one patch's, which no figure here is.
- The version before it and the version after it, each with its span, which opens that version's drill-down, its base face, and the change between the two definitions as the patch notes list it (`diffCard` and `ChangeList`, [[R388]], [[R507]]): before, what changed from it to this version; after, what changed from this version to it. Both definitions are their catalogs' at their last versions, the faces shown, so the list reads against the faces at a glance, and a rewording inside a version shows with the change that began the next. Where the definitions are the same, a version begun by a script change claimed through `plays` ([[R4675000]]), it says its card did not change and how it plays did. The first version has none before it and the live one none after, each said in a line.

**Loading.** It loads the catalogs of the three versions it draws, and only those, as the rows do ([[R4680000]]); one that fails says so with Retry.

**The address.** `?card=<card>&version=<first>` names it: the card as on every tab, and the version it began at, the second half of its id (`<card>~<first>`, [[R4675000]]), so the address needs no escaping. A link opens it from any tab, which reads the play versions for it; until they load it shows a skeleton, and a version the card never began at reads "Details unavailable.". Opening a neighbour changes the version; closing it forgets both.

Rejected:
- `?card=<card>@<version>`: `@` already marks a past version by its last version ([[R3538002]]), another thing, and `URLSearchParams` escapes it.
- The play version's id in `?card=`: the parameter names a card on every other tab.
- Diffing each neighbour against this version's first catalog: the faces shown are the last ones, so the list would not read against them.

Hearthstone has no counterpart.
