[[R2564000]] priced one use of the Activate of each unit acting on either field, and nothing for a Unit in the seat's hand, so a body whose worth is its Activate read as its bare stats until it stood on the field. Stinkyscape+ #126 Vela, a Tribute 2 0/10 that can neither attack nor defend and whose Activate deals 8 down a line, read as a 0/10 for two of the AI's units, and the shadow-ban sweep's first pass at Hard played it in none of its 13 playable turns. A unit's Death, turn hooks and wake trigger were already priced in the seat's own hand, placed first by the sandbox's spawn ([[R923001]]). No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, after the field's probe and from a cap of its own (`AI_ACTIVATION_HAND_PROBE_NODES`), each Unit in the seat's own hand with an Activate (`abilities_of`) whose key (definition, face and controller) no card acting on the seat's field already has is placed, one after another on one sandbox copy ([[R923000]]) with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), as [[R923001]] places it. The seat ends its turn, the enemy ends its, and each placed card's best use at the start of the seat's next turn is priced by [[R2564000]]'s `price_uses`, so the price is the one the same card standing on the field would hold. `unit_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.activation` of it once the card is played, as for any priced unit.

**What is not priced.** A card in the enemy's hand ([[R185]]); a card the placements leave no zone for; a hand card whose use the probe's nodes did not reach; every card while a prompt is open, outside the seat's own main phase, or once the game is over.

**Rejected.** Adding the price to the hand card's own worth, which would count the Activate before the card is played and pay its cost; drawing the hand's probe from the field's cap, which would take the nodes the field's cards drew before.
