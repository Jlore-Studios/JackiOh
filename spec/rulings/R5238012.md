Felinors vs Humans #105 attacks every other Unit, its own side's after the enemy's. A forced attack is made on an enemy ([[R173]]): against an ally the attacker is passed over in silence. Only the melee ([[R1848005]]) reached the attacker's own side.

**The rule.** `forced_attack` ([[R3550000]]) with `allies` may attack a Unit on the attacker's own side: R173's side test is waived, as the melee waives it, and nothing else of [[R53]]'s forced attack changes (no exertion spent, position, sickness and Taunt ignored, [[§4.2]] step 2's restrictions still holding, the target striking back). A Unit never attacks itself: naming the attacker as its own target does nothing. Without `allies`, an ally target is passed over as before.
