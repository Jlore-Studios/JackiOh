[[§5.2]] made Radiant an upgrade and the source says only "upgraded versions of normal cards", so how much stronger was never fixed, and a quarter of Core fell short of the rest (`docs/radiant-audit.md`). A Radiant face is about twice its base face. A Unit's attack and health are each at least double its base face's — a 0 stays 0, so #1 Big D-fender and #65.1 Spikey Pillow keep their 0 attack, and a token summoned X/X (the Bread Token, an Adaptive UI Ghoul Token) takes its X from the card that summons it, which scales X itself — and its effect is 100–150% stronger: 2 to 2.5 times the amount, a broader scope (one target to all, one side to a choice of sides), or an added rider or keyword, where a keyword-only unit's rider is one more keyword or a stronger one (Rush to Charge). A Spell, Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap scales its effect the same way and may add a draw or a tangential rider instead. A card whose base effect has no number is raised in scope or by a rider; an X-cost card's X is scaled (#24's 2X, #74's 2X and 3X); a formula is scaled as a formula (#31's Fib index moves two places, about 2.6 times; #40 and #70 double their exile term). The stat half is mechanical and a catalog-wide test holds every Unit face to it, listing any exception by name: there was none in Core, and patch v0.2.0 named two, both the designer's printed numbers — C #33 Joro's Radiant face stayed 1/1 and C #80 BOOM! Big Max kept its 26 attack. Patch v0.2.10 (balance patch 1, issue #88) drops both: Joro is 2/2 and Big Max is 13/8 → 26/16, doubling exactly, so no exception remains. An Animated card's unit face ([[R383]]) is held to it as a Unit is, and C+ #69 Buff Billy's X stats as multipliers. The effect half is the audit's judgement, recorded card by card, where the designer's word stands for C #60 Pile On and C+ #3 Second Amendment Snake
