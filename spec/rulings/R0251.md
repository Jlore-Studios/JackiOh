A card is its catalog id. Radiant is a flag on a card in play ([[§5.2]]), never a second id, so a deck cannot hold a Radiant copy and "no card shared" compares catalog ids; Token-tagged cards are never deckable ([[§2.6]]). The builder's mark on a card "used in" another deck of the trio and L4 read the same fact, one list of shared ids
