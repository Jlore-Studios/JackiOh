Fullsend 3's sweep flagged a block of Stinkyscape+ cards `neverPlayed` at Hard while Easy played them. Hard's handicap ([[R180]]) draws two cards a turn, so its hand runs larger, and a card drawn into a full hand is burned ([[§2.4]], [[R4]]). The evaluation priced every card in hand and never read the hand cap. A line that left the seat's hand too full to take its coming draws scored the same as one that left room, until the opponent's reply had played out to those draws. The beam prunes on the line's own end, before the reply, so it never weighed the burn. No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** `coming_burns` is the draws a side's coming start of turn takes past its hand cap (`hand_cap_of`). Those draws are DRAWS_PER_TURN plus its handicap's `extraDrawsPerTurn`, read off the state as public fact ([[R180]], [[R183]]), on a turn its `drawEvery` lets it draw ([[R3582000]]), and no more than its library holds, since the rest are fatigue and priced apart. Each one costs `AI_EVAL.burned_draw_share`, 1, of an unseen hand card's price (`hand_card + hand_per_cost × opponent_hand_cost`). A burned draw is a card the side would otherwise hold, and the drawn card is one nobody has seen yet, so it takes the price `material` gives a card in a hand it cannot see. The share is a whole card, derived rather than tuned. It is read only for the side whose turn is not running, whose hand nothing of its own changes before that draw. The side whose turn is running may still play its hand down. So a passed line prices the seat's coming burns, and a line scored after the reply, where the burns have happened, prices the enemy's coming ones instead, never the seat's twice. A draw a card or a trap adds is not foreseen. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, so the gate's greedy baseline is unchanged.

**What the sweep showed.** Before the change, pass 1 of `cargo jackioh sweep` over S+ #15, #98, #126, #129, #138, #146, #153 and #160 ran 8 games a tier, instrumented. Hard held 8.3 cards at turn start on average, where Easy held about 5, and 9 or more at 40% of Hard's turn starts. Yet Hard left 0.1 to 0.5 crystals unspent a turn, and 0.0 to 0.5 on the turns the swept card was playable. It ended 18% of its turns holding 9 or more, so its coming two draws burned one, about 0.6 burned draws a game. The swept card is never the one burned ([[R4]] burns the card arriving). So burns are not why Hard leaves these cards in hand. With more cards in hand the beam has more plays it rates above them for the same mana, and each card's own price is its family's matter. The price is kept because it is a true cost the evaluation could foresee from public state and never read.

**Numbers.** This branch against the same build without the price, on `main` plus fullsend 2's lift, one machine. `cargo jackioh sweep` over the eight cards: in pass 1 at Hard, #15 went from never played (7 playable turns) to played once (7), and the other seven stayed `neverPlayed` with the same counts, give or take a turn. Pass 2 cleared all eight at both tiers, before and after. Over every at-risk card pass 2 dealt, Hard's plays went from 628 to 646 (3,322 and 3,312 playable turns) and Easy's from 728 to 723 (2,756 and 2,741). The smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`): ai-vs-random 19 of 20 (17 needed), ai-vs-greedy 16 (10), hard-vs-easy 19 (16), no search panic, the perf gate's slowest decision 30.8 ms. The build without the price read the same three counts and 29.9 ms. The improve lane's screen against that build (`cargo jackioh arena --a self --b bin:<it> --set improve-dev`, 400 games, every one clean) read 200-200, edge 0.0 (0.0 to 0.0): the price neither gains nor loses there, and is kept because it is true, not for strength.

**Rejected.** A discount on each hand card past some size, which no rule sets. A burn price tuned to the sweep. Pricing the running side's own coming draws, which it may still make room for.
