#52 Silly Silas's base face, which every face-up play summons for the opponent ([[R3837000]]), and #55 Lava Golem's base face paid with an opposing Unit, which such a play summons for the opponent too ([[R360]]), when played face-down by a Cloaked they have or are lent ([[R1040]]), are set in the backrow zone of their own player that the play named, like every Cloaked play ([[R783004]]). Set, their text is inert ([[R3806000]]), so nothing hands them over: they stand set on their player's side under their player's control, and no `summoned` names the opponent. Their Activate: Reveal animates them into their controller's unit zone, entering it on that turn and so summoning sick then ([[R4237000]]), and their Cry runs there. A reveal is a firing, not a play ([[R783006]]), so the "summon this for your opponent" of a play never reads it. Hearthstone has no face-down play, so this follows the Cloaked row of [[§6.1]]: a set card is a Trap of its own player's until it reveals.

Engine: `play_steps::handed_over_zone` hands over no card stamped `set_as`, so [[§10.5]] step 4 places the set card in the zone the play named, taking a fresh id as any face-down card does ([[R227]]). Before this, the hand-over moved the set card into the opponent's unit row, where it stood still set and its later reveal stamped an entry no event made (fuzz seeds 9854 and handicapped 6928, invariant I4). A face-up play is unchanged: a Flying Golem named into the backrow is still handed over ([[R1081000]]).

Rejected: handing the card over at its reveal, since a reveal reads no play and #55's Tribute is paid at the set, not at the reveal. Also rejected: refusing these cards a Cloaked play, which would make a lent Cloaked ([[R1040]]) skip two Units with no text of theirs saying so.
