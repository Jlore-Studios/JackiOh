The client drew about fifteen dialogs, each its own way: fourteen scrims, raw stacking numbers, three
families of keys, four kinds of close control, the board's confirms putting the safe choice first and
the menus' putting it last. A dialog is now one component, `Dialog` (`apps/web/src/game/Dialog.tsx`),
and the board's confirms (Concede this game?, Leave the match? [[R2479000]] [[R2973000]], Leave this
game? [[R765]], Leave this fight? [[R2972000]]) and the practice result draw through it; each still
decides nothing, and a Leave makes the move its route's leave guard held ([[R1754000]]). Every later
dialog follows this convention.

**Close.** A `dialog` closes by its ✕, at its top right, named "Close". An `alertdialog` has no ✕: its
Cancel, the safe choice, is its close. Its title is an `h2`, and its line, when it has one, describes it.

**Order.** The safe choice comes first and holds the focus from the start, so a stray Enter or Space
never acts. The act is last, at the right, and a `danger` answer (issue #3219's role) is always last,
whatever order its caller gave. A third answer goes between, and the safe choice then takes the first
row. On the narrowest phones (360 px and under) the row stacks in the same order, the safe choice on top.

**Keys.** Escape, Back (a layer, [[R2127000]]) and a click on the scrim close it, wherever the focus
is. Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside it. When it closes, the focus goes back to the control that had it
when it opened, unless the caller has moved it by then (the board's Concede puts it on the match menu
when its row is folded away). The dialog takes no key handler of its own ([[R2126000]]): its keys are
`useModalKeys`'s.

**Layer.** Its scrim stands at `--z-dialog`, one surface token above the prompt's scrim, the result
overlay, the drag layer and the toasts, and is one color, the wood at 70 %.

**The practice result** is now a layer, where Back, Escape, its ✕ and a click outside the panel mean
View the board, so the board is one press away by any of them. This amends [[R2127000]], whose result
dialogs kept Back as it was; the match's and a lesson's result dialogs still do.

**Rejected:** `useModalOverlay` (`cards/inspect/store.ts`), whose trap needs `role="dialog"`, whose
Escape reaches the board and whose close puts the focus back over the caller's own move; ordering by
role alone, where a menu's Cancel and Send would put the act first; `--z-modal`, under the drag layer
and level with the prompt's scrim; the menus' order, the act first and Cancel last.
