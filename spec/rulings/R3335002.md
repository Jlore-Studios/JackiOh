The issue asked that the Fleet track's rewards be special the way the Jlorious card back is: earned,
never bought or rolled. This rules the gate.

**Earned only.** A registry entry marked `track` (with its `trackLevel`, [[R3335001]]) or `jlorious`
([[R2755000]], [[R3335003]]) is **earned only**. The registry's check refuses an earned-only entry that is
a chase, a default, a starter or a per-card cosmetic, one marked both `track` and `jlorious`, and a
`track` without a level or a level without a `track`. So [[R1333000]]'s chase mark is exactly the
Legendary and Mythic cosmetics but the earned-only ones, amending [[R2755000]]'s single exception.

**Nothing offers one.** No duplicate's global roll ([[§14.3]]) and no cosmetic pack ([[§14.6]]) drops an
earned-only cosmetic; no token buys one and the Chase Shard bar refuses one, each with a 400 that spends
nothing and the shop offering neither ([[§14.5]]); and a ladder bot's wardrobe never draws one however
many people own it (amending [[R1834005]]). Only its level grants a Fleet reward, with the reason
`track`, and only reaching Jlorious a Jlorious one.

**The dev fill.** A dev account's fill grants every Fleet reward, so a developer can wear each, and still
leaves out the two Jlorious cosmetics ([[§14.10]], [[R1300000]]). Practice, hotseat and the end-to-end
tests treat every one as owned ([[R1310004]]).

Hearthstone semantics: a reward track's cosmetics are earned on the track, never crafted or opened.

Rejected: leaving the Fleet rewards chases by their rarity, which the Chase Shard bar could then buy; and
leaving them out of the dev fill as the Jlorious cosmetics are, which would keep a developer from
checking the thirty looks without playing the track.
