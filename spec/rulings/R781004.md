Keymaster Keenus prints `Equip` and "Aura: Has {keywords|random combat keyword|random combat keywords}." on both faces (patch v0.3.X, the designer's), the number declared as `keywords`, 5 on the base face and 10 on the Radiant face, so a Nerf or a Buff tunes it ([[R386]], never below 1). Its stats, cost and rarity stay; the Radiant Death and the 21 printed keywords go ([[R1084]], [[R1085]]).

- **The pool.** A combat keyword is a keyword of the random keyword pool ([[R21]], §6.1's combat keyword pool), so never Equip, Immutable, Stack, Lucky, Untributable or Temporary, nor a harmful keyword. Armor and Spell Damage come as Armor 1 and Spell Damage +1.
- **When they are drawn.** §10.4's layers read with no rng, so the Aura cannot draw as it is read: the keywords are drawn with the match rng as the card arrives on the field (played, summoned, Flickered, back through Reborn) and kept on the card (`draw_combat_keywords`), where the Aura reads them back (`conditionalKeywords`). They stay until the card leaves the field, whose reset clears them ([[R78]]), so it draws afresh each time it arrives. A card that holds a draw keeps it.
- **What is drawn.** Different keywords, none it already has from any source, and no Taunt beside Indestructible, which an Indestructible card never has ([[R347]]); with fewer left than it should have, it takes every one there is. A Taunt drawn before an Indestructible is held down by it. The same rule now reads every random grant from the pool (Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao, a Buff's keyword row): none offers Taunt to an Indestructible card.
- **Vanilla.** The Aura is its text, so a Vanilla takes its keywords with the rest of its text (§10.4), where a keyword granted to it would stay.
- **Equip.** Played as Equip onto a Unit ([[R1086]]), Keenus fuses into it and the kept Unit carries its text, the Aura included; what Keenus drew ceased to exist with it, so the kept Unit draws its own as it fuses, as [[R43]]'s start-of-game roll runs for a kept card (`fusedInto`, run by the Fuse on the card it keeps).
