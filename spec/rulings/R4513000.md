Quality gates ([[§9.9]]) derive their pass thresholds (`gate_needed`) from measured win rates on tuning deals (`AI_GATE.measured_rate`). Prior to this measurement, those rates were held to the TypeScript AI's historical figures from September 2026 (94.5% vs random, 68.0% vs greedy, 91.3% Hard vs Easy).

On commit `39584da843` on Intel(R) Xeon(R) Platinum 8488C (2 vCPUs), `cargo jackioh gate --measure --games 50` measured 50 games per matchup on the tuning series (`AI_TUNING_SERIES`, `tune:<matchup>:<n>`) at `AI_GATE_BUDGET` (per maintainer decision on issue #4513 Option 1):
- `ai-vs-random`: 50 wins and 0 turn-cap draws of 50; rate: 1.000 (95% Wilson: [0.929, 1.000]); needed: 20 -> 19, 50 -> 48, 100 -> 95 (350.9s)
- `ai-vs-greedy`: 46 wins and 0 turn-cap draws of 50; rate: 0.920 (95% Wilson: [0.812, 0.968]); needed: 20 -> 14, 50 -> 35, 100 -> 70 (429.7s)
- `hard-vs-easy`: 47 wins and 0 turn-cap draws of 50; rate: 0.940 (95% Wilson: [0.838, 0.979]); needed: 20 -> 15, 50 -> 38, 100 -> 75 (876.3s)
Total wall time: 1656.9s (27.6m). Every game was clean (B31): 0 rejected, 0 thrown, 0 fallbacks, 0 sim panics, 0 replay errors, and replay hashes matched.

`AI_GATE.measured_rate` is updated to each measured rate that is higher than the baseline:
- `ai_vs_random`: 1.0 (raised from 0.945)
- `ai_vs_greedy`: 0.92 (raised from 0.68)
- `hard_vs_easy`: 0.94 (raised from 0.913)

No rate measured lower than today's baseline; all three rates increased. Against greedy the measured rate (92.0%) now clears the brief's 70%, raising the floor from 10 of 20 and 28 of 50 to the brief's 14 of 20 and 35 of 50.

Rejected: taking measurements from the fixed 20-seed gate smoke series instead of fresh tuning deals; lowering any rate without explicit user sign-off; evaluating floor pass/fail during `--measure`.

It changes no rule of the game: it updates the AI quality gates' measured baseline rates and adds the `--measure` CLI mode.
