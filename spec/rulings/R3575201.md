A hand card's price in the AI's evaluation starts from its printed cost, and `cost_shift` moved it by `hand_cost_delta` for each crystal its own `costMod` moves its cost ([[R65]]): an Upgrade's cheaper card counted more, a Degrade's dearer one less. A `costOverride`, which [[R65]] starts the cost from in place of the printed one and [[R78]] keeps in every zone, moved nothing, so a Mineral S+ #52 Airblast Drill and #53 Laser Drill add at (0) over its printed (1), or the Phase Fabric #57's Radiant face adds at (0), counted as the (1) card it is not (issue #3575). No game rule changes.

**What it counts.** With an override, `cost_shift` is the card's own cost (`own_cost`, the override plus its `costMod`, floored at 0) less its printed cost (`printed_cost`, floored at 0); without one it is what it was, the `costMod`'s move. So a card added at (0) over its printed (1) counts as the same card under an Upgrade's −1, and one added dearer than printed counts less. An X-cost card has no own cost and moves nothing, as before.

**Rejected.** Reading the override as the card's base cost in the hand price (`hand_per_cost`), which would count a free card as a weaker one, where the override says only what it costs.
