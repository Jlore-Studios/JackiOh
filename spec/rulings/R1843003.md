On Edge of the Universe's Radiant face the fleer chooses, as it resolves, whether the flight leaves the other player a draw or a loss: an ordinary `mode` prompt (`choose_mode`, two options) whose answer resumes the card at the step that flees with it ([[R1843002]]). Nothing about it is new to the answerers: `legal_actions` lists both answers to the fleer and none to the other seat, whose view shows only that a prompt is open; the random policy draws among them ([[R44]]); a timeout answers it as the AI policy does and the game ends ([[R79]]); and the practice AI's search answers it, scoring each answer through `reduce` (both are a win for it). Paused on the question, the state survives JSON as any prompt does.
