[[R1759000]] keeps past the beam's cut the first `AI_SEARCH.position_switches` stand-downs, a stand-down being a switch of a unit in Attack Position to Defense Position where a unit of the opponent's would kill it and survive the fight (`kills_and_survives`). That test read stats and combat keywords only. An enemy that could not attack at all on its next turn still made a stand-down, and each one took a kept slot.

A stand-down now counts an enemy unit only when it could attack the unit on its controller's next turn, by the reads the AI's face threat already makes ([[§9.9]]):

- it is in Attack Position, or in Defense Position with Deft, which switches and still attacks in one turn ([[R49]]); any other unit in Defense Position spends that turn's exertion switching back ([[§4.1]]);
- it may attack at all (`can_swing`): no "Can't attack", no status barring its attacks, and not Frozen through that turn ([[R1174000]]);
- no attack restriction of the engine's bars it from this unit (`attack_restriction`): a Dormant card neither attacks nor is attacked ([[R1839000]]), and a unit with Flying in Attack Position is attacked only by a unit with Flying or Ranged ([[R783002]]).

Only then is it asked, as before, whether it would kill the unit and survive. The check looks one turn ahead: an enemy in Defense Position may threaten the unit two turns out, and the AI plans again every turn. A Deft unit in Defense Position counts whatever the evaluation's `deft_defense_attacks` says, because R49 is a rule of the game. This adds no rule of the game, no constant and no evaluation weight. It reads only what both players see (positions, keywords, Frozen and Dormant, attack bars), so no hidden card is read. `kills_and_survives` keeps its other use, the attack tiers, unchanged.
