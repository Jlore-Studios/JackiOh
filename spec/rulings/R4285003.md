S #83 Halo since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes IV: "Cry: Discover a Rare Weapon (Radiant: an Epic Weapon) and Equip it into this, twice." The Cry is one standard Discover of three ([[§6.3]] Discover) from the Weapons of its rarity, and the one card picked is fused into Halo `weapons` (2) times, each a fresh copy and its own fusion ([[R102]], [[R1841002]]'s pick fused as an Equip play's card is; `equip_picks`'s `copies`), so it holds two of the same Weapon and wields both its Activates. The issue likened it to S #79 Orca's Radiant "Equip it into this, twice"; Orca's twice is two offers in turn, each pick its own, which is not this card's one Discover, so the card's own words are what is built. The text reads its declared number as "2 times", since no renderer writes a number as a word. Through v0.6.7k it made three picks in turn, each a choice of two, each fused once (`script_v0_6_7k`, kept, [[R767000]]).
