A ladder bot is an opponent the server plays itself, so a player who queues when no one else is there still gets a ranked game. It is a profile whose `kind` is `bot` (a person's is `human`; migration 0030, and no client role may read the column), a `bots` row holding its strength, speed, persona, schedule and decks ([[R1446]]), and a rating of its own ([[R610]]). A ranked match seats one only through the empty-queue fallback ([[R1445]]), and only while the server's `LADDER_BOTS` is `on`: unset or `off`, nothing seats a bot. [[R610]] rates a bot's games by the same rule as a player's, and the rated game records its side with the `ai` pilot and its bot id ([[R611]], [[R1447]]).

**How it plays.** A bot plays on this spec's resources, whatever its strength: no practice handicap applies to it ([[R180]]–[[R184]]). It decides with the practice AI's search ([[§9.9]]) at its own strength, one of five ([[R1446]]): a search budget, a softmax temperature over the search's finalist lines, and a chance to pass over a lethal line the solver found. At a temperature of 0 with no chance to pass, it decides exactly as the practice AI does. It reads only what [[R185]] lets the AI read, so no hidden card changes its moves either. The decision runs beside the match's actor, never holding it up, and is capped in wall-clock time (`BOT_DECISION_DEADLINE_MS`), past which it plays the best line found so far.

**Pace.** A bot moves at a person's pace. Each move waits a think time drawn from [[R1442]]'s fitted think times for its kind of move, first in the turn or later (`THINK_TIME_FITS`), or from hand-set priors until a fit for it lands, times the bot's own speed. The time the decision took counts toward it. The move goes out no sooner than `BOT_THINK_MIN_MS` (600 ms) after the bot came to owe it and no later than `BOT_THINK_MAX_MS` (30 s), and never so late that less than `BOT_CLOCK_MARGIN_MS` (5 s) is left on its acting clock ([[R79]]), so waiting never times it out.

**Replay.** A bot's random streams, its search's, its think time's and its persona's, are the server's own, each seeded from the match seed and the seat the bot began in, and none is the match rng (`state.rng`). They reach no state, log or replay hash, so a match with a bot replays from `(seed, log)` like any other ([[§9.5]]). An actor rebuilt after a restart resumes the bot's seat with its streams started again, which changes nothing a replay reads.

**The seat.** A bot has no socket and needs none. Its seat never counts as disconnected, so it never gets [[R744]]'s grace and never loses by `disconnectExpired`. [[R188]] holds for it as for the practice AI: it never concedes, never offers a draw and declines every draw offer. Its moves carry server nonces (`srv-bot-…`), the prefix no client may send ([[R270]]), so they are the server's own actions, and [[R1442]] keeps no think time for them. It emotes through its persona ([[R645]]), one per bot, choosing only within the hand its seat was dealt ([[R1344]]); the actor relays each emote to the player as it relays a player's, under the same rate limit ([[R643]]). It never offers or accepts a rematch ([[R672]]): with no socket, it reads as gone once the game ends, so the player is never shown the offer.

**What a client sees.** Nothing a client reads labels a bot or tells its seat from a person's (CLAUDE.md rule 7), and no response carries its `kind`, its pilot or its nonces. Its username, its portrait and the age of its account are drawn as a player's might be ([[R1446]]). A bot has no season row ([[R610]]), so the match screen's read of both seats' ranks ([[R612]]) shows a bot's seat the rank its rating calls for among the ratings of the season's placed players. Bots stay off the leaderboard ([[R610]]).
