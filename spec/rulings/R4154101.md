Issue #4154 rewrote S+ #82 Endless Expedition as "For the rest of the game, you have (1) less mana, and at the start of each of your turns, add a random Terminus card to your hand. It costs (3)." (Radiant: "a random Radiant Terminus card"). "You have (1) less mana" is [[R3569008]]'s loss, the verb its v0.6.5b script ran (`lose_max_mana`): as the Spell resolves its caster's persistent modifier to max mana falls by the declared `mana` (1), once, so max mana is that much lower at every refresh for the rest of the game, an empty crystal lost first; it is not a loss taken again at each turn, nor this turn's mana alone. The cards come from the rest-of-game step [[R458]] leaves, badged in the card's words, at the start of each of its caster's turns from the next on, in [[R62]]'s delayed stage before the draw; none comes as it resolves, the issue having dropped the "now". Each is one draw from the Terminus pool ([[R3569007]]), costing (3) whatever it printed, a Radiant one on the Radiant face. `mana` is declared again, as through v0.6.5b, so a Buff lowers the loss and a Nerf raises it; the (3) is a price ([[R1748003]]). Through v0.6.6g the card added a card costing (2) now and at each of its caster's turn starts and lost no mana (`script_v0_6_6g`, [[R767000]]). Rejected: losing a crystal at each turn start, which would read "lose 1 mana each turn"; and a card now, which the issue's text no longer prints.
