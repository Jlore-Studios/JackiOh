Allure marks each enemy Unit on the field at that end of turn (the top of its pile, an animated
card included) with a public `allure` mark ([[R437]]). At the start of the Allurer's next turn
([[R62]]'s start-of-turn delayed effects, as #50 K-Pop Fanatic's steal), each marked Unit still
under the opponent's control is stolen, in the order marked, which is lane order ([[R15]]). If no
unit zone is open, it is destroyed instead (`otherwise: destroy` on `steal`): an ordinary destroy,
so Death and Reborn apply and an Indestructible Unit stays where it is ([[R46]]). A marked Unit
that leaves the field loses its mark and is not stolen ([[R174]]). The Allure still lands if Jade
Beauty has left the field ([[R76]]), and a stolen Unit is summoning sick ([[R171]]). A Unit already
its taker's is neither stolen nor destroyed.
