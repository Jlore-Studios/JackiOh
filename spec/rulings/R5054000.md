**Why.** A card's adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]) reads its seen games, the games it reached its owner's hand, played or not, against its never-drawn games. The drawn games it was never played in are what keep a win-more card honest, since such a card is played when its seat is ahead and held when it is behind. [[R4673000]]'s faces kept the never-drawn baseline but read only the seats that played the card on the face ([[R1147000]]'s `playedNormal` and `playedRadiant`), the played-only reading [[R2777000]] replaced, so a win-more card read high on Normal and on Radiant. A record knew a card's face only at its play, so the fold had nothing else to read. The owner asked in issue #5054 that the face a card was seen on be recorded, and the faces read from it.

**The record.** Each seat of a game record ([[R376]]) written since this ruling carries `heldRadiant`: the dealt cards the seat held Radiant in its hand at some point from its opening hand on, each once, in the order it first held one so. `summarize_game` notes a card when it enters the hand Radiant (`addedToHand`), turns Radiant there (`radiantSet`, or a `transformed` that leaves a dealt card Radiant in the hand), or is Radiant in the hand as the first turn begins (the opening hand). A card Radiant only before the mulligans resolved was never seen, and a Created card is never drawn ([[R2089000]]), so neither is noted. The field is present on every fresh summary, empty when there were none, so the fold can tell it from an older record, which has none. A record whose `heldRadiant` is not a list of card ids is refused.

**The tallies.** Every card's totals gain `seenRadiant` and `seenNormal`, counted only from the seats whose record carries `heldRadiant`:

- **seen Radiant**: the seat saw the card, as [[R2777000]] reads seen (played, cast or summoned as [[R4279000]] counts it, or in the hand and never played), and it was in `heldRadiant`, cast or summoned Radiant, or played Radiant;
- **seen Normal**: the seat saw it and never on its Radiant face.

Over the records that carry the field, the two add up to the seen games. Each is absent from a stored total while empty, so a total folded before reads and is stored as before, and no migration is needed.

**The faces.** On Normal and Radiant, a card's seen side (`face_side`) is its seen tally of that face. So the face's games, win rate and range, adjusted win rate and range, tier, the face's field (`face_stats_field`) and the drill-down's face line and Radiant gap ([[R4673001]]) all read the seats that saw the card on that face. The never-drawn baseline, the field's method and the bands stay [[R4673000]]'s; All is unchanged.

**What stays on plays.** `playedRadiant` and `playedNormal` stay in the totals and the reports. The drills about plays keep them: the turn played and the co-played cards by face ([[R4673001]]) and a variant's share of the plays on a face ([[R4676000]]). A Created card's faces stay its uses' ([[R2089000]]): a Created copy is never drawn, so it has no seen side. Play rate and Decisiveness stay All's ([[R4673000]]).

**No backfill.** A record is folded once, and the raw records are purged a week after, so nothing is refolded. A total folded before this ruling has no seen-face tallies, and the faces read "not enough games" on it until new games arrive, as [[R4673001]]'s face drills read empty on older totals. The old played-only figure is never shown under a face's name. The AI's development runs fill the same tallies.

**Rejected.**

- **Falling back to the played split where a total has no seen faces.** It shows the inflated figure the owner asked to be rid of, under the same name.
- **The face a card entered the hand on.** A card upgraded in the hand would read normal, though the seat held it Radiant, and Radiant upgrades in the hand are the game's core loop.
- **A never-drawn baseline counted only from the records that carry `heldRadiant`.** It needs every never-drawn tally split by record, and a card's window reads one play of the card, so its never-drawn games read the same card either way.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
