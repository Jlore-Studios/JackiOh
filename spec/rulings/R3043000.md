Meditative #17 True Craft a Card crafts with about 20% fewer points ([[R880]]). Its `craft` prompt, the one of no mode, has `TRUE_CRAFT_POINTS_PERCENT` (80) of the crafter's budget at the chosen cost, `2 + 5 ×` the cost, rounded to the nearest whole point, a half up (`true_craft_points_budget`). That gives 2, 6, 10, 14 and 18 points at costs 0 to 4, where it had 2, 7, 12, 17 and 22. The per-mana rate falls by exactly a fifth, 5 to 4. The base, 2 × 80% = 1.6, rounds back to 2, so a cost-0 craft keeps its 2 points. The lines (24), effects (8) and numbers (1 to 10) are as before ([[R883]]). A recipe one point over is refused with the state unchanged and the prompt open, as any recipe over the points is.

- **Keyed on the mode.** The budget follows the prompt, not the card. An unframed recipe's points are 80% under a prompt of no mode and the crafter's whole under a prompt with a mode. The answer check, the editor's preview (`craft_preview`, so the meter shows the same budget), the offer's verbs that fit the budget alone ([[R1563007]]) and the presets ([[R881]]) all read it, so the Unit preset spends exactly the prompt's budget. Meditative #17 opens its prompt with no mode, so no new mode was needed. A custom card's `choose_craft` with no mode gets the same 80%.
- **Scratch Pad keeps the whole budget.** Meditative+ #26's `scratch` prompt still has `2 + 5 ×` the cost points ([[R1182002]]), and its presets are rolled within them. "From scratch" stays the canvas. The issue names one card, so the rejected alternative, Scratch Pad following True Craft down, would have cut a card nobody asked to change.
- **The mint's guard.** `craft_card` cannot see which prompt answered it, so its guard (unreachable from an answered prompt, [[R880]]) checks a recipe against the most any prompt grants it: its frame's points, or with no frame Scratch Pad's whole budget.
- **[[R1563008]] holds.** A Potion's 4 points are still fewer than the crafter's at the Potion's cost (1): Meditative #17's 6 and Scratch Pad's 7. Neither gives way. A HUGE Potion's 20 already exceeded the crafter's 17 at cost 3, and nothing ruled otherwise.

Rejected: rounding down (1, 5, 9, 13, 17). That halves a cost-0 craft, takes 25% off the five budgets together where nearest takes 17%, and at cost 0 leaves 1 point, under which no verb fits a Start or End of turn hat (twice the points), so a Field Spell preset drawn on one would drop out of [[R881]]'s four. Rounding up gives the same five budgets at 80% but drifts above the percent if the constants are retuned. Hearthstone has no rule for scaling a budget (where it halves a number, the card says which way it rounds), so "about 20%" decided it: the nearest point lands closest to it. The budget is an engine number, not a card's, so an older game that opened True Craft's prompt may no longer fold to its hash, since its offer, its presets and its answer all lie within the budget. Such a game is refused as `rules_changed`, as an engine rule's change retires a game ([[R767000]]). It edits [[§6.3]]'s Craft row, [[§8.8]]'s row 17, [[R880]] and [[R883]].
