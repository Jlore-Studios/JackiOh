TG #35 names no side, so a non-Token Unit's death on either side summons a Rush Token for the player who controlled it as it died ([[R172]]), after its own Death in [[R68]]'s order, one token per death. The token goes to [[R64]]'s leftmost open zone, not the zone the Unit died in (the `destroyed` event carries no lane, [[R89]]), and a full row summons none ([[§3.2]]). A Token's death gives nothing, so the Rush Tokens never feed it ([[§7]]); the Radiant face's are Radiant (6/6 Rush, Cleave).
