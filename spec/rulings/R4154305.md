Issue #4154's "Nerf each result slightly (that are easy to nerf)" for M #95, by [[R4154303]]'s rule: entry 4, "Your hero gains 5 Armor and you heal it 5", becomes "Your hero gains 4 Armor and you heal it 4" (`CHAOS_MED_HERO_ARMOR` and `CHAOS_MED_HEAL` 5 → 4), a fifth of each. It is the one entry with a number a step can cut slightly: [[R3135001]] already took the fuse to one copy ([[R1242]]) and the Acclaimed cards to two ([[R1243]]), and the CN and Prime cards are two, so a step off any of them would halve it or take the clause away; the bounce's one Nerf each ([[R1244]]), the Jade Beauty, the CN Golem, the start-of-turn cast and the recursion carry no number to cut, and the (0) prices are prices. The clause `chaosRolled` names ([[R436]]) and the reel's short words follow ("Hero gains 4 Armor and heals 4").

The numbers are the engine's, so the table as it was through v0.6.6h stays as `CHAOS_MED_EFFECTS_V0_6_6H` (entry 4 over `CHAOS_MED_HERO_ARMOR_V0_6_6H` and `CHAOS_MED_HEAL_V0_6_6H`, 5 each, under its old clause), which the card's `script_v0_6_6h` rolls, so a replay of v0.6.6h or before resolves as it was played ([[R767000]]). Its announcement's old clause has no reel words of its own any more and shows as the engine wrote it.
