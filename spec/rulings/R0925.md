## R925 — The enters_hand hook

`enters_hand` is a new `Script` hook ("When this enters your hand"), run by the NEW
`AddToHandEvent`-agnostic `draw::run_arrival_hooks` on every arrival in a hand (§2.4: a draw, an
add or a Discover, a bounce or return, a gift, a steal, and the opening deal and the mulligan's
replacements), beside [[R151]]'s start-of-game clause: the hand clause runs after the whole
start-of-game clause, only while the card is still in a hand. The replacement steps never fire it
for a card that moves without §2.4 (a shuffle into a deck, a burn). The mulligan and redraws at
the table do what an add in the middle does. The `transform_random` rider `arrives` (opt-in: only
the new verbs use it, so nothing shipped changes) runs the new card's own `run_arrival_hooks` when
it lands in a hand. The fuse hook copy in combine_objects and the registry fingerprint both name
it.

As built: never a deck arrival, and the hook is an effect list like any other — a question in it
pauses the effects after it.

Since patch v0.3.11 no shipped card uses it: Meditative #37 CN in a Bottle casts on draw instead
([[R1149001]]). The hook stays for custom cards and the replays of earlier versions (CLAUDE.md
rule 13, [[R767000]]).
