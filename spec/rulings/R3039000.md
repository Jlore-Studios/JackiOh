The owner's pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #3000, section 1) makes an awakening for good. Until then Dormant was a status that [[R78]]'s reset took off as the card left the field, and a card that prints it started it again whenever it next entered ([[R1839000]]), so an Imprisoned Unit killed after it awoke and brought back slept again and answered its "When this awakens" a second time.

**The mark.** `slumber::awaken_card`, the one path every awakening takes (its countdown's end, [[R1839001]]; its own Activate or the Awaken verb, [[R1839002]]; a Dormant Starship's launch, [[R1852003]]), writes `CardInstance.awakened`, only ever `true` and absent on every other card, so a game where nothing awakens serializes and hashes as before. It is what has happened to the card, not a status: it is kept in every zone and through [[R78]]'s and [[R766]]'s resets, as `flashbackSpent` was. `slumber::start_slumber_on_field` starts no Dormant on a card that carries it, however it arrives: played again from its graveyard, returned by Reborn, summoned from its graveyard, or returned to a hand and played. A card that left the field still Dormant never awakened, so it is Dormant again the next time it enters, its printed countdown afresh. A copy ([[R57]]) or a Transform is a new card without the mark, and so is the fresh Dormant Starship a dying piece summons ([[R1852000]]), which is Dormant. The mark is written on whatever card awakens, so it would hold as well for a card an effect made Dormant; no card does yet.

**The Imprisoned Units.** Stinkyscape #17 Imprisoned Ship Smith and #19 Imprisoned Cosmality, killed after they awoke and Reborn or played again from the graveyard, come back awake, so neither answers its "When this awakens" again: one Fiery Waraxe, one split of 10. Their rows are unchanged.

**An awakened Dormant Starship that returns.** A Dormant Starship awakens as it launches ([[R1852003]]), so a launched one carries the mark. Should it come back to the field (a Reborn given it, a summon from its graveyard) it is not Dormant: its hull starts a fresh, empty memory as any arrival's does ([[R1852005]]), `start_starship_on_field` unchanged, so it stands awake, holds no pieces, is not launched, draws no name and offers no launch, which only a Dormant Starship keeps. It is not its controller's Dormant Starship, so the next piece of theirs to die fuses into the Dormant Starship they have, a new one summoned when they have none ([[R1852000]]). Rejected: launching it again as it returns, which would fire its launch's triggers with nothing fused into it.
