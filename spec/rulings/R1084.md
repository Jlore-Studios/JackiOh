Keenus's 21 keywords (MD-D21) are the card's printed data, not "whatever the engine knows": Taunt, Armor 1, Rush, Charge, First Strike, Poisonous, Lifesteal, Reborn, Divine Shield, Trample, Cleave, Pierce, Indestructible, Immutable, Stack, Lucky 1, Spell Damage +1, Immune to Spells, Windfury, Deft and Magnetic. Can't attack, Brittle and Temporary only hurt; Animated and Animated on your turn mean something only on a backrow card; the static restrictions and the card keywords Cast on draw and Quickdraw are not keywords in this sense. A later keyword joins only by a patch. [[R347]] drops its Taunt while it is Indestructible, and its numbered keywords move under Nerf and Buff's X row ([[R386]]).
