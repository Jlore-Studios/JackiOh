The Anomaly Found! token replaces its caster's side of the field: it exiles every card on it, then lays an Anomaly's board in its place (`boardReplaced`). In the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 3565 it exiled S+ #112 Fortress, immune, and I21 named the Spell.

**The side goes with all it holds.** A board replacement is a board move, as a swap and a restore are ([[R4508001]]) and as a lane taken whole is ([[R4508004]]): after a `boardReplaced` in a Spell's run, I21 allows an `exiled`, a `controlChanged` and a `bounced` of an immune Unit.
