Issue #4154 rewrote Burn as "deal damage at end of turn, then is removed". At the end of every turn, whoever's it is, after the end-of-turn delayed effects and before cleanup ([[§2.2]], [[R62]]), every Burn on the field ticks once: the active player's hero and then their Units in [[R68]]'s order, then their opponent's hero and Units the same way. Each tick is one [[§4.4]] hit of the whole count from no source, unpierced, so Armor, a Divine Shield and a hero's caps meet it as before, and the Burn is then removed whatever the hit did. So a Burn put on during a turn lands at that turn's end, before the other player acts, and never again; a Burn put on after the tick (by a cleanup step) waits for the next turn's end. The targets are read before the first hit and no state check runs inside the tick ([[R59]]): the settle after it collects what it killed, and a hero it brought to 0 loses before the next turn starts. Toxic is unchanged ([[R3540001]]): the issue's "Poison deals damage at start of turn and lasts forever" is what Toxic already did. The AI reads a Burn on its first projected tick alone ([[R3822000]], [[R4065000]]). Games played before the change that a Burn touched no longer reach their recorded hash and are refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). Rejected: ticking at the end of the Burned card's controller's own turn only, which would give a Burned enemy Unit its whole turn first and make the one hit later than the start-of-turn tick it replaced; and keeping the drop by 3, which the issue took out.
