Issue #1953's client half, beside [[R1953000]]'s numbers. None of it is a rule of the game
(CLAUDE.md rule 7): it is how the client draws what the account owns and where it spends a token.

- **Chase cosmetics move.** Every Legendary and Mythic foil, finish, border, playmat, backdrop and
  card back in the registry, the chases of [[R1333000]] and [[R1833000]], the two starter chases
  among them, has a looping animation of its own wherever it is drawn: on a card face in a game, a
  board's playmat, backdrop and face-down backs, and the Collection's card view, its strip's
  samples, its mini boards and its tiles. A finish or border that moves takes one of the
  `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX` motion slots a foil does on a board ([[R1833000]]); the Collection's
  samples, bounded by the registry, move without one. Each is a CSS animation of transform and
  opacity, and each holds a still frame under reduced motion, with Animated foil off for a card's
  kinds, and on a tile that is neither equipped, hovered nor focused. None was dropped to Epic.
- **A card with no pick wears its kind's default.** The Collection's "Defaults for every card", the
  three selects of the account-wide foil, finish and border ([[R1295000]], [[R1579000]]), is gone.
  With no way left to change those picks, a card with no pick of a kind wears the kind's default,
  not the stored account pick, so no setting a player cannot see dresses their cards ([[R1655000]],
  *The fallback*). The `cosmetics` settings group keeps its six keys and their values, so an older
  client and this one never overwrite each other ([[R1655000]]); the playmat, backdrop and card
  back still read it. The Full-art cards setting stays a setting, the default of a card's full art.
- **A cosmetic token is spent in place.** The shop no longer picks a cosmetic for a token: it keeps
  the card token's picker, both bars and the cosmetic tokens held, and links to the Collection.
  Where the Collection shows a cosmetic locked that could otherwise be equipped, a card's own foil,
  finish, border or full art in its view, or a playmat, backdrop or card back tile, it offers a held
  token that covers it: "Unlock with an Epic token (2 held)". A Rare token covers Common and Rare, an
  Epic token Epic and below, and the cheaper of the two that covers it is offered; a chase, a
  default or a starter never shows the offer ([[R1305003]], [[§14.2]]), and a per-card one shows
  only on a card the account owns `MAX_COPIES` of, the only card the server lets a token's
  cosmetic go on. The offer, once confirmed ([[R2480000]]), sends `POST /api/tokens/redeem-cosmetic` and reads the account's
  cosmetics again, so the cosmetic shows owned; a refusal shows the server's words and changes
  nothing. The deck builder's slot selects list only what the account owns ([[R1833000]]), so they
  show nothing locked and offer no token.

This amends [[R1655000]] (*The fallback*: a card with no pick wears its kind's default, not the
account's pick) and [[R1833000]] (the account-wide foil, finish and border selects are gone, a
token is spent on the cosmetic's own preview, and the chases move in the Collection too).

Hearthstone semantics: a player crafts the card or cosmetic they are looking at from the collection,
where they see it, and a premium version is animated.

Rejected: keeping the account-wide picks dressing cards, which no screen could change any more;
deleting the `cosmetics` group's three card keys, which would make an older client and this one
overwrite each other ([[R1655000]]); dropping a chase that did not animate to Epic, which would have
broken the chase mark and the account cosmetics' floor ([[R1333000]], [[R1661000]]); and keeping the
shop's picker beside the offers, two ways to spend one token.
