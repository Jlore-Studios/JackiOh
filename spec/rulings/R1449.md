Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A portrait has two presses, and since 2026-10-11 they do different things. Until then a click opened the hero's inspect view, which held the menu ([[R1330]]). The user asked for the click to open the emote menu, as it had before [[R1330]], and for a hold to open the larger view.

**The click.** A click on a hero that is not a legal target, or Enter or Space on its portrait's button, toggles that hero's menu, hung off the portrait as before [[R1330]]. On your portrait it is the emote menu of your dealt hand, voice lines arced above the portrait and emoji in a row below ([[R1343]]); on the opponent's it is the one-item Mute emotes menu ([[R643]]). A second click on the same portrait closes it, and a press on that portrait is not a press outside the menu. The menu also closes on a press anywhere else, on Escape, on a pick, on the mute, on the start of any drag or targeting, on a hotseat hand-over and at the end of the match.

**The hold.** A hold opens the hero's inspect view ([[R1330]]), with the same menu drawn inside it. Any of these is a hold:
- a touch, or a mouse's or pen's main button, held on the portrait for `LONG_PRESS_MS` (450 ms) without moving more than `LONG_PRESS_SLOP_PX` (10 px);
- a right-click on the portrait;
- the context-menu key, or Shift+F10, on the portrait's button.

A hold on a portrait whose menu is open turns that menu into the view. The click that ends a hold is swallowed: it neither closes the view it opened nor opens the menu as well. A mouse that moves past the slop before the hold, or a button other than the main one, opens nothing.

**Targeting still wins** ([[R643]]). A hero that is a legal target takes the click as the target, and a hold on it opens nothing. While a card or an attacker is selected, neither a click nor a hold on a hero that is not a target opens anything; that is also when the board takes a right-click to cancel.

**The button.** The portrait's button is named for both presses ("Emotes for Mr. Vanilla, your hero; hold, or Shift+F10, to inspect"; "Mute emotes for …, the opponent's hero; …"), carries `aria-haspopup="menu"`, and its tooltip says the same in short. The portrait reacts as either opens ([[R1331]]).
