Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. The frame the signature tier ([[R3332000]], [[R3400000]]) gives an effects table entry grows so Meditative's Legendaries and Mythics can each have their own signature ([[R3405001]]):

- **Twelve more takeover looks with art.** A Mythic may name `ascent`, `workshop`, `brush`, `mine`, `quarry`, `pavilion`, `virus`, `dojo`, `studyHall`, `caneRack`, `warRoom` or `fortress` as its own (`OWN_TAKEOVER_LOOKS`), each with its tints (`TAKEOVER_TINTS`) and its art (`TAKEOVER_ART_PARTS`), drawn and stilled under Reduce Motion as [[R3400000]]'s are; YileGPT's `ai`, which had no art, draws its own now. Every part is a shape, never a card face ([[R202]]).
- **Five shared aura looks.** `ripple`, `orbit`, `steam`, `incense` and `glitch` join the shared twelve of [[R2787000]] (seventeen in all), each within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`. Unlike [[R3389000]]'s looks of a signature's own, each worn by one card, these are shared: `orbit` circles Feng Shui's compass, YileGPT Prime's brushes, Keymaster Keenus's keys and Mayor Medinamogger's coin, and `steam` curls off 饕餮 and Jade Beauty.
- **An entrance starts after its entry's hold.** Beside the landing [[R3389000]] passes an entrance (`landMs`), it is passed the hold itself (`leadMs`, the entry's lead), and a Mythic's entrance under its takeover starts no cue before it, its one shake at its landing after it, so the takeover plays first and the card lands as it ends. The entrances older than this one never read it and are unchanged.
- **A Mythic Field Spell put onto the field face up enters.** Beside a face-up play and a Unit put onto the field without a play ([[R2722001]]), a Mythic Field Spell's own `summoned` into a backrow zone with a readable definition and no `formerId` (The Cane, put there by Pareto Optimality) makes its entrance, takes its takeover and lands with its own entrance ([[R3332000]]); one set face down (its fresh id's `formerId`), a hidden one and a Legendary one do not, and a played one entered at its play.
- **One takeover per Mythic per action.** In one action's entries, a Mythic's entrance whose card already took over there yields its takeover ([[R2785000]]'s `yielded`), so a flood of Yile's Viruses takes over once; another Mythic in the same action keeps its own look (with no hold: one set piece an action), and Reduce Motion's still stays the action's first Mythic's, as before.
- **A sound as a Field Spell lands.** A signature sound sounds on a play (`cardPlayed`), a Unit's face-up landing in a unit zone, or a face-up landing in the backrow (a `summoned` into the backrow with a readable definition and no `formerId`, the row [[R3407001]] names), for a Field Spell that reaches the field without a play: The Cane. Meditative's other Field Spells sound on their play, so none sounds twice.
- **A signature's recipe draws in a sweep.** A run of hits or heals that reaches every Unit on a side plays as one sweep entry, its fog rolling over the row (`sweepAt`); a card's motif already drew over it ([[R2784000]]), but a signature's `{ bespoke }` recipe was never asked. Now the readable play or hook resolving, if its card's signature is a `{ bespoke }`, is asked about each of the sweep's events (`planSignatureSweep`), and those it replaces leave the fog, as a motif's do: Introspection's start of turn, which heals its hero and every one of its Units and hits every enemy, draws its mends and thought-darts in every game, not only against an empty board. A sweep no signature resolves rolls its fog as before.

Every new cue starts inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]). Rejected: one look shared by every Meditative Mythic, which the owner's ask (a takeover of its own, art filling the screen) rules out; a look of its own for each of the 25 permanents, as [[R3389000]] gave Classic's nine, where five shared looks in each card's own palette already set them apart; a takeover for every copy of a Mythic in one action, which a token flood turns into a strobe; starting the entrances at the entry's start under the takeover, where they played behind it unseen; leaving a sweep's fog over a signature's own hits and heals, which hid Introspection's darts whenever the other side had a Unit; and a sound per event of a card's text, which the audio budgets do not allow and which one sound at the moment its signature starts already carries.
