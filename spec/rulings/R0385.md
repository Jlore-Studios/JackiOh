Brittle N is a count on a card instance, kept in every zone the card passes through, hand to field included, as `radiant` is ([[R78]], [[R766]]), but ticking on the field only ([[R638]]). A count an effect gives is held until the card enters the field, and a printed Brittle starts when the card enters the field. At the start of its controller's turn, as a step right after the mana refresh ([[§2.2]]), the count of each card they control on the field drops by 1, but only once the card has had a full turn cycle with it: a count started on turn t ([[R638]]: the turn the card entered the field) first ticks at the first start of its controller's turn numbered t + 2 or later, and then at each start of its controller's turn, so a card that enters the field on its controller's turn 5 with Brittle 2 ticks to 1 at the start of turn 7 and crumbles at the start of turn 9. At 0 the card is destroyed: an ordinary destroy, which Indestructible ignores ([[R46]]), the count staying at 0 and checked again at each tick. "Give Brittle N" sets the count to N and "gain +N Brittle" adds N; Degrade lowers it by 1 and Upgrade raises it by 1 ([[R386]]); a Vanilla removes a printed Brittle and keeps a given one ([[§10.4]]); a copy never inherits a count ([[R57]]). The count is public on the field, except on a face-down card, whose count only its controller reads until it is revealed (C+ #74, [[R33]]), and shown to its owner in a hand; a card crumbling is reported by `crumbled { instanceId, zone }`, and the count's changes by `counterChanged` of kind `brittle`, which the other player reads only where they may read the count
