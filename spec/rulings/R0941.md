Meditative #26 Alternate Fate's "Players don't generate mana naturally" is MD-B2's aura held by a card: `static_flags.no_natural_mana`, which the start-of-turn refresh reads for both players while the card acts on the field, face-up. Max mana is still computed; current mana becomes the next-turn rider only (`refresh_mana_with`, `refreshed_mana`). Gains, prices, The Coin, Hinder and a Mana Well's start-of-turn gain are mana from effects, not generation, so they still work. Off the field the next refresh is natural.
