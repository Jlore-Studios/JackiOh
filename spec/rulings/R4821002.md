Since [[R4821000]] a `libraryOverflow` `ceased` may come from a deck with room, and the client reads the event alone (CLAUDE.md rule 7), so it cannot tell a full deck from one with room. A `ceased` card's notice on the deck pile therefore reads "Ceased to exist" where [[R318]]'s other refusals read "Deck full", and the game log says "<card> could not go into <whose> deck: it ceased to exist" in place of "<whose> deck is full: <card> ceased to exist", in both locales. `notCreated` and `graveyard` come only from a full deck and keep "Deck full" and their lines. The notice's motion, testids and `data-kind` stay as [[R318]] has them, and the words keep [[R373]]'s "deck".
