## Question

What does "Refresh" give back for Activates, and what does it never touch?

## Decision

"Refresh" for Activates gives back this turn's uses of every Activate on the controller's permanents —
"if you Refresh an Activate 4 you would get another 4 Activates" — except Activates whose effect is a
Refresh, which stay spent. Each card used this turn keeps only its Refresh abilities' counts, and its
uses record's `count` becomes their sum, so every other ability is offered again whole. Extra-turn
cooldowns are untouched, and no event is emitted, as `refreshPower` does. The engine's Refresh
ability id is `refresh`; a fusion's numbered copy (`refresh#2`) names along with it.

## Rejected alternative

Restoring the whole uses record including Refresh uses: a Refresh granting itself back would let one
Refresh pay for another forever, and the designer says Refresh never gives back Refresh.
