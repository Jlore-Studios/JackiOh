The designer's comment (MaxGoetzmann, 2026-10-01): Plague Tokens are Plague Counters everywhere a player reads them, to reduce confusion. [[§6.3]]'s row is the Plague Counter; every printed face, prompt, preview label, glossary row and error message reads Counter, called the Plague Token until patch v0.2.9. Engine identifiers (`counters.plague`, `placePlagueTokens`, the `tokens` param, `PLAGUE_TOKEN_MANA`) keep their names, and bare "token" stays the engine's counter shorthand
