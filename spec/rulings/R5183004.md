Armor a hero keeps runs out since issue #5183, each hit it meets wearing 1 off it ([[R5183000]]), so the owner raised the numbers of four cards that give it, numbers alone, their scripts unchanged: Stinkyscape #132 Defensive Crystal's Death `heroArmor` from 1 to 3, its Radiant face from 2 to 6 ([[R1860006]]); Stinkyscape #145 Fortify Flesh's roll from 3 to 5 to 5 to 8 on both faces, the Radiant face's Lucky 1 kept ([[R1867008]]); Stinkyscape+ #79 A Glimpse into the Endless Sea's Radiant Armor from 5 to 8 ([[R3569005]]); and The Enigmata's Radiant Armor `count` from 5 to 8 ([[R3569004]]). The shipped catalogs keep the old numbers for the games played on them.
