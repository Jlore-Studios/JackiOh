Stinkyscape #12.1 Shadow Word Death destroys a Unit with {attack} or more Attack, and #12.2 Shadow Word Pain a Unit with {attack} or less, read through the [[§10.4]] layers as the target is chosen and as it resolves ([[R81]]): an aura's Attack counts. The pick is needed ([[R703]]), so with no Unit to choose the Spell cannot be played. An Indestructible Unit survives ([[R46]]). Each Radiant face destroys the target's neighbours too, whatever their Attack — a 1-Attack Unit beside a Death target, a 9-Attack one beside a Pain target — all marked before one state check ([[R59]]).
