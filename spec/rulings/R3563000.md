The owner's words of 2026-11-09 (issue #3563, Stinkyscape+ E9): every card an effect generates at
random aims to be unique — no catalog card comes twice until its whole pool has come once, and only
then may it repeat, drawing evenly from the whole pool again. This is the owner's call, not
Hearthstone's: Hearthstone's random generators may repeat.

- **One run.** The scope is one run, meaning one `EffectContext`: a Cry, Death, trigger, activation,
  turn hook or continuation, with its composed parts and a fused card's ingredients. Each pool draw
  lands evenly on the run's least-drawn cards.
- **Unique.** "Unique" means a distinct catalog id. A card's two faces count as one, and cards already
  in hands or decks don't count against it.
- **The paths.** Every effect that generates random cards draws through the run's `draw_tally`:
  `add_random_from_catalog`, `summon_random`, `fill_board_random`, `cast_random`,
  `shuffle_random_from_catalog`, `replace_hand_with_random`, `transform_random`, `swap_book`,
  `fuse_random_into`, `fuse_generated`, `gain_resources` (which keeps its rarity odds over the
  least-drawn), the Call to Chaos chains' picks, #83 Transmogulate's replacements and M+ #1 Bob's
  Plan's Wincon pick. Discover, the Marketplace's roll ([[R3047000]]: it stops short when its lots run
  dry), the Night Market's shelves and [[R1183001]]'s distinct fuse were already distinct and keep
  their draws.
- **After the tally.** Grape and Glitch come after the tally ([[R382]], [[R673]]): the tally counts the
  pool's pick. Resources keep their rarity odds over the least-drawn ([[R1840002]]). The pool hold
  narrows the pool first ([[R2201001]]).
- **Prompts.** A prompt that pauses a run starts its continuation's tally afresh.
- **The flag.** The rule ships behind `UNIQUE_RANDOM_DRAWS`, off until the Stinkyscape+ release turns
  it on; till then [[R60]] stands. Once it is on, old replays whose draws repeated are refused as
  `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: a tally per `Effect` — Core #95's "Summon 3 random 3-cost Units" is three separate
`summon_random`, so it would not be unique; drawing with replacement once the pool is used up;
carrying the tally in `RunMarks`; counting the rolled Grape, which would bend [[R382]]'s odds.
