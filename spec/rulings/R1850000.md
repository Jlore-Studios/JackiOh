"Copy the text of a Unit on the field" (`copy_unit_text { target, radiant }`) gives the running card the text the target Unit runs now: its printed keywords, its aura, its triggers, its start- and end-of-turn hooks, its Death, its Activate abilities and the declared numbers all of these read, on the face the target wears, or on its Radiant face with `radiant: true` (Tnack Trav's Radiant "copy the Radiant text"). A copier's copy is what it copies, and a fused card's fused text ([[R77]]). Everything else is the copier's own: its name, cost, type, tags, rarity, printed stats and Radiant flag, so a copier made Radiant later keeps the text it copied. The copy is a record in the copier's memory (`subsystems::unit_text`), read by every reader of a card's text — its script, static flags, printed keywords, the continuations its hooks park and the triggers it queues — so leaving the field ends it ([[R78]]) and a Fuse onto it does too. It is reported by an in-place `transformed`, both its ids and both its definitions the copier's own, as a Vanilla is.

Nothing is copied when the copier is off the field or Immutable, whose text can't be changed ([[R23]]); nor from a card that is no Unit on the field, the copier itself, or a Vanilla Unit, which has no text. An Immutable Unit may be copied: copying changes nothing of it. The copy lands as its Cry runs, so the copied text's Cry does not run then; a later "trigger a Cry" runs the text the card has. Tnack Trav's "Legendary or Mythic" is its declaration's filter, not the verb's.

Rejected: copying the stats too, which is a Transform ([[§6.3]]); a new event for the copy, where the in-place `transformed` already says a card's text changed.
