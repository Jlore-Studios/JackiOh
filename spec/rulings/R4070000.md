Amends [[R1006004]], whose row held `evaluate`, the hand eval with no price, and [[R1006006]], whose reply was simulated with no price. Since [[R2424000]], [[R2477000]], [[R2564000]], [[R2688000]] and [[R3064000]] the search scores its finalists with the hand eval priced by the decision's root (`evaluate_priced` with its `Prices`, [[§9.9]]), after a reply simulated with those prices, and the learned evaluation corrects that score ([[R1012001]]). A row that held the unpriced score taught the network a card's Death, turn-hook, Activate, attack-hook or awakening worth as a residual on its features, which the search then added a second time on top of the price. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the rows change no game.

- **Turn and decision rows** are priced on their own state: the prices a decision of the seat's there would pass its search (`decide::decision_prices_at`), read from the state redacted for the seat ([[R185]]) and probed on the decision's own probe world, on the counter of every probe's cap together (`AI_DECISION_PRICE_NODES`). The row's `hand` is `evaluate_priced(state, seat, next, &AI_EVAL, &prices)`, over the true state as before.
- **Reply rows** are priced by the decision that ended the turn: the state just before the seat's End Turn, whose End Turn line the search scores after the reply. The reply is simulated with those prices, and the row is scored with them, so its `hand` is the hand eval `finalist_score_with` corrects at that leaf ([[R1012001]]).
- **What stays the same.** A row whose root prices nothing keeps the score it had, and the two root flags ([[R3038003]], [[R3479000]]) change no hand eval, only which score a finalist takes. A row is priced even where the decision there was forced or answered at once, since the tools do not repeat `decide`'s early exits. The features, `FEATURES_VERSION` and the header are unchanged: one export is folded by one build, so one corpus never mixes priced and unpriced rows.

Amended by issue #4159 ([[R4159000]]): no network corrects a row's `hand` or a finalist's score any more, and `Prices` carries no root flags; the rows are priced as before.
