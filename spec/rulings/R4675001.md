A play version's figures ([[R4675000]]) are its card's breakdowns ([[R377]]) summed over the shipped versions it spans, per pilot ([[R807000]]) and match type, the engine's `play_version_figures`. Its sources are read as a window's are ([[R1367000]]): once the live games played on its span, every live game and not only those holding the card, reach R654's gate, `PUBLIC_STATS_MIN_LIVE_GAMES`, its figures count live games alone, and below it live and development games together. A play version never borrows another's games, as a short window borrows its previous one's, so its figures are its own versions'. Rejected: the client's `STATS_PATCH_MIN_GAMES`, the patch tabs' threshold, which counts every game.

Its games, adjusted win rate and range, tier and whether it has `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games are read as `GET /api/stats/cards` reads a card's ([[R2777000]], [[R2895000]], [[R3022000]], [[R654]]), under All Random over the decks that saw it ([[R2777002]]), and the Dormant Starship's over the seats that made one ([[R3070000]]). The field it is read against is every play version's figures, so a version of v0.4 and one of today read on one scale. No figure names its source ([[R661]]).

The records began about v0.3 (migration 0014, [[R376]]), so a play version wholly before them has no totals: its games are 0 and every figure empty. The frozen win rates of record ([[R3882000]]) fill none of them: the tables in `patches/win_rates/` begin at v0.4.0 and those in `patches/records/` at v0.6.4c, so none covers a version the totals lack; each holds a card's in-deck wins and games alone, with no drawn delta, so it cannot be read against the field; it is a window's figures, which would count again versions the totals already hold; and every one of them is provisional.

`GET /api/stats/versions?pilot=&mode=` is public, sends `pilot`, `mode`, the field, the band, the floor and every deck card's play versions in catalog order, each with `id`, `card`, `name`, `first`, `last`, `live`, `games`, `adjustedWinRate`, `adjustedLow`, `adjustedHigh`, `tier` and `hasEnoughGames`, figures only, and is cached `CARD_STATS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS` as `GET /api/stats/cards` is. Each table is kept once per `?pilot=` and `?mode=` and cut from the one snapshot of every patch's totals the cards' windows read ([[R3067000]]), so the whole history costs one load. A client reads a play version's definition from its catalog at `last` (`GET /api/catalog/:version`). `jackioh-server stats-cards --versions` and `cargo jackioh stats report --versions` print the same table, with `--mode`, `--pilot` and `--card`, and `--json` writes it. Rejected: an export from the WebAssembly module, which costs the module's size and has no reader yet. Hearthstone has no counterpart.
