"Your table", the landing's floor below the first screen ([[R639]]), shows the device's statistics one at a time instead of four lists. Each stat has a title, the card it is about drawn as a real card face, the player's number for it, and an animation the board already has for what the stat counts. Presentation only: it reads the device's tally and the Pocket Pick's public rates, and shows no card the player was not shown ([[R202]], CLAUDE.md rule 7).

- **The stats.** In this order, each with its card, its line and the board animation its card plays: Most Toppled (`defeated`, "taken down 37×", the card dies, on the opponent's side), Most Coveted (`allured`, "stolen 6×", Allure's mark is laid on it and it is taken, [[R963]], [[R437]]), Pocket Pick (the pick of [[R1583000]], "you play it 4× as often as everyone else", a Trap is revealed; "nobody else plays it" at a base rate of 0, the ratio rounded to a tenth), Old Faithful (`played`, "played 9×", the card enters), Arch-Nemesis (`playedAgainst`, "played against you 8×", it lunges at the player, on the opponent's side), Fallen Hero (`destroyed`, "lost 6×", it dies), The Record (`seen`, the record line of [[R639]], the card turns Radiant), Last Laugh (`lethal`, "dealt the winning blow 3×", it lunges at the opponent), Springloaded (`sprung`, "sprung 4×", a Trap is revealed) and Survivor (`survived`, "survived to the end 7×", its Divine Shield breaks). The card of a counter is the one with the most of it that the catalog still holds, a tie going to the lower id; a card the catalog no longer holds is passed over for the next. The Record's card is the one seen most. The Pocket Pick is shown once its request has answered with a card the catalog holds.
- **No animation of its own.** Each animation is the board's, run on a card outside a game: the stage (`stats/showcaseStage.ts`) gives the board's planner (`planEntries`) a view with the card alone on one lane and the one or two events the board would send for it (`destroyed`; `marked` then `controlChanged`; `trapFired`; `summoned`; `attackDeclared`; `radiantSet`; `divineShieldLost`). The entry that comes back is played as the board plays it: its duration (the table's, not scaled), the `data-animating` type the board's keyframes key on, and the cues the effects layer's `planFx` draws, on a canvas laid over the page. The stage's card and lane carry the board's testids, so the entry's frames and the cues' anchors land on them. `planStage`, which acts on the board's own elements, is left out, and no effect, keyframe or cue is new. A card that dies comes back by the way it came in; a Trap stays face-up once revealed ([[R200]]).
- **Timing.** A stat shows for `SHOWCASE_INTERVAL_MS` (6000 ms). Its card comes in over `SHOWCASE_LEAD_MS` (1200 ms), the way [[R704]]'s swap brings a card in, plays each beat in turn with `SHOWCASE_BEAT_GAP_MS` (900 ms) after it, and a dead card returns over `SHOWCASE_LEAD_MS` again; all of that fits inside one stat's interval. A step by hand, or a pick, gives the stat it lands on its whole interval.
- **Controls.** Previous and next arrows step through the stats with wrap-around, and a dot for each picks one, the shown one pressed. They stay while the stat holds still.
- **The holds.** As the homescreen's hand ([[R639]]), the showcase does not move on while a pointer or focus is on it, nor while the page is hidden; a card already moving finishes its animation. Under reduced motion (the system's, the panel's or the effects setting's) it shows the still card and its number, plays nothing, draws no effects and does not move on by itself; the controls still step it. The cycle keeps running while the floor is off screen.
- **Effects off.** With the effects intensity off the keyframes still play and the effects layer is not drawn. The effects speed setting is not applied.
- **Skipping.** A stat the device holds no data for is left out. With no games the floor draws nothing, as before, and with games but no card to show it shows the record line alone. Clear, with its note, stays and forgets the tally.
- **One screen.** When it has stats, the floor (the stats and the footer) is exactly one screen high, `100dvh`, on a phone and on a desktop, and nothing in it scrolls: the showcase takes what the footer leaves, the card sized by the screen's height. Without stats the floor is the footer alone. The showcase and the stage load when there is a stat to show, so the first screen does not carry the effects.

Hearthstone semantics: a recap shows one highlight at a time. Choices a person may want to review: Old Faithful is the most played card, Arch-Nemesis the most played against the player and Fallen Hero the most destroyed; the animations beyond Most Toppled's, Most Coveted's and Pocket Pick's are the author's choice. It amends [[R1582000]]. Rejected: effects rebuilt for the landing, which would drift from the board's; a dead card left gone, which leaves an empty stage; an eager import of the showcase, which puts the effects layer on the first screen.
