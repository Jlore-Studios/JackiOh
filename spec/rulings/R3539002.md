The board exile reaches Dormant and buried cards, and spares an immune card. It launches Terminus
hulls only, free, for the rest of the game.

**The exile.** `exile_board` exiles every card on the named side of the board — the unit piles
whole, top first, then the backrow, the backrow piles and the carried Units. Dormant cards go too:
the exile is a board sweep, not an effect reaching for a card, so [[R1839000]]'s reach does not stop
it (unlike S #4.1 Farewell, whose piles leave a Dormant card standing, [[R1870001]]), and a card
beneath a pile goes with it. Only a card no effect reaches is spared, as a harmful walk spares it:
one that is not Dormant and is immune to the running Spell stays ([[R940]]). The ids are snapshotted
before anything moves, so a pile uncovered mid-sweep is reached; a card that left meanwhile is
passed by. A unit token ceases to exist instead of reaching exile ([[R11]]), and the Spell itself
resolves to its owner's graveyard as any Spell does.

**The launch.** `launch_random_starship` launches one Dormant Starship of the caster's whose hull is
in the named hulls — the ten Terminus Starships, and no other Dormant Starship — picked on the
match rng; a buried one stays buried ([[R1839003]]), and nothing is drawn when there is none to
launch ([[R129]]). The launch is an awakening by an effect, so it is free ([[R1852006]]) and fires
the launched ship's fused Cries and When launched triggers as any launch does ([[R1852003]]).

**For the rest of the game.** The Last Voyage leaves the launch as a rest-of-game step on its
caster (`for_rest_of_game`, step "launch"): another random Terminus Starship launches at the start
of each of their turns, and none at anyone else's. An awakened Terminus Starship never enters
Dormant again ([[R3039000]]), so the ten run out one by one. Its words are the badge.
