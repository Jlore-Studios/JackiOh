A Unit whose text says "Immune to Spell Damage" (`staticFlags.immuneToSpellDamage`) takes nothing from a Spell's hit while it is on the field. At [[§4.4]] step 0, after the Spell Damage that raised the hit ([[R1180000]]), the hit is no damage instance: nothing is dealt, its Divine Shield stays, no `damage`, `divineShieldLost` or `damageAbsorbed` is emitted, and a Spell's Trample excess (step 9) does not go on, there being no hit to exceed ([[R63]]). "Spell" is the Spell type, as for Spell Damage: a Field Spell's, a Trap's and a Unit's hits land as usual. It is only damage: a Spell may still target the Unit, and its other effects, a buff, a destroy or a bounce, still reach it; keeping Spells off it altogether is Immune to Spells' ([[R81]]). Rejected: blocking a Spell's targeting too, which would make it Immune to Spells under another name; and negating the hit after Divine Shield, which would spend a shield on a hit that deals nothing.

Since issue #2521 Paragon-X prints Immune to Spells instead, so no card prints this flag now; the rule stands for any card that takes it.
