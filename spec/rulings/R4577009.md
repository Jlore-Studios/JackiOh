Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The web's art tests hold every catalog entry, a held set's included, to its rarity's detail floor ([[R3331001]]) and every Legendary to a motion of its own ([[R3340000]]), so the Infinite Assault's entries draw art from the day they land ([[R4577000]]), as the Stinkyscape+ set's did while it was held ([[R3572001]]).

**The scenes.** While the set is held, every entry, its two Drone tokens included, draws a placeholder scene recipe of its own (`apps/web/src/cards/art/infiniteAssaultRecipes.ts`), merged into the scene recipes and drawn by the procedural painter as every recipe card is: its type's template in all seven layers (a Unit's drone bay, a Spell's or Trap's drone swarm through a warp gate, a Field Spell's production line), built from the space, craft and gear kits and varied by the hash of its id, so no two are alike; no part turns and none is gold. Every picture draws above its floor and stays under `ART_DETAIL_MAX_BYTES`, so none joins `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`. Only `infiniteassault-` ids are keys, so no shipped card's picture moves ([[R503]]). A later picture pass replaces them.

**The motion.** IA #23 The Generator and IA #24 Mass Deployment move by motion recipes of their own, appended after every earlier entry so no other card's motion moves.

**The set mark** ([[R2718000]]): a red infinity loop on a dark hexagonal chip, which is also its watermark.

**No default look while held.** A default look (Extended art on a Legendary, Full art on a Mythic, [[R3343000]]) is the server's to export, and it reads only the sets that ship ([[R3535000]]): a held set's Legendary draws its normal face until its set ships.

Rejected: listing the 26 entries in `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`, a ratchet that may only shrink; a painter module of the set's own routed by id prefix, as Stinkyscape+'s, which needs four dispatch sites and scene recipes for its Legendaries besides.
