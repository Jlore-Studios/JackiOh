Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #5264 gives the held Felinors vs Humans set ([[R5235000]]) its effects and the client pieces its cards need, while [[R1420]]'s gate still holds it, so nothing here reaches a player before the set's release lists it. Every piece reads the view alone.

**The effects table** ([[R2784000]]). `fx/cards/felinorsvshumans.json5` holds every card of the set, tokens included, one line each in catalog order, between Stinkyscape+'s file and the Infinite Assault's, as the catalog orders the sets. It draws shared motifs and looks only ([[R2787000]]): no recipe, set piece, entrance, takeover or signature of its own, so every Legendary and Mythic, tokens by the rarity they print, waits on the signature ratchet's list for the set's signature pass ([[R3332000]]). A Felinor's palette is Beast's felinor sub-look. Each of the 82 Plagiarized reprints takes its Infinite Assault original's entry, but the five whose originals are signatures (Ego Death, Bastion.exe, Eternal Return, Loopback, Model Collapse), whose recipes and entrances are their originals' alone, take shared motifs.

**The keywords** ([[R438]], [[R2577000]], [[R5236031]]). Durability, Fragile and Unlucky act on the field, so each draws there. Durability is a glyph, a hammer crossed with a spanner, with the count the view gives (the uses left, 0 included) on Lucky's corner badge; Fragile is a veil, a pane of glass crazed in a star of cracks over the portrait, with a glint crossing it; Unlucky is a glyph, Lucky's clover gone wrong, three wilting leaves under a dark cloud, its number on the same badge. They take the three priorities after every kind before them, and each its own loop and callout icon. Endless Evolve is no keyword of the view, so it has no visual.

**The other side's zones** ([[R5238002]]). A play the engine lists into a zone of the other side (FvH #42 Big Billy) lights that zone where it stands, on the other side, and a click or a drop there sends that play. A zone's key carries its side's player, so a lane of the other side is never read as the same lane of one's own; a zone of one's own side keeps the key it had. A zone list names such a zone as the enemy's, as a declared zone pick is named.

**The End of turn voice** ([[R655]], [[R2783000]]). `card-audio.json5` gains the `endOfTurn` hook, a Unit's or a Field Spell's (FvH #59 Voracious Reader's): its effect and its line play when the card's End of turn hook's `hookResolving` marker arrives, at the play's priority, the line after its effect as any hook's is. Every other hook's marker stays silent, and so does a card no seat may read ([[R203]]): the sentinel has no entry.

**The cat ears** ([[R923]]). A card whose view lists the Felinor tag its definition does not print (a grant: FvH #64's discovered Unit, #68's Evolve, #79's Human) wears cat ears on its top edge, on the board and in a hand, the scene kit's own "cat-ears" part seeded by the card's instance, still, aria-hidden and taking no pointer. A Felinor its definition prints is no grant, and a card the client has no definition for wears none.

**The hieroglyph** (FvH #84). Its name is one Egyptian hieroglyph, 𓃠, which neither web face holds. The display and text stacks name, after their metric-matched fallbacks, a face for that block alone (its `unicode-range`), the systems' own Noto Sans Egyptian Hieroglyphs and Segoe UI Historic, so no other text moves; no file is shipped for it.
