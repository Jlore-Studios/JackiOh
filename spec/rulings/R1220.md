## R1220 — Untributable: no Tribute cost can take the card, and every Sacrifice of it does nothing

Built (ME-UNTRIBUTABLE, Meditative #80 Aluneth). Untributable is a §6.1 keyword: no Tribute cost —
a play's ([[R101]]) or an activation's — may take a card that carries it, and every Sacrifice of it,
by its own text or another card's, does nothing. A declared Tribute never offers one, a play that
names one is refused, and an ability whose cost is "Tribute this" is unusable on one. A card whose
`tributeWhen` holds is still sacrificed by the state check — unless it is Untributable, whose
condition never fires (without the filter, the check would loop to its pass cap). It is still
exiled, bounced and stolen as usual. It is not in [[R21]]'s random keyword pool, and a Nerf never
removes it (`TUNE_HARMFUL_KEYWORDS`), since losing it would help the card.
