The AI's evaluation priced a unit with "when this attacks" or "after this attacks" text ([[§6.2]]: the `when_attacks` hook before any damage of each attack it makes, [[R2285000]], [[R2396000]], and the `after_attack` hook once the state check closing that combat has run, [[R1849001]], [[R2285001]]) by its body alone. Stinkyscape #91 The Propagation's Wasp, a (1) 3/3 that summons a copy of itself after each attack, read as a plain 3/3, so the AI's board evaluation ranked a kill of it as one of any 3/3 and an own Stinkyscape #75 Badger, which gains a Resource each attack, as a plain 2/3. Only the opponent's reply, in the one turn it plays out, saw an enemy hook fire; the board's evaluation, which the beam and the reply's trader (`kill_worth`) rank by, never did. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, once, on the same probe world as [[R2424000]]'s Death prices, [[R2477000]]'s turn-hook prices and [[R2564000]]'s activation prices (a function of what the seat sees, [[R185]]), the AI walks [[R2564000]]'s two turns: it ends its own turn and then, untouched, the enemy's, on a copy with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), each turn end one `simulate` step that answers the other seat's prompts on the way. At the start of the enemy's turn it plays each enemy unit's attack, and at the start of its own next turn each of its own units', for each unit acting on the field (never a Dormant card) whose face has an attack hook and is none of the cards the enemy holds unseen: the attack on the enemy hero when `legal_actions` lists it, else the first attack it lists for the unit (a Taunt's, when one guards the hero). Beside it, from the same state, it plays the same attack by the unit's hookless twin: the unit made Vanilla ([[§6.3]]), so none of its text runs, and granted the keywords it prints, as tuning leaves them, so it fights as itself (Rush still lets it swing, Pierce still pierces). Before the twin's state is scored its text is given back (Vanilla off, the printed keywords it was granted taken off again), so its Brittle, its lent keywords, its auras and its set stats read the same on both sides. The price is the controller's evaluation after the unit's attack less after the twin's, plus `AI_EVAL.next_turn_mana` for each crystal the one gained over the other. It is kept signed, 0 included: a hook that harms its controller is a debt, as [[R2565000]]'s turn hooks are. A card is keyed by its definition, its face and its controller, since each copy attacks a different board; one the same as a card already tried is not tried again. `unit_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.attack_hook` of the price, inside the Brittle share, since a card that crumbles takes its coming attacks with it, and only while the unit may attack at all (`can_swing`: no "Can't attack", no status barring it, not Frozen through its controller's next turn), for the seat's units and the enemy's alike, so `kill_worth` reads it through `unit_worth`, and through it the reply's trader and `even_trade` do too: destroying an enemy Wasp is worth the copy it would make. `AI_EVAL.attack_hook` starts at 0.5, untuned, as [[R2564000]]'s weight did (at 0.5 the smoke gate read 20, 20 and 20 of 20, against random, against greedy and as Hard against Easy; at 0, 20, 20 and 20); the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, and at 0 the evaluation is what it was. The probes run with no price passed, a Death, a turn-hook and an activation price included, and within `AI_ATTACK_HOOK_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes ([[R186]]'s budget), a cap of their own. The prices travel with that one decision in `Prices`, beside [[R2424000]]'s, [[R2477000]]'s and [[R2564000]]'s, so a call outside a decision is passed none and prices nothing.

**What is not priced.**
- A hand card, a Dormant card and a face-down card.
- A unit with no attack the rules allow at the start of its controller's turn.
- An attack pair where either attack is refused, or leaves a prompt open or the game over.
- Every unit of a turn the turn end does not reach (its player's own prompt, an extra turn, the game's end), and the seat's own units when the enemy's turn is not reached.
- A unit the probes' nodes did not reach.
- Anything while a prompt is open, outside the seat's own main phase, or once the game is over.
- Nothing at all, and no node spent, when no unit on either field has an attack hook.

**Approximation.** The twin switches off all of the unit's text for that combat, not its attack hooks alone, so any other text of the unit that acts in the same combat (a Death if it falls, an "after this is attacked" of its own) is counted in the price too. No shipped Unit with an attack hook prints such text, though a host that took one by Equip (Stinkyscape #38 Shield Amplifier, [[R77]]) may carry its own. A hook that pays only on a Unit or a kill (Core #32 Prem Panther, Stinkyscape #94 Tmack Trav, Classic+ #73.1 Classic Golem) prices about 0 against the hero, which is the attack the probe plays where it may.

**Rejected.** A flat premium per hook, which cannot tell a copy of a 3/3 from a Resource; firing the hook alone, with no combat, which Tmack Trav's and the Classic Golem's hooks cannot run without, since they read the combat's facts; probing the seat's own units in the current turn, which prices nothing once a unit has attacked; spawning and probing the seat's hand Units, as [[R2424000]] does, which a unit's next attack, a turn away, would price at the wrong time; drawing the probes from another probe's node cap, which would take the nodes [[R2564000]]'s Activates or [[R2424000]]'s Deaths drew before; and any change to the engine or a card's script, which the evaluation does not need.
