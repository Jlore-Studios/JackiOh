Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The issue (#3344, part of #3330) asked for the depth every card can have: until
it, only Immersive ([[R2944002]]) drew its picture in two planes, though the painters already tag each layer by its
role ([[R2715001]]) and can draw the backdrop and the subject apart ([[R2943001]]).

**The planes.** A face wanting depth (`hasDepth`, `cards/depth.ts`) is one that is Epic, Legendary or Mythic (the rarity
its frame shows) or prints full art at any level (every full-art-shaped level of every variant, the Full-art cards
setting's twenty-one cards among them) and whose picture the painter draws in parts. It draws that picture as four
planes, each the painter's layers of its roles (`PLANE_OF_ROLE`, `art/pictures.ts`): **far** the background (its sky,
rays, back layers and the vignette), **middle** the props (the motif's glyphs and the accent), **near** the subject
(its emblem and subject layers), **nearest** what lies over it, its light and its motes, and the particles a moving
picture draws. The far plane is the window's own picture (`.cf-art-frame > .cf-art`, `data-art-part="far"`); the other
three are `.cf-plane`s over it (`.cf-planes`, `data-plane`), each a transparent picture of the same painter and seed in
the window's shape, so stacked they are the whole picture. A painter that has no props (Stinkyscape's and Training
Grounds') leaves its middle plane empty. The whole, backdrop and subject parts keep their pictures byte for byte
(`partDraws`, `partHolds`).

**One plane.** A face stays one picture where it cannot be drawn in planes or where planes would be wrong: a Common, and
a Rare, outside a full-art look; a delivered art file, which has no layers (`hasArtPlanes`, `art/CardArt.tsx`); a
Starship in play and a Legacy card, whose painters draw one picture ([[R2943001]]); any restyle of the picture (the
Sketch, Pixel, Comic, a Set showcase, Immersive's haze, Prismatic's chrome, Woodcut, Neon sign and the four repaints'
treatments), which is a filter over one picture and would not be one over a stack, so the Sketch's full art, drawn on
opaque paper, is one plane too; Immersive, Out of frame and Extended art, which have layers and a geometry of their own;
and Glitch's blank card. A compact face (a face-up backrow card) has depth by the same rule.

**The glare.** While a face with depth tilts ([[R3344001]]) a white glare lies over it, opposite the tilt, in two layers: one
over the art window, blended with `color-dodge`, and one over the frame, under the words, blended with `overlay`, each
in an isolated group so it blends with its own area alone. A face at rest draws neither layer (`display: none`), since a blend
mode alone, even at opacity 0, prints the face's text with another antialiasing. Each foil finish catches it by area (`GLARE_BY_FOIL`, one
row of art and frame shares for every foil the cosmetics registry has and for none), and the glare is white and nothing
else, so no colour of it falls in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]). A Radiant face takes no glare on its frame, which
keeps its gold edge and its own shimmer (`.cf[data-foil]::after`, cards.css) untouched; the planes carry no foil
pseudo-element of their own, the far plane the window's, so no foil is drawn twice.

**The frame-break.** A Legendary or Mythic face in the viewer's own hand (`ownHand`) or in the hover preview or the
inspect sheet lets its near plane's subject stand taller from its foot (`CARD_FRAME_BREAK_SCALE`) and rise over the
window's top edge by `CARD_FRAME_BREAK_PX`, as Out of frame's does; the face's own clip (`.cf-scale`) still stops it at the
face's edge, and no other face (an Epic, a grid's, the board's) breaks. The near plane keeps its window's shape: its
clip is that shape (the oval, the window, the arch, the Trap's chamfered box or the Field Trap's hexagon) with its top
raised by the break, so the subject crosses the top edge only where the shape does and never draws over a corner the shape
cuts away.

**Flat.** The Reduce motion setting, the system's reduced motion and the fx intensity off draw the flat picture, no planes,
and with the fx intensity off a face that would have depth holds its foil still: the picture and the foil stand as the
face without depth wears them, and the CSS carries its own motion gate besides (`depth.css`).

Rejected: planes for a delivered file by cutting one into layers (a file has none, and a cut is a new picture);
planes under a restyle by restyling each plane (a filter such as the Sketch's hatching or Pixel's cells would be
seeded and cut apart, and the planes would not agree where they meet); planes for the Sketch's full art by letting
each plane paint its own paper (the near plane's paper would hide the far one); true depth by `translateZ` under
`preserve-3d` (the face is an isolated group, and `.cf-scale` clips it, both of which draw their contents flat, so the
planes shift by `translate` and only the face turns in 3D); a glare tinted by the foil (a coloured glare could land
in the gold window); and a frame-break on every face (it would cross the frame's words on a small face).

Amended by [[R3343001]] (issue #3343): a Legendary's Default draws Extended art, which keeps to one picture here, so a face drawing it so, in hand, a list, the hover preview or the inspect sheet, has no depth and breaks no frame; a Legendary wearing a level of another variant that has planes, Alt art say, has both; and a Mythic's Default, Full art, has depth as every full-art face has.

Amended by [[R3340000]] (issue #3340): the fx intensity off holds every face still in the one face-level motion decision, so a face with no depth holds its foil still under it too, not only a face that would have depth.
