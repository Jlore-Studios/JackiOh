[[§8]] #77: "Cry: (4)+ Cost cards cost (1) less on your next turn." A card qualifies when its cost is 4 or more at the point [[R65]] applies Curvature, after every other modifier, so a 6 costs 5 (4 on the Radiant face) and a card another discount has taken below 4 is not reached. [[R48]]'s timing is unchanged, and an X-cost card still ignores it ([[R65]])
