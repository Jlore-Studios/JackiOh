Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A Starship in play ([[R2249000]]) is drawn from what its view shows it holds: its pieces, oldest first (`StarshipFace.list`, public on both seats, [[R1852005]]), whether it has launched and the name it launched under ([[R1852004]]). Never from its definition's id or name, which a fusion changes ([[R3045000]]).

**Its modules.** Each piece's module ([[R3333001]]) mounts on the hardpoint its kind takes: the bow's deck for weapons, the spine behind it for defenses, Chassis and Salvage, the flanks for launchers and pods, port and starboard in turn, the keel for miners, the stern for engines. Each hardpoint spreads its own evenly, the oldest foremost (or highest, at the stern), and a deck of more than three raises every other one on a barbette. A piece on its Radiant face mounts its module gilded, and a Radiant Starship gilds every module. The picture mounts at most 16 modules (`STARSHIP_MODULES_MAX`), its oldest pieces'. A running light along the keel for each module, oldest nearest the bow and in that module's colors, shows the order its pieces went in wherever they mount, so two different lists of up to 16 pieces never draw the same picture.

**Its class.** The hull grows with its count of pieces: a frigate at 0 to 2, a destroyer (a bridge) at 3 and 4, a cruiser (a superstructure and a fin) at 5 to 7, a dreadnought (a dorsal stack and nacelles) at 8 or more (`STARSHIP_CLASSES`). Each class is drawn longer in its window than the last.

**Its design.** Launched, it wears the hull design of the name it took, each of the seven its own prow, window line, livery and emblem: The Ninth Life a whiskered prow, portholes and a cat in lilac; Frontier's Promise a wedge, a ribbon of windows and a flag in white, navy and red; The Celestial Archive a dish spire, a grid of windows and a book in deep blue and silver; The Lantern Ferry a junk's bow with its lantern, lanterns and a flame in crimson and jade; The Jlockeed Skylark swept canards, slits and a jet in sky blue; Ember of Departure a glowing ram, ember cracks and a star in charcoal and ember orange; The Silent Choir organ pipes, sparse windows and a note in slate. A name no design has (none today) wears one of the seven by its hash. No livery is in Radiant's gold ([[R2725004]]); the Radiant face gilds it. Its engines burn, warp streaks trail it and an escort flies with it for every 3 pieces, up to 3.

**The shipyard.** Dormant, still unnamed, it sits in a shipyard on a bare, unlit primer hull: X-braced gantries fore and aft under a crane, scaffolds along it, welding sparks where its newest piece went in, and a dock lamp whose light runs from 0.25 to 0.9 as its count runs from 0 to 8.

**The painter's inputs.** A design and a livery are inputs to the painter (`StarshipArt`: its pieces, its launch, its design, its livery, its face); a livery of `null` is its design's own. The cosmetic track of issue #3330 may hand it another design or livery; nothing hands one today. The picture's memo key holds every input, its pieces by id and face in order, so two Starships on one board never share a cached picture, and the biggest picture, 16 Radiant pieces, stays under `STARSHIP_ART_MAX_BYTES` (48 KiB).

**Its looks.** Pixel, Comic, the Sketch and a Set showcase restyle its picture ([[R2942001]]); Out of frame and Immersive still leave a Starship out, its picture drawn whole ([[R2943001]]). The collection's Dormant Starship, out of play, keeps the picture [[R503]] pins.

This amends [[R2249000]]: a Starship's art is no longer one capital ship grown from one seed by how many pieces it holds, but the ship those pieces build, in its name's design. Its hull plates' icons are [[R3333002]]'s.
