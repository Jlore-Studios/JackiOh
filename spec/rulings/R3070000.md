**Why.** The owner balances every card by its tier ([[R3022000]]): S is nerfed and F is buffed. So every card must have an adjusted win rate and a tier, and two kinds of card never had one.

- **A Quickdraw card** starts in its owner's opening hand ([[§6.2]], [[R640]]), so no deck ever holds it undrawn. [[R2777000]]'s rate compares the games a card was seen in with the games its deck never drew it. With the second group empty there was no rate, which left 15 cards, Heroic Power, Great Paperclip Theory and Bob's Plan among them, without a tier.
- **The Dormant Starship** is made only by a rule and never dealt ([[R1857001]]). Its row in the Cards table had no deck games at all. Its games are counted as a Created card's ([[R2089000]]): the seats that made one.

**The baseline.** The field ([[R3022000]]) gains `baseline`, the average deck's win rate, read as the average card's in-deck win rate over every card the page's report holds, a draw a game not won. It is read off the totals each card already has, so there is no new tally and no migration.

**Read against the average deck.** A card is read against the baseline when it has fewer never-drawn games than `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE`, the floor [[R2427000]]'s drawn delta waits for:

```
adjusted = 50% + τ²/(τ² + SE²) · (seen win rate − baseline),   SE² = ¼ / seen games
```

- τ is the field's measured spread, as for every other card.
- Only the seen side is noise, as the baseline is read over every deck.
- Its 95% range is [[R2895000]]'s, the rate ± `STATS_INTERVAL_Z` · √(weight · SE²).
- Its tier is [[R3022000]]'s, from the sample floor as ever.

Every Quickdraw card falls here, as does any card a Constructed deck almost always draws. A card with the floor's never-drawn games keeps its drawn delta. Under [[R2777000]]'s prior field (too few cards for a field) the baseline is still read off the report; the prior constant itself reads an even game.

**The Dormant Starship's row** in the Cards table reads its Created games: every counted seat that made one, used or not (`created_played` and `created_not_played`). Those games give the row's games, win rate and Wilson range. Every Dormant Starship is used as it is summoned, so it has no unused side to compare with, and is read against the average deck as above. It stays in the Created table too ([[R2089000]]). The figure measures the seats that made a starship, so it carries their Starship Pieces as well as the starship.

**Where it shows.**
- `GET /api/stats/cards` answers `tiers.baseline`.
- The Cards tab's tiers note says that a card almost never left undrawn and the Dormant Starship are read against the average deck, and what that deck wins.

**Rejected.**
- **An even game (50%) as the baseline.** Live human seats against the AI, and draws, move the average deck's win rate off 50%.
- **Choosing the comparison by the Quickdraw tag.** A Constructed deck that draws a card nearly always has the same gap, and a tag would leave it out.
- **The Dormant Starship read through its pieces' drawn deltas alone.** The page would still show the starship's row empty.
- **A new card class in the catalog for the starship.** The engine already holds it apart, as made by a rule ([[R1857001]]); the page needed only its games.

In Constructed, a chosen deck's strength reaches a card read against the average deck, which [[R2777000]]'s drawn delta keeps out. No Hearthstone rule is involved.
