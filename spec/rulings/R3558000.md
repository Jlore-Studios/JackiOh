Index 0 is the leftmost card; an added card takes the right end unless its effect names the left end, and no player reorders the hand — Sweet Essence reads its neighbours from this order, and Doomed Ship adds to both ends of it. Rejected: letting a player reorder, which would move lends and meetings without an event.
