"Reveals when one of your Units is about to die by combat" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #138 Pocket Peptides) is a `wouldDie` replacement ([[§4.5]] step 1, before any card moves; [[§6.3]] Replacement). A Unit dies "in combat" when a hit killed it ([[R42]]) and the last hit it took in the action was combat damage, a Cleave's included ([[§4.3]]); the would-die event says so of each Unit it names (`DyingUnit.combat`). A Unit a Spell's or a text's hit killed, or one a destroy marked, did not. The trap answers when one of its controller's Units the check collected died in combat; it fires, is spent, and its follow-up resolves at once (`instead.resolveFirst`), not after the check as a `then` otherwise does: a free cast of M #36 CN Peptides ([[R70]]) aimed at the first such Unit in [[R68]]'s order ([[R946]]). The state check that follows the cast's effect, and the check that starts over after the follow-up, judge every Unit again on the board the casts left ([[R460]]): a Unit the Buffs lifted past its damage lives, one the cast destroyed dies. The Radiant face casts a Radiant CN Peptides (Lucky 1, [[R1440]]) at each of its controller's Units, the dying one first, so its own cast decides it, then the rest in lane order. Each cast is aimed only (`aimOnly`, [[R1180005]]'s rule, now also a new card's cast): one whose Unit an earlier cast took off the field casts nothing rather than asking for another Unit. Rejected: a trap on the combat's `damage`, which would fire after the deaths, too late to spare one; a follow-up owed until after the check, the same; and the Radiant casts in lane order alone, where a cast at another Unit first would let the check after it kill the dying Unit before its own cast.
