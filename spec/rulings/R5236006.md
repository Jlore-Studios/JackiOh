The designer asked for every shipped card that has a cap on its uses to print Durability. Only #79
Twinspell's base face is one exactly: "Activate: … Then this goes to the graveyard." is Durability 1
under [[R5236002]]'s plain move, the move [[R3044000]] gave it. Its base face now reads "Durability 1"
and then its Activate, and prints `{ "kind": "Durability", "n": 1 }`; the Radiant face, which stays to
serve every turn, prints none.

It plays as before. Its Activate is once a turn ([[R384]]) and its use spends its 1 Durability
([[R5236000]]); its own list then moves it to the graveyard exactly where it always did, after the grant,
since its script makes the move whenever its Durability is spent (or it prints none, as on every older
catalog a replay folds under, [[R767000]]). A fused card carrying the base text keeps one counter
([[R102]]), so it is sent away by its first use as before, a Unit included.

Two things differ, both the keyword's own rules: a countered Activate now spends its Durability
([[R3550005]]), so the card leaves at the next settled state check where before it stayed to be used next
turn; and a Buff's X row may raise its Durability to 2 ([[R5236005]]), when its list keeps it on the
field after its first use for a second. No other shipped card is reworded: The House's Plague Counters
interact with other cards and keep it on the field at 0, and no Activate that Tributes or exiles its own
card is a Durability.
