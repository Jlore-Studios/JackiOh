The owner asked in #3015 (2026-11-04) that tokens and the Chase Shard buy only the **next** level: owning a
level means owning every level below it ([[R3015003]]), and only a pull's far rarer draw grants a level
past the next.

**The rule.** `POST /api/tokens/redeem-cosmetic` ([[R1305003]]) and `POST /api/cosmetic-packs/chase`
([[R1306002]]) aimed at a level some level below which the account does not own on that card (a row of it,
or of a retired entry that owns it there, [[R3015006]]) answer 400 `bad_request`, `"that level needs every
level below it owned first"`, and write and spend nothing (`economy::tokens::next_level_only`). The check
reads the account's holdings under its lock, after the unknown id's, the retired entry's and the offer's
400s ([[R2725001]]) and before every 409 (no token held, a cosmetic above the token's tier, a bar not full,
a cosmetic below Legendary, no card held for it, one already owned), so a request past the next level is
refused whether or not a token is held or the bar full. A level whose levels below are all owned is bought as
any cosmetic is: at or below the token's tier, no Mythic by a token ([[R2728000]], [[R3015001]]), a
Legendary or Mythic one by a full bar. Anything that is no level is always next.

**Retired entries.** A retired full art ([[R2725000]]) or augment ([[R3015000]]) is a 400, `"that cosmetic
is retired"`, before anything is read, as a full art was.

**In the Collection** a held token is offered on a level only when it is the next ([[R3015007]],
[[R1953001]]), so the client never sends what the server refuses; the server decides alone (CLAUDE.md
rule 7).

Rejected: buying a level past the next together with every level below it, which would make a token or a
bar a second, certain way past the order the owner kept for the rare draw; and spending the token on the
next level in its place, which would buy what the player did not choose.

Amends [[R1305003]] (a token buys a level only as its card's next) and [[R1306002]] (so does a full Chase
Shard bar).
