Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A launched Starship ([[R1852003]]) attacked with the generic Unit's lunge and swing.

**The broadside.** When a launched Starship's `attackDeclared` plays, its weapons fire where its picture mounts them ([[R3333000]]), in list order, at most `STARSHIP_BROADSIDE.gunsMax`, one after another from `fireAt` (the lunge's wind-up) to `lastAt` of the row (`broadsideFireMs`), every shot landing on the target as the row ends, so its sparks run past it only inside `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` ([[R200]]). The ship holds still where it stands (a still lunge). Each module fires by its kind (`BROADSIDE_WEAPONS`):

- a beam sweeps `beamSweep` strokes across its target, `beamStep` apart (the Beams, and S #105's and #108's modules);
- a repeater stutters `repeaterShots` bolts with spark trails, `repeaterStep` apart (the Repeaters, and S #100's and #122's rounds);
- a cannon kicks back where it stands for `recoilMs` and fires a shell in smoke with a muzzle burst and a shake (the Cannons, S #107's rail and S #128's, Embiggened);
- flak fires a shell that bursts in `flakClouds` clouds across its target;
- a launcher lobs what it carries in an arc: the Launchers S #140 to #143 a token, fruit, jade or a book, and the Missile Pod (S #129, Embiggened) arrows.

A module that is no weapon fires nothing.

**The ram.** A launched Starship with no weapons rams: its lunge turns `FX_STARSHIP_RAM_TURN_DEG` toward its target, prow first (counterclockwise toward one above), arcing when it flies as a Flying Unit's lunge does ([[R2789000]]), with exhaust from its engines as it leaves, sparks at its prow, a shard ring and a shake at the contact. Every other attacker's lunge is unchanged (its turn is 0).

**The sound.** In the swing's place the broadside sounds once for each kind it fires, as the first gun of that kind fires (`starshipBeam`, `starshipRepeater`, `starshipCannon`, `starshipFlak`, `starshipLauncher`), and a ram `starshipRam` at the contact (`starshipSounds.ts`). The swing gives way only to sounds that were sent: an attack voiced with no entry started (Reduce Motion) swings as any Unit's does.

**Hidden information.** It reads only the attack and the attacker's public `starship` list ([[R202]]); a Starship R97 hides ([[R97]]) attacks with the generic rows. Scaled with the speed ([[R201]]).
