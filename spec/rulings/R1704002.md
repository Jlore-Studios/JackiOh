Since issue #1704 Classic+ #7 The House's Cry places 3 Plague Counters on itself, one placement ([[R689]]), and its Activate spends one of them to summon a Right-house defender (#3, [[R406]]) or a Wrong-House Attacker (C+ #6). "Either" is its controller's choice: a mode, `defender` or `attacker`, declared in the `activate` action as any Activate's modes are ([[R81]]), so `legalActions` lists both and the AI picks between them.

The Activate is once per turn ([[§6.2]], [[R384]]). With no counter on the card, or with no unit zone of its controller's free, it cannot be activated, so a counter is never spent for nothing. The twin is a fresh card, its controller's, with no Cry ([[R1]]), in the leftmost free unit zone ([[R64]]): on its base face from the base face, and on its Radiant face from the Radiant face. A card that uses Plague Counters carries the Plague tag ([[§5]]), so The House does.

Through v0.4.0f its Cry and each start of its controller's turn summoned a twin at random, both on the Radiant face ([[R406]]); `script_v0_4_0f` keeps that for those versions' replays ([[R767000]]).
