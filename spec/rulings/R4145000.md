[[R1365000]] keeps a card claimed only through its script when its forced games diverge on the base
build. Playing those `FORCED_GAMES` (40) games one card at a time made a refactor across many scripts
cost one run a card, about twenty seconds each, until the check outgrew CI's time limit. This amends
that check: stage 2 forces the script-claimed cards it must check, after today's skips, in groups of
`FORCED_GROUP_CARDS` (5, `crates/tools/src/classify.rs`), every card of the group in both decks of the
group's one recorded run and one parent `golden check --file`, each card still in `FORCED_GAMES`
games.

- **Cleared together.** When none of the group's divergences touches one of its cards or touches no
  card, every card in the group is cleared at once: the question [[R1365000]] asks of each card is
  answered for all of them.
- **Falling back.** When a group has such a divergence, or the recorder or the parent cannot play it,
  each of its cards falls back to its own forced games exactly as before, and a card that cannot be
  forced still counts as changed. A change with a single script-claimed card records and checks what
  it did: a one-card group issues its record plus parent check, with the solo games behind it on a
  divergence.
- **Unchanged.** [[R3935001]]'s steered subjects never join a group; each plays its own steered games
  at once. The `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` stop is checked between groups as between cards, and what it leaves
  counts as without `--parent-bin`, its script claims unconfirmed.

Rejected: groups of ten, which would force two-fifths of a 25-card deck into every game and change
what the games play; and halving a diverged group instead of checking each card alone, which such
uncommon groups do not repay in reading cost.
