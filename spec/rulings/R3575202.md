The decision's root prices a hand card's turn hooks ([[R2477000]]), Death ([[R2424000]], [[R2687000]]) and awakening ([[R3064000]]) by setting or placing a fresh copy first ([[R923001]]): a Field Spell in the first open backrow zone, a Unit in the first open unit zone. A GIGA card fills its anchor's lane and the next in both rows ([[R3546000]]), so the sandbox refused that spawn whenever the first open zone's other-row lane, or either row's next lane, was taken, and the card went unpriced; once played, its hooks then counted nothing, since the root had no price for them. On a board with a Unit in lane 1, S+ #53 Laser Drill's three finds counted 0 in place of 4.6 (issue #3575). No game rule changes.

**What it does.** A hand card that enters GIGA (`enters_giga`) is spawned at the anchor a summon with no named lane takes (`entry_anchor`: the leftmost lane whose four zones are open, else the leftmost a move may take), in its own row; with none, it is not priced, as no play of it is offered. Every other card is spawned where it was.

**Rejected.** Trying each lane until the sandbox accepts one, which spends a node a refusal; reading the play's lanes off `legal_actions`, which would price a card the AI cannot afford this turn nowhere.
