[[R1579000]] had the Collection's Cosmetics section list what the account owns under an **Owned**
heading below the **Equipped** tiles: every starter but the defaults once, each per-card cosmetic once
for each card it sits on, and each owned portrait on no card ([[R1827000]]). An account that had
opened a few packs got a list too long to read, and it said nothing the screen did not show already:
the playmat, backdrop and card back tiles show each one owned or locked ([[R1833000]]), and a card's
own foil, finish, border and full art are picked on the card in the Cards section, owned or locked
([[R1655000]]).

The Cosmetics section is now the Equipped heading, its text and the account's tiles, with no Owned
heading, no list and no read of its own. `GET /api/cosmetics` is unchanged and still read where
ownership decides: through the providers the gated screens mount for the tiles and the cards
([[R1833000]]), by the deck builder, whose portrait picker offers the portraits the account owns
([[R1827000]]), and by the Shop. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's collection shows its card backs and heroes as a grid of every
one, the unowned marked, and keeps no list of owned cosmetics beside it.

Rejected: a shorter list, capped or folded behind a toggle, which still repeats what the tiles and
the cards show; keeping a list of the portraits alone, since the deck builder's picker already
offers exactly the owned ones.
