The AI decides from what its seat may know ([[R185]]). A custom card's definition is part of what it knows only once the card has been seen: its redaction keeps a custom card's definition and script only when a card it may read names it, and a hidden card is resampled from the official cards as before. It plays against a custom card through the engine like any other, and never holds one.
