Felinors vs Humans #32 replaces each card in hand "with a different one". `replace_hand_with_random` (Classic+ #65.5) drew every arrival from one pool, the running card's own definition the only one left out ([[R387]]).

**The rule.** With `excludeReplaced`, the hand is replaced as before, and each arrival is drawn from the pool without the definition of the card it replaces: the first arrival replaces the first card of the hand as it was, the second the second, and so on. No card is replaced by a card of its own definition. A slot whose pool is empty without that definition gets nothing, and the others still arrive ([[R129]]: an empty pool draws nothing). The draws are the effect's, unique within it while `UNIQUE_RANDOM_DRAWS` holds ([[R3563000]], repeats otherwise, [[R60]]).
