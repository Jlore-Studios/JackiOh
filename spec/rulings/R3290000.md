`moderate ban` sets an account's `profiles.status` to banned ([[§9.4]]). While it is, the standings reads mark each of the account's season rows, on every ladder, `banned`: a mark the server's two stores set from the status at each read and no client read carries ([[R612]]).

A banned row is not listed and does not count. It has no row in its Grape tier, no Jlorious place and no part in the Raisin count, and it is left out of the percentiles every other player's target is read against ([[R605]]), out of Jlorious ([[R608]]) and out of the bots' grind plan. This holds on every server, strict or not, unlike [[R3074001]]'s hand-set rating, and unlike a ladder bot ([[R640000]]), which stays listed in its tier and is left out of the other two only. The next account takes the place a banned one held.

Nothing is deleted. The account's rows, ratings, peaks and an owned Jlorious card back ([[R2755000]]) stay, and `moderate unban` restores its place at the next read, since the leaderboard and Jlorious are worked out from the standings on every read.

What a game writes is worked out when a ranked game is rated: Jlorious peaks and the card back are granted from the people a game leaves Jlorious. After a mid-season ban the leaderboard and the percentiles change at once; the account that takes a banned one's place gets its peak and its card back at the next rated game on that ladder, as anyone another player's fall brings into Jlorious does. That is enough. A grant or backfill at ban time was rejected: the ban lever writes `profiles.status` alone, and an unban would leave a grant to take back.

A game of a banned account that was already running is rated as ever, and the banned side takes no Jlorious place and its record is off the board. The match screen's seat and the play telemetry still read a banned account's own row as a tier, never a Jlorious place, and the dev dashboard's whole-ladder read ([[R3074001]]) still lists it.
