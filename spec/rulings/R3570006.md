S+ #142.1 Mindustrial Golem and #142.2 Swarm Golem summon one random permanent of their tag
(a Unit, Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap, never a Spell or a token, held sets' cards included) after each of their
attacks, as the state check closing its combat has run ([[R1849001]]), placed per [[R64]] with no Cry ([[R1]]) and
drawn only when a zone is open ([[R129]]). The Mindustrial Golem's lands with Can't attack, the Swarm Golem's
evolved ([[R3570001]]); on the Radiant face each is Radiant. A Golem gone from the field after its attack
summons nothing ([[R2285001]]). `summon_random_with` carries the riders: `keywords`, `giga` and `entersEvolved`.

Rejected: Units only, which "a Mindustrial card" and "a Swarm card" do not say.
