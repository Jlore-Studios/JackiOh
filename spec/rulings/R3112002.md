`cargo jackioh positions`, the training lane's fold step ([[R1006004]]), counts the copies of cards its rows name by id, `cards`, and of them those whose id the compiled-in network has no row for, `unknownCards`, and prints `unknownShare`, the second over the first (0 with no card), in the one line of JSON it ends with. A card is known when any of the network's keys names its id, in any zone and on either side (`Learned::known_card_ids`). Each copy counts once in each row: a Radiant card in the seat's own hand by its `hand` key alone ([[R1012004]]), and no attribute key ([[R3112000]]) counts at all. The lane keeps the line as its run's `positions.json`, so the share of the games' cards the shipped network never saw is the number a person reads to decide when it wants a refit.
