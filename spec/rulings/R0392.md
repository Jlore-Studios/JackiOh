C #15's "Discard a random card: Exile the bottom card of each opponent's deck" is an Activate ability used once per turn ([[R384]]): "cost: effect" is how a card is clicked to do an effect, and the random discard is its cost, so with an empty hand it cannot be activated. "Each opponent" is the multiplayer phrasing Heroic Power uses and means the opponent. The Radiant face also exiles a random card from the opponent's hand
