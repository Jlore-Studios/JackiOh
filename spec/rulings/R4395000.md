Shadowstep's base face read only who controlled a card, and [[§4.5]] step 1 also reports a backrow card marked destroyed as `destroyed`, so a Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap of yours that was destroyed sprang it and came back at cost 0 although no Unit of yours had died. Its text is "When any of your Units die", and Hearthstone's "a minion dies" trigger never answers a destroyed non-minion.

- **Only Units.** The trigger's `when` and its pass collection keep a `destroyed` only when its card is a Unit, read by its definition's type on the face it died on, as M #60 Eschews counts a death. A Unit standing in the backrow is a Unit and still counts ([[R1081000]]), and a Unit card set face-down as a Trap is read by its definition's type too.
- **Alone.** A Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap of yours destroyed while none of your Units dies leaves Shadowstep set, and the card stays in your graveyard.
- **Together.** In one pass that destroys Units of yours and such a card, Shadowstep fires once for the Units ([[R172]]) and returns only them at cost 0. The other cards stay in the graveyard.
- **Animated.** A Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap that died standing in a unit zone was a Unit while it stood ([[R383]]), so its death answers Shadowstep, read from the `destroyed` event's `animated` ([[R4395001]]), as the Radiant face's replacement answers it. The definition decides only for a card that died in a backrow zone.
- **Unchanged.** The Radiant face (its "would die" replacement already collects only cards in unit zones), the enemy's cards, tokens, Reborn, Indestructible and the hand cap.
- **Replays.** The games of v0.6.9g and before fold under the script they ran (`script_v0_6_9g`, and `script_v0_4_12b` for v0.4.12b and before), which answered every card of yours that was destroyed ([[R767000]]).
