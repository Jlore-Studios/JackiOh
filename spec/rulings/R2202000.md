Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #2202 gives the Training Grounds set ([[R2194000]]) procedural art of its own in place of [[R503]]'s, as [[R2170000]] gave Stinkyscape's: a training yard (a sky, clouds, hills, a palisade of pointed stakes and a sand floor, with a hay bale or a pennant on some faces, each drawn from the card's id) under one subject its family fixes, so that a family reads as one at a glance. Real art delivered for a card still wins over it, as it wins over [[R503]]'s.

**The families.** The first that fits draws: a Call to Chaos a die in a swirl; a Felinor a cat's head (Flaming in flames, Right-Hand with its paw raised, Quadrupling four heads, Guerilla in a bandana); a Jelly Bean a bean; then by its name, a Golem the Golem of stone blocks, a Dummy a straw dummy on its post with a target on its chest, Boulder Fist Ogre, Clobberer, Ravager and Goliath Situation an ogre (with a boulder, a club, horns or a crown), Fan of Knives a fan of knives and Knife Juggler knives circling an apple, a Totem a carved totem under a flame tongue, Celestial Caller a staff between a sun and a moon, the Sun a sun, the Moon a moon, Seance a ghost over candles and Floodgates a sluice gate; then by its type, a Trap or Field Trap a rune (Explosive Runes a glowing standing stone, any other a rune plate and its fuse), a Field Spell a poster on the yard's notice board (rations, a level's chevrons, a recruiting poster, recycling arrows), a Unit a recruit with a wooden shield and a practice sword (Spikey Steven spiked, Personal Trainer in a headband with a whistle, Group leader under a flag, Selfless Protector behind a tower shield, Tutorial Master in a mortarboard with a scroll, Trigor three heads, Occidental a wide-brimmed hat, Oriental a conical one), and any other Spell an archery target with its name's [[R503]] motif at its centre.

**The snapshots.** #41–#60 are Core cards frozen (TG2), so each draws the picture of the Core card it freezes, its art and any delivered file read by that card's id: a snapshot and its Core card look the same.

**Both faces.** A face's geometry comes from streams seeded by its id alone, so the Radiant face draws the same shapes, its paint gilded and gold rays behind its subject. No other set's card reaches this art, so no shipped card's picture changes ([[R503]]).
