#90.1's "shuffle copies of this into your deck" happens at the end of the turn it is cast on, whoever's turn that is — a draw can come on either player's turn (C #38 Jackiestan Auctioneer draws on every card either player plays) — as an end-of-turn delayed effect ([[§2.2]], [[R62]]), in [[R68]]'s creation order among that turn's others; its damage is still dealt at once. The copies go into the caster's own library, its owner's, with the flag of the face that was cast ([[R57]]), each at a random position, and [[R80]]'s cap and [[R316]]'s `libraryOverflow` apply to each. So a chain casts only the viruses the library held when it began ([[R58]]), and each cast arms its own shuffle. A virus cast after that turn's end-of-turn delayed effects have begun is due at the next such point for the same player ([[R62]]); one cast during setup, which is no player's turn, waits for the end of turn 1. [[R241]] does not drop it, since "this turn" is never another player's
