Fuse's (S+ #107) attack hits every enemy Unit in its lane for 5 times its attack (Radiant 7) and
every enemy Unit in the adjacent lanes for 3 times (Radiant 4), as one damage instance each; a
declared target outside the sweep takes a normal hit. The weight reads declared numbers first, so
the projections agree with combat. Rejected: one instance split across the lanes, which would let
one Divine Shield stop the whole sweep.
