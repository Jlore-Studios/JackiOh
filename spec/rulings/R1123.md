On the Radiant face of Meditative #64 Traitorous Blood (MD-D20, [[R409]]'s reading), once the
redirected combat's state check has run, its controller gets a fresh copy, by definition and face, of
each Unit that combat destroyed: one per `Destroyed` event whose killer was one of the two combatants,
in event order. A Unit a Death hook destroyed meanwhile is no copy. A copy a Death hook's question
stopped is owed as plain data (`@combatCopies`, [[R113]]).
