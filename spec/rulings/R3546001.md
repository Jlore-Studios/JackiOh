A GIGA is in both its lanes for every rule that reads a lane in its row: attacks across, Cleave,
lane effects, "this lane and adjacent", lane watches, lane Locks and the Gauntlet. An effect that
hits each lane once hits it once per lane — a GIGA beside a Cleave's target takes the one hit —
and a Unit's own attack is made once. Rejected: both rows, under which a Cleave would hit a GIGA
Field Spell from the unit row.
