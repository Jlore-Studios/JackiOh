Stinkyscape #144 Prepare For Liftoff: "Your Starship launch costs (0)." The verb is `starship_launch_costs { cost }`: a player modifier on its controller (`StarshipLaunchCost`), expiry `used`, public as a badge on both seats ("Your Starship launch costs (0)", [[R169]]). While they hold one, their launch costs the least `cost` they hold instead of `STARSHIP_LAUNCH_COST`, never below 0 (`starship::launch_cost`), which the launch's label, `legalActions` and the refusal all read ([[R1852003]]). A launch by the Activate spends every one they hold, as a price rule is spent by the play it priced. A Starship an effect awakens launches for nothing and spends none, so the price waits for the next launch the player pays for.
