The owner asked in #2715 for a signature that signs like it means it. [[R2655003]]'s waited 0.4 s,
then a square wipe uncovered the name over 2.4 s, eased in and out, once, and sat still, every card in
Dancing Script.

**Faster and harder.** The pen touches down `SIGNATURE_START_MS` (60 ms) after the face mounts and
writes the name in `SIGNATURE_WRITE_MS` (520 ms) on an ease-out curve, fast at first and slowing to
land, so there is no slow start; the landing takes `SIGNATURE_LAND_MS` (260 ms), so the signing is done
`SIGNATURE_SIGNED_MS` (840 ms) after the face mounts, and a test holds that under one second. Those
numbers are short enough to read as one stroke and long enough that the eye follows the pen across a
twenty-letter name. The name is uncovered through a mask whose edge leans as a pen stroke does
(`SIGNATURE_EDGE_SLANT_DEG`, 12°) and fades over `SIGNATURE_EDGE_FEATHER` (10 of the signature's 200
units), not a square one. A pen tip, a white point in a gold glow, rides that edge and lifts as the last
letter lands. The landing hits: a gold flash bursts where the pen lifts, a flourish slashes out under the
name from its start, and a shine runs through the ink. Once landed it does not just sit: the shine runs
through the ink again every `SIGNATURE_SHINE_PERIOD_MS` (4.2 s), crossing it in a fifth of that and
resting past it for the rest. Each duration reaches the stylesheet as a custom property the signature
sets, so the constants are the one place the numbers live.

**A face that suits the card.** Each set signs in a hand of its own, and a small map gives a card its
own: Core in Kaushan Script, a fast brush script; Classic in Pinyon Script, a copperplate; Classic+ in
Mr Dafoe, a showman's brush signature; Meditative in Nanum Brush Script, an ink brush; Stinkyscape in
Sedgwick Ave Display, a graffiti tag; Training Grounds in Permanent Marker, a coach's marker. Mr.
Vanilla (Core #8) signs in Dancing Script, the plain face, and "Miss" Mrow (Core #86) in copperplate.
A set with no face of its own signs in Dancing Script, and a listed token in its parent card's hand
([[R2655002]]). Each face is under the SIL Open Font License, or for Permanent Marker the Apache
License 2.0, self-hosted in `apps/web/public/fonts/` with its licence beside it, Latin only, one weight,
and served from no CDN; a browser fetches one only when a name is drawn in it, so a face costs nothing
until a Mythic of its set is shown. The name is drawn to a length measured for its face, its glyphs
scaled to it, so it always fits its box and the wipe, the pen, the burst and the flourish know where it
starts and ends.

**What stays from [[R2655003]].** Everything moves by `transform` and `opacity`, inside the motion
gate, while Animated foil is on; under reduced motion or with Animated foil off nothing moves, the pen,
the burst and the shine are hidden, and the name stands whole with its flourish from the first frame.
It signs the English name, and stays clear of the gems, the crest, the name banner and a Unit's stats
on every card type.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: a signature card's name is a hand that suits the card, and its motion holds
still where the player asks for less.

Rejected: animating an SVG stroke's dash, which [[R2655003]] rejected; a typewriter of letters, which
reads as print, not a pen; one face for every card, which the owner asked against; a face per card,
twenty-one files where six serve and the map names the cards that need their own; and re-signing on
every hover, which would replay the landing each time a pointer crossed a hand of cards, where the
shine keeps it alive.
