## R783009 — The AI and a hidden Cloaked card

Built (#783). `legal_actions` lists a Cloaked card's face-down plays and its Reveal only when they can
complete ([[R783004]], [[R783006]]), so the AI plays and answers them through the same list as a person.
`determinize` ([[R185]]) samples a hidden backrow card from the Trap pool widened by the cards that may be
set by Cloaked on that side: every catalog card that prints Cloaked on a face that is not a Trap
(`alt_play::printed_cloaked_def_ids`), and every one an aura acting now lends it to in that side's hand
(`lent_cloaked_def_ids`, [[R783007]]); one sampled from them that no permission covers is stamped
`cloaked`, with no rng draw. A card set while an aura lent it Cloaked stays set after the aura goes, and
no later world samples it: the sampler reads what the seat can see. With no such card, as in every game
the catalog can play before #781's part 2, the pool and the draws are what they were.
