"Every simple Human and Felinor card on your field, in your hand and in your deck" is every card of its caster's, on the field, in the hand and in the deck (never the opponent's), whose tags as they stand hold Human or Felinor, that is simple ([[R5239009]]) and is not a Starship Piece already. Of those, only a permanent card gains it: a Unit, a Field Spell, a Trap or a Field Trap. A piece fuses into its owner's Starship as it dies on the field ([[R1852000]]), which a Spell never does, so a Spell is passed by. A hand or deck card keeps the keyword onto the field ([[R1878003]]); a Unit Immune to Spells is passed by ([[§6.1]]). The Radiant face's discount is a change to each such card's own price, which rides it between zones ([[R766]]).
