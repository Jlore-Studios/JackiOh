A Unit set face-down by its Cloaked and revealed by its Activate: Reveal enters its unit zone on the turn it is revealed, whatever turn it was set on, as any Unit that comes into a unit zone enters it on that turn ([[§4.1]], [[R83]], [[R171]]; Hearthstone's minion that comes onto the board cannot attack that turn). So it is summoning sick for the rest of its reveal turn: it declares no attack then unless Rush lets it attack an enemy Unit or Charge an enemy Unit or the hero, it may switch to Defense as any sick Unit may, and it attacks from its controller's next turn on. This supersedes the half of [[R783006]] and [[R1041]] that had it keep the turn it was set as its `summonedTurn`, so that revealed on a later turn it could attack at once, the one exception to [[R83]] and [[R171]]; it follows [[R2259000]], under which a Dormant card that awakens is sick that turn.

Nothing else about the reveal moves: it is still a firing ([[R783006]]), offered only while the card can come out whole, the card turns face-up and takes its unit zone as `animated`, never `summoned` ([[R445]]), its Cry runs with its choices asked as prompts ([[R467]]), and a Crater Unit Locks the zone it came out into before that Cry ([[R783010]]). A revealed Spell or Field Spell, which declares no attack, is untouched.

Engine: `alt_play::reveal_effect` stamps the revealed Unit's `summonedTurn` with the turn under way, so `combat::is_sick` and everything that reads it follow from the one stamp: `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal, the view's `sick` and `attackReason` ([[R849000]]) and the AI's search and lethal solver, which simulate through the engine. The testkit's invariant monitor counts the reveal as an entry, as it counts any other card's animation. A replay recorded earlier in which a revealed Unit attacked on its reveal turn is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). Rejected: keeping the set turn, which made a set Unit a hidden Charge its opponent could not see coming.
