Issue #4154 took M #1 Disruptive Disruptor's Cry (and its `draw` and `mana`) away and gave it "Activate: Your opponent discards a random card." (Radiant: "a random Radiant card, or a random card if they hold none"). The issue's "Activate 1" is one use a turn ([[R384]]), free. It is an effect's discard ([[R16]], [[R682]]), so the opponent's own Disruptive Disruptor, which guards them on their opponent's turn, stops it, drawing no random number ([[R800]], [[R129]]). The Radiant face draws among the Radiant cards of that hand, among all of them when it holds none, one card drawing nothing; an empty hand discards nothing and the Activate may still be used. Rejected: a prompt (the card says random) and "Radiant" as the Radiant-face Activate's own face (the issue's "radiant random card" is the card discarded). Through v0.6.6e: "Cry: Draw 1" (Radiant 2 and 2 mana) (`script_v0_6_6e`, [[R767000]]).
