[[§13.7]] had an AI seat pass every card it could, even one the teammate could not use better than a
play it already had, or one worth more in the seat's own hand than to its teammate. This note has it
pass a card only when the trade is worth it.

- **The gain** of passing a card is what it adds to the team, in `evaluate`'s points: the teammate's
  best bounded follow-up with the card ([[R1127000]]'s score) or, when higher, the best of the card's
  own plays made after the teammate's own best play, since the mana that play leaves may pay for the
  card too; less the teammate's own best bounded follow-up with no pass (the baseline: the best of
  `evaluate` over the first legal `Play` of each of at most `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` of its hand
  cards, in `legal_actions` order, each applied by one engine step to its probe and scored as
  [[R1127000]] scores, the probe's own value when none applies), the card's worth in the seat's own
  hand (`hand_card_value`), `DUOS_PASS_MARGIN_PER_COST` per crystal of the team's `pass_cost` and
  `DUOS_PASS_MIN_GAIN`.
- **The pass is made only when the gain is above 0**: the teammate's value with the card beats its
  value without it by more than the card's hand value, plus `DUOS_PASS_MARGIN_PER_COST` per crystal
  of pass cost, plus `DUOS_PASS_MIN_GAIN`. Each card is weighed on its own, and the ones that pass
  keep [[R1127000]]'s order.
- **Unchanged**: a free pass of a card the teammate can only afford next turn has no lookahead and
  keeps cost order; a scoring panic falls back to the cost order with every card kept; scoring reads
  fixed-seed probes only, never a hidden opponent card or the caller's stream ([[R185]]); at most
  `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` more `reduce` calls per card, plus as many for the baseline.

Rejected: passing every card that qualifies (the old reading), and weighing only the best-ranked card
and ending the turn when it fails, which would keep a lower-ranked card that is worth passing.
