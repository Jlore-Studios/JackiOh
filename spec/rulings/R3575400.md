Amends [[R2564000]] and [[R2603000]], which price the Activate of a card already acting on a field and leave a hand card's unpriced. So the turn a card is played its Activate counts for nothing, and only a card that has already stood through a turn reads it; a card the AI never plays is never priced, which keeps it unplayed. [[R2424000]]'s Death probes, [[R2477000]]'s turn-hook probes and [[R3064000]]'s awakening probes already spawn the seat's own hand cards ([[R923001]]); the Activate probes did not. Stinkyscape+ #157 Phantom Shipyard, a (4) 0/0 hull Dormant until launched, is worth its launch ("Activate 3: Exile the shipyard and launch as a Starship", [[R3539004]]), which no probe priced while the card sat in hand, and the shadow-ban sweep ([[R186]]) found it playable on 225 of Hard's turns over 212 pass-2 games and never played. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, after [[R2564000]]'s two turns, each Unit and Field Spell in the seat's own hand with an ability its face declares (`abilities_of`) and no price yet is probed in turn: on the same sandbox copy, automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), the card is spawned where a play would put it ([[R923001]]'s `spawn_hand_unit` and `spawn_hand_field_spell`), the seat ends its turn and the enemy ends that one (`next_turn`), and at the start of the seat's next turn its uses alone are tried and priced exactly as [[R2564000]] prices a card already standing there — every other card of the seat's is marked tried, so none is priced twice. The price is the best use's, keyed by the card as the seat holds it: its own definition and face and the seat as controller, never the definition the probe's two turns made of it, since no line of this decision holds that card. A shipyard whose yard fused a piece in while the probe slept ([[R3539004]]) is priced under Phantom Shipyard's own id. A hand card is never probed for the enemy, whose hand is hidden ([[R185]]).

The probes spend at most `AI_HAND_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes, a slice of its own beside `AI_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES`, so a hand full of Activates cannot starve the board's: 16, a spawn, two turn ends and `AI_REPLY.visible_activations` uses for each of about two cards. Nothing is priced and no node spent at a zero `activation` weight, while a prompt is open, once the game is over, or outside the seat's own main phase, as [[R2564000]] holds. The weight stays `AI_EVAL.activation`, 0.5, and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0.

**What it changes.** `unit_worth`, `material` and `slumber_worth` read the price through the same lookup they already use, so a card the line plays this turn is worth `activation` of one use of its Activate the moment it lands.

**What is not priced.** The enemy's hand. A card of a type no play puts on a field (a Spell, a Trap). A card whose face declares no ability. A use that leaves a prompt open, ends the game or gives its controller nothing, as [[R2564000]] holds. A card the spawn cannot place (no zone it can clear), a turn the probe's two turn ends do not reach, and a card the nodes did not reach.

**Rejected.** Pricing the use on the current board rather than at the seat's next turn, which would read a board the card has not yet been answered on and break [[R2564000]]'s "its own turn is where its uses are fresh"; drawing from `AI_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES`, which would let a hand of Activates starve the board's prices; and raising `AI_EVAL.activation`, which would move every Activate to fit one card.
