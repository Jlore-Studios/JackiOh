`crates/ai/tests/ai/lost_mana.rs` holds two of the AI's decisions on Meditative #18 Expedition12 ([[R844]], #1224). Both are at the scenario's turn 9, with one spare crystal and Mr. Vanilla on each side. Holding two K-Pop Fanatics, the AI keeps the Expedition. Holding Silly Silas, (4) 4/4 and Radiant 8/8, it casts it on him. With the switch on (`AI_EVAL_KIND = Learned`, [[R1010000]]), the two tests check nothing. `evaluate.rs` holds the price of a lost refresh itself, with either evaluation.

They are the hand eval's calibration: its price of the lost refresh against the Radiant face it buys. Neither is the line that wins sooner. Each position was played to the end from each line by the hand-eval AI on both seats, 20 times on other AI seeds (#466, at 57e4dc9). The AI won all 20 from every line. With Silly Silas, keeping won on turn 14.3 on average and casting on turn 16.0. With the Fanatics, casting won on turn 18.0 and keeping on turn 20.0.

With the switch on, a finalist also takes the network's correction ([[R1012001]]). That is fitted to whole games, and it weighs the lost turn otherwise. Every network fitted on the 4-card opening hand's games kept the Expedition with Silly Silas: the six seeds of the learn lane's ([[R1012000]]) run 10, and the 2,500-game network #1258 ships. One of them, run 10's seed 3, also cast it with the Fanatics. A promotion must pass the AI's tests with the switch on, so these two would refuse every network for a choice no faster win supports.
