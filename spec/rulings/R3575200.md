Infinite mana ([[R3548004]]) lets its holder pay every price for nothing, and the AI's evaluation read none of it: S+ #56 Impact Reactor, whose Cry costs four crystals next turn and two the turn after and whose awakening gives infinite mana ([[R3556000]]), scored as its cost and its Cry's lost crystals against a backrow card's price, so the shadow-ban sweep never saw the AI play it (issue #3575). No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** `material` adds, for each side, `AI_EVAL.infinite_mana` (1.0) times a share times what one turn of infinite mana frees that side (`infinite_mana_worth`): its hand's cost past its crystals (`mana.max`), floored at 0, each crystal at `unspent_mana`, the worth the evaluation gives a crystal. The seat's own hand is read card by card at what each costs now (`effective_cost`); the enemy's, unseen ([[R185]]), at `opponent_hand_cost` a card. The share is 1 while the side acts with infinite mana (`has_infinite_mana`), else the largest slumber share ([[R2380000]]: `slumber_turn_share` per turn left on the countdown, or `slumber_waiting_share`) of a Dormant card of the side's on the field whose face carries the flag, since it acts once that card wakes; 0 with neither. One turn, as a turn hook counts one firing ([[R2477000]]): the turn after infinite mana arrives the hand it emptied holds only what was drawn since. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, and at 0 nothing is read.

**Rejected.** A worth over every turn left in the game, which the evaluation gives no other lasting effect; the crystals at `next_turn_mana` times `turn_hook`, the share a turn hook's crystals take, which would price a card whose whole text is its mana below its Cry's own losses; and a card id, where the flag is what the engine reads.
