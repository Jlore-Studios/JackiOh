Issue #4440, Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: S #89 Spice "sells for ~15% more". What Spice fetches as it is played or discarded is its quote ([[R4410000]], [[R2250001]]): the open Marketplace's, drawn each turn within `HAND_SALE_PRICE` (`HAND_SALE_RADIANT_PRICE` on the Radiant face, [[R1853002]]), else one draw in that range. As issue #4154's "~25% more" did ([[R4154206]]), each end of the range is raised by the share and rounded to the nearest Credit, a half up: 4 to 13 becomes 4.6 to 14.95, so 5 to 15; the Radiant 8 to 25 becomes 9.2 to 28.75, so 9 to 29. The average sale rises from 8.5 to 10 Credits (about 18%), and on the Radiant face from 16.5 to 19 (about 15%): rounding each end to the nearest Credit is the reading closest to the share on both ends at once. Spice's text names no price and is unchanged, and S #173 Abandoned Spice Tower names none either. The range is drawn in every game whose Marketplace is open and in every closed-Marketplace play or discard of Spice, so every such game draws otherwise from then on: a change to the engine's constants, which no kept script brings back, so such a replay of an older version is refused by its final hash ([[R767000]]). Rejected: rounding each end down (4 to 14, Radiant 9 to 28), whose base range would rise less than a tenth; keeping the Radiant range twice the base one (10 to 30), which no Spice range has been.
