A play that [[§10.5]] step 4 hands to the opponent, #55 Lava Golem's base face paid with an opposing Unit ([[R360]]) or #52 Silly Silas's base face in the replays of the versions that printed it ([[R3837000]]), may be an Equip play onto a host of its player's: S #16 Rod of Ages lends Equip to any Unit ([[R1869000]]), and a card may have Equip ([[R1086]]). The hand-over wins and cancels the Equip: the card lands in the opponent's zone under their control as [[R360]] places it, never stands on the host, and at its `cardResolved` nothing is fused, as [[R1086]] fuses nothing when the top card left first. The host is left as it was, so a Rod that holds no fusion still lends. It is still the play that was listed and paid: its Tribute is paid, it counts as a play, and nothing is refunded. With no open unit zone on the opponent's side the card is not handed over: it lands where the play named ([[R360]]), on its host, and fuses as any Equip play does. A carrier (C+ #33 Ivory Tower) fuses only the Unit that lands on it ([[R653]]), so a Golem handed over from its zone is neither carried nor fused, and the Tower takes the next Unit played onto it. Hearthstone fuses a Magnetic minion only into the Mech it is played beside on its own side, so a card that ends on the other side fuses into nothing.

Engine: `play_steps::place_card` clears `PlayRun.equip_host` when `handed_over_zone` moves the card, so step 4 places it as a plain arrival and `fuse_equip` has no host at step 7. Before this, step 7 fused the opponent's Golem into its player's Rod: three opposing Units tributed and the 10/5 Taunt body kept.

Rejected: refusing an Equip play whose Tribute takes an opposing Unit, which would have `legal_actions`, the reducer and the view's reason read the Tribute's picks and the opponent's row after the Tribute for one card's clause, as [[R3837001]] declined for Silas. Also rejected: having `fuse_equip` check that the top card still tops its host, which would reach routes no issue reports through three pile shapes (unit piles, backrow piles, carried Units).
