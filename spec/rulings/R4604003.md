The fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) blames what follows a Spell's `hookResolving` on that Spell, up to the next marker, and a play nested in the run is its own until its `cardResolved`. Two readings blamed the Spell for what was not its.
**A `cardResolved` with no `cardPlayed` in the step ends the run.** An answer step resumes the run its prompt paused, and in one game that run held M #30.1 Love Bomb's cast on draw, whose `cardPlayed` was an earlier step's. Its `cardResolved` ends the Spell, but I21 found no nested play to return to and left the Spell running, so the start-of-turn hits after it were blamed on it. Now such a `cardResolved` ends the run, and what follows is nobody's until the next marker.
**A card announced face-down runs as a Trap.** A Spell set face-down by Cloaked runs as a Trap as it reveals ([[R3806000]]), and I21 read that off the set card. Anomaly Found!, set face-down, exiles its own side and itself, so no set card was left by the step's end to say so. Now a `cardAnnounced` with `faceDown` marks its card as set, under the id it is then played as (`cardPlayed`'s `formerId`).
Rejected: marking the end of every hook in the engine. The engine marks where a hook begins, never where it ends ([[R2783000]]), and a new event would change nothing a player sees.
