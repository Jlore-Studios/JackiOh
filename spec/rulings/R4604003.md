The fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) blames what follows a Spell's `hookResolving` on that Spell, up to the next marker, and a play nested in the run is its own until its `cardResolved`.
**A `cardResolved` with no `cardPlayed` in the step ends the run.** An answer step resumes the run its prompt paused, and in one game that run held M #30.1 Love Bomb's cast on draw, whose `cardPlayed` was an earlier step's. Its `cardResolved` ends the Spell, but I21 found no nested play to return to and left the Spell running, so the hits after it were blamed on it. Now such a `cardResolved` ends the run, and what follows is nobody's until the next marker.
The note first also ran a card announced face-down as a Trap; issue #4599's [[R4599002]] reached `main` first with that reading, so this note keeps only the `cardResolved`.
Rejected: marking the end of every hook in the engine. The engine marks where a hook begins, never where it ends ([[R2783000]]), and a new event would change nothing a player sees.
