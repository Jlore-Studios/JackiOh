The AI's count of the damage a board can deal past the Taunts in its way (`damage_past_taunts_with`, [[§9.9]]), which `face_threat`, the threat and pressure terms, `wall_switches_priced`, the lethal solver's `ready_gap` and the Duos pool loss read, pushes one entry per swing a unit has ([[R636]]) and walks them past the Taunts ([[R3937000]]). The only strike back it read was a First Strike Taunt's that destroys the attacker before it lands. An attacker the Taunt's simultaneous strike back destroyed ([[§4.3]] step 2) kept every later swing, a Windfury unit's second among them, though the engine gives a destroyed unit none; and the walk never gave a Frenzy attacker the swing the engine frees when its attack destroys the Unit it attacked and it survives ([[R3543003]]).

With the flag `strike_back_survival_read`, each hit in the walk takes the Taunt's strike back, read through the [[§4.4]] steps that can stop it as the First Strike read is: Divine Shield, which it pops, Armor, which a Pierce Taunt skips, the attacker's own cap, Indestructible, the zero rule, a Poisonous strike, an exiling one, and with the pools read a Taunt whose hits convert and the attacker's pool. A Ranged attacker takes none from a non-Ranged Taunt ([[R783000]]), and one with First Strike none from a Taunt without it that its hit destroys. An attacker the strike back destroys still lands that hit ([[R59]]) but makes none of its later swings, at a Taunt or at the hero, and the damage and the popped shield a strike back leaves carry to its next swing. A Frenzy attacker whose hit destroys the Taunt it attacked, and that survives, has its swings back to `attacks_per_turn`, as the engine's fresh exertion gives them, each with the hit's own hero permission, so a Rush unit that entered this turn still lands nothing on the hero; that is one more swing for a one-swing unit, and two for a Windfury unit whose second swing makes the kill. An exiled Taunt is not destroyed ([[R1124]]), so it frees none, nor does a Taunt a Cleave splash destroys.

On the issue's boards, with Overdrive Projector awake (S+ #62: every Unit has Windfury), an enemy 4/4 against the AI's 4/4 Taunt read 4 on the AI's hero, though the 4/4 dies in the trade; it now reads 0, and a 4/5 Archivist, which survives it, still reads 4. Tmack Trav (S #94, 7/7 Rush Frenzy) against a 3/3 Taunt read 0; it now reads 7, two 3/3 Taunts in turn still 7, and against a 7/3 Taunt whose strike back destroys it 0. With the Projector, Tmack Trav against a 0/10 Thorium Wall read 0; its two swings break the Wall and Frenzy gives both back, so it now reads 14.

The flag is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick's walk counts every swing and frees none, as before. The walk reads its weights' flag, and everything that reads the walk is reached through it.

It follows Hearthstone semantics: a minion destroyed in combat makes none of its remaining attacks, Windfury's included.

Rejected:

- one more swing per kill for a Windfury Frenzy attacker, which the engine's fresh exertion does not give: it resets the unit to `attacks_per_turn` swings, whatever it had left;
- an exiling hit freeing a Frenzy swing, since the engine's Frenzy waits on the Unit's destroy, and an exile emits none;
- a search over the orders of the attacks, since the count runs at every evaluation; the walk's orders stay as they were, the larger result counting.

Not modelled: the attacking side's damage pools meet each strike back whole, as the First Strike read has them; lane strike ([[R94]]) and the combat-only modifiers ([[R1120]]) stay out, as they do for the walk's hits; the fresh swings follow the walk's existing orders; and a Reborn attacker the strike back destroys swings no more, though its body comes back ([[R83]]) and one with Charge could still attack.

It reads only public state, and it adds no rule of the game and no constant.
