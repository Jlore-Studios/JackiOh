`features_of(state, seat)` (`crates/ai/src/features.rs`) is `features(redact(state, seat), seat)`: the learned evaluation's inputs come from the seat's redacted view alone ([[R185]]), so two states that differ only in what the seat cannot see give the same features, as they give the same decision. The search scores its finalists on determinized worlds; the features of such a world are those of its redaction, which puts the sampled hidden cards back to placeholders.

The engine's projections the features use, a unit through §10.4's layers (`unit_view`), the hero's armor (`hero_armor_of`) and the face damage a board threatens (`face_threat`), read the text of every backrow card, and a redacted view's hidden backrow cards are placeholders with none. They are left out of those projections, not guessed: the features compute them on the view less the backrow cards the seat cannot read (`public_world`), by the rule `evaluate` reads a backrow card by (`backrow_is_public`, §10.8). The hidden cards still count: each side's backrow size, how many of its cards the seat can read and how many it cannot. A sparse feature names a card the seat can read and nothing else: its own board, hand and backrow, the enemy's board and the enemy's face-up backrow. The enemy's hand is a count.

The layout is `FEATURES_VERSION`. Any change to what a feature computes or where it sits bumps it, and the weights a fit makes record the version they were fitted on (`docs/learned-eval.md`, D10).
