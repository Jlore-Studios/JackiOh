The designer's rule fires as the piece "enters the GY": the copy fuses into the Starship of the player whose graveyard the piece entered, summoning one there, on their side, if they have none. Since [[R2618000]] a card that dies goes to the graveyard of the player who controlled it, as theirs, so a stolen piece builds the thief's ship and its owner builds nothing, as Hearthstone credits a minion that dies to the player who controlled it. Until then a Steal changed control only ([[R15]], [[R171]]) and a card that died went to its owner's graveyard ([[§3.2]], [[R12]]), so a stolen piece built its owner's ship. A card that has changed hands for good is its new owner's by [[R747]] and [[R12]] (a bounced stolen card, a steal off the field), and builds that player's ship. Its Death hook, if it has one, still runs for its controller, as every Death does.
