A Steal changes control only ([[R15]], [[R171]]), and a card that dies goes to its owner's graveyard ([[§3.2]]); the designer's rule fires as the piece "enters the GY". Hearthstone, which has no graveyard to own, credits a minion that dies to the player who controlled it; the spec's ownership rules ([[R747]]) decide instead, as the designer's words point: the copy fuses into the Starship of the player whose graveyard the piece entered, its owner, on its owner's side, summoning one there if they have none. Its controller builds nothing. A card that has changed hands for good is its new owner's by [[R747]] and [[R12]] (a bounced stolen card, a steal off the field), and builds that player's ship. Its Death hook, if it has one, still runs for its controller, as every Death does.
