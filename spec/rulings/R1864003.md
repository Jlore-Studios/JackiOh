"Whenever you Trade a card, the card you put into your deck has a {exileChance}% chance to be exiled and a {radiantChance}% chance to become Radiant" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #164) is a trigger on its controller's own `traded` ([[§10.3]]); the opponent's Trades roll nothing. `traded` names no card ([[R1838001]]), so a trigger on it is handed the card the Trade put into the deck in its queue entry (`tradedId`, which no view shows): the event owed right after `traded`, the Trade's `shuffledIn`. As the trigger resolves it rolls once out of `PERCENT` ([[R1849009]]): a roll below `exileChance` exiles the card from the deck ([[§6.3]] Exile), a roll below `exileChance` plus `radiantChance` makes it Radiant where it lies ([[§5.2]]), and any other leaves it as it was. The two chances are exclusive, the Radiant chance taking the next numbers after the exile's, so the base face's 30 and 30 leave the card 40% of the time and the Radiant face's 30 and 70 never; past 100 between them, Radiant takes what the exile leaves. Both chances are declared numbers read through `param()` ([[R386]]). A card no longer in its controller's deck when the trigger resolves (the Trade's own draw took it straight back, or something moved it) rolls nothing ([[R129]]), and the Trade's draw stands whatever the roll. The roll has no better side, so Luck does not roll it again ([[R987]]). The exile names the card, as every exile does; a card made Radiant in a deck stays hidden from the other seat ([[R97]]). As in Hearthstone, the trigger answers the Trade once it is made, its draw included. Rejected: two separate rolls, which would make a card both exiled and Radiant or neither at other odds than the card prints; and a roll on a card the draw took back into the hand, which the text does not name ("the card you put into your deck").
