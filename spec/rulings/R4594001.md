While Thousand Faces acts, its controller's face-down cards cannot be destroyed: a destroy from either side is stopped at [[§4.5]] step 1 beside Indestructible (`face_down_indestructible`), but reveal, Exile, bounce, steal and transform are not. A face-up card dies. A card its controller sets face-down arrives face-down in its controller's backrow — a face-down play and a summon landing face-down both emit `summoned` with the backrow row — so the one arrival event answers each set exactly once; face-up arrivals never do ([[R61]]). The draw cap is per turn on its controller's memory, on either player's turn; simultaneous sets count in order ([[R129]]).
