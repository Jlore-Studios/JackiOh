A result that ends a match is stamped with the seat it belongs to rather than whoever happened to be active, so folding the log never has to guess: a disconnect timeout belongs to the player who disconnected, because the loss is theirs, while reaching the turn ceiling belongs to neither and is stamped with the active seat as a convention. [[R79]] makes the ceiling a draw and [[R112]] covers the reaper's version; neither settles the seat
