Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. A fight tells why it went as it did through nine combat keywords, each with a beat: the smallest tier of issue #2782's animations, drawn inside the entry of the event it keys on, holding nothing and ending within the effects' tail ([[R200]]). Cleave, Trample, Pierce, First Strike, Lifesteal and Poisonous key on the `via` the engine puts on the event ([[R2783001]]); Windfury, Flying and Ranged on an attack declared by a unit the view shows with the keyword. Each is built from the cues and cue helpers of [[R2784000]]'s motif framework, with no new rendering.

**The beats.** Cleave: the attacker's blow continues from the target it attacked as a wide arc to each neighbour it hit, which lands a smaller splat. Trample: the overflow carries on from the dead unit as a bolt to the hero (or the unit beyond), with a second splat there. Pierce: a spear line is drawn through the defender, from the side the blow comes from, and the hit keeps its own row. First Strike: a quick-draw glint on the attacker, whose strike lands alone; when the defender dies to it (its death carries `via: First Strike`, the marker that no strike back came) the defender's counter-blow starts toward the attacker and breaks off in smoke without landing, and when it lives its counter-blow lands only at the land phase of its own entry, after a clear beat, never at once. Lifesteal: red motes stream from the damaged unit to the controller's hero and land with the heal's splat, which replaces the heal row's. Windfury: the second attack of a turn starts as an after-image (a card back) of the first attack's flight. Poisonous: a death keyed `via: Poisonous` draws the poison burst, which the client used to guess from whether the damage's source was Poisonous in the view; that guess is gone, so a Poisonous source's hit draws none and a Poisonous kill does. Flying: the attacker's lunge arcs over the lane, lifted toward the viewer. Ranged keeps its flavor shot.

**The callout.** One shared cue (`callout`), built once for every keyword part of issue #2782: the keyword's icon pops over the unit that has it for `FX_CALLOUT_MS`. A hit's beats start `FX_BEAT_STAGGER_MS` apart (scaled with the effects speed, and at most `FX_BEAT_STAGGER_MAX` of the entry in), and at most `FX_CALLOUTS_PER_HIT` of them pop a callout. A callout repeating a keyword over a unit still showing it takes its place. Each beat is planned in the entry's duration less its stagger, so it too lands inside the entry and ends within the tail, and the effects speed scales all of it ([[R201]]).

**Reduced motion** amends [[R200]]: no effect is drawn at all under the media query or the viewer's reduce setting, but a keyword's callout, which keeps its icon as a static one and drops its motion, so the keyword still reads. The animation queue signals each entry it plays with no duration (the row's table duration being above 0) to the effects layer, which holds nothing for it and waits for nothing, and which plans only the entry's still callouts; none with the effects `off`. The layer's canvas, particles, shake and every other cue stay off, and a dead unit's callout is skipped, the board having swapped.

**R202.** A beat reads `via`, the keywords the view shows on a unit and the ids it shows, never a definition: a hit whose source the view hides carries no `via` and so no beat, and no cue names a card. A Poisonous kill whose killer is hidden calls out over the unit that died.

**Rejected:** guessing a keyword from the view, as Poisonous was, which a hidden source or a granted-then-lost keyword gets wrong; callouts without a cap, which a Cleave of three neighbours and a Flying Windfury unit would stack over one unit; and holding the animation queue for a beat, since a beat holds nothing and an entry's duration is the table's.
