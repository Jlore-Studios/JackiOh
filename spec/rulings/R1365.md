Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Moments that shared a generic sound each have one of their own, procedural like the rest: a Brittle count running out (`crumbled`, [[R385]]) cracks dry and falls apart (`crumble`, in place of the shattered glass and the death's rubble); a Locked zone opening (`unlocked`) springs its latch (`unlock`, where `locked` keeps `lock`); a Counter (`countered`) slams the play shut and it fizzles (`counterspell`, in place of the called-off attack's sound and a puff); a fuse (`fused`) converges two tones on one bell (`fuse`, in place of the puff); a Nerf (`degraded`) goes out of tune (`degrade`) and a Buff (`upgraded`) tunes up (`upgrade`, in place of the stat buff's sweeps, [[R386]]); and a card transformed into a Sheep the viewer can read (`SHEEP_DEF_IDS`) bleats as it comes out of the transform's puff (`bleat`). None of them is a moment that speaks ([[R204]]): a transform still speaks nothing.

None of them varies with a card the viewer cannot read ([[R203]]): a transform whose new card is the sentinel only puffs, and a Nerf or a Buff sounds the same whatever it changed, `none` included, so the sound numbers the applications over a hidden pile and never the changes ([[R440]]). A burned card keeps the full hand's own sound ([[R319]]).
