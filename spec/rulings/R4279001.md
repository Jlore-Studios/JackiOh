Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes. Every figure is read off `GET /api/stats/cards` as before.

**Why.** The owner found it unclear on `/stats` that Sets and Players were places to click, and hard to find the token statistics. The view tabs were small dim words over a thin line, below a row of patch tabs drawn as buttons, so they read as headings. The Tokens and Other created cards sections ([[R3324002]], [[R4155000]]) stood below the deck table, after every deck card, where a reader who did not scroll to the end never met them.

**The tabs.** The page has four tabs: Cards, Tokens, Sets and Players. Each is a raised button with a border, its name over a line of what it shows ("Win rate and tier of every card", "Tokens and other cards made in games", "How each set does", "Every player's games and win rate"). The open one is filled and underlined in the selected colour. On a phone (600 px wide or less) the tabs show their names alone, so the row stays one line. The arrow keys move between them in that order, as before.

**The Tokens tab.** The Tokens and the Other created cards, their columns, sorts, rows and drill-down unchanged ([[R2089000]], [[R4155000]]), move from the foot of the Cards tab to a Tokens tab of their own, beside it. It shares the Cards tab's filters, one set of state across both: the search, the set, the rarity, the cost and the match type, each shown on it. The keyword, which only the deck table reads, is shown on the Cards tab alone. It also has the Plain text button ([[R3887000]]) and the Games column toggle. With no Created card for its filters it says "No tokens or created cards match the selected filters." A patch still collecting its data shows its note there, as on the Cards and Sets tabs ([[R3324000]]). `?tab=tokens` opens it, and opening it writes that address. The Cards tab keeps the games panel and the deck table.

Rejected:
- A link at the top of the Cards tab that scrolls to the sections: it leaves them after the deck table, and the tab row is where the page's places are.
- A Tokens tab with filters of its own: the same search would read two ways.
- Showing the hints on a phone too: the row would wrap or scroll off the screen.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
