Its controller's Spell, Field Spell and Trap plays of the turn (casts included, [[R70]]) are counted in the order they were played, a cast made inside a play's resolution coming after that play; Wardrum answers the resolution of the play whose place in that order is its threshold (3) and no other, so a 4th cast that resolves inside the 3rd's resolution does not set it off and the 3rd's own resolution does
