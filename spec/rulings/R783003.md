## R783003 — Flying lands in Defense Position

Built (#783). The designer's "'Lands' when in Defense Position to have Taunt": in Defense Position a
Flying Unit has no restriction of [[R783002]], anything may attack it, and the Taunt the position gives
([[§4.1]]), or its own, binds every attacker. Read off the unit's position through the layers, so it
holds the moment the unit switches, either way.
