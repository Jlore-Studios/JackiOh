S #151's first entry reads "Trigger any Anomaly" (ST-S25-7).

- **The board.** One of all ten Anomaly boards (`t_anomaly_found::ALL_BOARDS`): the seven Anomaly Found! lays on its base face and the three rich ones its Radiant face lays ([[R1879000]]). Each is equally likely, drawn from the match rng as the entry resolves ([[R60]]). The Scape Edition's own face does not choose between the two lists: a Radiant Scape Edition rolls three entries, and its board, if one is rolled, comes from all ten too.
- **The swap.** [[R1855000]]'s, on its caster's side alone (ST9 Q7): what the entry can reach there is exiled, a Dormant card staying, then the board is laid and every Unit it laid fires its Cry with its caster's choices random ([[R1855001]]). The enemy's side is not touched.
- **No card.** It makes no Anomaly Found! card; the swap is the entry's own.
- **Where the table lives.** The boards are the cards crate's data, so the edition's table is the card's (`CHAOS_SCAPE_EFFECTS` in its file), its other nine entries the engine's builders (`subsystems::scape_*`).
