"The previous card you played" (MD-D12) is the play before this one in this turn's log (casts count, [[R70]]), found wherever it is ([[R98]]): from your graveyard or exile it moves to your hand ("Return … to hand", [[R746]]); a permanent you control is Bounced ([[R692]], [[R747]]), and a unit token vanishes; anywhere else (a hand, a deck, ceased to exist, controlled by the opponent) nothing happens. A full hand burns it. The Radiant face then makes it Radiant in your hand.
