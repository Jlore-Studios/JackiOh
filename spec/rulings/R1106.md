- Spec: §7, §10.8

At its controller's end of turn Meditative #54 Money Machine makes two fresh shuffles: The Coin into the opponent's deck, then a card into its controller's. That card is rolled once, as the end-of-turn ability starts and before either shuffle: one draw of the match rng over `MONEY_MACHINE_ODDS` in engine config, The Coin 89, Jade (M #39.2) 10 and Red Jade (M #39.4) 1, Radiant on the Radiant face, whose opponent still gets a base Coin. The roll is unprinted hidden data, as [[R673]]'s Glitch odds are, and no luck-based roll: the Machine prints no Lucky and flips no coin ([[R1444]]), so no Lucky and no Luck ([[R987]]) reaches it. Both cards go in unseen (`shuffle_into`'s `unseen`): neither is a card its owner was shown going in ([[R311]]), so each owner's list counts it as unknown until it leaves the library ([[R312]]), and the `shuffledIn` events name it to nobody, so nothing tells a Jade from a Coin before it is drawn. [[R80]]'s cap turns a full library away; a full controller's library takes no card, so nothing is rolled for it and the card turned away is The Coin the text names, and the overflow it reports gives away nothing of the odds. The Activate either player may use is [[R1105]]'s.
