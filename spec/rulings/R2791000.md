Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Ten tempo and card keywords each get a beat: the smallest tier of issue #2782's animations, drawn inside the entry of the event it keys on, holding nothing and ending within the effects' tail ([[R200]]), through the shared callout and the stagger, callout-cap, reduced-motion and intensity rules of [[R2789000]]. The beats key on [[R2783001]]'s `via` where one exists, and otherwise on the existing events plus the keywords and ids the view shows, never a hidden card.

**The beats.** Charge: speed lines and a forward lean onto the enemy hero as it lands, then a dash on its first attack of the turn ([[§4.1]], [[§4.2]]). Rush: as Charge, but the lean points at the enemy units and never at the hero. Equip: the equipment flies onto its host and snaps on, with a click ring; the host's stat tick-up stays the bless row's ([[R1086]]). Tradable: the card swirls back into the Deck and the drawn card swirls out on the `flight` motif ([[R1838000]]). Stack: each copy lands on the stack with a stacked-card offset and a count pulse ([[§3.2]]). Spell Damage: a Spell's damage motif burns hotter, with a "+N" sigil on the source as the spell resolves, N the view's Spell Damage total. Lucky: clover glints over the extra rolls [[R2652001]]'s `rolled` event reports ([[R987]], [[R1438]]), the kept one the roll panel's own highlight. Deft: a quick feint before an attack that follows a same-turn switch ([[R49]]); no event marks an aimed random cast apart, so a switch-then-attack is the observable trigger. Flashback: the card rises from the graveyard pile wreathed in a ghost frame ([[R454]]), then dissolves as it is exiled after it resolves. Temporary: a fading hourglass on the card, and a dissolve when it leaves at the end of its time ([[R637]], [[R2179001]]). Since [[R2801000]] a Starship Piece's copy fusing into its Dormant Starship is no Equip: its fuse run draws it, and an Equip fused onto a Starship from the hand keeps its beat.

**The keying.** A landing reads the played card's hand keywords: the hand card's delta keywords, else its printed face (the Radiant one when Radiant) through the catalog or the view's definitions, as a motif reads its scale; a hand that is a count, a missing card or a hidden one gives nothing. A Stack landing reads the pile it lands on, a Spell's bonus the resolving play and the view's Spell Damage total, a Lucky glint the batch's extra draws, a Deft feint the attacks and switches of the turn, a Flashback exile the graveyard play in the history, and a Tradable draw the trade's own shuffle immediately before it (its first draw only, R1838001). Temporary's standing hourglass is the existing `Card.tsx` treatment; the beat is the dissolve.

**Speed and motion** follow [[R201]] and [[R2789000]]: D carries the effects speed, and so the stagger; under reduced motion a beat keeps its callout as a static icon and drops its motion; with the effects off, nothing. **[[R202]]:** a beat reads `via`, the keywords a view shows and the ids it shows, never a definition; a hidden hand card is never named, on either seat.

**Rejected:** guessing a keyword from a definition the seat may not read, which a hidden hand gets wrong; a second flight under a Tradable draw, which the `flight` motif already owns; holding the roll panel for Lucky's glints, which land inside its own timings; and a beat for a fusion whose result is fresh, which is no equipment landing.
