[[R2660000]]'s concession check counts only what shows on the board, and every doubt in it plays on. The audit of issue #4787 found two things it counted that the bot cannot know, so a ladder bot could concede a game it still had a way out of.

**A Rollback is a doubt.** `redact` drops C+ #35 Rollback's board history whole ([[R419]], [[R185]]): its snapshots hold face-down cards and cards since gone to a hand. The check's walk then played a Rollback as restoring nothing, a line that fails to stop the lethal, though in the game it returns the board to a turn before the lethal units stood there. Now each of the check's worlds holds one snapshot of its own, the field as it shows at the check, as this turn's, so a Rollback always finds one, and a step that returns the board to a snapshot (the engine's `rolledBack`) proves nothing, as a roll or a draw proves nothing: a bot that can pay for a Rollback plays on. No card is named in the AI; whatever returns the board to a snapshot counts. A Rollback the bot cannot pay for, or one an answer it can see stops, changes nothing.

**A secret it has not seen is left out.** M #22 Mind Games' choice is its caster's to read ([[R860]], [[R865]]), so `redact` drops it, and each of the check's worlds drew one of the three for it; its reward at the caster's next turn start ([[R861]]) read the guess. Where every world guessed Attack, its damage made a lethal the real choice might not. Now the check takes an unrevealed secret of the opponent's out, as it takes out the opponent's hand and face-down cards: its reward finds no choice and does nothing, and an M #22.1 Fortify Mind's guess finds nothing to judge. A lethal on the board alone still shows, the secret or not. A secret revealed to both players stays, its choice known.

**Rejected.** Keeping the guessed choice, as [[R2660000]] rejects trusting a world's rolls and draws. Never conceding while the opponent holds an unrevealed secret, which keeps a doomed bot playing although a lethal on the board needs no reward. Simulating a Rollback on a redacted history, which would put the snapshots' hidden cards back for the walk to guess at. Naming Rollback's card id in the AI.
