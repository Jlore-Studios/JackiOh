Each seat's Glitch board is the last board ([[R417]]) of another player's game, picked by the server at random among other accounts when the match is created and frozen into it as a setup input exactly as last boards are (`createGame`'s `glitchBoards`, [[R564]]'s rebuildable ids). When Glitch draws it, every card on both fields ceases to exist (no Death, no graveyard, [[R11]]'s way out), the Locks go, and each side takes its board in order: Units into the unit zones, the rest into the backrow, left to right while a row has room, each a new card its side owns on its entry's face, entered this turn ([[R171]]), placed rather than summoned or played, so nothing triggers. Hands, decks and life totals stay: only boards are stored. A side with no board frozen (hotseat, practice, or no other account has one) is left empty. `viewFor` never sends the frozen boards
