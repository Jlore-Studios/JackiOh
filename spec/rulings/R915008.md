title: "Custom cards are saved on the account, private to their owner (issue #915)"
cards: "§12.9, §9.4, R250"
proven_in:
  - crates/server/tests/api/custom_cards.rs
  - crates/server/tests/store/contract.rs

---
An active account's custom cards live in a table only their owner can read or write (row-level security, as saved decks have). The routes under `/api/custom-cards` list, read, create, update and delete them: a create mints the card's id (`custom-` and 32 lowercase hex digits) and version 1, an update replaces the card whole and counts a version, and every save is checked by the engine's validator ([[R915001]]) and refused with its issues. An account keeps at most `CUSTOM_CARDS_PER_ACCOUNT`. The server never compiles or runs a script.
