Fruit Launcher's "Choose a permanent. Cast a random Fruit on it" and Book Launcher's "Cast a random harmful Book on an enemy" are a random cast ([[R452]]: free and counted as played, made and owned by the caster and landing in their graveyard, [[R70]], [[R87]]) aimed ([[R946]]) at the Activate's declared pick: `cast_random_at` names it by its prompt key, and the cast card's first target declaration takes it where it is a legal pick, as Meditative #98's Book of Buff takes its aim. Where it is not (a Fruit whose target can't be that permanent, a Book of Blood aimed at a hero), the pick is the rng's like every other choice of the cast, narrowed by `targetEnemies` where the card asks it ([[R656]]); a card with no target is cast as it is. Hearthstone casts a spell "on" a minion the same way where the spell can target it. Fruit Launcher's pool is [[R382]]'s Fruit pool, the Grapes among it; the Fruit or the Book is cast Radiant on the Radiant face. Rejected: an aimed cast that casts nothing when the aim is not legal ([[R1180005]]'s `aimOnly`), which would waste most Fruits, and a new `CastRandomArgs` field, which would change five struct literals and the Luau card kit's argument for one reading.
