Mana caps at 4 ([[§2.3]]), and seven cards print "Float reduces cost" ([[§6.2]], [[R1563000]]): S #33 to #36 Drone Core VII to X, #29 GIGA Glowy Jelly Bean, #49 Snom Bunny Mind Control and M #49.1 YileGPT Prime, each a hand trigger ([[R464]], [[R2657002]]) that takes the Float off the card's `costMod`, kept until a graveyard or exile resets it ([[R766]]). A player saves mana for a turn or two to cast one. The AI never did: a passed line paid `AI_EVAL.unspent_mana` (0.8) for each crystal left unspent, and the cheaper card paid back only `hand_cost_delta` (0.5), so each crystal Floated toward such a card scored −0.3 whether or not it was what put the card within reach, and the hand prices every card above `hand_cost_cap` alike. No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** The engine marks the trigger `float_reduces_cost` builds (`TriggerDef.lowers_cost_on_float`), and `float::float_cuts_in_hand` reads, for a Float of n, n off each of a player's hand cards for each marked hand trigger its running script registers, so a fused card that keeps the trigger keeps the mark, and a Vanilla one, which runs no script, has none. On every passed line, `float_credit` adds `AI_EVAL.float_discount` for each crystal of the line's Float (its turn log's `unspentAtEnd`) that went toward a card still above the seat's crystals: read after the turn ended, where the cut is already on the card's `costMod`, a card's cost before it was `own_cost + cut`, and it counts `clamp(before − crystals, 0, cut)`, summed over the cards. The crystals are the seat's `mana.max`, never its handicap's cap or bonus ([[R180]]). `passed_score`, the search's static score of a passed line, and `replied_score`, a line scored after the opponent's reply, both add it, the latter from the state where the line ended, so a countdown's second scoring counts it once. `AI_EVAL.float_discount` is 0.8, equal to `unspent_mana`, the least the issue allows: a crystal Floated toward a card out of reach now nets `hand_cost_delta`, +0.5, and one past it still −0.3, so the AI does not hoard beyond what the card needs. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, and at 0 nothing is read.

**What is not counted.** A deck card's discount (it does not decide what the AI can cast soon), the enemy's hand, a card already within the crystals, an X-cost card (`own_cost` has none), a Tribute Float ([[R1841003]], a requirement and no cost) and a discount every card of a player takes.

**Approximation.** Below the mana cap, `mana.max` is this turn's crystals, so one crystal the next refresh would have covered can count. The Float is the turn log's `unspentAtEnd`, which a trap answering the end of turn could leave apart from the `turnEnded` event's `unspentMana`. A Float card that reaches the hand after `turnEnded` is counted a cut it never got.

**The sweep's second number.** The issue asked why Drone Core X, within mana on 18 turns of the 2026-10-29 sweep at Hard, was cast once. With X in reach and a free unit zone the AI casts it, at Easy (cost 4 on 4 crystals) and at Hard (7 on 7), on every cursor tried; with a stronger play in hand on 7 crystals it plays that instead, Genn (4) 14/14 or Greaser (2) 7/7. The Drone Battleship enters as one Stack pile ([[R1842000]]), so only its top layer, the Shield Layer, a 12/12 that can't attack, counts, and the two layers dormant under it count `AI_EVAL.dormant_share`, 0 since #1506's screen at 0.5 and 1.0 found no edge. On a bare Hard board the static evaluation gains 14.4 from casting X at `dormant_share` 0, 32.9 at 0.5 and 51.5 at 1.0. That is a screened weight every Stack pile reads, not the small fix the issue allowed here, so the build reported it; a person then asked for it in the pull request, and [[R3695001]] counts those layers.

**Numbers.** Main at `e8dfc7a2b7` against the same with the Float credit alone, one machine; [[R3695001]] has them with the Battleship's lower layers counted too. The smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`) read the same at both: ai-vs-random 19 of 20 (17 needed), ai-vs-greedy 16 (10), hard-vs-easy 20 (16), the perf gate's slowest decision 41 ms. `cargo jackioh sweep stinkyscape-033 stinkyscape-034 stinkyscape-035 stinkyscape-036` flagged none of the four in pass 1 at either. Pass 1's eight forced games a tier (drawn, affordable turns, casts) were the same for Drone Core VII (Easy 7, 4, 2; Hard 5, 3, 2), VIII (Easy 2, 1, 0; Hard 6, 2, 2) and IX (Easy 4, 1, 1; Hard 5, 3, 3), and for X at Hard (2, 1, 1); X at Easy, drawn in 5, went from 1 affordable turn and 1 cast to 3 and 2. Pass 2 took only X, over 24 games a tier: at Easy, drawn in 12, from 2 affordable turns and 1 cast to 1 and 0; at Hard, drawn in 16, from 4 to 3 affordable turns, never cast. That is 13 casts of the four before and 13 after, and against the 2026-10-29 report of record (X in pass 2: Easy drawn 12 of 18, 1 affordable turn, 1 cast; Hard 27 of 58, 18, 1) no more casts of X: the credit alone does not meet the issue's sweep criterion. Hard, whose crystals reach its handicap's 7, is one Float from X's 8 and leaves it in hand when it is in reach, the second number above. At `float_discount` 2.4 X's pass 1 read Easy 2 affordable turns and 2 casts, and Hard 4 affordable turns and none.

**Rejected.** The handicap's next-turn mana (`duos::next_turn_mana`) as the reach, which the evaluation never reads ([[R180]]); a hand-written list of the Float cards, where the mark reads what the engine registers, a fused card's too; raising `hand_cost_cap`, which moves every card's hand price; the largest cut alone in place of the sum, which would count one card where two are brought nearer; a weight under `unspent_mana`, which would keep a Float toward a card out of reach a loss; a weight of 2.4, three times it, which cast X no more often in pass 1 and never at Hard (Numbers), and risks more of the hoarding the issue warns of. The issue's two decision tests, Drone Core IX beside a one-drop at Easy and Drone Core X in reach with a free zone, pass with the weight at 0 too, since nothing else in either hand takes the crystals; they stand as guards, and a third proves what the credit buys: on 4 crystals with Drone Core X and a 4-mana 1/1 the AI spent them on the 1/1 at 0, and at 0.8 it Floats all four and casts X, at 4, the next turn.
