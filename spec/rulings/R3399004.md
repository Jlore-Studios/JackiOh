Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Each of Classic+'s 35 signatures ([[R3399000]]) names an effect of its own in `card-audio.json5` ([[R655]]), beside the voice it already had: on `play` for a Unit, on `cast` for every other card. Each effect is one of the procedural recipes of `sfx.ts`, the one nearest the first sound of its card's concept (a griddle's sizzle, a referee's whistle, a backward-ticking clock), with a pitch, gain and options no other of the 35 shares, so no two sound alike; each is used by its card alone. They render nothing and add no asset, so they stay inside the audio budgets, and the audio settings act on them as on every effect. An effect the bank held only for a card that now plays its own (Frozen Wastes' `iceCrack`) leaves the bank, which names no unused effect.

Rejected: rendered sound files, which would add assets and a render step for 35 sounds the procedural recipes already approximate.
