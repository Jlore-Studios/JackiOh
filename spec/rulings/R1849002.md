While a card acting on the field says "Anytime something would be Destroyed, it is Exiled instead" (`staticFlags.destroyedExiled`), every card [[§4.5]] step 1 collects (a Unit at 0 or less health or marked destroyed, a backrow card marked destroyed) that no "would die" replacement took ([[R462]]) is exiled instead. Every card is judged before any moves, so the aura card dying in the same pass exiles itself and what dies beside it. They are exiled first, in [[R68]]'s order, by [[§6.3]] Exile's one move: a unit token ceases to exist ([[R11]]), the exile counter counts the rest ([[R55]]) and `exiled` reports each. An exiled card was not destroyed: no Death, no Reborn, no `destroyed` and the destroyed counter unchanged, as for an exile mark ([[R461]], [[R1124]]). The Radiant face's "Anytime an enemy permanent would be Destroyed" (`enemyDestroyedExiled`) exiles only the cards of its controller's opponent; its controller's own, itself included, are destroyed as usual. "Destroyed" means collected: a Sacrifice, a Tribute's included, a discard and a spent Trap are not destroyed and still reach the graveyard. Rejected: exiling sacrifices too, which [[R462]] keeps apart from would-die; and judging each card as it moves, which would let the aura card's own exile end the aura for the cards after it.
