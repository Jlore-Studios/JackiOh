It still takes the leftmost empty, unlocked zone ([[R64]]). Stack lifts the occupancy refusal only for a zone the play **names**, so a full row refuses a zone-less Stack play even though every lane would accept a named one. The asymmetry is confined to the convenience path; a named zone answers the same in both directions, which is what [[R81]] and [[R90]] govern
