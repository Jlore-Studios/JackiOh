Training Grounds #37 Seance adds "an exact copy of a permanent" to its caster's hand, "keeping its cost changes". [[R1242]]'s `copyOf` copies only the radiant flag, the Chinese flag, granted tags, `statsOverride`, `armorOverride` and `tuning`. An `exact` rider on `add_to_hand { copyOf }` adds the rest of [[R57]]'s copy of a card on the field, its buffs, granted keywords and Vanilla state, and its price: `costMod`, `costOverride`, the Embiggened version's price and a set X, all of which a card keeps from the hand onto the field and back ([[R78]], [[R766]]). As in [[R57]], damage, exertion, counters and memory start afresh, and so do the Brittle and times-played counts ([[R385]], [[R429]]): "exact" is the card, not what has happened to it on the field. The copy is a fresh card owned and controlled by its caster, whoever's the copied card is ([[R12]]); a face-down card copied shows its face to the caster alone ([[R97]]); a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). Any permanent may be copied: a Unit, a Field Spell or a Trap on either side's field. A price the effect states (`costOverride`, the Radiant Seance's "It costs (0)") sets the copy's price outright, so the copied price is left off and the copy costs exactly that. It edits [[§6.3]] (Add to hand).
