Churnwalker's (S+ #7) Cry chains every Unit of one side for the rest of the game — only the Units
on the field when it resolves are in it, and a card that leaves and returns is unchained. What a
shared-chained Unit was dealt is dealt again to each other member; a hit on a split-chained Unit
is divided among every member, rounded down, each share landing as its own hit. A chain's own hit
never chains again. Rejected: echoing the pre-Armor amount, which would deal what nothing took.
