## R921 — Transform riders and the hand-wide transform

Built the same. Each new transformative verb in the LATER batches is to add its own `costOverride`
and `chinese` riders as it lands; this batch builds no other.

As built: `transform` and `transform_random` carry `costOverride` and `chinese`. `costOverride` is
a price, so it lands only on a replacement in a hand or a deck ([[R766]] takes it off a card that
reaches a graveyard or an exile pile); `chinese` marks a replacement anywhere. With `scope` and
`same: true`, `transform_random` replaces every card of the scope — in place ([[R31]], [[R671]];
an Immutable hand card too, [[R35]]) — with new cards of one shared definition, drawn from the
definitions every old card could take; without them it draws exactly as before. Each held
replacement rolls [[R673]]'s Glitch on its own. An empty scope draws nothing ([[R129]]).
