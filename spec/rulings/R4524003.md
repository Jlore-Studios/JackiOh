A Unit on a carrier stands in its backrow zone ([[R446]]), and `legalActions` asks each zone's own card for its Activates, the carrier, never the Unit it carries, so no carried Unit's ability was ever offered to a player, the AI or the view. The reducer still took one sent by hand: the fuzz's handicapped seed 1186 had a Dormant Starship hull ([[R1852003]]) carried, and its launch, the one ability a Dormant card keeps ([[R1839002]]), was accepted though nothing listed it (I8, [[R384]]). A carried Unit now uses no Activate: the reducer refuses one ("a carried Unit uses no ability") before it looks for the ability, its listing and its view agree, and a Dormant Starship carried waits to step down before it can launch. No game a player or the AI plays changes. Rejected: listing a carried Unit's Activates, which would give carriers' riders abilities no card was written for and the view never showed.
