Meditative+ #13 Living Health Potion ("Activate: Tribute this. Heal your hero and each of your Units 5") heals its controller's hero and each of their Units ([[§6.3]] Heal). This is Hearthstone's "all friendly characters": the hero has no heal cap, each Unit heals up to its max health, and enemy characters are untouched.

The base face's Tribute is paid as it activates ([[R384]]), so the potion leaves before the heal and does not heal itself. The Radiant face has no Tribute, so it stays and heals itself with the rest.
