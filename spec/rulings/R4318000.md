[[R4111000]]'s I18 judged an attack whose sweep killed the Units that held its target up. In fuzz seed 6180 p2's Radiant Stinkyscape+ #107 Fuse attacked Training Grounds #17 Flaming Felinor, shown 5/8 by the auras of two Classic+ #46.1 Felinor Flagbearer Primes. The forecast said the attack dealt 6 and did not kill; Fuse's sweep ([[R3554008]]) killed both Primes, and the Felinor, its auras gone, died for want of health at the next pass of the state check (`destroyed`, no killer). The forecast ([[R3100000]]) foretells the blow against the board as it stands. It does not foretell what the deaths of the attack's other hits (a Cleave's, [[§4.4]] step 10; a sweep's, [[R3554008]]), or of a combatant, take away at the state check after the combat ([[§4.5]]).

So I18 now skips, by two more named conditions: an attack where taking every Unit it strikes off a copy of the board (the combatants included, each against the other) lowers the other combatant's health or takes its Indestructible ([[R69]]); and an attack whose other hits strike a Unit with a card dormant beneath it or held by it ([[R4221002]]'s reason). It reads only the board.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and an invariant that checks it may judge only what it can see.

Rejected: projecting the deaths' cascade in the forecast, since the AI's trade reads are held to agree with it (R4171000), so the AI would have to project it too, and every view computes the forecast for every legal attack (R3477000); judging each removal by an aura source alone, which misses an aura whose size counts Units.

Amended by [[R4379000]] (issue #4379): the forecast now settles the check after the combat, so I18 judges the attacks this ruling skipped. Its two skips are replaced by one: an attack where a unit the settled check takes off has a card dormant beneath it or held by it.
