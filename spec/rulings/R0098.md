id: R98
title: A card that asks a question while it resolves
cards: "#22, #26, #74"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/prompts.rs
---
It is still itself: the resolving zone ([[§10.5]] step 4) is searched like any other, so a continuation resumed after the prompt finds the card as `ctx.self` with its counters, its X and its radiant face, and a step that says "this unit" means the card that asked. A card that has left the resolving zone before its own prompt is answered resumes with no self, and a step that needs anything about the card must have captured it in `resume.data`
