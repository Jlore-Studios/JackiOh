The issue asked that Animated and the card's foil, finish and border layer on each new version, and
that Signature go on none ([[R2949000]]).

**Animated** moves the version's own picture ([[R2715001]]): Extended art's normal picture across
the face, and Pixel's and Comic's restyled ones, whose layers move under their filter. It holds still
under reduced motion and with Animated foil off, and takes a motion slot, as on any face. It is
offered by its own offer, so it layers on a new version wherever both are offered.

**A foil, a finish and a border** wear on each as on the normal face ([[R1295000]]). On Extended art a
foil's sheen covers the picture across the face, and a finish shows through the translucent frame,
which is the panel itself, as [[R2720000]]'s band shows it on a full art. On Pixel and Comic a worn
border keeps its own edge and bezel: the retro and pop-art frames are drawn only on a base face
wearing no border, and the window's ring takes the border's bezel colour.

**Signature** goes on none of the three. It is worn only beside the Full art version and Animated
([[R2949000]]), so its own rule refuses it here and no mechanism of this part's is needed, as in part
2 ([[R2940002]]): a `PUT /api/card-picks` naming it beside a new version is the 400 R2949000 gives,
writing nothing; the Collection marks it waiting until the card wears Full art and Animated; and a
face wearing a new version never signs. No other pairing is ruled out: every new version shows every
other layer, so no `rulesOut` list is added.

Amends [[R2725003]] (the layering on part 3's versions).
