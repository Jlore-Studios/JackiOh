`Evolves when …` is a condition the engine checks, never a new kind of trigger. A standing condition (`Script.evolves_when`) is read at the state check once the board has settled ([[§4.5]], [[R68]]'s order): your hand holding at least N cards that cost (0), all your unit zones full, this below half health (current health times two below max), this Stacked on something (`zones::beneath_at` non-empty). An event condition is the card's own trigger evolving itself: your opponent plays a Unit (`cardPlayed`), this moves from Defense to Attack Position (`positionSwitched`), this survives damage (`damage` on itself while it still acts on the field), a friendly Unit dies (`destroyed`), this destroys a Unit (`destroyed` with `killerId` itself), you launch a Starship (`starshipLaunched`, [[R1852003]]), Start of Turn (`startOfTurn`), and this discarded to Temporary (`discarded` with `byTemporary`, [[R637]]), which the card answers in its graveyard. A fusion drops `evolvesWhen`, as it drops `tributeWhen`. Rejected: reading a standing condition before deaths, which would let a dying Unit evolve and count; and an engine enum of conditions, since ordinary triggers already say each event one.
