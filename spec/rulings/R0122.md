title: Who continues an interrupted sequence

The action that answers a prompt finishes what the prompt interrupted: `answer` re-enters the step its `resume` names and then drains `state.work` in [[R113]]'s order, stopping at the next prompt, which parks its own tail. The state check and the trigger queue stay with the resolution loop, which drains again and finds nothing owed. So a sequence is completed by the action that answered it and never by whatever happens to run next — which matters for any caller driving the engine directly rather than through the reducer, since otherwise a Spell stops short of its graveyard. The engine has one entry point for every answer: a prompt the play pipeline opened for itself — an Echo repeat's fresh pick, a cast's own choices ([[§10.5]] steps 4 and 6) — names that pipeline rather than a card's script, and the entry point hands it on, so a caller answering it directly finishes the repeat and lands the Spell as the `answer` action does
