Tracker #3203 asks for less compute and faster traffic. Every buy or open on `/shop` told the three other panels to read again ([[R1580000]]: "the others read again"), and the route forgot the owned cosmetics each time ([[R1955000]]), so a pack bought and opened cost about seven `GET`s and ten opened in a row about seventy, although the buy's answer already holds the Jlore balance and the unopened count, and the open's answer holds the pity, the unopened count and the bars. The Shop now folds each spend's answer into the panels that show it and reads what no answer carries once, when the opening scene closes. It amends [[R1580000]] and [[R1955000]] and leans on [[R3206000]], whose `net/api.ts` already folds each answer into the kept cache.

**What an answer folds into.** A bought pack's answer (`POST /api/packs/buy`) goes to the wallet (the Jlore) and to the count of its kind in the Shop's shelf and in the opening's held packs (`withBought`). An opened card pack's answer (`POST /api/packs/open`) goes to the Shop's shelf (the count of the kind and the pity, `withOpening`), to the opening's own held packs, and to the token picker's two bars (`withOpeningBars`: each bar's points after the opening and its size). A cosmetic pack's answer (`POST /api/cosmetic-packs/open`) goes to its own panel (the packs held and the Chase Shard bar) and, by its `bars`, to the token picker. A panel shows nothing before the answer, as before: nothing is computed on the client that the server decides, such as the pity, the odds or a balance.

**What is read, and when.** The held tokens, the next step, the cosmetic packs held and the chase choice's inputs are in no card pack's answer (an opening may pay a token or a cosmetic pack, [[R1953000]], [[R1999000]]), so the page reads them once, when the opening scene closes after at least one opening: `GET /api/tokens` and `GET /api/cosmetic-packs`, and `GET /api/collection` with `GET /api/cosmetics` only when the Chase Shard bar then stands full, since only the chase choice prices them ([[R1306002]]). Done, Close and Escape all end the scene; "Next pack" does not, so a batch of ten opens reads once, after the last, and a scene whose opens were all refused reads nothing. The owned cosmetics' ledger of the account (`forgetOwnedCosmetics`, [[R1955000]]) is forgotten once for the scene, not once for each opening, and also when the player leaves the Shop with an opening still unsettled. So a buy sends one spend and no reads, a buy and an open the two spends and, after the scene closes, two reads (three where a viewer reads the ledger again).

**A cosmetic pack's opening** has no scene: its answer folds as above, the ledger is forgotten straight after it ([[R1955000]]), and the picker's held counts, which the answer does not carry, are read once after it. It reads the collection and the cosmetics only when it leaves the bar full. **A chase** changes the ledger and the cosmetic packs panel's own answer and nothing another panel shows, so it forgets the ledger and reads nothing else. The panels keep their `revision` for a read of everything (a mount, "Try again"), and each spend stays one at a time ([[R2378000]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): every number shown is a server answer, either a spend's or a read's; the server still refuses whatever a stale panel asks for. No hidden information is read or kept.

Hearthstone semantics: the shop shows the packs you bought and opened at once, and brings the rest of the account up to date when the pack-opening screen closes.

Rejected: folding the held tokens or a paid cosmetic pack on the client (the server decides when a bar pays and which step it pays, so a client sum would be a second rule); folding the opening's bars into the kept cache (a fold keeps the age of the answer it finds, [[R3206000]], so the held counts would be served as fresh to the Collection); reading the collection and the cosmetics at every close (only a full bar's choice needs them); a debounce on the revisions (it would still read once for a batch and would run on a clock, with a read for a one-pack opening arriving late); and re-reading at every spend, as before.
