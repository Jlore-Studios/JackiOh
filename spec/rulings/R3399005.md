Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. [[R3332000]] held a signature's every cue, over its whole gallery play, within its rarity's and type's ceiling of the play's start, the rows every card's events share included. Under that measure no Legendary Spell could keep its budget, signature or not: its text's events come in entries after its cast, and a shared row there ends past a Legendary Spell's ceiling; nor could a Mythic Spell, whose shared takeover alone ran past three quarters of a Mythic's. A person chose (issue #3399's pull request, 2026-11-08) to count what the signature draws itself.

The budget counts a signature's own cues alone: its entrance, or a Mythic's takeover; its motif or its recipes (in a sweep or a pile's one wave, its own recipe's); its set piece or its Terminus run. The rows every card's events share, a cast's, a combat keyword's beat and a set's system moment are no signature's and count against none. Each own cue ends within its ceiling of the start of the entry it plays in, as the queue holds the play at speed 1 with Big moments full: a Legendary's within `SIGNATURE_LEGENDARY_MAX_MS`, a Mythic's within `SIGNATURE_MYTHIC_MAX_MS`, a Spell's within `SIGNATURE_SPELL_SHARE` of its rarity's. A Mythic's entrance beat, its takeover with its hold, keeps the Mythic's whole budget with no Spell's share, since the takeover is the Mythic's, whatever its type. Shakes stay within their type's, counted over the same own cues, and [[R200]]'s tail, one set piece per action and `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS` ([[R2785000]]) still hold every cue of the play, shared or own.

Rejected: new ceilings for the whole-play measure, set high enough for the shared rows (about 2.2 s for a Legendary Spell), which would loosen every set's signatures to fit rows no signature draws; and holding the Classic+ signatures back until the measure was settled, which would leave the issue's goal waiting.
