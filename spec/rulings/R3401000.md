Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) that every card's art be more its own and richer
in its set's style, simpler for a Common and elaborate for a Mythic, its Spells and Field Spells real scenes; this
issue is Meditative's pass, from a brief per entry, on the foundation [[R3331000]] and [[R3331001]] laid and in the
pattern Classic+'s pass set ([[R3390000]]).

**A recipe per entry.** Every Meditative entry, its 30 tokens included, draws from a recipe of its own
(`meditativeRecipes1.ts` to `meditativeRecipes5.ts`, in id order, spread into `SCENE_RECIPES` after Classic+'s), in
Meditative's family: ink wash, mountains in mist, gardens, scrolls and still water, slate ink on pale paper with jade
washes, diffuse light, a high and distant camera, and the family's brush line on its solid shapes. Each keeps its
brief's subject, setting and idea, its main subject within x 30 to 70 and y 25 to 85, its foreground in the near layer,
its middle in the mid and subject layers and its background in the far and sky layers, and its emblem small (size 8
at most) on its subject inside the band every face's window shows. Each clears its rarity's floor ([[R3331001]]) and
the byte ceiling on both faces: an Epic or rarer draws far, mid, subject and near layers, a Legendary or Mythic every
layer. The Radiant face draws the same shapes repainted toward gold, under the painter's rays, and no base face
paints in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]).

**One red seal.** The art bible's palette is slate ink, pale paper, jade washes "and one red seal": every Meditative
picture stamps exactly one `ink-seal`, a small red chop of abstract strokes with no glyph, in its near layer, unrepainted,
where a painter would stamp a scroll, clear of its subject and inside the shown band.

**Characters.** Each of the set's 43 Units that are a person draws a figure from the figure kit whose look starts
from its tag's people (`figureLookFor`) and is dressed as who it is, no two alike. The 21 that are no person draw
their own part of the scene kit in the subject layer, with no figure: the Rampaging Rhino, the Jlockwork Machine's
clockwork sentry, the Gatling Pea's vine, the Shade-iris, the taotie, Yile's Virus, Devin Bot, the Sentient Cat Ears,
the Montaña Giant, Volatility's jar, Tatches the Totem, the True Sheep, and the nine buildings Jlockeed's Evil
Blueprints raises (an empty plot, a wishing well, a school, a megachurch, a bunker, a university, the Great Wall, a
prison and Jlockeed's headquarters). The people round a subject (monks, villagers, students, crowds) are small brush
figures (`ink-person`), as the family's distant camera sees them, and a rival or companion who matters is a further
figure ([[R3390000]]'s `extras`); a foreground hand is a hand of the kit (`hand-open`, `hand-hold`).

**Props.** The two Meditative full-art cards whose motions move their props ([[R2715001]]), API Key Fishing and
Tatches the Totem, mark the near-layer placements that move with them (`props`, [[R3390000]]); no other Meditative
recipe marks any, and none in the subject layer.

**The kits.** The parts the briefs name join the kits: the figure kit's new headgear, outfits and held
items, and the scene kit's new parts in eight modules beside the first kit and Classic+'s, sharing their drawing
helpers (`kitDraw.ts`): ink-world terrain and architecture (`sceneKitInkPlaces.ts`), buildings
(`sceneKitInkBuildings.ts`), objects (`sceneKitInkObjects.ts`), curios (`sceneKitInkCurios.ts`), machines
(`sceneKitInkMachines.ts`), plants and weather (`sceneKitInkNature.ts`), creatures, people and hands
(`sceneKitInkCreatures.ts`) and the Units' characters (`sceneKitInkCharacters.ts`).

**The ratchet.** Every Meditative entry leaves `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`, and `ART_DETAIL_SHORT_MAX` falls from 289 to 157,
the list's length as this issue left it, so it may only shrink.

The plan wrote the recipes into a `scenes/` folder, the new scenery into `sceneKit.ts` itself and the two full-art
motions' `props` beats into `over`; Classic+'s pass landed first and set the pattern this follows instead: recipe
files beside its own, kit modules beside its own, and marked props. Rejected: characters that are no person drawn as
figures, whose person-shaped anchors fit no rhino or school; crowds as full figures, whose bytes a Mythic cannot
afford and which the family's distant camera does not see; no seal, or one stamped by the painter on every
Meditative card, which would print it on cards with no recipe and on Meditative+, which this issue does not touch;
rewriting the two motions' `props` beats to `over`, which would rewrite [[R2715001]]'s data where [[R3390000]]'s
marked props keep it; keeping the cap at 289, which would let the list grow back by the 132 entries this pass took
off; and four pull requests of about 33 entries each, which the five recipe files map onto should a person want
them split.

Amended by [[R3404000]] (issue #3404): a Meditative card offered Alt art draws an alt scene recipe of its own, its subject in another place, time or moment, at every Alt art level.
