JackiOh sends no player email for now, so the server itself keeps what happened to an account until the account has seen it: one row per event for one account in `public.notifications` (migration 0093), RLS on with no policy and no grant, so only the server reads or writes it. A row names its kind, its actor (the account that did it, a `{profileId, username}` as every social answer names a player), its subject and a payload of the few words it needs. The kinds, each written by the code that already handles its event:

- `dailyMove`: a daily game began waiting on its recipient's move ([[§9.13]]), a hold the action just started, the opponent's move or a prompt's answer handing it over; its subject is the match. The seeker whose open seek another account's seek paired is told the game began on their move. Nobody else is: at a challenge's accept the challenger is told by `dailyAnswered`, which links the board, and not by a `dailyMove` beside it, and the account that moved is never told of its own move.
- `dailyEnded`: a daily game ended, by concession, a draw, a hero's death or on time ([[R3727000]], whose server action ends the game as any other does), told to both players, the conceder included, with the result each sees, `{outcome: win | loss | draw, reason}`, `disconnect` being on time. A game a Glitch voids never happened ([[R679]]) and tells nobody.
- `dailyChallenge`: a friend challenged its recipient to a daily game, `{mode, perMoveDays}`, its subject the challenge.
- `dailyAnswered`: its recipient's daily challenge was accepted, `{answer: "accepted", matchId}`, or declined, `{answer: "declined"}`. A cancel and a lapse tell nobody.
- `friendRequest`: someone asked its recipient to be friends; `friendAccepted`: its recipient's request was accepted, by an accept or by the other side asking back ([[§9.4]]). A declined or cancelled request tells nobody.
- `lobbyInvite`: a friend invited its recipient into their lobby ([[R3730005]]), `{format}`, its subject the lobby's code. It is listed only while the lobby is open, unexpired and unstarted: a read that finds it otherwise deletes it.
- `replayShared`: a friend shared a replay, its subject the match.

A row is written after its event's own commit, in a transaction of its own, so the event never waits on the feed and never fails for it: a write that fails is logged and the row is lost. A new row replaces its recipient's unread row of the same kind, actor and subject, so a game's newer move, a re-sent invite or a re-shared replay never stacks, while a read row stays. A block either way between the actor and the recipient writes nothing, as the social reads hide each from the other ([[R1689000]]); a daily game the block leaves running ([[R3727007]]) goes on telling neither. An account keeps its newest `NOTIFICATIONS_KEEP` (100) rows, the oldest going as each new one is written, and the daily sweep deletes every row older than `NOTIFICATIONS_KEEP_DAYS` (30), read or not. A deleted account takes the rows that tell it and those it is the actor of.

`GET /api/notifications?before=<id>&limit=<n>` answers `{notifications, nextBefore, unread}`: the caller's own rows with an id below `before`, newest first, `limit` of them (`NOTIFICATIONS_PAGE`, 20, by default; 1 to `NOTIFICATIONS_PAGE_MAX`, 50), `nextBefore` the `before` of the page after or null on the last; a `before` or `limit` that is no such whole number is 400. `POST /api/notifications/read` `{upTo?}` marks the caller's unread rows with an id up to `upTo`, inclusive, or every one when it is absent or null, read, and answers `{unread}`; nobody else's row is touched. Both are signed-in routes held to the account's request budget as every other. Each row written goes at once to every `/ws/social` socket of its recipient as `{type: "notification", notification}`, the row as the page lists it, and the social read, `GET /api/social` and its `social` frame, counts the unread rows as `notificationsUnread` beside `dailyWaiting`. `GET /api/daily/settings` answers `emailAvailable`, whether the server sends player email at all.

Rejected: writing each row inside its event's own transaction, where a failed feed write would refuse a move, a challenge or a friend request; replacing only `dailyMove` rows, which would stack a re-sent lobby invite or a re-shared replay; keeping the feed in the server's memory, which a restart or a second machine loses.

Amends [[R3214000]], whose social socket gains the `notification` frame and whose `social` frame gains `notificationsUnread`; [[R3729002]], whose inbox was built from what the client already reads, with no state of the server's; and [[R3730005]], whose lobby invite nothing kept.
