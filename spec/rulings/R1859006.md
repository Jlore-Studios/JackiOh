Stinkyscape #137 Firststrike Capability: "Cry: If you are building a ship, launch it, then Discover a War Doctrine and cast it." (ST-S02-6.) With a Dormant Starship its Cry awakens it, which launches it for nothing and spends no "launch costs (N)" price ([[R1839002]], [[R1852006]]); then it Discovers one of the four War Doctrines, three offered, by id ([[R1857002]]), and casts the pick, free and counted as played ([[R70]]), Radiant on the Radiant face. With no Dormant Starship, nothing: no launch and no Discover. The order is the engine's ([[R113]]): the launch fires the Starship's fused Cries, and its fused When launched triggers wait for the work in hand, the Capability's Cry with its Discover and its cast, so the Doctrine resolves first and its Starship line reads the Starship before its When launched have run.
