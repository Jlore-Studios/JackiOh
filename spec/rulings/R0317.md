[[§2.4]]'s full hand sends a drawn or added card to the graveyard, and `burned` is the event that says so: it is the hand cap's only event, so it is what "hand full" means on the board ([[R318]]). A burned card never enters the hand, and [[R97]] judges it where it is now, the owner's graveyard (or nowhere, a unit-token card having ceased to exist, [[R11]]), so both players read which card burned, and read the `drawn` that brought it too; Hearthstone shows both players a burned card the same way.
