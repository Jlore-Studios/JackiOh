A judged secret is revealed once, and `predicted` is public ([[§10.3]]): Attack beats Greed, Greed beats Defend, Defend beats Attack, and the same choice is no win and no loss. A winning guess removes the secret and its reward — the reward's delayed effect goes with it. On a tie or a loss the reward still lands, and both players now read the choice: the reveal, when the secret was still hidden, comes with the judgement, and the owner's view and the other side's agree from then on.
