[[§13]] did not say what Glitch's reset ([[R676]]), seat swap ([[R677]]) and void ([[R679]]) do in a
co-op game, where a duel is one of two and every seat is paired with a team for the whole game.

- **Reset.** The duel is dealt again and its life totals start over: each of its seats' own HP is its
  team's `own_health` again, unspent, on its team's pool as it stands, since the pool is the team's and
  not the duel's. Its turn count starts over too, so the round no longer judges "the turn moved" by the
  turn's number: the round's turn in a reset duel is over at the reset, and its new first turn is a
  later round's there ([[§13.3]]), however its count runs. Judged by number, a reset whose new turn
  reached the round's own number froze the round, no seat able to act.
- **Swap.** No seat changes hands. The swap counts on the duel (`seatSwaps`), but in a co-op game every
  seat keeps its duel's side and its team ([[§13.1]]), so a person never plays the AI team's side.
- **Void.** The voided duel ends with no winner, and with it the co-op game: a draw, reason `voided`
  ([[§13.4]]), which the client explains as such. A co-op game is unranked ([[§9.12]]), so the server
  records the draw as it records any co-op result.
