Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads it, no state hash moves for it, and a recorded game's replay keeps its final hash. Hearthstone lights a card and plays its sound as a triggered effect of it resolves; [[R655]] gave a card sounds only at [[R204]]'s moments and the pick-up. The card parts of issue #5187 give cards sounds for their triggered effects, so `card-audio.json5` gains six hooks (`CARD_HOOKS` in `apps/web/src/audio/constants.ts`), each taking what [[R655]]'s hooks take, a voice, an effect or both, the effect first, its line's words under the hook's name in the card's `voice` in `crates/cards/card-text.yaml`:

- `startOfTurn`, `endOfTurn`, `whenever` and `activate` sound as [[R2783000]]'s `hookResolving` marks the card's start-of-turn hook (on either player's turn), its end-of-turn hook, one of its Whenever triggers (its condition held) or one of its Activate abilities beginning to resolve, the card named by the marker's `sourceId`. A marker never reaches a seat that could not read the card, and one naming the sentinel plays nothing ([[R203]]). The marker's `cry` and `death` add no hook: `play`, `cast` and `death` already sound at [[R204]]'s moments.
- `hurt` sounds for a Unit that takes damage (a `damage` on it, amount above 0) and is still on the field in the view its action ends in, so a hit that kills plays its `death` alone.
- `hit` sounds for the card a landing `damage` names as its `sourceId`, its Spell's, its effect's or its attack's, read where the seat's views show it, else as this turn's own events last named it (its `cardPlayed`, `summoned`, `trapFired` or marker), never the sentinel.

**When a recurring hook speaks.** These six can fire every turn, or many times in one action. Each play sounds the hook's effect, once per animation entry, and a condensed burst keeps one, as [[R655]] has it. Its line speaks the first time that card's hook fires in a game; after that it speaks at most once a turn per card, across its six hooks, with a chance (`TRIGGER_LINE_CHANCE`, one in three) drawn from the client's own cosmetic rng, never the match's. A first line counts as that card's line for the turn, and a line the flush leaves out still counts. The line has [[R204]]'s lowest priority (`VOICE_PRIORITY.recurring`): it never cuts in on another line, waits briefly behind the one speaking and is dropped if it waits too long. Both seats hear the hooks of a card they can read; `attack` stays the viewer's own pick-up's ([[R655]]).

**Pools on every hook.** [[R1088]]'s list of lines is no longer the trigger's alone: any voiced hook, `play`, `attack`, `death` and `cast` included, may give a list, one line of which the cosmetic rng picks each time it speaks; a hook with one line draws nothing. A pool's first line keeps the key and file a single line has (`<id>-play`), and the others are `<id>-play2`, `<id>-play3` and on, so no shipped file's key changes; a trigger's stay `<id>-trigger1` on. `gen-voice.mjs` renders and checks them.

**Effect sequences.** A hook's `effect` may be a list of the bank's effects, played in order, each `EFFECT_SEQUENCE_GAP_MS` after the one before ends: a wind-up, the release, a tail. The list is guarded as one sound by its first effect (the retrigger window and the cap on sounding effects), and each of its effects takes its own pitch variation.

This amends [[R655]] (its hooks), [[R1088]] (pools on every hook) and [[R204]] (the new lines' priority). Four real cards carry an effect-only hook as proof, one per marked kind: #37 Gravedigger's start of turn, #93 Combo-Index's end of turn, #91 Fed Fauci's Whenever and #33 Unstable Clone Machine's Activate. **Rejected:** keying the four marked hooks off the events the hook's effects make rather than its marker, which would play a card's sound for every effect it resolves into and none for a hook that fizzles, and miss a hook whose effects name no card; a once-a-turn limit per hook rather than per card, which lets a card with several hooks talk over every turn.
