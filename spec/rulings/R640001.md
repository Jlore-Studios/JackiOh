[[§9.5]]'s rating window still widens ±50 rating every 10 s from ±100, but it stops at `MAX_FAIR_GAP` (400) instead of going uncapped after 60 s. 400 is its width at that old 60 s mark, which it reaches at `RATING_WINDOW_FULL_AFTER_SECONDS` (60). Two people further apart than `MAX_FAIR_GAP` never pair, however long they wait; inside it, the mutual window and [[R166]]'s order are unchanged.

Past the gap, the empty-queue fallback ([[R1453]]) is a ticket's way to a game: a ticket of any mode past its fallback time with no person inside its window is seated against the closest free bot ([[R641000]] for Conquest), so a bot is preferred to a mismatched person. A ticket with no bot to fall back on, because `LADDER_BOTS` is off, its player is rated above every bot or no bot is free, waits on, until a person inside the gap queues, a bot comes free or its player leaves.

Rejected: the uncapped window, which paired a player rated 1000 with one rated 2000 after a minute; a cap of 300, the width at 40 s, which would leave more people with no one in reach; and a mismatched pairing as a last resort after some longer wait, which a cap rules out.

**Disclosure.** The terms of service say, in a section of their own: "When no suitable opponent is available, ranked may pair you with an AI opponent." The line names no seat and no match, so it labels no bot ([[R1452]], CLAUDE.md rule 7).
