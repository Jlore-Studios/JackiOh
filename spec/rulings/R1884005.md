[[R1850000]] makes a copier's declared numbers the copied text's: `param` reads `unit_text_of` and the face it copied. M #69 The Maestro's Activate checked its price in its own way, with `param_value` on the running card's definition, so a copier running its text (S #20 Tnack Trav copying a Legendary fusion of the Maestro) looked the price up on Tnack Trav, which declares none, and the legality check panicked as soon as the copier's Activates were listed (the release's handicapped fuzz wave, seed 1087).

So the Maestro's legality check reads its price through `param`, as its effect already did: the copied text's on a copier, and the running ingredient's on a fusion. Nothing else about the Activate changes.
