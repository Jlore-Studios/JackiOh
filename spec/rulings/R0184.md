title: Deck size for a handicapped seat
cards: "§2.6, §9.4 L2, L3, R380"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/handicap.rs

A seat's deck holds exactly its handicap's `deckSize` cards (30 for Medium, 35 for Hard, five and ten past `DECK_SIZE`; 25 and 30 before #1705, [[R1705000]]), an AI's dealt from the non-token cards of every set since patch v0.2.0 ([[R380]]), and still obeys [[§2.6]]'s other rules: no duplicate ids and no Token cards. [[§2.6]]'s 25 and [[§9.4]]'s loadout rules govern a player's loadout. An AI deck is never a loadout, nor is a lesson's human deck of 20 under `TUTORIAL_HUMAN` ([[R1705005]]), so the validator is unchanged and a 30-card deck never reaches a server match
