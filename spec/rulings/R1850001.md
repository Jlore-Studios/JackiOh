"Duo with a permanent and make it Radiant next turn" (`duo_radiant { target }`) marks the permanent it names, on either side and face-down or not, with the public gold `duo` mark ([[R437]]), and at the start of its marker's next turn makes it Radiant (Make Radiant, `radiantSet`), the mark coming off after. It is a delayed effect watching the card's stay, as Allure's steal is ([[R963]]): a card that leaves the field meanwhile is not made Radiant and loses its mark ([[R174]]), and the Make Radiant lands even after the card that made it has left ([[R76]]). The Radiant face's "Squad with all your permanents" (`squad_radiant`) is a Duo of every permanent its controller has on the field then, unit tops in lane order, then the backrow in lane order, a Unit standing there among them; a Unit a carrier holds is not reached, as Allure's walk reaches none. A face-up card Radiant already is passed by. A face-down one is marked whatever face it wears, since which face it wears is what the other seat may not read ([[R177]]), and Make Radiant reports a named card hidden from someone either way.

Both views carry the mark; the client draws it from `viewFor` alone, Allure's aura recoloured gold, with the words "Duo".

Rejected: a mark of Squad's own (the effect is the same); passing face-down cards by, which would tell the other seat nothing more but leave the designer's "a permanent" short of half the board.
