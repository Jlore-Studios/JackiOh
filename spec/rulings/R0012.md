Hand, library, GY, exile always belong to the owner; control matters only on the field. Three things change a card's owner: Pocket Chaos's library swap ([[R73]]), a Bounce of a card its owner does not control ([[R747]]) and, since patch v0.2.0, a steal off the field — a Spell stolen as it is cast (countered, then moved) or a card taken from a hand or a deck ([[§6.3]] Steal) — which puts the card in the thief's hand as theirs, so it goes to the thief's piles from then on and a full hand burns it into the thief's graveyard. A steal on the field changes control only ([[R15]], [[R171]]); a Bounce sends a card to its controller's hand as the controller's own ([[R747]])
