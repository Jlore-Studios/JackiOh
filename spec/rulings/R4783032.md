Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII asked of S+ #142: "Find a way to nerf what you think out of line modes are by ~20%". Each entry was read as the mana it hands its caster for the Call's (4). Most sit near 7: two of the three Paperclip Mythics at (0), about 5 mana of cards, and 2 replenished; three random Jlockeed cards; two (4) Golems; the recursion, the family's average. The Swarm cards (about 2 each), the two GIGA Units (often refused by a crowded board, [[R3570002]]), the afflict entry and the launch watch (nothing without Starships) sit at or under it. Two stand out. Entry 6 hands over S+ #121 Toxopid, a (10) Mythic, for nothing: the largest single result of any Call to Chaos, whose (0)-price entries elsewhere add cards worth about 3 each. Entry 5 replenishes 4, the Call's whole price ([[R364]]), so it is two (4) Terminus Mythics for no mana at all.

Each loses about a fifth of what it gives: the Toxopid costs (2), so its discount drops from 10 to 8, and the Terminus entry replenishes 3, a quarter of its refund, so the Call costs 1 mana net. [[R4154303]] left the Toxopid's price because a nerf would raise it; that is the nerf the designer asked for here. The Paperclip entry keeps its replenish, the smaller part of an entry whose cards are slow engines. The afflict entry keeps Toxic 3, the number issues #3996 and #4154 weighed, where one step is a third. Both new numbers are declared on both faces, `replenish` 3 and `toxopidCost` 2, and read with `param_or`: a definition that declares neither, as every one through v0.6.13h, resolves 4 and (0) as it printed, so a replay needs no kept script ([[R2657006]], [[R767000]]). The clauses `chaosRolled` names ([[R436]], [[R3570000]]) are the engine table's, now "Add 2 random Terminus cards to your hand, replenish 3 mana" and "Add the Toxopid to your hand, it costs (2)".
