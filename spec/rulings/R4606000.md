A replay step that remounts the board and a co-op portal flip are the same match for the music ([[§10.11]], [[R631]]): one replay (1v1 or co-op), or one co-op match, holds one music key for its whole life, and every board mounted under it shares that key's rotation, its wanted track and its resume points. A co-op replay's key lives on the replay player above the remounting board, as a 1v1 replay's does.

A backward replay step, a scrubber jump and a turn jump remount the board, and flipping the portal between the seat's own duel and the teammate's swaps one board for another, but none of them is a new match: the device's rotation counter (`jackioh.music.rotation.v1`) does not move, the wanted in-game track stays the one the match opened on, the station's tracks pick up where they left off, and the match-start sting plays at most once, on the key's first mount. No menu theme is asked for between the two boards: a leaving board hands the music back on a microtask, so a board entering in the same commit claims it first, while a board leaving with no board after it still brings the menu theme back.

A different replay is a different key, and a live, practice, hotseat or dungeon board mounts with no key, so each still moves the rotation on once, exactly as [[R631]] says. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): the client sends no new intent and reads no hidden information.

Rejected:

- Persisting the switched station per key: a remount recomputes the station from the settings, so a mid-match station switch a cast event made is forgotten by the remount. The proving cases hold the station constant; the extra map is not worth the change.
