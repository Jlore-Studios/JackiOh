The paid-for card waits in its player's resolving zone (out of every hand effect's reach) and `cardAnnounced` goes out. Traps answer first, then the triggers the announce woke, each followed by the state check. The first Counter sends the card to its owner's graveyard, to exile, or to the countering player's hand as theirs (their hand cap applies). Later traps stay set and later triggers fizzle. A countered play is never counted, has no Cry, `cardPlayed`, `cardResolved` or Echo, and its mana, Tributes and discards stay spent. A card being set face-down is its player's alone: the opponent sees its zone and cost, and the type "Trap"
