A hit from a source whose damage also Burns N, also applies Toxic N, or whose damage dealt also Burns, adds that counter to a target that survives it ([[§4.4]] step 7, [[R3540002]], [[R4783001]]), and a Burn ticks its whole count at the end of the turn it was put on ([[R4154000]]). The AI read the conversion of step 3a, a hit that turns into Burn or Toxic instead ([[R4064001]]), in every static read of a hit, but none of these riders: a 1-attack Scorch on a 4/4 read as a one-point chip, where its Burn 5 destroys the 4/4 at the turn's end, and Reign's two swings at a hero on 30 read as 24, where their Burn 8 takes the last 8. Issue #4783 moved S+ #151 Volcanic Rounds from the conversion to the rider, so the AI lost what it had read of that card.

**The rule.** The AI decides this; no game changes. The engine gains one public read, `riders_of`: the riders a source's hits carry, each N its declared number ([[R386]]), with `Riders::after(dealt)` the Burn and Toxic one hit that dealt that adds. `ride_damage` applies the same read, so the rule is written once. With `AI_EVAL.damage_rider_read` (read with `pool_and_conversion_read`):

- `blow` reports the riders of a hit that deals something and leaves its target standing, and none after a kill (a Poisonous or exiling one included), a Divine Shield, an Indestructible Unit, a converted hit or one the damage pools soak whole, as the engine adds none.
- `trade_value` prices them on the struck unit as the blow leaves it, its damage taken first, so a Burn that finishes what the hit started reads as the kill it becomes: on the target, on the attacker for the strike back, and on each Cleave splash at the splash's share.
- `face_value` adds the life their ticks take of a hero the hit leaves life (`hero_ticks_added_by`).
- The Taunt walk (`damage_past_taunts_with`, so `face_threat`, `ready_gap` and the walls) adds up the Burn of each hit on the hero that leaves it life and counts it as one more unpierced hit after the attacks, through what the pools have left: the tick at the turn's end. The lethal solver's own gaps leave the tick out (`LETHAL_WALK`): the solver never ends the turn, so it cannot prove a lethal the tick finishes, and a gap that counted it would send it after one.

`GREEDY_EVAL`, the gates' frozen yardstick, keeps the old read.

**What is not read.**

- The Burn the hero already carries, in the walk's tick: after one Reign swing at a hero on 30, the walk reads the second swing and its own Burn 4, a gap of 2, where the engine's tick of Burn 8 kills. The evaluation prices that Burn already (`coming_hero_ticks`).
- Toxic in the walk: it ticks at the hero's own turn start ([[R3540001]]), after the attacks the walk counts. The trades and the face hit price it.
- The riders a Trample excess adds to the hero (§4.4 step 9 sends it on as a hit from the same source), in the trades and the walk alike.
- "Immune to Toxic" ([[R4154001]]): a Toxic rider on such a Unit is priced as if it took.
- `kills_and_survives`, the move order's tier 1: a rider's Burn that destroys the target, or the attacker, at the turn's end is no kill there, nor a death.

**Rejected.** Pricing the counters on the target as it stood before the hit: Burn 6 on an 8/8 hit for 6 would then read as a tick of 6 on 8 health, not the kill it is. Copying the flags' reading into the AI, which is how the AI and the engine drift apart. And counting the tick in the solver's gaps, for the reason above.
