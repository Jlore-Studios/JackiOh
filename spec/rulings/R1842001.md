[[R1204]]'s lethal guard ("Fatal damage a friendly ally would take is redirected to this", a lethal-hit replacement with `redirect: "self"`) answers only from the top of a pile ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]). On top, it catches a lethal hit on its controller's hero or another of their Units through its own Armor. Beneath the top, it catches nothing, not even a hit that kills the card above it, and it guards again once it resumes. A lethal hit on the guard itself is never its own to catch ([[R460]]): it dies, and the card beneath resumes untouched. "A friendly ally" is the hero and the other Units, as for Unan (M #92).
