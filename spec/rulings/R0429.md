#31 deals Fib(times played + 1) damage to a target (Fib(times played + 3) on the Radiant face) and at the end of the turn returns to its owner's hand with its cost 1 higher, never above (4). Times played counts this card's plays, the current one included, on the instance ([[§10.1]]), kept in every zone and through leaving the field, like `costMod` ([[R78]]): so its 1st play deals Fib(2) = 1, its 2nd Fib(3) = 2, its 3rd 3, its 4th 5, its 5th 8, and the index still clamps at 11 ([[R25]]). A cast is a play ([[R70]]) and counts; a countered play is never played ([[§10.5]]) and does not; a copy or a Transform is a new card with a count of its own ([[R57]]). Its cost plays no part in the damage ([[R67]]). The return raises its own cost ([[R65]]: printed plus `costMod`) by 1 but never above (4), so at (4) it adds nothing
