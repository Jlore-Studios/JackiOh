#31 deals Fib(times played + 1) damage to a target (Fib(times played + 3) on the Radiant face) and at the end of the turn returns to its owner's hand with its cost 1 higher, never above (4). Times played counts this card's plays, the current one included, on the instance ([[§10.1]]), kept in every zone and through leaving the field, like `radiant` ([[R78]], [[R766]]): so its 1st play deals Fib(2) = 1, its 2nd Fib(3) = 2, its 3rd 3, its 4th 5, its 5th 8, and the index still clamps at 11 ([[R25]]). A cast is a play ([[R70]]) and counts; a countered play is never played ([[§10.5]]) and does not; a copy or a Transform is a new card with a count of its own ([[R57]]). Its cost plays no part in the damage ([[R67]]). The return raises its own cost by 1 but never above (4), so at (4) it adds nothing. Its own cost there is its printed cost plus the climb its own earlier returns gave it, by the owner's decisions on issues #557 and #572 (2026-10-08): [[R766]] takes the card's `costMod` off in the graveyard it returns from, as it does every card's, but the climb is noted on the card beside its `costMod` in the hand each return puts it in, it carries the note into its next play, [[§10.5]] step 7 notes it again as it lands the card flagged for its return ([[R155]]), and the return gives it back — [[R65]]'s printed cost plus that climb, plus 1, to a maximum of (4), as the card's `costMod` and its climb in the hand it returns to. Any other change to its price is no part of the climb: a discount, a surcharge or a cost step ([[R386]]) it was played at stays in the graveyard with the rest of its `costMod`, so a (3) #31 that never climbed returns at (2), and a climbed (2) one played at (1) less returns at (3) all the same. So the returns price it (2), then (3), then (4), and (4) from then on. A fused #31 ([[R77]]) climbs the same way from its fused definition's printed cost: from (3) its first return reaches (4). Its `costOverride` (#95's (0)) is no part of the climb either and stays in the graveyard ([[R766]]), and a #31 that reached its graveyard any other way (discarded, burned by a full hand, countered) makes no return and keeps no price
