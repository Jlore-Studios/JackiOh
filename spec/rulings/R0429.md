#31 deals Fib(times played + 1) damage to a target (Fib(times played + 3) on the Radiant face) and at the end of the turn returns to its owner's hand with its cost 1 higher, never above (4). Times played counts this card's plays, the current one included, on the instance ([[§10.1]]), kept in every zone and through leaving the field, like `radiant` ([[R78]], [[R766]]): so its 1st play deals Fib(2) = 1, its 2nd Fib(3) = 2, its 3rd 3, its 4th 5, its 5th 8, and the index still clamps at 11 ([[R25]]). A cast is a play ([[R70]]) and counts; a countered play is never played ([[§10.5]]) and does not; a copy or a Transform is a new card with a count of its own ([[R57]]). Its cost plays no part in the damage ([[R67]]). The return raises its own cost by 1 but never above (4), so at (4) or more it adds nothing, and it never lowers a price. Its own cost there is the price it was played at, by the owner's decision on issue #557 (2026-10-08): [[R766]] takes the card's `costMod` off in the graveyard it returns from, as it does every card's, but [[§10.5]] step 7 notes that `costMod` as it lands the card flagged for its return ([[R155]]), and the return gives it back — [[R65]]'s printed cost plus that `costMod`, plus 1, to a maximum of (4), as the card's `costMod` in the hand it returns to. So the returns price it (2), then (3), then (4), and (4) from then on. A fused #31 ([[R77]]) climbs the same way from its fused definition's printed cost: from (3) its first return reaches (4). Its `costOverride` (#95's (0)) is no part of the price it was played at and stays in the graveyard ([[R766]]), and a #31 that reached its graveyard any other way (discarded, burned by a full hand, countered) makes no return and keeps no price
