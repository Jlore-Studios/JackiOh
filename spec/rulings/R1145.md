## R1145 — A card taken from one hand into the other stays in a hand

Built (Meditative #76). A steal off the field from a hand into the other player's hand ([[R12]])
moves the card as a card: a unit-token card taken this way stays in a hand, so it lives on in
theirs — as on C #9's path — until a burn ends it ([[R11]]).
