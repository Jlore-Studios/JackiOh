S #151's seventh entry reads "Launch a ship with 4 Random Starpieces fused into it" (ST-S25-9). "Starpiece" is Starship Piece (ST5 D6).

- **The pieces.** Four picks from the match rng ([[R60]]) of the Starship Piece pool ([[R1878000]]), repeats allowed.
- **The fusion.** Each is a fresh card of its caster's, on its printed base face, fused into its caster's Dormant Starship as a dead piece's copy is ([[R1852000]]); the first summons them one when they have none, into their leftmost open unit zone or on top of their lane-3 pile.
- **The launch.** Then the Starship awakens, which is its launch ([[R1852003]]): it costs nothing, so no launch price is read or spent ([[R1852006]]), its fused Cries fire and then its "When launched" triggers. A Starship buried beneath a Stack pile cannot awaken, so it keeps its pieces and stays Dormant.
