"Your table" ([[R639]], [[R1584000]], [[R1743000]]) showed 35 stats and none about the game's own mechanics. It adds 30, 65 in all, almost every one about one mechanic: Temporary, Brittle, Feng Shui, Tradable, Created cards, kills, Reborn, Divine Shield, Armor, Windfury, Lifesteal, freezes, Flashback, counters, full hands, bounces and Starships. Presentation only, on the device (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no engine, event or endpoint change.

- **What a counter reads.** A counter names a card only where the view shows it, from the viewer's seat, through the sentinel check every counter already makes ([[R97]]). Where it needs what a card was before the batch (its keywords, whether it was in the hand, whether it was Frozen), it reads the view before the batch, as [[R1743000]]'s overkill does, and with no view before it counts nothing. A card's keywords are its keywords in the view, and a hand card that sets none has its catalog face's printed keywords ([[R243]]: a hand card's `keywords` are set only where they differ from its face). A card the viewer's side shows is looked up in the view after the batch, then in the view before, since it may have died in the batch.
- **Storage.** Each of the 29 new per-card counters is stored under the same key and version, read as 0 when missing or not a whole positive number, emptied by Clear and never synced ([[R1142000]]). `biggestKill` keeps the largest value of any game, as `biggestHit` does, and every other new counter is a sum. The log of one game also keeps the Created instances it has counted (`createdIds`) and this turn's attacks by instance id (`attackers`). Neither is ever stored.
- **The counters.** All of them are the viewer's own unless named otherwise.
  - `tempExpired`: a `discarded` of the viewer's card that came after the viewer's `turnEnded` and before any later `turnStarted` in its batch (the cleanup's discard, [[R637]], [[R2179001]]), the card Temporary in the viewer's hand in the view before.
  - `tempPlayed`: the viewer's `cardPlayed` of a card Temporary in their hand in the view before.
  - `crumbled` and `crumbledAgainst`: a `crumbled` of the viewer's card, and of the opponent's ([[R638]]).
  - `fengShuiGood` and `fengShuiBad`: a `fengShui` of the viewer's play, `positive` or `negative`, for the card its `instanceId` names ([[R983]]).
  - `traded`: for each of the viewer's `traded` in a batch, one card of their hand in the view before that was Tradable (`tradable`, [[R2574000]]) and that the view after shows nowhere, in hand order.
  - `tradeDrawn`: the viewer's first `drawn` after each of their `traded`.
  - `created`: each Created card ([[R943]]'s `created`) a view shows on the viewer's side (the hand, the field, a face-up backrow card, the graveyard, exile, what is resolving), once per instance in a game however often it moves or is seen.
  - `kills` and `biggestKill`: a `destroyed` of the opponent's card whose `killerId` names the viewer's card. Each counts a kill, and the victim's `maxHealth` may raise the killer's biggest kill. A crumble is no kill, and neither is a victim the sentinel hides.
  - `reborn`: the viewer's `destroyed` of a Unit that had Reborn in the view before, then their `summoned` of the same definition later in the batch, each summon taking one such death.
  - `shieldsLost`: a `divineShieldLost` on the viewer's Unit.
  - `armorAbsorbed`: on the viewer's Unit, each `damage`'s `absorbed` ([[R1360]]) and each `damageAbsorbed`'s `absorbed` ([[R1361]]).
  - `damageTaken`: the amount of each `damage` aimed at the viewer's Unit.
  - `doubleAttacks`: a viewer Unit's second `attackDeclared` in one turn ([[R636]]). The turn's attackers are kept across the batches of the turn and emptied at each `turnStarted`.
  - `lifesteal`: the first `healed` after a `damage` dealt by the viewer's card that had Lifesteal in the view before. That is its keywords there, or for a Spell in the hand its printed ones. A `healed` names no source, so the heal is the one [[R2648000]]'s heal source credits to that card.
  - `sacrificed`: a death of the viewer's card that [[R2648000]] counts for neither seat because the viewer gave it up. Its killer is not the opponent's card and the opponent was not the last to act before it. The viewer's side is its killer, or is the batch's actor: the last before the death, or, when no actor came before it, the first after it, since a Tribute's death comes before its play ([[R119]]). A crumble is never counted, and neither is a death with no actor and no killer at all.
  - `froze`: a freeze (`marked`, the `frozen` mark, `added: true`, [[R1174000]]) landing on a Unit the opponent's side shows. It counts for the viewer's card that [[R2648000]]'s source rule names at that point of the batch: the last play, activation, Trap, attack or damage source. None, or one of the opponent's, counts nothing.
  - `frozen`: a freeze landing on the viewer's Unit.
  - `thawed`: a thaw (`added: false`) of the viewer's Unit that the view after still shows on the field, so a death is never a thaw.
  - `shattered`: a `destroyed` of the opponent's Unit that was Frozen (`UnitView.frozen`) in the view before. It counts for the killer as `kills` does, or, with no `killerId`, for the viewer's card that acted last before it in the batch.
  - `flashback`: the viewer's `cardPlayed` with `from` the graveyard ([[R783012]]).
  - `manaSaved`: for the viewer's `cardPlayed`, the card's printed cost less `costPaid`, never below 0. The printed cost is the one out of play, an X card's 0 and an embiggen card's its base. A play with `x`, `embiggened` or `onDraw` is skipped, and so is a card the catalog does not hold.
  - `countered`: the viewer's card in a `countered` whose `player` is the viewer.
  - `counterspells`: a `countered` of the opponent's play whose `byInstanceId` names the viewer's card.
  - `burned`: the viewer's card in a `burned`, a full hand ([[R4]]).
  - `bounced`: the viewer's card in a `bounced`.
  - `launched`: each entry of `pieces` in the viewer's `starshipLaunched` ([[R1852003]]).
- **The stats.** Each takes its card by [[R1743000]]'s rules. A token is never a stat's card, though every counter counts tokens. A tie goes to the lower id. A share needs `SHOWCASE_MIN_PLAYS` of its whole, and its part is capped at the whole. A stat the device has no data for is left out. A line whose number is 1 says it in the singular where it has a word to bend.
  - Use It or Lose It (`tempExpired`), "slipped away unplayed 3×", `expire`.
  - Just in Time: the highest `tempPlayed` per `tempPlayed + tempExpired`, "played 4 of the 5 times it was Temporary", `inTime`.
  - Sand Castle (`crumbled`), "crumbled 3×", `crumble`.
  - Outlasted (`crumbledAgainst`), "waited it out 2×", `crumble` on the opponent's side.
  - Good Feng Shui (`fengShuiGood`), "blessed by Feng Shui 2×", `radiant`.
  - Bad Feng Shui (`fengShuiBad`), "cursed by Feng Shui 1×", `hit`. Its Brittle 2 is not a last count, so it wears no aura.
  - Hot Potato (`traded`), "traded away 3×", `bounce`.
  - Trade-In Value (`tradeDrawn`), "pulled from a trade 3×", `entrance`.
  - Mass Produced (`created`), "created 5×", `stamp`.
  - Executioner (`kills`), "took down 11 enemies" ("1 enemy"), `lunge`.
  - Giant Slayer (`biggestKill`), "felled a 9-health giant", `lunge`.
  - Second Wind (`reborn`), "rose again 2×", `reborn`.
  - Bubble Wrap (`shieldsLost`), "shield popped 4×", `shield`.
  - Iron Wall (`armorAbsorbed`), "shrugged off 13 damage", `armor`.
  - Punching Bag (`damageTaken`), "took 40 damage", `hit`.
  - Double Tap (`doubleAttacks`), "attacked twice in a turn 3×", `windfury`.
  - Vampire (`lifesteal`), "drained 18 health", `heal`.
  - Martyr (`sacrificed`), "gave itself up 2×", `doom`.
  - Cold Snap (`froze`), "froze 4 enemies" ("1 enemy"), `lunge`. The card acts, since it did the freezing.
  - Brain Freeze (`frozen`), "frozen 3×", `freeze`.
  - Spring Thaw (`thawed`), "thawed out 2×", `thaw`.
  - Shatter (`shattered`), "shattered 2 frozen enemies" ("1 frozen enemy"), `lunge`.
  - Encore (`flashback`), "played from the graveyard 2×", `reborn`.
  - Bargain Hunter (`manaSaved`), "saved 17 mana", `radiant`.
  - Denied (`countered`), "countered 2×", `exile`.
  - Nope (`counterspells`), "stopped 3 plays cold" ("1 play cold"), `trap`, or `cast` when the card is a Spell or a Field Spell.
  - Burnt Toast (`burned`), "burned from a full hand 1×", `death`.
  - Frequent Flyer (`bounced`), "bounced back to hand 2×", `bounce`.
  - Liftoff (`launched`), "flew in 2 launched Starships" ("1 launched Starship"), `launch`.
  - Brick: the highest `drawn - played` above 0, of a card with `drawn` of at least `SHOWCASE_MIN_PLAYS`, "drawn 19×, played 12×", `exile`.

  Each lunge fits the card's type as [[R2648000]] says: `cast` on a Spell and `trap` on a Trap. The bag ([[R1743000]]) takes the new keys as it takes the others.
- **The stage's new animations.** Each one hands the board's planner the board event its stat stands for, with the stage's card on the field as its target, as [[R1584000]] plays the others. There is no new keyframe, effect recipe or cue.
  - `crumble` is `crumbled` (`jk-dissolve`, death).
  - `expire` and `exile` are `exiled` (`jk-exile-fade`, void). The board's `discarded` aims at a hand, and the stage has none.
  - `inTime` is `cast`'s `cardPlayed` (`jk-card-played`, cast).
  - `doom` is Classic #20's `marked` `"destroy"` in red ([[R437]]), then `destroyed`: Allure's two-beat shape.
  - `stamp` is `summoned` three times.
  - `bounce` is `bounced` (`jk-bounce-to-hand`, bounce).
  - `hit` is a `damage`, `heal` a `healed` and `armor` a `damageAbsorbed`, each aimed at the card.
  - `reborn` is `destroyed`, then `summoned`.
  - `windfury` is `attackDeclared` twice.
  - `freeze` is the `frozen` mark added (frost, brand), and `thaw` is the same mark added and then lifted.
  - `launch` is `starshipLaunched` (`jk-radiant-pulse`). The board's brand recipe draws a mark's sigil, and a launch carries no mark, so the board plays the keyframe alone and so does the stage.

  `crumble`, `expire`, `exile`, `doom` and `bounce` bring the card back, as `death` does. `reborn`'s summon brings it back itself.
- **A warning before the consequence.** On the board, a card on the field at its last Brittle count wears Allure's aura in gray (`BRITTLE_LAST_COLOR`, issue #2270), and a hand card at its last Temporary count wears it in fuchsia (`TEMPORARY_LAST_COLOR`, issue #2437). Both come from the card's state, not a `marked` event. A stage script gains `aura`: gray for `crumble`, fuchsia for `expire` and `inTime`. The stage draws that aura on its card (the board's `CardMarks`, `bareAura`) from the card's way in until the first beat plays. The first beat then waits one `SHOWCASE_BEAT_GAP_MS` after the way in, so the warning reads first, and the aura is gone as the beat plays. Under reduced motion the still card wears the aura and nothing plays. Each step of a script draws the card afresh, so a beat played twice in a row (`stamp`, `windfury`, `thaw`) starts its keyframe again.

Choices a person may want to review, each with the alternative rejected:

- **Hot Potato.** The issue's reading was the `shuffledIn` after the viewer's `traded` whose `instanceId` the view before showed in their hand. A card shuffled into a library reads to no one, its trader included (`view_for.rs`'s `may_read`, a library card; [[R1838001]]), so that `shuffledIn` carries the sentinel and that reading would never count. It is read instead from the hand: a Tradable card the view before showed there and the view after shows nowhere. Rejected: the issue's reading.
- **Trade-In Value.** Only the first `drawn` after each trade counts. Rejected: every later draw of the batch, which would credit a card another effect drew.
- **Use It or Lose It.** Only a discard after the viewer's own `turnEnded` and before a later `turnStarted` counts. Rejected: any discard in a batch that holds a `turnEnded`, which would also credit a discard of the next turn in the same batch. A cleanup parked behind a prompt sends its discard in a later batch, which holds no `turnEnded`, so that discard is not counted. Rejected: remembering the turn's end across batches.
- **Martyr.** A death waits for the batch's first actor when none came before it, so a Tribute counts ([[R119]]). Rejected: only the actor before it, which misses every Tribute. A death with no actor and no killer still counts for nobody, as in [[R2648000]], and `countDeaths` is unchanged.
- **Executioner, Giant Slayer and Shatter.** A crumble and a hidden victim count nothing. Shatter credits nothing when the `killerId` is the opponent's own card. Rejected: falling back to the viewer's last actor there, which would credit a play that did not kill.
- **Vampire.** Lifesteal is read in the view before, and only the first heal after the hit is drained. Rejected: every heal until the source changes, which would credit a separate heal effect.
- **Mass Produced.** It counts by instance id, so a face-down Trap set again, which takes a fresh id ([[R227]]), counts again. It credits the seat whose side shows the card, the one the card ended up with. Rejected: [[R2089000]]'s `creator`, which no view forwards. The Coin (`core-t-coin`) is a token in the catalog, so it never wins the stat, as no token does. Rejected: exempting it, which the issue asked for ("The Coin is a card the catalog holds, so it can win") on the premise that it is not a token; a person may want it exempted.
- **Second Wind** matches the summon by definition, as the issue says. A Reborn body keeps its instance id, so matching by id would read the same events.
- **Bargain Hunter.** The printed cost is the cost out of play (`printedCost`), so a free cast by an effect saves its whole cost.
- **Liftoff** counts each entry of `pieces`, so two copies of one piece in one Starship count 2.
- **Nope** plays `trap` on any card that is not a Spell or a Field Spell. Rejected: a lunge on a Unit.
- **The aura's timing.** The first beat waits one `SHOWCASE_BEAT_GAP_MS` more. Rejected: showing the aura only over the card's way in, which is over before a player has read it. The card is drawn afresh each step. Rejected: restarting a keyframe by toggling its attribute.
