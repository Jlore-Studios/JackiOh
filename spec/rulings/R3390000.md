Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) that every card's art be more its own and richer
in its set's style, simpler for a Common and elaborate for a Mythic, its Spells and Field Spells real scenes; this
issue is Classic+'s pass, from a brief per entry, on the foundation [[R3331000]] and [[R3331001]] laid.

**A recipe per entry.** Every Classic+ entry, its 38 tokens included, draws from a recipe of its own
(`classicPlusRecipes1.ts` to `classicPlusRecipes3.ts`, in id order, spread into `SCENE_RECIPES` after the five
proofs), in Classic's family: temple stone, the slates and the woods, low light from the left or a moon, slate ink
on its solid shapes. Each keeps its brief's subject, setting and idea, its subject within x 30 to 70 and y 25 to
85, its foreground in the near layer, its middle in the mid and subject layers and its background in the far and
sky layers, and its emblem small (size 8 at most) inside the band every face's window shows. Each clears its
rarity's floor ([[R3331001]]): an Epic or rarer draws far, mid, subject and near layers, a Legendary or Mythic
every layer. The Radiant face draws the same shapes repainted toward gold, under the painter's rays, and no base
face paints in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]).

**Characters.** Each of the set's thirty humanoid Units draws a figure from the figure kit whose look starts from
its tag's people (`figureLookFor`) and is dressed as who it is, no two alike: Mommy Barker in a kerchief and an
apron with a wooden spoon, Wardrum a stone golem whose chest is a war drum, Brother Lar a tonsured monk. Four
Units are no person and draw a creature or a machine of the scene kit as their subject, with no figure: the
Second Amendment Snake, the Book Worm, Jlockeed's J15 Fighter and the Mulch Muncher.

**Extra figures and props.** A recipe may stand further figures (`extras`) in any layer, each drawn in front of
that layer's own parts and after the main figure in the subject layer, so crowds, companions and rivals are
figures: League of Losers' five, the cat warriors behind the Flagbearer, KY's students, Mommy Barker's
apprentices. A recipe with none draws exactly what it drew before. A placement may be marked `props`: its shapes
move on the props beat ([[R2715001]]), as a painter's glyphs and accent do, which a scene drops, so the seven
Classic+ full-art cards whose motions move their props (League of Losers, Tommy Tempo, Felinor Flagbearer,
Jlockeed's Lobbyist, KY's Papaya, Buff Billy, J-lease J-Jungle EX-plorer) still move them, and The Mother
Pancake's moves its near layer; a props placement
never stands in the subject layer, which Out of frame lifts out as the subject.

**The kits.** The parts the briefs name join the kits: the figure kit's new poses, heads, headgear, outfits and
held items, and the scene kit's new terrain, buildings, furniture, objects, creatures, food and weather, the
latter in three modules beside the first kit (`sceneKitPlaces.ts`, `sceneKitObjects.ts`, `sceneKitNature.ts`)
that share its drawing helpers (`kitDraw.ts`).

**The ratchet.** Every Classic+ entry leaves `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`, and `ART_DETAIL_SHORT_MAX` falls from 561 to
445, the list's length as this issue left it, so it may only shrink.

Rejected: crowds as silhouettes, which would lose League of Losers' five Losers each in their own lane; creature
bodies in the figure kit, whose pose limbs fit no snake or boar; dropping `props` from seven full-art motions,
which would rewrite [[R2715001]]'s data; keeping the cap at 561, which would let the list grow back by the 116
entries this pass took off; and three pull requests of about 40 entries each, which the three recipe files map
onto should a person want them split.

Amended by [[R3396000]] (issue #3396): a Classic+ card offered Alt art draws an alt scene recipe of its own, its subject in another place, time or moment, at every Alt art level.
