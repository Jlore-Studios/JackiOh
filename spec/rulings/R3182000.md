[[R2783000]]'s `hookResolving` marker named a hook that runs with no card as its `self` by its definition alone, with `sourceId` `""`: a Cry triggered out of a graveyard ([[R467]], [[R127]]), or an Activate whose card its own cost took out of the game (M+ #11 Living Mana Potion as a token or a fused card, [[R11]]). The view judges a marker by its `sourceId`, and an empty one by nothing, so the marker kept naming the card after every other event about it was hidden ([[R97]]). C #54 Rewind on C #47 Recurring Felinor in its graveyard showed it: Felinor's Cry casts C #34 Ancient Acquisition, which returns Felinor to its owner's hand, where the other seat reads Felinor's `addedToHand` as the sentinel and its marker as `classic-047`. The fuzz's I6 caught it on forced-card waves (issue #3182).

**The card is named.** The marker's `sourceId` is the card the hook belongs to wherever one exists: the graveyard Unit whose Cry was triggered, and the card an Activate's resume names. The hook itself still finds nothing as "this" ([[R467]]); only the marker carries the id, and nothing stores it, so no state or hash moves. The view judges the marker by that card now, as it judges the card's other events: a card back in a hand shows the other seat the sentinel, and a card gone from the game whose events were public stays public. The seats that could not read the card where it stood as the hook began are recorded on the marker, as for a hook with a card ([[R177]]), and never see it.

**Still `""`.** Only a hook no card holds at all, such as a random cast's Spell made from nothing, keeps `sourceId` `""`, as `rolled` does ([[R2652001]]). The client already treats `sourceId` as an opaque id, so a graveyard card's id anchors no animation on the board.
