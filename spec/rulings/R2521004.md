S #163 Swear Loyalities reads, since the first Stinkyscape balance pass (issue #2521), "Swear Allegiance to a faction: add its Faction card to your hand, then exile every card in your hand and deck that belongs to another faction". A faction is its Faction card and the cards that card adds: ETHICS is S #159 and S #110, #111, #109, #20, #37 and #6; Vanguard Coalition is S #95 and S #115, #96, #6, #103, #87 and #88; Golden Accord is S #160 and S #72, #73, #74 and #69; Pegasus is S #161 and S #113, #94, #123 and #114; Star-Jumper Core is S #162 and S #75, #76, #78, #79 and #80. The choice of the five is the declared mode it was ([[R1880002]]); the chosen faction's Faction card is added, Radiant on the Radiant face, a full hand burning it ([[§2.4]]).

Then a card in its caster's hand or library is exiled ([[§6.3]] Exile) when its definition is in another faction's list and not in the chosen one's, base or Radiant alike. That is the owner's narrower reading of the issue's "doesn't belong to that faction": a card of no faction belongs to none, so it is never exiled, where the wider reading would have exiled every card outside the chosen faction. S #6 Tylannt, in both ETHICS and Vanguard Coalition, belongs to the chosen faction when either is chosen and stays, and is exiled when any of the other three is. The piles are read once the Faction card is added, so it and any other copy of the chosen faction's cards stay; they are walked library first, then hand, each card its own exile, in Core #94 Genn's Greed's order ([[R135]]).

"Your hand and deck" are the only piles read. This narrows [[R1880001]]'s "wherever it is": its caster's graveyard is no longer reached, and a burned Faction card stays there. The field is not read, so a card of another faction on it stays, and the opponent's cards stay wherever they are, as before.
