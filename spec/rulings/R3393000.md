Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden.
The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) that every card's picture be more its own and richer in its set's style,
simpler for small cards and elaborate for Mythics, with Spells and Field Spells drawn as real scenes, and issue
#3393 gave every Meditative+ and Training Grounds entry a brief: its subject, its setting and its light.

**Every entry a recipe.** Each of the 33 Meditative+ entries (#1–#26 and their seven tokens) and the 41 Training
Grounds entries of its own (#1–#40 and the Golem token, #39-1) draws a scene recipe of its own
([[R3331000]]), drawn from its brief, on both faces with one geometry: Meditative+ in the Meditative family's
ink wash (layered peaks in mist behind, a high and distant camera, one red seal in the near layer), Training
Grounds in carved wood (the yard's palisade and sand wherever its brief is set in the yard, the sun from the
upper right but at night). A brief is the target, not a script: where the kit drew a detail another way, the
subject, the setting and the card's idea stay. Each clears its rarity's floor ([[R3331001]]) and is off
`ART_DETAIL_SHORT`; an Epic or rarer uses the far, middle and near layers, a Legendary or Mythic all seven.
The Training Grounds painter ([[R2202000]]) hands every such card to the scene, as it handed the proofs, and
draws only a card with no recipe; its golden record before #2724 now holds every Training Grounds card of its
own to a new picture ([[R3331002]]). No other card's picture moves ([[R503]]): the five proof cards draw what
they drew before, byte for byte.

**Units are characters.** Every Unit's recipe draws a figure from the figure kit in a look built from its
tags' with the brief's own parts, never the generic hooded traveller and never the painter's own subject;
a Unit that is a thing is built from figure parts too (the living potions a flask's body with eyes on
stubby legs, the totem, Floodgates and the Training Dummy on a post, the Golem of lashed posts with a sack
head). The parts the briefs named that the kits lacked joined them: the scene kit's peaks, stairs, tents,
pavilions, the yard's palisade, barracks, lanes, posters, dummies, dice and the like, and the figure kit's
new poses, heads, headgear, outfits and items.

**Crowds and fades.** A recipe may draw other characters, each a crowd figure standing at the end of its own
layer (the subject layer's unless it names one), before the recipe's figure; and a placement or a figure may
fade, its shapes' opacity scaled, for a ghost, a copy or a recruit flickering into being. A recipe that uses
neither draws byte for byte as before. Each depth plane ([[R3344000]]) holds a scene's layers by role, as it
holds every scene's.

Rejected: a painter per card, which would put back what the recipes replaced; kit files per set, which would
close a module cycle with the kit's helpers and make one more shared surface; keeping the yard painter for
the cards already over their floor, which would leave two looks in one set; and drawing extra characters as
scenery parts, which would leave them outside the figure kit that makes a character read as who it is.
