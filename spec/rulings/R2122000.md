title: "On a phone every control takes a tap across 44x44 px, and neighbours split the gap (issue #2122)"
cards: "R1581000, issue #2036, issue #2122"
proven_in:
  - e2e/cypress/e2e/47-tap-areas.cy.ts
---
On a phone (`(pointer: coarse), (max-width: 600px)`), every interactive element (`button`,
`a[href]`, `[role="button"]`, `input`, `select`, `[tabindex="0"]`) smaller than 44x44 CSS px gets a
hit area of at least that size. The minimum is one custom property, `--hit-min` in `index.css`,
and one constant, `HIT_MIN_PX` in e2e spec 47.

A control shorter than the minimum takes the tap through a transparent, absolutely positioned
`::before` centred on it, so nothing in the layout moves. A small input or select draws no
`::before` and takes the height itself (`min-height: var(--hit-min)` in its own stylesheet), and a
checkbox or a radio counts its labels as part of its box and its hit area.

Where two neighbours' hit areas would overlap, they split the gap: two hit areas meet halfway
between neighbours, so none covers another control. The deck builder's cost chips reach the middle
of their shared gap, a sortable statistics header's band stops at its first row, and a control on
a card stops short of the card's middle (the switch, the Activate badge), keeping B29's reading
that the middle of a tile is the card's own tap.

Exempt: a link inside a paragraph's or a label's sentence, a card (whose box is already larger),
and controls under an open modal's scrim (outside the topmost `aria-modal="true"`).

Hearthstone semantics do not reach page tap areas; this follows WCAG 2.5.5 (target size) and B29's
touch sizing. **Proof.** Spec 47 visits the practice board and every menu of [[R1581000]]'s list at
390x844, measures each control's hit box, and fails below the minimum in either dimension, naming
the control's testid. It runs on every pull request (`e2e/scripts/shard-specs.mjs`'s `SMOKE`).

Rejected: a rule per small control, which leaves the controls no spec sees still small; a
`min-height` on every input and select app-wide, which moves layouts no spec measures (the chat
panel, the prompts, the replay toolbar); and full-size hit areas through a neighbour, which hand
one control's tap to another.
