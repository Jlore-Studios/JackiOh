IA #63 Nitrous Injector's "Give your other Units +N Attack and Rush this turn" is a layer-4 Attack gain and granted keywords held on the instance (`CardInstance.turnBuff`) with the turn they were given, read only while that turn is the running one ([[§10.4]]). It holds through the turn given and stacks within it: two gifts in one turn add, and a gift on a later turn replaces the older one rather than adding to it. It is gone at the next turn. [[R78]]'s reset clears it with the card leaving the field; a copy keeps it ([[R57]]); and a Vanilla takes its Attack while its keywords stay ([[R781003]]).
