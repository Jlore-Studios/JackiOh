The opponent's reply ([[§9.9]]) swings with its board by static trade value, one hit at a time. Two attackers whose hits only together reach the AI's hero therefore saw no lethal: each hit on the face was worth its damage, a kill outranked it, and the reply spent an attacker on that kill and left the AI alive. The AI planned against an opponent that misses a lethal any player takes, and over-ranked lines that leave its hero in reach of two swings (issue #1634: at 8 health with Mr. Vanilla against two 7/7s, it swung at the face and died).

**The rule.** At each attack step the reply reads the board once: when `lethal::ready_gap` says the attacks it has left reach the AI's hero together past its Taunts, every attack on that hero is worth `AI_EVAL.win` (`face_value`), so the face goes first and no attacker is spent on a kill. Short of that, every hit is scored one at a time, as before. Only the reply's own attack step reads it; the Deft switch's look at the attack after it, and the seat's own move order, keep their own reads.

**Its value.** It ships on, with no switch. Behind `AI_REPLY.open_lethal` it was screened three times on the improve lane's dev set (training/improve.md, A screen), each inconclusive ([[R4319000]]): #1634's, 203-197, edge 0.015 (-0.002 to 0.032), 3 decided of 200 (won 3, lost 0); #4246's, against the control at baa7cdb93f, 200-200, edge 0.0 (0.0 to 0.0), 0 decided of 200, 21 diverged; and #4931's, on the reply since [[R4187000]]'s walls and R4810000's close steps, against the control at 328135b4ca, 198-202, edge -0.010 (-0.024 to 0.004), 2 decided of 200 (won 0, lost 2), 11 diverged, every game clean. An inconclusive screen neither keeps nor drops a change and leaves it to the scene tests, and those show the misplay it fixes, so the maintainer shipped it on issue #5027, with its switch, `simulate_reply_with` and the tests that pinned the one-hit reply removed. The code before it is at commit `798bc44a`.

Rejected: keeping the switch off for a fourth screen (its config comment barred one until the reply changes, and three read nothing either way); removing the open lethal at off (it leaves a misplay the scene tests show).

Hearthstone semantics: a player with lethal on board takes it. It adds no rule of the game and no constant.
