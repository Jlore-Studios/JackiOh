A target declaration may be marked as needed (`TargetDecl.required`). While the board offers fewer legal options for it than its minimum, the play is refused and `legalActions` never offers it, as Hearthstone keeps a spell that needs a target in hand when nothing can be targeted; every other declaration still follows [[R90]], playing and fizzling. The refusal and the listing are one check (`playChoices.ts`), so a client never offers a play the reducer refuses. A cast is never refused ([[R70]]), so a cast of such a card with nothing to target fizzles as before, and an Echo repeat or random cast skips the pick. #63 Plastic Surgery's target is needed on both faces (patch v0.2.7): with no Unit on either side it cannot be played
