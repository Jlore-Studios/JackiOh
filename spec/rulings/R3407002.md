Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). [[R2797000]] opens a card's hook entry (a Death, a turn hook, a Whenever trigger, an Activate) at its `hookResolving` marker ([[R2783000]]), so the recipes and motifs keyed on that hook draw on the events after it. The marker's row lasts 0 ms, and the runner used to skip an entry of no duration whose row had none either without a signal, so the effects layer, which remembers an entry only on its `start` or `still` ([[R2789000]]), never saw the marker and no hook entry opened on the live board: every beat keyed on a hook drew in the tests, which remembered each entry, and never in a game.

The runner now signals `pass` for each such entry (a hook's marker, an emote, the game's end), in order and at once, holding nothing and scheduling nothing, under any motion setting. The effects layer remembers its events, as it does a playing entry's, and plans nothing for it; the card showcase ignores it, as it ignores `still`. So a beat is never keyed on the marker itself, which draws nothing: [[R3407000]]'s IN33DH3 dissolves on his Death's shuffle, and the Wardrum Blob drums twice on the cast its turn hook announces. The signature passes' planning kit (`test/signaturePass.ts`) remembers a zero-length entry and plans no cue for it, as the board does. Rejected: giving the marker's row a duration, which would hold every hook's resolution by that much; folding the marker into the entry after it, which would change what every set piece and wipe run claims.
