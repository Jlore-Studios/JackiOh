In Too Deep's reward J, "gain 100 mana", is next-turn mana: a +100 rider on its controller's next mana refresh ([[§2.3]], [[R364]]'s next-turn mana), not mana for the turn it is granted. Quest 7, the only quest that leads to J, completes as its controller's turn ends (end a turn with 5 or more unspent mana), so mana for that turn would lapse unused; the reward has its intended effect on the next turn. [[§8.6]]'s row 90 reads "J, gain 100 mana" and is read this way
