A `target` prompt that offers cards or heroes is answered on the board as well as in its list: a
click on an offered card or hero, or on the zone of an offered face-down card (whose option names
its zone per [[R821000]]), picks that option — at once for a prompt of one pick, into the picker's
selection for a prompt of several, whose Confirm still sends. The list and its "Highlighted on the
board as well." note stay, since [[R3864000]] kept target lists on purpose; only a choice of one
board zone draws no list. Anything the prompt does not offer, another prompt kind, the other seat's
prompt and a replay answer nothing from a board click, and a click on an offered own unit shows no
attack notice. The answer sent is always one the engine lists. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7):
no engine, `PendingChoice` or action changes.

Rejected: drawing no list for a one-pick target prompt, as [[R3864000]] does for a single zone,
since [[R3864000]] kept target lists on purpose.
