The random keyword pool ([[R21]]) is the designer's combat keyword pool since patch v0.3.7: Taunt, Armor 1, Rush, Charge, First Strike, Flying, Ranged, Poisonous, Reborn, Divine Shield, Windfury, Cleave, Indestructible, Deft, Spell Damage +1, Lifesteal, Pierce and Trample, in that order. Flying, Ranged, Indestructible and Spell Damage +1 join, reversing R21's word on Indestructible; the order picks which keyword a seeded draw returns, so it moved the golden traces and the seed-pinned tests that rolled a keyword. "Combat keyword" is a word players read: any keyword of the pool, a term of [[§6.1]] whose glossary entry lists the eighteen. Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao and the Conjure Rush Tokens (+, ++) say "random combat keyword(s)" where they said "random keyword(s)", which is text alone (ten faces), and Keymaster Keenus's Aura draws combat keywords ([[R781004]]). Spell Damage +1 is numbered like Armor 1, so a unit with Spell Damage from any source is not offered it. Indestructible makes [[R347]] matter to a random grant: no draw offers Taunt to an Indestructible card, and a Taunt drawn before an Indestructible is held down by it. Immutable, Untributable, Stack, Lucky, Equip, Temporary, Cloaked, Crater and Flashback stay out, and so does every harmful keyword.
