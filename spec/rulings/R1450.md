A room or a reserved match id still `open` `OPEN_MATCH_REAP_GRACE_SECONDS` (300) past its `ceiling_at` is deleted by the reaper on its own cadence ([[R108]]). A room's `ceiling_at` is its expiry, claimed or not; a queue pairing's reservation's is the moment its tickets were claimed. The delete frees the room's code ([[R110]]) and lets go of every in-match flag and ticket link that pointed at the row, so a start that failed, and whose own cleanup failed too, cannot hold both players out of the queue and the rooms for ever, and a room that expired unjoined does not stay behind. A live or finished match is never touched. The grace is longer than any start in flight: a room can be claimed only before it expires, a reservation is started straight after its claim, and a Conquest series' first game starts or is given up within [[R333]]'s pick clock and [[R263]]'s give-up, three minutes in all.
