The owner's pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #3000, its part 8 issue #3045). The engine's Fuse ([[R77]]) unions the ingredients' tags and joins their names, and the client drew a card's art and name from the id, name and tags it was handed. So a Unit with a Miner or a Weapon equipped wore a different picture, and a Dormant Starship that had taken in pieces was named for all of them. Both are how a card looks, and neither is a rule: the engine's definition keeps its joined name and its union of tags, since a rule can read them, and nothing in `crates/` changes ([[R2529000]]). The client draws from the definition the view carries beside the card ([[R243]]), never from the engine's rules ([[§10.10]]).

- **A fused Miner or Weapon keeps its host's art.** A fused definition's ingredients ([[R468]], `CardDef.ingredients`) hold gear when one is a Stinkyscape Miner (Miner v1 to v5, Stinkyscape #62 to #66, which print no Weapon tag) or carries the Weapon tag. Its art is then that of the host: the last ingredient that is neither, since an Equip is fused onto its host as `[equip, host]` and the host is the card the Fuse keeps. `<CardArt>` draws it from the host's id, name and tags, so the delivered file, the Stinkyscape and Legacy checks, the seed (whichever picture the card is shown in) and the theme are the host's, whatever tribe the gear belongs to, and a card's art stays the same wherever it is shown ([[R503]]). A fusion of fusions is walked to its leaves, from the definitions the view carries or, for an inner one it does not, from the readable id that spells them ([[R179]], [[R469]]); a digest id whose definition is not held hides them, and the art stays as it was ([[R468]]). Gear fused onto gear has no host, two cards that are no gear change nothing, and a host the catalog lacks leaves the art as it was. A Starship in play keeps its own art, drawn from its pieces and its launch ([[R2249000]]).
- **A fused Dormant Starship keeps its name.** A face whose in-play view says it is a Starship shows the name it launched under, and until it has launched the hull's catalog name, "Dormant Starship", not "Dormant Starship + Miner v1 + …" ([[R1852000]], [[R3038000]]). A view with no in-play data does the same for a fused definition that holds the hull at any depth: the card a log line or the showcase names, a graveyard pile. A reader of Chinese, and a card the view shows in Chinese, read the hull's Chinese name ([[R1301]]); the rest of such a definition's words are its ingredients' as before.
- **Ingredients.** "Holding" a card is read through `CardDef.ingredients`, falling back on the id the Fuse wrote, where `+` joins the ingredients at the top level, a fused one is in parentheses and a Radiant one carries `*`. A definition that lists itself ends the walk, with nothing changed.

Rejected: renaming the Starship in the Fuse (`fuse.rs`), which is engine code and whose name a rule can read, where this is how a card looks ([[R2529000]]); and resolving the host in each face that draws art (`CardFace`, `MinionFace`), which every new face would have to remember, where `<CardArt>` is the one place that reads the id, name and tags. This ruling is a change to how cards look, so it ships as a Cards micro of no family ([[R2529000]]).
