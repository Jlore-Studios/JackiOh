Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or the engine. Every variant-level grant, a duplicate's roll ([[§14.3]]), a cosmetic pack's slot ([[§14.6]]), a token redemption ([[§14.5]]) or a Chase Shard buy ([[§14.6]]), reveals the card wearing it: first its previous look (the highest level below it, else the normal face), then a wipe into the new level's full-art frame with its animation starting and its signature drawing in ([[R2715002]]), captioned with the level's name and rarity. Several grants at once play in order, the rarest last, ties keeping their slot order; a pack's own pulls keep the pack's order instead ([[§14.3]], [[R3015003]]).

The treatment follows the level's own rarity ([[R1333000]]): Rare and Epic play on the panel with a sound by rarity; Legendary dims the panel and grows the card in with light from behind and its intro sting ([[R3332002]]); Mythic, and a Legendary or Mythic chase, take the screen in the full-screen view ([[R3345000]], [[R3344000]], [[R3344001]]) with its camera and music. A tap always moves on. A Legendary card pulled from a pack flares before it flips, by the card's own rarity; a Mythic one tears the pack open into its takeover with its own art, closing to the slots grid on tap. A duplicate's roll in a pack plays its reveal when its own slot is opened, by the level's own rarity rather than its card's, so a Mythic roll on a Common card takes the screen. No slot shows before it is opened, and Reveal all and reduced motion deal every slot face-up at once, skipping these moments. The Collection carries a "new" mark on a card with a newly earned level until its view or preview opens, its hover preview playing the new level moving ([[R3016001]], [[R1953001]]), and each ladder rung draws its own level's frame, motion and signature, not only its name.

Reduced motion shows each reveal as a still before and after (a card pack's scene instead deals every slot face-up at once, as it always has); `bigMoments` short holds the full-screen ones briefly and off shows a still frame ([[R2785000]]). The new-mark store holds only ids the server already granted this account, and an account cosmetic (a playmat, a portrait, a card back) marks no card, even where a pack's slot names one. The chase's ask-first dialog ([[R2683000]]) and the token's ([[R2480000]]) stand unchanged.

Rejected: sorting a pack's own pulls rarest-last, which would reorder what the pack rolled; a shared silhouette for any tier, since every reveal shows the card itself; stills of every roll over a reduced-motion pack's all-face-up scene, which would break that scene's one sting and its focus and Escape contract (#1757); and a new setting for these moments, which follow the existing motion and Big moments settings.
