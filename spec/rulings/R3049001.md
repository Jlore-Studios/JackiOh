A live table ([[R3049000]]) is thin early in every patch, so the card with the best raw rate is usually a small sample's luck: v0.5.10b's table led with Palantir at 25 wins in 32 games while the rows that qualified had a median of 36 games and a spread of about 9 points. So the ends of a game's snapshot are ranked by how sure the figures are.

- **Only a game's snapshot.** The ranking goes with the table (`win_rates::WinRateRanking`): a game handed a snapshot (`GameState.winRates`) ranks it by its bounds, and a game handed none ranks the table it reads, the frozen table of the shipped catalog its fold runs under ([[R1266000]]) or a test's, by [[R1100]]'s and [[R1183000]]'s exact rate, as the version that shipped it ranked it. Every record from before #3049 is such a game, so its CN Tech and NA Tech pick the cards they picked: the table v0.4.0 to v0.5.8 shipped is led by Lunar Eclipse at 33 wins in 48 games, where its bounds would put Fig of Life's 92 in 146 first, and its record would refold to another hash.
- **The leader** ([[R1100]]) is the card whose 95% Wilson score interval has the highest lower bound, **the trailer** ([[R1183000]]) the one whose interval has the lowest upper bound: the interval the public figures already show ([[R850000]], [[R1994000]]'s `wilson_interval`), `(2w + z² ∓ z·√(4w(n − w)/n + z²)) / 2(n + z²)` for `w` wins in `n` games.
- **Exact and deterministic.** No float ranks a card: `z` is `WIN_RATE_BOUND_Z_NUM / WIN_RATE_BOUND_Z_DEN`, 49/25, which is `STATS_INTERVAL_Z` (1.96) as a fraction, and each bound is computed in `u128`, scaled through by `Z_DEN²` and counted in millionths (`WIN_RATE_BOUND_SCALE`), the square root and each division rounded down (`win_rates::wilson_bounds`). It fits for any `i32` figures. A row with no games or with wins outside `0..=games` has no bounds and is never picked.
- **What stays.** The floor (`CN_TECH_MIN_GAMES`, 20 games), the exclusions (never the card asking, [[R387]]; no token, Glitch or vanished id; a held pool's Legendary and Mythic cards, [[R2386001]]) and the tie-breaks, now on equal bounds: more games, then the catalog id ascending. No win in any number of games has a lower bound of exactly nothing and every win an upper bound of exactly one, so those tie and fall to more games.

Hearthstone semantics: a ranking of cards by win rate counts how many games back each rate.

Rejected: floats, which [[R1100]] keeps out of every pick; exact real bounds, which compare square roots of rationals and need 256-bit integers for what a millionth already separates; ranking the frozen tables by their bounds too, which would change the card a record from before #3049 picked and refuse its refold as `rules_changed`.
