"Exile this" in a Spell's text names where [[§10.5]] step 7 sends the Spell instead of the graveyard. The Spell stays in the resolving zone until then, so it is still itself for the rest of its text and for its Echo repeats ([[§6.2]], [[R98]]), and step 7 exiles it, which is when [[R55]]'s exile counter moves. A Spell gains its Echo as it is played — "the next Spell you play gains Echo +1" — so Twinspell's grant is taken at [[§10.5]] step 4 from the player who played it, and Twinspell is consumed then ([[R30]]), whatever the Spell's own text goes on to do to it
