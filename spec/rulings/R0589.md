The card C+ #74 Twice Forward One Step Backwards fuses "if it still exists" is the played card on the field (either row) or in a graveyard as its play resolves. Exiled, returned to a hand or a deck, or ceased to exist, it is no card left to fuse: nothing is fused, and the even count still gives +1 Brittle, the trap staying face-down and unfired when it has fused nothing yet ([[R33]])
