Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Each of the fifty piece definitions, the 47 cards that print Starship Piece (`STARSHIP_PIECES`) and the three Jlockeed weapons that are pieces only when Embiggened (S #127 to #129), has a module of its own (`STARSHIP_MODULE_IDS`): a part drawn where it mounts on a hull, a function of the piece's definition alone, its kind and the tier its name marks (v1 to v5; 1 where it marks none). Its own card ([[R2170000]]), its Starship's hull ([[R3333000]]) and its icon ([[R3333002]]) all draw it.

**The kinds.** Weapons mount on the bow's deck: the Beam an emitter, the Repeater a gatling, the Cannon a turret, Flak twin barrels under its shells, AP and Vamp Rounds an ammo drum and its cartridges (steel tips, or red ones and a drop). The Mining Drills hang from the keel. The defenses stand on the spine behind the guns: the Shield Amplifier an emitter under its dome, Reactive Plating its plates, the Crystal its shards, the Capacitor its cells, the Booster a powered block. The engines trail from the stern: the After Burner a nozzle and its burn, Overdrive twin burners and a turbine, the Warpfield a ring, the Stimulus a fuel cell. On the flanks, the four launchers are tubes each loaded with what it fires (a Rush token, a fruit, a jade orb, a book) and the pods are capsules: Healing a cross, Poison bubbles and a drip, Frost a snowflake and icicles, Magic sparkles, Cloaking dashed and half-transparent, the Felinor Containment Chamber a cat at its porthole, the Patcher Nanobots a hive and its swarm, the Tractor Beam a cone holding a rock. The odd ones: Disintegrex, RR-5000 and Trila 3 on the bow, the Orca under the keel, the Spacemine trailing from the stern, the Ultralight Chassis an unfilled lattice and Salvage a chip and an arm on the spine; Jlockeed's Yamato Cannon on the bow, the Siege Tank under the keel and the Missile Pod on a flank. An id that is no piece (the hull itself, which a view may list) draws a riveted spare plate.

**The ladders.** The weapons and drills climb v1 to v5, each step bigger (scale 0.7 to 1.1) and busier, its tier in chevrons: a Beam `tier` emitter rings and `tier + 1` fins; a Repeater `tier + 1` barrels and a drum from v3; a Cannon one, two or three barrels (v1 and v2, v3 and v4, v5), a muzzle brake from v2 and cheek armor from v4; Flak `tier` shells and radar from v3; a Drill `tier + 2` bit bands, `ceil(tier / 2)` ore hoppers, arms from v3 and a conveyor at v5. A kind on no ladder draws at v3's size, with no chevrons.

**How it is drawn.** In flat fills of the paint it is given, never a gradient, so any picture mounts it without its defs: gunmetal, its kind's energy for its glow and its group's accent. No color of it is in Radiant's gold ([[R2725004]]); a Radiant face gilds it.

**Its own card.** A piece's card draws its module large on a cutaway of a hull, a cut deck section with bare ribs (for a flank module, a section of hull side), in a bay by its family, seeded by the card's id: a firing range with a target for the weapons, an ore bay with its piles for the drills, a shield lab's hex grid for the defenses, a test stand's baffles for the engines, a loading bay's crates for the launchers, a med bay's cross and bunks for the pods, a hangar's crane for the odd ones; the three Jlockeed pieces keep their blueprint grid. Each bay has a wall, a window on space, two to four light strips and a perspective floor. The Radiant face keeps the geometry, gilds the paint and adds the rays, as [[R2170000]] has it. The fifty entries' pictures are recorded again in [[R503]]'s fixture; no other card's picture moves.

This amends [[R2170000]]: a Starship Piece no longer draws its part in front of the fleet's warship hull, but its own module in its bay.

Amended by [[R3406000]] (issue #3406): a Starship Piece or a Jlockeed weapon #3406's table names draws an alt scene recipe under its Alt art seed, its piece a part of the scene kit in another place, time or moment; its normal face and every other seed keep its module in its bay.

Amended by [[R3394000]] (issue #3394): an early Starship Piece #3394's table names (cards 1 to 90) draws an alt scene recipe under its Alt art seed, its piece a part of the scene kit in another place, time or moment; its normal face and every other seed keep its module in its bay.
