A provider's sign-in that matches no account makes a new one, which a player who already had an account did not mean (always so for Roblox, [[R3147000]]). A pending account that signed up with a provider this build names (the token's `app_metadata.provider`, for the wording only) is offered "Already have a JackiOh account?" on the code screen and on the consent screen ([[R757000]]), so before its age is asked. It says that signing in with that provider made this new account, that it is removed first, and that the player then signs in to their account and links the provider on its account page; nothing is sent until the player presses "Remove this new account" ("Keep it" closes it). The press deletes the account at the server exactly as "Delete my account" does (`DELETE /api/account`), whose refusals it shows; then this device forgets the session and the pending addresses, remembers the provider for `AUTH_PENDING_ADDRESS_TTL_SECONDS`, and loads `/login`. While it is remembered, the sign-in screen says to sign in to the account the player already has and link that provider on its account page, and the sign-in that follows opens `/account` rather than the main menu; the account screen opens its management and, once, says to link that provider below to finish, unless it is linked already, and forgets it. A sign-out forgets it too. An active account is never offered this: it holds a collection, and "Delete my account" is its own choice.
