The owner asked on 2026-11-05 for "a button to get all cosmetics". The fill (`POST /api/dev/fill`, [[R1300000]]) gives every card too, and sets every card's proficiency to its top level, so a dev account trying a cosmetic had to take the whole collection with it. `POST /api/dev/cosmetics` grants the fill's cosmetics alone, in one transaction under the account's economy lock:

- every cosmetic of the registry but each kind's default, which every account owns with no row ([[§14.2]]), and the Jlorious card back, which only reaching Jlorious grants ([[R2755000]]);
- a per-card cosmetic on every collectable card it is offered ([[R2725001]]), owned or not, and a full art on the card it names ([[R1313000]]); an account kind once.

It writes no card, no collection row and no proficiency. It answers 200 `{ cosmetics }`, the rows written. What is owned already is left as it is, so a second press writes nothing and a fill after it writes no cosmetic. Every row is a cosmetics ledger row of reason `dev` marked `dev` ([[§14.11]]); the route writes its audit row ([[R3074000]]) and answers anyone `DEV_PROFILE_IDS` does not list 403 `forbidden`, writing nothing, as every dev tool does. The fill and the new route share one grant, so they cannot drift apart.

The Dev tools panel has an Unlock all cosmetics button beside Fill collection, and the dev dashboard's Holdings section, which offers every tool the panel does, has one too.

Rejected: a `?only=cosmetics` on the fill, since a dev route takes no query, and each tool is its own route and its own audit row.
