Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). [[R3395000]]'s signatures need four things of the frame:

- **Five Mythics' own takeover looks**, each with tints and art of its own (`fx/takeoverArt.ts`, fx.css's `.takeover-art-<part>`), mounted over the takeover's dark and standing still behind Reduce Motion's card ([[R2722001]], [[R3400000]]'s art): Tylannt's `vault` (a giant round door, its dial clicking round, swinging open on a bright gap), Rod of Ages' `rod` (a starless gulf, the colossal rod rising as its twelve bands light, then shrinking), Great Paperclip Theory's `wire` (one silver wire over a blueprint bending into a staircase of drone cores that grows huge and snaps back), Paragon-X's `whiteDwarf` (a white dwarf flaring, its halo ringing, a black-and-white blade cutting across it) and Prototype-X2's `forge` (a forge-sphere, five ships spiralling in, cracking in molten light as the hull bursts out). They join `OWN_TAKEOVER_LOOKS`; the shared sweep gives way to the art.
- **Figures** of each card's concept ([[R3400001]]'s `fx/figures.ts`): 27 shapes in a tint over their anchor's box, never a card face, painted by fx.css with transform and opacity alone.
- **Three signature aura looks** ([[R3389000]]'s, each worn by one signature alone and within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`): The Greatest Heist's sweeping green `laserGrid`, Rod of Ages' rising `silverRise` and The Narcor Project's slow `labHaze`, in their zones ([[R2787000]]).
- **A beat that opts in replaces its event's system moment** ([[R2784000]]'s `systemMoments.ts`), as it replaces the event's row: when a card's recipe returns `moment: "replace"` beside `row: "replace"` for an event (`CardFxResult`, `replaceMoment` in `signatureKit.ts`), that event's shared system moment is not planned (cues.ts). Only Tylannt's dial (`questCompleted`) and the Heist's lock rings (`questProgressed`, `questCompleted`) opt in, standing in for the shared quest tally, banner, rays and coins. Every other beat draws beside its event's system moment as before, a signature's or not: Red Jade's drop beside the jade tally, the MAGI's ballot beats beside the shared ballot, Edge of the Universe's flight beside the shared flee. Galaxy's bolt and the Prototypes' mounts and welds replace the `fused` row's transmute, which has no system moment of its own. Without it the shared quest banner alone overran a Mythic's ceiling ([[R3332000]]). On a sweep (a run of hits that reaches every Unit on a side, planned as one fog), [[R3405000]]'s `planSignatureSweep` draws a signature's own beat over the sweep's events, and the fog rolls over only the hits it does not replace, so Flak v5's waves over every enemy Unit draw its shrapnel and smoke and no fog.
- **A signature's ceiling counts its own beats** ([[R3332000]]'s budget): another signature's own entrance that lands inside its play is that card's, held to that card's own ceiling in its own gallery play (`tiers.test.ts`). Imitated Instrumentality's MAGI land with their own entrances beside its boots ([[R3407000]]), and from where they land those run past the Legendary Spell's ceiling, which its cores and boots keep. Every cue of the play, the MAGI's included, still ends within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its entry ([[R200]]), and the phone frame spec plays the whole of it as the board draws it.

Rejected: a look per Unit for its tile, which needs a tile frame of its own; reusing the shared looks for the Field Spells, which [[R3389000]] keeps for signatures alone to give each its own; keeping the shared quest moment beside Tylannt's and the Heist's beats, which would draw two animations of one event and break the budgets; and dropping the system moment under every signature beat that replaces its row, which would also take it from Red Jade, the MAGI and Edge of the Universe, whose issues kept it; and, for Imitated Instrumentality, dropping the MAGI's own entrances under its boots ([[R3407000]]'s choice) or cutting their landings from its gallery play, which would no longer show what a game draws.
