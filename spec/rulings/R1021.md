"Immune to damage" makes every damage instance to that hero 0 (a cap of 0 at §4.4 step 3), fatigue included. Lose health and Set health still happen. It lasts until its player's next turn begins.

This build's decision: the expiry is [[R757]]'s Armor Up expiry, so cast on the opponent's turn — with no turn of theirs to cover — it ends at that turn's cleanup. The rejected alternative was lasting through the opponent's next turn, which would have covered two of their turns.
