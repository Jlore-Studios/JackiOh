The owner asked that the tutorial explain the mechanics "in a way where they can discover stuff about
it themselves". So a lesson's coach is built around **moments**, not instructions: a `moment` step
shows once its board is set and poses the goal or the question ("That wall of Taunt is in your way.
Is there a way around it?"), pointing at nothing. It names the Field Guide entries it sets up, and it
is done once the game shows each of them found, however the player found them, or before it showed.

**Discoveries.** Each Field Guide entry (`discoveries.ts`) is a mechanic, its rule in one line and a
predicate that recognizes it. The predicate reads what [[R292]]'s coach reads: the fresh events, the
human's view and their legal actions. It also reads the public catalog ([[§5.1]]), a card's printed
type, keywords, text and rarity, and nothing else. Every lesson watches every entry. The first time
the game shows one found, the coach records it and, if this lesson lists it or the player has not
met it before, shows a discovery bubble that names the rule ("You found Cry: it happens when you
play the unit from your hand") with Got it. An entry met in an earlier lesson and not this one's is
recorded without a bubble. One move can trip several entries at once (a Cry that pops a Divine Shield and
kills a unit), so the coach queues at most `TUTORIAL_BUBBLES_PER_MOVE_MAX` (2) "Got it" bubbles
between two of the player's own moves (a play, an attack, a position switch, a prompt answered or
the turn's end), a queued tip and a shown `info` step counted; any further entry is found without a
bubble and reaches the Field Guide all the same ([[R2205002]]). An entry is named after what the
card does: a mechanic a lesson's cards do not print is not taught as a keyword (In a hurry's tokens,
destroyed at the end of the turn, are `temporary-tokens`, not Brittle).

**Hints.** A moment carries a nudge and a pointed hint. Its level is the player's own turn starts
since it showed plus the Hint presses on it, at most its hints. Level 0 is the question alone, the
nudge comes a turn later, and the pointed hint, a turn after that, rings the card, the lane or the
hero. The bubble offers Hint, a button that takes keyboard focus, while a hint is left.

**Nothing strands the player** ([[R314]]). A moment never holds the AI, whatever its script says,
and it has no Got it: the player moves it on by finding what it asks about. Still unfound after
`TUTORIAL_MOMENT_TURNS_MAX` (3) of the player's own turns since it showed, it retires as expired,
and so does one that never shows within `TUTORIAL_STEP_TURNS_MAX` (2). A `revisit` moment re-offers
an earlier lesson's mechanic and is moot once the Field Guide holds it. The win names each mechanic
the lesson lists that its game did not show found, with its rule ([[R2205002]]). Exit is in the HUD
throughout. A discovery bubble comes before a tip, and neither holds the AI unless its script says
so for a tip.

Every name, rule, question and hint is in `zh-CN.json` ([[R711000]]).

Hearthstone semantics: the tutorial sets a board where a mechanic pays off and lets the player try
it, naming it once it happens.

Rejected: a step list that tells the player where to click, which is what [[§9.10]]'s coach did; and
a hint that shows at once, which tells instead of letting the player find out.
