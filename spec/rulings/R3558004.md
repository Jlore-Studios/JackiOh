"Until you make one that costs at least (5)" stops once at least two picks' uncapped sum of printed costs reaches 5, at most `FUSED_CARDS_CAP` picks; an empty pool fuses nothing. The sum is uncapped because R77 caps a fused card's cost at 4, which would make every fusion read 4 and stop at once. Rejected: reading the capped cost, which never distinguishes one fusion from another.
