A sweep's weight ([[R3554008]]; Fuse's text reads "Its attacks deal…") is on the sweeper's own attacks
alone. A sweeper that is attacked strikes back with its plain attack ([[§4.3]]): 2 from a base Fuse
(S+ #107) and 4 from a Radiant one, whether its attacker stands in its lane or beside it, and the
strike back hits its attacker alone. Being Ranged, Fuse strikes back at every attacker ([[R783000]]).
Until this change the strike back took the sweep's weight on that one attacker: 10 across its lane
and 6 beside it (Radiant 28 and 16), while the rest of the lane took nothing.

Lane strike ([[R1283]], M #97.5 Bunker) is not a sweep: it applies in combat with the Unit across its
lane whichever side attacked, and it still multiplies a strike back.

The combat forecast ([[R3100000]]), My Pawn's First Strike projection ([[R44]]) and the AI's
exchange read the strike back through the same `combat::strike_back_multiplier_for` the combat does,
so all three agree with it.

Rejected: an attacked sweeper sweeping every enemy Unit in and beside its lane, because a strike back
is one hit on one attacker and the card's text gives the weight to its attacks.
