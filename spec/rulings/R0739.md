Rarity measures complexity and game swing, not stats ([[§8]]). Fifteen cards change rarity: C #3, #12, #16, #18 and #25 to Epic (the Book and in the System families), C #9 to Legendary and C #28 to Legendary, C #60 to Epic (a bigger Recruit than Call to Arms), C #61 and #76 to Rare (the Plague Bringer family), C+ #77 to Epic and C+ #29 to Mythic, and Core #27, #28 and #40 to Rare (the Glowy Jelly Bean and Forgotten families, let rare by the designer, 2026-10-02). Families share one rarity ([[§8]]'s table); tags are not families; tokens take their maker's rarity and generic tokens have none; real cards made by other cards keep their own; a bigger version is never a lower rarity; Radiant never changes rarity; one name names one card. C #58 Common Resources stays Common: it reads like a Rare engine, but the name is the joke. Classic is 35/26/18/10/1, Classic+ 13/24/25/13/3, Core 32/40/16/7/5
