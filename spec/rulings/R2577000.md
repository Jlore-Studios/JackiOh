[[R438]] drew every keyword the view lists for a unit on its board minion twice, a chip and a treatment, so a keyword that does nothing on the field could push a live one into the chips' "+n" fold or past its layer's cap. The test is whether the keyword can still change what happens while the card is on the field, or as it leaves. Seven cannot: Equip, a play option spent as it is played ([[§6.1]], [[R81]]); Tradable, a hand action ([[R1838000]]); Cloaked, a face-down play from the hand; Crater, which fires as the Unit arrives, its Lock drawn on the zone ([[R783010]]); Flashback, a play from the graveyard; Stack, a play onto an occupied zone, whose pile its depth badge already draws ([[§3.2]]); and Temporary X, which counts down in a hand only. Each is `onField: false` in the client's `KEYWORD_VISUALS`: the board minion draws no treatment for it, which takes no layer's slot and no loop, and no chip, its badge kept in the DOM but hidden and out of the "+n" count. Every other keyword stays, Starship Piece among them, since its owner's Starship takes it when it dies. The hover preview and the inspect sheet still list every keyword the view carries. Hearthstone draws no board mark for a minion's Magnetic or Tradeable either. The client still draws from the view alone and decides nothing (CLAUDE.md rule 7). It amends [[R438]]. Rejected: leaving the keywords out of the view, which the engine owns and the preview reads; and deleting their drawings, which a keyword that comes to act on the field would need again.
