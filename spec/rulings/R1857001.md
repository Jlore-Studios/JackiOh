The designer's list (`JackiOh_Stinkyscape_Cards.md`) leaves some of the set's data open; the catalog decides it so (`docs/stinkyscape-set.md`, ST12). The list's numbers stand, with its second #21 numbered **#174** Aerelret and its second #122 **#175** Frost Module, the two numbers after its last. The tokens a card defines are N.k after card N; an entry no one number holds gets a `T-` index (`T-drone-interceptor`, `T-reknite`, `T-anomaly-found`, …), and the ten AI generated cards are `T-AI-1` to `T-AI-10`, as Classic+'s are. The four War Doctrines (#137.1 to #137.4) are tokens, printed Legendary, though their headers print no Token: only #137 makes them. #91 and #92 are Units tagged Swarm; #125 and #164 are Field Spells, and #125 is Jlockeed; #40 Poison Capsule is Plague, as [[§5]] tags every card that uses Plague Counters. #69 Falcon's Radiant face is 8/2 and #86 Prototype-X2's 16/16, and #109 xxFreshJack, which prints no Radiant stats, is 12/22 ([[R275]]'s doubling); #16 Rod of Ages, an Animated Field Spell that prints no stats, prints 1/1 → 2/2, as an Animated face must. The Drone Battleship's three layers are named "Drone Battleship (Shield Layer)", "(Propagation Layer)" and "(Meltdown Layer)", so a text that says "the Drone Battleship" names all three ([[R279]]'s parenthesis rule). #120 Wild System's Radiant face gains 2 mana next turn, not 1, since a Radiant face differs from its base face ([[R276]]).

The **Dormant Starship** is the one card of the set outside its numbers: a (3) Unit, Legendary, 0/0, `token: false` at index `T-dormant-starship` (id `stinkyscape-t-dormant-starship`, the engine's `DORMANT_STARSHIP_DEF_ID`), because the list makes it "NOT a token so it can be shuffled into your GY" and [[R1852000]] lets it reach a graveyard. It prints no Radiant form, so its Radiant face is [[R349]]'s fallback (`radiantFallback`). `catalog check` counts it with the set's cards (`shared_cards`), beside its 175 numbered ones. It is **in no pool and no deck**: only a Starship Piece's death summons it ([[R1852000]]), as only [[R673]]'s roll makes Glitch, which [[R674]] keeps out of every pool. So `query` refuses it unless the query names it by id (`defId`), `deckable` refuses it, and the fuzz tool's deck pools leave it out (`made_by_a_rule`, with Glitch); a piece's death still summons it by its id.
