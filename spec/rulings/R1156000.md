Each seat's opening draw ([[§2.1]] step 2) is four cards: `OPENING_DRAW` is `[4, 4]`. It was `[3, 4]` until patch v0.3.Y raised Player 1's to four for seat balance (issue #1156). It is still a table indexed by seat, not two constants.

Nothing else in setup moves. Player 2 is still dealt The Coin once both mulligans are answered ([[R244]]), and Player 1 still takes the first turn and draws on it. [[R244]]'s "five cards at most" still holds: four, plus Medium's one extra card, which Hard carries. A handicap's extra cards still come on top of the table's entry ([[R182]]), so a Medium or Hard AI opens on five in either seat, and Quickdraw cards still replace draws out of that total ([[R640]]). In Duos every seat draws four as its duel's player ([[§13.2]]), an AI seat's handicap adding its extra card the same way.

Fatigue comes one player-turn sooner. A 20-card deck holds 16 after the deal, so one draw a turn takes its last card on the seat's 16th turn; the Nth empty draw deals N ([[§2.4]], [[R3]]), seven deal 28 and eight deal 36, so a hero at 30 health dies on the eighth, its seat's 24th turn. Player 1's 24th turn is player-turn 47, so two decks that do nothing end there, Player 2 winning, where before they ended a player-turn later, on Player 2's 24th. The cap of 60 player-turns stays a backstop ([[§2.5]], [[R2]], [[R389]]).

A game recorded before the change no longer folds to its recorded hash, since its opening deal differs, and is retired as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). The owner accepted that on the issue.
