While Meditative+ #22 Mountaintop Garden acts on its controller's field, it reaches every embiggen card ([[R1178001]]) of both players (the designer's "All embiggened") played from a hand, or from a graveyard where a permission allows it ([[R454]]). A play at the normal price is the card's Embiggened version: the engine stamps the card Embiggened at [[§10.5]] step 3, before the play hooks and the announce, whatever price it paid, fixed when step 1 accepted the play as the Radiant stamp is ([[R214]]); a card taken from where it was played before then is not played and is left as it is ([[R226]]). The dearer choice no longer costs the printed Embiggened price: it costs the normal price plus the Garden's premium (2, Radiant 1, the declared number `premium`, [[R386]]) and plays the card Radiant and Embiggened, and the play's choices are those of the Radiant face ([[R214]]). A card already Radiant has its normal price only: its dearer choice is neither listed nor accepted, and the view gives it no `embiggenCost`. Cost modifiers apply to both prices after the Garden ([[R65]]); two Gardens are one aura, the least premium applying. A cast ([[R545]]) and a card set face-down ([[R783005]]) are not plays it reaches, and they resolve as before. The aura is read as each price is, so it ends the moment the Garden leaves the field.
