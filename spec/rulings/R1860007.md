Stinkyscape #134 Felinor Containment Chamber's "Death: Summon a Felinor Token with this Unit's
Attack and Health" reads "stats equal to this unit's" as its Attack and its maximum Health as it
died — its current Health is 0 or less then — buffs included ([[R89]]). The token is the base
Felinor Token ([[§7]]), summoned X/X with a `statsOverride` into the leftmost open unit zone
([[R64]]), before the Chamber's own copy fuses into the Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]), which may
then stack on lane 3 if the token took the last zone; a full board summons none. The Radiant face
doubles both and grants Divine Shield through `summon`'s `keywords` rider: keywords the fresh
summon gains as it lands, which belongs to the summon because no `TargetSpec` can name a
just-created card. It takes R1860007 of issue #1860's block, and edits [[§6.3]]'s account of
`summon`.
