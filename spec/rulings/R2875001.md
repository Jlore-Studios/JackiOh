A news entry ([[§14.13]]) is live from its `postedAt` until `NEWS_TTL_DAYS` after it, on the server's clock: live at `postedAt` itself and one millisecond before the end, gone at the end.

**Before it** the entry is not posted: `GET /api/news` leaves it out, and a claim of it is 404, as a claim of an id the server does not hold is.

**After it** the entry has expired, whether or not the account claimed it: `GET /api/news` leaves it out, and a claim of it is 409 and pays nothing ([[R2875000]]).

**The client** decides no window: it shows the entries the server listed, and with none listed, the landing shows no bookmark (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: an event's reward is claimable through the event's window, and is gone once it ends.

Rejected: entries that never expire, which would grow the panel without end; expiry on the client's clock, which a player could move; and an expired entry kept while unclaimed, which would keep a patch's reward open long after the patch was news.
