Felinors vs Humans #82 fills its caster's board with Meditative #94 Shrinking Felinors and says "When the last one dies, you lose the game"; the designer marks the last one as the 3/3 its chain ends on (`docs/felinors-vs-humans-set.md`, FV2 #82).

**The rule.** After the fill, the Meow places a rest-of-game death watch on Felinor ([[R5238009]]) for its caster. After each Felinor's death, once the state check that killed it has run, so that a dying Shrinking Felinor's own Death has summoned its smaller self ([[R1828003]]), the watch asks three things: the dead card is a Shrinking Felinor, its controller as it died is the caster ([[R127]]), and the caster now controls no Shrinking Felinor on the field. When all three hold, the caster loses: the opponent wins at the state check closing the step. So a 9/9 or a 6/6 dying loses nothing, its smaller self standing in its zone, nor does one of several dying; the last 3/3's death, which summons nothing, loses the game. Any Shrinking Felinor counts, not only those the Meow summoned. A Shrinking Felinor lost another way (bounced, stolen, transformed, exiled) is no death and loses nothing by itself, and one of the caster's that dies on the opponent's side after a steal is the opponent's. Two Meows place two watches; the first to find the board empty ends the game.
