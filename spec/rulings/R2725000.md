The owner asked in #2725 for a deeper ladder of card looks after Pokémon TCG Pocket's art rarities:
more versions of a card, each a different picture or treatment, and full art rarer, with Radiant
still easy to tell apart. The issue is a tree; this is its first part, the model every later version
plugs into, and it ships the three looks #2715 already drew.

**The model.** A card wears one **version** (its look: the normal face, or one `version-…` entry)
and the **augments** layered on whichever version it wears (`augment-…` entries). Both are new kinds
of the registry, `version` and `augment`, per-card ([[R1295000]]): one registry entry per version and
per augment, owned card by card in `card_cosmetics` as a foil is, never one entry per card. Neither
is a default or a starter; neither names a card; each carries an **offer**, the cards that may own and
wear it ([[R2725001]]). Part 1 ships three, appended after #2715's 21:

| Rarity | Id | Kind | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legendary, chase | `version-full-art` | version | Full art: the art across the whole face, no words ([[R1017001]]) |
| Epic | `augment-animated` | augment | Animated: the art's own layers move ([[R2715001]]) |
| Mythic, chase | `augment-signature` | augment | Signature: the name signed across the art ([[R2715002]]) |

**The ladder.** Full art moves up from Epic to Legendary, and becomes general: offered on 243 cards
today, not the 21. Animated is an Epic as #2715's Animated art was; Signature, signed alone, is a
Mythic as the signed full art was. Full art with Animated is two cosmetics, a Legendary and an Epic,
and with Signature too a Mythic beside them, so the old combined Legendary and Mythic are each rarer
to complete. **No sixth tier.** The issue left the tier above Mythic to the plan: part 1 ships no
cosmetic that needs it (Prismatic and Immersive come in later parts), so the scale stays Common to
Mythic, `CosmeticRarity`, migration 0045's check, `COSMETIC_TIER_WEIGHTS`, pity, the token cycle, the
Chase Shard and every odds table untouched (§14.4 does not change). With more versions sharing a tier,
each is rarer to roll; a later part that adds the tier changes all of them in one change.

**Retired full arts.** The 84 full arts of [[R2655000]] and [[R2715000]] keep their ids, entries and
owners, so nobody's ownership changes ([[§14.12]]), but they are **retired**: no duplicate, cosmetic
pack slot, token or Chase Shard bar drops or sells one again (`pack_cosmetics` and
`cosmetic_pack_cosmetics` leave them out; a token's or the bar's request for one is a 400, "that
cosmetic is retired"), and the Collection lists them nowhere. Each counts as owning the version and
augments it combines ([[R2725002]]). The 21 full-art cards still print full art by the Full-art
cards setting alone, their still Epic as before: `true`, and the setting where a card has no pick,
still name the card's still Epic and nothing else, so practice, hotseat and every test that counts
every cosmetic as owned look as they did. Any other card wears the Full art version only by its own
pick of its id.

**The pick.** A card's pick keeps [[R2655000]]'s `fullArt`, which may now name a version
(`version-…`), and gains `augments`, a list of distinct augment ids, null or empty for none.
Migration 0079 adds `card_cosmetic_picks.augments` (`text[]`, each `augment-` and a slug, 40
characters at most, [[R633]]), widens the `full_art_id` check to take `version-…`, and counts a row
of augments alone as a pick; it rewrites no row. `PUT /api/card-picks` holds the version and every
augment to ownership on the card and to the offer, a 400 with nothing written otherwise
([[R1834001]], [[R2725001]]); the device's one-time upload keeps an augment only where it is owned and
offered, since no device held one before the picks moved to the account ([[R1834000]]). A match
freezes the version where it is owned and offered (`true` for a retired still Epic, else its id) and
the augments it owns and is offered there, once each, in the registry's order, and the other seat, a
watcher and a replay's viewer wear them ([[R1834002]], [[R1834004]]), behind their own Full-art
cards setting as before. The Collection lists a card's versions, the normal face first, and the
augments it is offered to toggle, owned or locked, with their rarities and a preview; choosing either
keeps the other, so a retired pick becomes the version and the augments it combines on its first
edit. A face shown as if picked without the store holding it (the Collection's preview, the
Stats drill-down's art buttons, [[R2756000]]) wears the version and the augments it is shown with,
none when it is shown none: the store's augments never layer on a shown pick. Presentation only: no rule reads a version, and nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the
log or a replay hash (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**Part 1's scope.** The ten new looks (alt art, sketch, extended art, pixel, comic, set showcase,
alt full art, out of frame, immersive, prismatic) are later parts of #2725, each a `version-…` entry
with its offer, its painter and its tests. The ladder bots wear no version or augment yet ([[R1834005]]
draws their wardrobe from what people own). Not a system release: `SYSTEM_RELEASES` is the owner's.

Amends [[R1313000]] (a full art is no longer the only per-card cosmetic of a card's look, and the 84
are retired), [[R1333000]] (the chases gain the Full art version and the Signature augment),
[[R2655000]] and [[R2715000]] (their full arts drop and sell no more, and count as owning what they
combine).

Amended by [[R2949000]] (#2949): Signature no longer layers on any version: it is worn only beside the Full art version and Animated, each owned and offered on the card, and shows the Signature look, a full art and a motion of its own; a pick naming it without both is a 400, and a match freezes it only beside both.

Amended by [[R2940000]] (#2940): part 2 appends three versions after these three, Alt art (Rare), Sketch (Rare) and Alt full art (Legendary, a chase), each per card and granted to no account; the scale stays Common to Mythic.

Amended by [[R2942000]] (#2942): part 3 appends three Epic versions after part 2's, Extended art, Pixel and Comic, each per card and granted to no account, none a chase; the scale stays Common to Mythic.
