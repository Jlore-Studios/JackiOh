The copy is fixed as Echo's play begins ([[§10.5]] step 1, or the start of a cast) and held until it lands: its declared choices, its resolution, its Echo repeats and its prompts' continuations run that Spell's face even if the play records a newer Spell; in hand and in a deck it follows the last Spell. The copied text is Echo's: "this" in it is Echo, its declared numbers are the copied definition's read on Echo's instance, and an Echo X the copied face prints adds to the Radiant face's Echo 1. Echo keeps its own name, (1) Cost, type and tags
