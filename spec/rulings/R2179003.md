Since issue #2179 an Easy problem of C+ #42 is generated from the match rng as a four-digit sum or
difference: one coin picks + or −, then a and b are each uniform from 1000 to 9999
(`KY_TEST_FOUR_DIGIT_OPERANDS`), a difference written larger first, so its answer is never negative.
Its three wrong options are the answer moved by three different entries of ±1, ±10, ±100
(`KY_TEST_FOUR_DIGIT_MISSES`, drawn as a shuffle of the six), so the four options always differ, and
the problem rides the answer prompt's resume data as `easy:<a>+<b>` or `easy:<a>-<b>` ([[R580]]). The
bank's old Hard problems are its Medium ones now, and its Hard ones are new problems at a doctoral
level, each with one answer and three wrong options ([[R420]]). A replay of v0.4.12d and before keeps
the two-digit sums and the old bank (`script_v0_4_12d`, [[R767000]])
