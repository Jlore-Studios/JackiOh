**Why.** [[R185]] resamples a seat's unseen cards from the non-token cards of every set ([[R380]]). An Infinite Assault enemy's deck is Infinite Assault cards, which the mode states openly, and the set is held ([[R4577000]]), so no unnamed pool reaches it. The human's lethal hint ([[R2102000]]) sampled the enemy's hand and face-down cards from shipped cards and could promise a lethal that a face-down Assault Trap would stop; the AI likewise guessed at cards the enemy's deck cannot hold.

**The setup.** `createGame` takes `deckSets`, per seat, the sets that seat's deck is dealt from. Every card of the seat's deck must be of one of them, or the setup is refused. A seat whose list is empty or absent names none. The state keeps a naming seat's sets (`PlayerState.deckSets`), the view shows them on both seats (`SideView.deckSets`), a replay passes them as it passes the deck marks (`ReplayInput.deckSets`), and a Glitch reset keeps them. A seat that names none stores nothing, so every other game is created, viewed and hashed as before.

**Determinization.** A seat that names sets has its unseen cards resampled from those sets' non-token cards alone: its hand and library from all of them, each face-down card it set from their Traps and Field Traps and the cards of theirs it may have set by Cloaked ([[R783009]]), still matching the cost the card shows ([[R762]]) and drawn from what that seat has not shown ([[R2826000]]). A seat that names none samples as [[R185]] says. Since the AI and the lethal hint share one determinization, both read the named sets.

**The Assault.** `assault_fight_setup` names `Infinite Assault` for the enemy (`enemySets`) when every card of the level's deck is of that set, and the practice host passes it as `deckSets` at the AI's seat. A day dealt from the shipped stand-ins while the set is held ([[R4577000]]) names none, so its fights sample every set as any game does.

**Rejected.**

- **Inferring the sets from the cards shown.** A guess from a few shown cards is not what the seat knows; the setup is.
- **Sampling every set, held ones included.** Every other game would hash and decide differently, and a held set's cards would leak into determinizations of games that cannot hold them.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
