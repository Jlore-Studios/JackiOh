`cargo jackioh selfplay` ends a game without a result when one turn of it takes more than `SELFPLAY_TURN_ACTIONS` (100) actions. The arena's own games and the gates' keep only the game's ceiling, `ARENA_MAX_ACTIONS` (3,000).

A whole self-play game takes about 60 actions, 131 at the 99th percentile of the first 448 games of `learned-dev` and 151 at the most, so no real turn comes near the ceiling. Game 474 of that base did: p2 found a loop, The Coin for a crystal and then a fused one-cost card (`classicplus-057` with an AI token) that hands back another Coin, and played it over 300 times in one turn, a second's decision each, so the game would have run to the arena's ceiling for most of an hour and held its shard back with it. Ended at the ceiling, it is a game without a result, which `positions` drops and counts ([[R1006003]]); the rest of the export is unchanged, byte for byte ([[R1006001]]). The loop itself is the engine's and the cards' to answer (#1121), not the export's.
