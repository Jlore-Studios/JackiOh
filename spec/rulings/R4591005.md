IA #154 Honeypot Protocol's "Summon a Decoy Server. Redirect the attack to it" summons first, then re-aims the open `declaredAttack` at the fresh Decoy (`takesAttack`, [[R1122]]), so the combat resolves against it. With no open zone nothing is summoned and the attack goes on. Forced attacks spring nothing ([[R121]]), and a canceled declaration is never re-aimed ([[R44]]).
