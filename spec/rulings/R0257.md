The queue and the room challenge offer Best of 1 (one saved deck), Conquest (a trio, [[R330]]; called Best of 3 before it, and still `bo3` on the wire and in the database) and All Random ([[R258]]). A ticket pairs only with a ticket of the same mode; inside a mode [[§9.5]]'s window and [[R166]]'s order are unchanged, and the queue population is reported per mode. A request that names no mode is Best of 1 and may name its deck by its place in the saved list, oldest first (`deckIndex`), which is what a client from before the modes sends
