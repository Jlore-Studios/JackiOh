A card taken from a hand, deck, stack or field becomes the taker's: owner and controller. `stolen` reads to whoever could read the card where it was taken (hand → its holder; face-down → its controller; face-up or public pile → both; library → nobody) or can read it where it is now. A card taken from the other player's library makes the rest of that library unknown to its owner ([[R312]]). A draw from the opponent's deck is the drawer's draw; an empty deck gives nothing and no fatigue.
