IA #90 Mass Production and IA #96 Exponential Growth summon a copy of every friendly Unit there as they resolve, in order; a full board takes fewer, and the copies are not copied themselves. "The copies" on the Radiant faces means what the card summoned ([[R136]]), read off `summoned_so_far`. Exponential Growth then doubles the Attack and Health of its controller's Units — copies among them — with `doubleStats`, each stat capped ([[R4079000]]).

Why: copies of a board are made of the board, and the doubling comes after the copies in the text, so it reads them too. Rejected: doubling before the copies land, which the text's order does not allow.
