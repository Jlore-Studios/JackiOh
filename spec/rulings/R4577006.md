IA #12 Recompile's "Transform a friendly Unit into a random Infinite Assault Unit that costs (1) more" reads the chosen Unit's printed cost ([[R65]]: its definition's, whatever an aura or a buff makes it cost) and draws from the set's Units printed at that cost plus the step. The new Unit keeps the face of the one it replaces, Radiant for Radiant. With no Infinite Assault Unit at that cost the pool is empty and nothing changes ([[R129]]); Immutable refuses the Transform ([[R23]]).
