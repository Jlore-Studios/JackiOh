S+ #76 Ayys LMAO's summons are each set face-down in the leftmost open backrow zone by their Cloaked (`setCloaked`, [[R4327000]]), Ayys and Tiny Asteroids alike: the text makes both kinds Cloaked ("Cloaked Ayy tokens or Cloaked Small Asteroids"), and the designer's note puts the Ayys in the backrow "if possible". The Ayy is set by the Cloaked it prints, the asteroid by the one the summon gives it, which lands silently. When the backrow has no zone open, each summon left is summoned face-up into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), the asteroid given Cloaked there as before, and a full board summons fewer ([[R129]]). The picks are drawn as before, one per summon in the Cry. The Radiant face is unchanged: Radiant Ayys (4/4, Poisonous) and base asteroids, each read as the card it is while set ([[R783005]]) and revealed so.

This supersedes [[R3557003]]'s placement, where an Ayy stood in the backrow as a Unit and an asteroid took the unit row, both face-up; games of v0.6.8b and before fold under that script, kept ([[R767000]]). S+ #85 Body Snatch, a transform into a face-up Ayy, is unchanged. Rejected: setting the Ayys only, when one text makes both kinds Cloaked; and leaving a summon unsummoned while the backrow is full, when "if possible" asks for the unit row then.
