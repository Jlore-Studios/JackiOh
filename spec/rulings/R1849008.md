A card's `allyAttacked` hook ("When one of your Taunt Units is attacked, give it +2 attack") answers a declared attack on a Unit its controller controls. Once [[§4.2]] step 4's trap window has closed on an attack that still stands ([[R220]]), every card acting on the attacked Unit's side with the hook, the attacked Unit itself included, runs it in [[R68]]'s order, each a whole effect followed by its state check ([[R59]]); then the combat resolves and reads both attacks as it starts ([[R94]]), so Arm of the Jempire's +2 counts in the strike back. The hook is handed `{ attackerId, defenderId }` (`combat::ally_attacked_of`) and filters the Unit itself: Arm of the Jempire checks that the defender has Taunt. The target it is handed is the one the window left, so a trap that re-aimed the attack hands it the new one. Once a hook has left the attack no longer standing, its attacker or its target gone, no further hook starts and the combat does not happen. A question in a hook pauses the rest of the hooks and the combat until it is answered, owed on `state.work` as `@allyAttacked` ([[R113]]). An attack on a hero wakes nothing, and neither does a forced attack, which no player declared ([[R121]]). Rejected: running it as the attack is declared, before the traps, where a trap that cancels the attack would leave the buff on a Unit nobody attacked; and waking it for forced attacks, which no trap keyed to a declaration answers either.
