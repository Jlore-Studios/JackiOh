Since patch v0.2.3 C+ #33 prints "The first Unit you stack onto this is fused into it" (Radiant: it becomes Radiant first) in place of its Stack aura and its carried Unit. A Unit its controller plays may name the Tower's zone while no Unit has stood on it this stay; it stands there as a carried Unit while its own play resolves ([[R446]]), and is then fused into the Tower per [[R77]], the Tower the kept card, which stays a Field Spell ([[R653]]). The Tower is a backrow card like any other, before and after: backrow effects and "all Field Spells" effects find it (C+ #5, C+ #24, T-AI-6, [[R588]]) and "all Units" effects never do. The Unit's text lives on in it where a Field Spell's text can work, and once its Unit is in, nothing stands on it
