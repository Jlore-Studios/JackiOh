Glitch (`GLITCH_DEF_ID`) is in no random pool or Discover, not even one that takes every token (C+ #23), and is found only by its id (`catalog.query`). The client leaves it out of the Almanac, the Deck Builder and the patch notes. A rule deals it, so no card's `refs` names it ([[R279]])
