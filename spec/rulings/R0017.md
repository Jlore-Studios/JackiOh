A counter answers the play's announce, before the card is placed or `cardPlayed` goes out ([[§10.5]]), so a countered card reaches no other trap. Sheepish, Bear Honeypot, Unstable Clone Machine and Unlicensed Experimentation fire after the card resolves, its Cry included (Sheepish since patch v0.2.0, [[R427]]; Unlicensed Experimentation only for played permanents, [[R61]]), and Bear Honeypot waits, face-down and unspent, while its controller's unit row is full ([[R430]]). Sheepish on an Immutable unit still fires and is consumed with no effect
