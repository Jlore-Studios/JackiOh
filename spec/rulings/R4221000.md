[[R4111000]]'s I18 judged an attack whose attacker, forecast to fall, was flickered home instead by a "would die" replacement acting for its side (a face-up fused Unit holding Classic #14 Shadowstep's Radiant text; fuzz seed 9561). The replacement answers the death check that follows the combat ([[§4.5]] step 1): it flickers the dying Unit back to its zone (E22), or resolves its own `then` first (Stinkyscape #138 Pocket Peptides), and neither emits a `trapFired` or a `hookResolving` the step's events would show. The forecast ([[R3100000]]) projects no replacement, so I18 now skips, by one more named condition, a board where a card acting for the attacker's side or the target's side (`acting_texts_of`: a face-down Trap's text sleeps, and a hand card is not on the board) has a `wouldDie` replacement.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and an invariant that checks it may judge only what it can see.

Rejected: making the forecast project "would die" replacements, since the same window also offers cards the attacking seat cannot see (a face-down Trap, a card in hand), so a forecast that projected the visible ones would show a seat less than it does today without showing it all.
