[[R1759000]] keeps past the beam's cut only the stand-downs, switches to Defense Position of a unit that an enemy unit able to attack it next turn would kill and survive ([[R2021000]]). A unit that survives its fight is no stand-down, so where the earlier moves filled the width its switch was cut, though in Defense Position it has Taunt ([[§4.1]]) and every enemy attack it may legally take must target it ([[§4.2]] step 3). At 7 life with a 7/7 in Attack Position and a hand of cheap Units against three 4/4s, the plays and attacks fill both widths and the 7/7's switch was tried at no level; against five enemy 7/7s the AI swung, played out its hand, ended its turn and died to the swing back, where the switch would have walled every attacker.

While the face damage the opponent's board could deal the seat next turn (`face_threat`, [[§9.9]]) reaches the seat's life (`effective_life`), the point where the evaluation's `lethal_threat` applies, the expansion now also keeps up to `AI_SEARCH.position_switches` (2) of the switches from Attack to Defense Position that the cut and the stand-downs left out and that lower that threat, after the stand-downs and before End Turn (`wall_switches`):

- each switch is tried on a copy of the state with only the unit's position changed, so no search node is spent;
- the least threat left comes first, ties in `candidate_actions`' order;
- a switch counts if it lowers the threat at all, whether or not it ends the lethal, so the first of two walls is kept;
- no switch is added twice, so a level holds at most the width, plus twice that constant, plus End Turn;
- off lethal nothing changes, and the policy prior ([[R1171001]]) still re-sorts the prefix only, adding and dropping nothing.

It reads only the public board (positions, keywords, attack, health and life), as the evaluation's threat already does, so no hidden card is read. It adds no rule of the game, no constant and no evaluation weight. A block that takes two steps (two walls, or a kill and a wall) stays out of reach while `lethal_threat` is one flat cliff.
