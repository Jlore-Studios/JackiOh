Amends [[R2688000]], whose hookless twin is the unit made Vanilla ([[§6.3]]) and granted the keywords it prints, and [[R3064000]], which wakes the same twin. A "your Units have X" lend never reaches a Vanilla card ([[R1859007]]), so a unit attacked with a keyword its side lent it and its twin without, and what the keyword did in that one attack was booked as the price of the unit's text: the AI's Stinkyscape #75 Badger (2/3, "When this attacks, gain a random Stinkyscape Resource"), probed into an enemy Midrange Menace (9/9 Taunt) with Poisonous lent to the AI's Units, killed the Menace where its twin did not, and its text read 23.9 against 1.0 without the lend. The shipped lenders are the War Doctrines (#137.1 to #137.4: Trample, Deft, Rush, Flying), #4.4 Last Word (Charge) and Training Grounds #39 Call to Chaos. And a unit already Vanilla that held a granted Frenzy, which counts as an attack hook ([[R3551004]]), had Vanilla taken off its twin alone before scoring, so its printed keywords came back on one side of the price: a Vanilla'd Midrange Menace read −1.5, its Taunt's worth. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**The twin.** It is still the unit made Vanilla and granted the keywords it prints, as tuning leaves them, and it is now also granted each keyword the unit holds through [[§10.4]]'s layers that this Vanilla copy does not, one for each entry it lacks: a lent keyword, and any other that Vanilla takes with the text (a conditional keyword, one its own aura gives it). So in its attack, or its wake, it holds what the unit holds. Before its state is scored, all it was granted is taken off again, one of each. A unit already Vanilla runs no text: it is its own twin, granted nothing and scored Vanilla, so its price is 0. A Dormant card takes no lend until it wakes, so its twin's wake is granted none; that wake runs nothing, and the lend is back before the twin is scored, so a lent keyword moves no wake price.

**What the twin still does not keep.** For that one attack or wake, Vanilla also takes from the twin a set stat (layer 2 of [[§10.4]]), the aura it projects and its other triggers, and an Attack lend ([[R4586006]]) never reaches it. These stay out. No shipped Unit with an attack hook or a wake trigger has a set stat, an aura or a conditional keyword, so they reach a probe only on a host that took them by Equip, which [[R2688000]]'s approximation names. The only Attack lend that lasts into the turn a probe plays is Infinite Assault #33 Uncanny Valley's, of a held set no pool reaches but one that names it ([[R4577000]]). A stat granted as a buff could not be taken back safely once the attack transformed or reset the twin, while a granted keyword is taken off only where it still is. And an aura or a trigger runs only with the unit's text, which the twin exists to switch off.

**Rejected.** Granting only the keywords read off the side's lend modifiers, which misses a keyword Vanilla takes by another road; and pricing an already-Vanilla unit at 0 in each probe, where the twin, which both probes build, holds the case once.
