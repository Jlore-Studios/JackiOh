Issue #4154 took C #37 Last Hurrah's delayed discard and its declared `discard` off both faces: the base face reads "Draw your deck." and the Radiant face "Draw your deck. Give {flashback} random cards in your hand Flashback." The count is the declared `flashback`, 4, the Radiant face's alone (`tunedOn: radiant`), read as the clause is reached ([[R386]]); 4 is about what a Radiant face's twice-the-base asks of a card whose base face draws the deck. The draw is [[R58]]'s as before. How the cards are picked and how long the keyword lasts is [[R4154202]]. Through v0.6.6h the delayed effect discarded `discard` random cards at the end of this turn (Radiant: of its controller's next) ([[R3134000]], `script_v0_6_6h`, [[R767000]]).
