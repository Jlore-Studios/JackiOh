The five Miners (S #62 to #66) print "Starship Piece, Equip / Activate N: Gain a random Stinkyscape Resource", Miner v1's with "Has an extra turn cooldown" (ST-S05-1).

- **The Resource.** A Stinkyscape Resource is any Resource the catalog holds: `gain_resources { count: 1 }` makes [[R1840002]]'s rarity roll and then picks one Resource of that rarity. The Resource goes into the tray of the controller of the Unit the Activate is on, never into a hand ([[R1840000]]), and a ninth Resource is sold at once ([[R1840001]]). On the Radiant face the Resource is Radiant.
- **Equipped.** Played as an Equip onto one of your Units ([[R1086]]), the Miner gives its host the Activate ([[R1751000]]), and the host's controller is the one who gains. The host is a piece only if it already was one ([[R1852001]]).
- **Activate N.** "Activate 2", "Activate 3" and "Activate 5" are that many uses a turn, and the next use that turn is refused ([[R384]]). The designer's "Activate 1" is a plain Activate, once a turn. Miner v1's extra turn cooldown also refuses it on its user's next turn ([[R1841000]]).
