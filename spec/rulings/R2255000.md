account screen's rank block shows, under the rank line, the season's ranked record that
`GET /api/ranked` already sends ([[R612]]'s `record`), as "This season (ranked): W–L–D", the
wins, losses and draws of the current season's rated games. The line is left out while the
season has no games, and when the rank cannot be read the screen shows what it showed before:
the Rank heading with nothing under it. The Record section below keeps the all-time count from
`GET /api/profile`, its heading and figures unchanged, so each record says which one it is.
The Play online lobby's rank panel stays as [[R661]] has it.
