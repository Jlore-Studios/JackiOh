id: R138
title: A cast permanent with no zone to enter
cards: "§6.3, §10.5, R64, R70"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/rulings_c.rs
---
A cast cannot be refused the way a play can ([[R70]]), so a cast permanent that finds no free unlocked zone of its row ([[R64]]) still counts as played and still resolves its script; the card is then in no zone, and [[§10.5]] step 7 sends it to its owner's graveyard. It never becomes a summon that "fails if there is none" ([[§6.3]]), because it was played
