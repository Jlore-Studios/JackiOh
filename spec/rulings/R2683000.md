Not a rule of the game (CLAUDE.md rule 7): how the client asks before it spends what the
server holds, and when it holds its buttons.

A full [[R1306002]] Chase Shard bar buys one chase for all of `CHASE_SHARD_PACKS` cosmetic
packs, and the server's ledger cannot take the purchase back ([[§14.11]]). The cosmetic pack
panel's chase Buy no longer sends on its first press. The press asks in the row's place, as
[[R2480000]] has the token offer ask: an alert dialog, "Buy Galaxy? This spends your full Chase
Shard bar.". It names the cosmetic in the reader's language and says the bar goes.

Confirm sends `POST /api/cosmetic-packs/chase` once, with the cosmetic and, for a per-card
chase, the card the row had chosen; the panel then shows the emptied bar as [[R1306002]] has
it. Cancel spends nothing, holds the focus as the question opens, and puts the row back with
the focus on its Buy. Escape answers Cancel and nothing else. While the question is open the
panel's other Buys wait, and a refusal (the server's words, nothing changed) shows as before.

The panel's Open and its chase Buy also wait, as the Shop's Buy does ([[R2378000]]), while any
spend of the tab is out, whether the panel's own or another's on the page: disabled, `aria-busy`
and turning the wheel, and their handlers check again, so a press in the tick before React
renders the wait still sends nothing. A Confirm met by a spend out sends nothing and keeps the
question open. The server still runs each spend once ([[R2124000]]) and orders every spend of
the account.

Hearthstone semantics: the shop asks before it spends gold or runestones on a purchase it
cannot take back, and the chase is the costliest spend of the cosmetic economy.

Rejected: asking only for a Mythic chase, since a Legendary one spends the whole bar too; and an
undo, which the server's append-only ledger cannot take back.
