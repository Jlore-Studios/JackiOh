An Anomaly scan (`scanForAnomaly { defId }`, the Scanning Probes) is one roll of `ANOMALY_ROLL_SIDES` (100). A roll below the Radiant chance adds a Radiant copy of `defId` (Anomaly Found!) to its controller's hand; a roll below the Radiant chance plus the plain chance adds a plain copy; anything else misses. The chances start at `ANOMALY_RADIANT_CHANCE` (5) and `ANOMALY_PLAIN_CHANCE` (20), and each earlier miss of the scanning player's adds `ANOMALY_RADIANT_STEP` (1) and `ANOMALY_PLAIN_STEP` (4) ("future Scanning Probes have +1% & +4% chance"). The Radiant chance is capped at 100, and the plain one at what the Radiant leaves.

The misses are counted per player, the scan's controller's (`PlayerState.anomalyMisses`, absent until their first miss). A find keeps the count, since the cards' text changes the chance only on a miss; resetting it on a find was rejected. The roll is luck-based ([[R1438]]): the scanning card's Lucky and its controller's Luck ([[R987]]) roll again that many times, keeping the best (Radiant over plain over a miss). A miss is silent: no event, and the count is in neither view. A find is the card's `addedToHand`, and a full hand burns it as any card added to a hand ([[§2.4]]).
