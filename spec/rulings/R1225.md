## R1225 — Use it or lose it: an unused credit line tributes its card

Decided (Meditative #89 Jlarna's base face). At the end of its controller's turn, if they borrowed
no mana during that turn, Jlarna is Tributed by its own text, the way #54 Money Machine and #60
Eschews are. "Using the credit line" means at least one spend that turn went past their current
mana; the turn's log records it, and the view shows it on the base face. The check runs with the
end-of-turn triggers ([[R62]]), on its controller's turns only. The turn Jlarna is played is not
exempt: its own price can't be borrowed, so the player must borrow later that same turn, or Jlarna
is Tributed at that turn's end. A Jlarna that reaches the field on the opponent's turn is first
checked at the end of its controller's next turn. A player who owes the full 4 can't borrow, so a
line used up to its limit loses Jlarna at that turn's end; the instalment taken at the start of
each turn frees part of the limit again. The Radiant face never tributes itself. Jlarna's debt
outlives it.
