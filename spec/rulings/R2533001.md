This amends [[R2434001]]'s "The AI's decks": every lesson's opponent deck (`aiDeck`) is twelve
Training Grounds cards, none Legendary or Mythic ([[R2533000]]), none a token, and none on the
shadow ban ([[R186]]). Each is themed to its lesson as the human's is, and holds nothing a later
lesson teaches:

| # | Id | The opponent's deck |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `basics` | three each of TG #1 Spikey Steven and #43 Mr. Vanilla, two each of #8 Clobberer and #4 Boulder Fist Ogre (Rush), and two Cry units, #44 and #22 |
| 2 | `keywords` | Divine Shield (#6), Taunt (#47), Pierce (#18) and Rush (#4) units, six Cry units and two vanilla ones |
| 3 | `casting` | seven units of lessons 1 and 2, and five spells: #11 Thwomp, #58 True Strike, #10 Fan of Knives, #15 In a hurry, #42 Stockpile |
| 4 | `backrow` | eight units of lessons 1 and 2 (they set off the human's Traps), #14 Explosive Trap, #24 Goliath Situation (a Field Trap), and two Field Spells, #19 Extra Rations and #60 Rush Token Farm |
| 5 | `finale` | a bit of everything: Auras (#3, #21), a Death (#32), Cry and keyword units, #48 Jlockeed Shredder-10, #11 Thwomp, #46 Hit Job, a Trap and a Field Trap |

**Copies.** As the human's lesson 1 deck needs copies to be mostly vanilla ([[R2433001]]), so does
the opponent's. `AI_TUTORIAL` sets `deckCopies` to `TUTORIAL_DECK_COPIES` (4), which amends
[[R2433001]]'s "not `AI_TUTORIAL`". A lesson's opponent copies only a vanilla unit, one with no text
or only Rush, as its human does; lessons 2 to 5 copy nothing. The tutorial tier's frozen games
([[R290]]) seat it without the copies, as their decks are distinct, and [[R1012003]] leaves them out
when it reads the tutorial opponent.

**The deals.** Lesson 2 keeps its seed. Lessons 1, 3, 4 and 5 take new ones, `tg-basics-1`,
`tg-casting-9`, `tg-backrow-47` and `tg-finale-38`, each a seed found by scanning on which the coach's
line shows every moment within the lesson's turns, and every other policy its suite plays still wins
([[R2201000]]).

Hearthstone semantics: a scripted encounter deals from decks of its own choosing.

Rejected:

- Twelve distinct cards in lesson 1, which would be mostly Cry units, not mostly vanilla.
- Copies on a lesson's own handicap rather than on `AI_TUTORIAL`, which would make two tutorial
  opponents.
