A Trade ([[R1838000]]) is reported in three events, in this order: `traded { player }`, new, public and naming no card; then `shuffledIn` for the card going into the deck; then `drawn` for the card coming out. The two card events are judged as every other is ([[R97]]): `shuffledIn` names a card now in a library, which no seat reads, and carries its slot blanked for both seats; `drawn` names a card in the trader's hand, which the trader reads and the other seat does not. So the other player learns that a card was traded, and a card back leaving and one arriving in the hand count they already see, but never which card went in, which came out, or where in the deck it went. The trader is shown the card they put in, as every shuffle-in shows its owner ([[R311]]): their own library list counts it among the cards they know ([[R310]]), never its slot. Unlike `stolen` ([[R466]]), `traded` names nothing a seat could read where the card was, so it needs no rule of its own beyond being public.
