The first and last cards in hand order as the Spell resolves (the Spell itself is already out of the hand,
[[R1563001]]) each take a `costMod` of −1 (Radiant −2). A cost never goes below (0), and an X-cost card is
unaffected ([[R65]]). A one-card hand's card takes the discount once, and an empty hand takes nothing.
Rejected: discounting a lone card twice, since it is one card; and counting the Spell itself as an end of
the hand, which it has left.
