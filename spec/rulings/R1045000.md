Allure ([[§6.3]], [[R963]]) is a delayed steal of a marked enemy permanent: a Unit, a Field Spell, a Trap or a Field Trap. The marked card carries the public `allure` mark, pink ([[R437]]); at the start of the Allurer's next turn, if it still stands on the field under the opponent ([[R76]], [[R174]]), it is stolen into its own row, the same lane if free, else the first free zone ([[R15]]), and with no open zone in that row on the taker's side it is destroyed instead: an ordinary destroy, so Death and Reborn apply and an Indestructible card stays where it is ([[R46]]). That is Hearthstone's rule for a minion taken with a full board, which is destroyed.

#50 K-Pop Fanatic's Cry printed that steal of one enemy permanent, so it prints Allure: "Cry: Allure an enemy permanent." It keeps the delayed entry it always had ([[R81]]'s declared target, [[R126]]'s `resume` step), so it still fires after the Fanatic has died ([[R76]]) and its Radiant rider still lands only on a card the steal took ([[R282]]), never on one destroyed for want of room. Until issue #1045 it marked its target purple and left one it had no room for with the opponent ([[R15]]); a replay of a game from then folds under that script (`KEPT_SCRIPTS`, [[R767000]]). Meditative #39.5 Jade Beauty still Allures only enemy Units, as it prints.
