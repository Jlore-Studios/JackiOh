[[§14.9]] let a quest leave an account only by completing it, so a quest the player does not want,
such as winning five games or playing eight cards of a set they do not own, held its slot until they
finished it, and while the slots were full the arrivals were skipped ([[R1304000]]). A held quest
may now be replaced, and the spec did not say how, so:

- **The route and the lock.** `POST /api/quests/:questId/replace`, for an active account while
  `ECONOMY_LIVE` is on, runs in one transaction under the account's economy lock ([[R1301003]]).
  It tops the account up first, as every read of its quests does ([[R1304000]]).
- **The refusals.** In this order: the day's replaces are spent, the quest is not held, nothing is
  left to draw (every other entry of the pool is held). Each writes nothing and changes nothing the
  player holds.
- **The draw.** [[R1304000]]'s draw over the pool's entries the account does not hold, the replaced
  quest among the ones held so it is excluded, by a fresh seed logged in the `arrived` row's `ref`
  ([[§14.11]]). The replacement is dated when the replace ran and starts at 0 progress, and it takes
  the freed slot at once. The replaced quest's progress is lost, and `next_due_at` is untouched, so
  the arrival rhythm does not move.
- **The count.** The day is the UTC day of [[R1483000]]. An account's replaces that day are its
  `removed` quest rows with the reason `quest`, so no table or migration is added, and the `removed`
  rows with the reason `launch` that going live wrote ([[R1310002]]) count for nothing. The count is
  read under the lock, so two replaces at once land one: the second waits, reads the first's row and
  is spent.
- **The answers.** `GET /api/quests` and the replace both answer `replacesLeft`, the replaces the
  account may still spend today, 0 while the economy is off. The client offers Replace on each held
  quest, behind a confirm, only while it is above 0, and draws the answer; it enforces nothing
  (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's quest log lets a player swap one daily quest a day for another
the player does not already have, drawn at random.

Rejected: a counter table, which needs a migration; reading the whole ledger on every quests read,
which grows with every game; keeping the progress, which would let a replace move a nearly finished
quest aside; a draw that may return the replaced quest, which would spend the day's replace on
nothing; restarting the arrival clock, which would make a replace delay the next quest; and banking
unused replaces, which would let an account away for a week reroll its whole log at once.
