## R783000 — A Ranged attacker takes no strike back

Built (#783, part 1 of #781; no card prints it until part 2). An attacker with Ranged, read through
the layers ([[§10.4]]), takes no damage back from the unit it attacks unless that unit has Ranged
too: its strike lands alone, so no strike back of any kind reaches it, a First Strike one included,
and nothing of the defender's Poisonous or Lifesteal comes of a hit it never made. A forced attack is
still an attack ([[R53]]), so the same holds there. Against a Ranged defender the exchange of
[[§4.3]] is unchanged: First Strike, the simultaneous step, Poisonous and the rest. Only combat's
strike back changes; damage from effects, Traps and Armor do not. One function answers it
(`combat::takes_strike_back`), read by the combat and by the lethal projection alike
(`subsystems::lethal`: a Ranged attacker never falls to a First Strike defender there, and the
defender's Lifesteal heals nothing), so the AI's lethal check counts what the combat does.

The AI reads the same function where it weighs a trade (#841): its move order (`candidates.rs`)
ranks a Ranged attacker's kill of a unit that is not Ranged as one it survives, and the opponent's
reply (`reply.rs`) prices it with no strike back. The Mayor's reroll ranking still assumes a strike
back: it only ranks moves `legal_actions` lists, so nothing illegal can come of it.
