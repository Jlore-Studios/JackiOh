"Float reduces cost" ([[R1563000]]) and "Float reduces Tribute requirement by 1" ([[R1841003]]) are hand triggers alone ([[R464]]): a Float of its owner's lowers the card's cost, or its Tribute X, only while the card is in their hand. A copy in the deck takes nothing, so it arrives at its printed cost and Tribute. What a Float took off in the hand stays with the card until a graveyard or an exile takes it off ([[R766]]), as before. Through v0.5.9f each card was a deck trigger too, so a Float lowered every copy in the deck at once; the scripts kept for those versions keep the deck trigger ([[R767000]]). This is the owner's balance pass (issue #2657).
