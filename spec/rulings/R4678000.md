Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state or event changes. [[R4678001]] says what its one view reads.

**Why.** The owner wants a place on `/stats` for figures that span patches and versions (issue #4671): first Running ([[R4678001]]), each card's current version over every patch since it last changed, then every older version as its own row and a Hall of Fame. No figure there is one patch's or one face's, so they need a tab of their own, not a control on the Cards tab under a patch bar that would misname them.

**The tab.** The page has five tabs: Cards, Tokens, Sets, Players and Experimental, after Players. It is a raised button like the others, its name alone ([[R4279001]], [[R4335000]]), and the arrow keys move between all five, wrapping at either end. `?tab=experimental` opens it, and opening it writes that address. It is called Experimental, not Legacy, which is a card name and tag in Training Grounds ([[R2511000]]).

**Its views.** It holds one Segmented of its views, in the order they land: Running, then the older versions, then the Hall of Fame. The Segmented lists only views that are built, and shows only once there are two: this part ships Running alone, so the tab shows Running with no Segmented. Its controls are the page's own ([[R3324003]]).

**Above the panel.** The patch bar with its search and its refused patch's line ([[R4677000]], [[R4677001]]), the games tile ([[R3447000]]) and the Face control ([[R4673001]]) each name a patch or a face, which no figure on this tab is read on, so none is shown on it. In their place, above the tab row, one line says what the tab shows: "Every card's current version, over every patch since it last changed". The patch and the face stay the page's, in its address and on the other tabs, so leaving the tab finds them as they were.

**One screen.** The line takes less room than the bar and the tile it replaces, so the page stays one screen at every menu size ([[R1581000]]). The tab row stays one line: where the five tabs are wider than the window it scrolls sideways, as [[R4335000]] lets it, and never wraps or shrinks. Spec 46 holds, at every menu size, that the row shows its tabs whole, that the Experimental tab is drawn inside the window once scrolled to, and that the page on it is one screen.

Rejected:
- Running as a view of the Cards tab: its patch bar and games tile would say its figures are one patch's.
- A Segmented with one segment: a choice of one does nothing.
- Leaving the patch bar or Face shown but disabled: they would still name a patch or a face the figures are not read on.
- Shorter tab names, or hints back on a desktop, to fit five: the names say enough ([[R4335000]]), and the row scrolls.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
