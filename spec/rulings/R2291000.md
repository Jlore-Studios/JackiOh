[[R2102000]]'s lethal hint was a teaching aid of the Easy tier's free games alone. It now lights in every game the practice worker plays against the AI ([[§9.9]]): a free game at Easy, Medium or Hard, a sandbox game, a game with custom cards, a Try it game, a game on a URL or server seed, and a tutorial lesson ([[§9.10]]). Everything else is [[R2102000]]'s: the check on the human's own redaction ([[R185]]), its clock and its stream, `lethal: true` on the snapshot, and the Lethal hint setting. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the hint changes no game.

**Where it never shows.** Where no practice worker runs or no AI plays: an online match, a Duos game against the AI team included, which the server plays; a hotseat game; and a replay ([[R768]]).

**In a lesson.** It shows beside the coach, whose own advice is unchanged; a lesson cannot be lost either way ([[R2201000]]).

**Rejected.** Keeping it to Easy, which hid it from the harder tiers' players, who miss a lethal too; leaving out the lessons, the newest players' games.
