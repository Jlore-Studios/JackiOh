A travel is an Activate ♾️ ability ([[R384]]) whose price is `STARSCAPE_TRAVEL_MANA` (1), paid as it is activated. On the Radiant face the player's first travel each turn is free: "first" is counted per player, as Hearthstone counts "your first … each turn", so a travel by any card on that player's map spends it (`StarscapeMap.travelledOn`, the turn of the last travel). The free travel and the paid one are two abilities of the card that `has` switches between ([[R752]]): the free one while the player has not travelled this turn, the paid one once they have, so the card has exactly one at a time and `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal and the view read the same one ([[R43]]). The price is the engine's constant, not a number the card declares, so a Nerf or a Buff ([[R386]]) changes neither the price nor the free travel.
