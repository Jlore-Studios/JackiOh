Part 4 of #2725's ten new card versions (tracker #2729) adds the two that restyle the whole card.
Each is a `version-…` entry of the registry ([[R2725000]]), appended after part 3's three
([[R2942000]]), per card, owned card by card, never a default or a starter, and Legendary, so each is
a chase:

| Version | Id | Rarity | Chase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set showcase | `version-set-showcase` | Legendary | yes |
| Out of frame | `version-out-of-frame` | Legendary | yes |

**Offers.** The owner asked (2026-11-03) that each version go to a varied set of cards of its own,
not to every card nor to another version's cards, and #2725 §1 names the cards each suits: Set
showcase each set's signature cards, Out of frame Units with a strong subject. Each offer is data
([[R2725001]]), its one clause sampling a seeded share by the version's own id as its seed
([[R2949001]]):

- **Set showcase**: 60 in 100 of the Legendary and Mythic cards, every type, each set's signature
  cards. 68 cards when it shipped, 2 of Meditative+'s its fewest in a set.
- **Out of frame**: 45 in 100 of the Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic Units, whose picture has a
  subject to step out of its window, but the twenty Legacy cards (`traininggrounds-041` to `060`) and
  the Dormant Starship (`stinkyscape-t-dormant-starship`), whose pictures have none of their own
  ([[R2724000]], [[R2249000]]). 73 cards, 3 of Meditative+'s its fewest.

Neither takes a Common card, and Out of frame no Spell, Trap or field card, so cards get different
mixes. Each reaches `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_CARDS` in all and `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET` in every set
that ships, neither is offered on every card, and no two of the nine versions (Full art, part 2's
three, part 3's three and these two) take the same set of cards. Neither holds a card that no other
version takes, which the owner's rule does not ask. A version a card is not offered is never dropped,
sold, picked or shown for it: the Collection's strip does not list it, and a pick, a token or the
Chase Shard bar naming it there is a 400 that writes nothing ([[R2725001]]). No retired full art
implies one of them ([[R2725002]]), and Signature fits neither ([[R2949000]]).

**Granted to no account.** Like every cosmetic added since #1833 (§14.12), the two start unowned on
every account, existing ones included; `POST /api/dev/fill` grants each on every card it is offered,
and `jackioh-server seed-catalog` upserts them into `public.cosmetics` as it does every registry
entry, so no migration is needed. A Legendary cosmetic token or the Chase Shard bar buys one on a card
it is offered. The scale stays Common to Mythic ([[R2725000]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a version, and nothing enters `PlayerView`'s
rules, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725000]] (two more versions) and [[R2725001]] (two more offers, each a seeded share of its
own).
