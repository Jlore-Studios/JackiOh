**Why.** A tier ([[R3022000]]) says where a card's adjusted rate sits, not how firmly. A B whose range spans C to A and a B whose range lies wholly inside B read the same. The owner asked in #3184 for the share of the range inside the tier.

**The figure.** A tier's band comes from the response's `tiers.bandLow` and `tiers.bandHigh`: S from `bandHigh` up, F from `bandLow` down, and A, B, C and D the band's quarters from the top. The fit is the overlap of the card's Adjusted 95% range ([[R2895000]], `adjustedLow` to `adjustedHigh`) with its tier's band, over the range's width:

```
fit = overlap([adjustedLow, adjustedHigh], [tierLow, tierHigh]) / (adjustedHigh − adjustedLow)
```

- **A range of no width** is its one point, the rate: 100% inside the band and 0% outside it.
- **An A or a D beyond the band** whose range still reaches 50% ([[R3022000]]) is measured against its own quarter all the same, so it reads a low fit, which is what it is.
- **No fit** for a card below [[R654]]'s floor, with no tier, with no Adjusted range, or from a server that sends no band.

The client computes it (`stats/tierFit.ts`) from figures the server sent. It is arithmetic on two intervals the server decided, not a statistic of the client's, so it decides nothing (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**Where it shows.** With Show 95% ranges on, under each card's tier chip, to a whole percent, styled as a range is, in the table and the grid. The ranges' line under the cards says what it means.

**The order.** Sorting by Tier is by tier first, as [[R3022000]] has it, then by the fit, the higher first descending, a card with none after those with one either way, and last by the adjusted rate. The fit orders the cards whether or not the ranges show. This amends [[R2895000]]'s "the cards sort by their figures, not by their ranges": the tier's order now reads the range, through the fit.

Rejected: the share of the posterior's probability inside the band (the range read as a normal), a better measure but a statistic the server does not send and the client should not compute ([[R2895000]]); showing the fit with the ranges hidden, which crowds the Tier column; and the rounded percent as the sort key, which ties more cards. No Hearthstone rule is involved.
