**Why.** With [[R5271000]] in place, staging's deploy of `06d34f1` still failed its statistics check on 2026-11-28 (deploy-staging run 46116907371), though the check now followed the load. Its first read of `GET /api/stats/cards?mode=all` timed out at 75 s. `GET /api/stats/loading` then answered every 10 s, reporting the load for over three minutes. Once the load ended, the second read timed out at 75 s too. Under [[R5225000]] a window's cut began only in the read that waited on the snapshot. The first read had given up before the snapshot landed, so no cut was under way, and the second read waited on a whole cut on staging's quarter of a processor.

**The shared window.** The snapshot's load ([[R3067000]]) cuts one window itself, at its last step, before it counts as done: the build's version, the default sources, both seats merged and every match type. `/stats`'s first read asks for that window, and so do the patch list every read of the page lists, and every match's win-rate snapshot ([[R3049000]]). The snapshot holds it, and the first read that asks for it fills its kept cell at once. `GET /api/stats/loading` reports the load until the window is cut ([[R5186000]]), so the next read after the load answers without a cut. A version the snapshot does not hold as filed is cut by its read, as before. Every other window is cut on a task of its own, as [[R5225000]] says.
