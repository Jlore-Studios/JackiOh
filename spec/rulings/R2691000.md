The practice AI decides from what its seat may know ([[R185]]), and its owner's list shows each card
of its own library as it went in ([[R310]]): a change made there, where nobody reads it, does not show
until the card leaves ([[R311]], [[R386]]).

**The rule.** When the AI redacts the state it decides from ([[R185]]), each card of the seat's own
library that has a `knownAs` record and is not hidden (the opponent's cards, [[R73]]; the cards it was
never shown, [[R312]]) becomes a placeholder and then takes the record's definition and face, keeping
the record and its Created mark ([[R943]]). So Selective Breeding's (#42) and Knockoff Temu Glowy Jelly Bean's
(#28) Radiant rolls, a Nerf or a Buff ([[R386]]) and a discount made inside the library never reach a
decision, as [[R1845000]] already keeps a hidden Nerf from every seat. The record stays, so redacting
twice is redacting once, and the library's order stays hidden: `determinize` shuffles it.

**The exception.** A card the seat's own open prompt offers (#51's reveal from the library) keeps its
live face, since that prompt shows it in full ([[§10.8]]) and the AI picks from what the prompt shows.

**Rejected alternatives.**
- Hiding the whole own library, as the dry run does ([[R222]]): it throws away what the owner was
  legitimately shown, and every draw of its own becomes a sample.
- Keeping a change the owner saw before the card went back into the library (a discount seen in hand):
  the list shows only the definition and the face, and [[R312]] lets it show less than its owner could
  piece together, never more.
