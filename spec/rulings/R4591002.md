IA #152 Rate Limiter's "your opponent can't attack with more than three Units each turn" (two on the Radiant face) is an attack-count limit (`StaticFlags.opponent_attack_limit`), counted in turn order across the field: a Unit already counted may attack again (Windfury), and Units that attacked and then left the field still count. Only declared attacks are counted or refused; forced attacks ([[R53]]) are neither, and spring nothing ([[R121]]). Rejected: counting forced attacks, which would punish the defender for the attacker's compulsion. With several limits, the lowest holds.
