The owner's answer to #2725's second question: Signature (`augment-signature`) stays its own Mythic augment,
owned card by card, and includes neither Full art nor Animated, each of the three owned and pulled on its own;
but a card wears it only beside the Full art version and the Animated augment, and with it on the face shows a
look of its own.

**The rule.** A card's pick wears Signature only where it also wears the Full art version and Animated, each
owned and offered on that card ([[R2725001]]). Wearing the version is a pick of `version-full-art`, of `true`
(a full-art card's still Epic) or of a retired full art that implies it ([[R2725002]]); wearing Animated is
listing it, or a retired full art that implies it (the Legendary and the Mythic). The server reads this in one
place, `cosmetics::signature_fits`, and the client in one, `signatureFits` (versions.ts), each reading no
ownership or offer; the callers hold those.

- `PUT /api/card-picks` whose `augments` lists Signature is a 400, `"augments" lists Signature, which needs the
  Full art version and Animated on this card`, and writes nothing, unless the card's pick, once changed, wears
  both, each owned and offered there ([[R1834001]]). A `PUT` that does not name Signature but leaves a held one
  without them (turning the version or Animated off) drops it and writes the rest, so a stale device's write is
  never refused for a pick it did not send.
- The device's one-time upload keeps Signature only beside both, each kept.
- A match freezes Signature only beside the version and Animated as frozen (`freeze_loadout`), the version read
  where it is owned and offered and the Full-art cards setting where the card has no pick of its own, so the
  other seat, a watcher and a replay's viewer never wear it without them ([[R1834002]]).
- The client: `lookOf` signs only where the pick fits; a write that leaves Signature without both drops it
  (`updateCardPicks`), as the server does; the Collection lists Signature on every card it is offered and, until
  the card wears both, marks it waiting (`data-waits`, "Needs Full art and Animated") and equips nothing on a
  click; a reveal's Equip offers it only on a card that wears both.

**The Signature look.** With Signature on, the face is the **Signature look**: the Full art face, its art drawn
by the card's own painter seeded apart (`artSeedOf(id, "signature")`, `data-art-picture="signature"`), so its
picture is none of the card's Full art, Animated or normal pictures nor any other version's, and moving by a
motion of its own (`SIGNATURE_MOTION`, its subject breathing, its back drifting and silver ink rising), which no
Animated motion is; the signature is signed over it as on Full art before ([[R2715002]]). Like every look it is
a pure function of the card's id, name, tags and type, base and Radiant faces both, and [[R2725004]] holds: its
ink is the signature's silver, no gold, and a Radiant face keeps every Radiant mark. It holds still under
reduced motion and with Animated foil off as the Animated augment does ([[R2725003]]).

**Never elsewhere.** No face signs on the normal face (the signature over its art window, [[R2725003]]'s, is
gone) or on any other version, the coming Alt full art (#2940) among them.

**Retired full arts.** A retired Mythic still counts as owning all three on its card ([[R2725002]]), so its
pick, and the Full art version with both augments, now shows the Signature look; nobody gains or loses a
cosmetic (§14.12). Presentation only: no rule reads it, and nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or
a replay hash (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Amends [[R2725000]] (Signature is no longer layered on any version), [[R2725002]] (the Mythic's look is now the
Signature look) and [[R2725003]] (Signature never on the normal face).
