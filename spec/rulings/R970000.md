`/replays` renders outside the gate, because its practice half needs no account ([[R768000]]), so the consent screen ([[R757000]], [[R757001]]) never met it, and an account that owed the terms could list its online games there and share one with a friend. An account that owes the age check or the terms in force now sees the practice half as anyone does and, in place of its online half, a line with Continue, which opens the consent screen as the gate shows it. Its online games, "Shared with me" and every "Share with a friend" wait until it answers there; the answer counts at once on the screen, as it does at the gate. A player who will not agree can still watch the practice games this device kept, or sign out from the consent screen.

R757001 rejected refusing an owing account on the server, so the client is the one place agreement is asked, and a signed-in screen outside the gate asks it itself. Rejected: putting the whole route behind the gate, which would close the practice half to a signed-in player who has not agreed when it is open to anyone signed out.
