The issue asked that most of the new looks be a new picture of the card's subject, as Alt art is, and a
few a restyling of the card's own, as the Sketch, Pixel and Comic are; each drawn by code in
`apps/web/src/cards/art/`, a pure function of the card's id, name, tags and type, base and Radiant, with
a delivered art file taking over later as ART.md says.

**Repaints and restyles.** Four of part 6's variants are repaints: the card's own painter (procedural,
Stinkyscape or Training Grounds) draws a new picture of its subject seeded by the card's id and the
picture's name (`core-022#night`), as Alt art's is ([[R2940001]]), and a treatment of its own lies over
it: **Night**, the picture dulled, dimmed and mixed toward a night sky, under a moon in its glow and
stars at fixed spots high up; **Watercolor**, its edges run by noise, softened and thinned onto paper,
grained; **Stained-glass window**, its colour pushed, brightened and cut to Pixel's 27 colours
([[R2942001]]), lit from behind, leaded along every edge between its panes and along a noise contour
that parts the broad ones; and **Tarot card**, its colour muted under a blue and pale duotone, in an arched border on two
pillars with a sun and a crescent in its spandrels and stars, no words. Two restyle the card's own
picture, seeded by its id alone: **Woodcut**, greyed and cut to three tones of ink and paper, its middle
tone gouged by long strokes; and **Neon sign**, a glowing tube along every step of its smoothed grey,
violet where the picture is dark and cyan where it is light, on a dark wall that shows the picture
faintly. Each is one `<svg>` and names no text, title or reference.

A Legacy card, whose painter draws its Core card's one picture ([[R2724000]]), and a Starship in play
([[R2249000]]) keep that picture under each treatment, as under the Sketch. A delivered file is the
normal picture's, so every part 6 look draws the painter's instead.

**Levels.** Every level of a variant draws its first level's picture ([[R3015002]]): the animated level
moves the picture's own layers under the same treatment, taking a motion slot and holding still under
reduced motion, with Animated foil off and on a lazy face. The normal frame stays, so a foil, a finish
and a border layer on each level as on the normal face ([[R2725003]]), and no level signs.

**No gold.** Every colour the six name is palette.css's, none in the Radiant gold window: the slates,
`--pal-blue-900`, `--pal-violet-700`, `--pal-purple-400`, `--pal-cyan-400` and white. So are the
colours their tone maps draw along their range, the 27 panes lit and under their lead, the tubes and
their glow over the wall, and the wall showing any picture at its share. A Radiant face's painted
picture is washed back over each treatment, so its Radiant paint shows, and it keeps every mark of its
own; a base face shows none ([[R2725004]]).

Rejected: a golden-hour scene, which sits in Radiant's gold window; a chibi take and a set's home
location, which need new subject drawing in every painter; a blueprint for constructs and Starships,
which needs an offer by tag; and frames of their own, which need more of `cards.css` for each.

Amends [[R2725004]] (six more looks, none gold) and [[R3015002]] (six more variants, one picture each).
