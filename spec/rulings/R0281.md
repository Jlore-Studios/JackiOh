title: Radiant Quickstriker's damage is one hit

proven_in:

[[§8]] #38's Radiant face grants "Combo X: deal 2X damage to the enemy hero", and 2X is dealt as one damage instance ([[§4.4]]), so Armor and Anti-oneshot Armor's cap apply to it once, as they do to the base face's X. Each Quickstriker on the field still grants its own Combo ([[R102]], [[R119]]), each at its own face's multiple, so a base and a Radiant Quickstriker together deal X and then 2X. A card fused from Quickstrikers has one face ([[R77]]), so each Combo its texts grant is at that face's multiple: X each on its base face, 2X each on its Radiant one
