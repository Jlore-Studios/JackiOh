**Why.** An All Random deck is dealt ([[R258]]). A card dealt and never drawn had no part in its game, yet it counted as one of the card's games and pulled every card's win rate toward its decks' average. The owner asked in #2777 that a card count in the random pool only once it is seen.

**The rule.** Under `?mode=random`, a card's own figures count only its seen games: the games it reached its owner's hand, from the opening hand or a draw, played or not. That is [[R2427000]]'s drawn side, and the engine's `seen`. The seen games decide:
- **`games`**, and so the sample floor `hasEnoughGames` ([[R654]]);
- **`winRate`**;
- **`playRate`**, seen games over the counted decks;
- **the drill-down's per-patch history** ([[R2491000]]).

A deck that was dealt the card and never drew it is not a game of the card's. It remains the never-drawn baseline the drawn delta and the adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]) compare against.

Every other mode counts every deck that held the card, as before: a Constructed player chose it, so holding it is a choice the figures measure. The breakdowns (opening hand, played, drawn but not played, the faces) are unchanged in every mode.

Rejected:
- Counting only played games. A card can matter from the hand without being played, a Trap's threat among them, and a play is conditioned on the board ([[R2427000]]).
- Applying the rule to Constructed too. Holding a card there is the player's choice.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
