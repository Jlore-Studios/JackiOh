[[R186]]'s sweep runs in two passes, so the AI is less inclined to ban a card and deals the cards on track to be banned more often. Pass 1 is the sweep as it was, over every non-token card of every set. A card is at risk when a pass's numbers meet a flag's condition at half strength — affordable on `minAffordableTurns` turns and played at most once, an average evaluation change below half of `selfHarmDelta`, or one play on which the AI lost the game — or when it is on the current ban or on `SHADOW_WATCH`, the cards an earlier sweep found at risk and cleared, which the next sweep counts as at risk from the start. A card's average evaluation change is taken over its plays after which the game went on: `evaluate` scores a finished game at ±`win` (1,000,000), so one play that ended the game decided the mean alone, either way (#623). Such a play still counts in `plays`; the AI winning on it counts in `winningPlays` and losing on it in `losingPlays` (a draw in neither), and losing the game on its own plays is a flag of its own, `selfKill`, raised at `minLosingPlays` (2). A slice written before the two counts reads them as 0, so it still joins a report. Pass 2 sweeps only the at-risk cards, `seedsPerCardAtRisk` (24) games each per tier, and in every pass-2 game the AI's filler draw multiplies at-risk cards' weights by `atRiskBoost` (4), as `buildAiDeck`'s theme boost does, so an at-risk card is dealt as the forced card of its own games and as filler in everyone else's, and its numbers add up over every game it was dealt in. A `neverPlayed` ban needs pass-2 evidence at that tier — 6 affordable turns (not 3) and no play in any pass-2 game — and a `selfHarm` ban 8 plays (not 4), and a `selfKill` ban 4 lost games (not 2); `error` and `timeout` ban as before, being bugs and not judgement. Pass 2's filler lifts the ban for at-risk cards banned `neverPlayed`, `selfHarm` or `selfKill` and keeps out cards banned `error` or `timeout`, so a known bug is never filler; an `error` or `timeout` bans only its game's forced card, is listed against any at-risk filler in that game as a `suspect`, and bans that filler only if its own forced games repeat it. Pass 1's at-risk list is a pure function of pass 1's results and pass 2's seeds are named, so slices still run in parallel and a sweep of record replays. Rewarding the AI's search for playing at-risk cards was rejected: it would measure a different AI from the one that plays. The ban's scope is unchanged ([[R186]])
