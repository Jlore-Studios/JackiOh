Presentation only, as [[R979000]] and [[R2975000]] are (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no state, event, replay, listing or AI decision changes, only what `viewFor` carries and what the fuzz monitor checks.

An Activate ability's `usable` came from its own refusal alone (its turn, uses, condition and costs, [[R384]]), but `legalActions` also drops an ability with no choice combination, a required pick the board cannot answer ([[R703]]), and one whose every combination costs discards the hand cannot pay ([[R450]], [[R682]]). Such an ability read usable with no reason, so its greyed button said only that it could not be activated right now. S #112 Job Application's Upload Resume with an empty hand is one.

`usable` now holds exactly when `legalActions` lists an `activate` of the ability, both read off the same list of what it offers. When none is listed, `reason` is the ability's own refusal; failing that, it is `reduce`'s refusal of the first choice combination the ability offers, carrying its first Tribute set, or, with no combination at all, of the ability sent with no picks: "Job Application cannot be played without 1 legal target", and for an ability that takes two enemy Units whose only ones are two C #89 Paul Allen's Ghosts, "targeting that costs 4 discards, and you hold 3 cards". The words are the reducer's, shared with plays. No reason an ability already gave changes, and none comes from a hidden fact ([[§9.1]]).

The fuzz monitor's I15 holds every sampled state to this: while a seat may act (R979000's window), each Activate ability its view shows on a unit or a backrow top of either side is `usable` exactly when `legalActions` lists an `activate` of it, and carries `reason` exactly when it is not. It reads the view and listing I6 already builds, so it is sampled with I6, as I13 is ([[R2975000]]). A carried Unit ([[R446]]) is not asked: the listing never walks one, and I8 holds what `reduce` accepts of it.

Rejected: a second view and listing for I15, which would double I6's cost; a reason helper restating the discard and Tribute filters, which could drift from the reducer, where putting each combination to `reduce`'s refusal cannot; activation-only wording ("cannot be activated without …"), since the target refusal is shared with plays and is the reducer's own; and `usable` from whether the reducer accepts some combination, which would hide a drift between the listing and the reducer from I15.
