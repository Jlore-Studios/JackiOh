**Why.** [[R4707000]]'s record of what each play rolled is purged with the record ([[R1044001]]), so it must reach the totals ([[R4672003]]), and the owner reads each outcome against the card's other outcomes, beside what the card's table says it should come up: "is this outcome better than this card's other outcomes", and a rare, swingy outcome shown for what it is.

**The totals.** Each card's choices ([[R4672003]]) gain `rolled`, by group, `<kind>:<card>` (the card whose table, coins or dice they are: the played card, or an edition its Recursion cast, [[R28]]), each split by face as every figure is:

- `rolls`: how many rolls of the group the card's plays made.
- per outcome (an entry's place, `<heads>/<coins>`, `hit` or `miss`, or a small die's face): `games`, the seats it came up for, a seat once a game as every figure counts it; `rolled`, how many times it came up; `expected`, how many times its table expected it to, summed roll by roll in whole millionths (`ROLL_EXPECTED_SCALE`), so rolls at different Lucky, faces or table sizes add exactly; and `label`, its words, the newest folded roll's.

A roll expects every outcome its table could have given it, so an outcome that never came up still has its expectation. A variant of rolls alone is no choice and adds no variant row ([[R4676000]]).

**The expected share** of one roll, as the card's table predicts it:

- an entry of a uniform table: the entries rolled over its size, one in N, three in ten for each entry of a Radiant Call to Chaos's three different of ten ([[R60]], [[R423]]);
- a batch of `n` coins landing `h` heads: the binomial chance, each coin heads with `1 − (1/2)^(1+X)` at Lucky X ([[R1440]]): a fair coin one half, Lucky 1 three in four, six fair coins three heads 20 in 64;
- a die checked for faces: the chance any of its dice lands there, each die's chance those faces over its sides raised by Lucky as a coin's is: Dice of Doom's 67 one in a hundred, its Radiant face's two d80s `1 − (79/80)²`, Unreal Estate's 1 to 50 one half;
- a small die's face: its dice over its sides.

No card's table prints other weights; a printed chance is the weighted case.

**A row's figures** (`rolled_figures`), on the face asked ([[R4673001]]'s `face`): one row per outcome of the card's groups its rolls on that face came up with or expected, that has words (an entry of a table that never came up on any face has none, and no row):

- **games**: the seats it came up for.
- **share**: how often it came up over its group's rolls, and **expected**, its expected count over them, both to three places. A share is per roll, not per seat, so a seat that rolled twice in a game makes a fair table read fair.
- **adjusted**, its range and **tier**: [[R4679000]]'s reading, the outcome's seats against its **siblings**', the seats its group's other outcomes came up for, every outcome's added less its own; against the rolled field (`rolled_field`), [[R3022000]]'s estimate over every rolled outcome of every card of the report the catalog holds on the same face, whatever the page filters, from the outcomes with `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games on both sides; no rate when its siblings are below the floor, no tier and "not enough games" below its own ([[R654]]).

**The order.** Best adjusted first, a row with no rate or below the floor after every row with one, then by games, then by table and key.

**The API.** `GET /api/stats/cards/:id` answers `rolled`, one group per kind the card has, `effect`, `coin` then `die` (`RolledKind`), each `kind` and `rows`, each row `key` (`<table>:<outcome>`), `label` (an edition's own entry, a Recursion's cast's, named `<edition>: <clause>`), `expected`, `share`, `games`, `adjustedWinRate`, `adjustedLow`, `adjustedHigh`, `tier` and `hasEnoughGames`, over the window and face `outputs` reads ([[R4679000]], [[R4677000]]). A card that rolled nothing answers an empty list; an older client ignores the field.

**What it still measures.** A large gap between expected and observed, past what its rolls allow, is a bug in the engine or its table, not a balance figure. Seats overlap as [[R4679000]]'s do, a Radiant Call to Chaos's three entries each counting its seat. A label is the clause the newest folded roll named, in the event's words (YileGPT Prime's are built as it resolves), not a table read at read time.

Rejected:

- **Rows inside `outputs`**: an output is a card, its row opens its drill-down and carries a cost and rarity; an outcome has neither.
- **A share per seat-game**: a seat that rolls twice reads twice as often, and a fair table would look biased.
- **An expectation from the table at read time**: one figure for rolls at different Lucky, faces and table sizes would be wrong for all of them, and the server holds no card's table.
- **Hiding an outcome that never came up**: Dice of Doom's 67 at 1% would vanish from a small sample, which is the case the expected share is for.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
