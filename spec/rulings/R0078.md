Leaving the field resets an instance's damage, buffs, granted keywords, Vanilla flag, counters, memory (its Activate uses included, [[R384]]), exertion, position, summonedTurn, `statsOverride`, `tauntSuppressedTurn` and controller; `costMod`, `costOverride`, `radiant`, and since patch v0.2.0 `tuning` ([[R386]]), the Brittle count ([[R385]]), the times-played count ([[R429]]) and an enchantment that rides the card (C+ #14, [[R410]]) persist in every zone. Animating is not leaving the field: a move between a backrow zone and a unit zone resets nothing ([[R383]]), nor does a carried Unit stepping down when its carrier leaves ([[R446]]), as the Unit stacked onto C+ #33 Ivory Tower does should the Tower leave before that Unit's play resolves; a Tower that leaves forgets the Unit it took, so back on the field it may take another ([[R653]]). Effects that react to a card leaving (Death, Corpse Eater, Carnivorous Cube's meal) read its last-known state from just before it left. A Reborn unit returns reset, at 1 health, without Reborn
