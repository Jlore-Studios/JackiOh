The brief of #1651 (its §8) gives a duplicate's cosmetic roll ([[§14.3]], [[R1301002]]) a chance at
an account cosmetic, the issue's reading being that a hit replaces the duplicate's per-card cosmetic
and leaves the rest of the dupe chain as it is:

- **When.** A duplicate, as it makes its cosmetic roll, first looks for an account cosmetic the
  account does not own at or above `GLOBAL_COSMETIC_FLOOR_RARITY`: a `table`, `field` or `cardBack`
  that is neither its kind's default nor a starter (`cosmetics::global_cosmetics`) and that the
  account holds no ledger row for. With one, it draws one integer below `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL`.
- **The rate.** A draw below `GLOBAL_COSMETIC_ROLL_WEIGHT` (100 of 1,000, so 10%) is a hit. A miss
  rolls the per-card cosmetic as [[R1301002]] says.
- **A hit** is a tiered roll ([[R1339000]]) on `COSMETIC_TIER_WEIGHTS` floored at
  `GLOBAL_COSMETIC_FLOOR_RARITY`, Epic (Epic 950, Legendary 45, Mythic 5), falling as a cosmetic
  pack's floored slot does ([[R1306001]]): its tier, then each lower one from the nearest down,
  then each higher one from the nearest up, every tier at or above the floor before any below it,
  where nothing unowned is ([[R1661000]]). The first tier with one unowned gives one, picked
  uniformly in registry order. The slot grants `SlotGrant::Cosmetic` with that cosmetic and its
  rarity, `{ kind: 'cosmetic', cosmeticId, cosmeticRarity }` on the wire as before, owned for the
  account on no card: a cosmetics ledger row with no card, reason `pack_open`, its `ref` the
  opening's log. A Legendary or Mythic one is a chase, which drops like any other ([[R1333000]]).
- **What it leaves alone.** Pity reads the slots' tiers alone ([[§14.4]], [[R1339002]]); a new card
  unlocks as before and takes no global draw; the hit is not a spent duplicate, so it adds no bar
  points ([[§14.5]]); and a miss is the dupe chain of [[R1301002]] unchanged.
- **Slot by slot.** An account cosmetic dropped earlier in the pack is owned in its later slots,
  so it drops once, and once none is left unowned the later duplicates take no global draw.
- **No draw when nothing can drop.** With nothing left unowned the draw is not taken at all, so a
  duplicate's draws are what they were. The shipped registry holds no account cosmetic that is not
  a starter ([[R1661000]]), so every opening today draws exactly as it did before #1661, and the
  roll waits for the first account cosmetic added without the starter mark.
- **The draw order** (amending [[R1339002]]): each slot's tier, in slot order; pity, which takes
  none; then, slot by slot, the card's one and, for a duplicate, the global draw when an account
  cosmetic is left unowned, then on a hit its tier's and its pick's, and on a miss (or with no
  global draw) its cosmetic tier's and the pick's when there is something to pick. The opening's
  logged draw count counts them all ([[§14.11]]).
- **The log and its replay.** The log's `cosmetics` hold the per-card entries a duplicate may drop,
  then the account ones the global roll may, each in registry order, so `roll_opening` over them
  and the holdings before the opening, the account cosmetics owned among them, replays it
  ([[R1301003]]). An opening logged before #1661 replays under the roll of its day ([[R1339002]]);
  on the shipped registry the two rolls are one.
- **Published.** `GET /api/packs` answers `odds.globalCosmetic`, `{ chance, tiers }`: `chance` the
  hit's share, `GLOBAL_COSMETIC_ROLL_WEIGHT` over `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL`, and `tiers` the floored
  table's odds, counted over every draw as the other odds are ([[R1339000]]). The client computes
  no rate (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: as [[R1301002]], a duplicate becomes something the account does not have
yet; the spec lets it become an account-wide cosmetic as well as a card's, at a published rate.

Rejected: always taking the draw, which would move every duplicate's draws while nothing can drop;
a hit that falls below the floor, which no unowned account cosmetic can be; and a field of its own
in the log for the account entries, which a replay does not need, since it reads each entry's kind.
