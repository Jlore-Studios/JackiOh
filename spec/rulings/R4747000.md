A card's keyword line ([[§5.4]], [[R366]]: its keywords first, as one comma-separated list on a
line of its own, with a Tribute or Echo count sharing it) prints its items in one fixed display
order, in three tiers: **economy** (cost, card flow and how or where a card is played), then
**powerful effects and drawbacks**, then **combat** (in the order a fight reads: when it attacks,
where it reaches, what its hits do, how it survives). The order is a display call, not Hearthstone
semantics, so it is a ruling here.

In full, with the issue's numbering (which skips 27): Tribute X (1) and Echo X (2) first, then
Tradable (3), Flashback (4), Temporary X (5), Cloaked (6), Equip (7), Stack (8), Flexible (9), GIGA
(10), Woozy X (11), Spell Damage +X (12), Lucky X (13), Indestructible (14), Immutable (15), Immune
to Spells (16), Untributable (17), Evasive (18), Can't attack (19), Can't Defend (20), Brittle X
(21), Crater (22), Animated (23), Animated on your turn (24), Starship Piece (25), Starship (26),
Charge (28), Rush (29), Windfury (30), Frenzy (31), Deft (32), Taunt (33), Flying (34), Ranged (35),
First Strike (36), Pierce (37), Trample (38), Cleave (39), Poisonous (40), Toxic (41), Burn (42),
Lifesteal (43), Divine Shield (44), Armor X (45), Reborn (46).

Tribute X and Echo X are not keywords; they already share the line ([[R366]]) and keep its head. A
unit that holds a numbered keyword more than once (Lucky, Armor, Spell Damage) keeps its entries
together at their kind's place. Evolve never prints as a bare word on the keyword line, only as
"Evolve:" ability text ([[§6.1]] Evolve), so it has no place in the order; the retired Immune to
tribal tag based hate (`KeywordKind::RETIRED`) is left out too. The engine's keyword set
(`layers::as_set`) keeps first-seen order: only the printed line and the face model sort, so no
game plays differently. Rejected: first-seen order, under which the same keywords read differently
from card to card ("Flying, Ranged" on one face, "Ranged, Flying" on another).
