"Heal X" is X even on an undamaged unit; heal to full or up to is what it would restore (0 is no heal). Would-die is [[§4.5]] step 1's collection only, never a Sacrifice. A unit token never reaches a graveyard ([[R11]]). A follow-up is owed work resolving after the effect that replaced, before queued triggers
