**Why.** A candidate's simulated step pays `AI_SEARCH.expensive_def_nodes` for each expensive card (`expensive_def_ids`) its events announce, and answers the opponent's prompts the step opens. An answer that starts a chain of such casts (a cast answered by the other seat's cast, each announcing one) can cost more than the decision's whole budget: on the sweep's seed `sweep2:easy:infiniteassault-035:7` every answer to a free "Cast: Polymorph" target prompt drained the beam's slice before its first child stood, so the beam closed no line and the AI fell back to the first candidate, an error the gates and the sweep count (B31, [[R4801000]]).

**The rule.** When the beam took at least one node and returned no line, each candidate is stepped once on the determinization the beam searched (`reduce`, its prompts left open, no node charged), and the one whose position `evaluate_priced` scores highest for the seat is taken, the first listed on a tie, with reason `search` and the beam's stop reason. A candidate the engine refuses or that panics is skipped; when every candidate is, the decision falls back as before. A beam given no node at all still falls back (B15).
