When the turn clock runs out ([[R79]]) on a question of a sequence ([[R1184000]]), the engine answers it with Stop (`prompts::stop_answer`), not with a uniform random answer, as it leaves a night market ([[R1002]]): the sequence ends with the right answers so far, their Lil' Kenny is summoned, nothing is drawn for the answer, and the turn then ends as any timeout ends it. A single `answer` prompt (C+ #42's) is answered as before ([[§10.7]]). The random policy still answers a question uniformly from its five answers, Stop among them, until the sequence ends. Since #1563 a sequence's host also answers it Stop 60 seconds after its first question ([[R1563006]]).
