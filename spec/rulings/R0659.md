[[§6.3]]'s Transform replaces a card with a new instance, and [[R174]] counts the replaced card as having left the field; neither said whether the new body may attack the turn it appears. It may not: a Unit a Transform puts on the field is a new arrival, summoning sick this turn like a summoned card ([[§4.1]]), with fresh exertions and none of the old card's readiness, whoever's turn it is and whether or not the old card had attacked or could. Only text that says otherwise lifts it (C+ #73.1 Classic Golem's "may attack again this turn", [[R424]]). Hearthstone's Evolve keeps the original's readiness; this game does not, a balance call the designer may revisit as a revision
