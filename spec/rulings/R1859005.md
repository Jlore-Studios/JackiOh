Stinkyscape #136 Magic Module: "When launched, add a random Spell that deals damage to your hand." (ST-S02-5.) "A Spell that deals damage" is a non-token Spell the catalog marks `dealsDamage` ([[R1748001]]), Field Spells out. A catalog query names it with `dealsDamage: true`, and `false` forbids the mark; absent, the field filters nothing. The pool names no set, so it draws from the sets that ship ([[R380]], [[R1420]]), repeats allowed, a full hand burning the rest. The Radiant face adds two; its Spell Damage +2 (+4) is a printed keyword that goes with it into a host or the Starship.
