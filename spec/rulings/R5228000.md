Since [[R5183000]] each hit that a hero's Armor meets wears 1 off the Armor the hero holds itself ([[§4.4]] step 2), so Armor A is gone after A hits. The AI's count of the damage a board can deal past the Taunts in its way (`damage_past_taunts_with`, [[§9.9]]), which `face_threat`, the evaluation's threat and pressure terms, `wall_switches_priced` and the lethal solver's `ready_gap` read, still set every hit against the hero's whole Armor on one unchanged state. A hero at 6 with 3 Armor of its own facing four ready 3-attack units read 0, where the engine deals 0 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 and ends the game.

With the flag `armor_wear_read`, the walk's hits on the defending hero share one worn count per walk, read through the engine's own `subsystems::projected_hero_damage_worn` so the AI restates no part of step 2: each Trample excess past a Taunt (a Cleave Trample splash's included, [[R4805000]]) as it lands in the walk over the Taunts, then the free hits, smallest first. Smallest first is the attacker's best order under the wear, a larger hit meeting less Armor, so the free hits are not sorted again and the walk still reads the opponent's best line, with each of its orders ([[R3937000]]) counting its own wear. A Pierce hit neither meets nor wears the Armor ([[R346]]); an aura's Armor (Going Long) and a modifier's never wear; a hero immune to damage wears nothing. The flag is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick reads every board as before.

On the issue's board the walk now reads 6, the evaluation's threat turns lethal, the AI's 2/2 beside it is offered its switch to Defense Position (its Taunt takes the first hit and the other three deal 0 + 1 + 2 = 3), and from the attacker's side `ready_gap` reads 0 and the lethal solver proves the lethal. A Trample 4/4's 2 past a 0/2 Taunt is taken whole by Armor 3 and wears it, so two 5/5s after it deal 3 and 4: 7, where the walk read 4. With Going Long's 2 alone four 3/3s deal 1 each, 4, flag or none; with 1 Armor of the hero's own beside it the first hit wears that 1 and the rest meet Going Long's 2: 3, where the walk read 0. A Pierce 3/3 beside three 3/3s at Armor 3 deals 3 and leaves the Armor to the other three: 6, where the walk read 3. A hero immune to damage takes 0 and keeps its Armor. The engine deals each of these, as the proving tests play them out.

No rule of the game covers what the AI reads, so its reads follow the engine.

Rejected:

- a second copy of the wear rule in the AI: `damage::hero_hit_wearing` holds it once, and the engine's own `project_hit` reads it too;
- wearing in `hero_ticks`: a Burn ticks once before the swings and Toxic pierces, so neither meets the Armor twice;
- retuning `hero_armor`, the evaluation's price of a point of Armor: a tuning question for a screen of its own.

Not modelled: a converting hit (step 3a), which the walk skips as dealing nothing, wears the Armor in the engine but not in the walk; and a floor ([[R2201000]]) is read as it stands, so where the walk's hits would bring its cap to 0, after which the engine wears nothing, the walk still wears the Armor.

It reads only public state, and it adds no rule of the game and no constant: the wear of 1 is the engine's own.
