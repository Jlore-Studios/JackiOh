The AI's evaluation priced a unit by its body alone, so an Activate it can use every turn counted for nothing: Stinkyscape #52 Cannon v3, a (2) 0/4 whose Activate deals 5 damage to an enemy Unit once a turn ([[R384]], [[R1862000]]), read as a 0/4 wall. Given one removal, the AI took a bigger plain Unit and left the Cannon to destroy one of its Units every turn, and it gave its own Cannon away as cheaply. Only the opponent's reply saw an enemy Activate, and only once, in the turn it plays out (`visible_activation`); the board's evaluation, which the beam ranks by, never did. The learned network, fitted before Stinkyscape shipped, has no weight for it either. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, once, on the same probe world as [[R2424000]]'s Death prices and [[R2477000]]'s turn-hook prices (a function of what the seat sees, [[R185]]), the AI ends its own turn and then, untouched, the enemy's, on a copy with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), each turn end one `simulate` step that answers the other seat's prompts on the way. At the start of the enemy's turn it tries each enemy unit's Activates, and at the start of its own next turn each of its own units', as the opponent's reply tries a visible activation: only a card acting face-up on the field that is none of the cards the enemy holds unseen, at most `AI_REPLY.visible_activations` uses of it, the best by its controller's own evaluation when that beats standing still. A unit's own turn is where its uses are fresh, so a Cannon the seat already fired this turn is priced all the same. The price of a use is the controller's evaluation after it less before it, plus `AI_EVAL.next_turn_mana` for each crystal it gained (less for each it spent). A card is keyed by its definition, its face and its controller, since each copy aims at a different board; one the same as a card already tried is not tried again. `unit_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.activation` of the price, inside the Brittle share, since a card that crumbles takes its coming uses with it, for the seat's units and the enemy's alike, so `kill_worth` reads it through `unit_worth` and destroying an enemy Cannon is worth the Unit it would destroy. `AI_EVAL.activation` starts at 0.5, untuned, as [[R2477000]]'s weight did (at 0.5 the smoke gate read 20, 16 and 20 of 20, against random, against greedy and as Hard against Easy; at 0, 19, 16 and 20); the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. The probes run with no price passed, a Death and a turn-hook price included, and within `AI_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes ([[R186]]'s budget). The prices travel with that one decision, in the one value that carries [[R2424000]]'s and [[R2477000]]'s, passed by reference from its root through the search to `unit_worth` (`Prices`, #2582), so a call outside a decision is passed none and prices nothing.

**A Starship Piece.** Cannon v3 is a Starship Piece: when it dies, a copy of it fuses into its owner's Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]), which can launch and fire it again ([[R1751000]]). That Starship is a Dormant card, counted as before and never probed, so a destroy or a tribute of a Cannon still leaves most of it to its owner, and the AI weighs that as the engine plays it. A transform, or a card exiled instead of dying ([[R461]]), takes the Activate with it.

**What is not priced.**
- A face-down card, a Dormant card and a hand card. A face-up backrow card's Activate, a Field Spell's, which this ruling left out, [[R2603000]] prices.
- A use that leaves a prompt open, ends the game, takes its own card off the field or gives its controller nothing, and a unit with no use the rules allow (no legal target, a cost it cannot pay).
- Every unit of a turn the turn end does not reach (its player's own prompt, an extra turn, the game's end), and the seat's own units when the enemy's turn is not reached.
- A unit the probes' nodes did not reach.
- Anything while a prompt is open, outside the seat's own main phase, or once the game is over.
- Nothing at all, and no node spent, when no unit on either field, and no face-up backrow card ([[R2603000]]), has an Activate.

**Rejected.** Probing the seat's own units in the current turn, which prices nothing once its Cannon has fired and so would give the Cannon away the next moment; reusing [[R2477000]]'s turn-hook prices (`TurnHookPrice`), whose key cannot tell the seat's copy of a card from the enemy's and which that ruling's tests build; an annuity of several uses, which a one-turn evaluation cannot weigh against the board it is played on; and pricing a Field Spell's Activate, which [[R2603000]] does.
