**Why.** The owner decided on 2026-11-24 to remove what ran the AI's retired training lanes (CLAUDE.md rule 13). The learn lane, retired by [[R4159000]] on 2026-11-18, kept running: `learn.yml` started twice a day and chained run after run, and each of its runs since was cancelled or failed after 4 to 30 hours of a hosted runner. The training box's lanes (`training/loop.sh improve|unban|gather`) had run on no box since 2026-11-04, the two LLM lanes since 2026-10-25 (#1677), and `gather.yml` plays the gather lane without them.

**What holds now.**

- `.github/workflows/learn.yml`, `learn-set.yml`, `selfplay.yml` and `selfplay-shards.yml` are gone, and no workflow names them, `training/loop.sh` or `training/ml/`. `gather.yml` is the one development lane that files into the card statistics ([[R1933001]], [[R1976000]]).
- `training/loop.sh`, `training/learn.md`, `training/unban.md`, `training/ml/` and `bot/machine/setup.sh --training`, with its `jackioh-train@` service, are gone; `setup.sh --training` stops with a message. `training/improve.md` keeps how a change to the AI is measured, its screens.
- The status issue draws no learn-lane section and no export bars: its chart is gather's alone.
- The learn lane's promotions ([[R1012000]]) and the filing of its exports ([[R1676000]]) no longer happen.

**What stays until #4702:** the commands the lanes ran (`cargo jackioh selfplay`, `positions`, `promote` and `stats from-selfplay`, with `crates/ai/src/features.rs`), CI's `rust (training gate)` for an `ai/*` branch, and the rulings that describe only them. #4702 removes them where they can be built and tested, which the session that removed the rest could not do.

**Rejected:** disabling the workflows rather than removing them, since a disabled workflow still reads as a running lane to every agent that reads the tree, and its upkeep (the status issue's section and its tests) stays; removing the Rust commands in the same change without building them.
