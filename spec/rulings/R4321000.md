S+ #33 True Conservative's Cry leaves every True Conservative on the field as it is, the one played and any other on either side, on both faces, even once its card has a past version ([[R3538002]]). A card never makes itself ([[R387]]), and a card's past versions count as the card for that, so the base face's random pool already holds none of them; the Radiant face's strongest pick ([[R3538003]]) skips them too, so both faces agree. Every other Unit turns as before. Rejected: letting the Radiant face turn its controller's True Conservatives into their strongest past version, which, once issue #4285's 4/4 ships over the 3/3 it was through v0.6.7k, turns the card just played into its own weaker 3/3 (Radiant 6/6), a downgrade the base face never makes.
