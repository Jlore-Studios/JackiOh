Vanilla clears a card's scripts, and both an aura and a set-stat hook are scripts, so a Vanilla'd permanent stops projecting its aura at [[§10.4]] layer 5 and stops setting its own stats at layer 2, keeping its printed face. It still *receives* aura grants from other cards, because another card's aura is that card's text and not this one's
