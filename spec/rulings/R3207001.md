The banner that shows the way back into a live game (a match, a series or a co-op game; [[R765]], [[R1566000]]) on the landing and on `/practice` read `GET /api/auth/me` every `SERIES_POLL_SECONDS` (2 s) for as long as the game was live. It exists to say that a game is still on, which a game outlasts by minutes.

**The rule.** `useLiveGame` re-reads the account every `LIVE_GAME_POLL_SECONDS`, a server constant (10 s) that `cargo test` exports to `apps/web/src/wire/serverConfig.ts` (CLAUDE.md rule 9). It sits in the issue's 10 to 15 s and above `SERIES_POLL_SECONDS`. The rest is as [[R3205000]] has it: a hidden tab reads nothing, and the tab showing again reads once at once and the pace starts over. A banner whose game ended shows for at most one period longer.

**What keeps its pace.** The queue follow in the same file (`useQueueFollow`) stays at `SERIES_POLL_SECONDS` and keeps running in a hidden tab: its reads keep the queue ticket alive ([[R897000]]) and take the player to the game the queue finds.

**Presentation only.** CLAUDE.md rule 7: the banner is a link the server's answer decides; a player who follows a link to a game that ended is told so by that game's own screen.

Hearthstone semantics: the "return to your game" prompt does not need to know the second a game ends.

Rejected: a constant of the client's own in `net/liveGame.ts` (every poll period of the neighbouring screens is a server constant, and this one is exported with the same test); 15 s (the top of the range, a banner that can outlive a game by a quarter of a minute); slowing the queue follow too (it holds a ticket, [[R897000]]); one period for the banner and the queue follow (the banner holds nothing and the follow holds a place).
