Meditative+ #1.1 Base Camp says "Every Reach the Summit you own goes up N Ascent levels". Meditative #8's Ascent level is its play count ([[R429]], [[R840]]), so the verb is `raise_times_played { defId, amount }`: the count of every card of that definition the caster owns ([[§3.2]]'s owner, whoever controls it) goes up by the amount, wherever the card is — hand, deck, graveyard, exile, the field and the resolving zone — as that many plays would have raised it. Only a card whose script counts its plays (`countsPlays`) carries a count, so any other card is passed by, and a card the opponent owns is untouched. A copy made later is a new card that starts at 0 ([[R57]]).

Nothing is played: no Cry, no line of the card resolves and nothing wins. The raised card's next play counts once more and resolves the level it reached then ([[R841]]) at the price that level sets ([[R842]]); a Reach the Summit raised to 10 or beyond wins only when it is next played, at the state check that closes its effects ([[R850]]). Each card raised reports its new count with `numberChanged` (`key` "timesPlayed"), hidden where the card is ([[R97]]): a hand card from the other player, a deck card from both.
