C #62's "they destroy all cards with a Plague Counter" means each player's own: at the start of each player's turn, the permanents that player controls with a Plague Counter on them are destroyed (on the Radiant face, only at the start of the opponent's turn), the only reading under which the Radiant face is the stronger. The designer's "Plague Counter" is [[§6.3]]'s Plague Counter, called the Plague Token until patch v0.2.9 ([[R740]]), and both players' turns take [[R68]]'s order
