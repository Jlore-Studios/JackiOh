The hand eval priced a library by its count alone, `library_card` (0.1) for each card up to `library_comfort` (10): a guard against fatigue that reads nothing of what the cards are. Forced Propagation (S+ #24, [[R3557001]]) changes what the seat will draw, ten copies of a card of its own (forty Embiggened), and the evaluation saw only a card leave the hand. No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** For the seat's own side, `library_draws_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.library_draws` × the library's mean `hand_card_value`, less the price the evaluation gives a card it cannot see (`hand_card` + `hand_per_cost` × `opponent_hand_cost`, 1.6, the enemy hand's), and with fewer cards than `library_draws` left, every card once. A library no better than unseen cards adds nothing, so the count stays `library_card`'s. Copies of a dear card shuffled into a cheap library raise it; a tutor that takes the dearest card out lowers it; a draw moves it by the drawn card's distance from the mean, which the worlds a determinization samples average out. The enemy's library is hidden ([[R185]]) and never read, so a world that resamples it moves nothing. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, and at 0 nothing is read.

**The weight is measured.** `library_draws` is `AI_LIBRARY_DRAWS`, 2.5: the draws a seat still takes from one of its own turns. Over 20 gate games each of ai-vs-greedy and hard-vs-easy (`gate:v3` seeds 0 to 19 at `AI_GATE_BUDGET`), the subject's 106 and 108 turns were followed by 2.42 and 2.54 more of its own turns, each a draw, before the game ended; the games ran about six turns a side.

**What it does not count.** The opponent's draws; a discount on later draws, since a hand card's price is not discounted by the turn it is played either.

**Approximation.** The 2.5 is a mean over every turn of a game, early or late. A finalist is scored after the opponent's reply and the seat's next draw, which the reply took from its sampled library, and still counts 2.5 draws after it.
