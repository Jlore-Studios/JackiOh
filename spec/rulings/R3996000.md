Issue #3996 rewrote S+ #4 Profe Yates's Cry as "Exile a permanent. Then summon two random Traps." (Radiant: "three random Radiant Traps") and took out "Then choose one of the following Activates". The Cry declares one target ([[R81]]) as TG #26 Path of Exile's "Exile a permanent" does: a Unit or a backrow card of either side, a face-down one and a CN card among them, never Yates itself, which is not on the field as the play declares it. While a permanent stands the play names one, as Hearthstone asks a targeted Battlecry for a target whenever one exists; on a board with none the play names none and the Cry summons its Traps all the same ([[R90]]). The exile comes first, so a Trap may take the zone it freed ([[R3567001]]). The three Activates are Yates's from the start, each offered while its target stands and each with its own uses ([[R384]], [[R1751000]]): Overwhelm once a turn, Wolf's Maw three times, Iron Mandate once. Overwhelm freezes Yates too ([[R3567003]]), so a frozen Yates uses none of them until it thaws ([[R2246000]]). Through v0.6.5b the Cry exiled every card named with M, O, U, S or E ([[R3567000]]) and Yates had only the Activate chosen at play ([[R3567002]]); those games fold under the kept script ([[R767000]]). Rejected: keeping the choice of one Activate, which the issue took out. Since issue #4154 the pick must hold M, O, U, S and E in its name and text and Yates has no Activate, through v0.6.6g the kept script running this ruling ([[R4154100]]).
