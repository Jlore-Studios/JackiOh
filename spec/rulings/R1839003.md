Hearthstone has no Stack, and the designer's Dormant Starship will exile a Stack card played onto it before that card resolves, which is its own text. Until a card says so, the rule only must not break: a Stack card may top a Dormant card as it may any card ([[§3.2]]), and the Dormant card then lies beneath a Stack ([[R13]]), still Dormant.

**Nothing the arrival does reaches it.** A Stack card's text that reaches the cards beneath it (Classic+ #4 Juhan Biggest Bat's "the cards beneath it become copies of this") passes a Dormant one by, as every effect does ([[R1839000]]).

**Its countdown waits.** A card beneath a Stack is on the field for nothing, so its countdown does not count while it lies there ([[R1839001]]), and an Awaken does nothing to it. When the cards above it leave it tops its zone again, still Dormant, and counts again from where it stopped.
