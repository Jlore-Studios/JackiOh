S+ #98 reads "Add a Kwzki and Shockwave447, Shadiris and xxFreshJack, or Tylannt and Siva 1 to the left and right most cards of your hand". The "or" is one of the three pairs, drawn from the game's Rng ([[R60]]); the first card named goes to the left end of the hand and the second to the right (docs/stinkyscape-plus-set.md, SP6). "Kwzki" is S #110 iKwzki, the one beside S #111 Shockwave447 and S #109 xxFreshJack (SP5); Shadiris is S+ #165, Tylannt S #6 and Siva 1 S #87. The pair is E8's meeting pair ([[R3558002]]): when the two sit side by side both become Radiant, and on the Radiant face cost (0). The catalog's text names the cards in full: the foundation had read the numbers in "Shockwave447" and "Siva 1" as tunable params, which they are not.

Rejected: the player choosing the pair (the text makes no choice), and adding all six cards.
