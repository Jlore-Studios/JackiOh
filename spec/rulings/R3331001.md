Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The issue asked for a measure that holds each rarity to a named floor,
proposed and ruled here, starting as a ratchet that each set's picture pass shrinks, with a ceiling that keeps
phones fast.

**The measure.** A card's base picture, as its face draws it (`proceduralCardArtUri`, whichever painter draws
it), counts the shapes it draws after its `<defs>`: its paths, circles, rects, ellipses, polygons, polylines
and lines. A scene ([[R3331000]]) also counts the layers it uses and its distinct kit parts, read from the
scene it composes; a picture drawn by no recipe uses none.

**The floors.** Common 30 elements, Rare 40, Epic 60, Legendary 90 and Mythic 140, as the issue proposed,
Legendary and Mythic using all seven layers (`ART_DETAIL_MIN_<RARITY>` in `richness.ts`). A token, whose
rarity is Token, is held to Common's floor. The Glitch is not measured: its window draws no picture.

**The ratchet.** `fixtures/art-detail-short.json` (`ART_DETAIL_SHORT`) lists, sorted, the 563 cards under
their floor as this issue left them, and `ART_DETAIL_SHORT_MAX` is its length. A card under its floor that the
list does not name fails, a listed card that clears its floor fails, and the list may never be longer than the
cap, so it only shrinks: each set's pass takes off the cards it lifts, and the last pass empties it.

**The ceiling.** No picture's data URI, base or Radiant, is longer than `ART_DETAIL_MAX_BYTES`, 48 KiB, about
twice the longest picture the painters drew before; the art cache's size and the lazy drawing of faces
scrolled past are unchanged.

Rejected: a floor per set, which would let one set's Mythic draw less than another's Common; counting a
scene's layers and parts from attributes in the markup, which adds bytes to every picture; measuring the
Radiant face, which only adds the rays; a list that may grow, which would let a new card ship under its floor;
and a ceiling by element count, which a few long paths would pass while their bytes slowed a phone.
