Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or the engine. The issue (#3345, part of #3330) builds on #3344's depth planes and Pokémon TCG Pocket's Immersive cards so that every Mythic-tier card covers the entire screen as it enters, and can be inspected full screen anywhere.

**The card's own art fills the screen.** Every Mythic's in-game takeover set piece ([[R2722001]]), including tokens printed Mythic, draws **its own picture**:
- The picture fills the window edge to edge in its four depth planes ([[R3344000]]): far, middle, near, and nearest;
- The camera dollies through them over the hold (`TAKEOVER_HOLD_MS`, 1000 ms): past the near layer toward the subject, with the far layer drifting;
- The card's name signs itself across the bottom in its set's signature hand (`signatureFaces.ts`, [[R2715002]]) while its intro sting plays ([[R3332002]]);
- On the downbeat at `entranceLandMs`, the stage shrinks onto the card's landing spot on the board as the queue moves on.
The four bespoke takeover cards (`core-100` void collapse, `core-096` pawn, `core-097` genie gust, `meditative-049` ai) preserve their custom look and choreography.

**The rules hold.** The takeover stays within `TAKEOVER_HOLD_MS` and the set-piece cap ([[R2785000]]). A tap skips it, Big moments "short" gives half the hold (500 ms) and "off" gives none (0 ms), and reduced motion shows `TakeoverStill` with the card's own picture in full layout. A hidden play shows nothing ([[R202]]). An opponent's Mythic play triggers the takeover for both seats only when its play is public (`defId !== HIDDEN_ID`).

**Press and hold, anywhere.** A long press (phone) or click-and-hold (desktop) on any Mythic card, or on any card wearing a full-art-shaped level (`isFullBleed`), opens a full-screen view:
- Available across hand, inspect (`CardInspect`, `InspectSheet`, `CardDetail`), the Collection, the Almanac, and pack openings;
- On the board, during a game, it opens only for your own cards (`owner === wearer`), and never while the animation queue is draining (`isHeld`);
- The picture fills the window edge to edge in its planes, the camera loops slowly through them with the card's intro music, and the tilt follows pointer or device orientation ([[R3344001]]);
- A tap pauses and resumes it, and releasing the hold or a second tap closes it; Escape closes it too.

**A mark of its own.** A Mythic's frame in hand carries a quiet mark that it can be held: a slow edge shimmer in its own Mythic colors (pink, cyan, mint, yellow), not Radiant's gold. Under reduced motion, the shimmer is held still.

Rejected: a generic silhouette for Mythics (cards deserve their own full art); opening full-screen on opponent board minions or while the queue drains (confuses board state and breaks combat pacing); true 3D scene meshes (unnecessary overhead beyond the four SVG depth planes); and letting the full-screen view block background network or game queue ticks.
