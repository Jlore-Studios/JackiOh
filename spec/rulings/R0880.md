Meditative #17 True Craft a Card (MD-A30) resolves as a chain of two answers. Its Cry opens a
`number` prompt for the crafted card's cost, 0 to 4; the answer re-enters the script, which opens
a `craft` prompt at that budget. The `craft` prompt's options are the four seeded presets, but
its answer is one `Selection::Craft` — a preset, or the player's own recipe — so the membership
and spend checks are skipped for the kind and the recipe is validated against the block table,
the point budget (`2 + 5 ×` the cost), the 24 lines and the type's rules instead. An invalid
recipe is refused with the state unchanged and the prompt still open; a valid one is compiled
(`craft_card`) into a transient definition and a fresh card of it reaches the hand at its cost,
or burns on a full one (§2.4, R4).

The turn clock keeps running while the player crafts (R79, as Classic+ #42's Test under R420):
no prompt of its own. The opponent sees only that a prompt is open, then a hidden card (R97) —
the recipe travels only to the prompt's holder (§10.8). The editor's preview is the engine's own
`craft_preview`, so what it shows and what the reducer accepts agree (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
