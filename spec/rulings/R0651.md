Joro leaves the hand only when an enemy Spell targets one of its controller's units: the Spell's declared target ([[§10.5]] step 1) or a `target` prompt answer whose prompting card is a Spell, read as the pick is answered. An attack ([[§4.2]] step 2), a Unit's Cry pick, an activation or a Trap's pick summons nothing, and neither does a Spell's random pick or "all" effect, which target nothing ([[R450]]). Like Hearthstone's Spellbender, the interception still moves only to a legal pick: a Joro that the declaration could not have named answers nothing
