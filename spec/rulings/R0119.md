A permanent whose trigger watches card plays does not fire on the play that put it onto the field: it starts counting from the next play. This is a deliberate exclusion, not a consequence of timing — [[§10.5]] step 4 places the card **before** it emits `cardPlayed` and only then runs the resolution loop, so the arriving card is already a registered watcher when [[§10.3]] dispatches its own arrival, and something has to take it out. For traps the engine does it, in `traps.ts`'s `isOwnArrival`: a trap is never offered the `cardPlayed`, `summoned` or `cardResolved` event naming the trap itself, which is where [[R17]]'s moments after the announce land (a trap is not on the field at its own announce, [[§10.5]]), and a trap is itself a card someone plays. A permanent that is **not** a trap gets no such filter for its own arrival and owes the check inside its own trigger, comparing the event's `instanceId` against `ctx.self`, as #33 does. A permanent that arrived on the field while the play resolved, whatever put it there — a unit its Cry recruited (#98's Recruit bringing out #33), the cards #95 summons, a body that came back through Reborn meanwhile, a unit a trap answering the play summoned — is in the same place and starts counting from the next play too: the play's `cardResolved` names those arrivals (`arrivedDuring`, the engine's bookkeeping, which a view never forwards), and neither a trap nor an ordinary trigger answers it for them. #38 Quickstriker has no trigger: [[§10.5]] step 5 reads it as a static flag off the playing player's side and leaves out the played card by its id, so a Quickstriker being played is on the field by step 5 and still does not count its own play. A granted Combo that is a modifier (#78's `comboDraw`) answers by ordering instead: step 5 resolves the granted parts before the played card's own `cry`, so a card that installs such a modifier has not installed it yet when its own play is counted. #64 Gifted Program, also a static flag, never meets its own arrival, since step 3 reads it before step 4 places the played card ([[R213]]). The arrivals are counted from the moment the play begins — [[§10.5]] step 1, or the start of a cast ([[R70]]) — so what a Tribute's Death puts on the field at step 2 ([[R210]]: #22's copies of what it ate) is an arrival as well: a Sheepish copy answers neither the play's `cardResolved`, where Sheepish now answers ([[R427]]), nor its `cardPlayed` or `summoned` at step 4, which name their arrivals as `cardResolved` does, and a Quickstriker or Clone Machine copy answers neither step 5 nor step 7. An Echo repeat is the same play resolving again ([[§10.5]] step 6), so its granted Combo parts count neither a Quickstriker the first resolution put on the field (#95) nor a Combo modifier the play itself installed: /fullsend echoed by Twinspell draws nothing from its own "Combo: draw 1". A card that lay dormant under a Stack pile as the play began, and resumed as its pile's top while the play resolved — the play's own Hit Job killed the card above it — is an arrival too: dormant, it registered nothing when the play began ([[§3.2]], [[R153]]), so it does not answer the play's `cardResolved`
