The game is a draw at the end of the 60th player-turn, 30 each (`TURN_CAP_PLAYER_TURNS`, [[§2.5]], [[R2]]), twice the 30 before this patch, as the designer asked; the other limit in the game, the 75-second turn clock ([[R79]]), is unchanged. One draw a turn empties a 20-card deck on the first player's 17th turn and the second player's 16th, and fatigue ([[R3]]) then kills a hero at 30 health on the eighth empty draw, so two decks that do nothing end by fatigue at player-turn 48: the cap is now a backstop, reached only by games that heal, gain Armor, refill a deck (C #30) or replace fatigue (#75), and fatigue is the usual end of a long game. The hard match ceiling rises from 60 to 120 minutes (`MATCH_CEILING_MINUTES`, [[R79]]), since 60 player-turns at a full clock take 75. The AI reads the constant ([[§9.9]]), and every fuzz game still ends within the cap
