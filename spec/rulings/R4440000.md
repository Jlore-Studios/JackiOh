Issue #4440, Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: "Axnit sells for 1 less", "Narcor sells for 2 less", "Red Narcor sells for 2 less". A Resource's sale fetches a whole number drawn uniformly in its face's printed range, or the Marketplace's quote within it while the Marketplace is open ([[R1840001]], [[R1853002]], [[R2250001]]), so "sells for N less" moves both ends of the base face's range N lower, which lowers the average sale, and every quote, by N. Its `buy` is the range's top on every Resource, and moves with it. The designer names no Radiant price; every Resource's Radiant prices are twice its base face's ([[R275]]), as issue #3951's lowering kept them ([[R3951003]]), so the Radiant range moves twice as far: Axnit sells 3–13 (Radiant 6–26), down from 4–14 (8–28); Narcor 8–19 (Radiant 16–38), down from 10–21 (20–42); Red Narcor 14–30 (Radiant 28–60), down from 16–32 (32–64). The prices are the catalog's `resource` blocks ([[R1840000]]), so the faces' `Credits: min–max` lines follow by themselves ([[R2521009]]); no text and no other Resource moves. Rejected: lowering the Radiant range by N too, which would leave it less than twice the base face's.
