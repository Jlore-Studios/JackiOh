Issue #3996 rewrote Meditative #47 饕餮 as a 2/2 (Radiant 4/4, its stats through v0.5.11r) with "Activate: Fuse a random enemy Unit into this, then Freeze this." (Radiant: "Activate: Fuse a random enemy Unit into this."), in place of its end of turn ([[R62]]). The Activate is once a turn with no price ([[R384]]) and fuses the field ingredient [[R1020]] draws, 饕餮 kept ([[R77]], [[R102]]); the kept instance carries the turn's use, so the fused card is not offered it again that turn. It is offered only while 饕餮 is not Immutable and an enemy Unit stands that is not Immutable ([[R23]]), as Hearthstone's Deadly Shot needs an enemy minion and the Stinkyscape+ weapons' Activates an enemy Unit ([[R430]]): a use that ate nothing would only freeze it. The base face then Freezes 饕餮 ([[§6.3]] Freeze): with its attack unspent it thaws at that turn's cleanup, so it loses this turn's attack; once it has attacked it thaws at the cleanup of its next turn and uses no Activate on that turn ([[R2246000]]). The Radiant face no longer eats a card of the opponent's deck. Through v0.6.5b it ate at its end of turn, the Radiant face from the deck too; those games fold under the kept script ([[R767000]]). Rejected: offering the Activate with nothing to eat.
