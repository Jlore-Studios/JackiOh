The designer's price of a card of printed cost c (`query_cost`, so an X card's is 0) is **10 × min(c, 4) + 5 × max(c − 4, 0)** Credits, and a (0) card's is 5 (`MARKETPLACE_CREDITS_PER_MANA`, `MARKETPLACE_FULL_PRICE_MANA`, `MARKETPLACE_CREDITS_PER_MANA_PAST`, `MARKETPLACE_ZERO_COST_PRICE`). So (1) is 10, (4) is 40 and (5) is 45. A Radiant lot is twice that (`MARKETPLACE_RADIANT_PRICE_MULTIPLIER`). That is the lot's base.

"Prices move every turn, drawn around that price with a spread of a tenth of it" is read in whole Credits. At every turn start while the Marketplace is open, and as it opens or re-rolls, each rolled card lot's price is its base moved by a uniform whole number in −s to +s, where s is its base divided by `MARKETPLACE_PRICE_SPREAD_DIVISOR` (10), rounded down. When s is 0 nothing is drawn. So a 40 moves within 36–44, and a 5 never moves. The draws are made in offer order. The rejected reading, a fractional draw rounded afterwards, would bring a float into every price for no gain.

A Resource's buy price never moves: a Resource lot costs its face's printed `buy`, and a Radiant Resource lot its Radiant face's printed `buy`, not twice the base face's, since the card prints its own. Its sell price moves within its printed range. At the same moments, each of the catalog's Resources is quoted, in sorted id order, as a whole number drawn uniformly in its base face's sell range and then one in its Radiant face's. Those are what a sale fetches that turn ([[R1853003]]). While the Marketplace is open, a Resource gained into a full tray sells at its quote with no draw, so [[R1840001]]'s "current sell price" is the Marketplace's.
