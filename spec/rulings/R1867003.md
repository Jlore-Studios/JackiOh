Stinkyscape #10.2 Divine Spirit is Hearthstone's Divine Spirit: a buff of the Unit's Health now, its current Health and not its maximum, so a damaged Unit doubles what it has left — a 3/4 with 2 Health left becomes a 3/6 with 4 left — current and maximum rising together ([[§10.4]]). The Radiant face's "Triple" buffs twice the Health now, tripling the total.
