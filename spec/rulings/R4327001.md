A card a summon sets face-down by its Cloaked ([[R4327000]]) arrives with a `summoned` in a backrow zone where it does not stand as a Unit: set, it is a Field Trap and a backrow card, and no Unit ([[R3777000]]). So nothing that answers "a Unit is summoned" answers it, since each reads the summon's row or a Unit standing in the backrow ([[R1081000]]): C #5 Tesla's "Reveals when your opponent summons a Unit" and every reader of `query::enemy_unit_arrival` among them. Its reveal turns it face-up and moves it into a unit zone, an animation (`animated`, never `summoned`, [[R445]], [[R783006]]), which none of them answers either, as for every card set by its Cloaked. No engine change is needed for it.

Hearthstone: a Secret entering play is no minion summoned. Rejected: answering the reveal as the summon (the issue's suggestion), which would make this card's reveal a summon when no Cloaked play's reveal is one; that would change [[R783006]] for every card set by its Cloaked and is an issue of its own.
