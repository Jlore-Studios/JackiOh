A card's ladders in the Collection ([[R3015007]]) follow [[R3343000]]: the Default's first rung of a
card with a default look is named by it, "Full art" on a Mythic and "Extended art" on a Legendary, in
place of "Normal", and the Default's rungs picture it across the whole face, as the card's large face
draws it ([[R3343001]]; a Mythic's normal face while the Full-art cards setting is off). A variant is
listed when any of its levels is offered on the card, so Full art on a Mythic and Extended art on a
Legendary are listed from the level above their first, under their own names, and that rung is next
with nothing owned, the one a held token is offered on ([[R3015004]], [[R2730000]]); a rung above it
still names what it needs.

Rejected: hiding the variant on those cards, which would hide the levels they are still offered.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): ownership and offers stay the server's.

Amends [[R3015007]] (the Default's first rung and the ladders that start above a default look).
