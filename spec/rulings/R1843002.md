`flee { player?, opponent? }` sets the player's `fled` (`PlayerState.fled`), what it leaves the other player: a draw by default, or a loss. Like `winGame` it takes hold at the state check that closes its effect list ([[R59]]), at the game-end point after the hero check and the vote tallies ([[R1843001]]): a hero at 0 still loses first ([[R850]]), the fleer's own or the other player's, so a flight in the list that killed the enemy hero wins by `hero-death`, a loss for them. A fleer who also holds another win (an effect's, an alternative condition met) wins by it, and the flight ends the game only as their one claim: then `fled` names the fleer and what it leaves, just before `gameOver`, and the game ends the fleer's win, `fled` (a draw for the other player, [[R1843004]]) or `fled-decisive` (a loss). Two players who flee at the same point draw (`fled`, both announced); a fleer and a player holding another win draw as two winners do (`alt-win`, [[R850]]). Once the game is over a flight does nothing ([[R216]]).
