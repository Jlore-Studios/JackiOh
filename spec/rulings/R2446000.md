Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes. The Cards tab's table on `/stats` offers no Cost, Rarity or Keywords column and no sort by cost, rarity or keyword, since its filter bar already filters by each of them; the rarity, cost and keyword filters and their `?rarity=`, `?cost=` and `?keyword=` addresses stay, and an address whose `?sort=` names a sort the table no longer offers opens on the default, win rate descending. The signed-in Your stats column is gone too: every row of it showed the player's overall win rate, not the card's, so every card read the same number; the device's player statistics ([[R639]]) and `GET /api/stats/player` are unchanged, and the landing page's Your table and the Players tab still read them. The Game figures panel ([[R1994000]]) shows the first player's wins with their margin, the median game's turns and the share of games that reach fatigue, and no longer how many set Traps fired, which told a player nothing: `GET /api/stats/games` still sends `trapsSet` and `trapsFired`, and `game_figures_of` and `cargo jackioh stats report --games` still compute them. Amends [[R1994000]].
