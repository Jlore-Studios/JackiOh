Meditative+ #21 Harmony Spirit's Cry sets every other Unit's Attack and Health to 3/3. The spec had no set, so it follows Hearthstone's: the Unit is exactly the stats set, whatever it had. The verb `set_stats_all { scope, attack, health }` writes them as the Unit's [[§10.4]] layer 1 (`statsOverride`, which wins over printed and X stats, [[R41]]), takes off its buffs and the stat half of its tuning (as a Vanilla does, [[R781003]]) and heals its damage, so a 10/10 with 5 damage and +2/+2 becomes 3/3 undamaged. A face that wears its X/X doubled (a Radiant Ghoul Token, [[R349]]) keeps the difference as tuning, so it wears 3/3 too. What is not the Unit's own stays: the auras of layer 5 still apply on top, and a card's own layer-2 count (#92 Felinor Fiender, [[R39]]) still adds to it, as Hearthstone's ongoing effects do after a set.

The scope is every other Unit of both sides: the top of each pile ([[R13]]; a dormant card is untouched), the Units a carrier holds ([[R446]]) and those standing in a backrow ([[R1081000]]), in [[R68]]'s order. An Immutable Unit is untouched ([[R23]]), and a Spell's set passes a Unit Immune to Spells by, as every scope does. Each Unit set reports its two numbers as it now wears them, auras included, with `numberChanged` ("attack" and "health"), public. The set is the Unit's on the field: [[R78]]'s reset takes the override off with the card leaving it, so a set Unit bounced or taken is its printed card again; a Vanilla keeps it, as it keeps any `statsOverride`.

The Radiant face then fills its controller's board with Rush Tokens (`fill_board`, [[R942]]; a Locked zone takes one, [[R1136000]]) in Chinese: `fill_board`'s `chinese` rider summons each with the Chinese flag ([[R1300]]), which issue #1176 makes CN. The tokens arrive after the set, at their printed 3/3.
