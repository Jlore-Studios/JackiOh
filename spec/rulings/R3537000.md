The Activate of S+ #8 Drone Assembler v3, #9 v4 and #10 v5, "Target an enemy, summon a Drone Interceptor (Corvette) to attack it", declares one target ([[R81]]): an enemy Unit on the field, as the Stinkyscape weapons' "an enemy Unit" does ([[R1862000]]), never the enemy hero. A declaration with no legal pick is passed, so the Activate is not offered while the opponent has no Unit, and a pick of the hero is refused.
