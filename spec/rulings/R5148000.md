How much mana can a player hold, and how big can an X get? Issue #5148 asked for a ceiling of 15 on every mana amount a card involves, temporary ("float") mana included, so that a loop or infinite mana never becomes an unbounded X or an unbounded pile of mana. Hearthstone holds a player's mana at 10 and loses what a gain would add past it; JackiOh does the same at `MANA_CEILING`, 15 ([[§2.3]]).

- **Mana held.** A player's current mana and their max mana are each at most 15. Whatever would take either past it stops at 15 and the rest is lost: temporary mana (every `gainMana`, #6 Mana Well, The Coin, M #6 Me no Likey's gain, S #153 Stimmycheck's, S #4.4 Last Word's Radiant 100), a refresh with its next-turn rider (C #90 In Too Deep's reward J, 100 more) and "+N mana per turn" ([[R4440052]]), persistent modifiers and crystals stolen past the cap ([[R1854003]], [[R4331001]]). A Replenish never passes max ([[R364]]), so it never passes 15 either. A loss comes off what is held.
- **X.** An X a player chooses is at most 15: the picker offers 1 to the lesser of their spendable mana and 15 ([[R348]]), and a play naming more is refused ("X is at most 15"). That holds under a credit line, which may lend past 15 ([[R1223]]), and under infinite mana, whose X is chosen out of the mana held ([[R3548004]]). A cast's X, a random cast's included, stops at 15; a set X ([[R1848003]]) is set at most 15; and the X an X-cost card counts on the field and as it resolves, its X steps included, is at most 15 (`counted_x`), as is the cost it reads off the field ([[R396]]); a step past 15 moves nothing. So C+ #40 Appropriations' Culture, 6X%, tops out at 90%. An X that is no price is not mana and is not capped: the X/X a summoner sets from a count, as M+ #20 KY's Competition's thirtieth right answer makes M+ #20.1 Lil' Kenny a 30/30 ([[R1184000]]).
- **Costs are not capped.** A card may cost more than 15 (#100 Ceaseless Void prints (100) and counts down), and the ceiling never lowers a price ([[R65]]). Such a card is out of reach of held mana alone; infinite mana (S+ #56 Impact Reactor) still pays it at any cost, since it pays every price at 0 ([[R3548004]]), and a credit line may pay up to its limit past what is held ([[R1223]]).

The fuzz monitor's I17 fails on any state in which a seat holds more than 15 mana, current or maximum.
