The brief of #1651 (its §2) takes the odds and the pity counters off the panels where [[R1339000]]
and [[R1580000]] put them, and gives them a page of their own. Only where they show changes.

- **The page.** `/purchases` is public, like `/terms`: the route table serves it with no gate. It
  opens with a short disclaimer: Jlore is earned by playing and never sold, nothing there is bought
  with money, and what a pack holds is the server's seeded, logged roll ([[§14.1]], [[§14.11]]).
- **Signed in**, it reads `GET /api/packs` and `GET /api/cosmetic-packs` and shows the server's odds
  as percentages, the fallback tier included: a card pack slot's and the guaranteed slot's, a
  duplicate's cosmetic, the global roll's line (`odds.globalCosmetic`: its chance, then its tiers
  from Epic, [[R1661001]]), and a cosmetic pack slot's and its guaranteed slot's. Then it shows the
  account's pity counters, `n / threshold packs` for Epic, Legendary and Mythic, once for every card
  pack ([[R1298000]]), with the line that pity guarantees each within its threshold. A read that
  fails says why in its place.
- **Signed out**, it shows the disclaimer and a link to sign in, and makes no request: both reads
  are the account's own (`AuthLevel::Active`), and an odds read open to anyone would be a server
  change this ruling does not make.
- **Linked from** the site footer, beside Privacy and Terms, and by one small `Odds & pity` link at
  the foot of `/shop`.
- **Gone from** the pack screen (the card packs panel), the Shop and the cosmetic packs panel: none
  of them shows an odds table, a pity counter or an odds line. The opening and store parts of #1651
  remove them there; this part adds the page.
- **Still the server's.** Every percentage, counter and threshold is what the server answers, only
  formatted, never a rate the client computes (CLAUDE.md rule 7), and the odds stay published
  ([[§14.4]]). No price, table, threshold or pity rule changed.

It supersedes the placement in [[R1339000]] (What players see) and in [[R1580000]] (Pity): both
rulings keep everything else, the roll, the tables, the one counter set and the Shop's panels.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone publishes its drop rates in a place of their own, apart from the
store where packs are bought; the odds and the pity here go the same way, and nothing on the store's
panels states a rate.

Rejected: keeping the odds on the pack screen beside the link, which would show them in two places
and keep the store's panels carrying them; gating the page behind an account, which would hide the
disclaimer from the people it is for; and an anonymous odds read, for the reason above.
