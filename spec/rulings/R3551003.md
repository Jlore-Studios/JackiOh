C+ #51 printed "Can't be attacked" beside Can't Defend. What does S01 print, and does an Evasive Taunt bind attackers? The Fighter prints Evasive alone: "Ranged, Flying, Rush, Evasive" (Radiant with Divine Shield), carrying no script — a card that has both Can't be attacked and Evasive shows Evasive alone, which implies Can't Defend ([[R3543002]]) and is never in Defense Position. An Evasive Taunt binds nobody, as a "Can't be attacked" one never did: the Rush Token Launcher may still choose a plain enemy Unit beside it. Through v0.5.13g the flag `cant_be_attacked` beside the Can't Defend keyword ran it.

Rejected: Keeping Can't Defend beside Evasive: it would print a keyword Evasive already implies, and a Buff could take it off while Evasive stayed.
