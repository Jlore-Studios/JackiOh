The designer's list of 2026-10-18 (issue #1173, `JackiOh_MeditativePlus_Cards.md`) numbers its cards 1 to 26, and the numbers stand. Where it leaves the catalog's data open, the foundation decides, each mark ⚠ designer in `docs/meditative-plus-set.md`:

- **One name names one card** ([[R381]], [[R739]]): Classic #60 holds the name Pile On, so M+ #17 is **Dogpile**, its text unchanged. The apostrophes are the catalog's plain `'` (Bob's Plan, KY's Competition, Lil' Kenny, KY's Intuition), and the designer's "Jlockheed" is the `Jlockeed` tag ([[R278]]); his "Spell damage" is the keyword Spell Damage.
- **Types**: M+ #24 Bob, Wisconsin's Strongest Soldier, which the list calls "Unit, Spell", prints stats and a Cry, so it is a Unit.
- **Rarity**: M+ #26 Scratch Pad, which the list leaves unrated, is Mythic, as M #17 True Craft a Card, whose crafter it opens, and a card that could not exist on a normal card ([[§8]]). The set is 5 Common, 9 Rare, 9 Epic, 1 Legendary and 2 Mythic.
- **Tokens**: M+ #16.1 Jazakus Prime and M+ #20.1 Lil' Kenny are tokens (`rarity: "Token"`, the Token tag), printing the designer's Epic and Rare as `printedRarity` ([[§7]]). Lil' Kenny prints 0/0 with `xStats` of 1/1 (Radiant 2/2), so its stats are its summon's X/X, as C+ #69 Buff Billy's are X multiples. M+ #1 Bob's Plan's AI cards ("you may make some AI cards to help") are two tokens tagged AI and Token with no printed rarity, as the T-AI cards: **M+ #1.1 Base Camp**, (0) Spell, "Every Reach the Summit you own goes up 2 Ascent levels. Add 2 Coins to your hand." (Radiant 3 levels and 2 Radiant Coins), the plan for M #8 Reach the Summit; and **M+ #1.2 Spa Day**, (2) Spell, "Heal your hero 25. Shuffle 2 Love Bombs into your deck." (Radiant: heal 50, Radiant Love Bombs), the plan for M #20 Aestheticize the Game's hero-health line. Being AI generated cards, they join an AI pool ([[R1421]]) once the set ships, beside T-AI-1 to T-AI-10.
- **Stats** are the designer's, every Radiant Unit at twice its base ([[R275]]'s stat half); every entry has a Radiant face of its own ([[R276]]).
