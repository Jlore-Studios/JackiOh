Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #3572 gives the Stinkyscape+ set its look and its sound while [[R1420]]'s gate still holds it, as [[R2202001]] gave Training Grounds' and [[R1883000]] Stinkyscape's, so nothing here reaches a player before the set's release lists it in `SHIPPED_SETS`.

**The set mark** ([[R503]]). A Stinkyscape+ face, and its pack, wears Stinkyscape's green planet with the plus mark every "+" set wears beside it ([[R2202001]]'s way), named "Stinkyscape+ set" by its title and label, where a set with no glyph wears the diamond. Stinkyscape's own glyph is unchanged, byte for byte.

**The Almanac and the deck builder** ([[R1380]], [[R1381]]). The set adds eight deckable tags (Mindustrial, Mineral, Automa-α, -β, -γ, -δ, -Ω and Paperclip), each a chip of its own in the deck builder and the Almanac; the Greek letters read as words in a chip's test id (`db-filter-tag-automa-alpha`). Automa-ζ is carried by tokens alone, so its chip shows in the Almanac only. With the set previewed, as the release will show it, the Almanac shelves every card of the set in its own mark and art, its set chip keeps exactly them, and the deck builder offers its cards and not its tokens. The `RELEASES` line is the release's: it names a version, which `patches ship` fixes.

**The keywords and the glossary.** The client knows the eight kinds the engine declared (GIGA, Flexible, Frenzy, Evasive, Tipsy, and the unprinted Evolve, Burn and Toxic), each with a glyph, a mark and a glossary entry; the five unprinted-or-new terms (Evolves when, Evolve, Burn, Toxic, Replenish) join the glossary, and [[R373]]'s Tipsy rule reads "drops by 1 at your Start of turn". A kind drawn on the field is drawn only where it acts (GIGA, Frenzy, Evasive); the rest show in a card's text and hand only. The inflections a new term adds to [[R910000]]'s bolding are aliases of the terms, and a word that merely resembles one (an overdraw's "burned") is plain.

**The intros** ([[R1352]]). The set's 59 Legendaries, Mythics and tokens printed so each have a derived intro of their own, rendered with the set, the Starship and Mindustrial tags with palettes of their own, the set's glint a glockenspiel. The music budget grows by two whole MiB (49 to 51), in `MUSIC_BUDGET_BYTES` and `gen-music.mjs` alike, for the 59.

**Its words** (§10.11, [[R1448]]). Every card has its flavour line, its voice lines (a Unit its play, attack and death, anything else its cast, within the limits of the Surface) and its Simplified Chinese name and faces in `card-text.yaml`, and its voices in `card-audio.json5`; Oct's and the Warhero Token's previews are written in both languages. #4 Profe Yates speaks the designer's lines cut to the Surface's limits, #141 Stinky 1x Engineer's play line is the designer's. The voice files are rendered with the release (`gen-voice` skips a held set).
