Each Training Grounds snapshot #41–#60 names the Core card it copies in its catalog entry (`snapshotOf`, "core-002" for TG #41 Bigot), and the two are one card for a deck: §2.6 L3 lets a deck hold either, never both, as the owner decided on 2026-10-28 (#2195). The pair shares one key (`catalog::pair_key`, the Core card's id), which `create_game`'s check (`validate_deck_with`) and the shared validator's L3 (`validate_loadout`, the server's too) refuse twice in one deck. A trio's L4 is unchanged: it judges a card id, so one deck may hold the Core card and another its snapshot. A draft (R250's D1–D5) is not judged by it, as a draft may hold too few cards: L3 refuses the deck when it is played or queued. Every random deck keeps the rule: `build_ai_deck` (the AI's decks, a player's All Random deck, the bot pool's and co-op's, [[R258]]) takes the other of a pair out of the pool once one is dealt or included (and [[R1370]]'s floors count a pair as one card, the union of the two's floors, so a floor never counts on the other of a card it deals: found when the set shipped, issue #2204, where TG #59 and Core #47 are both Fruit), and the fuzz tool's deal skips a card whose pair the deck holds, which over a pool with no snapshot is the deal it always was. The deck builder shows the other of a pair the open deck holds as unavailable, says which of the two the deck holds ("in this deck, as its Core card"), and refuses to add it, as it does a compared deck's card ([[R251]]). Field absent on every other card, so a game that holds no snapshot serializes and replays as before. While the set is held ([[R1420]]) no deck holds a snapshot at all; the rule is proven under the testkit's preview.

Superseded by issue #2511 ([[R2511001]]): the snapshots are the Legacy cards ([[R2511000]]), each its own card, so a deck holds a Core card and its Legacy card together; the validators, the deck builder and every random deck drop the pair, and the build's catalog sets `snapshotOf` on no card.
