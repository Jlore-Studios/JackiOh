Since balance patch 1, a discard is random cards from the discarder's hand, drawn through the match rng, unless the card says "of your choice" (or "of their choice"): no prompt opens, and with fewer cards than asked all there are go. "Of your/their choice" keeps a hand prompt the discarder holds. A targeting cost (Classic #89 Paul Allen's Ghost) is random at pay time too: it is checked at [[§10.5]] step 1 against the cards held outside the play or activation, and [[§10.5]] step 2 pays that count at random — never the card being played or a hand card the play or activation picks — whatever an interception did to the picks
