Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to "go harder on alt art": keep the theme but move
it to another style or place, so each Alt art is clearly its own. [[R3396000]] did so for Classic+; until this issue
a Meditative+ or Training Grounds card's Alt art redrew its own scene re-seeded ([[R2940001]], [[R3393000]]): the
same parts with another seed, which changed a median 26% of the picture's pixels and as little as 10% (Clobberer).

**An alt recipe per card.** Each of the 36 cards of the two sets offered Alt art (`version-alt-art`), 14 Meditative+
and 22 Training Grounds, draws an alt recipe of its own (`meditativePlusAltRecipes.ts` and
`trainingGroundsAltRecipes.ts`, spread into `ALT_SCENE_RECIPES` beside Classic+'s), written from #3397's brief for
it: the same subject and idea in another place, time or story moment, in its set's family. `sceneRecipeOf` reads it
under the card's Alt art seed (`<id>#alt`) alone, as [[R3396000]] set; the card's id, every repaint's seed
([[R3017002]]) and any other seed keep the normal recipe. Every level of Alt art draws the one alt picture
([[R3015002]]), base and Radiant. A Training Grounds card keeps its own painter's dispatch: its Alt art level reads
its alt recipe exactly as its normal face reads its normal one.

**The family.** A Meditative+ alt keeps the Meditative family's ink line and its one red seal, standing where the
normal picture's does, inside the band every face shows; the ink-wash peaks are the normal pictures' and stay out
where a brief sets its scene elsewhere (under a lake, in bamboo, on a picnic blanket). A Training Grounds alt keeps
the carved-wood family and, as its normal pictures, no seal; it leaves the yard wherever its brief does.

**The same subject.** A Unit drawn as a figure keeps its figure; a figure both pictures draw is the normal one's
person, its head and outfit unchanged (only its cloth, headgear or held item may change); a person the brief keeps
beside it (Bloodbloom's mage, Dogpile's foe, Group leader's column, Right-Hand Felinor's knight, Seance's recruits,
Tutorial Master's recruit, Call to Chaos's instructor) is the extra figure at the same index in both pictures, with
the normal one's head and outfit; and a subject that is a part of the kit (the bloom, the pouch and its flasks, the
tome, the gloves and the card, the hourglass, the snowplow, the code block, the card frame, the knives, the oak block,
the runner tokens, the mill, the napkin) stays in the subject layer.

**Measurably elsewhere.** An alt recipe's sky shares no palette colour with its normal recipe's, and its subject
moves at least 8 units, or grows or shrinks by at least a quarter, or a figure comes or goes: a close-up, a wide or
distant view or one from above. In a real browser each Alt art window changes at least `ALT_PICTURE_CHANGE_MIN`
(0.6) of the normal window's points sampled on card-versions.cy.tsx's grid, counted with its threshold for a point
that differs, base and Radiant.

**What still holds.** Each alt picture clears its rarity's detail floor and the byte ceiling ([[R3331001]]), on
both faces, and an Epic or rarer one has far, mid, subject and near layers; prints no text; draws one geometry on
both faces, the Radiant one gilded under its rays, with no base colour in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]); is
apart from every other picture ([[R503]]); stands its emblem small inside the band every face shows; keeps props out
of its subject layer; and Goetzmann Game Design, the one of the 36 with full-art motion, keeps a layer for each role
its motion names ([[R2715001]]). A Radiant face keeps its marks at every Alt art level. Thirteen parts join the scene
kit so that nothing a brief names is a stand-in: a goat, a deer, a duck and walnuts (`sceneKitNature.ts`); a
smoking crater, a sandcastle, a snowman, a mill's water wheel and a rowboat (`sceneKitPlaces.ts`); and a cork board,
a washing line, a picnic blanket and a hanging rope (`sceneKitObjects.ts`). A minor detail the kit lacks is left out
and named in the recipe's comment (the footprints, the bandages, a mirrored reflection), the hail falls as the
kit's snow, and KY's Competition shares the split melon [[R3401000]] added to the kit.

Rejected, beyond [[R3396000]]'s list: drawing Seance's ghost as the normal picture's siege tower where the brief names
a catapult (the recruits are what both pictures share); keeping the Meditative+ peaks in every alt, which several
briefs' places cannot hold; a seal on Training Grounds alts, whose normal pictures have none; standing the sailing
boat in for Tutorial Master's rowboat; and splitting the 36 cards into smaller pull requests, which the issue allowed
at about 40 cards a part.
