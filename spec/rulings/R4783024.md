Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: Metaglass, "Text: Give a unit Ranged and +1%+5 attack". Both faces of S+ #49 read "Give a Unit Ranged and +{attack} Attack.": the chosen Unit, either side's ([[R3547000]]), gains Ranged ([[R783000]]) and then a lasting `buff` of attack alone, the declared `attack`, 1 on the base face and 5 on the Radiant face ([[R386]]). The number is printed by both faces now, so it is no longer tuned on the Radiant face alone ([[R2261001]]), and a Buff or a Nerf moves it on either face. Replays of the versions before this patch fold under `script_v0_6_13h`, whose base face gives Ranged alone and whose Radiant face reads that version's `attack`, 3 ([[R767000]]).
