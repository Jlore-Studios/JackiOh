Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). @MaxGoetzmann asked on #822 to drop the Legendary and Mythic radio switch: a card's intro ([[R1350]]–[[R1352]]) was followed by a theme taking over the music ([[R631]]), and the two clashed. Now no card plays a theme: the stack is the result, then the station's low-health track, then the station's in-game track, and a readable Legendary's or Mythic's intro plays over it. The station switches stay, among them Classic+ #37 Wardrum (a Legendary) to Epic Orchestral, and the station's track comes in under the intro's duck ([[R1350]]). The 17 theme tracks (15 Mythic themes and the two shared Legendary entrance themes) are deleted with their scores, files, manifest rows and licence rows, which frees about 4.6 MB of `MUSIC_BUDGET_BYTES`, and `music-cards.json` refuses a `theme`. Their music lives on, clipped, in the Mythics' intros, each hand-tuned from its theme ([[R1352]]). Rejected: lengthening them into Epic Orchestral tracks, since the Legendary themes share `epic-1`'s and `epic-2`'s keys (D and G minor), a station track loops for two minutes or more, each Mythic theme belongs to one card and is scored for other instruments than the station's, and the renderer is not in the bot's environment. Rejected: keeping them as entrance themes for future cards, since a new Legendary or Mythic gets an intro derived from itself ([[R1352]]).
