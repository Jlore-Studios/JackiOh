[[R280]] gave a card whose text computes a number a `preview`, so #31 prints "Deal Fib(times played + 1) {3} damage" where its arithmetic would otherwise be the player's. A card whose text turns on something it or the game remembers left the player to remember it too: the Unit #22 ate, the Spell C #7 holds, the deaths M #60 has counted toward its Tribute, the card S #9 holds beneath it. Such a card now shows what it remembers the same way, after the words of its text that name it.

A card takes one when its text turns on something recorded before now, on its own instance (its memory, a card held beneath it, a rider written onto it, its base stats down a lineage) or in a player's or the game's record of earlier events (this game's or this turn's plays and draws, the last card played, its controller's Anomaly misses), and the view shows that nowhere on the card wherever a preview is asked ([[§10.8]]). It takes none when the view already shows it (a preview, the rolled power, a label, a quest badge, a tally, its counters, Brittle, its cost or stats, a modifier badge, the glow of [[R195]]), when the record lasts only inside one resolution, when it is a number or a price chosen at play ([[R280]]'s X, an Embiggened price), when the record matters only while the card lies where no preview is asked, or when it would show a viewer what they may not read ([[§9.1]]). Issue #2476's pass over the catalog went through every card against that measure, and its pull request carries the verdict on each.

A value that names a card carries the card's name as its `display` ([[R372]]), "Radiant " before a Radiant one, and 1; with nothing remembered yet it prints a dash, "{—}", and 0, as #40 Feng Shui's last element does before any play. The name is the catalog's, as on every line the engine writes. A value reads only what its controller may read and what the card's own resolution reads, through the same function: the meal Death copies, the stolen count the Tribute is held to, the pool the Death's Resources come from. What may stay private does: a Scanning Probe's chances grow with misses its owner alone saw, so they show only in its owner's hand, and Clip-Farming Critikal names the card played before it only in hand, since on the field the card it names could be a face-down one set earlier. Where the face cannot print the value — #39's copies once it lies in exile, a Cube that has left the field and forgotten its meal ([[R78]]) — it shows none.

A fused card's ingredients each show what their own text remembered: each ingredient's `preview` is asked as that ingredient's text reads the card, its memory under its own keys ([[R102]]), as its Activate's `canActivate` is, so two Carnivorous Cubes crafted into one card name two meals, each after its own "remember it". The Chinese label of a value shown only in a state the plain hand and field views never reach (C+ #34's chosen line, an Asteroid's pool) is held to the same texts as the others ([[R1303]]): its English in the English face, its Chinese in that face's Chinese.
