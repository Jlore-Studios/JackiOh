S+ #121 Toxopid's Cry opens one Mode prompt per Leg ([[§6.3]]). The base face offers both sides' unit zones (own first), the Radiant face the opponent's only. Never the Toxopid's own zone, a zone chosen earlier this Cry, or one the summon can't land on. A Leg belongs to the side it lands on, exiles the pile below, then Craters ([[R3554009]], [[§3.2]], [[§6.1]]).

Rejected: random zones.
