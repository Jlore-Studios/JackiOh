[[R1579000]] made `/collection` a hub of three doors (Decks, Cards, Cosmetics) inside the account page's tavern frame, 960 px wide,
with the Cards door an accordion of one set open at a time and the wallet, the tracks, the Fleet track and the quests under it.
A player could not search it, filter it or sort it, which Hearthstone's, Marvel Snap's and Legends of Runeterra's collections
all let them do. Issue #3734 makes it a place to find cards, on the menus' conventions
(`docs/polish/10-menus.md`, [[R3728000]]).

**The tabs.** `/collection` fills the window (the shell's column, one screen tall, [[R1581000]]'s `one-screen`) and holds four tabs: **Cards**,
**Decks**, **Cosmetics** and **Progress**, by `?section=` (`cards`, `decks`, `cosmetics`, `progress`). A name that is none of them,
or none, opens Cards: there is no hub to fall back to, and a link to `/collection` (the landing's door, the command menu's page)
lands on the grid. A tab moves `?section=` in place by `replace`, so Back leaves the Collection for the screen before it (the
landing, whose door it is) and the browser's Back and Forward still move between tabs a person has pushed; the Back link of the
top bar always leads to the landing. Each tab reads for itself ([[R2125000]], #1691): Cards reads the collection, Decks the decks
and the collection, Progress the wallet, the tracks and the quests, Cosmetics nothing. The route holds the Cards tab's search,
filters, sort and ownership view, and the Cosmetics tab's, in its own state, so each is as it was left when a person comes back
to its tab. This amends [[R1579000]], whose hub, doors and accordion are gone; its rule that nothing but Cosmetics equips a
cosmetic, and its rule that the page never scrolls (only a tab's body does), stand.

**Cards.** One grid of the whole catalog: every non-token card of a set that ships ([[§14]]'s total of a set), an unowned
card a locked silhouette that still opens its view ([[R1655000]]) and every overlay the accordion's tiles had (the hover preview,
[[R3016000]]; the held preview, [[R2730000]]; the card's view and its equip and buy screen). The deck builder pool's search,
filter and sort are reused, not written again (`game/deckbuilder/filters.ts`, `FilterBar.tsx`, `poolWindow.ts`): a search that is
always on show with the sort, the count and Clear in a toolbar above the grid; and chips for cost, set, type, rarity and keyword
(a keyword being any a shipped card prints on either face), and the ownership view All, Owned and Missing, which replaces the
builder's "Owned only" box and only hides cards: ownership is `GET /api/collection`'s alone (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Sorts are cost,
name, rarity and the two this issue adds, **Set** (the order the sets ship in) and **Newest** (the card the latest patch of the
history added first, a card no patch added last), which the deck builder's and the Almanac's bars offer too; the Collection's select
leaves out attack, health, type and win rate, which the deck builder's bar offers and which this grid has no use for (win rate
needs the statistics the builder reads). The grid is virtual as the pool's is (#2334): it mounts only the rows near the view, keeps the focused card mounted and
draws every card where the page has no layout (a test's jsdom). Over 1024 px (`tablet`) the filters are a sidebar at the left of the grid;
at 1024 px and under a Filters button, with the number of chips on, opens them in the Dialog's sheet ([[R3221000]]), and the search stays on
show. The page under 501 px tall is not one screen, so there the grid scrolls with the page. Above the grid sit the owned count over
the catalog (`312 / 480`) and a bar for each set (`{progress} of {target}` and a `<progress>`).

**Progress.** The wallet, the set tracks, the Fleet track and the quests moved out from under the card list to a tab of
their own, Progress, with the reads they had.

**Decks.** A tile for each saved deck: its hero portrait, its name, its cards against `DECK_SIZE`, its validity and the modes it
can play, and a New deck tile (not a link at the deck limit). The validity is the workshop's chip (`deckStatus`) of the validator's
verdict, and the modes are the validator's too: Best of 1 when the deck's own verdict is ok, Conquest when a trio that holds it
has an ok verdict, else "Not playable yet". A tile is a link to `/decks?deck=<id>`, which opens that deck in the workshop (an id
the account does not hold opens the first deck, as `/decks` does); the workshop's Back leads to this tab.

**Cosmetics.** The same search, rarity chips, view (All, Owned, Missing) and sort (its registry order, name or rarity) narrow
the tiles of the Board and Starship tabs, in the same sidebar or sheet; a row they leave empty is not drawn. The previews (hold
to preview, [[R2730000]]; the hover, [[R3016000]]) stay. Equipping is unchanged.

**Under a finger.** Every chip, select, search box, Filters button and tab is at least `--hit-min` square on a coarse pointer, at any
width, and at 600 px and under ([[R2122000]]).

Decisions the issue did not settle, and what each rejected:

- Progress is a fourth tab, not a strip over or under the grid, since a strip would eat the grid's height on every screen.
- The cost row stays 0 to 5, 6+ and X, the buckets `COST_BUCKETS` shares with the deck builder's mana curve and its saved
  filters; the issue's 0 to 7+ would change the curve of every deck and what a returning player's saved filter means.
- The type chips are the deck builder's five (Unit, Spell, Field Spell, Trap, Field Trap), not the issue's three, since a Field
  Spell is not a Spell chip's card and a second list of types would drift from the pool's.
- Newest is the latest patch of `PATCH_LOG` (the history the New ribbon reads, [[R1383]]); the rejected reading was set order and then
  catalog index, which says nothing of when a card came.
- The search and the sort are a toolbar of their own, not inside the sidebar or sheet, so a phone and an upright iPad have the
  search the issue asks to be always visible; `FilterBar` draws either part by a prop.
- The Cosmetics sort has its registry order as a third key, so the default shows each kind's default tile first, as before.
- An unknown or missing `?section=` opens Cards, not a landing of doors, which no longer exist.
- The Decks tab reads the decks and the collection itself (a failed collection leaves ownership unknown, "Complete" not "Ready"),
  and does not mount the workshop, whose store and autosave belong to `/decks`.

Hearthstone semantics do not reach a menu's layout; this follows the issue's conventions (one grid, a search always on show, filters
in a sidebar, and Hearthstone's collection as the model). **Proof.** `filters.test.ts` proves the keyword chips, the Set and Newest
sorts and `collectionPool`'s All, Owned and Missing; `savedBrowse.test.tsx` that the keyword chips persist and an older copy loads empty;
`poolWindow.test.ts` the cells and the mounted cards; `CardGrid.test.tsx` that 300 cards mount only the rows in view and the focused
one, and all of them with no layout; `browse.test.tsx` the bar's Collection groups and parts; `cosmeticsBrowse.test.ts` the
Cosmetics browse; `collection.test.tsx` that each filter, each sort and each ownership view narrow and order the grid as the pool
does, the sheet below 1025 px, a set's count and `<progress>`, and the modes' verdicts; `routes/collection.test.tsx` the tabs,
`?section=`, the Default of Cards, each tab's reads, that the filters survive a switch, the Decks tiles and the Progress panels;
`routes/decks.test.tsx` that `?deck=` opens a deck.

Rejected: keeping the hub's three doors as the way into the tabs, a second click on every visit; a left rail of tabs inside the
shell's own rail; one scrolling page of cards, decks and progress, which has no grid to virtualise; and writing a Collection filter
bar apart from the deck builder's.
