Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Every card intro ([[R1352]]) is rendered again as an orchestral fanfare around the melody it had, in `apps/web/scripts/music/tracks.mjs`, on FluidR3's instruments alone (no new SoundFont or sample set; `assets/music/LICENSES.md` records it):

- **A riser** into the first downbeat: a timpani roll and a cymbal roll swelling on the dominant over tremolo strings whose expression rises from 40 to full, one beat for a Legendary and about `INTRO_MYTHIC_RISER_S` (1.2 s) of whole beats for a Mythic. The cymbal is a roll on the kit's second crash (note 57, a cymbal in every kit the intros use; the orchestra kit's 49 is a timpani), not General MIDI's reverse cymbal, whose swell cannot be timed to the downbeat.
- **A Mythic's own motif**, picked by its id from five wide-leaping contours and a bar's rhythm, stated by the brass and a choir an octave apart over timpani, a step higher each bar, for the fewest bars that hold the file to `INTRO_MYTHIC_MIN_S` (9.25 s).
- **The full ensemble under the melody**: brass doubling it, the tremolo strings (and a Mythic's choir) holding the chords, a trombone doubling the bass, and an orchestra hit, a crash, a bass drum and a timpani stroke on the downbeat, at velocity 84, or 104 for a Mythic.
- **A real ending**: the tonic struck by everything with a second crash, held to the next bar line under a timpani roll that dies away, so the music still ends on a bar line ([[R631]]), then a 1.2 s ring-out whose last 0.8 s fade (`INTRO_TAIL_S`, `INTRO_FADE_S`).

A Legendary's whole file runs at most `MUSIC_INTRO_LEGENDARY_MAX_S` (9 s) and a Mythic's from `INTRO_MYTHIC_MIN_S` to `MUSIC_INTRO_MAX_S` (12 s, raised from 6 s), so every Mythic's intro outlasts every Legendary's; the scores throw on a file outside them, and `music-assets.test.ts` holds the rendered set to them. The 165 intros run 6.0 to 8.7 s for the Legendaries and 9.3 to 11.2 s for the Mythics, and the music set grows from about 27.2 MiB to about 32.1 MiB on disk, so `MUSIC_BUDGET_BYTES` is raised from 28 MiB to 35 MiB at the same encoding.

On the client the bed under an intro ducks to `MUSIC_INTRO_DUCK_GAIN` 0.12 (0.35 until now), so the other music drops away, and the intro plays at `MUSIC_INTRO_GAIN` (1.4) on the music bus, above the effects around it; cut short ([[R1351]]) it fades from that gain. The `entrance` sting is rebuilt to stand beside it at the same moments and length (1.4 s): a band-passed swell rising into a downbeat at a quarter second, where a sub boom, a brass chord opening through a low-pass and a cymbal's wash land together under high glints, a Mythic's with more glints over a choir of detuned saws, its loudest moment, a Mythic's too, no louder than the Legendary gong it replaced. It no longer ducks the music (`MUSIC_DUCK_SFX`), so it never dips the intro it plays beside. An intro still plays only for a card the viewer can read, at [[R204]]'s moment, and is cut short as [[R1351]] says.
