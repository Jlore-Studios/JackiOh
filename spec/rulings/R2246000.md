A Frozen card ([[§6.1]] Frozen, [[R1174000]]) uses none of its Activate abilities ([[R384]]) until it thaws. The Activate refusal reads the status before the uses and the costs, so `legalActions` lists none of its abilities, the reducer refuses an `activate` naming one with the Frozen words ("that Unit is Frozen") and the view greys each with that reason. A Unit Frozen forever ([[R1848000]], Stinkyscape #2 Absolute Zero) never activates again while it stays on the field. Its uses this turn are untouched, so a card that thaws at its controller's cleanup activates as before on their next turn. Another card's effect that runs its ability ([[R1876001]], Stinkyscape #26 ZolarKeth's "Activate your Universities") still runs it, as an effect's attack or switch still happens to a Frozen Unit ([[R20]]). Heroes are never Frozen, and a Dormant card is reached by no Freeze ([[R1839000]]), so a Heroic Power and a Dormant Starship's launch are unchanged. The spec said Frozen stops an attack and a switch and that exertion does not bind an Activate; the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2246) adds the Activate, since a frozen card that still fires its weapon read as no freeze at all.
