Only a match the queue paired — `ranked` on the match row — or a rematch of one ([[R672]]) moves a rating or a rank. A room challenge is unranked: its row records both ratings unchanged, and no season row is written. A game of a Conquest series is recorded but not rated; a ranked series moves the rating once, rated as one game between its two sides when it ends, from the ratings at the time it ends, and an abandoned series moves nothing
