The Radiant Meditative Journey shuffles in a Radiant Journey Complete. **⚠ designer**: the printed face says "a Journey Complete", but Journey Complete's Radiant face speaks of "the cards" where the base says "the two", which pairs it with the Radiant Journey's five, and a Radiant face making its token Radiant is the house pattern (CN-Viral Injection, C+ #48). The Radiant face's draw comes after the pick's step ([[R113]], [[R1246]]), so a card it draws cannot go on the journey, and it draws whether or not a card was picked.
