**Durability X** is printed `{ "kind": "Durability", "n": X }` and counts how many times the card's
Activates may be used in all, across every turn, where an Activate's own number ([[R384]],
[[R1751000]]) counts its uses in one turn. There is one counter per card, whatever Activates it has,
the ones a fusion or a grant gave it included: a card with two Activates and Durability 3 may use
them three times between them. A fused card that prints Durability twice keeps one counter, its first
entry's ([[R102]]), as it keeps one of any keyword that does not sum.

The counter is `CardInstance.durability_spent`, what the card's uses have spent on its stay on the
field; `durability_left` reads the card's Durability X as tuning leaves it, less what was spent, never
below 0 (`None` on a card without the keyword). It is no part of `memory.activations`, the per-turn
record, so nothing that rewrites that record touches it.

**What spends it.** A real use spends exactly 1 (`spend_durability`), reported as `counterChanged` with
the counter `durability` and the Durability left:

- the `activate` action, as the use is counted and before any cost is paid, anything can pause, or a
  counter can answer it ([[R5236003]]);
- an effect that spends a card's use, `run_activation` with `spend_use` ([[R2521003]]): 1, however many
  of the turn's uses that run takes, since the card was used once. A `run_activation` without
  `spend_use` spends no use, and so no Durability ([[R1876001]]).

**What does not.** The engine's own abilities are not the card's text: a Cloaked card's Reveal
([[R783006]]) and a Dormant Starship's launch ([[R1852003]]) spend none and are never refused for want of
it. A card set face-down by its Cloaked, or a Dormant Starship, has no other ability to use meanwhile.

Hearthstone's nearest is a weapon's Durability, which each attack spends by 1 and which a returned
weapon has whole again; [[R78]] makes the second true here too ([[R5236004]]). What happens at 0 is
[[R5236001]]'s and [[R5236002]]'s.
