**Why.** [[R3576000]] lets a tab hidden for `HIDDEN_QUIET_MS` close its social socket and stop its presence beat, so its server can sleep. A shown tab nobody uses kept all of it running, and an open WebSocket counts as a request in flight, so `SLEEP_AFTER` never starts:
- its social socket ([[R3214000]]);
- a beat every `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` ([[R3210000]]);
- every `pollWhileVisible` read ([[R3205000]]).

A desktop left on the site overnight kept a container awake all night. [[R3576000]] rejected a server-side cut of idle sockets because the server cannot tell a tab nobody sees from one somebody reads. The client can tell one nobody touches.

**The rule.**
- A shown tab with no input for `IDLE_QUIET_MS` (ten minutes) is idle. Input is a pointer pressed or moved, a key, a wheel or a touch anywhere on the page. One watch, `watchIdle` in `game/idle.ts`, tells every listener.
- An idle tab goes quiet exactly as a hidden one does under [[R3576000]] (`quietWhileAway` in `game/net.ts`, formerly `quietWhileHidden`):
  - it closes its social socket, a drop that reads nothing;
  - it sends no presence beat.
- Every `pollWhileVisible` tick of an idle tab sends nothing, unless the poll may run hidden (`keepRunning`), which runs idle too.
- The first input brings everything back:
  - the socket opens again;
  - each paused poll reads once at once and starts its pace over;
  - the beat is a full `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` after the last signed-in request, as on showing a hidden tab.
- A tab turning visible counts as input. A tab that was hidden and idle comes back as one that was only hidden, its polls read once by `pageWake` ([[R1441]]), not twice.
- What a quiet tab still sends is unchanged from [[R3576000]]: the queue's reads, the series pick, the co-op room wait, the poll with a challenge of the player's own open, and a live match's, a watch's and a co-op room's sockets.

**Presentation only.** CLAUDE.md rule 7: the client asks for less and decides nothing. Friends see an idle tab's player offline `PRESENCE_TTL_MS` after its last signed-in request, as they would after a closed or hidden tab.

**Hearthstone semantics.** Hearthstone's friends list shows a player who has stopped touching the game as away, and catches up when they come back. Ten minutes is long enough to read a screen or watch a friends list without a touch, and short enough that a forgotten tab costs the server ten minutes and `SLEEP_AFTER`, not a night.

Rejected:
- A shorter `IDLE_QUIET_MS`. Reading the patch notes or a deck for a few minutes without a touch would close and reopen the socket, each reopen one upgrade against the account's budget ([[R2478000]]).
- Treating only key and click as input. A pointer moving over the page is somebody there.
