Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The web's art tests hold every catalog entry, a held set's included, to its rarity's detail floor ([[R3331001]]), every Legendary and Mythic to a motion of its own ([[R3340000]]) and every Mythic, a token printed Mythic among them, to a diorama ([[R3350000]]), so the Felinors vs Humans entries draw art from the day they land ([[R5235000]]), as the Infinite Assault's did while it was held ([[R4577009]]).

**The scenes.** While the set is held, every entry, its 35 tokens and its 82 Plagiarized reprints included, draws a placeholder scene recipe of its own (`apps/web/src/cards/art/felinorsVsHumansRecipes.ts`), merged into the scene recipes and drawn by the procedural painter as every recipe card is. An original entry's scene is its template in all seven layers, family `classic`: a Human's town square at dusk, a Felinor's moonlit rooftops, KY's classroom under a pocket galaxy, a beast's crag, a construct's forge yard, a Jilliax module's workbench, a living potion's laboratory, #84's temple of Bastet, a Spell's battlefield between the two camps, a Book's library, a Field Spell's market square and a Trap's dungeon hall; its subject is the card's own part or figure, and the hash of its id varies offsets, flips and looks so no two are alike. Every Felinor-tagged entry wears the cat-ears part on its figure's head; #84 is an Egyptian Felinor beside a black cat sitting as Bastet. A Legendary, a Mythic, and a token printed either, draws a set piece over its template from parts whose shapes are counted (stars, motes, sparks and a halo), so it clears its printed rarity's floor. A reprint draws its Infinite Assault original's template, seeded by its own id so it is its own picture, with its sky and glow photocopied to greys, toner grain across the frame and a red stamp in its lower corner; a Mythic reprint adds stars and a drift of motes. No part turns and none is gold, and every part is one the shipped scenes already place. Only `felinorsvshumans-` ids are keys, so no shipped card's picture moves ([[R503]]). A later picture pass replaces them.

**The motion.** The 40 Legendaries and Mythics and the 23 tokens printed either move by motion recipes of their own, appended after every earlier entry so no other card's motion moves: a Felinor's particles are hearts, KY's bubbles, a reprint's its original's kind.

**The dioramas.** The eight Mythics, Yile Prime, the thirteen Jilliax modules and the four Mythic reprints each slice their scene's seven layers into a diorama of their own (`apps/web/src/cards/art/dioramas/felinorsvshumans/`), each idle loop its own.

**No default look while held** ([[R3535000]], [[R1420]]): a held set's Legendary or Mythic draws its normal face until its set ships. The set mark is [[R2718000]]'s, already drawn.

Rejected: listing the entries in `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`, a ratchet that may only shrink; a painter module of the set's own routed by id prefix, which needs dispatch sites of its own; and drawing a reprint with its original's very recipe, which would draw two cards' pictures alike.
