Issue #4154 capped S+ #61.1 Shawarma Law: "Units can only eat at most (3) Shawarma". The line "Every Shawarma after that: Freeze it." goes and the face says "A Unit eats at most three Shawarmas." At its controller's end of turn ([[R62]]) a Unit whose count ([[R3566006]], kept in its memory and cleared as it leaves the field, [[R78]]) is already three eats nothing: its count stays at three and no effect is served, the Freeze least of all. The cap is every Law's, as the count is. The Radiant face keeps "Your Units stop at one Shawarma"; its enemy Units now stop at three. The cap is the ladder's three rungs, not a declared number, so it is printed in words. Through v0.6.6e every Shawarma after the third froze the Unit, which `script_v0_6_6e` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
