The owner asked that the tutorial's opponent never play a card that is not a Core card, even one
added to its hand. Like [[R2201000]]'s floor, it is a rule the engine keeps, not a habit of the AI. A
handicap ([[R180]]) takes one more optional field, `coreOnly`, and `AI_TUTORIAL` sets it ([[R290]]).
No other tier sets it. Like `heroFloor` and `poolHold` ([[R2201001]]), it is serialized and stored
only when true, so no other game's state, hash, replay or golden trace moves.

"Core" is the Core set (`core-NNN`), read from the card's definition. Training Grounds' snapshots of
Core cards (#41–#60) are Training Grounds cards and are barred, as is every other set's card, a fused
or custom card among them.

A seat with it never plays, sets or casts a card of another set:

- **The play.** `legal_actions` lists no play of such a card, from the hand or from a graveyard a
  permission opens, and `reduce` refuses one ([[§10.5]] step 1). The AI, the random policy and the
  timeout's move ([[R186]]) all choose among the legal moves, so none makes one. A Trap or Field
  Spell set face-down is a play too, and is barred the same way.
- **However it reached the hand.** Dealt, redrawn by a mulligan, drawn, Discovered, added by a random
  pool, given by Call to Chaos (any edition), or returned from the field: the bar reads the card in
  hand, not how it got there.
- **Cast on draw** ([[§2.4]], [[R58]]). Such a card the seat draws is not cast. It is drawn into the
  hand as a card that does not cast on draw is, and setup does not cast one it dealt ([[R459]]'s
  path).
- **An effect that takes a card out of that hand into play.** A cast out of the hand casts nothing and
  the card stays. A summon out of the hand ([[R1083]]'s random Unit) picks only among the Units the
  seat may play, so with none it summons nothing, as an empty pick does. No shipped card offers a
  prompt that plays a card from the hand; the cast and summon verbs are where every such effect lands,
  so one written later offers no barred card either.

What it leaves alone: a card the seat holds on the field by other means, a Unit an effect summons
from a library, a graveyard or the catalog, or one it steals, is not played and stays as it is, attacking
and acting as ever. A card an effect casts that is not in the seat's hand (a random cast, a new copy)
is a Core card's effect, not the seat's play. The bar is the seat's own; the other seat is unchanged.

The tutorial tier's frozen win-rate games ([[R290]]) deal both seats from every set, which a lesson's
opponent never holds, so they seat the AI at `AI_TUTORIAL` without the bar and keep measuring the
tier by its resources. [[R1012003]] reads the tutorial opponent by its resources, so a lesson recorded
before the bar existed is still that opponent.

Hearthstone semantics: a scripted encounter's opponent plays the cards its script allows, and what
the rules put in its hand otherwise waits there.

Rejected: an AI that declines non-Core cards, which the random policy and the timeout would not; and
refusing a non-Core card into the hand, which would change what every effect that adds a card does.

Superseded by issue #2533 ([[R2533000]]): the owner ruled that the tutorial opponent holds and plays
only Training Grounds cards that are not Legendary or Mythic. The field is now `tutorialCardsOnly`,
and a Core card is one it may not play. Everything above but which cards are barred still holds:
however the card reached the hand, cast on draw, an effect that casts or summons out of that hand,
the field left alone and the rule the engine's, not the AI's. `coreOnly` is no longer a field, so a
handicap stored with it reads with no bar.
