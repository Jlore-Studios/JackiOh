`cargo jackioh classify --base <rev> [--parent-bin <path>] --claims` holds the pending fragments at the head to what the change does ([[R1365000]]). It fails, naming the family or the fragment, in three cases:
- a family plays differently and no fragment at the head claims it, through its catalog `cards` or its `plays` ([[R1366000]]);
- a fragment claims through `plays` a family that its games against the base build show playing as before. This is checked only once stage 2 has run, and only for claims this change adds: a fragment an earlier pull request left pending keeps the claims its own check confirmed;
- a divergence no card explains has no fragment claiming a rule, or a fragment newly claims a rule and stage 2 found none.

It needs `--parent-bin` whenever stage 1 leaves something unchecked, so a refactor is never asked for a claim. The catalog claims stay `patches check`'s ([[R646]]); this adds only what the catalog diff cannot see.

CI runs it on every pull request as `rust (classify)`, a piece of the required `rust` check. Stage 2 runs against main's build of the base commit: main's own run caches it per commit, or `rust (base build)` builds it beside the head's build. The pull request is labelled with its kind (`kind:cards`, `kind:ai`, `kind:app`, `kind:internal`) and, for a card change, its tier (`tier:major`, `tier:patch`, `tier:micro`). Any other kind or tier label comes off. No label overrides the classifier.
