The issue describes each version's look; this note fixes how each is drawn. Like every look, each is
a pure function of the card's id, name, tags, type and set, base and Radiant faces both, and wears only
a full face, as Full art does ([[R2725000]]). Neither is a full art: each keeps every part of the
normal face, the cost gem, the type's panel colour, the crest, the name banner, the type line, the
rules box and its words, the tags and the stats, and prints its words in the viewer's own hand
([[R2723000]] is Full art's alone).

**Immersive** (`version-immersive`) draws the normal picture in two layers, as Out of frame cuts it
in two ([[R2943001]]): its window draws the picture's backdrop, every layer but the subject's, hazed
as far off (`depthSvg`, `data-art-picture="depth"`, `data-art-part="backdrop"`): softened by
`DEPTH_BLUR`, its colour dulled to `DEPTH_SATURATE` and seen through sky-blue air (`DEPTH_HAZE`, the
palette's sky) at `DEPTH_HAZE_MIX`. Over it a near layer (`.cf-immersive`, in the window's own box and
shape, so clipped as the window is) draws the subject alone, unfiltered (`data-art-part="subject"`),
so the subject stands out of the haze. The near layer takes no ground, no ring and no foil, and no
pointer; the window keeps its bezel and its foil's sheen. It shifts and breathes as [[R2944002]] says.
A Starship in play and a Legacy card, whose painters draw one picture ([[R2249000]], [[R2724000]]),
draw their whole picture hazed with no near layer; the Starship is offered none, and no Legacy card is
Legendary. A picker's tile draws the whole picture hazed.

**Prismatic** (`version-prismatic`) draws the normal picture in mirror chrome (`prismaticSvg`,
`data-art-picture="prismatic"`): greyed, its grey banded light and dark in turn through
`PRISMATIC_CHROME_TABLE`, as polished metal catches what is round it, and mapped from the palette's
slate to near-white (`PRISMATIC_CHROME_DARK`, `PRISMATIC_CHROME_LIGHT`), under an iridescent sheen
corner to corner at `PRISMATIC_SHEEN_OPACITY`. The sheen is `PRISMATIC_SPECTRUM`, the palette's red,
purple, violet, sky, cyan and green, a rainbow with no yellow or orange, there and back
(`PRISMATIC_SHEEN`), so every colour's neighbours are the spectrum's own and no two of them mix to
gold. On a base face wearing no border it draws a rainbow edge of the same stops (a conic gradient),
a chrome bezel (`--pal-slate-100`) and a chrome name banner, slate to near-white, its name in the
palette's darkest slate at AA contrast. The issue's top chase stands where a gold crown would, and
is chrome, never gold, since gold is Radiant's. Its edge
takes the place of a Mythic card's rarity rainbow edge, whose yellow and orange are gold's; the
rarity gem still names its rarity.

Every restyled picture and every part is cached apart from the plain picture, which keeps its key and
bytes.

**Radiant.** [[R2725004]] holds under each: no colour of the two, haze, chrome, sheen, edge, bezel or
banner, nor any colour between two of the sheen's neighbours or of the sheen over the chrome, falls
in the `RADIANT_GOLD_*` window (the issue's `RADIANT_GOLD_CLEARANCE`, which the constants name as that
window), and neither tints a base face's picture gold. A Radiant face keeps its gold-foil frame and
gold bezel, its gilded panel and banner (Prismatic's chrome banner and rainbow edge are a base face's
alone), its gold rules box, [[R277]]'s marks wherever its words show, its gold stats and its picture's
Radiant paint: a Radiant face washes its painted picture back over Immersive's haze
(`DEPTH_RADIANT_WASH`) and Prismatic's chrome (`PRISMATIC_RADIANT_WASH`), and Immersive's near layer
draws its subject in its Radiant paint.
