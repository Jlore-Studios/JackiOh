Job Application's first stage rewards a sent resume with "Hear back in 2-6 turns", and its second stage, the Interview, follows. How many turns is the set's card's own evaluation of the resume (S10); the wait is the engine's. It counts as "Dormant for N turns" does ([[R1839001]]), Hearthstone's way of counting a card's turns.

- **The wait.** `hear_back { turns, stage? }` puts the running card's staged line ([[R1854000]]) on hold: the stage named (the one after the stage it stands at when none is) opens once `turns` of its controller's turns have started, the opponent's turns counting nothing. `memory.quest.hearBack` holds the stage and the turns left; a later wait replaces an earlier one, and 0 or less opens the stage at once.
- **When it opens.** At the start of each of its controller's turns, in [[R62]]'s Brittle stage right after the Dormant countdowns, each line of theirs that waits counts one down, in [[R68]]'s order, and one whose wait ends opens its stage there and then: before that turn's start-of-turn delayed effects, triggers and draw, so the stage's goal counts the turn's draw and every play of the turn ([[R404]]'s count from the moment it opens).
- **While it waits** the line has no stage open, so "complete the current stage" passes it by ([[R1854001]]), and opening a stage by an effect ends the wait. Leaving the field ends it too ([[R78]]).
- **Seen by both players.** The stage it waits for shows the turns left (`quest.stages[n].hearBack`), which the board's stage badge and the inspect overlay print ("hearing back in 2 turns").
