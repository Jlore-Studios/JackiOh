It is a hull. Its yard fuse runs right after the Dormant countdowns. Activate 3 needs at least one
piece, exiles the shipyard and launches a fresh Starship. Any other awakening launches the
shipyard itself.

**A hull with a yard.** Phantom Shipyard prints Dormant with its own `wakesBy` Activate, the
Starship's hull and the yard (`starship_yard`): it enters the field Dormant with a memory, and its
one ability is its text's ([[R1839002]], [[R3539000]]). The fuse keeps all three, so a shipyard
fused into anything is still a yard.

**The yard fuse.** At the start of its controller's turn, right after the Dormant countdowns
([[R1839001]], [[§2.2]]), each of their shipyards fuses in one random Starship Piece — a Radiant
one on a Radiant card — drawn from the match rng, oldest first into the memory. No draw when they
hold no yard ([[R129]]).

**The Activate.** "Activate 3" is three uses a turn ([[R1857003]]), costing no mana, refused while
the shipyard holds no piece. It exiles the shipyard itself, summons a fresh Dormant Starship where
it stood when that zone is still open, else in the first open Starship zone (`open_starship_zone`),
fuses every piece the shipyard held into it, oldest first, and awakens it — a free launch
([[R1852006]]). With neither zone the launch fizzles. The fresh Starship holds no price: it
launches for the launch cost as before.

**Any other awakening.** An effect that awakens the shipyard launches the shipyard itself with
whatever it holds ([[R1852003]]), as any hull's awakening is its launch.
