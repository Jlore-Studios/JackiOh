[[R3574000]]'s sweep of record banned four Stinkyscape+ cards. Fullsend 3 (issue #3575) measured each again on a quiet container, on the AI with its fixes (`training/history/sweep-2026-11-13-2.md`): S+ #47 Plastanium's `timeout` came from a step the search simulated up to four times from one state, which [[R3575603]] answers from a memo, so the decision is unchanged and inside `AI_SWEEP.decision_ms`; S+ #163 Accelerationist's `timeout` fell while other builds shared the sweep's cores and does not recur. That report clears both.

S+ #81 Doomsayer and S+ #157 Phantom Shipyard stay `neverPlayed` at Hard. [[R3575400]], [[R3575402]] and [[R3575404]] price a hand card's Activate, a shipyard's turn-start fuse and a Dormant card's launch, which clears Phantom Shipyard at Easy. At Hard, though, the best line that plays either card scored below the line the AI chose in every position probed (24 for Doomsayer, 10 for the Shipyard, by 20 to 47 points for the Shipyard), and forcing the Shipyard into every game changed no result. Clearing them would take a weight chosen to pass the sweep, which [[R186]] forbids. So, by the owner's call (2026-11-13), the unban lane lifts both ([[R2521010]]): their ids join `SHADOW_UNBANNED`, `sweep --write` leaves their rows out of `SHADOW_BAN`, and the AI deals itself every Stinkyscape+ card, as #2521 lifted S #149 Wildar and TG #25 Trigor. A later sweep of record that bans either again drops it from the list, as [[R2521010]] says. The unban list may name a card of any set the set table gives a shadow-ban share ([[R3114001]]), Stinkyscape+ among them.
