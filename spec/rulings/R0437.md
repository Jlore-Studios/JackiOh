A card an effect has marked for something still to come carries that mark in both players' views while it waits: #50 K-Pop Fanatic's pending steal ([[R76]]) marks its target from the Cry until the steal resolves or fizzles, and a client draws the mark on the card as a corruption sparkle in the mark's colour (purple for #50), one effect reusable in other colours for other marks. The target was chosen in a public play, so the mark tells neither player anything the play did not; on a face-down target the other player's back carries it ([[R33]]). It is a view rule, drawn by the client (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
