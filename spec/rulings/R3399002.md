Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. The issue asks that a Mythic's takeover be its own, "with its own art filling the screen". The catalog's card art is procedural and no painted art ships (`ART_MANIFEST` is empty), so each of Classic+'s four Mythics takes a takeover look of its own ([[R3332000]]'s `takeover`) and a picture drawn for it (`fx/takeoverArt.ts`, `TAKEOVER_ART_DRAWINGS`, a full-frame SVG keyed by look, drawn in the art layer that [[R3400000]]'s looks' parts stand in):

- `zealot` (Zephrys Zealotism): a robed wind-zealot at a lectern of cloud, finger raised, an indigo cyclone spinning across the frame.
- `portal` (Portal to the Past): a carved stone ring opening into a sepia vortex, ruins drifting toward the viewer.
- `stair` (Twice Forward One Step Backwards): a stone stair turning on itself, cyan cracks in violet void.
- `vineyard` (Mythic Grape): a violet grape swelling over terraced vines spiralling into it.

The takeover cue mounts its look's picture in that art layer (`.takeover-art`, over the darkened board and under the sweep, rays and wave) while it plays, fading in and out over the cue and turning or drifting its marked parts by transform and opacity alone; a look with no drawing draws none (the shared `prismatic` and the marquee looks; [[R3400000]]'s and [[R3405000]]'s looks draw their parts there instead, [[R3407000]]'s their card's own picture). Under Reduce Motion the takeover's still ([[R2722001]]) shows the same picture in the same layer, unanimated.

Since this ruling, [[R2722001]]'s "a card-shaped silhouette, never a face" holds as before: the picture is drawn for the look, is no card face, carries no card's name or text, and keys on the look alone, never on a definition the page could read ([[R202]]); a hidden Mythic takes no takeover, so no picture. The effects speed, Big moments and the intensity act on it as on the takeover ([[R201]]).

Rejected: enlarging the card's procedural art to fill the screen, which would show a card face over the board and needs a painted asset this repository does not have; and a canvas-drawn picture, which the DOM layer's existing takeover element and its still already host.
