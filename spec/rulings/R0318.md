The client shows each of [[§2.4]]'s overflows on both seats from its event alone (CLAUDE.md rule 7). `fatigue`: the owner's library pile shakes and dims as it comes up empty and a "Fatigue N" badge (the event's `count`) rises from it, then the `damage` after it lands on the hero with its number; a hit Armor took whole still shows the badge, and the hero takes [[R240]]'s hit of 0. `burned`: the card rises over its owner's hand under a "Hand full" tag, catches fire and burns away toward the graveyard, showing its face when the viewer reads the event and a back when the event carries the sentinel. `libraryOverflow`: a "Deck full" tag ([[R373]]) flashes on the owner's library pile and the refused card, face or back by the same test, bounces off it and fizzles into smoke (`notCreated`, `ceased`) or drops toward the graveyard (`graveyard`). The notices are the board's own elements, named by testid, and stay up until the board shows the next view, as a damage number does, each entry drawing its own, so #33's three refused copies are three refusals; a fatigue hit that ends the game, which the finished view's drain cuts short, is replayed by the effects layer with the empty library it came out of ([[R200]]'s killing blow); the motion is BUILD M5-T4's rows, whose durations [[R201]]'s speed scales and reduced motion zeroes, when nothing is drawn at all. The effects layer adds its recipes (`fatigue`, `overflow`, `burn`) under [[R200]] and [[R202]]. Like every event, each plays once on each seat, however [[R97]] has rewritten the window it arrives in (the event diff of [[§10.10]])
