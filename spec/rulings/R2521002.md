"After this destroys a Unit, it may attack again" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #94 Tmack Trav, [[R1869002]]) gives Tmack a fresh exertion and nothing else: the `mayAttackAgain` it answers its kill with carries `keepSick` (`MayAttackAgainArgs.keep_sick`), so the verb resets both halves of the exertion ([[§4.1]]) and leaves `summonedTurn` as it is. A Tmack Trav that entered the field this turn is still summoning sick after its kill, and its Rush lets it attack again an enemy Unit only ([[§6.1]]): the enemy hero is neither offered by `legalActions` nor allowed ("Rush cannot hit the hero on its summon turn"). One that entered on an earlier turn is not sick, so its extra attack may hit the hero as before. Each kill still frees it again, forced attacks included ([[R1869002]]). Without `keepSick` the verb is unchanged (B5 E35): it lifts the sickness for the rest of the turn, so Classic+ #73.1 Classic Golem's attack again may still go face. This is the owner's balance pass (issue #2521): a Tmack Trav played with Rush that destroyed a Unit was offered `hero-p2` the same turn, as the Golem's reading of the verb lifted the sickness Rush exists to keep. Rejected: changing the verb for every card, which would take the Golem's attack on the hero away with no text of its own saying so.
