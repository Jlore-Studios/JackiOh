## R1141 — A marked hand shows its owner each mark, the other player only the count

Built (Meditative #76). The hand's owner sees which of their cards carry the mark, on each marked
card; the caster sees only how many cards in that hand are marked, as `handMarked` on their view
of the other seat — never which, and each card met only as it reaches their hand ([[R97]]). The
viewer's own seat carries no count, and neither does a finished game, where both hands are
revealed ([[R434]]).
