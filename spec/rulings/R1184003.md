`summon` takes the X a card is summoned with (`x`), set before it lands, as if it were played for that X (B2.7): a face that prints its stats as multiples of X (`xStats`) is those multiples of it, and whatever reads the card's X (`x_of`) reads it. With none the card has no X and is its printed 0/0, as a Recruit or a copy of C+ #69 Buff Billy is. M+ #20 summons its Lil' Kenny so, with X its right answers in a row: the token prints 0/0 with `xStats` 1/1, Radiant 2/2, so it is X/X and its Radiant face 2X/2X as data. Its keywords are a keyword while a condition holds ([[§6.1]], C #69's shape) on its X, cumulatively: from X 4 Rush, 8 Divine Shield, 12 First Strike, 16 Cleave, 20 Lifesteal, 30 Indestructible. They read X, never its stats, so a buff gives none and the Radiant face has the same thresholds on X; a Nerf or a Buff that moves X moves both its stats and its keywords. A Vanilla takes them, being its text, and keeps X/X, X being no buff ([[R781003]]). A copy of a Kenny has no X, as a copy of Buff Billy has none (B2.7): it is a 0/0 with no keyword. A Unit token, it ceases to exist when it leaves the field ([[R11]]), so no Kenny carries its X anywhere else.
