"Your End of turn effects" are the `endOfTurn` hooks [[§2.2]]'s end-of-turn trigger step would queue
for you now (MD-A14): field cards and the return-flagged Spells in your graveyard ([[R153]],
[[R155]], M #9's multiplier included), settled after the Cry's list in [[R68]]'s order. The turn
does not end, and they run again at its real end; the trap window and the delayed effects are not
included ([[R62]]). A return Spell (M #8, Core #31) comes back now and can be played again this
turn. Each next round is queued as the round entry pops, so a unit summoned in round 1 answers
round 2; `times` bounds the rounds.
