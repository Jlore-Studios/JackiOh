Issue #4154 made S+ #121.1 Toxopid Leg "Immune to Toxic" (the issue's "Immune to poison": Toxic is the game's poison, [[§6.3]]). It is a static flag (`immuneToToxic`) read where every Toxic goes on (`afflictions::afflict`): while the Unit is on the field a Toxic verb on it adds nothing, a "damage also applies Toxic" rider after a hit it survived adds nothing ([[R3540002]]), and a hit from a source whose damage is Toxic, which deals nothing and becomes Toxic instead, leaves it untouched ([[R3540003]]); no `counterChanged` is reported for it. Burn and every other damage still reach it. A fused card takes the flag from any ingredient that has it, as every true flag combines. Rejected: a keyword in [[§6.1]]'s table, which a Nerf or a Buff could move and the random keyword pool could deal, for one token's printed line.
