Training Grounds #12 is named Fortify, and Meditative #22 Mind Games' text adds "a Fortify Mind" (M #22.1). [[R279]]'s whole-word matcher would read that text as naming Fortify too once Training Grounds ships, linking Mind Games to a card it never mentions. The designer's name is kept, and "Fortify Mind" joins [[R961]]'s rules phrases: a name that lies inside the phrase (the "Fortify" of "Fortify Mind") names no card. Unlike "Jade Counter", the phrase is itself a card's name, so a match that is the whole phrase is not inside it: "Fortify Mind" still names M #22.1, and its plural "Fortify Minds" too, while "Fortify" outside the phrase still names TG #12. The web's twin of the matcher (`apps/web/src/cards/refs.ts`) reads the phrases the same way.
