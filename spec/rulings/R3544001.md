The "Cleave" of S+ #143 Fire Beam v3, #144 v4 and #145 v5 puts the same Burn N on each Unit beside the chosen one — the Units of the same side and row ([[§3.1]], `adjacent_to`), read before the first Burn — as a Stinkyscape weapon's Cleave reads its hit ([[R1862000]]). Each Burn adds to the count already there ([[R3540000]]), and none lands on the enemy hero. It is the Activate's splash, not a keyword the Beam or its host gains. Rejected: granting the Cleave keyword, and Burning the chosen Unit alone.
