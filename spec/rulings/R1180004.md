"Your next Spell costs health equal to N times its cost instead of mana" (Meditative+ #6 Bloodbloom) is a player modifier until a Spell uses it, across turns. The next Spell its player plays from their hand, face-up, takes no mana: it loses N times its cost in health with the play ([[§10.5]] step 2), lost health and not damage ([[R18]]), the cost being the one every modifier left ([[R65]]; an X Spell's X, chosen as [[R348]] says, at most the player's mana). `legal_actions` and the refusal read the same rule, so a Spell only the health affords is offered; paying may take the hero to 0, and that player loses at the next state check, as Hearthstone's Bloodbloom allows. The Spell still costs its cost for every rule that reads a cost or the cost paid ([[R56]]): only what pays it changes, and the discounts and price rules it met are spent as usual. A (0) Spell uses it and loses nothing. A Field Spell, a Trap, a Spell set face-down (a Trap's play, [[R783005]]), a play from a graveyard and a cast, which pays nothing ([[R70]]), never use it. A newer one replaces an older one, so several never stack. The Radiant face's price also gives that Spell Echo 1, queued with its own as it is played ([[R30]]'s shape).
