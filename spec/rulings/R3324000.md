Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes. The client reads only the patch list `GET /api/stats/cards` already sends ([[R1973002]]).

**Why.** The patch bar was a `<select>` between a Previous and a Next button. After a new patch shipped, the page opened on it, and while the patch was short of games its cards showed the previous window's figures ([[R1367000]]) under the new patch's name. The owner asked in #3324 for a tab for each new patch (never a micro) that says its data is still being collected.

**The tabs.** The picker is a row of patch tabs, newest first, between the patch before and the patch after (each a compact arrow named for its patch, disabled where there is none). The row holds:

- the newest patch, whatever its games, so a patch gets a tab as soon as the server lists it;
- the `STATS_PATCHES_OFFERED` (5) newest patches with `STATS_PATCH_MIN_GAMES` (1,000) games;
- the patch shown, when it is not one of those.

The tabs are a `Segmented` group (#3222): each a pressed-state button that names its patch and shows its games under the name. The shown patch's tab is pressed, and choosing the newest asks with no `?patch=`, as [[R1973002]] had it. A micro opens no window of its own ([[R1973001]]), so it never adds a tab.

**Collecting.** The newest patch is collecting while it has fewer than `STATS_PATCH_MIN_GAMES` games, and its tab says "Collecting data" in place of its games. While it is shown:

- the Cards and Sets tabs say "Data for patch X is still being collected at this time." in the page's empty state, in place of the patch's figures, filters, Game figures panel and sets, and their button goes to the patch before;
- the games tile is hidden;
- the page reads no sets.

The page shows no count and no progress toward a floor ([[R661]]), and no figure of another window under the patch's name. The Players tab and the card drill-down belong to no patch and are unchanged. An older patch short of games is not collecting: its collecting ended when a newer patch shipped, so it shows its figures as before.

**Unchanged.** The games tile stays the patch's games and the fixed top's one tile ([[R1646000]]); it now stands under the patch tabs. A server with no patch list shows no tabs and nothing collecting, as it showed no bar.

Hearthstone semantics: a stats site's newest patch reads "collecting data" until its games come in.

Rejected:

- Opening the page on the newest patch with enough games, which needs a second read before the first draws.
- A floor of the gate's live games (`PUBLIC_STATS_MIN_LIVE_GAMES`), which the page never shows or names ([[R661]], [[R2325000]]).
- A new constant: the tabs' own floor already says when a patch has games enough to offer.
- Calling an older patch short of games collecting.
- `Tabs` (`role="tab"`) for the patches: they choose what the view tabs show, not a panel of their own.
