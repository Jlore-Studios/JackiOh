IA #15 Drone Foundry's "Summon a Drone. Then, if you have 4 or more Drones, give your Drones +1/+1" names the set's Drone token, IA #4.1. A "Drone" is that card alone: a Drone Escort (IA #10.1), a Drone Interceptor or any other card whose name holds the word is none. The count is read once the start of turn's summons have landed ([[R64]], none on a full board, [[§3.2]]), over its controller's Drones on the field; when they number `drones` (4) or more, each of them gets the buff, the ones just summoned included, and otherwise none does.
