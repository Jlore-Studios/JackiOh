Not a rule of the game (CLAUDE.md rule 7): how the Collection lets a player wear and buy a card's looks. The owner
asked in #3016 for an equip and buy screen beside the preview, where buying prompts for a token or such; this is
the issue's second part, built on #3015's ladders ([[R3015007]]).

- **Where it opens.** "Equip or buy" on a grid card's hover preview and on its fullscreen preview ([[R3016000]])
  opens the card's screen: fullscreen, modal and focus trapped. From the fullscreen preview it opens over it, and
  Escape, Close or Back close the screen alone. The focus goes back to what opened it, else to the card.
- **The ladders.** Each variant the card has, the Default first, its levels lowest first, as [[R3015007]] lists
  them, each owned, next or locked.
  - **Equip.** An owned level shows Equip, which wears it on this card alone ([[R3015005]]): the pick the device
    keeps and sends (`writeCardLevel`). The worn one reads Equipped. A level not owned has no Equip.
  - **Buy.** The next level, every level below it owned ([[R3015004]]), shows Buy. Buy asks, in the level's place,
    as the Chase Shard's purchase does ([[R2683000]], [[R2480000]]): an alert dialog naming the level and what it
    spends, a choice of the ways to pay, then Confirm and Cancel, Cancel focused. The ways are each held cosmetic
    token whose tier covers the level's rarity ([[R1305003]]; none covers a Mythic, [[R2728000]]), the cheapest
    first and chosen, and for a chase a full Chase Shard bar ([[§14.6]]). Confirm sends the one purchase,
    `POST /api/tokens/redeem-cosmetic` or `POST /api/cosmetic-packs/chase`, and ownership is read again. Cancel and
    Escape send nothing, and the focus goes back to Buy.
  - **Nothing pays.** With no way to pay, Buy is disabled and says what is missing: a card short of
    `MAX_COPIES`, on which no per-card cosmetic is bought (the server's own rule); else that no held token covers
    it and, for a chase, that the bar isn't full.
  - **Locked.** A level past the next has neither Equip nor Buy, and names the level it needs first.
  - **Refused.** A refusal shows the server's words under the level, and nothing on the screen changes.
- **Hovering a level.** On this screen and on the card view's ladders, a resting mouse or the keyboard's focus on
  a level opens the hover preview of [[R3016000]] beside it, wearing that one level in its three views, owned or
  not, as the card view's large face shows it; no pick is written.
- **Tokens stay off lists.** The screen is fullscreen and modal like the preview, not a list or a grid, so no list
  or grid carries a spend button ([[R1953001]], [[R2730000]]). The server still decides what a token or the bar
  buys; the screen only hides what it would refuse.

This amends [[R2730000]] (*The spend button lives only there*: a held token and the Chase Shard bar are also spent
from a card's equip and buy screen, a fullscreen screen of its own) and [[R3015007]] (hovering a rung also opens its
hover preview in three views).

Hearthstone semantics: a card is crafted from its large view in the collection.

Rejected: a Buy on every rung of the card view, which [[R3015007]] keeps to its fullscreen preview's offer; one Buy
per way to pay, which spends without the question [[R2683000]] asks; and drawing a level the player does not own as
its art alone (the card view's locked rung), where the three views show it whole.
