A Created card ([[R943]]) has its own definition, so it found no picks of its own and wore its kinds'
defaults ([[R1953001]]). It now wears the foil, finish and border its **generator** wears: a foil card's tokens are
foil too.

**What the engine keeps.** `GameState.generators` maps a Created card's instance id to the catalog id
of the card that generated it. A card whose script mints a card records it as it mints, through the
effect verbs that do (a card added to a hand or shuffled into a library, a copy, a cast, a summon, a
token, a craft, a transform's new card, Call to Chaos's recast) and Infinite Reserves' token. A card a
Created card mints carries the root, the first card of the chain, never the card between, so a token's
token wears the first card's dress. A card the generator gives the other player records nothing, nor
does a card a rule mints (The Coin, setup, a Resource, a Glitch reset, a Devil's Pact replacement, a
Starship), a fused card, or a card whose generator is no catalog card (a crafted, fused or custom card
wears no per-card pick). A continuation whose card has ceased to exist names its definition (R127),
which stands in for the card. A Created card that takes a fresh id going face-down ([[R227]]: a Trap
set from hand, summoned or flickered into a backrow) keeps its generator under the new id, as C+ #35's
history follows it ([[R419]]), so it still wears its generator's dress and what it makes still names
the root. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads the record, the
state hash leaves it out, as it leaves out the nonce log and the opening, and the golden replay's view
texts leave out the field that carries it, so no recorded game, trace, practice save, match hash or
shipped-catalog fold ([[R767000]]) changes, and nothing plays differently. A JSON round trip keeps it.

**Who is told.** A card view, a unit's and a public backrow card's carry `createdBy`, the generator's
catalog id, on the viewer's own Created card, and only where the view is built at all: where the
viewer may read the card, as `created` is ([[R943]]). Never on another player's card, a hidden hand, a
face-down card, a prompt's option or a library entry. The AI's redacted state keeps the seat's own
cards' generators, so its view of its own cards survives redaction ([[R185]]'s B10), and drops the
opponent's, which would name what made a hidden card.

**What is worn.** On a face that is the viewer's own copy ([[R1655000]]), a Created card wears the
foil, finish and border its generator's own copy would wear: the generator's own picks, else their
kinds' defaults ([[R1953001]]), each only where the viewer owns it on the generator. Its full art is its own card's,
never inherited, since a full art is made for one card, but for the tokens [[R2655002]] lists, which
print their parent card's. Its own rarity rules hold: a Mythic face wears
no cosmetic foil, and a Radiant face none of the three ([[R1295000]]). A Created card with no generator
the view names wears its own card's picks, as before. A face that is not the viewer's own copy (an
opponent's, or any on a watched game or an online replay) wears its seat's frozen loadout
([[R1834004]]), which dresses a Created card by its own card's entry alone, never its generator's:
the loadouts name no generator, and the generator is told to its owner alone ([[§14.2]]).

Hearthstone semantics: a golden minion's tokens are golden, and a card's "Created by" names the card
that made it, to its owner.

Rejected: the generator on `CardInstance`, a key inside every Created card that the state hash cannot
leave out, so every game that creates a card would hash differently and its golden traces, recorded by
an engine no longer in the tree, could not be recorded again; the intermediate card as the generator
of a chain; the full art inherited; and the generator told to the opponent, which would name what
made a card of another player's, where the frozen loadouts dress that card by its own card alone
([[R1834003]], [[R1834004]]).
