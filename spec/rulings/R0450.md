Targeting means a declared `target` pick or a `target` prompt answer. A targeting cost (Classic #89 Paul Allen's Ghost) is paid in random discards at pay time ([[R682]]): checked at [[§10.5]] step 1 against the cards held outside the play or activation, and paid at [[§10.5]] step 2 at random — never the card being played or a hand card the play or activation picks — and a prompt answer naming such a card pays before it goes on. It is not a legal target with too few other cards in hand, and the cost binds both players. A Spell's declarations and prompts never offer a card Immune to Spells. A hand interceptor is summoned (no Cry, summoning sick) and the first pick moves to it if it fits that declaration; a cost already paid stays paid
