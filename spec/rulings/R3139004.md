#89 Corpse Eater reads "Rush, Brittle 1 / While this is in your hand, it gains the attack and health of each Unit that dies. / After this attacks a Unit, it may attack again." and its Radiant face "Rush, Divine Shield, Brittle 3 / … twice … / After this attacks a Unit, it may attack again.". Its stats, 2/2 and 4/4, and its growth in the hand are as they were.

- **"May attack again" is Tmack Trav's refresh** ([[R1869002]]): the card runs after each of its attacks ([[R2285001]]), a declared one or a forced one, and only while it stands: an Eater that died in the combat attacks no more. When the target was a Unit it gets a fresh exertion this turn, still summoning sick if it arrived this turn, so on that turn Rush lets it attack again a Unit and never the hero ([[R2521002]]). An attack on the hero gives none.
- **No cap.** Each attack on a Unit it survives gives another, at the same Unit or another, until it dies or no Unit remains, so on the turn it arrives its Rush chain ends only against a Unit that kills it or an empty enemy side.
- **Brittle.** A printed Brittle starts on arrival and first ticks at its controller's start of turn t + 2 or later ([[R638]]), so Brittle 1 destroys the base face at its controller's next turn start, after the turn it arrived and the opponent's, and Brittle 3 the Radiant face at the third. It is an ordinary destroy, which Indestructible ignores.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script and the definition it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, with no Brittle and no repeat attack ([[R767000]]). Legacy: Corpse Eater, TG #56, is its own card ([[R2195003]]).
