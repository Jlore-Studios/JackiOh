A voice-line emote is the speaker's own line: each portrait has its own five, rendered through the voice-line pipeline as `emote-<portrait>-<line>` files that count toward the voice budget, spoken on the voice channel, which a playing card line yields to and a starting card line waits behind as it does a death line ([[R204]]), and captioned by a speech bubble beside the speaker's portrait for the line's audible span, two seconds at least and four at most, shown even with voice off. An emoji emote is the same five original inline-SVG stickers for every portrait — Sob, Yawn, Laugh, Angry and a Wah Wah whose small trombone slides in — popping out of the sender's portrait with a bounce, a hold and a fade over two seconds, a fade only under Reduce Motion; each is a Web Audio synthesis on the effects channel, no files: a wobbly falling whimper, a long falling breath, a quick bouncing "ha-ha", a short growl, and the sad-trombone sting of four descending notes whose last holds with vibrato
