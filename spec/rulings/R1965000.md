A card that lets its player play cards from their graveyard ([[R454]]: C #90 In Too Deep's reward L puts no
price on it) makes a (0) Spell there free to play again and again, since it lands back in the graveyard each
time. With its controller's deck empty, C #37 Last Hurrah ("Draw your deck") and M #62 Small Time Recruits
("Draw (1) Cost Units from your deck") draw nothing, Last Hurrah only queuing once more the discard of its
player's hand at the end of the turn, and The Coin gains mana this turn, which has no cap. Each
replay reads as a new position to the AI's search, the turn's record of plays being part of it, and none
moves the lethal solver's distance from lethal (`ready_gap`), whose best-first walk breaks a tie toward the
longer line. So the solver proved a lethal behind as many replays as its depth allowed, and `decide`, which
plays only a line's first action and then plans again ([[§9.9]]), replayed the card at every decision: the
turn never ended.

The AI decides this: `decide` plays a lethal the solver proved trimmed to the actions it needs
(`trim_lethal`). It keeps the shortest suffix of the line that still wins on every determinization the
solver proved it on, so the action it plays is one the rest of the line cannot win without. Then it drops,
one at a time from the front and starting over after each, any action the line still wins without on all
of them, until every action left is needed. Each try replays the line on those determinizations through
`simulate`, each step legal there, as the solver's own proof does and at its price. A line is at most
`AI_SEARCH.lethal_max_depth` long. When the decision's budget or clock stops the trim before it has found
that suffix, the line it holds may still open with a free play, and a clock that stops every decision at
the same point would have that play made at each: no lethal is played then, and the decision goes on as if
the solver had found none, its counter spent, so it ends the turn. Stopped after, the line is played as far
as it was cut.

The walks are unchanged, so every lethal they found they still find, a free replay that puts cards in hand
among them (M+ #1.1 Base Camp's two Coins, played again from the graveyard), and a step the lethal needs is
kept. A style that passes over a lethal ([[R1452]]) passes over the line as found. The beam needs no such
rule: the evaluation reads none of the play record and a hand discard queued twice as one queued once, so
a line of replays that draw nothing scores the same as the End Turn without them, and the tie goes to the
End Turn, which the beam closes first; mana a Coin leaves unspent counts against its line there.

The trim reads only the determinizations the solver was given, so no hidden card changes it ([[R185]]). It
adds no rule of the game: the replays stay legal, and In Too Deep, Last Hurrah, Small Time Recruits and The
Coin play as before.
