Classic #27 Pestilent Slime gained "Death: Spread its Plague Counters among your permanents" on both faces in the owner's first Stinkyscape balance pass (issue #2521). The spec did not say how counters spread, so this follows the counter's own rules ([[§6.3]] Plague Counter, [[R471]]) and Hearthstone's "split among" random spreads, each piece placed on its own.

- **What it spreads.** The counters the Slime held as it died, read off the Death's snapshot, which is taken before [[R78]]'s reset clears them ([[R89]]). A Slime that died with none places nothing and draws nothing ([[R129]]).
- **One placement each.** Each counter is one placement of one counter (`placePlagueRandom` of one card), so each is one `counterChanged` carrying `placed`, and a "whenever Plague Counters are placed on this" answers it once ([[R471]]). A placement onto another Pestilent Slime is multiplied by that Slime's multiplier (×2, Radiant ×3), as every placement onto it is.
- **Where.** A permanent its controller (the Slime's as it died) controls on the field: the top of a unit pile, a Unit a carrier holds or one standing in a backrow zone, and every backrow card, face-up or face-down, as `{ side: "self", rows: ["units", "backrow"] }` reaches them. The Slime itself is in the graveyard by then and is no candidate; the enemy's permanents never are.
- **Random, each its own.** Each placement draws its permanent from the match rng as it resolves, uniformly over that side's permanents then ([[R60]]), so one permanent may take several and the counters spread only as the draws fall. The placements are one effect list, so the state check waits for all of them ([[R59]]).
- **None there.** With no permanent of its controller's on the field, the counters are lost: nothing is placed and nothing is drawn.
