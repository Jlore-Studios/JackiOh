A cast of Mind Games gives the search three children, one per choice, that differ only in the secret record the cast files: the reward waiting in `delayed` carries the secret's id, never its choice ([[R861]]), and the id, minted off `next_id`, is the same in all three. `search_signature` read no secret, so the beam's duplicate check and the lethal solver's visited set kept only the first listed, Greed, and the AI never weighed Attack's damage or Defend's heal. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the search changes no game.

**What changes.** While a position holds a secret, its signature holds one more part: each record's owner, its choice (none where `redact` dropped it, [[R865]]) and whether it is revealed, sorted, without the id. A position with no secret signs as before. The AI searches only the redacted state and the worlds `determinize` samples from it, so the opponent's unrevealed choice is never read ([[R185]]). The three children are three positions, and the reply scores each at the caster's next turn start, where its reward lands ([[§9.9]], [[R3696001]]).

**Rejected.** Closing the two later choices as lane variants (#1797): a variant is played no further, and `AI_SEARCH.zone_variants` (2) keeps only two of three. Leaving the secrets out of the lethal solver's and the concession check's walks ([[R2660000]]): one signature keeps one meaning of a position, and each walk's node budget bounds the extra cost. Signing the id: it is the same in the three children. Accepted limit: where none of the three lines makes the shortlist on its own, [[R3696000]]'s one payoff line is the first of them, Greed, as before.
