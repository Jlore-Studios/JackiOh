A card that [[§4.5]] step 1 collects (a Unit at 0 or less health or marked destroyed, a backrow card marked destroyed, a Brittle crumble among them), or that a Sacrifice takes, a Tribute's included ([[§6.3]]), has died, whatever a replacement then does with it: C #50 Voidwalker's "would go to a graveyard" aura ([[R398]]) or Stinkyscape #105 Disintegrex's "would be Destroyed" one ([[R1849002]]) only changes where it goes, to its exile pile instead of its graveyard. This follows Hearthstone, where a Deathrattle and Reborn act on a death whatever becomes of the body.

**Death.** Its Death fires in [[R68]]'s order among the cards that died beside it, off its last-known state ([[R78]]), so M #94 Shrinking Felinor dying under either aura summons the next, smaller Felinor. The card is already in exile as the hook runs.

**Reborn.** A Unit with Reborn reserves its zone ([[R64]]) and returns there at 1 health without Reborn, taken out of the exile pile it died into, as [[§4.5]] step 4 takes one out of its graveyard. Step 4 returns it only from the pile it died into, so a body a Death hook moved on is not revived. Dying again, now without Reborn, it is exiled again and its Death fires again ([[R8]]).

**Reports and counts.** `destroyed` reports it, so "destroys a Unit" triggers and quests see the death, and the destroyed counter counts it ([[R55]]); then `exiled` reports where it went, and a card that reaches the pile counts as an exile too, which a Reborn return does not take back. A unit token ceases to exist instead, as on any death ([[R11]]), and its Death and Reborn act as on any other; `exiled` reports it all the same, as for any token exiled.

**Starship Pieces.** A piece that dies this way fuses a copy into its owner's Dormant Starship as any dead piece does ([[R1852000]]), so Disintegrex, itself a piece, fuses when it dies under its own aura; the original stays in exile.

**What does not count.** A card C #50 exiles on any other way to a graveyard (a discard, a resolved Spell, a burn at the hand cap, a spent Trap, a mill) has not died and fires nothing, as before, and neither does a plain exile: the Exile verb, an exile mark ([[R1124]]). A dying card a replacement sends to its library's bottom fires its Death but is not Reborn, which takes a body only from its graveyard or exile pile; no shipped card does that to a dying card.

Supersedes [[R461]], whose "not a death" no longer holds; only its landing in exile stands. Amends [[R1849002]], [[R398]], [[R1852000]] and [[R2564000]]. Rejected: keeping [[R461]], which let either aura hard-counter Death and Reborn though neither card's text claims to; and returning a Reborn body from whichever pile holds it, which would revive one a Death hook exiled out of its graveyard.
