Lobby on the row of a friend who is not offline, on Play together ([[§9.5]]), makes a 1v1 Best of 1 lobby ([[R3730000]]), whose mode its host may change there ([[R3730003]]), invites the friend into its empty seat ([[R3730005]]) and opens it. It is one of the page's requests, one at a time: from the press until the server answers, the page's Challenge, Lobby, Remove, Block and request controls are disabled, and a second press sends nothing, so a double press makes one lobby and sends one invite. A refused create opens nothing and says the server's words on Play together. A refused invite (a block, the challenger's `CHALLENGES_PER_HOUR`, a lobby already full) still opens the lobby made, which stands either way and counts against the host's `MAX_OPEN_ROOMS_PER_HOST` ([[R3730004]]). Its screen then says the refusal in the server's words, once, until the next request made there, and the host invites again from its list or shares the code. A reload of the lobby says nothing again. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Rejected: staying on Play together with only the error, which leaves a lobby made that the player never sees; closing the lobby when its invite is refused, a second request that can fail too, when the lobby is still of use by its code.
