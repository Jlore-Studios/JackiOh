The AI's evaluation prices an own hand card by its cost (`hand_card + hand_per_cost × min(cost, hand_cost_cap)`), the curve a deck is built on and the hand weights were fit to. A card a play makes need not sit on that curve, since its generator sets its price: S+ #129, #130 and #131 add an Automa card with no Tribute ([[R3565000]]), a 6/3 with Armor 2 at (2), a 12/8 Windfury at (4); S+ #133 adds Daglare, a (0) 3/2, and each next rung; S+ #98 adds two big Stinkyscape bodies. The AI counted each such card at its cost alone, so a play that made one, or a Field Spell whose start-of-turn hook does ([[R2477000]]), counted a card's worth of a cheap card, and the sweep found the cards almost never played at Hard and S+ #130 never at Easy. No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** An own hand card that is Created ([[R943]]: minted after the deal) and a Unit on the face it enters counts the larger of its cost price and a floor: `slumber_turn_share` (0.5, [[R2380000]]'s price of a unit one turn off) of its body (its face with buffs and its own keywords, as the face uplift reads it, by the same stat and keyword weights) less its crystals (`own_cost`, each at `unspent_mana`, a crystal's price at a turn's end) and less its Tribute (`tribute_cost_of`, so [[R3565000]]'s rider is read): the worth of the cheapest own units it would destroy, which a Tribute set takes first. A card without the units its Tribute asks, an X-cost card, a Spell and every dealt card keep the cost price, and the Radiant and extra-turn shares still add on top. A Field Spell's start-of-turn hook that adds such a card is priced by it through the hook's one firing, unchanged. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps `slumber_turn_share` 0, so it reads none.

Rejected: the floor for every hand Unit, dealt ones too, which moves every hand the weights were fit to; a curve of face worth per cost measured over the catalog, a table every balance patch would move; a share of its own, a weight no measurement sets; and counting each Created card's Tribute against units the others in hand already claimed, which a decision's evaluation cannot order.
