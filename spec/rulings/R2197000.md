"When you summon a unit, deal 1 damage to a random enemy" ([[§8.11]] TG #18 Knife Juggler, printed "After you summon a Unit") is a Unit arriving on its controller's side however it is summoned, read as [[R1875003]] reads an arrival turned to its own side: a summon (a Recruit, a token, a copy, Reborn) at once, a played Unit once it resolves, its Cry first, its play's own placement not counted twice; a Unit standing in a backrow zone counts ([[R1081000]]). Hearthstone's "after you summon" counts plays and summons alike. The Juggler's own arrival is not one; a steal ([[R171]]) and an animating card ([[R383]]) are no summon. Each arrival is one hit of the printed damage on one random enemy, the enemy hero or a standing enemy Unit, drawn from the match rng ([[R60]]), each hit followed by the state check ([[R59]]). The hit is the Juggler's, so its printed Pierce applies to it ([[§6.1]]). Rejected: counting the Juggler's own arrival, and an enemy Unit only, against Hearthstone's "random enemy".
