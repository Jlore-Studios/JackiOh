`AI_BUDGET` is 1,200 nodes, of which the lethal solver may spend 300, in its old shape (3 determinizations, a beam of 4, 20 root moves and 6 deeper, 8 deep, 3 finalists), at every tier ([[R180]]). It was 600 and 150 until the owner asked for it to be doubled. The quality gates and the shadow-ban sweep (`AI_GATE_BUDGET`, [[R186]]) and the lethal hint ([[R2102000]]) follow it. Its screen against the AI of before, 800 paired games of improve-dev, read 406-394, edge +0.015 (-0.007 to +0.037), inside noise as §9.9's earlier 4× search was; its worst game's msP95 was 2,683 ms.

`PRACTICE_AI_CLOCK_MS` is 3 s. In a browser, 600 nodes took 0.6–0.9 s, so the old 1.5 s cap would cut the doubled search short and the doubling would mostly not happen.

The ladder bots' two middle strengths keep 600 and 150 (`BOT_MID_STRENGTH_BUDGET`): their seed ratings were calibrated on that budget ([[R748000]]). So do the Duos AI seats (`DUOS_AI_BUDGET`, [[§13.7]]), so that the server's work per AI step does not double. Raising either is a person's call.

A fat state scales its allowance from at most `AI_SEARCH.fat_budget_nodes`, 600, so the states whose every step costs the most spend what they did. At 1,200 nodes the timing gate's pinned improve-dev:200 position took about 1.42 s against `AI_GATE.pinned_decision_ms`'s 1,000; with the cap it decides exactly as it did at 600 nodes, at the same cost. A state at or under `fat_reference`'s width gets the whole 1,200: the timing gate's slowest decision read 105 ms, and improve-dev:154's pinned one spent 995 nodes in 329 ms. The ladder's strongest strength, whose own budget is 1,200, takes the same cap in a fat state.

**Rejected.** Doubling the bots' and Duos' budgets too, which would void the bots' calibration and double the server's work; keeping the 1.5 s cap; and raising `AI_GATE.pinned_decision_ms` to let the fat states double too, since the gate's limits are a person's call and the reading sat close under `max_decision_ms`.
