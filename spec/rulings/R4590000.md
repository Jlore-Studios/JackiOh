IA #134 Rootkit Runner's second line runs on no face-up play: `Script.revealed` answers only the reveal path (`alt_play::run_revealed`), once the Unit set face-down by its Cloaked has come out and its Cry (`cry_trigger`) has run, as its controller, resumable like an evolve. A hook and a fuse entry carry it (`Script.hook_named`, `fuse::combine_objects`); the other parts' reveal hooks (C2, C10) re-use the name, and the reconcile settles the seam. A Cry that pauses on a question keeps the hook waiting with the rest of the reveal, and no card of this part has both a Cry and this hook.
