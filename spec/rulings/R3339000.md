Part of #3330 (issue #3339). The owner asked that full-art, extended-art and out-of-frame cards have their text gone while in play. In general, any card in play with elaborate art should remove as much text as it can, including its name, so the art stands out.

**Which cards.** On the board (both seats, the Unit rows and the backrow), a card renders art-first when either:
- it wears a full-art-shaped level: Full art, Alt full art (rank >= 2), Sketch's full art (rank >= 2), Extended art, Out of frame, Immersive, Prismatic, Set showcase, or any animated or signed level of them; or
- it is Legendary or Mythic, in any look.

Card settings (`cards/settings.ts`) provide "Names on the board" (`boardNames`):
- `artFirst` (the default): art-first cards hide their static name plate, type line, and rules words.
- `always`: all cards on the board show their name plate.
- `never`: all cards on the board hide their static name plate.

**What an art-first face draws.** Only the art, edge to edge, and the numbers a player reads at a glance: a Unit's attack, health and damage, armor, and a backrow card's counters. An art-first face displays no static name, no type line, and no rules words. Its keywords and states render as icon marks (`keywordMarks.ts`, `KeywordFx.tsx`) with no words, still all of them, folded past the limit as before, with accessible labels for screen readers.

**Hover and inspection.** Hovering an art-first card or giving it keyboard focus reveals its name on a slim plate over the bottom of the art within `--motion-fast` on desktop, and the full hover preview after its usual delay. A long press on a coarse pointer (phone) opens inspect as a tap does. The name remains accessible in the card's `aria-label`.

**Other cards print less too.** A normal Common to Epic Unit keeps its name plate, made smaller and translucent over the bottom edge of the portrait, allowing the art window to expand behind and below it. Its keyword chips become icon marks as on art-first faces.

This amends [[R3046000]] regarding the permanent name band below the art on board faces.
