`cargo jackioh positions --every reply` writes one row at each end of a seat's turn: the opponent's reply as the search simulates it (`simulate_reply`: the cards the line put in the opponent's hand, the Activates the seat can see, then its attacks, never a card from its hidden hand), played out from the true state with the true opponent's hand and library as the hidden cards. Then come the seat's features and the hand eval there, by the reply's swing rule ([[R1006004]]), and the game's outcome. A reply that ends the game, or runs out of its nodes, writes no row.

They are the positions the learned evaluation scores in the search (issue #1010), the finalists after the reply. The rows `--every turn` writes are real turn starts, where the opponent has played its turn: its mana spent and its hand played. The search's simulated reply leaves both unspent, so a network fitted on turn rows alone reads its leaves as positions it has never seen. A network meant for the search fits on reply rows.
