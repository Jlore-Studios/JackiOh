This amends [[R64]]. A Recruit's target is the first card in its scan order (a library top down, an
exile newest first) that is a permanent, is not a unit-token card, matches the filter, and can be
summoned now with the Recruit's lane and Radiant rider: its row has a zone it may enter ([[R64]],
[[R688]]), a Flying Unit's backrow zone included when its unit row is full ([[R2248000]]), a GIGA
finds its whole fit ([[R3546000]]), and the zone is on its summoner's half of a slashed board
([[R3554003]]). A match that no zone takes is passed over and keeps its place in the pile. When no
card in the pile can be summoned, nothing is.

[[R690]] reads only the cards that can be summoned: a scan takes the first of them that is not
(X)-cost, and the first (X)-cost one only when no other card that can be summoned matches. A
non-(X) card that cannot land no longer keeps an (X)-cost card that can from being taken.

"Recruit N" is N scans, and each one checks again against the board the last one left, so each takes
the next card that can be summoned, and the rest find nothing once none can. "Recruit every
permanent" (C #60 Pile On) lists the cards that can be summoned as it starts, R690 read among those,
and then summons each in turn, passing over one whose row has filled since.

A script that must name the recruited card before its Recruit runs reads it through the same scan
(`query::recruit_takes`). C #1 Curse of the Forgotten Classic's Radiant face names its forced attacker
that way, so the Unit its Recruit takes past a newer card no zone takes, or past a newer (X)-cost one,
is the one that attacks. Its own reading took the newest permanent of their exile, and when the Recruit
took another card, that card's attack was lost.

Before this ruling a scan took the first match by the filter alone, and when that card found no zone
the scan fizzled and the card stayed. With N scans every later one found the same card and fizzled
again: with your unit row full and a Unit on top of your deck, C #13 Boots on the Ground's Radiant
Recruit set no Trap from beneath it.

The check reads public board state and the pile the scan already reads in the engine, so no view
changes and nothing hidden reaches a player. A game played before this change whose fold it alters is
refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]), as with every change to an engine rule.

Rejected: keeping the fizzle, since "Recruit a card" with a Trap that could be set summons nothing;
summoning a card to find out whether it lands, which would move it and emit events; listing every match for "Recruit every permanent" and
applying R690 to the ones that land only as each is reached, which would let a non-(X) card that
cannot land keep an (X)-cost card that can; and gating old games by catalog version, since no engine
rule is gated that way.
