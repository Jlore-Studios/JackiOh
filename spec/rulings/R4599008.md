An aura that changes or leaves changes a Unit's max health with no event of its own ([[§10.4]]), and the state check destroys a Unit whose damage now meets it ([[§4.5]]). S+ #32 Z-Patch's Nerfs, and the death of the C+ #46 Felinor Flagbearer whose aura held its kin up, left immune Units at a max health their damage met, and their killerless `destroyed` landed in the Spell's run (the daily fuzz's plain seeds 2261 and 2374 and handicapped ones on main, #4599).

**The layers killed it.** I21 allows a killerless `destroyed` of an immune Unit whose max health at death (the event's `maxHealth`) is at most the damage it held as the step began plus the damage it took in the step. A Spell's own debuff is judged at its event, and a Spell's destroy of a Unit with health to spare is still a reach.
