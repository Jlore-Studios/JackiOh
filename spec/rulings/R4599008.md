A Unit dies at the state check once its health is gone ([[§4.5]]), and its health is its max health through the layers less the damage it holds ([[§10.4]]). An aura that changes or leaves changes it with no event of its own. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 11168 a Spell made Core #46 Suppressive Aura Radiant, whose aura took an immune S+ #112 Fortress to −1 max health; in seed 7834 the Anomaly Found! token exiled the cards whose auras held up an immune C+ #19.1 Top Loser carrying 6 damage, and its max health fell to 6. Both deaths came inside the Spell's run with no killer ([[R42]]), and I21 read them as the Spell's destroy.
**These are the layers, not the Spell.** The Spell reached the aura's source, which it may; the aura is that card's text, which reaches an immune Unit as every Unit's and Field Spell's does ([[R4453000]]).
**What I21 allows.** A `destroyed` with no killer of an immune Unit whose max health at death is at most the damage it held: what it held as the step began, plus each `damage` on it in the step, less each `healed`. A Spell's own debuff is judged at its own `buffed`. A death with health left over its damage is still the Spell's destroy.
Rejected: reading the layers before and after the step to name the aura that changed, which repeats the engine's reading and cannot see an aura source the step took away.
