Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The issue (#3350, part of #3330) asked, for the "super, super rare" cards, for quasi-3D
models with animations of their own: past the depth planes every Epic and up draws by its painter's roles ([[R3344000]]),
each of the 30 Mythic cards, and each of the 20 tokens printed Mythic (`printedRarity`), whose pictures have all landed,
gets a hand-composed **diorama**, a small model of its picture built from flat SVG.

**What a diorama is.** One file per card, `apps/web/src/cards/art/dioramas/<set>/<name>.ts`, cuts its card's still picture,
the one its painter draws for its id on each face (base and Radiant), in five to eight **slices** (`DIORAMA_SLICES_MIN`,
`DIORAMA_SLICES_MAX`), back to front. A slice picks the pieces of the picture it holds by their keys (`dioramas/pieces.ts`):
a scene card's ([[R3331000]]) each shape by its layer and part, a Stinkyscape painter's each element of its sky and subject.
The farthest slice holds the rest, the sky, glow, rays and vignette among it; every piece is in exactly one slice on both faces,
so the slices together are the still picture. A slice stands in one of the depth planes (far, middle, near, nearest, never
going back) at a depth from 0 to 1, rising slice to slice, and every slice after the farthest may bend about one axis, up to
`DIORAMA_BEND_MAX_DEG`, under a perspective of its own (`DIORAMA_BEND_PERSPECTIVE_PX`), so it reads as a curved surface: a
floor, a wall, a ship banking. Each slice is a picture of its own drawn from its card's id and face alone ([[R202]]): the
picture's head (its definitions) and its pieces in the order the picture draws them, a Stinkyscape subject's in its placing
transform, so it draws no colour, text or reference its picture does not and none in Radiant's gold window but the
picture's own ([[R2725004]]), and each fits `ART_DETAIL_MAX_BYTES` ([[R3331001]]).

**Its loop.** Two slices or more (`DIORAMA_LOOP_SLICES_MIN`) run an idle loop written for that card, one of the moves the
moving art uses ([[R2715001]]), in transform and opacity alone inside the `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` gate,
each piece of a slice a step along when it staggers: Zephrys's gusts circling, Dice of Doom's die turning on its faces,
Alteran Reach's lights coming on along its path, Ceaseless Void's sparks falling at every depth. No two cards' loops are
alike. A slice holding a Stinkyscape subject's pieces moves only about its own box, since its group is placed by a
transform. Where the card's art moves as well, at an animated or signed level ([[R2715001]], [[R3015002]]) and on the board
([[R3340000]]), each slice also moves its pieces as the whole moving picture does, under the picture's own style, each run
of one role in that role's layer, and the nearest slice draws the particles, so the slices together are the moving
picture and the level keeps what it moves.

**Where it draws.** Wherever a face with depth draws its planes and its picture is the normal one, a Mythic draws its
diorama in their place (`data-diorama`, CardFace): the farthest slice in the base window, each other a plane of its own over
it (`.cf-plane[data-slice]`), shifted by the tilt ([[R3344001]]) by its depth in `DIORAMA_DEPTH_STEPS` parallax steps under
the glare. Alt art draws its own picture, which has none, and so do a restyle, a Starship in play, an Anomaly holding a
board and every face [[R3344000]] draws as one plane. Its loops run in hand, in inspect and in the preview while the face
holds one of the motion slots ([[R3340000]], `data-diorama-motion`), held still with Animated foil off, on a grid's lazy face
and past the slots. Where the player turns the card to look at it, inspect's drag, the hover preview and the Collection's
grid, it turns up to `DIORAMA_TURN_MAX_DEG` each way, its parallax scaled with the turn; in hand it keeps
`CARD_TILT_MAX_DEG`. A Mythic Unit on the board draws every slice stacked in its window while its art moves, each on its loop,
the window answering a hover and a press as one image does; still, it draws its one picture. The full-screen view
([[R3345000]]) draws the slices in place of its planes, each shifted with the tilt by its depth
(`FULLSCREEN_SLICE_SHIFT_FAR_PX` far to `FULLSCREEN_SLICE_SHIFT_NEAR_PX` near) and bent, turning up to
`DIORAMA_TURN_MAX_DEG`; the takeover's camera flies through them, each toward it by its depth and past the nearest, which
fade out (fx.css `fx-takeover-fly`).

**Flat.** The Reduce motion setting, the system's reduced motion and the fx intensity off draw the flat picture, no
slices, as they draw no planes ([[R3344000]]); the full-screen view under them draws its slices still.

Rejected: slices by the painter's roles alone (those are [[R3344000]]'s planes, and a role holds a whole layer where a
diorama splits one into ranks); new drawing per slice, an echo or side silhouette of the subject included (it would add
colours a palette test must vet in Radiant's window, and bytes the Radiant pictures near the cap do not have); true 3D,
`translateZ` under `preserve-3d` (the face is an isolated group whose contents draw flat, so slices shift by `translate`
and only bend inside their own clip); loops outside the motion slots (fifty moving cards would break the slots' budget
and the frame budget); a bent farthest slice (its sky would pull away from the window's edge); bends in the takeover (the
camera's flight is its depth, and a bent slice at full-screen size would show its edge); and spinning Zephrys's cyclone
whole (a flat funnel turned about its middle stands on its head), so its gusts circle and the funnel sways from its foot;
and the loops alone at an animated level (the level's own moving art would be lost on every Mythic).
