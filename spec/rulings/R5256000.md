FvH #91 KY's Log Exp of the FFT deals ⌊X·√(log₂X)⌋, which no float may compute in a hashed game. It is the engine's table, `ky_fft_damage(points)` (`KY_FFT_DAMAGE`, worked out offline and clamped at `KY_FFT_MAX_POINTS`), read at X on the base face and at the padded X on the Radiant face: X rounded up to the next power of 2, at least 2 (1 → 2, 3 → 4, 5 → 8).

**Base.** One declared enemy, a Unit or the hero, aimed Harm, takes one hit. When X is a power of 2 the hit goes to every enemy instead, each enemy Unit and the enemy hero one hit each, with no state check between them ([[R59]]). 1 is 2⁰, so X = 1 is a power of 2, but the table gives 0 there (log₂1 = 0), and a damage of 0 deals nothing: no hit, no event. X is at least 1 ([[R348]]). A random cast takes X as its player's mana and aims at random ([[R656]]).

**Radiant.** No target: every enemy takes the padded X's table value, one hit each.

**Preview.** Each face's preview ([[R280]]) is labelled with the printed formula and shows the damage a play now could deal: X the mana its player holds, at least 1, padded on the Radiant face. The table is the formula, so the card declares no number ([[R1748003]]).
