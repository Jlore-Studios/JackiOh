Stinkyscape #112 Job Application's Stage 3, "Activate this to accept the offer, or to send in another application, back to Stage 1, with every reward tripled. Reward: From your next turn on, add a Paycheck to your hand at the start of each of your turns", and its "Death: Resummon this at Stage 1", read as follows.

- **Two Activates**, each once a turn under its own id ([[R384]]): Upload Resume's, offered while Stage 1 is open, and the Offer's, while Stage 3 is. So a re-application may take a new resume the same turn.
- **The Offer** is one Activate of two modes ([[R81]]). Accepting completes Stage 3, and from its controller's next start of turn on the card adds Paychecks (S #112.1, on their base face) at each of its controller's starts of turn while it stays on the field, a full hand burning them ([[§2.4]]). Re-applying opens Stage 1 again ([[R1854000]]).
- **The factor.** "Every reward tripled" is a factor the card remembers, 1 at first and tripled by each re-application, never past 9. It multiplies the Interview's Buffs and the Paychecks a turn; the wait is no reward, so it never moves it.
- **Death** ([[§6.2]]: destroyed or sacrificed from the backrow) summons the card from the graveyard into its own backrow zone while that zone takes a summon (empty and unreserved; a Lock refuses only plays, [[R688]]), else into [[R64]]'s zone, else it stays in the graveyard. The hook reads the card as it died ([[R89]]) and names it by its id. Summoned, it enters reset ([[R78]]): Stage 1 open, the factor 1, not hired. Its resume's holder has left the field, so the resume is exiled ([[R1865000]]). An exile or a bounce is no Death, and ends it.

Rejected: an uncapped factor, which a chain of re-applications would grow past any hand (after three, 135 Buffs on one card and 27 Paychecks a turn); one Activate shared by both stages, under which a re-application could take no resume until the next turn.
