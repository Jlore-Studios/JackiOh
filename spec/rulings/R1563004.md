The designer's text, "for (2) more, a card that isn't Radiant is also played Radiant", reads on every card, and the designer said so (#1563): [[R1181002]] gave the dearer price to embiggen cards alone, so a card of one price could never be played Radiant under a Garden. Since #1563, while a Garden acts, every card that isn't Radiant, played face-up from a hand or from a graveyard where a permission allows it, has a dearer price beside its own: its price plus the Garden's premium (2, Radiant 1), and that play is made Radiant at [[§10.5]] step 3, its choices those of the Radiant face ([[R214]]). An embiggen card keeps [[R1181002]]'s two prices (its Embiggened version at its normal price, and Radiant and Embiggened for the premium more). The play names the dearer price as an embiggen card names its own, `embiggen: true`, and the view gives the card its `embiggenCost` ([[§10.8]]); the choice rides the card to step 3 and comes off there, since a card of one price has no Embiggened version. An X card has no dearer price: it costs exactly X ([[R65]]). A Radiant card, a cast ([[R545]]) and a card set face-down ([[R783005]]) are as before. Cost modifiers apply to both prices ([[R65]]). It edits [[§8.9]] and [[R1181002]].
