The Cry of KY's Line Search Bot (S+ T-AI-7) is E7's line ([[R3554005]]): it takes any enemy tile and hits
every Unit on the line between it and the tile, friend or foe, for 2 (Radiant 4). Lancer, Vela and Corvus
print Pierce on their lines; this card's text prints none, so Armor reduces each of its hits like any other
damage ([[§4.4]]). The line's rules carry it as `LineRules.pierce`, which the three cards above set and this
one leaves off, with no Cleave and no laser either. Rejected: every line piercing, which would give a card
a keyword its text does not print.
