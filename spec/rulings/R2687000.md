Amends [[R2424000]], which priced the Death of a unit on either field and of a Unit in the AI's own hand and left a backrow card's out, though [[§6.2]]'s Death is a backrow card's too. Five shipped Field Spells print one: Classic+ #61 Bauble Bubble, Classic+ #12.8 Frostspatula, Stinkyscape #39 The Narcor Project, Stinkyscape #112 Job Application and Stinkyscape #155 Comet Crash. The AI's evaluation counted each by its cost alone, so it rarely played them: the AI's card sweep (`crates/ai/src/shadow_ban.rs`) flags both Comet Crash and The Narcor Project as at risk. It found Comet Crash affordable on 17 turns and played once, and The Narcor Project never played in its first pass. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** What [[R2424000]] counts, for more cards. After the units on either field, the Death probes list each backrow card the seat can read, its own and then the enemy's, but never a Unit standing there, which is one of the units already ([[R1081000]]). They then list each Unit and Field Spell in the seat's own hand. A hand Field Spell is set first by the sandbox's spawn ([[R923001]]) in the backrow zone a play would take, the spawn `turn_hook_prices` already makes for one ([[R2565000]]). Each card is probed, priced and keyed as a unit is: its controller's evaluation after the sandbox's destroy less after its remove ([[R923003]]). The order of [[R2424000]] and [[R2566000]] is kept: every live Death in the order above, then the Starship Pieces. The dedupe by key and `AI_DEATH_PROBE_NODES` ([[R186]]'s budget) are kept as well, and the budget stays 24. A destroy now reads as one when the card has left the field since the probe began (`left_field_after`, [[R174]]). The old test asked whether the card stood on the field again with its Reborn unchanged, and so read Job Application, whose Death summons the same card back at Stage 1, as a card the destroy spared, and never priced it. A Reborn body and a card the destroy really spares (Indestructible) read as before.

`material` adds `AI_EVAL.death` of the price for each backrow card the seat can read, on either side. The share sits outside the card's Brittle share, as `unit_worth` adds a unit's: Brittle's crumble is a destroy, so a card that crumbles still pays its Death (Bauble Bubble and Comet Crash, Brittle 1, keep their whole price). The weight stays 0.5 and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. Measured on the tests' boards, Bauble Bubble prices 2.6 (two Stockpiles), Comet Crash 3.0 (three random Resources) and Job Application 2.3. Destroying an enemy Bauble Bubble then costs the AI 0.75 rather than 2.05. An Animated backrow card waiting to be a Unit (Frostspatula) reads its price again in the share of it `material` counts as a Unit (`unit_worth`), as its turn hooks and its Activate do ([[R2565000]], [[R2603000]]). At `AI_EVAL.death` 0.5 the smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`) read 20, 20 and 20 of 20, against random, against greedy and as Hard against Easy (17, 10 and 16 needed).

**What is not priced.**
- A face-down enemy card, which is never probed or priced ([[R185]]). Two boards that differ only in which trap sits face-down there price the same.
- A hand Field Spell when the backrow is full.
- The Narcor Project's Death beyond 0: it pays only when Brittle destroyed the card ([[R1861001]]), and the sandbox's destroy is no crumble. It is probed and prices 0, which is correct.
- Everything else [[R2424000]] does not price.

**Rejected.**
- Counting the share inside the Brittle share, as [[R2603000]] counts an Activate's. A crumble ends a card's coming uses but still runs its Death.
- A budget of its own for backrow cards, which would draw more of the decision's nodes.
- Faking a Brittle crumble in the probe to price The Narcor Project. That would need a destroy the sandbox has no tool for ([[R923003]]).
- Keeping the zone-based spared test. It cannot tell a card its own Death returns from one the destroy spared.
