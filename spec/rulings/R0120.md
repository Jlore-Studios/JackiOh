[[§8]]'s conventions make a sentence beginning "Also" an independent clause, so it resolves even when the clause before it did not. A Transform refused by an Immutable target ([[R23]]) therefore still delivers the rest of the card's text, and the trap is still consumed ([[R61]]) — the refusal is not a fizzle of the whole card
