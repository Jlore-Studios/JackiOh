The learned evaluation's features (`crates/ai/src/features.rs`, [[R1006000]]) count the Radiant cards in the seat's own hand (`own.hand_radiant`, after the hand's cost bins). Each such card also has a second sparse key, `hand_radiant:own:<card id>`, beside its `hand:own:<card id>`. The seat's own hand is its to see ([[R185]]): the enemy's hand gives neither. This is `FEATURES_VERSION` 2, so a network fitted on version 1 fails [[R1010000]]'s test.

Before version 2 a card made Radiant in hand looked to the network as it did before, while the hand eval prices a hand card by the face it will enter with (#1143). A line that spends a card to make another Radiant ([[§5.2]]: Glowy Jelly Bean, Expedition12) then looked to the network like a card spent for nothing it could see. With the switch on (#466), networks fitted on version 1 failed the AI's tests on such lines:

- #1143's Glowy Jelly Bean test: seed 1's network of run 8, and two of the three TD(0.3) networks of run 5 at a share of 0.65.
- [[R844]]'s Expedition12 with Silly Silas: each of run 5's three TD(0.3) networks at half, against `main`'s hand eval at d981885.
