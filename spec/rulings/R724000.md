The Patch notes page ([[R388]], [[§10.10]]) shows the patches by release line, one collapsible bubble per set, newest line first. A release line is a set and the patches that came with it: the client's `RELEASES` (`apps/web/src/patches/releases.ts`) lists the lines oldest first, each with an id, a name, a summary, its technical changes and `first`, the version of its first patch. Walking `patches.json` in its own order, a patch named by a line's `first` opens that line, a patch before every line's first opens an "Earlier patches" line, and every other patch joins the line open before it; a version is compared for equality and never parsed or ordered ([[R105]]), so a lettered patch (`v0.2.10b`) sits in its line beside the others. The lines are Core (from v0.1.0), Classic & Classic+ (from v0.2.0) and Meditative (from v0.3.1); a major version that brings a set adds its line, its `first` the set's first card patch.

A line's bubble is a heading whose whole text is its control: the line's name, its span (its oldest and newest versions, or the one when it holds one) and its count ("2 patches", "1 patch"). Only the newest line is open on arrival (`RELEASES_OPEN_ON_LOAD`); a shut line renders none of its patches until it is opened. Opened, it shows its summary, then its technical changes under "Technical changes" (what players noticed since the line before that shipped no card data: a mode, a screen, the AI, the engine, sound, one line each), then its patches newest first. A patch shows its version, its date, its title and its player note ([[R724001]]) under the fixed headings New, Balance, Changes, Card text and Fixes, each only when it holds a line, and never its record's `source` or `notes`; its title is the note's, or the record's when it has no note. Its cards stay behind its own toggle as [[R507]] sets out, the newest patch's open on arrival, which sits in the line open on arrival.

The card detail view's History names each patch by the same title, and the static HTML of `/patch-notes` writes the same lines, summaries, technical changes, titles and note lines. Presentation only, over public data (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
