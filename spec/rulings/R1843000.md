A card votes to remove a player from the game with `vote_out { player? }` (default its controller, "votes to remove you"); the card's script writes its condition as its own `if` and returns the verb when it holds ("Start of Turn: votes to remove you if the enemy has more Units than you"). The engine keeps each player's tally for the turn (`PlayerState.votes`: the turn and the cards that voted, absent until a first vote). A tally counts each card definition once a turn, so two copies of one Brain, or one card whose hook runs twice ([[R820]]), are one vote; a tally kept for an earlier turn counts nothing, and the first vote of a later turn starts it again. A vote with no card behind it counts nothing, and once the game is over a vote does nothing at all ([[R216]]). Each counted vote is a `voteCast` naming its card, the tally after it and the `VOTE_TALLY_SIZE` (3) that removes, the card named as the card itself is ([[R97]]).

When the tally reaches `VOTE_TALLY_SIZE` the player is removed at the state check's game-end point that closes the effect list ([[R59]]): a loss for them and a win for the other player (`voted-out`, the designer's answer). The cards that agreed are named by `votedOut`, in the order they voted, just before `gameOver`, which is the client's animation of their agreement. A Brain votes at its controller's start of turn, among the start-of-turn triggers in [[R68]]'s order, so a card to its left that ends the game first leaves it nothing to vote on.
