**Why.** [[R4701000]] removed the training lanes' workflows and left the commands they ran for a change that could build them (#4702). Nothing runs those commands now: no workflow, script or change to the AI promotes through `promote`, no export is folded by `positions` or filed by `stats from-selfplay`, and `crates/ai/src/features.rs` fed only `positions`.

**What holds now.**

- `cargo jackioh promote` is gone, and with it CI's `rust (training gate)`, which played a lane's promotion again on an `ai/*` branch; the `rust` summary no longer waits on it, and `TRAINING_GATE` leaves `crates/engine/src/config.rs`. The arena and self-play still refuse a seed base that names a retired lane (`improve`, `unban`, `learn`), whose gates' deals `training/history/` records ([[R850001]], [[R1006002]]).
- `cargo jackioh positions` and `crates/ai/src/features.rs` are gone: [[R4159000]] retired the network and the prior they fed.
- `cargo jackioh stats from-selfplay` is gone. `stats --series` still refuses a series beginning `selfplay:`, since both databases hold the records the learn lane's exports were filed under ([[R1676000]]).
- `cargo jackioh selfplay` stays: `classify` plays it on the base's build and the head's to find the cards that play differently ([[R1365000]]), so its rulings hold ([[R1006001]], [[R1006002]], [[R1006005]], [[R1006007]], [[R1489000]]).
- The notes that described only the removed commands are retired, each saying so: [[R732001]], [[R1012000]], [[R1679000]], [[R1006000]], [[R1006003]], [[R1006004]], [[R1006006]], [[R1012004]], [[R1171000]], [[R3112000]], [[R3112002]] and [[R4070000]]. Their proving tests now hold that the commands stay gone.

**Rejected:** keeping `promote` for a lane that may come back, since nothing calls it, and a new lane would choose its own gate; keeping `positions` and the features without the network they were for.
