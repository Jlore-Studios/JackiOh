Amends [[R2479000]] for co-op games. While a co-op board shows a view with no result on a socket neither closed nor refused, every way off it in the client is held by the page's leave guard ([[R1754000]]): the board's Back, the browser's Back or Android's back gesture, and any other move in the app. Each asks "Leave the match? Your teammate plays on without you. After 60 seconds the AI plays your seat until you come back." The number is `DISCONNECT_GRACE_SECONDS`, the server's disconnect grace ([[§9.5]]), past which the AI plays the seat and the teammate is told ([[§13.8]]). Stay is the default. It has the focus, Escape, Back ([[R2127000]]) or a click outside the panel means the same, and it sends nothing. Leave makes the move the player took. A move the client makes for itself (`navigate`'s `force`) is never held. Once the view has a result, or the socket has closed for good or been refused, nothing is left to come back to: every way off goes at once, and a question still open closes.

Rejected: a required dialog prop, which would churn the 1v1 route for no change in its words; the dialog takes its sentence as a prop and defaults to the 1v1 line.
