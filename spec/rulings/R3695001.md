[[R3695000]]'s second number: with Drone Core X in reach at Hard, the AI left it in hand. The Drone Battleship enters as one Stack pile ([[R1842000]]), and the evaluation reads each card beneath one of the seat's own unit piles at `AI_EVAL.dormant_share` of its `unit_worth` (`dormant_worth`, [[§3.2]], [[R13]]). #1506 had set that share to 0 after its screen read 200-200 at 0.5 and at 1.0, edge 0.0 (−0.024 to 0.024), so casting X counted only the pile's top, the Shield Layer, a 12/12 that can't attack. On 7 crystals Hard cast Genn (4) 14/14 or Greaser (2) 7/7 over it. No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** `AI_EVAL.dormant_share` is 0.5, so each card beneath one of the seat's own unit piles, whatever put it there (a layered summon's lower layers, a Unit a Stack card was played onto), counts half its `unit_worth`. Half is a turn's wait in [[R2380000]]'s shares: the card acts again only once the cards above it leave. The share was chosen before the gate and the sweep ran, not tuned to them. It is the smaller of #1506's two screened arms, and the one that flips the case. On a bare Hard board, casting X gains 14.4 in the static evaluation at 0, 32.9 at 0.5 and 51.5 at 1.0 ([[R3695000]]). On 7 crystals, over 8 boards (X beside Genn, Greaser, Mr. Vanilla and Greaser, or Genn and Mr. Vanilla, each against an empty board and against a 4/4) and 4 cursors, the AI cast X first in 4 of 32 at 0, 24 at 0.5 and 32 at 1.0. At 0.5 it still takes two bodies over X on an empty board. A card beneath the enemy's unit piles still counts `dormant_card`, 0, for each card, since the seat sees them only as a count ([[R447]]), and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, so the gate's greedy baseline is unchanged.

**Numbers.** This branch at 0.5 against the same with `dormant_share` 0, the Float credit alone ([[R3695000]]), on main at `3d347d9e78`, one machine. The smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`) read the same at both: ai-vs-random 19 of 20 (17 needed), ai-vs-greedy 16 (10), hard-vs-easy 20 (16), no search panic, the perf gate's slowest decision 41.6 ms at 0.5 and 40.4 ms at 0. The improve lane's screen, 0.5 against 0 (`cargo jackioh arena --a self --b bin:<the build at 0> --set improve-dev`, 400 games, every one clean), read 200-200, edge 0.0 (0.0 to 0.0): each of its 200 deals went one game to each side, and #1506's screen had read the same 200-200, so the share neither gains nor loses there and is kept for what it does to the Battleship, not for strength. `cargo jackioh sweep stinkyscape-033 stinkyscape-034 stinkyscape-035 stinkyscape-036` flagged none of the four in pass 1 at either. Pass 1's eight forced games a tier (drawn, affordable turns, casts) were the same at both for Drone Core VII (Easy 7, 4, 2; Hard 5, 3, 2), VIII (Easy 2, 1, 0; Hard 6, 2, 2) and IX (Easy 4, 1, 1; Hard 5, 3, 3), and for X (Easy 5, then 2 affordable turns at 0.5 and 3 at 0, 2 casts; Hard 2, 1, 1). Pass 2 took only X, over 24 games a tier: at Easy, drawn in 12, 1 affordable turn and no cast at both; at Hard, drawn in 16, 3 affordable turns, cast once at 0.5 and never at 0. The four were cast 14 times at 0.5, 13 at 0 and 13 on main before the Float credit, and X 4 times where both others cast it 3. One cast is the least the sweep can move, and a sweep this size cannot tell it from chance. The 2026-10-29 report of record, a full sweep that dealt X in 18 games at Easy and 58 at Hard as forced card and filler, cast it in pass 2 once at Easy (1 affordable turn) and once at Hard (18); this sweep's pass 2, which deals X only in its own 24 games a tier, casts it once, at Hard, in 3 affordable turns. So it casts X more often than the same sweep without the share and than main, on a larger share of Hard's affordable turns than the report of record (2 of 4 over both passes, against 1 of 18), but not more times in pass 2.

**Rejected.** A share for a layered summon's layers alone, a second weight beside `dormant_share`: a Unit a Stack card topped resumes the same way, and the two would be one fact priced twice. 1.0, which counts a covered Unit as if it still acted, so topping one would cost nothing.
