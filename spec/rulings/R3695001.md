[[R3695000]]'s second number: with Drone Core X in reach at Hard, the AI left it in hand. The Drone Battleship enters as one Stack pile ([[R1842000]]), and the evaluation reads each card beneath one of the seat's own unit piles at `AI_EVAL.dormant_share` of its `unit_worth` (`dormant_worth`, [[§3.2]], [[R13]]). #1506 had set that share to 0 after its screen read 200-200 at 0.5 and at 1.0, edge 0.0 (−0.024 to 0.024), so casting X counted only the pile's top, the Shield Layer, a 12/12 that can't attack. On 7 crystals Hard cast Genn (4) 14/14 or Greaser (2) 7/7 over it. No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** `AI_EVAL.dormant_share` is 0.5, so each card beneath one of the seat's own unit piles, whatever put it there (a layered summon's lower layers, a Unit a Stack card was played onto), counts half its `unit_worth`. Half is a turn's wait in [[R2380000]]'s shares: the card acts again only once the cards above it leave. The share was chosen before the gate and the sweep ran, not tuned to them. It is the smaller of #1506's two screened arms, and the one that flips the case. On a bare Hard board, casting X gains 14.4 in the static evaluation at 0, 32.9 at 0.5 and 51.5 at 1.0 ([[R3695000]]). On 7 crystals, over 8 boards (X beside Genn, Greaser, Mr. Vanilla and Greaser, or Genn and Mr. Vanilla, each against an empty board and against a 4/4) and 4 cursors, the AI cast X first in 4 of 32 at 0, 24 at 0.5 and 32 at 1.0. At 0.5 it still takes two bodies over X on an empty board. A card beneath the enemy's unit piles still counts `dormant_card`, 0, for each card, since the seat sees them only as a count ([[R447]]), and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, so the gate's greedy baseline is unchanged.
