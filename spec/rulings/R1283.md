A Unit with lane strike ([[§4.3]]) has an attack multiplier (3× for Bunker) applied when in combat with the opposing Unit located directly in the same lane across the board. The multiplier is evaluated once per combat encounter ([[R94]]). Attacks directed at heroes or Units in different lanes deal standard 1× damage. The multiplier scales with First Strike and buffs, and subtracts target Armor after the multiplied damage is calculated.
