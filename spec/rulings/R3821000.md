Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no state, event, replay or AI decision changes, only what `viewFor` carries and what the web does with it. A unit's switch greyed out for many reasons and none was ever said, and a tap on a unit that had already attacked did nothing at all, since [[R849000]]'s `attackReason` is sent only while the unit `canAct`. The view now carries one more optional field on a `UnitView`.

`switchReason` is on the viewer's own unit while its controller may act (their turn, the main phase, no prompt open, the game not over), whether or not the unit `canAct`. It is the refusal the unit's own switch to the other position meets, spending its exertion ([[§4.1]], [[R49]]), in the engine's words (`combat::why_cannot_switch_in`): the card is Dormant ([[R1839000]]), the Unit is Frozen ([[R1174000]]), that unit has already acted this turn, or that unit cannot be in Defense Position (Can't Defend [[R2657000]], Indestructible [[R2179000]], Evasive [[R3543002]], or an aura barring Defense Position, [[R1849006]]). `legalActions` offers a `switchPosition` by the same call (`reduce::can_switch`), so the field is absent exactly when that switch is offered, and it says nothing the list does not; it reads only the unit's public field facts and public auras. It is never on the opponent's units, and an effect's switch ([[R20]]) is not what it describes.

The web shows it in three places, working none of it out:

- A click on that unit that `legal` offers no attack shows `attackReason` as before and, where the view gives none, `switchReason`, and sends nothing, so a spent unit says "that unit has already acted this turn". The board lets such a click on a unit in a unit zone through though the card is greyed, as it does a hand card's with a `playReason` ([[R979000]]), and lets it go on to the unit's zone, which a play being built may pick (a Stack or an Equip onto that unit); while a play or an attack is being built the card's own click changes nothing.
- The switch's accessible name and tooltip read "Switch position:" followed by the reason while it is greyed; the switch itself stays disabled.
- The inspect notes carry the reason as their own line, said once: where it is the attack reason word for word (a Frozen unit's is both) only the attack reason stands.

`golden::view_texts` leaves the key out, so no recorded hash or stored trace moves.

Rejected: [[R849000]]'s `canAct` gate, which the issue first proposed, since it leaves exactly the spent unit, the case the issue is about, with nothing to say. Rejected: making the greyed switch pressable, as [[R3768000]] made a greyed Activate or Trade button, in this part: the tap on the card now says it, and the switch's own press is left to the issue's second part. Rejected: the client choosing between the two reasons by what the unit could do (rule 7); the attack reason, when the view gives one, comes first, as it did. Rejected: letting the key reach the golden view text, which would move `HOTSEAT_HASH` and every stored trace for words that play no part in the game.
