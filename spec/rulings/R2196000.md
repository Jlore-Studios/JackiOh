The designer's base face says "a different ally Human" and the Radiant face "a friendly Human"; both are read as "one of your other Humans". The pick travels in the `play` action and is checked while Personal Trainer is still in its controller's hand ([[R81]], [[§10.5]] step 1), so it can never be the Trainer itself, on either face, and the Radiant face's upgrade is its doubled stats and its doubled buff. With no other Human of yours on the field the Trainer is still played and its Cry fizzles ([[R90]]): no self-fallback, which no Cry has. The brief's TG-P3-1 (`docs/training-grounds-set.md` TG5, TG6 reading 1).
