`craft_presets` rolls four complete recipes from the match rng — a Unit, a Spell, a Field Spell
and a Trap, in that order — within the chosen cost's budget (MD-A31): the Unit takes a 2-point
keyword when the budget holds one and all the rest in stats; the other three take one hat with
one of their verbs, affordable at N = 1, at the largest N that fits, up to 10. Every word, hat
and verb is an rng draw, so one seed names one set of four, and every preset is valid at every
cost 0 to 4.

The `craft` prompt's options are those presets, and `legalActions` enumerates them as its
answers — so the AI, the random policy (§10.7) and a timeout (R79) all answer with one. The
reducer still accepts any valid recipe: the enumerated answers are a convenience, not the rule.
