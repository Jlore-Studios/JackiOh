Issue #4154 asked to "make it easier to gain the rewards" of S #112 Job Application. Stage 1's resume now hears back in 1 to 2 turns (1 to 4 before): the weak table weighs them 1 and 3, the solid 1 and 1, the strong 3 and 1, so a better resume still waits less ([[R1865001]]). Of the Interview's three goals ([[R1854002]]) "play 3 cards of different costs in one turn" becomes 2, and "take or deal 12 or more damage in one hit" becomes 8; "play a card that costs the same as your resume" stays, and a re-application still triples every reward up to ×9 ([[R1865002]]). The stage texts print the numbers. Rejected: easing the Offer (its Activate is already free) and dropping the tripling. Through v0.6.6g: 1 to 4 turns, 3 costs and 12 damage (`script_v0_6_6g`, [[R767000]]).
