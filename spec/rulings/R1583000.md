The Pocket Pick is the player's card with the highest play rate next to the rest of the player base: a card they play far more often than everyone else does. The device knew only its own plays ([[R639]]), so the server now serves the player base's rates, read off the totals it already folds from every game record ([[R1044000]]), and the client sets the device's tally against them.

- **The measure.** A card's play rate is the share of games in which it was played from hand. The player base's is R377's `played` games over every human seat of every live game, on every patch and in every mode ([[R377]]). The device's is R639's `played` over its games, capped at 1. The two count a little differently. The base counts a seat once per game however often it played the card, and only when the seat's deck held it, while the device counts each play it was shown, a second play in one game and a card an effect put in the player's hand included, which can only raise a device rate. And the device counts its practice games against the AI, which the base never sees.
- **The route.** `GET /api/stats/play-rates` is open, and its body is `{ decks, cards: [{ id, rate }] }`: the human seats counted, and each listed card's rate over them, in catalog-id order. Only counts and rates are sent, so it names no game and no player. A ladder bot's seat never counts ([[R1451]]), nor does a development game ([[R378]]), so no AI game pads it. A shipped non-token card is listed once `PLAY_RATES_MIN_GAMES` (20) of those seats held it; a card held that often but never played is listed at 0.
- **Kept.** The server reads the rates off the totals at most once per `PLAY_RATES_REFRESH_SECONDS` (3600 s), as [[R978000]] keeps its reports: requests that miss together wait for one load, a load that fails is kept for no one, and the response's `Cache-Control` max-age is as long.
- **The pick.** The device names no Pocket Pick before it has logged `POCKET_PICK_MIN_GAMES` (10) games, and then asks for the rates. A listed card can be the pick once the player has played it `POCKET_PICK_MIN_PLAYS` (3) times, so one game does not win it, and only when the player's rate is above the base's. Among those the pick is the card with the highest ratio of the player's rate to the base's (a base rate of 0 ranks first), then the higher player rate, then the lower id. It carries the card and both rates. When the request fails, or no card qualifies, there is no Pocket Pick. The request sends nothing of the tally (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- **Rejected.** Plays per game: the totals count a seat once per game, so it would need a new column and every record folded again. A card's share of decks: the device logs no deck. The current patch alone: each new patch would empty it. A refresh on a timer: it would read the totals when nobody asks. A difference of rates instead of a ratio: it favours the staples everyone plays.
