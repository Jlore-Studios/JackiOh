In handicapped fuzz seed 4338 a fused Starship holding Stinkyscape #104 Reflective Plating attacked a Unit with Trample. The forecast ([[R3100000]]) foretold 9 trampling onto a hero at 13 with no kill, and the attack ended the game: the Plating dealt the attacker's strike back of 6 again to the enemy hero ([[R1849003]], [[§4.4]] step 8a) after the excess. The overflow's `kills` ([[R4290000]]) reads the hero's fall at the step's end from the excess alone, and the forecast projects no reflection. [[R4111000]] and [[R4221004]] already leave out of I18 the boards where the defending side's reflection changes what the forecast shows; I18 now also skips, by a named condition, an attack on a Unit whose attacker reflects what it takes onto the enemy hero, on either face of the Plating. An attack on a hero takes no strike back and is judged as before, and so is an attack by an attacker that reflects nothing.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and an invariant that checks it may judge only what it can see.

Rejected: counting the reflection in the overflow's `kills`. The figure the board draws as the Trample excess would then say the game ends on damage the excess does not deal; an attack without Trample would still have nowhere to show it; and the state-check passes cut short by the game's end would still be missed. Projecting the reflection in full is a forecast and wire change beyond a daily run's fix.
