A forced discard is any discard an effect makes from your hand: at random, of your choice, or of the whole hand (Meditative #1 Disruptive Disruptor). While it is not your turn, a card acting on the field that guards you (`Script.discard_guard`, beside [[R457]]'s `draw_limit`) stops the discard outright: no card moves, nothing answers it, and a random discard draws no random number ([[R129]]). One public `discardPrevented` reports how many cards were spared, naming no card. Several guards do no more than one.

A discard you pay as a price is not forced: an Activate's cost ([[R384]]) and a targeting cost ([[R450]]) still happen, and so does Temporary's discard, which fires at the end of your own turn ([[R637]]). C #8 Pickle's "they discard" option does nothing for a guarded player, so Pickle does not offer it (that card's "only choices that would do something"). The guard stops Meditative #2's discard when its controller's Rhino is hit on the opponent's turn, and #8's Ascent 3 against a guarded opponent.
