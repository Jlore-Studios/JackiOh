The AI prices a kill of a unit whose Reborn is unspent at what the kill really removes: the unit less the body that comes back ([[§4.5]] step 4, [[§9.9]]). `kill_worth` reads that body, and so do the reply's trade value and move order through it, the Taunt walk (a destroyed Reborn Taunt binding again, and what a later Cleave splash meets, [[R4805000]]), `afflicted_before_swing` ([[R4065000]]) and the Death probe ([[R2424000]]). The AI built it by resetting the unit itself ([[R78]]), which differed from the engine's return twice:

- **An X/X token kept the printed face.** The reset clears `statsOverride` and `armorOverride`, but a unit token's summoned X/X is its printed face and the engine gives it back to the body ([[R175]]). A 6/6 Bread Token (printed 0/0) with Reborn came back, in the AI's read, as nothing: the kill was priced as removing the whole 6/6, about twice what it removes (the engine's body is a 6/1), and in the Taunt walk a Reborn Taunt token's body had 0 health and bound no attacker.
- **A stolen unit went back to its first owner.** The move to the graveyard makes a card from the field its keeper's before the reset hands control back ([[R2618000]]), so the body returns on the side it died on. The AI's went back to its owner's side, out of the thief's auras and into every read keyed by controller.

**The rule.** The AI decides this; no game changes. The engine gains a read ahead of its own return, `reborn_body_of`: the card a Unit with its Reborn unspent would come back as were it destroyed now, built from the same steps the return runs, the keeper's reset (`reborn_token`, `reset_price` for a card that is no token) and `prepare_body` with the token's summoned face read off the unit before the reset. With `AI_EVAL.reborn_body_read` the AI's body is that card, its Reborn spent and at 1 health as before. `GREEDY_EVAL`, the gates' frozen yardstick, keeps the old read.

**What is not read.** What the return does as it places the body: its zone, position and summoning sickness ([[R83]]), which the AI's next-turn reads already allow for, a doubled Reborn's keep ([[R4589003]]), a Reborn Brittle ([[R3996100]]), and the arrival stamps of a card printing Dormant or GIGA. A printed Reborn unit that is not stolen reads exactly as before.

**Rejected.** Copying the engine's steps into the AI, which is how the two drifted apart; and playing the destroy out on a sandbox copy for every kill the AI prices, which costs a whole state's copy where the reply and the walk price many kills a node.
