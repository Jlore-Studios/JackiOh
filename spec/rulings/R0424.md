The `transformRandom` rider this reading made, `readyToAttack` (the new body is not summoning sick this turn), stays the engine's. Until then: C+ #73.1 transforms after the combat of an attack it declared on a Unit, so its own stats fight: it becomes a random non-token Unit of Classic or Classic+ (a Transform on the field keeps the type, as [[R35]]'s does, [[§6.3]]), and if the Golem was the defender's killer ([[R42]]) the new Unit may attack again this turn, with a fresh exertion and not summoning sick. "May attack again" passes to the new Unit, the one reading in which both of the Golem's lines can matter
