"When this attacks" resolves before the attack happens, before any of its damage. It is the attacker's `whenAttacks` hook, run once for each attack the card makes. A declared attack runs it once [[§4.2]] step 4's trap window has closed on an attack that still stands ([[R220]]), before the defender's side answers "When one of your Units is attacked" ([[R1849008]]) and before the combat. A forced attack ([[R53]]), which opens no window ([[R121]]), runs it just before its combat. The hook runs for the player who controls the attacker and is handed `{ targetId, forced }` (`combat::when_attacks_of`). It is a whole effect list followed by its state check, and a question in it pauses the attack until it is answered, owed on `state.work` as `@whenAttacks` ([[R113]]). An attack called off in its window runs nothing ([[R44]]), and a defending card runs nothing. Once the hook is done, the attack is fought only if it still stands: an attacker or a target that has left the field does not fight, and the exertion stays spent. So Classic #45 Nature Titan draws and heals before the damage, and still has when it dies in that combat, Stinkyscape #75 Badger and #76 Honey Badger gain their Resource before the damage, and Classic #13 Boots on the Ground draws or Recruits before it ([[R2396000]]). Meditative #91.1 Windfurious Prime's joiners already attack before its own combat ([[R1203]]). This is the owner's ruling (issue #2285). Rejected: answering the card's own `attackDeclared` as an ordinary trigger, which the loop reaches only after the combat ([[R212]]), so the effect landed after the damage it comes before; and Stinkyscape's S04 reading of "When this attacks" as an `afterAttack`, which gave the same Resource only while the attacker's survival did not matter. Hearthstone resolves "Whenever this attacks" between the attack's declaration and its damage, too. Issue #2396 ended the reading of "Whenever this attacks" as this timing: "Whenever" now resolves after the attack, survival not asked, and Nature Titan, Badger, Honey Badger and Windfurious Prime print "When" ([[R2396000]]).
