M #9 Joint Filing gains a Cry: base "Cry: Add a random card with a Start of turn or an End of turn effect to your hand.", Radiant "Cry: Add a random card with a Start of turn effect and a random card with an End of turn effect to your hand.", and keeps its Aura (extra triggers, 1, Radiant 2, [[R820]], [[R821]]), where through v0.5.11o it had the Aura alone. The Cry has no number, so the card declares no new one.

- **The pool.** The catalog query (§5.1) gains `turnHook`, `start`, `end` or `either`, true of a card whose base-face script sets `start_of_turn`, `end_of_turn` or either. It is read off the registered script without state, as `tribute` reads its flag ([[R1221]]), so the pool keeps up with every card that gains or loses a hook. A card has the effect when it prints it as a Start of turn or End of turn hook; a trigger that answers a turn event, an Aura, a hand trigger, a delayed effect and the Radiant face's hooks alone do not count, and a card with no registered script has neither hook. The pool is the non-token cards of every set that ships ([[R380]], [[R1420]]), never the card that generated it ([[R387]]: Joint Filing sets no turn hook itself, so the exclusion is the engine's general one), drawn through the match rng ([[R60]]), and a full hand burns the card ([[§2.4]]).
- **Two cards, one of each, on the Radiant face.** The request's second line reads "and" where the first reads "or", and is the Radiant face's. It adds a Start of turn card and then an End of turn card, two draws of the match rng, not one card that has both: only five scripts set both hooks, so that pool would be a handful of cards. The two draws are independent, so the Radiant face may add the same card twice, as any two random adds may ([[R60]]).
- **The Aura does not touch the Cry.** [[R821]] multiplies Start of turn and End of turn effects, which a Cry is neither of: the Cry runs once.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, the Aura alone ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: reading the card's printed text for "Start of turn", which names the words and not the hook and would miss a card whose text a patch rewords; one card with both hooks on the Radiant face, for the pool's size; a pool of the cards a Joint Filing would multiply (the hooks it reaches, [[R821]]), which also takes in the return-flagged Spells of the graveyard and the Animated cards.
