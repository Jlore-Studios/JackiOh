Part 5 of #2725's ten new card versions (tracker #2729), the last, adds the two Mythic chases. Each
is a `version-…` entry of the registry ([[R2725000]]), appended after part 4's two ([[R2943000]]), per
card, owned card by card, never a default or a starter, and Mythic, so each is a chase:

| Version | Id | Rarity | Chase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immersive | `version-immersive` | Mythic | yes |
| Prismatic | `version-prismatic` | Mythic | yes |

**Offers.** #2725 §1 gives both to a few marquee cards per set, and the owner asked (2026-11-03) that
each version go to a varied set of cards of its own, not to every card nor to another version's
cards. Each offer is data ([[R2725001]]), every sampled clause seeded by the version's own id
([[R2949001]]):

- **Immersive**: 40 in 100 of the Legendary and Mythic cards, every type, but the Dormant Starship
  (`stinkyscape-t-dormant-starship`), whose picture in play has no subject of its own to lift onto a
  near layer ([[R2249000]]). 50 cards when it shipped, 2 of Meditative+'s and of Training Grounds' its
  fewest in a set.
- **Prismatic**: every Mythic card, the top chase on the top cards, and 20 in 100 of the Legendary
  ones. 48 cards (the 30 Mythic and 18 Legendary), 2 of Meditative+'s and of Training Grounds' its
  fewest. One clause sampling Legendary and Mythic cards alike would have left Meditative+, which has
  two Mythic cards and one Legendary, with one card, under `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET`; its first
  clause takes the Mythic cards whole, so it samples nothing and names no seed.

Neither takes a Common, Rare or Epic card, so the Mythic looks stay on the marquee cards and cards get
different mixes. Each reaches `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_CARDS` in all and `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET` in every
set that ships, neither is offered on every card, and no two of the eleven versions (Full art, part 2's
three, part 3's three, part 4's two and these two) take the same set of cards. A version a card is not
offered is never dropped, sold, picked or shown for it: the Collection's strip does not list it, and a
pick, a token or the Chase Shard bar naming it there is a 400 that writes nothing ([[R2725001]]). No
retired full art implies one of them ([[R2725002]]), and Signature fits neither ([[R2949000]]).

**Granted to no account.** Like every cosmetic added since #1833 (§14.12), the two start unowned on
every account, existing ones included; `POST /api/dev/fill` grants each on every card it is offered,
and `jackioh-server seed-catalog` upserts them into `public.cosmetics` as it does every registry
entry, so no migration is needed. A cosmetic token buys up to its own rarity and none is Mythic
([[R2728000]]), so a Legendary token naming one where it is offered is refused (409) and a "Mythic"
token is no token (400): a duplicate's drop ([[R1301002]]) or the Chase Shard bar, on a card it is
offered, is how one is earned. The scale stays Common to Mythic ([[R2725000]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a version, and nothing enters `PlayerView`'s
rules, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725000]] (two more versions, the last of #2725's ten) and [[R2725001]] (two more offers,
each a share of cards of its own).
