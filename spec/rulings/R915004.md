A custom card's hook runs under the sandbox, the integer boundary and the memory cap ([[§12.5]]), and an instruction budget of its own, `CUSTOM_HOOK_INTERRUPTS`, smaller than an official script's was. A hook that errors, runs out of its budget or its memory, or returns what the kit or the host refuses:

- resolves none of its effects;
- resolves instead `scriptFailed`, an event naming the card's controller, its id and what went wrong (the hook, and the line when there is one), which the board shows;
- leaves the game going on, the card otherwise unchanged. A card whose script does not load at all says so whenever its Cry or Death would run.
