M+ #20 asks C+ #42's `answer` prompt ([[R420]]) again after each right answer: one question at a time, each rolled from the match rng as it is asked, never drawn from a bank. A question is two operands from the face's range, three-digit numbers (100 to 999, `KY_COMPETITION_OPERANDS`) on the base face and one- and two-digit numbers (1 to 99, `KY_COMPETITION_EASY_OPERANDS`) on the Radiant face, added or subtracted with equal chance, a subtraction's larger operand first, so no answer is below 0. Its three wrong options are the answer moved by three different misses drawn as a shuffle of the face's list, ±1, ±10 and ±100 on the base face (`KY_COMPETITION_MISSES`, a slip in one column) and C+ #42's Easy misses ±1, ±2 and ±10 on the Radiant face (`KY_COMPETITION_EASY_MISSES`, [[R580]]), skipping any that would fall below 0, so the four always differ. The statement is plain text, `a + b = ?` or `a − b = ?` with Unicode's minus. The four are shown under letters in an rng-shuffled order and the right letter stays in the prompt's resume data, which no view sends ([[R465]]).

A question of the sequence also offers Stop, listed after the lettered answers, a `none` pick under no letter (`ANSWER_STOP_KEY`), and carries the streak, the right answers so far (`PendingChoice.streak`), which its chooser's view shows; the other seat sees only that a prompt is open ([[§10.8]]). The streak rides the prompt's resume data (`kyCompetitionStreak`), plain data, so a game paused mid-sequence survives JSON and replays ([[§10.6]]). A right answer asks the next question with the streak one higher. A wrong answer and Stop alike end the sequence with the streak as it stood, so every answer counted was right and in a row, and a miss costs nothing a Stop would have kept. The answer that brings the streak to `KY_COMPETITION_MAX` (30) is the last: the sequence ends there with X 30. An ended sequence summons M+ #20.1 Lil' Kenny with X the streak ([[R1184003]]), Radiant on the Radiant face, whose own face makes it 2X/2X; a streak of 0 summons nothing, and the Spell was still played. The turn clock runs while a question is open, as for any prompt ([[R79]]); a timeout stops the sequence ([[R1184001]]).
