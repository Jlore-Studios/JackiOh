[[R44]]'s lethal is what [[§4.4]] steps 1 to 4 leave of the hit, as step 4a judges it: Divine Shield, Armor unless the hit pierces, the divisors and caps, then a conversion, which leaves nothing ([[R3540003]]), and what the target side's damage pools have left ([[R3550004]]), never spent on an Indestructible Unit. One read-only projection of those steps, in the pipeline's order, serves My Pawn, its glow ([[R662]]), the combat forecast ([[R3100000]]) and the AI's model of the opponent's reply. It keeps a per-side total of what the pools have soaked, so the hits of one attack (the blow, each Cleave hit and each Trample excess, in the combat's order) and the attackers the forecast's `lethal` adds up spend each pool once between them: two 3-attack blows into a pool of 5 deal 0, then 1. A strike back is projected separately, through its own target's side, so a First Strike or Lifesteal strike back the attacker's own pool soaks neither destroys the attacker first nor heals; the one case where the blow and the strike back meet the same pool, an attack on a Unit of the attacker's own side ([[R1122]]), is not ordered between them. Steps 0a and 0b and step 4a's replacements stay unprojected, and wherever the pipeline settles the pool against step 4a, the projection reads the same steps. Rejected: spending the pool in the state while projecting, which a projection must never touch; a fresh pool for each hit, which would forecast 3 + 3 into a pool of 5 as 0 + 0; and a pool-aware hero projection for every reader, which the AI reads for the Burn and Toxic still to tick ([[R3822000]]), damage a pool spent today may not meet.
