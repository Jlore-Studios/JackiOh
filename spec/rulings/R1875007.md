"Reveals when your opponent casts a beneficial Spell on themselves or a permanent they control" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #139 Wormhole) is [[R1850004]]'s reading: a Spell its opponent plays whose chosen targets, one at least, are all that player's own hero or cards they control on the field. A Spell with no target, or one aimed at Wormhole's own side, leaves it set. It answers in [[§10.5]] step 3a's announce window: the Spell is countered into Wormhole's controller's hand as theirs ([[R448]]) and cast by them ([[R70]]), every pick of the cast their own hero or a card they control, they choosing it, a declaration with none passed ([[R90]]); the Spell lands in their graveyard. The Radiant face makes it Radiant first, so it resolves Radiant and lands Radiant. Rejected: the Spell's own targets kept, which would let the stolen Spell still help the opponent.
