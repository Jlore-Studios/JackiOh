Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VII: Ticketed, "Simplify Cry and hold text to: Stack this on a permanent anywhere. It can be placed in any occupied zone, including the opponent's side. It functionally works like a dead card/zone block." S #9's play takes no zone of its own (`StaticFlags.stacks_on_target`): it declares one permanent on the field ([[R81]]), on either side, in either row, face-down or not, and as it is placed it lands on top of that permanent's pile, in its zone, and the card takes that side's control, as a card summoned for the opponent does ([[§3.2]]). The permanent lies beneath it as a card beneath a Stack does ([[R13]]): out of play, keeping its damage, and resuming on top when Ticketed leaves the field, any way it leaves. Its base face's Brittle 3 ticks at its controller's turns ([[R638]]), so on the opponent's side at theirs. On a unit zone Ticketed lies there with no position, no Unit (`zones::lies_on_unit_zone`, the one card a unit zone holds that is not one): no unit walk sees it, so nothing attacks it, no "all Units" reaches it and no health judges it; it fills the zone, so no play or summon takes it; and an effect that names a backrow card may name it, as the Field Spell it is, while one that sweeps every permanent sweeps it. A permanent that has left the field or been covered by the time Ticketed is placed leaves it nothing to stack on, and it lands in its graveyard as a permanent with no zone does ([[R138]]); a play with no permanent on the field to name is legal and does the same ([[R90]]). Cast with no play (a random cast), it takes its own side's leftmost open backrow zone as any Field Spell does. Through v0.6.12c its Cry held another face-up permanent beneath it in its own zone ([[R1850003]], [[R1875001]]), which `script_v0_6_12c` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
