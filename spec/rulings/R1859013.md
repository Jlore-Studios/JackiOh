The Dormant Starship (ST-S02-13) printed what the engine does until issue #2249 cut it to a line and its Activate, the rest left to the Starship and Launch terms ([[R2249000]]): a piece's death summons it and fuses into it ([[R1852000]]), it is Dormant until it launches ([[R1839000]]), a Stack card played onto it is exiled ([[R1852007]]), and its one ability is the engine's launch for (4) (`STARSHIP_LAUNCH_COST`, (3) until issue #3038), which takes a name from `STARSHIP_NAMES` ([[R1852004]]), fires its fused Cries, then its fused When launched ([[R1852003]]); the designer's "trigger all Cries" is those fused Cries. So its script is the hull alone, `StaticFlags { slumber, starshipHull }` on both faces, and adds nothing. It is no token, a Legendary at `T-dormant-starship`, its Radiant face [[R349]]'s fallback, in no pool and no deck ([[R1857001]]). Since issue #3038 it carries the Starship tag and prints Flying and Ranged on both faces, its text leading with the two, which the layers give every card holding the tag anyway ([[R3038000]]).
