The Dormant Starship (ST-S02-13) prints what the engine does: a piece's death summons it and fuses into it ([[R1852000]]), it is Dormant until it launches ([[R1839000]]), a Stack card played onto it is exiled ([[R1852007]]), and its one ability is the engine's launch for (3), which takes a name from `STARSHIP_NAMES` ([[R1852004]]), fires its fused Cries, then its fused When launched ([[R1852003]]); the designer's "trigger all Cries" is those fused Cries. So its script is the hull alone, `StaticFlags { slumber, starshipHull }` on both faces, and adds nothing. It is no token, a Legendary at `T-dormant-starship`, its Radiant face [[R349]]'s fallback, in no pool and no deck ([[R1857001]]).
