[[R750000]]'s stronger ladder bots lean their random decks on the cards that win, read off the compiled win-rate table, which [[R3049000]] retires. The deal now reads the table it is handed (`AiDeckOptions.winRates`): when the bot pool refreshes and deals a bot its Best-of-1 decks ([[R1454]]) or its trio's ([[R641000]]), it takes one snapshot of the server's cached card stats (`api::stats::win_rate_snapshot`) and every deal of that refresh leans on it. The deal is `jackioh_shared::bots::pool`'s (`draw_decks`, `draw_trio`), which the server's refresh and the bots' JSON boundary call alike: the boundary's `drawDecks` and `drawTrio` take the table as `winRates`. A deal handed no table leans on nothing, so at any strength it is the deck the seed deals at strength 0. Every other deal (a player's, practice's, the gates', the arena's) passes no strength and no table and deals as it did. The weight's formula, its floor and its cap are [[R750000]]'s. The pure crate reads no live data: the figures arrive as an input.

Rejected: dealing again at each match from the snapshot the match starts with, since a bot's decks are dealt once per catalog and kept on its row, and a deal at each match would change the deck a player saw it hold.
