Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Every face-up Legendary or Mythic permanent in play (Units and backrow alike) marks its tile (`Zone.tsx`, `.zone`) with an aura drawn as the first child of the tile element, painting beneath the card. Its colours are drawn from the card's picture (its painter's palette, shifted deterministically by its id's hash within `±TILE_HUE_SPREAD_DEG` so distinct Legendaries differ in look) and its pattern from its set's showcase family:
- **Legendary:** a soft edge glow (`TILE_GLOW_PX.Legendary`) and slow motes (`TILE_MOTES_LEGENDARY`).
- **Mythic:** a richer pattern in SVG paths under the card (circuitry for Core, runes for Classic, ink for Meditative, starfield for Stinkyscape, roots for Training Grounds), a wider edge glow (`TILE_GLOW_PX.Mythic`) and motes (`TILE_MOTES_MYTHIC`).

A card whose entry in the effects table carries its own standing `aura` (such as Feng Shui, `meditative-040`) wears that signature aura in the aura layer ([[R2787000]]) instead of the tile aura default.

What wears a tile aura is read off the view the board is showing and nothing else ([[R202]]). A face-down card wears none on either seat, nor does an unrevealed Field Trap or Trap, nor any hidden card. Tokens that print a Legendary frame rarity (`printedRarity`) receive one. The aura knows the state: it dims to `TILE_DIM` while its card is Dormant (`slumber`), Frozen, Frozen Forever or Silenced (`vanilla`), and fades out over `TILE_FADE_OUT_MS` (matching `ANIMATIONS.destroyed.durationMs` and `ANIMATIONS.exiled.durationMs`) when its card row is destroyed or exiled. Being damaged or losing divine shield triggers a quick flicker (`TILE_FLICKER_MS`). Being targeted by the viewer (`highlight.selected`) brightens the edge glow by `TILE_LIT_BOOST`.

The tile aura answers touch: hovering, pressing or tapping the card or its tile sends a ripple through the aura (`TILE_RIPPLE_MS`), plays a soft bell in the aura's hue (`auraTouch` in `audio/`), and on touch screens buzzes a short haptic (`Haptics.touch()` in `haptics/`, guarded by the Vibration setting and `HAPTIC_MIN_GAP_MS`). Rapid touches within `TILE_TOUCH_GAP_MS` are throttled.

Motion and budgets: motes are capped at `TILE_PARTICLE_SHARE` of the mobile particle cap (`FX_PARTICLE_CAP_MOBILE`), safely within `BACKDROP_PARTICLE_MAX` and the phone frame budget ([[R2803000]]). Under reduced motion ([[R438]], via media query or settings panel), animations stop, motes and ripples are omitted, and the sound still plays. With the effects intensity `off`, no tile aura is rendered.

Rejected: drawing tile auras on the canvas overlay (`AuraLayer.tsx`), which sits above cards and cannot paint beneath them; adding a tile aura field to the engine view, which would change the wire format and leak hidden information; and reading the opponent's relayed aim arrow across the network, which requires complex board plumbing and is not needed for local target feedback.

Since [[R3400000]] an aura a card's entry wears beside a bespoke recipe replaces the default too, as one beside hooks does, so ([[R3399001]]) a Classic+ signature permanent wears its own aura and no default.
