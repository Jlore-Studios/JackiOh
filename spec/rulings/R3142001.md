Stinkyscape #18.1 MELCHIOR's Activate ([[R384]], once a turn) reads "Give each of your Units a random combat keyword." on its base face and "Give each of your Units 3 random combat keywords." on its Radiant face, both `keywords` ([[§8.10]]: 1, Radiant 3, either face a Buff or a Nerf may move). Each of its controller's Units draws its own keywords from [[R21]]'s pool, never one it already has, as the Radiant face drew them through v0.5.11p ([[R1866001]]): no prompt, no shared draw. Through v0.5.11p the base face was a Discover ([[§6.3]]) of three different combat keywords ([[R781005]]) whose pick went to every Unit; that is gone, and the card that summons MELCHIOR, Imitated Instrumentality, is unchanged.

- **With no Unit.** Nothing is given and the turn's use is spent, as [[R1866001]] had it for the Discover.
- **Random draws.** Each Unit's draw is its own use of the match rng, in [[R68]]'s order, so two Units seldom end with the same keyword; a Unit that has every keyword of the pool gains none.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11p folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, whose base Activate Discovers ([[R767000]]); its Radiant face is the one below.

Rejected: one draw shared by every Unit, which "a random combat keyword" for "each of your Units" does not say, and which the Radiant face never did either.
