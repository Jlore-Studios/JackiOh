[[R1655000]] put a card's picks only on the viewer's own copy on a board they play, its inspect
overlays and the Collection's card view, so a player who equipped a foil in the Collection went back
to its grid and saw a plain card. More of the viewer's own copies now wear their picks, by the same
rules: a card's own pick of a kind, else the kind's default ([[R1953001]]), worn only where the viewer
owns it on that card ([[R1655000]], [[R1833000]]), and the rarity rules of [[R1295000]] whatever is
picked.

**The Collection's grid.** A card the collection holds is the viewer's own copy, so its tile in the
owned-cards grid wears its picks, as its card view does. A locked silhouette, a card not held, draws
the defaults.

**The deck builder.** Every face of the deck builder was already the viewer's own copy for full art
([[R1313000]]); its pool and deck list tiles now wear the foil, finish and border too, save a card the
collection says is not held, which draws the defaults (a collection that could not be read claims no
card unheld). A deck list tile is a strip of the card's art, with no frame and no paper, so of what it
wears only the foil shows, as one still sheen over the strip.

**The Almanac.** The Almanac stays public, and signed out it reads nothing and draws every card as
before. Signed in to an active account, it reads the account, its collection and, while ownership
decides, the session's one cosmetics ledger ([[R1313000]]): endpoints the client already reads, and
nothing new of the server. A card the collection holds is then drawn as the viewer's own copy,
behind that ledger, as on the gated screens: it wears its picks, and prints its full art as the
viewer's own copy does, by its pick and the Full-art cards setting behind ownership. Every other card,
and every card until both reads answer or after either fails, draws as before. Where ownership does
not decide (a development build, the economy off) every cosmetic counts as owned, as everywhere else.
It amends [[R630]], whose Almanac called no endpoint at all.

**At once, in every tab.** A pick shows on every face this tab draws on the render that makes it.
Both device stores (the account's picks and the cards', [[R1295000]] and [[R1655000]]) also listen,
from their first read for the page's life, for another tab's write to their key (or a cleared
storage), read it again and redraw, so a pick made in one tab shows in every open Collection, deck
builder, Almanac and board with no reload, and on a face mounted later too. What this page could not
save gives way to what storage then holds, which is what every other tab draws.

**Moving foils.** A face of a long grid (the Collection's, the pool's) holds a cosmetic it wears still
and takes none of the few moving slots, so the card a player opens over it, in its view, its detail or
a preview, keeps its motion ([[R1833000]]).

Every other face draws as [[R1655000]] and [[R1834004]] have it: an opponent's copy, and every copy
on a watched game or an online replay, wears its owner's frozen loadout, which the server sent; the
homescreen fan and a prompt's offered options draw the defaults ([[§14.2]]: the client never infers
another's cosmetics). Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches an action, the log, a
replay or another player's view.

Hearthstone semantics: the collection and the deck builder show the version of a card the player
owns and has equipped, golden or signature, wherever it is their card.

Rejected: the Almanac with no ownership mounted, where every cosmetic counts as owned and a stored
pick the account does not own would show; a new endpoint for the Almanac; the Almanac's own cards
keeping the catalog's full art whatever their picks, which would leave one of the four picks unworn
there; a storage listener held only while a face is mounted, which leaves a face mounted later on the
picks of before; and a grid's faces taking the moving slots, which would still the large card a player
opens over the grid.
