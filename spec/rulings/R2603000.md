Amends [[R2564000]], which priced the Activate of a unit acting on either field and left a backrow card's out. Twenty-two shipped Field Spells print an Activate their controller can use every turn ([[R384]]), Classic #20 The Power to Punish, Classic #81 The Power to Thrive and Classic+ #78 Claude's Datacenter among them, and the AI's evaluation counted each by its cost alone: it played them rarely, and an enemy's never counted as a threat, though the opponent's reply already plays a face-up one (`visible_activation`). No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** What [[R2564000]] counts, for more cards. At the start of each player's turn the probes reach, after that player's units, the AI tries the Activates of each card acting face-up in that player's backrow (`activate_sources`): a Field Spell, or a Trap or Field Trap once it is face-up, but never a Unit standing there, which is one of the units already ([[R1081000]]). Each is tried, priced and keyed (definition, face, controller) as a unit is. A card of the seat's own makes the probes play the second turn end, as a unit of its own does, and every card shares `AI_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES` ([[R186]]'s budget), which stays 20. `material` adds `AI_EVAL.activation` of the price for each backrow card the seat can read, on either side, inside the card's Brittle share as `unit_worth` adds a unit's: a card that crumbles, Stinkyscape #39 The Narcor Project, takes its coming uses with it. The weight stays 0.5 (at 0.5 the smoke gate read 20, 16 and 19 of 20, against random, against greedy and as Hard against Easy) and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. An Animated backrow card waiting to be a Unit reads its price again in the share of it `material` counts as a Unit (`unit_worth`), as its turn hooks do ([[R2565000]]); no shipped Animated card prints an Activate.

**What is not priced.**
- A face-down card, the seat's or the enemy's, which is never probed ([[R185]]), and a card in a hand or a library.
- Everything else [[R2564000]] does not price.

**Rejected.** Adding the price outside the Brittle share, as [[R2565000]] adds a turn hook's, since a Brittle card's coming uses crumble with it as a unit's do; a budget of its own for backrow cards, which would draw more of the decision's nodes; and taking the use back out of an Animated card's waiting Unit, for a case no shipped card has.
