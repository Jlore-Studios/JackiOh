`moderate ban` flips an account's `profiles.status` to `banned` and touches nothing else ([[§9.4]]): the daily seeks it left waiting and the daily challenges it sent to friends stay open. While the ban holds, those seeks pair with no one and those challenges cannot be accepted: `daily_seek_claim` passes over a seek whose `from_profile` is not `active`, and accepting a challenge whose challenger is not `active` answers 404 `no such daily challenge`, as to a non-friend ([[R1689000]]). The challenge is also left out of its recipient's open list, so the inbox never offers it, as if the account were gone. As in Hearthstone, an account that cannot play is never paired. Once the ban is lifted (`moderate unban`), before the rows lapse, the seek pairs again and the challenge accepts and lists again. A daily game already running when the account is banned still ends on time; ending it at the ban is the owner's call.
