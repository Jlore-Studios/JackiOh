When the turn clock runs out ([[R79]]) on an open `market` prompt ([[R1000]]), the engine answers it
with Leave (`night_market::leave_answer`), not with a uniform random answer: the market closes, the
yuan left is lost and nothing more is drawn. Any other prompt's timeout is answered as before
([[§10.7]]). The random policy still answers a market uniformly until it closes.
