The fuzz monitor's I19 (issue #4454) found the two board restores reaching Dormant cards, which no effect may otherwise do ([[§6.1]] Dormant, [[R1839000]]): Classic+ #35 Rollback, which returns the field to an earlier turn's ([[R419]]), and Stinkyscape+ #84 Bogos Binted's Death, which resets its side to its Cry's snapshot ([[R3550007]]). Both rebuild a side whole: a card the snapshot does not hold goes to its controller's hand, and each card it holds goes back to its place as the snapshot holds it. A Dormant card went too: to a hand when it arrived after the snapshot, or back as it stood then, a Dormant Starship holding the pieces it held then.
**They may.** A restore is the board going back to an earlier state, not an effect reaching for a card, as [[R3539002]] says of a board exile. A Dormant card sent to a hand loses its status there ([[R78]]); one the snapshot holds stands again as the snapshot holds it, still Dormant if it was then, entering the side if it stood on the other ([[R171]]). Bogos Binted's Radiant face then makes every card of the rebuilt side Radiant ([[R74]]), the Dormant ones among them.
**What I19 allows.** After a `rolledBack`, until the next card's hook, trap or turn edge: a `bounced`, a `controlChanged` or a `radiantSet` naming a Dormant card, and any record change of a Dormant card on a restored side.
Rejected: passing a Dormant card by as a board replacement does ([[R1855000]]). A restore must clear the side to rebuild it, so a Dormant card left standing would bounce the snapshot's card bound for its zone, and the side would not be the turn the card returns to.
