Everyone owns every card ([[§9.1]]), and [[R111]]'s launch grant rides only the pending → active transition, so an account already active when a patch adds cards would never own them. When `app.settings.catalog_version` changes — `db:seed-catalog` stamps it after writing every card row of the new version, in the same transaction — every active account is granted the launch quantity of each non-token card of the new version it holds no `launch` grant for, through `app.grant_launch_collection_all` and the ledger (reason `launch`, ref `launch:<version>`, migration 0015). It is idempotent: the same stamp again, or a round trip through another version, grants nothing twice; a pending account waits for its own activation; a card a later catalog drops is never taken back. A dev account is passed over ([[R1300001]]).

Superseded by the economy's going live (issue #1310, [[R1310001]]): migration 0048 drops the stamp's trigger, `app.on_catalog_version_stamped` and `app.grant_launch_collection_all`, so a new catalog version grants no account its cards, and a new set's cards come from its track and its packs ([[§14.12]]). Every card this grant gave before then stays owned ([[R1310002]]).
