Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) that every card's art be more its own and richer
in its set's style, simpler for a Common and elaborate for a Mythic, its Spells and Field Spells real scenes; this
issue is the pass over Stinkyscape's later cards, from a brief per entry, on the foundation [[R3331000]] and
[[R3331001]] laid, in the way [[R3390000]] drew Classic+.

**A recipe per entry.** Each of the 103 entries of #3403's table draws from a recipe of its own
(`stinkyscape91Recipes1.ts` to `stinkyscape91Recipes3.ts`, in id order, spread into `SCENE_RECIPES` as
`STINKYSCAPE_91_RECIPES` after Classic+'s), in Stinkyscape's family: deep space, the slates for hulls and armour,
cyan lights and shields, violet nebulae, a star's rim light, fine black line on its solid shapes. Each keeps its
brief's subject, setting and idea, its subject within x 30 to 70 and y 25 to 85, its foreground in the near layer,
its middle in the mid and subject layers and its background in the far and sky layers, and its emblem small (size 8
at most) inside the band every face's window shows. Each clears its rarity's floor ([[R3331001]]): an Epic or rarer
draws far, mid, subject and near layers, a Legendary or Mythic every layer. The Stinkyscape painter hands each to
the scene as it does Arg Arg Defender ([[R2170000]] stays its fallback, so `stinkyscape.ts` is unchanged), and
[[R3331002]]'s record holds every other Stinkyscape entry with no recipe ([[R3391000]]'s cards 1 to 90 draw theirs) to the picture it shipped with. The Radiant face draws the
same shapes repainted toward gold, under the painter's rays, and no base face paints in Radiant's gold window
([[R2725004]]).

**The cards other issues draw.** The table leaves out, and this pass gives no recipe to, every entry that prints
`Starship Piece` and Jlockeed's Siege Tank, Yamato Cannon and Missile Pod, which are Starship Pieces when
Embiggened, with the Dormant Starship (#3333); the Drone Battleship's three layers (#3336); Anomaly Found! (#3337);
and the six Terminus tokens of Olivian Expanse and Dice of Doom (#3338). Each keeps the picture it draws today.

**Characters and machines.** Each of the table's thirteen humanoid Units draws a figure from the figure kit whose
look starts from its tag's people (`figureLookFor`) and is dressed as who it is, no two alike: Tmack Trav in a red
flight suit with a shock-axe, Scornboi in a crimson greatcoat pointing with his short list, A Very Dandy Lad in a
top hat and frock coat, IN33DH3 seated in a hoodie and headphones. Fourteen Units are no person and draw a creature or
a machine of the scene kit as their subject, with no figure: the Propagation's Wasp and Hornet (a space wasp),
Megabase (a space station), the Sack, the Stinkyscape Golem (a scrap golem), the Tiny, Medium and Large Asteroids
(an asteroid), the Huge Asteroid (a moon-sized asteroid with a scowling face), the Abandoned Spice Tower, the Drone
Interceptor, the Drone Corvette, Escort Protocol (a hovering escort disc) and Combat Subroutine (a combat robot).
Crowds, crews, rivals and companions are `extras` ([[R3390000]]), figures in their own layers.

**The kits.** The parts the briefs name join the kits: the figure kit's neural crown, space helmet, silver
hair and top hat, its flight suit, greatcoat, sleeveless vest, plated parka, frock coat, cloak and lab
harness, and its shock-axe, dart, orb of power, slate, welding torch and vial; and the scene kit's space, in four
modules beside the others (`sceneKitSpace.ts` for decks, bulkheads, moons, stations and their fittings,
`sceneKitCraft.ts` for ships, drones, robots and creatures, `sceneKitGear.ts` for devices, props and energies,
`sceneKitCosmos.ts` for the weather of space), each drawing with `kitDraw.ts`'s helpers and spread into `SCENE_KIT`.
Every part they add is drawn by at least one of the pass's scenes. Where an earlier pass had already taken a name,
the pass wears that pass's part (the pointing pose and the hoodie are [[R3392000]]'s, the headphones [[R3401000]]'s) or names
its own for what sets it apart, so no module's spread replaces another's part: the sleeveless vest, the plated
parka and the shock-axe stand beside the spiked vest, the fur parka and the war axe, and the observation bench, the
lamp-lit tent, the ticker tape, the speed streaks, the surf wave, the crew crowd and the kicking sack beside the
park bench, the ridge tent, the confetti, the streaks, the great wave, the concert crowd and the sack, and the
shipyard crane, the space rift and the holographic blueprint beside [[R3401000]]'s crane, rift and blueprint.

**The ratchet.** Every entry of the table leaves `ART_DETAIL_SHORT` (28 of them were on it), and
`ART_DETAIL_SHORT_MAX` falls from 157 to 129, the list's length as this issue left it, so it may only shrink.

Rejected: putting creature and machine bodies in the figure kit, whose pose limbs fit no wasp, station or asteroid;
crowds as silhouettes only, where a crew or a rival is a person of its own; growing `sceneKit.ts` itself, which
#3390 had already split into modules sharing `kitDraw.ts`; keeping the cap at 157, which would let the list grow
back by the 28 entries this pass took off; and parts of about 40 entries each in their own pull requests, which the
three recipe files map onto should a person want them split.

Amended by [[R3406000]] (issue #3406): an entry of #3406's table offered Alt art no longer grows its own scene's parts from its Alt art seed but draws an alt scene recipe of its own, its subject in another place, time or moment, at every Alt art level.
