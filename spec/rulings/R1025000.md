The keyword overhaul of issue #781 meant the combat keyword pool ([[R21]], [[R781005]]) to hold keywords that need no number, and left Armor 1 and Spell Damage +1 in it. Patch v0.3.Y takes both out: the pool is Taunt, Rush, Charge, First Strike, Flying, Ranged, Poisonous, Reborn, Divine Shield, Windfury, Cleave, Indestructible, Deft, Lifesteal, Pierce and Trample, in that order, sixteen entries. The survivors keep their relative order, so a seeded draw still picks by the same list with two entries fewer.

No grant that draws from the pool can give Armor or Spell Damage: Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao, the Conjure Rush Tokens, a Buff's keyword row and Keymaster Keenus's Aura all read the same list, so they stop at once. Armor and Spell Damage stay keywords of [[§6.1]], printed on cards, given by Defense Position (Armor) and moved as numbered keywords by a Nerf or a Buff's X row; only the random draw no longer reaches them. R781005's clause that a unit with Armor or Spell Damage is not offered them is moot, and a unit is still never offered a keyword kind it holds, nor Taunt beside Indestructible ([[R347]], [[R781004]]).
