A forced attack whose attackers say `random` ("when the enemy summons a unit a random unit from your side attacks it") makes one of the named Units attack instead of each, drawn once from the match rng among those that may attack the target under [[§4.2]] step 2: an enemy of it ([[R173]]), not on a carrier, and barred by no restriction, Flying's among them ([[R783002]]). A Unit that could not attack the target is never drawn, and with none able nothing is drawn and the rng does not move. The drawn Unit's attack is a forced attack like any ([[R53]]). Without `random` (Warp Disruptor II's "all units from your side") each named Unit attacks in lane order, as before. Rejected: drawing among every named Unit and letting a barred draw do nothing, where Hearthstone's random picks draw among the valid choices.
