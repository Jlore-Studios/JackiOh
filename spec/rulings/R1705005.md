Each lesson's human deck is a fixed list of 20 cards, and its coach, its seed and its tests were
written for the deal those 20 give ([[R291]]). When `DECK_SIZE` became 25 ([[R1705000]]) a new game
would refuse that deck on a seat with no handicap. So a lesson seats its human under
`TUTORIAL_HUMAN`, a handicap ([[R180]]) that is a human's resources but for the deck, which holds
`EARLIER_DECK_SIZE` (20) cards: the human's mana, opening hand, draws and health are a human's, and
the seat's deck is held to its handicap's 20 ([[R184]]), so every lesson deals the opening hands and
draws it always dealt. The tutorial opponent keeps `AI_TUTORIAL` ([[R290]]).

`TUTORIAL_HUMAN` is not a human's handicap, so the seat stores it, and a lesson replays from
`(seed, decks, handicaps, log)` like any practice game ([[R187]]) with no need of
[[R1705003]]'s recorded size. The dev hotseat's end-to-end fixtures whose deals were tuned on
20-card decks carry the same handicap on those seats. No player picks it: only a lesson and those fixtures deal it.

Hearthstone semantics: a tutorial game is a scripted encounter with decks of its own, not a
constructed game, and the deck-building rules do not bind it.

Rejected: re-scripting the four lessons on 25-card decks and re-tuning the 25 seeded end-to-end
specs whose deals were tuned on 20, which would change the deal each lesson's coach and each of
those specs was written for.
