A saved deck in the workshop ([[§9.4]]) that holds cards the newest shipped patch changed wears "N cards changed in vX" on its row and in its open editor. The newest patch is the last in `patches.json`'s order ([[R388]]), and only while it is the catalog the builder shows.

**What counts as changed.** A card the patch's record lists as changed whose snapshot differs from the one before, on a face or only in unprinted data ([[R507]]). Flavor alone never counts ([[R1437000]]), nor Glitch ([[R674]]), nor a card the patch added or removed. The count is of distinct cards.

**What the badge opens.** Exactly those cards, each by name with its changes as the history words them ([[R507]]). A deck with none wears nothing.

**When it clears.** Seen means saved under the patch. The badge shows only while the deck's latest save carries an older catalog version: the server's stamp ([[R253]]), or this build's from the moment an edit is made here, which the autosave sends ([[R256]]). Opening a deck saves nothing.

Hearthstone semantics: after a balance patch, a deck holding a changed card says so until it is saved.

Rejected: counting only printed changes, a dismiss control, and a device-side list of seen decks (the server's stamp already crosses devices).
