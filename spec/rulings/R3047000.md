The owner's pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #3000, section 3) widens the Marketplace and reprices it, amending [[R2250002]] and [[R1853002]].

- **Four offers a turn.** At the start of each player's turn, and as the Marketplace opens, a roll draws `MARKETPLACE_OFFERS_PER_TURN` offers, 4 where it was 3, each as [[R2250002]] draws one: a lot by the lots' counts among those with a card left, a card of it not offered yet in the roll, whether it is Radiant, then what [[R3047002]] adds, then its price. A table with fewer cards than that offers every card there is, and nothing is drawn for an offer it cannot make.
- **The base price.** A card of printed cost c (`query_cost`: an X card's is 0) is **5 + 10 × min(c, 4) + 15 × max(c − 4, 0)** Credits, and a (0) card's is 8: 8, 15, 25, 35, 45, 60, 75, 90 and 105 for (0) to (8), then 15 more a mana (`MARKETPLACE_ZERO_COST_PRICE`, `MARKETPLACE_PRICE_BASE`, `MARKETPLACE_CREDITS_PER_MANA`, `MARKETPLACE_FULL_PRICE_MANA`, `MARKETPLACE_CREDITS_PER_MANA_PAST`). The designer gave the curve's first eight prices; the formula is the one line that holds them all and goes on past (8) by the same step. A Radiant lot is twice that, and the Buffs or Nerfs of [[R3047002]] and the spread of [[R1853002]] move the result in that order. `MARKETPLACE_CREDITS_PER_MANA` stays 10, so the practice AI, which reads it by name to price a Credit as the hand prices the mana it buys, values a Credit as before. Rejected: a lookup of the eight prices, which says nothing of a (9) card or a card dearer than that.

The table's lots, the stock of one copy, the Radiant chance and its doubling, the spread and the quotes are as they were. The roll draws more from the match rng (a fourth offer's draws, and [[R3047002]]'s), so a game with a Marketplace deals differently from one before this ruling. No shipped game has one: the Marketplace opens only on a Resource or Credits gain, which only a card of the held Stinkyscape set makes ([[R1881006]]).
