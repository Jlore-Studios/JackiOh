The designer asked (#1563) for card options for M #20 Aestheticize the Game's other two win conditions. M #20 lets its caster choose one of three lines, and [[R1185000]]'s plan table gave it Spa Day alone, the help for the health line. Since #1563 the table gives M #20 three plans, and Bob's Plan Discovers one of them after adding the card, the three offered in an order drawn from the match rng and the chosen one added (Radiant on the Radiant face); M #8 Reach the Summit still has its one plan, added with no prompt. A full hand burns the chosen plan as it burned Spa Day ([[§2.4]]).

The two new plans are AI generated tokens of the Meditative+ set, printing no rarity ([[R1178002]]), and join the pool of AI generated cards ([[R480]]):

- M+ #1.3 **Garage Sale** (1), for the graveyard line: as many Coins as its caster's hand has room for (their hand size, [[§6.3]], less the cards held), then a discard of the whole hand. A Coin is a Spell token, so it reaches the graveyard as any discarded card does; [[R11]] spares only a unit-token card. The Radiant face does it twice (`rounds`, a number only that face prints, [[R749]]), the second round filling the hand the first emptied.
- M+ #1.4 **Leg Day** (2), for the board line: every Unit its caster controls gains +5/+5 (Radiant +10/+10); the opponent's none.
