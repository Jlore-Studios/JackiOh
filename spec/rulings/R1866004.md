Stinkyscape #18.3 CASPER's Activate ([[R384]], once a turn) Allures ([[§6.3]], [[R963]]) one enemy Unit drawn from the match rng among the Units acting on the opponent's field, the top of each unit zone and a Unit standing in a backrow zone, never a carried one ([[R446]]) or a Dormant one ([[R1839000]]). The Unit carries Allure's pink mark in both views ([[R437]]) and at the start of its controller's next turn is stolen, or destroyed with no open zone in its row ([[R1045000]]); one that has left the field by then is not taken ([[R174]]), and the steal outlives CASPER ([[R76]]). Its Radiant face then draws one random Unit card of the opponent's hand, watched there with the same mark ([[R1140]]): at the start of its controller's next turn that card, if still in that hand, is taken into its controller's hand as theirs ([[R12]]), a full hand burning it ([[§2.4]]); played, discarded or otherwise gone from that hand meanwhile, it is not taken. Its owner sees which card is marked, the caster only how many ([[R1141]]). With nothing to pick on a side, nothing happens there, and the field's pick is drawn first.
