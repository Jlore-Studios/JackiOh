The designer asked for Hearthstone's Freeze: a Unit loses its next attack or change of battle position, and unfreezes at the end of the turn in which it could have done the action, so a Unit frozen on the opponent's turn can do nothing on its controller's turn and thaws at its end, and a Unit frozen on its controller's turn after it attacked does not thaw at the end of that turn. The spec had no freeze, so this follows Hearthstone where the designer's words stop. No shipped card freezes yet; M+ #24 Bob, Wisconsin's Strongest Soldier is the first (#1185).

**A status, not a keyword.** Frozen ([[§6.1]]) is `CardInstance.frozen`, the turn it thaws by, absent on every card nothing froze, so a game no card freezes in serializes, hashes and replays exactly as before. Freeze ([[§6.3]]) sets it: `freeze { target }` on one Unit and `freezeAll` over a board scope (`effects/statuses.rs`), as Berserk is a status `goBerserk` sets. It is no keyword kind, so [[R21]]'s random keyword pool, a Buff, a Nerf and Immutable never touch it.

**What it stops.** While a Unit is Frozen its controller can neither declare an attack with it nor switch its position: `combat::has_exertion_in` answers false for both halves of its exertion ([[§4.1]]), so `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal ("that Unit is Frozen", the state unchanged), the view's `canAct` and the AI's lethal solver agree. An effect's switch ([[R20]]) and a forced attack ([[R53]]) spend no exertion and still happen, as Hearthstone's forced attacks ignore Freeze. A Frozen Unit in Defense Position stays there with its Taunt and Armor and still strikes back; one in Attack Position can still be attacked.

**When it thaws.** The thaw is stamped as the freeze lands (`freeze::thaw_turn`), never worked out later:

- A Unit frozen during its controller's turn, in the Start or Main phase, while it still has an exertion to spend (an attack, a switch, Deft's other half, [[R49]], Windfury's second attack, [[R636]], or a summoning-sick Unit's switch to Defense, [[§4.1]]) has lost that opportunity, and thaws at that turn's cleanup.
- Any other (frozen on the opponent's turn, after it spent its exertion, or during its controller's End phase, by an end-of-turn trigger, a Trap or a delayed effect) stays Frozen through its controller's next turn and thaws at that turn's cleanup.
- The stamp is a turn number read only at its controller's cleanups: a Unit thaws at the cleanup of its controller's first turn numbered its stamp or later (`freeze::thaw_at_cleanup`, beside `modifiers::expire_modifiers` in [[§2.2]]'s cleanup), so an extra turn of either player moves nothing.
- A Freeze on a Frozen Unit keeps the later of the two thaws.
- A Unit that can never act (Can't attack and Cannot be in Defense Position) thaws on the same schedule: the opportunity is the exertion, not the legality of an action.
- A freeze during an attack already declared (its trap window, its damage) does not stop it: the attack spent its exertion, so the Unit stays Frozen through its next turn.

**What carries it and what removes it.** A change of control ([[R171]]) keeps the freeze, stamped again for the new controller as if it landed then (its exertion is fresh, so frozen in its new controller's main phase it thaws at that turn's cleanup). Rollback keeps a Unit's freeze where it keeps its exertion. It comes off at its thaw, when the Unit leaves the field ([[R78]]'s reset: a Bounce, a death, an exile; a Transform makes a new instance) and with a Vanilla, as Hearthstone's Silence removes Freeze. An Animated card that goes back to its backrow keeps it, as it keeps the rest of its state. Immune to Spells stops a Spell's Freeze ([[R81]]); Units, Traps and Field Spells still reach it. Heroes are never Frozen (they never attack), so "Freeze all enemies" freezes the enemy Units.

**Seen by both players.** R437's reusable mark says it in both views: `marked` "frozen", colour key "cyan", with `added: true` as the freeze lands and `added: false` as it thaws or a Vanilla takes it off, so the client's brand flourish, its sound cue and its log line need no new event. `UnitView.frozen` is on the Unit for both seats, the face draws a frost Frozen badge, and the controller's `attackReason` says "that Unit is Frozen" in its main phase, though `canAct` is false ([[R849000]] gave the reason only while `canAct`). The AI reads it through `legalActions`, and its evaluation leaves a Unit still Frozen through its controller's next turn out of the swing it counts (`can_swing`, `face_threat_with`), and its search signature tells two positions apart that differ only in a freeze.

**The card kit** ([[§12.3]]) gains `c:freeze(target)`: the Units its words name are Frozen, a hero among them passed by.
