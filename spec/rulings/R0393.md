C #28's Cry exiles its controller's deck and then discards their hand, and the discards land in the graveyard before its Aura begins, so the discarded hand is playable from there — the reading that gives the card its name. While it stands its controller may play cards of every type from their graveyard ([[§6.3]] Play), and on the base face their cards that would go to their graveyard are exiled instead (a replacement, [[§6.2]]); the Radiant face drops the replacement and allows only a play that would pay at least (1), which stops a (0) loop. With no deck left every draw is fatigue ([[§2.4]], [[R3]])
