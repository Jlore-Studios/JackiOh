Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A GIGA unit in Defense Position is drawn turned a quarter, as
every DEF card is, across exactly the four zones it covers in Attack Position ([[R3546004]]) and as
large: its face is laid out on the footprint's box with its sides swapped (two rows and the gap
wide, two lanes and the gap tall), centred on the footprint, so the turn lands it on the footprint
whole, with no shrink. The position switch turns it within the footprint, stretched as it turns,
from one resting box onto the other, so the box the new view rests on lies where the turn ends. Its
attack and health gems keep the face's own size in every layout, round in either position, where a
layout's one-tile gem size would squeeze them. Rejected: turning the footprint itself (stood on end,
it covered both heroes and left the board), and shrinking it to fit turned (a narrow card with half
its text size inside four zones of room).
