The owner ruled (#2533, 2026-10-30): "AI should also have only Training-Grounds {non mythic or
legendary} cards". This replaces [[R2433000]]'s Core-only bar. A handicap ([[R180]]) takes the
optional field `tutorialCardsOnly` in place of `coreOnly`, and `AI_TUTORIAL` sets it ([[R290]]). No
other tier sets it. Like `heroFloor` and `poolHold` ([[R2201001]]), it is serialized and stored only
when true, so no other game's state, hash, replay or golden trace moves.

**The tutorial's cards.** A card the seat may play is one of `TUTORIAL_CARD_SET` (Training Grounds)
whose rarity is none of `TUTORIAL_BARRED_RARITIES` (Legendary, Mythic), both named in
`crates/engine/src/config.rs`, read from the card's definition (`state::is_tutorial_card`). A token
is judged by its printed rarity: TG #39.1 Training-Grounds Golem prints Legendary and is barred; a
token that prints none is not. So TG #37 Seance, #38 Tutorial Master, #39 Call to Chaos
(Training-Grounds Edition) and #40 Goetzmann Game Design stay the player's alone, as the owner asked
in #2429. The Legacy cards #41–#60 are Training Grounds cards, none Legendary or Mythic, so the seat
may play them. A Core card, and every other set's (a fused or custom card among them), is barred.

Everything else [[R2433000]] says holds, with this bar in place of the Core-only one:

- **The play.** `legal_actions` lists no play of a barred card, from the hand or a graveyard, and
  `reduce` refuses one ([[§10.5]] step 1), a Trap or Field Spell set face-down included. The AI, the
  random policy and the timeout's move ([[R186]]) choose among the legal moves.
- **However it reached the hand**: dealt, redrawn by a mulligan, drawn, Discovered, added by a random
  pool, or returned from the field.
- **Cast on draw** ([[§2.4]], [[R58]]): a barred card the seat draws is drawn, not cast, and setup
  casts none it dealt ([[R459]]).
- **An effect that takes a card out of that hand into play** casts none, and a summon out of the hand
  ([[R1083]]) picks only among the Units the seat may play.
- **What it leaves alone**: a card on the seat's field by other means, a card an effect casts that is
  not in its hand, and the other seat.

`coreOnly` is no longer a field. A handicap stored with it (a lesson saved under #2433) reads with no
bar, so a game played under the Core-only rule, whose opponent held only Core cards, does not fold
under a rule that bars them. A lesson is a practice game the server never records ([[R187]]), so nothing it keeps
holds the field. [[R1012003]] reads the tutorial opponent by its resources, the bar left out.

Hearthstone semantics: a scripted encounter's opponent plays the cards its script allows, and what
the rules put in its hand otherwise waits there.

Rejected:

- Keeping the name `coreOnly`, which would say the opposite of the rule.
- Reading `coreOnly` as the new bar (a serde alias), which would refuse a saved lesson's Core plays.
- A list of allowed card ids, which would need updating with every Training Grounds card.
