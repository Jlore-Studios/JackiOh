**Reference.** The strongest of the five strengths ([[R1454]]'s `BOT_STRENGTHS`, the last) is the reference bot. Every other strength is measured as an Elo offset from it, and the reference itself is measured against people.

**Offsets.** `jackioh-server bot-calibrate` plays every pair of strengths at least `BOT_CALIBRATION_MIN_GAMES` (30) games, `BOT_CALIBRATION_GAMES_PER_PAIR` (100) unless told otherwise, with All Random decks ([[R258]]) and the seats alternating. Each seat runs `decide_styled` at its strength on its node budget, with no wall clock, no concession and no idle turn, and each game is a function of its seed, so the result does not depend on how many threads played. A game that reaches no result, or in which one turn takes more than `BOT_CALIBRATION_MAX_TURN_ACTIONS` (30) actions (a loop, with no acting clock to end the turn), is left out and counted as unfinished. A Bradley-Terry fit with one drawn game added to every pair turns the tallies into each strength's offset from the reference, and the added game keeps a sweep finite.

**Anchor.** The reference's rating is its performance rating against its opponents' ratings before each game, over at least 30 rated games against people (`jackioh-server bot-calibrate-live`, which needs `LADDER_BOTS` on in production for that long). Until that read is run, the seed of record stands as the anchor: 1200, the reference's seed at the first calibration.

**Seeds.** A strength's seed is the anchor plus its offset, rounded, and a person pastes the printed rows into `BOT_SEED_RATINGS` and `BOT_CALIBRATION_GENERATION` in `crates/server/src/config.rs`. They seed new bots only: a bot already in the pool keeps the rating its games have earned ([[R610]]), so live play goes on correcting them between calibrations.

**After an AI promotion** ([[R732001]]). The anchor moves by the Elo of each later promotion's `vsParent`, taken from `training/history/*.jsonl` for the generations after `BOT_CALIBRATION_GENERATION`, and the offsets are measured again on the new AI. Running `bot-calibrate` on the new `main` does both and prints the rows. The training lanes never edit `crates/server`, so a person or the night bot makes that pull request.

**The ceiling gate.** [[R1453]]'s rule stands as the gate: a player rated above every active bot in the pool is never given one. With a fresh pool the ceiling is the highest seed, and a player rated exactly at it is given that bot.

Rejected: measuring each offset against the reference alone, since a strength that loses every game to it would have its offset set by the added game alone, where the full round robin ties it to its neighbours; shifting the seeds automatically at build time, or a test that fails once the AI is newer than `BOT_CALIBRATION_GENERATION`, since the lanes cannot touch the server, so the first would move seeds nobody had read and the second would block every promotion; re-seeding the bots already in the pool, which would throw away ratings their games earned.
