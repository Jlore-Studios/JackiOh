**Reference.** The strongest of the five strengths ([[R1454]]'s `BOT_STRENGTHS`, the last) is the reference bot. Every other strength is measured as an Elo offset from it, and the reference itself is measured against people.

**Offsets.** `jackioh-server bot-calibrate` plays every pair of strengths at least `BOT_CALIBRATION_MIN_GAMES` (30) games, `BOT_CALIBRATION_GAMES_PER_PAIR` (100) unless told otherwise, with All Random decks ([[R258]]), dealt with no strength as every deal the AI makes for itself is ([[R750000]]), and the seats alternating. Each seat plays its strength as a ladder bot's seat does ([[R747000]]): the skill's roll first on the seat's own stream, then `decide_styled` on its node budget when the AI has the decision and the random policy's move when it does not, with no wall clock, no concession and no idle turn, and each game is a function of its seed, so the result does not depend on how many threads played. A game that reaches no result, or in which one turn takes more than `BOT_CALIBRATION_MAX_TURN_ACTIONS` (30) actions (a loop, with no acting clock to end the turn), is left out and counted as unfinished. A Bradley-Terry fit with one drawn game added to every pair turns the tallies into each strength's offset from the reference, and the added game keeps a sweep finite.

**Anchor.** The reference's rating is its performance rating against its opponents' ratings before each game, over at least 30 rated games against people (`jackioh-server bot-calibrate-live`, which needs `LADDER_BOTS` on in production for that long). Until that read is run, the seed of record stands as the anchor: `SKILL_ELO_ANCHOR` (1200, [[R747000]]), the strongest bot's old seed.

**Seeds.** A strength's seed is the skill curve's reading at its skill ([[R747000]]), and a test holds `BOT_STRENGTHS`' seeds to the curve. The calibration is the measurement of the strengths as built (their budgets, temperatures and missed-lethal chances with their skills), which the curve, measured at the arena's budget, is not: it prints each strength's seed as the anchor plus its offset, and a person compares those with the curve's. Where the calibration should win, the printed `BOT_SEED_RATINGS` row and `BOT_CALIBRATION_GENERATION` go into `crates/server/src/config.rs` together with the same readings over the curve's rows (`SKILL_ELO_TABLE`), since the test fails until they agree. At the first calibration, generation 9, the seeds stayed at the curve: the calibration put the two lowest strengths 66 and 82 Elo above it and the others within 34, and moving the curve's rows is a person's decision (`docs/bot-calibration.md`, Results). Seeds are for new bots only: a bot already in the pool keeps the rating its games have earned ([[R610]]), so live play goes on correcting them between calibrations.

**After an AI promotion** ([[R732001]]). The anchor moves by the Elo of each later promotion's `vsParent`, taken from `training/history/*.jsonl` for the generations after `BOT_CALIBRATION_GENERATION`, and the offsets are measured again on the new AI. Running `bot-calibrate` on the new `main` does both and prints the rows. The training lanes never edit `crates/server`, so a person or the night bot makes that pull request.

**The ceiling gate.** [[R1453]]'s rule stands as the gate: a player rated above every active bot in the pool is never given one. With a fresh pool the ceiling is the highest seed, and a player rated exactly at it is given that bot.

Rejected: measuring each offset against the reference alone, since a strength that loses every game to it would have its offset set by the added game alone, where the full round robin ties it to its neighbours; shifting the seeds automatically at build time, or a test that fails once the AI is newer than `BOT_CALIBRATION_GENERATION`, since the lanes cannot touch the server, so the first would move seeds nobody had read and the second would block every promotion; re-seeding the bots already in the pool, which would throw away ratings their games earned; pasting the calibration's rows over the curve's in the change that adds the calibration, since the curve is [[R747000]]'s measurement, held by its own test, and the two differ by amounts a longer run should settle first.
