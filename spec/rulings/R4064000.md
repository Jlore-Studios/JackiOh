[[§4.4]] step 3b ([[R3550004]]) has each card with a damage pool, S+ #42 Force Projector and S+ #123 Oct among them, soak what it has left of every hit on its controller's Units and hero, after Armor and the caps, never on an Indestructible Unit, refilling at the start of its controller's turn. [[R4032000]] brought My Pawn and the combat forecast into step with it, but the AI's quick arithmetic ([[§9.9]]) still counted every point. On the issue's board, the AI at 6 life behind its unspent Force Projector, facing a 4/4 and a 3/3 ready to swing, read 7 coming and lethal, and walled up, where 2 lands.

No rule of the game covers what the AI reads, so its reads follow the engine. With the flag `pool_and_conversion_read`, every static read of a hit soaks it through the target side's pools after Armor and the caps, as `project_hit` does, and spends what they soak. Each side has one pool, the sum of what its pools have left (`query::damage_pool_of`). The pool is spent once across the hits a read counts, in the order the read counts them:

- the Taunt walk under `damage_past_taunts_with` spends the defender's pool in its own order ([[R3937000]]): each hit on a Taunt as it is counted, then its Trample excess on the hero and its Cleave splashes, as the combat lands them (step 10), then the free attackers on the hero, smallest first;
- one attack, as the reply's `fight` and `trade_value` read it, spends the target side's pool on its blow, then on each Cleave splash; its strike back meets the attacker's side's pool.

What a pool has left depends on whose turn comes before the hits land:

- attacks this turn (the lethal solver's `ready_gap`, and the face threat of the player on turn while its attacks are still to come) meet each side's pools as they stand now;
- the face threat of the player not on turn meets the defender's pools as they stand now, since they refill only at the defender's own turn start, and the attacker's refilled;
- the face threat of the player on turn once its attacks are made meets both sides' pools refilled, since both turns start before those attacks.

A Burn or Toxic tick (`coming_hero_ticks`, `afflicted`, [[R3822000]]) meets its side's pools refilled, since the turn start refills them before it ticks ([[R3540001]]), each tick afresh. Each unit's ticks are read alone. A First Strike Taunt's strike back in the walk meets the attacker's whole pool.

The flag is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick keeps the old reads. The move order reads `AI_EVAL`'s flag, as it does for [[R3793000]]. The learned features' `face_threat` shifts with the walk, without a refit.

Rejected:

- calling `damage::project_hit` for each hit: it reads the pools only as they stand now, never refilled, and the walk's Taunts carry health and shields the state does not have;
- a fresh pool for each hit, which would soak 5 of each of the issue's two swings and read 0.

Not modelled:

- another tick spending the pools first (the engine ticks the hero, then each unit, against one pool);
- a Trample excess soaking pool before a Cleave splash in `trade_value`, which projects the excess apart;
- a strike back on a unit that stands down, which is read at what the pools have left now.

It reads only public state (`acting_texts_of` leaves out a face-down card's text), and it adds no rule of the game and no constant.
