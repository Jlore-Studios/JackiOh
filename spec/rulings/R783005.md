## R783005 — A Cloaked card's price is its own

Built (#783). A card set by its Cloaked is priced as the card it is in hand ([[R65]],
`alt_play::priced_type_of`), so no price rule that names Traps touches it: Classic #6 Cloaked Toe
Cracker's "Your Traps cost (0)" (the designer's answer, "Toe Cracker: no"), and with it every other
rule that prices Traps, Classic #2's next-Trap discount included, which a Cloaked Unit or Spell neither
uses nor spends (a Cloaked Field Spell still meets Classic #2, which names Field Spells too). This is
the open question's reading (#783, "every price rule that names Traps, not only Toe Cracker"); the
designer may narrow it. It still counts as a Trap play for what counts or answers plays: play counts by
type, Combo and "after you play a Trap" (a Radiant Cloaked Toe Cracker gains its mana as another Toe
Cracker is set). Since patch v0.3.7 a Spell Paranoia lets you set is a Cloaked set too, priced as
itself ([[R1044]]), and Radiant Paranoia's Echo is read as it reveals ([[R1045]]).
