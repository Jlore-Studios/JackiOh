Presentation and the response's shape only. No figure is computed differently, and the adjusted rates and tiers `GET /api/stats/cards` answers are the same for the same records.

**Why.** Four figures read a card: its tier ([[R3022000]]), its adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]), its raw win rate and its play rate, with its games as the sample behind them. Since [[R3184000]] cleared `/stats`' Cards tab of the rest, the owner asked in #3185 that nothing else show them and that the server stop sending what nothing reads.

**The card's Statistics panel.** The panel in the card detail view (`CardStatsBlock`, opened from the collection or the Almanac) shows Games, Tier, Adjusted win rate, Win rate and Play rate, in that order. The tier is `/stats`' chip (`stats/TierChip.tsx`), and the adjusted rate is to a tenth of a point, as `/stats` prints them. When drawn and Drawn vs not drawn are gone, and "not enough data" with them. Below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games, Win rate still reads "not enough games" ([[R654]]), and Tier and Adjusted win rate read "—", as on `/stats`. A card with no tier or no adjusted rate, as from an older server, also reads "—" there. Your record and View full stats are unchanged.

**No other place.** No other client screen shows a card's public figures. The deck editor's and the Almanac's Win % sort ([[R377]]) reads each card's win rate and whether it has enough games, and shows neither. The `/stats` drill-down, the Sets tab and the Created and Tokens panels are out of scope; they read their own figures.

**What the server stops sending.** Each card's row in `GET /api/stats/cards` no longer carries:
- `drawnGames` and `drawnWinRate`;
- `playedGames`, `playedWinRate` and `playedDelta`;
- `playedRadiantGames`, `playedRadiantWinRate`, `playedNormalGames` and `playedNormalWinRate` ([[R1147000]]);
- `drawnNotPlayedGames` and `drawnNotPlayedWinRate`;
- `notDrawnGames`, `notDrawnWinRate` and `drawnDelta` ([[R2427000]]).

It keeps `id`, `name`, `cost`, `rarity`, `set`, `games`, `winRate`, `winRateLow`, `winRateHigh`, `adjustedWinRate`, `adjustedLow`, `adjustedHigh`, `tier`, `playRate`, `hasEnoughGames` and `window`. Every field it dropped was searched for first: no client reads one, nor the bots' set report (`bot/go/internal/cardsets`, which reads `id`, `name`, `set`, `games` and `winRate`). The engine still keeps the tallies, and the server still reads the adjusted rate, its range and the tier off them (`adjusted_win_rate`, `adjusted_interval`, `tier_of`). `cargo jackioh stats report`, `jackioh-server stats-cards`, balance-watch and the analysis loader have their own figures and still print every breakdown.

**Rejected.** Keeping the fields for an older client. A page open across the deploy is the only one that reads them, and only until it reloads. It shows the old panel's When drawn as "NaN%" meanwhile, which a reload clears. No Hearthstone rule is involved.

Amended by [[R3447000]]: the panel also says, beside its title, the patch its figures were collected on.
