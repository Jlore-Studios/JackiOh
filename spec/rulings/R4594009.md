The pool is the recursion log's Units (`units_played_by`, distinct definitions in first-play order, [[R4589000]]): every resolved face-up Unit play as `record_play` saw it — casts count ([[R70]]); countered plays never get here ([[R448]]); a Cloaked set is a Trap, not a Unit ([[R451]]). The copies are fresh on their printed face, the Radiant ones Radiant. With `fill`, one unique pick per open unit zone, so the copies are distinct, uniform and balanced; otherwise one pick, summoned `copies` times — the Harvester's Radiant face is one pick twice. With nothing played or nowhere to go it draws nothing ([[R129]]).
