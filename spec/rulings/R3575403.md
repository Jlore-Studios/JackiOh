An Equip card played onto one of your Units fuses into it ([[R1086]]), and the host, the kept instance, takes the Equip's Activate and its triggers. The fusion is a fresh definition with an id naming its ingredients ([[R77]], [[R179]]), which no decision's root ever saw, so the host matched no [[R2564000]] activation price and no [[R2477000]] turn-hook price, and a line that equipped a card read the host as it was before: Stinkyscape #52 Cannon v3 and Stinkyscape+ #9 Drone Assembler v4 lost their Activates the moment the AI equipped them, and Stinkyscape+ #15 Hull Repairer mk2 its end-of-turn heal. Since [[R3575400]] prices a hand card's Activate before it is played, the play that reads it must be the play the card is made with. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** A card whose own definition has no price is read through the ingredients its fused id names (`fused_id_specs`: each ingredient's definition and the face it went in on, [[R469]]), which is the whole of what a fusion runs. For its Activate the fusion takes the best of its ingredients' prices under the same controller, as the probe prices one use of a card with several abilities; for its turn hooks it takes the sum of its ingredients' prices, since every one of their texts fires. Each is still gated on the fusion actually having what the price is for (`abilities_of`, `has_turn_hook`), so a Fuse that dropped a text pays nothing for it, and a card whose own definition the root priced reads that price and never its ingredients'. A card no Fuse minted names no ingredients and is unchanged.

**What is not priced.** An ingredient priced for the other controller. A fused id whose digest ([[R468]]) names a definition this state does not hold. A keyword the Fuse did not pass on ([[R1852001]]: an Equip leaves its host no Starship Piece), which `leaves_behind` reads off the card as it now stands and not off this list.

**Rejected.** Probing each fusion a line can make, which is a combinatorial number of probes at a decision's root; and summing the ingredients' Activate prices, which would pay for several uses where the rules allow one.
