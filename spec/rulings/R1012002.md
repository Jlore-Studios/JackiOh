With the switch on (`AI_EVAL_KIND = Learned`, [[R1010000]]), the finalists after the opponent's reply always take the network's correction at `LEARNED_CORRECTION_SHARE` ([[R1012001]]). `LEARNED_REACH` (`crates/ai/src/config.rs`) says where else the search scores a state that way (`learned::line_score`, `learned::open_score`); each step takes in the one before it:

- **`Finalists`**: nowhere else (`docs/learned-eval.md` D6's option B).
- **`Lines`**: also every complete line, wherever the search stops it (`static_score`, `terminal_score`). That covers a line that ended its turn or the game, a line still open at the beam's last depth or when the node budget runs out (scored where it stands), and a line `score_line` stops at a refused `endTurn`. A passed turn still pays for its unspent crystals. So the network also chooses which lines become the finalists.
- **`Beam`**: also every open line the beam ranks at each depth, its root and a child at the seat's own prompt included (D6's option A in the beam).

Where the reach stops short, the hand eval scores, so the beam's pruning stays the hand eval's below `Beam`. The best auto-answer (`best_auto_answer`) keeps the hand eval at every reach. So do a finished game and a Duos duel (D9), as they do for a finalist.

Measured with the learn lane's 100,000-game network (#466, 2026-10-18), at half the correction and 3 finalists, 1,600 paired games each against the hand eval on `explore-466`: `Finalists` +0.073 [+0.030, +0.115] edge, `Lines` +0.102 [+0.059, +0.145], `Beam` +0.054 [+0.011, +0.097]. At `Lines`, three quarters of the correction won +0.041 and 5 finalists +0.104. On 800 deals none of them was chosen on (`confirm-466`), `Lines` won +0.076 [+0.035, +0.116] and `Finalists` +0.071 [+0.030, +0.112]: a tie, so most of `Lines`' lead on `explore-466` came from its being chosen there. The beam's widths were tuned on the hand eval's scale, and ranking open lines on the network prunes them differently. Each game's p95 decision time stayed about 220 ms.

`LEARNED_REACH` is `Finalists`. With it, the beam and the shortlist that is replied to keep the hand eval, so the network can only choose among lines the hand eval found. A network's errors on positions unlike the games it was fitted on reach no further than that choice. The AI's puzzles are such positions: bare boards, empty libraries, turn 9 with 3 mana. With the switch on at `Lines`, a network retrained on all of run 5's games missed two of them. In P13 it ended its turn holding Hit Job. The reply step scores every Hit Job line about −1,000,000, a lost game's score, and the pass line −74. The learned scores ranked the pass line into the shortlist that is replied to, and the hand eval's ranking never reaches it. In the burn puzzle it kept two free Burns. At `Finalists` the same network passes every AI test.
