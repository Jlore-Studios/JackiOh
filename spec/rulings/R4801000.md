[[R3498000]] held the host's wrap-up off the beam until its slice of the node counter had drawn `root_branching` and one more nodes, so that a wrap-up that fired before the beam began would still leave it lines. That floor counted nodes, not moves. A surcharged step takes its extra nodes one at a time (`surcharge_nodes`): `AI_SEARCH.expensive_def_nodes` (100) for a play of #97 Zephrys, `ai_turn_trap_nodes` (40) for a lethal-size attack while a My Pawn lies in the enemy's backrow, `judged_card_nodes` (3) per card in hand for a play Pareto Optimality judges; a step's auto-answers and a child waiting on the seat's own prompt draw more. So a first root move dearer than the floor was refused inside its own step, the beam read the refusal as its stop with no child yet, and the decision fell back to End turn: the AI holding Zephrys alone at 3 mana played `Fallback endTurn` after 22 nodes, where with no clock it plays Zephrys. And when the root's moves together cost more than the floor, its End turn, last in the list, was never tried. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the AI's use of time changes no game.

**The rule.** The beam's slice polls the wrap-up only once the beam has tried every move at its root: `beam_search` calls `NodeCounter::end_root` as its first depth ends, and from there the slice refuses as R3498000 says, the beam explores no further and its open lines end their turn. The hard stop (`should_stop`) is polled before every node, the root's included, and still stops everything. With no clock, or a wrap-up that never fires, every take of the counter is the one it was, node for node, and every decision is the one it was.

**Rejected.** Never refusing inside one step's surcharge: an auto-answer or an own-prompt score in the first step would still be refused with no child, and the root's End turn would still go untried. Keeping the node floor beside the root's: past a cheap root the beam would explore on after the host asked it to stop. Counting `root_branching` and one more root moves in the counter: only the beam knows how long its root's list is (its kept stand-downs, its walls and its End turn ride past `root_branching`), so it says when the list is through.

**Cost.** Under a fired wrap-up the root costs what it costs with no clock, a surcharged play included; that is bounded by the beam's slice and by the hard stop, which R3498000 keeps as the cap on the whole decision.
