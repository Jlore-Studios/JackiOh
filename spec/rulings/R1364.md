Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A hit that does far more than the health its target had left is overkill, and a wet crunch (`overkill`) lands just after its impact (`OVERKILL_DELAY_MS`). Far more is at least `OVERKILL_MIN_EXCESS` (3) beyond that health, and at least that health beyond it: a 1-health unit taking 4, a 2 taking 5, a 5 taking 10, a hero at 3 taking 7; a hit that only kills is not. The health is the target's as the view the entry was planned against shows it, a unit's on the field or a hero's, both public on both seats ([[R203]]); a target that view does not show, or one already at 0, is never overkilled. The crunch is panned with the hit ([[R669]]).
