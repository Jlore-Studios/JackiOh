Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no state, event, replay or AI decision changes, only what `viewFor` carries. A unit that is summoning sick looked like any other unit, and a click on it did nothing. The view now carries two optional fields on a `UnitView`.

`sick: true` is on a unit of either seat while it is summoning sick ([[R83]], and [[R171]]'s change of control) with neither Rush nor Charge ([[§6.1]]), the condition `combat::is_asleep` names and [[§4.2]] step 1 refuses on. This matches Hearthstone's zzz, which a minion wears on the turn it is summoned unless it has Charge or Rush, so a Rush unit wears none. `sick` is absent otherwise, and it reads only public facts ([[§9.1]]): the turn the unit entered and its keywords as the view already lists them. `canAct` cannot say it, because it is false for every unit of the inactive player and a sick unit may still switch ([[§4.1]]).

`attackReason` is on the viewer's own unit that may act now (`canAct`), and it is the refusal [[§4.2]] step 1 gives that unit, in the engine's words (`combat::why_cannot_declare`), as an ability's `reason` is ([[R384]]). It is absent when the unit may declare an attack, even one that has no target, and it is never on the opponent's units. Which attacks are offered stays `legalActions`' answer.

The web draws the zzz where `sick` is set, with a still variant under Reduce motion, and shows `attackReason` in the unit's tooltip, in the inspect notes and as a notice when the player clicks that unit and `legal` offers it no attack. It works neither field out.

Since [[R1174000]] a Frozen unit, which has no exertion and so never `canAct`, carries `attackReason` ("that Unit is Frozen") in its controller's main phase all the same.

Rejected: a required `sick: false` on every other unit, since the view's other flags (`berserk`, `vanilla`, `created`) are absent unless set, and an absent key leaves a view with no sleeping unit byte for byte as it was. Rejected: a reason per target, since `legalActions` already decides each target; the unit-level refusal is the one `why_cannot_declare` gives, which is what a player who clicks a unit that cannot attack needs to read.
