Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). [[R2801000]]'s launch held a 3-2-1 countdown and an ignition over the ship's tile; the owner wants it "very big and epic".

**The cinematic.** A launch held as the `starship` set piece ([[R2785000]]: `STARSHIP_LAUNCH_HOLD_MS`, capped at `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS`, scaled by the speed, skipped by a tap, a click or Space) plays, at Big moments "full", a full-screen cinematic for the hold and `STARSHIP_CINEMATIC.tailMs` past it (`planStarship`):

- the board dims and the ship's own picture ([[R3333000]], launched under the name `starshipLaunched` gives) fills the screen, drawn `STARSHIP_TUNING`'s `zoom` close;
- the camera moves along the hull from stern to bow, from `panFrom` to `panTo` of the hold, past a stop at each mounted module timed by where it stands;
- its engines light one after another from `enginesFrom` to `enginesTo` (at most `enginesMax`), each throwing `STARSHIP_TUNING`'s `sparks` and shaking the board harder, the last as hard as the climb;
- its name stamps across the hull at `stampAt`, in its design's lettering (`STARSHIP_LETTERING`: a font, a weight, a tracking and a slant for each of the seven designs) in its livery's window colour edged in its hull's;
- at `climbAt` the ship climbs out of the top of the frame; as the hold ends it comes back to its tile, where [[R2801000]]'s lift-off, contrail, badge embers and landing still play.

A bigger ship pans closer and throws more sparks (`STARSHIP_TUNING`, small, medium and large). On a phone (narrower than `FX_MOBILE_WIDTH`) every particle of the Starship's moments is `phoneShare` as many, with the same beats, inside `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` ([[R2803000]]). The countdown is gone (`countsMs` is empty).

**The title card.** Big moments "short" and "off", an unheld launch and Reduce Motion show a still title card instead: the ship's picture centred with its name under it, nothing moving. Under Reduce Motion it stands for `FX_STARSHIP_STILL_MS` and holds nothing (`planStarshipStill`), as a Mythic's still card does ([[R2722001]]); otherwise it stands through the entry.

**The sound.** The launch's bed (`STARSHIP_LAUNCH_BED`) is the Starship's own: `starshipIgnite` as its engines light (`enginesFrom`), `starshipRoar` as it climbs (`climbAt`) and, after the hold, [[R2801000]]'s heavy `summon` at the landing; the countdown's beeps are gone.

**The War Doctrines.** A War Doctrine's `cardPlayed` (S #137.1 to #137.4, which S #137 Starfleet Commander and S #176 Preemptive Strike cast after their launch) is announced over its caster's launched Starship, the latest launched, else the board's middle: its name, as the card prints it, on an insignia in its own look for Land, Sea, Air or Space (`WAR_DOCTRINE_LOOKS`), with a burst, a ring and a shake at `STARSHIP_DOCTRINE.stampAt`, outlasting its row by `STARSHIP_DOCTRINE.tailMs`. Its row plays as ever. The launch is a batch before it, so it plays first.

**Hidden information.** The name a Starship launched under is public ([[R1852004]]); this amends [[R2801000]]'s "no cue names the ship's name". A War Doctrine is keyed on a readable `defId`, so a hidden play announces nothing, and a Starship R97 hides ([[R97]]) plans nothing ([[R202]]). Every cue starts inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of it ([[R200]]), scaled with the speed ([[R201]]).

This amends [[R2801000]]: the hold is the cinematic, not a countdown; its still frame is the title card; its bed ignites and roars rather than beeping and burning; and a cue now shows the ship's public name.
