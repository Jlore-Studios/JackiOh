A decision on a state wider than `AI_SEARCH.fat_reference` (30) scales its budget down ([[R2575000]]), floored so the widest states still search: at least `fat_min_nodes` (150), the lethal solver's share at least `fat_min_lethal` (60). `scaled_budget` applied the solver's floor on every state, so a budget that gave the solver fewer than 60 nodes saw it spend 60 on a board of ordinary width: 60 of a 150-node budget that gave it 40, and all of a 30-node budget, whose decision then fell back to End turn with no line searched. On a fat state either floor could raise a figure past the caller's own. A field at `usize::MAX` (a caller's "no limit") overflowed the beam's reserve and the replay fill's ceiling. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the AI's use of nodes changes no game.

**The rule.** On a state at or under `fat_reference`'s width a decision spends the caller's own budget, the lethal solver at most `lethal_nodes` and never more than `nodes`. On a fat state the floors hold the scaled figures up, but never above the caller's own `nodes` and `lethal_nodes`, so a solver the caller gave nothing still runs on nothing. The scaling, the beam's reserve and the replay fill's ceiling saturate rather than overflow. Every shipped budget that searches (`AI_BUDGET`, `AI_GATE_BUDGET`, `BOT_STRENGTHS[1]` to `[4]`, the Duos AI team's) gives the solver at least 60 nodes and the whole at least 150, so it decides node for node as before on every state. The weakest ladder strength's budget, 150 nodes and 40 of them the solver's, runs only where a test raises its skill: at its skill of 0 every decision is the skill miss ([[R2214000]]), so its play and its seed rating ([[R748000]]) do not change.

**Rejected.** Raising the weakest strength's `lethal_nodes` to 60, the figure the floor ran it at: none of its decisions searches, and the constant would stand for a floor that no longer applies. Computing the solver's share in 128 bits, exact at sizes no budget reaches, where every other bound here saturates.
