#98 Heroic Power reads "Indestructible. Cry: Discover one of 13 powers. Each is an Activate that spends its (X)." and the thirteen lines of [[R752]], where through v0.5.11o it read "Start of game: Gain one of 13 random powers." It still costs (0), is Quickdraw and Indestructible, and each power is the same Activate of the same X as before, once a turn ([[R384]], [[R752]]–[[R761]]); the numbers are unchanged.

- **The Cry offers 3 different powers of the 13.** Discover is a choice of 3 ([[§6.3]]): the offer is drawn without replacement from the thirteen through the match rng ([[R60]]) and put to the card's controller as a Choose one, a `mode` prompt whose options are the stored names ([[R103]]), each captioned by its title on the card's face, Armor Up reading Tank Up on the Radiant face ([[R757]]). It is not a card Discover: the powers are not cards, and the prompt's kind draws its options as cards by their ids. The answer is written to `memory.power`, replacing any power the card held, and is the card's power for the game: its Activate is listed from then on, and the Radiant face discovers among its own lines. The answer must be one of the three offered.
- **The other seat reads that a prompt is open.** Its view says whose prompt it is and how many options it offers, never which ([[R311]], [[R97]], [[R2739000]]): the offer is drawn from a public list, so the card keeps its option count public.
- **A copy in a hand or a library has no power until it is played.** The start of the game, a draw, a bounce and a return to a hand roll nothing for it, so a hand view names no power and the rng is not drawn from ([[R43]], [[R78]]); the Cry asks when it is played. The play itself does not roll: the card stands on the field with no power while its prompt is open, and no Activate is listed.
- **A copy that reaches the field with no Cry rolls one.** A copy Summoned, Recruited, fused onto a card on the field, Reborn or Flickered back has no Cry to choose with, so [[R151]]'s arrival roll stays for it: the card's start-of-game clause, which the engine runs as a card arrives anywhere, rolls from the match rng when the card stands on the field (`roll_power_on_field`) and does nothing otherwise, so a copy is never dead. A copy that keeps a power (a fused card carrying its target's) keeps it.
- **No card prints "Start of game" now.** The §6.2 row stays as engine vocabulary, and the card's hook keeps its name.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, which rolls every copy in a hand or a library at the start of the game and as it arrives, and has no Cry ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: all 13 powers on offer, which is not the Discover of 3 that every other card means ([[§6.3]]); keeping the hand roll and letting the Cry override it, which shows a power in the hand that the Cry then throws away and draws the rng at every arrival.
