id: R347
title: An Indestructible unit has no Taunt
cards: "§4.1, §4.2, §6.1, §10.4, R46, #55, #56, R1084, Meditative #65"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/layers.rs
  - crates/cards/tests/cross/vanilla_and_positions.rs
---
A unit with Indestructible never has Taunt, whether printed, granted, from an aura or from Defense Position: [[§10.4]]'s keyword set drops Taunt whenever it holds Indestructible, so an Indestructible unit in Defense Position has the Armor +1 and no Taunt, and [[§4.2]] step 3 never makes it the only legal target. It is a reading, not a removal: take the Indestructible away (a Vanilla) and any Taunt the unit has is back, unless [[R46]]'s stamp still names the turn. The patch drops Indestructible from the two faces that printed both, #55 and #56 radiant. Keymaster Keenus (M #65) is the exception that proves the reading: both its faces print Taunt beside Indestructible among its 21 keywords, and neither face ever has Taunt while it is Indestructible ([[R1084]])
