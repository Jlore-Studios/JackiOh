Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes IV: "You must spend at least (1) to cast a card from your graveyard by Flashback." The price a card's play from its owner's graveyard takes, as [[R65]] works it out after every discount, every price rule and the card's own floor, is then held at `FLASHBACK_MIN_PRICE` (1) or more whenever that play is Flashback's: the card has the keyword, printed or lent by an aura (S #152 Sanctum, C #28 Second Wind, [[R1869003]]), and no field permission covers the play at that price, so the play would spend it ([[R783013]]). An embiggen card's two prices are each held there apart ([[R3138000]]: M+ #5 Spirit Lash is (1) and Embiggened (1) from the graveyard, (0) and (1) from a hand), and so is an X card's X, which [[R65]] otherwise lets no modifier touch: an X of 0 played by Flashback costs (1). `legalActions`, the payment and `viewFor` all read the one price, so the floor is offered, paid and shown alike.

The same card in a hand costs what it costs. A play a field permission covers is the permission's, spends no Flashback and takes no floor ([[R454]]; the shape the kept C #28 Second Wind's had), nor does a card that prices itself there (`graveyardPrice`, S+ #120 Crawler, [[R3560001]]). C #74 Corpse Plantation's permission is paid only in its Plague Counters and covers no mana play ([[R783013]]), so a mana play there is Flashback's and floored; the price a counter then pays is that same floored price, which means a (0) Flashback Unit, which no counter could pay for before, may be played with one counter. Hearthstone has no such floor; this follows the designer's words, which name Flashback and nothing else.

The rule is the engine's and holds for every version: a stored game that played a Flashback card from the graveyard for less than (1) no longer replays and retires as a rules change ([[R767000]]), which the patch's fragment claims.
