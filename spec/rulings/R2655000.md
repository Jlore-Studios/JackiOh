The owner asked in #2655 for two chase tiers over every full art. [[R1313000]] gave each full-art
card one full art, Epic; each now has three, one of each rarity on the cosmetics' own scale
([[R1333000]]):

| Tier | Id | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Epic | `full-art-<slug>` | The still full art of [[R1017001]], unchanged |
| Legendary | `full-art-<slug>-animated` | The art drifts inside its crop and a light sweeps over it |
| Mythic | `full-art-<slug>-signed` | All the Legendary does, and the card signs its own name across the art |

[[R2715000]] adds a fourth, the Animated art (`animated-<slug>`, Epic, no chase): the card's normal
face with its art moving, which prints no full art and is earned, picked and frozen as the Epic is.

How they move, and how they hold still, is [[R2655003]]'s; which cards have them is
[[R2655001]]'s. J-lease J-Jungle EX-plorer's slug is `j-jungle-explorer`, since its full name's ids
would pass the 40 characters a setting's text carries ([[R633]]).

**The registry.** The Legendary and the Mythic carry the chase mark, as every Legendary and Mythic
cosmetic does ([[R1333000]]); the Epic does not. The registry's check refuses a card with two full
arts **of one rarity**, where [[R1313000]] refused a card with two full arts, and a full art whose
id does not start with `full-art-`, so a pick's text names a full art and nothing else
([[R2715000]]: or `animated-`, the Animated art an Epic beside the still one, one to a card). #2655
appends 57 entries after #1833's: the six portrait cards' Legendary and Mythic, then fifteen more
cards' Epic, Legendary and Mythic (15 Epic, 21 Legendary and 21 Mythic). `public.cosmetics` is
seeded from the compiled registry as before.

**How they are earned.** None is a default or a starter, so no account is granted one, existing
accounts included ([[§14.12]], as [[R1833000]]'s 36). A Legendary or Mythic full art drops on its
own card alone, by the chase routes of [[§14.3]] and [[§14.6]]: a duplicate of that card whose
cosmetic roll lands on its tier ([[R1301002]]), a cosmetic pack slot that lands on it
([[R1306000]]), or a full Chase Shard bar, on a card the account owns ([[R1306002]]). No token pays
for one ([[R1305003]]); an Epic cosmetic token buys a card's Epic full art, as it bought the six.

**The pick.** A card's own full-art pick is `false` (no full art), `true` (its Epic full art, as
every pick before #2655 named it) or a full art's id. The device store keeps a boolean or a short
text, as it keeps a foil's id ([[R1655000]]); the account keeps it in `card_cosmetic_picks`, the
switch in `full_art` and, from migration 0072, the id in `full_art_id`, beside a switch that is on
([[R1834000]]). `PUT /api/card-picks` and the device's upload take `fullArt` as a boolean or a
`full-art-…` id (an `animated-…` one too from migration 0074, [[R2715000]]), and the server holds it to ownership as it holds every pick ([[R1834001]]): `true`
needs the card's Epic owned on the card (its still `full-art-` Epic, never its Animated art), an id one of that card's own full arts owned there; another
card's full art is the wrong kind, and an unowned one is refused, 400 with nothing written. An upload
keeps an id the build does not know, as the device kept it, and leaves out one it knows that is
another card's or not owned. The web writes `true` for the Epic, so a server from before #2655
reads it; a Legendary or Mythic pick that such a server refuses stays on the device ([[R1834000]]).

**What is worn.** A card with a pick of its own wears the full art it names where its viewer owns
that one on the card; with no pick it wears its Epic where the Full-art cards setting is on and the
Epic is owned, never a rarer tier. A pick of a tier the viewer does not own prints the card's normal
face, as an unowned foil draws its default ([[R1953001]]), never a lower tier in its place. A match
freezes the full art a seat's card wears, owned, as `true` for the Epic and the id for a Legendary
or a Mythic ([[R1834002]]), and the opponent's, a watcher's and a replay's views print that tier
from the owner's frozen loadout ([[R1834004]]). The Full-art cards setting and a tutorial lesson's
board ([[R1108000]]) turn every tier off, as they turned off the one.

**Where it is picked.** The places [[R1655000]] and its amendments pick a card's full art pick its
tier: the Collection's card view offers No full art and the card's three (and from [[R2715000]] its
Animated art first of them), the owned ones equipped and the others locked; `EquipButton` and the pack reveal's equip ([[R1955000]]) pick the tier just
granted; and the token offer on a locked full art offers a covering token for an Epic and none for a
Legendary or a Mythic ([[R1953001]], [[R1305003]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a full art, and nothing here enters
`PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Hearthstone semantics: a card comes in versions a player owns one by one (normal, golden, signature,
diamond), the rarest chased rather than bought, and the collection picks which owned version a card
plays.

Rejected: the rarest owned tier as the default of a card with no pick, which would change what every
owner of an animated full art sees without their asking, where the setting already decides the
default; a second key, `fullArtId`, beside the boolean, two values that could disagree; any slug as a
pick's id, which would take a word such as `yes` for one; and granting the six portrait cards' owners
their new tiers, against the owner's "granted to no account".
