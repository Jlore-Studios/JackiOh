Stinkyscape #18.2 BALTHASAR's "less than half its starting health" compares its controller's hero health with the health that hero started the game with: its seat's handicap `heroHealth` when it has one ([[R290]], [[R180]]), else HERO_HEALTH (30), which the engine's `starting_health_of` reads. It votes ([[R1843000]]) at its controller's start of turn when twice the health is below that, so at 14 or less from 30 and at 9 or less from the tutorial's 20; Armor is not health. Its Activate ([[R384]], once a turn) copies ([[R57]]) the last card its controller played this game as [[R451]]'s record keeps it: a cast counts ([[R70]]), a Trap or Field Trap set face-down never does, so nothing hidden is copied, and an AI generated card is passed over. The copy is that card's definition on the face it was played, a Chinese card's Chinese ([[R1300]]), added to their hand with a cost modifier of `surcharge` (1) that nothing caps, a full hand burning it ([[§2.4]]); with no play yet, nothing. The Radiant face's copy is Radiant whatever face was played. Activating is no play ([[R384]]), so it moves nothing the record reads.
