"Draw a random Rush card from your deck" picks uniformly ([[R60]]) among its owner's deck cards whose keywords, read through the layers ([[§10.4]]), hold Rush: printed on the card's face or given to it in the deck. The pick is drawn as the Death's effect resolves and is then a draw of that card ([[§6.3]] Draw). A deck with no Rush card draws nothing and fatigues nothing ([[R129]]). The Radiant face makes the drawn card Radiant once it is in the hand ([[R1061]]).
