A card's Token and Created status ([[R1177001]], [[R943]]) is rarely relevant in combat, so no face draws it at a glance: it lives in the hover preview, as Hearthstone says a card was "Created by" another only in a hovered card's tooltip.

- **Token.** No face wears a token mark: not a face under 150px (a small hand, the phone, most face-up backrow cards), not a full-art face ([[R1017001]]) and not the board minion, whose stats row holds only its numbers. A full face's type line still prints "Token Unit", "Token Spell" (衍生物 before the type on a Chinese face), so the hover preview's face says it, as do a compact face's type line where it shows and the Almanac's "Token, printed Legendary".
- **Created.** No face wears the "Created" mark, on the board, in hand or in the backrow; the state notes beside the hover preview and the inspect sheet keep "Created: made during the game".
- **Unchanged.** `token` and `created` stay in the wire and every view ([[R943]]); only what the client draws changed, so nothing plays differently. Glitch wore neither and still does not ([[R674]]).

Rejected: a smaller mark (a dot, the letter alone), which still clutters a crowded board; and dropping the type line's word, which would leave no place on the card to read it.
