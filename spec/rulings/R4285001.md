Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes IV's "Great De-Keywordification": every card that printed both Starship Piece and Equip (53 on `main` at the time: 32 Stinkyscape and 21 Stinkyscape+, the issue's list) loses Equip on both faces and keeps Starship Piece, and the two cards that gave both give Starship Piece alone: S #129 Jlockeed's Missile Pod's "Embiggened (3): Starship Piece" ([[R1878001]]'s lent keyword) and S+ #39 Mindustrial Cross-over's "Give it Starship Piece" ([[R1878003]]'s keyword given in hand). Such a piece is played into a zone of its own, and its death still fuses a copy into its controller's Dormant Starship, which reads Starship Piece alone ([[R1852000]]).

Equip from a pick ([[R1841002]]: S #79 Orca's Miners, S #82 Phalanx's and S #83 Halo's Weapons) reads no keyword: its pool is a catalog query over names or tags and its fusion is a fusion, so the de-keyworded pieces are fused in as before, and so does a lent Equip (S #16 Rod of Ages, [[R1869000]]) or one given in hand. The keyword change is catalog data, so a replay folds under the catalog it was played with and sees Equip where it was ([[R767000]]); the two scripts that gave it are kept at v0.6.7k.
