[[R4601000]] lets I21 allow an `exiled` in a Spell's run while a card laying an exile aura ([[R1850002]]) stands after the step: the aura's exile at the state check is the board's rule. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 1920, S #150 Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition) filled the board with Humans, S #115 Scornboi among them, whose aura exiled IA #235.1, immune; Scornboi was gone again by the step's end, so I21 named the Spell.

**The aura is the board's however briefly it stands.** I21 also allows the exile when a card laying an exile aura acted on the field as the step began, or one arrived in it (its `summoned`, read off its scripts). With none, a Spell's exile of an immune Unit is still a reach.
