The fuzz monitor's I21 blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, up to the next card's marker. Two lasting board rules exile at the state check, which runs inside such a run with no marker of its own: a field card's exile aura ([[R1850002]], S #115 Scornboi's) and an open S+ #77 Black Hole's swallow ([[R3554000]]). In the daily fuzz's plain seed 2614 such a rule exiled an immune C+ #4 Juhan Biggest Bat during S #102 Book of Nono-Words' run, and in seed 2260's I14 branch one exiled an immune S #116 Megabase during S #151 Call to Chaos (Scape Edition)'s.
**These are the board's rules, not the Spell.** An aura's exile and the hole's swallow are the state check's, run whatever brings a card into their reach; neither is an effect of the Spell reaching for the card ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]), as [[R4508004]] holds for the hole in its own run.
**What I21 allows.** An `exiled` of an immune Unit while an exile aura with its lasting text on stands on the field or a Black Hole is open, as the step begins or as it ends, or once a card with an exile aura has arrived on the field during the step (the handicapped seed 11304: S #150 Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition) filled its caster's board with Humans, an S #115 Scornboi among them, which exiled an immune S #116 Megabase and was gone by the step's end). With neither, an exile in a Spell's run is still a reach. The cost: a Spell's own exile while such a rule stands goes unjudged, as [[R4508004]]'s lane takers' already does.
Rejected: re-running the aura's scope or the hole's lane on the monitor's side, which repeats the engine's reading, and misses an aura or a hole that the step itself opened or closed.
