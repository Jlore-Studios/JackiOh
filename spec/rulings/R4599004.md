The fuzz monitor's I21 blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, up to the next card's marker. A field card's exile aura ([[R1850002]], S #115 Scornboi's) exiles at the state check, which runs inside such a run with no marker of its own. In seed 2260's I14 branch of the daily fuzz's plain wave such an aura exiled an immune S #116 Megabase during S #151 Call to Chaos (Scape Edition)'s run.
**The aura's exile is the board's rule, not the Spell.** It is the state check's, run whatever brings a card into its reach; it is no effect of the Spell reaching for the card ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]), as [[R4508004]] holds for the Black Hole in its own run.
**What I21 allows.** An `exiled` of an immune Unit while an exile aura with its lasting text on stands on the field, as the step begins or as it ends, or once a card with an exile aura has arrived on the field during the step (the handicapped seed 11304: S #150 Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition) filled its caster's board with Humans, an S #115 Scornboi among them, which exiled an immune S #116 Megabase and was gone by the step's end). With no exile aura, an exile in a Spell's run is still a reach. The cost: a Spell's own exile while an exile aura stands goes unjudged, as [[R4508004]]'s lane takers' already does.
**The Black Hole is not one of them.** The first build of this ruling allowed any exile while an S+ #77 Black Hole was open ([[R3554000]]). The one seed that needed it, the plain wave's 2614, was the hole's pull at the end of p2's turn after S #102 Book of Nono-Words' trigger exiled an immune C+ #4 Juhan Biggest Bat. [[R4577008]] (#4616) allows that pull exactly, the side whose turn ended and the lane next to the hole, and keeps a Spell's exile elsewhere while the hole stands a reach. So the open hole is left to it.
Rejected: re-running the aura's scope on the monitor's side, which repeats the engine's reading and misses an aura that the step itself opened or closed; and keeping the open hole among the board's rules, which let a Spell's exile two lanes from the hole through.
