Stinkyscape+ is the set that follows Training Grounds in the catalog order ([[R1420]], [[R3114000]]): the row appended to the set table after Boss-X (so the reserved Boss and Boss-X sets, which `CATALOG_SETS` does not order, keep their places), its id segment `stinkyscapeplus` (`stinkyscapeplus-021`, `stinkyscapeplus-t-ai-03`), its abbreviation `S+` in a ruling's `cards` (`S+ #64`), its shadow-ban share 1/5 ([[R3114001]]) and its deck- and trio-code offset 7000, the place of the eighth set the catalog holds in thousands ([[R1410]]). It holds 166 cards, #1 to #166 (the second card on each number the designer's list reused moved to #161 to #166), and 38 tokens: the 16 tokens its cards define, the four Terminus `#Token`s, the Warhero Token, the ten Terminus Starships and the seven AI generated cards, T-AI-1 to T-AI-7, 204 entries in all.

Issue #3535 laid the foundation: the rows, the census, the tags and keyword kinds ([[R3535002]]), the numbers and references the set's texts read ([[R3535001]]) and a stub script for each card. The set was held through the build: in the catalog, its cards scripted and tested, and in no pool, deck, patch, seed or list a player meets ([[R1420]]). Issue #3573 shipped it: the set's row says `Shipped`, so it is the newest set in `SHIPPED_SETS` ([[R1371]]), its server offset 7000 is exported with the others' so a deck code names its cards, the migration seeds its entries, the census counts them, and one fragment claims all 204 and the shipped cards that play differently. It ships gated for the AI's own decks ([[R1390]]): a player's deck holds it, the AI answers every card of it, and the AI's randomized decks deal none of it until fullsend 2's sweep (issue #3574) measures the AI on it and empties the gate.
