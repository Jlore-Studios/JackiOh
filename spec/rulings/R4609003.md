A keyword kept for good ([[R1870007]], S #154 Dice of Doom's Lucky; [[R4154202]], Classic #37 Last Hurrah's Radiant Flashback) rides the card's `tuning` beside its Nerfs and Buffs, so that neither leaving the field nor a graveyard takes it, and is reported by a `keywordGranted`. I23 holds an Immutable card's tuning to the one it began the step with ([[R4609000]]), and no ruling said whether such a keyword changes it.
**It does not.** A keyword kept for good is a grant, as any card's "gains" is, and Immutable never stopped a grant: it stops the card's text being changed or rewritten ([[R23]]), and a Buff is the one draw from [[R386]]'s menu, reported by an `upgraded`. The tuning is where the grant is kept, not what it is. So a tuning whose keywords added grew by exactly the keywords the step's `keywordGranted` names on the card, with nothing else in it changed, is no finding; any other change of it is.
