A Brittle count ([[R385]]) ticks at the start of its controller's turn, after the refresh ([[§2.2]]), on the field only ([[R638]]), from the first such start numbered t + 2 or later for a count started on turn t, and at 0 it destroys its card, which Indestructible ignores ([[R46]]). The tick comes before that turn's attacks, so a unit its tick destroys never swings. The AI's face threat (`face_threat`, [[§9.9]]) still counted it: a grown Corpse Eater (#89, Rush, Brittle 1) at 8 attack read as lethal, and that count fed the walls ([[R2976000]], `wall_switches`) and the stand-downs ([[R2021000]], `stands_down`), which put phantom-lethal lines in beam slots real lines needed. S #101 Overdrive Module's Brittle 2, once at 1, and any Unit an effect gives Brittle are read the same way.

A unit now counts no attack when all three hold (`crumbles_before_swing`):

- its count in force (`active_brittle_count`) is at most one tick, a count at 0 included, which crumbles at its tick too;
- that tick is due at its controller's next start (`brittle_due_by`, the test the engine's `brittle_tick` makes, asked of that start's number);
- it is not Indestructible.

That next start is the next turn for the player not on turn. For the player on turn it is the turn after the other player's, or the next one when it owes an extra turn, which starts as its turn ends and is numbered like any turn ([[R845]], [[R846]]).

The evaluation reads a position with one side swinging first (`NextSwing`). When that side is the player on turn, its attacks come this turn, after this turn's tick, so none of its units crumbles first. Otherwise the player on turn has made its attacks, as `face_threat` reads every position.

It applies to both sides: an enemy unit adds no threat and the seat's own adds no pressure. It reaches the threat and pressure terms, `wall_switches` and the learned features through `face_threat`, and `stands_down` through `could_attack_next_turn`. `unit_worth` keeps its Brittle share, which already discounts the unit, and `can_swing` does not change. The flag `brittle_crumbles_before_swing` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick is unchanged; `stands_down`, which takes no weights, reads `AI_EVAL`'s.

Not modelled, each of which can only leave a doomed unit counted, as before, or a crumbling Taunt read as a wall:

- an extra turn owed to the other player, which only puts the controller's next start later;
- a unit that comes back after it crumbles (S #101's Reborn) or a card a Stack uncovers;
- a defender's Taunt that crumbles first when the defender owes an extra turn.

Rejected: the test inside `can_swing`, since `unit_worth` reads it too and would discount the unit twice; reading it in `stands_down` whatever the flag says, as [[R2021000]] reads Deft ([[R49]]), since then turning the one flag off would no longer restore the old reading; a weights parameter on `stands_down` and `wall_switches`, which would change their public signatures for a flag only the search's own weights set.

It reads only public state (the count on a field unit, the turn number, the player on turn and the extra turns owed, which the view shows), and it adds no rule of the game.
