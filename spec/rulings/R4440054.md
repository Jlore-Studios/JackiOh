Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: C+ #26 Tommy Tempo's "+1 % +2 Health" becomes "+1/+1 % +2/+2": "Cry: Lose all your mana. Gain +{per}/+{per} per mana lost.", `per` 1 (Radiant 2). The mana lost is read as [[R831010]] reads it, what is left once its price is paid, all of it on a free cast on draw ([[R1]], [[R70]]), and the gain is one permanent buff of its Attack and its Health, as through v0.6.5b, where [[R3996202]] had taken the Attack away. So drawn at the start of your turn it is a 7/7 (Radiant 14/14), and played from a hand with 4 mana a 4/4 (Radiant 8/8). The script after v0.6.5b through v0.6.9n, Health alone, is kept as `script_v0_6_9n` for those replays, and the one through v0.6.5b as `script_v0_6_5b`, which plays as the card does now ([[R767000]]).
