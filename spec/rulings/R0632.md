title: A session belongs to its tab

The access token and its renewal are kept in the tab's `sessionStorage`, never in `localStorage`, so a script that reads the shared storage finds no token and a token does not outlive its tab on a shared computer. A reload keeps the session; a new tab, or a browser that was closed, asks the player to sign in again, and a duplicated tab starts with a copy of its original's. A session an older build left in `localStorage` moves into the first tab that reads it and is deleted there, and nothing writes that key again: every sign-in and renewal also deletes any copy left. A tab's own session is preferred to the shared storage's. Signing out clears the tab's session, any copy in `localStorage` and the end-to-end fixtures' session (`localStorage["jackioh.e2e.session"]`, e2e/README.md A6, read only when the tab has no session of its own), and revokes this tab's session at the provider ([[R194]]); a tab signed in separately keeps its own. Reading never throws on blocked storage: with none, the player is signed out at the next page load. The privacy policy says where the session is kept
