The AI prices a unit's "when this attacks" and "after this attacks" text by one attack beside the same attack by its hookless twin ([[R2688000]]), and counted a unit holding Frenzy as hooked, since Frenzy answers a kill as an `afterAttack` hook did before [[R3551004]] made it a keyword. But the twin is granted every keyword the unit prints or holds ([[R4811000]]), Frenzy among them, so it frenzies exactly as the unit does and the probe read 0, after spending two of `AI_ATTACK_HOOK_PROBE_NODES`' nodes on each distinct Frenzy card (S #94 Tmack Trav). And a card counted as hooked has its evolution left to the hook's price ([[R4648000]], `own_hook_evolves`), which for Frenzy prices nothing.

**The rule.** The AI decides this; no game changes. Frenzy is no attack hook (`has_attack_hook` reads only the face's `whenAttacks` and `afterAttack` hooks): a unit that holds it and prints no such hook is not probed, and the evolution probe no longer leaves such a card to a hook price that is not there. What Frenzy gives is read where it acts, in the Taunt walk, where a Frenzy attacker that destroys the Taunt it attacked and survives swings again ([[R3543003]], [[R4402000]]). Since a Vanilla unit runs no text, no Vanilla unit is hooked any more, so [[R4811000]]'s Vanilla unit holding a granted Frenzy no longer reaches the probe.

**Rejected.** Taking Frenzy off the twin and probing a kill, which would price a quantity the AI has never priced, a change of its play to be screened on its own.

**Measured.** Screened together with [[R4799000]] and [[R4799001]] as one change (training/improve.md, A screen): a fork screen on improve-dev against the control at 38282eba85 (origin/main 5991e8da39 with issue #4798's commits) read 200 trunk games, 5 forks, 2 live, edge -0.016 (-0.046 to 0.015); inconclusive ([[R4319000]]), so its scene tests settle it.
