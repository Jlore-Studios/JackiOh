The Greatest Heist's and Job Application's stages ([[R1854000]]) need six goals [[R404]]'s list lacked, each a watcher of its own. Each counts from the moment its quest opens and for its card's controller, as every quest does, and its progress is public on the card.

- **"Make an Acclaimed Unit Radiant"** (`unitsMadeRadiant { count, tag }`): each `radiantSet` reported on a Unit of yours with the tag (its type and tags where it is, granted tags included; any Unit when no tag is named), wherever it is — your hand, your deck, your graveyard or exile, or your side of the field. A Make Radiant on a card nobody else reads is reported whether or not the card was Radiant already ([[R177]]), and the quest counts the report, so its public progress says that a card of yours made Radiant was an Acclaimed Unit and never whether it changed. The Greatest Heist offers a Jelly Bean for exactly this, and Core #26's pick is a hand card. An enemy's Unit made Radiant is not yours, and a card that arrives Radiant is not made so.
- **"Have 2 Acclaimed Units you control die"** (`unitsDied { count, tag }`): each `destroyed` of a Unit with the tag under your control as it died ([[R172]]), a stolen enemy one included and your own the opponent had taken excluded. A card exiled or bounced does not die.
- **"Steal an enemy card"** (`cardsStolen { count }`): a card your opponent owned taken into your hand (`stolen`, [[R1366]]'s steal, a draw from their deck included) or a card they controlled taken onto your side of the field by Steal (`controlChanged` with `how: steal`). The loop reads the event without its `how` ([[R1366]]), so the steal is counted where it happens, which by emit order is the same moment. A give, a board swap, a rotation and a card of yours your opponent takes are no steal of yours.
- **"Play a card that costs the same as another"** (`playCosting { cost, remembered }`, Job Application's "costs the same as your resume"): a card of yours played whose cost paid is the number, [[R56]]'s cost paid, a cast's 0 included ([[R70]]); a countered play is no play, and the quest card's own play, which is how it entered before its quest opened, counts for none of its goals. The number is `cost`, or the one the quest card remembers under `remembered` (`remember`) while it holds one, so the set's card names its resume's cost as it is sent in. The view shows the cost asked beside the quest.
- **"Play 3 cards of different costs in a single turn"** (`differentCostsInTurn { count }`): plays of yours in one turn, counted by the different costs paid among them; a cost already counted that turn counts nothing, and a new turn starts again from none, so its progress reads 0 on a turn with no play of yours yet.
- **"Take or deal 12+ damage in a single instance"** (`damageInstance { amount }`): one `damage` event, what got through ([[§4.4]] step 5; a hit of 0 is no instance, [[R63]]), of at least `amount`, dealt by a card or hero of yours (its side as it happened, [[R212]]) to anything, or taken by your hero. A hit on a Unit of yours from the opponent is not your hero taking it.
- **A goal only an effect meets** (`byEffect`, [[R1854001]]).
- Each new goal is absent from every shipped card, so no shipped game changes.
