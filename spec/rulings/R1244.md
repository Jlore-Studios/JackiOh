Meditative #95's seventh entry, "Bounce every enemy permanent, then Nerf each card bounced", reads the enemy permanents acting on the field as it resolves, in both rows, the tops of piles and face-down cards included, and Bounces them together (`bounce_all`) to their controller's hand ([[R747]]): a card beneath a pile resumes and stays ([[R13]]), a token ceases to exist ([[R11]]) and a full hand burns ([[R4]]). Then each of those cards that reached a hand is Nerfed `CHAOS_MED_NERFS` (1) time, one Degrade application ([[R386]]), hidden there as a hand card's changes are ([[R177]], [[R440]]); one that did not reach a hand is not.
