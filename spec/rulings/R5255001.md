Felinors vs Humans #72.1 Wild Growth reads "Gain 1 mana crystal" (Radiant 2). Hearthstone's Wild Growth gives an empty crystal inside its cap of ten, but a JackiOh seat reaches its cap of four by its fourth turn ([[§2.3]]), so a crystal inside the cap would be a dead card from then on; the set's plan of record takes the other reading (`docs/felinors-vs-humans-set.md`, FV2 #72.1).

**The rule.** "Gain N mana crystals" is `changeMaxMana` of +N, the verb [[R3538006]] lowers max mana with: N onto the seat's persistent modifier, so the crystals stand past the mana cap, as stolen ones do ([[R4331001]]), for the rest of the game. They come in empty: current mana is untouched as the card resolves, and every refresh from the next on fills up to the higher maximum. Max mana never passes `MANA_CEILING` ([[R5148000]]); a gain that moves no maximum announces nothing.
