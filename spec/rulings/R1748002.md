Since issue #1748 a Nerf and a Buff have a sixth row, last in the menu ([[R442]]): on a card on the field that uses the Plague Counters on itself, a Buff places `TUNE_PLAGUE_STEP` (one) Plague Counter on it and a Nerf removes one while it carries any. The Buff's counter is a placement like any other, multiplied by the card's own multiplier and answered by "whenever Plague Counters are placed on this" ([[R471]]); the Nerf's is a removal. The event is `upgraded` or `degraded` with the change `plague` and how many went on (+) or came off (−), after the `counterChanged` that moved them.

The catalog marks such a card `usesPlague: true` ([[§5]]): C #74 Corpse Plantation, C+ #7 The House and M #29 Forbiddenous Factory, the three Field Spells that spend their own counters; `cargo jackioh catalog check` refuses the mark on any card but a Field Spell whose text names Plague Counters. A card off the field carries no counters, a Vanilla card's use for them is gone, and a face-down card's text sleeps ([[R1487000]]), so none of them has the row. This amends [[R386]]'s list of changes.
