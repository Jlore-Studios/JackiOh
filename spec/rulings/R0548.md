Both of C #66's triggers answer a play of its owner's once it has resolved ([[§10.5]] step 7's `cardResolved`; a cast emits it, a countered play never does), as Hearthstone's "after you play" waits for resolution, and [[§8.6]]'s row 66 reads "after it resolves" for both. [[R401]] then holds by [[§10.3]]'s dispatch, which offers an event to each card where it stands when it is dispatched: the `cardResolved` of the Unit that summons the Striker reaches it in hand, never on the field, and that of the card that bounces it reaches it on the field, never in hand
