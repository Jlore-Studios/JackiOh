C #57 Echo reads "This has the text of the last Spell either player played, or of The Coin if none has been." Through v0.5.11p it read "…played", and with no Spell played yet it had no text, did nothing and recorded nothing ([[R399]]). Now, while `lastSpell` is null ([[§10.1]]), it has the text of The Coin ([[R244]]), `core-t-coin`, and a card that names no such fallback still has none.

- **The Coin's base face.** The text it has is The Coin's base face, "Gain 1 mana this turn", whatever face Echo wears: the fallback stands in for a Spell played, and a Spell's face is the one it was played on ([[R399]]), which for a Coin dealt by [[R244]] is the base one. The Radiant Echo's own Echo 1 ([[§6.2]]) then repeats it: base Echo is a Coin's mana, Radiant Echo two.
- **What Echo records.** It records The Coin, base face, as the Spell it copied: `lastSpell` and the player's last face-up play read The Coin once an Echo has been played ([[R451]]), and a second Echo has The Coin's text too, never Echo's, so two Echoes still never loop. Recording nothing was the other reading; it was rejected because a played Echo is a Spell play ([[R70]]) whose record is the Spell it copied (§10.1: overwritten by every Spell play), and an Echo that played a text and left no trace of it would be the one Spell play that records no Spell.
- **Until a Spell is played.** `lastSpell` is never cleared, so The Coin's text is Echo's only before the game's first Spell play. A Field Spell, a countered Spell and a Spell cast on draw are no Spell played ([[R399]], [[R2179002]]) and leave it in force. Echo's hand view, its play and a cast of it ([[R70]]) all follow it: its owner's `CardView.copies` names The Coin, its play declares nothing, and the copy is fixed as the play or cast begins ([[R546]]). The Coin has no preview and no yellow glow ([[R547]]); the opponent's view still never names the card.
- **Data.** Echo names it: `staticFlags.copyFallback` is `COIN_DEF_ID`, set on both faces; the engine's copied-text subsystem reads it where no Spell has been played. No other card copies the last Spell, and a card that does and names none (the engine's fixture copiers) has no text then, as before.
- **References.** Echo's text now names The Coin, so its `refs` lists it ([[R279]]) and its Chinese names 幸运币.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11p folds under `script_v0_5_11o`, Echo without the fallback ([[R767000]]).
