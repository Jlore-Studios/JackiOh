A card's hidden Nerfs ([[R1845000]]) land as it is next played or cast ([[R70]]), at [[§10.5]] step 3: after a replacement has had its say, so a card a `replacePlays` replaces ([[R449]]) ceases to exist with its count and the new card has none; after Feng Shui ([[R983]]), Gifted Program ([[R213]]) and the Garden, so they land on the face the play resolves with; and before any `onPlayHook` ([[R153]]), the announce and the Cry. The count is spent whole, and a play a Counter cancels afterwards has taken them.

Each is one [[§6.3]] Nerf drawn from every row but the cost's: the stats, a keyword, an X, a number (one only its Cry reads included, the Cry being still to come) or a Plague Counter. So the card's price is never touched, neither as it is paid, step 2 being over, nor later (#123: "nerfs avoid cost"), and a card only its cost could have Nerfed takes them as nothing. The card's own multiplier scales each ([[R1160]]). They are reported as every Nerf is: one `degraded` per Nerf, hidden from whoever cannot read the card where it is (a hand card's other seat, [[R177]]), with the change `none` where nothing could change ([[R440]]). So the count is seen as the card is played, and its result once the card is face-up.

A card that reaches the field without being played keeps its count until it is played again, and one never played keeps it. Rejected: landing them as the card enters the field, which misses Spells, and on the field would spare the Cry's numbers ([[R1748000]]).
