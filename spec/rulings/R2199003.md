TG #14 answers [[§10.5]] step 7's `cardResolved` of a Unit its controller's opponent played, after its Cry and every Echo ([[R427]]); a Unit cast by an effect counts as played ([[R70]]). A summon is no play (a token, a Recruit, a Reborn, [[R1]], [[R61]]), and a Spell, a backrow card or its controller's own Unit leaves it set, every condition in `when` ([[R99]]). A played Unit that is no longer on the field when the trap is asked, gone in its own resolution or taken by an earlier answer to the same play ([[R174]]), leaves it set too: there is nothing to hit. Rejected: Core #41 Sheepish's firing on a Unit gone in its own resolution, which Sheepish keeps only for its Radiant rider ([[R120]]); this card has no rider.
