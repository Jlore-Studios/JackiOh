Issue #814 asked that long card texts be shortened, with the whole text shown on the card's left on hover, rather than squeezed. A rules box that cannot hold its text at the reading floor (9 px) takes the long layout, which shrinks the art (`fit.ts`), so a face's words decide how much of its art shows. Issue #1017 measured the limit in Chromium on every catalog face: the Almanac's and the deck builder's tiles are 164 to 220 px wide, and a 170 px face's rules box holds 6 lines of about 28 characters at the floor (`FACE_TEXT_LIMIT`, counted by wrapping a text by words, each of its own lines starting a new one); the hover preview's 271 px face holds 10 lines of about 45 (`PREVIEW_TEXT_LIMIT`). Every catalog face's printed text fits the first, wording only, no card playing differently. A face no wording can fit prints a short form instead (`shortText.ts`): its first lines, which its text puts first, "…" taking the place of the last full stop (#98 Heroic Power, Meditative #8 Reach the Summit, Combo-Index, Mind Games, Fortify Mind, YileGPT Unleashed); its text up to its first colon, "…" in the colon's place (the three Call to Chaos editions, whose list stays whole in the catalog, [[R436]]); or its whole text, cut by the rules box at its last line with "…" (the few faces still a line over after the cut). A face that fits may end in a line the short form hides too, a reminder or the designer's flavour that the click-in carries (Classic #5 Tesla, Meditative #39.5 Jade Beauty, Meditative #97 Jlockeed's Evil Blueprints). Such a face never takes the long layout, so its art keeps its size, and the whole text, its numbers ([[R280]]), Radiant marks ([[R277]]) and references ([[R279]]) included, stands on the face's left in the hover preview, under it in the touch sheet and at reading size in the detail view; the Almanac's and the deck builder's grids open a hover preview on such a card, and on no other. In play the Summit prints its header and only its next Ascent line ([[R280]]'s level), held to the preview's limit, and a face in play past that limit (a fused card, a heavily Buffed one) clamps the same way with its whole text beside it. A face that prints something else in play (a #98 Heroic Power's rolled power, a Call to Chaos's "???", [[§10.10]]) takes no short form. Presentation only: no rule reads card text ([[R366]]).
