Presentation and intent only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no state, event, replay or AI decision changes, and the client still only narrows the plays `legalActions` lists. Dragging a card that may be Cloaked lights the zones of its set plays ([[R783004]], [[R1044]]) and the zones and targets of its face-up play. A drop on a lit backrow zone sets it, through the usual narrowing. A drop on the board elsewhere is free exactly when the face-up plays alone would make it free, as for a card without Cloaked ([[R658]]): no zone or target is left to pick, or only one remains. The face-up play is then cast, and any X, embiggen, Plague Counter or mode it still needs goes through the picker with no face-up or set question first. A face-up play with two or more zones or targets keeps its arrow or ghost and its drop on a lit spot, so a drop on empty board space is a miss. When no face-up play is listed, every play decides, so a drop elsewhere is a miss. The click path and "Play it how?" are unchanged.

Rejected: always casting face-up on a board drop, which would cast a two-target Spell with no target; and leaving the one-target Cloaked Spell not free, which would make it differ from the same Spell without Cloaked.
