Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes.

**Why.** The Cards tab built the same choice three ways:

- Table and Grid, and Show 95% ranges ([[R2895000]]), were buttons with `aria-pressed`.
- The Columns and Show tokens toggles were checkboxes.
- The sort direction was a link button.

Each control stood on a row of its own. The tavern's wood button rule outweighed the page's own pressed look, so a pressed button, a pressed patch and the view tab shown looked like any other. The owner asked in #3324 for the UI to be standardized.

**The controls.** On the Cards tab:

- Every on/off or one-of-a-few control is the tavern's wood button with `aria-pressed`: Table and Grid, Show 95% ranges, the Columns toggles (Games, Play rate), and the patch tabs ([[R3324000]]). The sort direction is a wood button too.
- Sort, View and Columns are labelled groups and share one wrapping row with Show 95% ranges, under the search and the filters.
- The selects and the search box are `--hit-min` tall everywhere, as tall as the buttons ([[R2122000]] had them so only on a phone or under a finger).

A pressed button, and the view tab shown, are pushed in, in gold: the tavern's gold border, its deep wood, its pale gold text and no drop shadow. The rule carries the wood button rule's weight and comes later, as the breakdown's Show Radiant does.

The app-wide `Tabs` and `Segmented` components, and arrow keys on the tabs, are #3222's (part of #3231): this changes only `/stats`.

Rejected: building those components here, which #3222 is queued to build across the menus, and a primary (gold) button for the pressed look, which #3222 rules out.
