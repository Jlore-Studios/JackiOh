Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes.

**Why.** The Cards tab built the same choice three ways:

- Table and Grid, and Show 95% ranges ([[R2895000]]), were buttons with `aria-pressed`.
- The Columns and Show tokens toggles were checkboxes.
- The sort direction was a link button.

Each control stood on a row of its own, and the page drew its own pressed look, which the tavern's button rule outweighed, so a pressed button looked like any other. The owner asked in #3324 for the UI to be standardized, after the shared `Tabs` and `Segmented` components (#3222) landed.

**The controls.** On the Cards tab:

- Every on/off or one-of-a-few control wears the shared `Segmented` look (`tabs.css`, #3222), pressed in its selected skin, with `aria-pressed`:
  - Table and Grid, and the patch tabs ([[R3324000]]), are `Segmented` groups.
  - Show 95% ranges and the Columns toggles (Games, Play rate) are on or off each on its own, so they are buttons with the same item class, not one of a few.
- The sort direction is a plain button with the secondary role (`buttons.css`, #3219).
- Sort, View and Columns are labelled groups and share one wrapping row with Show 95% ranges, under the search and the filters.
- The selects take the shared filter select of `tabs.css` and, with the search box, are `--hit-min` tall everywhere ([[R2122000]] had them so only on a phone or under a finger).

The page keeps no pressed look, select skin or sort-button skin of its own.

Rejected: the page's own pressed look, which the shared one replaces, and a `Segmented` for the independent toggles, which would let only one column show at a time.
