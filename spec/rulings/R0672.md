A double-or-nothing rematch is offered only from a ranked match: only a ranked match can spawn one, and offering double-or-nothing from an unranked match is refused. When the rematch ends, each side's Glicko rating moves twice the delta the single Glicko-2 update computed — `after.rating` is `before.rating + 2 * (single.rating - before.rating)` per side — while deviation and volatility come from that single update, so a doubled game does not over-sharpen confidence. A rematch of a ranked match is ranked too ([[§9.5]]), so a ranked rematch can itself spawn a double. Hearthstone semantics: there is no double-or-nothing in the source, and doubling the stakes of a rematch is the smallest rule that matches the button's words
