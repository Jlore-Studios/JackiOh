A GIGA moves only whole. A steal takes the same lane whole, else the leftmost fit, else fails
([[R15]]); a rotation ([[R14]]) or swap ([[R88]]) that cannot land it whole bounces it; it never
steps rows; Reborn and [[R210]] hold four zones for it. Rejected: splitting it at the center line,
which would leave half a permanent on each side.
