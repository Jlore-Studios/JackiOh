Issue #4154 asked to make the AI better at trading: when it knows it will not play a card this turn, trade it. Its search already tried a Trade ([[R1838003]]), after its plays and answers, but its evaluation priced the traded card at its full place in hand, more for a dearer card, against an unseen card's price for the card drawn, so it traded its cheap cards and never the expensive one it could not cast. This is a judgement about the AI, not a rule of the game, and changes no game.

**Dead.** A card in the AI's own hand is dead while it is Tradable now (printed, granted or lent by an aura, [[R1838000]]) and its own cost (`own_cost`, an X-cost card having none) is above the crystals its next refresh brings: this turn's maximum, one more while under `MAX_MANA`, moved by the next-turn rider, read off the public counts and never through the handicap (`evaluate::dead_in_hand`). So neither this turn's crystals nor the next turn's pay for it.

**The price.** `evaluate` holds a dead card at `AI_EVAL.dead_tradable_discount` (0.5) off its hand price, so a dead (5) card counts 1.25 against the 1.6 an unseen card is priced at, and the Trade that swaps it for a card drawn, putting it back in the deck under the top ([[R2657003]]), reads as a gain; a card its next refresh pays for keeps its whole price, so the AI keeps the card it will play next turn. `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0.

**The move order.** The Trade of a dead card is tried before the plays, after the hero attacks and the winning trades, so a level's `branching` cut keeps it and a line can play the card it draws; every other Trade keeps its place.

Not modelled: temporary mana a later play might make, which could pay for a dead card, and a dead card the AI would rather discard to an effect. Rejected: a discount on every card it cannot cast, which only a Trade turns into a play.

Amended by issue #4159 ([[R4159000]]): the turn test's board moves the crystal count the refreshes set the maximum from ([[R1854003]]) with the maximum it lowers, so the next refresh brings one crystal more and does not pay for the card. With the learned correction retired, the hand evaluation trades the card on that board.
