Issue #3951: "All items in the Stinkscape Resource store cost ~8% more". The store is the Marketplace ([[R2250002]]): every card it rolls is priced at `MARKETPLACE_MARKUP_PERCENT` (108) percent of the designer's price ([[R1853002]], [[R3047000]]: 5 + 10 × min(c, 4) + 15 × max(c − 4, 0), a (0) card 8), rounded to the nearest Credit, a half up, so a (0) card asks 9 rather than staying at 8 as rounding down would leave it: 9, 16, 27, 38, 49, 65, 81, 97 and 113 for (0) to (8). The Radiant doubling, each Buff's and Nerf's step ([[R3047002]]) and the turn's move within a tenth apply to that price as before. A lot an effect adds for the rest of the game keeps the price its card names ([[R1853005]], S #151's 20): that price is printed rules text, not the store's curve. What a Resource or a hand-sale card sells for is no price of the store's; issue #3951 lowered three Resources' own ranges in their catalog entries instead (Axnit, Narcor, Red Narcor).
