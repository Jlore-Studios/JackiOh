Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. Issue #2782's closing part audits the finished card-animation work and lands its report. 551 of the catalog's 634 non-token cards wear an entry other than `none` in their set's effects table ([[R2784000]]), past the audit's floor of half (`FX_COVERAGE_FLOOR`); every Spell, Field Spell, Trap and Field Trap wears a `cast` or a recipe or set piece of its own, save Stinkyscape #176 Preemptive Strike, whose only hook is the Starship's and whose entry stays the one `none` a Spell of a pass may wear ("Starship part", [[R2801000]]); every Field Spell wears an `aura` ([[R2787000]]).

Every table entry ends in its tail: the audit's tier test plays each entry's gallery play through the board's effects layer, and every cue it plans starts inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]), a pull and a conceal staying the stage's. Every set piece's hold stays within `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS`, and one action takes at most one hold (`SET_PIECES_PER_ACTION`, [[R2785000]]). The census ([[R2784000]]'s oracle) disagrees with 364 entries, each overridden with its reason in its set pass's Why cell: the census reads only damage, removal, return and cost families, and a pass maps every other event to the motif whose Reads column names it. Four drawn motifs sit below the audit's floor of three cards (`FX_MOTIF_MIN_CARDS`) and are folded, 31 drawn motifs remaining: `chain` into `nova` (Meditative+ #5 Spirit Lash hits every Unit at once, amending [[R2784000]]'s Examples-table citation); `mirror` into `transmute` (Rewind's repeated Cry, Juhan Biggest Bat's transformed Bat); `ward` into the rows (Armor's full shield and the Divine Shield break draw in `cues.ts` in Holy, shaking `FX_ARMOR_TRAUMA`; Argusland and Fortify Flesh name `bless`); `glimpse` into bespoke `mindJudge` (Fortify Mind keeps its judgment's sheen and flare on the hero's badge, shaking `FX_MIND_JUDGE_TRAUMA`) and `bless` (Feng Shui). No sub-look sits unused.

This amends [[R2722001]]: the shared entrances are reworked to read as the loudest tiers of the motif language, one language with the set passes' Legendary and Mythic looks. A summoned Legendary's ring carries its echo, the same wave behind as the takeover's second shockwave (`entranceEchoMs`), as does a Legendary Spell's cast or a reveal's; the shared prismatic sweep has its own keyframes (`fx-takeover-prism`), crossing farther and settling in two steps; every other card recoils from the landing in two steps with the two-wave landing; and the reduced-motion still echoes the takeover at rest, a motionless rays disc and the landing ring around the card. The music ([[R2722000]]), when and for whom an entrance plays ([[R2722002]]) and the one capped hold ([[R2722001]], as [[R2785000]] generalizes it) are unchanged, and everything stays within [[R200]], [[R202]], reduced motion and `transform` and `opacity` only.

On a phone viewport at the phone particle cap, all five scenes hold their mean frame time within `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` (50 ms): the cap check, Twisting Nether, the Starship launch, a Mythic takeover and a board with 4 auras. The two misses were scaled back with their composition untouched (same layers, choreography, holds and timing): Nether throws fewer pull and implode particles and shakes softer, and its vortex spiral rasterizes fewer pixels with its mask retuned to the same visible arms; the takeover throws fewer Mythic-burst particles and shakes softer, and its rays rasterize fewer pixels with their mask retuned to the same visible disc. The gallery captures every set piece mid-flight and at rest and every drawn motif mid-flight: `shift` proves its DOM ghosts rather than canvas pixels (firing ~40 cues in one tick stalls software rendering's frame loop past its bursts' lives; the ghosts are the motif's signature), its timing proven as ever by the tier test over every table entry.

Rejected: keeping the four under-floor motifs as reasoned exceptions, which leaves the design doc's floor unenforced; raising the phone budget instead of scaling the scenes; cutting layers, choreography, holds or timing (as opposed to the counts, shakes and off-screen raster the audit trimmed, which keep the looks); and restyling the per-card marquee entrances, which stay as their passes left them.
