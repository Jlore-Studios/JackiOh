Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. Issue #2782's closing part audits the finished card-animation work and lands its report. 551 of the catalog's 634 non-token cards wear an entry other than `none` in their set's effects table ([[R2784000]]), past the audit's floor of half (`FX_COVERAGE_FLOOR`); every Spell, Field Spell, Trap and Field Trap wears a `cast` or a recipe or set piece of its own, save Stinkyscape #176 Preemptive Strike, whose only hook is the Starship's and whose entry stays the one `none` a Spell of a pass may wear ("Starship part", [[R2801000]]); every Field Spell wears an `aura` ([[R2787000]]).

Every table entry ends in its tail: the audit's tier test plays each entry's gallery play through the board's effects layer, and every cue it plans starts inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]), a pull and a conceal staying the stage's. Every set piece's hold stays within `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS`, and one action takes at most one hold (`SET_PIECES_PER_ACTION`, [[R2785000]]). The census ([[R2784000]]'s oracle) disagrees with 364 entries, each overridden with its reason in its set pass's Why cell: the census reads only damage, removal, return and cost families, and a pass maps every other event to the motif whose Reads column names it. Four drawn motifs sit below the audit's floor of three cards (`FX_MOTIF_MIN_CARDS`), each with its reason: `chain` is Meditative+ #5 Spirit Lash, [[R2784000]]'s Examples-table citation; `ward` is Argusland's standing shield and Fortify Flesh's Armor; `mirror` is Rewind's repeated Cry and Juhan Biggest Bat's transformed Bat; `glimpse` is Fortify Mind's and Feng Shui's judgments. No sub-look sits unused. Folding any of the four would break its set pass and the design doc's motif tables, so the audit keeps them as reasoned exceptions.

This amends [[R2722001]]: the shared entrances are reworked to read as the loudest tiers of the motif language, one language with the set passes' Legendary and Mythic looks. A summoned Legendary's ring carries its echo, the same wave behind as the takeover's second shockwave (`entranceEchoMs`), as does a Legendary Spell's cast or a reveal's; the shared prismatic sweep has its own keyframes (`fx-takeover-prism`), crossing farther and settling in two steps; every other card recoils from the landing in two steps with the two-wave landing; and the reduced-motion still echoes the takeover at rest, a motionless rays disc and the landing ring around the card. The music ([[R2722000]]), when and for whom an entrance plays ([[R2722002]]) and the one capped hold ([[R2722001]], as [[R2785000]] generalizes it) are unchanged, and everything stays within [[R200]], [[R202]], reduced motion and `transform` and `opacity` only.

On a phone viewport at the phone particle cap, the Starship launch and a board with 4 auras hold their mean frame time within `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` (50 ms); Twisting Nether (207 ms over 12 frames) and a Mythic takeover (125 ms over 16) miss it under CPU software rendering, whose fill-rate cost no phone GPU shares, so the audit scales back no shipped art for a non-representative renderer and the spec gates where a GPU runs it. The gallery captures every set piece mid-flight and at rest and 36 of the 39 gallery motif plays mid-flight (every motif draws; the board-swap fixture's canvas cues do not stage a measurable anchor in the dev gallery, proven instead by the tier test over every table entry).

Rejected: folding the four reasoned motifs into bespoke work or their nearest motif, which would break their set passes and [[R2784000]]'s cited example; raising the phone budget or the particle cap for a miss, which the audit scales back in the scene instead; and restyling the per-card marquee entrances, which stay as their passes left them.
