#41 answers the opponent's play of a Unit after that Unit resolves, its Cry included, and then transforms it into a Sheep Token; the Radiant face still adds a Lava Golem that costs (0) to its controller's hand. So a Unit no longer loses its Cry to it, and a counter, which answers the play's announce before `cardPlayed` ([[§10.5]]), always comes first. It still fires and is consumed when the Unit is Immutable ([[R23]]) or has left the field in its own resolution ([[R174]]), its Transform finding nothing and the Radiant face's "Also" still adding the Golem ([[R120]]). It rewrites [[R17]]'s Sheepish half
