Every Prime card is a token: a Catalyst's Death shuffles its Prime into its owner's deck (C+ #38, C+ #46, Meditative #45, #91). So "a random Prime card" (Meditative #55 Dragon Fruit, #31's Radiant face, #95's "add 2 Prime cards") draws from the Prime tokens, as a Fruit pool holds the Grapes ([[R382]]): Prime is one of `POOL_TOKEN_TAGS`, and a pool that asks for the Prime tag, in `tags` or in `anyTags` ([[R770]]), takes the Prime tokens of the sets that ship ([[R768]]). The ten AI generated cards (T-AI-1 to T-AI-10) are tokens too, so an AI pool holds them the same way: "a random AI generated card" (Meditative #53 Prestige, #59, #60's "a random Human, Book, CN, or AI-Generated card") draws from the ten, as C+ #43 AI Slop's and C+ #78 Claude's Datacenter's pools, which ask for tokens outright, already do. No other pool reaches them, and the card that makes the pool is still left out ([[R387]]).
