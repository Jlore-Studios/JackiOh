Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Core's ten signatures ([[R3402001]]) need five things the frame of [[R3332000]] did not give, each following Hearthstone, where a Legendary's own animation replaces the generic one and its standing effect stays on its card:

- **A bespoke recipe may wear an aura.** A `{ bespoke }` entry may carry `aura: { look, palette?, scope }` beside its key and frame, as a hooks entry may ([[R2787000]]); `entryAura` reads either kind's, and every reader of a card's aura (the aura planner, the tile aura's signature replacement of [[R3342000]], the gallery and the tests) goes through it.
- **A Unit may wear a zone aura on its own unit zone.** The loader accepts `scope: "zone"` on a Unit, which stands on the unit zone it occupies (key `unit-<instanceId>`, its `row` the unit row), while it stands there face up and readable: a hidden Unit, the other seat's unknown, wears none ([[R202]]). A Unit's `lane` aura still fails the loader, a lane being a Field card's. A Unit's `side` or `board` aura is unchanged: it stands as a modifier's does.
- **A signature's own cast replaces the shared rays, burst and ring.** Where [[R3332000]] let a Unit's or Field Spell's own entrance stand in for the shared Legendary or Mythic landing, a signature whose first beat is a cast of its own (a `{ bespoke }` recipe or a `{ setPiece }`) now does too, so its own first beat is what lands (`ownCast`, `fx/takeover.ts`). A Mythic's takeover still plays, its rings and its shake with it. A Legendary Spell or Trap that is no signature still lands in the shared rays.
- **A Spell's takeover fades sooner.** A Mythic Spell's takeover lasts its entry and `FX_TAKEOVER_SPELL_TAIL_MS` (500 ms) more, where a permanent's keeps `FX_TAKEOVER_TAIL_MS` (700 ms) of [[R2722001]], so a Mythic Spell's signature, seen only briefly, fits its share of the budget. Each later action of a signature's gallery play (a Trap's firing, a trigger, an Activate) is timed against the budget from its own start, since a game deals it turns after the play.
- **A Mythic's own look may draw its own art.** The own looks gain `rotunda` and `forge`, and `pawn` turns indigo. A look may name a scene (`TAKEOVER_ART`, `fx/takeoverArt.ts`) drawn full screen over the board's dark and under the sweep: shapes on a 100 × 100 view sliced to cover the screen, at most `TAKEOVER_ART_MAX_SHAPES` (48), painted by part from the look's tint and moved by transform and opacity alone (`fx.css`), within the phone frame budget. Under Reduce Motion the still card ([[R2722001]]) stands over the same scene at rest (`board.css`, [[R438]]). A scene holds no text and no card's face, id or name, so it names no card ([[R202]]). `pawn`, `genie`, `rotunda` and `forge` have one; `void`, `ai` and the shared `prismatic` keep their takeover as it was.

Three signature aura looks join `AURA_LOOKS`, each within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`: `mirrorball`, `stadium` and `pedestal`.

Rejected: keying a takeover's art by the card's id, which would put the card's id on the page; drawing the card's own picture full screen, which would show a card face in a layer that reads no view; and letting a Unit's aura cover its lane, which would stand on zones its card does not occupy.
