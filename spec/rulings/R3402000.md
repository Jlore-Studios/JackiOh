Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. [[R3400000]]'s frame (a bespoke entry's aura, a Unit's `tile` aura, a signature Spell's or Trap's own cast and its shorter takeover tail) carries Core's ten signatures ([[R3402001]]); they need five things more, each following Hearthstone, where a Legendary's own animation replaces the generic one:

- **Two more own takeover looks.** `rotunda` (Heroic Power's marble hall) and `forge` (Craft a Card's crystal forge) join `OWN_TAKEOVER_LOOKS`, each with its own tints (`TAKEOVER_TINTS`), and `pawn` turns indigo for My Pawn's hooded hand.
- **A look may draw a scene.** Beside [[R3400000]]'s parts, a look may name a scene of SVG shapes (`TAKEOVER_ART`, `fx/takeoverArt.ts`), drawn full screen over the takeover's dark and under its sweep: shapes on a 100 × 100 view sliced to cover the screen, at most `TAKEOVER_ART_MAX_SHAPES` (48), painted by part from the look's tint and moved by transform and opacity alone (`fx.css`), within the phone frame budget. Under Reduce Motion the still card ([[R2722001]]) stands over the same scene at rest (`board.css`, [[R438]]). A scene holds no text and no card's face, id or name, so it names no card ([[R202]]). `pawn`, `genie`, `rotunda` and `forge` have one; `void`, `ai` and the shared `prismatic` keep their takeover as it was.
- **Three signature aura looks.** `mirrorball` (Silly Silas's, on his tile), `stadium` (Combo-Index's) and `pedestal` (Heroic Power's) join `AURA_LOOKS`, each within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`.
- **One reader of a card's aura.** `entryAura` reads a hooks entry's or a bespoke entry's `aura`, and the aura planner, the tile aura's signature replacement of [[R3342000]], the gallery and the tests read it through that.
- **A gallery play's later actions.** Each later action of a signature's gallery play (a Trap's firing, a trigger, an Activate) is timed against the signature budget ([[R3332000]]) from its own start, since a game deals it turns after the play.

Rejected: keying a takeover's art by the card's id, which would put the card's id on the page; drawing the card's own picture full screen, which would show a card face in a layer that reads no view; and redrawing [[R3400000]]'s parts as scenes, or these scenes as parts, which would change Meditative+'s and Training Grounds' art for no reason of theirs.
