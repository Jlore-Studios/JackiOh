S #150's fifth entry reads "Place all enemies in attack position and Freeze them" (ST-S25-4).

- **"All enemies"** are the enemy Units on the field, the tops of their piles. A hero has no position and Freeze passes it by.
- **The switch first.** Each enemy Unit active on the field is switched to Attack Position by the effect ([[R20]]): no exertion is spent, a Unit already in Attack Position is left as it is with no event ([[R91]]), and one that may not be in Attack Position stays where it is.
- **Then the Freeze.** Every enemy Unit is Frozen ([[§6.3]], [[R1174000]]), so the Unit switched a moment ago cannot switch itself back on its controller's next turn.
