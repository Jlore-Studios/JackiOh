Since [[R5109000]] the Embiggened version costs the normal price plus a premium, and the words say so. An embiggen card's text labels what its Embiggened version does "Embiggened (+N): …", N its Embiggen premium (B − A), where [[R1175000]] wrote its Embiggened cost: "Embiggened (+2): −4/−4 instead." on a 1 embiggen 3 card, "Embiggened (+1): Equip." on a 2 embiggen 3 one. Every embiggen card's premium is at least (1), and no face prints the old price label. The Chinese table writes it "加码(+N)：" (`chinese-terms.json`'s `labels`). The labels in use are `Embiggened (+1):` to `Embiggened (+3):`, so Stinkyscape #79 Orca, 4 embiggen 5, prints `Embiggened (+1):` where [[R1857003]] listed `Embiggened (5):`, a label other sets print too. A patch diff or a static page writes the price "(2) Cost, Embiggened (+2)" (Chinese "(2)费，加码(+2)"). The "+" puts Meditative #21 API Key Fishing's Radiant face a line over the face text limit ("Embiggened (+1): 10% instead." wraps), so that face prints words written for it, its whole text's with "to your hand" as "to hand" ([[R1017000]], [[R3941001]]).

The second cost badge beside a card's gem stays a price, never the premium: the Embiggened version's realized price, the view's `embiggenCost` wherever the view gives one ([[§10.8]]), else the printed one while the gem shows the normal price, and it still reads "Embiggened (N)" to a screen reader and on hover. Since #5109 it shows under a discount or a surcharge too, beside the gem's moved normal price, since the engine's number moves with every price change; a card the view gives no `embiggenCost` (a card on the field, a card not in its owner's hand) shows the gem's number alone, as before. The client never works out a price (CLAUDE.md rule 7), so a kept `embiggenCostOverride` ([[R1181003]]) and a card with no Embiggen premium ([[R4350000]]) show what the engine priced. The engine's identifiers, the glossary's Embiggened entry and the price picker's words are [[R1175000]]'s, unchanged.

Rejected: a badge showing "+N", which the designer ruled out ("the pip stays the realized price"); and the client working the premium out as `embiggenCost − cost`, which a Flashback floor or a Garden makes wrong and which rule 7 forbids.
