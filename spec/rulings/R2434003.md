The owner asked that the coach not show up in the middle of the screen (#2429). On a desktop the floating bubble docks to a corner of the viewport, under the HUD: bottom right first, beside your hero's row and over the log, then bottom left, top right and top left. It takes the first corner that covers neither its anchor nor what a play in progress asks for nor the hand, End turn or an open prompt, and when every corner covers something it covers its anchor least, then what a play asks for, then the rest. It is never in the middle of the board, an info step and the waiting line included (they used to float in the middle and in the corner). The pointing at a target is the ring, a highlight round the card, lane or hero ([[R2205000]]), not the bubble's place. On a phone it is out of the middle too: the coach was already a panel in the page between the HUD and the board, over nothing, and stays so. At 390x844 the panel ends above the board's top edge in every lesson (measured: the panel's 72 to 207 px, the board's from 213 px), and the board is laid out in the height left below it. Screenshots at 1280x720 and 390x844 are in the pull request.

Hearthstone semantics: a tutorial's helper speaks from the side of the board while the board shows what it points at.

Rejected: a bubble beside its anchor, which put it over the middle lanes whenever the anchor was there.
