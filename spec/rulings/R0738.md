While a player aims a play, an Activate or an attack — a drag in flight or a click-selected source, the board's one interaction — the client sends a top-level `{ type: "aim", aim }`, `aim` being `{ source, target }` or `null` when the aim ends, sent only when it changes; the actor relays it to the opponent alone as `{ type: "aim", from, aim }`, and the opponent's board draws the targeting arrow from source to target (only the source marked while `target` is null), static under Reduce Motion, cleared by a `null`, by the sender's socket closing (the actor sends the `null`), by the opponent's own socket dropping and by the game's end. Hidden information stays hidden ([[R97]], [[R177]]): every end is a public handle — a hero by its seat, a field card by its zone (never its instance id, so a face-down card is only its zone), a hand card by its position in the sender's own hand, drawn as the card back there — and only an aim at a declared target is sent, never a zone a card is being placed in, a Tribute or a discard, so the arrow says of a hand card only that it is aimed and at what. The parse rebuilds each end field by field and answers anything else `malformed`; at relay time the actor drops an aim, silently, whose hand position is past the sender's hand or in the opponent's hand, or whose zone is off the board, clearing the arrow instead if one was up. Relays are coalesced per seat to one per `AIM_RELAY_INTERVAL_MS` (100 ms), the newest pending aim going alone when the interval ends, so the arrow always finishes where the aim did. An aim is never an `ActionBody`, so it reaches neither `reduce`, the log, the replay hash nor a game record, is never part of `PlayerView`, and no rule is decided from it (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Hotseat and practice show no aim
