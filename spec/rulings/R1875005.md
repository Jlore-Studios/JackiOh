"Death: Summon the Unit stuffed into this" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #135.1 Sack) is the Sack leaving the field any way — it dies, is Bounced, exiled, transformed or held itself — and at the first state check after, the Unit beneath it is summoned for the Sack's controller, summoning sick, no Cry ([[R1]]), at [[R64]]'s zone, or exiled with no zone open ([[R1850003]]). The release is the engine's, so the Sack runs no text of its own, and a Sack holding nothing summons nothing. The Radiant face's Brittle 1 ([[R638]]) crumbles it within a turn cycle, which releases its Unit. Rejected: release at its Death alone, which would keep the Unit beneath a Sack that a Bounce made cease to exist ([[R11]]).
