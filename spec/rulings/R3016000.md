Not a rule of the game (CLAUDE.md rule 7): how the Collection shows what a card looks like where it
is played. The owner asked in #3016 to see, on hovering a card in the Collection, the card, what it
looks like in the hand and what it looks like on the board.

- **Hover.** On a card of the owned-cards grid, a resting mouse or pen opens the hover preview after
  `HOVER_DELAY_MS`, the board's own hover delay; so does the keyboard's focus (`:focus-visible`, not a
  click's focus). It opens beside the card, as the board's hover preview does, and stays while the
  pointer is on the card or on the panel, so its toggle can be reached; it closes
  `LOOK_HOVER_CLOSE_MS` after the pointer has left both, and at once when the focus leaves the card,
  on Escape, a scroll or the window's blur, and on a press on the card. Both "Hover previews"
  switches govern it, as they govern the board's. One is open at a time.
- **The views.** The card's look three ways, each the very face a game draws (`CardFace`, `MinionFace`),
  never a copy: **the card**, its whole face; **in your hand**, the hand's face, which is a full-art
  card's art alone ([[R2723000]]) on the viewer's own copy; **on the board**, a Unit as the minion a lane
  holds (its printed attack and health, its keywords, in attack position, awake), a Spell as its whole
  face as it resolves, and a Trap, a Field Trap or a Field Spell as the backrow's face. The face's own
  type decides (a Radiant face may be another type). An owned card wears the viewer's picks, its foil,
  finish and border ([[R1655000]]), and moves where its look is animated, under Reduce motion as every
  face does; a locked card wears none. A toggle, "Show Radiant", flips all three to the Radiant face.
- **Touch and the fullscreen preview.** Touch has no hover. Its long press, like a mouse's hold and the
  inspect key, still opens the fullscreen preview ([[R2730000]]), which for a grid card now shows the
  same three views and the toggle, outside the picture's hidden-from-readers box, where it showed one
  face. A plain tap or click still opens the card's view.
- **Tokens.** The hover panel carries no spend button. A held token is still spent only from the
  fullscreen preview of the locked item it covers ([[R1953001]], [[R2730000]]), not from a list or a
  grid; the panel is a preview of one card, not a list.

The card's look strip (the card's view, R1655000) and the equip and buy screen the issue asks for are
#3016's second part: they show the variant ladders #3015 builds, so they wait for it.

This amends [[R2730000]] (*Hold to preview*: a grid card's preview shows its look three ways, and a
hover or the keyboard's focus opens a lighter preview of its own) and [[R2723000]] (*Every other copy
prints the whole face*: the Collection's preview draws the hand's face in its "in your hand" view, so
the player sees the hand as it is).

Hearthstone semantics: its collection enlarges a card on hover. It has no hand or board view; JackiOh
draws them because a card's look can change with where it stands (a full-art card in hand, a Unit's
minion).

Rejected: the board's own hover preview (`useInspectTrigger`), which takes no pointer and closes on
any press, so its toggle could never be clicked; a new hover delay beside the board's; drawing the
game's `Card` with a made-up `CardView`, which needs a game's wearer and catalog around it, where the
faces it draws need neither; and a hover preview on the look strip now, which #3015 replaces with its
ladders.
