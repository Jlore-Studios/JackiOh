Not a rule of the game (CLAUDE.md rule 7): how the Collection shows a card before the player plays it.

- **Three ways.** A card's look is drawn as its full card face, as the viewer's own hand draws it and
  as it stands on the board: a Unit as its lane's minion, at its printed attack and health and in
  attack position, and a Spell, Trap or Field card in its face-up field form. The hand's and the
  board's are the game's own card (`game/Card.tsx`), never a copy, laid out by the board's own
  stylesheet, so the preview cannot drift from a match. An owned card wears the device's picks on
  all three, as the viewer's own cards do on a board they play ([[R1295000]]), so a full-art card's
  hand view is its art alone ([[R2723000]]); a card the account does not own wears the defaults.
  The numbers are the printed card's, and nothing is live.
- **The hover preview.** A mouse or pen resting on a card of the owned-cards grid for
  `HOVER_DELAY_MS`, the game's hover preview's delay, opens the three views beside it, base, placed
  as the game's preview is; so does a keyboard's focus landing on it, after the same delay. Both
  open only while the settings' hover previews are on. It takes no pointer and is aria-hidden, as
  the game's is, so it never covers what a click aims at; the pointer leaving, the focus leaving, a
  press, Escape and a scroll close it. A focus a press gave opens none, and a click still opens the
  card's view.
- **The fullscreen preview.** A touch has no hover: a hold opens the fullscreen preview, as it did
  ([[R2730000]]), and so do the inspect key and a mouse's hold. It now draws the same three views,
  with a Radiant toggle under them ("Show Radiant", pressed while the Radiant face shows) that turns
  all three, and keeps its name, rarity, owned or locked line, card token offer and Close. At phone
  width the views stack, and the page never scrolls sideways. The toggle changes how the card is
  drawn and nothing else: no pick is written and no request is sent.

Tokens stay off every list and grid ([[R1953001]]): this preview is the card's own, and the only
spend it shows is the fullscreen preview's, as before.

This is part 1 of #3016. The card's look strip and the equip and buy screen of its variants'
ladders, which #3016 also asks for, wait for #3015's ladders.

Hearthstone semantics: hovering a card in the collection enlarges it.

Rejected: making the hover preview itself hold the Radiant toggle, which would have to take the
pointer and so cover the next card the pointer heads for; drawing the hand and the board with copies
of their faces, which the issue ruled out; and a new hover delay beside the inspect layer's.
