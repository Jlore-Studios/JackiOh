The owner decided on 2026-11-06 that Mythic-tier cards come in full art by default, and that
Legendaries come up a step, to Extended art. The rule is data in the registry, so the server, the
Collection and every face read one source and no client special-cases a rarity.

**The data.** A level of `crates/shared/cosmetics.json` may list `defaultOn`, the card rarities whose
Default variant draws its look (`VariantLevel::default_on`). `version-full-art` lists Mythic and
`version-extended-art` Legendary. A card's rarity here is the rarity its face prints: a token's
printed rarity (none when it prints none), any other card's own, as the engine reads it for the
tutorial's bar. On such a card that level is its **default look** (`is_default_look`,
`default_look`), and `tests/export_cosmetics.rs` writes each card's into `COSMETIC_DEFAULT_LOOKS`,
tokens included, so the client never compares a rarity itself. The registry's check refuses a
`defaultOn` on any level but a variant's first, or on the Default variant's own, one that lists
Token, which no card's face prints, and a rarity two levels list.

**Owned by everyone, offered nowhere.** A card's default look is owned there by every account with
no ledger row, as every Default is (`owns`), and is offered there nowhere (`offered`). So every write
that holds a level to its offer refuses it as for any level a card is not offered: a pick of it is
the 400 `"fullArt" is not offered on this card` (`check_pick`), a token or the Chase Shard bar naming
it the 400 "that cosmetic is not offered on that card", spending nothing; a duplicate's roll and a
cosmetic pack slot never drop it (the in-order and out-of-order pools hold it as owned, so it is
never open and never among an out-of-order pull's `below`); and `POST /api/dev/fill`, which grants
every level where it is offered, writes no row of it. A pick of `true` on the three Mythics of the
twenty-one full-art cards ([[R2655001]]), stinkyscape-154, classic-090 and traininggrounds-040,
names Full art's first level ([[R2655000]]) and is refused the same way. Practice, hotseat and the
end-to-end tests, which count every level offered on a card as owned, need nothing more: the Default
is every account's ([[R1310004]]).

**The ladder starts higher.** Since the level is owned, the level above it in its variant is next with
nothing owned ([[R3015003]], [[R3015004]]): on a Mythic, Full art starts at its animated level, a
Mythic, and its signed level needs that; on a Legendary, Extended art is its animated level alone, a
Legendary. A duplicate grants it in order, a token or the bar buys it as the next level, and nothing
else moves: the rolls take the same draws, only their pools are smaller, and an opening's log keeps
the registry it was rolled against. Both rolls read the default look from the entry they were given,
never the live registry, so an opening logged before this ruling, its entries with no `defaultOn`,
replays with the level unowned as it was rolled ([[R1306003]]). Every other level of every card
keeps its offer, so a Legendary is still offered Full art where its share takes it and a Mythic
Extended art.

**Nobody loses anything.** An account whose ledger held `version-full-art` on a Mythic, or
`version-extended-art` on a Legendary, keeps the row: `GET /api/cosmetics` lists it, a retired full
art that maps to it still does ([[R2725002]], [[R3015006]]), and it still counts as owning that
level, now every account's, so it buys nothing more and no grant is made in its place. A stored pick
of it wears the highest level at or below it that is offered and owned there, none, so the Default
([[R3015005]]), which draws the same look ([[R3343001]]).

Rejected: a client special case by rarity, which the server and the Collection would not share; a
migration granting every account a row of the level on every such card, rows the rule makes
redundant; retiring the level outright (`is_retired`), which would refuse it on every card, Commons
included; and retiring the animated levels too, which the issue did not ask for. Whether owners of
the level on those cards are made good with something more is the owner's call, as [[R3015006]] left
Signature's.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): the default look depends only on a shown card's rarity, and
nothing enters `PlayerView`'s rules, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725000]] (two levels are a rarity's default look), [[R2725001]] (a level is offered
nowhere a card's Default draws it), [[R3015000]] (a variant's ladder starts above that level there)
and [[R3015003]] (that level is held with no row, so the level above it drops in order).
