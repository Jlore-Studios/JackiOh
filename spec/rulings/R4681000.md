Every list is computed on the server from X0's versions and figures (`PlayVersionTable`, [[R4675001]]),
only from versions with enough games (`hasEnoughGames`, [[R654]]), and ranked by the bound of the range,
not the rate, so a lucky small sample never tops a list. A version short of games may still appear as the
"before" or "after" of a change, shown as "not enough games", but never ranks a list on its own figures.
Each list's length is `HALL_OF_FAME_LIST_LEN` (10).

**Most broken**: the versions with the highest Adjusted of all time, live or past. Only a version with
`hasEnoughGames` ranks, ordered by `adjusted_low` descending (ties: the rate, then the id). Each entry shows
that version and, if it was changed, the version live today (its card's live play version; the version itself
where it is live or its card has none).

**Most nerfed**: the biggest falls in Adjusted from one version of a card to the next. Only consecutive
play versions of one card pair up, and only a pair whose before version has `hasEnoughGames` ranks, ordered
by `before.adjusted_low − after.adjusted_high` descending where `before.adjusted_win_rate` is above
`after.adjusted_win_rate`. Each entry shows the version before and the version after, both with figures; the
after version may be short of games. A pair neither side of which has figures ranks nowhere.

**Most buffed**: the biggest rises, the same way mirrored: a pair whose after version has `hasEnoughGames`
ranks, ordered by `after.adjusted_low − before.adjusted_high` descending where `after.adjusted_win_rate` is
above `before.adjusted_win_rate`. The before version may be short of games.

**Most changed**: the cards with the most versions. Cards count their play versions ([[R4675000]]), most
first; ties go to the most fields changed, counted from `patches.json`'s `changes`; then the card id. A card
with one version is in none of the changed, nerfed or buffed lists. Each entry shows its first version and its
live one (its newest where it has none live), and how many changes lie between: that card's change lines in
shipped versions after its first version's first.

Rejected: ranking by the posterior mean (the Adjusted rate itself) or by the raw win rate: both let a thin
sample's swing top a list, which the bound ranking discounts, as the tier already does ([[R3022000]]).
Measuring a fall by the posterior difference, `before.rate − after.rate`, was taken in spirit and refused as
the order: the bound gap, `before.low − after.high`, is the fall the figures still show once both sides'
noise is taken out, so a fall that survives wide ranges outranks a bigger point fall on thin games.

Hearthstone has no counterpart.
