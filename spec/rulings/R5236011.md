Fragile ([[R5236010]]) is a harmful keyword, so a Nerf and a Buff ([[R386]]) meet it as they meet
Crater ([[R2657005]]):

- **A Nerf may add it** to a Unit that lacks it, on the keyword row after Crater
  (`TUNE_NERF_UNIT_KEYWORDS`), and only to a Unit: a Field Spell or a Trap has no Health for it to
  guard. Like every keyword the row adds, only where R3888000's table calls it live ([[R3888000]]):
  Fragile lasts, so wherever the card's lasting text is.
- **A Nerf never removes it**: it is in `TUNE_HARMFUL_KEYWORDS`, whose loss would help the card.
- **A Buff removes it** where the card has it of its own (`TUNE_BUFF_REMOVES`, beside Can't attack and
  Can't Defend); never one an aura lends.

It is no combat keyword: it is in no random keyword pool, R21's or a Buff's ([[R21]]), so no card that
gives a random keyword gives it.
