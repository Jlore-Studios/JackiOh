title: What the coach reads, and how it moves on

The coach reads what the page already holds — `viewFor(state, human)`, the human's `legalActions` and whether the AI owes an action — and nothing else, so it can show no more than the board does, and it decides nothing (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A step that asks for an action points at it and waits: the engine accepts or refuses whatever the player does, and the coach offers an attack or a play only while `legalActions` holds it. A step shows once its moment comes, completes when the view shows it done (even when the player did it before the step showed), and retires without a word when it can no longer happen. A step still current after `TUTORIAL_STEP_TURNS_MAX` (2) of the player's own turn starts retires by itself, except the last, which ends with the game; and the coach is finished once the game is over. There is no Skip step ([[R314]]). A tip shows once, the first time its moment comes. A tip or an `info` step that asks the player to read something first may hold the AI's next step until it goes, as [[§9.9]]'s pacing holds for a voice line; an `act` step never does ([[R314]])
