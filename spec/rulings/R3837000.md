Issue #3837 gives #52 Silly Silas's base face "Summon this for your opponent.", which the owner settled as #55 Lava Golem's base face ([[R360]]) without Lava Golem's condition. Every play of the base face lands Silas at [[§10.5]] step 4 on the opponent's side, under their control: in their leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), whatever zone the play named. It is still the player's play and the player's card: `cardPlayed` names the player and the player owns it, while step 4's `summoned` names the opponent and the lane he took. The play still names one of the player's own open unit zones, which step 2 holds for it and step 4 releases ([[R210]]). Only a play hands him over: a cast ([[R70]]), a replaced play, a summon and a Reborn put him on their own controller's side as they do any card. The Radiant face prints no such line and stays with its player. The engine reads the face's `summonForOpponent` flag.

Rejected: Lava Golem's order, the opponent's zone in the named lane first and their leftmost open zone only when that one is taken ([[R360]]): the owner's answer names the leftmost open unit zone.
