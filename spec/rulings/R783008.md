## R783008 — A set Cloaked card that leaves unrevealed

Built (#783). A card set by its Cloaked that is destroyed or bounced before it reveals is a Trap for
those rules and reaches its owner's graveyard or hand as its printed card ([[R78]], [[R1045]]): no Cry
and no Death, since its text is dormant while it is set. Back in a hand it has its Cloaked again and may
be set anew.
