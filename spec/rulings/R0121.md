[[§4.2]] and [[R53]]: a forced attack skips declaration steps 1 to 3, spends no exertion, and may happen on the compelling player's own turn, so it is not "the opponent declaring an attack". The `forced` flag on the event exists for exactly this distinction, and a trap or trigger keyed to an opponent's declaration does not arm on one
