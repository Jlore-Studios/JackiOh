Every draw of its controller that took a card from their deck counts toward quest 1 ("draw 2 cards"), whether the card was kept, burned at the hand cap ([[R4]]) or cast on draw ([[R58]]), as #100 Ceaseless Void's draw counter counts draws ([[R55]], [[R225]]). A draw the draw limit stopped never happened ([[§2.4]]) and a fatigue draw takes no card, so neither counts
