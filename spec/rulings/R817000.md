[[§9.9]]'s deck builder (`build_ai_deck`) deals at most `AI_DECK.maxQuickdraw` (2) Quickdraw cards ([[§6.2]]) into any deck it deals: the AI's own at every tier, a player's random deck, All Random's and practice's, and a ladder bot's and its trio's. Once the deck holds that many, the cards an `include` forced in counted, the rest of the draw passes over every Quickdraw card; until then the draw is untouched. An `include` is always dealt, so a sweep that forces a Quickdraw card in keeps it, and the draw adds at most one more.

Quickdraw is never a theme. A theme owes `themeMinShare` of the deck (6 of 20), which two cards cannot meet, so the theme is rolled among the other tags the pool holds `minThemeSize` cards of. A caller that names Quickdraw as its theme still gets at most two, as a floor whose cards run out owes only what it has ([[R1370]]). Leaving Quickdraw out of the candidates moves the theme some seeds roll, so their decks are dealt again: the tutorial tier's frozen run ([[R290]]) was frozen again on a new series, and a practice game saved or kept before this whose deck is dealt again no longer folds to its hash, so it is refused as [[R668]] and [[R768]] refuse one. A match row keeps the decks it dealt, so no online match or replay moves.
