The greedy baseline (`greedy_action`: the quality gates' yardstick, the Duos greedy team's seats) and the skill miss (`one_ply_scores`, [[R2214000]], a ladder bot's missed decisions, [[R747000]]) simulate each candidate once, one ply ahead. Both sized one counter for all of them at `1 + AI_SEARCH.max_auto_answers` nodes a candidate, 9, while `simulate` charges a step's up-front surcharge first (`surcharge_nodes`): 100 for a play of #97 Zephrys or C+ #27 Zephrys Zealotism, `ai_turn_trap_nodes` for an attack My Pawn would answer, and `judged_card_nodes` a hand card for a play while M #71 Pareto Optimality judges the seat. A step whose surcharge the counter cannot pay is refused and scored as standing still ([[R4807000]]). So with fewer than 12 candidates a Zephrys play was never simulated, and when a surcharge did fit, it took the nodes of the candidates after it: with five cards in hand under Pareto Optimality, the greedy baseline never simulated the lethal True Strike it plays with four. Their comment said every candidate gets its one simulation.

**The rule.** The AI decides this; no game changes. Each candidate is simulated on a counter of its own (`one_ply_step`), sized to what that step may take: its own node, its up-front surcharge and `max_auto_answers` answers. So every candidate is simulated whatever the ones before it took, a surcharged one included, and a surcharge announced only by the step (a random cast that turns out to be a Zephrys, `announced_surcharge_nodes`) is taken from that candidate's counter alone. A candidate without a surcharge is simulated exactly as before.

**What it moves.** The greedy baseline now plays a card it used to skip, so the `ai-vs-greedy` gate's yardstick moves a little; `decide`, which sizes its counters from its budget, is untouched, and so is the 1v1 arena, where both seats play `decide`.

**Measured.** No arena screen reaches it, both seats there playing `decide` at full skill. The gates at smoke size, on origin/main 5991e8da39 with issue #4798's commits, with the three fixes of issue #4799 and without them: `ai-vs-greedy` 17 and 17 of 20 (14 needed), `ai-vs-random` 19 and 19, `hard-vs-easy` 17 and 17.
