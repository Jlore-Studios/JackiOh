This extends [[R185]]'s hidden-information rule to secrets. `redact` drops the other seat's unrevealed choices — the seat's own stay, and a revealed choice is public history both seats watched happen — and blanks the modes of their in-flight play of a `secret_modes` card, wherever the run is parked (the trigger queue, the work queue, a delayed continuation, the open prompt). `determinize` samples each dropped choice uniformly, as its last draw. States differing only in a dropped choice redact to the same hash. A play with no mode keeps no secret.
