## R926 — CN in a Bottle's (X) is printed only

Built. (X) is only in `cost`; outside a play, where no X is chosen, the card costs 0 ([[R65]]),
and nothing ever pays it: the card is replaced before it could be played, so its printed (X)
matters only to the catalog and the deck builder. The two scripts declare no cry hook; a cast of
one resolves with no effect. Glitch's aura, which numbers (X) off the stack, was never an
exception to this — it numbers the cost a card would pay, and this card pays none.

Superseded by patch v0.3.11 ([[R1149001]]): it costs (1) and casts on draw, so a bottle left in a hand is played at (1) and does what its draw would have done.
