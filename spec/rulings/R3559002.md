S+ #90 Meltdown reads "When this attack, gain +2 attack and take 2 damage". The brief reads it as the
existing `afterAttack` hook, which resolves once the combat's state check has run. By [[R2396000]],
the owner's ruling, "When" is `whenAttacks`: it resolves before any damage of the attack
([[R2285000]]), so it is that hook here.

On each attack, declared or forced, Meltdown first gains a lasting +N attack (+2, Radiant +4), then
takes 2 damage from itself, then strikes with the raised attack; Windfury's second attack does it
again from where it stands. A Meltdown that the 2 damage kills does not fight ([[R220]]). The
second line, "When this is Frozen, fully heal it", is the trigger on the Frozen mark landing on it,
which answers every freeze that lands ([[R3550002]]).

Rejected: `afterAttack`, which would strike with the old attack and gain the attack and take the
damage only after the fight.
