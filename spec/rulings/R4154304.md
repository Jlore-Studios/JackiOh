Issue #4154's "Nerf each result slightly (that are easy to nerf)" for TG #39, by [[R4154303]]'s rule (one step of the number an entry is for, a count of 2 or less and a price left alone):

| # | Entry | Through v0.6.6e | Now | Constant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | fortify | Summon 3 Rush Tokens, then Taunt and Divine Shield | 2 | `CHAOS_TG_RUSH_TOKENS` 3 → 2 |
| 2 | designs | Fill the hand with Goetzmann Game Designs, replenish 3 | replenish 2 | `CHAOS_TG_REFRESH` 3 → 2 |
| 3 | cards | 4 random Training Grounds cards at (1), gain 2 mana | 3 cards | `CHAOS_TG_CARDS` 4 → 3 |
| 4 | mana | Gain 6 mana, draw 2 | gain 5 | `CHAOS_TG_MANA` 6 → 5 |
| 5 | permanents | Summon 5 random Training Grounds permanents | 4 | `CHAOS_TG_PERMANENTS` 5 → 4 |
| 7 | army | Summon 3 Rush Tokens, Rush, Trample and Pierce for good | 2 | `CHAOS_TG_ARMY_TOKENS` 3 → 2 |

Entry 2's number is its Replenish, the fill having none; entries 1 and 7 lose a token and keep their keywords. Left as they were: entry 6 (two Radiant cards at (1) and 1 mana, each number 2 or less), the Golem, the start-of-turn Call ([[R3135000]]'s entry 9) and the recursion.

The numbers are the engine's (`config.rs`), not declared, so unlike [[R3135000]], which kept nothing, the table as it was through v0.6.6e stays in the engine as `CHAOS_TG_EFFECTS_V0_6_6E`: the same names, order and builders over the `_V0_6_6E` constants, each label the clause the card printed then, and the card keeps `script_v0_6_6e`, which rolls it, so a replay of v0.6.6e or before resolves and announces ([[R436]]) as it was played ([[R767000]]). It amends [[R2200000]] (three cards, four permanents), [[R2200001]] (two tokens, Replenish 2) and [[R2200003]] (two tokens).
