Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A hit whose `damage` says Armor took half or more of it ([[R1360]]: `absorbed * 2 >= absorbed + amount`, at exactly half too) plays a dull clank (`armorClank`, a muffled plate struck low) under its impact, and the effects layer flashes a small shield up over the target as the hit lands. A hit Armor took whole (`damageAbsorbed`, [[R1361]]) plays a bright ring (`armorRing`, a struck shield's metallic partials) and the layer blooms a full shield with a ring and sparks over the target; the target itself braces with a steel glint and no number, since nothing was dealt. A hit Armor took less than half of, or no part in, sounds and looks as before.

Both sounds are effects like any other, under the effects volume, and both come from the unit's lane, a hero's centred ([[R669]]). Both read the event's public numbers and nothing else, so a source or a target the viewer cannot read changes nothing ([[R203]], [[R202]]). The shield is a DOM flourish of the effects layer that paces nothing and is gone within the layer's tail of its entry ([[R200]]); under Reduce motion, the panel's or the system's, the layer plans nothing and no shield is drawn. A board-wide hit the fog sweeps over blooms the shield where a unit's Armor took its hit whole, and glances it where the Armor took half or more.
