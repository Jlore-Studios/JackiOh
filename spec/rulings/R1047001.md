`ended_at` is written by the match's own actor, so a live row whose fold keeps failing would hold both people (a new room, `DELETE /api/account`) for ever, which [[§9.5]]'s "no match and no player is stuck forever" forbids ([[R112]] holds it for a 1v1 match). The co-op sweep that folds a live match with no actor back tries again each tick until the match's ceiling ([[R1047000]]); a match past it that still cannot be folded back is ended by the sweep: it writes the `ceiling` draw, the result the actor would have written, and `ended_at` through the one write-once statement (`duos_matches_finish`), so the first writer stands and an actor racing it leaves one result. Both people are free from then on. It appends nothing to the log, and the end is logged as `duos.abandoned.ended`.

Rejected: ending a match after a count of failed rebuilds, which a short store outage would trigger on a healthy match.
