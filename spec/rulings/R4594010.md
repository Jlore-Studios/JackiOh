A permanent is any card in a unit or backrow zone — a face-down card in, a Dormant one out — exiled at random (`exileRandomFrom` off the field); with nothing to exile the count draws nothing ([[R129]]). Then this gains +5 Health, with nothing exiled too. It cannot attack ([[R61]]: it declares none).
