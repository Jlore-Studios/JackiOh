Twelve cards printed the same rider as a sentence — "lose N mana next turn", "you have N less mana
next turn" and their kin — for the −X side of [[R1848004]]'s next-turn rider (`next_turn_mana
{ amount: -X }`). They now print it as one keyword, [[§6.3]] "Overload X": "Overload (X)" is "you
have X less mana next turn", and "Overload your opponent (X)" (Core #21 Hinder) puts the rider on
the opponent's refresh instead. Nothing else changes: the rider is still summed with the turn's
other riders, floored at 0 by the refresh and shown as the existing badge ([[R169]]); S+ #82 Endless
Expedition and S+ #95 JC Backed keep their "for the rest of the game" sentence, and the cards that
gain mana next turn keep theirs.

- **Paid where the text puts it.** Unlike Hearthstone's Overload, which is paid on the play, the
printed Overload is paid as its text runs: as a Spell resolves, as a Cry runs, as a trigger fires.
A countered Spell pays nothing, and a Cry that never runs pays nothing — the same as today, since
no script, number or effect changed.
- **"(X), then (Y) the turn after".** M #19 Expedition123 and S+ #56 Impact Reactor already set the
second rider at the start of the next turn; the comma phrase prints that pair as one rider.
- **Jlarna's instalments.** M #89's repayments stay in their own ledger ([[R1224]]): each instalment
is taken off a refresh as an Overload is, so the word describes them without changing the credit
line.
