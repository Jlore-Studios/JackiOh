A trade offer (`trade_offer`; Stinkyscape #77 Yuma: "Trade offer your opponent at the start of their turn. You give them any card in your hand and they have to Discover a card in their own to give to you") is a delayed effect of its controller's, due at the start of the opponent's next turn, counted in that player's own turns (`TRADE_OFFER_TURNS`, [[R1181000]]), at [[§2.2]]'s delayed stage and so before that turn's draw ([[R62]]). While it waits it is a countdown badge on the offerer's side in both views ([[R169]]), and it comes whatever becomes of the card that made it ([[R76]]). Its steps are the engine's own, whatever card made it, as Meditative #95's rest-of-game effect is ([[R1241]]):

1. With a card in each hand, the offerer picks one of their own hand cards: a `hand` prompt of theirs, which opens on the opponent's turn as a prompt its card's controller holds may ([[R1279000]]). With either hand empty, nothing happens and nothing is asked.
2. That card goes to the opponent's hand as theirs, a change of hands as C #9's ([[R12]]), a full hand burning it into its new owner's graveyard ([[R317]]). Then the opponent Discovers one of their other hand cards: `DISCOVER_OPTIONS` of them drawn at random as the options of a `discover` prompt of theirs, on their own turn, its answer continuing the offerer's sequence ([[§10.6]]). With no other card in their hand, the trade ends there.
3. The Discovered card goes to the offerer's hand as theirs, the same way.

Each move is a `stolen` event, named to whoever can read the card where it lay and where it went ([[R466]]). Both prompts are ordinary ones, so `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal, the view, the random policy, the timeout ([[R79]]) and the AI answer them as they answer any other. The given card goes first, so the opponent sees it before choosing, and it is not among their options.

Rejected: a simultaneous swap with the offer hidden until both have chosen (Hearthstone's Theotar, the Mad Duke), which needs a view no prompt has; and offering the given card back, which would let the opponent undo every trade.
