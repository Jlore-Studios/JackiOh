## R783006 — Cloaked: Activate: Reveal

Built (#783). A card set by its Cloaked has one ability while it is set, its Activate: Reveal
(`alt_play::CLOAKED_REVEAL`), used in its controller's own main phase like any Activate ([[R384]]) and
by nobody else. It is offered, and the reducer accepts it, only while the card can come out whole
(`alt_play::reveal_can_complete`): a Unit needs an open, unlocked, unreserved unit zone, its own lane's
or else [[R64]]'s leftmost, and with none it stays hidden until one opens; a Spell needs what playing it
needs, a legal pick for each declaration it could not go without ([[R703]]); a Field Spell, standing in
its zone already, needs nothing. It has no timed reveal of any kind — not Knowledge Breaker's next start
of turn nor Paranoia's three chosen times — and may stay hidden as long as its controller likes.

The reveal is the firing a timed reveal is ([[R1041]], [[R1044]]): `trapFired`, then what the card
is. A Unit turns face-up and takes that unit zone ([[R383]]'s move: `animated`, never `summoned`,
[[R445]]), its Cry runs with its choices asked as prompts ([[R467]]), and it keeps the turn it was set as
its `summonedTurn`, so revealed on a later turn it may attack at once and revealed the turn it was set it
is summoning sick unless it has Rush or Charge. A Spell resolves at once, its targets, modes and X asked
as prompts, Echo repeating, then goes to its owner's graveyard or wherever its text says; its effects come
from a Trap, so no Spell Damage, and Immune to Spells does not stop them. A Field Spell turns face-up
where it stands, its Cry runs and its Aura starts. The Reveal spends none of the card's own Activate
abilities, and a paused reveal survives a JSON round trip like any firing ([[R113]]). Rejected: a reveal
that is no firing, which could not park a paused Spell and would answer nothing a Trap's reveal
answers.
