"Lose N mana for the rest of the game" is `changeMaxMana`: N onto the seat's persistent modifier,
max mana recomputed, and a loss comes off current mana too, never below 0, as stolen crystals take
it. It lasts because every refresh recomputes max mana from the modifier. A change that moves
nothing announces nothing. Rejected: a one-turn rider, which ends at the next refresh instead of
lasting the game.
