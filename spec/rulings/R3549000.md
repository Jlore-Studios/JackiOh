S+ #39 Mindustrial Cross-over reads "Discover a random Mindustrial Unit, give it Starpiece & Equip",
and its Radiant face "… a random Radiant Mindustrial Unit, give it Starpiece & Equip, it costs (1)
less". The spec has no Discover of a tag with a keyword given to the pick, so it is read as follows.

- **The pool.** One Discover of three ([[§6.3]] Discover), drawn without replacement, from the
  non-token Units of the Stinkyscape+ set tagged Mindustrial, the pool naming its set so that it
  reaches them while the set is held ([[R1420]]). It never offers the card that made it ([[R387]]),
  a Spell, so a Spell tagged Mindustrial is never an option either: the text says Unit.
- **"Starpiece".** It is Starship Piece (SP5), with Equip ([[§6.1]]). The pick reaches its caster's
  hand given both, as a card is given a keyword as it reaches the hand ([[R1878003]]): they are the
  card's own from there on, onto the field, so played as a Unit it may be Equipped into one of its
  owner's Units and its death fuses a copy into their Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]). A full hand
  burns the pick ([[§2.4]]). Added with `addRandomFromCatalog`'s `keywords` rider over a pool of the
  one card picked, since `addToHand` has no rider; [[R673]]'s Glitch odds therefore apply to the pick, as
  they do to every card added from a pool.
- **Radiant.** The pick is Radiant and costs 1 less, never below (0) ([[R65]]); the discount is
  printed, so a number of the script's and no param ([[R1748003]]).

Rejected: offering Units of every Mindustrial tag and type (a Spell is no Unit); giving the keywords
only while the card is in the hand; and a `keywords` field on `addToHand`, a change to the engine
beyond this part, which the one-card pool does without.
