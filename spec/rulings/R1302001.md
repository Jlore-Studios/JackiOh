A ladder game that ends by concede pays as the result the writer records ([[§2.5]]): the winner
`JLORE_LADDER_WIN` with `XP_WIN_BONUS`, the conceder `JLORE_LADDER_LOSS` with none ([[§14.8]]).
The hook reads the recorded winner alone, so a concede the engine produced and a forfeit the
server wrote for a disconnect pay alike. A bot's side earns nothing ([[R1454]]), winner or
conceder.

Hearthstone semantics: conceding is losing, as in Hearthstone, where the conceder takes the
loss and the opponent the win with its rewards. A concede that paid the conceder the win rate
would pay quitting, and one that paid nobody would tax a game the loser ended early.
