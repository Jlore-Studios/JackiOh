"Evolves after this attacks the enemy hero" is the card evolving itself ([[R3536001]]) once the combat of an attack whose target was the enemy hero is over, a forced attack ([[R53]]) as much as a declared one, and only while it is still on the field ([[R2285001]]); an attack on a Unit evolves nothing. With Endless Evolve ([[R5236030]]) every such attack evolves it again. Each evolution Discovers a combat keyword of [[R21]]'s pool it lacks ([[R5239004]]), granted to it: it is lost as the card leaves the field ([[R78]]), as any keyword a card is given. The Radiant face's "then another" is a second Discover once the first pick is in, so it never offers that pick again.
