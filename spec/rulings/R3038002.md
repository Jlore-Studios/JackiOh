The Drone Battleship's three layers (S #36 Drone Core X's pile, [[R1842000]]) printed the Starship keyword on both faces, which no rule read but #115 Scornboi's aura. They were never hulls: no piece fused into them and no launch was theirs. Since issue #3038 the keyword is Flying and Ranged on both faces, in its place in their keyword lines ("Flying, Ranged, Can't attack, Taunt, Armor 4", "Flying, Ranged, Can't attack", "Flying, Ranged, Charge, Windfury, First Strike, Brittle 3"), and everything else about them stays.

They carry no Starship tag, so they are no Starships ([[R3038000]]): no launch is offered or taken, and a Starship Piece that dies beside one fuses into its controller's Dormant Starship, summoned first if they have none, never into a layer ([[R1852000]]). Their Flying and Ranged rule as any Unit's: in Attack Position a layer is attacked only by Units with Flying or Ranged, so the Shield Layer's Taunt binds only them ([[§6.1]] Flying, [[R783002]]), and the Meltdown Layer takes no strike back from a defender without Ranged ([[R783000]]). Scornboi, which spares a Starship by its tag now, exiles them ([[R1850002]]).
