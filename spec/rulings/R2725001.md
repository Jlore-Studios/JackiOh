The issue asked that not every card take every version, Pocket giving its special arts to some cards,
and left the rule to the plan, recorded as data beside the registry. Each `version` and `augment`
entry of `cosmetics.json` carries an `offer`: a list of clauses, each naming any of `types`,
`rarities`, `sets` and `cards`. A clause takes a non-token card of the shipped catalog when every
field it fills lists the card's type, rarity, set and id and its `except` does not name the card; a
card is offered the entry when any clause takes it (`cosmetics::offered`). No other kind has an offer; a full art is offered on its own card
alone ([[R1313000]]), anything else everywhere. The registry's check refuses a version or augment
with no offer, the wrong id prefix, a card of its own, or a default or starter mark, an offer on any
other kind, and an offer naming no card of the catalog, in `cards` or in `except`.

Part 1's three share one offer: every Rare and Epic Unit, every Legendary and Mythic card, and the 21
full-art cards ([[R2655001]]), 243 cards across all seven sets that ship today; the Animated augment's
excepts the four Legacy cards it would take, whose picture never moves ([[R2725003]]), so it is offered
on 239. A test holds each
version and augment to at least `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_CARDS` cards and `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET` of
every set that ships, never a token, never every card, and the 21 among them. The client reads the
server's own reading of each offer, the offered cards by id (`COSMETIC_OFFERS`, generated beside
`COSMETICS`), never the clauses.

A version or augment a card is not offered is never dropped for it: a duplicate's roll and a cosmetic
pack's placement take only offered cards ([[R1301002]], [[R1306000]]). It is never sold for it: a
token or the Chase Shard bar asked for one is a 400, "that cosmetic is not offered on that card",
before anything is read or written ([[R1305003]], [[R1306002]]). It is never picked: `PUT
/api/card-picks` answers 400, `"fullArt"` or `"augments"` "is not offered on this card", and writes
nothing, and a match freezes none ([[R1834001]]). It is never shown: the Collection lists a card's
offered versions and augments alone, and a face wears none elsewhere. The dev fill grants each on
every collectable card it is offered (§14.10).

Amended by [[R2949001]] (#2949): a clause may take a seeded share of its cards, and the three no longer share one offer: Full art's is a seeded 60 in 100 of its Rare and Epic Units and its Legendaries and Mythics, the 21 always in, Animated's is as above, and Signature's is where both are.

Amended by [[R2940000]] (#2940): three more offers, each a seeded share of cards of its own (Alt art on half of every card but the Legacy ones, Sketch on Units and Legendary and Mythic cards, Alt full art on Epic Units and Legendary and Mythic cards but the Legacy ones), none on every card and no two on the same cards; part 1's three alone take every one of the 21.

Amended by [[R2942000]] (#2942): three more offers, each a seeded share of cards of its own (Extended art on Spells and Field Spells and Legendary and Mythic cards, Pixel on Rare and Epic Units, Traps and Field Traps and Legendary and Mythic cards, Comic on Rare and Epic Units, Spells and Traps and Legendary and Mythic cards), none on every card and no two of the seven versions on the same cards.

Amended by [[R2943000]] (#2943): two more offers, each a seeded share of cards of its own (Set showcase on Legendary and Mythic cards, Out of frame on Rare to Mythic Units but the Legacy ones and the Starship), none on every card and no two of the nine versions on the same cards.

Amended by [[R2944000]] (#2944): two more offers, each a share of cards of its own (Immersive on a seeded share of the Legendary and Mythic cards but the Starship, Prismatic on every Mythic card and a seeded share of the Legendary ones), none on every card and no two of the eleven versions on the same cards.
