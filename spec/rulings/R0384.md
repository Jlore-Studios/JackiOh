A card that prints "Activate: …" may use that ability once per turn, "Activate X: …" up to X times, and "Activate ♾️: …" any number of times, bounded by `ACTIVATE_UNLIMITED_CAP` (100) per card per turn so a game still ends. Only its controller activates it, in their own main phase with no prompt open and the game not over, while the card is on the field and acting — the top of its pile or a face-up backrow card; summoning sickness and exertion do not apply, since activating is not attacking. Uses are counted per card per turn on the instance (`memory.activations`), and leaving the field resets them ([[R78]]), so a card bounced and played again starts afresh, as a copy does. A cost written into the ability ("Tribute a Unit.", "Discard a random card.", a mana price) is paid as it is activated, an ability whose cost cannot be paid cannot be activated, and everything after the cost is the effect. The targets and modes the ability declares travel in the action, `activate { instanceId, ability?, targets?, modes?, tributes? }`, as a play's do ([[R81]], [[R90]]), a Tribute cost's paying unit travelling in `tributes` as a play's does ([[R101]]), `ability` naming which one when a card has several, and choices made while it resolves are prompts. Activating is not a play: it counts for nothing that counts plays (Combo, Quickstriker, Ceaseless Void, "whenever you play a card"), though what its effect plays or casts counts as usual ([[R70]]). `legalActions` lists `activate` from the same check that refuses it, as it lists #98's power ([[R43]]), and `activatePower` is kept as its alias so old logs replay; since the Heroic Power patch #98's powers are Activate abilities with a mana price ([[R752]]). Degrade and Upgrade move an Activate X by 1, never below 1, and Upgrade turns "Activate" into "Activate 2"; neither touches ♾️ ([[R386]]). An activation is reported by `activated { instanceId, ability }`
