Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to "go harder on alt art": keep the theme but move
it to another style or place, so each Alt art is clearly its own. [[R3396000]] did so for Classic+ and [[R3397000]]
for Meditative+ and Training Grounds; until this issue a later Stinkyscape card's Alt art redrew its own picture
re-seeded ([[R2940001]], [[R3403000]]): its scene's parts, or its own painter's picture, from another seed, which
changed a median 20% of the picture's pixels and as little as 1% (Jlockeed's Missile Pod).

**An alt recipe per card.** Each of the 54 cards of #3406's table, every Stinkyscape card from #91 on and every
Stinkyscape token offered Alt art (`version-alt-art`) but the Dormant Starship, draws an alt recipe of its own
(`stinkyscape91AltRecipes1.ts` to `stinkyscape91AltRecipes3.ts`, `STINKYSCAPE_91_ALT_RECIPES`, spread into
`ALT_SCENE_RECIPES` beside the other sets'), written from #3406's brief for it: the same subject and idea in another
place, time or story moment, in Stinkyscape's family. `sceneRecipeOf` reads it under the card's Alt art seed
(`<id>#alt`) alone, as [[R3396000]] set; the card's id, every repaint's seed ([[R3017002]]) and any other seed keep
the normal picture. Every level of Alt art draws the one alt picture ([[R3015002]]), base and Radiant.

**A piece of its own painter.** Eighteen of the 54 have no normal scene recipe: the fourteen Starship Pieces, whose
normal face draws its module in its bay ([[R3333001]]); Jlockeed's Siege Tank, Yamato Cannon and Missile Pod, drawn
the same way; and Dice of Doom, a Terminus card's own painter's ([[R3338001]]). Their normal face, and every seed but
the Alt art one, keeps that painter's picture byte for byte; only their Alt art seed finds the alt recipe, through the
scene the Stinkyscape painter tries first (svg.ts `sceneArtSvg`). Each stands its piece in the alt scene as a part the
pass added to the scene kit, never the module's own markup: a swarm of nanobots, a hull plate, a cartridge, an
overdrive engine, a warp ring, a rail gun, a great cannon, a missile pod, a capacitor cell, a flank pod, a launcher
tube and a hundred-sided die with no digits (`sceneKitStinkyAlt.ts`, spread into `SCENE_KIT` after every other
module), or a part the kit had (the tank, the crystal), under a sky that is neither black nor slate-900, the deep
space their normal pictures share. Dice of Doom, the one of the 54 with full-art motion, keeps a layer for each role
its motion names ([[R2715001]]).

**The same subject.** A figure both pictures draw is the normal one's person, its head, outfit and skin unchanged
(only its cloth, hair or held item may change); a person the brief keeps beside it (Scornboi's aide, ETHICS's five
members) is the extra figure at the same index in both pictures, with the normal one's head, outfit and skin; a subject
that is a part of the kit (Tmack Trav's and No-No Words' robots, the surfer's wave, the scout ship and its ore, the
light diagram, the souvenir drone, the console, the crate, the rocket and its capsule, the sack and its alien, the
wormhole, the ice block, the spirit, the shuttle, the stink cloud, the interceptor, the porthole, the credit chip,
the comet's ice, the force walls and their mech, the fighters, the warships, the probe, the asteroids, the spice
tower) stays in the subject layer; Pocket Peptides' vial, held in its normal picture, stands as a vial on the table;
and Megabase and Huge Asteroid, seen from inside, stand what is inside them (an escalator, a reactor core) in their
subject layer.

**Measurably elsewhere.** Each of the 36 alt recipes with a normal one shares no sky colour with it, and its subject
moves at least 8 units, or grows or shrinks by at least a quarter, or a figure comes or goes: a close-up, a wide or
distant view or one from above. In a real browser each Alt art window changes at least `ALT_PICTURE_CHANGE_MIN`
(0.6) of the normal window's points sampled on card-versions.cy.tsx's grid, counted with its threshold for a point
that differs, base and Radiant.

**What still holds.** Each alt picture clears its rarity's detail floor and the byte ceiling ([[R3331001]]), on
both faces, and an Epic or rarer one has far, mid, subject and near layers; prints no text; draws one geometry on
both faces, the Radiant one gilded under its rays, with no base colour in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]); is
apart from every other picture ([[R503]]); stands its emblem small inside the band every face shows; and keeps props
out of its subject layer. A Radiant face keeps its marks at every Alt art level. Besides the pieces, three parts join
the kit so that nothing a brief names is a stand-in: a pineapple, an escalator and a glowing seashell. A minor detail
the kit lacks is left out and named in the recipe's comment: Vxyl keeps the cloak on, A Very Dandy Lad strolls
without his parasol, Arctic Citadel keeps his parka on, the wizard's robe is plain (its stars the motes about him),
the Commander stands with her hands before her for want of a chin-on-fist pose, No-No Words' grandmother raises her
hand for want of a hand-over-mouth gesture, IN33DH3 sits rather than cross-legged, and Core System's tactical floor
has no dust sheets; and Faction: ETHICS's papers are
scrolls under every other member's arm, so six do not take its picture past the byte ceiling.

Rejected, beyond [[R3396000]]'s and [[R3397000]]'s lists: drawing a piece's Alt art as its module's own markup
converted into kit shapes, which would tie the scene kit to `starshipModules.ts`; the module redrawn in another bay,
which keeps the normal picture's look; adding the pass's parts to #3403's kit modules, each of whose parts some
normal recipe draws; new figure parts for the parasol, a starry robe and a folded cloak; giving the Dormant Starship,
which #3406's table leaves out, an alt recipe; and splitting the 54 cards into smaller pull requests, which the
issue allowed at about 40 cards a part and the three recipe files map onto.
