S+ #79's "Exile your deck. Start a 10 turn countdown; upon it reaching 0 win the game" exiles the whole library with no draw, so no fatigue as it goes ([[§2.4]]), and starts a delayed step "in `count` turns" ([[R1181000]]): a badge on its owner's side, read by both seats, counts down the turns the owner starts, the opponent's turns not at all, an extra turn counting ([[R846]]). At the start of the owner's `count`th turn from now, in the start-of-turn delayed stage before the draw ([[§2.2]]), the owner wins the game ([[R850]]): a hero at 0 still loses first. A second cast starts a second countdown. An empty deck is exiled as nothing, and the countdown still starts. The deck being gone, every draw after it is a draw from an empty deck and costs fatigue ([[§2.4]]); that is the race the card sets, which Bob's Plan's helper for it answers ([[R3569006]]).

Rejected: ticking on both players' turns (it is "turn count down", the owner's turns, as Supply Drop's are); and winning at the end of the countdown's last turn (the badge reaches 0 as that turn starts).
