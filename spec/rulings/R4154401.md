Issue #4154 asked to make the AI better at selling Stinkyscape #89 Spice, which sells from the hand at the Marketplace ([[R1861000]]). Its search already tried a sale ([[R1853004]]), but its evaluation priced Spice in hand as any (1) card, worth several times the Credits a quote brings at `market_credit`, so no sale ever read as worth making. This is a judgement about the AI, not a rule of the game, and changes no game.

While the Marketplace quotes it (`sell_quote`, [[R1853002]]), a card in the AI's own hand that sells from there (`sells_from_hand`) counts as the Credits its sale brings now, at `AI_EVAL.market_credit` each (`evaluate::hand_sale_worth`, `AI_EVAL.hand_sale_credits`), not as a card: a sale is on offer every turn of its holder's at no mana ([[R1853003]]), so a Spice it does not cast is Credits it has not cashed. The sale itself then moves nothing; a sale that completes a buy gains what the buy gains, a quote above the one the next turn start draws gains the difference (the line's end reads the requoted Spice on each determinization), and the cast, a Discover for (1), stays a play the search weighs against both. Without a quote, the Marketplace closed, it is priced as any hand card. `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps the flag off.

Not modelled: the cast Spice gives up when it is sold, a Discover the evaluation would price above the Credits. Rejected: pricing every Credit higher, which would move every Resource's quote and every buy.

Superseded by issue #4410 ([[R4410001]]): with the sale gone ([[R4410000]]), Spice in the AI's hand is priced as any (1) card and its Credits count once its play or discard gains them; `hand_sale_worth` and `AI_EVAL.hand_sale_credits` are gone.
