[[R2783000]] marked a card's Cry, Death, start-of-turn and end-of-turn hooks, Whenever triggers and Activate abilities, and left every other hook unmarked, so a card's "when this attacks" or "when drawn" had no moment for its sound to start on (issue #5189, part 2 of #5187). Hearthstone lights the card as each of its triggered effects resolves, whatever kind it is. The engine now marks the rest the same way, each `hook` named as its `Script` hook is: `onPlayHook`, `whenAttacks`, `afterAttack`, `afterAttacked`, `allyAttacked`, `allyAttack`, `startOfGame`, `entersHand` and `delayed`.

**When each is marked.** As [[R2783000]]'s are: once per run, after whatever announced the hook and before any of its effects, outside the run's own window of events ([[R136]]); a hook that fizzles is marked, and a continuation after a prompt is the same run ([[R113]]), so a hook that asks is marked once.
- An `onPlayHook` at [[§10.5]] step 3, each holder in [[R68]]'s order, before the played card's `cardAnnounced`.
- A Unit's `whenAttacks` ([[R2285000]]), then each `allyAttacked` of the defender's side ([[R1849008]]), after `attackDeclared` and its trap window ([[§4.2]]), before the combat's damage.
- After the combat's closing check: the attacker's `afterAttack` ([[R2285001]], run as the snapshot it fought with where [[R2396000]] runs it so), the defender's `afterAttacked` ([[R1026]]), then each `allyAttack` of the attacker's side ([[R2199000]]). `controller` is the seat each runs for, the defender's as it fought for `afterAttacked`.
- A `startOfGame` clause ([[R43]], [[R151]]) after its card's arrival (its `drawn`, its `summoned`, …) or at the start of the game, and an `entersHand` ([[R925]]) after it.
- A delayed effect at its [[R62]] point ([[§2.2]]), after that turn's `turnStarted` or `turnEnded` and, for a countdown, after its badge's `modifierChanged`. A rest-of-game effect ([[R458]]) is marked each time it runs; it has no card ([[R127]]), so its marker names its definition alone, `sourceId` `""`, to both seats, as its badge shows both seats its card's words ([[R169]]).

**What is not marked.** A hook the engine skips before running it (its card no longer acting, an attack that no longer stands, an attacker gone for an "After this attacks"), as [[R2783000]] has it; an "After this attacks" step that runs only Frenzy's "this may attack again" ([[R3543003]]), as no hook of the card's runs; the engine's own delayed kinds (a destroy, a discard), which run as verbs; and the watchers that re-enter a card's step outside [[R62]]'s stage.

**Otherwise as [[R2783000]].** Nothing answers the markers: they are withheld from the trigger loop, take no number in [[R68]]'s order and move no hash, so a recorded game replays to the same hash, and the golden traces' texts leave them out ([[R1956001]]). A seat that could not read the card where it stood as the hook began never sees the marker ([[R177]]; a drawn card's two reach its owner alone), and one naming a card that went to a hand since names it as the card is named now ([[R3182000]]). Unlike [[R2783000]]'s, these markers take no room in [[R3925000]]'s count, so a capped loop stops where it stopped before they were marked; [[R2783000]]'s still count. The testkit's invariants I18, I19 and I21 read through them, as they read past these hooks before ([[R4599007]]).

**Rejected:** leaving every marker out of the loop cap's count, which would move where recorded capped games stopped; reading the new markers as runs in the testkit's walks, which would end a Cry's run at an `entersHand` its own draw met; and the name `onPlay` for `onPlayHook`, beside the card's `play` sound hook. This amends [[R2783000]] (what is not marked) and [[R3925000]] (what counts).
