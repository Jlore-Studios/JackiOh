M #92 Unan and the Drone Battleship's Shield Layer redirect lethal damage their controller's hero or other Units would take to themselves ([[R1204]]). The fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) found the redirect carrying a Spell's lethal hit onto a guard that was Immune to Spells, which then took it: Unan's Armor absorbed it, or its Divine Shield popped, or it took the damage.
**The guard passes the hit by.** [[§6.1]]: a Spell doesn't affect a Unit Immune to Spells, and the guard catching the hit would put the Spell's damage on it. So a `self` redirect whose guard the hit's Spell cannot reach is skipped at [[§4.4]]'s lethal-hit point, as if it were not there: the next guard in the walk may catch the hit, and with none the hit lands where it was aimed. A hit from anything but a Spell (a Unit's, a Trap's, a Field Spell's, a sourceless one) is caught as before.
Rejected: letting the guard catch the hit and take nothing from it. That would cancel every lethal Spell hit on its side, which is more than the immunity gives: it keeps the Spell off the guard, not off the guard's allies.
A replay this alters is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]).
