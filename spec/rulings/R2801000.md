Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. The Starship is Stinkyscape's signature system ([[R1852000]], [[R1852003]]), and its fuse and its launch each played one plain row (issue #2801). The owner wants the launch to be a dramatic moment, issue #2782's set-piece tier, and the fuse a Signature.

**The fuse run.** The engine reports each fusion as a `fused` into the Starship and the `starshipFused` right after it ([[R1852000]]). The runner plays every fusion from there on into the same Starship of the same player, one after another, as one entry (`fuseRunAt`), of `starshipFuseMs(n)`: `STARSHIP_FUSE_MS` (0.4 s) and `FX_STARSHIP_FUSE_STAGGER_MS` (0.25 s) for each later piece, up to `STARSHIP_FUSE_STAGGERED_MAX` (6) pieces. It is not pinned, so the burst budget may squeeze it, and every beat keeps its share. Each dead piece's card back (never a face, [[R202]]) flies from where the board shows it, else from its owner's graveyard, to the Starship, one stagger after the one before. It lands at `FX_STARSHIP_FUSE_LAND_AT` of its share and bolts on there: a spark burst, a clank ring and a short shake. The run centres on the zone a Starship the pass summoned in the same batch entered (the board does not show it yet), else on the Starship. Any other `fused`, an Equip's, plays as its own entry, and [[R2791000]]'s Equip beat stays off a fuse run, whose piece copy never came from a hand. A Dormant Starship's face bolts on a hull plate for each piece fused into it, up to `STARSHIP_HULL_PLATES_MAX` (8), on its flanks, port and starboard in turn as its art's modules are, read from the view's `UnitView.starship`; a launched one wears none.

**The launch.** A `starshipLaunched` the seat can place claims its entry as the `starship` set piece (`starshipClaim`, after a wipe's and a quest's): `STARSHIP_LAUNCH_HOLD_MS` (2.4 s) at speed 1, held, capped at `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS`, scaled by the speed, set by Big moments and skipped by a tap, a click or Space exactly as [[R2785000]] holds every set piece, one per action. Its awakening plays first, as its own entry, which breaks the Dormant seal (#2435). Through the hold, the effects layer draws over the ship (`planStarship`):

- a 3-2-1 countdown, each count at its `FX_STARSHIP_COUNT_AT` share of the hold, the board shaking harder at each;
- its engines igniting in Tech's rocket exhaust (issue #2792) at `FX_STARSHIP_IGNITE_AT`.

As the hold ends, the ship lifts off and lands (`jk-starship-launch`, its row's 600 ms):

- the engines' full burn and a contrail climbing from it;
- an ember burst where its Dormant badge burns away;
- the landing as its launched self, its name on its face ([[R1852003]]), with a dust ring.

A bigger ship means more exhaust, a longer contrail and a heavier shake: each is the `STARSHIP_TUNING` step for its pieces, `small` from 0, `medium` from 3 and `large` from 6. Unheld (Big moments "off", or a second set piece in the action), it plays inside its plain entry with no countdown and lifts off at once. Under Reduce Motion nothing holds: the queue drains at once, and the layer draws one still frame of the launch (`planStarshipStill`), its flame and contrail standing for `FX_STARSHIP_STILL_MS`, holding nothing, as a Mythic's still card does ([[R2722001]]). Everything it starts lands inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]), and the effects speed scales it with the entry ([[R201]]).

**The sound.** The launch's bed (`STARSHIP_LAUNCH_BED`) is a sequence of existing `sfx.ts` recipes sent as the held entry starts: a `clockTick` at each count, each one higher, a `burn` at the ignition and, after the hold, a heavy `summon` at the landing; a bed's step may now be laid across the entry's motion after the hold (`after`). Its own row's `entrance` roars as the hold ends, when it lifts off, and a fuse run sounds once, condensed as a sweep's is.

**The cards.** Each gets an entry in Stinkyscape's table ([[R2784000]]) in Tech's rocket look:

- Jlockeed's Starship Retrofitting Program (#130), `cast: send`: the Jlockeed card it adds, a Starship Piece from then on, flies to the hand;
- the Launchers (#140 to #143), `cry: conjure`: each lands in a ring of exhaust;
- Launch Sequencer (T-AI-5), `cast: tithe`: its "Your Starship launch costs (1)" glints on its player's modifier badges and on their Dormant Starship, whose launch it prices ([[R1852006]]); with no Starship yet the price waits ([[R1881003]]) and only the badges glint;
- the Dormant Starship, `none`: its fuse and launch are the Starship's own.

**Hidden information.** The Dormant Starship and its launch are public. A Starship R97 hides (one since bounced into a hand) is the sentinel to the other seat: its fuse run and its launch place nothing, claim no hold and draw no cue, and its launch's own row plays. No cue names a card's definition or the ship's name ([[R202]]).

This amends [[R2784000]] (`tithe` also reads a price set for a player, an added `modifierChanged`), [[R2785000]] (a set piece's bed may lay a step after its hold, and the launch's still frame, unlike the `SET_PIECE_STILL_MS` it rejected, holds nothing) and [[R2791000]] (a fuse run's `fused` is no Equip).

**Open questions, rejected alternatives.**

- No still frame at all under Reduce Motion, as [[R2785000]] has for every other set piece, was rejected because the issue asks for one. The still frame holds nothing, so nothing waits on it. A person may still prefer none.
- An `activate` motif for the Launchers, whose Activates a launched Starship inherits, was rejected: `activate` hooks draw nothing yet. Their play's `conjure` ties them to the ship's exhaust instead.
- Keying the Starship's glint on the modifier badge's label was rejected as fragile (the view's `ModifierView` carries no kind). The glint fires on any price a `tithe` card adds while its player has a Dormant Starship, and today only Launch Sequencer names `tithe`.
- Widening `flight` to read `addedToHand` for the Retrofitting Program was rejected: `send` already flies a made card to the hand.
- Folding the `awakened` before the launch into the launch's entry was rejected: #2435's seal release plays on it.
