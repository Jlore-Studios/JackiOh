The Cry, when the Wall is played ([[R1]]), Locks each of its side's five unit zones, the Wall's own zone and the occupied ones included. Each zone it Locks gets one `locked`; a zone that is Locked already gets none, and nothing is evicted ([[§3.2]]). Until a zone is Unlocked, no Unit may be played there, a Stack play included ([[R688]]). A "fill your board" ([[R64]]) fills nothing there, whoever's effect it is (Meditative #97.9's fill among them), while a summon or a move still enters ([[R688]]). The Locks outlast the Wall, and an Unlock (Meditative #27, C+ #77) frees them. The Radiant face is the same, with Armor 2.
