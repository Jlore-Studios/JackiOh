While a card with the static flag `log2Utility: all` (FvH #92's base face) acts on the field, every card's Attack and max Health on the field, and every card's own cost, count as their number of binary digits, ⌊log₂N⌋ + 1 (`numbers::log2_utility`: 1 is 1, 2 and 3 are 2, 4 to 7 are 3, 9999 is 14); 0 and below are unchanged. The stats are counted after every layer of [[§10.4]], so current Health is the counted max Health minus damage and a damaged Unit whose Health falls to 0 dies at the state check. The cost counted is the card's own: its printed price or override with its own cost changes ([[R65]]), before its player's discounts and price rules, which apply to the counted number; an X cost is never counted. Two such auras do not stack: a number is counted once however many act. When the aura leaves, every number reads as before. The Radiant face's reach is [[R5237012]].
