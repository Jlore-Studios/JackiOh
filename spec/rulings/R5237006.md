A grant ([[R1107]], `grantAbility`) may now be an End of turn ability: `on: endOfTurn` on the grant (`Grant.on`, `GrantAbilityArgs.on`; absent is a Death, as every grant was). A card on the field holding one is an end-of-turn holder whether or not its own text has an End of turn, and at its controller's end of turn ([[§6.2]], [[R62]]) it runs `grants::end_of_turn_hook_of`: its own End of turn first, then each granted End of turn in the order granted, each with its grant's numbers, one list in R68's place among the end-of-turn holders ([[R68]]) and resumed as one. So FvH #79 Post Human Aesthetist's granted "End of turn" runs as the Human's own: for its controller, on its controller's turn, multiplied as its own end-of-turn hooks are. An End of turn grant never runs as a Death, nor a Death grant at the end of a turn. Like a granted Death, it shows on the card, a copy keeps it ([[R57]]) and it is lost when the card leaves the field ([[R78]]).
