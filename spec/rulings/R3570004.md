death watch is ([[R1882004]]): after each `starshipLaunched` of its holder, and of nobody else, the step runs as the
holder, casting a random Call to Chaos as a base cast with no running card, so a new chain with [[R28]]'s cap of its
own, then summoning a random (2) cost Weapon drawn only when a zone is open ([[R129]]). A player holds at most
`CHAOS_MS_LAUNCH_WATCH_CAP` of them, a roll at the cap resolving into nothing, and the watch answers at most
`CHAOS_MS_LAUNCH_WATCH_ANSWERS_PER_TURN` launches a player makes in one turn ([[R2176001]]'s shape), so the Scape
entries that launch Starships cannot loop. Its badge reads the clause as printed ([[R169]]).
