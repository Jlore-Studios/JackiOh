The client could not tell why a hit landed or a unit died: Cleave's extra hits were plain combat `damage`, and nothing said that a Trample excess reached the hero, that Lifesteal healed, that Poisonous killed or that Reborn brought a unit back; the poison cue guessed from the view (issue #2782). Hearthstone shows each of these. The spec says nothing of a cue, so `damage`, `damageAbsorbed`, `healed`, `destroyed` and `summoned` now carry an optional `via`, the keyword (`KeywordKind`) behind the event, on the wire only when set. No rule changes.

**One keyword per event, the hit's route first.** A hit's route is set by the verb that sends it: Cleave on each neighbour hit of §4.4 step 10 ([[§4.4]]); Trample on an excess step 9 sends on to the hero, or onto the target's neighbours and their hero ([[R1848001]]); First Strike on the strike [[§4.3]] moves ahead of the other, which needs exactly one First Striker in the combat — never two, never against a hero, and never for a Ranged attacker that takes no strike back ([[R783000]]). A hit with no route carries Pierce when it skipped Armor its target had at step 2 ([[R346]]; a hit Pierce skipped no Armor on carries none). A `damageAbsorbed` ([[R1361]]) carries its hit's route. A `healed` carries Lifesteal when it is step 8's heal ([[R85]]'s stated Lifesteal included).

**A death's keyword.** A `destroyed` carries Poisonous when a Poisonous hit (the source's keyword or a stated `poisonous`) marked the unit when nothing had killed it yet ([[R42]]); else First Strike when it fell to the strike §4.3 moved ahead, so it struck nothing back — the First Strike marker: the hit says First Strike, and the death says that no strike back came. The first keyword recorded for a unit is kept. A Sacrifice carries none, nor a death whose state check runs in a later action than the hit (a prompt between), nor a unit the hit marked for exile ([[R1124]]), which has no `destroyed`. A `summoned` carries Reborn for §4.5 step 4's return ([[§4.5]], [[§6.1]]).

**Who sets it.** The engine, at the verb that applies the keyword, never a card's script (CLAUDE.md rule 5): a hit's route rides its flags unserialized, and a death's keyword lives in the action's frontier, never in the state. The trigger loop holds every event without it (`as_rules_read`, as [[R1360]]'s `absorbed`), so nothing answers it and no hash moves. A view hides it with the card it is the card's: a hit's with a hidden source, a death's or a summon's with a hidden card ([[R97]]).

**Rejected:** the numbered `Keyword` object (Armor 2), which no cue needs; a list of keywords per event, which would have to order a Trample excess's Pierce against its Trample; and a `combatStep` field on combat hits, with which the client would still infer that the strike back never came.
