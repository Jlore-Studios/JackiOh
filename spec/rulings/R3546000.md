A GIGA permanent stands in its anchor (lane L of its row) and covers the other row's lane L and
both rows' lane L+1: four zones nothing else enters. Only fitting anchors are offered, zones its
own Tribute empties counting ([[R391]]); a Unit anchors in its unit row despite Flexible. A summon
takes the leftmost fit ([[R64]], [[R688]]) or a named lane that fits, else fails; a play is not
offered where nothing fits. Nothing stacks, equips, carries onto or captures with a GIGA, covered
zones included. Rejected: storing it in four zones, which would quadruple every zone walk for one
size.
