Meditative+ #5 Spirit Lash ("Deal 1 damage to all Units. Embiggened (1): With Lifesteal and Pierce") hits every Unit on both sides, one hit each, all landing before the state check ([[R59]]). The designer's "all permanents" are the permanents that take damage: a backrow card takes none unless it is an animated card standing as a Unit, and neither hero is hit.

The Embiggened hits carry Lifesteal ([[R85]]) and Pierce ([[R346]]) as stated on the effect, so the card prints neither keyword ([[§6.1]]). The Radiant face's Echo 1 ([[§6.2]]) repeats at the version played: an Embiggened play repeats Embiggened ([[R81]]).
