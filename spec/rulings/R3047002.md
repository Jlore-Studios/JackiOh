After a roll has drawn an offer's card and whether it is Radiant ([[R2250002]]), it draws what the offer is Buffed or Nerfed by, in this order and nothing else:

1. **Buffs.** A roll at `MARKETPLACE_BUFF_CHANCE`, 0.2, one draw. While it lands, another roll follows, so an offer is Buffed 0 times with chance 0.8, once 0.16, twice 0.032, and so on.
2. **Nerfs, only when no Buff landed.** A roll at `MARKETPLACE_NERF_CHANCE`, 0.1, repeated the same way. An offer that took a Buff draws no Nerf roll, so it is never both and the Nerf chance is that of the offers with no Buff: 0.08 of all of them.
3. **The price spread** of [[R1853002]], as before.

**The bound.** The repeat stops at `MARKETPLACE_TUNE_MAX`, 8 Buffs or 8 Nerfs: at the bound no further roll is made and no draw is spent. "Again while it succeeds" read without a bound leaves a roll with no worst case, and its chance of passing the bound is 0.2⁸, about 3 in a million, for a Buff and a hundred-millionth for a Nerf, so the bound is never met in play. It is what keeps a Nerfed lot priced: the cheapest base is 8, and 8 × 85%⁸ rounds down to 2, never 0.

**The price.** The offer's base is the designer's price ([[R3047000]]) with the Radiant doubling, then each Buff × `MARKETPLACE_BUFF_PRICE_PERCENT` (120%) or each Nerf × `MARKETPLACE_NERF_PRICE_PERCENT` (85%) of what it was before that Buff or Nerf, compounding, rounded down to a whole number of Credits once, after the last, as the spread's tenth is read in whole Credits ([[R1853002]]). `base` is that price, and the spread then moves `price` within a tenth of it. Three Buffs of 8 come to 8 × 1.728 = 13.8, which is 13. Rejected: additive steps (+20% a Buff, −15% a Nerf), which take a price to 0 after seven Nerfs; rounding each step, which turns three Buffs of 8 into 9, 10 and 12 where they compound to 13, and walks a Nerfed 8 down to 0 in seven steps; and rounding to the nearest, which stalls a Nerfed price at 3.

**Stored and shown.** The offer keeps the counts (`MarketOffer.buffs` and `nerfs`), absent while 0 (D14), and the view gives them to the player the offer is rolled for (`MarketOfferView`), so the client can mark it. **A lot an effect added for the rest of the game** ([[R1853005]]) is never rolled, as it is never Radiant: it holds neither count, and the roll draws nothing for it. What the counts do to the card bought is [[R3047003]]'s. The draws are in the order [[§10.7]] lists, which the rules test replays by hand.
