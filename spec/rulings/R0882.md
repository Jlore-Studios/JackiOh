A valid recipe compiles into a transient definition (MD-A32) whose id is `craft:` plus the digest
of the recipe's canonical JSON — the R179/R468 pattern, kept whole on `CardDef.craft`, from
which the scripts rebuild in any process, as a Fuse's rebuild from its ingredients. The
definition is Mythic, of the Meditative set, not a token; its `loc` is the counted lines
(`CRAFT_LOC_SKELETON` plus the hat and verb lines); every number is a declared param `bK`
(numbered in hat-then-effect order, the one walk the definition and the scripts share), so a
Nerf or a Buff moves it like any tuned number (B3.4). A crafted card survives the JSON round
trip and replays exactly.

The Radiant face doubles every number and stat (`CRAFT_RADIANT_MULTIPLIER` 2, R275 by
construction): the params carry base and Radiant values, the stats and Armor double, and a
crafted Unit with no number beyond its stats gains the cheapest table keyword it lacks (Deft).
Armor and Echo are face numbers, not params — Nerf and Buff never move them.
