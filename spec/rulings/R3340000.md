Part of #3330 (issue #3340, blocked by #3339). The owner asked that interactive cards respond instead of their art just moving a bit, and that the animated looks shine in play. [[R3046000]] held a board face's art still, so a player's animated or signed look stopped moving the moment the card was played.

**Motion on the board.** A board face (a Unit's `MinionFace`, a face-up backrow card's compact `CardFace`) moves its art as the full face does ([[R2715001]]) when its card wears an animated or signed level ([[R3015002]]), and when it is a Mythic, in any look, as its rarity's due. `boardLookOf` carries the look's `animated` and `signed` flags, so the board face and the full face read one decision. A board face draws no signature, no band sweep and no drift: its name is hidden or sits in a band, and the moving art is the picture's own layers. Every other board face draws its still picture, as before.

**One budget.** The motion slots (`COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX`, [[R2715001]]) are shared by the hand, the board, the foils, the finishes and the borders, and are no longer first come first served: they go, in order, to
1. the face under the pointer or holding focus, and the unit that has declared an attack;
2. Mythics, the newest one played first;
3. Legendaries;
4. then every other face, newest first.
A face's age is fixed for its mounted lifetime (for a board face, when that face enters play), and changes of rank keep it, so leaving a card does not make it the newest. A face that stops wanting a slot gives the claim up, but a pause while the tab is hidden or the face is off screen keeps that age for when it returns; only unmounting discards it. A slot freed by a release (a face unmounting, wanting no more, a tab hidden) passes to the next face in the same tick, which is inside `--motion-base`; there is no extra fade. A face that holds no slot draws its still picture. Motion pauses when the tab is hidden (`visibilitychange`) and when the face is off screen (an `IntersectionObserver`, where the browser has one), giving the slot back. Animated foil off, the Reduce motion setting, the system's reduced-motion setting and Fx intensity "Off" still hold every face still (so a face with no depth holds its foil still under the intensity off too, amending [[R3344000]], whose faces with depth alone did); at intensity "Low" only the faces that rank first, a Mythic or a Legendary may take a slot. A grid's lazy faces claim none.

**Cards that answer.** A board face whose art is moving draws its picture in three layers inside its window: the backdrop (the picture with its subject and its particles left out), the subject (its layers alone) and the motes (the particles alone), each an image of the one picture, so the layers' roles are the painters' own and every card, procedural, Stinkyscape and Training Grounds, gets them. Reactions use `transform`, `rotate`, `translate`, `scale` and `opacity` alone, inside the reduced-motion gate:
- *Hover or focus:* the subject turns and leans toward the pointer (`--art-aim-x`, `--art-aim-y`, written on the face from the pointer's place in it), and the motes scatter and settle. Keyboard focus alone, which has no pointer coordinate, uses a small fixed upper-right turn and lean; the pointer's place wins while both are on the face. The focus a click or a tap gives the card it lands on is the pointer's, not the keyboard's, so it ends as the pointer leaves.
- *Press:* the subject flinches inward and springs back.
- *Attacking:* as `attackDeclared` plays, the subject swings toward its target on the lunge's own vector and the motes fire out and fade back.
A face whose art does not move (no slot, art that never moves, a delivered file, a restyled picture, a Starship's, a Legacy card's) answers a hover or a press with its whole art swelling slightly or pressing in. So does a face that draws depth planes ([[R3344000]]: an Epic or rarer face's picture, whose window holds its far plane and whose other planes stand over it): the planes are its layers and take the motion between them, and the window is not split again.

**Marks of game state.** A Unit's art wears what the view says of it, read from `UnitView`'s numbers and keywords alone (CLAUDE.md rule 7):
- *Damage:* cracks, one more of them for each quarter of its health it is missing (`ART_WEAR_LEVELS`, 1 to 4), each a stroke grown from the art's edge toward its middle from the card's id, so a crack at one level is the start of the same crack at the next.
- *Buffed:* a glow in its card's theme colours, when its attack or its health reads buffed.
- *Frozen:* frost over the picture.
The marks are still drawings, so a face under reduced motion, with Animated foil off or holding no slot shows them all the same; only their fades are gated. They sit over the picture inside the window, and a face that is not art-first and the art-first one wear them alike.

**More recipes.** Every Legendary and Mythic of the catalog has a motion recipe of its own (`FULL_ART_MOTION`), none borrowing one by id: `motion.test.ts` lists those that still borrow, and the list is empty. The recipes are built from the card's type (a Unit's subject bobs, breathes, sways or hops; a Spell's breathes with a flaring light; a Field's back and over swell; a Trap's props turn or tick), its tags and set (the particle kind), and a running count that makes each one unlike the next. Every other card keeps the motion it borrowed before, so no still picture or golden hash changes.

Amends [[R3046000]]: a board face's art is still unless it holds a motion slot as above, and its tiers' motion, drift, sweep and signature remain the full faces' alone.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. The marks read only the numbers and keywords a unit's view already shows its viewer; a face-down card has no `MinionFace`.

Rejected: a first-come-first-served queue, which let the oldest cards on a long board hold every slot while a new Mythic, or the card the player pointed at, stood still; drawing the signature on a board face, which has no name to sign; one image per face with the particles left in it, whose motes could not scatter apart from the subject; a fade between a released slot and the next face's, which would hold a free slot idle; and cracks for each missing point of health, which on a 30-health unit would bury its art.
