S+ #61.1 feeds "every Unit" a Shawarma at its controller's End of turn ([[R62]]), the effect depending on how many that Unit has eaten: the first +3 health, the second Can't attack, the third Can't defend and Brittle 2 ([[R385]]), every later one Freeze. Both sides' Units eat, a Dormant one not. The count is the Unit's own, in its `memory` (the new verb `remember_onto` writes a plain key onto another card), so every Law shares it and [[R78]] clears it when the Unit leaves the field. On the Radiant face "discipline" is read as stopping at one: the Law's controller's Units that have eaten one eat no more, while the enemy's go on.

Rejected: one count per Law, which two Laws would each start over.
