Classic #5 Tesla deals its damage to the one enemy Unit whose arrival reveals it, and then remembers that it has revealed (`memory.revealed`): its trigger never answers again, so a Unit summoned or played after it, that turn or later, is not hit, and Tesla stays where it animated (in Defense Position, [[R383]]) or, with no open unit zone, face-up in its backrow zone. When one effect summons several Units (a Cry's tokens, a recruit), the first to arrive reveals it and is the only one hit. An Immutable arrival still reveals it and takes nothing ([[R781002]]).
