**Why.** Decisiveness ([[R4552001]]) asks how far a card's play moves its player's chance of winning, so each turn needs a chance of winning at its start. The AI's static evaluation scores a state but is no probability, and the engine cannot call the AI.

**The rows.** Each seat of a game record written since this ruling carries `swingTurns`, one row per turn the seat took, in the order taken:

- `turn`, the turn counter (§2.5) as the turn began;
- `eval`, the AI's evaluation (`evaluate`) of the seat at the start of its turn (`NextSwing::Seat`) less its opponent's (`NextSwing::Enemy`), in thousandths of the AI's units (`SWING_EVAL_SCALE`), rounded. It reads the true, whole state: the game is over and nothing is hidden from a statistic;
- `acting` and `facing`, the cards acting on the seat's field and on the opponent's (Units, Field Spells, Laws among them, and face-up backrow; a face-down Trap is left out), each a dealt card's catalog id or a Created card with its maker ([[R4552002]]).

The row is written after the turn's start has resolved (its draw and its start-of-turn triggers), and no row is written for a turn that begins on a finished game. A turn that begins and ends inside one action (one with nothing but ending it left to do ends by itself, [[R345]]; the autopilot plays one, [[R1183002]]) still has its row, read off the state the action left, since the fold sees no state between. The record keeps the raw evaluation, never a probability, so a refit of the weights rewrites no record. A record written before this ruling has no rows and counts toward nothing of [[R4552001]]'s; nothing is backfilled, since a record is filed once and the totals cannot fold it again.

**Who writes them.** The AI's fold (`jackioh_ai::swing_turns`) replays the log from the same start and through the same steps as the engine's `summarize_game` (`recorded_start`, `read_steps`), so the rows are the game the record summarizes. The server calls it under the record's catalog pin beside `summarize_game` (R767000), and so do the development runs ([[R378]]) and `stats from-selfplay`, each through `jackioh_ai::summarize_with_swing_turns`, which reads the summary and the rows off one fold of the log (`summarize_game_with`), so a finished game's log is still folded once (#2213).

**The win probability.** `P_s = σ(β₀ + β₁·eval + β₂·turn + β₃·[s went first])`, eval in the AI's units. The four weights are named constants (`WIN_PROBABILITY_INTERCEPT`, `_EVAL`, `_TURN`, `_FIRST`), the logistic fit of each row's seat result (a win 1, a draw ½, a loss 0, as [[R1843004]] reads a result) on the 1,990 rows of 120 development games (Brier score 0.216, against 0.249 for the turn and the first player alone), by `cargo jackioh stats calibrate`. `WIN_PROBABILITY_AI_GENERATION` names the AI generation ([[R1367000]]) they were fit against, since a change to the AI's evaluation moves the raw scale. The fit's rows are kept as a fixture, and a test holds the weights to the fixture's fit and checks the model's reliability over it: in each tenth of P with 100 rows or more the observed win rate is within 10 points of the mean P, and its Brier score beats the model of the turn and the first player alone.

**Rejected.**

- **Storing P.** A refit would have to rewrite every record.
- **The rows in the engine's own fold.** The engine cannot call the AI (CLAUDE.md rule 4's crate graph), and the evaluation is the AI's.
- **Forcing a refit when the AI's evaluation changes.** Nothing in the learn lane reads the weights, so a stale fit only blurs Decisiveness until the next `calibrate`; the generation constant says which evaluation they fit.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
