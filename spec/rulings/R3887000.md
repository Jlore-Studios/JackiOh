Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes. The text comes from the public catalog and the face model (`jackioh-shared` `face`, issue #3106); the client restates no number (rule 9).

**Why.** "Show card text" ([[R3838000]]) puts the words back on each face, but they stay on a small card face, sized to fit its box, and are hard to read while browsing; the Cards tab's Table view shows no card text at all. The breakdown's "Tokens this card makes" lists only the tokens a card's text names ([[R279]]), as small faces, so a non-token card it names (a Wincon card Bob's Plan can give) is missing and none shows its words at a size you can read. Someone browsing the statistics could not read what a card does without leaving the page. The owner asked in issue #3887 for a button that shows each card, and the cards it references, as readable text.

**The button.** The Cards tab's options row holds a "Plain text" button after "Show 95% ranges": an on/off button in the `Segmented` look with `aria-pressed`, as the page's other on/off controls are ([[R3324003]]). It is not the header's "Show card text" and does not replace it.

**Cards.** While it is pressed, every card the Cards tab lists shows as text instead of a face, in the Table and the Grid views and in the Tokens and Other created cards sections (The Coin, which is no catalog card, keeps its plain name):

- its name (as the reader reads it), cost, type and tags;
- its Attack and Health, where it has them;
- its whole rules text at reading size, and its Radiant text under a "Radiant" label, with the Radiant type line when it differs from the base's.

The words are the face model's, drawn by `RulesText`, so glossary terms, Radiant marks and reference names print as they do everywhere else; a reference stays a mark and opens nothing, since the text sits inside a row or a button. Pressed again, the page is as it was.

**The breakdown.** While it is pressed, the card breakdown dialog shows the card's base and Radiant text as plain text in its details column, and below it a "Reference cards" list of every card in the card's `refs` ([[R279]]), tokens and non-tokens alike, the card itself left out. Each entry shows the card's name and its own plain text and links to the card in the Almanac. The "Tokens this card makes" section is not shown, since the list holds the tokens. With the button off the dialog is as it was.

**Where it is kept.** The choice is the page's own, kept on the device (`localStorage`, `jackioh.plainText`), apart from "Show card text"'s key: the next visit opens as the last one left it, and where storage is blocked it lasts the page. It never writes the Full-art cards setting and touches no game.

Rejected:

- A three-way choice with Show card text (Full art, Card text, Plain text), which would change the Almanac's shared button, out of scope here.
- Printing the words inside the face's fitted box, which is what Show card text already does and is what made them hard to read.
- Listing the tokens in their own section beside the Reference cards, which would show each twice.
