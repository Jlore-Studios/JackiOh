Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or the engine. The issue (#3893) resolves touch hold behavior for Mythic-tier cards and cards wearing full-art levels (`isFullBleed`) on phones and coarse-pointer touch devices.

**Touch hold shows words.** On a touch screen, pressing and holding a Mythic card or any card wearing a full-art level opens its inspect sheet (`InspectSheet` in game) or detail view (`CardDetail` in the deck builder, Almanac, Collection, and pack opening) with its full rules text (`Extended`, [[R2718000]]), glossary, and state notes:
- The card face prints no rules box in full art, so the hold provides the only way to read the full text on mobile devices without hovering;
- The inspect sheet and detail view each contain a "Full screen" button (`FullScreenButton`, [[R3345000]]), making the full-screen art view one tap away;
- The view opened by touch hold stays open when the finger lifts, so players can comfortably read rules text and glossary entries.

**Mouse click-and-hold keeps opening full-screen view.** On desktop, holding the primary mouse button on a Mythic or full-art card continues to open the full-screen art view directly ([[R3345000]]). Hovering with a mouse already displays the enlarged hover preview with the card's full text panel (`Extended`), so no rules text access is lost.

Amends [[R3345000]]: replaces the touch long-press trigger for the full-screen view with the inspect sheet/detail view trigger.
