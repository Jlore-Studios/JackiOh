The offering is rolled from a table of lots, `MarketLot { pool, count, stockMin, stockMax }` in `config.rs`, in table order. Each lot draws `count` different cards from its pool, uniformly, one at a time, each taken out of the pool, while the pool lasts. A pool is one of three things: every Resource in the catalog, as "gain a random Resource" reads it ([[R1840002]]); the ids the lot names, those the catalog holds; or every card of a set that is no token, in catalog order. A set lot names its set, so [[R1420]]'s gate on pools that name none does not reach it. Each card is Radiant `MARKETPLACE_RADIANT_CHANCE` (20%) of the time. Each card is stocked a whole number in the lot's range, drawn once and the same for each player, and each player buys from their own stock ([[R1853003]]). The draws are made in this order: the card, its Radiant roll, then its stock, with no draw for a range of one number.

The designer's "every 6 turns" counts the game's own `turn`, which counts every player's turn ([[§2.5]]). The offering re-rolls at the start of the first turn `MARKETPLACE_CYCLE_TURNS` (6) or more after the turn it was last rolled. A Marketplace opened on turn 9 holds its offering through turn 14 and re-rolls as turn 15 starts. A re-roll is a fresh roll of the whole table, and it zeroes every purchase, so both players' stocks are full again. The rejected reading, six of each player's own turns, would make a cycle twelve turns of the game and tie a shared offering to one seat's clock.

The set's table is its data (S21): four of the Resources at stock 4–8, three of the twelve v3–v5 Weapons, both Warp Disruptors, both Scanning Probes (stock 6 and 3), two Drone Cores, one Launcher, two Starship Pieces, two Stinkyscape cards and one of the 10 AI cards (stock 1–3 for the rest). Until that patch ships, `MARKETPLACE_TABLE` is empty, so an open Marketplace offers only the lots effects add. The mechanic reads any table, and its tests register a fixture one (`testkit::register_marketplace_table`). No replay fold reads the table. The patch that ships it owes each shipped version its own table, as [[R1846003]] says of the card metrics, if a later patch ever changes it.
