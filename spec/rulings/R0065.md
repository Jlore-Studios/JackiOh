Start from `costOverride`, else the printed cost (Ceaseless Void's computed cost, the chosen embiggen price); add `costMod`; add player discounts and increases (since patch v0.2.0 also Traps that cost (0), (3)+ Cost cards that cost (1) more, Spells that cost (1) more, and a next Trap or Field Spell discount); apply Professor Curvature if the result is then 4 or more ([[R363]]); floor at 0; then apply any floor the card carries (C+ #14 Forever&'s, [[R410]]), which holds after every discount; and last, a rule that says a card can't be played (C #68's Radiant face) takes it out of play whatever it costs. Outside play (library, hand, GY, pools, filters, comparisons) an embiggen card's printed cost is its base price and an X-cost card's is 0, except that an X-cost card on the field costs the X it was played for wherever a rule compares or counts costs ([[R396]]). An X-cost card being played costs exactly X: `costMod` and discounts don't change it, and a `costOverride` makes it free while X is still chosen up to current mana. Heroic Power costs (0), and its powers' X is a mana price paid as each is activated ([[R752]]). The player discounts and Professor Curvature are prices for a play — [[§6.3]]'s cost is what a card costs to play now, #35's discount is for "the next Spell you play" and #78's for "your cards" this turn — so they apply to a card where a play takes it from (its controller's hand, or a graveyard its controller may play from, [[§6.3]] Play, [[R393]]) and to no other: a card in a library, or in a graveyard no permission lets its controller play from, is read at its own cost, its `costOverride` or printed cost plus its `costMod`, by #30 ([[R24]]), #94 ([[R66]]) and a Recruit's filter alike, as Hearthstone's hand discounts never reach the deck or the graveyard
