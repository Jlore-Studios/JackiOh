[[R3100000]] gave the engine's `combat_forecast` to a tutorial lesson's coach alone. Players misjudge trades everywhere else too: a blow passes a Divine Shield, Armor, Pierce, the target's cap and Indestructible on its way ([[§4.4]]), and First Strike decides who lands first ([[§4.3]]). Every seat's own view now carries the forecast of its attacks:

- every practice snapshot, a lesson's and a free game's and a dungeon fight's (`PracticeSnapshot.forecast`);
- the 1v1 server's `view` frame to a seat, and the shared match host's (`SeatMessage::View.forecast`);
- a Duos seat's `view` frame, holding only the entries of the attacks its `legal` lists, in that order, since Duos offers fewer of a duel's attacks than the duel allows.

A watcher's view frame never carries one, as it carries no legal action. The server reads it under the match's catalog, as it reads the legal actions ([[R767000]]), and it enters no log, state or hash.

While the viewer has an attacker selected or dragged, hovering a legal target with an entry previews that one attack alone ([[R3615000]]): the target wears the damage the blow deals (`−N`) and the attacker wears what the target strikes back (`−N`, on a unit), each as a tentative hit marker, with a skull on whichever is destroyed (`kills`, and `survives` false) and the Divine Shield mark struck through when the blow breaks one (`pops`). A target with no entry, and any target while none is hovered, shows nothing. Each marker sits over its own card out of flow, takes no click of its own and shows only while its target is hovered. The client picks the entry by the `attack` action's ids and draws its numbers as they are; it computes none (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The forecast reads the face-up board alone, no hand, library or face-down card ([[R292]]), so it is a derived fact and no promise: a face-down trap or another hidden answer can still change the outcome, and the label's title, its accessible name and the setting's hint say so. A setting, Show attack outcomes, on by default, hides it. A lesson's board draws no label, so the trades it teaches stay the player's to find, and its coach reads the snapshot's forecast as before. A replay draws none.

Hearthstone semantics: damage reads as the damage pipeline reads it, the label is a derived fact and not a rule, and a hidden card's effect is not foretold.

Rejected: working the numbers out in the client (CLAUDE.md rule 7, the copy [[R3100000]] removed); sending the forecast in practice only, which the issue leaves to the owner and which one `None` in each server's `push_view` would restore; drawing labels in a lesson, which would answer the questions the lesson asks; sending a Duos seat the raw duel's list, which would name attacks it is not offered.

Amended by [[R3615000]] (#3615): the preview shows only for the hovered target, each number on the card that takes it, as a tentative hit.
