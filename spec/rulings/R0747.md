The designer's follow-up comment (MaxGoetzmann, #354): a card should go back to the hand of the player who controls it, not of the player who owns it. A steal on the field changes control only ([[R15]], [[R171]]), so a bounced stolen unit used to land in its owner's hand, the seat that no longer had it. Bounce returns a permanent from the field to its controller's hand, and the card is the controller's from then on: its owner becomes the controller, as it does for a card a steal takes off the field ([[R12]]), so a hand holds only its holder's cards and every rule that reads a hand card's owner (its price, its discard, its hand triggers, Joro's interception, a Transform, a neighbour check) reads the holder. A death or a burn after that lands in the new owner's graveyard ([[§3.2]]); the `bounced` and `burned` events name the new owner. For a card its owner controls nothing changes. [[R78]] still resets the instance on the way out; the hand cap is the entering hand's; a unit-token card still vanishes ([[R11]]). The same bounce serves a rotation ([[R14]]), a board swap ([[R88]]) and Rollback's step 1 ([[R419]]). It covers a permanent the field gives up: a card returned from the graveyard or exile, or a Spell after it resolves, goes to its owner's hand ([[R746]]), and so does C #14 Shadowstep's return of a Unit that died (a stolen Unit of yours goes back to its owner's hand, [[§3.2]])
