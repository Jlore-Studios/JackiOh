The daily fused-pair sample played Stinkyscape+ #160 Icarus fused with Classic #88 Siphon Squad, so the Unit carried the Squad's "When your opponent has no Units, Tribute this" ([[R403]]). A Mr. Vanilla attacked it, and the forecast ([[R3100000]]) said 4 dealt, 4 taken, no kill, attacker falls. The attack went as forecast, but the Mr. Vanilla was its controller's last Unit. The state check after the combat found the condition holding and sacrificed the fused Unit, so I18 read the attack as a kill. The forecast settles the deaths after a combat ([[R4379000]]) but models no Tribute condition, as its own documentation says. I18 now skips, by a named condition, any attack made while a permanent of either side has a "When …, Tribute this", face-down ones included, as R403's check reads them. An attack with no such text on the field is judged as before.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and an invariant that checks it may judge only what it can see.

Rejected: modelling Tribute conditions in the forecast's settle. A condition is a script hook that reads the whole board, which may hold a face-down card the forecast must not read ([[R292]]). A face-down Siphon Squad is live ([[R403]]), so a forecast that ran it would show what the board hides.
