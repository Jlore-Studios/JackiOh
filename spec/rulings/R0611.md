Every rated game is recorded under its id — a match's, or a series' — once; rating the same id again changes nothing. The record carries the season, the patch and build version, the catalog version, each side's pilot (`human`, or `ai` with the bot id), the result (the winning side, or a draw) and the reason, and both sides' hidden ratings and visible ranks before and after (a ladder bot's side carries its season row's ranks as a person's does, never a Jlorious place, [[R640000]]; a bot side recorded before that ruling, or one whose id names no bot profile, has a rating and no visible rank, [[R610]]).
