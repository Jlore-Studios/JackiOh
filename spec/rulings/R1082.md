The deaths Eschews counts (MD-D15) are your Units' deaths, tokens included and each death of a Reborn Unit counted, since this entered the field ([[R78]] resets the count). It is Tributed at the next state check once they reach the number (C #88's Tribute-when, [[R403]]). Its end-of-turn cast draws from the non-token cards tagged Human, Book or CN plus the ten AI generated cards ([[R1421]]), each definition once, never Eschews ([[R387]]).
