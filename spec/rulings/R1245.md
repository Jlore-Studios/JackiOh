CN Golem shuffles `viruses` (1) CN-Virus (#90.1) into its controller's opponent's deck for each Unit it is [[R42]]'s killer of, Cleave and Poisonous kills included, while it still stands on the field as that `destroyed` is dispatched: a Golem that died in the same combat shuffles nothing, as for every field trigger. The virus is the opponent's card in their deck, as #90's are, shuffled in by a public trigger and recorded in their list ([[R311]]); a full deck refuses it ([[R80]]). The Radiant face shuffles Radiant CN-Viruses and adds Windfury ([[R636]]), the first card to print it ([[§6.1]]).
