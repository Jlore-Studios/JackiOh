CN Golem shuffles `viruses` (1) CN-Virus (#90.1) into its controller's opponent's deck for each Unit it is [[R42]]'s killer of, Cleave and Poisonous kills included, even when it has left the field since ([[R2396000]]): its "Whenever this destroys a Unit" answers the kill as the snapshot it killed with, so a Golem that died in the same combat shuffles its virus all the same, for the player who controlled it then. Classic+ #19.5 Bot Loser's and Training Grounds #9 Ravager's "After this destroys a Unit" answer only while the card still stands on the field as that `destroyed` is dispatched: one that died in the same combat gains nothing, as for every field trigger. The virus is the opponent's card in their deck, as #90's are, shuffled in by a public trigger and recorded in their list ([[R311]]); a full deck refuses it ([[R80]]). The Radiant face shuffles Radiant CN-Viruses and adds Windfury ([[R636]]), the first card to print it ([[§6.1]]).
