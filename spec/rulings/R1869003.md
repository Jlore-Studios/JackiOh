"Aura: Every card in your deck and graveyard has Flashback" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #152 Sanctum) is an aura ([[§10.4]] layer 5) whose one entry reaches the cards in its controller's library and graveyard, read off their zone alone, so it reads no layer and cannot recurse. While Sanctum acts on the field each card in that graveyard has Flashback ([[R783012]]) and is offered as a play from there at its cost, once: the play writes its `flashbackSpent`, which keeps the lent keyword off it for good ([[R783013]]). The opponent's cards are not reached, and Sanctum leaving the field ends the aura at once. A deck card holds the keyword, as [[R1194000]] lets an aura reach a hand, but nothing plays a card from a deck, so it matters only once the card is in the graveyard. No view carries a card's layered keywords, so none shows the lent Flashback; the viewer's own graveyard cards it admits are listed as plays, or say why they are not ([[R1571000]]). Rejected: lending to the graveyard alone, against the text's "deck"; and refreshing a spent Flashback, which [[R783013]] keeps gone for good.
