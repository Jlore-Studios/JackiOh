Meditative #95's ninth entry gives its caster a rest-of-game start-of-turn effect ([[R458]]): from their next turn on, at the start of each of their turns, it casts a random Call to Chaos ([[R1240]]), a base one, with no `self`, so each cast starts a new chain at cast depth 1 under [[R28]]'s cap per chain. The effect is the engine's own: its hook is `@chaosEternal`, which `work::script_step_for` answers itself, whatever card's script the effect names, so it re-enters as any rest-of-game effect does ([[R127]]) and leans on no card file. It is not one of the engine's delayed hooks (`effects::delay::run_engine_delayed`'s destroy and discard), which a rest-of-game effect never reaches. Its badge reads "At the start of each of your turns, cast a random Call to Chaos" on both seats ([[R169]]). The effects stack, and a player holds at most `CALL_TO_CHAOS_ETERNAL_CAP` (3) of them: a roll of the entry for a player who holds 3 resolves into nothing, with no re-roll, as [[R87]]'s recursion at the cap does.
