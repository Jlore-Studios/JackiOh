`decide` searches its turn on the first sampled world (determinization 0) alone and replays each finalist's line on every other world ([[§9.9]], The opponent's reply). A line that draws a card and then plays it is cut on each world whose shuffle of the seat's own library dealt another instance: that world never drew the card the line plays ([[R4447000]]). With `AI_SEARCH.replay_fill_steps` at 0 the replay ended its turn at the cut with its mana unspent, and since [[R4608000]] a line that won on world 0 alone is ranked by its mean on those other worlds, so a win that every draw gives read as "draw and pass" wherever world 0's draw was not repeated. Issue #4697 measured it on 70926614d9: on the AI's turn at 2 crystals, Stockpile and Mr. Vanilla in hand over a library of True Strikes, the opponent on 4 health, the AI won the turn on 13 of 40 seeds and on the rest played the 4/4, then Stockpile, with no crystal left for what it drew. The lethal solver cannot take the line either: it keeps a line only when each step, by instance, is legal on every world.

**The rule.** `AI_SEARCH.replay_fill_steps` ships at 3, the value [[R4447000]]'s tests replay with, and nothing else of that ruling changes: a cut replay drops the rest of its line and plays that world's own turn on greedily (`fill_turn`) before it ends, on its slice of `steps × (branching + 1)` nodes. A cut replay is then evidence of what the turn gives on its world, not of a turn passed with mana unspent, so [[R4608000]]'s test of a lone win on world 0 sees the win on every world that drew a winning card. `decide` keeps that slice back beside each other world's reserve, up to 126 more of `AI_BUDGET`'s 1,200 nodes (21 for each of three finalists on two other worlds), taken from the beam's slice, so other decisions move a little. The ladder bots and the Duos AI team decide through `decide`, so they move with the practice AI. Where only some draws win, the fill finishes each world's turn with that world's own draw, so the line is ranked by how many worlds win, as R4608000 intends.

**What moved.** Two tests pinned what the shipped switch gave. `r4447000_a_pinned_decision_keeps_its_action_line_and_nodes` (`crates/ai/tests/ai/replay_fill.rs`) pins the decision as shipped: Stockpile, then the 1-drop it drew on world 0, then the 4/4, in 171 nodes, where at 0 it was the 4/4, then Stockpile, then a drawn card, in 137, the other worlds' cut replays of the Stockpile line having ended with their crystals unspent. `r3696000_on_a_tight_budget_the_supply_drop_line_costs_no_world_its_score` (`crates/ai/tests/ai/meditative_plus.rs`, [[R3696000]]) sweeps budgets to 330 nodes instead of 200: the other worlds keep 126 more nodes back, so its Supply Drop line is first replied to at 295 nodes rather than 165, and the sweep must still reach a budget that replies to it.

**Its value.** FORK_SCREEN_PLACEHOLDER

Rejected: ranking a finalist by world 0's score alone when its other worlds' replays were cut (it ranks a draw-then-play line by world 0's own draw, and gambles again when only some draws win; it is the fallback should the screen drop the fill); a lethal solver that matches a drawn card by its definition instead of its instance (it weakens the solver's proof on every world); and keeping the fill unless the screen's `forkEdgeHigh` is below 0, as the issue first proposed (the maintainer held it to `training/improve.md`'s rule instead).

Hearthstone has no counterpart: it adds no rule of the game; its one constant is `AI_SEARCH.replay_fill_steps`.
