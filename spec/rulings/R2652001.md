Before issue #2652 a player saw only what a coin or a die did. Since then every coin flip and die roll a card makes is reported in the event stream ([[§10.3]]) as `rolled { player, instanceId, defId, kind, sides, rolls }`: `kind` is `coin` or `die`, `sides` the die's faces (a coin is a two-sided die, face 1 heads and face 2 tails), and `rolls` one entry per coin or die in draw order, each holding `faces`, every face it drew (its own roll, then each extra roll the card's Lucky and its controller's Luck made of it, [[§6.1]], [[R987]], [[R1438]]), and `kept`, the index of the face the roll kept: the first heads ([[R1440]]), or the first face Dice of Doom wants, 67, else the first face drawn.

One batch is one verb call or one hook. #4 Gary the Gambler sends two: his stat coins (5, or 10 on the Radiant face) before the `buffed` they decide, then his rider's one coin before its `keywordGranted`, since the buff is shown between the two flips. C #65 Ace in the Hole's end-of-turn coin, C+ #19.3 Mid Loser's Cry coin and S #154 Dice of Doom's die (two d80s on its Radiant face, one batch) are each one batch, reported before anything they decided. A verb whose target is missing draws nothing and reports nothing. Call to Chaos's own report is `chaosRolled` ([[R436]]), and a pack's, an offer's or a craft's roll is no coin and no die.

The coins and dice draw exactly as before: the verbs `flip_coin` and `roll_die` take the draws `Rng::lucky_coin` and `Rng::lucky` take, and a card script that rolls in its hook returns `report_rolls` with what they drew, so no card mutates state ([[§10.7]]). Nothing answers a report: it never joins the trigger loop's frontier and takes no number in [[R68]]'s order ([[R1361]]), so every state a game passes through hashes as it did and a recorded game replays to its final hash ([[R768]]). Who sees a batch is [[R2652000]].

The client plays each batch as one panel ([[§10.10]]): every coin spins and every die tumbles at once, each lands on its kept face with the extra draws beside it, and the outcome's own animation plays after it. Its timings are named constants scaled by the effects speed ([[R201]]) and cut to nothing under reduced motion, and the game log and a live region say the result in words.
