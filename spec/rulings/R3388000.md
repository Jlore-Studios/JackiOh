Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to "go harder on alt art": keep the theme but move
it to another style or place, so each Alt art is clearly its own. [[R3396000]] and [[R3398000]] did so for Classic+
and Core; Classic's Alt art still redrew the card's own scene re-seeded ([[R2940001]], [[R3352000]]), Corpse
Plantation's its proof scene ([[R3331000]]), which changed a median 53% of a Classic picture's pixels, nearly all of
it the sky behind the same subject, and as little as 13% (Exile).

**An alt recipe per card.** Each of the 50 Classic cards offered Alt art (`version-alt-art`) draws an alt recipe of
its own (`classicAltRecipes1.ts` to `classicAltRecipes3.ts`, spread into `ALT_SCENE_RECIPES` after the other sets'),
written from #3388's brief for it: the same subject and idea in another place, time or story moment, in Classic's
family. `sceneRecipeOf` reads it under the card's Alt art seed (`<id>#alt`) alone, as [[R3396000]] set; the card's
id, every repaint's seed ([[R3017002]]) and any other seed keep the normal recipe, Corpse Plantation's proof among
them. Every level of Alt art draws the one alt picture ([[R3015002]]), base and Radiant.

**The same subject.** A figure both pictures draw is the normal one's person, its head and outfit unchanged (only its
cloth, headgear or held item may change: the toe cracker's cloak is full of autumn leaves, the soldier carries the
card in place of his spear, the duelist on the winter roof wears red, Divine Favor's figure is the old fisher with
silver hair, the Plague Bringer clutches his souvenir); a Unit drawn as a figure keeps one; Felinor Feelings keeps its
kneeling warrior, Ancient Acquisition its digger (in a diving helmet), Nature Titan its resting hero, Common Resources
its rival farmer and Pile On its two warriors, each the normal picture's; and a subject that is a part of the kit
stays in the subject layer. Where a brief brings a new person, an extra figure stands for them: Shadowstep's
merchant, Anti-Greed Machine's treasure hunter, Corpse Plantation's three farmhands and King Wagtoggle's shepherd.
Where a brief is a close-up of hands or of an object, the figure goes: Book of Blood, Prep and Final Gambit draw open
hands, Plague Nuke gains one, Flame Lance draws the lance's fiery tip and the shield alone, and In Too Deep's
adventurer has gone down the shaft.

**Measurably elsewhere.** An alt recipe's sky shares no palette colour with its normal recipe's, and its subject
moves at least 8 units, or grows or shrinks by at least a quarter, or a figure comes or goes: a close-up, a wide or
distant view, one from above or from below. Both hold for every card, not one or the other. In a real browser each
Alt art window changes at least `ALT_PICTURE_CHANGE_MIN` (0.6) of the normal window's points sampled on
card-versions.cy.tsx's grid, counted with its threshold for a point that differs, base and Radiant.

**What still holds.** Each alt picture clears its rarity's detail floor and the byte ceiling ([[R3331001]]), on
both faces; prints no text; draws one geometry on both faces, the Radiant one gilded under its rays, with no base
colour in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]); is apart from every other picture ([[R503]]); stands its emblem small
inside the band every face shows; keeps props out of its subject layer, and In Too Deep, the one Classic full-art
card offered Alt art, marks its rope and packs `props`, so its props beat still moves ([[R2715001]]). A Radiant face
keeps its marks at every Alt art level. Two parts join the scene kit for the briefs' key creatures, in a module of
their own (`sceneKitClassicAlt.ts`) spread in after Classic's own and before the Stinkyscape Alt art pass's: a frog
(Transmutable Toxins' frogs swelling and shrinking on the pink river) and a shark (the one Turtinator swivels its
cannon toward). No existing part changed, so every normal picture draws as before.

**Stand-ins.** A brief's smaller details are drawn with parts the kits already hold, each named in its recipe's doc
comment: cyan rays fanning down from the Palantir's orb for its beam, a gloved reaching hand holding the
InfiniScepter, lanes and red flowers for Pickle's paths and poppies, a small void swirl for Exile's portal, a road
painted red and white trees for Book of Blood's stream and birches, a black ghost figure for Shadowstep's shadow,
violet flowers for the lavender and the crocuses, a mesa for Counterspell's sea stack, three steam curls for Lizard's
Breath's wisps, a temple for the tribunal, bushes and ferns in coral paint for Turtinator's reef, a snowfield for
Rapid Draw's roof, a cascade for the alpine stream, an awning for the Cookie Guild's umbrella, sparks for Joro's silk,
a space helmet for the diver's, a big crate for the auction block, the vat spilling its brew for the lab's outflow,
grey spores for Plague Nuke's ash, a cargo net for the fisher's net, a cot for Hired Shrimp's bunk and a helmet set
down beside him, steam for the farmers' breath, boards for Pile On's pier, ice shards for the Goliath's icicles, a
boat for Living Bomb's barge and the Power to Thrive's riverboat shrine, a statue and a boulder for the recycler's
broken golems and the glow chute for its hopper, brimmed hats and rakes for Corpse Plantation's straw hats and
scythes, an item sack for the Plague Bringer's souvenir, the fireball for Flame Lance's tip, rows of whitewashed
houses for Lockdown's city seen from above, and, for In Too Deep, the spiral shaft small for the cavern's mouth, a
thread painted brown and tied to a post for the rope, and duffels for the packs. Joro hangs upright from his strings:
a figure cannot be turned upside down.

Rejected: splitting the 50 cards into pull requests of about 40, which the three recipe files map onto should a
person want them split; a slime or a koi for the frogs and the shark, which would not read as the brief's creature;
a turned figure for Joro hanging upside down, a new figure field for one picture; a figure behind the close-ups of
hands and objects, which would hide what the brief frames; a different sky or composition rather than both, which
lets a picture change by its sky alone, as before; changing a figure's head or outfit, which would make it someone
else; new parts for the small details the stand-ins draw; and alt recipes for the repaints, which the issue does not
ask.
