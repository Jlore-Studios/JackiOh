Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): it is the client's — no rule reads it, no state hash moves for it, and a recorded game's replay keeps its final hash. A card's sounds sit on its hooks, from one list in the client: a Unit's `play`, `attack` and `death`, a Spell's, Field Spell's, Trap's or Field Trap's `cast`, and a summoning card's `trigger` (M #63, [[R1088]]). A hook gives a voice line (a voice and its text), a named effect from the effects bank (one of the procedural recipes, at its own pitch and gain, its pitch varied a little on each play), or both, and with both the effect starts first and the line a moment after. `play`, `death` and `cast` sound at [[R204]]'s moments, under [[R203]]'s redaction: a card behind the sentinel plays no effect, and a Trap's set none. An effect is a sound, not a line: it never waits for the one voice channel, so it plays at its moment whatever is speaking, while its line takes its turn on the channel by [[R204]]'s priorities like any line; where a condensed burst keeps one line it keeps one effect too, the most important by the same ranks. An effect plays under the effects volume and mute, with voice lines on or off, and ducks the music like a line ([[R631]]). A Unit's `attack` hook plays when the viewer picks up their own Unit that `legalActions` lets attack: the drag lifting it, or the click that chooses it as the attacker (click-click counts once). It plays again each time the Unit is picked up, but a pick-up within a short gap of the last one plays nothing, and each one cuts off what the last is still playing, its line included; its line cuts in on any other. Nothing plays on the drop, on a cancel or when the attack happens, and the opponent's attacks play no attack hook. The voices bank, the effects bank and every card's hooks are one hand-edited JSON5 file, its cards in catalog order with each card's name in a comment beside its id; it is checked when the client loads, and a bad voice, effect, recipe, hook or card id, or a missing text, fails naming its path. A voice line's file is rendered from its voice's backend fields and its text alone, so moving the lines into the file rendered none again
