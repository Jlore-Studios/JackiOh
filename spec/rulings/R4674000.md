Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes. The client reads only the patch list `GET /api/stats/cards` already sends ([[R1973002]]).

**Why.** The patch tabs read newest first, left to right, but the arrows ran the other way: the left arrow stepped to an older patch and the right arrow to a newer one. Pressing the right arrow, toward the older tabs, moved left to a newer patch. The owner found this confusing and asked for the arrows to follow the tabs.

**The rule.** The patch arrows sit at either end of the tabs and follow them as drawn: the left arrow (`←`) steps to the newer patch (`next`, [[R1973002]]), toward the current patch, and is disabled on the newest; the right arrow (`→`) steps to the older patch (`previous`), toward the older tabs, and is disabled on the oldest the page lists. Each names its patch: `Newer patch (v)` on the left, `Older patch (v)` on the right, in its `aria-label` and its `title`, with no patch to name at its disabled end. The test ids stay tied to direction, not side, so `patchPrevious` is the older patch (now right) and `patchNext` the newer patch (now left), and the e2e helpers keep working. Arrow keys inside the tabs ([[R3324000]]) are unchanged: they still move along the tabs as drawn.

Hearthstone semantics: a stats site's steppers follow the tab order.
