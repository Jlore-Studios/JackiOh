Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: Toxopid, "No longer ignores Taunt". Both faces of S+ #121 lose "Ignores Taunt." and the `ignoresTaunt` flag [[R3560007]] gave it, so [[§4.2]] step 3 binds it as it binds any attacker: with a Taunt it may legally attack on the other side, a printed one or Defense Position's, it may attack only a Taunt. No card prints the flag since; the engine and the AI still read it, for the replays. Replays of the versions through v0.6.13h fold under `script_v0_6_13h` (and through v0.6.6h under `script_v0_6_6h`), each carrying the flag as they did ([[R767000]]).
