Stinkyscape #4 Olivian Expanse reads "Discover a Terminus card. If your deck is empty, it costs (0)", and its Radiant face adds "If this was also the last card in your hand, you win the game when you play it." [[§6.3]]'s Discover says nothing of a pool named by a tag no pool takes, and nothing in the spec lets a card carry a win.

- **The pool.** The Terminus cards are the set's six Terminus tokens, #4.1 to #4.6 ([[R1857002]]), and Core #100 Ceaseless Void (tagged Terminus since [[R1837000]]): seven, one of each. Terminus is no `POOL_TOKEN_TAGS` tag ([[R382]]), so the Discover names the seven by id while the set is held ([[R1420]]) and offers three different ones to its caster alone ([[§10.8]]). The pick reaches the hand on its base face, the Radiant face's too.
- **Its price.** Both conditions are read as the Spell resolves (this card is out of the hand by then) and carried to the answer: a library with no card in it gives the pick a `costOverride` of 0; with a card left, it keeps its printed cost. A full hand burns the pick ([[§2.4]]).
- **The win.** On the Radiant face, with the library empty and no other card in the hand, the pick carries the win: `effects::WIN_ON_PLAY_KEY` in that one instance's memory. Its play, a cast too ([[R70]]), takes the key off it at [[§10.5]] step 4 and sets its player's won flag, which the state check that closes that play holds (`winGame`), a hero at 0 losing first ([[R850]]). Only that instance carries it: a copy of it, a Transform of it and a pick a full hand burned carry none, and it goes with the rest of its memory once the card reaches a graveyard or an exile pile from the hand ([[R215]]). Holding it wins nothing.
- The key is absent from every game no Radiant Olivian Expanse is played in, so no shipped game changes.
