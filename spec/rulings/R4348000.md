Blocking an account deletes the notification rows between the two accounts, either way: every row whose recipient is one of the two and whose actor is the other goes, read or not, from both feeds at once ([[§9.4]], [[R4039000]]). No third party's row is touched, and none with no actor. The unread counts drop with the rows, since the rows are gone rather than hidden: the list, its pages and the badge stay in agreement.

Unblocking restores nothing, as with the shares the block ends, and a row written after an unblock arrives as before.

Rejected: hiding the rows at list time instead. The list pages rows and the social read counts them, so a hidden row would still light the unread badge and shorten pages.
