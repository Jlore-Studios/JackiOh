Stinkyscape #4.1 Farewell reads "Exile all permanents and every card in both graveyards", and its Radiant face adds both hands and both decks. [[§6.3]]'s Exile of "all" reaches what stands on the field, and a card dormant under a Stack pile is not on the field for effects ([[R13]]), so a plain exile of every permanent leaves the cards beneath each pile's top.

- **A pile goes whole.** `exile_all_piles` exiles what the scope names, both sides' unit zones and backrows in [[R68]]'s order, face-down cards included, then the cards that pass uncovered, until a pass finds nothing new: every card of a Stack pile goes. A Dormant card stays, since no effect reaches it ([[R1839000]]), and the walk ends on it.
- **The order.** The field goes first, then its caster's graveyard and the opponent's, so nothing the field loses lands in a graveyard already emptied; the Radiant face then each hand and library, its caster's first. Each player's next draw is a fatigue draw.
- An exile fires no Death ([[§6.2]]), and a unit token ceases to exist ([[R11]]). Farewell itself is resolving throughout and lands in its owner's graveyard after it ([[§3.2]]).
