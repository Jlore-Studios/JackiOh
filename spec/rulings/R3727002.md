[[R265]] opens both mulligans at once and [[R268]] gives them one shared deadline in a live match. In a daily game each seat still owing its mulligan holds the move with its own window of the whole time per move, from the moment the game began; a seat that has answered holds nothing while it waits, and the other keeps its own deadline. The first turn starts once both have answered, with a fresh window for its active player, not what was left of the mulligan's. Two mulligans that run out at the same moment expire in seat order: Player 1's is the one that loses ([[R3727000]]), since one loss ends the game and the engine has no "both lose".
