S+ #142's tenth entry, "Cast a random Call to Chaos", draws from the Call to Chaos pool of the sets that ship
and never this card ([[R387]]: a random pool never offers the card that made it). Only the entry's own pick leaves
this definition out: another edition's recursion may still draw it, and every cast counts toward [[R28]]'s chain
cap, which ends the chain whatever is drawn.

Rejected: a chain-wide exclusion, which no card prints and which would change the other editions' pools.
