The Terminus token Tears of the Kwzki Tree says "All cards for the rest of the game are Radiant". The verb `make_all_radiant` sets one flag on the game, `GameState.radiantWorld`, absent until the card resolves so that a game that never casts it hashes as it did before. While the flag is set, every settled state check (§10.3, after the Deaths and before the Evolve texts) makes every card of both players Radiant, in every zone but the resolving and the gone: the hands, libraries, graveyards and exile piles, the field and a pile's buried cards, the active player's side first ([[R68]]). A card made later, a draw from a library, a card added to a hand or a Unit summoned, is therefore Radiant by the next check, and one a De-Radiant turned back is made Radiant again there. It is [[R74]]'s Make Radiant for each card, so a Unit's damage and buffs stay and no Cry fires ([[R22]]), and the sweep draws no random number.

A public pile (the field, a graveyard, an exile pile) cues the cards the sweep changes, as Make Radiant does. A hand, a face-down trap or a library that holds any card not yet Radiant cues all its cards, the Radiant ones too, so the number of cues says nothing the other seat may not read about which of them changed ([[R97]], [[R177]], [[R440]]).

Rejected: reading the face off the flag instead of the card's `radiant` (every layer, view and replay would read it, and De-Radiant and the copies of a card that keep their face would have no instance to ask); and making only the cards present at the cast Radiant, which is not "for the rest of the game".
