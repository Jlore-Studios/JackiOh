Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A held set's lines are written with its cards and rendered only when it ships ([[R1420]]), so the Stinkyscape set's release (issue #1884, [[R1857000]]) owes the files of every one of its 442 lines at once: a play, a death and an attack line for each of its 107 Units, tokens included, and a cast line for each of its 121 other entries. The shipped sets' 887 files took about 9.6 MiB of [[R1448]]'s 12 MiB, counted in 4 KiB blocks, and the set's lines, at the same mean size, take about 4.8 MiB more, so they do not fit.

`VOICE_BUDGET_BYTES` (`apps/web/src/audio/constants.ts`, and `gen-voice.mjs`'s `BUDGET_BYTES` beside it) is 16 MiB from the release, still counted in 4 KiB blocks, and every line still stays within 4 s ([[R501]]). Nothing else changes: the renderers, the voices, the per-line caps, and the client's fetch of a file only when it needs that line (`engine.ts`'s `fetchCached`), so the budget bounds what is deployed, not what one player downloads. Rejected: a lower bitrate for every file, which would re-render and re-check every shipped line for the sake of one set, and dropping lines from the set, since every Unit has its three lines and every other card its cast line ([[R1448]]).
