A fusion joins its ingredients' texts, one after another ([[R77]], [[R102]]), and each opens with its keyword line ([[R366]]), so a fused face printed every ingredient's keyword line: an Equipped Unit printed its Weapon's Equip and Starship Piece on a line of their own, and a keyword its host prints twice. The face model (`fused.rs`, `merged_keyword_lines`) prints a fused definition's keyword lines (a definition whose id is a fusion's, [[R179]]) as one at the head of its text: a keyword line is a line every item of which is a keyword kind's label, numbered or not, or a Tribute or Echo count; the items keep their order, one printed twice printing once, and every other line keeps its place after them. A text with fewer than two keyword lines is unchanged, and so is a face printed in Chinese. The fusion's keywords were their union already ([[R77]]), so only the words change, and a Starship still prints its pieces in place of its text ([[R2249000]]).
