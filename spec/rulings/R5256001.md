FvH #39 Whisperer of KY's list names no zone for "every spell". The build reads it as the Spells in its controller's deck as the Cry runs, read once ([[R113]]): the copies it shuffles in are Spells too and are not copied again. Each Spell gets the declared number of copies (`copies`, 1, Radiant 3), each a copy of that card ([[R57]]: its tuning rides it, and a Radiant Spell's copy is Radiant), each with Cast on draw ([[R5238004]]). The deck cap holds, so copies past it are not added ([[R80]]), and the chain of casts its draws make stops at the engine's cap ([[R58]]). A deck with no Spell gets nothing, and Units are never copied.
