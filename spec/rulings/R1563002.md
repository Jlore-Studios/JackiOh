Devil's Pact prints Spell Damage +1 (Radiant +2) since #1563, the first Field Spell to print it. [[§6.1]]'s sum read only the Units' keyword, the auras and the player modifiers ([[R1180000]]); a card in the backrow had no Spell Damage to give. As Hearthstone counts Spell Damage from every minion and weapon its player has in play, a player's Spell Damage now also sums the "Spell Damage +N" among the keywords of each card acting face-up in their backrow (`damage::acting_texts_of`): its printed keywords as tuning leaves them, with any granted, so a Nerf or a Buff moves the number as on a Unit ([[§6.3]]). A face-down card's text sleeps ([[R1487000]]), and a Unit standing in the backrow counts once, among the Units. It raises only its controller's Spells ([[§4.4]] step 0). Devil's Pact also prints Indestructible, which a Field Spell already reads as a Unit does (C #84 Lockdown, C #90 In Too Deep): a destroy leaves it on the field. It edits [[§6.1]] and [[§8.6]].
