The issue asked that a stamp show on the player's own copies, on the full card face, in hand, on
the board, in the deck builder and in the Collection, and to other viewers through the loadout
frozen with the match, like the other per-card picks. This rules how.

**Own copies.** A face the viewer owns wears the viewer's own stamp, read from the account's rows
([[R3013001]]): the full card face, a hand card and a board unit, base and Radiant alike, and every
face of the deck builder and the Collection.

**Other seats.** As a match starts, each seat's loadout ([[R1834002]]) freezes the stamps its cards
wear, one per card its deck dealt, `stamps: [{ cardId, stamp }]`, left out when none does, so a
loadout frozen before #3013 reads as wearing none. A card's stamp travels in the `cardLoadouts`
frame and on a replay page's `cardLoadouts` entry, `{ seat, cards, stamps? }`, by [[R1834003]]'s
send rule: only once a view has shown a card dealt from that seat's deck, still as dealt, and once
per socket until its next hello or attach; an entry may carry a stamp and no picks. No stamp is part
of `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. The client draws another seat's stamp on that
seat's faces where it draws its loadout ([[R1834004]]), so a viewer's switch that turns the other
seats' cosmetics off hides the stamps too. A bot wears none ([[R3013001]]); Duos shows none.

**The stamp.** Drawn by the client in SVG, one design per level, each more ornate than the one
below: a ring; a ring and a star; those and an inner dashed ring; a twelve-point rosette and a star;
the rosette, its inner ring and ribbon tails. Its colours are palette colours, none of them in
Radiant's gold window, which the palette test holds ([[R2725004]]).

**Where it sits.** In a fixed slot of the face that covers none of its parts, mirroring the set mark
across it: on the full card face in the band between the name ribbon and the rules box, left of the
rarity gem, moving with that band in the long, longest and compact layouts; on a full-art face under
the cost gem, at the art's left edge; on a small face, a hand card whose text is cut, at the foot's
centre between a Unit's attack and health; on a board unit at its right edge just over its name
plate, outside the portrait. It never covers the cost gem, the name, the stats, the rules text, the
crest, the set mark or a Radiant mark, and a Radiant face keeps every mark under it. A token and a
Created card wear none, as neither counts ([[R3013000]]). The component test measures each slot,
base and Radiant, against every part.

Amends [[R1834003]]: its `cardLoadouts` frame and replay pages carry a card's stamp beside its picks.
Amends [[R2725004]]: the rule that no look wears Radiant's gold covers the stamps too.

Rejected: sending each seat's stamps whole as the match starts, which would list the cards of its
deck that wear one (R1834003's own reason); and drawing a stamp as a ribbon across the art, which
would cover a full art's picture and a Radiant face's paint.
