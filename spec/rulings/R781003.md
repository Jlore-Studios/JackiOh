A Vanilla now also returns the card to its base stats (patch v0.3.7, the designer's): the attack and health printed on the face it wears, as the almanac shows the card. Its buffs go and so does the stat half of its `tuning` (a Nerf's or a Buff's stat change, [[R386]]); the rest of its tuning (cost, X, numbers, keywords) stays, and so do its granted keywords ([[§10.4]]). A Radiant card stays Radiant (the designer: yes), so it keeps its Radiant face's numbers, and a token's summoned X/X (`statsOverride`, [[R349]]) is what it was summoned as, not a buff, so it stays. Damage stays, but a Unit that the lost stats would kill stays alive at 1 health (the designer: yes): its damage is capped at its max health minus 1. A copy made Vanilla (Core #61 Prejudiced Postdoc's) is a Vanilla'd Unit like any other: the target's form and Radiant flag at their printed numbers, with none of the buffs [[R57]] would carry. An Immutable card is still untouched ([[R23]]).
