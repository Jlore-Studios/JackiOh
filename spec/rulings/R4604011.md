In the daily fuzz's plain seed 7360, Stinkyscape+ #165 Shadiris stood on the field Immutable only by a grant. One step bounced it into a full hand, which burned it into the graveyard, and then Buffed every card of its owner's, Shadiris there among them. A card that leaves the field keeps no grant ([[R78]]), so in the graveyard it was no longer Immutable and the Buffs landed lawfully. I23 ([[R4609000]]) judged it by the keywords it held as the step began. [[R4783002]] already lets a Replace in an exile or a graveyard the step sent the card to go. I23 now also lets a `degraded` or an `upgraded` there go, when the card's own keywords no longer make it Immutable. A card Immutable by its printed keyword keeps it in the graveyard and is still judged.

Hearthstone semantics: an effect that left a minion's zone is gone with it.

Rejected: letting every change in an exile or a graveyard go. A printed Immutable card stays Immutable there, so a Buff the engine let through would be the engine's bug, which I23 is there to catch.
