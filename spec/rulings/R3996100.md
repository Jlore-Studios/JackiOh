Issue #3996 made M #56 House Party cost (3) and gave the Right-house defenders it fills the board with "Brittle 1, and so do their Reborn copies" (the Radiant face's Radiant defenders the same). The fill gives each defender it summons Brittle 1, a given count ([[R385]]) started as the defender lands ([[R638]]), so it crumbles at the start of its controller's next turn; its Reborn copy, the body Reborn returns ([[§4.5]], [[R78]]), has Brittle 1 again, started as it returns, however the count stood as the defender died: a count its crumble spent would otherwise have gone with [[R78]]'s reset ([[R441]]), and a count still unspent is set back to 1. Hearthstone's Reborn returns a minion without its enchantments; the card names its exception, and only that one. The base Right-house defender a Radiant one's Death summons is a new card, no Reborn copy ([[§8]], #3's row), so it has no Brittle, as a Hearthstone Deathrattle's summon carries none of its source's enchantments. So at the caster's next turn the defenders crumble together and come back, their zones held for Reborn, so a Radiant one's Death finds no room ([[R64]]); a turn on, the copies crumble with no Reborn left, and each Radiant copy's Death summons a base defender into the zone it left, which stays. The engine carries it as the fill's `brittle` (`SummonPlacement.brittle`): the count, and a rider in the card's memory that [[§4.5]] step 4 reads off the dying card's snapshot ([[R89]]); a defender that leaves the field another way and comes back keeps the count it has ([[R638]]) but not the rider, as its memory goes with [[R78]]'s reset. The number is the declared `brittle`, read as the Spell resolves. Through v0.6.5b the defenders had no Brittle and the card cost (4) (`script_v0_6_5b`, [[R767000]]).
