#43 Big Felinor reads "Cry: Destroy {destroys} random non-Felinor Units." (Radiant: "… random enemy non-Felinor Units."), `destroys` being 3 on both faces. The base face picks among the Units on both sides, as "all" reached them before, the Radiant face among the enemy's. A Felinor is never in the pool, nor is a Unit buried under a Stack pile ([[R13]]).

- **Three different Units.** The Units in the pool are listed once as the Cry resolves, and each pick is drawn from the match rng among those left and taken out, uniform and without replacement ([[R60]]): three different Units, or all of them when fewer than three stand, and nothing is drawn from an empty pool.
- **One state check.** The picks are marked together and die together at the check after the Cry ([[R59]]), no Death trigger running before the others have died.
- **Indestructible stays and its pick is spent.** An Indestructible Unit is in the pool like any other. If it is drawn it survives, put into Attack Position as [[R46]] has it, and it is one of the three, so three picks may destroy fewer than three Units. Rejected: drawing only among the Units that can die, which would make Indestructible a protection against the count as well as against the destroy.
- **A declared number** that only the Cry reads ([[R1748000]]): a Buff or Nerf on the field moves nothing, one in the hand or the deck takes it to 4 or 2 ([[R386]]).
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11p folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, which destroyed every non-Felinor Unit of its scope ([[R767000]]). Legacy: Big Felinor, TG #51, still destroys them all ([[R2195003]]).
