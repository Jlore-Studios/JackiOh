A full-art face ([[R1017001]]) lays its art over the whole panel, so a finish that paints the panel or its frame color would show only on the name banner. On a full-art face each finish also paints a band of itself round the art's edge: its own panel (matte ink, steel, velvet, the leaded panes, obsidian, the starred midnight), or the linen, parchment or gold of the three that paint none. The band lies over the art and its scrim at no more than FULL_ART_FINISH_OPACITY at the rim — 0.6 — and fades to nothing 14% in from the sides and 10% from the top and foot, the art's middle clear. On the panel's `::before` at z-index 1 it sits under the name banner, the crest and the signature (z-index 2) and the gems, the set mark and a Unit's stats (z-index 3), so the art, the Mythic's signature, the cost gem and a Unit's stats stay readable. It applies at every tier ([[R2655000]]) and every face size, and a board minion, which never prints full art, draws none of it.

Gilded edge, Obsidian and Starlit move on their finish layer as on any face, each in a `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX` slot inside the motion gate ([[R1833000]]), and hold still under reduced motion and with Animated foil off. The band itself never moves.

Every new rule needs `data-full-art="true"`, so a normal face looks exactly as it does today. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Rejected: recoloring only the name banner and the gems' plates, which a gem's cost tone and rarity color must keep; and changing the finish layer every face shares, which would redraw normal faces.
