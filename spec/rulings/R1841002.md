"Discover a Weapon; Equip it into this", "a random Miner is Equipped to this" and "choose between 2 Miners to Equip this", once or twice (`equip_picks`): each pick is a fresh copy of a card of the pool, fused into the unit running the effect the way an Equip play fuses its card ([[R1086]], [[R77]]): the unit is the kept instance, keeping its zone, damage and exertion, and the fused cost is R77's. It never passes through a hand, and it is no play: the pick's Cry never runs, and nothing that counts or answers plays sees it. A Discover offers `options` cards (`DISCOVER_OPTIONS` unless the card says, 2 for "choose between 2") and its answer is the engine's own step, whatever card asked (`EQUIP_PICK_STEP`); a random pick asks nothing. A Radiant pick goes in on its Radiant face ([[R469]]). Each pick is its own fusion ([[R102]]), and a Discover asks for the next once the last is in. Each pick's pool leaves out the unit as it stands then, its own card and every card an earlier pick fused into it ([[R387]]: a fused card excludes every ingredient), so the picks are different cards, and a pool the unit already holds whole offers nothing more. Nothing is offered, drawn or fused when the unit is off the field or not on the stay the effect began on ([[R174]]), buried, or Immutable ([[R23]]), or when the pool is empty ([[R129]]). The pick is whatever the card's pool names, the Equip keyword not checked. Rejected: one pool for every pick, taken as the effect began, which lets a pick repeat: the unit is a fused card from the first pick on, and [[R387]] leaves a fused card's ingredients out of its pools.
