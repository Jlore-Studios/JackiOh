A card's figures ([[R377]]) split the decks that played it into two disjoint breakdowns: `playedRadiant` (the deck played the card Radiant at least once during the match) and `playedNormal` (the deck played the card, but never Radiant). A deck that never played the card is in neither, and their sum equals the deck's count in `played`.

- **Per-play face in game records.** Each seat in a game record keeps `playedRadiant: Option<Vec<bool>>` ([[R376]]), recording whether the card was Radiant as played for every entry in `played`, in the identical order. When nothing was played, or when the record was written before this field existed, the field is absent. A record whose `playedRadiant` length does not match `played` is refused.
- **Face tracking during summary.** In `summarize_game`, the reader tracks the face of every card in each seat's hand across the game's log:
  1. A card entering the hand (`AddedToHand`) records whether it entered Radiant.
  2. In-hand face transformations (`RadiantSet`, `Deradianted`, and `Transformed`) update the card's tracked face.
  3. When a card is played from hand (`CardPlayed` or `PlacedCard`), the tracked face at that moment is recorded into `played_radiant`.
- **Counting and totals.** `StatsTotals::fold` ([[R1044000]]) counts a card into `played_radiant` if the seat's flags contain at least one Radiant play for that card; otherwise, into `played_normal`. If a record lacks `played_radiant` or does not have a flag for every play, neither breakdown is counted.
- **Reporting.** The split is reported by `cargo jackioh stats report`, `jackioh-server stats-cards`, `GET /api/stats/cards` (`playedRadiantGames`, `playedRadiantWinRate`, `playedNormalGames`, `playedNormalWinRate`), and the public `/stats` UI as dedicated sortable columns. Each rate requires the minimum sample floor (20 games) to display a percentage, showing `—` below the threshold.

Rejected:
- Counting every play individually instead of games/decks: rejected because every other figure in JackiOh is deck-based, and play counts would overweight cheap cards bounced and replayed.
- Marking Radiant status off the card's state at game end: rejected because a card's Radiant status can change in hand before being played, or after being returned to hand.
- Classifying a deck that played both normal and Radiant copies under both: rejected to keep the two groups disjoint and preserve additive proportions against `played`.
