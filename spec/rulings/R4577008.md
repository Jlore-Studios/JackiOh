S+ #77 Black Hole's base face pulls at the end of each player's turn, a stage of the turn's end after its triggers, its trap window and its delayed effects, with no marker of its own, since the hole is no card ([[R4440050]]). A Spell's trigger can run just before it: S #33 Drone Core VII's "Float reduces cost" answers the `turnEnded` from the hand. I21 reads a run as lasting until the next card's hook, trap or turn edge ([[R4508004]]), so it read the hole swallowing an immune Unit one lane from it ([[R3554001]]) as Drone Core VII's exile, and failed the fuzz gate's seed 1 with Core #90 forced once the Infinite Assault's cards joined the deal ([[R4577000]]).
**The pull is the hole's.** As [[R4508004]] rules for the hole's state check, the board takes what the hole swallows; no Spell reaches for the card ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]). So I21 allows an `exiled` of an immune Unit when the step began with a hole that pulls at a turn's end and pulls the player whose turn has ended in the step, and the Unit stood on that player's side in a lane next to the hole's, the only place one step toward it swallows ([[R3554001]]). A Spell's exile of an immune Unit two lanes away, or on the other side, is still a reach.
Rejected: a marker event for the pull, which would change every replay's events and hash for a check of the test kit.
