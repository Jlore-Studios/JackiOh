**Why.** Radiant faces and normal faces are balanced as separate levers, so a nerf can aim at one face. A Radiant face is about twice as strong at the same cost ([[§5.2]]), and it is played in games where the seat has already spent resources upgrading. A raw Radiant win rate therefore reads high for every card, and All's band ([[R3022000]]) would call most Radiant faces S. Each face needs a scale of its own.

**The faces** (`StatsFace`). Every figure of a card reads one face:

- **All**: every seat, as [[R2777000]] and [[R3022000]] read the card. It is unchanged, figure for figure.
- **Normal**: the seats that played the card and never on its Radiant face ([[R1147000]]'s `playedNormal`).
- **Radiant**: the seats that played it Radiant at least once (`playedRadiant`).

These tallies have been folded on every record since [[R1147000]], so the faces need no backfill and no migration.

**A face's adjusted win rate** is [[R2777000]]'s, read against that face's own field (`face_stats_field`, `face_adjusted`):

- **seen side**: the face's seats (`face_side`);
- **baseline**: the card's never-drawn games, the same baseline as All's;
- **delta**: the face's win rate minus the baseline's;
- **field**: DerSimonian and Laird's random-effects estimate ([[R3022000]]) over every card's delta for that face alone, from the cards with `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games on both sides ([[R654]]'s floor), whatever the page's filters show. It gives the face its own `center` and spread `τ`, as [[R4082000]] gives the Created cards a field of their own. With fewer than `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` (30) such cards the face keeps `StatsField::PRIOR`'s centre and spread, as All does. Its `baseline`, the average deck's, is All's;
- **adjusted**: `50% + τ²/(τ² + SE²) · (delta − center)`, `SE² = ¼ · (1/face games + 1/never-drawn games)`, with [[R2895000]]'s 95% range.

The face's centre takes in what every card's face shares: a play's selection over a mere draw and, on Radiant, the premium of being upgraded. A face at 50% plays as a typical card's face does, and S and F mean "S among Radiant faces" or "F among normal faces".

**Short of games.** A face's games, win rate and Wilson range are its seats'. Below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` of them it reads "not enough games" and has no tier, as All does ([[R654]]). A face whose card has fewer never-drawn games than the floor has no adjusted rate at all.

**The bands** are named constants of their own (`config.rs`: `NORMAL_TIER_BAND_HIGH`, `NORMAL_TIER_BAND_LOW`, `NORMAL_TIER_A_FROM`, `NORMAL_TIER_C_FROM` and the four `RADIANT_` ones), read through `TierBand` and `tier_in`. They start equal to All's (45% to 55%, quarters at 47.5% and 52.5%). The face's spread does not widen them: the face's rate is already shrunk by its own field's `τ²/(τ² + SE²)`, so 55% on Radiant means as many of the face's own standard deviations as 55% on All.

**The Radiant gap** (`radiant_gap`) is the card's Radiant adjusted rate minus its Normal one, each on its own centred scale. It says whether this card's Radiant face gains more over its normal face than a typical card's Radiant face does, which is the lever for a Radiant-specific nerf. Its 95% range is `gap ± √(h_n² + h_r²)`, each `h` half the width of a face's range. It is read only when both faces have enough games and a rate.

**Created cards** get the same faces ([[R4082000]]): a face's games are the seats that used a copy on that face (`createdPlayedRadiant`, `createdPlayedNormal`), read against the Created field of that face (`created_face_field`), whose lift is the face's win rate less All's average deck. Below 30 Created cards it is the deck field with a centre of 0. The Coin and the cards only a rule makes, the Dormant Starship's deck row among them ([[R3070000]]), are read against All's average deck on every face.

**Play rate** ([[R3950000]]) stays All's on every face: it says how often the card is played when seen, which no face changes.

**Rejected.**

- **Borrowing All's field for a face.** All's centre does not hold the Radiant premium, so every Radiant face would read high.
- **[[R3070000]]'s reading against the average deck** for a face short of never-drawn games. It reads the play's selection, and on Radiant the upgrade, as the face's strength.
- **Widening a face's band by its spread.** The rate already sits on the face's own `τ`; widening the band as well would count the spread twice.
- **The faces' joint posterior for the gap's range.** The two faces share the card's never-drawn games, so their errors are correlated. Summing the variances as if they were independent makes the range a little wide, never narrow, and needs no figure the totals do not hold.
- **A face's own play rate.** It is a different figure, the share of plays made on that face, and no part of this lever.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
