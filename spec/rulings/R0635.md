A card that casts on draw ([[§2.4]]: the printed keyword, the `castOnDraw` enchantment, or a copied Spell's text) is never dealt by [[§2.1]]'s opening draw or by [[R9]]'s replacement draws while another card is left ([[R748]] fills a hand the others cannot), so setup casts none and a mulligan chooses between cards that are only what they look like. The cards stay in their owner's library through the deal and the mulligans, behind every card the draws can reach, so the library count says nothing of them ([[§9.1]], as [[R225]]). Once both mulligans have resolved, each seat's are shuffled into its library, each at a uniformly random place by the match rng, before The Coin ([[R244]]) and the start-of-game effects ([[§2.1]] step 4): they wait at the bottom of the library, so left where they lay they would be the last cards drawn, and they are taken out and put back one at a time instead. Nothing is reported for the shuffle-in: a `shuffledIn` per card would count them for the other seat ([[R97]]). A Quickdraw card that also casts on draw is a Quickdraw card first: dealt as the draw it replaces ([[R225]]) and never cast. A seat's setup draws are capped at the cards it may draw: a hand the other cards cannot fill is filled by [[R748]], so a hand is short only when the library runs out of cards setup may deal (a Quickdraw card that casts on draw is never one of [[R748]]'s), and setup never deals fatigue ([[R3]]); the first draw that meets a library of nothing but cast-on-draw cards is turn 1's, a chain bounded by [[R58]]'s cap like any other. What can still ask a question in setup is a start-of-game clause as its card arrives in a hand ([[R151]]), which [[R224]] holds setup for. Not reachable with a legal deck (a deck holds one copy of a card, L3, so at most five non-token cards cast on draw), but `createGame` takes any deck
