[[R602]]'s redaction keeps every unit's Attack as the board shows it, and the difference the placeholders made goes on the unit's buffs. That difference is taken on the Attack the unit shows, which [[§10.4]] floors at 0, while its raw Attack stays unfloored ([[R150]]). A unit whose shown Attack is the same with the placeholders as on the true board keeps its buffs as they are, below 0 Attack included, so a card the seat reads in full passes through the redaction unchanged, and a later buff on it counts in the AI's search and in the lethal hint ([[R2102000]]) only as far as it does on the true board. A unit whose shown Attack the placeholders change takes the shown value as its raw Attack, never the true raw one: how far below 0 a hidden aura took it (a face-down card live under a catalog older than [[R1487000]], which a replay folds under, [[R767000]]) is not on the board, so the AI does not learn it ([[R185]])
