S+ #72 Foreshadow reads "When an enemy unit is summoned attack it and lose 1 mana next turn". The
spec has no such cost, so it is read as follows.

One trigger's two effects, each doing what it can (Hearthstone's order): the forced attack on the
arrival ([[R53]], [[R3550000]]), then 1 less mana next turn ([[§2.3]]). An enemy that can't be
attacked (Evasive, [[R3543002]]), or one gone before the trigger resolves, still costs the mana.
Several summons stack.

Rejected: losing the mana only when the attack is made.
