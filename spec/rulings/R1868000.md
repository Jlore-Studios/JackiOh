Stinkyscape #99 No-No Words reads "(X) Destroy a Unit with less than 4X Attack" (its Radiant face adds "and the Units adjacent to it"). [[R81]] has the X travel in the `play` action beside the targets, but §10.5 step 1 stamped the X on the card only after it had checked the choices, so a declaration's named check (`targetChecks`, [[§10.6]]) read a card with no X and could not say which Units an X reaches.

- **Checked at the play's X.** A declaration's named check is asked of the card with the play's X stamped, as step 1 stamps it before it reads the price, or with its set X ([[R1848003]]), which is on the card already: `legalActions` pairs each X with the picks it allows, and the reducer refuses a pick its X does not allow. A cast already chose its X before its targets ([[R453]]), so a play and a cast now read the same card. Only a check that reads X sees the difference: none of the shipped sets' does.
- **The limit.** "Less than 4X" is strictly below `per` × X (4 × X as printed, ST-S14-4), the Unit's Attack as it is shown through the layers ([[§10.4]]): an X of 1 reaches a Unit of 3 Attack or less, an X of 2 one of 7. X = 0 is no play ([[R348]]).
- **Needed.** The pick is one the play needs ([[R703]]), as Hearthstone's Shadow Word: Pain needs a target and as S #12.1 Shadow Word Death's and #12.2 Shadow Word Pain's are ([[R1867005]]): an X whose limit no Unit is below is not offered and is refused, and a copy whose set X reaches none stays in hand.
- **Not again as it resolves.** The check is made with the play and not made again as the Spell resolves, as Hearthstone checks a target's requirement only when it is chosen: Shadow Word: Pain still destroys a Mana Wyrm its own cast raised past 3. So a Unit raised past the limit after the pick (a trigger on the play at §10.5 step 4) still dies. Rejected: a second check at resolution, which would make the play fizzle on a target the player could legally choose.
- **Radiant.** The neighbours in the target's row on its side die with it whatever their Attack ([[§3.1]], [[R59]]); only the target is checked.
