Stinkyscape #21 Moonberry names the first pool by the Other-Origins tag: "a random Other-Origins card" is any of the tag's tokens, its only cards ([[R1837000]]), since a tag pool holds non-token cards and Other-Origins is in no `POOL_TOKEN_TAGS` ([[R382]]). The pool is the sets that ship ([[R1420]]): C+ #32.1 to #32.3 now, S #10.1 to #12.3 once the set ships. Moonberry itself is a Fruit, so it is in no Other-Origins pool. The pick is one draw from the match rng ([[R60]]) into the hand through [[§2.4]]'s pipeline, a full hand burning it. The Radiant face's card is Radiant and costs the declared discount less, never below (0) ([[R65]]). Flashback needs no ruling: it is the catalog keyword ([[§6.1]]).
