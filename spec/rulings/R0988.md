title: An English face that names the element glyphs is R366's English shape

proven_in:

---
Meditative #40's faces are printed in English but name the five element glyphs (水, 木, 火, 金, 土). [[R366]]'s gate reads a face as Chinese when it is punctuated in Chinese — [[R1302]] ends every other line with 。, so a face printed in Chinese always is — not when it merely names a glyph. An English face with the glyphs answers R366's English checks; a punctuated face answers the Chinese ones, glyphs or not.
