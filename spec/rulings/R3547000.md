S+ #22 Cryofluid and the seven Minerals, #44 Blast Compound, #45 Surge Alloy, #46 Phase Fabric, #47
Plastanium, #48 Thorium, #49 Metaglass and #50 Graphite, all print "Give a Unit …" and name no side.
The play declares one target ([[R81]]): a Unit on the field on either side, as Training Grounds'
Fortify and Stinkyscape's Meteor declare theirs, so a player may give a Mineral to their own Unit or
their opponent's. The hero is not a pick, since the text gives a Unit something. A Spell cannot pick a
Unit that is Immutable ([[R781001]]) or Immune to Spells, so the offer leaves those out, the Unit a
Phase Fabric has just changed among them. Rejected: enemy-only picks, which would leave the cards no
use on the player's own Units, and the hero as a pick.
