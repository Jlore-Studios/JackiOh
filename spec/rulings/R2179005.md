Since issue #2179 C+ #11 Anime Armor's "Aura: Your hero and this can't take more than {cap} damage at a
time" caps each damage instance on the unit itself as it caps one on its controller's hero: its
`heroGuard` entry carries `guardsSelf`, and [[§4.4]] step 3 lowers a hit on that unit to the cap after
Armor ([[R1360]]), the lowest of the unit's own caps winning (a fused card may hold two). A Trample hit
on it is capped before its excess is reckoned, so nothing tramples past the unit while the capped hit
leaves it standing. Lose health and Set health are no damage and are not capped. A unit reads its own
guards only, never another card's; the hero's cap is unchanged ([[§4.4]], #73's beside it). Through
v0.4.12d the cap guarded the hero alone, which `script_v0_4_12d` keeps ([[R767000]])
