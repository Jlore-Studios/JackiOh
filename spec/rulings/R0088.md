[[R14]] ruled that a rotation bounces a card whose destination is Locked to its controller's hand ([[R747]]) until patch v0.3.10, when a rotation began to enter a Locked zone ([[R831000]]); a board swap (#87) still does, and so does any other effect that moves a whole board at once. A zone reserved for a dying Reborn unit ([[R64]]) counts as closed the same way. The bounce is an ordinary return to the hand: [[R78]] resets the instance, the hand cap applies ([[R4]]) and a unit token ceases to exist ([[R11]])
