I23 judges a card Immutable as the step begins ([[R4609000]]). Three things change what such a card holds, or what stands in its place, with no text rewritten, and no ruling said whether I23 allows them.
**A Fuse onto a card the step made.** S+ T-Supernova summons Alnilam, an Indestructible Unit, and fuses the boards into it in the same effect ([[R3569002]], [[R4353003]]). Alnilam did not exist as the step began, so it is no card I23 judges, and the Fuse passes; every other Fuse onto an Immutable card is a finding ([[R77]], [[R23]]).
**A card reset in place.** A card `flickered`, or `summoned` back through Reborn, leaves the field and returns at once, reset as any card leaving it is ([[R78]]; a Reborn clears its Vanilla). Its record is not held to the one it began the step with from then on; an event after it that rewrites its text is still a finding.
**A board restore or a Glitch.** C+ #35 Rollback puts each side it restores back as a snapshot held it ([[R419]], [[R4454002]]), and Glitch's reset and boards re-deal the match or its fields ([[R676]], [[R678]]). A step with a `rolledBack` or a `glitched`, or whose Glitch reset count moved, holds no record to the one it began with; its `transformed`, `degraded`, `upgraded` and `fused` are still judged.
