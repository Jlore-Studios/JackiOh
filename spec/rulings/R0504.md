Patch v0.2.0 asked that empty hands not minimise during a battle. A hand with no cards, either seat's, keeps its row: the client draws one card-sized dashed outline worded "Hand empty" in it, sized in every layout (desktop, a tablet either way, a phone upright or on its side) exactly as that seat's cards are, so the row keeps a card's height and the field does not move when the last card leaves or the first arrives. The outline is not a card: nothing plays, drags or targets it, and the hand count reads 0. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
