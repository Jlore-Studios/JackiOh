Impact Reactor's Cry queues two stacking next-turn losses: minus `mana` at the next refresh and,
unwatched, minus `mana2` at the start of its controller's next turn ([[R1181000]]), so the second
lands if the Reactor leaves. It enters Dormant for `dormant` turns ([[R3546000]]), and while it acts
awake its infinite mana spends nothing ([[R3548004]]). Rejected: tying the second loss to the
Reactor, which would let a bounce dodge it.
