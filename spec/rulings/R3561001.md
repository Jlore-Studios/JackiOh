A Spell's half ([[R3554004]]) is settled as its resolution begins: a Spell cast on a slashed board
([[R3554002]]) asks, or takes, its half then, and a Spell whose own resolution slashes the board
asks none afterwards. Slashed Dream Cries in Red's (S+ #78) Radiant face is cast on an unslashed
board, asks its lane, slashes it, and is done: the lane pick is its only prompt. Rejected: asking
whenever step 5 is re-entered with the board slashed, which put a "choose the half" prompt after
the slash on a Spell that has nothing left to cast.
