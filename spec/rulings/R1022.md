It costs (1) less for each mana its owner has unspent as each of their turns ends, while it is in their hand or deck. It is a permanent `costMod`. Since #1563 the face prints it as "Float reduces cost", the engine's `float_reduces_cost` ([[R1563000]]), with the same rule.

This build's decision: a hand trigger and a deck trigger on its owner's `turnEnded`, reading the event's unspent mana into a `costMod` of minus that — the number Bread and Butter reads. The rejected alternative was a single trigger with a zone condition, which would have missed the copy in the other zone.
