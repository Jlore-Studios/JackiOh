A news entry with a reward mints currency for every account that claims it ([[R2875000]]), so the dashboard posts news with none. `POST /api/admin/news` with `{ title, summary }` writes a post (`news_posts`) after trimming both and checking them as `news.json`'s entries are checked ([[R2875003]]): a title that is not empty, a summary that ends a sentence and holds 1 to `NEWS_SUMMARY_MAX_SENTENCES` of them, each within `NEWS_POST_TITLE_MAX_CHARS` or `NEWS_POST_SUMMARY_MAX_CHARS` characters, and no line break or control character in either; otherwise 400, naming every problem, writing nothing. A post's id is `post-` and twelve hex digits, a prefix no `news.json` entry may take, so a claim never mistakes one for the other.

`GET /api/news` lists a post beside the file's entries for the same `NEWS_TTL_DAYS` from when it was posted ([[R2875001]]), newest first, as an alert whose `reward` is null, marked claimed or not as an entry is. Its claim, through the same route and the same turn, keeps the account's claim of it and pays nothing; the client shows it no reward and a Mark read button in place of Claim, and the bookmark counts it unread until then ([[R2875002]]). Its title and summary show as text, never as markup.

`DELETE /api/admin/news/:postId` takes a post down: `GET /api/news` no longer lists it, and its claim is 404 as for no post; the row stays, with when it was taken down, and a second take-down is 409. `GET /api/admin/news` lists the newest `NEWS_POSTS_PAGE` posts, taken down or not, each with its author's username. Posting and taking down each write an audit row ([[R3074000]]). A post with a reward still goes through a pull request to `crates/server/news.json`, where `patches check` and a person review it.
