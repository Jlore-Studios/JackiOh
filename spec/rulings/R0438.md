The board draws the keywords a unit has — Taunt, Divine Shield and the others the view carries for it ([[§10.4]]'s computed set) — as visuals on the unit itself, not only in its text, so a player reads them at a glance on both seats. Each is drawn from the view alone and holds still under the reduce setting and `prefers-reduced-motion` ([[R200]]); none is a rule a client enforces (CLAUDE.md rule 7)

Amended by issue #2577 ([[R2577000]]): the board draws only the keywords that can still act while the card is on the field or as it leaves. Equip, Tradable, Cloaked, Crater, Flashback, Stack and Temporary X get neither a treatment nor a chip there; the hover preview and the inspect sheet still list every keyword.
