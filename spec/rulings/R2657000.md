Can't Defend ([[§6.1]]) is a keyword (`KeywordKind::CantDefend`), the short successor to "Can't be in Defense Position", which was a static flag on the card's script (`StaticFlags::never_defense`, #65.1 Spikey Pillow's) through v0.5.9f. A Unit with it, printed, given or added, is never switched to Defense Position, by its own action or an effect's (`combat.rs`, [[§4.1]]), so `legalActions` offers no switch for it; and one standing in Defense Position, one that arrived there or gained the keyword there, switches to Attack Position at the state check, spending no exertion and reported by `positionSwitched`, as an Indestructible one does ([[§4.5]] step 1, [[R2179000]]). Every card that printed "Can't be in Defense Position." prints the keyword on its keyword line instead, and K-Pop Fanatic gains it; Jlockeed's J15 Fighter loses it with the rest of its old text. The flag stays in the engine for the scripts kept for those versions' replays, which set it again on the faces that printed the line ([[R767000]], `never_defense_kept`), and keeps a hidden Unit that never printed it (Meditative #49.2 Yile's Virus) out of Defense too. It is harmful, so a Nerf never removes it, and a Buff may ([[R2657005]]). This is the owner's balance pass (issue #2657).
