S #79 Orca's designer text reads "(4) [Embiggen 5] … Cry: Choose between 2 [Radiant] Miner vXs to Equip this", and its Radiant face adds ", twice" (ST-S05-3, ST8, ST9).

- **The offer.** The offer is a Discover of 2 (`options`) from the five Miners, S #62 to #66 ([[R1841002]]). The Miner chosen is fused into Orca with its Activate. The Radiant face asks twice: the second offer comes once the first pick is in, and leaves the first pick out.
- **Embiggened.** The designer's "[Radiant]" belongs to the Embiggened version (his answer, ST9). Played at (5), the Miners offered are Radiant ([[R469]]): the Cry reads the play's stamp ([[R81]], [[R1175000]]). The Radiant face changes only "twice", and Embiggened its offers are Radiant too. (5) is past `MAX_MANA` (4), so Orca is played Embiggened only with mana an effect gave ([[R1857001]]).
