[[§14.5]] says the cosmetic token bar pays the next step of `COSMETIC_TOKEN_CYCLE`, Rare, Epic,
Rare, Epic, Rare, Cosmetic Pack, and then the same again, and the account keeps its place in the
cycle:

- **The order** is `COSMETIC_TOKEN_CYCLE`'s, verbatim: Rare, Epic, Rare, Epic, Rare, Cosmetic
  Pack. The seventh payout restarts at Rare.
- **The stored position** is the `CosmeticTokenCycle` counter: the step paid is the cycle's
  entry at `position % 6`, and one counter row advances it by the payouts, reason `token_bar`,
  in the opening's transaction ([[R1305000]]).
- **A Rare or Epic step** grants one cosmetic token of that rarity, counted under its rarity's
  name, one inventory row per step.
- **A Cosmetic Pack step** adds one unopened cosmetic pack to the inventory (`CosmeticPack`
  under no key, [[§14.6]]), which #1306 opens. It is a pack like any earned one: nothing sells
  one, and the bar's own row still says `token_bar`.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone has no cosmetic cycle; the spec's order and stored position
are read literally, and this only fixes where the position is kept and what each step writes.

Rejected: keeping the position in the bar's own counter, which would tangle points with steps;
and paying the pack as an immediate opening, which would roll cosmetics in a card pack's
transaction instead of granting a pack #1306 opens.
