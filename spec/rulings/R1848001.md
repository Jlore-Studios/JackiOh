Stinkyscape #3 Meteor reads "Deal 15 damage to a Unit. Lock its zone. Tramples onto adjacent Units", and its Radiant face "Deal 20 damage to a Unit. Lock its zone. Tramples onto adjacent Units and the enemy hero". [[§4.4]] step 9's Trample sends a hit's excess to the target's controller's hero; this one carries it onto the Units beside the target first. The spec did not say how the excess splits between two neighbors, so this follows Hearthstone's Rolling Fireball ("any excess damage continues to the left or right"), whose excess rolls one way, chosen at random, and Trample's own rule that each Unit counts a trampling hit only up to its health.

- **Stated on the damage.** A damage effect's `trampleOnto` (`adjacent`, or `adjacentAndHero` for the Radiant face) makes its hit trample, as `trample` states a Spell's Trample ([[R346]]'s way for Pierce), so the target counts the hit only up to its health ([[R63]], [[§4.4]] step 5) and the rest is its excess.
- **The neighbors.** The Units acting in the zones beside the target, in its row and on its side ([[§3.1]]: adjacency never crosses sides, as Cleave's never does; beside a Unit standing in the backrow only a Unit standing there, [[R1081000]]). A Unit a Spell may not affect (Immune to Spells, [[R81]]) is not one of them.
- **How the excess splits.** With two neighbors the first is drawn from the match rng, one draw; with one there is no draw. The excess goes to the first as a new instance of the same hit — not raised again by Spell Damage ([[§4.4]] step 0), through that Unit's own Divine Shield, Armor, Indestructible and lethal replacement — and it counts only up to that Unit's health; what passes it goes to the other neighbor the same way. A neighbor that takes the hit whole (its Divine Shield, an Armor that takes all of it, Indestructible) stops the rest, as a target that takes a trampling hit whole sends nothing on. A neighbor's Poisonous, Lifesteal and kill credit are its own hit's, as Cleave's are.
- **The hero.** With `adjacentAndHero` what passes both neighbors (all of the excess when there are none) goes to the target's controller's hero as one more instance of the same hit: Trample's hero ([[§4.4]] step 9, which [[R1750000]] keeps for a Spell), the enemy hero whenever the Meteor is aimed at an enemy. With `adjacent` it is lost.
- **What sends no excess.** As for any Trample: a Divine Shield or Indestructible on the target, or a hit its Armor takes whole.
- Absent on every damage effect but the ones that state it, so no shipped card plays differently.

Since issue #2521 Meteor's Radiant face deals 15, as its base face does; what passes both neighbors still goes on to the target's controller's hero.
