S+ #17 Smoothie and #18 Large Smoothie read "When added to your hand, select two (three) random fruits for it". As the card reaches a hand, E8's `fuseRandomInto` fills it with that many picks at once, as one fusion ([[R3558003]]): it keeps its instance and its price ([[R470]]), and played it casts every Fruit in it, each asking its own targets ([[R77]]). The picks come from the Fruit Spells of the shipped sets ([[R1420]]), never this card ([[R387]]) and never the other Smoothie, as the plan reads it (docs/stinkyscape-plus-set.md, SP3 E8): a Smoothie inside a Smoothie would fill itself as it was fused. A card already a fusion is left out, so a Smoothie that returns to a hand is not filled again. The Radiant face's Fruits go in on their Radiant faces ([[R469]]), "Radiant X, Radiant X, & Radiant X" on #18 meaning X, Y and Z (SP5).

Rejected: the Fruit Units too (the text's X and Y are cast as one Spell), and a pool that keeps the other Smoothie.
