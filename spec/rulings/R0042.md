A death whose lethal damage instance came from this unit, Cleave included. A unit is killed once, by the first thing that dooms it before the state check collects it: the hit that takes it to 0 or less health, or the Poisonous hit that marks it ([[§4.4]] step 7), whatever health that left. Nothing that lands on a unit already doomed changes who killed it — not a later hit (a second part of one effect list, a Death of the same pass), not a Poisonous one, and not a destroy — and a destroy that dooms a unit no hit has doomed leaves it with no killer
