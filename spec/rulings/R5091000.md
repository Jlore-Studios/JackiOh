Issue #5091 is the after-merge spot-check of #5073 ([[R5014000]]). Taking the menus' gradients out left seven `::before` and `::after` boxes with no fill, so they drew nothing, two of them while still running an animation.

**A flat fill, its gradient's old middle stop, where the box moves or marks a state:**

- the skeleton's sheen: `--skeleton-sheen`, one band slid across each block once ([[R3608000]]), clipped by the block while it rests;
- the practice HUD's scan while the AI thinks: `--opponent`, one run along the lower edge, and the whole edge under Reduce Motion;
- the thinking orb's ring: `--opponent` at 70%. It no longer turns, since a ring of one color has nothing to turn; the core's pulse is what plays once;
- the deck builder's title hairline: `--pal-gold-400` at 55%.

**Gone with its rule,** as R5014000's "a fade drawn on a `::before` or `::after` went" says: the landing's pool of hearth light under the fan, the 404 hand's hearth light, and the 404 card's spark with its `nf-spark` keyframes. A flat fill would draw a hard-edged oval, not a light or a spark.

**The check.** Every `::before` or `::after` a sheet makes (a `content` other than `none` or `normal`) paints something in some rule of the same last compound, in any sheet: text of its own, a background, a border, an outline, a shadow or a backdrop. The hit areas and float spacers that paint nothing by design are named in `EMPTY_PSEUDO` (`apps/web/src/surfaces.test.ts`) with their reasons, and the test fails on any other (CLAUDE.md rule 15).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. Hearthstone semantics do not reach a stylesheet's pseudo-elements.

**Rejected:** restoring the gradients, which would raise `UNTOKENED_SURFACES`, which only falls; flat fills for the two pools and the spark, which would draw hard-edged shapes; a gradient `mask` or a blurred glow, which would bring the fades back by another property; keeping the ring's spin, an animation that shows nothing; and checking only animated boxes, which would miss the still ones (the hairline and the two pools).
