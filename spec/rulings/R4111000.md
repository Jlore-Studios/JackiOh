[[R3100000]]'s forecast read Meditative #72 The Banisher's exile on damage ([[R1124]]) as plain damage: a 2-attack Banisher on a 3/3 was forecast to leave it standing, and a 3/3 struck back for 2 by one to survive, though each is exiled at the next state check. The forecast now counts a source that exiles what it damages as it counts Poisonous: its blow of 1 or more `kills` the Unit it lands on; its strike back of 1 or more takes away the attacker's `survives`; and a First Strike defender that exiles, against an attacker without First Strike, marks the attacker with its first blow, so the attacker lands nothing ([[§4.3]] step 1: a marked unit has fallen). A blow that a Divine Shield, Armor, an Indestructible unit or a damage pool takes whole deals nothing and exiles nothing, as the pipeline marks nothing then ([[§4.4]] step 7).

The fuzz's I18 (`crates/engine/src/testkit/invariants.rs`) holds every plain attack it plays to the forecast its seat was shown: `dealt`, `taken`, `pops`, `kills` and `survives`, read off the step's events and the state it left by the one reading the forecast's own rules tests use (`testkit::PlayedAttack::read`). It judges an attack only when the forecast could foretell all of it, and it skips, each by a named condition: a step that ran a marked hook ([[R2783000]]), fired a trap or left a prompt open ([[R3477000]]), cancelled or redirected the attack or one of its hits (§4.4 steps 0a and 4a, which [[R4032000]] leaves unprojected), was forced or a substitute's, or ended the turn; and a board where the attacker or its Unit target is in a damage chain ([[R3554007]]), where an attack hook no marker reports would run (When this attacks, After this attacks, After this is attacked, After one of your Units attacks, When one of your Units is attacked), or where a card on the defending side reflects an attacker's strike back onto it (Stinkyscape #104 Reflective Plating's Radiant face, [[R1849003]], §4.4 step 8a), since `taken` is the strike back alone until the forecast projects the reflection. Duos is not judged: a Duos seat is shown a subset of its duel's entries. The fuzz's summary and `--json` count the attacks I18 judged, so a filter that judges nothing shows.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and what it shows of a hit is what the hit does.

Rejected: leaving The Banisher out of I18 by a condition, which would keep a misleading preview players see today; and a blanket skip of attacks I18 disagrees with, which would check nothing the next card changes.
