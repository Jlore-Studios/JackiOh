A Tommy Tempo cast on draw while its owner has no open, unlocked, unreserved unit zone is not cast: it goes to its owner's hand uncast (burned when the hand is full, [[R317]]), as [[R58]]'s cap sends a card, and ends nothing. This follows the card's own text and is the exception to [[R138]], under which a cast permanent with no zone still counts as played and resolves. A free Taunt that ends your turn (a 9/9, a 5/5 that eats your mana since patch v0.3.10, [[R831010]]) is worth casting only if it stands on the field; a cast that resolved with no body would take the turn for nothing. The engine's general rule for any Unit cast on draw ([[R459]]) gives the same result
