When a player's Jade Counter goes from below 5 to 5 or more, a Jade Beauty is summoned for them
([[R64]], no Cry). With a full row none is, and that threshold is spent. When it goes from below 10
to 10 or more, every Jade Beauty they control becomes Radiant, and if they control none, a Radiant
one is summoned. Each threshold acts once a game; one add that crosses both does 5 first, then 10.
The crossings run inside the same `add_jade` effect that raised the counter.
