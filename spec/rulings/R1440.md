The designer's rule (issue #571): any card that flips a coin can take Lucky, and heads is the Lucky side. A coin flip is a luck-based roll ([[§6.1]]), its better side heads unless the card's text names another: Meditative #36 CN Peptides keeps the result better for its caster ([[R902]]). For each flip, a card with Lucky X flips X more coins and keeps heads if any of them lands heads; each flip is its own roll, so each of #4 Gary the Gambler's coins, the last one included, rolls on its own. The Lucky is the flipping card's own, printed plus given ([[R1438]]), so a Lucky given by Meditative #101 Gachaholic makes a Gary or a C #65 Ace in the Hole lucky. This revises [[R32]] and [[R130]] for coin flips: they read a flip as a roll with no best, which no Lucky touched. Every flip goes through one helper, `Rng::lucky_coin(x)` (`rng.lucky(x, coin, keep heads)`), C+ #19.3 Mid Loser's flip included. With Lucky 0 it draws exactly one number, as `coin` does, so [[R130]]'s replay concern holds, [[§10.7]]'s cursor moves as before and no shipped game changes: no shipped coin-flip card has Lucky but the Radiant Mid Loser, whose flip already kept heads.
