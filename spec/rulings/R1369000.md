A change that ships no card data never bumps the catalog version ([[R105]]): it is logged on the patch that was live when it reached players. `cargo jackioh patches log <base>..<head> [--bodies <path>]` classifies each first-parent commit of the range against its first parent with stage 1 of `classify` ([[R1365000]]). A Cards commit adds nothing, its patch records it already. An App, AI or Internal commit appends one entry to `crates/cards/patches/technical.json`, `{ version, commit, pr?, title, kind, player? }`: the newest shipped X's version, the commit, the pull request number its subject ends with (`(#1394)`), the subject without that number or its convention prefix, its kind (`app`, `ai` or `internal`), and an optional player line. Entries are keyed by commit, so a commit the log holds already, under any version, adds nothing and running a range twice is running it once.

The player line is the first line under a "Player note" heading of the pull request's body (a list marker dropped, HTML comments ignored), or the rest of a line that starts `Player note:`. The body is `--bodies`' `{ "<pr>": "<body>" }` when it names the pull request, else the commit's message. A line that breaks the note rules ([[R724001]]: blank, over `PLAYER_LINE_MAX` characters, or naming a ruling, an issue or card number or a §) is left out of the entry and named on stderr, so the log never stops a promotion.

The newest shipped X is the last entry of `patches.json` whose tier is a patch or a major. An entry records its `tier` from issue #1366 on; one shipped before has none, and is a major when it adds a card of a set (the part of a card id before its first dash) no earlier entry held, a patch when 10 or more families (`PATCH_MIN_CARDS`) play differently by its `changes` (added, removed, or a changed field other than the wording fields), and a micro otherwise. Nothing parses or orders a version ([[R105]]). So the real history's majors are v0.1.0, v0.2.0, v0.3.5 and v0.3.15.

The Patch notes page ([[R724000]]) shows, under each patch, a "Technical changes" list of the App and AI entries logged on it that have a player line, in the log's order; an Internal entry, or one without a line, stays in the record only. The static `/patch-notes` writes the same lines, and `apps/web/src/wording.test.ts` holds them to today's words as it does every note.

A release line's first patch is its Z's first version, the line's major, by the same tier and fallback: the n-th major opens `RELEASES`' n-th line, a major with no line written yet opens one named by its note, and patches before every major sit under Earlier patches. Only the summaries and the line's technical changes stay hand-written. Meditative's line now opens at v0.3.5, where the set shipped, so v0.3.1 to v0.3.4 sit with Classic & Classic+, as #1360's version map has them.
