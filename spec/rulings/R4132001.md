[[§9.9]]'s quality gates held Hard against Easy to the brief's 80% (`AI_GATE.brief_rate`): 16 of the smoke run's 20 games and 40 of the full run's 50, since the count an AI as strong as measured (91.3%) reaches in 95% of runs was higher, 16 and 42. Before the changes beside it below, the build without [[R4132000]] won that smoke run's 16 exactly and the build with it 15: it lost games 1 and 17, which the build without it won, and won game 5, which that build lost. Played again move by move, both lost games part at a move of the Easy seat's, which set a Field Spell (Combo-Index) or a Trap (Pocket Peptides) before R4132000 and spent its turn on another play after it, the Hard seat's moves the same up to there. The two seats run one search at one budget and differ only in their handicap ([[R180]]), so a change to the search changes both and this matchup cannot say whether it made the AI weaker; the full run (42 of 50, 40 needed) and R4132000's paired screen showed no loss.

The user lowered Hard against Easy's floor to 75% (`AI_GATE.brief_rate.hard_vs_easy`, 0.75): a run needs 15 of 20 and 38 of 50. `gate_needed` keeps its rule, the lower of the floor's share and the count an AI as strong as measured reaches in 95% of runs, and here the floor's is the lower. An AI at the measured rate now fails the smoke run by chance about once in 170 instead of once in 40, and the full run about once in 3,800 instead of once in 300; a run fails with 90% probability once the win rate falls to 66% (full) or 59% (smoke), not 71% or 64%. The other two matchups keep the brief's 95% and 70%.

Merged with the AI changes that landed beside it ([[R4170000]], [[R4171000]]), R4132000's smoke run reads 19, 16 and 16 of 20 (17, 10 and 15 needed) and its slowest decision 122 ms against `AI_GATE.max_decision_ms`'s 1,500, so Hard against Easy clears the brief's 16 again, on its count exactly; the floor stays at 75% as the user set it, a game of room below a run that sits on the brief's count.

Rejected: lowering `measured_rate`, which [[§9.9]] holds to a measurement of fresh deals and not to a choice; a 75% floor for every matchup, which nobody asked for; and keeping the brief's 80%, whose count this run meets exactly, so that the next change to the AI that moves one of its games turns `cargo jackioh gate` red.

It changes no rule of the game: it changes one constant of the gates.
