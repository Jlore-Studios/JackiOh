Patch v0.3.12's balance pass (issue #1149) prints Core #13 Jlockeed Shredder-10 at 6/10 and its Radiant face at 8/20: the designer's numbers, which lower the base Attack from 8 and the Radiant's from 16 and leave the Radiant Attack below twice the base's. [[R275]]'s stat half lists such a card by name, as patch v0.2.0 named its two, so the catalog-wide test holds every other Unit face to it and names Jlockeed Shredder-10 as its one exception. The Radiant face is still the stronger one: twice the Health and twice the end-of-turn damage (2 [4]). Superseded by issue #1219 before patch v0.3.12 shipped: the designer raised the Radiant Attack to 12, twice the base's 6, so the Radiant face is 12/20, [[R275]]'s stat half holds it as it holds every other Unit, and the test names no exception again.
