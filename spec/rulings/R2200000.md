TG #39's entries 3 and 6 ("add 4 random Training Grounds cards", "add 2 random Radiant Training Grounds cards", which cost (1) and gain 2 and 1 mana, [[R3135000]]; (0) and no mana through v0.5.11p) and 5 ("summon 5 random Training Grounds permanents", 6 through v0.5.11p) name the Training Grounds set, not the Call to Chaos editions: each is a pool that names the set ([[R2194001]]), so it reaches the set's cards while the set is held ([[R1420]]) and the snapshots #41–#60 once they land, never a token and never TG #39 itself ([[R387]]). "Permanents" are the four types that stay on the field ([[§5.1]]): Units, Field Spells, Traps and Field Traps. Each pick is its own ([[R60]]): the cards are added at `costOverride` `CHAOS_TG_CARD_COST` (1; 0 through v0.5.11p), entry 6 Radiant, a full hand burning the rest (the mana is gained all the same), and the permanents are summoned by their types, a Trap or Field Trap face-down, each drawn only when its zone is open ([[R129]]).
