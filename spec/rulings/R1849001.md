"Evolve: X" is a hook of the card (`Script.evolve`), and the `evolve` verb is the only thing that runs it: it runs the target Unit's hook for the Unit's controller while the Unit acts on the field, the resumable way, so a question in it pauses the list that evolved the Unit ([[R113]]). A Unit with no Evolve text, or one that has left the field, evolves into nothing and nothing is emitted. "After this attacks, summon a copy of this, then evolve" is an `afterAttack` list that summons the copy and then evolves, in the text's order: the copy is made from the Unit before it evolved, so the wasp's copy has no Flying and the wasp has it. A wasp that died attacking still leaves its copy, made from the snapshot it fought with ([[R89]]), but it is off the field and does not evolve. Rejected: Hearthstone's Evolve, which transforms a minion into a random one that costs (1) more, since these cards print what evolving does; and evolving before the copy is summoned, against the text's "then".
