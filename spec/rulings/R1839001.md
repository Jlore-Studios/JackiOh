"Dormant for N turns" counts its controller's turns: the designer's words, and Hearthstone's Imprisoned minions. The count is the card's `slumber.turns`, set to N as it enters the field Dormant ([[R1839000]]); "Dormant for 0 turns" is no Dormant at all.

**When it counts.** At the start of each of its controller's turns, in the stage of [[R62]]'s opening that ticks the Brittle counts and right after that tick ([[R385]]), every Dormant card with a countdown that tops a zone on that side counts one down, in lane order (`slumber::awaken_at_turn_start`). A count that reaches 0 awakens the card there and then ([[R1839002]]), so it awakens at the start of the Nth of its controller's turns after it arrived. The opponent's turns count nothing, and an extra turn of its controller's counts like any other. A card with no countdown waits for an effect.

**Before the triggers.** The awakening comes before the "Animated on your turn" step, the start-of-turn delayed effects, the start-of-turn triggers and the draw, so its "When this awakens" answers first and its own start-of-turn hook, awake by then, runs that same turn.

**Not summoning sick.** It entered the field turns ago, and the awakening lifts sickness besides, so it may attack on the turn it awakens, as Hearthstone's awakened minions may.

**Seen by both players.** The view's `slumber.turns` is the count left on both seats, which the Dormant badge prints.
