A hero's Armor was [[§6.1]]'s flat reduction of every hit, kept for the rest of the game ([[R1860006]], [[R124]]), and nothing took it away. Against Armor 3, 59% of the collectible Units dealt the hero nothing; against 5, 80%, and fatigue goes through Armor too ([[R125]]), so an armored hero outlasted the fatigue race and games stalled to the turn cap. The owner asked on issue #5183 that any hit on a hero's Armor take 1 off it, and that an aura's Armor not wear.

**The rule.** At [[§4.4]] step 2, a hit on a hero whose `absorbed` ([[R1360]]) is above 0, a hit its Armor takes whole ([[R1361]]) included, takes 1 off the Armor the hero holds itself, `hero.armor`: what it gained (`gain_hero_armor`) and what its seat started with ([[R2922000]]), when it holds any. The Armor of a backrow grant (#84 Going Long, Radiant #73 Anti-oneshot Armor, [[R124]]) and of a player modifier never wear, nor does a Unit's. A hero immune to damage (step 3's cap of 0: M #48, the Infinite Assault's Impenetrable) wears nothing: its hit touches nothing, as an Immune hero's in Hearthstone leaves its Armor whole. Each hit wears 1, whatever the Armor took off it, so Armor A is gone after A hits: against hits at least A it takes off A + (A − 1) + … + 1, against single points A.

**When.** The wear comes once the hit is spent at that hero: it stops at the zero rule ([[R63]]), converts at step 3a, or goes on past step 4a. A hit a lethal replacement cancels or moves at step 4a wears nothing on the hero it left, as it spends no pool there ([[R4022000]]); a moved hit wears the Armor of the hero it lands on. Fatigue and Burn's hits wear it; a Pierce hit, Toxic's tick and lost health do not, since the Armor never meets them.

**Reported.** `armorWorn { player, armor }`, `armor` the hero's Armor after as the badge reads it (`damage::hero_armor_of`, [[§10.8]]), after the hit's `damage` or `damageAbsorbed`: a report nothing answers, withheld from the trigger loop as `armorGained` is ([[R3857000]]), public, taking no room in [[R168]]'s floor and left out of the golden texts. The log writes "Your hero's Armor wore down to 2"; the client ticks the hero's Armor badge, silent, the hit that wore it carrying its own sound and effect ([[§10.10]]).

**Projections.** `damage::project_hit` wears the Armor hit by hit in its `ProjectedPools`, so My Pawn, the lethal glow and the forecast count several hits on one hero as the pipeline deals them ([[R44]], [[R4032000]]).

**Replays.** A game played before this whose hits met a hero's kept Armor no longer folds to its hash and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]'s final-hash check); the three cards whose scripts changed with it keep their old scripts ([[R5183001]], [[R5183002]], [[R5183003]]).
