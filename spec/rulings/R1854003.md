The Greatest Heist's last reward reads "Steal your enemy's mana crystals"; the issue says the enemy loses their maximum mana and you gain it, up to the cap. [[§2.3]] has no crystals, only max mana = min(turns started, 4) plus persistent modifiers, and nothing ever wrote the persistent modifier. This follows Hearthstone, where mana crystals are a count that grows by one each turn and never past the cap: the turns a player has started are their crystals.

- **The count.** `PlayerState.manaCrystals` holds the crystals effects moved on a player's count, added to the turns they have started and their seat's mana bonus ([[R181]]) inside the min, so max mana is min(turns started + bonus + moved crystals, cap) plus persistent modifiers (`mana_crystal_count`, `max_mana_for`). It is absent while none moved.
- **The victim.** `steal_mana_crystals` takes the running card's controller's opponent's crystals: their count drops to 0, so their maximum mana falls to their persistent modifiers alone (0 on every shipped card), and their current mana falls by as much, never below 0, so mana gained above it this turn stays. Their crystals come back as anyone's do, one a turn, at their refreshes (1, 2, 3, then the cap).
- **The thief.** Gains as many crystals as the victim lost, full (current mana rises with maximum mana, as Hearthstone's "gain a Mana Crystal" is a full one), and never past the cap ([[R180]]'s seat cap for an AI seat): a thief at the cap gains none, and one below it keeps the crystals inside the cap as its own turns grow.
- **Seen by both players.** Both sides' `mana.max` is set at once and a `manaChanged` reports each side whose mana changed, the victim's first; a player with no maximum mana has nothing to steal, and nothing is reported. No badge: the mana bar is the view's.
- Absent from every game no card steals crystals in, so no shipped game changes.
