The designer's "Add 1-2 Auspicious Rocks into your hand {33% 1, 66% 2}" is read as one Auspicious Rock (M #39.1, the Rock M #40 Feng Shui adds) and then one roll of the declared `chance` ([[R386]], 66 as printed) for a second: a hit adds another Rock, a miss adds nothing more. The printed 33 and 66 do not sum to 100; the 66 is the number the card declares. The roll is a luck roll, rolled as C+ #53 Book of Tokens rolls its count: the card's net Lucky ([[R1438]]) and its controller's Luck ([[R987]]) roll it again and keep a hit, and a net below 0 rolls it again and keeps a miss ([[R5236020]]). Each Rock goes into its caster's hand as any card does, and a full hand burns what does not fit ([[R317]]).

Both faces print the same odds. The list's Radiant face adds plain Rocks too; the build reads the Radiant face as adding Radiant Auspicious Rocks, as every other Book of the set's Radiant face adds Radiant cards (FV2 #25).
