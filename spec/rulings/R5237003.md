`effects::mana::raise_mana_cap { amount, player }` raises the player's mana cap by `amount` for the rest of the game (FvH #18 Villa-D Moneybags, 2 and Radiant 4): `PlayerState.manaCapBonus` adds to the seat's `Handicap.manaCap` wherever the cap is read (`mana::mana_cap_of`: max mana, a steal up to the cap, a steal counted from the capped maximum; [[§2.3]], [[R181]]). §2.3's formula then reads min(turns started + bonus, the raised cap), so from the next refresh on the player's crystals grow past the old cap one a turn as before. Max mana is worked out again as the cap rises: crystals the player had earned past the old cap (turns started beyond it) arrive at once, empty, and current mana is unchanged until the refresh fills them. Raises add up. Nothing goes above `MANA_CEILING` ([[R5148000]]). A raise of 0 or less does nothing, and one that moves no maximum announces nothing.
