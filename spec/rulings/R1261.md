The Activate ([[R384]]) fills each empty, unlocked, unreserved unit zone of its side, left to right ([[R64]]), as C+ #2 does. Each zone gets one random non-token Jlockeed Unit of the sets that ship ([[R380]], [[R1420]]; never itself, [[§5.1]], [[R387]]), with repeats allowed ([[R60]]). Each is summoned, so with no Cry ([[R1]]). With no such zone it summons nothing and draws nothing ([[R129]]), and the use is still spent. On the Radiant face, Activate 2's second use fills what the first left empty, and the Units are Radiant.
