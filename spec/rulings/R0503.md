Every face shows its set as a small mark on its frame: a ringed orb for Core, a columned temple for Classic, the temple with a plus for Classic+, and a plain diamond for a set the client has no glyph for. The mark is named by its title and label ("Classic+ set"), sits clear of the cost gem, name, rarity gem, rules box, stats, crest and Radiant marks, and is hidden only on faces under 100 px. A token that prints a rarity ([[§5]]'s `printedRarity`) wears that rarity's frame, gem, crest and foil, on the board too; its rarity stays Token everywhere else. The procedural art gains the Book, Pancake and AI families (tag themes: AI after Call to Chaos, Book after KY, Pancake before Fruit, so a family's token reads as its family) and a motif read from each card's printed name. A plain card or Token wears its motif as its emblem; a tribe or family carries it beside its own emblem and skips the motif its own picture already draws. Every catalog entry draws a distinct picture on each face, and a Core card whose name calls up no motif draws exactly the picture it drew before. Presentation only: no rule reads the art, the mark or the printed rarity
