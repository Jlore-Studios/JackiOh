The news ([[§14.13]]) has no read marker of its own: an account has read an entry once it has claimed its reward ([[R2875000]]), so unread is unclaimed.

**The list.** `GET /api/news` answers an active account the live entries ([[R2875001]]), newest first, of two posted at once the one later in the file first, each with its title, summary, reward and whether this account claimed it. A claim answers the same list, the entry claimed. A pending account is refused, as on every route of the economy.

**The bookmark.** The tavern landing shows a News bookmark beside the quests' ([[R2035000]]), built the same: a glyph on the doors' box that unrolls a parchment panel, rolled up by a second press, Escape, which gives the focus back to the bookmark, a press outside it or Back ([[R2127000]]). The panel lists the entries, each with its reward and a Claim button, or Claimed. The bookmark carries a red count of the entries the server marked unclaimed, and no bubble at zero; a claim takes the count down once the server has answered, never before, and a refused claim keeps it and shows the server's words. It shows only to an active account while `ECONOMY_LIVE` is on, as the quests' does ([[R1304002]]), and hides when the read fails or lists no entry.

**The client** counts only the entries it was sent and the `claimed` each carries (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: the client's news and rewards badge counts what is waiting to be collected and clears as each is collected.

Rejected: a read marker apart from the claim, which would let the count clear with the reward still unclaimed; a count kept by the client, which could disagree with the server; and the bookmark shown with nothing to claim or read, which would add a dead control to the doors.
