A face-up card on top of its controller's backrow zone, acting there and no Unit already ([[R1728000]]), that meets both of [[R2280000]]'s conditions, plain Animated and stats, for the first time animates at the state check that follows ([[§4.5]]). It comes to have the one it lacked through a Fuse that keeps it ([[R77]]: S #16 Rod of Ages once the Unit played onto it fuses in, [[R1869000]]; M #75 Ever Growing Tree fusing in a plain Animated Field Spell, [[R1087]]) or through a granted Animated. Its placement is [[R383]]'s: its own lane's unit zone when open, else [[R64]]'s leftmost open, unlocked, unreserved one, as an entry, summoning sick; with none open it stays where it is and is owed nothing more, so a unit zone opening later moves nothing. A Trap or Field Trap counts only once it has fired face-up: a face-down one never animates before it fires ([[R33]]), and one only revealed is still armed. The first time only: a card that had both already, one that entered with no unit zone open or a fired Trap that stayed for want of one, keeps [[R383]]'s own moments. An "Animated on your turn" card waits for its controller's start of turn ([[R1087]]). The engine marks the card as the change lands (`memory.__animateOwed`, engine memory that [[R78]]'s reset clears) and the state check animates every marked card, the active player's side first, backrow lane 1 to 5. Rejected: checking every backrow card at every state check, which would animate a fired Trap or an Animated Field Spell a full unit row once held back as soon as a zone opened, and watching auras, since no card lends Animated through one.
