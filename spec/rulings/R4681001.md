Presentation only beside the lists (CLAUDE.md rule 7): the lists are [[R4681000]]'s; no rule, state, event
or response beside them changes.

**The endpoint.** `GET /api/stats/hall-of-fame?mode=` answers the four lists, computed on the server from
X0's versions and figures ([[R4675001]]). It is public and cached: anyone reads it, `?mode=` alone names what
its figures count (every mode when missing or unrecognized, the merged pilots as the tab's versions fetch),
and its answer is kept `CARD_STATS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS` like the versions' ([[R978000]]), cut from the same
snapshot. Its body carries `pilot`, `mode`, `minSample`, `bandLow`/`bandHigh`, and the four lists, each entry
its before and after play-version rows with figures, the ship dates of `before.first` and `after.first` from
`patches.json`, the patch's player-facing balance line, and, on the changed list alone, `changesBetween`.
The balance line is the first `player.balance` line of `after.first`'s patch entry that names the card
(case-insensitive), else null, and always null where before equals after; a line that names no card is no
entry's line rather than a wrong one. All four lists' figures are already public on
`GET /api/stats/versions`, and the faces come from shipped catalogs, so nothing hidden is shown.

**The page.** The Experimental tab's Segmented gains Hall of Fame, after Versions (`?experimental=hall`),
with one line naming what it shows. It shows four sections in this order: Most broken, Most nerfed, Most
buffed, Most changed. Each entry is one row: its rank; the before face and the after face, small, side by
side (`PatchFace`, each from its own catalog as the drill-down draws them, [[R4680001]]), with the arrow
between (one face and no arrow where before equals after); the versions and their dates; Adjusted and tier
before and after ("not enough games" where a side is short of them); and the patch's player-facing balance
line, when `patches.json` has one for that change. Pressing an entry opens X2's drill-down on the before
version. The lists read no card filter: they are all-time lists. On a phone each entry stacks the faces above
the figures.

Rejected: one endpoint per list: the four are cut from one table, so one answer carries them; and opening
the drill-down on the after version: the Hall of Fame is read backward from what was, so the before version
opens.

Hearthstone has no counterpart.
