Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Every catalog card, tokens included, has one entry in its set's effects table, one JSON5 file per set in `apps/web/src/fx/cards/` (Stinkyscape's in two, `stinkyscape-1.json5` to `stinkyscape-090` and `stinkyscape-2.json5` from `stinkyscape-091`, its tokens included), one line per card in catalog order under its name. An entry is `"none: <reason>"` (the event rows alone), `{ bespoke: "<recipe>" }` (a recipe of its own, the card's file in `fx/cards/<set>/`) or a motif per hook: `cast` for a non-Unit's play, `cry` for a Unit's, and `death`, `trigger` and `activate`, which the table accepts and which plan nothing until issue #2783 marks the hook resolving. A motif is one of a shared vocabulary of pure planners over the effects layer's existing cues (bolt, volley, barrage, chain, lance, nova, wipe, vanish, scorch and negate so far; a name the design doc gives whose planner has not landed, such as `aura`, is accepted and plays the event rows only). Its palette is the card's flavor's look unless the entry names a flavor or one of its sub-looks (`"<Flavor>/<sub-look>"`, issue #2792), which changes no card's `flavor`; its scale grows with the card's rarity and cost, capped, times the effects intensity.

It keys off the definition of the play resolving now, from its `cardPlayed` to its `cardResolved` or `countered`, which a hidden play never has, so a hidden card plays exactly what no card would ([[R202]]); no cue carries a definition or a name. It draws over that play's own events: a damage another card deals while the play resolves (one whose source is not the play's) keeps its own row, and a run the animation queue plays as one wave over a whole pile keeps the wave. Its four beats (gather, travel, land and linger) are fixed fractions of the entry's duration, so everything it starts lands inside the entry and is gone within the effects' tail, through the existing tails ([[R200]]), and the effects speed scales it with the entry ([[R201]]); the effects off or reduced motion draws none of it, no shake and no flash. With `row: "replace"`, the default, the motif draws the numbers (the damage splat, an Armor flash) in place of the events it replaces, and with `row: "keep"` it plays over their own recipe. A run the queue splits into an entry per hit still reads as one motif: the play remembers the public ids its hits landed on, so a chain's next hop leaves from the last, and a card it already struck is destroyed, exiled or returned under its own row, so a lethal bolt flies once.

This amends [[R502]] and [[R670]]: their one per-card effects table (`CARD_FX`) becomes the per-set tables, its seven cards `{ bespoke }` entries that play exactly as before. Since [[R2800000]] an entry may also be `{ setPiece: "<key>" }`, a board clear's set piece, which holds nothing else; #100 Ceaseless Void's is one, and it still lands with its collapse. Since [[R2794000]] a Unit's hooks entry may also name its marquee entrance (`entrance: "<key>"`) beside its hooks, and the `recall` motif draws a card back from its owner's graveyard pile into the hand. Rejected: one shared file for every card, which every set's pass would conflict on, and keying a motif on an event's own `defId`, which a destroyed or exiled card carries whoever removed it and so would play the victim's animation, not the caster's. Since [[R2801000]] `tithe` also reads a price set for a player (an added `modifierChanged`), glinting on their modifier badges and their Dormant Starship, and the Dormant Starship's own entry is `none`: its fuse run and its launch are the Starship's own, drawn whatever card caused them.
