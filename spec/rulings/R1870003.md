Stinkyscape #4.3 Event Horizon reads "Take an extra turn after this one", its Radiant face with Echo 1. [[R847]]'s once-a-game limit is printed on M #19.1 Temporal Rift alone, so Event Horizon has none: each resolution owes its caster one extra turn ([[R845]]), a whole turn taken as this one ends ([[R846]]), its max mana grown and counted by the turn cap. Two Event Horizons in a game owe two turns, and the Radiant face, resolving twice, owes two in a row.
