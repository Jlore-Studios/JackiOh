One draw casts at most 20 cast-on-draw cards (`CAST_ON_DRAW_CHAIN_CAP`); the next one goes to the hand uncast (burned if the hand is full) and ends the chain. A cast-on-draw card is cast even with a full hand. "Draw N" is N draws; "draw your whole library" uses the library size when the effect starts
