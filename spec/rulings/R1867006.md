Stinkyscape #12.3 Psychic Scream's "all permanents" is every card on the field of both sides, unit rows and backrow, face-down ones included, its caster's own too: each leaves the field reset with no Death ([[R78]]) and is shuffled into the opponent's deck at a random position ([[§6.3]]) as the opponent's card, owner and controller alike as a steal off the field makes a card the taker's ([[R12]]). A unit token ceases to exist instead ([[R11]]); a Dormant card is out of reach ([[R1839000]]), as is anything beneath a Stack pile ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]). The cards go in [[R68]]'s order, and a deck at its cap refuses the rest ([[R80]], [[R316]]). The Radiant face's "Each costs ({surcharge}) more (up to (4))" raises each card shuffled in by the declared surcharge, taken back as far as it went past (4) and whole from an X-cost card, whose X is no cost a number may move ([[R65]], [[R1704003]]); a price a graveyard takes off ([[R766]]).
