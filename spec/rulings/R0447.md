A Stack card (keywords where it stands, hand auras and grants included) tops an occupied backrow zone unless it is Locked, held, or carrying a Unit; only the top acts (a trap beneath never fires, an aura beneath is off, a buried Heroic Power can't be used); both seats see a count, never an identity, and the AI's redaction hides a face-down card beneath ([[R185]]); a resumed card answers nothing of its uncovering ([[R212]]); whole-board moves carry the pile whole
