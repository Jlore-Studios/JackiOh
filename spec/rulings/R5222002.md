Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A held set's lines are written with its cards and rendered only when it ships ([[R1420]]), so the Felinors vs Humans release (issue #5222, [[R5222000]]) owes the files of all 515 of its lines at once: a play, an attack and a death line for each of its Units, tokens included, and a cast line for each of its other entries ([[R1448]]). The shipped sets' 1,908 files take about 20.5 MiB of [[R3596000]]'s 24 MiB, counted in 4 KiB blocks, and at their mean of about 11 KB a file the set's lines take about 5.7 MiB more, about 26.2 MiB in all, so they do not fit.

`VOICE_BUDGET_BYTES` (`apps/web/src/audio/constants.ts`, and `gen-voice.mjs`'s `BUDGET_BYTES` beside it) is 30 MiB from the release, still counted in 4 KiB blocks, and every line still stays within 4 s ([[R501]]). That leaves about 3.8 MiB for the lines waves 2 and 3 may reword, as [[R3596000]] left 3.5 MiB. Nothing else changes, and the alternatives stay rejected as [[R3596000]] rejected them: a lower bitrate for every file, and dropping lines from the set.
