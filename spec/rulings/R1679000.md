Every training lane's promotion gate (`cargo jackioh promote`, `TRAINING_GATE` in `crates/engine/src/config.rs`) is a sequential probability ratio test on the lane's hold-out deals ([[R850001]]), each deal played twice with the seats swapped ([[R850000]]), the unban lane's too. A deal's edge for the candidate is its wins less the parent's over the deal's two games, halved, from −1 to 1; a draw or a game without a result is nobody's win. The test weighs H0, a mean edge of 0 (a successor no stronger than its parent), against H1, a mean edge of +0.06 (a 53% win rate, about +21 Elo), with a 5% chance of promoting a successor at H0 and of failing one at H1. After `n` deals of edges summing to `S` it reads the log-likelihood ratio of two normal laws with the deals' own variance `σ²` (never below 0.01): `0.06 · (S − 0.03 n) / σ²`. From the 100th deal on it stops at the first deal whose ratio reaches `ln(19)`, about 2.94, a pass, or falls to `−ln(19)`, a fail; a test still between the two at the 1,000th deal fails, since it found no evidence the candidate is stronger. The unban lane also needs strictly fewer shadow bans than its parent ([[R732001]]), and every game the test counts must be clean ([[R1489000]]).

The deals are played 100 at a time and read in deal order, and the games of a batch past the deal the test stopped at are played and set aside: the deals it reads, its verdict and the record's `vsParent` (the candidate's wins out of the games of those deals) never depend on the thread count, so CI's `promote --verify` plays the same deals and stops at the same one. A tree gets one test: the deals are named for the committed AI, so the same tree gated again plays the same deals to the same verdict. Continuing one test across small changes to a tree is not built: no code can say which change is small, the LLM lanes that gated near-identical trees until one passed are stopped (#1677), and the learn lane gates a network once, and again only a pass when `main` moves (`training/learn.md`).

On #1058's paired deals, whose edges had a variance of about 0.27 (of 200 deals, 25 won twice, 146 split and 29 lost twice), the test promotes an equal successor about 4.5% of the time and one that truly wins 52%, 53% or 55% about 60%, 91% or nearly all of it, where 220 of 400 let those through about 6%, 15% and 53%. It plays about 800 games at H0 and at H1, 1,050 between them, about 300 for a clear loser, and never more than 2,000. The fixed bars it replaces, 220 of 400 paired games for the improve and learn lanes ([[R850000]], [[R1012000]]) and 60 of 100 unpaired games for the unban lane ([[R732001]]), stand in those notes as history. Rejected: a fixed bar on more games, which spends as many games on a clear loser as on a close call, and the pentanomial model of the chess engines' testing, which reads a deal's five outcomes where this reads its edge, the same test at these sizes. Hearthstone has no counterpart.
