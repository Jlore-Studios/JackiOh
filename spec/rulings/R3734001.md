The deck builder's pool dragged a card with the browser's HTML5 `draggable`, which never fires on a touch screen, so on an iPad
only the "+" added a card; and the workshop's list, pool and deck sat side by side only from 1277 px ([[R3728000]]'s
`WORKSHOP_SPLIT_MIN_WIDTH_PX`), so an upright iPad (820 px) showed half a builder at a time. Issue #3734 fixes both.

**The drag.** `DeckDrag` (`game/deckbuilder/DeckDrag.tsx`) takes the place of the HTML5 drag and drop of the pool, the editor and the
deck's sidebar, which are gone; the board's [[R658]] drag of a prompt's option (`game/drag/OptionDrag.tsx`) is its model. A
primary press on a card of the deck editor's pool (`.db-card[data-legal]`; the read-only Almanac's cards have none) that travels the drag threshold lifts it,
and anything below is a click (the card's detail, [[R2730000]]'s hold to preview unchanged). A mouse or a pen lifts in any direction. A
touch lifts only when it has moved more across than down: the card takes `touch-action: pan-y`, so the browser keeps a vertical start
for the pool's scroll (and sends `pointercancel`, which cancels nothing here), and a lifted finger does not pan the pool under it.
A lifted card closes any preview, sets `data-dragging="deck"` on the page, and a ghost with the card's name follows the
pointer; the deck's box takes `data-drop="over"` while the pointer is in it. Released over the deck, the card is added by the
editor's own `add`, the same call the "+" makes, so the deck builder's refusals ([[R251]]) read as they do for a tap, and the click that
the release makes is swallowed; released anywhere else, or on Escape, a canceled pointer or the window losing focus, nothing is added.
A card the deck would not take (one it holds, or a compared deck does) is lifted too and refused on release, the refusal read
out as it was for the browser's drag and for a tap on "+", which adds as before. On a phone in portrait the deck stacks over the
pool, so there tapping the "+" is the way to add.

**The split.** The workshop's rail, its open item's pool and the deck's sidebar sit side by side from 601 px (`over("phone")`), the
labelled shell rail's 1277 px exception gone from `breakpoints.test.ts`. The workshop's own rail is 208 px, and 148 px from 601 to
1024 px (an upright iPad), where the shell's rail is icons and the editor needs the room: its two buttons stack and its rows leave out
their status chip (the status stays in the row's words, for a reader); the status toast centers on the pool between the rail and the
sidebar by those widths. At 600 px and under the list and the editor take turns as before (`data-view`, "All decks"), and focus
follows the half that appears by the same named breakpoint (`upTo("phone")`). At 601 to 760 px the editor is one column, the
sidebar over the pool, beside the 148 px rail.

Decisions the issue did not settle, and what each rejected:

- A touch lifts a card on a move across, not on a press and hold: a hold is the preview's gesture (#2730, [[R2730000]]), which would
  open at once on the card a person is picking up.
- The rail narrows to 148 px between 601 and 1024 px; the issue's other option, a deck tray along the bottom, would restyle the whole
  sidebar.
- The HTML5 drag is removed, not kept beside the pointer drag: on a mouse the browser's own drag sends `pointercancel`, so the two would
  fight over the same press.
- `halvesTakeTurns` reads the named phone breakpoint instead of a number of its own, so the script keeps no size query and
  `breakpoints.test.ts` keeps no exception for it.

Hearthstone's collection lets a card be dragged into a deck with a finger or a mouse. **Proof.** `DeckDrag.test.tsx` proves that a mouse past
the threshold released over the deck adds the card, that a touch moving across lifts and drops and one moving down first adds nothing,
that a release outside the deck, Escape, a cancel and a blur add nothing, that a press below the threshold stays a click and the click after
a drag is swallowed; `browse.test.tsx` that the pool card is no longer `draggable` and a pointer sequence lands it in the open deck;
`breakpoints.test.ts` that the workshop's script and sheet name the phone breakpoint and the 601 px split and keep no 1277 px; and
`MenuShell.test.tsx` the status toast's widths. The component specs `deckbuilder-layout.cy.tsx` (rail, pool and deck at once at 820x1180 and
1180x820) and `deckbuilder-touch-drag.cy.tsx` (a touch drag at 820x1180) measure the drawn layout and the drag.

Rejected: a drag that begins on a long press (see above); a bottom deck tray; the sidebar as a sheet on an iPad, which would hide the deck
the pool is built for.
