Durability counts uses for the card's whole stay on the field, not for a turn ([[R5236000]]):

- **Refresh** (`refresh_activates`, [[R3548003]]) gives back this turn's uses of the Activates, and only
  those: it rewrites `memory.activations` and never `durability_spent`, so a Durability spent stays
  spent. A Heroic Power reroll's refund ([[R757]]) likewise gives back a turn's use and no Durability.
- **A countered Activate** keeps its use and its costs spent ([[R3550005]]), and so its Durability: the
  `activate` action spends it as it counts the use, before a counter can answer, and an owed effect a
  counter answers later spent it then too. A card brought to 0 this way leaves or is refused as any
  other at 0.

Hearthstone's weapon loses its Durability as it attacks whatever then happens to the attack; the use
here is the activation, which happened.
