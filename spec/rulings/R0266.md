Until both players have answered, an answer is read by nothing: the answering player's hand stays as it was dealt, and the other player learns only that it is in (its `promptAnswered`, public like every prompt's, and the view's `mulligan.opponentReady`), never what was kept or offered ([[§9.1]]). Each player's view shows their own prompt until they answer, then `pending: { forYou: false, pendingFor: <the other player> }` and their own kept ids ([[§10.8]]); the AI's `redact` strips the other player's options and answer the same way ([[R185]]). Sealing is what makes the two simultaneous: an answer that resolved at once could show the other player a replacement's cast, a public play, before they had chosen
