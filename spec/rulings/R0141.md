id: R141
title: L5 is never a sole failure for an account that owns every card
cards: "§9.4, R111, R1310000, R1310001"
proven_in:
  - crates/server/tests/api/catalog.rs
---
With [[R111]]'s launch grant of one copy of every non-token card and a one-copy limit, no loadout can break L5 without also breaking L3, L4 or L6: using more copies than are owned requires a second copy of a card, a Token, or an id outside the catalog. A test for L5 therefore asserts it alongside the rule that made it reachable

Since the economy went live ([[§14.12]]) this holds only for an account that owns every card: a dev account after its fill, the active end-to-end fixtures, and an account active before the launch while the catalog holds no card added since ([[R1310001]]). A new account owns the starter cards alone ([[R1310000]]), so any other card in its deck breaks L5 and nothing else, and the queue refuses the deck naming that card
