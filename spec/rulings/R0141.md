With [[R111]]'s launch grant of one copy of every non-token card and a one-copy limit, no loadout can break L5 without also breaking L3, L4 or L6: using more copies than are owned requires a second copy of a card, a Token, or an id outside the catalog. A test for L5 therefore asserts it alongside the rule that made it reachable
