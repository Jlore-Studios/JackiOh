Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A card is shown in Chinese when its instance carries `chinese: true` (ME-CN, `docs/meditative-set.md`). The flag is only ever `true` and is absent otherwise, never `false`, so a game in which nothing is translated serialises, hashes and replays exactly as before. `translate` sets it on one named card (a target, or an id a script captured, aimed at the stay it had when the run began, [[R174]]) or on every card of a card scope; a card already Chinese, or one that has ceased to exist, is left alone. No rule reads the flag: no scope, filter, cost, condition or AI decision changes with it. Immutable does not stop it, since the text stays the same and only the language it is shown in changes.

Nothing removes it. It rides the card through every zone and through [[R78]]'s and [[R766]]'s resets, and Make Radiant, Vanilla, Degrade, Upgrade, a steal, a Bounce and death all leave it on.

A copy of a Chinese card is Chinese, as a copy of a Radiant card is Radiant ([[R57]]): a copy summoned from a card (`clone_of`), one added from a deck (`add_library_copies`), one shuffled in as a copy of a card (`shuffle_into`'s `copyOf`), and one added to a hand by a copier, through `addToHand`'s `chinese` rider: T-AI-5 Autocomplete (the last face-up play's record keeps the flag of the card played, though not of a card that records its play as another card's, C #57), C #17 Counterspell's Radiant copy and Core #39 Recycling Initiative. The rider goes on before the card moves, as the radiant one does, so a copy burned at a full hand is Chinese in the graveyard. A Transform's or an off-field Replace's new card is a new instance and is not Chinese unless its own text makes it so (#37). A Fuse that keeps an instance keeps its flag with its id ([[R77]]'s field target, [[R470]]'s hand or deck card); a Fuse that makes a new card in a hand makes it Chinese when any ingredient was.

A copy remade from a definition by `summon`, `fill_board` or `cast` (Core #22 Carnivorous Cube, C+ #12.8 Frostspatula, C+ #37 Wardrum, C #14 Shadowstep) is made as printed: Meditative batch 08 ([[R921]], [[R925]]) gives the rider only to the verbs its own cards use, so such a copy of a Chinese card is not Chinese until a card needs it.
