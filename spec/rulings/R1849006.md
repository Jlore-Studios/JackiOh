While a card acting on the field says "Aura: Units cannot be in Defense Position" (`staticFlags.unitsNeverDefense`), every Unit on either side is as Spikey Pillow is ([[§4.1]]): it is switched to Defense neither by an action nor by an effect ([[R20]]), so `legalActions` offers no such switch, the reducer refuses one ("that unit cannot be in Defense Position") and an effect's switch does nothing. A Unit in Defense Position, whether the aura arrived while it stood there or it entered in it, switches to Attack Position at the next state check ([[§4.5]] step 1), the active player's first ([[R68]]), as an effect's switch: no exertion is spent and `positionSwitched` reports it. Once the aura is gone Units may switch to Defense again. "Units" is every Unit, as the text says and as Hearthstone's auras that name "minions" reach both sides. Rejected: the aura's controller's Units only, against the text; and leaving a Unit already in Defense there until it next switches, which would read "cannot be" as "cannot become".
