S, A, D and F are the card metrics' tiers of record: R3022000's tier read off the winrate of record
(R3538000) — the exact rate against the band and its quarters, the S/F certainty against the Wilson
bounds — computed in integers, with no record meaning no tier, so a tier pool never holds an
unmeasured card. "Or A tier if none exist" (and "or D tier") means an empty pool: the fallback tier
is read only when the first tier's pool holds nothing. "Fill your hand" adds until `HAND_LIMIT`.
Repeats are allowed until E9 lands uniqueness. Rejected: thresholds of our own, which would drift
from what `/stats` shows.
