Series seat p1 is the player who queued first, or hosted the room; it goes first in odd games and series p2 in even games. Drawn games count, so the seats alternate game by game whatever the results, and game n's seed is the series seed and n. This is [[R259]]'s order, kept: alternating by game number is simple, known before anyone picks, and does not make the first seat a prize for losing
