One Dogpile answers each Spell, and its own cast, a Spell that targeted an enemy, can call the next ([[R1180005]]). With Unstable Clone Machine (Core #33) on the answerer's side, every answered cast shuffles 3 copies of itself into the library ([[§8.2]] row 33, a cast counting as a play, [[R70]]), so each answer mints its successor and the answers never run out: a mandatory loop — free aimed casts, no choice anywhere — that the resolution loop ([[§10.3]]) cannot settle (fuzz seed 934, issue #1216). Hearthstone bounds its forced chains rather than settling them forever, as this game's cast-on-draw chains already are ([[R58]]): a game names at most `SPELL_ANSWER_CAP` (200) spell answers, counted on the state, and a resolution past that names none, ending the chain. The count is monotonic and absent until the first answer, so a game that names none hashes as before (D9).
