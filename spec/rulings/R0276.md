[[§5.2]] listed five cards with no Radiant form, which becoming Radiant left unchanged, so a Make Radiant spent on them (#26, #28, #29, #81, #93's B step) bought nothing, and [[R275]] has nothing to hold them to. Each now has one: #38 Quickstriker's Combo deals 2X, #80 Zao Gao's Rush Tokens are Radiant, #93.1 Combo-Fodder deals 4, #95.1 Chaos Golem is a 20/20 with Charge for Rush, and #96 My Pawn also destroys the attacker. So every card's Radiant face differs from its base face, in its text, its stats or its keywords, and a test holds every catalog entry to that. [[R74]]'s flag is unchanged: it is set, never unset, and now always changes the card
