S #112 Job Application's "Death: Resummon this at Stage 1" ([[R1865002]]) brings a Unit that carries its text, fused ([[R102]]) or granted, back into its zone. Classic #88 Siphon Squad's "When your opponent has no Units, Tribute this" ([[R403]]) is a condition the state check reads on every pass, and fused onto the same Unit with the opponent holding no Units it sacrifices the Unit again as soon as it returns, which returns it again: §4.5 never settled and the daily fuzz panicked on it (1v1 seed 7243, Duos seed 1231, where M #47 饕餮's end-of-turn fusing had built the Unit).

Job Application's Death now resummons nothing when the dying body is not Job Application itself (a fusion, a granted text) and arrived this turn, read off the card as it died ([[R89]]); the same terminator [[R2197005]] gave Floodgates' sweep on such a body, and REVIEW A3.6 asks one of every self-recursive Death. So a Unit that stood on the field before this turn comes back once, arriving now, and the Tribute that takes it out again leaves it in the graveyard; a Unit played this turn and Tributed or destroyed the same turn does not come back at all. Job Application on its own, a Field Spell, is resummoned as before ([[R1865002]]), and so is a Unit killed by damage or a destroy on a later turn than it arrived ([[R1884004]]'s case for max health at 0 is unchanged). Rejected: ending the loop in Siphon Squad's Tribute, which would leave every other self-resummoning Death to loop with every other "Tribute this" text, [[R1220]]'s Untributable guard being the only one the Tribute step has; and the engine's state check skipping a Tribute it already made in the same check, a change to §4.5's loop that decides for every card what only this Death needs.
