Redemption has no global circuit breaker. [[§9.4]] steps 2 and 3 are its only blocks: a profile past its 5 attempts in the hour, or an address past its 20, is refused (a 429 with its wait, [[R192]]) until its oldest counted attempt leaves the window, while every other profile and address redeems as before. No count of failures across addresses pauses redemption for everyone, since codes carry 80 bits: a guess spread over thousands of addresses has no real chance of finding one, and a breaker it could trip would shut sign-ups for every honest player for as long as it ran. This replaces [[R106]]'s breaker, which opened on 100 failures system-wide in 600 seconds, and with it the alert [[§9.4]] said it raised (issue #4131 had asked for that alert). The operator's switch (`moderate redemption off`, `app.settings.redemption_enabled`) is the one global pause: the database refuses with `circuit_open` while it is off, logging the attempt (step 4), and the server mirrors the refusal for `REDEMPTION_PAUSE_MIRROR_SECONDS` (600), so the code screen says paused and further presses spend no try; the pause's 503 stays outside [[R145]]'s identical error.
