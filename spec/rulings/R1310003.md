[[§14.10]] lists a dev account's tools: XP to a track, Jlore, a full collection, the clears and the
quests. None of them fills a token bar, and a bar fills only from spent duplicates ([[§14.5]]),
which a collection mostly owned gives at about `CARD_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS` card packs a bar, and
a collection of the starter cards hardly at all: its packs unlock new cards ([[§14.3]]). A
developer checking what a token buys, and the end-to-end spec that walks the economy
([[R1310004]]), need a bar that pays now. So a dev account can also fill a bar, for itself alone:

- `POST /api/dev/bar/:bar`, where `:bar` is `card` or `cosmetic`; any other is 404 `no such bar`.
  Like every dev route ([[R1300000]]) it acts on the caller's own account, answers an account
  that is not a dev account 403 `forbidden` and writes nothing, and is one transaction.
- It brings that bar to its size, so the bar pays at once, as a bar that reaches its size pays
  ([[R1305000]]). The card token bar pays a card token; the cosmetic token bar pays the next step
  of `COSMETIC_TOKEN_CYCLE`, Rare, Epic, Rare, Epic, Rare, Cosmetic Pack, and the account's place
  in the cycle moves on ([[R1305001]]), so two fills from the cycle's start pay a Rare token, then
  an Epic one.
- Every row it writes, the bar's, the cycle's and the token's or pack's, has the reason `dev` and
  is marked `dev` ([[§14.11]], [[R1300000]]).
- The Dev tools panel on `/account` has a button for each bar.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone has no dev tools a player sees; [[§14.10]]'s are the spec's own,
and this one writes as they do.

Rejected: the end-to-end spec opening about 30 card packs per bar, which a new account could not
do anyway, since its packs unlock cards long before they spend a duplicate; and a dev grant of
tokens straight into the inventory, which would skip the bar's payout and the cycle's place, the
path a player's tokens take.
