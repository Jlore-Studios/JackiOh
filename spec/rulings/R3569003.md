Alnilam's "Death: Lose the game" is `winGame` for the other seat in its Death hook, as Last Word's is ([[R850]]): a hero at 0 still loses first. It is Indestructible, so a destroy does nothing to it ([[R46]]), and Untributable, so a Sacrifice cannot take it ([[R1220]]); only a Unit whose health has run out dies past it ([[R69]]). An exile or a Transform takes it out of the game with no Death and loses nothing.

Its Brittle count (3, Radiant 5) is spent at 0, and a count at 0 crumbles its card, a destroy that Indestructible ignores, the count staying at 0 and checked again at every tick ([[R385]]). So the card would stand for good, which is not what "Brittle 3" on a card that loses its owner the game means. A spent count therefore is its death: at the start of its controller's turn, in the start-of-turn triggers after the Brittle tick, Alnilam loses its controller the game when its count is spent. It is the only way the count ends it, and the third (Radiant: fifth) turn of its controller's after it lands is the turn it does.

Rejected: a Brittle count that never ends the card (the designer's list says Brittle is how it goes, and the plan's reading "only Brittle's count, an exile or a transform removes it" would be false of the exile alone then); and a Brittle that bypasses Indestructible (R46 holds for every destroy).
