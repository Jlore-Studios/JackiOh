# R1103: Prestige may be played with no Radiant card to choose

- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: accepted
- Decides: MD-D7 (docs/meditative-set.md M5)
- Spec: §8.8 row 53
- Proven in: `r1103_with_no_radiant_card_the_play_is_legal_and_only_adds` (M #53)

M #53 Prestige's declared target is one Radiant Unit or face-up backrow card on either side, or one
Radiant card in its controller's hand. With no Radiant card anywhere, R90 already allows the play
with nothing chosen: the clear fizzles and the AI card is still added. The play is never refused
for lack of a target.
