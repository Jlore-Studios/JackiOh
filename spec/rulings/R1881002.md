T-AI-4 Predictive Shield is a face-down Trap in [[§4.2]] step 4's window. It reveals on `attackDeclared` when an enemy Unit declares an attack on a Unit its controller has; its Radiant face also when the attack is on its controller's hero. Every condition is in its `when`, so an attack on the hero (base face) or by its own controller leaves it set ([[R61]]).

A forced attack declares nothing ([[R53]]): it opens no trap window and springs nothing (ST-S21-4). On a declared attack the trap cancels it ([[R44]]): no combat resolves, and the exertion the declaration spent stays spent. Then it Freezes the attacker, which thaws at the cleanup of its controller's next turn ([[R1174000]]), so it cannot attack on that turn either. The Freeze is the second half of one answer: a Freeze alone, landing after the declaration, would not stop the attack it answers.
