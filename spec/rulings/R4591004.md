IA #168 Impenetrable's "Your Units are immune to damage until your next turn" is `makeUnitsImmune`: a player modifier, so it covers every Unit the player controls while it holds — summoned later ones included — never only the Units there when it was cast. Rejected: covering only those, which would read a player modifier as a set of instances. A hit on one is no damage instance at all, so Divine Shield stays (as in [[R1849000]]). The expiry is Meditative #48 Tranquility's ([[R1021]]): made on their turn it lasts through the opponent's next; made on the opponent's, it ends at that turn's cleanup.
