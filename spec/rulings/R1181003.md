Meditative+ #23 Enormous Elderberry's "its Embiggened version costs (0)" sets the price of an embiggen card's Embiggened version alone (`embiggenCostOverride`): a play of that version starts from it in place of the printed dearer price, and in place of any `costOverride`, which then prices the normal version only; every cost modifier then applies as to any price ([[R65]]). The normal version keeps its price. Under Mountaintop Garden ([[R1181002]]) the override prices the dearer choice, which then plays the card Radiant and Embiggened for it. It is a price, so [[R766]] takes it off a card that reaches a graveyard or an exile pile, as it does `costOverride`. The AI's placeholder for a card its seat cannot read carries none ([[R185]]). The view's `embiggenCost` reads it, and the card face's second badge, beside the gem, shows the view's `embiggenCost` wherever the view gives one while the gem shows the normal price ([[§10.8]]).
