A step whose `reduce` does far more work than most is charged extra nodes up front (`surcharge_nodes`, issue #1240; [[§9.9]], Budgets): a Play of #97 Zephrys or C+ #27 Zephrys Zealotism (100), and an Attack #96 My Pawn would answer ([[R4809001]], 40). Where the counter's slice cannot pay that past the step's own node, `simulate` refuses the step, and the beam tries its siblings ([[R4809000]]). [[§9.9]]'s lethal solver walks depth-first on a slice of `AI_SEARCH.lethal_quick_nodes` (40), which never pays either surcharge, then best-first on the rest of its allowance. The first walk skipped such a step like one the engine refused, so a tree whose only way on was that step read as searched to the end, and the solver gave up without starting the second walk. Against a hero at 3, the AI holding only Zephrys, `find_lethal` gave up after 1 of its 300 nodes, where the best-first walk alone proves the lethal (Zephrys, its pick of C #69 Plague Charger, the Charger's play, its attack) in 107. A line the first walk found and then replayed on another determinization was judged lost there the same way when the replay met such a step. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the search changes no game.

**What changes.** `simulate`'s refusal of an unpaid surcharge is one named message, `UNPAID_SURCHARGE`. The first walk still skips such a step, but then ends cut, not exhausted, so the best-first walk runs on what is left of the allowance; so does a line whose replay on another determinization meets one (`Verdict::Unpaid`). The beam, the reply, the second walk, whose slice is the whole rest of the allowance, and `trim_lethal` ([[R1965000]]) read the refusal as before. The board above's lethal is now found in 108 nodes.

**Cost.** The allowance is unchanged ([[R2575000]]). A decision whose first walk meets such a step now spends up to its 300 lethal nodes where it spent a few, and its beam has that many fewer. A Zephrys lethal costs about 104 nodes a world, so three worlds cannot prove it inside 300, and `decide` and the lethal hint ([[R2102000]]) still miss it; one world fits, as `find_lethal_with_quick_nodes` and the concession check's proof ([[R2660000]]) give it on a My Pawn board.

Rejected:
- Checking `surcharge_nodes` against the slice in the walk before simulating: it restates `simulate`'s own test.
- Refusing such a step as `None`, as the reply does ([[R4809000]]): in the first walk `None` ends the walk at once, so the steps it had not tried yet would go untried in it.
- A larger `lethal_quick_nodes`: every decision would spend more on the first walk to pay for a few.
- Reading `Unpaid` as a cut in the best-first walk too: nothing runs after it, and a cut and an exhausted second walk both find no lethal.

It adds no rule and no constant. Issue #4790, which reworks the same two walks and is not built, keeps `Verdict::Unpaid`.
