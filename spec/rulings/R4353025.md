Core #52 Silly Silas since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V costs (6), from (4); his base face no longer prints "Summon this for your opponent.", and both faces print "Float reduces cost." as their first line. Both faces now land where the play named and stay with their player, as the Radiant face always has, so his Cry rotates the field from his own side ([[§10.5]]). "Float reduces cost" is the engine's hand trigger ([[R464]], [[R1563000]]), as #29 GIGA Glowy Jelly Bean's: each Float of his owner's lowers his cost by that much while he is in their hand, never in the deck ([[R2657002]]), kept until a graveyard or exile; at (6) he is past the 4 mana a turn gives, so he is played after a Float or with mana an effect gave. [[R3837000]], [[R3837001]] and [[R3837002]], and [[R4265000]]'s Cloaked play of him, now describe the replays from v0.6.2d through v0.6.8b, whose base face `script_v0_6_8b` keeps summoned for the opponent, with no Float ([[R767000]]); the engine's `summonForOpponent` flag stays for #55 Lava Golem and any card that prints it.
