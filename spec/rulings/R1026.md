"After this is attacked" runs once for each combat in which this was the attack's target, declared or forced, after that combat's state check, on its snapshot even if it died ([[R426]]'s mirror). It Nerfs the attacker only if the attacker is still on the field.

This build's decision: the attacker's `afterAttack` runs first, and the hook's facts are `{attackerId, forced, attackerSurvived}`. The rejected alternative was interleaving the two hooks, which would have ordered two cards' texts by nothing the cards print.
