Every signed-in session sends the presence heartbeat on every screen, whether or not the account has a friend, so the Privacy Policy names the last time the browser said it was online for every signed-in account, not only for Play together. The heartbeat also beat while the consent screen was up, before the account had agreed to the policy that names it. `POST /api/presence` now reads the account's agreement and, while it owes the terms in force ([[R757001]]), answers 204 and records nothing; friends see the account offline (or in its match, which the in-match flag says) until it agrees, and its next beat counts.

Rejected: holding the beat in the client until the gate's read says nothing is owed, which the shell, outside every gate, does not hold, and which the server would still have to trust; holding it until the account has a friend, which the policy would then have to explain as well. The read costs one query a beat, on this route alone ([[R757001]] rejected one on every route).
