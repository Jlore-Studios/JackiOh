Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes.

**The rule.** From 601 to 760 px wide, on a screen over 500 px tall, the deck editor's "Compare with" sits over its select, as it does from 761 px, and the select is drawn at the body's 13 px, as it is at 820 px. Both the label and the option the select shows ("Another deck or a trio…") are whole.

**Why.** [[R3956000]] gave the editor a 232 px sidebar from 601 to 760 px, so at 744 px the picker has 210 px. The phone's rules (760 px and under) set "Compare with" beside its select and the select to 16 px, which a phone needs so that it does not zoom into the field. At 744 px they left the select about 104 px wide, against 178.5 px for the text, and it read "Another (". Stacking the label alone is not enough: the text at 16 px needs a select about 218 px wide, and 210 px is all there is. At 13 px the text is 145 px and fits with room to spare.

**Unchanged.** Phones (600 px and under), 761 px and over (820x1180 and the desktop sizes among them) and a phone on its side (500 px tall or less) keep the rules they had. A phone on its side that is 601 to 760 px wide keeps the 16 px field, which stops it zooming on focus, and with it the cut picker; it is the next thing to decide, not part of this ruling.

**Proof.** `layout.test.ts` reads the sheet: the block applies from 601 to 760 px above 500 px tall, comes after the 760 px block it overrides, and the phone's row and its `flex: 1 1 auto` stay in that block. Spec 46 opens a deck with two others saved, so the select is enabled and shows its placeholder, and at every menu size measures the option's text in the select's own font against the select's room (its width less its padding and its arrow), since a native select's `scrollWidth` never says its text is cut. It also fails when the label runs past the picker or onto the select, or when the sidebar, the select or the editor spills sideways. At 744 px the room before the change was 66 px against 178.5 px of text; after it, 172 px against 145 px.

Rejected:
- A shorter placeholder: it changes the words at every size and in Chinese, where each character is 1 em wide, so it would still not fit at 16 px.
- Stacking the label at 16 px alone: the text is still about 8 px too wide.
- A sidebar wider than 232 px at 744 px: it takes the room from the pool's two columns ([[R3956000]]).
- Trimming only the select's padding: 16 px has no margin to give.
- 15 px (`--type-lg`): it fits with about 7 px to spare, where 13 px keeps the field the size of the rest of the sidebar's.
- 13 px for a phone on its side as well: it would zoom the phone into the field on focus.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
