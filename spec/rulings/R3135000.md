TG #39's list of ten loses a step in eight entries, the owner's list of issue #3128. The numbers are `config.rs`'s (`CHAOS_TG_*`), the words the entries' `label`s, the card's printed text and §8.11 row 39 carry, which `chaosRolled` names to both players ([[R436]]) and the card's tests hold to the printed list in its order.

| # | Entry | Through v0.5.11p | Now | Constant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | fortify | Summon 3 Rush Tokens, then Taunt and Divine Shield | unchanged | `CHAOS_TG_RUSH_TOKENS` 3 |
| 2 | designs | Fill the hand with Goetzmann Game Designs and refresh 4 mana | refresh **3** | `CHAOS_TG_REFRESH` 4 → 3 |
| 3 | cards | Add 4 random Training Grounds cards, which cost (0) | which cost **(1)**, and gain **2** mana | `CHAOS_TG_CARD_COST` 1, `CHAOS_TG_CARDS_MANA` 2 |
| 4 | mana | Gain 8 mana and draw 2 cards | gain **6** mana | `CHAOS_TG_MANA` 8 → 6 |
| 5 | permanents | Summon 6 random Training Grounds permanents | **5** | `CHAOS_TG_PERMANENTS` 6 → 5 |
| 6 | radiant | Add 2 random Radiant Training Grounds cards, which cost (0) | which cost **(1)**, and gain **1** mana | `CHAOS_TG_CARD_COST` 1, `CHAOS_TG_RADIANT_MANA` 1 |
| 7 | army | Summon 5 Rush Tokens, and Rush, Trample and Pierce for good | **3** Rush Tokens | `CHAOS_TG_ARMY_TOKENS` 5 → 3 |
| 8 | golem | Summon a Training-Grounds Golem | unchanged | none |
| 9 | alternate | A Call to Chaos at (0) now and at the start of every second turn | at the start of every turn, at its printed price, none now | `CHAOS_TG_EVERY` 2 → 1 |
| 10 | recast | Cast a random Call to Chaos | unchanged | none |

- **Entries 3 and 6.** The price is the same `costOverride` the (0) was, now `CHAOS_TG_CARD_COST` (1): a fixed price, not a discount, so a Buff or a cost rule reads it as it reads any override ([[R65]]). The Call's own price (4) is no part of it, which is why the old shared constant `CHAOS_TG_COST` is gone. The cards are added first, then the mana is gained with `gain_mana`: this turn's mana, which may pass max mana ([[§2.3]]), the same verb as entry 4's. It is gained whatever the hand took: a full hand burns the cards ([[§2.4]]) and the mana still comes, as Hearthstone's "Add 3 cards to your hand. Gain 2 Mana Crystals" does not ask what was added. On the Radiant face the entries resolve in list order, so a roll of 3, 4 and 6 gains 2, then 6, then 1.
- **Entry 9, the owner's confirmed reading.** "At the start of every turn, add a Call to Chaos to your hand" is taken as it is written: the entry adds nothing when the card resolves, and gives its caster a rest-of-game start-of-turn effect ([[R458]]) that adds one random Call to Chaos at its printed price (4), no `costOverride`, at the start of each of the caster's own turns. The designer's "every turn" is read as the caster's turns, not the opponent's, as [[R2200002]] read "every other turn": the Call goes to the caster's hand, and their opponent's turns never count. An extra turn is one of the caster's turns and adds one ([[R846]]). The Call is drawn from the pool that names no set, this edition among them ([[R380]], [[R387]], [[R2200004]]), on its base face, and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). The effects stack up to `CHAOS_TG_ALTERNATE_CAP` (3) a player, and a roll of the entry at the cap resolves into nothing, with no re-roll ([[R87]], [[R1241]]); three held effects add three Calls a turn. The step is still [[R2200002]]'s `alternate_step`, `CHAOS_TG_EVERY` now 1, so the multiple of 1 is each turn begun since the entry resolved, the turn it resolved in not counted. Its badge ([[R169]]) and the entry's `label` are the one clause, `CHAOS_TG_ALTERNATE_LABEL`: "At the start of every turn of yours for the rest of the game, add a random Call to Chaos to your hand".
- **Replays.** No script is kept. The card's script does not change and its numbers are the engine's, so a replay of a game that rolled a changed entry (2 to 7 or 9) folds under the new engine and is refused by its final hash, never shown as another game ([[R767000]]); a game that rolled entries 1, 8 or 10 folds as before.

Rejected: the other reading of entry 9, "every turn, free, and one now" (the old (0) and "now", only "second" changed to "every"), which would make the entry stronger in a pass that lowers every other one; the owner chose the literal reading. Also rejected: dropping the cap, which is the entry's terminator (REVIEW A3.6) and bounds the Calls added each turn.

It amends [[R2200000]] (entries 3 and 6 add at (1) and gain mana, entry 5 summons five), [[R2200001]] (Refresh 3), [[R2200002]] (entry 9 as above) and [[R2200003]] (three tokens). The Core, Classic+ and Stinky editions are not changed.
