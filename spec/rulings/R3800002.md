A card in a graveyard or an exile pile costs its printed cost ([[R766]]). So when C+ #41 KY's Constant sets the cost of a card an earlier effect of the same resolution moved to a graveyard or an exile pile (its chosen hand card, discarded first), the cost is not written and no `numberChanged` is sent. Its other numbers are `tuning`, which a card keeps in every zone ([[R386]]), and are set as before. Rejected: having a chosen hand card that left the hand fizzle for every effect, a wider change to how targets resolve (seed 6140).
