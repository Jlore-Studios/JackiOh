The owner asked M #95 and S #151 to "drop a few percent" by tuning down the stats of a few of their modes (issue #3128) and gave no figures; no per-mode win-rate data exists in the repo (`analysis/`, `training/`). So each edition has three of its ten modes cut one step, the three that hand over the most material for one roll, and the rule for the choice is: every nerf is a number in `config.rs`, at most four modes, none to zero.

| Edition | Mode | Through v0.5.11p | Now | Constant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M #95 | fuse | Fuse the hand, add 2 copies, all three at (0) | add **1** copy, both at (0) | `CHAOS_MED_FUSE_COPIES` 2 → 1 |
| M #95 | armor | the hero gains 6 Armor and heals 6 | **5** and **5** | `CHAOS_MED_HERO_ARMOR`, `CHAOS_MED_HEAL` 6 → 5 |
| M #95 | acclaimed | Summon 3 random Acclaimed cards | **2** | `CHAOS_MED_ACCLAIMED` 3 → 2 |
| S #151 | corvettes | Summon 3 Drone Corvettes | **2** | `CHAOS_SCAPE_CORVETTES` 3 → 2 |
| S #151 | pieces | Add 4 random Starship Pieces, at (0) | **3** | `CHAOS_SCAPE_HAND_PIECES` 4 → 3 |
| S #151 | launch | Fuse 4 random Starship Pieces into the Dormant Starship, then launch it | **3** | `CHAOS_SCAPE_FUSED_PIECES` 4 → 3 |

The other seven of each are unchanged: M #95's two CN cards, two Prime cards, Jade Beauty, bounce and Nerf, CN Golem, the eternal cast (`CALL_TO_CHAOS_ETERNAL_CAP` 3) and the recast; S #151's Anomaly, Battleship, two Systems, Interceptors, two Weapons, the Marketplace lot (20 Credits) and the recast.

- **Why these, and how much.** Each mode is one tenth of a base roll and three of ten of a Radiant one, so a step off a mode is a tenth of that step off a roll. M #95 loses a third of the fuse's cards (three to two), a sixth of the Armor and heal (6 to 5) and a third of the Acclaimed cards (3 to 2); S #151 a third of the Corvettes (3 to 2) and a quarter of each Pieces mode (4 to 3). Were the ten modes worth the same, that is about 8 percent of the material of a roll for either card, which "a few percent" reads as once the card's (4) price, the Golem, the eternal cast, the recast and the other seven modes are counted. It is an estimate of material, not a measure of play: the paired comparison of `docs/issues-and-patches.md` (Win rates in a balance patch) can measure it.
- **M #95's fuse.** The hand is fused into one card as before ([[R1242]]), and `CHAOS_MED_FUSE_COPIES` (1) copy is added, so two cards at (0) come to the hand; one fusable card takes the (0) and the one copy. A fusion past `FUSED_CARDS_CAP` is refused as before ([[R874000]]); a hand of the entry's own cards now doubles with each fusion where it tripled, so six fusions are made (2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 cards fused in) before the cap refuses the seventh, where four were.
- **Words.** The labels `chaosRolled` names to both players ([[R436]]), the card's printed text, §8.8 row 95, §8.10 row 151 and the client's short names for M #95's reels say the new numbers: "add 1 copy of it to your hand, both of which cost (0)", "gains 5 Armor and you heal it 5", "Summon 2 random Acclaimed cards", "Summon 2 Drone Corvettes", "Add 3 random Starship Pieces", "Fuse 3 random Starship Pieces".
- **Replays.** No script is kept: neither card's script changes, and S #151's table is a list of labels and builders whose numbers are the engine's. A replay of a game that rolled one of the six changed modes folds under the new engine and is refused by its final hash, never shown as another game ([[R767000]]).

It amends [[R1242]] (one copy), [[R1243]] (two picks) and [[R1882008]] (three pieces). It is the same arithmetic as [[R3135000]], which tunes TG #39 by the owner's own list.
