A season is named by the minor version of the game (`v<major>.<minor>` of the newest patch): every patch of v0.2 plays in season `v0.2`, and the first patch of v0.3 opens the next. Opening a season past the first runs the soft reset over every rated player in the same transaction as the season row: each rating pulled `SEASON_RESET_STRENGTH` of the way toward the players' mean, each deviation widened by `SEASON_RESET_DEVIATION_BOOST` in quadrature and capped at a new player's, volatility kept. Everyone starts the season back in Raisin placements; bots are not reset. The reset runs the same path against a copy of real data first (`--dry-run`, which reports and rolls back) before going live
