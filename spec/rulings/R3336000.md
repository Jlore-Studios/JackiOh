Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. Drone Core X (`stinkyscape-036`) summons the Drone Battleship as three tokens in one zone, bottom first ([[R1842000]]), and its Shield redirects lethal damage to itself while it tops the pile ([[R1842001]]). Each layer drew as a picture of its own, and the stack as none. The owner asked for the ship as a whole (issue #3330), so the three layers are the decks of one battleship.

**The art.** A layer is the reactor keel (Meltdown: a hull plate whose vents show the core), the hangar decks (Propagation: two bay doors with drones racked inside and a light over each) or the command superstructure (Shield: a tower, two emitter spires with glowing tips and a dome), each in its layer's color and gilded inside the Radiant rules when Radiant ([[R2725004]]). A printed card draws its own deck large in the middle, with the other two faint above or below it where the stack puts them. A layer on the board draws its own deck and every deck beneath it (`UnitView.buried`), stacked, so the top card shows the whole ship as far as it stands: three decks while all three live, two once the Shield is gone, one for the keel alone (`data-art-decks`). The keel's core glows hotter with the health it has lost, in `SHIP_HEAT_STEPS`, and its cracks spread with that and with a Brittle count at or under `SHIP_BRITTLE_CRACK_AT`; the hangar's lights stay lit for the bays that still hold drones (an Activate with uses left; the opponent's view carries none, so both show lit). The art is memoised by that state, quantised so the cache stays small. The six pictures of the three layers in `stinkyscape-art-v0.5.0.json` were recorded again for it, as #3142 did for its two cards ([[R503]]); no other entry's hash moved.

**The arrival.** Drone Core X's `battleshipWarp` ([[R2797000]]) is one beat per layer it summons, `FX_SHIP_ARRIVAL_BEATS` of them, each on its own `summoned` in stack order: the warp drops the reactor keel from the board's top edge in starlight, as before; the hangar decks lock onto it with two bolts from above its corners, a ring, a spark burst and a shake (`FX_SHIP_LOCK_TRAUMA`); the superstructure comes down on them and its dome flares in a full shield (`FX_SHIP_DOME_TRAUMA`). A horn sounds with the keel, a lock with the hangar and an armor ring with the dome. The engine reports three summons and the runner plays each as an entry of its own, so the arrival is three entries (about 1.65 s with their tails), tested to end inside `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS` ([[R2785000]]), and not one held set piece: a hold would stop the game for a beat the summons already pace. A Battleship that Call to Chaos (Scape Edition) or Anomaly Found! summons gets the layer beats below and no arrival, which belongs to Drone Core X's play.

**The shield takes the hit.** `planBattleship` (`fx/battleship.ts`) reads the public events and the layers the view draws. A `redirected` onto a Shield layer is a bolt from the protected target (the hero or the Unit) to the dome, `FX_SHIP_DOME_AT`, which flares in a full shield and ripples `FX_SHIP_RIPPLES` rings as it lands, and shakes the board; it draws in place of the redirect's own row. The hit the redirect sent (the latest redirect onto that Shield, with no hit on it since) flares the dome again and lights the armor plates: one per point of Armor up to `FX_SHIP_PLATES_MAX`, the first as many as Armor took of the hit (`absorbed`) bright, each dying down to the dim every plate rests at, one after the next. The `damageAbsorbed` of it draws in place of the armor row, whose bloom the dome stands in for; a `damage` keeps its row, so its number shows. Any other hit on the Shield is a flicker: a sheen on the card, a small shield on the dome and a light shake (`FX_SHIP_HIT_TRAUMA`).

**Drones launch from the bays.** An `activated` of a Propagation layer opens a bay, a `ship` cue over its card: two doors slide apart and stay open, and a light stays on for each bay that still has a use after this one. The drone its Activate summons, a `summoned` Interceptor or Corvette while the memory's resolving hook is that Activate, leaves the bay for its tile: an Interceptor as a fast bolt (`FX_SHIP_INTERCEPTOR_FLIGHT` of the entry), a Corvette as a slow exhaust trail (`FX_SHIP_CORVETTE_FLIGHT`) that lands with a burst and a shake. A drone summoned by anything else draws no launch.

**The meltdown glows to the end.** The Meltdown layer's end-of-turn damage to itself pulses its core: a fire ring, an ember burst and a shake, all growing with how much of its health is gone, counting the tick (`level`, 0 to 1; `FX_SHIP_TICK_TRAUMA` times it), so each turn's pulse is brighter than the last. Its attacks (Charge, Windfury, First Strike) fire from the core, and its death is the reactor's blast: two rings, a fire, an ember and a shard burst, rays and the hardest shake the ship makes (`FX_SHIP_BLAST_TRAUMA`).

**A layer breaks.** A Shield or a Propagation layer that dies with a deck beneath it (`buried` above 0) shears its deck off: a crack over the card, a burst of plates and a shake (`FX_SHIP_BREAK_TRAUMA`), then, at `FX_SHIP_LIGHTS_AT` of the entry, a sheen and a ring over the zone, the next deck's lights coming up. A layer with nothing beneath it breaks no deck.

**Sounds.** Each beat has its own: a horn, a dome flare, a bay door, a reactor pulse that is deeper, longer and louder with the damage (`amount` 1 to 10, the blast 10) and a deck shear, with the lock, the armor ring, the armor clank, the burn and the summon the sound table already has (`audio/battleshipCues.ts`).

**Hidden information.** The three layers and their drones are public on the board, so the planner reads only the view's units and the public events ([[R202]]): no cue or sound names a card's definition, a hidden id never reaches a lookup, and a layer the seat is not shown plans nothing. Everything starts inside its entry and ends within `FX_SHIP_TAIL_MS` of it, inside `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` ([[R200]]); the effects speed scales it with the entry ([[R201]]). Under Reduce Motion nothing of the ship cue draws, since it has no still frame.

**Open questions, rejected alternatives.**

- The issue says the plates "glow down as `Armor` is spent", but Armor is a reduction taken from every hit and is never spent. The plates glow for what Armor took of the hit and dim back to rest, so the number of lit plates is that hit's. A person may want the plates to track something else.
- Signature entries in `fx/cards/stinkyscape-*.json5` (item 7 of the issue) were not given: #3332 is not on `main`, and a table `bespoke` entry plans only from a play or a hook, while the Shield's guard, a break and the blast belong to no hook. The three layers read `none: Battleship planner` until it lands; the Propagation layer's old `activate: conjure` gave way to the planner, so a bay and a conjure do not both draw.
- One held set piece for the arrival was rejected for the three entries above (see The arrival).
- Reading the bay lights from the sum of `usesLeft` was rejected for the count of Activates that still have a use, which is what the art draws.
- A ghost copy of its own deck among the faint ones on a printed card was rejected: only the other two decks are faint.
