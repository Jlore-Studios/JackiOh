A Resource ([[R1840000]]'s (0) Spell token that carries `resource`) is a card of its holder's hand like any other. "Gain a Resource" rolls it as [[R1840002]] does and adds it to its gainer's hand (`draw::add_to_hand`): it takes a hand slot, it arrives with `addedToHand`, hidden from the other player as any gain is ([[R97]]), and a full hand burns it ([[R4]]). "Fill your hand with Resources" gains as many as the hand has room for under its cap. It is played from the hand with the `play` action, as any card is, and every hand rule reaches it: the cap, a discard, a steal, a Trade, the mulligan and the AI's redaction. "Discard a Narcor" (`discard_resource`) discards the Resource of those names longest in the hand, with no prompt, as a hand discard (`discarded`, its graveyard), which a discard guard does not stop: it is the card's own price, as [[R800]] reads a cost. The tray of [[R1840000]], its cap and sale ([[R1840001]]), its play ([[R1840003]]) and its discard ([[R1840004]]) are gone: `PlayerState.resourceTray`, `Zone::Tray`, `cardPlayed.from: "tray"`, the view's `resourceTray` and the events `resourceGained`, `resourceSold` and `resourceDiscarded`. The owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2250) asked for one hand, so a Resource competes for the room every card does.
