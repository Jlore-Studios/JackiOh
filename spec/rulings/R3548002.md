What is a Warhero Token worth, when does that change, and how is it paid?

Every Warhero Token is worth one game-wide value in Credits. It starts at 0 once the first Warhero
Token has been printed anywhere — minting the card is what starts it, wherever the card arrives —
and grows by a random `WARHERO_GROWTH_MIN` to `WARHERO_GROWTH_MAX` (0 to 10, from the match rng) at
the end of every turn after one has, reported as a `warhero` `counterChanged` with the growth it
added. A Radiant one is worth double. "They do nothing when played other than give credits": playing
one gives its value in Credits, discarding one pays the same, and selling one at the Marketplace
quotes the same value — through the Resource's own rules, with no draw. The Token carries no
`resource` block, so no Resource roll or requote ever lists it; its hand card quotes the worth even
with the Marketplace closed.

Rejected: a per-card worth rolled as each Token arrives: the designer says all Warhero Tokens share one value
that grows every turn once the first is printed.
