MN03's fourteen emoji are stickers like [[R644]]'s five: original inline SVG in the tavern's flat gold-and-ink style, drawn the same for every portrait, no borrowed art and no `<text>`, popping out of the sender's portrait with the same bounce, hold and fade over two seconds, a fade alone under Reduce Motion. Wave is a smiling face and a waving hand; Clap a grinning face over two clapping hands; Thumbs Up a fist with its thumb up; Facepalm a face behind its palm; Shrug a face with raised brows and upturned palms; Thinking a face with a raised brow and a finger on its chin; Heart a red heart; Fire a flame; Skull a skull; Sweat a nervous grin with a drop running down; Cool a smirk behind dark shades; Gasp wide eyes over an O; Salute a hand snapped to the brow; Party a popper bursting with confetti. The parts that move inside a sticker (the waving hand, the clapping hands, the heartbeat, the flame's flicker, the running drop, the confetti) are still under Reduce Motion, by the media query and by the app's own flag. Each has an accessible name — its menu item's label and title, Wave, Clap, Thumbs Up, Facepalm, Shrug, Thinking, Heart, Fire, Skull, Sweat, Cool, Gasp, Salute and Party.

Each sounds its own Web Audio synthesis on the effects channel, no files: a bright two-note "hi-ya"; a ragged burst of claps; a warm two-blip "uh-huh"; a soft slap and a falling groan; a lilting "meh"; a rising "hmm" with a wobble; two heartbeat thumps and a chime; a crackling whoosh; a hollow run of bony knocks; a nervous gulp; a smooth glide up under a shimmer; a sharp rising intake of breath; a two-note bugle call; a popper's pop and a shower of tinkles. Every recipe keeps the effects' contract (nothing before its start, silent by its length, peak under 1) like every other.
