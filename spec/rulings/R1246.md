Meditative Journey's choice is a `pick` prompt (`choose_pick`, the chooser's alone, [[§10.8]]) of up to `cards` cards of its caster's hand as it resolves: a resolution prompt rather than a declared hand pick, because a declaration's maximum is fixed and `cards` tunes ([[R386]]). Its step exiles the picks in the order picked, which is the order the prompt offered them, hand order ([[R221]]), whatever order the answer lists them in (a unit-token card ceases to exist instead, [[R11]]), then shuffles one Journey Complete (Meditative #96.1) into its caster's deck, publicly and recorded in their list ([[R311]]), remembering the picked cards' ids in that order. With no card picked nothing is exiled and no Journey Complete is shuffled in; a full deck refuses it ([[R80]]) and the cards stay in exile. The remembering is `shuffle_into`'s `memory`: plain JSON written on each new card's `memory` ([[§10.1]]), here `memory.journey`, so a card shuffled in remembers what made it. It survives a JSON round trip and folds from the log like any memory, and a `shuffle_into` without one writes nothing.
