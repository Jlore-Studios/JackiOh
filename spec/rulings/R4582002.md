An Infinite Assault fight's rail names the enemy and its level, beside the chip that lists the
live modifiers in full, as every other table's rail lists them ([[R169]], [[R3912000]]): the
enemy's name over its list, and the level beside it. A Commander's name stands on its hero, where
a normal enemy's hero keeps the default label. The enemy's upgrades sit in a tray beside its hero,
each by name, stacks and effect, listed from the level the setup names, which the Assault states
openly. The view's modifiers carry no handicap or deck mark ([[§10.8]]), so the tray reads the
level, never the game.

This is presentation only (rule 7): the board draws what it is handed and reads nothing itself.

Rejected: upgrades as engine modifiers, which would change every game's view and hash for a label.
This follows Hearthstone semantics: the board's information is the same at every table.
