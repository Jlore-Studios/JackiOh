Issue #4154 asked to nerf each of C+ #40 Appropriations' four options "by some margin, consider adding floor functions". Each number but the Armor is cut to a formula in X that rounds down (⌊ ⌋, an integer division), about 15% below the v0.6.6h number at X = 4 and never above it at any X, as [[R3996201]] cut it:

| Option | Base through v0.6.6h | Base now | Radiant through v0.6.6h | Radiant now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Military: +Attack and Rush | 2X−3 | ⌊5X/3⌋−2 | 4X−5 | ⌊17X/5⌋−4 |
| Education: Radiant Books shuffled in | 2X−3 | ⌊5X/3⌋−2 | 4X−3 | ⌊17X/5⌋−2 |
| Culture: % chance to become Radiant | 5X | ⌊17X/4⌋ | 14X | 12X |
| Healthcare: +Health | 2X−3 | ⌊5X/3⌋−2 | 4X | ⌊17X/5⌋ |
| Healthcare: Armor | X | X | 2X | 2X |

At X = 4 that is 5 → 4, 5 → 4, 20% → 17% and 5 → 4 on the base face, and 11 → 9, 13 → 11, 56% → 48% and 16 → 13 on the Radiant; the base's ⌊5X/3⌋−2 matches 2X−3 at X = 1 to 3 and cuts 11% to 20% from X = 4 on, and the Radiant's ⌊17X/5⌋ terms are 85% of 4X, rounded down. The Armor is kept, as [[R3135002]] and [[R3996201]] kept it: the Health's cut is the option's.

- **The floor.** [[R3135002]]'s floor stands: a number below 1 reads 1, so at X = 1 every base number but Culture and Armor reads 1 as before, and so do the Radiant Military and Education.
- **Culture** still rolls each card on its own; a chance of 100% or more, turning every card, now comes at X = 24 on the base face and X = 9 on the Radiant, where it came at X = 20 and X = 8.
- **The client** restates the option texts the Discover shows, ⌊ ⌋ and all (`modeText.ts`, their Chinese in the web's `zh-CN.json`), and the card's words do not change.
- **Replays.** The script through v0.6.6h is kept as `script_v0_6_6h`, beside `script_v0_6_5b` and the older ones ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: a flat 85% of each v0.6.6h number, rounded down, which cuts unevenly as the rounding falls (29% at X = 5 on the base face), and cutting the Armor too.

It edits §8.7 row 40.
