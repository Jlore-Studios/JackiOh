The engine deals Cleave ([[§4.4]] step 10, [[§3.1]]) by one walk, now the board fact `query::cleave_struck`, which its lethal projection reads too: a GIGA beside the target in either of its lanes is struck once, by id, a GIGA target strikes the units beside both its lanes ([[R3546001]]), and beside a Unit standing in the backrow only another Unit standing there is adjacent ([[R1081000]]). The AI's static splash (`cleaved_neighbours`, [[§9.9]]), read by the opponent's reply (`trade_value`) and by the count of the damage past the AI's Taunts (`damage_past_taunts_with`), was an older copy that read only the zones beside the target's anchor lane, written before both rulings. A GIGA's card sits only in its anchor zone, so the copy never reached the unit past a GIGA target's second lane, never reached a GIGA whose second lane is beside the target, and counted a Trap or a Field Spell beside a standing Unit.

With the flag `giga_cleave_read`, every static splash is `cleave_struck` less the target. The Taunt walk ([[R3937000]]) also tries a third order: at each Taunt, the first Cleave attacker in the walk's order that destroys it alone goes in first, when its splash meets a Taunt the walk has still to reach and either of the two is GIGA. The third walk runs only when the first met such an attacker behind another one; the count is the largest of the orders walked.

On the issue's board the AI is at 6 life behind the Jlockeed Large Language Model token (a 2/5 GIGA Taunt) in lanes 1-2 and Thorium Wall (a 0/8 Taunt) in lane 3, against the CN Golem token (10/10, Poisonous, Cleave, Pierce) and Voidwalker (6). The Golem's swing at the GIGA destroys it, and its splash, meeting the Wall beside the GIGA's second lane, destroys the Wall, so Voidwalker's 6 goes face for the lethal. The AI counted 0: the old splash never reached the Wall, and with it fixed, Voidwalker breaking the GIGA first still leaves the Golem on the Wall. It now counts 6.

The flag is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick's walk keeps the one-lane splash and its two orders. The walk reads its weights' flag; the reply reads `AI_EVAL`'s, as it reads [[R4064000]]'s. Everything that reads the walk is reached: `face_threat` and the threat and pressure terms, `wall_switches_priced`, the lethal solver's `ready_gap` and the Duos pool loss.

It follows Hearthstone semantics: a minion fills its whole space, so whatever stands beside any part of it is beside it.

Rejected:

- rebuilding the walk in the AI from `adjacent_slots`, `occupant_at` and `is_adjacent_card`, which keeps two copies of one rule that can drift apart again, as this one did;
- the Cleave-first order on every board, which would change play on boards with no GIGA, where [[R3937000]] left it out of its model;
- a search over the orders, since the count runs at every evaluation.

Not modelled: the Cleave-first order where neither Taunt is GIGA, as [[R3937000]] has it.

It reads only public state, and it adds no rule of the game and no constant.
