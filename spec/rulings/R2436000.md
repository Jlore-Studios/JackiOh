Since issue #2436 a Buff's keyword row offers a Field Spell Immutable as well as Indestructible (`TUNE_FIELD_SPELL_KEYWORDS`, Indestructible then Immutable), each only while the card lacks it, one drawn uniformly when both are open ([[R442]]) and none drawn when one is ([[R129]]); a Field Spell that deals damage draws among those two, Pierce and Lifesteal ([[R1748001]]). The owner asked for it on the issue. A Unit still draws from [[R21]]'s pool, which leaves Immutable out, and a Spell, a Trap and a Field Trap gain no Immutable from a Buff.

The keyword is `tuning`, as every Buff's is ([[R386]]), so the Field Spell is Immutable for every rule from then on, in every zone: no later Buff or Nerf changes it and nothing is drawn for one ([[R23]]), no Vanilla, Transform or Fuse-onto reaches it, and while it is face-up on the field no Spell, Field Spell or Field Trap targets it ([[R781001]]). One application adds both keywords only on a card whose text makes Buffs several times as effective ([[R1160]]). A game recorded before the change in which a Buff reached a Field Spell no longer folds to its recorded hash and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). This amends [[R1748001]] and [[R386]]'s keyword clause.
