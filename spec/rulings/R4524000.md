The fuzz monitor's I19 ([[R4454002]]) found S+ #77 Black Hole exiling a Dormant card that lay beneath a Stack card ([[R1839003]]): the hole passed a Dormant card by only when it topped its zone ([[R3554000]], [[R3554001]]), and a pull or a swallow took the pile above one whole, the Dormant card with it. No effect reaches a Dormant card ([[R1839000]]), and a pile steps or goes whole, so the hole now passes by a pile that holds a Dormant card anywhere in it, on top or beneath: neither its swallow as it opens nor its pull, at a turn's start or, since [[R4440050]], its end, moves it or exiles any of it. The lanes around it are pulled and swallowed as before. Rejected: exiling the cards above the Dormant one and leaving it in the zone, which splits a pile no other rule splits; and moving the pile with the Dormant card in it, which reaches it.
