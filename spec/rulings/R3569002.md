Supernova's "Fuse all Units in the board into an Alnilam" is the verb `fuse_board_into`, which names the card to be made. It takes every Unit the Spell reaches, on both sides, as an ingredient: a Unit standing in a unit zone or in a backrow zone as a Flyer, the caster's first. A Dormant Unit ([[R1839000]]), an Immutable one ([[R781001]]) and one Immune to Spells stay where they are. The ingredients cease to exist as a fused card's ingredients do (R77, R86: no Death, nothing destroyed, no graveyard), a fresh Alnilam, Radiant on the Radiant face, is summoned into the caster's leftmost open unit zone, and the ingredients fuse into it, so the one card on the board is "Alnilam + …" with every ingredient's stats, keywords and texts. The fusion is the subsystem's own call, so the new Alnilam is the kept card though it is Immune to Spells. With no Unit to take, the bare Alnilam is summoned. With no unit zone for it, neither free nor freed by a Unit of the caster's that goes, nothing happens at all.

Rejected: a Transform of each Unit (it would make many Alnilams, not one fused card); and keeping an existing Unit as the kept card (the text names the fresh card).
