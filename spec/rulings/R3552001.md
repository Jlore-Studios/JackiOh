S+ #156 Horizon Breaker reads "When launched, cast a random Terminus Card", and its Radiant face
"… a random Radiant Terminus Card". The spec has no such pool or cast, so it is read as follows.

- **"When launched".** The Starship's launch ([[R1852003]]), as for S+ #154 ([[R3552000]]): only as part
  of the Starship the piece is fused into.
- **The pool.** One uniform pick from every non-excluded card tagged Terminus of the shipped sets
  ([[R380]]), tokens included ([[R382]]: the Terminus Spells and Traps of the Stinkyscape set's #4 are
  tokens), the pool naming no set, so the held S+ Terminus cards join it at the release ([[R1420]]). It
  never picks this card ([[R387]]).
- **The cast.** One `castRandom`, free, from no zone, every choice random so that nothing pauses for a
  prompt. A pick that is no Spell is cast as its own type, through the same pipeline a played card takes:
  a Unit is summoned, a Trap is set, a Spell resolves and goes to the graveyard; it is not re-rolled.
  The Radiant face casts the card Radiant.

Rejected: re-rolling a pick that is no Spell; restricting the pool to Spells; and a pool naming the S+ set
(it would reach held cards before the release).
