Fused, FvH #112 Jilliax Deluxe costs [[R77]]'s min(the sum of the printed costs, 4), its own read as the X paid ([[R65]]), and is no X card. A Recursion Module (#112.1) gives it Flashback ([[R783012]]). On the base module's face, its Flashback play runs no Cry ([[R5239006]]), so it keeps its modules and discovers none. On the Radiant face, its Flashback play runs the Cry again with X 0, the X a play of a card without X pays ([[R348]] asks for at least 1 only where X is chosen). The offer is then the (0) modules not already fused in, at most Recursion and Durability, and a second offer with nothing left fuses the one pick ([[R5262001]]). The price the replay paid is not read as X: X is what the card's cost names, and a fused card's names none.
