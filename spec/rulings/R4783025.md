Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: Quick Chaos, "Normal version gains: Discard 1 random card". The base face of S+ #158 reads "Cast on draw: Cast a random Spell with random targets. Discard {discards|random card|random cards}.", the declared `discards` 1, a number the base face alone prints ([[R1431]], [[R386]]), as #21 Hinder's is. The discard is the list's second part: the random Spell ([[R3557005]]) is cast and resolves whole first, every choice it makes random, and then `discards` random cards of the caster's own hand go to the graveyard ([[R682]]), drawn from the hand as that Spell left it, so a card the Spell drew or added may be the one discarded and a hand the Spell emptied loses nothing. Quick Chaos itself is resolving, in no hand, so it is never the card. The Radiant face is unchanged and discards nothing. Replays of the versions before this patch fold under `script_v0_6_13h`, whose base face casts and discards nothing ([[R767000]]). Rejected: discarding before the cast, which reads the text out of its order.
