[[§5.1]]'s query gains `anyTags`: a card matches when it has at least one of the listed tags, where `tags` asks for every one. "A random KY or CN card" (Meditative #38 H1B Printer) and "a random Human, Book, CN, or AI-Generated card" (#60 Eschews) are one pool each, every matching definition one entry, so a card with two of the tags is no likelier than a card with one. A Fruit or Prime tag among them takes its tokens as it does in `tags` ([[R382]], [[R769]]), and `tags`, `notTags` and `anyTags` together narrow as each one does alone.
