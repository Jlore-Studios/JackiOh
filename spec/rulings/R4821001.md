A Trade puts a hand card into its owner's deck and draws one ([[R1838000]]), and its draw cannot fatigue, since the deck holds at least the traded card ([[R1838002]]). A unit-token card in a hand (#75 Infinite Reserves' Rush Token cards, or a copy of one) would cease to exist on its way in ([[R11]]), so the deck would hold nothing it did not hold before, and from an empty deck the draw would fatigue its player. So the deck does not take it, as a deck at [[R80]]'s cap does not: `trade::why_trade_refused`'s last check refuses a unit-token card with "<name> is a Unit token, which would cease to exist instead of going into your deck", and since `legal_actions`, the reducer and the view's `tradeReason` all read that one function, no Trade of it is listed, the reducer refuses one in those words and the view gives the viewer the same reason. Tradable still reads on the card, printed, given or lent by an aura over its controller's hand (S #164 The Syndicate's, S #165 The Trade Union's); only the Trade is refused. This was chosen over an exception to [[R11]] that would let a traded token live on in the deck: the rule that a unit-token card ceases to exist when it leaves a hand other than by being played stays whole.
