Two rings (units, backrow): your lane 1→5, opponent's 5→1; a Locked destination bounces the card, and radiant bounces go to the card's controller's hand ([[R747]]); damage and buffs travel with a rotated card. The radiant face bounces only the cards that would move to the opponent — the ones leaving Silas's controller's side — as [[§8]] #52 and the source's "cards you would lose" say; a card crossing the other way, onto that side, still crosses and changes control like any other ([[§8]] Conventions, [[R171]])
