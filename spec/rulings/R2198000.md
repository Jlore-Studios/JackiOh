Training Grounds #34 Dino-size sets one Unit's Attack and Health to 10/10 (Radiant 20/20). The engine had the set only for every Unit of a board scope (`set_stats_all`, [[R1185002]]), and a scope names no single card, so the verb `set_stats { target, attack, health }` does [[R1185002]]'s set to the one Unit a `TargetSpec` names: its [[§10.4]] layer 1 becomes the stats, its buffs and the stat half of its tuning go, its damage is healed, and the auras still add on top. The guards are `set_stats_all`'s: an Immutable Unit is untouched ([[R23]]), a target that is no Unit acting on the field (or has left it) fizzles, and the Unit reports `numberChanged` "attack" and "health". Who may be picked is the card's declared target's business ([[R81]]): any Unit on either side, as Hearthstone's Dinosize targets any minion, and a Spell is never offered an Immutable Unit ([[R781001]]). [[R78]]'s reset takes the set off with the card leaving the field. It edits [[§6.3]] (Set stats).
