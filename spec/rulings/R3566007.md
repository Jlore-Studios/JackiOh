S+ #70 reads "Cast on draw: shuffle 3 Cast on draw into your deck". The three are drawn from the new `castOnDraw` pool: the non-token cards of the shipped sets whose base face casts on draw (`StaticFlags.cast_on_draw`, read off the registered script as `tribute` is), the held set's out ([[R380]], [[R382]], [[R1420]]), never Spaceberry itself ([[R387]]), so it cannot chain forever. The Radiant face shuffles Radiant ones. The cards are shuffled in hidden, and shuffling is no draw, so none casts until it is drawn.

Rejected: a list of card ids in the script, which would skip the pool's own rules ([[R1420]]).
