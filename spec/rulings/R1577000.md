[[§14.1]] gives the shop its stocks and [[§14.3]] its packs, but neither says which existing
screen each of the tavern landing's three doors opens. They open the screens that need no new
hub, and nothing else moves: every old URL still resolves.

**Play** leads to `/practice`, the one play screen that needs no account, keeping the door's "No
account needed" promise. The gated `/play` lobby stays where it is for anyone who wants it.

**Collection** leads to `/collection`, the account's collection, wallet, tracks and quests.

**Shop** leads to a new `/shop`, which renders the same panel the account screen shows behind
its packs toggle until the Shop part removes it there. Both read the server per mount, so
neither drifts from the other.

The doors are plain links to gated routes: the gate still decides access (CLAUDE.md rule 7),
including the pending account's bounce to `/invite`.

Hearthstone semantics: one glowing Play button first, the rest of the menu behind it. Pointing
Play at the lobby instead was rejected, since the lobby needs an account and the door promises
none is needed; pointing Shop at the account screen was rejected, since the account menu keeps
account things and the Shop part moves packs out of it, not this one.

Rejected: moving any old path or adding a redirect, since no path moved.
