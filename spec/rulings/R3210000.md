**One social read.** `GET /api/social` answers `{incoming, outgoing, unread}`: the caller's open challenges that have not lapsed, received and sent, and its unread counts by friend, what `GET /api/challenges` and `GET /api/chat/unread` answer, read by the same reads in one transaction for the caller alone. A tab's social poll ([[R1831000]]) makes it once a `SOCIAL_POLL_MS` in place of the two. Since it is the same reads behind the same gate, a pending or banned account, a request with no token and a blocked pair get from it what the two routes give them ([[§9.4]]). The two routes stay for older open tabs and other callers.

**Presence on every signed-in request.** Every request whose caller passes auth, the rate limit and the gate refreshes its presence to friends, beside its queue presence ([[R897000]]): never for a request refused there, and never for a pending or banned account, even on a route open to it. The router writes one account's row at most once a `PRESENCE_TOUCH_EVERY_MS` (30 s), keeping when it last did in memory, one per server, as [[R897000]]'s queue presence is. A write reads the account's agreement first and records nothing while it owes the terms in force ([[R970002]]), so it costs one consent read and one write a period per active account, which keeps [[R970002]]'s one read a beat and stays clear of the read on every request [[R757001]] rejected. Agreeing to the terms clears the mark, so the next request counts at once. A write that fails is logged, refuses nothing and clears the mark, and the next request tries again. `POST /api/presence` answers 204 and records only what the router's touch records.

**The heartbeat as a fallback.** The client sends `POST /api/presence` only once `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` has passed with no signed-in request of its own: a busy tab sends none, and a hidden tab whose polls pause ([[R3205000]]) sends one a heartbeat. None goes at mount, since the shell's social poll reads at once. A client so says something at least every `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS`, so two writes of its row are at most `PRESENCE_TOUCH_EVERY_MS` plus `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` (75 s) apart, under `PRESENCE_TTL_MS` (90 s): a friend who keeps a tab open never reads as offline, until it has been hidden for `HIDDEN_QUIET_MS` and goes quiet ([[R3576000]]). What friends see is as before: online while a tab holds a session, offline `PRESENCE_TTL_MS` after its last request.

**Hearthstone semantics.** A friend is online while their client talks to the server; nothing beyond that tells it apart from a beat.

Rejected: a write on every request, which costs a consent read and a write each; a period of a whole heartbeat, under which two writes can drift `PRESENCE_TTL_MS` apart and a friend flicker offline; counting a pending or banned caller, who can befriend no one; a beat at mount, which the shell's social poll makes needless; and a client that falls back to the two reads when the one is missing, since a deploy ships the client and the server together (`docs/deploy-cloudflare.md`, Known gaps).
