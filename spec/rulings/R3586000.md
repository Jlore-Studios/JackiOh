An enemy's strength came from its kind of fight: normal fights and ghosts played with Easy's
resources, elites and mini-bosses carried Medium's, the final boss Hard's. Now it climbs with the
map. Each fight node's enemy holds a tier from 1, set by the row it stands on (`row_tier`), and the
map from [[R3583000]] deals it with the node.

Act `a` (from 0) spans tiers `a × (RUN_ACT_TIERS + 1) + 1` upwards. Its row `i` before the boss
(from 0) stands at its lowest tier plus ⌊`i` × `RUN_ACT_TIERS` / `RUN_ACT_ROWS`⌋, so its first row is
its lowest tier, its last row before the boss its highest, each step is 0 or 1, and each tier holds
as many rows as another or one more, the lower tiers taking the extra rows (2, 2, 2 and 1 of the
seven). Its boss stands one tier above the act's highest. With three acts of four tiers that makes
act 1 tiers 1 to 4 and its mini-boss 5, act 2 tiers 6 to 9 and its mini-boss 10, and act 3 tiers 11
to 14 and the final boss 15. A const assert holds `RUN_ACT_ROWS` at least `RUN_ACT_TIERS`, so the last
row before a boss always reaches the act's highest tier.

Every route takes each row once, in order, so whatever way a player goes their fights meet the
tiers in rising order; a route with fewer fights (the fast way, [[R3583001]]) simply skips some.

The tier takes no draw of the `Rng`: `fight_node` gets it as an argument and draws what it drew
before, in the same order. A seed deals the same map as it did, and a kept run file folds to the
same nodes, now each with its tier.

Rejected: rounding each row to the nearest tier, which puts one row at the act's lowest tier and two
at its highest, so a run opens on a single weak row; and a tier counted by the fights a route has
met, which makes the same node a different fight by the way it was reached and could not be shown on
the map before the player chooses. It takes R3586000, the first number of #3586's block.
