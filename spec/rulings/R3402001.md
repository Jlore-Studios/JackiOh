Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). After Hearthstone, where every Legendary has an animation of its own, each of Core's Legendaries and Mythics but #92 Felinor Fiender ([[R3332001]]) and #100 Ceaseless Void (issue #3338) gets its signature ([[R3332000]]): a Unit plays an entrance of its own and then its text's beat, a Spell a cast of its own, a permanent its arrival, its text's beat and a standing aura of its own on its tile ([[R3400000]], [[R3402000]]), and a Mythic a takeover of its own with its own art. Each recipe is its card's file in `apps/web/src/fx/cards/core/`, each anchors on heroes, piles, zones, the board, the viewport or a card the viewer reads, and none names a card ([[R202]]). Each keeps its budget by rarity and type, one set piece per action and [[R200]]'s tail, and a tap, the speed, the intensity, Big moments and Reduce Motion act on it as on every cue. A concept is the target: a beat moved to fit the budgets or the events the view carries is named below.

- **#52 Silly Silas** (`silasSpotlight`, `mirrorballShuffle`, a `mirrorball` aura on his tile): he pirouettes down a spotlight from the top of the screen, cracks a crater into his zone and tips his hat in a glint. On his Cry's `rotated` a mirrorball ring and confetti spin at the board's centre, a sheen on each occupied unit zone, and each card it hands over takes a brand in its new owner's colour on its `controlChanged`, in place of that row's flight. Moved: the shuffle-step glides are the rotation's own rows; his toes do not tap, an aura being motes and a glow.
- **#83 Transmogulate** (`legendaryTide`): at its cast a crucible tips over the caster's hero (a poison gather, Legendary rays, a prismatic ring and bursting vials); its first `transformed` washes the caster's Deck and hand piles in rings and a sheen, each hand card the owner reads takes a sheen, and Embiggened each Unit it re-forms ripples. Moved: the transforms draw in place of their rows, inside a Spell's share.
- **#85 Unlicensed Experimentation** (`clampWeld`): face down it is a card back and draws nothing; at its `trapFired` its zone fractures open in green arc light and smoke; on `fused` the seized permanent rings and the result welds in a seam, a sheen and a flash. Moved: the clamps' tether is the two rings, the cackling silhouette left out, for a Trap's budget.
- **#87 Pocket Chaos** (`pocketSwap`): a pocket opens at the board's centre, both heroes' light tugged into it, and a cloud of dice-bright confetti spills; on `swapped` the pair trades places, the boards' (through the middle unit zones) and the Deck piles' as card backs crossing, the heroes' health as rings and sheens; the card it hands the other player drops into their hand pile as a card back. Moved: the figure-eight is two crossing flights.
- **#93 Combo-Index** (`comboStadium`, `gradeRings`, a `stadium` aura on its zone): it lands as three rings rising in its zone under radiant rays and a confetti whirl; each trigger is a beat of its own, the grade's rise (the engine's `counterChanged` of its `grade`) lighting a ring, and its grade's events show, each kind at most `FX_SIGNATURE_BURSTS_MAX` an entry: a card back into the hand pile, gold tags, a burn at the other hand pile, a spark and a Lifesteal beam. Moved: the ring lights on that `counterChanged`, not the `questProgressed` the concept named, which Combo-Index never sends; the grade E its play writes is no trigger's and lights none; the aura does not light the current grade, a recipe drawing only while its card resolves.
- **#95 Call to Chaos (Core Edition)** (`chaosReels`): a slot-wheel's rays and ring drop over the board and three reels blur in sparks, left to right; on `chaosRolled` the roll's own reveal, shown to both seats ([[R436]]), stands `FX_REELS_MS` with a gold ring and its jackpot shake, and the effect's rows then play. Moved: the reveal is shortened to fit a Spell's share.
- **#96 My Pawn** (`pawnStrings`, takeover `pawn`): face down it draws nothing; its firing is its indigo takeover, the hooded hand over a glass chessboard its art; strings snap from the top of the screen to every enemy Unit and the enemy hero, at most `FX_PAWN_STRINGS_MAX`, and the enemy hero takes a sheen; on `attackCancelled` the attack shatters into shards at the attacker. Moved: the strings for the rest of the turn are a beat of its firing.
- **#97 Zephrys** (`perfectWish`, takeover `genie`): its takeover is a wind spirit uncoiling in a cyclone of silk, card backs orbiting him; through its hold the silk gathers at the board's centre; its Discover stays in the caster's prompt, and on `addedToHand` one card back gusts from the far hero into the caster's hand pile, which rings and takes a sheen.
- **#98 Heroic Power** (`heroicPedestal`, `heroicDoors`, takeover `rotunda`, a `pedestal` aura on its zone): its takeover is a marble rotunda of thirteen glowing doors; once that takeover's hold ends (its entrance reads [[R3405000]]'s lead) it lands as a crystal pedestal in holy light with a diamond glint for its Indestructible; its Discover's doors open in the caster's prompt; each Activate is a beat of its own, its `activated` flaring the pedestal in place of the shared arcane flare (keyed on the card's own `activated` whatever entry is open, since the engine sends it before the Activate's `hookResolving` marker opens its hook), and inside that hook its power flies from the pedestal where it went: a fire dart to a `damage`'s target, a dust sprinter to a `summoned` Unit's zone and a card back into the hand pile on a `drawn` or an `addedToHand`. Moved: the pedestal does not pulse its power's colour, for the same reason as Combo-Index's.
- **#99 Craft a Card** (`fusionForge`, takeover `crystalForge`): its takeover is a floating crystal forge, two rails converging on an anvil under a hammer, gears overhead; through its hold two card backs ride in from the board's edges and meet in frost sparks; the hand pile rings as the crafted card is added, and on `fused` it flies from the forge into the hand pile with the strike's shake, both in place of their rows.

A Mythic's takeover holds as [[R2722001]]'s; a Spell's or Trap's own cast replaces the shared rays, burst and ring, and its Mythic takeover keeps only its own look ([[R3400000]] `ownCast`). Each card has a sound of its own (`audio/signatureCues.ts`): Silas a record scratch and a two-step tap, Transmogulate a bubbling crucible, a choir swell and glass chimes, Unlicensed Experimentation a hatch clank, a servo whine and a welder's arc, Pocket Chaos a zipper, dice and a slide whistle, Combo-Index a rising cheer and a horn, Call to Chaos a lever, reels and a jackpot bell, My Pawn a chess piece's clack and a music-box waltz, Zephrys a rising wind, a hum and a chime, Heroic Power a door's groan and a fanfare, and Craft a Card two footsteps, an anvil strike and steam. Each sounds once, on its first beat: a Trap's `trapFired` (never a firing again, [[R2657001]]), Silly Silas's landing face up in a unit zone (`summoned`, as [[R3400001]]'s Units sound), every other card's `cardPlayed` (never a face-down set, [[R227]]), after the event's own row and beside its play sting ([[R3332002]]), within its card's signature ceiling. A card the viewer cannot read carries [[R97]]'s sentinel and sounds none ([[R203]]).

A Cloaked copy of one of the five Spells (#83, #87, #95, #97, #99) makes its cast as it reveals, never as it is set ([[R2722002]]): its face-down set (a `cardPlayed` with `formerId`, [[R227]]) is no first beat, and its reveal's `trapFired` ([[R783006]]) is, so the cast and its text's beats play once, from the firing, like a Trap's (`isFirstBeat`, `fx/cards/core/flourish.ts`). The engine runs a revealed Spell's cast as its Cry, marked by a `hookResolving` cry of the same card after the `trapFired` (`reveal_set_spell`); that marker leaves the firing's `cast` open in the effects layer's memory ([[R2797000]], `fx/memory.ts`), so the text's events (Call to Chaos's `chaosRolled`, Transmogulate's `transformed`) still reach its recipe. Rejected: drawing nothing at the reveal, which with the shared rays already replaced by the card's own cast would leave a Legendary's reveal without an entrance.

Rejected: one horn per grade of Combo-Index, which its grade events' own sounds already mark; and remembering a card across the turn so its aura or strings change with it, which would need a memory past its play and is left to a later issue.
