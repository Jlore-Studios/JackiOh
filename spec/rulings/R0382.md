A Fruit pool — a card that makes random Fruit — is the non-token Fruit cards of every set plus the five Grapes, C+ #65.1 to C+ #65.5, Tokens the designer made to be generated by any Fruit card; each definition in it is one entry, a Grape weighing as much as any card (only C+ #65 and C+ #66 roll a Grape by `GRAPE_ODDS`), and the card that makes the pool is still left out of it ([[R387]]). A Grape any pool picks is not the generated card: the pick is rolled again on `GRAPE_ODDS`, and the Grape that roll names is generated instead — one extra rng draw, only when a Grape was picked — so the Grape rarity pool persists across every kind of Grape generation. A Fruit pool's Grape entry, and a Grape in C+ #23 Dropshipping's pool, alike. A Prime pool holds the Prime tokens the same way ([[R769]]). The Grapes are in no other pool but one: C+ #23 Dropshipping's "(including tokens)" is the designer's own exception to every token-free pool, and it draws from every card and token of every set but Dropshipping itself ([[R387]]) — a Grape, a Loser ([[R411]]), an AI card, The Coin ([[R245]]) — each at its printed cost (the Radiant face makes it cost (1)), repeats allowed ([[R60]]); a unit-token card it hands out sits in the hand as [[R11]] allows
