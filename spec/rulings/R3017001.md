The issue asked for a floor, a named number of variants besides the Default every card is offered,
Legacy cards included; that each variant still reach every set that ships; and that, as the owner
asked on 2026-11-03, cards still differ, each variant's offer its own share where the style suits the
card, kept as data in the registry.

**A sample's start.** An offer clause's sample ([[R2949001]]) takes a card when the FNV-1a hash of
`<seed>/<card id>`, modulo `OFFER_SAMPLE_PERCENT_SCALE`, falls below its `percent`. It may now also
hold a `from`, 0 when absent and left out of the registry's text, and then takes only the cards whose
hash falls at or above it. Two samples of one seed, one below a cut and one from it to the scale, split
their cards between them exactly. The registry's check refuses a share (`percent` less `from`) of none
or of the whole scale, and a `percent` past the scale.

**Three pairs.** Part 6's six variants ([[R3017000]]) make three pairs, each pair's two offers the same
clauses, one seed each pair, the first variant's samples below a cut and its partner's from it:

| Pair | Seed | 60 in 100 of | 40 in 100 of |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night, Neon sign | `version-night-neon` | Units, Field Spells, Field Traps | Spells, Traps |
| Watercolor, Stained-glass window | `version-watercolor-stained-glass` | Units, Field Spells | Spells, Traps, Field Traps |
| Woodcut, Tarot card | `version-woodcut-tarot` | Units, Traps, Field Traps | Spells, Field Spells |

The first of each pair takes the share the table gives of each type and its partner the rest, so the
weights tilt each style toward the cards it suits, the three pairs tilt differently, and every
non-token card is offered exactly one variant of each pair. So every card is offered at least
`VARIANT_MIN_PER_CARD`, three, variants besides the Default, a Legacy card too, at their art level
([[R3015000]]); and since the three splits are seeded apart, no card's mix of the six is set by design.
Each first level took 306 to 328 cards when it shipped and at least eight of every set that ships,
past `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_CARDS` and `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET`; no two of the sixteen variants past the
Default take the same cards, and none takes every card.

Rejected: a hand-kept list of cards for the floor, which every later card would need; one sample per
variant without a `from`, which leaves cards below the floor by chance; and a floor of two, which is
not several.

Amends [[R2949001]] (a sample may start at a `from`) and [[R2725001]] (six more offers and a floor of
variants on every card).
