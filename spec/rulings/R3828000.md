Flexible belongs to Units alone. A card that is not played as a Unit reads no Flexible: a Field
Spell keeps to its backrow with stats or without, and a play, a default zone or a preset board never
puts it in a unit zone. `catalog check` refuses Flexible on a non-Unit face, and Flexible gives no
face a stat line, which only Animated does ([[R2280000]]). A Flexible card is therefore always a Unit,
in its unit row or standing in its backrow ([[R1081000]], [[R3543000]]), and its play is a Unit's
play everywhere: it counts among its player's Unit plays and every card that answers a Unit played
or arriving answers it. S+ #40 Mender and S+ #59 Mass Driver lose their printed Flexible, and Mass
Driver its 0/6 (0/12 Radiant) stat line, which did nothing in its backrow. This supersedes
[[R3543001]]. Rejected: teaching every arrival reader to judge a Field Spell in a unit zone by where
it stands (issue #3825), which would make a Field Spell's play a Unit's for some cards and not for
others.
