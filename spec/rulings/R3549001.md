S+ #54 Pneumatic Drill adds "a random Minerals or Resources" to its controller's hand at the end of
their turn, #53 Laser Drill "three random Minerals (that cost 0) or Resources", and #52 Airblast
Drill fills the hand with "random Minerals (that cost 0) and Resources", each Radiant on the Radiant
face. The spec says neither what the two pools are nor how a card is drawn from both.

- **The pools.** A Mineral is a random non-token card of the Stinkyscape+ set tagged Mineral, S+ #44
  to #50, the pool naming its set so that it reaches them while the set is held ([[R1420]]). A
  Resource is rolled by [[R1840002]]'s own odds from every Resource the catalog holds, the six
  Stinkyscape Resources and the Warhero Token once it prints `resource`; a held game leaves out the
  Legendary and Mythic ones ([[R2201001]]).
- **One flip per card.** Each card a drill adds is its own flip of a fair coin between a Mineral and
  a Resource, so a Laser Drill's three may be any mix and an Airblast Drill's fill is a mix of
  both. Neither pool is weighted by its size (7 Minerals against 6 Resources, whose own odds
  differ).
- **"(that cost 0)".** Every Mineral prints (1), so the parenthesis is read as the price the drill
  gives it: the Minerals of #52 and #53 cost (0) as they reach the hand. Pneumatic Drill prints no
  such parenthesis, so its Mineral costs its printed (1). A Resource costs (0) already.
- **"Fill your hand".** #52 adds as many cards as there is room in the hand as its end of turn
  resolves (`HAND_CAP`, [[§2.4]]): none in a full hand, and no burn.
- **The turn.** At the end of the turn of the drill's controller only ([[R62]]); the opponent's end
  of turn adds nothing.
- **Radiant.** Every card added is Radiant, a Mineral's price unchanged. The list prints GIGA twice
  on #53's Radiant face; it is one keyword ([[R3546000]]), as on its base face.

Rejected: one uniform pool of the thirteen cards; weighting the flip by the pools' sizes; a Mineral
filter on the printed cost (none costs 0); and a drill that fills the hand with a Mineral for every
free slot and Resources for none.
