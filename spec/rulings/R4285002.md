S #129 Jlockeed's Missile Pod since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes IV: "End of turn: Deal damage equal to its Attack split among all enemies" (Radiant: "twice its Attack"). "Split among" follows Hearthstone's Avenging Wrath: the damage goes one point at a time, each point to an enemy picked at random among those still standing as it lands, the enemy hero and the enemy Units alike, drawn through the match rng ([[R60]]). That is the engine's split verb (`damage_split`, as S #137.3 Air Doctrine and TG #33 Guerilla Felinor use it), whose default reach is the enemy hero and every enemy Unit. The Attack is read through the layers as the hook fires ([[R62]], [[R1878002]]), buffs and auras included, times 1 on the base face and 2 on the Radiant face; an Attack of 0 or less deals nothing ([[R63]]). The `divisor` it declared is retired; the script through v0.6.7k, its one hit of ⌈Attack ÷ divisor⌉ to every enemy, is kept for those replays ([[R767000]]).
