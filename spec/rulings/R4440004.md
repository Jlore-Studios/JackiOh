Issue #4440 adds "Profe Yates Prime: cost (2); gains existing text of Profe Yates, e.g., the exile and summon a trap", the card S+ #4 Profe Yates's new Death shuffles in ([[R4440003]]). It is modelled as every Prime a Catalyst's Death makes (C+ #46.1, M #45.1, M #49.1, S #147.1): a token on its card's first sub-number, S+ #4.1 (`stinkyscapeplus-004-1`), named "<card> Prime", `token: true`, rarity Token and printed as its card is, Legendary, with its card's flavor and its tags, the Catalyst traded for Prime: Human, Felinor, Acclaimed, Prime, Token. Being a Prime it is in every Prime pool ([[R1421]]); being a token it is in no deck and in no pool of non-token cards.

"Existing text" is Yates's as it stood when the issue was written, v0.6.9n's, word for word on each face: "Cry: Exile a permanent with M, O, U, S and E in its name and text (CN cards are immune). Then summon a random Trap.", three random Traps on the Radiant face, none of them Radiant. Its Cry plays as Yates's did ([[R4154100]], [[R3996000]], [[R3567001]]): one declared face-up permanent of either side whose name and text hold the five letters, a CN card immune and a face-down card never offered ([[R33]]), named whenever one stands, the Traps coming all the same with none ([[R90]]), the exile first so a Trap may take the zone it freed. The designer gives its cost, (2), and no stats, so it keeps the stats that text was printed with, 6/6 and Radiant 12/12, which also meet [[R275]]. Its script is built by Yates's file's `exile_then` and `exile_traps`, which Yates's kept v0.6.9n script shares, so the two never drift apart. Rejected: Yates's new 3/7 (6/14), the stats of the card that lost the exile; giving it Yates's new Tribute or Death, which the designer's "existing text" does not hold; and a non-token card, which a Catalyst's Prime never is.
