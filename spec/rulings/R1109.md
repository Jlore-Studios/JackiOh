# R1109: `manaSpent` is reported only while something answers it

- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: accepted
- Decides: MD-D26 (docs/meditative-set.md M5)
- Spec: §10.3
- Proven in: `r1109_reports_play_and_activate_prices_when_heard`, `r1109_silent_without_listener` (engine), `places_a_counter_per_mana_spent`, `its_own_play_and_the_opponent_spending_count_nothing` (M #69)

`manaSpent` reports the mana a play's price or an activation's price actually took, and what it
paid for. It is public — a player and a number, never a card — and it is emitted only while a
non-trap card on the field or in the backrow answers it, so a game with no listener keeps its
events, and its `next_seq` hashes, exactly as before (D14). Nothing is emitted for an amount below
1.
