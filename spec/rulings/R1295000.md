A player picks three card cosmetics, one per kind, which their own cards wear: a **foil** over the art (Holo sweep, Rainbow, Cracked ice), a **finish** on the print (Linen, Matte-black ink, Gilded edge) and a **border** round the frame (Wood, Bone, Circuit). Each kind has a fourth entry, its default — No foil, Plain finish, Standard trim — which draws the face as it was before cosmetics existed. All twelve are unlocked for every account; there is no collection or ownership for them yet, as there is none for the hero portraits ([[R641]]).

**The registry.** The cosmetics are listed once, in `crates/server/cosmetics.json`, each with an id, a kind, an English name, a rarity (Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary, for a later pack roll) and whether it is its kind's default. Its check refuses an id listed twice, across kinds as well as within one, an id that is not a lower-case slug, an id longer than `PLAYER_SETTINGS_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH` (40) characters, since a pick travels as a settings text ([[R633]]), and a kind with no default or with more than one. The server reads the file; the client reads the same entries from `apps/web/src/wire/generated/cosmetics.ts`, which a test writes from it.

**The picks.** The picks are a settings store of their own, kept on the device and on the account as the `cosmetics` group, merged like every other group ([[R633]], [[R634]]), with a select per kind in the settings' visuals. The store keeps any short text it is given, so an id a newer client wrote comes in and is kept, and this device never overwrites it; a face draws an id the client does not know as its kind's default, and the select shows that default, never an error.

**Whose cards wear them.** A card wears the device's picks only where it is the viewer's own card, by its owner, on a board the viewer plays: an online match, a practice game (a lesson included), a hotseat game and a co-op seat's own duel. A unit the opponent has taken keeps its owner's look. Everything else draws the defaults: the opponent's cards, a card in the resolving strip, a prompt's options, the showcase of a card played, a replay, a watched game, a teammate's duel, the hover preview and the inspect overlays, the piles, the log, the collection, the Almanac and the homescreen. Showing an opponent's cosmetics waits for a `Cosmetics` frame and a ruling of its own.

**On the face.** Each kind is its own attribute on the face, apart from the rarity foil Mythic and Radiant faces wear, so a decorative foil never blurs that signal: a Mythic face wears no cosmetic foil, and a Radiant face no foil, finish or border, its gold its own ([[R277]]). A default adds nothing to the face. No border's colours fall in the Radiant gold, a window in hue, saturation and lightness that holds every colour the Radiant face declares, so no border passes for a Radiant trim. A foil covers the art window only; it moves only while the effects allow motion (not under the system's reduced motion or the settings' Reduce motion, which leave every foil its still version), while the Animated foil setting is on, and only on `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX` (6) faces at once, the others wearing the still version until a slot passes to them.

None of it is a rule: a cosmetic never enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log, a replay hash or a game record, and nothing the engine does reads one (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
