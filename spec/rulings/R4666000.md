Issue #4666 is the owner's request of 2026-11-24. Military and Healthcare stop reaching the deck, so they cover only your Units on the field and in your hand. In return they get back some of their numbers on both faces, and so does Culture. Education stays the same.

"Some" is read as the two most recent cuts, both made while the buffs still reached the deck: issue #3996's ([[R3996201]]) and issue #4154's ([[R4154105]]). Military, Healthcare and Culture go back to the numbers they had through v0.6.5b. Education keeps the numbers [[R4154105]] gave it, and the Armor is the same in every version:

| Option | Reach through v0.6.12b | Reach now | Base through v0.6.12b | Base now | Radiant through v0.6.12b | Radiant now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Military: +Attack and Rush | field, hand, deck | field, hand | ⌊5X/3⌋−2 | 2X−2 | ⌊17X/5⌋−4 | 4X−3 |
| Education: Radiant Books shuffled in | your deck | the same | ⌊5X/3⌋−2 | the same | ⌊17X/5⌋−2 | the same |
| Culture: % chance to become Radiant | field, hand, deck | the same | ⌊17X/4⌋ | 6X | 12X | 16X |
| Healthcare: +Health | field, hand, deck | field, hand | ⌊5X/3⌋−2 | 2X−2 | ⌊17X/5⌋ | 5X−1 |
| Healthcare: Armor | field, hand, deck | field, hand | X | X | 2X | 2X |

At X = 4 that is +4 → +6 Attack and Health on the base face and 17% → 24% for Culture. On the Radiant face it is +9 → +13 Attack, +13 → +19 Health and 48% → 64%.

- **The reach.** Military and Healthcare reach the tops of your field's piles and the Unit cards in your hand. A hand card carries its buff and keyword onto the field as it enters ([[§10.4]]). A Unit card in your deck gets nothing, so drawing it later brings no buff. Culture still rolls every card on your field, in your hand and in your deck.
- **The floor.** [[R3135002]]'s floor stands: a number below 1 reads 1. At X = 1 the base face's 2X−2 reads 1, and so does the Radiant face's 4X−3.
- **Culture** still rolls each card on its own. Every card turns at X = 17 on the base face, where it turned at X = 24, and at X = 7 on the Radiant, where it turned at X = 9.
- **Immune to Spells.** Military, Healthcare and Culture still pass your field Unit Immune to Spells by and reach its copies in your hand. Culture also reaches its copies in your deck ([[R4453000]]).
- **The client** restates the option texts the Discover shows (`modeText.ts`, with their Chinese in the web's `zh-CN.json`). The card's words do not change.
- **Replays.** The script through v0.6.12b is kept as `script_v0_6_12b`, beside `script_v0_6_6h` and the older ones ([[R767000]]). Every kept script reaches the deck, as those versions ran.

Rejected: putting back every nerf since issue #1149 on the smaller reach, which would undo #3135's and #1704's cuts as well. Also rejected: cutting the Armor along with the reach.

It edits §8.7 row 40.
