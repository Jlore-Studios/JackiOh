## R1043 — The Prime's Aura is Knowledge Breaker's

Built (Meditative #45.1; patch v0.3.7, #781). The Prime prints "Aura: Your Units have Cloaked." on
both faces, the same Aura as Knowledge Breaker's ([[R1040]]), and the Units it lends Cloaked to are set
and revealed in the same way ([[R1041]]). Until patch v0.3.7 its Aura's "Animated Field Traps" was
the same face-down play as Knowledge Breaker's permission, so the two wordings differed in nothing.
