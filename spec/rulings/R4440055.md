Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: M #9 Joint Filing's Cry ([[R3134001]]) is replaced with "Activate: Discover a card with a Start of Turn or End of Turn effect.", printed on both faces after the Aura as "Activate: Discover a card with a Start of turn or End of turn effect." in the card's own capitals. It is M #10 Double Counting's Activate on this card's pool ([[R4154211]]): once a turn ([[R384]]), no price, a Discover (§6.3) of three of the catalog pool `turnHook: either` names, the non-token cards of the sets that ship whose base-face script sets a Start of turn or an End of turn hook ([[R380]], [[R1420]]), never this card ([[R387]]), drawn through the match rng ([[R60]]); the pick reaches the hand on its base face, the Radiant face's included, a full hand burning it. The Radiant face differs only in its Aura's `extra`. An Activate is neither a Start of turn nor an End of turn effect, so the Aura does not multiply it ([[R821]]). Played, the card adds nothing now. Through v0.6.9n the Cry added the random cards and the card had no Activate, which `script_v0_6_9n` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
