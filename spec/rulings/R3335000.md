The issue asked for Starship cosmetics that dress every Starship an account launches. This rules what
they are, who sees them and when a Starship wears them.

**The kinds.** Four account cosmetic kinds, picked on the account alone and never on a deck
([[§14.2]]): `starshipHull`, a hull design; `starshipLivery`, the hull's colours and emblem;
`starshipTrail`, its engine glow and launch trail; and `starshipPieceSkin`, a recolour of every module
it mounts, the fourth kind the issue proposed. Each has one default, Common and a starter, that every
account owns and that changes nothing: the standard hull is the design the Starship's name gives it
([[R3333000]]), the standard livery that design's own colours, the standard trail its livery's glow and
the standard skin the modules' own plating. Every other Starship cosmetic is earned only
([[R3335002]]): the Fleet track's ([[R3335001]]) and the Jlorious Starship ([[R3335003]]).

**Picks and loadouts.** The four picks live in the account's `cosmetics` settings group beside the
board, field and card back, and the server checks each as it is written, an unowned one refused
([[R1834001]]). The match freezes them into each seat's loadout, the account's pick when owned and the
kind's default otherwise ([[R1834002]]), so the opponent, a watcher and a replay's viewer see a seat's
Starships as it dressed them, and a viewer's switch that turns the other seats' cosmetics off turns
these off too ([[R1834004]]). A loadout frozen before #3335 has no Starship picks and reads each kind's
default, so old replays still draw.

**Where they show.** Only a launched Starship wears them: its hull pick's design in place of its name's,
its livery, its trail and its piece skin; a Dormant Starship keeps its shipyard ([[R3333000]]), whatever
the look. No Starship cosmetic wears Radiant's gold, and a Radiant Starship still gilds as it did
([[R2725004]]). They never reach the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. Practice, hotseat and
the end-to-end tests treat every Starship cosmetic as owned ([[R1310004]]).

Hearthstone semantics: a cosmetic the opponent sees, as a card back and a hero skin are, chosen on the
account and shown on the board.

Rejected: a deck slot for each, which the issue did not ask for and would make a deck's save check four
more picks; and dressing a Dormant Starship too, which would hide the shipyard that tells a Dormant one
apart.
