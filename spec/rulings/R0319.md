Each of the three has a sound of its own, played when the runner starts its entry ([[§10.11]]): `fatigue` a hollow knock on the empty library before the hit's impact, `libraryOverflow` a dull two-note refusal, `burned` the burn crackle. None varies with the card, so a card behind the sentinel sounds like any other ([[R203]]), and none speaks a line, being no play, cast, death or trap firing ([[R204]])
