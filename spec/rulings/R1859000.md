Stinkyscape #90 After Burner: "When launched, your Starship gains 2 random combat keywords." (ST-S02-1.) "Your Starship" is the Starship the piece is fused into, the only card its When launched runs on ([[R1852003]]); on the field the text does nothing. The grant is `grant_random_keywords` onto it, [[R21]]'s draws from the combat keyword pool, each a keyword it lacks and none twice, fewer when fewer are left. The Radiant face grants 4.
