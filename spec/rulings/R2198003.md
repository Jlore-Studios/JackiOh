Training Grounds #26 Path of Exile and #27 Hopeful Jelly Bean print Cleave on their Radiant faces, Spells whose effect lands on a picked permanent. [[R1563001]] reads a Spell's Cleave on a hand pick as the cards beside it in the hand; on a board pick it is its board twin, Core #34 Collateral Damage's Radiant "the permanents adjacent to it": the effect also lands on the permanents in lanes N−1 and N+1 of the pick's own row on its own side ([[§3.1]]), never across sides or rows, a backrow pick reaching its backrow neighbours. The neighbours are read while the pick still stands in its lane, so they are taken first. A neighbour Immune to Spells is passed by, as every Spell's effect passes it. The pick is still the one declared target ([[R81]]): no neighbour is a target, so none is offered or checked as one. It edits [[§8.11]] (#26, #27).
