A Trade ([[R1838000]]) is two ordinary steps and nothing else, as Hearthstone's is. The card goes in through the engine's one shuffle-in ([[§6.3]] Shuffle into: [[R80]]'s cap, which the Trade's legality already keeps clear, and [[R151]]'s arrival roll). The card comes out through [[§2.4]]'s draw, a draw for every rule: it counts as the turn's draw (`turnDraw`, [[R457]]), a cast-on-draw card it takes casts itself ([[R58]]), a full hand burns it, a trigger that answers a draw answers it, and a draw limit stops it as it stops any draw, so the card stays in the deck and nothing comes out ([[R457]]). Fatigue cannot follow, since the deck holds at least the traded card. The Trade itself is no play and no cast ([[R70]]): no Cry runs, and nothing that counts or answers plays sees it (`turnLog.cardsPlayed`, the game's `played` counter, Combo). It is no discard either, and no action "you may take N more actions" counts ([[R456]] counts plays, attacks, switches and activations).
