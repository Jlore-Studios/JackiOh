The AI's evaluation priced a unit by its stats, its keywords and a few counters, so a unit that comes back bigger when it dies, or gives its owner cards, read as a plain body: the sweep of record of 2026-10-29 (`training/history/sweep-2026-10-29-4.md`) banned Training Grounds #25 Trigor as never played, and its Tribute 3 read as three units given up for a 3/3. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, once, the AI prices every Death it can read on its probe world (a function of what it sees, [[R185]]): each unit on either field whose Death is live, and each Unit in its own hand with one, placed first by the sandbox's spawn ([[R923001]]) in the zone a summon would take, a full row's first single-card zone cleared by the sandbox's remove. The price is the controller's evaluation after the sandbox's destroy, which runs the Death, less its evaluation after the sandbox's remove, which does not ([[R923003]]), less the body a Reborn brings back, which a kill already takes off. The probes run on a sandbox copy ([[R923000]]) with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), with no price passed, and within `AI_DEATH_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes ([[R186]]'s budget). A card is keyed by its definition, its face, its granted Deaths and its stat override, so a copy the search moves or makes reads the same price, and a Vanilla one reads none. `unit_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.death` of the price, outside the Brittle share as the counters are, and `kill_worth` takes the whole price off a kill, as it takes Reborn's body. `AI_EVAL.death` starts at 0.5, untuned, as [[R2380000]]'s shares did; the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. The prices travel with that one decision, passed by reference from its root through the search to every scorer that reads them (`Prices`, #2582), so a call outside a decision is passed none and prices nothing.

**What is not priced.** A unit the destroy spares (Indestructible); a Death that leaves a prompt open or ends the game, which the probe neither answers nor scores; a card the root did not see or a probe the budget did not reach; a hand card the sandbox may not spawn; and every Death while a prompt is open, which refuses the sandbox's tools.

**Rejected.** A flat weight per Death, which cannot price a Death that harms its owner (Training Grounds #30 Floodgates); the destroy's change plus the unit's own worth, which counts an aura the unit takes with it as its Death's; a kill less only `AI_EVAL.death` of the price, which would make killing a Trigor worth what killing a plain 3/3 is; answering a Death's prompt inside the probe.

Amended by issue #2566 ([[R2566000]]): a Unit that is a Starship Piece is priced too, by the copy its death fuses into its owner's Starship, its probe after every Death's.
