"Reveals when your opponent plays a Unit. Stuff it in a 0/4 Sack" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #135 Sacked) answers the play once it resolves, its Cry and Echo repeats first, as Core #41 Sheepish does ([[R17]], [[R427]]); a Spell, a backrow card or its own side's Unit leaves it set ([[R61]]). The Sack (S #135.1) is summoned first, for the Unit's controller, into the zone the Unit has just left or another open one ([[R64]]), and the Unit is held beneath it ([[R1850003]]), leaving the field with no Death. "For yourself", the Radiant face, is the Sack summoned for the trap's controller, so the Unit comes back to them when the Sack leaves. Rejected: answering the Unit's placement, before its Cry, which would take a Cry the text lets it keep.
