The designer's answer on #355 (jgoetzmann): a card that casts on draw is not in an opening hand, and when a hand has to hold some — the deck has too few other cards, or an entire hand would be cast on draw — their cast is suspended until the start of the game. [[R635]] set them aside and dealt a short hand instead; now setup deals a hand's other cards first ([[R635]], [[R640]]), and what the hand still lacks is dealt from the waiting cast-on-draw cards, the first ones in the library's order, never a Quickdraw card (one that also casts on draw is dealt as a Quickdraw card, [[R635]]). Each is dealt as a draw, reported and counted as one ([[R225]]), and not cast, and a mulligan's replacements are filled the same way. The mulligan may return one like any other card: it goes back with the other returned cards ([[R9]]) and is a waiting card again, shuffled in with [[R635]]'s, and never cast. After the start-of-game effects ([[§2.1]] step 4) and before turn 1, each one still in a hand is cast as its draw would have cast it ([[§2.4]]), Player 1's first, each hand in order: a whole play ([[R70]]) with a state check after it ([[R59]]), and no draw repeats, since its draw was made in setup. One that no longer casts on draw, or a Unit with no open unit zone ([[R459]]), stays in the hand. A cast that asks holds the casts after it and turn 1 until it is answered, as [[R224]] holds setup ([[R113]]). Setup still deals no fatigue. Like [[R635]], not reachable with a legal deck (L3)
