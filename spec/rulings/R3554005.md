Lancer (S+ #109) Discovers an enemy tile from three, Vela (S+ #126) and Corvus (S+ #127) take any
of them, and the line between the source and the tile hits every Unit on it — friend or foe —
piercing Armor, as one damage instance each. The line is the lattice the curve of KY's Papaya
([[R422]]) would meet, a vertical line included, drawn in public as `lineDrawn` (positions only).
Vela's and Corvus's Radiant faces Cleave each neighbour not yet hit, and Corvus draws the line as
a laser. Rejected: stopping the line at the first Unit, which would make the tile a target rather
than a direction.
