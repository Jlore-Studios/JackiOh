S+ #46 Phase Fabric gives a Unit Immutable and Immune to Spells, and on its Radiant face Evasive too
([[R3543002]]), and the Unit has every keyword the face lists when the Spell is done. A Unit Immune
to Spells is not affected by a Spell's effects, so a grant of Immune to Spells that came before
another of the same Spell's would turn that one away. The Spell therefore gives Immutable and
Evasive first and Immune to Spells last, which is not observable beyond the end state: the printed
order of the keywords is the text's, not the order they land in. Rejected: giving them in the
printed order, which would leave the Radiant face's Evasive off, and a verb that grants a list at
once, which no other card needs.
