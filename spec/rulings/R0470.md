A Fuse may keep a hand or deck card, which stays where it is. Its type wins, and an Immutable card is never kept. Keeping the cost means: an existing costOverride stays; else its current printed cost becomes its costOverride; an X-cost or embiggen card keeps that printed form. costMod stays. The `fused` event is hidden as the card is. A refused random fusion draws no rng.
