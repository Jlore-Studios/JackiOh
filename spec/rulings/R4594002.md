"Your Units have +N Attack and Charge this turn" is two player-level modifiers with `ThisTurn` expiry: Units arriving later read them through the layers as they land, and [[§2.2]]'s cleanup takes them back. A Spell's lend passes a Unit Immune to Spells by (`from_spell`, [[R4453000]]), as a keyword lend does.
