Meditative #98 Showdown's lane is a play-time choice of five modes (`lane-1` … `lane-5`, [[R81]]), random under a random cast. It Locks every zone of the four other lanes, both sides and both rows, that is not already Locked (Lock / Unlock, §6.3; [[R688]]: plays refused, summons still landing), then a delayed effect at the start of the caster's next turn ([[R62]]'s delayed stage) unlocks exactly those zones still Locked: a zone Locked before it resolved stays Locked (MD-B21).
