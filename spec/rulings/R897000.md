A queue ticket lived until its owner pressed "Leave queue": nothing cancelled on tab close, the
sweep qualified tickets with no presence check, and with `LADDER_BOTS` on an abandoned ticket met
the empty-queue fallback 15–40 s later ([[R1453]]): the bot's seat attached at once while the absent
player's seat started 60 s of disconnect grace ([[R744]]), and `disconnectExpired` then recorded a
ranked loss the player never saw, moving their Glicko-2 rating ([[§9.12]]). Two abandoned tickets
that paired made a ghost match nobody attended, and a stale ticket that paired a present player
wasted a minute of theirs for a hollow win. Where the spec is silent this follows Hearthstone
semantics: a player who is gone is not seated.

**Presence.** Every signed-in request refreshes its caller's presence, piggybacked on the router so
no new route and no client change was needed: a queued client already polls `GET /api/auth/me`
every `SERIES_POLL_SECONDS` ([[R765]]) from wherever it is. Presence is in memory, one map per
server: a restart loses it, and live clients re-touch within one poll. A caller that is not the
browser, a script or an end-to-end spec's second seat, must send a request of its own while it
waits: two players further apart than the window's start wait, by [[R640001]], up to
`RATING_WINDOW_FULL_AFTER_SECONDS`, which is far longer than the TTL.

**The TTL.** Each sweep first cancels every open ticket unheard from for longer than
`QUEUE_TICKET_STALE_MS` (12 s: six missed polls, so a flaky connection does not eject a real
player, and under the fallback's 15 s, so expiry always precedes bot seating): strictly longer,
like the queue's other thresholds. A profile never heard from is judged by its `enqueued_at`,
which covers an old client that never touches for one window. A restarted server judges nothing
until it has listened one window. The cancel is the ticket's own, so a claim that landed first
stands, and the sweep pairs only survivors, people before bots as ever ([[R1453]]): no person, no
bot, no grace charged, no phantom loss. A ticket that keeps being touched never expires, in any
mode ([[R257]]) and past any fallback, Conquest's included ([[R641000]]).

**The client reads it.** `GET /api/auth/me` answers `queued` while the caller holds an open
ticket: its own row only, like `currentMatchId`, so the readout still counts no open tickets. A
client waiting in a queue that reads it gone `QUEUE_ENDED_AFTER_READS` (2) times in a row stops
waiting with a rejoin message, and the lobby's Find-a-match button is the way back in. A room
wait never ends this way: its host holds no ticket, so ticketless reads say nothing gone. One
false read, a failed read, or a server that never names it keeps the wait.

**Rejected.**

- A dedicated touch route: the poll the client already sends carries it.
- A larger TTL: one past the fallback's 15 s would let a stale ticket meet a bot.
- A second sweeper for idle presence entries: entries die with their tickets, and a restart
  clears the map.
- Purging long-dead `cancelled` tickets: `matches_mode_of` still reads claimed rows, and the
  phantom loss needs no purge.
