**Why.** The owner asked in #2895 for `/stats` to show how sure each card's figures are. Without a margin, a card at 53% and one at 56% read as different when their ranges almost entirely overlap. Only the Sets tab showed one, a set's standard error ([[R1966000]]).

**The ranges.** Both take `STATS_INTERVAL_Z` (1.96), the two-sided 95% quantile [[R1994000]]'s first-seat share already takes; no new number.

- **Win rate:** the Wilson score interval of the card's wins over its games, a draw a game not won (`wilson_interval`, [[R850000]], [[R1994000]]). It is taken over the games `winRate` counts, so under All Random the seen games ([[R2777002]]).
- **Adjusted:** [[R2777000]]'s own model gives it. The adjusted rate is the posterior mean of the card's lift under a normal prior of spread τ (`ADJUSTED_WIN_RATE_PRIOR_SPREAD`); that posterior's variance is `weight · SE²`, which is `τ²·SE² / (τ² + SE²)`, so the range is

  ```
  adjusted ± STATS_INTERVAL_Z · √(weight · SE²)
  ```

  It is never wider than ±z·τ (about ±9.8 points), and narrows to the drawn delta's own ±z·SE as the games pile up. [[R2777000]]'s +20-point delta over 40 games a side reads 53.3% (44.4% to 62.3%); over 4,000 a side, 69.0% (66.9% to 71.2%). It is a credible interval under [[R2777000]]'s prior, not a frequentist one. The engine's `adjusted_interval` computes it beside `adjusted_win_rate`; `None` when that is.

The Win rate's range says how sure the rate is, not whether it is the card's own: a strong deck still lifts it, which is what Adjusted removes.

**Where it shows.**

- **`GET /api/stats/cards`** answers `winRateLow`, `winRateHigh`, `adjustedLow` and `adjustedHigh` on every card, as fractions, null where the rate is. The client does no statistics.
- **`/stats`' Cards tab** has a **Show 95% ranges** button (`aria-pressed`), off by default and kept in the address as `ranges=1`. On, each Win rate and Adjusted figure shows its range under it, in the table and the grid: Adjusted's to a tenth of a point, as Adjusted reads, Win rate's to a whole point. One line under the cards says in plain words what a range means.
- **A row below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games** shows no range, as it shows no rate ([[R654]]).
- **The order** is unchanged: the cards sort by their figures, not by their ranges.

Rejected:
- Computing the ranges in the client from the tallies. The engine already holds both models; a second copy could drift.
- A frequentist interval for the adjusted rate (the delta's ±z·SE shrunk by the weight). It is not an interval of the shrunk figure and reads narrower than the posterior allows at a small sample.
- Marking the cards whose range clears 50%. That can follow if wanted.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
