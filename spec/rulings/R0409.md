C+ #12.8 Frostspatula's "Resummon all Units destroyed by this" makes copies, as Hearthstone's Frostmourne does, and the originals stay in their owners' graveyards: it remembers each Unit it killed ([[R42]]) by definition and face, and its Death — as a Unit, or destroyed in the backrow, since Death is leaving the field for a graveyard ([[§6.2]]) — summons a fresh copy of each for its controller, tokens included, until the board is full (on the Radiant face, made Radiant)
