A menu panel that waits on a read no longer prints a "Loading …" line: it draws a skeleton shaped
like what will arrive, from one component (`skeleton/Skeleton.tsx`) with its own stylesheet.

**Four shapes, a fixed count each.** A card grid (8 tiles), list rows (4), table rows (a header row
and 5 rows of 4 cells) and a generic panel (a heading bar and 2 lines). The counts are constants,
never taken from the read, which has not answered yet (CLAUDE.md rule 7: presentation only).

**The words stay.** The sentence the line carried is kept word for word as a visually hidden
`role="status"` line, with the testid the old line had, so a screen reader still hears it. The
blocks are `aria-hidden`. The skeleton unmounts with the read, never staying behind its answer.

**The mark.** Over the blocks sits the JackiOh mark (`public/brand/jackioh-mark.svg`), centered and
no taller than the skeleton, a dark halo around its cards so the blocks fade out near it rather than
run across it. It is drawn by the blocks' layer, so it is `aria-hidden` with them and hides with
them until they show ([[R2108000]]), and it never moves, whatever the motion setting. A skeleton in
a shell panel draws none: the logo heads that screen already.

**No shimmer under Reduce motion.** The blocks shimmer only under full motion; under the OS's
`prefers-reduced-motion` and under the settings panel's Reduce motion ([[R200]]) they stay still.

**One screen.** A skeleton fits its menu's region and never makes the page taller or wider than
[[R1581000]] allows: its root clips its content, its hidden words included.

**What stays text.** The deck builder's win-rate line, whose cards are already on screen in cost
order; the chat's "Load earlier" button and the replays' "Show older games" button, which are
buttons; the practice start screen's shuffle and its try line, and the boards' "Joining …" panels
([[R1581000]] excludes the boards); the dev routes; and "Sign in to continue." and "Redeem an
invite code first.", which wait on nothing.

Hearthstone semantics: a menu keeps its frame while its contents load.

Rejected: a spinner beside the sentence, which says nothing of the shape; counts taken from the
read, which are unknown until it answers; and a shimmer under Reduce motion.
