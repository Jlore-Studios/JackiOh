IA #157 Containment Field's "can't attack or be attacked until your next turn" is Containment (`effects::firewall::contain_until_next_turn`), a status on the Unit: `restrictions::cannot_attack` and `cannot_be_attacked` both read it, so it bars attacks by the Unit and on it, declared and forced alike (B5 E35; a forced attack still fights nothing past it, as [[R121]] fights nothing past a cancel). It ends as the caster's next turn begins — held past the intervening turn — or when the Unit leaves the field. IA #148's set Attack is no Containment: it leaves the Unit free to attack.
