Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A card's entrance is its intro ([[R1350]]), its rarity's sting ([[R669]]), its animation and a Mythic's takeover ([[R2722001]]). A hidden card makes it when it reveals, and never when it is set face-down (`apps/web/src/game/entrance.ts`):

- **A Trap or Field Trap** reveals at its first firing, a `trapFired` without `again` ([[R2657001]]). Only its controller's seat can read it there ([[R154]], [[R763]]), so only that seat plays a Legendary's or a Mythic's intro, its entrance sting beside the trap sting, its rays and a Mythic's takeover, centred on the zone that flipped. The other seat reads the sentinel and plays the trap sting every Trap shares, whatever its rarity: this changes no redaction. A firing again, and a Trap's set, play nothing of it. A Trap of a lower rarity keeps the trap sting alone.
- **A Cloaked card** (§6.1, [[R783004]]), whatever its rarity, reveals on its Activate: Reveal ([[R783006]]): an `activated` that names it to both seats once it stands face-up or has resolved ([[R97]]), then the `trapFired` that is its firing. Its controller's seat makes the entrance at that `trapFired`, the other seat at the `activated`, so each seat makes it once, centred on the backrow zone the firing flips: a Common's, Rare's or Epic's play sting, or a Legendary's or Mythic's intro, entrance sting, rays and (for a Mythic) takeover, for a revealed Unit as it animates into its unit zone, a Spell as it resolves, and a Field Spell as it turns face-up. Its face-down set, a `cardPlayed` with the fresh id's `formerId` ([[R227]]) on its controller's seat and the sentinel on the other, plays the set sound or the plain play on either seat and nothing that names or hints at the card: no intro (which its controller's seat opened until now, since the music director skipped only a play whose card is a Trap), no rarity sting, and no family accent on its burst. Destroyed or bounced unrevealed ([[R783008]]), it makes no entrance.

This amends [[R203]] and [[R669]]: a Trap's first firing, on the seat that can read it, may now vary with the card's rarity, and a hidden card's reveal stings as the card's own play would; and [[R1350]]: a Cloaked card opens its intro at its reveal, on both seats, and never at its set. Rejected: an entrance for a real Trap on the other seat at a later event that names it (its `enteredGraveyard` or a Field Trap standing face-up), which comes after the Trap's effects and reads as a death rather than an arrival; and naming the Trap to the other seat at its firing, which would change [[R154]] and [[R763]]'s redaction, an engine rule.
