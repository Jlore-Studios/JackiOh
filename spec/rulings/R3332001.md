Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). #92 Felinor Fiender has the stats of every Felinor its controller has, those under a Stack included ([[R39]]), and no hook, so it played the shared Legendary entrance. It is the first card with a signature ([[R3332000]]), `{ signature: true, entrance: "felinorAssembly" }`, its entrance the beat its text plays: as it lands, each Felinor its side shows face up sends a streak of cat light (Beast's Felinor look) into the zone it lands in, in lane order, the first at `FX_FELINOR_STREAK_AT` of the entry and each next `FX_FELINOR_STREAK_STEP` later, at most `FX_FELINOR_STREAKS_MAX` (one per unit zone), all arriving at the slam (`FX_SLAM_AT`); there the light gathers in a ring and a burst, a crown of gold rises, and the board shakes once at the Legendary's weight. It throws no gold area burst, which is the shared rarity entrance's.

A Felinor is a unit the board's view shows on top of its zone, read by its public tags (the view's own where they differ, else its definition's), so a granted tag counts. Cards buried under a Stack lie face down ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]): they count toward the stats but send nothing, and neither does a face-down card, the other side's Felinors or the landing card itself ([[R202]]). A Felinor it stacks onto, still on top in the view the entry is planned against, streaks up from its own foot. With no Felinor it lands alone, and no cue names a card. The `/dev/fx` gallery plays it over its fixture board with a Felinor in lanes 1, 2 and 5 (`felinorBoard`), the lane-4 Stack pile left buried, and the component specs capture it at 1280x720 and 390x844 and hold it within `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS`. Rejected: a streak per point of stats it gains, which would count the buried cards a viewer cannot see and flood a large board.
