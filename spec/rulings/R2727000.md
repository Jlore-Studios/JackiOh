A Buff's keyword row ([[§6.3]], [[R386]]) also offers Cloaked to a Unit, a Spell or a Field Spell that lacks it of its own while the card is in a hand (`TUNE_BUFF_HAND_KEYWORDS`), as the row's last addition: after [[R21]]'s pool for a Unit or a Spell's and a Field Spell's own keywords ([[R1748001]], [[R2436000]]), then Flashback ([[R2657005]]), the order a seeded draw follows. It mirrors the Crater a Nerf adds only in a hand. "Of its own" is the row's reading in a hand, the card's printed, given and tuned keywords, so a Cloaked an aura lends ([[R783007]]: M #45 Knowledge Breaker's to the Units in its controller's hand, [[R1040]], M #99 Paranoia's to the Spells, [[R1044]]) does not count, and a card an aura cloaks may still take a Buff's, which outlasts the aura. Never to a Trap or a Field Trap, which no Cloaked sets ([[R783004]]), never to a Vanilla card, whose text is gone, and never in a deck or on the field, since Cloaked acts only from a hand ([[R2577000]]). Cloaked stays out of [[R21]]'s random keyword pool, so Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao, the Conjure Rush Tokens and Keymaster Keenus still never give it.

A Nerf's row removes a Cloaked the card has of its own, printed (C #6 Cloaked Toe Cracker), given or added by a Buff, as it removes every keyword of the card's own that is not harmful, and never one an aura lends ([[R442]], [[R783007]]); a Nerf never adds Cloaked. Its removal of a printed Cloaked in a deck or on the field is as before.

One draw still picks uniformly among the row's removals and additions, removals first ([[R442]]), with no draw where there is one choice ([[R129]]), and a card a multiplier tunes more than once keeps to its first draw's way ([[R1160]]). The change is `tuning`, part of the card in every zone ([[R78]]): a Buff's Cloaked opens the face-down play (`faceDown: "cloaked"`, [[R783004]]) at the card's own price ([[R783005]]), the set card reveals by its Activate: Reveal ([[R783006]]), and destroyed or bounced before it reveals it comes back with it ([[R783008]]); a copy keeps it ([[R57]]), a Fuse sums it ([[R102]]), a Transform drops it and a Vanilla hides it. On the field Cloaked does nothing, and the board draws nothing for it ([[R2577000]]). A Buff or a Nerf on a hand card is hidden like any hand event ([[R440]]) and a set card hides as every set card does ([[R1046]]); `determinize` keeps sampling a hidden backrow card from the Trap pool, the printed Cloaked cards and the aura-lent ones only ([[R783009]]), since the seat cannot see a Buff in a hand, and the AI plays its own Buff-given Cloaked cards through `legal_actions`, as it does every other play. The owner asked for it (issue #2727); the cards whose Buffs reach a hand include C+ #70 Chaos Machine, Radiant C+ #71 Book of Buff, T-AI-10 Fine-Tuning, M #82 Medina Outfitter, M #83 Medina Enforcer and M #87 Tatches the Totem. A game recorded before the change in which a Buff reached a card in a hand that could take Cloaked no longer folds to its recorded hash and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). This amends [[R386]]'s and [[R2657005]]'s keyword clauses.
