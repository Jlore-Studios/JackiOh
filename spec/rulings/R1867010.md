Stinkyscape #156 Catalyst Comet's "a random Catalyst card" is one pick from the match rng ([[R60]]) among the tag's non-token cards in the sets that ship ([[R380]], [[R1420]]), added to the hand through [[§2.4]]'s pipeline. "Shuffle this into your deck" is the Comet itself, once it has resolved, moved into its owner's deck at a random position instead of staying in the graveyard (`shuffle_card_into`, C #30 Recycle's verb, [[R80]], [[R316]]): a full deck leaves it in the graveyard by the verb's own refusal, and the moved instance keeps its Radiant flag ([[R78]]). The Radiant face's card is Radiant; the Comet goes back as it is, Radiant.
