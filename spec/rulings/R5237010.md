A card with the static flag `immuneToTag: <tag>` (FvH #100.1 Felinor Golem's Human, #100.2 Human Golem's Felinor) is, while it acts on the field, out of reach of every card whose tags (`query::tags_of`, granted tags included) hold that tag, as a card Immune to Spells is of a Spell's ([[R4453000]]): such a card's declarations, prompts and random picks never take it, its effects pass it by (scopes, every Unit, a card-scope walk), and its hits on it, combat damage included, are no damage instance at all ([[§4.4]] step 0, as [[R1849000]]'s). One `restrictions::tag_cannot_reach(state, source, card)` answers each place Immune to Spells is asked (`spell_cannot_reach`, `unaffected_by`, `spell_damage_cannot_reach`). A continuation re-entered once its card has gone is judged by its definition's printed tags ([[R127]]). It bars no attack: a tagged Unit may still declare one on it and deal it nothing. Its own effects reach it, a hit with no source (a Burn or Toxic tick, fatigue) carries no tag, and off the field it has no immunity.
