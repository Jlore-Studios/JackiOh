Since issue #1704 Classic+ #62 KY's Papaya always Discovers 4 cells, one per lane, and the "done" of [[R831003]] is gone: the board has five lanes, so the fourth Discover still finds a lane not used yet. Each Discover is a `mode` prompt of random cells drawn from the match rng among the cells of the unused lanes, as [[R831003]] draws them: `PAPAYA_BASE_OFFERS` (2) on the base face and `PAPAYA_OFFERS` (3) on the Radiant face. After the first, the prompt reads "KY's Papaya: Discover another cell".

The curve through the four cells is [[R422]]'s, announced by `curveDrawn` as before, and every card on it is destroyed rather than exiled (on the Radiant face, every enemy card): one destroy each, collected together at the next state check ([[§4.5]]), so an Indestructible card stays ([[R46]]), a Reborn Unit comes back, Deaths fire, a face-down card goes to its owner's graveyard and a token ceases to exist ([[R11]]); a card beneath a top resumes and is not destroyed by the same curve ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]). Hearthstone's Destroy reaches the same cards.

Through v0.4.0f the curve took 1 to 4 cells, each among 3 on both faces, and exiled what it met; `script_v0_4_0f` keeps that for those versions' replays ([[R767000]]). It amends [[R422]] and [[R831003]].
