FvH #81 Old-Timer Catboy's Cry reads "Discover a combat keyword to gain. Add 1~3 to your Woozy counter." (Radiant: "a pair of combat keywords"). The designer's note says each keyword, or each set of two, comes with a Woozy 1-3; FV2 gives no reading of it.

**The rule.** The base face Discovers one of three combat keywords of [[R21]]'s pool that it lacks now ([[R5239004]]); the pick is granted to it for good. The Radiant face offers three options of two keywords each: six different keywords of the same pool that it lacks (no Taunt for an Indestructible one), drawn by the match rng as the effect resolves ([[R60]], [[R113]]), two to an option, each option naming its pair; the pair picked is granted together. With fewer than six left it offers the pairs that can be made, with one left that one alone, and with none nothing. The Woozy is its controller's Woozy counter ([[R3548000]]), not a Woozy keyword on the Unit: each pick raises it by a uniform 1 to 3, rolled as the pick is gained (`gain_woozy`, [[R5238011]]), once per pick on both faces, so a Radiant pair rolls once. With nothing offered there is no pick and no Woozy. The roll is the text's range, no declared number and no luck-based roll. The AI, the random policy and a timeout answer either offer as any Discover or Choose one ([[§10.6]]).
