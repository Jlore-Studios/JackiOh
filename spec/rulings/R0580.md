An Easy problem is generated from the match rng, never drawn from the bank: a + b with a and b each uniform from 10 to 99 (`KY_TEST_EASY_ADDENDS`), its three wrong options a + b moved by three different entries of ±1, ±2, ±10 (`KY_TEST_EASY_MISSES`, drawn as a shuffle of the six), so the four options always differ; the problem rides the answer prompt's resume data by its addends (`easy:<a>+<b>`), and the options are shown in an rng-shuffled order ([[R420]], [[R465]])
