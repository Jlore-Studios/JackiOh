Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Every Classic card has its final entry in `apps/web/src/fx/cards/classic.json5`: 79 of its 90 cards an entry other than `none`, every Spell, Field Spell, Trap and Field Trap a `cast` motif, a Flourish or a set piece, and every Field Spell an `aura` ([[R2787000]]). Each is picked from `cargo jackioh fx census`, and overrides it only with a reason: its motif families predate issue #2786's motifs (so a draw takes `flight`, a summon `conjure`, a counter placed `tally`, a steal `tether`), its `bolt` came from fatigue damage or another stray event, or it read a cost (a Tribute) as the card's effect. A palette names a sub-look where cards of one flavor would blur together, and the scale is the card's own ([[R2784000]]). The pass's per-card table, `apps/web/src/fx/cards/classic.md` (one row per card, its token included: entry, scale, the census's suggestion and why), is held row by row to the catalog and the table by `classic.test.ts`, as a set's pass asks, with every hook a drawn motif and every play's motif drawn on its gallery fixture.

A Unit's hooks entry may carry `entrance: "<key>"`, its marquee entrance ([[R670]]) beside its hooks: #45 Nature Titan keeps `titanBloom` and its Cry draws `flight`. An entrance is a Unit's (the loader fails it on any other card), and an entrance alone stays `{ bespoke: "<key>" }`. The `recall` motif reads `addedToHand` and replaces nothing: a card back flies from its owner's graveyard pile into the hand, with a gather on the pile and the hand's sparkle as it lands, and no shake. #34 Ancient Acquisition, #14 Shadowstep and #47 Recurring Felinor take it, as issue #2793's `send` flies a made card from its caster's hero where these return one from the pile it left; the event names no zone, so a Radiant Ancient Acquisition's card from exile flies from the graveyard pile too. Three cards have a Flourish of their own, built from the motif primitives in `fx/cards/classic/`, each over its events' own rows (`keep`) and keyed only off the readable play resolving ([[R202]]): #3 Book of Heal's `pageMend` (its pages fly from the caster's hero to what it heals, where issue #2793's `mend` glows on the healed card alone), #30 Recycle's `recycleLoop` (the graveyard looping into the deck, once a play, on its first shuffle; a shuffle of a whole pile keeps its one wave) and #85 King Wagtoggle's `crownSwap` (the two decks crossing to each other's pile). Each starts inside its entry and is gone within the effects' tail ([[R200]]), its beats fractions of the entry ([[R201]]).

The `death`, `trigger` and `activate` entries stay inert until the effects layer keys on [[R2783000]]'s marker, and a Trap's `cast` until a firing opens a play. The `/dev/fx` gallery plays every Classic entry other than `none` as issue #2793's gallery plays a card's (a play's motif, a hook's motif in its palette at its card's scale through the gallery's own director, a Flourish on its own fixture, #43 Plague Nuke's set piece), and a marquee entrance as its Unit lands, Nature Titan's before its Cry. No entrance or takeover is restyled: #85 King Wagtoggle and #90 In Too Deep keep the shared Legendary and Mythic looks, their music ([[R2722000]]) and their one capped hold ([[R2722001]]). Rejected: dispatching the inert hooks here, which changes the effects memory and the motif dispatch of every set and is its own part of issue #2782; restyling a Legendary's entrance or the Mythic takeover, which issue #2803 reworks; and a separate entrance table, where the field beside the hooks keeps one line per card.
