A Brittle count is kept on the card in every zone ([[R78]], [[R385]]) but ticks only while the card is on the field, in [[R68]]'s order at the start of its controller's turn (units by lane, the top of each Stack pile only, then the backrow). A card in a hand or a deck holds its count: it does not drop, nothing crumbles there and no event reports it, however long it waits. A count a card holds off the field starts its turn cycle as the card enters the field from a hand, a deck, a graveyard or the resolving zone, so its first tick is the first start of its controller's turn numbered t + 2 or later of the arrival (`BRITTLE_FIRST_TICK_TURNS`, [[R385]]), however long it was held; a move from one field zone to another (a steal, a swap, a rotation, an Animated step) is no arrival and starts nothing. A card that leaves the field keeps the count it has, held until it is back. [[R13]] and [[R441]] stand: a card dormant under a Stack is not on the field, so it does not tick, and a spent count (0) is dropped by [[R78]]'s and [[R215]]'s reset. A Brittle crumble is a destroy on the field, so `crumbled` always reports `zone: "field"`
