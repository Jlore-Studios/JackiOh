On handicapped fuzz seed 2788, S+ #6.2 Stinky Cheese evolved in its graveyard ([[R3566004]]), and TG #55 Reminisce then put it in its owner's hand still evolved, with its Evolve's Flashback, Animated and +5/+5 and its 0/0 stat line: a hand card holding field-only state, against [[§3.2]]. A card that evolved in a graveyard or an exile pile and goes from there to a hand or a library gets [[R78]]'s reset, as [[R215]] gives it a card going the other way, so what arrives is the printed card, which may evolve again ([[R3536000]]); Hearthstone returns a fresh copy of a card from the graveyard. A Flashback play from the pile is no such move, so it keeps the Evolve, as R3566004 has it.

Rejected: keeping the Evolve in the hand, which leaves a hand card with a Unit's stat line and keywords no face prints.
