Presentation and intent only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): drag stays two clicks with the pointer held down between them, and click-click works everywhere it did. A play or an activation that a drop or a click has narrowed but not finished (a Unit placed in its zone whose Cry still wants a target, a Tribute paid with a target still to name) is lifted again by a press on what it has picked so far, the chosen zone, a Tribute, a declared target or the card whose ability is built, which draws the targeting arrow from there to what `highlightFor` still lights; everything picked is kept, the drop is the click that narrows it, and a release on nothing, a lost pointer or Escape goes back to that build rather than to idle (a second Escape then cancels it). A press on the play's own hand card still lifts it afresh. A face-up backrow card of yours with one ability that aims at nothing is dragged as a card onto the board, where a drop builds it as its click would (an activation sent at once, or a mode or a Tribute still to choose), as a spell with no target is; an aimed one keeps [[R510]]'s arrow, and a card with several abilities is dragged from the control of the one meant. A prompt's option is dragged out of its panel, and a release outside the panel is a click on that option, so a one-of-N picker answers and a picker of several picks toggles that pick; a release over the panel does nothing. Card options lift on touch, and list and plain options, in a panel that scrolls, under a mouse or a pen. All of it is off with "Drag to play", and "Reduce motion" gates the ghost's motion as it does a hand card's
