"Summon every Unit with a Death from your graveyard" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #70 Z'Zoth) and "Summon every Unit with a Cry from your graveyard" (#71 A'Zoth) read the Unit cards in their controller's graveyard as the clause begins ([[R113]]), filtered by the running face's own text: a printed Death (`Script.death`) or a Cry that can be triggered there (`has_triggerable_cry`); a Death or Cry granted on the field is gone in the graveyard ([[R78]]). They are taken oldest first and summoned into [[R64]]'s zones with no Cry ([[R1]]); with no room the rest stay in the graveyard. Z'Zoth's Radiant face gives each Unit its Cry summoned Reborn. A'Zoth then triggers the Cry ([[R467]]) of each Unit this Cry summoned ([[R136]]), in the order summoned, never a card left in the graveyard and never A'Zoth itself, with every choice of its controller's drawn from the match rng as [[R1855001]]'s are (`trigger_cry`'s `random`), so nothing is asked of them; the other player's prompts are asked as usual. A declared target with no legal pick is passed by, so what aims at it does nothing and the rest of that Cry still runs. On the Radiant face each Cry fires `repeats` times before the next one's. An A'Zoth it summons triggers only what its own Cry summoned. Rejected: triggering the Cries of the Units left in the graveyard, which the summon did not bring back; and interleaving the repeats, Hearthstone's "cast twice" resolving one in full before the next.
