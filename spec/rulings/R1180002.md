"Aura: Your Spells have Lifesteal" (Meditative+ #7 Life Mage) is a layer ([[§10.4]]), as [[R1044]]'s Cloaked aura is over the cards of a type in a hand: while its source acts, each Spell its controller is resolving has the keyword — a card in that player's resolving zone whose type is Spell, so not a Field Spell or a Trap, nor a Spell set face-down, which is a Trap until it resolves ([[R783005]]). [[§4.4]] step 8 reads the source's keywords through the layers, so each hit of such a Spell heals its controller's hero by the damage dealt, once per hit, whatever else states Lifesteal ([[R85]]). A keyword is had once ([[§6.1]]), so two such auras give it once. It ends the moment the source leaves the field, and the opponent's Spells never have it.
