Expedition12's Radiant face prints Cleave in place of Echo 1 since #1563. [[§6.1]]'s Cleave is a Unit's ("also damages units adjacent to the target", combat only); on a Spell whose effect lands on a picked card it reads as Hearthstone reads a spell that "also hits adjacent": the effect also lands on the cards beside the pick. A hand has no lanes, so beside means hand order as the Spell resolves (the Spell itself is resolving, out of the hand): the card before the pick and the card after it, one or none at either end. Expedition12's Radiant face makes the pick and both its neighbours Radiant ([[§6.3]] Make Radiant: a Radiant neighbour is left as it is), and opens no second pick, as its Echo did. The pick is still the declared one ([[R81]]). It edits [[§8.8]].
