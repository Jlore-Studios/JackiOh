S #7 The Greatest Heist's Cry reads "Discover an Acclaimed Unit or a Jelly Bean" (the designer's "Jellybean", ST8; ST-S09-3).

- **One pool.** It is one Discover of three over the union: the non-token cards tagged Acclaimed or Jelly Bean (`anyTags`), from the sets that ship ([[R380]]). Every Acclaimed card is a Unit, so the tag names the Units. The Jelly Beans are Core #26 to #29 ([[R1837000]]). While the Stinkyscape set is held the pool is seven cards; its own Acclaimed Units join it when the set ships.
- **The pick.** The pick goes to the hand, Radiant on the Radiant face, and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). A Jelly Bean picked can meet Stage 1, "Make an Acclaimed Unit Radiant", on an Acclaimed card in your hand ([[R1854002]]).
