Stinkyscape #153 Stimmycheck reads "Gain 1 mana, cards in your hand and deck have a 15% chance for Inflation to hit which causes them to cost (1) more. At the end of your turn, add a Stimmycheck to your hand with +10% chance at Inflation"; its Radiant face prints Lucky 1.

- **The roll.** `inflate { chance, player }` rolls once for each card in the player's hand, in hand order, and then in their deck, from the top, each roll one draw of the match rng out of `INFLATION_ROLL_SIDES` (100), a hit when it lands below the chance. A card it hits gains `INFLATION_COST` (1) on its `costMod`, a price change like any other: it rides the card through the hand and the deck, and [[R766]] takes it off in a graveyard or an exile pile. An X-cost card, whose price no change reaches ([[R65]]), is passed by without a roll; so is the Stimmycheck itself, which is resolving.
- **The chance.** `chance` is the printed one (`INFLATION_CHANCE`, 15, when absent). A card that carries its own, the copy a Stimmycheck adds to its hand, rolls at that instead: it rides the card's memory (`inflationChance`), written by `add_to_hand`'s `memory`, and the card reads its next copy's with `next_inflation_chance`, `INFLATION_CHANCE_STEP` (10) higher and never past 100. A chance of 0 rolls nothing, and one of 100 hits every card it can reach with no roll. As memory, the chance goes with [[R78]]'s reset, so a Stimmycheck played again from a graveyard rolls at its printed chance.
- **Luck.** The roll is luck-based ([[§6.1]]): the running card's Lucky X ([[R1438]]) and its controller's Luck ([[R987]]) roll each card X more times and keep the better, which on the controller's own cards is a miss and on the opponent's a hit, as [[R1440]] keeps a coin's better side.
- **Silent.** A hand and a deck are cards no two players both read, so, as [[R440]] has a change over a hidden scope, no event announces the rolls or their hits: the owner reads the new prices off the view, and nobody learns how many hit.
- Nothing reaches it until a card plays it, so no shipped game changes.
