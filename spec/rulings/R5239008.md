FvH #113 Ball Knower Discovers a Unit, "one of which is a version from the past". A Discover from the catalog may name `pastOptions: N`: as the effect resolves, the match rng draws N of its query's past versions (`past: only`, [[R3538002]]), fewer when fewer exist, then the rest of its `count` from the query's current cards, and shuffles the whole offer ([[R60]]); both pools leave out the generating card ([[R387]]). A past id names its version (`<card>@<version>`), so every option of such an offer is keyed by its place (`offer-0`, `offer-1`, …), labeled with its card's name, the cards behind the places riding the prompt's resume data, which no view sends, as [[R1183005]]'s code Discover keeps its cards ([[§10.8]]): the chooser is never told which option is the past one. The resume step reads its card with `discovered_card`, which names the card behind a place-keyed or a code pick, or the card a plain card option names, and `split_past_id` tells a past pick. The pick goes on as any card does; with no past version in the pool the offer is all current cards.
