The fuzz monitor's I21 ([[R4453000]]) blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, up to the next card's marker. Issue #4747's pull request had its changed cards' Duos rotation deal a game in which S #150 Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition) rolled "Fill your board with random Human Stinkyscape Units" and summoned S #115 Scornboi among them. At the settled state check ([[§4.5]]) Scornboi's exile aura ([[R1850002]]: "Every Unit that is neither Human, a Starship Piece nor a Starship is exiled") exiled S+ #67 Phase Wall, which holds Immune to Spells. The state check exiles with no marker of its own, so the `exiled` landed in the Spell's run and I21 named the Spell. The same seed fails on `main` from before that pull request: its fragment's cards, forced together, brought the board out.

**This is the aura's card, not the Spell.** [[§6.1]] keeps a Spell's effects off the Unit, and [[R4453000]] leaves Units, Field Spells and Traps reaching it. An exile aura is its card's standing text ([[§6.3]]), read for every card on the field at each state check, an immune one included. The Spell changed the board, and the Unit it summoned exiled the immune one, as [[R4623000]]'s standing Evolve answers a Spell's summon. The engine already plays it so: the aura's walk runs with its own card as the source.

**What I21 allows.** An `exiled` of an immune Unit while a card acting on the field after the step, face-up and awake, holds an exile aura whose side reaches the side the Unit stood on: either side for "any", the other side for an "enemy" aura (a Radiant face), its own for a "self" one. With no such aura standing after the step, the `exiled` is still a reach: then only the Spell can have exiled it. This joins the lane a Black Hole or a slash takes ([[R4508004]]) and the hole's pull at a turn's end ([[R4577008]]) as the exiles of an immune Unit I21 lets pass in a Spell's run.

Rejected: a marker of its own for the state check's exiles. The engine marks where a hook begins, never the rules' work ([[R4623000]]), and a new event would change every replay's stream. Rejected too: reading the aura's whole reach (its tags, cost and spared keywords) at the exile, which needs the board as it stood mid-step; the side alone is what the step's end still shows.
