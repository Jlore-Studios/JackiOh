Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. After Hearthstone, where every Legendary has an animation of its own, a set's effects table (`apps/web/src/fx/cards/<set>.json5`, [[R2784000]]) gives every entry but a `none` a frame beside its kind:

- **`entrance: "<key>"`**, the entrance its card lands with in place of its rarity's ([[R670]]): beside any entry of a Unit or a Field Spell, or alone (an entry of no hook). A Field Spell's plays as it lands face up in the backrow, never as it is set face down (its fresh id's `formerId`), and stands in for the shared Legendary or Mythic rays, burst and ring there, as a Unit's does in its zone. The loader fails one on any other card: a Trap's or a Field Trap's entrance is its firing, which its `cast` already draws ([[R2797000]]).
- **`takeover: "<look>"`**, the look of its own a Mythic's takeover wears ([[R2722001]]): `void`, `pawn`, `genie` or `ai`, since [[R3400000]] `summit`, `canvas` or `napkin`, which draw art of their own, and since [[R3405000]] `ascent`, `workshop`, `brush`, `mine`, `quarry`, `pavilion`, `virus`, `dojo`, `studyHall`, `caneRack`, `warRoom` or `fortress`, which do too (as `ai` does since); every other Mythic wears the shared `prismatic`, which no entry names. The loader fails one on a card that is no Mythic, a token by the rarity it prints.
- **`signature: true`**, the card's signature: a Legendary's or a Mythic's (a token's by the rarity it prints), naming its first beat, or the loader fails it: a Unit's entrance; a Field Spell's entrance or a cast of its own; any other card's cast of its own, a `{ bespoke }` recipe or a `{ setPiece }`. Its second beat is what the card's text plays (its Cry, cast, trigger, Activate or Death), in its entry or in its entrance, and it may wear an `aura` of its own ([[R2787000]]), as Hearthstone's Legendaries enter and then show what they do.

This folds [[R670]]'s marquee entrances and [[R2722001]]'s looks into the table, their recipes unchanged in `fx/cards/<set>/`: #100 Ceaseless Void is `{ setPiece: "voidFold", entrance: "voidCollapse", takeover: "void" }` (the set piece names no entrance any more, [[R2800000]]), C #56 Spell Tyrant `{ entrance: "tyrantSigil" }`, C #80 BOOM! Big Max `{ entrance: "bigBoom" }`, C #45 Nature Titan keeps its entrance beside its Cry ([[R2794000]]), #96 My Pawn, #97 Zephrys and M #49 YileGPT name their looks beside their hooks, and `{ bespoke }` names a card recipe alone ([[R2794000]]'s entrance alone in a `{ bespoke }` ends here). Every entrance is a recipe of `ENTRANCES` and every look one of `OWN_TAKEOVER_LOOKS`; an entry that names none of the three keeps exactly the keys it had.

A signature keeps a budget by rarity and type, inside [[R200]]'s `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS`, one set piece per action and `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS` ([[R2785000]]): over its gallery play, held as the queue holds it at speed 1 with Big moments full, every cue of a Legendary's ends within `SIGNATURE_LEGENDARY_MAX_MS` (1.6 s) of the play's start, a Mythic's, its takeover's hold included, within `SIGNATURE_MYTHIC_MAX_MS` (2.6 s), and a Spell's, seen only briefly, within `SIGNATURE_SPELL_SHARE` (three quarters) of its rarity's. A Spell's shakes are the loudest, at most `SIGNATURE_SPELL_MAX_TRAUMA` (1), a permanent's at most `SIGNATURE_PERMANENT_MAX_TRAUMA` (0.8), and a signature aura's mote alpha, tint and edge glow at most `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY` (0.8), the quietest once in play. A signature lives in its entries, so a tap, the effects speed, the intensity, Big moments and Reduce Motion act on it as on every cue ([[R201]]), and it paces nothing.

`SIGNATURE_PENDING` (`fx/cards/signature.test.ts`) lists, in catalog order, every Legendary and Mythic, tokens by the rarity they print, whose entry is no signature: 164 of 165 when this lands, Felinor Fiender's the first ([[R3332001]]). The test fails on a Legendary or Mythic missing from it and on a listed card that has its signature, so a set's pass takes each card off as it gives it one, the list only shrinks, and the last pass empties it; a new Legendary or Mythic comes with its signature or joins the list. The `/dev/fx` gallery lists every signature by set, for a person to review each one, and plays a signature whose entrance reads the board over a board of its own (`galleryBoardOf`). Every field keys on the readable card's definition alone ([[R202]]): a hidden play, a face-down card and a card the viewer may not see key none, and no cue names a card.

Rejected: a separate entrance or takeover table, which splits one card's animation across files where the field beside its entry keeps one line per card; an entrance on a Trap at its reveal, which would draw its firing twice; and deriving the tier from an entrance, which would count Nature Titan, whose Cry is still a shared motif. A Field Spell is the permanent that may take an entrance beside a Unit, as the cards that land face up as they are played; a person may widen it.
