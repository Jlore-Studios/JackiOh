"Summon or cast 5 random Jlockeed cards" (S+ #142's fourth entry) is five `summon_or_cast_one_random` picks of
the Jlockeed pool, Units and Field Spells among them (the owner, 2026-11-09). A pick is a random cast's: never the
casting card's own definition ([[R387]]), through the run's tally of unique draws ([[R3563000]]), refused past the
random-cast chain's cap ([[R452]]). A Spell is cast with random choices ([[R452]]); a permanent is summoned with no
Cry ([[R1]]) into the zone [[R64]] names, and one with no zone lands nowhere, its draw spent.

Rejected: casting a Unit with its Cry, which a "summon" of the owner's wording does not do.
