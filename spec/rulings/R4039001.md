The client shows the notification feed the server keeps ([[R4039000]]) and decides nothing of it: which rows exist, what each says happened and where it leads are the server's, and the screen a row opens reads its own state.

- **Recent.** The inbox ([[R3729002]]) holds two sections, "Waiting on you", what it listed before, and "Recent", the feed's newest page: one line each in the game's words, the actor's name drawn as every username is ([[R1436]]), how long ago and Open, which goes where it happened (the daily game, the Daily screen, the friends list's requests or friends, the lobby, the shared replay) and closes the panel. A row unread when the page was read carries a dot. Opening the panel reads the newest page and marks every row up to its newest one read, so a row written after the read is not marked unseen; a row pushed while the panel is open reads the page again. "Show older" reads the page before. A failed read keeps the rows shown and says so under Recent, apart from the panel's alert for the answers it shows.
- **The bell.** Its badge still counts only what needs the player. While it counts nothing and the feed holds unread rows, the bell carries a dot and no number.
- **The toast.** A row the social socket pushes while the app is open shows one toast, the line, Open and Dismiss, in the challenge toast's style; a newer row replaces it, so there is never more than one. A `lobbyInvite` has its own toast ([[R3730005]]) and shows none here. While a board is shown the toast waits and shows once the board goes, so it never covers a game's controls.
- **The tab's title.** While the tab is hidden, its title starts with the bell's count, `(2) JackiOh`, and gets its own back as the tab shows; a board's title and the Daily screen's, which say whose move it is themselves, gain none.
- **The browser's notifications.** The notification settings, on the Daily screen and in the account's Notifications section, offer "Show notifications on this computer while JackiOh is open" where the browser can show them. Turning it on asks the browser's permission; refused, it stays off and says so. The choice is the browser's own, kept on the device. While it is on and the tab is hidden, a pushed `dailyMove`, `dailyChallenge`, `lobbyInvite` or `friendRequest` row, or a `dailyAnswered` row whose challenge was accepted (its game now waits on the challenger), each asking something of the player, shows one with the line, and its click brings the tab forward and opens the row's place. It needs no push service and no key. A tab hidden past `HIDDEN_QUIET_MS` has let its socket go ([[R3576000]]) and hears no row, so shows none; Web Push stays for a closed app.
- **Email.** The settings offer "Email me when it's my move" only while the server sends player email (`emailAvailable`); otherwise they say, in the game's voice, that JackiOh tells the player in the bell, with a toast and on this computer.

Rejected: toasts over a board, which would cover a match's controls; one toast per row, which stacks after a busy night; keeping the desktop setting on the server, where one device's permission would turn another's on; a count on the bell for what is only news, against the menus brief's badges for what needs the player (#3728).
