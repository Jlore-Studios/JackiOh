S+ #24 reads "Choose a non-fused card in your hand or field, shuffle 10 (embiggen 40) copies of it into your deck, they keep no cost changes or buffs". The pick is one of the caster's own cards: in their hand, or a Unit or backrow card of theirs on the field, a declared target ([[R81]]). A fused card, a definition a Fuse made ([[R179]]), is no card to copy, and nothing happens with it. The copies are fresh cards of the chosen card's definition, ten, forty when it was played Embiggened ([[R81]]), each on the face the chosen card wears (a Radiant card makes Radiant copies) and with none of its cost changes, buffs, tuning or granted keywords ([[R57]]'s riders are left out). The Radiant face then Buffs each of this cast's copies once and no card else, silently while they are in the deck ([[R440]]); the copies carry a mark in their memory by which the Buffs find them.

Rejected: an exact copy of the chosen card (R57's whole copy), which would keep the changes the text says it drops; and Buffing every card of the deck.
