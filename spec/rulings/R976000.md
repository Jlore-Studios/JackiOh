Each seat of an arena game (`cargo jackioh arena`) or a promotion's (`cargo jackioh promote`, which plays the arena's games) is `build_ai_deck` on its seat's stream (`<seed>:deck:<seat>`) at Easy's resources, minus its own AI's shadow ban ([[R186]]), and nothing else: `AiDeckOptions::shape`, which multiplies each card's weight by `shaped_weight` (the unban lane's `DEALT_Q`), is off for both seats, as for practice, the quality gates, the sweep, Duos and the server's decks. The option is the only switch: a ban list equal to this build's `SHADOW_BAN_IDS` shapes nothing by itself, and `shape: true` shapes a deal whatever its ban list. Before, the builder shaped exactly the seat whose ban list was this build's own under a mana cap, so the unban lane's candidate (strictly fewer bans than its parent, [[R732001]]) was always shaped and its parent never: generation 12 changed only ban entries and still beat its parent 76 of 100. The shaped mulligan (`mulligan_keep_shaped`) is the AI's play, not its deal, and is unchanged on both sides. Hearthstone has no counterpart; the rule is the improve lane's, whose two seats were always dealt alike ([[R850000]]). Whether practice should deal shaped decks is a person's decision, one flag away.
