Core #52 Silly Silas's rotation moves every card one step whatever its destination's Lock: a Locked zone takes the card that rotates into it, its damage, buffs and position kept, as [[R688]] already lets a summon land in one (a Lock refuses plays only). A reserved zone ([[R64]]'s Reborn reservation, an "Animated on your turn" card's home) still refuses it, so the rotation's only bar is `is_reserved`. Before patch v0.3.10 a card whose destination was Locked was Bounced instead. It amends [[R14]], [[R88]] and [[R688]]; the Radiant face's crossing to the other side is still a Bounce that costs (0).
