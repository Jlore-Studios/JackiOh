In fuzz seed 6180 a sweep ([[R3554008]]) killed the two Units whose auras held its target up, and the target died at the next pass of the state check for want of health ([[§4.5]] step 1). The combat forecast ([[R3100000]]) read every death against the board as it stood, so it said the target lived, and [[R4318000]] had I18 skip every attack where that could happen. The tutorial's coach, the aimed preview's marks ([[R4290000]]) and the seat's view all read the same wrong answer.

The forecast now settles the check after each attack on a scratch copy of the board. It marks what the attack's hits deal and destroy: the blow, the strike back, each Cleave hit ([[§4.4]] step 10, its sweep's hit after it, [[R4290001]]) and each other hit of a sweep, on a Unit target or a hero. Then it runs the check's passes until nothing changes, in [[R68]]'s order, the active player's side first:

- Each pass exiles the units marked for exile first ([[R1124]]), with no Reborn, then takes off every unit at 0 or less health or marked destroyed, all together ([[R463]]).
- A unit whose view has Reborn comes back in its zone at 1 health without it before the next pass, its aura with it ([[R83]]).
- A unit atop a pile goes with every card dormant beneath it, so nothing buried comes to the top. The forecast is shown a pile's count, never what it holds ([[R13]], [[R4221002]]'s reason).
- A hit that ends the game ends it after the first pass ([[§4.5]] step 2), so no later pass is counted.
- When no destroyed unit is reached, nothing is cloned. When no aura is in play and no unit sets its stats from the board ([[§10.4]] layers 5 and 2), the first pass is the whole check and nothing is cloned either.

Only the death verdicts read the settled board: `kills`, `survives` and each splash hit's `kills` count every pass (`attack_deaths` lists them). Every hit of a combat lands before any check ([[R59]]), so `dealt`, `taken`, `pops`, each splash hit's `dealt`, the Trample `overflow` and the seat's `lethal` stay the standing board's. The strike back still follows the standing board's kill ([[§4.3]] step 1).

I18 ([[R4111000]]) judges these attacks again. [[R4318000]]'s two skips are replaced by one: an attack where a unit the settled check takes off has a card dormant beneath it or held by it, whose leaving brings that card in. The rest of I18's skips (hooks, traps, chains, reflections, "would die" replacements and the combatants' own piles and held cards) stand.

Hearthstone semantics: deaths resolve together at the check after the combat, and again until nothing changes.

Rejected:

- Projecting the blow, the splash and the lethal sum again from the settled board. Every hit lands before any check, so a Trample blow would be dealt against health the target no longer has.
- Simulating the attack with `reduce`. [[R3100000]] and [[R4290000]] rejected it, since it would run face-down traps and reach for prompts.
- Letting the card beneath a dying pile's top surface on the scratch board. It would read hidden information ([[R13]], CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- Taking a Reborn unit off for good. Its body returns before the next pass, so the target it holds up stands, and the old forecast's answer there was right.

Not modelled, each left to I18's other skips or to what the board shows: Death hooks and traps (I18 sees their `hookResolving` or `trapFired`), Tribute conditions ([[R403]]), carriers ([[R446]]), the card-on-card standing steps, Starship fusion ([[R1852000]]), the rest of [[R78]]'s reset of a Reborn body, and the AI's trade reads (`fight`, `kills_and_survives`), which still read the first pass alone: [[R4171000]]'s agreement test leaves out the attacks a later pass changes.
