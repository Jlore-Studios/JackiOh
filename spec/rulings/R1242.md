Meditative #95's first entry, "Fuse your hand into one card and add 2 copies of it to your hand, all three of which cost (0)", fuses every card in its caster's hand as the entry resolves, in hand order, but the Immutable ones, which stay ([[R23]]), into one fresh hand card per [[R77]], [[R102]] and [[R469]] (no kept card: the shared type, else the first's; a Token only if every ingredient is one), its `fused` event hidden as the hand is ([[R470]]). Then `CHAOS_MED_FUSE_COPIES` (2) copies of it are added to that hand by `add_to_hand`'s `copyOf`: a fresh card of the copied card's definition carrying [[R57]]'s riders, the radiant flag, the Chinese flag ([[R1300]]), granted tags ([[R923]]), `statsOverride` and `tuning`, the card copied staying where it is; a `copyOf` that names no card adds nothing. All three cost (0) (`costOverride`), and a full hand burns a copy ([[§2.4]]). With one fusable card there is no fusion: that card takes the (0) and the copies. With none, the entry does nothing.
