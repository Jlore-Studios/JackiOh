title: "Crater on a GIGA card Locks all four zones it fills"
cards: "§6.1 Crater, §6.1 GIGA, §6.3 Lock, R688, R783010, R783011, R1850006, R3546000, R2657005; issue #4693"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/crater.rs
---
A GIGA card with Crater Locks every zone it fills ([[R3546000]]): its anchor, the other row's zone
in the same lane, and both rows' zones in the next lane. It does so wherever Crater Locks one zone
for any other card: a GIGA Unit as it arrives, any way it arrives, before its Cry ([[R783010]]); a
GIGA Field Spell as it enters ([[R1850006]]); a GIGA Trap or Field Trap as it fires face-down
([[R783011]]). Each zone not Locked already is reported `locked`, in `giga::spanned_slots`' order,
and each is [[R688]]'s Lock: it refuses plays, never a summon or a move, and outlasts the card, so a
GIGA Crater leaves four Locked zones behind it. A card that is not GIGA Locks its one zone as
before. It is reachable with no card printing both, since a Nerf may give Crater to a GIGA card in a
hand or a deck ([[R2657005]]). Rejected: the anchor alone, which leaves three of the zones the card
stood in open and makes a GIGA the only card Crater does not crater under; and the unit row's two
zones alone, since a GIGA is in both rows ([[R3546000]]) and Crater already Locks a backrow zone for
a Trap, a Field Spell and a Flying Unit standing there.
