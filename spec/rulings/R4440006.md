Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: "Rework such that the fruits aren't fused into them, rather it gains the effect of the fruits." As S+ #17 Smoothie or #18 Large Smoothie is added to a hand, its two (three) random Fruit Spells ([[R3566001]]) are drawn as before ([[R3558003]]), but `fuseRandomInto`'s `asText` mints their fusion as a definition of its own, kept by no card ([[R77]]'s faces: both texts, each Fruit on its Radiant face from a Radiant Smoothie, [[R469]]), and writes [[R1850000]]'s record on the Smoothie naming it. The Smoothie runs that text, the way a Unit that copied another's does: its scripts, keywords, declarations, prompts and numbers are the Fruits', its instance, name, cost, type, tags, rarity, art and Radiant flag its own. It is no fusion, so nothing that reads or splits a fusion sees it as one, and played it does each Fruit's effect in turn, each asking its own targets, and plays no Fruit. Its owner's view shows the text it runs as a copier's (`CardView.copies`); the opponent's names neither it nor its Fruits, and the in-place `transformed` that reports it is hidden as the card is. A Smoothie that runs its Fruits' text is not filled again; the record is memory, so [[R215]]'s reset ends it as the Smoothie lands from a play, and one that comes back to a hand is filled anew. Through v0.6.11d the Fruits were fused into it ("Smoothie + X + Y"), the script the replays of those versions keep ([[R767000]]).
