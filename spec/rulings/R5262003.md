FvH #100.1 Felinor Golem prints "Allure every Unit this is in combat with". That is the Unit it attacks, Allured as the attack is declared (`when_attacks`, before any damage), and the Unit that attacks it, Allured after that combat (`after_attacked`). Each is named to `allure_unit` ([[R5238008]]): stolen at the start of the Golem's controller's next turn, or destroyed with no open zone, dropped if it leaves the field first ([[R174]]), and landing though the Golem has gone since ([[R76]]). A Cleave splash's side victims (the Radiant face) are in no combat with it, and a hero is no Unit, so neither is Allured. Immune to Humans bars no attack ([[R5237010]]), so a Human that attacks it is Allured as any other attacker is.
