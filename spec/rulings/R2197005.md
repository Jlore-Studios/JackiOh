TG #30 Floodgates' "Death: Destroy all your Units" ([[§8.11]], [[R2197003]]) never takes the body it dies on: a body that resummons itself as the same card, as Stinkyscape #112 Job Application's "Death: Resummon this at Stage 1" does once fused with it ([[R77]], [[§6.3]] Fuse), stays. And run on a body that is not Floodgates itself (a fusion or a granted text) that arrived this turn, the Death destroys nothing. So each such body's sweep fires at most once a turn, and two of them on one side cannot destroy each other's return forever, which REVIEW A3.6 asks of a self-recursive Death. A printed Floodgates is never such a body, so it plays as before: its Death destroys every other Unit its controller has ([[R89]]). The AI's sweep found the loop (`sweep:hard:traininggrounds-014:6`): a fused Unit carrying both texts died, Job Application resummoned it, Floodgates' Death destroyed it again, and the board did not settle. Rejected: R2197004's per-turn bound on every Floodgates, which would stop a printed one played and destroyed in the same turn; and sparing the dying body alone, which two such bodies on one side still loop past.
