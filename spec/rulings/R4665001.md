Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VII: Mend Projector "only affects your units". At the end of its controller's turn ([[R62]]) S+ #41 heals each of its controller's Units to full, then gives each +{health} Health ([[R4353024]]) and, on the Radiant face, Divine Shield, and reaches no Unit of the opponent's, where [[R3556003]] had it mend both sides, its controller's Units first. Its text says "your Units" on both faces. Replays of the versions through v0.6.12c fold under `script_v0_6_12c`, which mends both sides as they did ([[R767000]]).
