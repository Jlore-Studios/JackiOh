Stinkyscape+ added Burn and Toxic ([[§6.3]]): counters that tick at the start of their controller's turn, Burn's hit met by Armor and dropping by `BURN_DECAY` after it (8, 5, 2, none), Toxic's piercing Armor and lasting ([[R3540001]]). The AI's evaluation read neither, so a hero on Toxic 3 scored as one without it and a Unit its next tick kills counted its whole body, a kill the AI might spend an attack on. Both counters are public ([[R3540005]]), so pricing them reads nothing hidden. No game rule changes: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** Over `AI_AFFLICTION_TICKS` (4) of the counter's controller's turn starts, each tick as [[R3540001]] runs it, Burn's hit first and then Toxic's ([[§4.4]]):
- *A Unit* (`evaluate::afflicted`, inside `unit_worth`'s Brittle share). An unspent Divine Shield negates the first hit, Burn's or Toxic's, and its keyword worth goes; Armor meets Burn's hit and Toxic pierces it; the unit's own cap holds both ([[R2179005]]); an Indestructible unit takes none and is read as it is. For each point the ticks will deal, up to its health, the unit loses `AI_EVAL.unit_affliction` (0.5) of a `health` point, and a lost shield costs that share of `keyword.divine_shield`. A unit the nth tick kills keeps `1 − unit_doomed / n` (`unit_doomed` 0.5) of what is left, or its Brittle share when that is smaller, since Brittle n crumbles at the same stage (B3.3). Its Plague Counters, its Death price and its turn hooks stay outside the share, as with Brittle. `kill_worth`, and through it the move order and the reply, read the new worth.
- *A hero* (`evaluate::coming_hero_ticks`). Each tick's hit is what `subsystems::projected_hero_damage` reads (Burn's through the hero's Armor, Toxic's piercing it; the divisors and caps for both), summed and held to all but 1 of the hero's life: a tick that kills is a loss the reply already plays out. `hero_side` loses `AI_EVAL.hero_affliction` (0.5) of what that life costs on the hero's concave curve, and the enemy's coming life also counts at the race's flat rate, `enemy_health` × `hero_affliction` × the life, so each point of it counts at least `hero_affliction` of what a point of damage dealt now counts in the race.

The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps all three at 0, and with no counter, or both unit weights at 0, or `hero_affliction` at 0, nothing is read: a board without a counter scores bit for bit as before.

**Monotone.** A doomed unit of worth V before the counters and health h is worth (V − a·h_w·h)·s, at most V − a·h_w·h (a = `unit_affliction`, h_w = `health`, s ≤ 1 its share). The same unit one health higher either survives, at V + h_w − a·h_w·D with D ≤ h the damage it takes, or dies at the same tick or a later one, at (V − a·h_w·h + h_w·(1 − a))·s′ with s′ ≥ s: above the first either way while a ≤ 1 (which `config.rs` asserts) and V − a·h_w·h is not below 0, as it is not for a unit whose other terms are not. A surviving unit gains h_w per health point. The first plan's formula, a doomed unit at its share alone with no damage taken off, scored a 4/8 on Toxic 2 above a 4/9 on Toxic 2 and was dropped.

**What is not counted.** A heal or a cleanse to come: a Vanilla's clearing ([[R3540004]]) or Midrange Menace's end-of-turn heal, so such a unit reads as more doomed than it is. The once-a-turn nullifier ([[R3550004]]). A doomed unit's attacks in `face_threat` and `pressure`: `face_threat` feeds the learned features, and moving it would change the network's inputs without a refit ([[R3535002]]). The closing term, which reads the enemy's life as it stands. A card dormant under a pile, which does not tick, is read with any counter it carries. The Death price stays whole, as with Brittle ([[R2424000]]).

**Approximation.** The line's EndTurn and the opponent's reply already play one tick a side, so on a finalist this term reads the ticks they do not; an open beam line, scored before them, reads all of them. The learned correction re-scores finalists on top of the hand evaluation, whose new terms reach them whole; its layout still leaves both counters out until a refit ([[R3535002]]).

**Tests.** `crates/ai/tests/ai/evaluate.rs`, `r3822000_burn_and_toxic`: an enemy unit's counters raise the seat's evaluation and its own lower it; a 4/4 on Burn 4, Toxic 2 and Toxic 1 keeps its doomed share at the first, second and fourth tick, and Burn 2 costs two points; worth falls strictly with damage across the survive/doomed boundary and never rises with Toxic; Divine Shield, Armor and Indestructible meet the ticks as [[R3540001]] says; the earlier of Burn's end and Brittle's counts; two tests play the engine's own turn starts and hold the predicted tick and the hero's lost life to what the engine did; a hero's Toxic weighs more than an equal Burn, both ways; at 3 life the ticks take at most 2; `GREEDY_EVAL` and zero weights read nothing; the redacted view prices them alike. `crates/ai/tests/ai/decide.rs`, `r3822000_afflicted_targets`: Reno against two Mr. Vanillas, one on Burn 4; the move order tries the kill the Burn will not make first, on either lane, and the AI attacks the plain Vanilla. The move-order test failed before the change (a tie kept lane order, the burning Vanilla first in lane 1); the decide test already passed, since the search's reply plays the tick, and stands as a guard. With the three weights at 0, every test but the greedy one and the decide guard fails.

**Gate.** The smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`, fixed seeds) on base `5ab816ceda`: with the three weights at 0, ai-vs-random 19 of 20 (17 needed), ai-vs-greedy 18 (10), hard-vs-easy 18 (16), as the plan read on `be9a6505ce`; at 0.5 each, 20, 18 and 18, with no search panic either way. The weights are untuned starts, to revisit after #3574's sweep of the set, as `AI_AFFLICTION_TICKS` is.

**Rejected.**
- The earlier plan's doomed formula without the damage taken off, which is not monotone in health.
- Multiplying the doomed share by the Brittle share: one end comes, at one stage, so the earlier counts.
- Reading the hero's raw `hero_armor_of` in place of `projected_hero_damage`, which also applies the divisors, the caps and the floor.
- Pricing a hero's ticks on the curve alone: an enemy's Toxic 2 would count less than 2 damage now does.
- A smooth doom curve in place of `1 − unit_doomed / n`, and separate horizons for Units and heroes: one more tuning number each, with no measure yet to set them.
- −win for a hero whose coming ticks reach 0: the reply plays the first such tick, and one further off can be healed.
- Teaching `features.rs` the counters, or discounting a doomed unit's `face_threat`: both move the learned network's inputs without a refit.
- Reusing `brittle_discount` itself for the doomed share, which leaves no switch to turn the new term off.
