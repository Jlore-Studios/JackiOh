title: Enchantments on copies and fusions
cards: "C+ #14, C+ #40"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/effects_enchant.rs
---
A copy, on the field or shuffled into a deck, carries its source's enchantments with its tuning. A Fuse unites its ingredients' enchantments, each once. A Transform makes a card with neither. The same enchantment is never on a card twice; two of one kind that differ are both kept.
