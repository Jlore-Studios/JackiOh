title: "An open Infinite Assault attempt continues on the UTC day it started"
cards: "issue #4581"
proven_in:
  - apps/web/src/assault/route.test.tsx
  - apps/web/src/assault/store.test.ts
---
An open Infinite Assault attempt is folded from its file on the UTC date and catalog version in that file, even after the device's clock has moved to another UTC day. The new day's deal is offered only after that attempt ends or is abandoned, and every attempt started after that, Try again's included, is dealt on the current UTC day and catalog version, from that day's record.

Hearthstone semantics: a started single-player encounter keeps the encounter that was dealt rather than changing its opponent during play.

Rejected: redealing an open attempt from the current UTC day, which would change its enemy and upgrades without a player action.
