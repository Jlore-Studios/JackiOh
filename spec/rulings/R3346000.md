Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). `cardPlayed.costPaid` is public on the wire and no sound read it: the play sting is sized by rarity ([[R669]]) and the landing thud by the Unit's stats ([[R702]]), so a 1-mana 20/20 and a 10-mana 1/1 sounded as they looked, not as they cost. Now every play the viewer can read also plays an impact, sized by the mana it cost and coloured by the card's type.

**The tiers.** `playTier` (`game/damageFeel.ts`, `PLAY_IMPACT_TIERS`) puts a cost of 0 to 2 in light, 3 to 5 in medium, 6 to 8 in heavy and 9 or more in massive; a cost that is not a finite number is light. Each tier has a weight, which the impact's length, pitch and voices grow with.

**The colour by type.** A Unit's impact is a body landing, from a tap to a ground-shaking slam; a Spell's is a charge and its release, from a flick to a thunderclap; a Field Spell's is the ground settling under it, from a hum to a rumble. A Trap fires with a snap (`trapSnap`), beside its sting, on the first firing only (a Field Trap's later firing is `again` and has the plain spell sound). The sounds are made in code (`sfx.ts`), end within half a second and sit on the effects bus, so they duck under a voice line like every effect ([[R669]]) and count against `SFX_MAX_VOICES`.

**Which plays sound.** Only a play whose card the viewer can read from the public catalog as a Unit, Spell or Field Spell. A face-down card is the sentinel and keeps its plain whoosh, so no sound names it ([[R203]]); a Trap's set, and any play that puts a card face-down (`formerId`), keeps its click, and a card cast as it is drawn keeps its own sting ([[R502]]).

**The other seat.** The other seat's impact and snap are heard at `PLAY_IMPACT_OPPONENT_GAIN` of the viewer's own, a step quieter, through a cue's own trim (`SfxParams.gain`), which the engine multiplies in beside the effect's gain.

**Beside the stings.** The impact starts with the card whoosh (`PLAY_IMPACT_DELAY_MS`), the rarity sting follows at `STING_DELAY_MS` and the voice line at `VOICE_DELAY_MS`, so a Mythic's three are heard as three. The Unit's thud stays the stats tier (`UNIT_SLAM`) and still lands `PAIR_OFFSET_MS` after the play's cues: a cheap, huge Unit gets a light impact and a giga thud, an expensive, tiny one a massive impact and a tiny thud. The idle flush's cap on distinct effects is raised to five for it.

**The board and the phone.** A heavy play shakes the board briefly (`FX_PLAY_HEAVY_TRAUMA`) and a massive one hard (`FX_PLAY_MASSIVE_TRAUMA`), through the effects layer's shake and under its intensity setting, for either seat's play the viewer can read and not for a Trap, a hidden card or a card cast as it is drawn. Under Reduce Motion the effects layer draws only keywords' still callouts ([[R2789000]]), so the shake is off there too. On a phone that can vibrate, a heavy or a massive play buzzes by its tier (`HAPTIC_PATTERNS`), for either seat's play that is neither hidden nor a set, behind the Vibration switch, never under Reduce Motion, and no closer than `HAPTIC_MIN_GAP_MS` to the last buzz; a lighter play keeps the viewer's own tick and the other seat's none.

`/dev/fx` plays every type at every tier, as the viewer's and as the other seat's, with its shake, and a trap's click and snap, for review by ear.
