The cost of targeting a card ([[R450]]) is read from the text it runs. A copier runs the text it copied ([[R1850000]]), and a fused card's text is every ingredient's, the stricter of their costs holding ([[R102]]), so a copier that copied a fused card asks that fused card's cost: a Tnack Trav that copied Nature Titan + Paul Allen's Ghost asks the Ghost's discards. Until this ruling the copy asked none, since the fused card's composed script declares no cost of its own and only the fused card itself was read ingredient by ingredient. A fused copier asks the cost of the text it copied, as [[R1850000]] says.
