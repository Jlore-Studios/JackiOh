The three keywords are keywords like any other in what a view shows of a card ([[R243]]): a Unit's
`keywords` on the field, and a hand card's view's `keywords` wherever they differ from its face's. A
face-up backrow card that has Durability carries its `keywords` always, as its controller's hand would
read them, since its count left is its own and no definition prints it.

Durability shows the count the card has **left** ([[R5236000]]), one entry for all its Activates,
`Durability n` with n its X less what its uses spent, as a Brittle count in force shows its count
([[R385]]): a Field Spell printing Durability 3 that was used once shows `Durability 2`, and one at 0
shows `Durability 0`. A card not yet used shows its printed X: on a backrow card still said, in a hand
(where nothing spends it) matching its face, so the hand view leaves it out. Each `ActivationView` of the card carries the same count
(`durability_left`, [[R5236001]]), and `counterChanged` with the counter `durability` reports each use.

Fragile shows as `Fragile`, and Unlucky as `Unlucky n`, with n its sum as tuning leaves it.
