The statistics page is the players' view of the cards. The Daily runs tab ([[R1935000]]) showed the daily super run's results there, which is the project's concern, not a player's.

- **What goes.** The tab, its button and its insights (how many runs passed, the streak, the job that failed most often), and the client's read of `GET /api/stats/ci`. A `?tab=ci` address opens the Cards tab, as any tab the page does not have does.
- **What stays.** `GET /api/stats/ci` and the server's reading of GitHub, as [[R1935000]] has them: a route a person or another service may call is never removed without asking one (CLAUDE.md rule 13), and only the tab was asked off.
