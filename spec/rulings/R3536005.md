An evolved card carries `evolved: true` on its views — evolved, or marked to enter the field evolved — wherever the viewer may read the card ([[R97]]), and its evolving emits `evolved`, named as `awakened` is ([[R1839002]]): both seats see the mark and the event. The face draws the Evolved badge by it (a star, `data-evolved`), with the inspect line "Evolved: its Evolve has happened, or happens as it enters the field; it evolves only once", and the client glows the card green while it wears it. Rejected: a mark only its controller sees, since the evolved card stands face-up on the field.
