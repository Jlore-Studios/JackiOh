S+ #94 PIP's "they have 1 turn" is a number the card declares (`count`, `count2`), so a Buff to it must lengthen the deadline: [[R3550006]]'s kill deadline falls at the end of the Unit's controller's `turns`th turn from now, the first of them as it always did. Each turn past the first is another round of both players' turns after [[R458]]'s "next turn" boundary. A Unit that kills meets it at once, whatever the count.

Rejected: reading the number as a count of the caster's turns, and fixing the deadline at one turn.
