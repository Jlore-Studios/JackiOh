Meditative #75 Ever Growing Tree's Activate Discovers one of `TREE_OFFERS` (2) different random non-token Field Spells of every set that ships, never the Tree itself or one already fused into it ([[R387]]), and fuses the one chosen into the Tree, the kept instance ([[R77]], [[R1087]]); on the Radiant face the pick goes in on its Radiant face ([[R469]]). With no Field Spell to offer the Activate does nothing.
