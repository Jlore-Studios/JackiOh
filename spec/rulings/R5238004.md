Felinors vs Humans #39 shuffles copies of Spells that cast on draw, and #99 shuffles enchanted cards; `shuffleRandomFromCatalog` carried enchantments, `shuffleInto` did not.

**The rule.** `shuffle_into` takes `enchantments`: each is put on each fresh card it shuffles, after [[R57]]'s riders (so a copy's own enchantments stay beside them, each once), before it goes into the library. They ride the card as every enchantment does: Cast on draw casts it as it is drawn ([[§2.4]], [[R58]]). A card the full library refuses ([[R80]]) is judged as any shuffle's is.
