S+ #102 Cosmic Bard's "Combo: Draw 2 Units" is the draw of a kind of card from the deck, so it takes the two topmost Units, top first, and draws no random number ([[R801]]); fewer Units draw fewer, and none draws nothing and causes no fatigue ([[R129]]); each is a named draw of its own ([[R135]]). The Combo is one card played earlier this turn ([[§6.2]]). The Radiant face's "set both their costs to the cheapest drawn" reads each drawn Unit's price as it stood in the deck, as a filter reads it ([[R65]], [[R66]]: an X-cost Unit's is 0, and no discount of the player's applies), takes the least, and sets every drawn Unit that is in the hand to it with a `costOverride`; a cost change a card carries is taken off the override, so each card ends at that one price. A Unit the hand cap burned or a cast on draw took still counts toward the least and is given nothing.

Rejected: random picks, as Small Time Recruits draws its three ([[R1061]], its brief asking for different random cards); reading the price in the hand, where a discount of the player's would be counted twice once it applies on top of the set price; and the printed cost, which ignores what the deck already changed.
