[[R1563006]] gives Meditative+ #20 KY's Competition's question sequence `KY_COMPETITION_SECONDS` (60) from its first question, a window its host keeps. A daily game's host keeps no window of seconds between two requests a day apart, and a sequence's player may answer one question tonight and the next tomorrow. So in a daily game a sequence arms no window of its own: its questions are prompts for one player inside one move, which keep that move's hold ([[R3727001]]), and the move's deadline is the sequence's window. The suggestion is the issue owner's (#3727). It edits [[R1563006]] for daily games only; a live match and practice keep the 60 seconds.
