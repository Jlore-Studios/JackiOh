FvH #93 Twin Tyrant's Cry deals its damage to two different random enemies, the enemy hero or standing enemy Units a harmful effect may reach ([[R60]]), one hit each; with fewer standing, fewer are hit. The draw of the pair is one luck-based roll ([[R1438]]): it shuffles the enemies and takes the first two, and Lucky X (the card's own, the Radiant face's printed Lucky 1 among it, plus its controller's Luck, [[R987]]) draws X more pairs and keeps the one that would destroy more enemies, the earlier draw on a tie, as [[R3567005]] ruled Snom Bunny's dearer permanent. A hit would destroy a Unit when its Health and Armor through the layers come to no more than the damage and it has no Divine Shield, and the enemy hero when its Health and Armor do. A net luck below 0 keeps the worst ([[R5236020]]). Both faces run the one script, so a base face given Lucky rolls the same way, and with no luck it is the one plain draw. The pair is drawn as the Cry's list reaches it and kept, so a pause after the first hit resumes over the same two.

Rejected: Lucky with no roll; a better pair as the one that hits more Units over the hero (a hit on the hero is no worse a hit).
