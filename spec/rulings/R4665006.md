Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VII: "Legacy: Glowy Jelly Bean: new cost (2); new text: make a card in your hand radiant, it costs (1) more; new radiant text: make a card in your hand radiant". A Legacy card is a frozen copy no Core patch moves ([[R2195003]]); this patch moves the copy itself and leaves Core #26 as it is. TG #50 costs (2), from (3). Its base face makes the chosen hand card Radiant and then raises that card's `costMod` by the declared `surcharge` (1, [[R386]]), as #72 Reminisce prices a chosen hand card ([[§6.3]] Cost); its Radiant face picks one card, not two, and adds no surcharge. Replays of the versions through v0.6.12b fold under `script_v0_6_12b`, the copy as it was ([[R767000]]).
