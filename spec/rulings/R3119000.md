The client's 404 panel (`main.tsx` `NotFound`) and the page Cloudflare serves for a path nothing routes (the build's `404.html`) are built from what the client already ships: the catalog's cards and their voice lines, Glitch's glyphs and the brand's card back. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**The lost card.** One of seven cards that suit a missing page (Wrong-House Attacker, Echoes of the Forgotten, Voidwalker, Paul Allen's Ghost, Ceaseless Void, Empty Plot, KY's Empty Notebook), picked by a hash of the path, so one wrong link always shows the same card. Glitch's blob seeps from behind it, its flavour is printed under the row, and a tap says its first voice line through the audio engine, so muting and the voice setting hold ([[R630]]).

**The glitched path.** The path the panel names is drawn in Glitch's glyphs, seeded by the path, and settles one character at a time from the left; a pointer over it glitches it again. The element's words are the path throughout, so a reader and a copy get it as it is; the glyphs are drawn over it.

**Three ways out.** Three face-down card backs deal in and turn over into choice cards naming Play, Practice and the Almanac. A tap on a face-down card turns it over and the next follows it; a mouse over it or keyboard focus turns it over first, so one click follows. "Back to the start" stays below them as before.

**The static page.** Superseded by [[R3127000]]: the build writes it from the client's page, and its hand is three card faces.

**Still.** Under Reduce motion ([[R200]]) the cards lie dealt and face up and the path is settled. Both pages hold the one-screen rule ([[R1581000]]) and the tap areas ([[R2122000]]), and the shell panel's frame and logo are unchanged ([[R2108000]]).

Hearthstone semantics: an error screen in a card game's own look, which a player can still play with for a moment.

Rejected: the player's own card back (a back is a deck's loadout, not the account's, and a 404 reads no deck); a canvas particle burst (the fx director is the board's, so a CSS spark stands in); a lost card at random (one link would show a different card on every visit).
