The Drone Interceptor or Corvette an Assembler summons enters its controller's side as every summon with no named lane does ([[R64]]): the leftmost open unit zone, else the leftmost empty Locked one ([[R688]]), and being a Flying Unit, with its unit row full the leftmost open backrow zone ([[R2248000]]); with no zone in either row nothing is summoned. It then makes one forced attack on the chosen Unit ([[R53]]), the only attack it makes, so it has no summoning sickness, and whatever strikes back does (Ranged drones are not struck). The Radiant face summons the Radiant drone. S+ #160 Icarus's Radi is no Flying Unit, so it enters a unit zone alone, and its Cry is triggered as if it was played ([[R411]]). An Activate that would summon into no zone is not offered ([[R430]]): Icarus's with no unit zone, an Assembler's with no zone in either row or no enemy Unit; the drone is not given Taunt-first targeting, which S #140 Rush Token Launcher's text asks for and these cards' does not.
