A board restore rebuilds a side as its snapshot holds it, a Dormant card awake again if the snapshot holds it awake ([[R4454002]]), so once a `rolledBack` names a side, the cards that stood Dormant there as the step began are no longer the Dormant cards the step began with. The fuzz monitor's I19 kept them marked until the next card's hook, trap or turn edge and allowed only the restore's own `bounced`, `controlChanged` and `radiantSet`, so the handicapped wave's seed 1573 failed on a Flak activation whose first damage killed S+ #84 Bogos Binted, whose Death restored its side, and whose next damage hit a Unit the snapshot held awake. I19 now releases a restored side's marks for the rest of the step: any event may name them, and their record is not compared. One the snapshot holds Dormant is marked again from the next step, as every Dormant card is. A card reached before the restore, or on a side no restore names, is still a finding. Rejected: reading the snapshot to keep the cards it holds Dormant marked, which the monitor cannot see once the restore has run and which the next step marks anyway.
