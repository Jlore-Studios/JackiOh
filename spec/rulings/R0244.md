Hearthstone gives the player going second The Coin after the mulligan, and the source now says so too (JackiOh_Mechanics.md, at the user's request of 2026-09-24), without fixing the moment exactly. Player 2, who takes the second turn, is dealt one ([[§7]]) once both mulligans are answered and before [[§2.1]] step 4's start-of-game effects, as the last card of their hand: never offered in, returned by or redrawn for a mulligan ([[R9]]). It is an add to hand, not a draw ([[§6.3]]): `addedToHand` alone, #100's draw counter unmoved ([[R55]]), and a full hand would burn it to the graveyard like any card ([[§2.4]], [[R4]]), though no Core opening hand gets near one (five cards at most, [[R182]]). The seat going second gets it whatever its handicap: a handicap is a tier's resources ([[R180]]), and The Coin answers going second, so a Medium or Hard AI seated second holds five opening cards and The Coin, a human seated second against an AI gets it the same way, and the seat going first gets none at any tier. Setup deals it from the seed and the decks alone, with no rng draw, so a game still folds exactly from `(seed, decks, handicaps, log)` ([[§9.3]], [[R187]]). It changes neither the turn order nor the cap: Player 1 still takes the first of the 60 player-turns ([[R389]]) and Player 2 the last ([[§2.5]], [[R2]]). A Coin in hand is always an affordable play, so, like any 0-cost card, it keeps [[R82]] from ending its holder's turn by itself
