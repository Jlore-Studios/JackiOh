C+ #10's Reborn is a granted keyword ([[§10.4]]), recorded even on a Unit that already has Reborn: while the printed Reborn stands it changes nothing (keywords are a set, so the Unit still returns once, at 1 health, without Reborn), but a later Vanilla, which removes printed keywords and keeps granted ones, leaves it the granted Reborn
