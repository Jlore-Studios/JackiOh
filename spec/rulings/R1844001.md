- Decides: the Pareto hint (issue #1844, Stinkyscape's M10)
- Spec: §6.3 Pareto hint, §10.1, §10.8

"An AI will help you make the Pareto Optimal decisions" is a hint: while it is on, the player's own view marks each hand card that ME-PARETO's judge (Meditative #71, [[R1125]]) would call optimal to play now, `paretoOptimal: true`, and the client lights those cards. The `pareto_hint` verb (#147 IN33DH3's Cry, "for the rest of your turn") turns it on for its caster's turn running, on their own turn only, until that turn's cleanup (`paretoHint`, the turn); on the opponent's turn it does nothing. A card with `hintsPlays` (#147.2 Wardrum Blob's aura, "always") turns it on during each of its controller's turns while it acts on their field, and stores nothing.

It covers plays only, never an attack, a position, an activation or the end of the turn: each card the player may play now is scored as the judge scores a play, on their concealed copy of the board ([[R222]], [[R1714000]]), and every card whose score is at least every other's carries the mark. So ties share it, hidden cards never move it, and a hinted play never sets off the opponent's Pareto Optimality. It is shown only while the player may play, their main phase with nothing open, and only on their own hand: the key is absent otherwise, never `false` and never on the opponent's cards, the rules [[R195]]'s glow and [[R667]]'s warning follow. A view of that seat carries it wherever it goes, to a watcher of the seat or a Duos teammate, as it carries the hand. It is the view's alone, so no state hash moves for the marks.

Rejected: the autopilot's `choose` ([[R1183003]]), which would also light an attack or End turn and is not the judge "Pareto Optimal" names on Meditative #71.
