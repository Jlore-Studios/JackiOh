"When your opponent casts a beneficial Spell on themselves or a permanent they control" is a Spell announced ([[§10.5]]'s step 3a) whose chosen targets, one at least, are all its player's own hero or cards they control on the field (`query::beneficial_on_own_side`); a Spell that chose no target is not beneficial, and a card being set face-down announces no Spell. Wormhole answers it as a Trap: the Spell is countered into its hand, its owner becoming the Trap's owner ([[R448]]), and the Trap's owner casts it ([[R70]]) with `friendsOnly`. Every target pick of that cast, and every target its text asks for while it is cast, is the caster's own hero or a card they control on the field (a pick from a hand, a pile or of a zone or a mode is no target and is offered as it is); the caster is asked, so `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal and the prompt offer only those. A declaration with none of them is passed, as a declaration with no legal option is ([[R90]]), and the Spell resolves with what it has. The Radiant face's "its Radiant version" makes the countered card Radiant before the cast, so it resolves Radiant and lands Radiant in its new owner's graveyard.

Rejected: aiming the cast in its caster's favour when it can ([[R656]]'s `prefer_friends`), which would let it fall back on the enemy's side.
