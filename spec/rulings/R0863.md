A lost guess's penalty, and every penalty of a discard, falls on whoever plays the card — or on its owner when it is discarded — even when that player owns the secret being guessed. The sides are the player's own: Greed taxes their own next refresh and discards at random from their own hand, Attack hits their own hero and each of their own Units, and Defend heals and armors the opponent's side instead.
