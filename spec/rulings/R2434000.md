Issue #2434 remade the five lessons with one subject each, and three of the subjects moved: lesson 2 now teaches Cries and keywords, lesson 3 spells, and lesson 4 Field Spells and Traps. An id that named its old subject would mislead, and reusing `spells` or `traps` for a lesson in another place would make an id on the account ([[R320]]) mean two lessons. So the three lessons between the first and the last take new ids, `keywords`, `casting` and `backrow`, and `basics` and `finale` keep theirs. An id of the lessons before #2434 reads as the lesson now in its place on the path: `spells` as `keywords` (lesson 2), `traps` as `casting` (lesson 3) and `advanced` as `backrow` (lesson 4). A player who won the old lesson n has won lesson n: nothing won is un-won, and the next lesson stays open ([[R294]]). The reading is the client's (`EARLIER_LESSON_IDS`, `currentLessonId` in `lessons.ts`), done wherever progress is read, the device's or the account's, and merged as ever ([[R321]]). The server keeps any slug ([[R320]]) and needs no change: an account that lists an earlier id lacks nothing the device would send for it, and the device sends the new id beside it once, the merge never removing either.

Hearthstone semantics: a reworked encounter keeps the player's progress through it.

Rejected:

- Keeping the old ids on lessons whose subjects moved, which would name lesson 3 "traps" and lesson 2 "spells".
- Mapping the old ids by subject, which would mark the old lesson 2's winner as having won lesson 3 (spells) and leave lesson 2 open before it.
