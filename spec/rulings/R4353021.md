Core #2 Speciesist since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V: both faces' Cry ends "You have {mana} less mana next turn." It is the self side of #21 Hinder's modifier, M #18 Expedition12's: `next_turn_mana` of minus the declared `mana` (1 on both faces, [[R386]], read by the Cry alone, so a Buff or Nerf moves it only until the Cry has resolved, [[R1748000]]) on the Cry's controller, whose next refresh gives that much less, never below 0, and stacks with any other such loss ([[§2.3]]). The loss is a clause of the Cry, so it comes with every Cry that runs: when the base face's declared enemy non-Human is gone or never was, the destroy fizzles and the loss still comes, as the rest of a Cry does after a fizzled clause ([[R90]], [[§8]]'s conventions), as Hearthstone's Overload comes with a play whatever its Battlecry finds; a Cry that runs twice loses twice; and a Speciesist summoned rather than played runs no Cry and loses nothing. Rejected: losing the mana only when something is destroyed, which the designer's text, a sentence of its own after the destroy, does not say. TG #41 Legacy: Speciesist, frozen against later Core patches, does not print it ([[R2195003]]). Through v0.6.8b the Cry lost nothing, which `script_v0_6_8b` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
