The keyword row of a Nerf and of a Buff ([[§6.3]], [[R386]]) gains two-way changes. A Buff's row adds a keyword the card lacks, as before (a Unit from [[R21]]'s pool, a Field Spell Indestructible or Immutable, [[R2436000]], a damage-dealing Spell or Field Spell Pierce or Lifesteal, [[R1748001]]), and now Flashback to a card of any type that lacks it (`TUNE_BUFF_ANY_KEYWORDS`); or it removes Can't attack or Can't Defend the card has of its own (`TUNE_BUFF_REMOVES`). A Nerf's row removes a keyword the card has of its own, never a harmful one: Can't attack, Brittle, Untributable, Can't Defend and Crater (`TUNE_HARMFUL_KEYWORDS`); or it adds Crater to a Unit, a Field Spell, a Trap or a Field Trap that lacks it while that card is in a hand (`TUNE_NERF_HAND_KEYWORDS`). A Nerf never adds Can't attack or Can't Defend, and nothing adds a keyword to a Vanilla card. One draw picks the change uniformly among the row's removals and additions, removals first ([[R442]]); a card a multiplier tunes more than once keeps to the first draw's way for its later picks ([[R1160]]), so a row with only one way draws as it did. The owner settled the request's two conflicting lines on Can't attack this way: a Buff removes it and a Nerf never adds it, and the same holds for Can't Defend ([[R2657000]]). This is the owner's balance pass (issue #2657).
