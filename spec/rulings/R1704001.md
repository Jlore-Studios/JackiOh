Since issue #1704 Classic #52 Final Gambit cancels the hit instead of redirecting it. At [[§4.4]] step 4a, when a hit alone would bring its controller's hero to 0 or less, after Armor, multipliers and caps ([[R44]]), the trap fires (`trapFired`) and the hit deals nothing: no `damage`, no `redirected` and no new instance, so nothing that answers damage sees it. The heal and the draw follow as its owed step, as they followed the redirect. The `lethalHit` replacement's `instead: { cancel: true }` answers only for its own hero, as the redirect to the enemy hero did, and it is spent as any Trap that fires is ([[R460]]).

A card that cancels and stays on the field (a Final Gambit fused into C+ #74's Field Trap) cancels one hit a turn. The engine notes the turn on the card as it fires (`CANCELLED_MEMORY_KEY`), and a later lethal hit that turn lands. With no limit, the follow-up's draws from an empty deck would each be cancelled and owe another follow-up, without end. Hearthstone's Ice Block prevents fatal damage in the same way and is spent by it.

Through v0.4.0f the hit was redirected to the enemy hero, and the step did nothing once that hit had left the enemy hero at 0 or less ([[R216]]); `script_v0_4_0f` keeps that for those versions' replays ([[R767000]]).
