A delayed destroy of a unit is keyed to its stay on the field ([[R174]]) and Indestructible ignores it; a scope reads the Units standing then. "This turn" is the running turn; "your next turn" skips the current one. A rest-of-game effect is the player's own (no `self`), runs among the delayed effects by `seq`, once per turn, and stacks.
