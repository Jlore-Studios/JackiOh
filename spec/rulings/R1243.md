Meditative #95's sixth entry, "Summon 3 random Acclaimed cards", makes `CHAOS_MED_ACCLAIMED` (3) independent picks ([[R60]]) of the non-token Acclaimed permanents (Units, Field Spells, Traps and Field Traps) of every set that ships ([[R380]]): C #80 and C+ #37, and Meditative #49 once its set ships ([[R1420]]). Each is summoned per [[R64]], a Trap face-down, with no Cry and no Tribute paid ([[R1]]). The Acclaimed tokens, Meditative #49.1 to #49.3, are not in the pool ([[§5.1]]).
