TG #31's "your units attack 3 times" counts each of its controller's Units apart, not attacks among them all: every combat a Unit fights as the attacker while this acts on the field, across turns, from this card's arrival on (the designer's "start tracking after this is played"). A forced attack counts ([[R53]]); an attack called off before it fought does not ([[R44]]). After the combat of its third (Radiant: second) attack closes, through [[R2199000]]'s hook, a Unit still on the field becomes Radiant ([[§5.2]]: the base layer swaps, damage and buffs stay, no Cry, [[R22]], [[R74]]); one that died in it does not, and one already Radiant is counted and unchanged. A Unit that left the field and came back, a bounce or a Reborn, is a new arrival and starts again ([[R174]]). The counts are this card's own `memory` ([[§10.1]]): it leaving the field resets them ([[R78]]), and two Level Ups count apart. A stolen Unit counts for whoever controls it as it attacks ([[R171]]).
