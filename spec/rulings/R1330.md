Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A portrait is something to look at, like a card (`docs/meditative-set.md`, MN02). A hold on either hero opens that hero's inspect view: a touch's or a mouse's hold on its art, a right-click on it, or the context-menu key or Shift+F10 on it. The art is a button named for the hero ("Emotes for Mr. Vanilla, your hero; hold, or Shift+F10, to inspect"), laid over the oval and given no test id, so that a drag or a click still resolves to the hero. A click on the hero, and Enter or Space on the button, hang the hero's menu off the portrait instead ([[R1449]], which since 2026-10-11 gives the click back to the menu: until then a click, a touch's long-press and Enter or Space all opened the view).

A legal hero takes the click as a target, as before ([[R643]]): the hero is then the one tab stop, a hold on it opens nothing, and with a card or an attacker selected neither a click nor a hold on a hero that is not a target opens anything.

The view is a dialog over a dim scrim, drawn like a card's touch sheet. It shows the portrait large ([[R1332]]), the portrait card's printed name, its title (the roster's own line, which the deck builder's picker shows too), its flavour line ([[R660]]), and the hero's health, clamped at 0 as the badge is, and its Armor, 0 included, both read from the view. It holds the menu the click hangs off the portrait: your emote menu, or the opponent's one-item Mute emotes menu ([[R643]]), drawn inside it, wrapped to fit it, which fits a 360 px screen. A hold on a portrait whose menu is open turns that menu into the view.

It closes on its Close button, on a press on the dim around it, on Escape, on a pick, on the mute, on the start of targeting, on a hotseat hand-over and at the end of the match. Focus goes to Close when it opens and returns to the portrait's button when it closes. A press inside the view closes nothing, and the click that ends a hold neither closes the view it opened nor opens the menu.

A portrait and its words are public ([[R642]]): the view reads the portrait's id and the numbers the viewer's own `PlayerView` carries, and nothing hidden.
