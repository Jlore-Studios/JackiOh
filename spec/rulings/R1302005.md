title: "A set track stops at its last level: XP past it lands but reaches no level (issue #1302)"
cards: "§14.7, §14.11"
proven_in:

  - crates/server/tests/api/rewards.rs

A set track has `SET_TRACK_LEVELS` levels ([[§14.7]]), so its last level is the last it
reaches and the last that pays: XP that would carry it past the level curve's total for
`SET_TRACK_LEVELS` leaves it there, and no level beyond it is reached, written or paid
([[§14.11]]). The XP still lands, since every active track gains all of a game's XP: the
track's total keeps counting the account's play, and its row's level stays the last. A track
on its last level reads no next level: `GET /api/tracks` reports its `xpToNext` and its
`nextRewards` as `null`. It stays active, so the XP of later games keeps landing on it, and,
being past `SET_TRACK_UNLOCK_LEVEL`, it keeps unlocking older tracks as [[R1302003]] rules.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's reward track keeps counting XP after its last numbered
level; its bonus levels past it are Hearthstone's own, and the spec gives a track a fixed
count of levels, so none follows the last. Keeping the XP rather than dropping it keeps
"each gaining all of it" true and loses nothing a later table could read.
