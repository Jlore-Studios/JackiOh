"Destroy all Field Spells" takes the Field Spells acting in the backrow zones it names (the top of a backrow pile, and a carrier beneath the Unit standing on it, [[R446]]: C+ #33 Ivory Tower while the Unit stacked onto it resolves, [[R653]]; a Tower that has fused a Unit is a Field Spell destroyed and counted like any, [[R418]]); an Animated Field Spell standing in a unit zone is a Unit there ([[R383]]), so it is neither destroyed nor counted, and it is swept again once it is back in its backrow. Traps, Field Traps and Indestructible Field Spells are not destroyed and not counted
