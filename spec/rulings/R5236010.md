**Fragile** is a Unit keyword, `{ "kind": "Fragile" }`: any damage that lowers the Unit's Health
destroys it, as if every source were Poisonous. It is read at [[§4.4]] step 7, beside Poisonous, as its
twin on the target's side: when the target is a Unit with Fragile (through the layers, so an aura's or a
grant's counts) and the hit dealt 1 or more, the Unit is marked destroyed, and the state check collects
it ([[§4.5]]). Its `destroyed` names Fragile as the route, unless something recorded another first
([[R2783001]]); a source, when there is one, is credited with the kill ([[R42]]).

- **With or without a source.** Unlike Poisonous ([[R1848002]]), Fragile asks nothing of the source: a
  hit from no card, and a Burn or Toxic tick ([[R3540001]]), destroy a Fragile Unit they damage.
- **A hit dealing 0 does not.** Divine Shield, an immunity, or Armor taking the hit whole stops it before
  step 5, so no damage instance lands and nothing lowers the Unit's Health.
- **Indestructible survives it**, as it survives a Poisonous hit: the mark destroys only a Unit that can
  be destroyed ([[R69]]).
- A Unit something had already killed before the hit dies as it would have, its credit unchanged
  ([[R42]]).

The combat forecasts read it too: a Fragile defender falls to any hit that damages it, and a Fragile
attacker falls first to a First Strike defender that damages it. How a Nerf and a Buff meet it is
[[R5236011]]'s.
