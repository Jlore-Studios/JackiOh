A Flying Unit standing in the backrow is a Unit for every rule ([[R1081000]]), and a count of permanents counts it once. The AI's evaluation (`material`) follows that count: the unit walk prices every Unit on the field, `units_on_field_of` reading the standing Units after their unit zones' and their carriers', so a Unit standing in the backrow ([[§3.2]]) is priced there as the Unit it is. The backrow walk behind it skips every card `is_standing_unit` already priced, behind the `standing_unit_priced_once` switch: on in `AI_EVAL`, off in the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL`, which keeps the old double price, as it did.

A Dormant Starship standing in the backrow ([[R2249000]]) counts its slumber price alone ([[R2380000]]), never a backrow price on top, as every Unit standing there counts its own price once. An Animated card that is standing as a Unit at the moment, a standing Unit granted Animated, counts its unit price alone too, with no `animated_share` top-up; an Animated backrow card that is no standing Unit keeps its backrow price plus `animated_share` as before ([[R2280000]]). A face-down Trap, a face-up Field Spell and a set card's grade counter in the backrow are priced as before.

**Rejected.** Keeping the backrow price, or the `animated_share` top-up, on top of the unit price: the rules make the standing card the same Unit wherever it stands, so the extra points pulled the search toward parking its flyers in zones its Traps and Field Spells need.
