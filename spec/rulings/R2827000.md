[[§13.7]] had an AI seat pass only cards it could not play itself, so it kept a card it could play
weakly when its teammate won with it, the tribe piece that completes its partner's combo played for
little on its own full board. This note admits own-playable cards to this turn's passes.

- **Candidacy**: a card the seat can play this turn is a pass candidate like any other, when its
  teammate can play it this turn: a round seat that has not ended, affording the card priced in its
  own duel ([[R1168000]]), with a zone for it. The free-pass next-turn list is unchanged.
- **The own-use hurdle**: beside [[R1723000]]'s worth-it check, the teammate's best bounded
  follow-up with the card ([[R1127000]]'s score, also tried after the teammate's own best play) must
  beat the seat's own best bounded use of that same card by more than the pass's price:
  `DUOS_PASS_MARGIN_PER_COST` per crystal of the team's `pass_cost`, plus `DUOS_PASS_MIN_GAIN`. The
  seat's own use is the best of `evaluate` over at most `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` legal `Play`
  actions of that card on the seat's own probe, the probe's own value when none applies. A card the
  seat cannot play faces no hurdle but [[R1723000]]'s.
- **Unchanged**: [[R1127000]]'s ranking, the worth-it check, the scoring panic's fallback to the cost
  order with every card kept, the bounds (at most `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` `reduce` calls per card
  on each side), and the fixed-seed probes, which read no hidden opponent card and no caller stream
  ([[R185]]).

Rejected: comparing hand values only, which never prices the seat's own play; and replacing the
worth-it check with the own-use hurdle, which would pass a card the teammate does not need beyond
its baseline.
