[[§13.7]] had an AI seat pass only cards it could not play itself, so it kept a card it could play
weakly when its teammate won with it, the tribe piece that completes its partner's combo played for
little on its own full board. This note admits own-playable cards to this turn's passes.

- **Candidacy**: a card the seat can play this turn is a pass candidate like any other, when its
  teammate can play it this turn: a round seat that has not ended, affording the card priced in its
  own duel ([[R1168000]]), with a zone for it. The free-pass next-turn list is unchanged.
- **The own-use hurdle**: beside [[R1723000]]'s worth-it check, the pass must also beat the seat's
  own play of the card, gain against gain: [[R1723000]]'s gain (the teammate's value with the card
  over its best without it, less the card's worth in the seat's hand, `DUOS_PASS_MARGIN_PER_COST`
  per crystal of the team's `pass_cost` and `DUOS_PASS_MIN_GAIN`) less what the seat's own play
  gains it must be above 0. The seat's own gain is the best of `evaluate` over at most
  `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` legal `Play` actions of that card on the seat's own probe, less the
  probe's own value, which still counts the card in hand; 0 when no play applies. The lower of the
  two rules, so an own play that loses the seat value leaves [[R1723000]]'s check alone. Since both
  gains have the card's hand worth off them, a card the teammate uses no better than the seat is
  kept by the pass's price and `DUOS_PASS_MIN_GAIN`.
- **Unchanged**: [[R1127000]]'s ranking, the worth-it check, the scoring panic's fallback to the cost
  order with every card kept, the bounds (at most `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` `reduce` calls per card
  on each side), and the fixed-seed probes, which read no hidden opponent card and no caller stream
  ([[R185]]).

Rejected: comparing the teammate's score with the card against the seat's score after its own play,
since the two are `evaluate`'s whole-position scores of two different duels and would weigh which
duel goes better rather than what the card does in each; comparing the teammate's gain with the
seat's without taking the card's hand worth off the pass's side, which would pass a card the
teammate uses exactly as well as the seat; comparing hand values only, which never prices the seat's
own play; and replacing the worth-it check with the own-use hurdle, which would pass a card the
teammate does not need beyond its baseline.
