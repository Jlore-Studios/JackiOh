A steal that reaches a card on the field but has nowhere to put it on the thief's side (no free zone in its
row, [[R15]]; no fit for a GIGA, [[R3546003]]) destroys it where it stands instead, and nothing prevents
that destroy ([[§6.3]] Steal). Every steal of a card on the field does so: `steal`, `stealAll`, Allure's
delayed steal ([[R963]], [[R1045000]]), the random-permanent steals and the Activates that steal. The card
is marked destroyed as any destroy marks it, so [[R59]]'s state check collects it with whatever else the
effect destroyed, in [[R68]]'s order, and `stealAll` takes what fits before the rest die. At that check
([[§4.5]] step 1) Indestructible drops no mark of it and switches nothing ([[R46]]), Untributable does not
apply because it is no Sacrifice ([[R1220]]), and no "would die" replacement is offered it (C #14
Shadowstep's Radiant flicker), as [[R462]] keeps a Sacrifice out: a flicker would keep it on the field.
A destroy-to-exile aura (S #105 Disintegrex) still exiles it instead ([[R1849002]]), since that aura
replaces the destroy rather than protecting the card. It dies in full either way, exiled or not ([[R4207000]]):
its Death fires and Reborn returns it, on its own side, and, unless so exiled, it goes to the graveyard of the
player who controlled it, as theirs
([[R2618000]]), through any graveyard redirect. Nothing changed sides, so there is no `controlChanged`, no
[[R171]] entry and no step of a "steal an enemy card" quest ([[R1854002]]); the log shows the stealing
card, then `destroyed`.

A card no steal reaches is neither taken nor destroyed: one off the field ([[R12]]'s steal from a hand or
a deck goes to the thief's hand as before), Dormant ([[R1839000]]), beneath a Stack ([[R13]]) or already
the thief's ([[R76]]). Giving control the other way ([[R1423]]: M #6 Me no Likey, Meditative #30 Fickle
E-Kitten) still leaves a card the receiver has no room for where it is, its price unpaid ([[R803]]), and
"take into your hand" is no steal of the field ([[R1185003]]). A Radiant rider that makes the stolen card
Radiant (#49 Snom Bunny Mind Control, M #97.4 Mega Church, now `steal`'s `radiant`) lands only on a card
the steal took, as K-Pop Fanatic's does ([[R282]]), never on one destroyed for want of room. C #39 Outbreak's "otherwise, draw"
still draws nothing, its condition having held. It is Hearthstone's Mind Control on a full board, which
destroys the minion and fires its Deathrattle.

Until issue #4205 such a card stayed with its controller, except under Allure (`otherwise: destroy`), whose
ordinary destroy an Indestructible card survived; a replay of a game where one did folds to another state
and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). Rejected: an `otherwise` per card, which left most
steals a dead card on a full board; an ordinary destroy, which Indestructible and a "would die"
replacement would stop; destroying the card at once inside the steal, which would run its Death mid-effect
and split `stealAll`'s deaths across checks ([[R59]]); offering it to "would die" replacements; and a new
event, which the log does not need.
