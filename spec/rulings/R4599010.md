[[R4508001]] lets a board restore's `controlChanged`, `bounced` and `radiantSet` reach an immune Unit: the restore rebuilds a side whole. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 9357, C+ #35 Rollback's restore ([[R419]]) also exiled S+ #114 Reign, immune, which stood where the snapshot held nothing, and I21 named the Spell.

**The restore takes the side as it stands.** After a `rolledBack` in a Spell's run, an `exiled` of an immune Unit is the restore's, as the other board moves of [[R4508001]] are.
