A board restore rebuilds a side as its snapshot holds it, so a Unit that is Immune to Spells now may stand there as it was before it was immune. The fuzz monitor's I21 allowed the restore's own changes ([[R4508001]]) but kept judging the Units its side began the step with. In the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 8698 C+ #35 Rollback restored both sides to three turns before, and S+ #85 Body Snatch then targeted and transformed an S #115 Scornboi that had been immune as the step began and was not in the snapshot, rightly, as the engine read it no longer immune. I21 named the transform as a reach.
**What I21 allows.** Once a `rolledBack` names a side, I21 no longer judges, for the rest of the step, a Unit that stood immune on it as the step began and is gone from the field by the step's end, as I19 releases a restored side's Dormant cards ([[R4524001]]): gone, it is judged by its keywords as the step began, which the restore may have rebuilt without the immunity, as a change of hands may ([[R4599009]]). A Unit still on the field as the step ends is judged as it then stands, as any other: one the restore left immune is still reached by a Spell's later change, which [[R4508001]]'s restore window lets through only as its own move. A Unit on a side no restore names is still judged.
Rejected: releasing every Unit of a restored side, which let a Spell's buff after its own restore reach a Unit that stood immune before and after it; and reading the snapshot to keep the Units it holds immune marked, which the monitor cannot see once the restore has run.
