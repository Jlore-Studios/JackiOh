Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: Meltdown, "No longer heals when Frozen". Both faces of S+ #90 lose "When this is Frozen, fully heal it" and the trigger on the Frozen mark [[R3550002]] gave it, so a freeze that lands on it, a re-freeze included, leaves its damage where it was; "When this attacks" ([[R3559002]]) and its keywords are unchanged. No card prints the [[§6.2]] row since; the engine keeps it, for the replays. Replays of the versions before this patch fold under `script_v0_6_13h`, which heals it to full on every freeze as they did ([[R767000]]).
