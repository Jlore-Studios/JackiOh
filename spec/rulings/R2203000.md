[[R732000]], [[R1186000]] and [[R1885000]] keep the AI's shadow ban ([[R186]], `SHADOW_BAN` in `crates/ai/src/shadow_ban.rs`) to the Meditative, Meditative+ and Stinkyscape sets. The Training Grounds set joins them, held as the Meditative sets are to half of its non-token cards (30 of its 60; its token #39.1 is no card a deck holds): a sweep of record may ban up to half the set, and `cargo jackioh sweep --write` refuses reports of record that ban more. Each set keeps its own ceiling, and no card of an earlier set is shadow-banned again.

So the sweep of record over the set, run with the set previewed ([[R1420]]) before it ships, writes its bans, and the set ships ungated: it is not listed in [[R1390]]'s `AI_DECK_GATED_SETS`, so the AI deals itself Training Grounds cards from the release, less the bans, as it dealt Meditative+ cards after #1186. Since the ban may now hold Training Grounds cards, `sweep --due` also names the watched Training Grounds cards when the AI's generation moves.
