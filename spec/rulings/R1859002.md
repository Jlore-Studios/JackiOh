Stinkyscape #106 Warpfield Amplifier: "When launched, switch each of your Units to Attack Position and give it +2 Attack." (ST-S02-2.) "Your Units" are every Unit its controller has as the Starship launches, the Starship among them, in lane order. Each is switched to Attack Position by `switch_position_of { to: ATK }`, so a Unit already there stays there ([[R91]]), never the flip `switch_all_positions` makes, and an effect's switch spends no exertion ([[R20]]). Then each gains +N Attack, a lasting buff, not one for this turn. The opponent's Units are untouched. The Radiant face gives +4.
