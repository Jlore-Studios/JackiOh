"Copy the text of a Legendary or Mythic Unit on the field" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #20 Tnack Trav) declares its target with the play ([[R81]]): a Unit on the field, on either side and never itself, whose card's rarity is Legendary or Mythic, read from its definition as the game runs it (a fused card's fused definition, a copier's own). A token's rarity is Token, whatever it printed (its `printedRarity`), so no token may be copied (ST-S17-1). With none on the field the play declares nothing and copies nothing ([[R90]]). The copy itself is [[R1850000]]'s: an Immutable target may be copied, and an Immutable Tnack Trav copies nothing (ST-S17-2).
