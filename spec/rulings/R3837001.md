When the opponent has no open unit zone as #52 Silly Silas's base face lands ([[R3837000]]), he lands in the zone the play named, on the player's side and under the player's control, as #55 Lava Golem's base face does with a full row ([[R360]]) and a steal with no zone does ([[R15]]). The play is never refused for it, and his Cry runs as ever.

Rejected: not summoning him (his play would end in the graveyard with nothing done), and refusing the play while the opponent's row is full (a play's legality would read the opponent's row for one card, which Lava Golem's never does).
