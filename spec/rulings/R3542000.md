S+ #87 Frontier Flora is an Animated Field Spell whose text gives it "3X/3X stats for each Plague Counter" and prints none of its own, so by [[R2280000]] it would never become a Unit: with no stat line it enters the field and stays a Field Spell, and a printed 0/0 would animate and die as it entered. A card whose own text gives it stats by its Plague Counters (`Script.stat_line_when`, read by `layers::has_stat_line` beside the printed, fused, `statsOverride` and X stats) has a stat line while it carries a counter. With none it enters the field and stays a Field Spell. The first counter, placed by anything ([[R471]]), owes it its animation at the next state check, as [[R2280001]] owes a card that first has both (`plague::place_plague_on` marks it), by [[R383]]'s placement: an entry into its own lane's unit zone or R64's leftmost open one, summoning sick, with 3 Attack and 3 Health per counter. With no open unit zone it stays where it is and is owed nothing more. Once it has animated it stays a Unit as [[R2280000]] has it, and losing every counter leaves a 0/0 that dies. A fusion drops `statLineWhen`, as it drops `evolvesWhen` ([[R3536001]]). Rejected: printing 0/0 on its faces, which would animate and die on entry; and letting auras make stat lines in general, which [[R2280000]] rules out.
