Stinkyscape #18 Imitated Instrumentality's "the 3 Brains" are named, MELCHIOR, BALTHASAR and CASPER, so its catalog entry refs S #18.1 to #18.3 ([[R279]]). As it resolves it summons them in that order ([[§6.3]] Summon), each a fresh Field Spell into its caster's leftmost open backrow zone ([[R64]]); a Brain left with no open zone is not summoned, and those before it stay. Summoned, none is played, so no Cry runs ([[R1]]) and none counts as a play. The Radiant face summons each of them Radiant. The warning the card prints is the Brains' own ending: each votes at its controller's start of turn on its own condition ([[R1843000]]), so three votes in one turn remove the caster, a loss for them and a win for their opponent, the designer's answer (`docs/stinkyscape-set.md` ST9 Q9), read after the hero check ([[R1843001]]).
