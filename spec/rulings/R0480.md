The eight Pancake tokens and the ten AI generated cards reach play only as members of a pool a card names by its tag — "a random Pancake token" (C+ #12 The Mother Pancake, C+ #13 Mommy Barker), "a random AI generated card" (C+ #43 AI Slop) and "a random AI Generated card" (C+ #78 Claude's Datacenter, balance patch 1) — never by name, so [[R279]]'s rule that every token is named by some card counts a text that names that pool as naming each of its members. A card's `refs` still lists only the cards its texts name by name ([[R279]], both ways). Since balance patch 1 hides the odds and the reward lists in player text, two more pools work the same way: "Grape" (C+ #65 Two Grapes, C+ #66 Vine of Grapes) names the five Grapes, and "a random reward" (C+ #42 KY's Test) names KY's Gift. The Coin, which a rule deals ([[R244]]), is named by no card. Since the Meditative+ set shipped in v0.3.15 ([[R1178000]]) the AI generated cards are twelve: M+ #1.1 Base Camp and M+ #1.2 Spa Day, which M+ #1 Bob's Plan names by name, are in an AI pool beside T-AI-1 to T-AI-10 ([[R1178002]], [[R1421]]).
