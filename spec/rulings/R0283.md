[[§8]] #96's Radiant face destroys the unit whose lethal attack it cancels, as part of the cancel and before the AI turn it hands over: an ordinary destroy ([[§6.3]]) of the attacker the cancelled declaration names, on the stay it declared from ([[R174]]), so an Indestructible attacker is knocked down instead ([[R46]]), a Reborn attacker comes back, and a My Pawn whose cancel finds no open attack — the second half of a My Pawn fused onto a My Pawn, which runs once the first has played the turn out ([[R102]]) — destroys nothing. The attack is cancelled either way ([[R44]]). The AI then takes over a settled board: inside the trap's list, between the destroy and the AI turn, the state check collects the attacker, the traps answer what it said, and the ordinary triggers its death woke resolve (a Corpse Eater in the AI's hand eats it) — as they would between two actions of a turn, which the AI's are — while the triggers the enclosing action had queued before keep their place ([[R117]]). This is a check inside one effect list, an exception to [[R59]]'s check after the whole list made because the list's last effect is a whole turn; C+ #32.3 Blade Storm's rounds are the other ([[R59]])
