No two cards share a name. C #16 is the Book of Flame, the one C #23 Devil's Pact and C #29 Book of Vital Kill name; C #55, which the designer's list printed as a second Book of Flame, is Book of Wildfire, a (1) Spell, Book, Epic: "Deal 4 damage.", Radiant "Deal 8 damage.", to a target, as C #16 — a name of its own, so nothing names it by accident. C #17 keeps Counterspell and C #72 is Grand Counterspell; no card names either. Cards named like a rules word keep their names — C #10 Exile, C #36 Burn, C #57 Echo (also the keyword Echo X) and C #30 Recycle (also inside C #64 Malzahar's Recycler) — and [[R279]]'s proof keeps a named list of rules words that it never reads as a reference to such a card unless the card's `refs` lists it, so "Exile" in twelve Core texts and #79's "Echo 1" are not references to C #10 or C #57, and `refs` stay curated. The Classic+ cards spelled "Jlockheed" keep the designer's spelling under the one Jlockeed tag ([[R278]]). Classic+ numbers the designer wrote twice move: Jogg's Box is C+ #47, Brother Lar C+ #76 and his Brother Ping C+ #76.1, Anti-Softlock C+ #77 and Claude's Datacenter C+ #78, and the two tokens the list numbered as cards take the number of the card that makes them, Felinor Flagbearer Prime C+ #46.1 and the J-lease J-Jungle EX-plorer Pack C+ #75.1. "Luck-based" (Meditative #101) is a rules word that names no card: it names a pool, the cards with a roll Lucky improves ([[R1442]]). "Jade Counter" is a rules phrase whose "Jade" names nothing ([[R961]]).
