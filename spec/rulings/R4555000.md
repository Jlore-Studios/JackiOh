In handicapped fuzz seed 6138 the fuzz monitor's I19 found Meditative #97.4 Mega Church's wide Tribute ([[R1282]]) paying with the opponent's Stinkyscape+ #63 Overdrive Dome while it lay Dormant in their backrow. The legal Tribute units' backrow walk read whatever topped each zone, and only the Units' walk (`active_units_of`) passed a Dormant card by. A Dormant card does not act, is no Unit, and a Tribute passes it by ([[R1839000]]); the card's catalog row, "every permanent acting on the field", says the same. `legal_tribute_units` now passes by a Dormant card in either backrow on either side: `legalActions` lists no Tribute set holding one, the reducer refuses a play naming one ("cannot be tributed"), and an awake permanent beside it pays as before. A card dormant beneath a Stack pile was never offered ([[R13]]) and still is not.

Hearthstone semantics: a Dormant minion can be neither chosen nor affected until it awakens.

Rejected: letting a Tribute, a cost rather than an effect, take a Dormant card, against [[R1839000]]'s list of the rules that pass one by.
