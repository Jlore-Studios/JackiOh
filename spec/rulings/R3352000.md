Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a scene reads a card's id alone, never anything hidden. On 2026-11-06 the
owner asked that every card's art be more unique and richer, in its set's style: simpler for small cards,
elaborate for Mythics, the Spells and Field Spells real scenes rather than a few glyphs over a sky (#3330). The
issue gave each Classic entry but Corpse Plantation (#74, [[R3331000]]'s proof card, whose scene #3331 owns) a
brief, and asked that each draw from a scene recipe of its own, from that brief.

**Every Classic entry has a scene.** Classic #1 to #73, #75 to #90 and the Glitch draw from recipes
(`sceneRecipesClassic1.ts` to `sceneRecipesClassic3.ts`, in Classic's family, the temple stone of ART.md's art
bible, inked in slate), each from its brief: the subject, the setting and the card's idea stay, and a detail the
kits could not hold was changed to one they could, the recipe's doc comment saying so. Each scene clears its
rarity's floor ([[R3331001]]): a Common one subject in a simple setting, a Rare its props, an Epic its far, middle
and near ground, a Legendary every layer with its light and its moment of action, In Too Deep, the set's Mythic,
every layer and a part no other scene draws; their median element count rises rarity by rarity, and every one of
them is off `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`, whose cap falls from 129 to 40, the list's length as this issue left it. The
Radiant face draws the same shapes, repainted toward each layer's gold, as every scene's does. The Glitch's window
draws no picture (glitch.css), so its scene shows only where its picture is drawn outside its face (a contact sheet,
the art a collection page or a patch note prints); it has one so that no Classic entry is left on the old painter.

**Characters.** A Unit draws its character from the kits: a figure, its look built on its tags' (`figureLookFor`)
with the head, headgear, outfit and item its brief names, no two Classic Units in one look; or, when its character
does not stand like a person (Turtinator's cannon tortoise, the Pestilent Slime, the Hired Shrimp, the Plague
Crawler, the Plague Charger's warboar, the Lane Eater's worm-maw), a creature part drawn for it in its subject
layer. A scene's other people stand as extra figures (`extras`, [[R3390000]]), each in its layer.

**The kits.** The parts the briefs named that the kits lacked join them: the figure kit's crouch, three heads (a
great nose, a sheep's, an ape's), five headgears (a jester's cap, a cat-eared hood, a plague doctor's beak mask, a
king's lopsided crown, a sorcerer-king's jagged one), three outfits (a harlequin coat, a long cloak, Joro's
four-armed vest) and six held items (a fan of cards, a rake, a cleaver, a sealed cookie, a magnet grapple, an
abacus), in `figureKit.ts` after the old ones of each kind; and the scene kit's creatures (a cannon tortoise, a
slime, a shrimp, a crawler, a worm-maw, a skeleton, a horned lizard, a deer, a mule), props (a pickle, a crystal
sceptre, a spike trap, a tesla coil, a web, a vat, a chalice), a crater and In Too Deep's own spiral shaft, in a module of
their own (`sceneKitClassic.ts`, its props in `sceneKitClassicProps.ts`) spread into `SCENE_KIT` after the earlier
passes', none of them taking an old part's name (the deer and the crater are `leaping-deer` and `blast-crater`,
beside the `deer` and `crater` [[R3397000]]'s Alt art added, and the long cloak `outfit-travel-cloak`, beside the
`outfit-cloak` [[R3403000]]'s Stinkyscape scenes added). Every part this issue added is drawn by a Classic scene.

**Moving art.** A scene draws no motif glyph and no accent, so a full-art card's props beat ([[R2715001]]) would
have nothing to move: In Too Deep, the one full-art Classic card, marks the placements that move on it `props`
([[R3390000]]), outside its subject layer: the ladders and ropes dangling over its shaft. Its motion is unchanged.

Rejected: a figure body for beasts and machines, which would build a tortoise or a slime round a person's neck,
hip and hand; leaving the Glitch on the old painter, which would leave a Classic entry without a scene; a new
`crowd` field beside [[R3390000]]'s `extras`, which already stands a scene's other people in their layers; moving
In Too Deep's motion from its props beat to its motes, which would rewrite [[R2715001]]'s data as [[R3390000]]
declined to; adding the new scenery to `sceneKit.ts` itself, whose own entries two earlier passes' tests count;
and leaving `ART_DETAIL_SHORT_MAX` at 129, which would let 89 new shortfalls in.

Amends [[R3331000]] (every Classic entry but Corpse Plantation draws a scene of its own).

Amended by [[R3388000]] (issue #3388): a Classic card offered Alt art draws an alt scene recipe of its own under its Alt art seed, in place of its normal scene re-seeded; every other seed, a repaint's among them, still draws its normal scene.
