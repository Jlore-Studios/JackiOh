A lesson is a practice game ([[R187]]) with a fixed seed, a fixed seat for the human and two fixed decks, named by the lesson's id in the worker's start; nothing else the page sends changes them. The human's deck is a legal [[§2.6]] deck of 20, mostly Common, Rare and Epic cards (at most two Legendary or Mythic). The AI's is a fixed list of exactly `AI_TUTORIAL.deckSize` distinct non-token cards in place of `buildAiDeck`'s draw: none on the shadow ban ([[R186]]) unless the lesson names it with its reason — the ban keeps a card out of a dealt deck because a sweep saw the AI hold it unplayed at some tier, and a fixed list's own tests show what the AI does with it (a lesson has named one: #3, whose Taunt, Divine Shield and Reborn lesson 2 teaches, until patch v0.1.1's sweep took it off the ban; no lesson's list holds a banned card since) — none Legendary or Mythic, none that reads its hero's health against 30 (#53 Reno would lift a hero that starts at 20 to 30, and #70 Spiteful Stab would count ten health as already missing, [[R72]]), and none the lessons have not taught yet — units only, none whose keywords change combat, in lesson 1, and no Trap, Field Trap or Field Spell before lesson 3. The Coin goes to the seat going second as always ([[R244]]), so the lesson that teaches The Coin seats the human second and every other lesson seats the human first
