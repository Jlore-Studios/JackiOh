IA #144 Total Compromise's "every (2) Cost or less enemy Unit" is read as it resolves ([[R65]]): every enemy Unit whose cost where it stands is at or under the face's cap ((2), Radiant (3)), stolen in the scope's walk ([[R68]]), each by `steal` with its own id — a Unit with no room on the thief's side is destroyed ([[R15]], [[R4205000]]). The text is reworded to "(N) Cost or less" ([[R432]]); the caps and the hike are fixed prices ([[R1748003]]).
