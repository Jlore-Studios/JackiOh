Issue #5014, part of the not-generic UI pass: when everything on the screen shines, nothing tells the player where to look. In the web client's stylesheets an animation that repeats forever (`infinite`) now marks only something the player can act on right now, or the turn timer's warning, and each such loop is named in `IDLE_LOOPS` (`apps/web/src/compositor-loops.test.ts`) with the state it marks. The list holds at most `IDLE_LOOPS_CEILING` (12) and only shrinks; a new idle loop takes one of its places or plays at most once. It holds all twelve:

- a playable card in hand or a unit that can attack (`glow-breathe-ready`), and a playable card whose condition is met (`glow-breathe-condition`);
- the lanes the card being placed can take (`lane-pick-breathe`), and the legal target under the aim (`drag-reticle-pulse`);
- End turn when it is the only move (`end-turn-ready-pulse`, `data-turn-state="waiting"`);
- the turn timer's final stretch, each loop its part: the pill's glow, the gauge's shiver, the heartbeat under the hand, the burning fuse's head and the sparks it throws (`clock-pill-pulse`, `clock-shiver`, `clock-heartbeat`, `clock-spark-flicker`, `clock-spark-throw`);
- the control the lesson asks for next (`coach-pulse`), and the run map's nodes the player can travel to (`map-pulse`).

**The rest play at most once.** An `infinite alternate` loop became `2 alternate`, one way out and back that ends where it rests; an `infinite reverse` one became `1 reverse`; any other became `1`. That covers a card face's decoration (the keyword treatments of [[R3607000]], a Mythic's hand shimmer and the full-screen view's camera of [[R3345000]], the zzz, the Dormant seal and the shipyard idle of [[R3334000]], the marks, Glitch, the animated rarity foil), the face-down breath, light and veil of [[R2111000]], the drag arrow's dots and the opponent's arrow ([[R3608000]]), emotes, the effects layer's Divine Shield cocoon and a hindered mana crystal's glint, the Black Hole's breath, the spent and countered marks, the market, the showcase halo, an aura tile's pulse and motes, and the menus' loaders and ornaments: the spinners, the search ping, the skeleton's sheen, the spend wait, the code field's caret, the landing's hearth, embers and floating cards, the practice screens' shuffles, scans and orbs, the rank crest's shimmer, and the Duos portal's and ping's pulses. A loader now runs once and rests; a loop resting at `opacity: 0` (a particle) is gone after its run, as meant.

**Effects end with their cue.** An effect that looped for as long as its cue stood now runs for its cue's length: the Starship launch's flame flickers ⌈(`durationMs` − `igniteMs`) / `FX_LAUNCH_FLICKER_MS`⌉ times each way, at least once, and the cinematic's sparks turn 12 times (⌈1 / 0.09⌉, the whole cue). State-change motion (damage, a death, a trap springing) keeps BUILD's event durations (`motion.test.ts`): this takes away decoration, not feedback.

**What keeps its loops.** A cosmetic a player owns keeps its look: every loop in `cards/cosmetics.css`, `emotes/portrait.css` and `game/table.css`, a loop in another sheet on keyframes they declare (the collection's table preview), and `COSMETIC_LOOPS`, each named with its cosmetic kind (the Immersive version's breath, [[R2944002]]). A picture keeps its own motion: `PICTURE_LOOPS`, #3674's diorama slices, and the loops drawn inside a card's art. Any other stylesheet loop fails the test.

**The primary button is a flat plate.** The one primary button's tokens (`--primary-face*` in `buttons.css`) read as a pressed brass plate rather than gel: a face of at most two color stops, and a shadow (`--primary-shadow*`) of at most two layers, one inset top highlight and one hard bottom edge, with no blur. The menus' chrome lost its gradients and blurred shadows with it: an opaque gradient became a flat color, a fade drawn on a `::before` or `::after` went, an inset blurred shadow went, a colored glow went, and a neutral blurred drop became the surface scale's nearest `--shadow-N`. Card faces, the table, `GRADIENT_KEEP` and the glows that carry information (a pack slot's rarity, a Duos ping, a bursting bar) keep theirs, as do the gradients that draw a value (the settings slider's fill, a radio's checked dot). `UNTOKENED_SURFACES` totals at most `UNTOKENED_SURFACES_CEILING` (797, a quarter under the 1,063 it held) and only falls (`surfaces.test.ts`).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. Hearthstone semantics do not reach a stylesheet's loops, though Hearthstone too keeps its glow for what can be played.

**Rejected:** an entry for a group of states, which would hide many loops behind one name; deleting the decorative animations, which would leave hidden what starts unseen and appears through its run, where one run ends at its rest style; `1` for an alternate loop, which would snap back to rest at its end; and allow-listing the loaders, which the issue does not name and which would take places the twelve already fill.

Withdrawn by [[R5130000]] (issue #5130): the budget is reverted whole, with issue #5091's follow-up. The loops, gradients and shadows it took away come back, the title screen's gold wordmark, hearth and embers among them, and `IDLE_LOOPS` and the flat primary button's test go with it. The proving test now holds the withdrawal.
