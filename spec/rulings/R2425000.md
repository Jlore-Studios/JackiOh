**Why.** [[R186]] and [[R390]] counted a turn as affordable when, at the AI's turn start, the card's `effective_cost` was within its mana. So a turn on which a Tribute could not be paid, a required target was missing or a play condition was unmet counted as a miss. `training/history/sweep-2026-10-29-4.md` banned TG #25 Trigor (Tribute 3) on 184 such turns over 99 pass-2 games. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the sweep changes no game.

**What it counts.** `playableTurns` counts the AI's turn starts on which `legal_actions` ([[§10.2]]) lists a `Play` of an instance of the card in its hand. `neverPlayed` (`minAffordableTurns`, 3), its at-risk form and its pass-2 ban (`banAffordableTurns`, 6) read it, and the constants keep their names. A turn start with a prompt open lists no play. A play that borrowed mana or a health price allows counts as playable ([[R1223]], [[R1180004]]). Unswept is still "never affordable". Rows, `--json` lines and reasons show both counts. A slice written before reads the count as 0. The reports of record and `SHADOW_BAN` stand until the next sweep of record.

**Rejected.** Renaming the constants. Checking only the Tribute (`legal_tribute_sets`). Counting a turn as playable only when it is also affordable.
