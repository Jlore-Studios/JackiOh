[[R1483000]] bounded an account's place in the claim queue by the seeds it holds, and counted only the unclaimed
ones, since the burn was then all that recorded a claim. A claim burns its seed before its check waits for a
permit of `PRACTICE_VERIFY_CONCURRENCY` ([[R1303001]]), so a claim still waiting freed its place at once: an
account could take a new seed, play and claim again as fast as it liked, and the queue of folds grew without
bound on a server that checks two at a time.

**What changes.** A seed counts toward `PRACTICE_SEEDS_OPEN_MAX` from its issue until it is claimed and settled,
paid or refused ([[R2422000]]), or until it lapses at `PRACTICE_SEED_TTL_SECONDS`: the count is the account's
seeds issued within the lifetime that are unclaimed, or claimed with their claim still kept and no settle mark.
Both stores count it so, the in-memory fake as Postgres, and `practice_seeds_profile_issued` still serves it, so
no migration is needed. A claim whose pay failed every try holds its place only until its seed lapses, so it
cannot hold one for ever; a seed claimed before migration 0071 holds no claim and never counts. An honest
player's claim answers after its settle, so one claim at a time frees its place before the next request.

The retention loop's sweep ([[R2422000]]) skips a claim the same server is still settling: each settle, live or
swept, is entered under its seed in a set the process keeps, and a second entry for it is refused, so a live
claim still waiting for a permit past `PRACTICE_SETTLE_GRACE_SECONDS` is not folded a second time. A claim the
sweep skips is met again by the next sweep if it is still unsettled. The set is per process, as the settles are;
two servers at a deploy's handover may both settle a claim, and the settle's mark, written only where there is
none ([[R2422000]]), still lets the pay land once.

**Reach.** The 409 an account past the limit gets keeps its words and its status ([[R1483000]]); a release
([[R2379000]]) still deletes only an unclaimed seed. A seed request's count reads under the account's economy
lock as before.

Hearthstone semantics: a reward is granted once, and an account cannot queue more checks than its place allows.

Rejected: a new column or counter for the claims being checked, since the kept claim already says it; counting
past the seed's lifetime, which would let one claim whose pay never landed hold a place for ever; a limit on the
claims waiting in the process, which would be one number for every account where this is the account's own; and
reading the sweep's skip from the store, which would need a lease column for a fact the process already knows.
