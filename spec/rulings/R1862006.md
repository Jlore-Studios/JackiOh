Book Launcher's two Activates, each once a turn and counted apart ([[R1751000]]), split the Book tag ([[§5]]) by what a Book does to its declared target. Harmful, cast at a declared enemy Unit or the enemy hero: Books of Blood, Flame and Wildfire, which deal damage, and of Nerf. Helpful, cast at your hero or one of your Units: Books of Heal, Stats and Buff. In neither: Book of Vital Kill, which sets a hero's health to 13 and ends its caster's turn, a harm or a help by the hero it names and a loss to its caster either way, and every Book with no declared target (Knowledge, Plague, Tokens, Books, Greed, Pain, Potions, Nono-Words). The lists are named card by card, so a Book printed later joins neither until a card's list names it. Each cast is [[R1862004]]'s, with `targetEnemies` ([[R656]]), so a pick a Book makes itself goes to the enemy when it harms and to your side when it helps. Rejected: reading each Book's `aim`, which most Books leave unset, and every Book in both lists, which the designer's "harmful" and "helpful" rule out.
