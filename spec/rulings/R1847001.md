A travel moves its player one node along their starscape map ([[R1847000]]), reports `starscapeTravelled` (the player, the node from 1 to `STARSCAPE_EDGE_TRAVELS`, and its reward; public on both seats) and then gives the node's reward: Heal heals the player's hero, Draw draws, Mana gains mana, Damage deals damage to the enemy hero, each by its amount in `STARSCAPE_REWARDS`. The twentieth travel reaches the edge, which adds the card the travelling ability names (#148.1 Edge of the Universe for #148) to the player's hand on its base face; a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]), and the edge is reached all the same. Nothing travels past the edge: a player standing on it is offered no travel, a travel is refused "can't be activated now", and the card's view keeps the ability listed and unusable, all three reading the ability's one condition ([[R43]], [[R384]]).
