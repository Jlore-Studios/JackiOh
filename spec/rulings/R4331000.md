The owner's rule for every Quest: once a Quest card has given a final reward that is single-use and not an Aura, nothing of the card is left to do, so it goes to the graveyard. Hearthstone's Questlines likewise leave once their last reward is given.

- **The verb.** `end_quest_line` is a final reward's last effect. The running card, on the field on its run's stay ([[R174]]), goes to its graveyard as `send_to_graveyard` moves a card ([[R3044000]]): a plain move, not a death, so no `destroyed`, no Death trigger and no Reborn, and Indestructible does not hold it.
- **What keeps it.** The card stays while its line is not over: a quest still open, opened but not yet counting, met for the next state check, or a stage to hear back with ([[R1854000]]). It also stays while it holds a reward's aura (`memory.quest.auras`), since an Aura's reward lasts only while the card stands ([[R78]]).
- **The cards.** Stinkyscape #7 The Greatest Heist goes after Stage 3's steal. Classic #90 In Too Deep's base face goes after J (gain 100 mana next turn, which stays with the player) or K (exile the opponent's deck), and stays after L or M, both Auras. Its Radiant face follows every path, so its line ends only once L and M are held, and it stays. Stinkyscape #112 Job Application never ends its line: its accepted Offer pays a Paycheck at every start of turn while the card stands, and re-applying returns to Stage 1. It stays.
- **Replays.** The games of v0.6.9c and before fold under the scripts they ran, in which these cards stayed (`script_v0_6_9c`, [[R767000]]).
