The the client draws the words BUILD M5-T4 promises for five events, from the events alone (CLAUDE.md rule 7) and never from a card's name or id ([[R97]]). `drawLimited`: a "Draw limit" tag on that seat's Deck pile, drawn as [[R318]]'s "Deck full" is (the pile's notice, the newest entry per pile, on each side). `attackCancelled`: a "Canceled" tag on the attacker. `countered`: a "Countered" tag on the element the event animates, which is the viewer's own held hand card or, for the opponent's play, the graveyard or exile pile the card went to; a card countered back into a hand shows no tag, since a hand region carries no animation. `turnCutShort` and `autopilotEngaged`: the board's `turn-banner`, the live region, reads "Turn cut short" and "The AI takes the turn" (the effects layer's own words, `fx/constants.ts`). The tags and the notice are the board's own elements, not the effects layer's, so they show with the effects On or Off ([[R200]]: effects only decorate); each shows only while its entry plays and goes with it, and reduced motion starts no entry, so none draws there, as "Deck full" does not. The banner follows the burst's entries ([[R1183002]]'s autopilot turn among them), so each word holds until a later turn event (`turnStarted`, `turnAutoEnded`, `turnCutShort`) starts. A banner that is not playing no longer says the AI takes the turn: the view's event window spans several actions, and an `autopilotEngaged` in it may be long past ([[R1844000]]'s aura hands the turn back), so only the burst's own entries speak for it; rejected: holding "The AI takes the turn" until the next `turnStarted` in the standing view, which would say it through a human's own actions. BUILD M5-T4's `turnCutShort` row says "Turn cut short", as the effect does, not "Turn ended". The words have Chinese in `zh-CN.json`. It extends [[§10.10]]'s table and [[R318]]'s overflow notices.
