A Degrade applies one change to a card, drawn uniformly with the match rng from the changes that can apply to it now, and an Upgrade one from the mirror list; "Degrade a card 5 times" is five draws, and a card no change can reach is left alone with no draw ([[R129]]). An Immutable card is never changed (no draw); otherwise either reaches a card in any zone its text names — the field, a hand, a deck. The changes, Degrade then Upgrade: its cost 1 more, never above (4), or 1 less, never below (0), for a card whose own cost ([[R65]], out of play) is below 4 or at least 1, never an X-cost card; its stats −4 or +4 split by a roll of k from 0 to 4 as k attack and 4 − k health, attack floored at 0 and current health never below 1, what the floors refuse being lost, for a Unit or an Animated card; a keyword it has removed (never Immutable and never a harmful one: Can't attack, Cannot be in Defense Position, Brittle) or one it lacks added from [[R21]]'s pool (a Unit only); one X 1 worse or better — an X-cost card's X as it resolves (never below 1) or a numbered keyword's number (Armor, Lucky, Echo, Activate, Brittle and Spell Damage, more being better; Tribute, less being better, never below 1); or one number the card declares one step worse or better. The changes are the card's: kept on the instance as `tuning` (the cost change as `costMod`) in every zone and through leaving the field ([[R78]]), kept by a copy ([[R57]]), summed by a Fuse ([[R102]]) and gone with a Transform. A card declares its tunable numbers per face in the catalog as `params` — each with its base and Radiant value, whether more or less is better, a step (1 up to 5, 2 from 6 to 12, a quarter above, unless the card says otherwise) and its bounds — writes them into its text as `{key}`, and its script reads `param(ctx, key)`; an amount never drops below 1, and a threshold moves toward harder for a Degrade. The view carries the current values, which a client fills in ([[R280]]), and [[R277]]'s mark compares the two faces with their values filled in. On the field the stats change moves max health; in a hand or a deck it changes the face the card enters with. A changed card shows its current cost, stats, keywords and numbers wherever its viewer may read it, and its owner does not see a change made inside a deck until the card leaves it ([[R311]]); `degraded` and `upgraded { instanceId, change }` are hidden like any other hand or deck event. C+ #41 KY's Constant reads "a number on a card" the same way: its cost (not X), attack, health, a numbered keyword's value or a declared number
