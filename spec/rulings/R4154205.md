Issue #4154 gave S #70 Z'Zoth "Trigger their Deaths." (Radiant: "Give them Reborn. Trigger their Deaths twice.") after its summon ([[R1869001]]). "Them" are the Units its Cry summoned, read once, after the Radiant face's Reborn and before any Death runs, so a Unit a Death summons is none of them. Each runs its Death, its own and any granted, as [[§4.5]] step 3 runs a dying card's, with its controller's choices and the card read as it stands ([[R89]]), but nothing dies: no Reborn, no `destroyed`, no graveyard step, and no state check of its own, the Cry's following ([[R59]]) (`trigger_death`, the engine's `run_death_hooks`). Hearthstone's "trigger a minion's Deathrattle" runs it on a living minion the same way, and Baron Rivendare doubles it, so Double Counting's extra runs apply ([[R823]]). A Unit an earlier Death took off the field runs none ([[R174]]); a Death that asks parks the rest ([[R113]]). The Radiant face runs the round twice, every Unit once and then every Unit again. Rejected: the deaths in pairs (each Unit twice before the next) and killing the Units. Through v0.6.6g no Death ran (`script_v0_6_6g`, [[R767000]]).
