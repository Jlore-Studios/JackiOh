**Why.** [[R4707001]]'s figures are read on the page beside what a card made, with no new control: the question each row answers, "is this outcome better than this card's other outcomes", is the one the Outputs segment ([[R4679001]]) asks of a made card.

**The segment.** The Extended stats panel's Outputs segment ([[R4676001]], [[R4679001]]) is also shown for a card the server sends a rolled outcome for, so a card that rolled and made nothing (Gary the Gambler, #4; Dice of Doom, S #154) has it. Its outputs table comes first, as before, and is left out when the card made and offered nothing but rolled; "No outputs recorded yet." is shown only for a card with neither. This amends [[R4679001]], whose segment needed an output row or a token.

**The tables.** One small table per kind the server sends, in its order, each under its own heading: **Rolled effects** (a table's entries, Call to Chaos's), **Coin flips** or **Dice**. Its rows are in the server's order, best adjusted first, each: the outcome in the server's words, **Expected** (its table's share) beside **Share** (how often it came up), each a percentage to `ROLLED_SHARE_DIGITS` places, its games, the deck table's `TierChip`, its Adjusted ("not enough games" below the floor) and, with Show 95% ranges on, its range under it. A row opens nothing: an outcome has no drill-down. The table scrolls sideways in its own box on a phone, as every `/stats` table does. The client restates no number of the table's: Expected is the server's.

**The address.** `extended=outputs` opens the panel on Outputs for such a card too, as [[R4679001]] reads it.

Rejected:

- **A segment of its own**: the issue asked for no new control, and the Segmented already holds three.
- **Expected after Share**: the eye reads what was due first, then what came, so a gap reads left to right.
- **A row that opens its card**: the outcome is no card, and an edition's entry would open the edition, not the outcome.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
