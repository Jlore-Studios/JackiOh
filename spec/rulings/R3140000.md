Meditative #40 Feng Shui reads "Aura: Cards have elements (水, 木, 火, 金, 土); good feng shui is rewarded, bad punished. / Activate: Add an Auspicious Rock to your hand. / Your last element, your opponent's last element." and its Radiant face "… good feng shui rewards you, bad hurts your opponent heavily. / Activate: Add a Radiant Auspicious Rock to your hand. / …", and costs (3). Through v0.5.11o it cost (2) and read "You have Luck {luck}" on both faces ([[R987]]), with the `luck` number a Buff or a Nerf could move; that line, the number and the script's `luck` flag are gone, and no shipped card gives Luck.

- **The element judging is unchanged.** The records, the judging at [[§10.5]] step 3, the Brittle and the hit, the shown elements and the two `preview` lines work as [[R980]] to [[R986]] have them, base and Radiant.
- **The Activate** ([[R384]]) is once a turn, its controller's alone, and costs nothing. It adds one Auspicious Rock (M #39.1) to its controller's hand, the Rock Radiant on the Radiant face as every other Rock source has it (M #39, #43, #44), and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). The Feng Shui stays on the field. An Activate is no play, so it is not judged and writes no record ([[R982]]); the Rock it adds is judged like any card when it is played.
- **Luck stays in the engine.** `luck_of`, the `luck` static flag, the Luck badge on the board and the custom-card kit's read of it are kept, with the engine's own tests on the fixture script of `crates/engine/tests/rules/fixtures/feng_shui.rs`; a card that gives Luck, shipped or custom, would work as it did. The glossary's "Luck X" row names no card now, and [[R1444]]'s pool, which never held Feng Shui, is untouched by it. The card tests that rolled with Feng Shui in play (M #39.1, M #97.2, M #97.8, M #102, S #142, S #164, C+ #19.3, C+ #25, C+ #53, Core #23 and its Training Grounds snapshot) roll with a Lucky X given to the rolling card instead ([[R1438]]), the same best-of rolls ([[R1440]]); and a Feng Shui standing is tested to add no roll.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`: the judge with Luck 1, no Activate and its declared `luck` ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: removing the engine's Luck read with the only card that gave it, which would break the custom-card kit and the fixture for a rule that costs nothing to keep; and a Rock that is Radiant on the base face too, which no other Rock source has.
