"If there isn't one, summon a random Acclaimed Unit for your opponent, Frozen, and Allure it" (Radiant Stinkyscape #87) is read as the Cry resolves: with no candidate [[R1864000]] would pick, the Cry summons a random non-token Acclaimed Unit drawn from the sets that ship ([[R380]]: a pool that names no set), never Siva 1 itself ([[R387]]), onto the opponent's side, into their leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]) with no Cry ([[R1]]). The Unit that summon made ([[R136]]) is then Frozen ([[§6.3]] Freeze, [[R1174000]]) and Allured as [[R1864000]] Allures its pick ([[R1045000]]), so it is stolen at the start of its controller's next turn. A full enemy unit row summons nothing, so nothing is Frozen or Allured and nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). This is Hearthstone's "summon a random minion for your opponent": one pick of the pool, and nothing on a full board. Rejected: Alluring some other enemy Unit when the summon cannot land, which the text does not say.
