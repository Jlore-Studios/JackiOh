CN Jade Well is a Field Spell; the designer typed it Spell. A start-of-turn text needs a card that
stays on the field, as [[R402]] reasoned for C #78. C+ #52's "For the rest of the game" Spell is the
other reading, rejected: it would be an unanswerable Rock every turn at (3).
