A Luck-based card is a non-token card that prints Lucky on either face ([[§6.1]]) or whose text, on either face, says "Flip a coin": the cards with a roll that Lucky improves, a coin flip being one since [[R1440]]. It is read off the definition (the printed keywords of both faces and their text), never off an instance, so a Lucky given to a card ([[R1438]]) never puts it in the pool, and the catalog query names it `luckBased`. The pool names no set ([[R380]], [[R1420]]), so until the Meditative set ships it holds #4 Gary the Gambler, #23 Reoccurring Dream, #42 Eugenics, C #65 Ace in the Hole, C+ #25 Soul Shot, C+ #53 Book of Tokens, C+ #65 Two Grapes and C+ #66 Vine of Grapes, and from then on also Meditative #36 CN Peptides, #86 Mayor Medinamogger and #102 Catboy Maid SSR+. Tokens are left out ([[§5.1]]), so C+ #19.3 Mid Loser and the Lucky buildings of Meditative #97 are not in it. Meditative #40 Feng Shui gives a player Luck but makes no roll of its own, so it is not in it either. Meditative #101 Gachaholic adds one card of the pool to its controller's hand, hidden from the opponent, on its base face at its printed cost, and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). "Luck-based" is a rules word that names no card ([[R381]]).
