A unit switches to Attack Position and loses Taunt this turn; an Indestructible Field Spell (Heroic Power) simply stays. Both halves are reported ([[§10.3]]): the switch by `positionSwitched`, and only when there is one ([[R91]]); the Taunt the knock-down takes — one the unit still has in Attack Position, printed, granted or from an aura — by a `keywordGranted` naming Taunt with `lost` set. So a unit already in Attack Position (#56 radiant Jilliax) does not lose the Taunt that decides what may be attacked ([[§4.2]] step 3) with no event at all, and one with no Taunt left to lose (The Rock, or a unit knocked down twice in a turn) reports nothing more. No event type says a keyword went, and this adds none. Since [[R347]] (patch v0.1.1) an Indestructible unit has no Taunt at all, so the knock-down finds none to take while the unit is still Indestructible and reports only its switch; the turn it stamps (`tauntSuppressedTurn`) still holds a Taunt off for the rest of that turn if the unit loses its Indestructible (a Vanilla) before the turn ends
