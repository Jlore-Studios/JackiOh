[[§4.3]] step 1 says an attacker that a First Strike defender destroys first deals nothing. The opponent model's static trader (`fight` and `trade_value`, [[§9.9]]) knew such a blow landed nothing, so it counted no kill, splash, conversion or heal for it. It still credited the blow's chip per point dealt and the Divine Shield it would have broken. A 1/2 attacking Radiant Pointmaster (#20, a 12/2 with Rush, First Strike and Divine Shield) read as worth that shield, and a 4/4 attacking a plain Pointmaster (6/1) as worth a point of chip, so the AI expected its opponent to make attacks that gain nothing.

`fight` now reads a blow that never lands as no blow at all: it deals nothing, kills nothing and pops nothing. It also reads the strike back that a First Strike attacker pre-empts, by destroying its target first, as none, so nothing is taken. Both are how [[R3100000]]'s combat forecast shows them. The kill, the death and whether the blow lands are read as before.

A unit test holds the AI's per-attack reads to that forecast on real boards. It plays 16 seeded games of [[§10.7]]'s random policy, both decks dealt by `build_ai_deck` from every set. On every state the games pass through, it takes every Unit-target entry of either seat's `combat_forecast` and compares `fight` and the move order's `kills_and_survives` with it field by field: what the attacker deals (up to the target's health for a Trample hit, [[§4.4]] step 5), what it takes, whether it kills, whether it survives, and whether it pops a Divine Shield. A mismatch fails the test, naming the seed, the action, both cards and both reads. The test asserts floors on the attacks it judged (1000), the distinct cards at either end of one (150) and the stopped blows that would otherwise have dealt damage or popped a shield (5), so it cannot pass by checking nothing. On this branch it judges 2064 attacks over 246 distinct cards, with 80 stopped blows, and finds no other mismatch; run once on seeds 0 to 223 it judged 31257 attacks over 1061 cards, with 411 stopped blows, and found none either, and with every random pool rotated one place (the engine's `pool-probe` feature) its 16 games judge 2117 attacks over 224 cards, with 12 stopped blows. Against the old `fight` it fails, as it does when only the stopped blow is left unfixed. The hand-built-body tests ([[R2179005]], [[R4064000]]) stay, for the faces and keywords random play rarely shows (Anime Armor's cap, the damage pools and the conversions), as does [[R4065001]]'s test of The Banisher's exile.

Hearthstone semantics: a minion that dies before it strikes deals no damage, and an AI's read of an attack is the game's own forecast of it ([[R4111000]] holds that forecast to the real combat).

Rejected:
- Gating the chip and the pop in `trade_value` alone. That leaves `fight` reading the stopped blow as landed, so the agreement test would pass with the fix reverted.
- Normalising the forecast's fields in the test instead. That restates [[§4.3]] in a test meant to hold the AI to the engine.
- Putting the test's game count and floors in `config.rs`. They are test sizes, not numbers the AI plays by.
- Filtering states by phase and prompt. `combat_forecast` lists only what `legal_actions` allows, so a state where no attack may be declared has no entries.

**Gate.** The smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`, fixed seeds) on base `8c6074f25b` with this change: ai-vs-random 19 of 20 (17 needed), ai-vs-greedy 16 (10), hard-vs-easy 16 (16), with no search panic.
