A card's coins and dice are reported as `rolled` batches ([[R2652001]]), and a batch reaches both players, the faces in full and its card named as the card itself is ([[R97]]: by where it is now), except one case the spec did not decide. Hearthstone shows a Secret's effect only once the Secret is revealed, so a batch a card made while it was face-down in a backrow ([[R33]]: a Trap or Field Trap not face-up and not Revealed, [[R686]]) reaches its controller alone. The other player's view leaves it out altogether, rather than redacting it as [[R97]] redacts a card's identity, because the flip itself, at the end of a turn, would tell them a trap is set. It reaches them too when that trap's own `trapFired` follows in the same action, as C #65 Ace in the Hole's heads does in the end-of-turn trap window ([[R62]]): the trap is public by then, and the flip that fired it is part of what they see happen.

It is decided inside the action that rolled and never changes later, since a client diffs each view's window of events against the one before ([[§10.8]]): a trap that fires in a later action leaves the flip of an earlier one hidden. The engine marks such a batch `faceDown` as it rolls; that mark is bookkeeping, and no view forwards it.

So base Ace in the Hole's tails is its controller's alone and its heads is both players', and its Radiant face, Revealed before it flips, shows every flip to both. #4 Gary the Gambler, C+ #19.3 Mid Loser and S #154 Dice of Doom roll face-up, and both players see every roll.
