Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Each piece's module ([[R3333001]]) has an icon: the module on a small riveted slate plate, 3 wide by 2 high, gilded when the piece fused in on its Radiant face; an id that is no piece shows the spare plate. A Dormant Starship's hull plates ([[R2801000]]) each show their piece's icon, oldest first, port and starboard in turn, up to `STARSHIP_HULL_PLATES_MAX` (8), each plate naming its module's kind (`data-module`); a launched Starship still wears none. Its pieces list in play ([[R2249000]]) shows the same icon before each piece's name, Dormant or launched. The list is the view's, public on both seats ([[R1852005]]), and the icons are hidden from assistive technology, the names beside them saying what they show. A plate is drawn 10.5 by 7 (in hundredths of the card's smaller side), up from 8.5 by 5.5, so its icon reads.

This amends [[R2801000]]: a plate shows its piece's module, no longer a plain plate.
