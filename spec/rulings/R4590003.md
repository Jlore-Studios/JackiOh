IA #138 Social Engineer's "Swap control of this and" the named enemy Unit (`swapControl`) does nothing unless both are on the field, both act on it ([[R13]]) and their controllers differ. Otherwise both cards move at once: off the field first, then each placed by [[R15]] with the zones they left counted free, so nothing is destroyed, the taken Unit as the thief's steal and the given one as their give. Both are summoning sick ([[R171]]). The base face picks the enemy Unit at random ([[R60]]); the Radiant face takes the one with the highest Attack, ties broken at random.
