A profile saves up to `MAX_SAVED_DECKS` (10) named decks. A save checks structure only — D1 a name of 1 to `DECK_NAME_MAX_LENGTH` (40) characters once trimmed, with no control characters; D2 at most `DECK_SIZE` cards; D3 every card a deckable card of the catalog (it exists and is not a Token); D4 at most `MAX_COPIES` of a card — and never legality: an incomplete deck, a card the player does not own and a banned card are all saved, and judged when the deck is queued ([[R253]]). A refused save names its first issue and lists at most `DRAFT_ISSUES_REPORTED_MAX` (50) of them. The loadout this replaces was one all-or-nothing save of three legal decks, so a half-built deck could not be kept at all, and the brief asks for the opposite: "save partial progress" and "never lose work"
