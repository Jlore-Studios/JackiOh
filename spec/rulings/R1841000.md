An Activate ability whose cost says it "has an extra turn cooldown" (`ActivationCost.extraTurnCooldown`) can't be used on its user's next turn after a use. The use writes `memory.cooldowns[<ability>] = { player, turn }`, `turn` being that player's `turnsStarted`; while that player is active on one of the `ACTIVATE_COOLDOWN_TURNS` (1) turns of theirs after it, the ability is refused, "that ability is cooling down this turn", on top of its uses per turn ([[R384]], [[R1751000]]). The turn after that it is usable again. The clock is its user's own turns, so an extra turn taken right after the use ([[R845]]) is the turn it sits out, and the opponent's turn between counts for nothing. One check is the list, the refusal and the view ([[§10.2]], [[R43]]): `legalActions` offers no `activate` for it, and the card's view shows it unusable with that reason. The cooldown lives in `memory`, so leaving the field clears it with the uses ([[R78]]): a bounced card or a copy starts fresh. Each ability of a fused card keeps its own, by its id ([[R1751000]]). An ability without the rider keeps nothing. Rejected: the game's turn count, which would put the cooldown on the opponent's turn, where an Activate can't be used anyway; and a field of its own on the instance, which would survive a bounce as `costMod` does ([[R766]]) but widen the state, the wire and the AI's redaction for one rider.
