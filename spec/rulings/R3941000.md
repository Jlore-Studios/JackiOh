Issue #3941's pass over every card found terms that did not print as their labels, so the face model ([[R910000]]), which matches a term's own spelling, printed them plain and without their rule: S+ #57 Phase Weaver's "end of its owner's turn" (End of turn), S+ #56 Impact Reactor's "When awoken" (When this awakens), S+ #61.1 Shawarma Law's "Can't defend" (Can't Defend), S+ #46 Phase Fabric's "Can't be Attacked" (Can't be attacked) and S+ #39 Mindustrial Cross-over's "Starpiece" (Starship Piece). [[§5.4]] writes down the conventions, and `card_text.rs` sweeps every English face of the catalog for them, beside [[R366]]'s shape: each keyword kind's label, and End of turn, Start of turn, When this awakens, Cast on draw and Can't be attacked, spelled as the label past its first letter; no line opening in lower case; no "its owner's turn" (S+ #26 Jlockeed's Forever War and S+ #166 Mindustrial Revolution read as C+ #52 does, "For the rest of the game." over a Start of turn line); no cost spliced onto an added card with a comma outside a Call to Chaos list, whose clauses are `chaosRolled`'s labels ([[R436]]); a hand or deck trigger opening "While this is in your …" (M+ #17 Dogpile, C+ #37 Wardrum and M #87 Tatches the Totem were "In your hand or deck", "While in your hand or deck" and "While in your deck"); no "Evolve: When …"; no "+N attack" or "+N health" in lower case; and neither "When awoken" nor "One of the following random effects". A second test holds each check to the wording it retires and the wording that replaced it. The pass changes words only: no card plays differently, and the scripts that print their face's words into a badge read them from the definition a game folds under, so an older game's replay keeps its words ([[R767000]]).
