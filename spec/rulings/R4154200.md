Issue #4154 asked that S+ #79 A Glimpse into the Endless Sea "also exiles the board"; both faces now read "Exile your deck and every permanent." "Every permanent" is both sides' boards, the caster's first, then the opponent's, each by `exile_board` as S+ #80 The Last Voyage's Radiant face exiles both ([[R3539002]]): every card acting on the field and every card beneath it in a pile, face-down backrow cards and Dormant cards included, so a Dormant Starship goes too; only a card no effect reaches is spared ([[R940]]). The exile follows the deck's and comes before the Radiant face's Armor and the countdown, whose badge is its owner's, not a card's, so the exile takes nothing from it ([[R3569005]]). Rejected: the caster's board alone ("every" names no side) and sparing face-down cards (an exile of the board reaches them). Through v0.6.6h it exiled the deck alone (`script_v0_6_6h`, [[R767000]]).
