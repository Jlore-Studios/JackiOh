A Best-of-1 deck must pass L2, L3, L5 and L6, the [[§9.4]] rules about one deck, and a Conquest trio ([[R330]]) all of L1–L6, an empty slot failing L1: a trio is the loadout [[§9.4]] first described. Checked by the shared validator at enqueue and at room create and join, with the decks' own names in its messages; the deck or trio is then frozen into the ticket or the room. The catalog version a client sends is checked at save; a saved deck's own version is no reason to refuse it, because the queue checks its cards against the current catalog (L6)
