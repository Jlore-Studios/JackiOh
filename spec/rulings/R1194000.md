## R1194000 — Ivory Tower's Aura gives your Units Flying and Stack in hand too

Built (patch v0.3.13, #1194), so that C+ #33 Ivory Tower's "Aura: Your Units have Flying and Stack."
changes where its controller may play a Unit. While the Tower acts on its controller's field, the Aura
reaches that player's Units on the field, in a unit zone or standing in the backrow ([[R1081000]]), and,
as [[R783007]] lets an aura do, the Unit cards in that player's hand and the one their play holds in the
resolving zone. A play reads both keywords where the card is, through the aura layer ([[§10.4]]):
`legal_actions` and the reducer read them off the card in hand, so a Unit there is offered every
occupied zone a Stack card may enter and every empty backrow zone a Flying Unit may stand in, and
[[§10.5]] step 4 reads Stack again in the resolving zone as the card lands on its pile. The Tower's own
zone stays a carrier's ([[R446]]): a Unit enters it only as the Tower's Unit, which is how "the first
Unit you stack onto this" is met, and once the Tower has taken its one Unit no Unit is offered that zone,
Flying and Stack or not ([[R653]]). The opponent's Units get neither keyword. When the Tower leaves the
field the Units in hand lose both at once, and a Unit already placed stays where it stands
([[R1081000]]), as a Hearthstone aura's grant lapses without undoing what it allowed. Rejected: the
field only, which no play could use, since placement is read in the hand; and every zone, which would
give Units in a deck or a graveyard keywords that pools and filters read.
