Fingerprinter's Cry on its base face picks a random Spell in the opponent's hand and adds a copy of it to its controller's hand; if the opponent holds no Spell, nothing is added. On Radiant, it opens a prompt of the opponent's hand shown to the chooser alone ([[§10.8]], [[R81]]), and adds a copy of the chosen card to the controller's hand.
