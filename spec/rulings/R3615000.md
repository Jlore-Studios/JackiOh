[[R3477000]] wore both numbers of an aimed attack on every legal target at once: the damage the blow
deals and what the target strikes back, on pill-shaped chips over each target. With several targets that
crowds the board, and the attacker's own damage shows up on the target's card, not on the attacker. The
preview now shows only for the target under the pointer: picking or lifting an attacker shows nothing
yet, and once the pointer (or a drag) is over a legal target that has a forecast entry, the preview
shows for that one attack; leaving the target takes it away. It reads the hovered target from the same
reader the aim relay already uses, never a second hit test.

Each number goes on the card that takes it: the target wears its own damage (`dealt`) and the attacker
wears the damage it takes back (`taken`), whatever the forecast entry says. A hero target never strikes
back ([[R3100000]]), so the attacker shows nothing for a hero target, and a strike back of 0 shows no
marker on the attacker.

Each marker reuses the real damage hit's design, toned down so it reads as tentative: the `fx-splat`
starburst with its signed amount when effects are on, and the `.damage-pop` number when effects are off.
Same shape, size, font and placement, through a `data-tentative` modifier on the same classes; lowered
opacity plus a ghosted fill, so it cannot be mistaken for a hit that already landed, and no animation, so
it holds still while hovered. The other marks carry over in the same language: a skull on whichever card
the forecast says is destroyed (`kills` on the target, `survives: false` on the attacker) and the
struck-through Divine Shield mark when the blow pops one (`pops`).

Everything else in [[R3477000]] stays as it is: the numbers come from the engine's forecast as sent; the
"Show attack outcomes" setting turns the preview off; a lesson's board and a replay show none; the marker
takes no click (a press on it belongs to the card under it); it sits out of flow, so nothing moves; a DEF
card's marker is turned back to read level; and the title and accessible name still end on the note that a
face-down trap can still change the result ([[R292]]). Color never carries meaning on its own: the minus
sign and the skull do.

Hearthstone semantics: damage reads as the damage pipeline reads it ([[§4.4]]), the strike back as
[[§4.3]] reads it, the preview is a derived fact and not a rule, and a hidden card's effect is not foretold.

Rejected: a third design beside the splat and the pop, which would drift from the hits it previews; a
marker on every legal target at once, which crowds the board; the attacker's number on the target's card.
