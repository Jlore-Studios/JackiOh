The owner's decision on issue #473 (2026-10-08): a card that enters a graveyard reverts to its printed state, with no buffs and no cost changes. [[R78]] and [[R215]] already reset a card that reaches a graveyard or an exile pile from the field, a hand, a library or the resolving zone; since this ruling the reset takes its price too. Its `costMod` (a discount or a surcharge, a Degrade's or an Upgrade's cost step, [[R386]], the cost C+ #41 KY's Constant set) and its `costOverride` (a "(0)" a card was given in a hand) both go as it arrives, from wherever it came, a graveyard to an exile pile included, so it costs its printed cost ([[R65]]) wherever it is read from there and when a permission plays it from there ([[§6.3]] Play). It keeps what the printed card is and what has happened to it, which are no buff and no price: its Radiant face, its `tuning` ([[R386]]), its Brittle count ([[R385]]), its times-played count ([[R429]]) and an enchantment that rides it (C+ #14, [[R410]]). A fused card keeps its fused definition ([[R77]], [[R470]]): it is that definition's printed card again, at that definition's cost, and does not split back into its ingredients. A card a replacement sends elsewhere instead of a graveyard (C #28's exile) lands as the graveyard would have had it land, its price gone. Nothing else changes: a price given as a card reaches a hand ([[R215]]'s list, [[R742]]'s gift) is its price in that hand, and a card that goes from the field to a hand or a library keeps its price ([[R78]]). So C+ #54 Book of Books' (0) no longer follows a C+ #57 Book of Stats into its graveyard: C #90 In Too Deep's reward L and C #28 Second Wind play it from there at its printed (1), and the free replay loop of issue #473 is gone. One card is the exception, by the owner's decisions on issues #557 and #572 (2026-10-08): #31 KY's Math Equation's own end-of-turn return gives back the climb its own earlier returns gave it, and nothing else of the price it was played at. #31 lands in its graveyard between its play and that return, and lying there it is its printed (1) card like any other; but each return notes the climb on the card in the hand it returns to, beside its `costMod`, the card carries the note into its next play, and as [[§10.5]] step 7 lands it, flagged for the return ([[R155]]), the engine notes the climb again, and the return puts that climb back on the card in the hand it returns to, plus the (1) the return adds, to a maximum of (4) ([[R429]]). So its climb survives the graveyard: (2), then (3), then (4), where it stays. The note goes with [[R155]]'s flag, as the card leaves the graveyard or as the turn ends; a discount, a surcharge or a cost step it was played at, and its `costOverride`, are no part of it and stay gone; and a #31 that reaches a graveyard any other way (discarded, burned by a full hand, countered) makes no return, so nothing is noted and it is its printed card there and after. No other card keeps a price through a graveyard, a Spell with an end-of-turn return of its own (#23) included. Hearthstone resets a card's cost when it leaves the hand
