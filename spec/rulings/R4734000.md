Not a rule of the game (CLAUDE.md rule 7): what the board shows once the view carries a result.

**End turn after the game.** A view with a result gives End turn its own state, `over`
(`data-turn-state="over"` on the button and its `.turn-mechanism`): it reads "Game over", the turn
plate's word, stays disabled as no move is legal, and is sunk and dimmed as in the opponent's turn
(`game/board.css`, `buttons.css`). This holds for a win, a loss and a draw, whoever's turn the game ended
on; before, every finished view read "Enemy turn", the only turn wording on a phone held upright. The
opponent's live turn still reads "Enemy turn". Hiding End turn instead was rejected: the rail's layout
would shift as the result comes in.

**The result waits only for what plays.** The effects layer plays the killing blow and Victory / Defeat
([[§10.10]], [[R200]]) only when the shown view goes from no result to one on a board that is already
mounted, and a silent board (the Duos teammate's) has no effects layer. So the result panel or chip
waits `resultRevealMs()` (`FX_LETHAL_LEAD_MAX_MS` + `FX_RESULT_MS`) only on a board that showed the game
unfinished and is not silent (`Game`'s `arrived`). A board mounted on a finished game, such as the one
Back from Watch the replay ([[R768000]]) or a reload mounts, shows its result with `data-revealed="true"`
at once, Rematch included; before, it stayed transparent and took no pointer for 3.8 s waiting on an
animation that never played. Reduced motion and effects off wait for nothing, as before.
