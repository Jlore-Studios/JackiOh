[[R3535000]] gives the Stinkyscape+ set a shadow-ban share of a fifth ([[R3114001]]), 33 of its 166 non-token cards, and ships it behind [[R1390]]'s gate, so the AI deals itself none of it until a sweep of record ([[R186]], [[R390]]) has measured the AI on it. The sweep `.github/workflows/sweep.yml` started on the release's push ([[R1678000]]) reached GitHub's 350-minute job limit in its pass 2 and wrote nothing, since a card's pass 1 took about 2.3 core-minutes against Stinkyscape's 1.3 (`training/history/sweep-2026-10-28.md`). The sweep of record ran instead in a cloud container, as the workflow runs it, on the AI that ships with the gate lifted: `training/history/sweep-2026-11-13.md`, pass 1 over the 166 cards, pass 2 over the 70 at risk. It banned 4 (S+ #47 Plastanium and #163 Accelerationist for a `timeout` at Easy, #81 Doomsayer for an `error`, the panic [[R3574001]] ends, and #157 Phantom Shipyard as `neverPlayed` at Hard), within the 33, and `cargo jackioh sweep --write` wrote them from it. Issue #3574 empties `AI_DECK_GATED_SETS`: the AI's own decks are dealt from the set at every tier, every card the ban leaves it, and a lean on the set leans on it.

Issue #3575 (fullsend 3) lifts the four ([[R3575000]]): two cleared by a sweep of record after its fixes, two through the unban lane, so that no Stinkyscape+ card is shadow-banned. The ban's scope is unchanged ([[R186]]): a banned card stays legal for every player, and the AI still answers it.
