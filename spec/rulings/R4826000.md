S+ #74 Galaxia's "Aura: The first Unit you play each turn gets +3 Attack" is spent on the first Unit her controller plays each turn that resolves while she stands on the field, once its play resolves ([[R3568000]]), its Cry included. If that Unit is no longer standing face-up on the field under her controller's control as its play resolves (such as when it destroys itself with its own Cry, is sacrificed, bounced, or stolen), it receives no Attack buff, and the turn's offer (`grantedTurn`) is spent nonetheless: a second Unit played that turn is not the first Unit played and receives no buff. A Unit whose play was countered never resolved ([[R448]]) and counts as never played, so the next Unit played to resolve is the first resolved Unit play of that turn.

Rejected: treating a first Unit that left the field before resolving as though it was never played, which would give the Attack to the second Unit played that turn.
