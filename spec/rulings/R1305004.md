[[§14.5]] fills two bars, pays tokens and redeems them, and an account reads its own bars and
tokens back (nobody else's, [[§14]]): one `GET` reads both bars' points and size, the cycle's
next step and the tokens held.

- **`GET /api/tokens`** answers both bars as `{ points, size }`, the cycle's next step
  (`rare`, `epic` or `cosmeticPack`) and the tokens held (`card`, `cosmeticRare`,
  `cosmeticEpic`, `cosmeticPack`, the unopened cosmetic packs among them). A bar never written
  reads 0 points; the sizes are `CARD_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE` and `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE`; the next
  step is `COSMETIC_TOKEN_CYCLE` at the stored position modulo its length ([[R1305001]]).
- **Read-only**, like the collection: no ledger row, no other account's values (CLAUDE.md
  rule 7). The two redemptions answer the same shape after they spend, so a client re-renders
  from the answer without a second read.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone shows duplicate protection and its rewards on the pack
screen; the spec shows points, sizes, the next step and the holdings, and this only fixes the
shape they travel in.

Rejected: a read per bar, which would triple the reads for one screen; and folding the
holdings into the inventory's own read, which counts packs and tokens of every kind together.
