title: A transformed Unit keeps its battle position

Since balance patch 1, stated: a Transform that replaces the card with a new instance puts it in the same zone and the same position — an ATK Unit stays ATK, a DEF Unit stays DEF — while damage, tuning and sickness do not come with it. (Vanilla's same-instance scrub is untouched: buffs, damage and granted keywords stay there)
