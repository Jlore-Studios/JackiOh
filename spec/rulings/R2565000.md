Amends [[R2477000]], which priced a start-of-turn or end-of-turn hook by one firing for a card in the AI's own backrow and left a unit's turn hooks and the enemy's backrow out. The AI's evaluation therefore counted Meditative #13 Gatling Pea ("End of turn: Deal 1 damage to the enemy hero. Then this damage permanently goes up by 1") and Core #37 Gravedigger ("Start of turn: Return a random card from your graveyard to hand") as bodies, its own and the enemy's, and `training/improve.md` traces the same loss in game after game: an engine, a unit that grows or makes value every turn, priced at its body and left unanswered. The learned weights know none of the newer sets' cards, so the hand evaluation's price is the only one they get. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** What [[R2477000]] counts, for more cards. At a decision's root, once, on the same probe world (a function of what the seat sees, [[R185]]), the AI fires each start-of-turn and end-of-turn hook of every unit on either field that has one, of every card in its own backrow that has one and of every card in the enemy's backrow that has one and that it can read, and of every Unit and Field Spell in its own hand that has one, a Unit set first by the sandbox's spawn ([[R923001]]) in the zone a summon would take, as [[R2424000]]'s Death probes place one (on a full unit row, the first zone whose pile is a single card is cleared first), and a Field Spell in the zone a set would take. The hand's cards come after the boards', the seat's board before the enemy's. A hook is fired as its controller's turn fires it ([[R62]]) and priced as that controller's evaluation after it less before it, plus `AI_EVAL.next_turn_mana` for each crystal of mana it gained, so the enemy's Gatling Pea is priced by what its hit gives the enemy. It is queued and drained as [[R2477000]] fires a hook ([[R68]]), under its game's pool hold ([[R2201001]]), on the sandbox copy ([[R923000]]) with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), so no step ends the turn ([[R82]]). A card's price is the sum of its hooks'. A card is keyed by its definition and its face, so a copy the search brings later, or the enemy's copy of a card the seat holds, reads the same price, and the first card listed, the seat's own, sets it. `unit_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.turn_hook` of the price for every unit it counts, outside the Brittle share and for either side, and `kill_worth`, which leaves out only a Death's price and a Reborn body's worth, keeps the whole of it in a kill, so removing an enemy engine is worth its price and trading an own one away costs it. `material` adds the same share for each backrow card the seat can read on either side, where [[R2477000]] added it for the seat's own alone. A hook that harms its controller prices below zero: Stinkyscape's Drone Battleship (Meltdown Layer), which takes 6 damage itself at its controller's end of turn, is worth less than its body. Probes spend at most `AI_TURN_HOOK_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes ([[R186]]'s budget), 24 where [[R2477000]] gave 12, one node a firing and one a spawn; the enemy's hooks, which a decision's turn would not otherwise price, would crowd out the hand's at 12. The weight `AI_EVAL.turn_hook` stays 0.5, and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0.

**What is not priced.**
- An enemy card the seat cannot read: a face-down Trap or Field Trap is never probed, so no hidden card changes a decision ([[R185]]); a card the seat can read, a Field Spell or a revealed or face-up trap, is.
- A hook that leaves a prompt open (Core #37 Gravedigger's Radiant face, whose Discover opens one), ends the game or panics, which the probe neither answers nor scores; a panic counts as a failed simulation.
- A card the probes' nodes did not reach, a hand Unit with no zone to place it in, a hand Field Spell on a full backrow, or one the sandbox may not spawn.
- The enemy's hand, and a Unit or Field Spell in either library or graveyard.
- More than one firing, so a growing engine's growth, Gatling Pea's second hit and after, counts only through the first.
- Anything while a prompt is open, which refuses the sandbox's tools, or once the game is over.

**Rejected.** Probing the enemy's face-down cards, which a hidden card would then decide; pricing a hook from the seat's view whoever's it is, which would count the enemy's Mana Well's mana for the seat; a second weight for units apart from `AI_EVAL.turn_hook`, for a price both kinds of card read the same way; a flat price of a unit's body for any unit with a hook, which cannot tell a Gravedigger from a Meltdown Layer; and probing each copy of a card on its own, which spends a node more for the same price.
