Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes IV left M #95 to the builder: "Find a way to nerf what you think out of line modes are by ~20%." No record holds per-mode numbers (a game records the card, not the entry it rolled), so the four entries that hand out the most value at once, for nothing, are taken as out of line: (1) the hand fused into one card plus a copy, (2) two CN cards and (3) two Prime cards, each card at (0), and (4) 4 Armor and a heal of 4. Entries 1 to 3's cards now cost (1) (`CHAOS_MED_COST` 0 → 1), which [[R4154305]] had left alone as "prices are prices": their cards are random, so their price, not their count, is what a step can cut without halving the entry or taking it away, and (1) is the least price that is not free. Entry 4 is cut 4 → 3 and 4 → 3 (`CHAOS_MED_HERO_ARMOR`, `CHAOS_MED_HEAL`), a quarter, the nearest whole step to a fifth. The card's shipped win rate measures the card, not the entry it rolled, and every Call to Chaos edition's sits within two points of it, so it picks out no mode. The Jade Beauty, the Acclaimed pair, the bounce, the CN Golem, the start-of-turn casts and the recursion are left alone. Both faces roll the same table, and the clauses `chaosRolled` names change with it ([[R436]]).

The numbers are the engine's, so the table through v0.6.7k stays as `CHAOS_MED_EFFECTS_V0_6_7K` (entries 1 to 3 at `CHAOS_MED_COST_V0_6_7K` (0), entry 4 at 4 and 4), which the card's `script_v0_6_7k` rolls; the v0.6.6h table keeps its (0) prices through the same builders ([[R767000]]).
