Four destructive actions go at once, with no question first: Delete deck and Delete trio in the deck workshop ([[§9.4]]), Delete in the card creator ([[§12.11]]) and Remove on a friend's row in Play together ([[§9.5]]). The item leaves the screen at once, and a toast says "Deck deleted · Undo", "Trio deleted · Undo", "Card deleted · Undo" or "Friend removed · Undo" for `UNDO_WINDOW_MS` (8 seconds).

**The held request.** The `DELETE` (for a friend, the unfriend) waits for the window to close and is then sent once. It goes at once when another of these deletions takes the toast (one is held at a time, so the first is sent and its toast replaced), when the page hides (`pagehide`, or `visibilitychange` to hidden), as a `keepalive` request so it outlives the page, and when the player leaves the screen. In the workshop it also goes at once before anything that needs the item gone: a new deck or trio, a duplicate and either import, since a held deck still counts against the caps the server keeps. In the creator a new card's first save sends it and waits for its answer first, for the same reason.

**Undo** puts the item back where it was and sends nothing, then or later. A deck or trio comes back open in the editor; a custom card comes back in its place in the list, and opens in the editor if it holds a new card nobody has touched; a friend comes back in their place in the list.

**No resave.** While a deck's or a trio's deletion is held the workshop's store hides it: no list shows it, no trio's slot names a hidden deck, the autosave ([[R256]]) sends no edit of it, and a trio edited meanwhile keeps the hidden deck in its slot. Undo shows it again, every trio slot that held the deck filled as before, and sends the edit the hold kept back. When the window closes the deck is deleted as [[R252]] has it, its trio slots emptying, and the store saves at once.

**Failure.** A held request the server refuses puts the item back and shows the server's words: the workshop's status line and the deck's or trio's own line (a refusal, as [[R256]]'s), the creator's save error, Play together's notice. A deck or trio whose `DELETE` could not reach the server stays deleted and is tried again, as every save is ([[R256]]), so a deletion made offline is not undone by the network. A custom card or a friend whose request fails in any way comes back, since nothing tries those again.

**What still asks.** Block keeps its question on the friend's row, and Concede its confirm, since it is a game action; neither is one of the four. A friendship the held unfriend ends closes its challenges then, as [[R1689000]] has it, not when the row leaves the screen.

The toast is a status (`role="status"`): it takes no focus, its Undo is the keyboard's next stop, it sits inside the safe-area insets and, under Reduce motion (the media query or the settings panel's switch), appears without sliding. Presentation and timing only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): the server decides every deletion as before.

Hearthstone asks before deleting a deck and has no undo; the issue chose an undo toast, so its window and held request follow common practice.

Rejected: a 5-second window, short for a screen reader's user to reach Undo; restoring a deck or trio on every failure, which would undo a deletion made offline; leaving a held deck out of the workshop's caps, which would let it make a deck the server then refuses; sending a request with `keepalive` when the player merely leaves the screen, which a single-page move does not need.
