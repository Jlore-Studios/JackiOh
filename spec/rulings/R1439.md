Meditative #102 Catboy Maid SSR+'s Cry, "Draw 1-2. Add 1-3 to your Jade Counter" (1–6 on the Radiant face; 1-4 and 1–10 until patch v0.3.10, #831), makes two separate rolls of the match rng, in its text's order: first how many cards to draw, 1 or 2, as C+ #53 Book of Tokens rolls its count, then how much to add, uniform over 1–3 (1–6). Both are luck-based rolls ([[§6.1]]) whose better side is the higher number, and each takes the same Lucky: the Lucky on the card as it stands, printed as a Nerf or a Buff has moved it plus any given ([[R386]], [[R1438]]), and its controller's Luck ([[R987]]). So the base face's Lucky 1 makes each roll two draws keeping the higher, the Radiant face's Lucky 2 three, and a Lucky given or a Luck adds one draw to each roll. Both rolls are made before either result applies; then the draws are [[§2.4]]'s, the hand cap burning a card past it and an empty deck dealing fatigue, and the amount goes through the Jade Counter's own add, so reaching 5 summons a Jade Beauty and reaching 10 makes it Radiant, both inside the one add ([[R962]]). The ranges are the printed text's, not declared numbers, so a Nerf or a Buff moves only the Lucky. The card prints Lucky, so it is a Luck-based card ([[R1444]]).
