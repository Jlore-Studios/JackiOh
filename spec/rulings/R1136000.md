"Fill your board" summons into every empty, unreserved unit zone of its side, left to right, a Locked one included: a Lock refuses only plays ([[R688]]), and a fill is a summon. Before patch v0.3.Y a fill skipped a Locked zone ([[R64]]), so Meditative #56 House Party cast after Meditative #98 Showdown summoned one Right-house defender, into the lane Showdown left open, where five were expected; C+ #46.1's fill, one summon per zone, already took a Locked one. It holds for every unit-row fill, `fill_board` and `fill_board_random` through `fill_board_zones` (#22, #60, #62, C+ #2, M #49.1's Viruses, M #51, M #56, M #97.9), so The Great Wall's Locks ([[R1260]]) no longer keep a fill out. The Lock stays on the zone a fill enters; a zone reserved for a Reborn unit or an animated card's return still takes nothing ([[R64]], [[R383]]); a fill with no zone to take still draws nothing ([[R129]]). Two rules stay: Meditative #26's backrow fill still skips a Locked zone ([[R942]]), and #60 Bear Honeypot still waits while its controller has no empty, unlocked, unreserved unit zone ([[R430]]), filling the Locked ones too once it fires. It amends [[R64]], [[R405]], [[R1080]], [[R1260]] and [[R1261]].
