Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Before issue #3338 one shared black hole (`stinkyscape.ts`'s Terminus
family) drew Stinkyscape #4 Olivian Expanse and its six Terminus tokens, Stinkyscape #154 Dice of Doom drew the
set's planet, and Core #100 Ceaseless Void the procedural painter's Unit figure. The owner asked that each of the
nine draw a picture of its own (part of #3330).

**The Stinkyscape eight.** `TERMINUS_KINDS` maps each of the eight by id to a kind of its own, which
`stinkySubject` returns before it reads a name or a tag (Dice of Doom prints no Terminus tag, so its id is what
finds it): Olivian Expanse `expanse`, an endless violet expanse with the Terminus cards as distant falling stars;
#4.1 Farewell `lamp`, an empty table under a single lamp, every card gone; #4.2 KY's Last Exam `exam`, one desk in
an exam hall at the end of time under one impossible problem chalked across the sky; #4.3 Event Horizon `edge`, a
light caught at the edge of a black hole; #4.4 Last Word `crown`, a hand catching a falling crown; #4.5 1v1 Final
Destination `destination`, one lane of light over an abyss with a hero at each end; #4.6 The Big Crunch `crunch`,
the universe collapsing to a point; and Dice of Doom `d100`, a hundred-sided die on black velvet, 67 glowing
faintly on one face, its digits drawn as strokes and never as text. Each is drawn in the set's style from the
subject's stream alone, so Alt art lays each out anew ([[R2170000]]), and none draws the shared black hole's
accretion disk (`TERMINUS_DISK_BACK`), which a Terminus card the table does not list still draws.

**Ceaseless Void.** It is a procedural card, so it takes a composition of its own, `"void"`, which no type has:
`CARD_COMPOSITIONS` names it and `compositionOf(defId, type)` gives every other card its type's composition, as
`compositionFor` did, wherever a procedural picture is drawn (the card's art, the patch notes' pictures and the
replay export's). The `voidGiant` composer draws a black giant's silhouette in one of its poses, rimmed in the
glow behind it, with stars falling into its outline.

**Radiant.** Each Radiant face keeps its base face's geometry and gilds its paint as its set's painter gilds every
Radiant face, inside [[R2725004]]'s Radiant rules, so the nine draw eighteen pictures, each apart from the others.

**The fixtures.** [[R503]] holds a shipped card to the picture it shipped with, so this issue records the eight
Stinkyscape cards' hashes in `stinkyscape-art-v0.5.0.json` again and Ceaseless Void's in `core-art-v0.1.json` and
`art-before-2724.json` ([[R2724000]]'s), each test's comment naming the change. Holding each picture to its
rarity's floor waits for issue #3331.

Rejected: tinting the shared black hole a colour per card, which would leave them one picture; a Stinkyscape painter
for Ceaseless Void, which is a Core card and draws the procedural set's art; and keying the eight by name, which a
rename or a fusion would move.

Amended by [[R3398000]] (issue #3398): Ceaseless Void's Alt art draws an alt scene recipe of its own, the void before it wakes, without its giant; its normal picture keeps the void composition and its giant.

Amended by [[R3406000]] (issue #3406): Dice of Doom draws an alt scene recipe under its Alt art seed, the die a part of the scene kit tumbling down an observatory's steps; its normal face and every other seed keep its own painter's picture.

Amended by [[R3394000]] (issue #3394): Olivian Expanse draws an alt scene recipe under its Alt art seed, a survey ship's cockpit facing a flat olive-grey nothing; its normal face and every other seed keep its own painter's violet expanse.
