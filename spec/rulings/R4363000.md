While `navigator.connection.saveData` is true the music player fetches no background music bytes:
neither track preloads nor intro preloads start, and the ids already held stay queued ([[§10.11]]).
The current track and a played card's intro or sting still fetch on demand, and the held preloads
drain on the next pump after the signal clears. Hearthstone makes no save-data decision; this
follows the browser signal per [[R2116000]]'s precedent, as the voice prefetch already does per
[[R4316000]]. Rejected: prefetching anyway, since the roughly 51 MB music folder is what the player
asked to avoid.
