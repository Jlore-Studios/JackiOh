"Summon two random Radiant Units, make them GIGA" is a `giga` rider on `summon_random_with`: the card drawn
enters GIGA whatever it prints, by a granted GIGA keyword that the field placement stamps as it stamps a printed
one ([[R3546002]]), in the leftmost fit of four free zones ([[R3546000]], [[R64]]). With no fit nothing lands and,
as for every random summon, nothing is drawn ([[R129]]): the draw is made only when a zone fits a card of the pool
(a flier's backrow stand never saves one for a GIGA). The second Unit finds the first's four zones taken.

Rejected: granting GIGA after the card landed, which would leave a one-zone card that no longer fits its stamp.
