Core #42 Eugenics' "Exile all cards in your Deck that cost (2) or less" reads each library card's cost per [[R65]] as the clause resolves (`exile_matching` with `maxCost`): an X card out of play counts as 0 and an embiggen card at its base price, so both are exiled, and a card's cost changes count. Each card is its own exile ([[R135]]). The chance then rolls over the cards left, one roll per card ([[R60]]): 30% on the base face; the Radiant face makes every card left Radiant and prints no Lucky, since nothing is left to chance, so Eugenics leaves the Luck-based pool ([[R1444]]).
