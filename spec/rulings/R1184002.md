The practice AI answers a question of a sequence ([[R1184000]]) at once, before any search, with the option whose words are what the statement it is shown works out to (`ky_competition::worked_answer`: `a + b = ?` or `a − b = ?`), as a player with a pencil would; it reads the redacted prompt, never the key ([[R185]], [[R465]]), and two states that differ only in the key get the same answer. It never answers Stop: a miss would end the sequence with the streak a Stop keeps, so answering is never worse. Why not search: the redacted copy judges no answer right ([[R465]]), so a search would score the four answers and Stop alike and spend a whole decision's budget on a guess, up to 30 times in one turn, against R186's two seconds a decision and the turn clock's 75 ([[R79]]); worked out, each answer costs one redaction, so the AI answers every question inside its budget and reaches the cap of 30. A statement it cannot work out goes to the search as any prompt does. It is the AI's answer only; the random policy and a timeout answer as [[R1184001]] says. Since #1563 it keeps its worked answer 85% of the time (93% on the Radiant face) and otherwise gives a wrong one ([[R1563005]]), so it reaches the cap only rarely.
