A layered summon (`summonPile`) summons its Units into one unit zone in the order listed, the first at the bottom and the last on top. The first takes the zone a summon would: the named lane when it is empty and unreserved, Locked or not ([[R47]], [[R688]]), else the leftmost open zone ([[R64]]), else the leftmost empty Locked one. Each next card is summoned onto the pile as its top, Stack or not, so only the last acts and the rest lie dormant in order ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]). With no such zone nothing is summoned. Each card is its own summon: no Cry ([[R1]]), its own `summoned`, summoning sick, and Radiant when the summon is; a card that is not a Unit is passed over. A reaction to a lower card's `summoned` resolves once the pile stands and finds that card dormant, so an effect aimed at it does nothing ([[R13]]). An "all" effect reaches only the top, and when the top leaves, the next card resumes with nothing the top took. A question a card asks as it arrives ([[R151]]) pauses the rest, which resumes onto the same zone ([[R113]]).
