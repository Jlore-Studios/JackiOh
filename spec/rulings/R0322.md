While a lesson is still to do, the lesson path's header offers Hide tutorial. It folds the whole path to one line in its place ("Tutorial hidden", the lessons done, and Show tutorial, which brings the path back). Both are buttons that take keyboard focus, focus moves to the one that undoes the press, and both are 44 px touch targets that fit a phone's width. The choice is stored with the progress, on the device and on the account ([[R321]]), and holds whatever the progress: a path hidden before its last lesson stays hidden after it. A finished path offers no Hide, because it already folds to its header by itself ([[§9.10]]), and its Show lessons opens it for the session as before
