A game with a friend, 1v1 or co-op ([[§9.5]], [[§13.9]]), starts in a private lobby, `/lobby/:code`, as a party screen does: its host, one guest seat, the host's settings, each member's pick and Ready, and the host's Start ([[R3730001]] to [[R3730004]]). Its code is a room code's: 6 characters, never one a 1v1 or co-op room holds when it is made, and no room opens under a code a lobby holds.

- `GET /api/lobbies/:code` shows the lobby to its members and to anyone holding the code while the guest's seat is empty: each seat's player (`name#tag` and presence), the host's crown, and its deck state, choosing, chosen or ready. Rule 7 holds: no card leaves the server, and a 1v1 opponent's deck is shown by its state alone, never by name, as the queue never names it; a co-op teammate's is shown. Missing, expired, full, started and blocked are one 404 to anyone not in it ([[§9.4]]).
- `POST /api/lobbies/:code/enter` takes the empty seat. A block either way between the host and the caller refuses it as an unknown code, and a guest a block now separates from the host is dropped from the seat as the lobby is next read ([[R1689000]]'s reading of a block).
- A member holds no seat of the seat ledger until the Start ([[R1523000]]): the lobby is a place to wait, not a game, so its members may queue or play elsewhere, and the Start checks both again ([[R3730002]]).
- The lobby is a row of `public.lobbies` (migration 0090), the server's alone, and every change is a compare-and-set on its revision.
- Every change is pushed to its members, old and new, as a `{type: "lobby", code}` frame over `/ws/social` ([[R3214000]]), which carries nothing of the lobby; the tab reads it, and polls it while it has no socket.

Rejected: a pending seat for each member, which would hold the queue shut for a lobby's whole 15 minutes; a lobby kept in the server's memory, which a deploy would lose.

Amends [[R3214000]], whose list of the social socket's frames gains `lobby`.

Amended by [[R3731000]] (issue #3731): a lobby's 2v2 format has four seats in two teams (`host`, `guest`, `guest2`, `guest3`); an entrant takes the first empty one, a visitor reads it while any is empty, a teammate's deck is named and the other team's shown by its state alone, and a block with any member keeps a visitor out and drops the later of two members it separates.
