Mass Driver's "in N turns" is the start of the Nth own turn ([[R1181000]]): two random S+ Minerals
at (0) and a Resource ([[R1840002]], [[R3549001]]), Radiant ones on the Radiant face, from an
unwatched delay, so the delivery lands after the Driver is destroyed. Rejected: losing the delivery
when the Driver leaves, which would punish protecting it.

Until issue #3828 it printed Flexible and 0/6 (0/12 Radiant) and, played into a unit zone, was a
Unit there ([[R3543001]]); since [[R3828000]] it has neither and keeps to its backrow.
