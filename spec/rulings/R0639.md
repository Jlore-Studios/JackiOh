The device keeps its player's tally ([[§9.11]]): games finished, won, lost and drawn, and per card the games it was seen in, the times the player played it, the times the opponent played it where the player could read it, the times a copy of the player's own was destroyed and the times one of the opponent's was. A game counts when its view says it is over, once, from the viewer's seat; online matches and practice games count, a tutorial lesson and a hotseat game do not. It is read off the `PlayerView`s the board is handed and the events new with each (the view's window slides, so only what the last view lacked is counted), so a card the view hides (the sentinel, a face-down trap, the opponent's hand, the viewer's own library) is never named. It lives in `localStorage` and nowhere else, tolerates a missing or hand-edited value, and is clearable. Once the device has logged `ROTATION_MIN_GAMES` (10) games the homescreen's hand is dealt from every shipped set's non-token cards, weighted so a card that prints at full size is `FEATURE_WEIGHT_PLAIN` (4) times as likely as one that needs shrinking (never excluded), and swaps one slot every `ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS` (7 s) for a card of the same rarity it is not showing, from that pool; below the threshold [[R374]]'s Core deal stands, and since [[R704]] it swaps too, among Core's cards. The hand holds still under reduced motion, under a pointer or focus, with a card open and while the page is hidden. A face opens its card's detail view on a click, a tap, Enter or Space. The weighting draws from the page's injected random source like [[R374]]'s deal. Presentation and bookkeeping only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
