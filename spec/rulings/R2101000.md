While a player's ranked win streak ([[R606]]'s, the server's: ended by a loss, a draw leaving it) is `RANK_STREAK_FLAME_LENGTH` (3) or more, a flame with its count shows beside their rank on their profile and after their name on their seat of a ranked match's line. Below that, as after a loss, nothing shows. Both seats of `GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks` carry their `streak` off their season row (0 with none), as `GET /api/ranked` carries the caller's ([[R612]]), a bot's like a person's ([[R640000]]). Only a ranked match's line draws it: no unranked line, practice, tutorial, Duos or lobby screen does.
