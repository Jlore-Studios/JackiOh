[[§14.6]] says the Chase Shard bar fills one per cosmetic pack opened and buys a chase when full.
The issue fixes what it buys and when:

- **It counts one per opening**, in the opening's own transaction ([[R1306003]]): the
  `ChaseShardBar` counter gains 1, reason `pack_open`, one row carrying the opening's log. It stops
  at `CHASE_SHARD_PACKS`: a full bar holds, so an opening adds nothing and writes no row. It never
  counts past full, which "then empties" would contradict.
- **A full bar buys** one Legendary or Mythic cosmetic of the player's choice, on
  `POST /api/cosmetic-packs/chase` with `{ cosmeticId, cardId? }`: a per-card one for a card the
  account owns (a full art on its own card alone, [[R1313000]]) or an account one, which the
  player does not already own. A chase is allowed here, and a starter or default is owned already.
  Nothing pays for it automatically; the player chooses.
- **It empties**: the purchase writes the cosmetic, reason `chase_shard`, and takes the bar to 0
  with one counter row of `-bar`, reason `chase_shard`, in one transaction under the account's lock
  ([[R1301003]]).
- **It refuses with 409, writing nothing**, in this order: a bar not full; a cosmetic below
  Legendary; a per-card cosmetic for no card or a card not owned, or an account one given a card;
  and one already owned. An unknown cosmetic id is 400.
- **No Legendary or Mythic cosmetic exists** that a player can own yet, so a full bar holds, and
  buys nothing, until one does.
- **`GET /api/cosmetic-packs`** reads `{ shardBar: { points, size }, unopened }`: the bar and the
  cosmetic packs unopened. The purchase answers the same.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone has no equivalent bar; this follows the issue.

Rejected: counting past full, which the bar's emptying contradicts; and paying the cosmetic
automatically when the bar fills, which takes the player's choice away.
