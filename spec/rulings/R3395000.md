- **A Field Spell** (The Greatest Heist, Rod of Ages, The Narcor Project) lands face up with its own entrance, plays its text through its recipe and stands in its zone under a signature look of its own.
- **A Spell** (Imitated Instrumentality, Drone Core VIII, Drone Core IX, Great Paperclip Theory) casts through its own recipe, every particle in its cast's entry so the cast fits the Spell's ceiling; Imitated Instrumentality's `magiBoot` grows from its flourish ([[R2797000]]): three core silhouettes behind its caster's hero, then each MAGI booting in its zone in its own hue, beside the MAGI's own entrance ([[R3407000]]).
- **A Mythic** (Tylannt, Rod of Ages, Great Paperclip Theory, Paragon-X, Prototype-X2) takes over the board in its own look with its own art ([[R3395001]]); its entrance starts after the takeover's hold (the entry's `leadMs`, [[R3405000]]), its figure, burst, ring and shake landing as the takeover ends rather than unseen behind it.
- **Its sound**: each sounds an effect of its own once (`audio/signatureCues.ts`), a Unit's as it lands face up in a unit zone, a Field Spell's as it lands face up in the backrow ([[R3407001]]'s moment, its entrance's), a Spell's as its play is read, after the issue's Sound column, within 1.6 s and gain 0.8.

Each recipe keys on its own play or hook (or [[R3389000]]'s claim of an event naming its card), its own public id and how many events of a kind it has seen, never on a card's definition: a card added to a hand flies as a card back to its owner's hand pile, a Discover's offer and a fused pick show nothing, a Resource's glint plays on its owner's seat alone, and from the other seat no beat names a hidden card ([[R202]]).
