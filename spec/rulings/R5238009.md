Felinors vs Humans #82 answers each death of a Shrinking Felinor for the rest of the game. The engine's death watch ([[R1882004]]) was built only by Stinkyscape #150's Call to Chaos.

**The rule.** `watch_deaths { tag, step, label, hook?, data? }` places that death watch on its controller, expiry never, its `label` the badge on its side ([[R169]]): after each `destroyed` of a card whose definition prints `tag`, either side's, the card's `step` re-enters (under `hook`, the resume table by default) as a queued trigger once the state check that killed it has run, so the dead card's own Death has run first. As every death watch's, the step runs as the dead card's controller as it died, with no `self` ([[R127]]); its data holds the dead card (`instanceId`), that controller (`watcher`) and the player who placed the watch (`holder`), so a step that means its holder reads it there. Several stack, each its own.
