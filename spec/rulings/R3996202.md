Issue #3996's "+1/+1 → +1 health, Radiant +2/+2 → +2 health" rewrites the gain of C+ #26 Tommy Tempo's Cry ([[R831010]]): "Cry: Lose all your mana. Gain +{per} Health per mana lost.", `per` still 1 (Radiant 2). The mana lost is read as before, what is left once its price is paid, all of it on a free cast on draw ([[R1]], [[R70]]), and the gain is a permanent buff of its health and max health, as "+N Health" is everywhere; its Attack stays as printed. So drawn at the start of your turn it is a 4/8 (Radiant 8/16), and played from a hand with 4 mana a 4/5 (Radiant 8/10). Through v0.6.5b it gained +`per`/+`per`, which `script_v0_6_5b` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).

Superseded by Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI (issue #4440, [[R4440054]]): the Cry gains +`per`/+`per` again. This ruling describes the replays after v0.6.5b through v0.6.9n, whose script is kept as `script_v0_6_9n` ([[R767000]]).
