An "Animated on your turn" card standing a Unit away from its home lane ([[R383]]) whose home zone Slashed Dream Cries in Red (S+ #78) then voids ([[R3554002]]) has a home that takes nothing. At its controller's cleanup it does not return: it stays a Unit in the unit zone it stands in, and its home stays held for it, as one that lies beneath a Stack does. A zone the slash voided accepts no card stacked onto it either: no move lifts a card off the field toward it, and no Stack card ([[§6.1]]), which may enter an occupied zone, is offered it as a play's zone, as no other play is (fuzz seed 9484: Meditative #71.1 The Cane, animated into unit lane 3, had its home in backrow lane 2 voided, and its return removed it from the field into a zone that refused it, losing the card with no event).

Hearthstone semantics: a card with nowhere to go stays where it is.

Rejected: letting the voided lane's homes go so the card returns to its controller's leftmost open backrow zone, which would move a card its own text says goes home to a zone its text never names.
