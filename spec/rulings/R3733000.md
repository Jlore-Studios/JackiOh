`/account` was one column: who is signed in, both ladders, the record, a "Manage sign-in & account"
disclosure holding the rare options, and Sign out. It had no portrait and no history. Issue #3733 makes it
two things, who the player is (a profile) and what can be changed (settings), laid out as iPadOS Settings is,
to the conventions of `docs/polish/10-menus.md` ([[R3728000]]).

**The sections.** A profile header sits over a list of six sections: Overview, Match history, Sign-in &
security, Notifications, Privacy & data and Delete account (`routes/accountSections.ts`). Each is at
`/account?section=<id>` (`overview`, `history`, `security`, `notifications`, `privacy`, `delete`), and only the
open section's panel mounts, so a screen that asks nothing of the network until asked keeps doing so. A URL
that names no section, or an unknown one, shows Overview. A pending account has no record, history or
notifications ([[§9.4]]) and sees Overview, Sign-in & security, Privacy & data and Delete account. Sign out
sits at the foot of the list.

**Wide and phone.** Over 600 px the list sits beside the open section. At 600 px and under `/account` is the
list alone and `?section=` shows one section alone, so Back returns to the list (a list item is a link that
pushes a history entry), and the section's own "‹ Account" link is the way to the list from a deep link. A
provider link's outcome ([[R3025000]]) or the provider waiting to be linked ([[R3147002]]) opens Sign-in &
security once, on mount, by a replacing navigation, so the list is not out of reach behind it.

**What each holds.** The header: the portrait, `name#tag` with Copy, when the account was made, and each
ladder's crest, division, season record ([[R2255000]]) and win-streak flame. Overview: the season's and the
all-time record, the badges, the Leaderboard link and the account's email and status. Match history: the
newest `ACCOUNT_HISTORY_ROWS` online games of `GET /api/replays`, each with its result, mode, opponent and
date worded as `/replays` words them (`routes/replayWords.ts`, so a ladder bot stays a player, [[R1452]],
and a gone account is "a deleted account"), and Watch replay. Sign-in & security: the username ([[R1435]]), the
email change, two-step, the linked accounts and the secret ID behind Reveal ([[R1300000]]). Notifications:
`/daily`'s own settings (`NotificationSettings`). Privacy & data: what is kept, the Privacy policy link and the
watch rules ([[R2615000]]). Delete account: the typed confirmation, as before, in the danger style
([[R3221000]]). The screen still links no other menu but the Leaderboard and `/replays` ([[R1646000]]).

**The header's portrait is the last game's.** The portraits are account cosmetics ([[R1827000]]), but an
account does not equip one: the one a game wears is the deck's pick ([[R641]]). The header shows the portrait
the player's seat wore in their newest kept online game, and the default, `vanilla`, before any. Rejected: a new
account-level equipped-portrait cosmetic, which is a server, cosmetics and shop change of its own; and the
portrait of the most-played deck, which needs a read of every deck for a picture.

**What it changes of R2255000 and R2778000.** The rank block is gone as a block: the Rank heading and the
Record heading with it. [[R2778000]]'s "Constructed rank, record and badges, then the All Random ones" is now
split between two places: the header holds each ladder's rank line and season record ([[R2255000]]'s "This
season (ranked): W–L–D", still left out at 0 games), and Overview holds the badges and, under "This season",
each ladder's record again. [[R2255000]]'s all-time Record is Overview's "All time", the same figures under a
new heading. When a ranked read fails the screen shows no rank line, season record or badges and no heading
over where they were: the header carries only the player's name, portrait and "Member since" and Overview
carries "All time" alone, with no alert, as before.

**Member since.** `GET /api/profile` carries `createdAt`, the epoch milliseconds the profile was made; an older
server omits it and the header then shows no "Member since".

**Watch replay opens `/replays`, not the one game.** `/replays` has no URL for a single game, so the link goes
to the list that holds it. Rejected: a `?match=` deep link, which needs `/replays` to open a match from its URL
and is a change of its own.
