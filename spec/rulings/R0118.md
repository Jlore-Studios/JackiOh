id: R118
title: A trap's prompt does not eat the play's Cry
cards: "#41, #85, all Cry units; R174, R427"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/rulings_c.rs
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/rulings_config.rs

A trap that interrupts a play resolves to completion first ([[§10.3]]), and the play then resumes at the step after the one that paused, so the Cry still fires — exactly once ([[R1]]). A Cry is lost only where a trap has taken the card off the field before it resolves ([[R174]]), which the resolving check already covers; Sheepish no longer does, since it waits for the played Unit to resolve ([[R427]]), and a counter stops the play before the card is placed ([[§10.5]])
