Stinkyscape #146 Flash Freeze's "Freeze all permanents" Freezes every Unit, the only permanents a Freeze reaches ([[§6.1]] Frozen): the base face both sides', the Radiant face the enemy's only. Cast on draw ([[§6.2]]) it is cast at no cost and counted as played when drawn, and its player draws again ([[R40]], [[R70]]), inside [[R58]]'s chain cap and never dealt in the setup ([[R635]]); played from the hand at (0) it does the same. Each Freeze is [[R1174000]]'s, so a Unit Frozen on its controller's turn loses that turn's attack: drawn at the start of its player's turn, their own Units lose the attack.
