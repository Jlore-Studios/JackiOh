The issue asked that Animated and the card's foil, finish and border layer on each new version, that
Signature go on neither ([[R2949000]]), and that Immersive's motion take motion slots
(`COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX`), so a grid of cards stays smooth, and hold still under reduced motion
and with Animated foil off.

**Immersive's depth.** While its face holds a motion slot (`data-immersive-motion="animated"`), the
near layer ([[R2944001]]) shifts against its still, hazed backdrop with the pointer's place in the
viewport, or the device's tilt (`deviceorientation`: left and right from flat, forward and back from
`IMMERSIVE_TILT_REST_DEG`, the far shift `IMMERSIVE_TILT_RANGE_DEG` past it), up to
`IMMERSIVE_SHIFT_PCT` of the window each way, and breathes, swelling to `IMMERSIVE_BREATH_SCALE` and
settling over `IMMERSIVE_BREATH_S` each way. It moves by transform alone, inside the motion gate, and
only the near layer moves: a moving backdrop would show the window's edge. The shift follows the
pointer's place in the viewport rather than in the card, so it reads no layout and works though the
face takes no pointer; a listener writes two custom properties and nothing else. A device that asks
leave to report its tilt (a phone's browser may) is not asked here: the pointer, or a touch, drives
the depth there.

**One slot a face.** Immersive's depth takes a motion slot as a moving foil or Animated's art does
([[R2715001]]); on a face wearing Animated too, the art's own motion and the depth move in that one
slot together, so past `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX` faces the rest hold still, depth and art alike.
Only a face whose depth holds its slot listens to the pointer or the tilt. Under reduced motion (the
setting or the system's), with Animated foil off and on a lazy grid face, it holds no slot and its
near layer rests at its window's middle, unscaled.

**Animated** moves the version's own picture ([[R2715001]]): Prismatic's chromed one, whose layers
move under its filter, and on Immersive both layers, the backdrop's in the haze and the subject's on
the near layer, which share the card's one motion and so move together. It is offered by its own
offer, so it layers on a new version wherever both are offered. Without Animated, Prismatic holds
still, as part 4's chases do ([[R2943002]]): a chase's own loop is a foil's, finish's, border's,
playmat's, backdrop's or card back's ([[R1953001]]), and Immersive's is its depth.

**A foil, a finish and a border** wear on each as on the normal face ([[R1295000]]). On Prismatic a
worn border keeps its own edge and bezel: the rainbow edge and chrome bezel are drawn only on a base
face wearing no border. Immersive draws no frame of its own, so a border's and a Radiant face's stand;
its foil's sheen covers the window, and the near layer takes none, which would lay a second sheen over
the first.

**Signature** goes on neither. It is worn only beside the Full art version and Animated
([[R2949000]]), so its own rule refuses it here and no mechanism of this part's is needed, as in parts
2 to 4: a `PUT /api/card-picks` naming it beside a new version is the 400 R2949000 gives, writing
nothing; the Collection marks it waiting until the card wears Full art and Animated; and a face
wearing a new version never signs. No other pairing is ruled out: each new version shows every other
layer, and nothing is hidden, unreadable or loses a Radiant mark, so no `rulesOut` list is added.

Amends [[R2725003]] (the layering on part 5's versions).
