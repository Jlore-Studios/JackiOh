Every target declaration says whether it harms ("harm") or helps ("help"), harmful by default (`TargetDecl.aim`). A cast that targets enemies (C+ #38.1 Solarius Prime, C+ #40 Appropriations' Education Books through the `targetEnemies` enchantment, the `targetEnemies` cost rule) aims each target pick by that aim: a harmful pick narrows to enemies when one is legal, a helpful one to friends when one is legal; with too few of either, every option stays. Jogg's Box and other truly random casts carry no `targetEnemies` and pick as before
