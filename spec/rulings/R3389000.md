Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. After Hearthstone, where every Legendary has an animation of its own and wears its look while it holds, a signature ([[R3332000]]) whose kind is a `{ bespoke }` recipe grows these, so that every Classic Legendary and Mythic plays its own ([[R3389001]]):

- **Its aura.** A signature's `{ bespoke }` may carry an `aura: { look, palette?, scope }` beside its recipe and frame ([[R2787000]]); any other `{ bespoke }` or `{ setPiece }` still holds nothing but its key and its frame. A Unit's aura, a hooks entry's or a signature's, may now be `zone` (never `lane`), and it stands on its own unit zone while the Unit the view shows face up holds it, keyed `unit-<instanceId>`; a hidden Unit, and a Field card standing in a unit zone ([[R383]]), wear none, and what the Unit's play installs wears no second copy of it. Nine signature looks join the nine shared ones (`AURA_SIGNATURE_LOOKS`), each a card's own, each within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`, and the loader fails one on any entry that is no signature. A card's own aura, a signature's among them, replaces the default tile aura ([[R3342000]]).
- **What it claims.** While its card's play or hook resolves, its recipe claims that window's events, as every recipe does ([[R2784000]], [[R2797000]]). When the play resolving draws nothing of an event, a signature's recipe may claim it too: first that of the innermost readable play still open around the one resolving whose card has a signature recipe (a cast its play makes: Spell Tyrant's), then that of the card the event names as its source (a `redirected`'s or a `countered`'s `byInstanceId`, an `activated`'s, a `questProgressed`'s or a `questCompleted`'s `instanceId`), found face up on the board the entry is planned against or in a readable `summoned`, `cardPlayed`, `hookResolving` or `trapFired` the planner remembers. A whole pile's one entry, which plays its one wave and no event's row, asks the readable play resolving's signature recipe about its first event, and its cues play beside the wave. None of it reads a hidden card ([[R202]]): a hidden play, a face-down card and a sentinel landing key none, and no cue names a card, a card it moves being a card back.
- **Its landing.** An entrance is told when its card lands after any hold (`landMs`: a Mythic's takeover's, [[R2722001]]), so a Mythic Field Spell's own entrance lands with its takeover, and a Field Spell's entrance, its takeover's included, centres on the backrow zone it lands in face up in the same entry, as a Unit's does on its unit zone, never on one set face down. A Spell's, a Trap's or a Field Trap's signature `{ bespoke }` is a cast of its own and stands in for the shared Legendary or Mythic rays, gold burst and ring, as a Unit's or a Field Spell's own entrance does; a Mythic's takeover stays.
- **Its takeover.** A Mythic signature may name a takeover look of its own; In Too Deep's is `abyss`, a spiral shaft plunging into violet dark, black-blue water rushing down it and shrine lights in rings, its tint its own (`TAKEOVER_TINTS.abyss`). Under Reduce Motion the still card stands in its card's own look (`data-takeover-look`), drawn at rest.
- **Its sound.** Each signature lays a bed of its own (`SIGNATURE_BEDS`, at most `SIGNATURE_BED_MAX_STEPS` of the existing effects, each at its share of the entry) across the entry its card makes its entrance in, sent as that entry starts, beside its intro sting ([[R3332002]]) and its events' own sounds, so it stays inside the voices' cap ([[§10.11]]).

Every beat still lives in its entries, inside its tier's budget ([[R3332000]], [[R200]]), so a tap, the effects speed, the intensity, Big moments and Reduce Motion act on it as on every cue ([[R201]], [[R2785000]]). Rejected: a separate dispatch per card for the beats no window carries (Palantir's draw limit, Nature Titan's attack, Big Max's blows), which would key on events that name no card; a Unit's aura over its lane, which would cover the other side's zone the Unit does not hold; and a sound file per signature, which would add assets past the audio budgets. Two choices are a person's to widen or narrow: a `zone` aura is allowed on any Unit, not only on a signature's, and a Spell's or a Trap's own cast drops the shared Legendary rays.
