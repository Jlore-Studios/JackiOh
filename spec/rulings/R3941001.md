[[R1017000]] gave a face no wording could fit a short form: its first lines, its text up to a colon, or its whole text cut by the rules box at its last line with "…", which 135 faces did. Issue #3941 asked that the in-game text be its own, shorter than the text a player reads in the Almanac, so each of those faces but five now prints words written for it, a form `{ "en": … }` in `jackioh-shared`'s `data/short-forms.json` beside the line counts, "colon" and "clamp". The face model fills the words as it fills a face's whole text, from a definition whose faces print them: the printed numbers in the collection and the view's in play ([[R386]]), and on a Radiant face the marks of what its base face's written words lack, or its whole text where the base face has none ([[R277]]). The words fit the face's limit, name only numbers the face declares, and say what the face does in fewer words, never a rule its whole text lacks; the whole text stays where [[R1017000]] put it, in the hover preview, the touch sheet and the detail view. A face that prints other words in play (a Buff's moved number, a copy, a Vanilla) takes no short form, as before. The words were written for the build's own catalog text, so a face whose text is not that text under the same id (a shipped patch's older face on the Patch notes page, a replay folded under an older catalog, [[R767000]]) clamps as it did. The five that still clamp are the staged Quests' (S #7's Radiant face and both of S #112's, which print one stage, [[R2450000]]) and S #116 Megabase's two, whose keyword line alone fills four of the six lines. A written form may carry its Chinese words (`zh`); a face printed in Chinese without them clamps, as it did. Presentation only: no rule reads card text.
