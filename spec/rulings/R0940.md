"Immune to tribal tag based hate" (Meditative #25 Blue-Eyes White Felinor, MD-B1) is a §6.1 keyword, read through the layers as Immune to Spells is, protecting the card only while it acts on the field: a card in a hand or a deck is no unit, and its printed keyword protects nothing there. A harmful effect whose card filter names a tribal tag ([[R1424]]'s `TRIBAL_TAGS`) in `tags` or `not_tags` can neither pick the card (a declaration or target prompt of `aim: harm`, [[R656]]) nor reach it through a board scope (`cards_in_scope_aimed`, `adjacent_to_aimed`) or a card scope (`CardScopeOptions.aim`). A scope with no tribal tag still reaches it, and so does every helpful effect: M #34 Gaokao still Buffs a Made-CN Felinor while its destroy passes it by. M #52 Economic Anxiety's combat bonus reads the defender's tags, so it never applies against the card, whatever the attacker's tags (an untagged attacker included): its hook asks `immune_to_tribal_hate`, and the yellow `conditionTargets` ([[R1121]]) follows it.
