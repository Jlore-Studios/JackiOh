"Immune to tribal tag based hate" (Meditative #25 Blue-Eyes White Felinor, MD-B1) is a §6.1 keyword, read through the layers as Immune to Spells is, protecting the card only while it acts on the field: a card in a hand or a deck is no unit, and its printed keyword protects nothing there. A harmful effect whose card filter names a tribal tag ([[R1424]]'s `TRIBAL_TAGS`) in `tags` or `not_tags` can neither pick the card (a declaration or target prompt of `aim: harm`, [[R656]]) nor reach it through a board scope (`cards_in_scope_aimed`, `adjacent_to_aimed`) or a card scope (`CardScopeOptions.aim`). A scope with no tribal tag still reaches it, and so does every helpful effect: M #34 Gaokao still Buffs a Made-CN Felinor while its destroy passes it by. MB18 reads `tribal_hate_cannot_reach` for M #52.
