Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: M #18 Expedition12, "Make a card in your hand Radiant" becomes "Make a card in your hand Radiant, then Buff it." on both faces. "Buff it" is one application of [[§6.3]]'s Buff ([[R386]]) on the declared pick ([[R81]]), right after it turns Radiant, so the Buff draws from what the Radiant face can change. The Radiant face's Cleave lands the whole effect on the cards beside the pick, as [[R1563001]] reads a Spell's Cleave on a hand pick ("the effect also lands on the cards beside the pick"), so each neighbour is made Radiant and then Buffed once, the pick first, then the card before it, then the card after it; a neighbour already Radiant, which the Make Radiant leaves as it is, still takes its Buff, as Hearthstone's "give a minion +2/+2 and Taunt" still buffs a minion that has Taunt. With no pick the Spell Buffs nothing and still resolves. Replays of the versions after v0.4.0f through v0.6.13h, which Buffed none, fold under `script_v0_6_13h` ([[R767000]]).
