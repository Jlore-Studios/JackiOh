Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Every Anomaly Found! drew one shared spiral, whatever board it held. An Anomaly Found! in play that holds a board, its label ([[R2247000]]), now draws that board's own picture, one of ten in the set's deep-space style: the Wormhole's rings folding into a tunnel of light, the Battlefields' wrecks around the Battleship's silhouette, each Asteroid Field's own rock (large, small, Narcour's red crystal, Axnit's veined ore), the Wrecked Spice Platform venting spice, the Monument's relic in the void, the Drone Hideouts' rock of drone eyes and the Frontier Outpost's lit station; a Radiant face draws its picture rich, and the code stays printed as its text.

The face model names the code (`FaceModel.anomaly`) only for Anomaly Found! (`ANOMALY_FOUND_ID`) with a label in play. A label reaches only its owner's own hand ([[R2247000]]), so the other seat, the collection and a card with no label draw the spiral as before, and no picture tells anyone a board their seat does not read ([[R202]]). The pictures and their codes are the client's (`cards/anomalies.ts`, `anomalySvg`), held to the cards crate's boards by a test; the codes are [[R3337000]]'s.
