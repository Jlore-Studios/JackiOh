[[R2564000]] priced an Activate only for a card already acting on the field or face-up in a backrow, keyed by its definition, its face and its controller. So a Unit the AI held, a Fire Beam or a Scourge Mortar, had no price: once the search played it, it read as its 0/3 or 0/2 body until its Activate was used, and the beam kept only the best four lines after each step, so the play was cut before its use was reached. Equipped instead ([[R1086]]), the piece fuses into its host, which then takes the Activate ([[R1751000]]) but is keyed by the fusion's own definition, which the root never priced either. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**A Unit in the seat's own hand.** After the board's cards, within the same `AI_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES`, each Unit in the seat's own hand whose script declares an Activate, one of each key and none the board already priced, is placed first on a sandbox copy of the probe world ([[R923001]]'s spawn, as [[R2424000]]'s Death, [[R2477000]]'s turn-hook and [[R3064000]]'s awakening probes place a hand Unit), and priced as [[R2564000]] prices the seat's own units: the seat's turn and then the enemy's ended untouched, one use of the placed card alone tried at the start of the seat's next turn, at its controller's evaluation after less before. It never reads the enemy's hand ([[R185]]).

**An Equip's host.** A card with no price of its own whose abilities a fusion composed reads the best price among the ingredients that print them (`activation_sources`, [[R3291000]]), each looked up as its own card under the same controller, so a beam equipped during the search counts what it would played plainly. A host that has a price of its own keeps it.
