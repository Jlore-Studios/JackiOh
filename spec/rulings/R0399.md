C #57 has the text of the last Spell either player played (a cast is a play, [[R70]]): the game records it by definition and face ([[§10.1]]), in hand the view carries that Spell's face under Echo's name, as it carries a fused card's ([[R243]]), and played, Echo declares and resolves that Spell's choices and script. A played Echo records the Spell it copied, never Echo, so it cannot copy itself into a loop; with no Spell played yet it has no text and does nothing. "Spell" is the Spell type, not a Field Spell. The Radiant face adds Echo 1 ([[§6.2]])
