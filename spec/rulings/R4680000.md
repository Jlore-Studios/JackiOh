Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event or response changes. [[R4680001]] says what a row opens.

**Why.** The owner wants to see how every card stacks up in each of its older states (issue #4671): a card added in v0.5.8 and changed in v0.6.10 is two rows, the version live today and the v0.5.8 version as it was, each with its own figures from when it was played.

**The Segmented.** The Experimental tab now has two views, so its Segmented of views shows ([[R4678000]]), labelled Experimental views, at the top of its panel: Running ([[R4678001]]) then Versions. `?experimental=versions` opens Versions, and Running, the first, needs no address. The line above the tab row names the view shown: on Versions, "Every version of every card, each over the patches it was played on".

**The rows.** Versions is Running's table and grid over every play version ([[R4675000]]) of each card the Cards tab lists under the shared filters, in that order and each card's oldest first before the sort: a card changed twice is three rows. Each row is its version's figures from `GET /api/stats/versions` ([[R4675001]]), read against the play versions' one field and band, so an old version and a new one compare on one scale. The columns are Running's:
- Card, the cost badge in its rarity as that version printed them, and in the Grid its face as it was, its references not interactive ([[R279]]); a live version's are the Cards tab's row's, as on Running.
- Name, its name at its last version; Tier and Adjusted, "—" below [[R654]]'s floor; Win rate after Adjusted; Play rate and Games behind the Columns toggle.
- Since, its span, "v0.5.8 – v0.6.9" (a version alone where it began and ended on one), or "v0.6.10 – live" for the card's live version, "live" marked as the header marks the current patch, then the patches it reaches as on Running ([[R4678001]]).

**Filters, sort, view and ranges.** The page's one state, as on Running ([[R4083000]], [[R4678001]]): the search, set, rarity, cost, keyword and match type pick the cards, each read on the card as the Cards tab lists it today, so a card that costs 3 today shows every version of it, the one that cost 4 among them; the sort offers Name, Games, Tier, Adjusted, Win rate, Play rate and Date ([[R3184002]], [[R5033000]]); Table or Grid, Show 95% ranges with the tier fit on the versions' band ([[R2895000]], [[R3184001]]), and Plain text, which prints each version's own text ([[R3887000]]). Versions adds one on/off button, **Past versions only**, a pressed button in the Segmented's look ([[R3324003]]) kept in the address as `?past=1`, which leaves out every live version.

**Loading.** A play version's definition is its catalog's at its last version ([[R4675001]]), and a live one's the catalog the client carries. A row draws its old version's Card only once its catalog has loaded, and asks for it only once the row nears the screen, as a grid's lazy art does (`cards/art/near.ts`, `ART_NEAR_MARGIN_PX` and `ART_DWELL_MS`), so a page loads the catalogs of the rows on screen and none other, never the whole history at once, and each catalog once, whichever rows share it. The catalog is the file of that version in the patch history the browser carries (`patches/source.ts`, one lazily loaded file per shipped version from v0.1.0, as the Almanac's History and `/patch-notes` read it, so it adds nothing to the build); a version that history does not hold, one shipped after the build, is read from `GET /api/catalog/:version` ([[R388]]), which the Worker's edge cache keeps ([[R4691002]]). One that fails leaves its row's Card empty and is asked again once the rows are drawn anew or a drill-down needs it.

Rejected:
- Reading every version from `GET /api/catalog/:version`: the browser already carries each shipped catalog as a file of its own.
- The engine module's carried catalogs (from v0.3.0c, [[R4675000]]): they serve replays, and the module gives no page a past catalog.
- Loading every row's catalog when the view opens: about 240 catalogs of about 600 KB each.
- Filtering on a version's own cost or rarity: the client would read the server's filters a second way (CLAUDE.md rule 9).

Hearthstone has no counterpart.

Amended by [[R5037000]]: Versions' rows also carry the Cards tab's Win rate and Play rate, summed over each version's span, after Adjusted and behind the Columns toggle, and the sort offers both.

Amended by [[R5040000]] (issue #5040): the Segmented reads Major patch running, Historical, Versions (and the Hall of Fame, [[R4681001]]); `?experimental=running` opens Major patch running and `?experimental=historical` Historical.

Amended by [[R5035000]] (issue #5035): each row also shows its play version's Iteration right of its span, in the table and the grid; Past versions only moves no number.

Amended by [[R5036000]] (issue #5036): Versions shows each version's Decisiveness right after Adjusted, in the table and the Grid, "not enough games" below the floor or with no turns, before [[R5037000]]'s Win rate, and the sort offers Name, Games, Tier, Adjusted, Decisiveness, Win rate, Play rate and Date.
