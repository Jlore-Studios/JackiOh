The homescreen's hand ([[R374]], [[R639]], [[R704]]) holds [[R1017000]]'s standard: no card in it gives its art's room to its words. Its faces are smaller than the grids' (104 to 158 px wide on a wide screen, `landing.css`), and at the widest (`FAN_CARD_MAX_PX`) the rules text prints at the reading floor, where a box holds 5 lines of about 25 characters (`FAN_TEXT_LIMIT`, measured in Chromium on every catalog face, counted as [[R1017000]] counts); a narrower fan card only shrinks its text. The hand has no hover preview, and a card's opening on a click is the only place it would print the rest, so the hand leaves out what would not fit rather than print a short form: a card is dealt or swapped in only if both its faces print whole on a fan card, either [[R1017001]]'s full art, which prints no words, or the face's whole catalog text, with no short form, within the limit. Every rarity keeps at least two such cards in [[R374]]'s Core pool and in [[R639]]'s every-set pool, so the hand still deals four rarities and every slot can swap. With the Full-art cards setting off a hero's card is held to its text like any card (Gary the Gambler's coin flips run past the limit). While the setting is on, a hero's full-art card is `FULL_ART_WEIGHT_FACTOR` (2) times as likely as it would be otherwise, in the deal and in the swaps, below [[R639]]'s threshold, where every other card stays equally likely, and at it, where it weighs as a card that prints at full size. The draws come from the page's injected random source, as before. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
