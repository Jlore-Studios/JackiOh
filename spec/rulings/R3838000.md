Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes.

**Why.** A full-art card prints its art across its face and no words ([[R1017001]]). On the Almanac ([[R630]]) and the Statistics page ([[R654]]), where people browse cards to read them, a reader could not tell what a full-art card does without opening it. The owner asked in issue #3838 for a button on those pages that turns full art off, so every card has its text.

**The button.** Both pages' headers hold a "Show card text" button, an on/off button in the `Segmented` look with `aria-pressed`, as the Statistics page's other on/off controls are ([[R3324003]]). While it is pressed:

- Every face on the page prints its normal face, its words on it, as a tutorial lesson's do (`FullArtAllowed` false, [[R1108000]]): the grid, the detail view, the hover previews, the reference tooltips, the Statistics page's grid and its breakdown ([[R3011000]]), whatever the Full-art cards setting says and whatever look a pick or an owned copy would wear ([[R1313000]]).
- Pressed again, the faces are as the setting and the picks have them.

**Where it is kept.** The choice is the pages' own, one for both: the device keeps it (`localStorage`, `jackioh.cardText`), so the next visit to either page opens as the last one left it, and where storage is blocked it lasts the page. It never writes the Full-art cards setting, so a game's faces are as they were.

Rejected:

- Flipping the Full-art cards setting from the page, which would turn full art off in every game too.
- The setting alone, which leaves an owned copy's picked full art on ([[R1313000]]), so not every card would have its text.
- A button per page, each with its own choice, which would make a reader turn it off twice.
