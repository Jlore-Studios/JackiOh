A press on a greyed Activate control ([[R510]]), the lightning button of a unit or face-up backrow card `legalActions` lists no `activate` for, reaches the board, which shows the view's `ActivationView.reason` for it ("can't be activated right now" when the view gives none) in the board's reason line, as a hand card's refused click shows its `playReason` ([[R979000]]) and a unit's its attack reason ([[R849000]]), and sends nothing; `data-legal` stays false, and a card the view lists no activation for (Heroic Power has its own control) gives no notice. A greyed Trade control ([[R1838000]]) is `aria-disabled` rather than `disabled`, so it stays focusable and enters the tab order, its `tradeReason` its tooltip and its accessible description; a press shows that reason in the same line and sends no `trade`. A live Activate and a live Trade behave as before. Both pass on the 1v1 board; the Duos card bar's greyed Trade gets the same focus and description, but its press shows no notice yet, since that board's notice state is the hosted Game's. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no state, event, replay or AI decision changes. Rejected: capitalising the notice, which the play and attack notices are not; and an activation-reason line in the inspect sheet, left as a follow-up.
