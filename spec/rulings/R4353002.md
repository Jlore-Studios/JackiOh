Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V: "Evasive -> no longer can be targeted by spells or field spells." Evasive keeps its attack half ([[R3543002]]: no attack may target it, and it can't be in Defense Position) and gains a targeting half, Hearthstone's Elusive: targeting is [[R450]]'s choosing, so no `target` declaration of a Spell or a Field Spell offers an Evasive card in play, and no `target` prompt they open lists it; `legal_actions` and the reducer's refusal ask one function (`restrictions::evasive_bars`, §10.2), as Immutable's half did until [[R4353001]] took it away.

- **Whose card it is.** The source is read as [[R781001]] read it: anything in a unit zone is a Unit (an Animated card there included), any other card its running face's printed type, so a revealed face-down Spell is a Spell.
- **Friend and foe.** Its controller's own Spells and Field Spells can't target it either.
- **What still reaches it.** A Unit's choice (a Cry, a Death, a trigger, an Activate), a Trap and a Field Trap (the designer named Spells and Field Spells alone), and every effect that does not choose it: "all" and "every" effects, Cleave's splash, an effect on the Units beside a target, and a random pick. A Spell whose only would-be target is Evasive has no legal target, and a random declared target ([[R1200]]) draws from the legal ones.
- **Where.** In play only: on the field and face-up. A card in a hand, a deck or a graveyard may still be picked, and a face-down card's Evasive is inert.
- **The cards.** Meditative #39.5 Jade Beauty and every card that prints or grants Evasive ([[R3551003]]) keep their data.
