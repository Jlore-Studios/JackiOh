A stronger ladder bot ([[R1452]]) is dealt stronger decks, from the win rates the game already measures and with no card or set named. It amends [[R1454]]'s **Decks** and [[R641000]]'s trio deal in two places.

**People's lists, by the bot's strength.** The pool counts each of the patch's distinct human Best-of-1 decklists ([[R1454]]: sorted copies compared, so a reordering is the same list) over the live games people played with it: its games, and the games it won, a draw counting as a game not won, as [[R377]] counts one. A list with at least `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` (20) games, the floor under which [[R654]] shows no rate, is ranked: the best win rate first, compared exactly (`wins_a × games_b` against `wins_b × games_a`, as MD-D1's leader is chosen), then more games, then the ids joined, ascending. The ranked lists come first, nearest first to the bot's place among them by its strength share `s` (below): the best list first for the strongest bot (`s` = 1), the worst ranked list first for the weakest (`s` = 0), a tie going to the better list, so a stronger bot holds the lists that win more. The lists with fewer games follow, shuffled from the bot's seed as before. With no list ranked, the draw is the one [[R1454]] always made. A list that won its one game never goes ahead of one that won 90 of 200, and the order changes only as people's games come in, so it is not a lever one player can pull.

**Random decks that lean on winning cards.** A bot's strength share `s` is its seed rating's place between the weakest and the strongest of `BOT_STRENGTHS`: 0 for the weakest, 1 for the strongest. Its random decks, its Best-of-1 decks past people's lists and its trio's decks past its own ([[R641000]]), are still dealt by All Random's deal ([[R258]]), with no shadow ban ([[R186]]) and no soft gate ([[R1390]]), but each card's weight in the draw is multiplied by a factor read off the compiled win-rate table (MD-D1's: [[R377]]'s in-deck wins and games per card). At share `s`, a card whose row has at least `AI_WIN_RATE.min_games` (20, `CN_TECH_MIN_GAMES`) games is weighted by `1 + s × AI_WIN_RATE.max_boost × (rate − 0.5) × 2`, held between `AI_WIN_RATE.floor` (0.25) and `1 + max_boost` (4.0, with `max_boost` 3.0, under the theme's 4.0 boost so a rate never outweighs the theme or the curve). A card with a thinner row or none keeps its weight, and so does every card for the weakest bot. The factor takes no draw from the rng, so a seed's deck moves only by the weights, and a deal at share 0 is the deck the seed always dealt.

**What stays as it was.** Every player's deal (All Random, a rematch's, a room's) and every deal the AI makes for itself (practice, the gates, the arena, development runs) passes no strength and deals exactly the deck it dealt before. The table is the one CN Tech reads, compiled in with the catalog and changed only by a card patch, so the pure crates read no live data and a bot's deck never depends on what it is about to face ([[R185]]'s reasoning holds: the rates are public aggregate figures, never a game's hidden state). A row for a card the catalog no longer holds is never read, since the weight is looked up by the id of a card in the pool.

Where the spec is silent this follows Hearthstone semantics: a ladder's stronger opponents bring the decks that win more.
