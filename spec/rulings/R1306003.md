As [[R1301003]] for a card pack: the seed is 16 random bytes as hex, drawn afresh for the
opening; the log is the `ref` of every economy row the opening writes; a replay is
`roll_cosmetic_pack` over the holdings before it; it takes the account's lock and commits whole or
not at all. What differs is the roll:

- **Its draws** come from the engine's seeded generator from cursor 0, slot by slot in this order
  and no other: the slot's rarity, a tiered roll's one integer draw since #1339 ([[R1339002]]),
  then its cosmetic's pick and, for a per-card cosmetic, its card's pick, each only when there is
  something to pick ([[R1306000]]). A spent slot
  ([[R1306001]]) takes only its rarity's draw. Every list it walks is in registry or catalog order,
  and the holdings' sets are read for membership alone.
- **Its log** holds `catalog` (the catalog version the pool came from), `seed`, `cosmetics` (the
  droppable entries it picked from, whole), `slots` (each slot's rolled rarity and grant) and
  `draws`. The rows it writes are the pack spent, each cosmetic granted and the Chase Shard bar's
  one ([[R1306002]]), and each token bar's row when a slot was spent; the collection's rows share
  the opening's `at`.
- **A replay** is `roll_cosmetic_pack` over the shipped sets' pool in the logged catalog, the
  holdings as they stood before the opening (its cards and cosmetics) and the logged cosmetics,
  with the logged seed: the logged slots and draws, however the live catalog or registry has moved.

Hearthstone semantics: as [[R1301003]]; Hearthstone shows only the result.

Rejected: sharing the card pack's log type, which would carry a pack name and pity counters a
cosmetic pack has none of.
