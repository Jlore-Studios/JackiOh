A patch's tier is its record's `tier` in `patches.json` (`major`, `patch` or `micro`, [[R1369000]]); a record with none is a major when it adds a card of a set no earlier patch held and a patch otherwise, and no version is ever parsed or ordered ([[R105]]). On the Patch notes page ([[R724000]]) a major and a patch are entries of their own, newest first, and a micro patch (the letter after a version, `v0.Z.Xb`) is not: it appears as a compact sub-entry inside the entry of the nearest major or patch before it in `patches.json`'s order, in that order, oldest first, with its version, date and player note ([[R724001]]), its technical lines and its own Show / Hide the cards control, and no art ([[R3012001]]). A micro after a major goes under the major; a micro with no major or patch before it in its line is an entry of its own, with no art. Its cards load as [[R507]] sets out, when its control is opened. A release line's span and count still count every version, micros included.

A legend sits on the side of the page: a sticky `<nav>` named "Patch notes legend" that lists each release line and, under it, its majors and patches newest first, each patch's micro patches under it behind a button that starts collapsed (it shows while one of them is the entry in view). Following a link, whether by mouse, touch or keyboard, opens the entry's release line, opens the entry's cards (an entry with cards loads its snapshot and the one before, as an entry opened by hand does, [[R507]]), scrolls to the entry at once and moves focus to it, and writes `#<version>` into the address with no new history entry; a link with a modifier key or another mouse button keeps its own address. A line's link does the same for the line. An address hash (`/patch-notes#v0.5.3`) or a later change of it does the same: a hash that names no entry of the page, or is no valid escape, opens nothing. The entry in view, the last one in document order that touches the top fifth of the page (`PATCH_LEGEND_BAND_MARGIN`), so a micro before the patch holding it, carries `aria-current="location"` in the legend, with a gold bar and bold text so that it does not rest on colour alone.

On a phone (`PATCH_LEGEND_PHONE_QUERY`, 760 px wide or less) the legend is one collapsed bar, "Jump to a patch", that sticks to the top of the page and opens a list under it; following a link closes it again. The page has one column there and wraps every long word, so it never scrolls sideways, and every legend control is a 44 px target on a touch screen or a narrow window ([[R2122000]]). With a mouse its controls are at least 24 px tall too (WCAG 2.5.8, e2e spec 38); the micro patches button is 32 px, as a card's name button on this page is. Presentation only, over public data (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
