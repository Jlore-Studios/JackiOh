FvH #92's Radiant face, `log2Utility: helpful`, counts numbers as [[R5237011]] does, but only where that helps its controller: the costs of the cards its controller controls, and the Attack and max Health of the opponent's cards on the field. Its controller's own Units keep their Attack and Health, and the opponent's cards keep their costs. A base face acting beside it reaches every number, and two Radiant ones of different players each help their own controller, so every number is reached; a number is still counted once ([[R5237011]]).
