Meditative #37 CN in a Bottle is a (1) Spell that casts on draw ([[§2.4]], [[R58]]): "Cast on draw: Add a random Radiant card to your hand. [It costs (0).]" Drawn, it is cast at once and the draw repeats, as every Cast on draw card is; its card is one non-token card of every open set but this one ([[R380]], [[R387]]), made Radiant and shown in Chinese, a card generated into a hand, so [[R673]]'s Glitch roll applies and it runs its own arrival hooks ([[R151]]); only its owner sees it ([[R97]]). A full hand burns that card, not the bottle, which was cast ([[R4]]). Setup never deals it ([[R635]], [[R748]]). A bottle that reaches a hand any other way (an add, a bounce, a steal, a draw past [[R58]]'s chain cap, `CAST_ON_DRAW_CHAIN_CAP`) stays there as a (1) Spell, and played, it does the same. This is Hearthstone's Cast When Drawn. It supersedes [[R926]]'s printed-only (X): the card is played now, so it has a real cost. [[R925]]'s `enters_hand` hook, which the card used to be replaced on any arrival in a hand, has no shipped card now and stays for custom cards (CLAUDE.md rule 13). Through patch v0.3.7b it was replaced on arrival; a replay of a game from then folds under that script (`KEPT_SCRIPTS`, [[R767000]]).
