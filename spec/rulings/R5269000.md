The practice AI's persona ([[R645]]) is `jackioh-shared`'s `bots::persona`, the module the ladder bots' personas roll through, and the page reaches it through the WebAssembly module's `bots` call; no copy of its tables or its rolls is kept in the client. The page deals the persona with `dealPersona` once a game, on a stream it seeds itself (`practice-persona:` and a draw of the page's own), apart from the game's seed, the engine's rng and the AI's search: the deal is `pick_persona` off the stream's first draw, Silent with no draw in a lesson, and the persona chooses within the AI seat's dealt hand ([[R1344]]) with the practice AI's weights and delay window, never the ladder's ([[R639000]]). Each later call (`personaEvents`, `personaPlayerEmote`, `personaTurnLong`) takes the persona and the stream as the last answer left them, rolls on the stream where it stopped, and answers the emotes with the persona and the stream to hand back, so a persona held as JSON between calls sends what one held natively sends on the same draws. How long a player's turn runs before `playerTurnLong` rolls is `AI_EMOTE`'s, which the page reads from the module's constants.
