Classic #81 The Power to Thrive's "or reduce the Cost of a card in your hand by (1) [(2)]" chooses the card as the mode resolves, a `hand` prompt of its controller's ([[§10.6]]), not as a play-time target ([[R81]]), so the mode is offered whatever the hand holds. The chosen card's cost is lowered by the declared `discount` while it stays in hand. With an empty hand the mode does nothing ([[R90]]).
