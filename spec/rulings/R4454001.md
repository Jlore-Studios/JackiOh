"All cards for the rest of the game are Radiant": while Tears of the Kwzki Tree's world holds (`GameState.radiantWorld`), every settled state check makes every card of both players Radiant in every zone, a pile's buried cards included ([[R3569000]]). The fuzz monitor's I19 (issue #4454) found it making Dormant cards Radiant, which no effect may otherwise do ([[§6.1]] Dormant, [[R1839000]]).
**It stays as it is.** The world is a rule of the game for the rest of it, not an effect reaching for a card, and "all cards" holds the Dormant ones: a Dormant card turns Radiant with the rest, keeping its status and countdown, as [[R74]]'s Make Radiant keeps a card's state. Its text is off while it sleeps, so the face shows only on the card and in its stats, and it wakes Radiant as it would have at the next check anyway.
**What I19 allows.** A `radiantSet` naming a Dormant card, and its Radiant face changed with no event, while the world holds.
Rejected: having the state check pass a Dormant card by until it wakes. It plays the same once the card wakes, adds a second Radiant moment to every Dormant card under the world, and goes against the card's "all cards".
