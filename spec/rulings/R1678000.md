A shadow ban ([[R186]]) is a measurement of one AI: a `timeout` row is a decision that AI took over two seconds, and a promotion changes how long the AI's decisions take. So every push to `main` that moves the AI's generation (`crates/ai/generation.json`'s `generation`, which every promotion writes) or adds a set to `SHIPPED_SETS` ([[R1420]]) starts a sweep of record on GitHub's runners (`.github/workflows/sweep.yml`): [[R390]]'s two passes at both tiers over the ids `cargo jackioh sweep --due <the push's base>` names. When the generation moved, they are every card `SHADOW_BAN` holds and every card of the sets that may hold bans ([[R732000]], [[R1186000]]) that `SHADOW_WATCH` holds; a watched card of an earlier set can never be banned, so no sweep is due over it. When a set ships, they are every non-token card of that set. A push that does neither, the sweep's own pull request among them (it changes `generation.json`'s `shadowBan`, never `generation`), starts none.

Its report goes under `training/history/` as a report of record and `sweep --write` reads it last, so a card it swept takes its row, or none when it cleared the card ([[R1186000]], [[R1675000]]), and every card it did not sweep keeps its row. A changed table arrives as a pull request with auto-merge on, held by CI to `sweep --check` like any other; an unchanged one opens nothing. [[R732000]]'s "no lane adds one" holds for the training lanes alone: the sweep after a promotion may ban a card again when the new AI is slow with it, within each set's half. A sweep `--write` refuses (more than half a set banned, or a ban of a set outside the sets that may hold bans, as a set that just shipped is until a ruling adds it) writes nothing and opens an issue for a person. `SHADOW_UNBANNED`'s cards, the unban lane's removals ([[R732001]]), are in neither table, so no sweep is due over them. The sweep plays on GitHub's runners, not the owner's PC, whose runner throttles a job to half a CPU while the PC is in use, which would read as timeouts. Hearthstone has no counterpart.
