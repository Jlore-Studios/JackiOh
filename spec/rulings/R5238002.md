Felinors vs Humans #42 "may be played onto either side's open zone". A play's zone ([[§10.2]]) named a row and a lane of the player's own side only; the cards summoned for the opponent ([[R360]], [[R3837000]]) are handed over by the engine, never by the player's pick.

**The choice.** `ZoneChoice` gains `player`, the side the zone is on; absent is the player's own, and naming one's own side reads the same as naming none. A card whose flags say `playsOnEitherSide` may name a zone of the opponent's side: every open zone of its row there ([[§3.2]]: empty, unlocked, not reserved), or for a Stack card every zone there that takes a Stack play, by the predicates its own side's zones are read by. It may do so only as a plain play: an Equip play and a face-down set take their own side. A GIGA permanent is offered none. Any other card naming the other side is refused at [[§10.5]] step 1 ("is played on your own side"), as is a zone there that is not open.

**The listing.** `legal_actions` lists such a card's other-side zones after its own side's, so a game no such card is in lists what it listed before; a card whose own row is full but whose other side has an open zone is still playable there.

**The landing.** The card lands in the named zone at step 4, in that side's control as every card placed in a zone is (its owner unchanged, [[§3.2]]); the play is still its player's, for every rule that counts or answers plays. Its text reads which side it went to by its controller against its owner.
