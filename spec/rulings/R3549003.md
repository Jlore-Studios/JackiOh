S+ #71 Mindustrial Contract reads "Add 3 random Mindustrial cards to your hand", its Radiant face
"Add 3 random Radiant Mindustrial cards". The pool is every non-token card of the Stinkyscape+ set
tagged Mindustrial, Units, Spells, Field Spells and the Field Trap alike, the pool naming its set so
that it reaches them while the set is held ([[R1420]]) and a Mindustrial tag being the set's own. Each
of the three is its own uniform pick, so a card may come twice ([[R60]]); a full hand burns what
does not fit ([[§2.4]]). The card is no Mindustrial card itself, and would be left out of its own pool
all the same ([[R387]]). The number, 3, is declared (`card`, [[R386]]'s tuning reaches it). The Radiant
face adds the three Radiant at their printed price: unlike the Radiant faces of S+ #39 and #166 its text prints no discount.

Rejected: a discount on the Radiant cards that the text does not print; and picks without repeats.
