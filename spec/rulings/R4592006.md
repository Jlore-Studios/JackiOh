Mimic Unit's Cry gains the keywords of enemy units on the field: base gains keywords from one random enemy unit, Radiant from every enemy unit in lane order. Granted and printed keywords are copied, while temporary keywords, Brittle, spent Divine Shield and spent Reborn are not gained.
