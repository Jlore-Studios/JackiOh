Status never hides (issue #3728's fourth convention, `docs/polish/10-menus.md`). The pieces were in
different places: the live game's banner ([[R765]], [[R1566000]]) on the landing and practice's menu
alone, and this tab's queue search on `/play` alone, though the queue follows the player on every
screen ([[R765]]).

The menus' shell ([[R3728000]]) holds one status row and hands it to the screens inside it. It shows
the player's live game to go back to (a match, a Conquest series between its games, or a co-op game, as
the menus' banner offered it, [[R1566000]]), unless the screen is that game's own; else this tab's
queue search, "You're in the queue", its mode named, with Leave the queue, which asks the server to
take the ticket out (`DELETE /api/queue`) and forgets the queue, as `/play`'s Leave the queue does,
unless the screen is `/play`, which shows its own search; else nothing, as it is while a board is on
screen (a replay, a dungeon fight), whose rail holds no status row. The account's live game is
read as before (`useLiveGame`, again every `LIVE_GAME_POLL_SECONDS` while there is one), once, by the
shell, where the landing read it before. BackLink's top bar draws the row as its fifth slot, after the
end group, on a row of its own under everything else (`.screen-nav__status`, `routes/top-bar.css`),
and takes no room when it is empty: this adds a slot to [[R3218000]]'s four. Home, which has no
BackLink, draws it under its corner where the live game's banner was. "Match found!" (`QueueFound`)
and practice's own banners are as they were, and a lobby the player is in has no status yet (none
exists off `/play`).

The challenge toasts (`.challenge-toasts`) take the toasts' layer, `--z-toast`, under a dialog's, in
place of a raw 95, and sit inside the safe-area insets; inside the shell they clear its rail and sit
above a phone's tab bar.

Nothing here is a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7): the live game is what `GET /api/auth/me` said, the queue
what this tab remembers, and the server still holds the ticket and refuses what it would. **Proof.**
`MenuShell.test.tsx` drives the route table: a live match's banner on Home and in `/privacy`'s status
row, the bar's last slot; a queue search off `/play` and not on it, and Leave the queue sending
`DELETE /api/queue` and forgetting the queue; and the toasts' layer and insets from the sheets.
`nav.test.tsx` proves the bar inside the shell ends with the status row and holds no Friends, and
`top-bar.test.ts` the row's place in the sheet.

Rejected: a banner of its own fixed under the top bar, over the screen's content, which a one-screen
menu ([[R1581000]]) would have to leave room for whether or not it shows; and a second queue search on
`/play`, which already says it is searching.
