The Enigmata's "Players take turns casting Jogg's box until the game ends" is `forRestOfGame` ([[R458]]) once for each player, at the start of that player's own turns: the player casts a fresh base Classic+ #47 Jogg's Box (`castNew`), every choice of the cast random ([[R452]]), so its ten casts open no prompt of their own. Its "End the turn" ([[R456]]) ends that turn once the casts have resolved, so the two players alternate with nobody acting, until a hero dies or the turn cap draws the game ([[R2]]). The opponent's turn is the first to start after the cast, and the badge on each side says so. The Radiant face gives its caster's hero `count` Armor first, as the card resolves.

A random cast may reach a card that asks a question of its own (Meditative+ #20's quiz). That is an ordinary prompt of that card's, answered as any prompt is, by the player, the random policy or the timeout.

Rejected: a loop of casts inside one turn until someone dies (it could never end within the match's action cap); and skipping the Box's "End the turn" (the text says players take turns).
