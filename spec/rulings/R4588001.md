IA #86 Replica Hatchery's "your cheapest Unit" is the lowest `cost_now` ([[R396]]) among the Units acting on its controller's side, and IA #94 Batch Monarch's "your highest-Attack Unit" the highest shown Attack among them, read by `forExtremeUnit` as the effect resolves. A tie is one match-rng pick of the tied Units, kept in the part's memo ([[R113]]); with no Unit nothing happens. The copy follows [[R57]], and the base face's copy gets Brittle 1.

Why: cheapest is a price and highest-Attack a sight, so each is read off the board as it stands, and a tie no face breaks is what the match rng is for. Rejected: the leftmost of the tied Units, which the text does not name.
