**What holds.** IA #188 Overfitting reveals only if its controller has at least one unit zone that `fill_board_zones` yields: empty, not reserved ([[R64]]'s dying Reborn unit, an animated card's home), not covered by a GIGA, not voided and not in the hole ([[R3554000]]). With none, the trap stays set and the Unit resolves as if the trap were not there, even when the played Unit is Flying or Flexible and its copy could stand in the backrow ([[R2248000]]): the gate counts unit zones only, so a backrow zone does not open it. Likewise it asks for one free unit zone, not the wider fit a GIGA copy needs ([[R3546000]]), so a GIGA's copy can still fizzle after the reveal. The gate is read as the opponent's Unit resolves, before the trap reveals; a zone that opens later does not matter.

**Why.** For an ordinary Unit, `summon_copy` with no lane lands only in such a zone (`entry_zone`, [[R688]]'s `takes_move`), so a reveal with none would fizzle and waste the trap. Hearthstone's secrets that summon (Mirror Entity, Snake Trap) likewise do not trigger on a full board, and a plain full-board rule is the one a player can read off the lanes. The Flying case is the one place the gate and `summon_copy` part: `entry_zone` falls back to the backrow for a Flexible Unit when the unit row is full, so the copy could land where the gate sees no room. That is accepted here, and pinned by a test, rather than widened. [[R4592000]] built the card; #4960 moved the gate to this reading without a ruling or a test, and this note records it and pins it.

**Rejected.** Reading the played Unit's own landing zone (the engine's `summon_lands`, which counts the Flying backrow fallback and a GIGA's fit): it is the more exact gate, but it is a script change that makes Overfitting reveal on a board whose unit row is full, and widening a card's behaviour is a decision for its own issue; it also needs that helper made public. And the raw "any empty pile" gate that #4960 replaced: it revealed the trap on a board whose only open zone was reserved, in the hole, covered or voided, and the copy then fizzled.
