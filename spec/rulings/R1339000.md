The owner's drop math in #1339, which replaces the per-pack chance rolls and their tuning
([[R1301000]]): every drop the economy makes, a card pack slot's card rarity ([[§14.3]]), a
duplicate's cosmetic rarity ([[R1301002]]) and a cosmetic pack slot's ([[§14.6]]), is a tiered
roll.

- **Two draws.** The tier is one integer draw below `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL` (1,000) against a table
  that sets the weight of each tier above the lowest. The lowest tier, Common, is the fallback: it
  takes whatever weight is left, `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL` less the rest, computed and never stored, so
  raising one tier takes from the fallback alone and nothing else needs adjusting. A tier's chance
  in one roll is its weight over the total, so the table is the published odds, and an integer
  draw makes its outcome exact. The item within the tier is the roll's second draw: a card of that
  rarity weighted by `PACK_UNOWNED_WEIGHTS` ([[§14.3]]), a duplicate's unowned per-card cosmetic
  ([[R1301002]]), or a cosmetic pack slot's unowned cosmetic ([[R1306000]]).
- **The tables.** `CARD_TIER_WEIGHTS` (Rare 300, Epic 70, Legendary 20, Mythic 10, so Common 600)
  for a card pack slot, and `COSMETIC_TIER_WEIGHTS` (Rare 250, Epic 150, Legendary 45, Mythic 5, so
  Common 550) for a duplicate's cosmetic and a cosmetic pack slot, each on its own scale: a
  cosmetic's rarity is never tied to a card's ([[R1333000]]).
- **A floor is a fallback too.** A slot that must be at least some tier rolls the normal table,
  and a result below the floor becomes the floor tier; the tiers above it keep their own odds. One
  slot of each pack, `PACK_FLOORED_SLOT`, the first, is floored: a card pack's at
  `PACK_FLOOR_RARITY`, Rare (Rare 900, Epic 70, Legendary 20, Mythic 10), and a cosmetic pack's at
  `COSMETIC_PACK_FLOOR_RARITY`, Epic (Epic 950, Legendary 45, Mythic 5). Since the floor changes
  only results below it, a card pack's Epic, Legendary and Mythic rates are the table's.
- **One helper.** Every roll goes through `roll_tier` (`crates/server/src/economy/tiers.rs`), pure
  from the set tiers' weights, the fallback, an optional floor and a draw to a tier. Set weights
  summing past the total are refused, and `crates/server/src/config.rs` asserts the shipped tables
  are within it when it compiles. Pity sits on top of the card pack's slots ([[R1339001]]).
- **An empty tier.** A card slot whose rarity the pool lacks draws from the highest rarity below it
  that the pool holds, down to the slot's fallback tier (its floor, for the floored slot), then
  from the nearest above, and only then below the floor, keeping its rolled rarity for pity
  ([[R1301001]]). A cosmetic pack slot with nothing unowned at its tier falls the same way
  ([[R1306001]]). A duplicate does not fall: with nothing unowned at its rarity it is spent
  ([[R1301002]]). No Legendary or Mythic cosmetic can drop yet, so those cosmetic rolls fall down
  today.
- **What players see.** `GET /api/packs` answers one roll's odds of a slot, of the floored slot and
  of a duplicate's cosmetic, and `GET /api/cosmetic-packs` a slot's and the floored slot's: each
  tier's count of draws over the total, the fallback included. The pack screen and the cosmetic
  packs panel show them as percentages, the pack screen with a line that pity guarantees each
  tracked rarity within its threshold; the client computes no rate (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone states its odds per card, by rarity, and guarantees one Rare or
better card in every pack; a weight table per roll is that, with the guaranteed card as a floor.

Rejected: keeping per-pack chances and tuning their base rates until the rates with pity land on
targets, which the owner replaced so that a published rate is a plain number and changing a drop
is one edit; storing the fallback's weight, which could disagree with the rest; shares as floats
summing to one, which round; the last slot or a random one as the floored slot, where the first is
the one the pack shows first and the one [[R1301000]] already guaranteed; and showing a rate the
client computes.
