A landing Unit's summon thud is weighed by its tier, from a soft tap (Tiny) through a soft, solid and heavy thud to an impact with a crack under a Huge Unit and a deep boom under a MASSIVE one, at a pitch within ±5% (`SLAM_PITCH_SPREAD`), on the SFX bus. The crowd answers a Large Unit with a murmur, a Huge one with anticipation and a MASSIVE one with excitement, on the Crowd bus, through the same 400 ms quiet period as a hit's (#57's `CROWD_FEEL.reactionDebounceMs`), so a resolution reacts once, to its strongest moment; it is neutral, so a landing draws no cheer or applause after it. A Unit not in the newest view keeps the plain summon thud
