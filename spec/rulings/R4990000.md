For a card that enters the field GIGA ([[R3546002]]: C #33 Joro or #55 Lava Golem made GIGA in the hand by S+ #100 Plesiosize or S+ #23 GIGA-ify), "an open unit zone" in [[R394]]'s and [[R450]]'s interposition and in [[R360]]'s hand-over means an anchor whose four zones are all free ([[R3546000]]; [[§6.1]]'s GIGA row: "only fitting anchors are offered or summoned into"). A card that interposes from the hand answers only when such an anchor exists on its side, and is summoned at the leftmost one, an open fit before a Locked one as any summon with no named lane ([[R64]], [[R688]]). With none it does not answer and stays in hand, and the next card in that hand that interposes and has room answers instead; with no such card the attack or the Spell goes on at the target it chose, as a Hearthstone Secret that has no room to summon stays hidden. The three reads that decide it, whether a card answers (`targeting::interceptor_for`), whether its pick may move to it (`play_choices::interceptor_fits_decl`) and where it lands (`replacements::answer_targeting`), take their zone from one helper, `giga::summon_anchor`, so the checks and the answer cannot disagree. #55's base face made GIGA and paid with an opposing Unit takes the opponent's anchor in the lane the play named when all four of its zones are open, else the opponent's leftmost anchor whose four zones are open, as a cast GIGA takes its zone; with neither it lands where the play named, on its player's side ([[R360]]), whose four zones its play held.

Rejected: taking the card out of the hand for the one zone [[R64]] names and losing it when its other three zones are not free, which no Hearthstone summon does, and leaves a `summoned` and a `redirected` naming a card in no zone. Also rejected: a Golem with no fitting anchor on the opponent's side going to its graveyard, which [[R3837001]] rejected for #52's hand-over.
