A player's Spell Damage, which [[§4.4]] step 0 adds to each hit of their Spells, is one sum, whatever gives it: the Spell Damage keyword of the Units acting on their side ([[§6.1]]), every Spell Damage aura acting on either side that reaches them, and their "Spell Damage +N this turn" ([[R1180001]]). An aura is a card's static text (`spellDamageAura`): while the card acts on its controller's field — face-up, the top of its pile — it gives its controller its amount, and the opponent too when it says both players (Meditative+ #9 Magic Amplifier). Its amount moves with the card's declared number `spellDamage`, so a Nerf, a Buff or KY's Constant moves it ([[R386]]); a card set face-down or gone gives nothing. The sum is read as each hit lands, and only a Spell's hits are raised: a Field Spell's, a Trap's or a Unit's never are, nor is a Spell's once it was set face-down as a Trap. Without such an aura or modifier the sum is the Units' alone, as before. Hearthstone has no aura that gives the opponent Spell Damage; the designer's card asks for both players, and they share the one rule.
