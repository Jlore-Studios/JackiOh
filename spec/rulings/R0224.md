[[§2.1]]'s opening draw and [[R9]]'s replacement draws are draws, and a draw can ask: a card's start-of-game clause runs as the card arrives in a hand ([[R151]]), and its question is its owner's ([[R81]]). (A cast-on-draw card, whose cast asked, was the other source until [[R635]] set those cards aside; setup casts none now, [[R748]]'s waiting casts asking at the start of the game as [[R224]]'s clauses do.) That question is state until it is answered ([[§9.3]]), and [[§10.1]] allows one prompt at a time, so setup does not open the mulligans over it, or go on resolving them: the draw owes its own remainder ([[R158]]) and setup owes the rest of itself — the other seats' opening draws and the mulligans, or the returned cards' shuffle-back, the seats still to resolve and the game ([[R265]]) — which the answer's drain finishes ([[R122]]). The returned cards wait with that remainder, in no pile, until they go back, and are read meanwhile as the library cards they are about to be: nobody reads them ([[§3]], [[§9.1]]), so the events of the opening deal that name them stay redacted in the other seat's view, as they are once the cards are back. The mulligan's answer names the prompt it answers, as every other answer does ([[§10.6]])
