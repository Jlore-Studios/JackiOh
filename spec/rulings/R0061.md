Fires only when the opponent plays or casts a permanent from hand, after its Cry (a cast is a play, [[R70]]); tokens, Recruit, copies, Reborn and Transform results never set it off. Field Trap counts as Trap, and the firing trap is neither matched nor fused onto. Immutable permanents are never chosen as the Fuse target ([[R23]]); when no legal target of that type remains, the trap fires, is consumed and does nothing, and the played permanent stays (designer, 2026-09-17)
