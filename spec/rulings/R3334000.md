Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. [[R2801000]]'s fuse run flew each dead piece's card back to the Starship; now each piece's own module ([[R3333001]]) docks where the ship's picture ([[R3333000]]) mounts it.

**Docking.** Through a fuse run (`fuseRunAt`, [[R2801000]]) each piece's module (`starshipModuleUri`, its icon's part without its plate) flies from where the board showed the piece (else its owner's graveyard) to its own hardpoint on the Starship's picture: the point `starshipMountPoints` gives it in the list the ship holds once the run is over (the `count - n` pieces it held before the run, then each `starshipFused`'s `defId`, in order), as the card's art draws it (an anchor's `art: "picture"`, the square its cover-fit picture fills, `artBoxOf`). An action's entries are all planned on the board before it, so a second fuse run of one action (a piece a later death pass killed) reads the pieces the earlier runs fused from the board after the action, the first `count - n` it shows in the order they fused, and docks on the slot of its own count, the one its hum and its dock's sound count too. A piece past the picture's `STARSHIP_MODULES_MAX` (16) docks at the ship's middle. It leaves and lands at [[R2801000]]'s beats (`starshipFuseStartMs`, `starshipFuseLandMs`), so several fusing in one action dock in order as one run, and lands into a weld: two clamps closing on it and a flash for `STARSHIP_DOCK.weldMs`, its hum `min(1, count / STARSHIP_DOCK.humFull)` for the pieces the ship then holds. [[R2801000]]'s spark, clank ring and shake stay at each landing.

**The sound.** Each docking sounds `starshipDock` as it lands, a clank, a weld's crackle and a hum a step higher for each piece the ship then holds (`amount`, `starshipSounds.ts`); the run's rows still sound once, condensed. The director sends the run's docks as the entry starts, each delayed to its landing, so the sound engine's retrigger guard reads a dock at the time it sounds (`SCHEDULED_SFX`): two a beat apart both sound, two at one beat (a run past `STARSHIP_FUSE_STAGGERED_MAX`) stack as one. Every other sound's guard still reads the time it is sent.

**The shipyard idle.** A Dormant Starship holding at least one piece idles on its tile (`ShipyardIdle`): its dock light sweeps slowly across the hull about its lamp (`SHIPYARD_IDLE_SWEEP_MS`) and `SHIPYARD_IDLE_SPARKS` welding sparks flicker where its newest piece went in (`SHIPYARD_IDLE_WELD_MS`), both where its picture draws them (`shipyardIdleOf`). It takes one of the board's moving-art slots (`useArtMotion`, `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX`), as moving art does, so past the cap a yard holds still; it never moves under Reduce Motion, with Animated foil off, with no pieces or once launched. It moves by transform and opacity only.

**Hidden information.** Every input is public: the run's events and the view's `starship` list ([[R1852005]], [[R202]]). A Starship R97 hides ([[R97]]) docks nothing, and no cue names a card: a module is a picture of a public piece. Everything lands inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]), scaled with the effects speed ([[R201]]).

This amends [[R2801000]]: a fuse run's piece flies as its module to its hardpoint, not as a card back to the ship.
