#79 Twinspell reads "Activate: The next Spell you play this turn gains Echo +{echoGain}. Then this goes to the graveyard." and its Radiant face "Activate: The next Spell you play this turn gains Echo +{echoGain}." (`echoGain` 1 on both faces), where through v0.5.11o it was a lasting grant with no Activate, "Your next Spell gains Echo +1 (Radiant +2). Then this goes to the graveyard." ([[R30]], [[R209]]). Its Activate is once a turn, its controller's alone ([[R384]]).

- **The grant is its player's.** The Activate puts an `echoNextSpell` rider on its controller with no `sourceId`, its amount the `echoGain` the face reads as it is activated, expiring `thisTurn`. A rider no permanent owns grants its own amount: the next Spell that player plays takes it at [[§10.5]] step 4, a cast included ([[R70]]), and a Unit, Field Spell or Trap played meanwhile leaves it waiting; a countered Spell never reaches step 4, so leaves it too ([[R448]]). This turn's cleanup ends it unused ([[§2.2]]). It outlives the Twinspell that gave it, which may be bounced, destroyed or stolen without taking it, and a Twinspell standing on the field gives nothing until it is activated, by whoever controls it then.
- **Grants add up.** Each rider gives its own amount and the one next Spell takes them all, as two Hearthstone "your next spell this turn" effects both apply to it: two Twinspells, or a Radiant one bounced and played again (leaving the field reset its uses, [[R78]]), give Echo +2.
- **"Then this goes to the graveyard" is a plain move, `send_to_graveyard`, and no death.** After the grant, the base face moves itself from the field to the graveyard, as a fired Trap goes there and as [[R30]]'s consumed Twinspell always went: no `destroyed`, no Death trigger, no Reborn, no count of deaths ([[R55]]) and no credit for a kill, and Indestructible does not hold it, since nothing destroys it. It lands in the graveyard of the player it stood for, as a dying card does ([[R2618000]]), or wherever a replacement sends a card bound for one ([[R460]]); a unit token ceases to exist ([[R11]]). The verb moves only a card on the field and leaves any other where it is.
- **Fused.** Twinspell's text fused onto another card ([[R77]]) gives that card the Activate, and the card is what goes to the graveyard, a Unit carrying the base text included. Fused onto a Twinspell, the card has two Activates ([[R102]]), but the first one used sends it away, so it gives Echo +1 once.
- **Radiant.** The same grant, and the card stays on the field, so it serves once each turn. `echoGain` is at its floor of 1 on both faces, so a Nerf cannot move it, and a Buff makes it 2 ([[R386]]).
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, the lasting grant the engine still keeps ([[R30]], [[R209]], [[R767000]]).

Rejected: the `sacrifice` verb, a death that fires Death and Reborn and counts toward the destroyed counter, where the card's own words say only that it goes to the graveyard; `destroy`, a death too, which waits for the state check and which Indestructible ignores, so a base Twinspell a Buff made Indestructible ([[R2436000]]) would stay to serve every turn like the Radiant face; and a `tributeSelf` cost, a Sacrifice paid before the effect, where the words put the move after the grant. Moving it first would change nothing the rider does, since it has no source, but the words say "then".
