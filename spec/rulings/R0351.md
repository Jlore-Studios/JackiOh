A face-down Trap or Field Trap shows its cost to both players: the other player's view of its zone is `{ faceDown: true, cost }`, the cost the controller's own view shows ([[§6.3]]'s cost read on the field: its `costOverride` or printed cost, plus its `costMod`, [[R65]]), and nothing more. It is a deliberate reveal, which the designer asked for; [[R33]] is otherwise unchanged. The controller's own view marks each card the other player sees face down `unrevealed`, until it is public (a Field Trap that has fired). This row is the view's half; [[R370]] draws the cost and [[R371]] the mark, on the client
