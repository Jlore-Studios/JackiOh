S+ #141 Stinky 1x Engineer reads "Cry: Nerf {nerf} other random permanents", "Destroy {nerf}
other random permanents" on the Radiant face. "Permanents" is the cards of either field — Units
and backrow ([[§3.2]]) — "other" everyone but itself, and "random" uniform picks without
replacement ([[R60]]), drawn as the Cry resolves ([[R1]]): the base face `degrade`s that many
(C+ #70's shape), the Radiant face `destroy`s that many, one `destroy` per pick in one state
check ([[R59]], [[§4.5]]). A hand, a library and a deck are never in scope. Fewer standing than
`nerf` takes them all; an empty scope fizzles and the Engineer still lands ([[R129]]). (The voice
line, "Prod is down. Report the intern.", is S26's render; S+ cards have no text/audio entries
yet.)

Rejected: the hand or the library in scope; exactly three or nothing; and the Radiant face
reaching only the enemy's side.
