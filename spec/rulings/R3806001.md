Found by issue #3806's fuzz gate (seed 74 of its changed cards' wave). Stinkyscape #111 Shockwave447's
Cry ("Replace both players' hands with random complicated cards") fused with Stinkyscape+ #23
GIGA-ify's text ("Make a permanent in your hand GIGA and Indestructible") put the card GIGA-ify picked
from the hand into the graveyard, and the grant after it still landed there: a graveyard card held
`grantedKeywords`, field state a card off the field never keeps ([[R78]]).

[[R174]] already passes a pick on the field by once an earlier effect took it off. The spec says
nothing of a pick off the field, so this follows Hearthstone: an effect whose target has moved to the
graveyard or been removed from play does nothing to it. A card chosen with the play ([[R81]]) that
lies in a graveyard or an exile pile an effect of this same run put it in (an `enteredGraveyard` or
`exiled` event in the run's own window, [[R136]]) is gone for every effect of the run after that: a
grant, a buff or a Make Radiant aimed at it does nothing, and the run goes on. A card chosen where it
already lay, a graveyard pick, is still the card chosen, and a pick still in its hand takes what it is
given there, keeping it onto the field ([[R1878003]], [[R3555000]]).

Rejected: dropping every grant or buff that would land on a graveyard or exile card, since a card
evolved in its graveyard keeps what its Evolve gave it ([[R3566004]]).
