Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI printed S+ #153 Noxious Rounds at 3/1 with a Radiant face of 6/1, from 1/1 (Radiant 2/2), and v0.6.11d shipped it so: [[R275]]'s stat half holds a Radiant Unit's health to at least twice its base face's, which a 1 → 1 breaks, so it stood as the standard's second named exception beside S #83 Halo ([[R4285006]]). The designer then normalized it: the Radiant face is 6/2, twice both of the base face's stats, so Noxious Rounds is no exception and `STAT_EXCEPTIONS` holds Halo alone again. Its Toxic hits read its attack, 3 and Radiant 6. The replays of v0.6.11d fold under that version's 6/1 ([[R767000]]).
