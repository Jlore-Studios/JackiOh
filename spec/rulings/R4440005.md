Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI prints S+ #153 Noxious Rounds at 3/1 with a Radiant face of 6/1, from 1/1 (Radiant 2/2). [[R275]]'s stat half holds a Radiant Unit's health to at least twice its base face's, which a 1 → 1 breaks. The designer's printed numbers stand, as [[R275]] lets them for a named card and as [[R4285006]] let S #83 Halo's 3/1 → 6/1: Noxious Rounds is the second entry of `STAT_EXCEPTIONS`. Its Toxic hits read its attack, which doubles, so the effect half still has its Radiant face twice as hard. Should the designer mean 6/2, the catalog's Radiant health is the one change and this exception goes.
