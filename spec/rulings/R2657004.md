"It may attack again" (`mayAttackAgain`, B5 E35) gives a fresh exertion and nothing else: a Unit that entered the field this turn stays summoning sick ([[§4.1]]) through every attack an effect gives it that turn. A Rush Unit refreshed on its summon turn may attack again a Unit only, never the enemy hero ([[§6.1]]): a Chaos Golem or a Classic Golem that kills on the turn it is summoned cannot aim its second attack at the hero. [[R2521002]] made it so for Tmack Trav first, with `keepSick`; since this ruling every refresh does, so `keepSick` says what the verb does anyway. The older reading, which lifted the sickness for the rest of the turn, is `liftSick`, which only the scripts kept for those versions ask for (Classic Golem's and Tmack Trav's, [[R767000]]). This is the owner's balance pass (issue #2657).
