The opponent's reply ([[§9.9]]) tried a switch to Defense Position only once no attack was worth making (issue #1696), and a hit on the hero always is. A unit able to reach the seat's face therefore spent its one exertion of the turn on that hit ([[§4.1]]) and could no longer wall. Take the seat's 7/7 against the opponent at 7 with a 4/4: the reply swung the 4/4 at the face and left it open, so the line was scored as lethal on board (the evaluation's `lethal_on_board`), when one switch puts the 4/4's Taunt in the 7/7's way and the opponent lives. The AI's own seat already walls there ([[R2976000]]); its model of the opponent never did, so it planned against an opponent weaker than itself and over-ranked lines that leave a blocker standing.

Now, when `wall_switches` ([[R2976000]]) offers any wall to the opponent, the reply step tries the best ranked attack and the first `AI_REPLY.defense_switches` of those walls through `best_try`, one node each, judged by the opponent's own evaluation with the seat swinging next, as issue #1696's switches are judged. The attack is tried first, so an attack that wins the game is taken at once. A wall is kept only when it beats both standing still and that attack; then the reply goes on, and the units left attack as before. When the attack wins the comparison, the reply goes on from it.

Two guards keep it to a real block. It walls nothing while the opponent's attacks left reach the seat's hero together past its Taunts (`ready_gap`), since a player with lethal takes it. A Deft unit that may still attack is left out of the walls: it may attack and then switch ([[R49]]), so it switches after its attack, by issue #1696's step or by this one on a later step.

Off lethal nothing changes: `wall_switches` returns nothing there, so no node is spent and every reply is what it was. It adds no constant, no weight and no rule of the game, it reads only the public board, and `defense_switches` at 0 turns it off with issue #1696's switches. Hearthstone semantics: a player facing lethal blocks rather than chips.

Rejected:
- A separate on/off flag. `AI_REPLY.defense_switches` at 0 already turns off every switch in the reply.
- Trying every trade that kills the seat's lethal attacker in the same comparison. It costs more nodes, and the best ranked attack is already in the comparison whenever it is such a trade.
- Walling while the opponent's attacks win only together. `AI_REPLY.open_lethal` is off, so the step's attack is one hit, and the wall beat it by the evaluation's lethal threat: with two 4/4s against the seat at 8, the opponent walled and missed its own lethal.
- Walling a Deft unit before its attack. It gave up the hit it could still make and switch after.
- Trying only the walls that end the lethal alone. `wall_switches` does not return the threat each leaves, and a wall that only lowers it loses to standing still on the evaluation's flat cliff, for one node.

Known limits: a block that needs two walls stays out of reach while `lethal_threat` is one flat cliff, as with [[R2976000]]. A Deft unit in Defense Position still switches to Attack to swing (`deft_switch`, [[R49]]) whatever the threat.
