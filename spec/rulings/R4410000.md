Issue #4410: players could not find how to sell Stinkyscape #89 Spice, the one card the Marketplace still bought, from the hand through the panel's Sell button ([[R1861000]]), while every Resource pays its Credits as it is played or discarded ([[R2250001]]). Spice now pays the way a Resource does.

A face that sets `paysCredits` (`StaticFlags.pays_credits`, which replaces `handSale`; only Spice sets it) pays its owner its quote in Credits when they play it and when it is discarded from their hand, at the moments and with the exclusions of [[R2250001]]: a play pays as it is made, right after `cardPlayed` and before its Cry ([[§10.5]] step 4), so Spice's Cry Discovers after the Credits arrive; a discard pays after the card reaches its graveyard, whatever made it; a cast by an effect is no play of its player's and pays nothing; a card that leaves the hand any other way pays nothing. It pays by its card, as a Resource pays by its definition, not by its text: a Vanilla Spice pays too, as a Vanilla Resource does. The quote is the Marketplace's this turn for its face while it is open: each registered `paysCredits` card is quoted at every requote after the Resources, its base face then its Radiant face, in sorted order, where and as [[R1861000]] drew the hand-sale quotes, so every game draws as it did ([[§10.7]], [[R1853002]]). While it is closed the quote is one draw of the match rng uniformly in `HAND_SALE_PRICE`, 4 to 13 Credits, or `HAND_SALE_RADIANT_PRICE`, 8 to 25, on the Radiant face (5 to 15 and 9 to 29 since issue #4440, [[R4440001]]; [[R4154206]]; `resources::sell_price`), and the gain opens the Marketplace as any first Credits do ([[R1853000]]). While it is open, Spice in its owner's hand shows its quote in braces as a Resource does ([[R3047004]]): the same R280 preview, labeled `Credits`, its owner's alone.

Nothing sells at the Marketplace any more. `marketSell { instanceId }` stays on the wire, so an older client's action and a stored log still parse, and the reducer always refuses it with "nothing sells at the Marketplace" (`marketplace::NOTHING_SELLS`), once the checks every action takes have passed: on another seat's turn it is refused as any action is ([[§10.2]]). `legalActions` lists none, the view's `marketplace.sales` stays on the wire and is always empty ([[§10.8]]), the panel draws no Sell section ([[§10.10]]), and nothing emits `marketSold`, which stays a wire event so an older log's events read. `sales` is kept rather than dropped so every view that holds the Marketplace reads as it did, without a Spice in it: dropping the key changes the view of every such game, which the cross-build replay (`classify`, [[R1365000]]) reads as some fifty cards that play exactly as before playing differently, and an older client that reads it would find nothing there. With no sale on offer, [[R1853004]]'s "a deal on offer" is a buy alone. [[R1853003]]'s buys are unchanged.

Spice is no Resource: it keeps its (1) cost, its Rare rarity and its Discover, and the Credits come on top of them (the issue's default). `is_resource_card` is false for it, so no Resource roll ("Gain a Resource") gives it, no "discard a Resource" takes it and no Resource count reads it; Abandoned Spice Tower (S #173) still adds three to the hand. Its face prints no `Credits: min–max` line, which [[R2521009]] writes from a Resource's `resource` block. A replay of a game that played, discarded or sold Spice under an older version is refused by its final hash rather than shown wrong, since the payment and the refusal are the engine's and no kept script could bring the sale back; no script is kept ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: giving Spice a `resource` block, which would make it a Resource (a tray roll, the Resource counts, a printed range); deleting `marketSell` and `marketSold`, which older clients and logs carry, and the view's `sales`, for the reason above; printing the range on its face.
