The issue describes each version's look; this note fixes how each is drawn. Like every look, each is
a pure function of the card's id, name, tags, type and set, base and Radiant faces both, and wears only
a full face, as Full art does ([[R2725000]]). Neither is a full art: each keeps every part of the
normal face, the cost gem, the type's panel colour, the crest, the name banner, the type line, the
rules box and its words, the tags and the stats, and prints its words in the viewer's own hand
([[R2723000]] is Full art's alone).

**Set showcase** (`version-set-showcase`) wears its card's set's look (`data-showcase`). Each shipped
set has one: Core's, Classic's (Classic+ too), Meditative's (Meditative+ too), Stinkyscape's and
Training Grounds'; a card of a set with none, a boss's or a custom card's, draws its normal face. Its
picture is the normal one, whichever painter draws it, under one SVG filter of its set's
(`showcaseSvg`, `data-art-picture="showcase-<family>"`), as the Sketch's, Pixel's and Comic's are
([[R2940001]], [[R2942001]]):

| Set | Picture | Base face's frame and banner |
|---|---|---|
| Core | a glass orb: its colour and contrast pushed, a blue rim and a glint over it | a blue orb edge, a sky bezel and glow, a capsule banner in the text face at its boldest |
| Classic | temple stone: greyed, its lightness lit as relief in stone | an edge of stone courses, a grey bezel, a stone lintel, the name spaced in capitals |
| Meditative | ink wash: greyed, softened and cut to four tones of ink on paper, a red seal | an ink edge, a paper bezel, a brush stroke of paper, the name in italics |
| Stinkyscape | deep space: its lightness mapped from the dark of space to violet, sparse stars in its dark | a starry slate edge, a violet bezel and glow, a console banner, the name wide in capitals |
| Training Grounds | carved wood: cut to three tones of wood and grained | a rope edge, a wood bezel, a plank, the name in capitals at its boldest |

The banner's shape and lettering are the set's on either face; its colours, the edge, the bezel and
the window's ring are a base face's wearing no border, so a worn border keeps its own edge and bezel,
the ring taking its colour. Every name keeps AA contrast on its banner, and no rule sets a size or the
type's panel colour, so the name's fitting stands. The lettering is the display and text faces: the
sets' signature hands ([[R2715002]]) have no metric-matched fallback, and a fitted name would shift
when one loads.

**Out of frame** (`version-out-of-frame`) draws the normal picture in two parts (`ArtPart`): its
backdrop, every layer but the subject's, in a smaller window, inset `OUT_OF_FRAME_WINDOW_TOP_PCT` from
the art frame's top and `OUT_OF_FRAME_WINDOW_SIDE_PCT` from each side on its foot, the frame's panel
showing round it; and its subject alone, on nothing, in a square `OUT_OF_FRAME_SUBJECT_PCT` of the art
frame's height whose foot sinks `OUT_OF_FRAME_SUBJECT_SINK_PCT` below the frame's, under the name banner
(`.cf-art--cutout`, `data-art-part="subject"`), so the subject rises above the window and past its
sides as if stepping out of the card. Both stay inside the art
frame, inside the face's clipped panel: the subject never passes the card's rim, a worn border's edge
or a Radiant face's gold edge, never covers a neighbouring card, and the crest, name and type line,
the gems and stats, and the rules box lie over it, so each stays readable. A delivered file has no
parts, so Out of frame draws the painter's picture until an artist delivers theirs; a Starship in play
and a Legacy card, whose painters draw one picture ([[R2249000]], [[R2724000]]), are offered none, and
a face of one would draw its normal window.

Every restyled picture and every part is cached apart from the plain picture, which keeps its key and
bytes.

**Radiant.** [[R2725004]] holds under each: no colour of the two, edge, bezel, ring, banner, glow,
treatment, tone, seal or star, falls in the `RADIANT_GOLD_*` window, and none tints a base face's
picture gold (Training Grounds' wood is brown, short of the window's hue). A Radiant face keeps its
gold-foil frame and gold bezel, its gilded panel and banner, its gold rules box, [[R277]]'s marks
wherever its words show, its gold stats and its picture's Radiant paint: a Radiant Set showcase washes
its painted picture back over every treatment but Core's (`SHOWCASE_RADIANT_WASH`), whose glass keeps
the picture's own colours, and a Radiant Out of frame draws its backdrop and its subject in their
Radiant paint.
