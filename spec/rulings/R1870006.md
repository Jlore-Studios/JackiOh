Stinkyscape #4.6 The Big Crunch reads "Cast one of each Call to Chaos", its Radiant face "Cast a Radiant one of each Call to Chaos." "Every Call to Chaos" is one of each edition, never every copy in the game: one new card of each Call to Chaos definition, in catalog order (Core #95, C+ #73, M #95, S #150 and S #151, the set's own two named by id while it is held, [[R1420]]), each its own cast and a play for every rule, its choices its caster's ([[R70]]), rolling and naming its own effects to both players ([[R436]]) and landing in its caster's graveyard after it ([[R87]]). Their nested casts are held by `CALL_TO_CHAOS_CHAIN_CAP` ([[R28]]) and `RANDOM_CAST_CHAIN_CAP` ([[R452]]). The Radiant face casts each Radiant, three effects each ([[R423]]).
