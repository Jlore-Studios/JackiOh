Amends [[R2214000]], whose skill miss listed the seat's candidates on the true state and dealt the world it priced them on with [[R762]]'s shown-cost match off, as the greedy baseline deals its own. A ladder bot below full skill plays the miss at the decisions its roll does not give to the AI ([[R747000]]), against real people, and the miss was the one decision path that read the true state past [[R185]]'s two reads: its list could answer from the cards the seat cannot see, and its world could hold a face-down card at a cost other than the one the card shows ([[R351]]). On #4216's Doom Shroom board (p2 at 7 Health with one face-down card showing (4), the other two Traps at (4) seen in p2's graveyard, and p1's two units swinging for exactly 7), the miss swung in 158 of 200 seeds, pricing a lethal on worlds that held a Trap at another cost, where every world at the shown (4) holds Doom Shroom, which exiles both attackers.

**The list.** The miss lists the seat's candidates (`candidate_set`, as before) on the probe world: `redact(state, seat)` dealt once on `AI_SEARCH.probe_seed` (`decide::probe_world`), the world the AI's own decision lists its candidates on ([[R1171000]]). Not on the redaction itself, whose face-down placeholders have no definition for a play's targets to read: Core #50 K-Pop Fanatic's Allure, listing the enemy's permanents, panicked there. The probe draws on its own stream, so a lone candidate still takes no draw from the seat's.

**The world.** The world the miss prices its candidates on is dealt from the same redaction with the default sampler, so a face-down card showing a cost takes a Trap of that cost while one is unseen ([[R762]]), as every world the search reads does. On the Doom Shroom board the miss now swings in none of the 200 seeds. The greedy baseline keeps the match off (`greedy_action`), so the quality gates' yardstick does not move; the miss is no gate's yardstick.

**What it reads.** Past `ai_to_act` and `redact`, the miss reads nothing of the true state ([[R185]]): it is a function of `redact(state, seat)` and the seat's stream, and handed the redaction it plays the move it plays on the state, with the same draws. Its draws are as many as before: none for a draw offer, a mulligan or a lone candidate, otherwise the world's and the pick's, both on the seat's own stream, so a seeded game still replays.

**Legality.** The seat's own legal actions read no hidden card: a face-down card passes its chooser's tag filters ([[R924]]) and its lasting text is inert until revealed ([[R1487000]]); of what a live one on a kept older script did, the Attack and Health it moves are kept by the redaction, and no dealt world samples a live aura the board does not show ([[R602]], [[R767000]]). So the probe's list is the true state's, and the reason [[R2214000]] gave for listing on the true state (a list read off a dealt world could hold a move the true state refuses) does not arise. Measured over 30 seeded games of `build_ai_deck` decks with both seats playing the miss at every decision: 2,494 decisions, the probe's list equal to the true state's, in order, at every one, the miss handed the redaction equal to the miss handed the state (move and draws) at every one, and no move refused. A move the true state still refused would meet the ladder host's one fallback, as any refused move does.

**Ratings.** [[R748000]]'s seeds stay as measured: the change moves few picks (#4216's plan counted 27 of 1,251 sampled decisions moved by the shown-cost match), and the next calibration measures it.

**Rejected.**

- Listing on the redaction itself, as #4216 first proposed: it panics on a face-down placeholder whenever a play's targets read one.
- Listing on the miss's own world: it would draw from the seat's stream before a lone candidate is known.
- Trying the pick on the true state before playing it, as `decide` does with its own move: a second read of the true state, for a refusal no measured decision produced.
- Keeping the shown-cost match off for the miss: it is what let the miss price worlds the seat was shown could not be, and the gates read the greedy baseline, not the miss.
