Five of [[R2655001]]'s cards make tokens that print full art with them:

| Parent | Its tokens that print full art |
|---|---|
| League of Losers (`classicplus-019`) | Top Loser `-019-1`, Jungle Loser `-019-2`, Mid Loser `-019-3`, Support Loser `-019-4`, Bot Loser `-019-5` |
| Bread and Butter (`core-018`) | Bread Token `core-t-bread` |
| The Mother Pancake (`classicplus-012`) | Devour `-012-1`, Death Boil `-012-2`, Fluffy Grip `-012-3`, Powder Spray `-012-4`, Anti-Waffle Shell `-012-5`, Frozen Wastes `-012-6`, Legion of the Hungry `-012-7`, Frostspatula `-012-8` |
| Felinor Flagbearer (`classicplus-046`) | Felinor Flagbearer Prime `-046-1` |
| J-lease J-Jungle EX-plorer (`classicplus-075`) | J-lease J-Jungle EX-plorer Pack `-075-1` |

The map is data beside the full-art list, `apps/web/src/cards/fullArtTokens.json`, each parent's
catalog id to its tokens' ids in full, and no name in code. A token is never owned and never in a
pack, so it has no registry entry of its own.

**What a listed token wears.** It prints full art, at the tier, wherever its parent's copy would:
the face asks of the parent what it would ask of a full-art card ([[R2655000]]), the parent's own
pick, the viewer's ownership of that full art on the parent, the Full-art cards setting for a parent
with no pick, and a tutorial lesson's board, which prints none ([[R1108000]]). So a token whose
parent's full art is unowned or switched off prints its normal face, and a token not in the map
never prints full art. Its art is its own ([[R660]]: a delivered file first, the procedural picture
otherwise), cropped to the full face, and its face says it is a token as every full-art token's
does ([[R1017001]]'s face, with the token mark at its foot).

**Why the map and not the generator.** A Created card wears its generator's foil, finish and border
by `createdBy` ([[R1956001]]), which only the viewer's own card's view carries. A token's full art
follows its parent through the map instead, so it reads the same on every board, and another seat's
token follows the parent's entry of its owner's frozen loadout ([[R1834004]]). That entry travels
once a card dealt from that deck has shown ([[R1834003]]), so another seat's token prints its
parent's full art once the parent has shown, and its normal face until then; no frame ever names a
card the receiver has not seen. A listed token wears its parent's pick whichever card made it, since
the map names the card it belongs to.

This amends [[R1956001]]'s "its full art is its own card's, never inherited": a listed token's full
art is its parent's. A Created card that is no listed token keeps its own card's.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a
replay hash, and the engine reads none of it.

Hearthstone semantics: a golden or signature card's tokens come in its version, as a golden minion's
tokens are golden ([[R1956001]]).

Rejected: the generator (`createdBy`) as the parent, which an opponent's copy never carries, so the
owner's token and the opponent's view of it would differ; registry entries for the tokens, which
nobody can own; and the tokens' parents in code by name.
