Part of #3604 (issue #3608): an animation that repeats forever in the web client moves only `transform` and `opacity`, with the individual `translate`, `scale` and `rotate` that compose with them, so the compositor runs it and the browser repaints nothing per frame. A keyframe that sets `box-shadow`, `filter`, `backdrop-filter`, `background-position` (and its `-x` and `-y`), `stroke-dashoffset`, `clip-path` or `text-shadow` makes the browser repaint its element every frame, and a loop never stops. A test reads every stylesheet under `apps/web/src` and fails on a loop over one of them; the allow-list of loops that may keep a paint property, each with its reason, is empty, since every loop in the client has a compositor form.

The technique is one rule: the glow, shadow, tint or sheen is drawn once on a pseudo-element, invisible at rest, and the loop moves that layer's `opacity` (or its `transform`). The hand's glow ([[R195]]) draws its inner rim on the card's `::before`, because `.card` clips what lies outside it: the card's own box-shadow, the 2px ring the component spec reads and the halo after it, stays at its rest value, so the breathing is the rim inside the border. A face-down card's brightening and a Mythic's hand shimmer ([[R3345000]]) are layers on the card's `::before` and `::after`, End turn's, the match clock's, the lane picker's, the Duos portal's and the coach's pulses are layers on the element's own `::after`, a skeleton's sheen is a band sliding across its block, and the veil's hatch is a layer one tile wider than the card that slides one tile. A path cannot be moved by transform, so the drag arrow's dots and the opponent's arrow's dashes march in four steps: four copies of the shaft, one quarter of the dash period apart, each shown for a quarter of the loop. Every duration keeps its `--anim-scale` factor and every loop its selector's conditions, and reduced motion still stops each one on its still frame ([[R200]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. Hearthstone semantics do not reach frame budgets.

Rejected: allow-listing the two dashed-arrow loops as "one SVG path, no compositor equivalent", which would leave two repainting loops for a guard meant to leave none; and drawing the hand's outer halo on `.hand-slot` or `.zone`, since `.zone` already uses both its pseudo-elements and a field card has no wrapper of its own.
