The AI's evaluation priced a readable backrow card by its cost alone, so what a Field Spell does every turn counted for nothing: Core #6 Mana Well's mana and Core #59 Unbiased Immigration's card were worth what a blank card of their cost is. The sweep of record of 2026-10-29 (`training/history/sweep-2026-10-29-3.md`, pass 2) found Mana Well played 4 times in 62 affordable turns on Easy, its mean evaluation change exactly the backrow price less the hand price, and Mana Well, Unbiased Immigration and Meditative #26 Alternate Fate are on `SHADOW_WATCH`. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, once, on the same probe world as [[R2424000]]'s Death prices (a function of what the seat sees, [[R185]]), the AI fires each start-of-turn and end-of-turn hook of every card in its own backrow that has one, and of every Field Spell in its own hand that has one, set first by the sandbox's spawn ([[R923001]]) in the zone a set would take. A hook is fired as its controller's turn fires it ([[R62]]): queued on the engine's trigger queue and drained by its resolution loop ([[R68]]), under its game's pool hold ([[R2201001]]), on the state's own random stream, so a random hook (Unbiased Immigration's card) is priced by one draw of the probe world's stream, as a Death probe's is. It is fired where the decision stands, not at the turn's start or end. The price of a firing is the controller's evaluation after it less before it, plus `AI_EVAL.next_turn_mana` for each crystal of mana it gained, which the evaluation does not count otherwise; a card's price is the sum of its hooks'. The probes run on a sandbox copy ([[R923000]]) with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), so no step ends the turn ([[R82]]), with no price passed, a Death price included, and within `AI_TURN_HOOK_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes ([[R186]]'s budget), one node a firing and one a spawn. A card is keyed by its definition and its face, so a copy the search sets later reads the same price. `material` adds `AI_EVAL.turn_hook` of the price for each card in the seat's own backrow it can read, outside the Brittle share. `AI_EVAL.turn_hook` starts at 0.5, untuned, as R2424000's weight did (at 1.0 the smoke gate's Hard against Easy read 16 of 20, its floor; at 0.5, 17); the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. Both prices travel with that one decision as one value, passed by reference from its root through the search to `material` (`Prices`, #2582), so a call outside a decision is passed none and prices nothing.

**What is not priced.**
- A hook that leaves a prompt open (Core #65 Masochism Mask's choice), ends the game or panics, which the probe neither answers nor scores; a panic counts as a failed simulation.
- A card the probes' nodes did not reach.
- A hand Field Spell on a full backrow, or one the sandbox may not spawn.
- A unit's turn hooks, which [[R2565000]] prices.
- The enemy's backrow, which is priced as before, so no hidden card changes a decision; [[R2565000]] prices the part of it the seat can read, never a face-down card.
- Anything while a prompt is open, which refuses the sandbox's tools, or once the game is over.

**Rejected.** A new sandbox tool that fires a hook, which is an engine, wire and client change for what the engine's public trigger queue already does; playing the turn out, which prices every card's turn at once and costs about five nodes a card; probing the enemy's face-up Field Spells too, which [[R2565000]] does; a thread-local holding the prices for the decision, as both rulings first did, which hid an input from every scorer (replaced by the `Prices` parameter, #2582); and a flat weight per hook, as [[R458]]'s modifiers have, which cannot price a hook that harms its holder.
