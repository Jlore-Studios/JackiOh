A Unit's Allure ([[R963]], M #39.5 Jade Beauty's) waits for the start of the Allurer's next turn and then steals the Unit, or destroys it where it stands when there is no room for it, a destroy nothing prevents ([[R4205000]]). Jade Beauty is a Unit, so its Allure reaches a Unit Immune to Spells ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]). The engine runs the Allure itself in [[R62]]'s delayed stage, with no `hookResolving` of its own, so when the same stage cast a Spell first (a delayed Call to Chaos whose prompt paused the stage, here the C+ #73's "Destroy all enemy permanents"), the Allure's destroy of an immune Stinkyscape+ Alnilam token landed in that Spell's run and I21 blamed the Spell (fuzz seed 164 on this issue's pull request).

**The Allure took it.** I21 allows one `controlChanged` or killerless `destroyed` of an immune Unit when the step began with an Allure waiting on it for the start of a turn, the walk is in that turn's start stage (the step began in it, or a `turnStarted` of that player opened it), the Allure's mark came off ([[R437]]) before the event, and the entry is gone after the step. The entry resolves before its steal names the Unit, where a Spell's destroy names the Unit first and only then drops the entry and its mark, so a Spell's reach is still a finding. A second event naming the Unit after the steal is judged as any other.
