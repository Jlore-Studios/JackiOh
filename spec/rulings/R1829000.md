When every card above a card in its Stack pile has left (destroyed, bounced, exiled, stolen, captured ([[R1284]]), tributed, ceased to exist), the card that resumes as the top ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]) is summoning sick as a card that entered the field on that turn is, as a Reborn body is ([[R83]]). It takes that turn as its `summonedTurn` ([[§4.1]]): with neither Rush nor Charge a Unit cannot attack for the rest of that turn, Rush lets it attack a unit and Charge a unit or the hero, and on its controller's next turn it attacks freely. That holds in a unit pile and in a backrow pile, for a Stack base resuming as the Unit on it leaves ([[R1281]]), and when the top steps off the pile without leaving the field (an Animated card stepping into a unit zone or back to its backrow zone). It keeps its exertion: a unit that attacked before it was covered does not attack again that turn.

It never left its zone, so a zone [[R64]] reserved for a Reborn top stays reserved and the body returns onto the pile as before ([[R175]]). No event reports it, and it does not answer the event whose removal uncovered it ([[R212]]).

Not uncoverings: an Equip card fusing into its host, as any ingredient fusing into the card a fusion keeps, which keeps the turn it arrived ([[R77]], [[R1086]]); a move that lifts a whole pile (a board swap, a rotation); and Rollback setting a restored side's piles down, where a card that stood on that side keeps its sickness ([[R566]]).

Hearthstone has no piles, so this follows its rule for a minion that enters the board again, as the designer asked after Classic+ #4 Juhan Biggest Bat's copies, left beneath it, attacked the moment the top died on a later turn of their controller's. Refreshing the exertion was rejected: a Rush or Windfury unit covered and uncovered in one turn would attack again.
