A rematch copied its finished match's catalog version, and a match plays under the version it began on ([[R767000]]). So a rematch made after a card patch went live played the cards as they were before the patch while the client drew today's, and an All Random rematch dealt fresh decks from today's catalog to play under the old rules ([[R1372]], [[R924000]]). Every other door into a match already refused a stale catalog ([[§9.4]]): the queue, a room's join and a co-op room's join.

Now an offer of a rematch ([[R672]]) on a match whose catalog version is not the server's is refused before it is recorded, a person's offer and a ladder bot's alike ([[R639000]]): `409 update_required`, its `details` the co-op join's `{ expected, received }` (the server's version, then the match's), its message "The cards changed since this game. Start a new one.", which the rematch panel shows. No offer is kept and no match is made, so the players start a new game. A rematch of a match on the server's catalog is made as before. This follows Hearthstone, where a player on an earlier version updates before their next game.

Starting the rematch on today's catalog and checking its frozen decks again was rejected: a frozen deck may not be legal under the new catalog, as the co-op join found, and it would add a validation path no other door has.
