S #137 Starfleet Commander's Cry and S #176 Preemptive Strike read "If you have a Dormant Starship" once, as the text begins, and that read holds for the whole text. The Starship found then is launched ([[R1852006]]), and the Discover of a War Doctrine and its cast follow ([[R1859006]]), though the launch leaves no Dormant Starship behind. Hearthstone checks a Battlecry's condition once, as the Battlecry resolves, and what the Battlecry's own first step does to the board does not undo its later steps.

- **A fused Cry that asks.** The launch runs the Starship's fused Cries ([[R1852003]]), and one of them may ask, as S #40 Poison Capsule's placement does. Its prompt pauses the text ([[R113]]); once it is answered, the Discover opens. The read is the part's memo (`for_each_card`), so the list rebuilt after the answer is the launch and the Discover again, resumed after the launch.
- **No Dormant Starship.** With none as the text begins, nothing happens: no launch and no Discover.
- **Replays.** Before issue #5106 the read was made again each time the list was built, so the rebuild after a fused Cry's answer found the Starship launched, built nothing, and lost the Discover. Each card's `script_before_launch_read_once` keeps that read for the replays of the versions before the fix ([[R767000]], [[R4779000]]).
