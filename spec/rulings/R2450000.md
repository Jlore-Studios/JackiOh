The spec said what a Quest card's view carries ([[R404]], [[R1854000]]) but not what its face prints, and each
Quest card ([[R1837000]]) printed its quest its own way: The Greatest Heist and Job Application every stage, cut
short to their first lines ([[R1017000]]) before the stage they stood at, and In Too Deep a summary and none of
its quests. Hearthstone's Questlines print only the part they are on, the next shown once the last is done, and
so does a Quest card's face now.

- **The wording.** A staged Quest prints each stage as a line of its text, `Quest: Stage 2, Comradery. <goal>.
  Reward: <reward>.` (a Chinese face's `任务：第2阶段，同袍之谊。…奖励：…`, [[R1301]]), so its face can print one.
  Job Application's stage lines take The Greatest Heist's layout, and its intro line goes.
- **On the field.** The face prints its lines that are no stage (its keywords, its Cry, its Death) and, in place
  of its stage lines, the stage the view's line stands at ([[R1854000]]), as `Stage 2 of 3, Comradery. Quest:
  Have 2 Acclaimed Units you control die (1/2). Reward: …`: while the stage is open, the quest the view has open
  on it with its progress (the goal it drew, where it draws one, [[R1854002]]) and its printed reward; while the
  line waits to hear back with it, the turns left, `Stage 2 of 3, The Interview, hearing back in 2 turns.`, and
  its printed goal ([[R1854004]]); once every stage is done, `Quest complete`.
- **In Too Deep.** Its tree has no stages: its "current stage" is the quests open on it ([[R404]], and every
  path at once on its Radiant face, [[R543]]). On the field its face prints its text and then each open quest
  with its progress and the rewards it offers, `Quest: Draw 2 cards (1/2). Rewards: Heal your hero 6; Deal 3
  damage to a target.`, or `Quest complete` when none is open, and then each aura it holds.
- **Off the field** (a hand, the deck builder, the Almanac, the collection, a Discover's picks, a reveal) no
  stage has opened, and a staged Quest prints its first as the first of n, `Stage 1 of 3, Initiation. Quest:
  Make an Acclaimed Unit Radiant. Reward: …`; In Too Deep prints its text alone.
- **The whole text stays readable.** A staged face prints less than its whole text, so its whole text, every
  stage, stands on the left of its hover preview, in the touch sheet and in the detail view ([[R1017000]]), and
  the inspect overlay lists every stage ([[R1854000]]). Measured by the stage it prints, The Greatest Heist's base
  face fits the face text limit whole; its Radiant face and both of Job Application's, a line or two over,
  clamp. A Radiant face's marks ([[R277]]) and in play its tuned numbers ([[R386]]) stay on the words they mark.
- **The same for both players.** It is all the catalog face's words and the view's public quest line, which
  both seats see ([[R1854000]], [[R404]]); the client works out no rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
