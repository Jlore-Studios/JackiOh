A card that doubles or triples a Unit's stat takes it no higher than `MULTIPLIED_STAT_CAP` (999) as shown ([[§10.4]]): S #119 Unsecure System gives each Unit +Attack up to the cap ([[R1868001]]), and S #10.2 Divine Spirit gives +Health up to it, current and maximum together ([[R1867003]]). A Unit whose stat is already at or above the cap gains nothing. A flat Buff (+N) is unchanged and may still take a stat past the cap. The scripts through v0.6.6d, which had no cap, are kept, so a replay on those versions still doubles past it ([[R767000]]).

Why: the spec does not say, and Hearthstone has no cap. But copies from Deadman's Hand let one game of the handicapped fuzz wave cast Unsecure System ten times, and the Attack it doubled reached 3,066 (seed 9203), past the fuzz's `GROWTH_NUMBER_BOUND` (2,000). That is the same kind of runaway [[R884000]] bounded for University. 999 is far above any number a game reaches without such a chain.

Rejected: (a) raising `GROWTH_NUMBER_BOUND`, or measuring growth per action, which would weaken the check that catches runaway scaling. (b) A cap on every stat, which would change every Buff to fix two cards.
