The aura is a hero guard with a cap of 0: every damage instance on its controller's hero is capped to nothing while it stands. Fatigue goes through `deal_damage`, so the cap stops it; lose-health and Set health are not damage ([[R18]]) and land. The guard follows control: a stolen Bastion guards its new controller's hero. As printed it is unconditional; a person may still prefer a cap (IA16).
