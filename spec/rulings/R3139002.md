#49 Snom Bunny Mind Control costs (5) and reads "Float reduces cost. Steal an enemy card." (Radiant: "… Make it Radiant."). The owner confirmed the reading of "card": it is any enemy permanent, a Unit, a Field Spell, a Trap or a Field Trap, the target v0.3.7b declared and issue #1149 narrowed to Units. It is a declared target of the play ([[R81]]), checked as every target is, only the top card of a Stack pile on offer ([[R13]]) and a face-down Trap offered by its id alone ([[R177]]); the steal is [[§6.3]]'s and [[R15]]'s, and the Radiant face's flag is [[R74]]'s, the card keeping its damage and counters ([[R22]]). A stolen Field Spell or Trap is the caster's from then on, its aura and its trigger too. An enemy side with no permanent offers nothing and the Spell still counts as played.

- **Float reduces cost.** The engine's hand trigger ([[R1563000]]) on both faces: each Float of its owner's, mana left over as the turn ended, takes that much off its cost while it is in their hand, and never in the deck ([[R2657002]]). At (5) the card is past the 4 mana a turn gives and is played after a Float, or with mana gained.
- **The Legacy card.** TG #52 is its own copy ([[R2195003]]), and the request gives it its own words. The base face reads "Steal an enemy Unit. Set its stats to 4/4.": the target is an enemy Unit, which after the steal has Attack and Health 4/4 (Set stats, [[§6.3]]; damage healed, buffs gone). The Radiant face, literally, "Steal an enemy card.": the same target as Core #49's, any enemy permanent, and it does not make the card Radiant. It costs (4) still. The number 4 is no declared one, as Dino-size's 10: whether it helps hangs on the Unit picked.
- **A steal that finds no free zone** ([[R15]]) leaves the card with its owner, as it always did, and the Legacy base face still sets its stats, as the Radiant face still makes the card Radiant.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11p folds under the scripts it ran: `script_v0_5_11o`, the Unit-only steal at (4) with no Float (the Legacy card's making the stolen Unit Radiant on its Radiant face), and `script_v0_3_7b` for the versions before issue #1149 ([[R767000]]).
