[[R1365000]] confirms a script-only claim by `FORCED_GAMES` games with the card forced into both decks,
played by the random policy. A change that shows only when a second card acts on the first never
diverged there: an Embiggened Wall that is set or Buffed ([[R3935000]]) is a play the policy makes
about once in a thousand games, so `classify --claims` read the change as playing as before and a
fragment could not claim it. This amends that check.

- **Steered games.** `STEERED_GAMES` (`crates/tools/src/classify.rs`) names, for a group of subject
  cards, the cards that act on them: for Stinkyscape+ #64 to #66, Meditative+ #21 Harmony Spirit (a Set
  stats) and Classic+ #71 Book of Buff (an Upgrade, which writes the card's `tuning` as a Buff, [[R1320]]). A subject's forced games force these into both decks
  too and record with a **steer** (`crates/tools/src/steer.rs`): a seat holding the subject plays it, at
  its embiggen price when it has one, and ends its turn while it cannot pay for it, until
  `STEER_HOLD_TURNS` (12), so nothing goes ahead of it; once the subject stands on its field the seat
  plays the cards that act on it, in the order given, each aimed at the subject when its plays name
  targets. Every other action is the policy's, so the recorded file replays on any build like any
  other. Phase Wall (#67) has no entry: Immutable refuses a set and a Buff or Nerf ([[R23]], [[R386]]),
  the two changes its bonus read, so no steered game plays it differently and its change claims no play.
- **Which divergence counts.** As before a divergence that touches the subject's family, or touches no
  card, confirms the claim; in a steered game one that touches a card that acts on the subject does
  too, since the diverging play is that card's and only the subject's change explains it. A card that
  acts on the subject and whose own script the change also moves is left out of the steer, so its own
  divergence never stands for the subject's.
- **Unchanged.** A card with no entry in `STEERED_GAMES` plays the games it played, and the unforced
  games, the self-play check and the claims' rules are as they were.

Rejected: a hand-written claim `classify` cannot back, which would set a precedent for bypassing
`--claims`; a scripted state with no game behind it, which no build could replay, since a recorded
game starts from `begin_game` and holds actions only.
