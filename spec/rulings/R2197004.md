TG #25 Trigor's Death ([[§8.11]], [[R2197002]]) summons nothing when the dying Trigor is one that a Trigor's Death summoned this turn: that summon marks it in its memory with the turn ([[R2206000]]), read off the card as it died ([[R78]]). Its stat override ([[R1181]]) is no mark, since §6.3's Set stats (TG #34 Dino-size, [[R2198000]]) and a Seance copy ([[R2198001]]) give a played Trigor one too. So each lineage adds at most one body a turn, and a played Trigor, one set up earlier or one a chain made on an earlier turn still summons its successor at its base stats plus the growth. [[R2197002]]'s growth alone was no terminator, as REVIEW A3.6 asks of a self-recursive Death: a hit that kills whatever the size does not care that each body is larger. The handicap fuzz found two such chains endless in one resolution loop ([[§10.3]]): Stinkyscape #24 Warp Disruptor II forcing a Poisonous Unit into each new Trigor ([[§6.1]], [[R1849007]]), and two Trigors on opposite sides, each side's Disruptor forcing its Trigor into the other's arrival so both die and both return. Rejected: a numbered cap on bodies per turn, a rules number (CLAUDE.md rule 9) where this bound needs none.
