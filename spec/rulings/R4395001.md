A Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap that dies standing in a unit zone was a Unit while it stood ([[R383]]), and its definition's type alone does not tell it from one destroyed in the backrow, where it was none. Since issue #4395 the `destroyed` event of the first carries `animated: true`, set by [[§4.5]] step 1 from the zone the card stood in as it died ([[R89]]).

- **Only ever `true`.** The field is absent on every other death, a Unit's included, so the event of a death that is not an Animated card's does not change.
- **The client's report, read off the action.** Like `via`, it is left out of the events the trigger loop holds, so no state hash moves for it, the golden texts leave it out of a `destroyed` and a recorded game's replay keeps its final hash ([[R768]]); Shadowstep's base face reads it off the `destroyed` of the same card in the action's own events, to tell an Animated card's death from a backrow card's ([[R4395000]]).
- **Hidden with the card.** A viewer who cannot read the card has the field removed with its identity, as `radiant` is ([[R97]]).
