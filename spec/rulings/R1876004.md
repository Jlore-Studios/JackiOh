"Aura: Each enemy Unit played that costs less than this is Allured" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #37 Kwzka) is a trigger on the opponent's `cardPlayed` while Kwzka acts on the field: a Unit played, not summoned, a cast counting as a play ([[R70]]) (ST-S17-5), whose cost paid ([[R56]], the event's `costPaid`) is less than Kwzka's cost now ([[R66]]), is Allured ([[R963]], [[R1045000]]): a delayed steal at the start of Kwzka's controller's next turn, destroyed with no room, aimed at the Unit's stay and carrying the pink mark while it waits. It answers once the play has put the Unit on the field ([[§10.5]] step 4) and only while the Unit is still there; a Unit its own Cry summons is no play and is not Allured. Kwzka's controller's own plays are never Allured. The Allure outlives Kwzka unless its Death removes it ([[R1876002]]).
