Every other ending is the same for both sides, the winner's win and the other's loss or a draw for both; a `fled` game ([[R1843002]]) is a win for the fleer and a draw, not a loss, for the other player (the designer's answer, `docs/stinkyscape-set.md` ST9 Q10). It is rated as a draw is, never as a loss, so against a lower-rated fleer the other side's rating still moves down a little, as any Glicko-2 draw's does. The engine names such endings once, `LOSER_DRAWS_REASONS` (`config.rs`, generated into the client's `engineConfig.ts`), and reads a seat's result through `seat_outcome`, and everything that reads one does the same. The ladder rates each side by its own score against the other's rating from before the game ([[R603]]), 1 for the fleer and 0.5 for the other side, so the other side's move is exactly a draw's; each season row moves by its own result ([[R606]]); the rated-game record keeps the fleer as the winning side ([[R611]]). A player's record counts a win and a draw, and a `fled` row whose fleer has since deleted their account stays a draw for the player left, while a `voted-out` or `fled-decisive` one stays a loss, both being decisive ([[R1412]]). The rewards pay a win and a draw ([[R1302002]]), the replays list shows a draw, the card statistics count a draw, and the AI scores the other player's flight as its draw. The client shows the other player's flight as a draw from the view alone: the result panel, its sound and its effects.
