**Why.** The owner asked in #3022 for one figure on `/stats` to balance by: every card into a band of 45% to 55%, a card clearly above it nerfed and one clearly below it buffed. [[R2777000]]'s adjusted win rate was close, but it fell short in three ways:

- **It read 50% as a drawn delta of 0, not as the average card.** Games in which a deck sees more of its cards (longer games, more draw, the second seat's extra card) need not split their wins evenly. So the average card's drawn delta need not be 0, and every card read shifted by it.
- **It assumed how far real cards differ.** `ADJUSTED_WIN_RATE_PRIOR_SPREAD` is 5 points, while `stats sample-size` assumes 2. With thousands of games the page can measure the spread itself.
- **It shrank a small sample toward 50%.** A rate alone inside 45% to 55% therefore reads a card with few games as balanced when it is only unknown.

**The field.** The cards a page reads give the field. These are every deckable card of its report, whatever the page filters show, with `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games on each side of its drawn delta ([[R2427000]]'s floor). Each such card gives its drawn delta `d` and that delta's sampling variance `v` at its widest. DerSimonian and Laird's random-effects estimate (`stats_field`) then reads the field from them:

```
w       = 1/v
mean    = Σw·d / Σw                             the fixed-effect mean
Q       = Σw·(d − mean)²                        Cochran's
τ²      = (Q − (k − 1)) / (Σw − Σw²/Σw)         at least ADJUSTED_FIELD_SPREAD_FLOOR² (one point)
center  = Σw*·d / Σw*,  w* = 1/(v + τ²)
```

With fewer than `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` (30) such cards, the field is [[R2777000]]'s: a centre of 0 and τ of 5 points (`StatsField::PRIOR`).

**The adjusted win rate** keeps [[R2777000]]'s model, read against the field:

```
adjusted = 50% + τ²/(τ² + SE²) · (delta − center)
```

It is the posterior mean of the card's lift under a normal prior of spread τ about the field's centre. An average card reads 50%. Its 95% range is [[R2895000]]'s, the rate ± `STATS_INTERVAL_Z` · √(weight · SE²), at the field's τ.

**The tier** (`CardTier`, `tier_of`), the owner's rule. "2 SD" is read as the 95% range the page already shows (`STATS_INTERVAL_Z`, 1.96), so a tier never disagrees with the range beside it:

| Tier | When | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | the rate is above `TIER_BAND_HIGH` (55%) and its range lies wholly above 50% | a nerf candidate |
| **A** | otherwise, a rate of 52.5% (`TIER_A_FROM`) or more | the band's top quarter |
| **B** | otherwise, a rate from 50% up to 52.5% | |
| **C** | otherwise, a rate from 47.5% (`TIER_C_FROM`) up to 50% | |
| **D** | otherwise, a rate below 47.5% | the band's bottom quarter |
| **F** | the rate is below `TIER_BAND_LOW` (45%) and its range lies wholly below 50% | a buff candidate |

A card beyond the band whose range still reaches 50% is A or D: the games do not yet say more. A card below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games has no tier, as it shows no rate ([[R654]]).

**Where it shows.**
- **`GET /api/stats/cards`** answers `tier` on every card, null below the floor. It also answers `tiers` at the top level: the field's `center`, `spread` and `cards`, and the band's `bandLow` and `bandHigh`.
- **`/stats`' Cards tab** shows a sortable Tier column before Adjusted, and the grid shows the tier too. Sorting by tier puts S first, then orders by the adjusted rate within a tier.
- **A line under the cards** says what the tiers mean from the server's band. It also says how far real cards differ, from the measured spread, or says that the spread is assumed when the field is too small.
- The default order stays the adjusted rate's.

**Rejected.**
- A tier by quantiles of the rates. It puts a quarter of the cards in each tier however alike they are.
- A fixed τ. The owner's other band, ±2 SD of the cards, needs the measured spread anyway.
- The band's edges as the test for S and F (a range wholly above 55%). At the games a patch gathers it flags almost nothing.
- Reading the field from the filtered cards alone. A set's page would then read its cards differently from the whole page.
- A frequentist test against 50% with no shrinkage. Over some 600 cards it flags dozens by chance.

The cards' deltas share games, which the estimate ignores: a card shares about one game in twenty-five with another. No Hearthstone rule is involved.
