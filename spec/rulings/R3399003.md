Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. Several of Classic+'s signatures ([[R3399000]]) show what a card's text does with cards the other seat may not read: a card added to a hand or shuffled into a deck, a card stolen from a deck, a Discover's options, a test's problems, a hand card's new cost or Radiance, a fusion that lands in a hand. Each plays as [[R202]] already holds every cue:

- **Owner-only beats.** A beat that would show what only the owner may see (Portal to the Past's sepia wash over the Discover's options, KY's Test's three problems over the caster's hand) plays only when the card's player is the seat's viewer (`play.player === view.viewer`); on the other seat its `promptOpened` draws nothing.
- **Cards in flight are backs.** A card flying to a hand or a deck pile is a ghost, always drawn as a card back, between piles, zones and heroes; no cue anchors on a hand card's id or names a definition, so a recipe planned from the other seat's redacted events (an `addedToHand`, `shuffledIn` or `stolen` naming `hidden`, a `costChanged` with no cost, a `fused` or `radiantSet` on a hand card hidden) draws the same public beat with nothing of the card. Golden Grape's splash on a card in hand is a shimmer over the hand pile.
- **A hidden play keys nothing.** A card played face down, or a play the seat may not read, keys no signature: its `cardPlayed`, its own landing and its hooks' markers name the sentinel, so neither its entrance nor its recipe plays (Twice Forward One Step Backwards set face down among them), while a public card it summons still lands with its own entrance (League of Losers' Losers).

`classicplus/signatures.test.ts` plays every Classic+ signature with a recipe from the opponent's seat, its events redacted as the engine redacts them, and fails on any cue that names a secret card's id or definition, and on any cue a prompt draws there.

Rejected: drawing a card's face for its owner in flight, which the board's own hand already shows when it lands, and which would need the layer to tell the seats apart cue by cue.
