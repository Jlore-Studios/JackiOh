The playmat was a fidget surface only where it was bare: a press on a tile's own element drew a mark
and a sound, a press on a card or on a legal tile (a button) drew nothing, and a hover and a touch
did nothing to a tile at all. The owner asked that a tile answer wherever it is pressed.

**What answers.** A press (`pointerdown`) on any tile, `zone-<side>-<row>-<lane>`, on either side,
or on anything it holds, adds one answer over the tile, in the playmat family of that tile's half
([[R1834004]]: the viewer's half wears the viewer's playmat, the other half its seat's, a default
playmat taps as sand): a press-in of the tile's whole box and a ripple ring in the family's look
(`SandSurface.tsx` `TileAnswer`, `game/arena/arena.css`). A tile holding a card ripples round the
card's own box; an empty one ripples from the press. A legal tile (`data-legal="true"`) answers
harder: a deeper press-in, a thicker and wider ring, and the sound at `TILE_FEEL.legalBuild` rather
than `TILE_FEEL.baseBuild`. An answer lasts `TILE_FEEL.rippleMs`, at most `TILE_FEEL.maximum` show
at once, and the answer reads only what the tile already draws, a legal flag and a card
([[§10.8]]).

**Its sound.** The family's field-tap recipe (`TAP_SFX`). A press that also made the mat's own tap
(a bare tile) sounds once, from that tap; one that did not (a legal tile, which is a button, or a
card) sounds from the tile's answer. So every press on a tile sounds once. This amends #2332, which
left a card's and a legal tile's press silent: it still draws no mark on the mat there, but the
tile answers and sounds.

**Touch.** A press whose `pointerType` is `touch` buzzes (`HAPTIC_PATTERNS.tile`, `tileLegal`)
through `tapHaptic`, behind the Vibration switch, reduced motion and the device's `vibrate`
([[R669]]), on a gap of its own so a tile's buzz never holds back the game's own drop. A mouse
press and a key press never buzz.

**Settings.** Under reduced motion, or with effects intensity `off`, the answer is drawn still
(`data-play="still"`: the same press-in and ring at rest, no animation) and the sound stays.

**What it never does.** It never stops, prevents or changes the press: a legal tile still plays, an
illegal one still does nothing in the game, and a card press still starts its drag
([[R2122000]]'s targets are unchanged). A press during a drag (`data-dragging`) answers nothing.
Hovering a tile on a desktop pointer (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) lights its edge with a
thin brass ring, except a locked, reserved, glowing or selected tile, which keep their own.

Rejected: lifting a legal tile to meet a dragged card (#3347's drag layer is not on `main`; the
legal tile's harder answer is the hook for it); a new sound recipe for tiles, which would need a
new Web Audio recipe and test table for what the seven field-tap recipes already say; and stopping
the press at the answer, which would make a tile swallow a tap the game needs.
