An Evolve text ([[§6.3]]) runs once, the first time its card evolves ([[R3536000]]), either when its "Evolves when" condition ([[§6.2]]) holds at the settled state check or when an event or a text evolves it ([[R3536001]]). The AI's evaluation priced none of it: S+ #2 Lone Drifter, a Radiant 4/4 that becomes a 12/12 once every unit zone on its side is full, read as a 4/4 forever. So the AI never filled its last zone for it, never guarded one a zone from evolving, and never answered the enemy's before it grew. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, once, on the same probe world as [[R2424000]]'s Death prices and [[R3064000]]'s awakening prices (a function of what the seat sees, [[R185]]), the AI prices each card that can still evolve (`can_evolve`: not evolved yet, and its face prints an Evolve text), each once, in this order:
- every unit acting on the seat's field;
- every one on the enemy's, whose face and evolved mark are public, each as its controller's;
- each Unit in the seat's own hand, placed first by the sandbox's spawn ([[R923001]]). One that cannot evolve once placed, because filling the row evolved it as it entered, prices nothing.

On a sandbox copy ([[R923000]]) with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), it evolves the card as the engine's `evolve` verb does (`evolution::evolve_now`: the text's first run inline, the evolution multiplier's extra runs queued) and lets the resolution loop drain it, under the game's pool hold on the copy's own random stream. The price of one run is the controller's evaluation after it less before it, plus `AI_EVAL.next_turn_mana` for each crystal gained, as [[R2477000]]'s firings are priced, divided by the runs the text took there (1 + the evolution multiplier's extra runs, [[R3536003]]). It is kept signed. A card is keyed by its definition, its face and its controller.

**How close it stands.** The same root reads the card's road to evolving (`EvolveCondition`), on throwaway copies of the state that are never settled nor kept:
- **Zones full** when its "Evolves when" holds once every empty unit zone of its side holds a Vanilla copy of it (S+ #2 Lone Drifter). It counts the share of its side's unit zones filled, a zone a GIGA covers or the slash voided counting as filled, as the Drifter reads it.
- **Free cards** when the condition holds once the seat's own hand gains enough (0) Cost Vanilla copies (C+ #49 Jay Fungus, [[R3551000]]). It counts the free cards the hand holds over those it needs, so a Fungus with no free card in hand counts nothing. Never for an enemy card, whose hand the seat cannot see.
- **Damage** when the condition holds once the card is left on 1 health (S+ #27 Gargantuar, "below half health"). It counts the share of its health lost, which stays under ½ until the condition holds, so a hit toward it never looks cheap.
- **Event** for every other road: a card with no "Evolves when" whose own trigger evolves it (S+ #101 A Wee Whelp's kill), a Stack (S+ #83 Offworld Replicant), an enemy's hand, or a condition that already holds while something holds the text off. It counts `AI_EVAL.evolve_event_share`, 0.25.

`unit_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.evolve` (0.5) × the price × the runs its text would take now × that closeness, inside the Brittle share: a crumbled card never evolves. So a Drifter beside High Evolutionary (S+ #3), whose Aura makes evolutions trigger twice, counts its evolution twice, and a line that removes High Evolutionary reads the run it loses. The term counts for the seat's units and the enemy's alike, and `kill_worth`, the trades and the move order read it unchanged. The probes run with no price passed, within `AI_EVOLVE_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes, a cap of their own, and spend none when no card can evolve. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps both weights at 0, so it reads no price, and `evolve` at 0 is the term's one switch: nothing reads it and no node is spent.

**What is not priced.**
- A card in the enemy's hand, a face-down card and a Dormant one, whose text is off.
- A card that already evolved.
- A card its own turn or attack text evolves (S #91 the Propagation's Wasp, S #92 the Propagation's Hornet, S+ #161 Space Invader, S+ #T-AI-5 Lantern Distiller): its face prints no "Evolves when" and no trigger, only a turn or an attack hook. [[R2477000]]'s and [[R2688000]]'s probes already fire that hook and its evolution with it, so counting it here would count it twice.
- An evolution that leaves a prompt open or ends the game, which the probe neither answers nor scores.
- A card the probes' nodes did not reach, and every card while a prompt is open or once the game is over.

**Approximations.**
- The probe evolves every card on the seat's turn.
- Splitting the price evenly over its runs is exact for an additive text such as the Drifter's buff. A "Set stats to" text such as the Whelp's gains nothing on its second run, but each run is counted at the same share.
- An "Evolves when this is Stacked on something" reads as an event, though a Stack is a play the seat could make.
- The event share is one number for every event, whether it comes every turn or seldom.

**Rejected.**
- A condition enum in the engine, or a per-card list in the AI. Each would need every new Evolve card registered twice. The root reads the condition from the card's own `evolves_when` on filled, fed and hurt copies instead.
- A hookless twin as the baseline, as [[R3064000]] uses. A Vanilla twin cannot evolve, so its branch is the same state settled again, and the state before the evolution is the baseline.
- Classifying the condition inside `unit_worth`, which runs at every search node. The root stores it with the price.
- Full information-set search over the enemy's hidden Evolve marks, a separate project.

**The scenarios.** The setup test gives the AI three Radiant Drifters in lanes 1 to 3, one crystal, a Felinor Token and Hotfix (Infinite Assault #6, +2/+2 on a friendly Unit). At body value the best Hotfix leaves the AI's evaluation 4.1 above where it stands and the Felinor 1.4 above. With the price, at 21.6 a run, the Felinor's fourth zone adds 3 × 0.2 × 0.5 × 21.6 ≈ 6.5, and Medium and Hard both play it. The answer test gives the human a Radiant Drifter, a 5/5 and two 1/1s, four of five zones, and the AI one Polymorph. At body value the 5/5 is the better kill (11 against 8.8). With the price the Drifter's kill is worth 17.4, and the AI Polymorphs it, in either lane order. At 0.5 and 0.25 the smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`) read 20, 16 and 18 of 20, against random, against greedy and as Hard against Easy (floors 19, 14 and 15), with no search panic.
