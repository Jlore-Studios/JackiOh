No attack may target a Unit with Evasive, declared or forced ([[§4.2]] step 2's restriction), and
its Taunt binds nobody, since no attacker may legally attack it ([[§4.2]] step 3). It implies
Can't Defend as an Indestructible one does ([[R2179000]]): it is never switched to Defense, by its
own action or an effect's, and one standing in Defense switches to Attack at the state check. The
keyword is never added, so the card lists Evasive alone and a Buff cannot remove Can't Defend from
it. Effects still reach it, and a Nerf may remove it, with the implied Can't Defend going with it.
C+ #51 keeps its flag until S01 moves it onto Evasive. Rejected: granting the Can't Defend
keyword, which a Buff could then take off while Evasive stayed.
