A Starship Piece that dies Radiant fuses into its Starship on its Radiant face, so its Radiant script runs there ([[R469]]); a number its text reads is then the Radiant face's too. Since issue #4334, `param` reads it on that face for every piece, and the Starship's view shows it ([[R4334000]]); until then `param` read the Starship's face, and five pieces read theirs through `piece_number`, since retired. A Radiant After Burner fused into a Starship grants 4 keywords, a base one 2.
