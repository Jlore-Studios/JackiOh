The issue asked that Animated and the card's foil, finish and border layer on each new version, and
that Signature go on neither ([[R2949000]]).

**Animated** moves the version's own picture ([[R2715001]]): Set showcase's restyled one, whose layers
move under its filter, and on Out of frame both parts, the backdrop's layers in the window and the
subject's own layer in the cutout, which share the card's one motion and so move together. It holds
still under reduced motion and with Animated foil off, and takes a motion slot, as on any face. It is
offered by its own offer, so it layers on a new version wherever both are offered. Without it each
version holds still, as Full art and Alt full art do though they too are Legendary chases
([[R2725000]], [[R2940002]]): a chase's own loop is a foil's, finish's, border's, playmat's,
backdrop's or card back's ([[R1953001]]), and on a version Animated is what moves the art.

**A foil, a finish and a border** wear on each as on the normal face ([[R1295000]]). On Set showcase
a worn border keeps its own edge and bezel: the set's edge and bezel are drawn only on a base face
wearing no border, and the window's ring takes the border's bezel colour. Out of frame draws no frame
of its own, so a border's and a Radiant face's stand; its foil's sheen covers the window, and the
cutout takes none, since over its empty corners it would lay a square across the frame.

**Signature** goes on neither. It is worn only beside the Full art version and Animated
([[R2949000]]), so its own rule refuses it here and no mechanism of this part's is needed, as in parts
2 and 3 ([[R2940002]], [[R2942002]]): a `PUT /api/card-picks` naming it beside a new version is the
400 R2949000 gives, writing nothing; the Collection marks it waiting until the card wears Full art and
Animated; and a face wearing a new version never signs. No other pairing is ruled out: each new
version shows every other layer, and nothing is hidden, unreadable or loses a Radiant mark, so no
`rulesOut` list is added.

Amends [[R2725003]] (the layering on part 4's versions).
