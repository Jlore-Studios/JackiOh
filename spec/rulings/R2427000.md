**Why.** [[R377]]'s delta compared the decks that played the card with the decks that drew it and held it. A seat plays a card when it can afford it and when it is ahead, so that figure measures the game as much as the card: the decks that could play it were already winning. The games where the card was drawn, played or not, against the games where it stayed in the deck, is the cleaner measure of how much a card helps once held.

**The two figures.** Never drawn, the decks that held the card and never had it in hand, is in deck less played less drawn but not played, for games, wins and draws alike. The drawn delta is the drawn win rate (played and drawn but not played together) minus the never-drawn win rate, in percentage points. Both are read off the totals, never stored: the record, the stored totals and the store are unchanged, so there is no migration and no back-fill.

**Where it shows.** The drawn delta replaces the played delta everywhere a figure is shown, in the played delta's place: `stats report` prints Not drawn and Drawn Δ in its table (and `--window` with it), `--json` adds `notDrawn` and `drawnDelta` to each card's row, `GET /api/stats/cards` answers `notDrawnGames`, `notDrawnWinRate` and `drawnDelta`, `/stats` shows a sortable Drawn vs not drawn column beside When drawn, the card's stats block shows the figure, and the analysis loader and notebook 01 read and rank by it. What still keeps the played delta: `played_delta()` itself, the API's `playedDelta` field and balance-watch's Played Δ flag, since removing a served field breaks old clients and balance-watch is outside issue #2427.

**The floor.** The drawn delta waits for `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games on each of its sides, as [[R654]]'s played delta does: until its drawn games and its never-drawn games each reach the floor, the API sends no delta and the row and the detail display "not enough data". The engine's `drawn_delta` itself has no floor, like `played_delta`: the floor belongs to the page.

Rejected: showing both deltas on the page, since the maintainer asked for the new one to supersede the old; storing the tally, which is derived exactly, so storing it would need a migration and a back-fill for nothing; and a floor in the engine, which belongs to the page ([[R654]]). No Hearthstone rule is involved.
