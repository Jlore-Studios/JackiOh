[[R366]] starts each labelled ability on a line of its own, and that is the card's own abilities. An ability a card grants another card is the other card's text: the granting sentence quotes it in double quotes, its label first and its full stop inside the closing quote, as #38 quotes the Combo it gives your cards and C+ #14 the text it gives your next Spell. So a label that opens a double-quoted ability may stand inside a line, as the designer wrote Meditative #58 Permanent Underclassman's and #59 Permanent Upperclassman's granted Deaths ([[R1107]]): `Cry: Summon a random (1) Unit. It gains "Death: Add 1 random Book card to your hand."` The Unit that gains it prints the ability as its own, on a line of its own ([[R1107]]), and a Chinese face quotes it in “ ” the same way ([[R1302]]). Every other label, single quotes included, still starts its line.
