S #78 Ozark's designer text reads "Cry: A random [Radiant] Miner vXs is Equip this", and its Radiant face adds ", twice" (ST-S05-2).

- **The brackets.** The designer's "[Radiant]" marks what the Radiant face changes, as his brackets do on his one-face entries. So the base face's Miner is plain, and the Radiant face's two Miners are each Radiant ([[R469]]).
- **The picks.** "Miner vXs" means the five Miners, S #62 to #66, named by id so the pool reaches them while the set is held. Each pick is one draw of the match rng ([[R60]]) by `equip_picks` ([[R1841002]]): a fresh copy fused straight into Ozark, with no hand, no play and no Cry, its Activate coming with it. "Twice" is two picks, the second from the Miners Ozark does not hold yet, so the two are different. Off the field, Immutable, or already holding every Miner, nothing happens ([[R129]]).
- **Still a piece.** Ozark is the kept card, so it stays a Starship Piece ([[R1852001]]).
