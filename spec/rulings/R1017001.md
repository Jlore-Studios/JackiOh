With a face's whole text one hover or click away ([[R1017000]]), the most iconic cards give their picture the whole face: the cards [[R641]]'s hero portraits are drawn from (Mr. Vanilla, Gary the Gambler, Tempo Timmy, Big D-fender, Duplicating Felinors and Jlockeed Shredder-10) print their art across the whole full face, base and Radiant, over the frame's panel, which shows only as the rarity rim around it. A scrim darkens the top and the foot, and the rules box is smoked glass with light ink (gilded on the Radiant face), so the words read over any picture; the name ribbon, the gems, the tags, the type line and the stats sit over the art as on every face. The list is the portrait roster itself, by card name, so a card that becomes a hero's portrait becomes a full-art card. A compact face and a board minion keep their windows. The art is the card's own ([[R660]]: a delivered file first, the procedural picture otherwise), cropped to the face. Presentation only: no rule reads it.
