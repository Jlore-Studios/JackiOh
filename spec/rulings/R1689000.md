[[§9.5]] lets a player challenge only a friend, and [[§9.4]]'s block cancelled every open challenge between the two and freed its room, but removing a friend left them open: the friend could still join the room by its code and play someone who was no longer a friend until the challenge lapsed. Hearthstone's friendly challenge exists only between friends, so it ends with the friendship. Either friend removing the friendship cancels every open challenge between the two, either way, in the same transaction, and frees the room of each that has not lapsed ([[R1075000]]); one that lapsed freed itself, and its code may be another room's. A challenge's room is its friend's to join only while the two are friends neither of whom blocks the other: a join by anyone else, or by the friend once that has ended, gets the answer an unknown code gets, so no refusal tells that the code exists. A challenge whose friendship ends, or whose pair is blocked, while it is being made is not opened: the challenger gets the 404 a challenge to a non-friend gets, and its room is freed. Making friends again restores no challenge.
