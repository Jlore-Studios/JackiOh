The Cry steals every reachable enemy Unit on the field costing no more than the cap now (`cost_now`), in lane order — with no room the card is destroyed where it stands instead ([[§6.3]], [[R4205000]]). The aura is The Jailbreaker's `random_opponent_spell_targets` static flag ([[R4590001]]): the declared targets of every Spell its controller's opponent plays face-up are drawn at random from the legal ones, by [[R1200]]'s draw; questions asked while a Spell resolves, modes and Discover are not drawn, and attacks, activations and `mayor_luck` keep `targets_random`. A stolen Zero-Day applies to its new opponent.
