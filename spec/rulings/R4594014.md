The Cry steals every reachable enemy Unit on the field costing no more than the cap now (`cost_now`), in lane order — with no room the card is destroyed where it stands instead ([[§6.3]], [[R4205000]]). The aura is `random_enemy_spell_targets`: the opponent's Spells' declared targets and target prompts are drawn at random from the legal ones; modes and Discover are not targets. Attacks, activations and `mayor_luck` keep `targets_random`. A stolen Zero-Day applies to its new opponent.
