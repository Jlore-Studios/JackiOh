The trap's `row` and `lane` alongside its controller, so a client can point at the zone that flipped without being told which card it was. Its `instanceId` and `defId` follow [[§10.8]]'s redaction ([[R97]]) alike for both seats: named while the player may read the card where it is now, the sentinel in a library or a hand not theirs ([[R763]], [[R2734000]]; before issue #2734 the other player always read the sentinel), while the flip animates in the right lane for both. Without the lane the opponent's face-down trap has nothing to animate on, since a face-down card is given no instance id
