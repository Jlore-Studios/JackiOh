Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no state, event, replay or AI decision changes, only what `viewFor` carries. A card in hand that `legalActions` offers no play of did nothing when clicked. The view now carries why, on a `CardView` field beside [[R849000]]'s `attackReason`.

`playReason` is on the viewer's own hand card while they may act — their turn, the main phase, no prompt open — and `play_actions_for` lists no play of it. It is the first refusal in a fixed order, in the engine's words (`play_choices::why_hand_card_unplayable`), as an ability's `reason` is ([[R384]]): a play ban, no price it can pay ([[R1223]]'s `credit::why_unaffordable`, shared with [[§10.5]] step 1's own refusal), no open zone, no legal Tribute set, no legal choice for a required declaration. The cost is said first, since a card its player cannot pay for is refused whatever the board holds, and within it the ban ahead of the price, as the reducer reads them, since a banned card is refused at every price it could be played at; the rest follows [[§10.5]] step 1's own order, the zone, the Tribute, then what the card declares. Every branch reads the function the listing and the reducer already call, so the reason and the listing cannot disagree. It is absent when a play is listed, on every other card, and on the viewer's own hand on anyone else's turn; a view with no unplayable card is byte for byte what it was ([[R849000]]'s precedent).

No refusal can come from a hidden fact alone, so no reason ever hints at one ([[§9.1]]): every pick the refusals read names only public cards — board units and zones, the chooser's own hand, either graveyard — and every sentence names only the viewer's own card, its public price, public zones and counts of public units and targets. An Equip with no host on a board with open zones, and a targeting cost the hand cannot pay, say nothing rather than the wrong thing. Plays from the graveyard keep no such field; they can follow the same pattern later.

The web shows the reason in the hand card's tooltip and inspect notes, and as the same notice `attackReason` uses when the player clicks the card. A hand card the view says cannot be played stays greyed out (`data-legal="false"`, as `legalActions` offers it nothing), and its click still reaches the board, which shows the reason and sends nothing. It works nothing out itself.

Rejected: one reason per price, since the card-level refusal is what a player who clicks an unplayable card needs to read; a `false` or empty key on every other card, since the view's other flags are absent unless set; marking the card legal so its click gets through, since `data-legal` then no longer mirrors `legalActions`, the card no longer looks unplayable, and every e2e helper that plays the first legal hand card clicks it and waits for a play that never comes; and folding the graveyard plays in, which are out of scope for this issue.
