The brief of #1651 (its §1, built by #1659) puts a small Jlore pill, Jlore's coin and the balance
`GET /api/wallet` answers, beside the language and settings buttons in the top bar of every screen
of a signed-in person that shows one, and clicking it opens `/shop`. [[R1646000]] took the Shop link
off the account screen, whose top bar is one of those. The owner's later brief is the call, so the
account screen's top bar holds the pill, and the pill links the Shop there as everywhere.

- **The screen's own links.** The account screen's body still links neither the Collection nor the
  Shop, as [[R1646000]] has it: the pill is the top bar's, the same on every screen, and is the one
  link to `/shop` the account screen holds.
- **Where the pill is not.** On the board, which supplies settings itself, and for a visitor with no
  session. Nor on the sign-in and password-reset screens, which are a signed-out person's: a session
  only lands there as the screen leaves for the main menu, which reads the wallet itself.
- **Still the server's.** The pill shows the wallet's answer, or the balance a later answer carries
  (a bought pack's), never a sum the client makes (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: the gold counter is on every menu screen, and choosing it takes the player to
the shop.

Rejected: leaving the pill off the account screen, which would break the brief's "every screen";
and a pill that shows the balance without linking, which the brief rules out.

It amends [[R1646000]] (The account screen).
