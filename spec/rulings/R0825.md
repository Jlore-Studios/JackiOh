"Permanently" is the card's own tuning (MD-A15, [[R386]]): Gatling Pea's `damage` number stays in
every zone and through leaving the field ([[R78]]), a copy keeps it ([[R57]]), and a Nerf or a Buff
moves the same number. The growth runs after the hit through C+ #41 KY's Constant's `set_number`
verb (`tuning.set`, reported by `numberChanged`), even when the target's Armor took the whole hit.
Every extra trigger (M #9, M #12) hits and grows. "Your opponent" is the enemy hero.
