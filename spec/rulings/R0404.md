C #90's quests count from the moment each opens, on either player's turn, and completion is noticed at the state check after the event that completes it, when the card's controller chooses a reward (their prompt, even on the other player's turn), which sets the next quest: 1, draw 2 cards (rewards A or B); 2, two enemy permanents destroyed, by anything (C or D); 3, control 3 or more permanents at once (D or E); 4, end a turn with 3 or more mana unspent (F or G); 5, your cards deal 12 damage to enemies in total (G or H); 6, your Units have 10 or more total attack and 10 or more total health at once (H or I); 7, end a turn with 5 or more mana unspent (J); 8, three cards enter either exile (K); 9, a draw of yours takes the last card of your deck, one already empty when the quest opens completing it at once, and never a card leaving it any other way (L); 10, six or more Units in your graveyard (M). A leads to quest 2, B to 3, C to 4, D to 5, E to 6, F to 7, G to 8, H to 9 and I to 10, and J to M end a line: A heal your hero 6; B deal 3 damage to a target; C return 2 random cards from your graveyard to your hand; D place 3 Plague Counters; E a random Unit of yours gets +3/+3; F bounce a target permanent; G your opponent discards 2, their choice; H draw 2; I Recruit a card; J gain 100 mana; K exile your opponent's deck; L Aura: you may play cards from your graveyard ([[§6.3]] Play); M Aura: your Units have Indestructible — the two auras holding while In Too Deep is on the field. The quest state is on the instance ([[§10.1]]), and the view shows the open quests with their progress and the rewards on offer. The Radiant face grants every reward a completed quest offers and opens every quest they lead to; a quest reached by two paths opens once, and a reward two completed quests offer is granted by each
