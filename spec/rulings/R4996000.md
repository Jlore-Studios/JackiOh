PIP names the plan word for word. Every face of Stinkyscape+ #94 PIP (base and Radiant) contains the exact phrase "performance improvement plan" in English, and in every other language the client shows (`card-text.yaml`'s locales: Simplified Chinese, `zh`, today) that language's standard term for one. In Simplified Chinese that is 绩效改进计划. No face says it any other way, such as "performance enhancement plan", "performance plan" or "PEP". A language added later must name the plan by its own standard term, and this ruling's test covers it from the day it lands. It edits [[§8.12]]'s S+ #94 row, the faces' words alone.

Hearthstone semantics: no Hearthstone rule names the plan; the English words are fixed as above.
