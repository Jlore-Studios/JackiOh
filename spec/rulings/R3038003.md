Part 1 of issue #3000 made Starship a tag and gave every Starship Flying and Ranged ([[R3038000]]), and the AI's removal test of [[R2566000]] failed: given one Hit Job, a Starship Piece and a vanilla 4/8 on the enemy's field, the AI destroyed the piece, which fuses into the enemy's Dormant Starship, over the vanilla. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**The cause.** The hand evaluation still prices the piece's death below the vanilla's ([[R2566000]]), and the reply it simulates has the enemy launch the new Starship for all its crystals ([[R1852003]]), which leaves the piece's line below the vanilla's in every determinization: −62.8, −63.8 and −64.0 against −50.7. A finalist is scored by the network's correction on top of that, at its share ([[R1012001]]), and the network was fitted on catalog v0.3.17, before any Starship: it reads the position after the reply as it reads any other, from the units' stats and keywords and the crystals each side holds. It moves the vanilla's line by −23.7 and the piece's by +3.3, −8.6 and +8.3, so the piece's mean (−62.55) rose above the vanilla's (−74.36). Flying and Ranged, which the dense features count as any unit's keywords, are part of it (to the network an enemy unit with both is worth +0.3 of a logit to the AI, about 9 points of a finalist in the first determinization), not the whole: most of it is the enemy's crystals. The reply plays no card it cannot see ([[R185]]), so the enemy keeps its crystals in every line but the one that gives it a launch to pay for, and the network charges the AI 0.2 to 0.25 of a logit (6 to 7 points of a finalist) for each crystal the enemy holds when the AI's turn starts: 0.87 of a logit, 26 points, for the four. The line that feeds the enemy's Starship is the line that spends them. With Flying and Ranged out of the features and the launch at (3), as before part 1, the same arithmetic on the first determinization leaves the vanilla ahead by less than half a point.

**What changes.** At a decision's root (`plan`), the AI reads whether its redacted view holds a Starship (`holds_starship`): a Dormant Starship or a launched one on either side ([[R1852000]]), found by the hull and never by the tag, or a Starship Piece standing on either field or in its own hand. The enemy's hand and both libraries are hidden cards and a graveyard is not read, so the fact is the same for any two states that differ only in hidden cards. It rides with the root's prices (`Prices.starship_root`), and with it set `finalist_score_with` returns the hand evaluation, as it does for a Duos duel ([[R1010002]]) and for the tutorial's opponent ([[R1012003]]). Every line of the decision, on every determinization, is scored the same way, so the lines stay comparable; the fact is not read off the position scored, which would score a line that removed the last piece by one evaluation and its rivals by the other. The hand evaluation holds the prices that a Starship leaves behind ([[R2566000]], [[R2380000]], [[R3064000]]), which the network cannot read and a few points of its correction would overturn. A call outside a decision (`Prices::NONE`) reads no fact, so the probes, the baselines and the tools are as they were.

**Reach.** Only a decision whose root holds a Starship, which is a game with Stinkyscape's pieces on a field or in the AI's hand, and the decision where a Dormant or launched Starship stands. Everywhere else the network's correction is taken as before, and a game with no Stinkyscape card plays as it did.

**Rejected.** Leaving Flying and Ranged out of the dense features, as the Starship keyword is ([[R1010000]]): the first determinization's piece line still scored 6 points above the vanilla's, for the enemy's crystals. Zeroing the enemy's crystals in the features of every replied position: it moves every decision's correction, for a fault only the launch shows. Reading the fact off the position scored: a line that exiled or returned the last piece would change evaluation in the middle of a decision. Raising `AI_EVAL.death` or the slumber share until the hand evaluation outweighs the network: the network's swing here is 27 points, and any number would be tuned to this one board. Retraining the network: a person's work (`training/README.md`), and the exception stays right until it has seen a Starship.
