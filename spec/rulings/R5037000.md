**Why.** Running and Versions showed only Games, Tier, Adjusted and Since. The owner asked for the Cards tab's Win rate and Play rate beside them (issue #4670), so a play version is read by the figures a card is read by.

**The figures.** Both are plain tallies, so a play version's are sums, never an average of its versions' rates. `play_version_figures` ([[R4675001]]) sums a card's breakdowns over the versions its play version spans and the sources its span counts (`span_sources`), as it sums its games, and each row of `GET /api/stats/versions` sends:
- `winRate`: wins over games, as `GET /api/stats/cards` reads a card's ([[R3185000]]), over the games the row counts: every deck that held the card, under All Random the decks that saw it ([[R2777002]]), and for the Dormant Starship the seats that made one ([[R3070000]]). It is the raw rate, not the adjusted one, and `null` when no game was counted.
- `playRate`: its played games over its seen games ([[R3950000]]), in every mode; the Dormant Starship's its used Created games over the games a seat made one ([[R3070000]]). It is the engine's `play_rate`, the function `GET /api/stats/cards` reads its own through, so the two tabs share one reading.

A span of two versions that won 1 of 1 and 0 of 3 games reads 25%, not the 50% the two rates average to. A play version alone reads what the Cards tab reads for the same records and window.

**Missing figures.** `playRate` is `null` for a play version never seen (no game reached a hand), where the Cards tab prints 0%: a card never seen was never passed over, so 0% would say it was seen and never played. The client reads `null` as "—". The oldest totals hold both tallies: every record's `played`, `drawn` and `opening` and every card total's `played` and `drawnNotPlayed` are required, so no span lacks them; a play version wholly before the records ([[R4675001]]) has no totals at all and reads `null` for both. A server deployed before this ruling sends neither key, which the client also reads as "—", never "not enough games" or 0%.

**The columns.** On Running and Versions:
- Win rate follows Adjusted. Below [[R654]]'s floor, and for a play version with no games, it reads "not enough games", as on the Cards tab; the figure is the server's and the floor is the row's `hasEnoughGames`, so the client counts neither (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- Play rate follows Win rate, behind the Columns toggle, which now offers Play rate beside Games on these views (the Tokens tab still offers none). It is on by default, as on the Cards tab, and is no floor's: a rate over few seen games shows, as it does on the Cards tab.
- Both print as whole percents, as the Cards tab prints them.
- If the Decisiveness column reaches these views, it sits between Adjusted and Win rate, as on the Cards tab.

**The Grid.** Each card shows Win rate between Adjusted and Games, as the Cards tab's grid does; its grid shows no Play rate, so neither does this one, nor the win-rate range under Show 95% ranges.

**The sort.** The sort select offers Name, Games, Tier, Adjusted, Win rate and Play rate, and every header sorts, highest first at the first press ([[R3184002]]). A row with no figure, below the floor for Win rate, `null` or from an older server, sorts after those with one, either way, and ties keep the order of the cards the Cards tab lists ([[R4678001]]). [[R4678001]]'s list of sorts Running reads as Adjusted loses Win rate and Play rate: only Decisiveness remains. The Tokens tab's list is unchanged.

**Scope.** The text report (`cargo jackioh stats report --versions`) keeps its format.

Rejected:
- Computing either rate on the client from the row's games: it holds no tallies (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- Storing a 0 for a play version never seen: it reads as a measured 0%.
- Averaging a span's versions' rates: it weights a version of 3 games as a version of 3,000.
- A win-rate range on these views: the Cards tab shows one only under Show 95% ranges and the owner asked for the figures alone.

Hearthstone has no counterpart.

**After [[R5040000]].** Running is two views, Major patch running and Historical, both on the Running panel: both show these columns, and the Major patch running table's Win rate and Play rate are summed over each version cut to the newest major's line, as its games are.

Amended by [[R5036000]] (issue #5036): the Decisiveness column reaches these views, between Adjusted and Win rate in the table and in the Grid; the sort select offers Name, Games, Tier, Adjusted, Decisiveness, Win rate, Play rate and Date ([[R5033000]]), and no sort the running views lack is left to read as Adjusted.
