At the start of each player's turn the hole pulls that player's permanents one lane toward it
([[R3554000]]), each row walking the lanes nearest the hole first, so a card the pull frees moves
into the freed zone in the same pull. A card whose shifted lanes reach the hole is exiled and
counted as in [[R3554000]]; a GIGA steps whole from its anchor or is swallowed whole; anything
whose newly entered slots do not all take the move stays. Dormant tops and covered zones are
passed by, and no state check runs inside. Rejected: pulling both players' permanents at once,
which would move a card twice a round and reorder the two sides' steps.
