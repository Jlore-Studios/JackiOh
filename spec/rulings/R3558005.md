Fine Fermented Grape's End of Turn fuse reads its owner's turn end in the hand and fuses another card whose English name holds "Grape" (`nameContains`, case-insensitive) into itself — never itself, its own pool excluding it, and never a card whose name holds no Grape. Each fuse narrows the next pool by the fusion's ingredients, so the third turn fuses nothing. Rejected: matching by tag, which the designer's "Grape" is not — it is a word in the name.
