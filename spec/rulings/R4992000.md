[[R1181000]]'s view writes " in N turns", " in 1 turn" or " now" after the label of every countdown badge ([[R169]]), so a `delay`'s label states what is due and never the count. Mass Driver wrote its own count, so its badge read "Mass Driver: two Minerals and a Resource in 2 turns in 2 turns", and the next turn "… in 2 turns in 1 turn". Doomsday Clock's fixed label repeated its countdown ("… in 4 turns", then the view's) and said 10 damage on the Radiant face, which deals 20.

Mass Driver's label is now "Mass Driver: two Minerals and a Resource", and its badge reads "… in 2 turns", then "… in 1 turn" ([[R3556007]]). Doomsday Clock's is "Doomsday Clock: {damage} damage to the enemy hero", built from its declared `damage`, so the Radiant face reads 20 and a Buff or a Nerf moves the number with the hit ([[R4593005]]).

Before this patch Mass Driver's label held the count (`script_v0_6_14i`, kept, keyed by the patch's fragment, [[R4779000]], [[R767000]]): the label is hashed state, so a recorded game with a Mass Driver delay pending folds under it. Doomsday Clock is a card of a held set ([[R4577000]]), so no version carried its label and it takes no fragment and no kept script.

Rejected: the view stripping a count a label already holds. It cannot tell a count from a card's own words, and every other countdown label already leaves the count to the view.
