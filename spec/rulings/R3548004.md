## Question

While a card giving infinite mana acts, what do costs spend and what does the tray show?

## Decision

While the giver acts on its controller's field, that player's mana is not spent by paying costs:
every price is listed, every cost is paid, the payment is 0 and nothing is borrowed. The view shows
infinity on both seats. It ends when the card leaves the field; a Dormant giver sleeps through its
own flag until it awakens. An X is still chosen out of the mana held — infinity never widens its
range. Every refusal and listing reads the one `can_pay` check, so the reducer and the views agree.

## Rejected alternative

Setting the player's mana to an unbounded number: the tray, the X range, the credit line's offer
and every "more than your mana" sentence would all need special cases for a value that is never
held — spending nothing keeps every number honest.
