With the switch on (`AI_EVAL_KIND = Learned`, [[R1010000]]), a finalist scored after the opponent's reply takes `hand + share·(points − hand)`, where `points` is the network's whole score in the hand eval's points ([[R1010001]]) and `share` is `LEARNED_CORRECTION_SHARE` (`crates/ai/src/config.rs`, at least 0 and at most 1). The network is fitted to the games' outcomes, so its whole correction says how much more often a position is won than the hand eval thinks. A decision compares lines a move or two apart, where much of that difference is noise.

Measured with the 36,000-game network (#466, 2026-10-18): the whole correction changed the chosen action at about one search decision in four and won +0.010 edge over 1,600 paired games against the hand eval; half of it won +0.064 [+0.024, +0.105] on the same deals. The learn lane ([[R1012000]]) screens and gates its networks at the share `main` holds.
