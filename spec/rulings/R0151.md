A Heroic Power rolls its power as it **arrives** anywhere a card can be looked at — the opening hand, a draw, a return from the graveyard — not only at the start of the game. [[R43]] says it rolls as it arrives, and a copy that reached a hand some other way would otherwise carry no power and cost 0 for ever. A start-of-game clause is an effect list like any other ([[§10.9]]), and a Choose is one of the primitives it may return, so one that asks pauses where it asks ([[§9.3]]): the effects after the question wait for the answer ([[R113]]) rather than applying over the open prompt, and a second question is asked in its turn rather than dropped.
