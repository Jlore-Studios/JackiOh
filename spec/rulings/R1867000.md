Stinkyscape #1 Freeze Ray's "the Units adjacent to it" are the target's neighbours in its row on its side, Cleave's adjacency ([[§3.1]]), whichever side the target stands on: the declared target is any Unit on the field ([[R81]]), and a Unit with no neighbour is Frozen alone. Each Freeze is [[R1174000]]'s: the Unit loses its next attack or switch and thaws at the end of the turn it could have made one. A neighbour Immune to Spells is passed by, as every effect of a Spell passes one ([[R81]]); an Immutable neighbour is Frozen anyway, since a Freeze is a status and not text. The Radiant face declares no target and Freezes every enemy Unit instead, leaving no friendly Unit Frozen ([[§6.1]] Frozen reaches Units only).
