The trio mode is Conquest (its queue name stays `bo3`, [[R257]]): a player takes the series by winning one game with each deck of their trio, `SERIES_WINS_NEEDED` (3) game wins. A deck that wins a game is locked for the rest of the series, so no deck wins twice and a side's wins are its decks that have won; a deck that lost or drew may be picked again, as often as its player likes. Hearthstone's Conquest format is the model. Which decks have won is read off the series' record of its games, so the series row [[R263]] describes kept its shape and needed no migration. It replaces [[R259]]'s first to two wins with each deck played once
