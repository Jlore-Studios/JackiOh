Burn and Toxic ([[§6.3]]) are counters, not keywords: a Unit on the field carries each as `counters.burn` and `counters.toxic`, a hero as `PlayerState.heroBurn` and `heroToxic`, each absent at none so a game without one hashes as before. A new Burn or Toxic adds to the count already there: "Burn N" and "Toxic N" (`burn`, `toxic`, and `burnAll`, `toxicAll` over a board scope and, with `heroes`, each scoped hero) add N, the riders add N after a hit ([[R3540002]]) and the conversion adds the hit ([[R3540003]]). Every gain is reported as one `counterChanged` of the kind `burn` or `toxic`, its `value` the new count and its `placed` the amount added; a hero's names `hero-<player>`, as `damage` names it. A gain on a Unit that is gone, dormant under a Stack, carried or off the field, on a face-down card, or with an amount that is not positive, changes and reports nothing, and none lands once the game is over. [[R78]]'s reset takes a Unit's counters off as the card leaves the field, so a card back afterwards carries none. Rejected: storing them as `Keyword::Burn` and `Keyword::Toxic` in the layers, where a Nerf would move them and a hero has none to hold.
