An ability whose cost carries `either_player` may be activated by the seat that does not control the
card — M #54 Money Machine's shape, proved here with the engine fixture while #54 waits for #525's
Jade. The activator uses it in their own main phase, paying the mana price from their own mana and
sacrificing their own Units to its Tribute, and the run pays them: costs and effects read the
activator wherever they read the controller. The mana price is the declared number `mana_param`
names, read off the card as it stands, so a Buffed price reaches the cost (B3.4). Every other
refusal stands word for word — a controller-only ability still answers "that card is not yours" —
and a viewer who is not the controller is listed only the either-player abilities.
