A fork screen (`cargo jackioh arena --a <candidate> --b <control> --set improve-dev --forks`) measures a change where it acts. The trunk plays each deal of the dev set once with the control on both seats, dealt as the paired screen deals it ([[R850001]]). At each trunk decision the candidate gets the same request the control's seat got — the same `redact(state, seat)`, seat, stream seed and cursor, never the true state (CLAUDE.md rule 7) — and when its action differs (`jackioh_ai::action_key`) that decision is a fork. The trunk always plays on with the control's action; a game keeps its first `FORK_MAX_PER_GAME` (2) forks, and the candidate is not asked after that.

Each fork plays `FORK_ROLLOUTS` (8) pairs from the fork's state: pair r plays the state once after the candidate's action and once after the control's, each through the referee's `accept` (a refused action is replaced as today; a `None` first action is a fault), then both arms play on to the end with the control on both seats, on the streams `<deal>:fork:<k>:<r>:ctl:<seat>`. The control plays both arms on, so a fork measures the changed move alone and not what the changed AI would do later: a change whose gain compounds reads low, never high. Playing the candidate's arm on with the candidate was rejected (the arms would then differ by more than the fork, and the read is the deal screen's again), as was forcing the knob's cards into the deals (helps only knobs tied to named cards; #4246's change diverged in 21 deals unaided).

A fork's edge is the forking seat's wins after the candidate's action less its wins after the control's, over `FORK_ROLLOUTS` (-1 to 1). The tally adds `trunkGames`, `forks`, `liveForks` (edges not 0), `forksPerGame`, `rollouts` (arms played, faulted ones included) and `forkEdge` with its 95% interval over the forked deals, each deal's forks averaged first since one game's forks are not independent ([[R850000]]'s interval over deal means). A screen with fewer than `SCREEN_MIN_DECIDED` (20) live forks prints `inconclusive: true` and says nothing ([[R4319000]]). `--extend` plays on in 100-deal batches to that floor or `SCREEN_MAX_GAMES`, reading the edge only at the end; the stop looks at how many forks are live, never which way they went. A fork screen keeps a change when `forkEdgeLow` is above 0. The kind of screen a change gets is chosen before it runs and recorded with it; a change still gets one screen under [[R850001]]'s rule against second reads. Every rollout arm that throws, falls back, catches a search panic or aborts ([[R1489000]]) contributes no win and is counted and named by its deal, fork and pair.

Hearthstone has no counterpart: there is no public fork screen to tune an AI on.
