The owner asked in #3015 (2026-11-04) that **the art be the same within a variant**: every level of it
draws exactly the same picture, and a level adds only the full-art frame, its picture's own motion layers
([[R2715001]]) or a signature. This note fixes how each level of [[R3015000]]'s ladders is drawn. Like
every look, each is a pure function of the card's id, name, tags, type and set, base and Radiant faces
both.

**One picture.** A face wearing a level draws its variant's first level's picture (`artPictureOf`):
every Full art level the card's normal picture; every Alt art level Alt art's re-seeded one (`"alt"`,
[[R2940001]]); every Sketch level the sketch (`"sketch"`); and each one-art variant's animated level its
first level's own, Extended art's normal picture past its window, Pixel's cells, Comic's pop art, Set
showcase's set treatment, Out of frame's cutout, Immersive's two layers and Prismatic's chrome
([[R2942001]], [[R2943001]], [[R2944001]]); the Default's animated level draws the normal face's. No
level draws a picture of its own, and none takes a new painter, seed or filter.

**The full-art frame.** A level prints the full-art layout, the art across the whole face with no words
([[R1017001]]) and in the viewer's own hand its art alone ([[R2723000]]), when a level at or below it adds
the full art (`isFullBleed`): Full art's from its first, Alt art's and the Sketch's from their second. The
**Sketch's full art** (`version-sketch-full-art`) is the sketch across the whole face: the normal picture
under the Sketch's filter at the full-art shape, its base face's frame the pencilled outline the Sketch
draws ([[R2940001]]).

**Motion.** An animated level and the signed one above it move the variant's picture by the card's own
motion (`artMotionOf`, the one motion a card has, or the one it borrows, [[R2725003]]), all its parts
together on Out of frame and Immersive ([[R2943002]], [[R2944002]]); on a full art they lay and drift the
band, as the Animated augment did. Each takes a motion slot and holds still under reduced motion, with
Animated foil off and on a lazy face ([[R2725003]]). A Legacy card, whose picture never moves
([[R2724000]]), is offered no such level ([[R3015000]]).

**The signature.** A signed level is its variant's full art, moving, with the card's name signed across it
as on Full art before ([[R2715002]]), in the signature's silver ([[R2725004]]): Full art's third level,
Alt art's and the Sketch's fourth. It replaces R2949000's **Signature look**, a picture of its own
(`artSeedOf(id, "signature")`, `data-art-picture="signature"`) and a motion of its own
(`SIGNATURE_MOTION`), which are gone, and nothing is redrawn in their place. A retired Mythic full art,
which showed the Signature look, shows Full art's signed level ([[R3015005]]): the card's own picture
full bleed, its own motion, signed. Rejected: keeping the Signature look's picture for Full art's signed
level, which breaks the owner's rule that a variant's levels draw the same art; and a picture or a motion
of its own for each signed level, which the rule forbids.

**Layers.** A foil, a finish and a border layer on every level as on the normal face ([[R1295000]]), a
finish painting its band round any full-art level's art ([[R2720000]]), and each variant's own frame rules
hold at every level of it: a worn border keeps its edge and bezel on the Sketch, Pixel, Comic, Set
showcase and Prismatic, and Out of frame's cutout and Immersive's near layer take no foil ([[R2940002]],
[[R2942002]], [[R2943002]], [[R2944002]]). The face says what it wears: `data-level`, the level's id (none
for the normal face), and `data-version`, the highest art or full-art level at or below it (none on the
Default variant); `data-augments` is gone.

**Radiant.** No level wears Radiant's gold, and a Radiant face keeps every Radiant mark under every level
([[R2725004]]): its gold-foil frame, [[R277]]'s marks wherever its words show, its gold stats and its
picture's Radiant paint, each variant washing its painted picture back as its first level does.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a level, and nothing enters `PlayerView`'s rules, an
action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725003]] (motion and the signature are levels of a ladder, not augments layered on a version),
[[R2725004]] (its rule covers every level), [[R2940001]], [[R2942001]], [[R2943001]] and [[R2944001]] (each
version's picture is drawn at every level of its variant, and the Sketch's full art draws the sketch),
[[R2940002]], [[R2942002]], [[R2943002]] and [[R2944002]] (each version's animated level moves its own
picture, and Alt full art and the Sketch's full art are signed at their top level) and [[R2949000]] (the
Signature look is gone: a signed level is its variant's full art, moving, signed).

Amended by [[R3017002]] (issue #3017): six more variants, each drawing its one picture at both levels: four repaints of a picture of their own under a treatment, and two restyles of the card's own picture.
