[[R4111000]]'s I18 judged two attacks whose target's death brought a card onto the field at the state check after the combat. In fuzz seed 4897 a Rush Token killed Stinkyscape #135.1 Sack, whose leaving released the Unit it held ([[R1850003]]), Stinkyscape #115 Scornboi, and Scornboi's aura exiled the Rush Token the forecast had shown surviving. In seed 6161 the Rush Token killed Stinkyscape #140 Rush Token Launcher atop a pile with Scornboi dormant beneath it, and the death uncovered Scornboi ([[R1829000]]) with the same result. The forecast ([[R3100000]]) foretells the combat and nothing its deaths bring in after it, and a pile shows only how many cards are dormant under it ([[R13]]), so I18 now skips, by one more named condition, an attack whose attacker or Unit target has a card dormant beneath it in its pile or holds one. It reads only that there is such a card, which the board shows (the pile's count, the public `held` event), never which card it is.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and an invariant that checks it may judge only what it can see.

Rejected: projecting what a death brings in, which would need the identity of a dormant card the attacking seat is not shown; and an event-based skip of any step that summons, which would miss the uncovering, which no event reports ([[R1829000]]).
