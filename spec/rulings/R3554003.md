On a slashed board ([[R3554002]]) the lanes below the slash are the Left half and those above the
Right half, and a Unit or Field Spell affects only its own half: an effect's pick, its stay's
answer and its scopes stop at the slash, an aura buffs its own half alone, a summon lands on its
summoner's half or fizzles, and an attack across the slash is refused — which narrows Taunt walls
and forced attacks too. Heroes and the hand stay shared, so a hero is attacked from either half,
and a Heroic Power reaches both. Effect verbs that walk every acting Unit directly are not
narrowed. Rejected: splitting heroes and Health with the board, which would end games a half's
Units cannot reach.
