"Float reduces Tribute requirement by 1" (`float_lowers_tribute`) is a hand trigger and a deck trigger, as "Float reduces cost" is ([[R464]], [[R1563000]]): whenever the card's owner Floats, never the opponent, its Tribute X is `FLOAT_TRIBUTE_REDUCTION` (1) lower, whatever the Float's size. What the Floats took is kept on the card (`memory.tributeLowered`), in hand and deck alike, and the play's Tribute is the printed X less it, never below 0 ([[R41]]): `legalActions` offers the lowered sets and the reducer takes only those, so once nothing is left to pay it refuses a play naming a Tribute ("needs no Tribute"). It lives in `memory`, so a graveyard, an exile ([[R215]]) or the field's reset ([[R78]]) takes it, and a bounce does too, though a bounce keeps a `costMod` ([[R766]]). Rejected: a field of its own on the instance, which would keep it through a bounce but widen the state, the wire and the AI's redaction for one rider.
