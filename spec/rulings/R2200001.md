TG #39's first entry summons its three Rush Tokens first, base faces, then grants Taunt and Divine Shield to every Unit card of its caster's on the field, in hand and in the deck, so the tokens are among them; the opponent's cards and the caster's non-Unit cards are untouched, and a card hidden from the opponent changes silently ([[R440]]). The second entry adds TG #40 Goetzmann Game Designs until the hand is full, then "refresh 4 mana" is [[§6.3]]'s Refresh ([[R364]]): up to 4 spent mana back, never past max mana, and nothing when the player is already at or above max. It is not a gain of 4.
