A finished 2v2 ([[R3731002]]) records its result once, for both teams' people, as a co-op game does for its two.

- **Pay.** Each of the four is paid for their own seat at co-op rates ([[R1502000]], [[§14.8]]): a win when their team won, the loss rate otherwise, team B's people as their duels' Player 2. The economy locks are taken in profile order, as a co-op result takes its two.
- **Replays.** Each of the four may open their own seat's replay, its views after each step, and no one else; the match row keeps team B's people (`b1`, `b2`, migration 0091) as it keeps a1 and a2, a deleted account's seat emptying once the game has ended and a live one refusing the delete ([[R1497000]]).
- **No Play again.** A 2v2 was made in a lobby, where four people pick and ready again: `POST /api/duos/matches/:matchId/again` answers `409` "a 2v2 game cannot be played again" and keeps nothing, and its `GET` answers `unavailable: true`, which the result panel reads to offer no Play again.

Rejected: a four-way Play again, which would need a rendezvous of four in place of [[R2092000]]'s pair.

Amends [[R1502000]] (every person of a 2v2 is paid) and [[R2092000]] (a 2v2 offers no Play again).
