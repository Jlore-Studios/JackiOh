A destroy only marks the card, and the next state check moves it (§6.3, [[R59]]); it leaves no event of its own until then. In the daily fuzz's Duos seed 1967 p1 paid S #18 Imitated Instrumentality's Tribute 1 ([[R101]]) with a fused Unit that held TG #30 Floodgates' "Death: Destroy all your Units." The Death ran as the cost was paid and marked p1's Units, S+ #111 Mace (Immune to Spells) among them; the Spell was then played, and the state check after its Cry collected the marks: four `destroyed`, no killer ([[R42]]), inside the Spell's run. I21 named Mace's as the Spell's reach.
**The destroy is the Death's.** The engine is right: Floodgates is a Unit, which Immune to Spells does not stop, and the Spell's own text summons and destroys nothing.
**What I21 allows.** A killerless `destroyed` once a Death hook (`hookResolving` with `hook: death`) has run since the last `cardResolved` or turn edge: a play's state checks have collected every mark by its resolution. A killerless death with no Death hook since then, and any other change of the Spell's to the Unit, is still a reach.
Rejected: running the state check between a play's cost and its resolution, which would change when every tribute's Death resolves and every replay with one; and adding an event for a destroy mark, which changes the wire for a monitor's sake. The cost: a Spell's own destroy of an immune Unit after a Death hook in the same play goes unjudged, as R4599004's board exiles already do.
