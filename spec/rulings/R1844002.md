- Date: 2026-10-25

- Decides: the helpful cast (issue #1844, Stinkyscape's M10)
- Spec: §6.3 Do something helpful, §10.7

"Start of Turn: Do something helpful", which the designer glosses as reading the board, a quasi Zephrys doing actions for no additional cost, is the `helpful_cast` verb. The perfect pool, every non-token card of every set that ships but the running card's own ids ([[R1446]], [[R387]]), is ranked for its caster as Classic+ #27 Zephrys Zealotism ranks a hand ([[R416]]), best first and ties by card id, and its top card is cast as `castNew` casts one ([[R70]]): a new card its caster owns, free, counted as a play and announced as one, its choices its caster's, landing in their graveyard after it resolves ([[R87]]). With `radiant` ("REALLY helpful") the pool is ranked by its Radiant faces and the pick is cast Radiant.

The ranking is [[§10.7]]'s scorer on the caster's concealed copy, so the same state casts the same card and nothing hidden decides it. Outside the caster's main phase with nothing open, at the start of their turn where #147.2 does it, the scorer reads printed data alone, as it always does there. An empty pool casts nothing.

Rejected: a Zephrys Discover of the top three, which "for no additional cost" does not ask for and which would open a prompt at the start of every turn; two casts for the Radiant face, which the card's own issue may still choose.
