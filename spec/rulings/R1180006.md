A Dogpile's own cast is a Spell that targeted an enemy, so it can call the next answer ([[R1180005]]). A deck that refills itself with answering cards, aimed at an enemy no hit can fell, would answer forever and never let the action settle. As [[R58]] does for cast-on-draw chains, one chain of answers names at most `SPELL_ANSWER_CHAIN_CAP` (20): the Spell that started it is answered, then each answer's own cast, and the resolution that would name answer 21 names none, which ends the chain. The player's `answerChain` keeps the card last named and the count so far. That card's resolution continues the chain; any other resolution of the player's starts a new one, so the next Spell they play is answered afresh. The designer's text sets no bound; Hearthstone ends such loops by its own caps, and this is the engine's.
