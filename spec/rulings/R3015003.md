The owner asked in #3015 (2026-11-04) that owning a level mean owning every level below it, that pulls
grant the **next** level in order, and that a separate, far rarer draw may grant a level past the next one
together with every level below it. The issue left the draw's weight and its bound to the plan.

**The next level.** A level is the next on a card when every level below it in its variant is owned there
(`cosmetics::is_next`, `levels_below`), a retired row counting as the levels it maps to ([[R3015006]]);
the normal face, the Default's first level, is owned by every account. Anything that is no level (a
foil, a finish, a border, an account cosmetic) is always next.

**A duplicate's cosmetic** ([[R1301002]]) rolls its tier as before. The open entries at that tier are
those the card is offered and the account does not own there ([[R2725001]], [[R2725002]]): the next ones
are **in order**, the levels past the next **out of order**. Where an out-of-order level is open, one extra
draw below `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL` comes first: below `LEVEL_SKIP_WEIGHT` the pick is among the out-of-order
levels, uniform, and the duplicate grants that level **and every level below it** the account does not
own there, lowest first (the grant's `below`, each a ledger row of its own, reason `pack_open`); at or past
it, the pick is among the in-order ones as before, and with none the duplicate is spent. With nothing open
out of order the draw is not taken, so a duplicate draws as it did.

**A cosmetic pack slot** ([[R1306000]]) does the same at each tier it tries, in its fallback order
([[R1306001]]): where some level of that tier is open out of order on a pool card (offered there, not
owned, some level below it not owned), the extra draw comes first, and below `LEVEL_SKIP_WEIGHT` it picks
such a level, uniform, then its card among the pool cards it is open on out of order, weighted by
`COSMETIC_PACK_CARD_WEIGHTS`, and grants it with every level below it the card lacks, and a copy of the
card where the account lacks one; at or past it, the tier is tried in order as before, a level being open
only on a card where it is the next.

**The draws** are the opening's, seeded and logged ([[R1339002]]): the extra draw comes after the tier's
and before the pick's. Ownership moves slot by slot, an out-of-order pull's levels below it included, so
a later slot of the same pack finds them owned.

**The numbers.** `LEVEL_SKIP_WEIGHT` is 40 out of `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL`'s 1000, 4% of the draws where an
out-of-order level is open, and an out-of-order level is open less often than a next one, so out-of-order
pulls stay far rarer than in-order ones. A seeded simulation of duplicates on one card counts both and
holds out-of-order pulls to at most `LEVEL_SKIP_MAX_PERCENT_OF_IN_ORDER` (10) percent of in-order ones.
Rejected: granting an out-of-order level alone, which leaves a ladder with a gap against the owner's
rule; folding the levels past the next into the tier's uniform pick, which would make them as common as
in-order ones wherever both are open; and a weight per level skipped, a table the owner did not ask for.

**The odds.** `GET /api/packs` lists `odds.levelSkip` and `GET /api/cosmetic-packs` its own, the chance
`LEVEL_SKIP_WEIGHT` over `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL` as the server computes it, and the Purchases page shows it
under a duplicate's line and under a cosmetic pack slot's ([[R1665000]], [[§14.4]]). A reveal says when a
pull granted the levels below it too. Nothing reaches `PlayerView` (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Amends [[R1301002]] (a duplicate's per-card cosmetic is its card's next level, or by the extra draw a
level past it with every level below it) and [[R1306000]] (a cosmetic pack slot's likewise, at each tier it
tries).

Amended by [[R3343000]] (issue #3343): a card's default look is held with no row, so it is never open and never among an out-of-order pull's `below`, and the level above it drops in order.
