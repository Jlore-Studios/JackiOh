Stinkyscape #120 Wild System reads "All units attack each other until only one survives", the designer adding "allies can attack each other, max 20 attacks". Nothing in the engine made Units fight their own side, and nothing drew who attacks whom.

- **Forced attacks.** `melee { maxAttacks }` (`MELEE_MAX_ATTACKS`, 20, when absent) makes a run of forced attacks ([[R53]]): position, summoning sickness, exertion and Taunt are waived, the target strikes back, and each attack is its own combat with its own state check ([[§4.5]]), its Deaths and their questions included. A Frozen Unit fights, as any forced attack makes it.
- **The pair.** Before each attack the fighters are read again: every Unit acting on the field, the active player's in lane order and then the other's, so a card beneath a Stack ([[R13]]) and a carried Unit ([[R446]]) take no part, and a Unit summoned during the melee joins it. The attacker is drawn from the match rng among the fighters that have a Unit they may attack, then its target among those Units; one may attack another under [[§4.2]] step 2's restrictions alone (Can't be attacked, Can't attack or be attacked, a lane restriction, Flying, [[R783002]]) — Can't attack bars a declared attack at step 1, which every forced attack waives, so a Unit with it fights too — and an ally is as good a target as an enemy — the one change to [[R173]], which keeps every other forced attack on the enemy. A hero is never a target. Two draws per attack.
- **When it ends.** Once one Unit or none is left, once no fighter has a Unit it may attack (no draw is made then), once the game is over, or after the 20th attack, whatever is left standing.
- **Waiting for an answer.** A question a combat's Deaths ask, or an attack summon's ([[§4.2]]), stops the melee between two attacks; what it still owes is parked as `@melee` with the attacks it has left ([[R113]]) and made once the answer has been given.
- No new event: each attack is an `attackDeclared` with `forced`, as every forced attack's is. Nothing reaches it until a card plays it, so no shipped game changes.
