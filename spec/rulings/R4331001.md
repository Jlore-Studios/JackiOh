The owner amends [[R1854003]]: a thief that steals its opponent's mana crystals keeps every one of them, even past the cap of 4.

- **The count.** `PlayerState.stolenManaCrystals` holds the crystals a player stole. Max mana is min(turns started + the seat's bonus + moved crystals, cap) + stolen crystals + persistent modifiers ([[§2.3]], [[R181]]). The stolen crystals stand outside the min, past a seat's cap too ([[R180]]). The field is absent while none were stolen, so no other game hashes differently.
- **The thief.** It gains as many crystals as the victim lost, full: max mana and current mana both rise by that much. Its own crystals keep growing with its turns up to the cap, and the stolen ones stay on top. A thief at 2 that steals 1 has 3, then 4 and 5 over its next two turns, and stays at 5.
- **The victim.** As [[R1854003]] says, its own crystals drop to 0 and come back one a turn. A victim that had stolen crystals itself loses those too, and they never come back. A Heist that steals from a thief at 8 (its own 4 and 4 stolen) takes all 8.
- **The card text.** Players cannot work the exception out from [[§2.3]]'s cap, so The Greatest Heist prints it: Stage 3's reward reads "Steal your opponent's mana crystals, even past 4." on both faces (Chinese "夺取你对手的法力水晶，可超过4个。"), and the quest view shows the same words, the 4 being `MAX_MANA`. The patch claims the card by this text, since `classify`'s games never reach Stage 3 to show its play change ([[R1370000]]).
- **Replays.** The games of v0.6.9c and before fold under The Greatest Heist's kept script, whose steal stops at the cap as [[R1854003]] read (`steal_mana_crystals_to_cap`, [[R767000]]), its reward shown without "even past 4".
