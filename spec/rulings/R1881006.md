`MARKETPLACE_TABLE` in `config.rs` is the set's table ([[R1853001]]), ten lots in the order ST3 lists them, each named in `config.rs`:

1. four of the catalog's Resources, stock 4 to 8;
2. three of the twelve v3–v5 Weapons (S #50 to #61);
3. both Warp Disruptors (S #23, #24);
4. Scanning Probe Mk1 (S #166), stock 6;
5. Scanning Probe Mk2 (S #167), stock 3;
6. two of the ten Drone Cores (S #27 to #36);
7. one of the four Launchers (S #140 to #143);
8. two Starship Pieces;
9. two Stinkyscape cards;
10. one of the ten AI cards (T-AI-1 to T-AI-10, ST15).

Every lot past the first stocks 1 to 3 but the Probes'. "Both Scanning Probes (stock 6 and 3)" is two lots of one card each, so the Mk1 always stocks 6 and the Mk2 3.

The Starship Pieces' lot is a fourth kind of pool, `MarketPool::SetKeyword`: every non-token card of a set that prints a keyword on its base face, in catalog order, [[R1878000]]'s pool. It names its set, as the Stinkyscape cards' lot does, so [[R1420]]'s gate does not empty it while the set is held. The named lots name ids, which reach held cards too. Each card's price is [[R1853002]]'s, by its printed cost.

The table changes no shipped game: the Marketplace opens only on a Resource or Credits gain ([[R1853000]]), which only a card of the held set makes. A later patch that changes it owes each shipped version its own table ([[R1853001]]).
