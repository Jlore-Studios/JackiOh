Felinors vs Humans #46 Manifestor summons a random (2) Cost Unit to the left of itself and one to the right, then has its player "secretly choose one of them to gain Fragile". An edge lane or a filled zone summons nothing there ([[R129]]), so the Cry may summon two, one or none, and the text does not say what the choice is then.

**Two summoned.** A target prompt ([[§10.6]]) to the card's controller alone, whose options are exactly the two Units this Cry summoned beside it, on its side: no other Unit, and no hero. Its answer grants Fragile in secret ([[R5238001]]) to the pick, both summoned Units its decoys, so the opponent sees Fragile on both.

**One summoned.** That Unit is the one: it takes Fragile at once by the same secret grant, itself its only decoy, and no prompt opens, since a choice of one asks nothing. The opponent sees Fragile on it, which is the truth.

**None summoned.** Nothing is granted and nothing is asked.
