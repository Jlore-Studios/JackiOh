The device's progress and the account's are merged as a union of completed lessons, and for Hide/Show the newest explicit choice wins, by the time it was made on the device that made it (a new choice is always stamped later than the one it replaces, and a tie keeps the device's). So a completed lesson never becomes uncompleted, and Show on one device is not undone by an older Hide on the account. On `/practice`, a page with an active account reads the account's progress once, merges it into the device, and sends the merged progress up if the device holds something the account lacks: a lesson it does not list, or a later choice that says otherwise. After that, each change on the device (a lesson won, Hide or Show) is sent up as it happens, one request at a time, and the answer merged back in. The device's copy is the one the page renders and is authoritative for the session: a request that fails or never answers changes nothing on screen and raises nothing, and the next load catches the account up. Signed out, pending or banned, progress stays on the device and nothing is sent ([[R294]]). Progress belongs to the device as much as to the account, so signing in on a device adds that device's lessons to the account
