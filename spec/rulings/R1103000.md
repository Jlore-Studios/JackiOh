[[R1441]]'s client half probes a socket the page has heard nothing from for longer than `WS_PING_INTERVAL_SECONDS` when the tab wakes: it sends a `hello`, keeps the socket if anything comes back within the probe deadline, and replaces it if nothing does. The watch screen ([[§9.5]], "Watching a friend") now probes its socket the same way, but the actor read nothing a watcher sent after its first hello, and a watcher is sent a view only when the friend's seat changes, `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` later. A live watch socket therefore never answered, and every wake after a quiet stretch replaced it, each time with a new sign-in and the store's checks. As a match socket's hello is answered with a full view:

- **A watcher's hello after the one that let it in is answered with the view it was last sent**, the same frame again, and nothing else. It is never a newer view: a state is still shown no sooner than `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` after it, so the answer tells a watcher nothing it was not already told.
- **A watcher sent no view yet is sent nothing**: there is no view old enough to send, which happens only in a match's first `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS`, since a watcher let in later is sent the newest due view at once. Its page then replaces the socket, and the new socket of the same account takes the old one's place.
- **Every other frame a watcher sends is still dropped unread**, and no frame of a watcher's reaches the reducer, a log, a relay or a clock.

The hello's `matchId` and `friendId` are not read again: the socket was let in for one seat of one match, and a match socket's later hello is answered without reading its `matchId` either. Each hello is answered, with no budget of its own: a flood of them is bounded as every socket's frames are, by `WS_OUTBOX_MAX_FRAMES` ([[R1441]]), which closes a socket whose answers outrun its reading.

Rejected: replacing the watch socket on every wake after a quiet stretch, which the client could do alone but which signs in and runs the store's checks again on every tab switch and never keeps a healthy socket; sending the newest view already due when the wait has not yet sent it, which the wait sends anyway within moments and which would make the answer depend on the timer's race; answering with the watcher count, which is the players' frame and not the watcher's; a budget of answered hellos, a new number for nothing a page needs, since a page probes only once its socket has been quiet past the ping interval.
