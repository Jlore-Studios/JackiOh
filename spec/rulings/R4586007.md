A Figment's own firing is an ordinary trigger that runs after the combat ([[§10.3]]). Its `trapFired` carries the attacker of the attack it answered (`attackerId`) — absent when it answers nothing — and answers once per firing: a refire is `again` ([[R2657001]]). The Sovereign's copy is made only of an attacker still on the field ([[R57]]).
