"Spells gain Echo +N" is an aura (`StaticFlags.spellEchoAura`): each Spell (type Spell only) its
controller plays or casts while the card acts resolves N more times, fixed as played ([[R802]]); a
base face with `both` reaches the opponent's Spells too, a Radiant face its controller's alone. N is
the card's declared `spellEcho` ([[R386]]). A Reveal keeps its stamped Echo ([[R1045]]). Rejected:
reaching Field Spells and Traps too, which never resolve and would owe repeats for ever.
