[[R33]] lets a trap's controller read it while the other player sees a back, and the board drew it like any public card, so nothing said it was still hidden. `viewFor` marks the controller's view of a Trap or Field Trap that has not flipped face-up `unrevealed: true` ([[R351]]), from the same answer that gives the other player a back (`preview.isFaceDown`), so the mark and the back cannot disagree; it follows control as [[R33]] does, and a Field Spell or a fired Field Trap never carries it. The client draws the face, since the controller reads it, under three marks that each read by shape, so none rests on colour: a dashed frame, a diagonal veil over the face that drifts slowly and holds still under reduced motion ([[R200]]), and a "Face down" tag with a struck-through eye, whose tooltip and hidden text say "Face down — your opponent can't see this card" and which keeps only the eye on a card too narrow for the word. Its hover preview and its sheet carry the same line over the face
