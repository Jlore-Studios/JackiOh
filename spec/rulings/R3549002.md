S+ #58 Phase Conveyer reads "Start of turn: add a Mineral and Resource to your hand", its Radiant face
"a Radiant Mineral and a Radiant Resource". At the start of its controller's turn ([[R62]]), before
their draw, it adds exactly one of each, a Mineral first and then a Resource, and opens no prompt:
nobody picks. The Mineral is a random non-token card of the Stinkyscape+ set tagged Mineral, S+ #44
to #50, the pool naming its set so that it reaches them while the set is held ([[R1420]]); the
Resource is [[R1840002]]'s roll. The Mineral costs its printed (1): the text prints no (0), as the
drills' do ([[R3549001]]). The Radiant face adds both Radiant. A full hand burns what does not fit
([[§2.4]]); the opponent's start of turn adds nothing.

Rejected: a Mineral at (0), which the text does not say; a choice between a Mineral and a Resource,
since the text adds both; and a Discover.
