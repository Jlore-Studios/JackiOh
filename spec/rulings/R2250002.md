While the Marketplace is open (it still opens on the first Resource or Credits either player gains, [[R1853000]]), at the start of each player's turn it rolls that player `MARKETPLACE_OFFERS_PER_TURN` (3) offers of their own, replacing the offers it rolled them before. Each offer draws a lot of `MARKETPLACE_TABLE` by the lots' counts, among those with a card left (a lot's count is its share of the offers, as it was of the whole offering, [[R1853001]], [[R1881006]]), then a card of that lot uniformly among those not offered yet in the roll, so the three differ; then whether it is Radiant (`MARKETPLACE_RADIANT_CHANCE`, 20%), then its price, the designer's base moved within a tenth of it ([[R1853002]]), which then stays until the offer goes. Each offer holds one copy (`MARKETPLACE_OFFER_STOCK`), its owner's to buy. The table's Resources lot is gone and no lot holds a Resource: a Resource pays its Credits as it is played or discarded instead ([[R2250001]]). Opening the Marketplace rolls the player whose turn it is their offers, then the other player theirs, then the quotes. The quotes are drawn at every turn start as before ([[R1853002]]). The six-turn cycle and its `rerollsOn` are gone.

A buy asks what [[R1853003]] asks, with room in the hand for every card; a sale takes only a card that sells from the hand ([[R1861000]]). A lot an effect adds for the rest of the game ([[R1853005]]) stays its owner's, at its price, and its copy is renewed at its owner's turn start. A player's offers are theirs alone: their view lists the lots offered to them, the ones they may buy, and `marketBuy { offer }` names a lot by its place among those (`marketplace::offered_to`), so the other player's, which the AI's redaction drops ([[R1853004]]), never move the index. The owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2250) asked for three fresh cards each turn in place of a shared shop restocked every six turns.
