The two mulligans share one clock: the server starts one deadline for both players when they open (`MULLIGAN_CLOCK_SECONDS`, 45 s, a server config value beside [[R79]]'s), and one player's answer neither stops nor extends it for the other. When it runs out the actor sends a `timeout` for each player who still owes a mulligan, and a `timeout` while the mulligans are open answers only that player's own mulligan, by keeping the whole hand — Hearthstone confirms the hand as it stands when its mulligan timer runs out — with no draw from the rng. It never ends a turn there, since setup is no one's turn ([[§2.1]]). A match actor rebuilt while the mulligans are open ([[§9.5]]) starts their clock afresh, as it restarts the turn clock from full: the deadline is kept in the running actor, and the stored copy is written after the fact. [[R79]]'s AI policy is not used for it: its uniform draw over the subsets would send back part of a hand the player never chose to send back
