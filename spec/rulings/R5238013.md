Felinors vs Humans #107 swaps hands, and at the end of its player's turn every swapped card still in a hand goes back. `swap_hands` ([[R4586008]]) swapped for good.

**The rule.** With `untilEndOfTurn`, the hands swap as before, and an engine delayed effect waits for the end of the swapping player's turn ([[§2.2]], in [[R62]]'s delayed stage): this turn, or their next when the swap came on the other player's turn. It watches each swapped card's stay in the hand the swap put it in, as a hand watch does ([[R1140]]): a card that leaves that hand (played, discarded, moved) is dropped from it and stays where it went, and the effect goes once none is left. As it runs, each card still watched goes back from that hand to the hand it came from, owned and controlled by that hand's player again, at that hand's right end in the order it left; no hand cap burns it, as the swap burns none. A card the swapping player took gives back the swap's `costMod`. Cards that joined either hand after the swap stay put.
