[[§9.5]] says what Play online, practice and Play together are, but not which screen a player starts
each from. `/play` is that screen, the Play hub, and the landing's Play door opens it: this amends
[[R1577000]], whose Play door led to `/practice`. It edits [[§9.5]]: the Play-together bullet's
landing sentence now names the hub.

**One screen.** The hub does not scroll the page, on a desktop or on a phone. Its shell is the
height of the viewport. A desktop shows everything at once, and where a deck list is longer than its
room the setup panel scrolls inside itself. A phone stacks the panels, the online panel first, and
shows all of it and the ways and the room at once at 390x844: the three modes stand side by side,
the ways are one row and their notes go. A screen shorter than that scrolls the hub's one
region, never a panel inside it.

**Online first.** The online panel is the first and largest thing on the screen: the player's rank,
the mode and the deck or trio they will queue with, and Find a match, the one lit action. Beside it
(under it on a phone), smaller and plainer, are the other ways to play, each a link to the screen that
starts it: Practice (`/practice`, the AI and its tutorial), Play together
(`/together`: friends, challenges, watching, co-op Duos) and Replays (`/replays`), and the room panel,
Play a friend ([[R264]], [[R767]]). Conquest and All Random are queue modes of the online panel, and
Hotseat is a developer's tool, offered to no player. The Card creator (`/creator`) was a fourth way until
[[R1646000]]; the Collection's door leads to it ([[R1579000]]).

**No account.** A visitor with none sees the same hub: the online panel is the invite-only sentence
and a Sign in link to `/login`, and the page reads nothing from the server: no decks, rank, queue
population or open rooms. The other ways still lead on, `/practice` as ever with no account needed,
the rest to the sign-in gate. A stored session, even a stale one, still meets the gate as before, and
so does a visitor who opened a room link: they are sent to sign in at once, the link waiting for them
([[R767]]).

**The way back.** A player whose online game is live is taken back to it when the hub opens
([[R765]]); the main menu and the practice menu still show the banner.

**Practice stands alone.** Its "More ways to play" links ([[R1577000]]) are gone, since the hub is one
Back away, and its lobby's Back leads to `/play`.

Hearthstone semantics: one glowing Play button first, the other modes smaller around it. Rejected:
a tile for the tutorial, since `/practice?lesson=` ends a game saved with Save and leave
([[R765]]); and a banner on the hub, since arriving there already takes a live player to their game.

No path moved or went away, so no redirect is added.
