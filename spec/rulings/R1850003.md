"Stack a permanent under this" (Ticketed) and "stuff it in a 0/4 Sack" (Sacked) hold a permanent beneath a card (`hold_beneath { target, release, holder }`). The permanent the target names, face-up and acting on the field and not the running card, leaves the field as an exiled card does: no Death, reset as every card leaving the field is ([[R78]]), the card beneath it in a Stack pile resuming ([[§3.2]]), every effect aimed at its stay ended ([[R174]]). The `held` event reports it, naming the card that holds it, and it waits in `state.held`. Both are face-up on the board as it happens, so both seats read the event; like `exiled` it is judged by where each card is now ([[R97]]), so a held card released and since bounced into a hand, or a holder now somewhere hidden, reads as a card back to the seat that may not read it. The holder is the running card, which must itself be on the field, or a card the verb summons for it (`holder`: the Sack, for its target's controller on the base face and for its caster on the Radiant), whose `summoned` comes before the `held`. At the first state check after its holder has left the field, any way it leaves — it dies, is bounced, exiled, transformed or held itself — the card is summoned for the player it returns to, fixed as it was taken: its controller then (Ticketed's "return it to its controller") or the holder's controller (the Sack's "summon the Unit stuffed into this"). It is a summon: R64's zone ([[R64]]), summoning sick, no Cry ([[R1]]). With no zone open it is exiled. A Sack that found no zone holds nothing, so the card comes back at the next check. A unit token ceases to exist as it leaves the field ([[R11]]), so nothing is held, and a face-down card is never held, so nothing hidden goes beneath a card.

What a holder holds is not on the view; Stinkyscape's cards may add it.

Rejected: release at the holder's Death only, which would keep a card held beneath a holder bounced or exiled for the rest of the game; sending a card with no room to its graveyard, where a Death-reading card could reach a card that never died; a `held` event public for good, which would go on naming a released card after it is bounced into a hand.
