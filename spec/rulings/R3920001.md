On handicapped fuzz seed 4517, a Core #33 Unstable Clone Machine answered each cast of S+ #89 Animosity from the deck ([[R3558007]]) by shuffling three copies of it into the deck, each of which then cast itself while the exile held enough cards, at most `DECK_CAST_ACTION_CAP` an action, until the game held 2,106 instances. A cast from the deck is counted and answered as [[R2179002]]'s cast on draw: a cast and no play, which nothing that counts plays counts and no text that answers a play ("after you play", "whenever your opponent plays", M #3 Jlockwork Machine's among them) answers, its `cardPlayed` and `cardResolved` saying so with `onDraw`. Hearthstone's Casts When Drawn is no play either, and the deck cast is the same kind of cast: the card's own text casts it from a hidden zone. It amends [[R70]]'s "a cast counts as a play" and R3558007.

Rejected: only the Clone Machine passing a deck cast by, which leaves every other answer to a play free to grow the same loop.
