Mender's "adjacent Units" are its controller's Units standing in its lane or a lane beside it, in
any row: lanes meeting its own within one. It heals them to full at the end of its controller's
turn, and on the Radiant face gives each of them Divine Shield too. Rejected: [[§3.1]]'s same-row
reading, which would strand a backrow Mender with no neighbor, and both sides, which the text never
offers.
