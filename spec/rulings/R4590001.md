IA #130 Prompt Injection's "Your opponent's next Spell has its targets chosen randomly" is a `used` modifier each (`effects::random_targets_next_spells`, one per affected Spell): only the declared targets of that player's face-up Spell plays are drawn at random, by [[R1200]]'s draw (`random_targets::play_targets_random`), and each Spell spends one modifier at §10.5 step 4. Questions asked while a Spell resolves ([[§10.6]]), casts ([[R70]]), face-down sets, Units, attacks and activations are untouched, and a countered Spell spends none ([[R90]]), since step 4 never runs for it. The modifier shows as a badge. IA #142 The Jailbreaker's aura is the same draw without the spend: its `random_opponent_spell_targets` static flag draws every Spell its controller's opponent plays face-up, and a Mayor keeps drawing every play's exactly as before.
