A Unit with Windfury, read through the layers ([[§10.4]]), may declare `WINDFURY_ATTACKS` (2) attacks each turn, where every other Unit declares one ([[§4.1]]). Each is a full attack: it is validated by [[§4.2]]'s steps 1 to 3 and opens its own trap window, and a Unit that is dead, no longer in Attack Position or no longer legal to attack with is refused the second as it would be the first. Declaring the first spends the exertion a position switch needs, so the Unit cannot switch afterwards, and a Unit that switched cannot attack ([[R6]]); a Unit with Deft ([[R49]]) keeps its independent switch. The count is read when the second attack is declared, not when the first was: a Unit that gains Windfury after one attack may attack once more, and one that loses it after the first may not attack again. `legalActions` and the reducer's refusal ask the same function (`combat.hasExertion`), so they agree on the second attack. An attack an effect forces ([[R53]], [[R20]]) spends no exertion and is not counted. Windfury is a Unit keyword in the random keyword pool ([[R21]]), so Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao and an Upgrade can reach it; no card prints it yet
