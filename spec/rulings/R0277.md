The collection printed a Radiant face as its base clauses and then [[§8]]'s shorthand cell ("9", "+3 damage") under a gold rule, which a player had to fit back into the sentence it changes. The catalog now carries each Radiant face's whole text — the cell read by [[§8]]'s Conventions and written out — and a client prints it whole wherever a Radiant face's text is shown: the collection and its detail view, the hand, the field, a prompt's options, the showcase, a pile, the log's cards and every inspect overlay. The words and numbers in it that the base face's text does not have are marked: gold, and bold with an underline, so the mark does not rest on colour alone, in a gold dark enough to read on the light face and bright enough on the dark one. The mark is a word-level diff of the two catalog texts, compared without regard to case, so what it marks is public catalog text and the same in play as in the collection; a word the Radiant face drops is simply absent. A Radiant unit's attack and health, which [[R275]] doubles, are marked the same way where a printed face shows them. It is presentation only: no rule reads it (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
