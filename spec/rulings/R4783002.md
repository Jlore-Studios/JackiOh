Immutable stops a change to a card's text on the field, and a Transform elsewhere is a Replace, a new instance in the old one's place ([[R35]]), which Immutable does not stop ([[§6.3]]). I23 judges an Immutable card on the field, in a hand and in a library, and allowed a Replace only where its new card is hidden from a player, as a hand or a library card is ([[R4609001]]). IA #43 Cognitive Dissonance answers a play whose Unit left the field in its own resolution ([[R427]]): an Immutable S+ #111 Mace played into a lane that exiled it at once was replaced in exile, in public, and I23 judged it as the hand card it was when the step began (the handicapped fuzz wave's seed 1461 in this issue's merge-queue build).

**A Replace where Immutable stops nothing.** I23 allows a `transformed` that makes a new instance of a card the step sent to an exile or a graveyard (an `exiled` or an `enteredGraveyard` naming it earlier in the step) and has not brought back to the field since (a `summoned` or a `flickered`). A Transform of an Immutable card on the field, and a public replace of a card that went nowhere, are still findings.
