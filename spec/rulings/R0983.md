MD-C8: the reward makes the card Radiant at [[§10.5]] step 3 (already Radiant: nothing more). The punishment gives the card Brittle 2 there (a given count, [[R385]], [[R638]]) and deals its damage from Feng Shui to the playing player's hero through [[§4.4]] once the play has resolved. Feng Shui does not judge its own play, since it is not on the field at that step.

The verdicts are fixed at step 1 as [[R214]] fixes Gifted Program's ([[R213]]); a cast or a replaced card is judged off the board at step 3 ([[R70]], [[R449]]). The damage comes at step 7, after `cardResolved`, from the judge wherever it now is. Rejected: a `cardPlayed` trigger, which resolves before the Cry ([[R17]]).

A public `fengShui { instanceId, player, outcome }` names each judgement, with the judging Feng Shui as `sourceId`, so the client can draw 吉 or 凶.
