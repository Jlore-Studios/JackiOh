Each of the ten playmats ([[R1297000]]) has two corner props to find, one by each side, drawn in the
mat's look (`game/arena/props.ts`, `PropArt.tsx`): the tavern's candle and mug, the wood's dice
and coin, the felt's chip stack and ace, the neon grid's ghost and bolt, the marble's urn and
laurel, the chessboard's knight and clock, the desert sand's cactus and skull, the obsidian slab's
crystal and rune, the ancient map's compass and serpent, the astral mat's comet and satellite.

**A tap.** A prop answers a tap with a small motion and a sound: one of the seven field-tap recipes
(`RAPID_SFX`) with its own variant and pitch, so a prop never spends the game's voices.

**Escalation.** Taps on one prop, each within `CORNER_STREAK_GAP_MS` of the last, count as a
streak. The streak's goal is drawn when it starts, from `CORNER_ESCALATE_TAPS` (5 to 7, both
included); the tap that reaches it plays the prop's bigger payoff (the candle flares, the knight
leaps, the serpent surfaces), with a three-cue sound, and the streak starts again.

**The rare outcome.** Each tap rolls `CORNER_RARE_CHANCE` (1 in 1,000) from the client's own random
source (`arenaRandom`, `Math.random` unless a test seeds it), never the game's RNG ([[§10.8]]). A
rare outcome plays a gold shimmer and four astral chimes, and wins over a sequence and an
escalation due at the same tap. The goal is drawn first, then the rare roll.

**A hidden sequence.** The tavern, the chessboard, the ancient map and the astral mat hide one: tap
the right prop, the left, then the right again, each within `CORNER_SEQUENCE_GAP_MS` of the last,
for a special moment that lights both props and plays four cues. A wrong tap loses the place,
except the sequence's first prop, which starts it again. It wins over an escalation due with it.

**Yours alone.** Corner play is local to the viewer's screen: nothing in `game/arena/` or a tile's
answer imports the net layer, the wire or the replay, sends an action, opens a connection or writes
the log, and none of them reads a `PlayerView`: a playmat is a cosmetic and its id is public
([[R1834004]]). The viewer's own half answers taps; the opponent's half draws their playmat's props
([[R1834004]]), still and `aria-hidden`, taking no press. A prop never holds the game up or paces
it. While the animation queue drains (`draining`) a prop still answers, quietly: one cue at
`QUIET_SFX_GAIN` of its gain, the change shown still, no buzz.

**Where they sit.** Outside the lanes and the hands: each prop is `--hit-min` square
([[R2122000]]) and sits in the board's sidebar column beside the field where the board has one,
else at the field's corner. On a tall phone (390 wide, 840 high or more, `arena.css`) the field keeps
a strip of mat, one `--hit-min` tall, above its first row and below its last, a half's two props in
theirs, one in each corner. The strips come out of the zones' spare height, since a card there is
width-bound: a card keeps its width and loses a few px of its height (it stays between the field
shape's 0.72 and 0.86), and the rows' floor gives up a tenth of `--card-h`. A shorter phone has no
height to give, so it gets no strip. `CornerProps` measures each prop against `CORNER_OBSTACLES` (a
tile, a hand, a seat, the controls, the log) and the screen, and a prop that would cover one or leave
it is not drawn and takes no tap (`data-fit="false"`): the one-screen rule is not given up for a prop,
and a card is not shrunk on a phone that has no spare height.

**Settings.** Reduced motion keeps the sounds and shows still changes. A touch press buzzes
(`corner`, `cornerBig`, `cornerRare`) behind the Vibration switch ([[R669]]); effects intensity `off`
quiets a prop to its sound.

Rejected: a bespoke sound recipe for each prop, which is about twenty new Web Audio recipes and
their test tables for what a recipe with a variant and a pitch already gives; props inside the
lanes, which would cover tiles; shrinking the phone's cards on every phone to make room, which the one-screen
rule (#2876) already pays for in height (only a tall phone's spare height is used); and sending corner play to the opponent or the log, which
the issue rules out.
