[[R176]] makes an attacker that a First Strike defender fells in [[§4.3]]'s step 1 deal nothing. [[R44]]'s projection (`subsystems::lethal::falls_to_first_strike`) knew two ways the attacker falls there: a hit that reaches its health, and a Poisonous hit ([[§4.4]] step 7). It did not know a defender whose damage exiles (Meditative #72 The Banisher, [[R1124]]), whose hit of 1 or more marks the attacker for exile, and a marked unit has fallen. [[R4111000]] had already made the combat forecast read it, but #96 My Pawn still fired on a Trample attack into a First Strike Banisher that deals the hero nothing.

In R44's projection, a First Strike source whose damage exiles, against a combatant without First Strike, fells it with any hit of 1 or more that [[§4.4]] steps 1 to 4 leave, as a Poisonous one does. So the attacker's attack deals nothing and is not lethal. With the sides swapped, a First Strike attacker whose damage exiles and lands 1 or more on a Lifesteal defender leaves it no strike back, so nothing heals. A hit that a Divine Shield, Armor, a cap, a conversion, a damage pool or Indestructible takes whole deals nothing and fells nothing. The combat forecast's attack entry reads the same function, so the two cannot drift apart.

Hearthstone semantics: the projection is what the combat does, and a marked attacker never strikes.

Rejected: amending [[R4111000]], which is the forecast's ruling and I18's, not My Pawn's. Also rejected: keeping the forecast's own `exiled_first` check beside the fixed function, which would leave two reads of one rule that have already drifted apart once.
