**Why.** A finished 1v1 actor left memory only once its rematch window had passed with no seat's socket open ([[R672]]), and a finished co-op actor only once nobody was attached. A tab left on the result screen held its socket, and its pings kept it alive, for as long as the tab lived: the finished game's actor, its state and its frames stayed in memory for good, the socket took one of its address's `WS_MAX_CONNECTIONS_PER_ADDRESS` slots, and the open socket counted as a request in flight, so the container never slept. [[R3576000]] let a hidden tab's social socket go and left match sockets alone, and [[R1285000]] refused only a new socket on a finished match past its window, never one already open.

**The rule.** On each reaper tick a finished 1v1 match whose rematch window is over (more than `REMATCH_OFFER_TTL_MS` since the actor's `finished_at`) leaves memory, whoever is still connected: its seats' and its watchers' sockets still open are closed with `MATCH_OVER_CLOSE_CODE` (4411, "the match is over"), and the actor goes in the same pass, the check and the removal under the registry's lock as before. The co-op sweep does the same, timed from the co-op actor's `finished_at`, the end [[R1398000]] counts its window from; a finished co-op game nobody is on still goes at once, and inside its window it goes as a restart's does, with 1001, so a socket that attached as it went reconnects and folds it back ([[R1398000]]). As [[R672]]'s reaper already counted it, an actor rebuilt on a finished match counts its window from the rebuild.

No client reconnects on 4411, as on [[R679]]'s 4410 and unlike the 1001 a restart closes with ([[R1115000]]): both the 1v1 and the co-op socket go to `closed` with no error and keep the last view. On `/match/<id>` a finished match whose socket closed for good keeps its result panel, shows no error line and no "no longer available" notice (a voided result still does), offers no rematch buttons, and reads the rematch status once, a rematch made in the window's last moment being found that way, instead of polling it ([[R3213000]]'s poll is what a dropped socket falls back to). The co-op Play again poll is unchanged: it never had a pushed status to fall back from. The code is the server's, exported to the client through `serverConfig.ts` (CLAUDE.md rule 9).

**Hearthstone semantics.** The client leaves the game server once the end screen's choices have lapsed: the result stays on screen, and nothing on it can still be answered.

**Rejected.** 1001, which both clients reconnect on, into [[R1285000]]'s refusal, again and again; restating 4411 in the client as 4410 is; keeping a finished match's watchers past the window (they are let go in the same pass); counting a rebuilt actor's window from the row's `finished_at`; keeping the 1v1 rematch-status poll after the close, about thirty reads a minute from a visible tab with nothing left to offer; a "no longer available" notice on a finished, non-voided result. Also rejected: checking the window under the actor's own lock together with the offer path, as the issue suggested. A claimed rematch creates its game after a store write, so no lock spans the claim and the push to the seats. Instead the claim follows its clock read with no await between, `RematchBook::sweep` keeps the book's entry for `REMATCH_OFFER_TTL_MS` after its latest offer, and the client's one read after the close finds a game claimed in the window's last moment.
