Every card of the Infinite Assault ([[§8.13]]) is an AI generated card, so each carries the `AI` tag the AI generated cards carry ([[R1421]]): a card that counts or names AI generated cards counts it once a pool may reach the set ([[R4577000]]), and, as for every AI card, [[R451]]'s last-face-up rule passes it by (`LAST_FACE_UP_SKIPPED_TAGS`). A token keeps `Token` beside it and takes its card's doctrine; the Assault Core (IA #1 to #24 and their tokens) carries no other tag.

The set's later parts need nine more tags, declared now so they share one wire, as [[R3535002]] declared the Stinkyscape+ set's: the eight doctrines, Hallucination, Overclock, Batch, Recursion, Jailbreak, Firewall, Scraper and Singularity, and Commander, the set's boss cards'. They read and write their printed names, follow Paperclip in the `Tag` union and come before Token, are admitted by the cards crate's `ALLOWED_TAGS` and the Chinese terms, and count for nothing in the tag census, since no card of a set that ships carries them. The server's `cards.tags` check admits them from migration 0099, though `seed-catalog` writes no card of a set that does not ship, so the check names the whole `Tag` union whichever set is seeded first. No rule reads them.
