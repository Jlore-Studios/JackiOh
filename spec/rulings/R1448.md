While the server's `LADDER_BOTS` is `on` ([[R1452]]), a Best of 1 or All Random queue ticket that has waited past its own fallback time pairs with a ladder bot. The queue's matches are ranked ([[R604]]), so this is a ranked ticket. Its fallback time is drawn uniformly from `BOT_FALLBACK_MIN_SECONDS` (15) to `BOT_FALLBACK_MAX_SECONDS` (40) seconds from the ticket's id and a key the server draws as it starts and sends to no one, so it is the same at every sweep. The key is what keeps the moment a bot comes from telling the player anything: the ticket's id reaches its player, and a time drawn from the id alone is one a client could work out, so a pairing that came at that moment would be a bot's. A server that restarts, or two that overlap during a deploy, may draw a waiting ticket's time again, still 15 to 40 seconds after the ticket joined the queue. Each sweep first pairs every two people it can inside their rating window ([[§9.5]]) and only then turns to the tickets left, oldest first, so a person inside the window always comes before a bot. A Conquest ticket never gets a bot.

**Which bot.** The ticket pairs with a bot that is in the pool (not retired, [[R1449]]), on its schedule at the hour (UTC), in no match, not one the player faced in their last `BOT_RECENT_OPPONENTS` (5) finished matches and, for Best of 1, holding decks dealt for the catalog version the server plays ([[R1449]]); of those, the one closest in rating to the ticket, ties broken by id. A player rated above every bot in the pool, free or not, is never given one. A ticket with no such bot waits on as before.

**The claim.** The ticket and the bot are claimed together in one statement, single-shot, as a pair of tickets is ([[§9.5]]). A claim that loses (the ticket has left, or the bot was taken first) seats nothing, and the sweep goes on to the next ticket. A pair of people's tickets whose claim lost one of them to a bot's claims neither, so the other stays queued, as it does when the pair loses one to another pair. The player is taken to the match as to any pairing.

**The match.** Its seed is the server's, as a pair's is. The player is always Player 1, who takes the first turn ([[§2.1]]), and the bot Player 2: between two people the older ticket is Player 1 ([[§9.5]]), and a ticket that waited past its fallback time is always older than the person who would have come to pair with it, so a coin for the seats would put a lone player second half the time, which no pairing with a person does. In Best of 1 the player plays the deck frozen into their ticket and the bot one of its decks, picked by the seed, with that deck's portrait ([[R642]]). All Random deals both seats as it deals two people's, a deck and a portrait each from the seed and the seat ([[R258]], [[R642]]); the player's deck leans on the newest set when their ticket asked ([[R1372]]), and the bot's never does. The bot's deal is a person's in every other way, so [[R1390]]'s soft gate does not hold it back from a set as it holds back the decks the AI draws for itself: a gated deck would tell the bot's seat from a person's once a gated set ships. The match is ranked: the queue paired it ([[R604]]).
