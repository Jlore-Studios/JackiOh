Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #103 asked for the voice lines' cleanup and, from #146, for attack lines: until then a Unit's attack hook ([[R655]]) played only a sound effect, or nothing.

**Attack lines.** Every Unit, tokens included, has an attack line in `card-audio.json5` (175 of them): four words at most, in the voice of its play and death lines, flavour only, under the same character set and the same allowance for rules words as every other line (issue #115). It is spoken where [[R655]] already plays the attack hook, as the Unit's controller picks it up to attack, by dragging it or by clicking it to choose a target, at the pick-up's priority ([[R204]]); a hook that had an effect keeps it, and the effect starts first. A file renders as `<defId>-attack.m4a`. The opponent hears nothing: a pick-up is the viewer's own.

**Cleanup.** Three lines that repeated another card's line word for word were rewritten: Dropshipping (C+ #23), Zephrys Zealotism (C+ #27) and Touched by KY's death line (M #79). Glitch keeps Glitch in the System's line on purpose, so its render is that file's.

**The budget.** The Meditative set's 396 lines and the 175 attack lines do not fit [[R501]]'s 6 MiB. `VOICE_BUDGET_BYTES` (`apps/web/src/audio/constants.ts`, and `gen-voice.mjs`'s `BUDGET_BYTES` beside it) is 12 MiB, still counted in 4 KiB blocks, and every line still stays within 4 s.

**Rendering.** A line still renders only on macOS (`say`) or Windows (SAPI and `ffmpeg`), as [[R501]] has it. The `voice` workflow (`.github/workflows/voice.yml`) runs that on GitHub's own runners, so nobody needs either machine. On a push whose head commit's message holds `[gen:voice]`, or a manual run on any branch but `main` and `production`:
1. a macOS runner renders the `say` lines and commits them back to the branch;
2. a Windows runner renders the `sapi` lines on top of that and commits them;
3. a Linux job runs `gen-voice --check` on the result.

A key whose voice a runner lacks fails that check by name.
