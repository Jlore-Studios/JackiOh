When a pick phase opens and a player has one deck left that has not won, it is picked for them; when both players are down to one, there is nothing to choose and the game starts at once, without a pick phase. A pick made this way is a pick like any other: sealed, shown to the other player only as "picked", and one for [[R333]]'s clock. It widens [[R259]]'s "the third game's picks are made for them"
