C #90 In Too Deep's quests were too easy to finish ([[R404]]), so each quest that counts asks for about a fifth more, and the last quest before reward M asks for half as much again. Both faces share the goals. Quest 9 has no number (a draw of yours takes the last card of your deck) and the rewards' numbers (heal 6, 3 damage, 2 cards, 3 Plague Counters, +3/+3, a discard of 2, a draw of 2, 100 mana) and the tree's paths are as they were. The quest texts the card prints in its quest line carry the new numbers, and the Chinese lines in the web's `zh-CN.json` with them.

| Quest | What counts | Through v0.5.11o | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | cards drawn ([[R541]]) | 2 | 3 |
| 2 | enemy permanents destroyed ([[R542]]) | 2 | 3 |
| 3 | permanents you control at once | 4 | 5 |
| 4 | unspent mana at the end of your turn | 3 | 4 |
| 5 | damage your cards deal to enemies ([[R542]]) | 15 | 18 |
| 6 | total Attack and total health of your Units, each, at once | 13 | 16 |
| 7 | unspent mana at the end of your turn | 5 | 6 |
| 8 | cards entering either exile | 3 | 4 |
| 9 | the draw of yours that takes your deck's last card | none | none |
| 10 | Units in your graveyard (reward M's quest, reached from quest 6 by reward I) | 6 | 9 |

- **The rule for the number.** Each goal is its old one times 1.2, rounded up, and at least one more: 2 → 3 (2.4), 3 → 4 (3.6), 4 → 5 (4.8), 5 → 6 (6.0), 13 → 16 (15.6) and 15 → 18 (18.0). Quest 10 is the exception the owner asked for, "clearly larger": 9, half again its 6, where the rule alone would give 8 (7.2 rounded up), one more Unit than the rule gives for a goal that small and not clearly more.
- **Each goal is met at its number and not one below.** A float quest completes at the end of a turn of its controller's with its mana unspent, `mana` or more ([[R404]]), so a turn that ends with one less completes nothing; the counting quests complete at the state check after the event that reaches the goal ([[R404]]), and a count one short asks again at the next event.
- **Quests 4 and 7 against the mana cap.** A player's own mana tops out at 4 (`MAX_MANA`, [[§2.3]]), so a float of 4 is a turn that spends nothing, and a float of 5 or 6 can only be reached with temporary mana, which adds to the current mana and can pass 4 (Mana Well, The Coin and others, [[§2.3]]). Quest 7 asked for temporary mana at 5 already and asks for a little more at 6; the issue's own table has it 5 → 6, and it stays a goal the game can meet.
- **The kept script.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under `script_v0_5_11o` ([[R767000]]), whose goals are those in the table's third column, and the older `script_v0_4_0f` and `script_v0_3_16` keep theirs, which differ from it in quests 3, 5 and 6 alone ([[R404]]).

Rejected: the plain rule for quest 10 (8), which is a fifth and not the clearly larger bump; doubling it (12), which would put reward M, the Indestructible aura, out of reach of most games that get there; scaling the rewards' numbers too, which the owner did not ask for; and a new number for quest 9, which has none.
