Adjacent is [[§3.1]]: the same row, lanes ±1, no wrap, never the target itself; an empty or dormant neighbour contributes nothing. The exile pairs `exileAdjacentTo` over enemy Units in the unit row and the backrow with a plain `exile` on the chosen Unit — adjacent first, while the target is still in its lane. Immune neighbours are passed by ([[R4453000]]). The Radiant face first Locks the exiled Units' zones; the lock persists after they leave.
