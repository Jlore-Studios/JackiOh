A card whose text computes a number from the board by more than a plain X — a Fibonacci of the times it has been played (#31, [[R429]]), a sum over missing health and exile (#70), twice the exile (#40 radiant) — left the player to do the arithmetic, and a client may not (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The engine does it: a card's script may declare `preview`, a pure read (no writes, no random draws) that returns labelled numbers for the card's situation now — the label the formula as the running face prints it, the value what it would come to if this card alone resolved now — built on the same function its own resolution uses so the two cannot disagree. Something else resolving first can still move the number (two #40s at one start of turn: the first exiles a card before the second counts), which a value asked of one card cannot foresee. `viewFor` carries the result as `preview` on a card view the viewer may read and nowhere else: the viewer's own hand, a unit on top of its pile and a face-up backrow card (either seat's, since those are public), and a face-down card only for its controller; never an opponent's hand card, a library card or a card dormant under a Stack. A hook reads only what the card's controller may read ([[§9.1]]), and each Core hook says what: #18 the active player's current mana, #31 the times it has been played, #38 its controller's plays this turn, #40 and #70 its controller's exile count and hero health, #91 its own Plague Counters — never a library's contents or order or a hidden hand. A client prints each value in braces after its label, "{7}", in hand and on the field; the collection prints none. Values the view already shows elsewhere are not repeated: #92's stats, #100's cost and #89's hand stats are on the face, and #24's and #74's X is chosen at play
