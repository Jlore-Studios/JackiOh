The issue asked that the other pulls layer on any version: Animated on any version whose art can
move, Signature on any, and a card's foil, finish and border as on a normal face, ruling out a
pairing that cannot work in the ruling and in the picker and the server. Part 1 has two versions,
the normal face and Full art, and both augments work on both, on every card but one kind:

- **Animated** moves the art's own layers on either face ([[R2715001]]), taking one of
  `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX`'s slots and holding still under reduced motion, with Animated foil
  off and on a lazy grid face; on Full art it also lays the band over the art and drifts it, as the
  animated full art did. A card with no motion of its own borrows one of the 21's that draws no gold
  particle, its particles placed by its own id (`artMotionFor`).
- **Signature** signs the card's name on either face ([[R2715002]]): over the art below its middle on
  Full art, as before, and inside the art window's lower part on the normal face, so it never covers
  the name ribbon, the type line or the rules text. It signs in the motion gate while Animated foil is
  on and stands whole otherwise.
- **Foil, finish and border** wear on either face as they do today ([[R1295000]], [[R2720000]]).

**Ruled out: Animated on a Legacy card.** A Training Grounds Legacy card ([[R2511000]]) wears its
own yard picture with its Core card's subject, and that picture never moves ([[R2724000]]), so the
Animated augment would print nothing there. Its offer's Rare and Epic Unit clause names the four it
would otherwise take in `except` (Legacy: Duplicating Felinors, Big Felinor, Prejudiced Postdoc and
Corpse Eater), so nothing drops, sells, picks or shows it on them ([[R2725001]]); the Full art version
and Signature are offered there as on any Rare or Epic Unit. A test draws every offered card's Full
art and Animated looks, base and Radiant, and holds each apart from its normal face and the Animated
one to a moving picture, so a card whose look cannot show fails it.

A later version that cannot take one lists the cards in its offer's `except`, or the augment it
rules out in its entry, and the picker and the server refuse it there. The face says what it wears: `data-version`, `data-augments` (space-joined) and, for the four
looks #2715 drew, the `data-full-art-tier` they printed as.

Amended by [[R2949000]] (#2949): Signature signs on no normal face and no version but Full art, and there only beside Animated, as the Signature look; the signature over the normal face's art window is gone. Animated, a foil, a finish and a border still wear on either face.

Amended by [[R2940002]] (#2940): Animated, a foil, a finish and a border layer on Alt art, the Sketch and Alt full art as on Full art, Signature on none of them by [[R2949000]]'s rule, and a worn border keeps its edge and bezel on a Sketch.
