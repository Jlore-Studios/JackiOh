C+ #42 asks two prompts: the three difficulties, each labelled with the reward rolled for it as the Spell resolves (one random entry of its list), then the chosen problem, its statement and four options in an rng-shuffled order, from a question bank that is data in the cards package, public like the catalog, with at least 30 problems per difficulty (the Easy ones generated from the rng). The engine checks the answer and the key never leaves it: the options go to the chooser, the key stays in the prompt's resume data, which the view never sends ([[§10.8]]), and the opponent sees only that a prompt is open, then the reward's ordinary events. A wrong answer gives nothing, the Spell still played. Statements are plain text with Unicode maths, so a client needs no maths renderer; the turn clock runs while a player works, as for any prompt ([[R79]]). "3 Coins" are three The Coin tokens ([[§7]]), and "fill your hand" adds random Books until the hand holds 10 ([[R4]])
