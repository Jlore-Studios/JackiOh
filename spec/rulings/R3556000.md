A face-up Field Spell printing Dormant enters Dormant as a Unit does ([[R1839000]]): any arrival from
off the field starts its countdown, tuned by its declared `dormant` ([[§5]], [[R386]]). It counts
down after the Units, at its controller's turn start, and while it sleeps its text and triggers are
off; no effect re-triggers its Cry, though the Cry of its own play runs. Both views show it with its
countdown. Rejected: Dormant for Units only, which would leave the Mindustrial reactors and
overdrives with no way to sleep.
