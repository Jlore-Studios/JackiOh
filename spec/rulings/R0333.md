Picking closes `SERIES_PICK_SECONDS` (60) after it opens: one deadline for both players, which a pick neither stops nor extends. A player who has not picked by then is given their first deck in trio order that has not won, and the game starts; if neither player has a pick in by then — a pick [[R332]] made counts — the series is abandoned, with no winner and no rating move ([[R262]]). It replaces [[R260]], which gave a late player their first deck not yet played
