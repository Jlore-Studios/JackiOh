[[§6.3]] Crush ([[R5048000]]) destroyed every Unit in its zone whatever ran it. A fusion takes its kept card's type ([[R77]]), so a Spell can hold S+ #121 Toxopid's Cry: the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 9498 played S+ #121 fused into S #126 Jlockeed's Permanent Procurement Lobbying, a Spell, and its crush destroyed an enemy S+ #111 Mace, which is Immune to Spells.

**A Spell's crush passes the immune Unit by.** A Spell passes an immune field Unit by, whatever its wording ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]), so `crush` asks `restrictions::unaffected_by` of each Unit in its zone, as the other walks of the field do, and so passes a Dormant card by too ([[R1839000]]), though the Cry never draws a zone holding one. The immune Unit stays, so its zone is not free and no Leg lands there, as with a Unit Indestructible kept ([[R5048000]]). The Cry's draw of zones is unchanged, and a Unit's crush, Toxopid's own, still destroys the immune Unit.

**Replays.** A replay in which a Spell's crush met an immune Unit plays differently and is refused as `rules_changed` rather than shown wrong ([[R767000]]). No card script is kept, since the change is in the engine's verb.

Rejected: leaving the immune Unit's zone out of the Cry's draw: the draw would then depend on what runs the Cry, unlike a zone an Indestructible Unit keeps, which is drawn, and `crush` would still reach the Unit for any other caller; and an allowance in the fuzz monitor's I21, since the engine broke [[§6.1]].
