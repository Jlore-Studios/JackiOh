Issue #4154 rewrote S+ #4 Profe Yates's Cry as "Exile a permanent with M, O, U, S and E in its name and text (CN cards are immune). Then summon two random Traps." (Radiant: "three random Radiant Traps") and took out its three Activates. The Cry still declares one permanent of either side, a Unit or a backrow card, as [[R3996000]] has it ([[R81]]), through a target check: the permanent qualifies when its name and the text of the face it wears hold each of the letters M, O, U, S and E at least once, in either case, the two read together ("Mana Well" and "Start of turn: Gain 1 mana." hold all five between them). The text is the face's printed words with its declared numbers filled in at their printed values, so a `{key}`'s name counts for nothing, and a fused card is read by its fused definition's name and text. A CN card is immune whatever its letters, read through `tags_of` as [[R3567000]] read it, so a card in Chinese is one too. A face-down card is never offered, on either side and to its own controller neither: its name and text are hidden ([[R33]]), and no hidden card may decide what a play may target. While a qualifying permanent stands the play names one, as Hearthstone asks a targeted Battlecry for a target whenever one exists, even when the only one is Yates's controller's own; with none the play names none and the Cry summons its Traps all the same ([[R90]]). Yates has no Activate on either face, and the `damage` its Wolf's Maw read goes with it. Through v0.6.6e the Cry exiled any declared permanent and Yates had Overwhelm, Wolf's Maw and Iron Mandate (`script_v0_6_6e`); those games fold under the kept script ([[R767000]]). Rejected: reading only the name, which the issue's "name & card text" rules out; letting a face-down card qualify by its hidden words, which would let the play's legality tell the other seat what the card is; and exiling every qualifying card, as [[R3567000]]'s named exile did, where the issue keeps "a permanent".
