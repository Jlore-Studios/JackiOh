[[R195]] left four kinds of condition dark: a scaling amount, a condition another card grants, a trap that waits for an event and a cost reduction, so ten Core cards never glowed. Each now glows where the board can say it, read by the same function the card's resolution uses, and only ever for its controller ([[R195]]'s rule 2). **Granted** (the engine's, in `condition.ts`, since the card that takes the branch is not the card that prints it): a card in the viewer's hand glows while a play of it would take a Combo branch another card gives "your cards" (#38 Quickstriker's flag on one of their permanents, a `quickstrikerDamage` rider, #78 Radiant's `comboDraw` rider) and they have played a card this turn, or while #64 Gifted Program would make it Radiant at the cost a play of it pays now ([[R56]], [[R213]]); a card already Radiant does not. The granting cards themselves print no condition of their own and do not glow, and #78's base face grants only a discount, which is not a condition. **Scaling** (#70, in hand): either term adds damage, so the amount is above the face's base. **Traps** (on the field, either turn): the half of the trigger the board decides holds now: #18 the active player holds unspent mana; #60 its controller has an open unit zone ([[R430]]); #85 its controller has a permanent other than the trap that is not Immutable ([[R23]]), somewhere a Fuse could land; #96 an enemy unit acting on the field would be lethal attacking its controller's hero now, by [[R44]]'s projection, whether or not it can be declared this moment. #41 Sheepish waits on nothing the board holds (any Unit the opponent plays, an Immutable one included, [[R17]]), and the one useful fact, whether the opponent holds a Unit, is their hidden hand, so it never glows. **Cost** (#100, in hand): the cost a play of it pays now is no more than its controller's mana, the moment the count has done its work; whether a zone is free is the green glow's
