Three Jlockeed Units print "Embiggened (N): Starship Piece" (S #127 Siege Tank at (4), S #128 Yamato Cannon at (3)) and "Embiggened (2): Starship Piece and Equip" (S #129 Missile Pod), the designer's "Embiggen: gain Starship Piece" (ST-S19-3).

- **Lent by the text.** The Embiggened version has the keyword for good, from the moment its play is stamped (`instance.embiggened`, [[R81]]), as Core #56 Jilliax's Equip comes with its price ([[R831013]]): the card's `conditionalKeywords` returns it on a card stamped embiggened. The normal version never has it. So the Embiggened version dies as a piece and fuses a copy into its owner's Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]); the copy is a fresh card with no stamp, and the Starship is never a piece ([[R1852001]]). Missile Pod's Equip is lent the same way, so it is a play option only at (2), as Jilliax's is at (3).
- **Through a Fuse.** [[R1852001]] keeps the keyword only from the card a Fuse keeps. A fused card's texts lend no Starship Piece, since the fused card runs every ingredient's text and an ingredient's lend would read the stamp of the card it was fused onto: an Embiggened Missile Pod equipped onto a host played at its own Embiggened price would make that host a piece. Instead, a kept card that lent itself the keyword as it was fused keeps it as a grant, so an Embiggened Siege Tank that a Hull Booster is equipped onto stays a piece, as [[R1852001]] keeps a Unit equipped onto a piece a piece. Equip is lent through a Fuse as before.
- **The rest of the text.** Siege Tank's "End of turn: This attacks a random enemy" is a forced attack ([[R53]]) on an enemy it may attack under [[§4.2]] step 2, drawn from the match rng, Taunt not binding; Yamato Cannon's Ranged and Cleave are printed keywords ([[R783000]], [[R63]]).
