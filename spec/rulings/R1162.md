Meditative #87 Tatches the Totem (MD-E11) waits in its owner's deck: after its owner plays or
casts ([[R70]]) a card with a tribal tag ([[R1424]]'s `TRIBAL_TAGS`) and the play resolves, it is
summoned into the leftmost open unit zone with no Cry ([[R97]]), the first either player sees of
it. With no open zone it stays in the deck. The opponent's plays do nothing, and a face-down play
is a Trap with no tags until revealed ([[R448]]), so a face-down tribal Trap does nothing. Its
tags list all five tribes, so any tag filter for one of them sees it; the frame prints "All
Tribes" in their place, read off the tags by the client. At its owner's end of turn
([[R62]]) one random card of the hand and deck together takes one Buff ([[R440]]); the Radiant
face then Buffs each Unit adjacent to it.
