Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: C #28 Second Wind's Aura gains "You have +{mana} mana per turn.", `mana` 1 (Radiant 2), a declared number ([[R386]]). It is read as "You have N less mana next turn" turned around and made standing, Hearthstone's Overload in reverse: while the card acts face-up on its controller's field, a Unit a carrier holds included ([[R446]], [[R1824000]]), each of their refreshes gives N more mana on top of what it gives, as the next-turn rider does ([[§2.3]]): temporary mana past max mana, as #6 Mana Well's gain is, never a raise of max mana itself, which its formula leaves as it was. It is read as the turn begins, before the refresh, off the field as it stands then, as MD-B2's aura is ([[R941]]) (`StaticFlags.mana_per_turn`, `mana::mana_per_turn_of`, `mana::refresh_mana_with_bonus`); several add; it counts with the rider, so with the rider alone under no natural mana ([[R941]]) and against a rider below 0 (#21 Hinder), and a lost refresh gives none of it ([[R844]]); a credit line's instalment comes off afterwards as before ([[R1224]]). It is no Start of turn effect, so nothing that multiplies those ([[R821]]) reaches it. Rejected: raising max mana by N while the card stands, which would move every "max mana" read and the mana a refresh after its loss would take away, where the card's words speak of what a turn gives. Through v0.6.9n it gave none, which `script_v0_6_9n` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
