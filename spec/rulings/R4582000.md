An Infinite Assault level's fight is a practice game ([[R187]], [[§9.9]]) started from the Assault's
setup: its seed, the player's deck and handicap at the human's seat, and the enemy's deck, handicap
and deck marks at the AI's, whatever the start config's own seed, difficulty and deck say, as a
dungeon fight is ([[R2706000]]). The human sits at p1 and the AI plays the enemy at p2, paced as
practice paces it. It is a game of its own kind: it brings no last board and leaves none
([[R508]]), and the page's memory of the free game stays as it was. It counts in the attempt's own
record, not the device's ([[R639]]), and earns nothing. The board draws no Offer draw, since the AI
declines every draw offer ([[R188]]), and shows the lethal hint as in every game against the AI
([[R2102000]]).

Once the fight is over, and only then, the worker answers `assaultReport`: the Assault's fold of
the machine's setup and log (`assaultFightReport`) for the seat the human ended in ([[R677]]), the
attempt's `fightOver` or its refusal, never the log itself (rule 7). The result panel's Continue
sends that action to the attempt once, and then no more.

Rejected: reporting automatically on arrival, which cuts the board's end for a device-only record;
a report folded on the page, which would hand the page the log's hidden cards. This follows
Hearthstone semantics: a Dungeon Run fight is played out on its board, and only its outcome is
written down.
