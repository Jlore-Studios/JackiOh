A dungeon run's early enemies must be weaker than Easy (issue #3581), and a handicap ([[R180]])
could lower a seat only through its mana cap ([[R181]]), its deck size ([[R184]]) and its hero's
health ([[R290]]). So a handicap takes two more optional fields, each a way the run can thin a
seat's cards.

`fewerOpeningCards` takes cards off the seat's opening draw: the draw becomes the seat's entry in
[[§2.1]]'s table plus `extraOpeningCards` ([[R182]]) minus this field, never below
`MIN_OPENING_HAND` (1), since Hearthstone never deals an empty opening hand and a seat with none
would have nothing to mulligan. Quickdraw cards replace draws out of that smaller total, and the
mulligan offers, returns and redraws that hand as [[§2.1]] says ([[R9]]). The Coin is dealt as ever
([[R244]]).

`drawEvery` n lets the seat take its start-of-turn draws ([[R183]]'s `DRAWS_PER_TURN` plus
`extraDrawsPerTurn`, [[§2.4]]) only on its own turns whose count, `turnsStarted`, extra turns
included ([[R845]]), is a multiple of n: with 2 its 2nd, 4th and 6th turns, with 3 its 3rd and 6th,
and with 1 every turn, as before. The skipped turns come first, so the seat is weaker from its
opening; Hearthstone has no such rule. On a skipped turn the seat draws nothing as the turn begins:
no card, no fatigue step on an empty library ([[R3]]), no cast on draw ([[R58]]), and nothing for a
draw count or limit to see ([[R457]]). A card's or an effect's own "draw" draws on any turn.

`validate_handicap` refuses a negative `fewerOpeningCards` and a `drawEvery` below 1, naming the
seat, the field and this ruling. An absent field, a `fewerOpeningCards` of 0 and a `drawEvery` of 1
change nothing, and, like `heroArmor` ([[R2922000]]), each is serialized only when set and stored
only when it changes the seat's draws, so no other game's state, hash, replay or golden trace
moves. A Glitch reset deals its new game under the stored handicap ([[R676]]), so the opening hand
is thinned again and the turn count starts over. No practice tier, lesson, Duos seat or ladder bot
sets either field; only the dungeon run will (issue #3581).
