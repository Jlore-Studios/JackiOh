title: Playing from the graveyard

While a card on its controller's side grants it (every type, or Units only; an optional least price as it would be paid; or paid with that card's Plague Counters), `legalActions` offers a play of a card in the player's own graveyard, taken out at step 4, with choices, counting, Cry and [[R65]]'s prices as from hand; such a card is priced as a play wherever it is read. A Plague payment spends at least MIN_PLAGUE_PAYMENT counter, at most the counters there and the price, the rest in mana; `costPaid` is the whole price; a hand play spends none. A card gone from the graveyard by step 4 is not played ([[R226]])
