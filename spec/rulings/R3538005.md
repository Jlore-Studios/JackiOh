xxFreshJack's BUSTED pool is S tier of record (R3538001), current or past, of every set: `{ tier:
"S", everySet: true, past: "also" }`. No hand-kept list needs upkeep each patch: the pool ranks
every card of every shipped version by its winrate of record, past patches included, and a shipped
version keeps its own list, so replays fold under the list of their version. This amends R1846000
and R1846001 for #109 alone; every other reader keeps the code-line lists. The script through
v0.5.13g is kept, so older folds stay on their code-line lists (R2173000); the release re-tags its
`KEPT_SCRIPTS` row to the newest version shipped before it. Rejected: a separate bar of its own,
which would drift from what `/stats` shows, and a per-version file, since the inputs never change.
