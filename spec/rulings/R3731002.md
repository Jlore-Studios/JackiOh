In a 2v2 ([[R3731000]]) team B's two seats are people's, and every rule of [[§13]] that names the AI team for team B reads "team B, the AI team or two people" (`DuosTeamB`, kept in the match's options as `teamB: "people"`, absent for the AI team, so every co-op match, its rebuild and its replay read as before).

- **Rules.** Team B plays `DUOS_HUMAN_TEAM` with no handicap, as team A does ([[§13.4]]), and brings decks picked as team A's are: a saved deck or one dealt as All Random deals one, so [[R433]]'s dealt seats include its random ones ([[§13.2]]). A 2v2 has no AI team and no difficulty; its match row stores `easy`, which nothing reads.
- **Clocks and the AI.** Each of team B's people is a person to the clocks as team A's are: the round clock runs in every round, a prompt held outside its own round has its prompt clock, the mulligan clock keeps a hand still owing, and each has a disconnect grace from the start ([[R744]], [[§13.3]]). The AI plays a team B person's seat only past its grace, until the person is back, or for a clock that ran out, as it plays team A's ([[§13.8]]); it never decides for a team B person who is here. A team never loses to a disconnect.
- **Views.** Each of the four is sent `view_for` of their own seat and nothing else (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a team B person sees their own duel as its Player 2 and their teammate's, and the other team's hands and hidden zones stay hidden as an AI seat's opponent's do ([[§13.6]]). The clock frame's `graceDeadlines` carries `b1` and `b2` while a team B person's grace runs, and leaves them out otherwise.
- **Pings, concede and watching.** Pings reach the sender's teammate alone, never the other team ([[§13.10]]). Each team's concede takes both of its people ([[§13.8]]), as team A's does. A friend may watch any of the four from their seat, `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` behind, and a block with that person's teammate refuses it as it does for team A ([[R2881000]]); the presence that offers Watch hides a 2v2 standing blocked with any of its four people.

Rejected: a team of one person and the AI (out of scope but for the disconnect fallback); a round clock only in team A's rounds, which would let team B's people stall the game.

Amends [[R2881000]], whose watched seats are any person's.
