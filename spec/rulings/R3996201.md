Issue #3996 asked to slightly nerf C+ #40 Appropriations by about 15%. Its numbers are the `BASE` and `RADIANT` `Face` constants of the card's file, each `aX − b`, and X = 4 is where [[R3135002]] measured its own cut. Each number but the Armor is cut to the whole-number formula nearest 15% at X = 4 that never gives more than before at any X:

| Option | Base through v0.6.5b | Base now | Radiant through v0.6.5b | Radiant now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Military: +Attack and Rush | 2X−2 | 2X−3 | 4X−3 | 4X−5 |
| Education: Radiant Books shuffled in | 2X−2 | 2X−3 | 4X−1 | 4X−3 |
| Culture: % chance to become Radiant | 6X | 5X | 16X | 14X |
| Healthcare: +Health | 2X−2 | 2X−3 | 5X−1 | 4X |
| Healthcare: Armor | X | X | 2X | 2X |

At X = 4 that is 6 → 5, 6 → 5, 24% → 20% and 6 → 5 on the base face (17% each), and 13 → 11, 15 → 13, 64% → 56% and 19 → 16 on the Radiant (15%, 13%, 12.5% and 16%). A smaller slope (3X−1 for Military, 3X for Education) would cut more at a larger X but give more at X = 1, so only the Radiant Health, whose 4X gives no more at X = 1 and cuts 14% to 17% from X = 3 to X = 6, takes one. The Armor is kept, as [[R3135002]] kept it: the Health's cut is the option's.

- **The floor.** [[R3135002]]'s floor stands: a number below 1 reads 1, so at X = 1 every base number but Culture and Armor reads 1 as before, and the Radiant Military reads 1; the Radiant Education reads 1 there, where it read 3.
- **Culture** still rolls each card on its own ([[R581]]); a chance of 100% or more, turning every card, now comes at X = 20 on the base face and X = 8 on the Radiant, where it came at X = 17 and X = 7.
- **The client** restates the option texts the Discover shows (`modeText.ts`, their Chinese in the web's `zh-CN.json`), and the card's words ("Choose one: Military, Education, Culture, or Healthcare.") do not change.
- **Replays.** The script through v0.6.5b is kept as `script_v0_6_5b`, with the older `script_v0_5_11o`, `script_v0_4_0f` and `script_v0_3_7b` ([[R767000]]).

It edits §8.7 row 40.

Since issue #4154 each option is cut about 15% again, with floor functions ([[R4154105]]); the numbers above are those of v0.6.6g and before (`script_v0_6_6g`).
