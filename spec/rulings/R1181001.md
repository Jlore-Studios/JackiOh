Meditative+ #4 Supply Drop's modes are declared with the play ([[R81]]); the one chosen is public, as Hearthstone shows a Choose One card's choice, and its countdown badge names it ([[R1181000]]). Its numbers are fixed as the Spell resolves, the Embiggened version's where it was played Embiggened, and the delayed effect carries them. Combat ("deal 4 damage to each enemy, with Pierce and Lifesteal") is one hit on every enemy Unit and then on the enemy hero, each ignoring Armor ([[R346]]) and each healing the caster's hero by the damage it dealt, so a hit on three enemies heals three times, all before one state check ([[R59]]). Each hit's source is Supply Drop wherever it is now, still a Spell, so its owner's Spell Damage raises each hit as it lands ([[R1180000]]). Supplies adds random non-token Jlockeed cards of the sets that ship ([[R380]]), never Supply Drop itself ([[R387]]), each Radiant and costing the (2) the card prints, (0) on its Radiant face (a `costOverride`); a full hand burns the rest ([[§2.4]]). The Radiant face's Embiggened Supplies ("fill your hand with them") adds as many as the hand has room for when the effect resolves, at the start of that turn and before its draw, so the draw then burns; the base face's Embiggened count is a number the Radiant face does not print ([[R1431]]).
