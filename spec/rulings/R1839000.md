The designer asked for Hearthstone's Dormant for the Stinkyscape set, ahead of its cards: a Dormant card is on the field and can't attack, be attacked, be targeted, or be reached by any effect (destroy, exile, damage, bounce, transform, steal, Allure, Freeze, buffs) until it awakens, and its auras and triggers are off. Where those words stop this follows Hearthstone: it fills its zone, and it counts for board space but not as a Unit. No shipped card is Dormant yet; the set's two Imprisoned Units and its Dormant Starship are the first.

**A status, not a keyword kind.** Dormant ([[§6.1]]) is `CardInstance.slumber`, absent on every other card, so a game with no Dormant card serializes, hashes and replays exactly as before and no golden trace moves. A Unit that prints it (`StaticFlags.slumber`, its `turns` the "Dormant for N turns" countdown, [[R1839001]]) enters the field Dormant as it arrives from off the field, any way it arrives: played, summoned, put there by a setup. A move from one field zone to another is no arrival, and a card set face-down has no text in force ([[R1487000]]), so neither starts it. It is no keyword kind: [[R21]]'s pool, a Buff, a Nerf and the AI's keyword features never see it. It goes with [[R78]]'s reset as the card leaves the field, so a card that prints it is Dormant again the next time it enters. The engine says slumber and awaken for it (`slumber.rs`), since dormant already names a card beneath a Stack ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]).

**It fills its zone.** A play, a summon and "fill your board" read a zone's room off the zone itself, so a Dormant card's zone is never offered and never filled while it lies there. A Stack card may top it, as it may top any card ([[R1839003]]).

**It is no Unit.** `active_units_of` leaves it out, so every rule that reads a player's Units does too: "count your Units", a Tribute, a random pick of a Unit, the attack and Taunt reads, the triggers' and the replacements' holders, the AI's evaluation and its lethal solver. `units_on_field_of` reads the same walk with it kept, for the one rule that needs it there, its countdown.

**It does not act.** `zones::acts_on_field` answers false for it, and `combat::has_exertion_in` too, so its controller declares no attack with it and makes no switch, and no attack is declared on it, declared or forced; `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal ("that card is Dormant", the state unchanged) and the view's `canAct` agree, and its Taunt binds nobody. The one ability it keeps is the Activate its text names for its awakening ([[R1839002]]).

**No effect reaches it.** `restrictions::unaffected_by` passes it by for every source, so a chosen card, a card named by id, "this" and every scope and neighbour walk skip it, and every verb written in that vocabulary leaves it be; no declaration and no `target` prompt offers it, and a play naming it is refused. The walks that read the board straight (Allure's marks, a Fuse's hosts, a card scope, a Radiant pick, the cards beneath a Stack a Transform reaches) skip it as well. No aura reaches it.

**Its text is off.** Its auras, cost rules, permissions, replacements and every other lasting text are off (`preview::lasting_text_off`, the face-down gate [[R1487000]] made, widened to it), and it registers no trigger and no start- or end-of-turn hook. A Cry runs as the card is played, as Hearthstone's Magtheridon's Battlecry does, and, like any effect, passes the Dormant card itself by.

**Seen by both players.** `UnitView.slumber` is on the card for both seats, `{ turns }` with its countdown's turns left and `{}` for one that waits for an effect; the board draws a Dormant badge, a moon with the countdown beside it, from the view alone. The AI reads it through `legalActions` and `active_units_of`, so it never acts through a Dormant card and counts one as no threat until it wakes, and its search signature tells a position with one apart from one without.
