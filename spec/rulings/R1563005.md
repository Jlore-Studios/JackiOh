The designer asked (#1563) that the AI be "slightly slower at solving problems": right 85% of the time on KY's Competition's base face and 93% on its Radiant face. The practice and ladder AI still works each question out from its statement at once ([[R1184002]]), and then keeps that answer with `AI_KY_COMPETITION_ACCURACY` (0.85, base) or `AI_KY_COMPETITION_RADIANT_ACCURACY` (0.93, Radiant: the face the prompt's continuation names); otherwise it gives one of the three wrong lettered answers, uniformly. Both draws come from the AI's own stream (`AiOptions.rng`), never the match's, so the game the engine replays is the same whoever answered, and a decision still replays from its seed. It still never answers Stop and never reads the key ([[R185]], [[R465]]). A miss ends the sequence with the streak so far, as anyone's miss does ([[R1184000]]), so a base sequence runs about 5.7 questions on average and a Radiant one about 13. It edits [[§8.9]] and [[R1184002]].
