While a card acting on a player's side says "Redirect all damage you take on your turn to your opponent (up to 20)" (`staticFlags.ownTurnRedirect`, `{ cap, multiplier? }`), a hit on that player's hero on their own turn moves at [[§4.4]] step 0a, after Spell Damage and before Divine Shield, the Armor and the caps. As much of it as the card's cap has left this turn is taken off that hero and dealt to the opponent's hero as a new instance from the same source, through that hero's pipeline, times the card's multiplier; `redirected` reports it, its `byInstanceId` the card. What the cap does not take is the rest of the same hit and goes on through the player's own Armor and caps. The cap counts what is taken off its controller, per card and per turn (`memory.ownTurnRedirect = { turn, moved }`, cleared when the card leaves the field, [[R78]]): 15 and then 10 move 20 and leave 5. The Radiant face's "and double it (up to 40)" is a multiplier of 2 on the same cap of 20, so at most 40 is dealt a turn. Several such cards take a hit in [[R68]]'s order, each to its own cap, and each reads its declared numbers `cap` and `multiplier` where it declares them ([[R386]]). On the opponent's turn nothing moves. A Field Trap that "Reveals when you take damage on your turn" fires on the hit's `damage`, after the hit, so the hit that reveals it is taken and the ones after it move. Rejected: moving the hit after the Armor, which would spend the Armor on damage the player never takes; capping what is dealt rather than what is taken, which would halve the Radiant face; and one cap shared by several cards.
