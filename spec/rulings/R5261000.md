"Whenever your opponent draws a card, add a copy of it to your hand" answers every `drawn` of its controller's opponent, wherever the draw came from (a turn's draw, a card's draw, a named card drawn from their deck): a card cast on draw ([[R58]]) was drawn, and is copied. A card the hand cap burned ([[§2.4]]) is no card they hold, and leaves nothing to copy: at the copy it lies in a graveyard without casting on draw. A draw from an empty deck draws no card ([[R3]]) and copies nothing. The copy is a fresh card of the drawn card's definition and face with [[R57]]'s riders, its price set at the declared `setCost` ([[R65]]); its holder reads it, and the opponent's view tells them a card was added and never which ([[R466]]).
