Amends [[R2477000]] and [[R2565000]], which priced a card's start-of-turn and end-of-turn hooks by one firing. The engine runs a player's own start-of-turn and end-of-turn hooks 1 + N times, N the extra runs of the highest turn-hook multiplier acting on their side ([[R820]], [[R821]]; Meditative #9 Joint Filing gives 1, its Radiant face 2), and at the start of each turn it queues the opponent's start-of-the-opponent's-turn hooks after the active player's own ([[R68]]); Classic #62 Living Bomb's Radiant face has only that hook. The AI counted each hook once whatever multiplied it, and never fired the third, so it underrated a board with Joint Filing on it and read Radiant Living Bomb as a blank Field Spell of its cost (on `SHADOW_WATCH`, mean evaluate change -0.6). No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** The probes of [[R2477000]] and [[R2565000]] fire each hook of a card once, its start-of-the-opponent's-turn hook among them, queued as its controller's, on the same probe world and sandbox and within the same `AI_TURN_HOOK_PROBE_NODES`, one node a firing. Each is fired on the decision's state alone, so the order the hooks would meet in a game prices nothing. A card's price keeps two parts (`TurnHookPrice`): `price`, its start-of-turn and end-of-turn firings summed, and `opponent_turn`, its start-of-the-opponent's-turn firing. `turn_hook_price` counts `price` 1 + N times, N being `multipliers::extra_runs` for the card's controller on the state being evaluated, floored at 0 as the engine floors it, so a line that removes or plays Joint Filing moves the count and a multiplier on the other side multiplies nothing. It counts `opponent_turn` once, since no multiplier reaches the start of the opponent's turn ([[R821]]). A face-down card multiplies nothing, as in the engine, so no hidden card moves the count ([[R185]]). Core #91 Fed Fauci with one Plague Counter counts its crystal twice beside its controller's Joint Filing, three times beside a Radiant one and once beside the enemy's; Radiant Living Bomb counts what destroying the opponent's Plague-Countered permanents gives its controller, more for more of them and nothing without them, and Joint Filing does not multiply it. The weight `AI_EVAL.turn_hook` is unchanged, and at 0 nothing is counted.

**What is not priced.** A firing that would give something else the second time counts as the first, 1 + N times: Meditative #13 Gatling Pea's grown hit under Joint Filing counts as its first hit twice. What [[R2477000]] and [[R2565000]] leave unpriced stays so.

**Rejected.** Multiplying the whole price, as the issue first proposed, which would count Living Bomb's base face's start-of-the-opponent's-turn firing twice beside Joint Filing where the engine runs it once; firing each hook 1 + N times in the probe, which spends N nodes more and reads the multiplier of the root's board rather than of each line's; and a weight of its own for the start-of-the-opponent's-turn hook, for a price read the same way as the others.
