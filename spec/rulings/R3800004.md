Found by the daily fuzz (1v1 seed 9999 of shard 4, on `main`'s build as well). A Unit of P2's had S #112 Job Application's "Death: Resummon this at Stage 1" fused in. Each time it returned, P1's two Radiant S #23 Warp Disruptors answered the arrival ([[R1875003]]), and a Unit of P1's made a forced attack on it ([[R53]]). The Disruptors' aura gave that Unit First Strike, so the arrival died without striking back, returned, and was answered again. §10.3's loop did not settle in its pass cap.

REVIEW A3.6 asks every self-recursive effect for a terminator. [[R2197005]] and [[R2500000]] put one in the other card of such a loop, not in Job Application's Death. Here the other card is the Warp Disruptor, and the spec does not say how often it answers one card. So each Warp Disruptor, and each S #24 Warp Disruptor II, now answers a given card at most once a turn (`state.turn` counts both players' turns). It keeps the cards it has answered in its memory under the turn's number, so R78's reset and a new turn both clear the list. A card it answered and killed that returns the same turn, by its own Death or by Reborn, is not attacked again by that Disruptor; another Disruptor still answers it once, and every other arrival is answered as before ([[R1849007]]). Patch v0.6.2b's scripts, which [[R767000]] keeps for those versions' replays, answer every arrival, as they ran.

Rejected: ending the loop in Job Application's Death, which R2500000 rejects for R2197005's sake; a cap on a Disruptor's answers, a number the card does not print; and passing by only a card that summoned itself, which would read `summoned`'s `sourceId`, a field no rule reads (R1088).
