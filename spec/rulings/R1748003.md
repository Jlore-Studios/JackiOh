Issue #1748 audited every Spell and Field Spell, tokens included, for numbers a Nerf or a Buff could move. Eleven now declare a number they printed ([[R386]]): Core #21 Hinder's discard (1, less better, its base face only), #23 Reoccurring Dream's chance (30%, Radiant 40%) and #42 Eugenics' (30%, its base face only), each stepping 10 up to 100, #69 Call to Arms' recruits (3) and #80 Zao Gao's tokens (2); C #39 Outbreak's draws per counter (1); and the set costs of C+ #78 Claude's Datacenter, M #38 H1B Printer's Radiant face, M+ #3 Wondrous Wand and M+ #19 Grubby Gloves' Radiant face ((0), less better, never below (0)), with M #37 CN in a Bottle's raise ((1), its base face only) and its Radiant face's set cost.

Every number one still prints is fixed for a reason `crates/cards/tests/cross/params.rs` names card by card, and the test fails on a number no reason covers: a numbered keyword's count, which the X row tunes ([[R482]]); a price a filter, a threshold, a dearer play or a cap names; a table the engine holds; a term of a formula; what the card's text is built on; a count of declared targets; a number whose better way hangs on the target (M #36 CN Peptides' 50%, which [[R902]] reads as the Buffs on your Unit and the destroy on an enemy's); an AI generated card's ([[R482]]); and an Immutable card's.

Through v0.4.4b the eleven printed those numbers; each keeps that script as `script_v0_4_4b`, which `KEPT_SCRIPTS` folds those versions' replays under ([[R767000]]).
