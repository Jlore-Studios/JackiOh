"Whenever a unit is summoned gain +1 Armor" ([[§8.11]] TG #30 Floodgates) counts a Unit arriving on either side as [[R1875003]] reads an arrival: a summon (a Recruit, a token, a copy, Reborn) at once, a played Unit once it resolves, each once; a Unit standing in a backrow zone counts ([[R1081000]]), a Field Spell or a Trap placed does not, and Floodgates' own arrival is not one ([[R2197000]]'s reading). Hearthstone's "whenever a minion is summoned" counts plays. Several arrivals give one gain each. Each gain is a granted Armor of the printed number, which stacks with the printed Armor ([[§6.1]]) and is kept until it leaves the field. "Death: destroy all friendly units" destroys every Unit its controller has as it died ([[R89]]), in one sweep ([[§6.3]]); an Indestructible one stays ([[R46]]), the opponent's are untouched, and the destroyed Units' Deaths fire after it. Rejected: its own side's arrivals only, against "a unit".
