[[R732000]] and [[R1186000]] keep the AI's shadow ban ([[R186]], `SHADOW_BAN` in `crates/ai/src/shadow_ban.rs`) to the Meditative and Meditative+ sets, at most half of each. The Stinkyscape set joins them, held instead to `STINKYSCAPE_BAN_CEILING`, 35 of its 175 non-token cards (20%, `docs/stinkyscape-set.md` ST6): a sweep of record may ban up to 35 Stinkyscape cards, and `cargo jackioh sweep --write` refuses reports of record that ban more. The Meditative sets keep their half, each set held to its own ceiling, and no card of an earlier set is shadow-banned again.

So the sweep of record that follows the set's release ([[R1678000]]) writes its bans, as #1186's sweep wrote the Meditative+ set's rows: it sweeps the Stinkyscape cards alone, every set previewed as `fuzz` previews them ([[R1420]]), and a card takes the row of the latest report that lists it. Until a sweep bans at most 35, the set stays in [[R1390]]'s `AI_DECK_GATED_SETS`, so the AI deals itself none of it. Since the ban may now hold Stinkyscape cards, `sweep --due` also names the watched Stinkyscape cards when the AI's generation moves.
