While a card acting on the field has the Gauntlet (`staticFlags.gauntlet`, "The middle 3 unit lanes are trapped in a Gauntlet. No other unit can target them and they can only target each other"), the unit zones of lanes 2, 3 and 4 on both sides (`GAUNTLET_LANES`) are in it. At [[§4.2]] step 2 a Unit in it may attack only an enemy Unit in it, never a hero, and a Unit outside it, in lane 1 or 5, may attack none of the Units in it. It is one of step 2's restrictions, as Flying's is ([[R783002]]): forced attacks obey it too ([[R53]]), and a Taunt binds only the attackers that may attack it (step 3), so a Taunt inside binds only the attackers inside and one outside binds only those outside. `legalActions` and the reducer's refusal ("the Gauntlet's Units fight only each other") are one check ([[R43]]). "Target" is read as an attack's target: a Unit's Cry or text still targets a Unit in the Gauntlet, and Spells are untouched. Rejected: also barring a Unit's effect from targeting across it, which would split one Unit's targeting between its attacks and its text; a person may widen it before the card is built.
