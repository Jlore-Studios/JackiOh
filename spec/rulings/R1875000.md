"Reveals when you take damage on your turn" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #5 Masochism Unmasked) is the `damage` of a hit on its controller's hero while that player is the active player, an extra turn of theirs being their own turn too. It fires while it is face-down and only then: the reveal does nothing of its own, so a Field Trap that is face-up answers no later hit and emits no `trapFired` again ([[R33]]). The hit that reveals it is taken; the hits after it move as [[R1849004]] says, up to its declared `cap` a turn, the Radiant face dealing what moves twice over on the same cap. Rejected: a face-up Field Trap firing again on every hit, which would announce a reveal that changes nothing each time.
