Not a rule of the game (CLAUDE.md rule 7): how the client shows what the account owns and where
it spends a token.

- **Hold to preview.** Every account cosmetic tile (a playmat, a backdrop, a card back), every
  item of a card's cosmetics strip and every card of the owned-cards grid, locked or owned, opens
  a fullscreen preview when held: `LONG_PRESS_MS` past `LONG_PRESS_SLOP_PX` unmoved, by touch or
  by mouse, through the inspect layer's press handling, and the hold's click is swallowed
  (`CLICK_SUPPRESS_MS`). The inspect key on a focused item opens the same preview. A plain tap or
  click does what it did: it equips an owned tile, picks an owned strip item and opens a card's
  view.
- **The preview.** Fullscreen, modal and focus trapped: the item large, a playmat or backdrop as
  the board, a card back as the back, a card cosmetic as the card wearing it, with no pick
  written ([[R1834000]]), a card as its face; under it the name, the rarity and whether it is
  owned or locked. Escape, the Close button and a tap on the backdrop close it, and the focus
  goes back to the item that opened it.
- **The spend button lives only there.** No tile, strip, grid or shop list carries one. When the
  item is locked and a held token covers it, the preview offers the token: a cosmetic token where
  [[R1953001]] offered it ([[R1305003]], [[§14.5]]), a card token on a card the account does not
  own. The offer asks as [[R2480000]] does, "Unlock Bigot? This spends 1 card token.", with
  Confirm and Cancel, Cancel focused; Cancel and Escape spend nothing. Confirm sends the one
  redemption ([[R2124000]]), `POST /api/tokens/redeem-cosmetic` or `POST
  /api/tokens/redeem-card`, and renders the server's answer: on success the item reads owned in
  the preview and in the grid behind it, on a refusal the preview shows the server's words and
  nothing changes. The shop keeps its bars, the cycle's next step and the held counts, and links
  to the Collection; its card browser is gone.

This amends [[R1953001]] (*A cosmetic token is spent in place*: its fullscreen preview offers a
held token, and the shop's card token picker went with it) and [[R2480000]] (the question stands
in the item's fullscreen preview, a modal, so it is inside the modal).

Hearthstone semantics: a card is crafted from its large view in the collection.

Rejected: a button on every tile, which crowds a menu of cosmetics with spends; keeping the shop
browser, which gives one token two ways to be spent; and a new hold timer beside the inspect
layer's.
