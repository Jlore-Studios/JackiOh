Each AI bot keeps its own internally maintained Glicko-2 rating, starting at a new player's values (a ladder bot's rating starts at its strength's seed instead, beside a new player's deviation and volatility, [[R1454]]), so a rated game that seats a bot rates it by the same rule as a player and records its side with the `ai` pilot and its bot id. A ladder bot holds a season row as a player does, which its ranked games move by the same rules, and stands on the leaderboard at the rank it earned, but never in Jlorious, and the percentiles leave it out; a bot side whose id names no bot profile holds none ([[R640000]]). A ranked match may seat a ladder bot, which the server plays itself ([[R1452]]), seated by the empty-queue fallback ([[R1453]]); practice still runs in the player's browser and records no result ([[R187]]). A rated game against a bot counts like any other
