Each AI bot keeps its own internally maintained Glicko-2 rating, starting at a new player's values, so a rated game that seats a bot rates it by the same rule as a player and records its side with the `ai` pilot and its bot id. A bot has no ladder rank, no season row and no place on the player leaderboard. No live mode seats a bot today — practice runs in the player's browser and records no result ([[R187]]) — and a rated game against the AI, when a mode produces one, counts like any other
