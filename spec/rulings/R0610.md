Each AI bot keeps its own internally maintained Glicko-2 rating, starting at a new player's values (a ladder bot's rating starts at its strength's seed instead, beside a new player's deviation and volatility, [[R1449]]), so a rated game that seats a bot rates it by the same rule as a player and records its side with the `ai` pilot and its bot id. A bot has no ladder rank, no season row and no place on the player leaderboard; the match screen shows its seat the rank its rating calls for ([[R1447]]). A ranked match may seat a ladder bot, which the server plays itself ([[R1447]]), seated by the empty-queue fallback ([[R1448]]); practice still runs in the player's browser and records no result ([[R187]]). A rated game against a bot counts like any other
