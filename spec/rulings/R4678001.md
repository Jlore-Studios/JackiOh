**What it shows.** Running, the Experimental tab's first view ([[R4678000]]), is the Cards tab's table and grid over each card's live play version ([[R4675000]]), the version the newest shipped catalog holds, with its figures from `GET /api/stats/versions` ([[R4675001]]): one row per card, its games, Adjusted and tier summed over every shipped version since its play last changed, pooled across those patches. A card last changed two patches ago shows those two and the current one together, with more games than any one patch holds; a card unchanged since it was added shows every patch since. Every figure is the server's, read against the play versions' own field and band ([[R4675001]]); the client counts none (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The pooled figures read All: a face is out of scope (issue #4671).

**Columns.** Card (the cost badge in its rarity), Name, Tier and Adjusted, as the Created tables show them ([[R4083000]]); Win rate after Adjusted; Play rate and Games behind the Columns toggle; and Since, the version its live play version began at, then how many patches that reaches ("v0.6.10 across 3 patches", "across 1 patch"). Iteration, right of Since, prints the live play version's iteration ([[R5035000]]). Below [[R654]]'s floor a row reads "—" for its tier and Adjusted. With the ranges shown, the Adjusted range ([[R2895000]]) and the tier fit ([[R3184001]]) show under them, the fit measured against the play versions' band, not the Cards tab's.

**Patches.** A patch is the page's: a window every card shares, as the patch bar lists them ([[R1973002]]), a micro no step of its own and a patch no game was filed under before the next replaced it folded into the one before ([[R1973001]]). The server counts them: each row of `GET /api/stats/versions` also sends `patches`, the windows its versions reach, each once (`play_version_figures` reads the shipped history, `PlayVersionQuery::history`, through `patch_windows`). The client cannot: `GET /api/stats/cards`' `patches` lists the caller's patches alone ([[R4677000]]), past which an old play version often begins. The count is not limited, since a pooled view is no patch query ([[R4677000]]). A server deployed before the count shows the version alone.

**Filters, sort, view and ranges.** The Cards tab's, one state for the three tabs ([[R4083000]]):
- The search, the set, the rarity, the cost, the keyword and the match type. The match type is asked of the route (`?mode=`). The rest are the Cards tab's own reading: Running lists the cards the Cards tab lists under them, as `GET /api/stats/cards` answers its set, rarity and cost and the page its search and keyword, each on its live play version, so both tabs list the same cards and the client restates no filter of the server's. A card no shipped version holds yet has no live play version and no row.
- The sort select offers Name, Games, Tier, Adjusted, Win rate and Play rate; the direction button and every sortable header set the page's one sort, kept in the address ([[R3184002]]). [[R5033000]] adds Date. A sort Running lacks (Decisiveness) reads as Adjusted in its direction, as on the Tokens tab, and Tier sorts by tier fit within a tier.
- Table or Grid, Show 95% ranges and Plain text ([[R3887000]]) as on the Cards tab; the Face control is not shown ([[R4678000]]).

**The drill-down.** A row, Enter or Space on it, or a grid card opens the card's drill-down, as a deck row does. From this tab, which shows no patch and no face, it reads the newest patch on All, whatever the address keeps for the other tabs ([[R4673001]]).

**Loading.** The route is read when the tab opens and again for a new match type, behind the table's skeleton; it is open to everyone and sent no token. A failed read, the route's or the cards', offers Retry.

Rejected:
- Counting a play version's shipped versions as its patches: a micro is no patch on this page.
- Counting the patches on the client from `GET /api/stats/cards`' list: it lists the caller's alone.
- Filtering the set, rarity and cost on the client from the catalog, or with the route's own query: either reads the cards' filter a second way, and the client would restate the server's cost buckets (CLAUDE.md rule 9).
- A Since sort: the Cards tab has none, and its one state would gain a key the other tabs ignore (reversed by [[R5033000]]).

Hearthstone has no counterpart.

Amended by [[R5036000]] (issue #5036): Decisiveness shows right after Adjusted, in the table and the Grid, before [[R5037000]]'s Win rate, sortable and in the Sort select, and leaves the sorts this view reads as Adjusted, which leaves none: it offers every sort the Cards tab does.
