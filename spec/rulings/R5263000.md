[[R275]]'s stat half holds a Radiant Unit's attack and health to at least twice its base face's. The Felinors vs Humans list prints five Units below it: #10 Shadow Judge 4/3 → 6/4, #16.2 Travertine Golem 0/5 → 0/5, #54 Intrepid initiate 2/3 → 2/6, #74 Yile, Acceleration Supremist 2/6 → 2/6 and #107 KY, Himself 1/3 → 2/3. Each Radiant face's text carries the rest: Shadow Judge's Cry reaches the permanents beside its target, the Golem counts three times the Float, the initiate gains after every Spell, Yile's Cry and Death are Radiant, and KY's swapped cards cost less. The designer's numbers stand, as R275 lets them for a named card, and as Halo's do ([[R4285006]]).

The sixth is #112 Jilliax Deluxe, whose 0/1 body ([[R5262001]]) prints the same on both faces so it lives to its Cry: its Radiant face's power is its Radiant Modules', fused in by that Cry.

**The rule.** The six are entries of `STAT_EXCEPTIONS` in `crates/cards/tests/cross/radiant_standard.rs`, each with its reason, and each still prints a Radiant text that differs from its base face's ([[R276]]). Should the designer raise a Radiant face's stats to the standard, the catalog's numbers are the one change and its exception goes.
