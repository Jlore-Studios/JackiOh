While a Mayor Medinamogger acts on the field, for both players, every declared target of kind `target`, every `target` prompt and every attack's target is drawn at random from the legal ones (ME-RANDOMTARGETS, after Hearthstone's Mayor Noggenfogger). `legal_actions` offers each play, cast choice and activation once per set of its other choices, its targets left out, and each attack once per attacker naming `"random"`; the reducer draws each declared target at [[§10.5]] step 1, before anything is paid, so [[R450]]'s targeting point, targeting costs and C #33 Joro's "a friendly unit is targeted" replacements meet the drawn target. A `target` prompt is answered at once at random, for whoever it opens for, and a pick so answered meets no targeting point, as [[R452]]'s random picks do. An attack's target is drawn from those the attacker may legally attack ([[§4.2]] steps 2 and 3: Taunt, lane restrictions, Rush on its arrival turn); a named target is refused while a Mayor acts, and the sentinel with no Mayor is refused the other way round. The view flags random targets, so the client asks for none; every draw is the match rng's. "Targets" are a play's or activation's declared targets of kind `target` (a hand card included), every `target` prompt (Echo repeats and re-asked Cry targets included) and every attack's target; Discover, modes, hand picks, numbers, cells, zones and Tributes stay choices. A declaration takes as many picks as [[R452]]'s random cast takes for it, different cards ([[R60]]), and one with no legal pick plays and fizzles unless it requires one ([[R703]]). Once the Mayor leaves the field, every choice returns.
