Each seat is dealt a hand of `EMOTE_HAND_SIZE` (8) emotes each game, and may send those and no other ([[R1342]]). A hand is always `EMOTE_HAND_VOICE` (3) of the five voice lines and five of the nineteen emoji, each part a uniform draw without repeats, and it is listed in the pool's order whatever order the draw took. A fixed split rather than eight drawn from the whole pool: a uniform draw would leave about one hand in ten with no voice line at all, the one emote that speaks in the seat's own portrait's voice, and would change the menu's shape from game to game (anywhere from no line on its arc to five); three lines and five emoji keep the portrait's voice in every hand, give every line three games in five and every emoji about one in four, and keep one menu shape on every screen. The split does not look at the portrait, since every portrait has the same five lines.

The deal is `deal_emote_hand(seed, seat)` in the engine's `wire/emotes.rs`, a pure function of the match seed and the seat, drawn from the seat's own stream `"{seed}:emotes:{seat}"` through the engine's `Rng` (Fisher–Yates), as [[R642]] deals All Random's portraits from `"{seed}:portrait:{seat}"`. It never draws from the match rng: no state field, action, log row, replay hash, game record or golden trace sees it, and dealing a hand moves no cursor. Being a function of the seed, the hand is the same on a reconnect, after a server restart and in a replay. The server deals each account's hand by the seat it began the match in, so a Glitch's swap of seats ([[R677]]) leaves each player their own. Hotseat and practice deal both seats the same way from the game's own seed, through the engine's function compiled to WebAssembly (`dealEmoteHand`), so a seat holds the eight a match on that seed would deal it, and a resumed practice game the eight it began with. The two seats are dealt independently.
