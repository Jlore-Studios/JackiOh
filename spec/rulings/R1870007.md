Stinkyscape #154 Dice of Doom reads "Roll a 100-sided die. If it lands on 67, add every Terminus card to your hand. They cost (0). End of turn: Add this back to your hand. Embiggened (1): This also gains Lucky +1 for good"; its Radiant face rolls an 80-sided die twice, either 67 a hit. Lucky given to a card goes as a Spell reaches a graveyard ([[R1438]]), and the spec has no Lucky kept for good.

- **The roll.** A luck-based roll ([[§6.1]] Lucky) of 1 to 100 from the match rng, a hit on 67: the card's Lucky (printed plus kept) and its player's Luck roll it again, any 67 a hit ([[R1440]]). The Radiant face rolls two dice of 1 to 80, each lucky the same way, either a hit. Every die is rolled, hit or not.
- **Lucky for good.** An Embiggened play ([[R1178001]]) first gains Lucky +1 for good, `gain_keyword_for_good`: the keyword is written into the card's tuning, as an Upgrade's is ([[R386]]), so it is kept in its hand, its deck and its graveyard and through every reset, and each Embiggened play adds one more. The Lucky it gains counts toward that play's roll.
- **A hit** adds one new card of each of the seven Terminus cards, never more (S #4's pool, [[R1857002]]), at `costOverride` 0, a full hand burning the rest ([[§2.4]]).
- **Back to hand.** At its controller's end of turn, the card goes from their graveyard back to their hand, the move [[R746]] calls Return to hand, on the turn it was played ([[R62]], [[R155]]); a full hand burns it.
