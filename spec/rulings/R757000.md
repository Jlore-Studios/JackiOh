A person may hold an account from `MIN_ACCOUNT_AGE_YEARS` (13, the owner's to choose; 16 if players in the EU are expected) in `crates/server/src/config.rs`, exported to the client. Sign-up asks for the month and year of birth before the account is made: two selects whose first option, "Month" and "Year", is chosen until the player chooses, the years running from this one down to `BIRTH_YEAR_EARLIEST` (1900), and nothing on the screen names an age or says who may not join. The age is whole years in UTC, the birth month counted as not yet reached (born October 2013, 12 in October 2026 and 13 in November). An answer under the minimum makes no account and calls no provider: the form gives way to a neutral refusal ("We can't create an account for you.") with the way to Practice, which needs no account, and the device remembers it in `localStorage` (`jackioh.signup.refused`, holding no age and no date) so that going back and choosing another year shows the refusal again. The lock is never lifted on that device.

The client decides nothing (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A passing answer waits on the device with the address it was given for, as long as the device remembers that address, since the account cannot be told until it first signs in after confirming its email. A pending account that has not passed the check owes one (`ageCheckOwed` on `GET /api/auth/me`) and meets the consent screen at the gate, before the code screen: it posts the held answer without asking, or asks again on another device or after an OAuth sign-up, and a device that refused a sign-up before shows only the refusal and Sign out. `POST /api/account/consent` counts the age itself from the month and year sent, refuses a year outside `BIRTH_YEAR_EARLIEST` to now, a month outside 1–12 and a month to come, and keeps only that the check passed, when, and the minimum it was checked against (`age_checks`, migration 0034), never the birth month or year, and it never logs the request. An answer under the minimum records nothing, deletes the account exactly as "Delete my account" does, and answers 403 `under_age`; the client then signs the device out and locks it as the form does. Redemption refuses an account that owes the check ([[R757001]]). An account already active before 0034, and a player who only plays Practice, are not asked. An account later found to be under the minimum is deleted the same way, by the owner (`docs/compliance.md`).

Hearthstone's own sign-up asks the date of birth through Battle.net. Rejected: storing the birth month and year, which is more than the site needs; keeping the answer in the auth provider's user metadata, which the user can edit; a parental-consent route, which needs a verified parent and is left to a person.
