The content is read by name: `ASSAULT_SET` for the set, the file's doctrine ids for the tags, and
`ASSAULT_COMMANDER_TAG` for Commander cards, so the mode compiles before C1 lands them. A card's
doctrine is the first of the file's doctrines whose id one of its tags names; a token never
deals. A Commander ships only when every one of its cards is a Commander card here. The
placeholder deals legal decks from shipped cards instead: the stand-ins whose every card is
`deckable`, every other `deckable` card dealt the next group in turn, neutral then the doctrines
in file order, a stand-in's cards flagged with its doctrine.

A Commander level takes its Commander's doctrine (or none) and name; anywhere else the seed
draws a doctrine that is neither the previous level's nor the next level's Commander's, and the
enemy is one of its doctrine's names. The Commander's cards join the deck first, each off the
lowest slot holding one. `ASSAULT_DOCTRINE_SHARE` percent of the remaining slots read the
doctrine's bucket (any doctrine's when the doctrine is none), the rest the neutral cards, then
any doctrine; a slot whose bucket is exhausted falls back to the neutral cards, then any
doctrine, then a rarity lower. Radiant's stacks mark that many more cards Radiant and Buffed's
that many more with one Buff each, each with its own shuffle, so a card may be both
([[R2702000]]). Every deck holds no card twice and is its handicap's `deckSize`
([[R184]]), so `createGame` takes it as it takes every practice deck.
