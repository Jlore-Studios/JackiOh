S #130 Jlockeed's Starship Retrofitting Program reads "Add a random Jlockeed card to your hand. It gains Starship Piece." (its Radiant face adds "and costs (1) less"). No effect could name the card it had just added, so nothing could give it a keyword (ST-S19-5).

- **The rider.** `add_random_from_catalog` takes `keywords`: each card added is given them once it is in the hand, as [[R1438]] gives Lucky, so a card a full hand burns is given nothing ([[§2.4]]). Given in the hand, a keyword is the card's own from there on: [[R78]]'s reset takes a granted keyword off only as the card leaves the field, or reaches a graveyard or an exile pile from a hand, so it rides the card from the hand onto the field, where the layers read it ([[§10.4]]).
- **The piece.** Played as a Unit, the card is a Starship Piece, and its death fuses a copy into its owner's Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]); the copy, a fresh card, carries no granted keyword. A Spell or a Field Spell given it fuses nothing unless it dies, and a Spell that resolves never does.
- **The pool.** The non-token cards tagged Jlockeed ([[R278]]) but this one ([[R387]]), from the sets that ship ([[R380]]). Radiant: the card is Radiant and costs `discount` less, never below (0) ([[R65]]).
