The Radiant Mayor Medinamogger's Lucky 1 rolls each of its controller's random targets twice and keeps the better: first the side [[R656]]'s aim prefers, then for an attack a target ranked by stats alone — one whose strike back the attacker survives, then one it destroys. The opponent's random targets are rolled once, helped by no Lucky of the Mayor's controller.
