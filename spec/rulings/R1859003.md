Stinkyscape #131 Spacerace Stimulus: "Reveals when either player launches a Starship" (Radiant: "or plays a Starship Piece"). (ST-S02-3.) Set face-down, it answers `starshipLaunched` of either player's Starship and, on the Radiant face, `cardPlayed` of a card that is a Starship Piece as it is played (`is_starship_piece`: the keyword on a face-up card, printed, granted or lent), either player's, its own play left out. A card set face-down has no text in force ([[R1487000]]), so it is no piece played and its play reveals nothing: the reveal never turns on a hidden card. The reveal is the animation, nothing more ([[R383]], [[R64]]): a 6/6 Unit (12/12 Radiant) with Starship Piece, whose death fuses a copy into its owner's Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]); with no open unit zone it stays face-up in its backrow zone. As [[R831007]]'s Tesla, it remembers that it revealed and answers nothing after that, and it answers nothing but from its backrow zone, so its copy fused into a Starship never animates it again.
