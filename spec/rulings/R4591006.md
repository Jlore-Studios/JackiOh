"A Taunt Unit" (IA #161 Defense in Depth's "If you control a Taunt Unit") reads layered keywords ([[§10.4]]), so a Unit in Defense Position counts ([[§4.1]]), never only printed Taunt. IA #166 Aegis Prime's "your hero is immune to damage" is a hero guard with a cap of 0, judged at each hit ([[§4.4]] step 3, as Argusland's divisor is): on the base face only while another of the controller's Units has Taunt — never itself — on the Radiant face always.
