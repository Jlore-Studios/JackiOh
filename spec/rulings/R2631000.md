Nothing said in one place where JackiOh uses AI, which a launch checklist ("no AI policy", "no proof of AI") counts against an app. `/ai`, "How JackiOh uses AI", is a public page beside `/accessibility`, linked from the site footer ("AI use"), in the sitemap and quoted in `static-pages.ts`, and it says only what the repository does:

- **The computer opponents.** Practice's and the tutorial's, which run in the browser ([[§9.9]]); ranked's AI opponents, which the server plays and nothing marks, pointing at the Terms' "AI opponents" ([[§9.12]], [[R2614000]]); and the co-op team ([[§13.7]]). Each plays by the rules and reads only what its seat may ([[R185]]); they search the legal moves with a program tuned on self-play, are not chatbots, and say nothing but emotes.
- **Made with AI.** The music, composed as code by an AI model (`assets/music/LICENSES.md`, linked); the voice lines, operating-system text-to-speech (`assets/voice/SOURCES.md`); the card art, drawn by code, not by an image model; and the code, written with AI coding assistants.
- **What AI does with a player's data.** Nothing a player writes and no account details go to an AI service, and online games' records are used as the Privacy Policy says: the card statistics, the ranked AI opponents' pace and habits ([[R1442]]) and the decks they bring ([[R1454]]).

It is a statement, not a term: no part of the Terms or the Privacy Policy, so changing it moves no `TERMS_VERSION`, and it is in English with the "The English text governs." line for another language, as those are. It must stay true: a change to where AI is used changes the page (`docs/compliance.md` §9).
