Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView`, the log or a replay. Hearthstone semantics do not reach composited layers.

On phones the largest animations, a Mythic's takeover above all, made the browser reload the page: iOS WebKit, and Chrome on Android, kill a tab whose process passes its memory, with no error to catch. The effects canvas was capped at 2x density, but every DOM cue's root (`.fx-dom > *`) and every part it animates by transform or opacity is a GPU layer of its own, held at the device's full density, 9 times its pixels at 1x on a 3x iPhone. Headless Chromium measured a takeover through Ceaseless Void's seven-slice diorama ([[R3350000]]) at 21 screens' worth of layers, about 190 MB at 3x. The phone checks ([[R2803000]]'s five scenes) read frame time alone, and the spike lands within a frame or two of the mount.

**The stage merges on a phone.** A phone is a window whose narrower side is under `FX_MOBILE_WIDTH` (600 px), so a phone on its side is one too. There a takeover's stage draws at most `FX_PHONE_STAGE_LAYERS` (3) layers: its slices ([[R3350000]]) or depth planes ([[R3345000]]) run far to near in that many groups of adjacent ones, whose sizes differ by at most one and the larger nearest (7 slices run 2, 2 and 3; 8 run 2, 3 and 3; the four planes 1, 1 and 2). Each group is one element whose backgrounds are its pictures, the nearest on top, so no picture is lost. A merged slice stands at its group's farthest depth and plane, so the camera flies through three depths instead of seven. Merged planes keep their four places: the near place draws the nearest picture over its own, and the nearest place, left empty, mounts no element. Desktop keeps every slice and plane.

**The plane is the window.** `.fx-takeover-plane` carries no `will-change`, so its animation promotes it only while it runs, and no overscan (`inset: 0`, where it was -10%, 1.44 screens a plane): its dollies only grow it about its middle (1.04, 1.1, 1.25 and 1.6), and the far dolly's shift (1.04vw, 0.52vh) stays inside the 2vw and 2vh it grows either way, so no edge comes into the window.

**No element covers more than `FX_LAYER_MAX_SCREENS` (3) screens at its largest scale**, at 390x844. That is a little more than the square on the window's diagonal (2.63 screens), which a radial look turning about its middle needs. Each element that passed it is trimmed, its look kept:

- the takeover's rays, 92vmax across where they were 120vmax (110vmax at 1.2, 2.64 screens), their mask's stops again by the same ratio (x120/92), so the disc keeps its radius;
- the takeover's wave, 23vmax where it was 40vmax (115vmax at scale 5, 2.86 screens), its ring still reaching the window's corners as it fades;
- the void look's collapsing sweep, from scale 5 where it was 6 (2.86 screens);
- Ceaseless Void's fold edges ([[R3338000]]), from 1.7 times the window where they were 2.6 (2.89 screens);
- The Big Crunch's collapse ([[R3338000]]). Its ring was 160vmax with a 40vmax border outside it (240vmax) from scale 1.4 (336vmax). It is now 115.2vmax with its 19.2vmax border inside it, from scale 1 (2.87 screens) to 0.1667. It ends as the same donut (19.2vmax across, a 12.8vmax hole, a 16 px glow), and the dark comes in from the window's corners as it fades in;
- the light-ray fan (`.fx-rays`), at most 100vmax across (114vmax at 1.14, 2.81 screens), where a fan on the whole board was 2.8 times the board's longer side, near 12 screens on a phone; and the holy shaft at most the window's height;
- the Starship's cinematic camera ([[R3334001]]), which on a phone either way up draws no closer than `STARSHIP_CINEMATIC.phoneZoom` (1.15; the picture is a square as wide as the window's longer side, 2.86 screens), shortening its pan.

Every other DOM cue (every set piece shape, the Anomaly rift and title card, the Battleship's moments, every signature figure, every takeover look's art, [[R3400000]], and picture, [[R3407000]]) is within the cap at its largest case.

**Two checks keep it there.** Per pull request, `layerBudget.test.ts` mounts every DOM cue kind through `mountDomEffect` at its largest cases: the deepest diorama merged, every set piece shape with its most beats, every takeover look in each of its forms, every figure, each Starship size, and a fan, a sheen and a figure on the whole board. It estimates each promoted element from `fx.css` (`src/test/layerEstimate.ts`: its box from the cascade, at its largest keyframe scale) and fails on any element over `FX_LAYER_MAX_SCREENS` at 390x844. A fixture cue twice the window each way at three times its size proves it fails. Daily, `fx-phone-layers.cy.tsx` plays the phone scenes in a real browser at 390x844, with Chrome's device metrics at 3x: Twisting Nether, a Mythic takeover, a takeover through Ceaseless Void's diorama, Ceaseless Void's fold, the Starship launch, the Drone Battleship's arrival, the Anomaly cast and The Big Crunch. On every frame it reads each element and pseudo-element an animation runs on, and each with `will-change`, at its box times its scale that frame. Each must stay within the cap, and the diorama's stage within `FX_PHONE_STAGE_LAYERS` planes, so the estimate cannot drift from real layout.

This amends [[R2803000]], whose Rejected paragraph ruled out cutting layers: a phone's stage cuts layers, deliberately, for memory rather than frame time; [[R2803000]]'s five scenes still hold `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS`.

Rejected: exempting the cinematic or any one element from the cap, since an exemption is how the next big one would ship; keeping seven layers on a phone, whose stage alone held ten screens; merging on every viewport, when a desktop has the memory for every slice; and keeping the fan's gradients at their old size inside its capped box, which would show a hard edge in the window's corners where the fan faded softly.
