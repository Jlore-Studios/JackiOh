An ingredient that went into a fusion on its Radiant face gives that face, its text and its scripts to both fused forms ([[R469]]), and its declared numbers go with them ([[R386]]).

**Its texts read its numbers on that face.** Each text of the ingredient that runs in its place in the fusion (an Activate, a trigger, a Cry, a Death, an end or start of turn, a When launched) reads `param` on the Radiant face, whatever face the kept card shows. So does the text of an ingredient fused inside one that went in Radiant: the mark holds for everything below it. An ingredient that went in on no mark reads the face the fused card shows, as before.

**The card shows the numbers its texts use.** A fused card's own numbers are its ingredients', the first declaration of each key ([[R102]]), each now read on the face its ingredient went in on. They are what `CardView.params` shows and what a read that names no place in the fusion (an aura's, a condition's) reads, so the number on the card is the number its text uses. Every reader of the whole card's number reads it on that face too: a Tribute's worth, an Activate's mana price, the Armor a Hero gains off it, KY's Constant's list and the numbers it picks from, and a static flag a tune moves (`declared_or`).

**A Radiant Starship Piece.** A Radiant piece's copy fuses into its Starship on its Radiant face ([[R1852000]], [[R2521000]]): a Radiant Beam v3 in a base Starship deals 8 to its target and to each Unit beside it, and the Starship shows 8; a base one deals and shows 4. This replaces [[R1859001]]'s per-card read (`piece_number`, which five pieces called and the other eighteen did not), and every piece reads the same way through `param`.

**Unchanged.** A fused card keeps one tuning record, the kept card's ([[R102]]); the steps it records apply to each number on the face that number reads on. Which numbers a Nerf or a Buff may draw is still read off the kept card's face. Two ingredients that declare the same key still show one number, the first's.

Hearthstone semantics: a text keeps the numbers of the card that printed it wherever it goes, as a Golden Magnetic minion's doubled text stays doubled on the Mech it joins.

Rejected:
- Calling `piece_number` in the eighteen pieces that read `param`: it fixes today's pieces, but every new piece would have to remember a second way to read a number, which is how eighteen missed it.
- Keeping a read that names no place (an aura's) on the kept card's face: the card's view would then show one number and its aura use another.
- Moving the Nerf and Buff draws to each ingredient's face: it would change which numbers a random tune draws, and so the replays that drew one, for no number a player sees.
