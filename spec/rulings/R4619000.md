A replay is drawn in place over the screen it was opened from ([[§10.10]]): the Replays list
([[R768000]]), a finished practice, online, daily or co-op game's result, and Shared with me. It was
no layer of [[R2127000]], so the browser's Back and Android's back gesture went past it and off the
whole screen, losing the list, with the older games it had loaded, or the result panel, Rematch
included.

**Layer.** An open replay drawn in place is now a layer, while it loads and after it fails to load
too. Back calls the leave its corner arrow calls, so the first Back puts the list or the result back
at the same address, and a finished match's asks no leave question ([[R2479000]]). The next Back
leaves the screen, as it did. After the arrow closed it, its spent entry is stepped past
([[R2127000]]), so one Back then leaves.

**Order.** Each screen mounts the layer before the replay, so what the replay opens as it mounts or
later, a card's inspect sheet among them, is a layer above it: Back closes the sheet and the replay
stays, and the next Back closes the replay. The Share as GIF row under the replay's toolbar is part
of the replay and no layer, so with it open one Back closes the replay.

**Where it was opened.** A friend's replay opened by a `?replay=` link ([[R3729002]]) returns to
Shared with me on the first Back, as its arrow's replacing move does, and the next Back goes on to the
page the link was pressed on. On a finished practice game Watch the replay takes the place of its
result's layer ([[R3220000]]), and Back returns to the finished board.

**Rejected:** a layer inside `ReplayPlayer`, which `OpenedReplay`'s loading and failure screens and
the co-op player come before, so a replay could hold two layers and take two Backs; a layer for each
of `/replays`' 1v1 and co-op branches, where one above the module's wait covers both and the wait
itself; the Share as GIF row as a layer, where Share the last turn would take two Backs to leave; no
layer on a replay a link opened, where Back and its arrow would disagree.
