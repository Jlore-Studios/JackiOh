Each member of a private lobby ([[R3730000]]) picks their own deck, trio or random deck, `POST /api/lobbies/:code/pick`: a 1v1 lobby takes the queue's choice in the lobby's mode, and one in another mode is refused with the lobby's named ([[R264]]); a co-op lobby takes a co-op room's, a deck or a random deck. The pick is checked legal and frozen when it is made, as a queue ticket's is ([[R253]]), and the validator's refusal is the deck builder's `loadout_invalid`, keeping no pick.

`POST /api/lobbies/:code/ready` turns Ready on only over a pick, and off at any time. A new pick takes the member's own Ready off, as a party's Ready is a promise about the deck shown.

Rejected: a Ready with no pick that plays a default deck, which the host's Start would then seat without its player having chosen.
