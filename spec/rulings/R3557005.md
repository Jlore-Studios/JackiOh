S+ #158 reads "Cast on draw: cast a random spell (targets chosen randomly)". The Spell is one pick from the collectible Spells of the shipped sets (tokens out, [[R380]], [[R382]], [[R1420]]), never Quick Chaos itself ([[R387]]), cast free ([[R70]]) by the engine's random cast, which draws every choice of the cast (its targets and modes) from the match rng, so nothing pauses for a prompt. The Radiant face casts a Radiant Spell. The held set's Spells are not in the pool until the set ships.

Rejected: a pool that includes the held set's Spells, and letting the caster choose the targets.
