The menus named themselves five ways, at sizes from 17 px to 44 px:

- **A.** A brand `h1` ("JackiOh") and an `h2` screen name set between two brass rules in the tavern board (21 px,
  `auth/tavern.css`): shop, leaderboard, account, collection, the AI page, the legal pages, purchases, sign-in,
  reset, invite, the dungeon start.
- **B.** A brand `h1` and a `<p>` styled as the title (22 px): play, together, and Conquest.
- **C.** The `h1` was the screen's name, each in its own size and face: Statistics (26 px sans), the workshop and
  the Almanac (21 px serif), the patch notes (`clamp(28px, …, 40px)`), the dungeon stats (`--type-2xl`), the replays
  (the bare `index.css` `h1`).
- **D.** A visually hidden `h1` beside a `clamp(28–40px)` `h2` that did its job: Practice.
- **E.** No `h1` at all: the dungeon run screen and the replay player.

The decks route's loading shell also said "JackiOh — decks" where the loaded workshop said "Decks", and Practice's
"Learn to play" (a small sans panel heading) sat over "Practice against the AI" (a large serif display heading).

One `PageTitle` (`routes/PageTitle.tsx`) is now the screen's `h1`: `--font-display` at one step of `typescale.css`
(`--type-3xl`), in `routes/page-title.css`. A menu passes its name to `BackLink`'s `title` (`routes/nav.tsx`) and
never draws an `h1` of its own, so the title sits in the top bar's title slot ([[R3218000]]), which a phone's bar
already puts on a row under Back. The two prompts that stand in place of a menu and have no bar (the username and
the consent) draw a `PageTitle` in their panel. The brand is `Wordmark` (`routes/Wordmark.tsx`, a `div.brand`
holding a `span.brand__word`), not a heading; `ShellPanel`'s logo (`main.tsx`), which stands for a gate or a
loading screen and has no title, is the page's one `h1` there. The screens that title themselves in the bar are
every one that was A to E above, and the replay player (in the replays list, on the opened replay and in a Duos
replay) is titled "Replays", the tab's own word. Practice's failed-game screen and the dungeon fight's draw a
`PageTitle` too.

A section heading inside a screen is an `h2` of class `section-heading` (the same sheet, `--type-2xl`): the
legal pages' sections (once `h3`, which the removed `h2` had stood above), the replays' two lists, and on
Practice "Learn to play", "Choose your opponent", "Your deck" and "Practice against the AI", the last two of
which were the pair that mixed two styles. On the account, collection, leaderboard, purchases and shop screens, whose
title `h2` the bar took over, the section headings (once `h3`) are `h2` and their sub-sections `h3` (they keep their own
classes where one sets their size), so no heading skips a level under the `h1`; `expectOnePageTitle` checks this on every
route it covers. The document `<title>` (`net/head.ts`) is as it was, and the dungeon
stats' heading, once "Dungeon leaderboards", says "Dungeon Run stats" as its tab does. The decks loading shell
and the loaded workshop both say "Decks". `Terms of service` and `Privacy policy` move no word: only where their
page's title sits in the DOM changes, which changes the order of the text `privacy.test.tsx` hashes (R970001), so
the current `AGREED_TEXT` row is re-hashed and `TERMS_VERSION` stays.

Hearthstone semantics do not reach a page's chrome. **Proof.** `routes/page-title.test.ts` reads the sources: only
`PageTitle` draws a menu's `h1` (the boards' hidden headings, the settings dialog, the landing's wordmark, the
shell's logo and the developer pages are the exceptions it lists), no stylesheet but `page-title.css` sets a
font-size on an `h1` (`index.css`'s base `.app-shell h1` and the landing's own are the two it names), none styles the
brand as an `h1`, and `page-title.css` holds the title and the section heading to `--font-display` and a
`--type-…` step. `routes/page-titles.test.tsx` renders the five legal routes and `PageTitle` and `Wordmark`
themselves; each other menu's route test renders it loaded and asserts `expectOnePageTitle` (`test/pageTitle.ts`):
exactly one `h1`, the `PageTitle`, named for the screen and not visually hidden, and no heading below it skipping a
level. Practice's two section headings are
asserted to be `h2` of one class, and the decks loading shell and the loaded workshop to carry one title. Spec 46
(the menus fit one screen at 1280x720 and 390x844) and spec 47 (tap areas) measure the drawn bars, whose titles are
at the top and keep the page where it was.

A board's rail (the replay player's) carries the title too, and its phone height budget (`--board-rail-phone-h`, one
44px row) has no second row for it, so `routes/top-bar.css` keeps the title beside Back on that row on a phone, a step
smaller (`--type-xl`), on one line and ellipsized, never hidden; `page-title.test.ts` reads that rule.

Rejected: a bigger step (`--type-4xl`), which is taller than a bar's row; dropping the brand, which empties the
tavern panels, or the stacked logo image in its place, which breaks one-screen heights; a new "Replay" title for the
player, whose zh-CN entry means "replay a lesson"; a hidden `h1` that names the page for readers of a screen reader
beside a visible title of another word, which is what Practice did; and leaving the remaining classed panel headings
(`play-panel__heading`, `stats-games-title`, `together-panel__heading`, `ws-group-title`, the dungeon blocks' `h2`)
on their own sizes, which stays a follow-up of the issue's "section headings in one shared style".
