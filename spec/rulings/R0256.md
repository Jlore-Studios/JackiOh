Every deck and trio has an id the client mints, so saving one is an idempotent upsert of it alone that can be retried after a dropped connection without making a second. The builder saves `DECK_AUTOSAVE_DEBOUNCE_MS` (800) after the last edit, keeps every unsaved edit in the browser's storage, retries a failed save after `DECK_AUTOSAVE_RETRY_SECONDS` (5), and on return puts unsaved local edits back over the server's copy; it always shows which of Saved, Saving, Offline (kept on this device) or Couldn't save is true. A refused save is shown and not retried until the player edits again
