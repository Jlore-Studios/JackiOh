A line of a card's text starts "Aura:" when it is standing text that works while its card is face-up and acting on the field (the top of its pile, [[§3]]) and reaches beyond the card: other cards on either side, a player's rules or a hero ([[§6.2]], Aura). Nothing else prints the label.

- **The words decide, not the engine.** Every such line prints the label whatever runs it: a layer ([[§10.4]]), a static flag, a replacement, the engine itself (S #148 Alteran Reach's free travel, [[R1847001]]), or a field trigger when the line names no moment of its own (C #87 Plague Chalice, M #11 Double Header, S #37 Kwzka, S+ #74 Galaxia). A line that names its moment ("When …", "Whenever …", "Once per turn, when …", Start of turn, End of turn) is a trigger and prints its own words instead.
- **One line a face.** A face prints one "Aura:" line, its standing sentences together, as C #28 Second Wind's does: Core #73 Anti-oneshot Armor's Radiant face reads "Aura: Your hero can't take more than {cap} damage at a time. Your hero has Armor {armor}."
- **A Trap's replacement is its reveal condition.** A Trap or Field Trap that replaces an event fires there ([[§6.2]], Replacement), so its words are its reveal condition and its firing, never an aura.
- **Not an aura**, so bare or under its own label: hand, deck or graveyard text (Hand aura, Hand and deck triggers), a lasting effect a Spell or a Trap leaves ("this turn", "from now on", "your next Spell"), a keyword, a trigger or timing word (Cry, Activate, "Reveals when …"), an Embiggened line, a "While …:" condition, and the card's text about itself alone: its stats and keywords, its attacks, the damage it deals or takes, how it is played, stacked or targeted ("Has +{buff}/+{buff} for every card that Evolved this game", "This can't go Berserk", "This is a 2X/2X"). Such a line reads as a keyword does, so M #65 Keymaster Keenus, S #111 Shockwave447, the Drone Interceptor and Drone Corvette tokens, S+ #87 Frontier Flora and S+ #163 Accelerationist print "Has …" with no label, whatever their scripts run ([[R781004]], [[R3778000]] keep their mechanics; only the words change).

The measure goes by what each face's script sets: a field it reads while the card acts on the field and that reaches beyond the card (`aura`, `cost_aura`, `hero_guard`, `draw_limit`, `discard_guard`, `attack_mods`, `graveyard_play`, `reveal_echo`, the `*_extra` hooks, `trades_per_turn`, `exile_auras`, `resolves_set_spells`, a field `replacements` entry on a face that is no Trap, or a static flag sorted as an aura) owes an "Aura:" line, and an "Aura:" line owes one. The cards whose words and scripts differ for a reason (a self aura, an Activate's or a quest's permission, a field trigger, the engine's travel) are listed with it, and each static flag is sorted as an aura or not with its reason, so a card or a flag added later is sorted as it lands. The issue's pass added the label to Core #38, #64, #73, #75 and #84, M #11 and #92, S #148 and #157, the Drone Battleship (Shield Layer) token, S+ #30, #42, #62 and #63 and IA #95, #118, #152, #187, #191 and #218, and took it off the six "Has …" faces above, in English and Chinese (光环：), the faces' words alone. A card's §8 row says "Aura:" in its Base effect cell exactly when its base face prints the label, so the spec and the catalog read the same.

Rejected: "Aura:" on a card's own text, as M #65 printed it since patch v0.3.7 ([[R781004]]). It would label some forty cards' keyword-like lines, among them "Damage also Burns" on fifteen Stinkyscape+ cards, where the label says nothing a keyword does not. Rejected: measuring by the script alone, since field triggers run standing words and some static flags are keywords or a card's own text. Rejected: two "Aura:" lines on Core #73's Radiant face, which would read as two separate effects.

Hearthstone semantics: an aura is an ongoing effect on other cards or on a player ("Your other minions have +1 Attack", "Your hero is Immune"), and a minion's ongoing text about itself prints bare ("Has +1 Attack for each other minion").
