Each of S+ #133.1 to #133.5 is "a fusion of" two cards (Dagger and Flare, Mace and Horizon, Fortress and Zenith, Scepter and Antumbra, Reign and Eclipse), with art and a name of its own. Each card is read as its two ingredients' keywords and text at the cost and stats the catalog gives it.

- **Keywords** are catalog data, each face the union of its two ingredients' faces ([[§6.1]]).
- **Tribute** is the one the two ingredients share: Fortnith 1, Sceptumbra 2 and Reignclipse 3 (the owner, 2026-11-09: Reignclipse is Tribute 3, not the list's 4) ([[R101]]).
- **Burns.** "Damage also Burns N" is `burnsOnDamage` ([[R3540002]]), N declared as `burns` per face ([[R386]]): Macizon 1 (2), Fortnith 3 (6), Sceptumbra 4 (8), as the cards it is the fusion of. Reignclipse's two lines, Reign's "Damage also Burns 4 (8)" and Eclipse's "Dealing damage Burns 5 (10)", are one rider of their sum, 9 (18), as a Fuse sums `burnsOnDamage` ([[R102]]); its declared `burns` is Eclipse's line and moves that sum, its `damage` number being display only.
- **Armor** 4 and Armor 3 on Reignclipse stack to 7 ([[R77]]).

Rejected: two riders on one card, which the engine has no flag for; and Tribute 4 for Reignclipse, which neither ingredient asks.
