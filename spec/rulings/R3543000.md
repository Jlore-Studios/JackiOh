A Flexible Unit may be played, summoned, filled by "fill your board", or laid by a preset board
into its backrow as well as its unit row, and it is a Unit there for every rule: it attacks, is
attacked, defends, counts among its controller's Units and keeps its text, as a Flying Unit in the
backrow already does ([[R1081000]]; summons and "fill your board", [[R2248000]]). Played face-down,
it is a Trap like any other face-down play. Flying grants Flexible innately as a read
(`layers::is_flexible`), never added to the keywords, so a Flying Unit's view lists Flying alone
and every golden replay holds. A Nerf may remove a printed Flexible, and then no backrow zone is
offered. Rejected: adding Flexible to the keyword layer for Flying, which would change every Flying
view and move every golden replay.
