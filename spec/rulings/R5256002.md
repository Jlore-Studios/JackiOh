FvH #16 Travertine's list reads "Add 1 embiggen 6 Travertine Stacks" and its token's "Temporary 1-6 (normal gives temporary 2; embiggened gives temporary for each mana cost 1-6)". The build reads it as: played normally it adds one Travertine Stack (FvH #16.1) with Temporary 2; Embiggened it adds the declared `stacks` (6) instead, the first with Temporary 1, the second Temporary 2, and so on to Temporary 6 ([[R5238000]]). Each counts down at its owner's ends of turn ([[R2179001]]), so one Stack is discarded at each of the caster's next six ends of turn, the first at the end of this one, and each discard pays out as the Stack's own text says (mana, Credits and a Travertine Golem). The Stack prints no Temporary, so no printed count floors these. A Stack a full hand burns is no discard and pays nothing ([[R317]]). The 2 and the counts 1 to N are printed, not declared ([[R1748003]]); a Buff of `stacks` adds a Stack with the next count.
