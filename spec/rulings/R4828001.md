The fuzz monitor's I21 ([[R4453000]]) blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, up to the next card's marker. Issue #4828's pull request had its changed cards' Radiant rotation deal a game (seed 4, with C #3, C #98, S #148 and S #169 forced) in which p1's turn ended, S #34 Drone Core VIII's "Float reduces cost" ran from p1's hand, and Burn then ticked on p1's S+ #133.2 Macizon, Immune to Spells, for what Health it had left ([[R4154000]]). No state check runs inside the tick ([[R59]]): the settle after it collected the kill, a `destroyed` with no killer and no marker of its own, so it landed in Drone Core's run and I21 named the Spell.

**The kill is the tick's.** [[R4508005]] already holds that an affliction's tick is the game's, not the Spell's, and reaches an immune Unit, and I21 allows the tick's hit and the Burn's removal. What the hit killed is collected by the settle ([[§4.5]]), the game's own work as well ([[R4863000]]). The engine already plays it so; only the monitor's reading was short.

**What I21 allows.** A `destroyed` that names no killer, of a Unit that took a tick's hit that landed (a sourceless `damage` via Burn or Toxic) earlier in the step since the run's last marker. A tick Divine Shield took (`damageAbsorbed`), a tick on another Unit, a tick before the Spell's own marker and a death credited to the running Spell ([[R4599001]]) still leave the death the Spell's, unless another ruling explains it. Since #4599, a standing trigger from a turn's end opens no Spell's run ([[R4599007]]), and a killerless death whose damage meets the Unit's max health is allowed as the layers' ([[R4599008]]), which also allows a tick's kill once the damage the monitor counted meets it. This ruling allows the kill from the tick alone, whatever the `destroyed`'s max health reads.

Rejected: a marker for the tick and the settle in the engine. The engine marks where a hook begins, never the game's own work ([[R2783000]], as [[R4508005]] rejected), and a new event would move every replay's hash while showing a player nothing.
