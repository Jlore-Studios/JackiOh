S+ #83 Offworld Replicant evolves when it is Stacked on something, and "Add a copy of this to your hand" adds one new Offworld Replicant to its controller's hand: Radiant when the evolving card is Radiant, base when it is not. Like a copy made into a hand or a library, it is a fresh card carrying only that face ([[R57]]), so it keeps none of the Replicant's buffs, damage, counters or tuning, and it is not evolved: it evolves when it is itself Stacked on a Unit ([[R3536000]]). A full hand adds none, and the Replicant has evolved all the same. Rejected: a copy of the instance with its riders (`copyOf`), which would carry a Nerf or a buff from the field into the hand.
