`GET /api/queue/population` answers the queue readout instead of the open tickets' count. Every number in it is one the server counted over the last `QUEUE_READOUT_WINDOW_SECONDS` (3600 s):

- `gamesLastHour`: the results that ended in that hour, games against a ladder bot included.
- `expectedWaitSeconds`: per mode ([[R257]]), the expected wait the tickets claimed in that hour call for. A ticket's wait runs from its enqueue to its claim, a claim for a bot ([[R1453]]) as much as a pair's; a ticket still open, or cancelled, has none. Each store stamps a claim with the sweep's clock.

A mode's expected wait is the nearest-rank `QUEUE_WAIT_QUANTILE` (0.75, the upper quartile) of its waits, rounded up to the next multiple of `QUEUE_WAIT_STEP_SECONDS` (10 s) above it, so a wait of exactly 30 s reads 40 s and "usually under" holds for at least three waits in four. With fewer than `QUEUE_WAIT_MIN_SAMPLES` (5) waits it is `null`. Rejected: the median, which would say "usually under" of a wait half the queue goes past, and a wait read off fewer than five, which one long wait would swing.

**No count of open tickets.** No response carries one, `POST /api/queue` included: a lone queuer who read "1 waiting" and was then paired at once would know their opponent was a bot ([[R1452]]).

**The lobby.** Each mode's tile carries its expected wait, "Usually under 30 s", or from a minute up "Usually under N min" with the minutes rounded up; a mode whose wait is `null` shows none. One line under the mode picker gives the hour's games ("12 games played in the last hour"), and is hidden at 0. The Find a match box and the queued notice repeat neither ([[R505]]). A server from before this ruling sends neither field, and the lobby shows nothing for them.
