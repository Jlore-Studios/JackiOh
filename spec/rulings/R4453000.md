[[§6.1]] says a Unit with Immune to Spells is one "Spells can't target … or affect", and that "every Spell's effect list skip[s] it" ([[R81]]). The engine kept that only where a Spell named its cards through a target or a board scope (`effects/targets.rs`, which asks `restrictions::unaffected_by` of each card; [[R1185002]]'s set is one). Several other walks of the field never asked, so a Spell still changed an immune Unit when its wording reached it some other way. The spec decides this one: "can't … affect it" is wider than Hearthstone's Elusive, which only bars targeting and is JackiOh's Evasive.

**A Spell now passes a field Unit Immune to Spells by, whatever its wording.** Each walk asks `unaffected_by` of every field card it meets:

- **A card scope** (`card_scope::cards_in_card_scope`'s field cards), which every verb over one reads: `buff_cards`, `grant_keyword_cards`, a scoped `upgrade` or `degrade`, `transform_cost_step` and the rest. The Spells this changes: M #34 高考's Buff (its destroy half already spared the card), S+ #32 Z-Patch's Buffs and Nerfs, S+ #20 B-Patch's Buff or Nerf, C+ #40 Appropriations' Military and Healthcare, C+ #77 Anti-Softlock's Stack and Pierce, S+ #103 Transcend's Transform, and the end-of-turn Buff of the Stinkyscape+ Tears of the Kwzki Tree token.
- **"Every Unit"** (`buff_all_units`, `switch_all_positions`): C+ #50 Adaptive Growth, M+ #1.4 Leg Day, Core #62 Friend of Felinors' Radiant +2/+2 and Core #48 5pek Controller.
- **A random pick of the field** (`radiant.rs`'s field pool, which `set_radiant_random` and `radiant_chance` both read): Core #28 Knockoff Temu Glowy Jelly Bean never draws the Unit, and C+ #40 Appropriations' Culture never rolls it.
- **A Plague Counter placement** (C #70 Book of Plague and every other Spell that places them): the pool it draws from at random, inside a random cast ([[R452]]) or while a Mayor acts ([[R1200]]), leaves the Unit out, so the draw lands where it can change something, as [[R4322000]]'s leaves out a Dormant card. A placement its player chooses already offered no immune Unit, since a Spell's `target` prompt drops every option it may not target ([[R450]]); the prompt and the draw now read one pool.

**A copy in a hand or a deck is still reached.** Immune to Spells protects a Unit only on the field, as [[R940]]'s tribal immunity does, so Appropriations' Military still buffs your immune Unit in your hand and deck, and it carries that buff onto the field when it enters. Units, Field Spells and Traps still reach the immune Unit, so a Unit's every-Unit buff changes it.

**Help is passed by as harm is.** "Affect" makes no difference between them, so your own immune Unit misses your Leg Day, your Friend of Felinors' +2/+2, your Appropriations and your Transcend. A designer who wants helpful Spells to reach it changes [[§6.1]].

**A hidden card stays in its pile.** The card scope marks the immune card unmatched rather than dropping it, so a verb that cues every card of a hidden pile still cues the whole pile ([[R440]]). Every card that prints the keyword is a Unit, which is never face-down, so this changes no cue today.

**With nothing else to reach, nothing happens.** Knockoff draws among the hand and the library alone, and a placement with no other permanent on the field fizzles and draws nothing ([[R129]]).

**S #3 Meteor's spill passes a Dormant neighbour by.** [[R1848001]]'s neighbours are the Units acting beside the target. A Dormant card acts not ([[§6.1]] Dormant, [[R1839000]]), but it stayed among them: it could be drawn first, the hit refused it, and the excess stopped there, so the other neighbour and the Radiant face's hero got nothing. Now it is no neighbour, so the excess goes on to the other one (no draw for a lone neighbour) and, on the Radiant face, to the hero.

**Evasive is unchanged.** It bars aim alone, and scopes and random picks still reach it by design ([[R4353002]]). The Arcane Missiles token's random hits (S #11.2) are issue #4424's.

**Replays.** A replay in which one of these Spells met an immune Unit, or a Meteor spilled beside a Dormant card, plays differently and is refused as `rules_changed` rather than shown wrong ([[R767000]]). No card script is kept for older catalogs, since the change is in the engine's verbs and holds for every catalog.

Rejected:
- A guard in `apply_buff` and `switch_position`: it would stop a Unit's or a Field Spell's effect too.
- Dropping the card from the scope's field cards: a whole-pile walk would count it out ([[R440]]).
- Letting helpful Spells through: [[§6.1]] says "affect", not "harm".
