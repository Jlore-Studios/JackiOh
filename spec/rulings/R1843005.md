`public.results` and `public.rated_games` each check a finished game's reason against GameOverReason's strings as they stood, so migration 0059 puts both checks back, as 0031 did for the Meditative set's two endings ([[R1412]]), with `voted-out`, `both-voted-out` ([[R1843000]], [[R1843001]]), `fled` and `fled-decisive` ([[R1843002]]) added and nothing else changed; without it a game they ended would fail its result write, and [[R1437]] would keep the match live. `voided` stays out of both ([[R679]]). It widens two checks and rewrites no row, so it is safe on any earlier database and safe to apply before any card reaches the endings. `voted-out` and `fled-decisive` always have a winner, so they join the decisive endings the server's record reads (`DECISIVE_REASONS`); `both-voted-out` is a draw, and `fled` leaves the winner's opponent a draw whether its winner is still there or not.
