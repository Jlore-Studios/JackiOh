KPIs destroys the permanents of both rows whose rate is strictly lower than its own
(`winRateBelow`): every card on either side on the base face, only the enemy's on the Radiant face.
Both rates read the 50% default with no record (R3538000), so an unmeasured card survives an
unmeasured KPIs. Rejected: destroying on a tie, since "lower" is strict.
