An Infinite Assault level's fight ([[R4582000]]) that ends is kept on the device with the finished
free practice games ([[R768]]): numbered after the newest kept game, sharing their
`PRACTICE_REPLAYS_KEPT`, and named by the snapshot once it is over ([[R768000]]), whether the fight was
won or lost. A fight a Glitch voided is kept nowhere, as no voided game is ([[R679]]), and a dungeon
run's fight and a tutorial lesson are still never kept ([[R2706000]], [[§9.10]]).

A kept game's setup ([[R767000]]) holds what `create_game` was given, an Assault fight's enemy deck
marks ([[R2702000]]) and sets ([[R4595000]]) among them, and its config holds the level's seed, so its
replay folds to the fight's own hash; a game created without marks or sets keeps neither key, so every
other kept game's file is as before. Its summary names the level and the enemy (`assault`): the recent
games (`/replays`) list it as an Infinite Assault level against that enemy, and its replay, as the
fight's result opens it, names the enemy on the enemy's hero.

The fight's result offers Watch the replay once the worker has kept it, after Continue and Main menu
([[R4742000]]). Watching sends nothing to the attempt, and Back returns to the result: the fight's
report still goes once, on Continue or on leaving ([[R4733000]]).

Rejected: a separate store or cap for Assault fights, which would split the recent games into two
lists; keeping a dungeon fight too, which is its run's to record.
This follows Hearthstone semantics, where a recent game is the last games played in any mode a
replay viewer can show, and the Infinite Assault is played as ordinary practice games.
