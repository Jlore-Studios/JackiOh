"Jlockeed" is a tag ([[§5]]) on the six cards: #13 Jlockeed Shredder-10 and #14 Jlockheed's Weapons, whose names make them Jlockheed's, and, since patch v0.2.0, C+ #48 Jlockheed's Lobbyist, C+ #51 Jlockheed's J15 Fighter and C+ #52 Jlockheed's Permanent Defense Contract, which keep the designer's spelling in their names and are one faction with Core's two under one tag ([[R381]]), plus, since balance patch 1, Classic #4 Palantir, inducted without renaming it. It is no longer only a filter: C+ #48's and C+ #52's pools ("a random Jlockheed card") are the non-token cards with the tag, each leaving itself out ([[R387]]), so they find Core's two and Classic #4 as well. No Core card's text or rule reads it, the deck builder offers it among its tag filters, and it is not a tribe any card counts. One spelling for the tag and every name would be a patch ([[R388]])
