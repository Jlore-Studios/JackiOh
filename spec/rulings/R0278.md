"Jlockeed" is a tag ([[§5]]) on the six cards: #13 Jlockeed Shredder-10 and #14 Jlockeed's Weapons, whose names make them Jlockeed's, and, since patch v0.2.0, C+ #48 Jlockeed's Lobbyist, C+ #51 Jlockeed's J15 Fighter and C+ #52 Jlockeed's Permanent Defense Contract, spelled Jlockeed's like Core's two since patch v0.3.Y (#1067) and are one faction with Core's two under one tag ([[R381]]), plus, since balance patch 1, Classic #4 Palantir, inducted without renaming it. It is no longer only a filter: C+ #48's and C+ #52's pools ("a random Jlockeed card") are the non-token cards with the tag, each leaving itself out ([[R387]]), so they find Core's two and Classic #4 as well. No Core card's text or rule reads it, the deck builder offers it among its tag filters, and it is not a tribe any card counts. Patch v0.3.Y (#1067) gave every name the tag's spelling ([[R388]])
