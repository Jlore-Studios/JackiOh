Meditative #64 Traitorous Blood redirects a declared attack only (MD-D19): a forced attack never opens
a trap window. The attack goes to a Unit adjacent to the attacker that it could legally attack, a
random one of two, and the open attack is marked `redirected`, so [[§4.3]] resolves it as a combat
between allies: a redirected attack on a Unit its own controller holds still stands, where any other
target must still be the attacker's enemy ([[R173]]). A later trap in the same window that needs an
enemy target skips it.
