The targets on which [[R1120]]'s bonus would apply (`conditionTargets`, MD-D5) are computed by the
engine for the viewer's own attackers only, during their own main phase with no prompt open. They are
absent on the opponent's turn, while a prompt is open and on enemy Units, so the client draws them and
decides nothing (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
