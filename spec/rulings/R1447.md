Two leaks that the fuzz gate's hidden-information invariant I6 found on `main`, in the daily super run of 2026-10-11 (#659, seeds 5245 and 8178). Each sent one seat the instance id of a card in the other seat's face-down trap, which [[§10.8]] never sends.

**A preview's ids.** A preview value may name the cards it counts ([[R372]], "highlight targets"). The hook reads as the card's controller does ([[R280]]), so a count of "all permanents" includes the controller's own face-down traps, and `view_for` sent those ids to both seats. A value's `ids` now keep only the cards the viewer may read where they lie: a face-down card only for its controller, a hand only for its holder, a library for nobody, and a card that has ceased to exist never (the readers [[R466]] names). The number is unchanged: it still counts every card, since the other seat sees a face-down card's back.

**A Heroic Power on a face-down card.** A Trap fused with #98 keeps the power #98 rolled in its memory ([[R43]]). But a face-down card has no ability anyone can use: activation refuses it ("a face-down card has no ability to use"), so its power was never usable ([[R752]]). The hero's `power` and `powers` listed it anyway, naming the hidden card's id and its fused definition to the other seat. They now list a backrow card's power only while the card is face-up, for both seats, as activation already did. Once the card is face-up, or revealed, its power is listed again, and it is public ([[R43]]).
