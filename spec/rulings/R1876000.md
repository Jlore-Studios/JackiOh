"Summon a Radiant University. It can't use its own Activate" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #26 ZolarKeth's Cry) summons M #97.6 University on its Radiant face ([[R64]]) and then refuses that University every use of its own abilities (`refuse_own_activate`): a mark on the card, `memory.ownActivateRefused`, that the Activate refusal reads before the uses and the costs, so `legalActions` lists none of its abilities, the view greys them with the reason ("that card can't use its own Activate") and the reducer refuses an `activate` naming one. It is the designer's "Its Activate is locked" (ST-S17-3): no zone is Locked ([[§3.2]]), and another card may still run the ability ([[R1876001]]). The mark is the card's memory, so it ends as the card leaves the field ([[R78]]); a University summoned any other way, or one already on the field, keeps its Activate. A full unit row summons nothing and so refuses nothing. Rejected: Locking the University's zone, which would also stop a play into it and outlive the card ([[R688]]).

Amended by [[R2521003]] (issue #2521): ZolarKeth's Cry now summons a University on its base face, from either of its faces, still refused its own Activate.
