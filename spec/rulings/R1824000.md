Wherever a rule walks the cards acting on a side of the field, a Unit a carrier holds is one of them, a Unit for every rule ([[R446]]): "your Units on the field" in a card scope, the permanents a Plague count, placement or random field exile reads ([[R471]]), price rules, graveyard plays, Quickstriker, natural mana, a credit line, Echo grants, a reveal Echo, a random Make Radiant and a Fuse onto your card. It is read once, after the side's unit zones and before its backrow, as [[R68]]'s triggers and the auras read it. A Unit standing in a backrow zone ([[R1081000]]) is read once too: among the Units for triggers, hero and discard guards, draw limits and trigger multipliers, at its own backrow lane for a count, a pick or an offer, so no random draw over a board without a carried Unit changes.
