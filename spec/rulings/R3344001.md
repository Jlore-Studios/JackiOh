Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): the tilt reads an event and the host's box, writes six custom properties on the
face and reads no view.

**The tilt.** A face with depth ([[R3344000]]) turns toward the point it is shown: the pose (`pointerPose`, `cards/tilt.ts`)
is the pointer's place in its host's box, level at the middle and `-1` to `1` each way, held at the edge beyond it; the
face turns by `-y * CARD_TILT_MAX_DEG` about x and `x * CARD_TILT_MAX_DEG` about y, so it never passes the cap whatever
the pointer does, and its planes shift by their depth (`CARD_DEPTH_STEP_PX`, scaled to the face's width), with the glare
opposite it (`glareOf`). The face's parent gives it `CARD_PERSPECTIVE_PX` of perspective. When the pointer leaves, the
properties are removed and the face springs back by `depth.css`'s transition, which overshoots a little. The pose is read
from the host's box, never the face's own, which rotates.

**Where it runs.** `CardFace`'s `tilt` is the face's mode: **host** (a card in hand, on the board and in the Collection),
the pointer over the face's parent or a touch pressing it, and a touch released puts it level; **window** (the hover
preview, which takes no pointer of its own), the window's pointer, level when it leaves the document or the window loses
focus; **drag** (the inspect sheet), the window's pointer as the preview's and a touch drag on the face, which holds off
the sheet's own swipe (`useSheetSwipe`) and the browser's scroll on the face (`touch-action: none`). A list, a picker and the
`MinionFace` take no mode and never tilt.

**The device.** `deviceorientation` drives the same pose (`devicePose`: left and right from flat, forward and back from
`IMMERSIVE_TILT_REST_DEG`, as Immersive reads it, [[R2944002]]) only while the device-only "Tilt with my phone" setting
(`deviceTilt`, off by default) is on, from the start of a window or a drag face and while a pointer hovers or presses a
host face. Where the platform asks leave (iOS Safari's `DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission`, which only a tap may
call) the switch asks inside its tap and holds on only if the player grants it; where it does not it turns on at once.
Without the setting a phone tilts by touch alone: a press in hand and a drag in inspect.

**One face at a time.** The tilt takes one slot (`claim`): a face that starts to tilt takes it and the holder lets go (and
springs back), unless the holder's mode outranks it (drag over window over host, one level each). While the board's
animation queue is draining an entry (`Game`'s `inFlight`, `useTiltHold`) no face holds the slot or may take it. Under
reduced motion, the system's and the fx intensity off a face has no depth to tilt ([[R3344000]]).

Rejected: tilting every face in a hand at once (a hand's cards overlap and each would need its own box); a tilt that
follows the pointer's place in the viewport as Immersive's shift does (the card would turn toward the page, not toward
the point it is touched at); asking leave for the orientation at the first tilt (iOS wants a tap, which the switch is);
and a tilt on the board's minions, whose faces are small and many.
