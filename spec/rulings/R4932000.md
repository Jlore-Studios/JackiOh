[[R1127000]]'s follow-up score and [[R1723000]]'s baseline each weigh at most
`DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` of the teammate's plays, and both cut them in `legal_actions` order, which is
hand order: a card that arrived late went unweighed however good it was, so the seat could pass a card
the teammate could not use, or keep one it needed, because the teammate's best play sat past the cut. A
hand's size moves ([[R1143]]), so the cut is no fixed count of cards either. This note has both scores
weigh the teammate's best-valued cards when they cannot weigh all of them.

- **Past the bound, the best-valued cards**: when the teammate lists more plays than the room left,
  the kept ones are the first listed play of each card it names, the card the teammate's probe values
  highest in hand (`hand_card_value`) first, ties in list order, a play from outside the hand (a
  graveyard play, [[R454]]) last. The follow-up score keeps the passed card's own plays first, as
  [[R1127000]] has it, and ranks the teammate's other plays into the room they leave; the baseline
  ranks the first plays of all its cards into the whole bound.
- **Within the bound, unchanged**: a list that fits is weighed whole, in `legal_actions` order, as
  before.
- **Unchanged**: the bound, so no engine step is added; the own-use gain ([[R2827000]]) and the
  card's plays after the baseline, each one card's plays, which tie on hand value; the fixed-seed
  probes, never a hidden opponent card or the caller's stream ([[R185]]).

Rejected: raising the bound to the hand's size, whose steps are unmeasured; ranking each card by one
engine step of its own, which spends the steps the bound saves; and putting a card's other lanes and
targets ahead of another card's first play, which lets one Unit's lanes fill the room.
