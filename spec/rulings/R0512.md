The glossary has a row, in players' words, for every [[§6]] term a v0.2.0 card prints: the [[§6.1]] statuses Can't be in Defense Position (SPEC's "Cannot be…" an alias), Can't be attacked, Only Units in this lane can attack this and Berserk; [[§6.2]]'s Activate (one term for "Activate", "Activate X" and "Activate ♾️"); [[§6.3]]'s Counter, Steal, Unlock, Flicker, Plague Counter, Set health, Redirect, End the turn, Trigger a Cry and Look at a hand, each matched by the words the cards print; and Nerf and Buff, one row each (Degrade and Upgrade until patch v0.3.4, [[R1320]]), which split SPEC's one "Degrade / Upgrade" row by pairing the words either side of its slash with the rest of its sentence. A rule is printed without the ruling numbers SPEC cites. Matching stays case-sensitive, so a lower-case "steal it" or "can't attack or be attacked" stays plain words. The glossary beside a face in play also explains the states it wears
