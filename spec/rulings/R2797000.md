Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. A card's entry in its set's effects table ([[R2784000]]) may give a motif to its `death`, its `trigger` (a start-of-turn or end-of-turn hook or a Whenever trigger) and its `activate`, and these now play. Each such hook's `hookResolving` marker ([[R2783000]]) opens an entry in the effects layer's memory for its card, innermost, so the events after it are the hook's and not those of any play around it: its card's entry plans on that hook as a play's plans on its `cast` or `cry`. A Cry's marker opens none, since a Cry runs inside its card's own play, which already keys it. A Trap's `trapFired` opens its card's `cast` the same way, so a Trap's entry plays as it fires. The hook's entry ends at the next marker or firing, at a `cardResolved` or `countered`, at an `attackDeclared`, at a turn's end or start and at the game's end, whichever comes first; plays stay open around it. A later move of the same window, such as a `positionSwitched`, reads as the hook's until one of those closes it; no entry of this pass gives `shift` to a hook.

A marker the viewer may not read names the sentinel ([[R2783000]], [[R202]]): it opens its entry all the same, keyed by no definition, so its events go to no play around it and nothing of either card's entry plans; no cue names a definition. A modifier a hook adds wears no aura: [[R2787000]] attributes a modifier to the readable play that added it, and a Death, a trigger, an Activate or a Trap's firing is no play. The `/dev/fx` gallery plays a card whose entry gives a motif only to its `death`, `trigger` or `activate` through the board's effects layer as the board now plays it, from its hook's marker under the card's play, in place of #2793's gallery-only director.

One motif joins the vocabulary, for three or more of the range's cards that show an event no motif read. `hex` reads `marked`: a mark put on (a Freeze, an Allure) closes a ring on the card, crosses it with a sheen and flares as it takes on Land; a mark lifted flares in a ring of sparkles; one shake. It replaces nothing, so the mark's own row keeps the mark's color. `transmute` also reads `fused`, drawing its smoke and sparks at the host as an Equip fuses a pick into it (the Equip Cries of S #78 to #83, #85 and #86). The cards made into a hand or a deck take #2793's `send`, which reads `addedToHand` and `shuffledIn`; a twin of it this branch first drew is dropped.

Three cards take recipes of their own (fx/cards/stinkyscape/), each built from the motif primitives at the card's motif scale and drawn over its event rows (`keep`), each once its play or once its event, inside the entry and its tail ([[R200]]) and keyed by a readable play only ([[R202]]). S #4 Olivian Expanse's `terminusRift` tears a void rift over the board's centre at the play's first card added, which flies out of it into its caster's hand, the board shaking `FX_TERMINUS_TRAUMA`. S #18 Imitated Instrumentality's `magiBoot` beams each of its three Field Spells in from its caster's hero, in the hologram sub-look, booting with a ring and a burst in its zone; summons in one entry leave `FX_MAGI_STAGGER` of the entry apart, at most `FX_MAGI_BOOTS` of them staggered, none landing past Linger, and the first shakes the board `FX_MAGI_TRAUMA`. S #36 Drone Core X's `battleshipWarp` gathers stars where the Drone Battleship will stand and streaks it in from `FX_WARP_FROM` of the board, its top edge, landing in exhaust and starlight, the board shaking `FX_WARP_TRAUMA`.

The pass (stinkyscape-1.json5, stinkyscape-001 to stinkyscape-090 and the tokens listed among them, its per-card table stinkyscape-1.md) gives at least `FX_SET_PASS_FLOOR` of the range's non-token cards, and of all its entries, an entry other than `none`; every Spell, Field Spell, Trap and Field Trap that is not a token a `cast`, a recipe or a set piece; and every Field Spell, tokens included, an aura. A token gets an entry only where it earns one, so the `cast` measure is held to the non-tokens. A hook no marker reports (an attack's own, a Starship's launch, which is #2782's Starship part) is `none`.

Rejected: keying a hook on an event's own `defId`, which a hidden card's events do not all carry and which no motif reads; extending `bind` or `flight` to read marks and made cards, which `cues.ts`'s event rows delegate to (`MOTIF_ROW_LOOK`), so every game's rows would change; and keeping this branch's twin of `send` beside it.
