[[R3477000]] listed the views that carry the engine's forecast of a seat's attacks: a practice snapshot, the 1v1 `view` frame and a Duos seat's. Daily games ([[R3727000]]) came after it, so a daily game's read carried none, "Show attack outcomes" drew nothing there, and the board lacked three more things every other table shows: its opponent hero read "Opponent", its rail listed no modifier ([[R169]]), and a finished game's result offered no replay.

**The read.** `GET /api/daily/games/:matchId`, and every answer to an action (a refusal's `game` among them), carries `forecast`: the engine's `combat_forecast` for the caller's seat, read under the game's own catalog as its legal actions are ([[R767000]], [[R3727005]]), so an older-catalog game shows the numbers its cards play with. Its entries are those of the attacks the read's `legal` lists, in that order, since a daily game offers fewer actions than the engine's seat does ([[§9.13]]). While the other seat holds the move `legal` lists no attack, so `forecast` is `{ attacks: [], lethal: false }`.

**The board.** `DailyBoard` passes `<Game>` the read's `forecast`, so an aimed attack draws its outcomes as everywhere else ([[R3477000]], [[R3615000]]) under the one setting, Show attack outcomes. Its opponent hero reads the opponent's username. Its rail lists the modifiers in play ([[R169]]), headed You and Opponent. A finished game's result offers Watch the replay beside Rematch: it opens the server's replay of the game (`GET /api/replays/:matchId`), as a 1v1 result does, and Back returns to the board. A voided game offers none, as a 1v1 one does ([[R679]]: the server keeps no record of it).

Presentation only: the forecast enters no log, state or hash, reads the face-up board alone ([[R292]]) and goes to its own seat only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A client older than its server draws no marker, and a client newer than its server finds no `forecast` and draws none.

Hearthstone semantics: the board's information is the same at every table.

Rejected: the account token passed down from the route, since the daily module reads the tab's session itself on every call and a Try again then uses a renewed one; working the numbers out in the client (CLAUDE.md rule 7); sending the engine's unfiltered forecast, which would name attacks the seat is not offered, as the Duos rejection of [[R3477000]] did; Share the last turn on the result, which the issue did not ask for.
