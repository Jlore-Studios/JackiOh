**Why.** `/stats` and the card's Statistics panel ([[R3185000]]) show a Play rate beside the win rates. It was the card's counted games over every seat its window counts: in Constructed the share of decks that held it, and under All Random ([[R2777002]]) the share of seats that saw it. That is how common a card is, which in a dealt pool says almost nothing and in Constructed is a deck-building figure, not a balance one. The owner asked for the figure a balance pass reads beside the tier ([[R3022000]]): how often the card is played when it is seen, so a card held in hand and passed over (too dear, too narrow, a Trap kept as a threat) is told from one cast at the first chance.

**The rule.** A card's `playRate` on `GET /api/stats/cards` is its played games over its seen games: the games it reached its owner's hand, from the opening hand or a draw, and was played from there, over every game it reached that hand, played or not (the engine's `seen`, [[R2427000]]'s drawn side). Both count each deck once a game ([[R377]]).

- It is the same in every mode, since it reads only the decks that saw the card: under All Random and in Constructed alike.
- A card only a rule makes, the Dormant Starship ([[R3070000]]), reads the games a seat used one over the games a seat made one ([[R2089000]]'s two Created tallies).
- A card never seen reads 0, as before. It shows no other figure below [[R654]]'s floor, and the play rate keeps the same place in the row.

It is still a share of games, not of copies: a deck that drew two copies and played one counts as played.

**Rejected.**
- Played over held. A card in the deck that was never drawn had no chance to be played, so long games and draw would raise it.
- Copies played over copies drawn. The records keep each deck's cards once a game ([[R377]]), not each copy.
- Keeping the old figure under another name. No client or route reads it, and a deck share means nothing under All Random.

[[R1583000]]'s Pocket Pick keeps its own play rates (played over every human seat), which it compares with the device's own and which this does not touch. No Hearthstone rule is involved.
