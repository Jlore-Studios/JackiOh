[[R1578000]], [[R1579000]] and [[R1580000]] made the Play hub, the Collection and the Shop one
screen each: the page never scrolls, a body does. This rules the same for every other menu, so no
menu is a page to scroll down.

**The menus** are the screens of spec 46's list: the tavern home, the sign-in, sign-up and
password-reset screens, the almanac, the patch notes, the statistics, the practice setup, Play,
the deck builder, the account, the leaderboard, the card creator, the replays, Play together, the
Collection with its sections, the Shop, a series between its games, the invite and consent
screens, and the page for a path nothing is at. Not the boards, and not the static legal pages.

**One screen.** At 1280x720 and 390x844 the page is no taller and no wider than the window. A
menu's top (Back, the gear, its title) stays put, and its one region scrolls whatever exceeds the
room, so a long list or a long form is scrolled in the menu, never in the page. No type shrinks to
make it fit. Under 501 px tall (a phone on its side) the page scrolls as before: a region there
would show a row at most.

**The one exception** is the tavern home, which is its main screen and exactly one screen below
the floor ([[R1584000]]'s floor is one screen too), so it may be two windows tall.

Hearthstone semantics: its menus are fixed screens, and a long list scrolls in its own frame, never
the whole screen.

**Proof.** Spec 46 visits every menu at both sizes and fails when the document's scrollHeight is
more than its allowance (a window, or two on the tavern home) or its scrollWidth more than the
window's. It runs on every pull request (`e2e/scripts/shard-specs.mjs`'s `SMOKE`).

Rejected: smaller type, which a phone cannot read; redesigning each long form to fit whole (the
account in two columns, the practice setup in steps, one patch version at a time), which is a
screen's design apiece and is left to follow-ups; and a guard that fails on a scroller inside a
menu, which [[R1579000]] and [[R1580000]] allow, since their bodies scroll by design.
