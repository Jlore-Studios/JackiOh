Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Opening a hero's inspect view ([[R1330]]) makes the portrait on the board answer: its oval squashes and a glint crosses it, for `PORTRAIT_REACT_MS` (320 ms, `apps/web/src/emotes/config.ts`). Only the portrait whose view opened reacts, and closing the view does not. Under Reduce Motion, whether from the system or from the settings panel's switch ([[R644]]), nothing on the oval moves: the view still opens and no squash or glint is drawn.
