Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Opening a hero's menu on its portrait or its inspect view ([[R1446]], [[R1330]]) makes the portrait on the board answer: its oval squashes and a glint crosses it, for `PORTRAIT_REACT_MS` (320 ms, `apps/web/src/emotes/config.ts`). Only the portrait whose menu or view opened reacts; a menu turning into the view does not react again, and closing either does not react. Under Reduce Motion, whether from the system or from the settings panel's switch ([[R644]]), nothing on the oval moves: the menu and the view still open and no squash or glint is drawn.
