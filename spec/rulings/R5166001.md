In the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 5834, on a branch I14 tried and did not play, an attack killed Stinkyscape+ #165 Shadiris, which stood on the field Immutable only by a grant. Reborn returned it ([[R78]]), and the returned card keeps no grant, so it was no longer Immutable. S+ #33 True Conservative's Cry then turned it into a version from its past, which the engine rightly let through. I23 ([[R4609000]]) judged the old instance, gone after the step, by the keywords it held as the step began ([[R4353000]]), granted Immutable among them, and named the Transform.

This carries [[R4604011]], a card the step sent to a graveyard without its granted Immutable, to a card the step brings back to the field: [[R4609002]] already lets a reset card's record go from its `flickered` or `summoned` on, and I23 now judges such a card's later events by the keywords the reset leaves it too.

**What I23 allows.** After a `summoned` or a `flickered` of a card in the step that the reset reached, an event changing its text, when the card's own keywords, read as [[R78]]'s reset leaves them (`zones::reset_instance`: no granted keyword), no longer make it Immutable. The reset reached it when it began the step on the field, or when the step flickered it or sent it to an exile or a graveyard before the return (every way there resets it, `zones::move_to_zone`). A card Immutable by its printed keyword comes back Immutable and is still judged, and so is a card Immutable by a grant that the step never returned. So is a hand or library card granted Immutable that the step summons straight from there: no reset touches it on the way (a grant rides a hand and a library, `KEPT_IN_HAND_OR_LIBRARY`), so it arrives with its grant.

Hearthstone semantics: a minion that returns to the battlefield is a new one, with none of the enchantments the old one held.

Rejected: reading every `summoned` as a reset, which would let through a Transform of a hand card that arrives with its grant (the first review of #5172). Rejected too: scanning the step for returns before the walk, which would also let go a Transform made before the return, while the grant still held.
