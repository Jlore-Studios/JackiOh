"A lot can be added for the rest of the game at a fixed price" is Call to Chaos (Scape Edition)'s: a Call to Chaos at 20. It is the verb `add_market_lot { pool, price, player? }`. It opens the Marketplace if it is closed ([[R1853000]]). It then draws one card of its pool from the match rng as it is added, among the ids the catalog holds; with none, it adds nothing.

The text leaves four things open, and this note decides each:

- **Whose lot it is.** It is offered to the player it names, by default its caster, and to no one else. The other seat's view does not show it, their listing does not offer it, and a buy of it is refused as no offer, as Hearthstone's Discover-made shop items are the player's own.
- **Its face.** It is never Radiant.
- **Its price.** It stays at the price the card names: no turn moves it ([[R1853002]]).
- **Its stock.** It holds `MARKETPLACE_FIXED_LOT_STOCK` (1) copy a cycle.

A re-roll keeps it, after the freshly rolled lots, and renews its copy ([[R1853001]]), so its owner may buy one each cycle for the rest of the game.
