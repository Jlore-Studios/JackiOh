Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). After Hearthstone's legendary music, a Legendary or Mythic card the viewer can read opens its play with a few bars of its own: its intro, the track `music-cards.json`'s `intro` names ([[R1352]]). It plays at the moment [[R204]] gives the card's play or cast line: a played card's `cardPlayed` (a Unit's or a Spell's, casts and casts on draw included), a Unit an effect puts onto the field without playing it (a token, a copy, Recruit or Reborn) on its `summoned` into a unit zone, and a Trap or Field Trap at its first firing. A Unit whose own play was heard opens nothing again on its `summoned`, a Trap's set never opens one, and a Trap that fires again (Classic+ #74 on every fuse) opens it at its first firing only. A card behind the sentinel opens nothing, so a Trap's firing opens its intro on its controller's seat alone, where [[R154]] lets the card be read, and every other card's on both seats, whose views name it ([[R203]]). The same card played again opens its intro again once the last one's music has ended, though its theme does not restart ([[R631]]); while that music runs, the intro is not restarted ([[R1351]]).

The intro plays once, at once, on the music bus: it follows the music volume, the master volume and the mute, and the page's focus, as every track does, and nothing is fetched or started while it could not be heard. It does not pass through the opponent's-turn low-pass. While its music runs, to its last bar line, every other track the music plays (the bed) is ducked to `MUSIC_INTRO_DUCK_GAIN` (0.35), dipping with `MUSIC_INTRO_DUCK_ATTACK_TC_S` and coming back with `MUSIC_INTRO_DUCK_RELEASE_TC_S` under the intro's ring-out. The theme the same card starts ([[R631]]) is asked for after its intro, on its bar line as ever, and comes in under the duck until the intro's music ends. [[R669]]'s entrance sting and the card's voice line stay as they were, and the music bus, the intro on it, still dips under voice lines and the biggest effects while `duckMusic` is on. With dynamic music off no intro plays, since nothing in the game then moves the music. Reduce motion, the panel's or the system's, does not silence it: it is sound, and the sound director resolves every event a reduced-motion burst flushes. It holds nothing: it is no voice line, so `data-speaking` and `data-animating`, which practice's AI waits on, never wait for it. The intros of the cards in the viewer's own hand are fetched ahead between animation bursts; an intro asked for is fetched at once, even mid-burst, as a voice line is.
