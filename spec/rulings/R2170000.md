Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #2170 gives every Stinkyscape entry ([[R1857000]]) procedural art of its own in place of [[R503]]'s: a space backdrop (a starfield, two or three nebulae and, on most faces, a planet, each drawn from the card's id) under one subject its family fixes, so that a family reads as one at a glance. Real art delivered for a card still wins over it, as it wins over [[R503]]'s.

**The families.** The first that fits draws: an AI card the AI's chip; a Terminus card (and Olivian Expanse) a black hole; a War Doctrine (and Firststrike Capability) its doctrine's badge; a Drone Core, or a card whose name says Drone, the set's one drone (a sphere with a glowing eye: the bare core, an Interceptor, a Corvette, a Battleship by its layer, or a hive); a Swarm card an insect ship; an Acclaimed card a pilot under a laurel; a Jlockeed card its blueprint; a Weapon, or a card whose name says a weapon, that weapon on the rack's one turret mount (a Cannon a cannon, a Repeater a gatling, Flak twin barrels and bursts, a Beam a beam emitter, a Launcher its tube and what it launches, Rounds cartridges, a Missile Pod a pod of missiles, a Ray a ray gun, RR-5000 a railgun, Trila 3 three barrels, Spacemine a mine, the Siege Tank a tank, the Tractor Beam its cone); a Miner a drill and the Miner ships a hull with one; a Resource, asteroid or comet its ore or rock; an Anomaly or Scanning Probe a vortex; a Starship Piece, the Dormant Starship or Megabase the fleet's one warship hull, or the piece (a plate, shield, pod, engine or module) before it; any other Unit a crew member; anything else a planet, a planet's horizon or a reticle, wearing its name's [[R503]] motif. A name's mark (v3, Mk2, X2, a Roman numeral) adds the weapon's chevrons, a drone core's nodes or a drill's length.

**Both faces.** A face's geometry comes from streams seeded by its id alone, so the Radiant face draws the same shapes, its paint gilded and gold rays behind its subject. No other set's card reaches this art, so no shipped card's picture changes ([[R503]]); the set's mark stays [[R1883000]]'s.
