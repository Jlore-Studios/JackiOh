Stinkyscape #112 Job Application's "Activate this to send a card from your hand in as your resume" keeps the card until its Interview's reward, "Add your resume to your hand and Buff it 5 times. It costs (0)". The spec holds a permanent beneath a card ([[R1850003]]) and no hand card; this is the same holding for a card taken from a hand.

- **Sending.** The resume is the Activate's pick from its controller's hand ([[R384]], [[R81]]), one the Activate needs ([[R703]]): with no card in hand it is not offered. `set_aside_from_hand { target }` takes that card out of its owner's hand as it is, with no reset, and holds it in `state.held` beneath the running card, which must be acting on the field on the stay its run began with; `held` reports it. A card in no hand is passed by. A unit token ceases to exist as it leaves the hand ([[R11]]): `held` still reports it, and nothing is held.
- **Seen.** While it waits it is in no pile, so both seats read it, as a Hearthstone discard is shown to the opponent; back in a hand it reads as that hand does ([[R97]]).
- **Coming back.** `return_held_to_hand { costOverride, upgrades }` gives every card the running card holds on its stay, in the order they were taken, to its owner's hand: with `costOverride` its price is reset and set to that number, and once in the hand it is Buffed `upgrades` times ([[§6.3]] Buff). A full hand burns it unbuffed ([[§2.4]]), the printed card again ([[R215]]).
- **Its holder gone.** A holder that leaves the field first, any way, has the next state check exile the card, reset to the printed card ([[R215]]), where a held permanent would be summoned ([[R1850003]]): a hand card has no claim to the field.

Rejected: exiling it as it is sent, which every card that reads or plays from exile would reach while the Interview still owes it back; keeping it hidden, when the Interview's first goal shows its cost on the card to both seats anyway.
