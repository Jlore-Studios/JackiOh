Stinkyscape #153 Stimmycheck reads "At the end of this turn, add a Stimmycheck to your hand. Its Inflation chance goes up by 5%." on both faces ([[§8.10]]), where through v0.5.11o it read 10%. The step is the engine's `INFLATION_CHANCE_STEP`, a rules number (CLAUDE.md rule 9), 5 now and 10 before. So a Stimmycheck rolls at its printed 15, the copy it adds at 20 and that copy's at 25, and a chance never goes past 100 (`next_inflation_chance`). The 15% start, the 1 mana, the (0) price, `INFLATION_COST` and the Radiant face's Lucky 1 and Radiant copy are as [[R1848006]] has them, and so is the rest of its Inflation: a lower step is a buff, since Inflation costs its caster's own cards first.

- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, whose copies carry the older step, `INFLATION_CHANCE_STEP_V0_5_11O` (10): 25 and 35 ([[R767000]]). The step is the script's argument (`next_inflation_chance_by`), so a patch to it keeps no copy of the engine's verb.

Rejected: a step of 5 on the copy alone while the copy's own copy kept adding 10, which "Its Inflation chance goes up by 5%" says nothing of; and reading 5% as a share of the chance, 15 to 15.75, where every other "goes up by N%" of a chance in the set adds points.
