When an opponent's played Unit resolves ([[§10.5]]), a captor (Prison, M #97.8) may trigger to capture it. On trigger success (rolled against `chance`), the resolving Unit is moved from the opponent's field to stack dormant beneath the captor ([[R13]]), transferring control to the captor's controller while ownership remains unchanged ([[R15]]). Summons and tokens do not trigger capture. Capturing a unit that stacked onto another uncovers the underlying unit on the opponent's field. When the captor leaves the field, the most recently captured Unit resumes on the field under the captor's controller.
