## R783013 — A used Flashback is gone for good

Built (#783). A card loses Flashback as it is replayed by it (the designer: "the card loses the
keyword after it is used the one time"): `flashbackSpent` is written on it as it leaves the graveyard,
and the layers drop the keyword, printed or given, while it is set, as they drop a used Reborn. Unlike a
used Reborn the mark is kept in every zone and through [[R78]]'s and [[R766]]'s resets, so nothing
restores it; a copy ([[R57]]) or a Transform is a new card without it. Then the card goes where any
card goes: a Spell to the graveyard, a Unit that dies later to the graveyard, with no Flashback, never
offered again; a unit-token card ceases to exist. A given Flashback lasts as long as any granted keyword
does ([[R78]]). A play from the graveyard that a
permission on the field allows (Classic #28 Second Wind) spends no Flashback, which is read as the play
is checked, before a Tribute could take the permission away. "Allows" is the play as made: a
permission covers it only if it admits the card's type, its price is at or over the permission's floor
(Radiant Second Wind's (1) or more), and it is paid as that permission lets it be. A play paid with a
permission's Plague Counters (Classic #74 Corpse Plantation) is that permission's and spends no
Flashback; a play in mana that only a Plague permission or a floor above the price would otherwise
meet is Flashback's alone and spends it.
