[[R1748000]] marked a number `cry: true` only when every line writing it, on both faces, is a Cry. A number one face's Cry reads, while the other face's lasting text reads it too, went unmarked, so a Buff on the field could still be spent on a Cry that had already resolved. M #101 Gachaholic's `lucky` is that case: its base face's Cry reads it, and its Radiant face's Activate.

Since issue #2261 such a number is marked with the face whose Cry alone reads it, `cry: "base"` or `cry: "radiant"`. While the card is on the field showing that face, a Nerf or a Buff never draws it. On the other face, and off the field, it is in reach as before. `cargo jackioh catalog check` holds the mark to the text face by face. A face whose lines writing the number are all Cries, with the other face's not, gets that face. Two faces whose lines are all Cries, or one such face where the other never writes the number, get `true`. A Patch notes change shows the mark as "tuned only off the field on the base face". Gachaholic's `lucky` is `cry: "base"`. KY's Constant is no Nerf or Buff and still reaches it.
