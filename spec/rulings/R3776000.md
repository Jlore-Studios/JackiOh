The owner's call: a unit's health number already says it is damaged, and the cracks drawn over its art covered the picture.

[[R3340000]]'s damage cracks (`ART_WEAR_LEVELS`) are removed. A Unit's art wears the buffed glow and the frozen frost, read from `UnitView`, and nothing for missing health. A whole unit, and a damaged one with no buff and no freeze, wear nothing at all: no `.cf-art-wear` element and no `data-art-damage` attribute. The code, the constant and the tests that existed only for the cracks are deleted, not left behind a flag.

These cracks stay, as they are not damage marks: Brittle's keyword cracks ([[R385]]), the Starship keel's cracks, the freeze FX's `cracks` cue, the canvas `crack` cue ([[R502]]) and the Cracked ice foil.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing in the engine, `PlayerView` or the log changes.

Rejected: keeping the cracks behind a setting, which leaves the dead code and the constant; fewer cracks, which still cover the art.
