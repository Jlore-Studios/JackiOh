S #151's fourth entry reads "Cast two of the following: Core System, Secure System, Unsecure System, Wild System".

- **Two different ones.** Two of the four drawn from the match rng without replacement ([[R60]]), cast in the order drawn.
- **Each cast** is a new card of its caster's, made, owned and cast by them ([[R453]]), a play for every rule ([[R70]]), its choices theirs; it goes to their graveyard once it resolves ([[R87]]). A cast that asks a question pauses the second, which waits for the answer ([[R113]]). The draw is kept with the entry, so the resumed entry casts the same second System.
