[[R1295000]], [[R1297000]] and [[R1655000]] dressed the viewer's own side alone: an opponent's
cards and half, a watched game and a replay drew the defaults, since the client was told no other
player's cosmetics and must never infer them. The server now sends each seat's frozen loadout
([[R1834002]], [[R1834003]]), and the client wears it. It amends [[R1295000]] (*Whose cards wear
them*), [[R1297000]] (*Whose side wears them*) and [[R1655000]] (*Who wears them*).

**Who wears what.** A board that carries loadouts (an online match the viewer plays, a watched game
and an online match's replay) dresses every seat but the viewer's own on a board they play in that
seat's loadout. The viewer's own side there still wears their own picks, judged by the client's
ownership check, as [[R1655000]] has it. On a watched or replayed game, where nobody plays, both
seats wear their loadouts. A seat's loadout dresses:

- its half of the board: its playmat under its half of the field, its backdrop behind its half, and
  its card back on its face-down cards, its hand's backs and its deck pile ([[R1297000]]);
- its cards, by their owner and never their controller, so a stolen unit keeps its owner's look
  ([[R1295000]]): each wears its card's own picks where the loadout has an entry for it, and its
  kinds' defaults where it has none or leaves a kind out, as its owner's own client draws it: the
  seat's foil, finish and border dress no card ([[R1953001]]). That holds for a card on
  the field, in a hand shown face up and in the showcase of the seat's play, and for its hover
  preview and inspect overlays, which sit below a `TheirCopy` of it. A card in the resolving strip,
  which names no owner, and every face [[R1655000]] leaves plain (a prompt's options, the piles,
  the Almanac, the homescreen) draw the defaults;
- a full art: an opponent's copy, or any copy on a watched or replayed game, prints its full art
  where its owner's loadout says so for that card and the viewer's own Full-art cards setting is
  on, and never on a tutorial lesson's board ([[R1108000]]).

The client reads a loadout as untrusted input: an id it does not know draws as its kind's default,
and a malformed frame dresses nothing. It asks nothing of ownership, since the server judged every
pick against its own account before it froze it ([[R1834001]]), so the client still infers no
other player's ownership. The rarity rules hold on every face, whoever's loadout it wears: a Mythic
face wearing its rarity foil ([[R2721000]]) wears no cosmetic foil, a Radiant face no foil, finish or border ([[R277]]), and a card back is
its seat's alone and says nothing of its card (`Card.test.tsx`'s B16).

**Show opponents' cosmetics.** A switch of the display settings' visuals tab, on by default and kept
in the `cards` settings group with the Full-art cards setting, so it roams with the account; its
reset turns it back on. Off, every seat but the one the board is seen from draws the defaults: on
a board the viewer plays and on the replay of a match they played, every seat but theirs; on a
watched game, every seat but the friend's they watch. It changes only what this viewer draws:
nothing is sent, and the other player still sees their own look and this viewer's.

**Where nothing changes.** Practice keeps [[R1310004]]'s every cosmetic counted as owned for the
viewer's side, and the practice AI's side draws the defaults. Hotseat is unchanged: its development
decks carry no picks, and each seat's own side wears the device's as before. Duos, a downloaded
replay file and the shared GIF carry no loadout ([[R1834002]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a
replay hash, and no rule reads it.

Hearthstone semantics: both players see each other's card back and each card's version, and a
player may turn other players' cosmetics off for themselves.

Rejected: printing an opponent's full art whatever the viewer's Full-art cards setting says, which
would leave a viewer who turned full art off seeing it on the other side only; drawing only the
defaults on a replay or a watched game with the setting off, which would strip a player replaying
their own match of their own look, where the switch keeps the viewer's side whatever it says (a
watched game keeps the seat it is seen from by the same rule); and a setting per kind, where one
switch answers the one wish to see opponents plain.
