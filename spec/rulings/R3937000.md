[[§4.2]] step 3 makes an attacker target a standing Taunt, but the attacking player picks which attacker goes first. The AI's face threat (`face_threat`, [[§9.9]], through `damage_past_taunts_with`) sent the opponent's attackers at the Taunts smallest first, and spent an attacker the Taunt stopped whole as though it had attacked. So the issue's board, a Defense Position guard at 3 health and Armor 1 against attackers of 3, 3 and 4, counted 4 where the opponent deals 6 (the 4 breaks the guard, both 3s go face), and at 6 life [[R2976000]]'s `wall_switches` looked for no wall.

The count is now the larger of two orders, each walked over the Taunts as before:

- smallest first: the attackers that may not hit the hero, then the rest, each group ascending by attack, as before;
- the same, except that at each Taunt the first attacker in that order that destroys it alone goes in first.

An attacker destroys a Taunt alone when no Divine Shield takes its hit ([[§4.4]] step 1), the Taunt's First Strike does not destroy it first ([[§4.3]]), and what its hit deals past the Taunt's Armor ([[R346]]: all of it when the hit pierces) is the Taunt's health or more, or any of it from a Poisonous attacker. The second walk runs only when the first met a Taunt whose lone breaker had a smaller attacker ahead of it; otherwise the two orders are the same walk.

In both orders an attacker the Taunt would stop whole waits instead of being spent: one whose hit the Taunt's Armor takes whole ([[R1361]]; a standing Divine Shield still takes such a hit, so it still goes in to pop one), and one the Taunt's First Strike destroys before it hits. A waiting attacker lands no hit and no Cleave splash ([[R63]]). When the Taunt is destroyed, the waiting attackers are free for the next Taunt or the hero (a Reborn Taunt's body binds them again); while it stands they stay bound to it.

The flag `best_attack_order_past_taunts` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick keeps the old walk. It reaches everything that reads the walk: the threat and pressure terms, `wall_switches`, the lethal solver's `ready_gap` and the learned features' `face_threat`, which moves as [[R3793000]]'s flag moved it, without a refit.

Rejected:

- the lone breaker first as the only order, as the issue proposed: on a full-health Defense guard (4 health, Armor 1) against four 2s and a 9 it counts 8, where the 2s break the guard and the 9 goes face for 9;
- a search over the orders, since the count runs at every evaluation;
- keeping the learned features on the old count, which would need a second walk at every evaluation for one input.

Not modelled:

- a choice among several standing Taunts, which are still walked from the least health and Armor up;
- a Cleave attacker sent at an armored Taunt only for its splash, which now waits instead;
- a Taunt's strike back that destroys an attacker with a second swing to come ([[R636]]), whose swings still count, as before.

It reads only public state, and it adds no rule of the game and no constant.
