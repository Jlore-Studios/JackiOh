[[§9.10]] and [[R2205003]] gave lesson 1's AI deck "units with no keyword", and the M13 audit (#2206, REVIEW B-5) found it holding Aura and Death units, which lessons 3 and 4 teach. Every card of a lesson is a Training Grounds card and an AI deck holds 12 distinct cards ([[R291]]), but the set has only three units with no text at all, TG #1 Spikey Steven, TG #8 Clobberer and TG #43 Mr. Vanilla. So lesson 1's AI deck holds those three and nine units with no keyword whose only text is a Cry, the ability lesson 2 teaches first: a Cry is one the AI plays and the board shows, and it asks nothing of the player. No Aura, Death, Trap or Spell reaches it. The default of the fixer, as the owner's brief makes no choice here; the owner may rule otherwise. Rejected: copies of the three (against [[R291]]'s distinct cards), and new vanilla Training Grounds units (a designer's call, not a fix).
