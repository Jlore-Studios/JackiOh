Classic #4 Palantir answers the opponent's play of a Spell whose own cost per [[R65]] is (2), where [[R831001]] had it answer the (1) Cost ones. Its words are "When your opponent plays a (2) Cost Spell, Tribute this to steal and cast it." (Radiant: "… steal and cast it. Once it has stolen {threshold}, Tribute this.") and the one number that moves is the cost it answers, 1 to 2. Everything [[R831001]] ruled stands: the announce window and the traps it wakes ([[R448]]), the counter into the thief's hand and the cast ([[R70]]) whose every choice is random and aimed ([[R452]], [[R656]]), the hand cap, the Radiant face's count and its threshold of 3, and the draw limit, which is the Aura and does not look at costs.

- **The cost is the Spell's own, out of play.** A Spell printed at (2) is answered, and so is one a `costMod` has made (2) ([[R65]]: a Degrade changes the card, not a price for a play); a Spell printed at (2) that a cost change lowers to (1) is not. A (1) Cost Spell, a (0) Cost one and an X Spell, which counts 0 ([[R396]]), are played as usual, so a Book of Knowledge or a Stockpile at its printed (1) now resolves for its own player, and a Palantir standing in front of it keeps its Tribute.
- **A Spell with a choice is cast with a random one.** Classic #70 Book of Plague, a (1) Cost Spell that offers two modes, is stolen only when made dear; its thief casts it with a mode drawn at random ([[R452]]), counters on the one permanent a prompt would have named or three Plague cards in the thief's hand.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, whose only difference is the cost it answers ([[R767000]]).
