While Tipsy is up, how does the engine choose what blurs, who sees it, and when does it clear?

A card the Tipsy player newly plays, summons or draws is stamped with the counter as it stood then —
whose, how high, on which turn — read before the arriving card's own Tipsy counts, so the played
card carries the pre-play level. While the counter is above 0, the stamped card may have one of its
words blurred on its owner's view alone: one whitespace word of its name, its cost, its attack, its
health (units only), or one keyword kind it carries, in that order. The chance per card is ten points
a Tipsy level (`TIPSY_BLUR_CHANCE_PER_STACK`), capped at fifty (`TIPSY_BLUR_CHANCE_CAP`). The pick is
drawn from a stream of the game's seed and the card's instance and stamped turn — never from the
game's `Rng`, which never moves for a blur — so a reload or a replay shows the same blur. The stamp is
dropped by R78's reset as the card leaves the field; a stamp older than the counter's latest rise
stays clear, so falling to 0 clears every blur and a risen counter blurs only what arrived since.
Going from 2 to 1 clears nothing: only the odds for new cards fall. The client renders the blur and
never changes a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Rejected: drawing the blur from the game's `Rng`: two views of the same state would blur differently, a reload
would reshuffle every blur, and the rng cursor would move for a purely cosmetic read.
