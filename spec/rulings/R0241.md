[[§6.2]]'s "End of turn" is the controller's turn end, and a delayed effect a card arms for it (#39's copies, #78's exile) is the end of the turn the card was played on. One armed on the other player's turn — the card was cast on draw there ([[R70]]) — has no end of its controller's turn to wait for, so it is not armed at all: waiting for the next one would run it at the end of a later turn the card was never played on, when its own "this turn" riders have long ended ([[§2.2]]) and #39 would read a turn log it was never in ([[R71]]). [[R155]] keeps a return Spell cast then in the graveyard for the same reason. Setup is no player's turn either (it runs at turn 0, BUILD M1-T1, though the engine names p1 active there until p1 takes the first turn, [[§2.1]] step 5), so a clause a card cast during setup makes — a mulligan's replacement draw cast it — is not armed, for either seat alike. A clause armed on its controller's own turn, even as that turn's delayed effects resolve, still waits for its point as [[R68]] says
