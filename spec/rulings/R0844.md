"Lose all mana next N turns" (Meditative #18 Expedition12, #19 Expedition123) means the refresh of
each of your next N turns gives 0 mana ([[§2.3]]), and the next-turn rider is spent with it. Extra
turns count among the N. Mana gained during such a turn still adds. Overlapping losses keep the
latest end. Since #1563 neither card loses all its mana: #18 loses (2) next turn and #19 (2) next turn, then
(3) the turn after, each through the next-turn rider ([[§2.3]]); the engine keeps this rule for any
text that loses all its mana, and a game played before #1563 folds as it did ([[R767000]]).
