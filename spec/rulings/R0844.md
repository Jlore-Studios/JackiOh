"Lose all mana next N turns" (Meditative #18 Expedition12, #19 Expedition1234) means the refresh of
each of your next N turns gives 0 mana ([[§2.3]]), and the next-turn rider is spent with it. Extra
turns count among the N. Mana gained during such a turn still adds. Overlapping losses keep the
latest end.
