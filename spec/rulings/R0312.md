A library card with no record counts as unknown, and a client draws it as a card back. That is every card of a library Pocket Chaos swapped (#87, [[R73]]): its new owner never saw it, and what its old owner was shown goes with neither, so a library swapped back is unknown too. It is also every card Transmogulate puts in a library (#83, [[R35]]), a new instance that the `transformed` event names to nobody ([[R177]]). A card stays unknown until it leaves the library, even one a prompt has since shown its owner (#51's options): the list may show less than its owner could piece together, never more. A path that forgets to write the record shows a back, never a card. The practice AI hides the same cards from its own seat when it redacts the state it decides from ([[R185]])
