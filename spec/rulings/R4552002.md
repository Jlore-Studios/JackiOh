**Why.** A token's arrival and its maker's play are always in the same turn, so no regression can split them: the share either gets is its prior's, which means nothing.

**The maker.** The AI's fold ([[R4552000]]) follows every Created instance (R943) through the whole log, so no instance carries a new field. A Created card on the field names its maker: the card whose effect summoned it (a summon's `sourceId`, [[R1088]]) or else the card whose play or trigger was resolving as it appeared (a Field Spell an effect cast, S+ #61.1 Shawarma Law from S+ #61 Jayaad's Shawarma Law). A Created card a hand held first names none: playing it was the seat's decision (The Coin, S #8.1 Rosemary Triscuit).

**The credit.**

- A Created card a hand held and played has its own play term, its play turns counted as its seat's `createdPlayed` ([[R2089000]]), and its Swing counts the turns those copies then act.
- A Created card an effect put onto the field or cast has no play term. Its arrival is in its maker's play turn, so the maker's Decisiveness carries it (Jayaad's carries the Law's first end of turn); the card reads Board swing for every turn it acts, and its Decisiveness off those acting turns, its Board swing in place of a play term.
- The maker's Swing adds, per play, the turns each card it made acted times that card's Board swing ([[R4552001]]). Those turns are kept on the maker's own row: its deck row when its seat's deck holds it, else its Created row (a token a token made).

Created cards are read against the Created field, their own term spreads and their own typical Decisiveness, apart from the deck cards' ([[R4082000]]). The Coin, Glitch and the Dormant Starship, which a rule makes, are read against the deck cards' and count toward neither field ([[R3070000]], [[R4082000]]).

**Rejected.** **A field on the card instance naming its maker**: the fold replays the whole log and knows it, and a new instance field would change every state hash.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
