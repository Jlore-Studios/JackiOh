The designer's answers on #271 (MaxGoetzmann, 2026-10-05): it can never become Book of Wildfire or the Book it is now, and it can become Book of Flame; a Book it becomes takes that Book's text entirely, keeping only the end-of-turn swap; a Radiant Wildfire always becomes a Radiant Book; and the faces' whole text is "Deal {damage} damage. Becomes a different [Radiant] Book at the end of your turn.", which the faces print in [[R366]]'s house wording for a turn trigger (patch v0.2.1's vocabulary table): "Deal {damage} damage." and, on a line of its own, "End of turn: Become a different Book." ("… a different Radiant Book." on the Radiant face). Wildfire keeps its own text (its hit) and adds the swap. At the end of its owner's turn (that turn's `turnEnded`, which hand triggers answer once the end-of-turn triggers have run, and before cleanup), a Wildfire in that player's hand is Replaced ([[§6.3]], [[R35]]) by a Book drawn with the match rng from [[§5.1]]'s pool: every non-token card tagged Book of every set ([[R380]]), Book of Flame included, but never Wildfire and never the Book it is now. Not at the end of the opponent's turn, and not from the library, graveyard or anywhere else. The new Book is on the old card's face, so a Radiant Wildfire becomes the Radiant face of the Book; it takes the old card's place in the hand (a Replace in a hand keeps the hand's order, as in a library), and the opponent is not told which Book ([[R177]]). The new Book has that Book's own text, and the swap rides it as an enchantment (`swapsBook`, B5 E39) that grants the same hand trigger, so it changes again at every end of its owner's turn while it stays in hand. It is otherwise a new card, as any Transform result: its cost changes and other enchantments stay behind, except that a Temporary card's new Book is Temporary too ([[R637]]), so a Temporary Wildfire (C+ #54) is still discarded at cleanup. A Book it has become shows its own text, with the swap as a badge in the same words ("End of turn: Become a different [Radiant] Book", [[R432]])
