A practice seed ([[§14.8]]) is the account's own: a claim of another account's seed, or of one the
server never issued, is an unknown seed. The first claim of a seed burns it, whether it pays or
is refused, before the server checks the game, so no seed pays twice and two claims racing for one
are one claim. A seed unclaimed `PRACTICE_SEED_TTL_SECONDS` after it was issued has lapsed, and a
claim of it pays nothing. A game resumed after a reload ([[R668]]) is the same game, so it claims
on the seed it began on: the device keeps, with the rest of its setup, that it is on a server
seed. A game left before it ends claims nothing and pays nothing, and the account's next seed
request deletes every seed of its issued more than `PRACTICE_SEED_TTL_SECONDS` ago, claimed or
not.

Hearthstone semantics: a reward is granted once per game; a game abandoned is a game unfinished,
and earns nothing.
