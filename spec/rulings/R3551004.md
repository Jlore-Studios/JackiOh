Two shipped cards print "after this kills a Unit it may attack again". What do they print now, and what joins the random-keyword pool? Both print the Frenzy keyword: Tmack Trav "Rush, Frenzy" (Radiant "Rush, Pierce, Frenzy"), Classic Golem "Rush, First Strike, Trample, Frenzy" (Radiant "Rush, Trample, Divine Shield, Frenzy"), dropping "If it dies, this may attack again" ([[R3543003]] runs the keyword as their old hooks did). Both carry no attack hook of their own anymore — the Golem keeps its transform hook — and the AI prices them through the keyword. Frenzy joins the random-keyword pool as its fifteenth entry, so a Buff, Charm or Keenus Aura may grant "Frenzy: this may attack again" as a combat keyword. Through v0.5.13g their `after_attack` hooks ran it.

Rejected: Keeping the hooks beside the keyword: two wordings would answer one kill, and a Nerf of the keyword would leave the hook attacking.
