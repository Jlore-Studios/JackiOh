[[§13.3]] moves each duel's turn counter once a round, and a duel whose turn ends by itself
([[R456]]) counts its round seat as ended while the duel's new active player waits for the round to
close. A turn can end as it begins: C+ #26 Tommy Tempo drawn at its start, at the round's close or
during a turn the close began. One step then moves that duel two turns, and the next round opened on
its new active player, which put that duel a turn ahead of the other for the rest of the game: the
two humans never acted together again, and that duel reached the turn cap a round early.

- **A round's turn in a duel is the next turn that duel has begun, in order.** One the duel has
  already moved past opens the round with its seat counted as ended and its End turn already reached,
  so the duel waits out that round while the other plays it, and the two are in step again after it.
  Round 1's first turns were already read this way.
- **The view names each duel's round seat** (`roundSeats`), which is public as each duel's turn is.
  The client's round line and teammate strip follow it, not the duel's active player, which can be a
  turn ahead of the round; the waiting notice shows whenever the seat has ended ([[§13.6]]).
