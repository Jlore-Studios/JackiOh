C #78 is a Field Spell (the designer wrote Spell), since an Activate ability needs a card that stays on the field, as [[§5.3]] corrected #68's type. Its Activate ♾️ ([[R384]]) removes a Plague Counter from a permanent, a target declared with the activation; then an enemy permanent is exiled, a backrow card of its controller's draws them 2 (4 on the Radiant face), and a Unit of theirs makes a forced attack on a random enemy, hero or unit ([[R53]]; twice on the Radiant face). The Radiant face fuses the enemy permanent onto a card of its controller's of that type which they choose — on the field, in their hand or in their deck, the deck's options shown to them only ([[§10.8]]) — the enemy card ceasing to exist ([[R77]]), or exiles it when there is none
