The friends drawer ([[R3728001]]) and the Friends page's first tab list the friends in groups, in this order, each with its count: **Requests** (the incoming ones, Accept and Decline), **In a game**, **Online**, and **Offline**, collapsed until opened (and open while a search is typed). An empty group is not shown. A search box over the list filters the friends by name, whatever the group. Within a group the server's order (by name) holds.

Each friend's row says what they are doing in words (the status line: "In a game · turn 7", "In a game", "In a series", "In a co-op game", "In a lobby", "Online", "Last seen 3 h ago", or nothing for an offline friend with no heartbeat on record; [[R3732001]]) and carries one primary button chosen by their state: **Watch** for a friend in a game the player may watch ([[R2615000]]), **Join** for an online friend with a lobby open ([[R3730000]]), **Challenge** for any other online friend, and **Daily** (a daily challenge, [[§9.13]]) for everyone else, an offline friend and a friend in a game that cannot be watched among them. None is the Primary button: the screen's one primary is its Add. Everything else is in the row's **⋯** menu: Chat (with its unread badge), Daily challenge, Invite to lobby ([[R3730005]], [[R3874000]], not for an offline friend), Watch (when the primary is not Watch), Remove (whose Undo is [[R2119000]]'s) and Block (which still asks first). The menu is a layer of its own: Escape and Back close it alone, a press outside closes it, and the focus returns to its ⋯, as [[R3221000]] has every layer.

Chat opens as the drawer does, beside the screen over 1024 px and over it below ([[R3728001]]), in the place of the list. `/together` is **Friends**, with five tabs by `?section=`: Friends (the list above, the add form, and the note that friends may always watch), Requests (in and out, and the challenges the player sent), Recently played ([[R3732000]]), Blocked and Shared with me (`#shared` still names it). The co-op panel leaves the page: a co-op lobby is made and joined from `/play`, beside the duel's, and `/together?coop=` still opens a lobby as it did ([[R3730000]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): which button shows is read off the server's facts and never enforced. Steam's and Discord's friends lists group by status and show one action a row, which this follows. Amends [[R3728001]], whose drawer held the list as it was, and [[R3730005]], whose Lobby button is now the menu's Invite to lobby.

Rejected: one button per action on every row, the issue's complaint; and showing every group open, which buries the online friends under the offline ones.
