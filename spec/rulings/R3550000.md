A summon onto the enemy side, or a played enemy Unit once it resolves, is attacked by a forced attack: no exertion, Can't attack ignored, no window ([[R121]]), [[§4.2]] step 2 holding as for any forced attack. Rejected: a declared attack and a prompt, which would let the summon's controller answer what the card answers at once.
