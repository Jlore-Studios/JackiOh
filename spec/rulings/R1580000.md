[[R1577000]] gave the landing a Shop door to `/shop`, which showed the Jlore shop alone, while the
card packs the account holds, the tokens and the cosmetic packs sat behind a toggle on the
account screen. `/shop` is now the one place for spending and opening. It amends [[R1310000]] and
[[R1577000]].

**The panels.** `/shop` holds, in this order: the shop and its wallet ([[§14.1]], [[§14.3]]), the
card packs with their pity, odds and the opening ([[§14.4]]), the cosmetic packs ([[§14.6]]) and
the tokens ([[§14.5]]). Each panel reads for itself and says why in its place when its read fails,
and when one changes the account the others read again: a pack bought is held and can be opened
at once, and an opening's bars show in the picker. The body scrolls and the page does not, on a
desktop or on a phone ([[R1579000]]). The opening of a pack still plays full screen, over the
Shop.

**Pity** shows once, next to the card packs held and apart from the odds. One counter set counts
every card pack ([[R1298000]]) and a cosmetic pack has none ([[R1306000]]), so a line per pack
would read as a counter per pack. The panel says so, and every number in it is the server's
(CLAUDE.md rule 7): no price, odds or pity rule changed.

**The account screen** offers none of these: no packs, no opening and no pity. It linked to the
Shop instead until [[R1646000]]; the landing's Shop door ([[R1577000]]) is the way in. No address moved, so there is no redirect (CLAUDE.md rule 13). `packsShown` is
gone: the Shop door of [[R1577000]] already opens `/shop` to every active account, and the server
decides what it answers.

Hearthstone semantics: packs are bought and opened from the main menu, never from the account
settings.

Rejected: a hub of doors, since the Shop is one short screen and a door more is a click more; a
pity line per pack, which reads as per-pack counters; keeping `packsShown`, which gated a toggle
that no longer exists; and a toggle left on `/account`, which would be a second way in to the same
panels.
