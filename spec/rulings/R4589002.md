R4589002 (what the recursion cards read in [[R4589000]]'s log): IA #105 Callback takes the newest
entry whose type is Spell — a Field Spell is none. IA #113 Rerun takes the last N Spells that are
not Rerun, oldest first, each cast new on its recorded face ([[R57]]) with every choice random
([[R452]]), inside `RANDOM_CAST_CHAIN_CAP` ([[R453]]). IA #120 Eternal Return shuffles every logged
play but itself, with its discount and face, up to the library cap ([[R80]], [[R316]]). IA #107
Recursive Call counts only the earlier Recursive Calls: the log's entries with its own id, minus
this resolution. IA #114 Ouroboros Engine's graveyard, read as its Death runs, includes itself.
With nothing to read each does nothing ([[R129]]); costs read now ([[R65]]).
