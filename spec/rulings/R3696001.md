Once the AI casts Mind Games, the opponent it imagines holds the Fortify Mind the cast handed it, a card it watched arrive, so the reply's first step tries playing it. That step compared each play with the position as it stood, where a winning guess changes nothing the evaluation reads (the reward it cancels is a delayed entry), a tie nothing at all and a loss its penalty, so the imagined opponent kept the card, and its turn's cleanup then discarded it into all three penalties ([[R862]]). So the AI overrated Mind Games whenever a line casting it reached the reply, and would have on nearly every board once the search kept it a place there ([[R3696000]]). No Hearthstone rule is involved: the search changes no game.

**What changes.** While the opponent holds a hand card the seat can see (not one of the cards it held unseen when the decision began) that its own turn's cleanup discards, Temporary with a count of 1 left ([[R637]], [[R2179001]]), `known_play` judges each play, and standing still, by the opponent's evaluation of the position once it then ends its turn, so a play that keeps the card out of that cleanup is weighed against what the cleanup does; where the end of turn is refused, the position itself is judged. A card whose link names a secret of the seat's that is not revealed (a Fortify Mind's guess, [[R860]], [[R865]]) is tried with one of its guesses, not all three: `one_guess_per_card` draws it on its own stream (`AI_GUESS_SEED`, the world's seed and the card's id) before anything is weighed, so which guess is tried never depends on the secret's choice, and whether it wins, ties or loses ([[R864]]) is then what the play does on that world. A revealed secret is no guess: every play of the card is tried, as before. The other steps of the reply compare as they did.

**Rejected.** Weighing all three guesses and taking the best: it would read the secret through the evaluation ([[R185]]). Averaging them: three times the nodes for one card. Drawing the guess from the decision's own `options.rng`: it would move that stream, and every later decision with it. Judging every step of the reply after the turn's end: one more simulation for every try, where only a card about to be discarded is weighed wrong. Two accepted limits. Whether to play the drawn guess at all is judged on the world where the secret is real, but a loss (one penalty) beats the discard (all three), so only a Radiant Fortify Mind, which has no discard clause, could be kept on that knowledge. And on each world the seat's own choice for its Mind Games meets that world's one guess, so the choice it makes follows the guesses of the worlds its line is scored on (each world's seed draws its own), not a guess it cannot know.
