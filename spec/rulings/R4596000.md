Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #4596 gives the single-player Infinite Assault set its look and its sound while [[R4577000]] holds it, as [[R3572000]] gave Stinkyscape+'s, so nothing here reaches a deck: [[R1846001]] puts no single-player card in one.

**The mark and the accent** ([[R503]]). A face of the set keeps the red infinity loop [[R4577009]] drew, and wears an accent of its own over its frame, `data-set-accent="glitch"`: its name split into red and cyan as a glitching screen splits it, and a red and a cyan edge either side of its picture's window, on base and Radiant faces alike. A full-art, art-first or extended-art face keeps its own window, and a Glitch face its own look. No other set wears an accent.

**The chips** ([[R630]]). The set's eight doctrines (Hallucination, Overclock, Batch, Recursion, Jailbreak, Firewall, Scraper, Singularity) and Commander are Almanac chips, after Automa-ζ and before Token, each with its own id (`db-filter-tag-hallucination`). They are never the deck builder's, since no deck holds a single-player card ([[R1846001]]). The chips show the tags of the cards the Almanac shows, so they appear once it shows the set.

**The signatures** ([[R3332000]]). Each of the twelve Commanders (#217 to #239, odd numbers) boots onto the board with an entrance of its own: a prop of its doctrine in its Tech look, a burst and a ring at its slam, a beat of its own and one shake, a Mythic's after its takeover's hold. Each of the eight doctrine Mythics (#48 to #216, each doctrine's last card) has a cast of its own. A Mythic Spell's signature ends within its ceiling, its hold included, so each cast draws on its own play alone and leaves what its effect reports to that effect's rows. The set's other twenty-four Legendaries stay owed their signatures. Three Field Spells the foundation left without one take a cast and the set's glitch aura (#95 The Botnet, #160 Zero Trust, #183 Shadow Library), as does #238 Critical Temperature. #237 Thermal Runaway's End of turn takes a bolt in place of a nova, whose shake no signature may make. A Mythic's tile of a set with no showcase family, the Assault's, wears circuitry, the machine's own printing ([[R3342000]]).

**The intros** ([[R1352]]). [[R1352]] gives intros to the sets that ship, which a single-player set never joins, so it is silent here. By Hearthstone, a boss's Legendary has its own play music, so a single-player set takes intros as any set does. The set's 44 Legendaries and Mythics each have their own, derived from the card, all in the ai family (every card carries AI). The set's glint is the synth "brightness", a glassy digital bell, and each Mythic's intro outlasts every Legendary's ([[R2722000]]). The music budget grows by three whole MiB (51 to 54), in `MUSIC_BUDGET_BYTES` and `gen-music.mjs` alike.

**Its voices.** The set's voice lines are written in `card-audio.json5` and `card-text.yaml`. `gen-voice` renders no file for a held set, so their rendering is owed by the release, as it was for every held set.
