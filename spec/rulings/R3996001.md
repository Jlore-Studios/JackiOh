Issue #3996 made S+ #9 Drone Assembler v4 "Activate: Summon a Drone Interceptor to attack an enemy. Has an extra turn cooldown." (twice a turn with no cooldown through v0.6.5b) and S+ #10 Drone Assembler v5 "Activate 2: Summon a Drone Interceptor to attack an enemy. Has an extra turn cooldown." (a Drone Corvette once a turn with no cooldown through v0.6.5b); each Radiant face summons the Radiant Drone Interceptor. The cooldown is [[R1841000]]'s, on top of the uses a turn ([[R384]]): every use starts it, and the turn of the use is not one it sits out, so v5 makes both its uses in one turn. On the next of its user's turns the Activate is refused, "that ability is cooling down this turn", and the turn after that its uses are back; a turn with one use of v5 starts it as well. The target and the drone are [[R3537000]]'s and [[R3537001]]'s. Games of v0.6.5b and before fold under the kept scripts ([[R767000]]). Rejected: starting v5's cooldown only once both uses are spent, which the text does not say.
