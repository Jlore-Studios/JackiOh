A practice claim ([[§14.8]]) passes only when every action of the AI's in its log is the one the
practice AI takes there: `decide` on the AI's own stream (`"{seed}:ai"`, kept for the whole game),
at `AI_BUDGET` and with no clock, reading the game only through its redaction ([[R185]]); then,
when that decision is one [[R188]] forbids or the engine refuses, the worker's fallback, End turn
first. Each action is checked from the seat its player plays at that step ([[R677]]). The
browser's wall-clock cap ([[§9.9]]) is not replayed: a decision it cut short to a different move
makes the claim pay nothing, though the game stands as played. A game on a server seed plays under
the far longer cap of [[R2208000]], which is not replayed either.

Rejected: replaying a node count the client logs with each decision. The client could then log
a tiny count and play a weakened AI; a server re-run at the full budget is the only AI a paying
game is played against.

Hearthstone semantics: no reward is granted for a game the server cannot show was played against
its own opponent.

Superseded by issue #2560 ([[R2560000]]): the server no longer re-runs a claim's AI moves; an AI
action is held only to its nonce, its seat and the engine's acceptance, as the human's are.
