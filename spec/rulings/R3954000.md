Why: at a phone's size and at 1280×720 the lobby's seats fill the screen in co-op and 2v2 ([[R3731000]]), so Ready ([[R3730001]]), Leave and the host's Start fell below the fold, and on a co-op phone Ready sat half under the tab bar ([[R3728002]]). The menus' brief is one primary action per screen, never hidden.

Rule: Leave, the member's Ready and the host's Start share one bar that sticks to the foot of the screen, clear of a phone's tab bar and the bottom inset (the shell's `--shell-bottom`), at every menu size ([[R3728000]]) and in every format. The seats and settings scroll behind it; the Verdict stays beside the deck picker. The mark in "Ready to queue" has a space after it.

Rejected: compact seat cards on a narrow or short window, because four seats and the settings do not fit a phone's height even compact, and a size-by-size layout is two rules where a bar is one. A `position: fixed` bar, because it ignores the shell's offset and would cover the tab bar.

Client only: no Hearthstone rule is involved.
