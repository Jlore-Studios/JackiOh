S+ #103 Transcend, #104 Tireless Cultivation and #105 Spacersi "Transform ... into ones that cost (1) more" (Radiant (2) more), and the spec has no Transform by price. Each card the effect reaches is Replaced in place ([[R35]]) with no Cry ([[R691]]) by a card of its own type: one drawn with the match rng from the non-token cards of the shipped sets ([[R380]], [[R1420]]) of that type that cost exactly its price plus the step ([[R65]]: printed prices, an X cost read as 0, an embiggen card at its base price, the old card's own cost changes on the field ignored), never the card running the effect ([[R387]]). With none at that price it takes one of the cheapest price above it, a random one among ties; with none dearer at all it stays as it is ([[R129]]). It keeps its Radiant face. An Immutable card refuses ([[R23]]), a Dormant card is passed by ([[R1839000]]), and a card beneath a Stack pile is in no scope. Transcend reaches the caster's permanents, Spacersi's Cry their other Units, and #104 the one permanent chosen. #104 is a Spell, the designer's list giving no type, with Temporary (1, Radiant 2, the designer's: the catalog prints no count, so the Radiant face's card gives itself Temporary 2 as it returns, the larger entry counting, [[R2179001]]) and "End of turn: return this to your hand" as Core #23 Reoccurring Dream's. The return is an end-of-turn step, so [[R637]]'s cleanup after the steps counts the returned card down in the same turn end: the base face's Temporary 1 discards it again at once (a discard, it ends in the graveyard), the Radiant face's Temporary 2 leaves it in the hand with one turn left.

Rejected: a Temporary that does not count the card it returned; a pool of every type; the nearest price below when none is above; a base card from a Radiant one; and the printed price of the old card without its cost changes.
