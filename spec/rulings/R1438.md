"Give it Lucky X" (Meditative #101 Gachaholic, the `lucky` rider of Add to hand, [[§6.3]]) gives the card a granted Lucky X, put on once the card is in a hand, as [[R637]]'s Temporary is: a card a full hand burns gets none, and no event announces it. The given Lucky is the card's own. It is written on the instance, kept while the card is in the hand, ridden onto the field as every keyword granted in a hand is ([[§10.4]], E38) and through a Spell's resolution, and shown on the card's face to its owner. It goes when the card leaves the field ([[R78]]), or when a card in a hand or a Spell reaches a graveyard or an exile pile ([[R215]]). It adds to any Lucky the card has, Lucky being a numbered keyword whose values sum, so Lucky 1 given to a Lucky 1 card makes Lucky 2, its printed Lucky read as tuning leaves it ([[R386]]). So every Luck-based card's roll ([[R1437]]) reads the Lucky on its instance, printed plus given (`lucky_on`), never a number fixed on its face: given Lucky 1, a base face with no Lucky rolls twice and keeps the better, a coin flip included ([[R1440]]), and [[R902]]'s Peptides roll counts it. Two shipped cards keep the printed Lucky their rolls always read, untouched by tuning, and add only the Lucky given (`given_lucky_on`): Core #23 Reoccurring Dream and Core #42 Eugenics, whose Radiant faces rolled a fixed Lucky 1 before this ruling, so no shipped game changes (D14, [[R1440]]).
