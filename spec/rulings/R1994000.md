**Why.** The totals keep every card figure for good ([[R1044000]]), but nothing of a game as a whole: who went first and won, how it ended, how many turns it ran and [[R1256000]]'s counts of fatigue, Traps, position switches and Radiant upgrades lived only in a record's detail, which [[R1044001]] empties a week after it is folded. Those figures existed only offline, in `cargo jackioh stats report --games` over exported files, so a seat decision such as [[R1156000]]'s had no figure from people's games older than a week.

**The totals.** Each record folded also folds its whole game under its records key (patch, source, mode, both seats' pilots): games, draws, the games the seat that took the first turn won, games per ending, games per turn count, and, over the records whose both seats carry all six of [[R1256000]]'s counts, their sums and how many records they are. Folding stays commutative, so records folded in any order or in pieces make the same totals. A win is the seat the record names, as `stats report --games` always read it, so a fled game is the fleer's ([[R1843004]]), and a draw is a game nobody won. The store keeps them in `game_totals` (migration 0063), server-only like the other totals, written with the card totals in the same transaction, so the figures outlive the purge.

**One computation.** The game figures are read off those totals by the engine (`game_figures_of`), and `stats report --games` folds its records and reads them the same way (`game_report`), so the tool and the server cannot count differently: the first seat's share with its 95% Wilson interval, the endings, the turn quartiles by nearest rank, and the fatigue, Trap, switch and upgrade figures over the counted records, unknown rather than zero when none carries the counts. The analysis fixtures' committed reports are what the command prints.

**The route and the panel.** `GET /api/stats/games?patch=&mode=` is public and cached `CARD_STATS_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS`, kept with the cards' windowed report. It reads the window every card shares at the patch ([[R1367000]]) under [[R654]]'s gate as `GET /api/stats/cards` does: below `PUBLIC_STATS_MIN_LIVE_GAMES` live games its source is provisional and AI games pad it. A game is read whole, whoever piloted its seats, and `pairs` splits the same figures by both seats' pilots, so the games against a ladder bot read apart from people's ([[R807000]]). `totalGames` counts every record of the window, those folded before migration 0063 among them. The Cards tab of `/stats` opens with a Game figures panel for the picked patch and mode: the first player's wins with their margin (half the interval), the median game's turns and the share of games that reach fatigue ([[R2446000]]); below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games it says only that there are not enough games on the patch yet, and when it counts fewer games than the patch's it says how many of them and why. It shows no source and no gate ([[R661]]), and sits in the scrolling panel, not the fixed top ([[R1646000]]).

**Not done.** No back-fill: a record folded before migration 0063 counts in the patch's games and not in its game figures, and only a week of them could have been read back, by a second fold path with a flag on each record; the panel says where its figures start instead. The pairs are the seats' pilots, p1's first, not who went first.

Rejected: a single version's figures, which would count fewer games than the cards beside them after a micro; one column per count, which still needs the read, add and write back in Rust for the ending and turn maps; redefining `?pilot=` for whole games, where a seat filter has no meaning; the fixed top or a fourth tab for the panel; and holding the fixtures in the engine's tests, which would run every Rust group whenever a notebook fixture changes.

Amended by [[R2446000]]: the Game figures panel no longer shows the set Traps that fired; `GET /api/stats/games`, `game_figures_of` and `stats report --games` still carry them.

Amended by [[R2567000]]: the first seat's share and its interval are over the games somebody won, not every game, and are unknown when every game was drawn.
