C+ #22's "is healed" is read as "would be healed" (a replacement, [[§6.2]]), so the heal that sets it off is converted too; otherwise the first heal would always escape it. From then, for the rest of the turn (on the Radiant face, a Field Trap, for as long as it is on the field), a heal on an enemy — the enemy hero or an enemy unit; Lifesteal, "heal up to" and "heal to full" are heals, setting health is not — deals that much Pierce damage to it instead, from Blood Moon ([[§6.3]] Heal). The Radiant face's type is its own ([[§5.2]]), so making the card Radiant in hand turns it into a Field Trap there
