**Why.** The owner found `/stats`' win rates misleading in #2777. A card with 20 to 80 games topped the table on noise: about 150 of 2,000 All Random games hold a given card, and #1149's notes put that noise at five points. A deck's win rate is also mostly the deck's: a card in a strong Constructed deck shows a high win rate it did not earn.

**The figure.** A card's adjusted win rate comes from two groups of games:
- **seen**: the games it was in hand, played or not (*n*ₛ of them, won at *p*ₛ);
- **never drawn**: the games its deck held it and never drew it (*n*ₙ, won at *p*ₙ, [[R2427000]]).

```
delta    = pₛ − pₙ                         R2427000's drawn delta
SE²      = c(1 − c) · (1/nₛ + 1/nₙ)         its sampling variance at its widest, c = ADJUSTED_WIN_RATE_CENTER (50%)
weight   = τ² / (τ² + SE²)                  τ = ADJUSTED_WIN_RATE_PRIOR_SPREAD (5 points)
adjusted = c + weight · delta
```

This is the posterior mean of the card's lift under a normal prior of spread τ, the spread real card lifts show. Some examples:
- A card that moves its deck not at all reads 50%, whatever its deck wins.
- A +20-point delta keeps a sixth of itself over 40 games each side (53.3%).
- The same delta keeps 95% of itself over 4,000 games each side (69.0%).
- A losing card is pulled up toward 50% just as far.

It counts the seen games, not the played ones: a card is played when the board allows it and the seat is often already ahead, which is why [[R2427000]] moved the delta off the played games. It is `None` when either group has no games. The engine's `adjusted_win_rate` computes it, beside `drawn_delta`, from the same totals; nothing is stored, so there is no migration.

**Where it shows.**
- **`GET /api/stats/cards`** answers `adjustedWinRate` on every card and lists the cards by it: enough games first, then the higher adjusted rate, a card with none after those with one. The summary's best and worst card stay [[R654]]'s raw win rate's.
- **`/stats`'s Cards tab** shows an Adjusted column before Win rate, to a tenth of a point, and sorts by it by default; an address with no `sort` reads it. The grid shows it first.
- **A row below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games** still reads "not enough games", as Win rate does ([[R654]]). The rate itself needs no floor of its own, since it discounts a small sample by construction.

Rejected:
- A Beta-shrunk win rate (wins plus pseudo-wins over games plus pseudo-games). It shrinks noise but keeps the deck's own strength, which is half the complaint.
- The played delta. Its baseline is chosen by the board.
- A floor in the engine, which belongs to the page ([[R654]]).
- A weight that reads each side's own win rate. At 50% the variance is widest, so a lopsided card is never trusted more than a balanced one.

No Hearthstone rule is involved. The figure is 17lands' "improvement when drawn", shrunk by its own sample.

**Amended.** [[R3022000]] reads the delta against the field: its distance from the mean delta of every card with enough games, kept at the spread τ those cards show. This ruling's centre of 0 and τ of 5 points remain the field below `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` such cards.
