Since issue #1797, a line that differs from an earlier one only in where its Units stand is a lane variant of [[§9.9]]'s beam: it takes no place in the beam, and up to `AI_SEARCH.zone_variants` lines of one position, the first included, end their turn beside the kept line. That position was read off `search_signature`, which lists each side's Units in lane order and signs the backrow zone by zone, an empty zone as `-`. So one Field Spell or Trap set in two backrow lanes ([[§3.1]]), and a Unit played left of a standing one or right of it, were two positions and two full lines with equal scores, since the evaluation reads no lane but through a Unit's stats. The root tries every distinct placement (issue #1635), three for a backrow card on an empty backrow, so one Field Spell took three of the beam's four lines, and every line grown from a copy kept its lane, so each copy's best continuation was copied again at every depth.

The beam's count of a position's placements, its lane variants and its filter of the variants it closes beside a kept line now read `lane_free_signature`: `search_signature` with each side's Unit parts sorted (each leads with its Unit's id, so the order is canonical) and each side's backrow signed as its cards sorted, empty zones left out. What a lane changes on a card still splits two positions: a Unit's stats under an adjacency aura (TG #21 Flame Tongue Totem), its damage, a card's face, what a Cry did. The beam's duplicate check keeps `search_signature` with the Units' lanes beside it, exact as before, and the lethal solver's and the unstoppable walk's visited sets keep `search_signature`, whose output is unchanged.

A backrow card that cares about its lane (S+ #40 Mender heals its controller's Units in its lane and the lanes beside it at the end of that player's turn) now reaches the end of its turn in at most `zone_variants` lanes, not in up to four lines of the beam: the trade issue #1797 made for Units. The static evaluation could not rank those lanes; the endTurn each variant is closed with and the opponent's reply still score its lane. The beam's slots go to plays that differ, at no cost in nodes, where [[R2575000]]'s budget found nodes alone buy little. Screened paired on the improve lane's dev set against the build before it (`cargo jackioh arena --set improve-dev`, 2026-11-18), it won 206 of 400 games over 200 deals, an edge of +0.030 (95% interval -0.038 to +0.098), inside noise as R2575000's was, with no fallback, search panic, refused action or aborted game on either side. Its smoke run of `cargo jackioh gate` won 15 of Hard against Easy's 20 games on that build, one short of the brief's 16, and 16 once merged with [[R4170000]] and [[R4171000]]; [[R4132001]] lowers that matchup's floor.

Rejected: signing twin Units alike regardless of their ids, and sparing the root's `root_branching` slots the placements the beam then drops, both smaller and able to follow once this is measured; and folding the backrow alone, since a Unit on the other side of a standing one wastes the beam the same way.

It adds no rule of the game and no constant.
