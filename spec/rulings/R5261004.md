The designer's note "you also gain the Discovered Unit" makes the Discover an ordinary one: the pick always reaches its controller's hand (a past version as [[R3538004]]'s cards do, Radiant on the Radiant face), and picking the option that is a past version ([[R3538002]], one of the three by [[R5239008]]) also gives Ball Knower its declared +`buff`/+`buff`, a lasting buff. A current pick gives nothing more. With no past Unit in the catalog the three options are current ones and no bonus can be had.
