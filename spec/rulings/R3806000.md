Built (issue #3806). Meditative #99 Paranoia reads "Aura: Your Spells have Cloaked. Spells you set
face-down resolve when set and when revealed. Cry: Draw {draw}.", its Radiant face keeping "Spells you
reveal have Echo +{echo}" ([[R1045]]). The issue's suggested "resolve as you set them and again as you
reveal them" put the Radiant face over the face text limit ([[R1017000]]), so the card says it shorter.
A Spell set by its Cloaked while a Paranoia
acts on its controller's field resolves its Spell text as it is set, stays set, and resolves it again as
its Reveal fires ([[R783006]]). The spec decided none of what follows; each part follows Hearthstone,
whose Secrets and Twinspells it borrows from, where it has an answer.

**Which Spells.** A card whose printed face is a Spell, set by its Cloaked however it came by it
(printed, lent by Paranoia, given by a Buff, [[R2727000]]), while a card acting on the setter's field
with its text awake resolves set Spells (`Script::resolves_set_spells`; a fusion has it when an
ingredient does). It is read as the set resolves, [[§10.5]] step 5: a Paranoia that left in step 4's
window gives nothing. A Spell set with no Paranoia out, by a Buff's Cloaked, resolves only as it
reveals, as before; a set Spell whose Paranoia has left by its reveal still resolves then. A Unit or a
Field Spell set by its Cloaked is no Spell and resolves nothing as it is set.

**Choices.** The set declares nothing ([[R783004]]): the set-time resolution asks its targets and modes
as prompts once the card is placed, in [[R467]]'s order, and draws them where a Mayor or a random cast
draws a Cry's ([[R1200]]). A Spell under Paranoia is offered set, and the reducer accepts the set, only
while each declaration its play could not go without has a legal pick and its play gate holds, the test
its Reveal waits for ([[R703]], [[R1851001]], `alt_play::spell_text_can_resolve`), read off the card at
the set's X and embiggen; one the board then fails as it resolves fizzles that resolution ([[R90]]).
Rejected: setting it anyway and fizzling, which would let a needed target be skipped as a free set. X
and embiggen are chosen with the set, as before, and both resolutions read them.

**Where the effects come from.** A Trap, both times ([[R781001]]): no Spell Damage, a counter for
Spells never sees them, and Immune to Spells does not stop them, though Immutable still bars its aim. The
card's own Echo repeats each resolution with fresh prompts; Radiant Paranoia's Echo is added only as it
reveals, as its text says ([[R1045]]).

**Hidden information.** The card stays face-down in its zone throughout, and the opponent is never told
which Spell resolved: they see that a prompt is open and whose ([[R81]]), never its title or options;
the hook's marker is withheld from them ([[R2783000]]), no `trapFired` follows, and the card's zone
shows a card back with its cost ([[R1046]]). What its effects do is public, as any effect is, and may
let them guess. Rejected: naming the card while it resolves and hiding it again, which would show the
opponent what the set card is, the one thing a set card keeps.

**"This turn" and where it goes.** "This turn" in its text is the turn of each resolution: the set turn
for the first, the reveal turn for the second. A destination its text names for itself ("Exile this",
"Shuffle this into your deck") is where its reveal sends it ([[R178]]), so as it is set the card stays
set in its backrow zone; at its reveal it goes there, or to the graveyard ([[R766]]). A text that acts
from where "this" went after it resolves (an "End of turn:" on the card) acts only after the reveal,
since the set card's text sleeps again once its set-time resolution ends.

**Counting.** The set-time resolution is no play ([[R70]]): no `cardPlayed`, no play counter, no
Combo; the set is one Trap play and the reveal none, as before ([[R1044]]).

**No Cry at a face-down play.** A card played face-down as a Trap runs no Cry at its play: its text is
inert until its reveal ([[R783004]]), and only a Spell set under Paranoia resolves its Spell text then.
A Cloaked Unit or Field Spell with a Cry (S #103 Vxyl, S #123 Gokugains, M #45.1 Knowledge Breaker
Prime's Units, Paranoia itself when a Buff cloaks it) cries on its reveal, never as it is set. Until
this issue the play pipeline ran a set card's printed Cry hook at [[§10.5]] step 5, its text read past
its dormancy, so a Cry that needs no choice ran as the card was set; it no longer runs. An effect a
Trap or Field Trap is meant to have as it is set is written "When played face down:", never "Cry:",
and `cargo jackioh catalog check` fails a Trap or Field Trap face whose text holds "Cry:".
Stinkyscape+ #31 Soul Read, the one Trap whose designer's note prints "Cry:", is worded so on both
faces ("When played face down: Look at your opponent's hand and choose a card secretly."): its pick
still runs as it is set, through the same play-time hook, and nothing else about it changes.

Replays of v0.6.0g and before fold under Paranoia's kept script, which resolves nothing as a Spell is
set ([[R767000]]).
