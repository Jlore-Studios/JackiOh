In fuzz seed 9305 an attack on a fused Unit holding Stinkyscape #104 Reflective Plating's base face was forecast to take nothing back and took 3. The Plating dealt what it was dealt again to the attacker's hero ([[R1849003]], [[§4.4]] step 8a), and Stinkyscape+ #42 Force Projector's pool on the attacker's side ([[R3550004]]) soaked that hit, spending the pool before the strike back landed. The forecast ([[R3100000]]) reads each of its hits against the pools the board shows ([[R4032000]]) and projects no reflection, so it had the pool soak the strike back. [[R4111000]] already left out of I18 a board where the defending side reflects onto the attacker itself (the Radiant face); I18 now also skips, by a named condition, an attack on a Unit whose side reflects damage onto the attacker's hero while the attacker's side has pool left. With no pool on the attacker's side the reflection changes nothing the forecast shows, and the attack is judged.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and an invariant that checks it may judge only what it can see.

Rejected: projecting the reflection's hit into the forecast's pools, which would need the reflections of every Unit the attack strikes (Cleave's, a sweep's) and each First Strike order, for a case the forecast's `taken` reads wrong only while a pool is on the attacker's side.
