Meditative+ #24 Bob, Wisconsin's Strongest Soldier's base Cry is "Choose one" of four, declared modes that travel in the play ([[R81]]), so the choice never pauses and the AI weighs four plays, not a prompt: `freeze` (Freeze every enemy Unit, [[R1174000]]), `take` (an enemy Unit into your hand, [[R1185003]], and Coins to the opponent), `discover` (Discover a Unit, the pick added Radiant) and `copies` (draw 1, then fill your hand with copies of it). The take's target is declared with the play for that mode alone and is needed ([[R703]]): with no enemy Unit to take, the take mode is not offered, as Hearthstone keeps a Choose One option that needs a target out of reach, and the other three are. A cast is never refused ([[R70]]), so a cast taking with no target does nothing.

The Coins are the price of the take: the opponent gets them whenever the play declared a Unit to take, even one gone or a token that ceased to exist by the time it resolves, and never when none was declared. The Discover offers three different non-token Units of the sets that ship ([[R380]]), never Bob ([[R387]]). The fourth mode is one draw, then copies of the card that draw put in the hand (`draw_priced`'s `fillWithCopies`): [[R1242]]'s copies with [[R57]]'s riders, one at a time while the hand has room, so none is burned; a card cast on draw ([[R58]]), burned, or a draw that brought nothing (fatigue, a draw limit) leaves nothing to copy ([[R596]]).

The Radiant face does all four in that order, each its own step: it declares no mode, and the take's target is declared with the play but not needed ([[R90]]), so with no enemy Unit that step does nothing and gives no Coins. Its list is the same length whatever the board holds, so the Discover that pauses it resumes it where it stopped ([[R113]]): the pick is added, then the draw and its copies fill the hand.
