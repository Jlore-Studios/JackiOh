The AI's count of the damage a board can deal past the Taunts in its way (`damage_past_taunts_with`, [[§9.9]]), which `face_threat`, the threat and pressure terms, `wall_switches_priced` and the lethal solver's `ready_gap` read, landed a Cleave splash ([[§4.4]] step 10) only on a later Taunt of its walk. A splash on a unit without Taunt, or on a Taunt the walk had already passed, counted nothing, so a Trample attacker's excess over it ([[§4.4]] step 9, [[R63]], [[R1750000]]) never reached the hero, and the damage pools it spends ([[R4064000]]) stayed whole for the hits after it. On the issue's board, the AI at 8 with a 0/9 Taunt between two 1/1s against a 5/4 with Cleave and Trample, the walk read 0, where the engine's one attack on the Taunt tramples 4 over each 1/1 and ends the game.

With the flag `splash_trample_read`, a Cleave splash in the walk lands on every unit it strikes. A later Taunt is met as before. A unit without Taunt, or a Taunt the walk has passed, is met as the walk's earlier hits and splashes left it: its Divine Shield popped, its health lowered, gone once destroyed, or back as its Reborn body ([[§4.5]] step 4) unless it was exiled ([[R1124]]). Each splash meets what the defender's pools have left in the engine's order: the hit on the attacked unit and its excess first, then each neighbour's splash and its excess in turn. A Trample attacker's splash sends what it deals beyond the unit's health to the hero, through the hero's Armor and the Anti-oneshot cap as the walk's other excesses go.

On the issue's board the walk now reads 8, and `ready_gap` 0 once the AI passes. Two Cleave Trample 5/4s at a 0/20 Taunt between a 1/1 and a 2/3 Reborn read 10: the first tramples 4 over the 1/1 and 2 over the 2/3, whose body comes back at 1 health, and the second finds the 1/1 gone and tramples 4 over the body; a splash read fresh from the state at each swing would read 12. A 0/2 Flying Taunt, which binds no ground attacker ([[R783002]]) and so is passed first, takes the splash from the 0/9 beside it and lets 3 through. Behind a Force Projector's 5, a Cleave 2/4's hit on a 0/3 Taunt and its splash on the 1/1 beside it spend 4, so a Trample 6/6 after it deals the Taunt 5 and tramples 2, where the walk had read 0. The engine deals each of these.

The flag is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick's walk splashes only a later Taunt, as before. The walk reads its weights' flag, and everything that reads the walk is reached through it, the orders of [[R3937000]] and [[R4257000]] and the strike backs of [[R4402000]] included.

No rule of the game covers what the AI reads, so its reads follow the engine.

Rejected:

- reading a unit without Taunt fresh from the state at each splash, the issue's sketch: a second Cleave Trample swing would trample over a unit the first destroyed, where the engine finds its lane empty;
- tracking those units for Trample attackers only: a plain splash also spends the pools and wounds the unit, which a later Trample splash meets.

Not modelled: a Taunt the walk has passed that a splash destroys comes back as its Reborn body for later splashes, but that body binds no attacker of the walk again, though a Reborn body with Taunt would in the engine; and a Lifesteal Taunt's strike back heals the defending hero, which the walk does not read.

It reads only public state, and it adds no rule of the game and no constant.
