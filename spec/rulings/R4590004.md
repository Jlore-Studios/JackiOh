IA #123 Cost Injection's and IA #144 Total Compromise's "Your opponent's cards cost (1) more during their next turn" is T-AI-7 Alignment Tax's price rule turned outward (`add_cost_rule` lasting `theirNextTurn`): it reaches every card they play during that turn ([[R48]]), including cards drawn during it. The hike is a fixed price ([[R1748003]]).
