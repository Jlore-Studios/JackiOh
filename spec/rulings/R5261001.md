The designer's "Add a playable Spell" is read as affordable: a random non-token Spell of the sets that ship ([[§5.1]]) whose printed cost, an X read as 0 ([[R65]]), is at most the mana its controller has left as the Cry resolves ([[§2.3]]), the Forager's own price already paid. Whether the Spell has a legal target now is not asked: Hearthstone has no such filter, and a pool that read the board would change with every target the Spell names. It reaches the hand with the declared Temporary count ([[R5238000]]). With no mana left it is a (0) Spell.
