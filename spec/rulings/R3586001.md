`run_fight_setup` takes the enemy's handicap from its tier ([[R3586000]]). `RUN_TIERS` holds one
row per tier from tier 1, each a whole handicap ([[R180]]): its mana cap and bonus ([[R181]]), more
opening cards ([[R182]]) or fewer (`fewerOpeningCards`, [[R3582000]]), its extra draws ([[R183]]),
how often it takes its turn draws (`drawEvery`, [[R3582000]]) and its hero health ([[R290]]). Every
fight but an elite reads it, ghosts and the three bosses included. An elite reads `RUN_ELITE_TIERS`,
its own hand-tuned table of `(tier, row)` pairs, one for each tier an elite can stand on (every tier
but a boss's, since an elite is never a boss), each at least as strong as the same tier's normal row
in every field and with more health. A row's `deck_size` is not read: the enemy's is its deck's
length, as before.

Tiers 5, 10 and 15, the bosses', are `AI_DIFFICULTY`'s Easy, Medium and Hard resources, built from
the engine's constants rather than restated (CLAUDE.md rule 9), each with its own hero health. The
other rows are built by one `const fn` from `HUMAN_HANDICAP`, so a field no row names stays a human's.
Both tables are strawmen, the issue's starting numbers taken as given, to tune against run-sim's
table by tier ([[§15.13]]).

Curses still strengthen the enemy on top of its tier's row, and Combat artifacts the human, as
[[R2922001]] has them. `RUN_ENEMY_HEALTH` retires, its numbers moved into the rows, and so do the two
enemy artifacts that gave their carrier Medium's and Hard's resources, with their constants, their
placeholder entries and every check of what an enemy carries. A boss's own artifact stays on it and
changes nothing in its fight, as before.

`RunEnemy` carries its `tier` in place of its health, so a screen can show it: the map labels each
fight node "Tier N", and the fight shows "Tier N" beside the enemy's relic tray, "Elite, tier N" for
an elite (the client only reads it, CLAUDE.md rule 7). A tier no table holds sets up no fight
(`None`): a kept fight from before tiers ([[R3476000]]) parses with tier 0 and resumes on its own kept
setup, never a new one. `cargo jackioh run-sim` prints a table by tier, every fight but an elite
under its tier and the elites apart, for tuning.

Rejected: keeping the health on `RunEnemy` beside the tier, two places for one number that could
disagree; finding an elite's row by its index in the table, which breaks as soon as a tier is added
or dropped; and falling back to `RUN_TIERS` for an elite whose tier its own table lacks, which would
hide a hole in the elites' table behind a weaker fight. It takes R3586001, the second number of
#3586's block.
