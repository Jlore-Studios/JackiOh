"Gain a random Resource" draws one rarity by `RESOURCE_RARITY_ODDS` (Common 55, Rare 25, Epic 14, Legendary 5, Mythic 1), one draw of the match rng over the weights' sum walked in that order, then one Resource of the rolled rarity, uniformly ([[§10.7]]). The table is taken over the rarities its pool holds: a rarity no candidate has is left out and the rest keep their weights, so a pool of a Legendary and a Mythic rolls them 5 to 1.

The pool is every Resource in the catalog, sorted by id — shipped or not, since the Resources are tokens and [[R1420]]'s set gate does not reach them — or the ids a card names ("only gives Narcor and Red Narcor"), kept to those that are Resources. A pool that holds none gains nothing and draws nothing. Each Resource of a gain of several is rolled as it is gained. A Radiant gain gives a Radiant Resource.
