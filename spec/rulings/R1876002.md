"Death: Remove every Allure on the field" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #37 Kwzka) withdraws every Allure ([[§6.3]], [[R963]], [[R1045000]]) whose marked card is on the field, on either side and whoever made it, Kwzka's own included (ST-S17-6): its delayed steal is forgotten, so nothing is stolen at the start of the Allurer's next turn, and its pink mark comes off at the next sweep, said by a `marked` event with `added: false` ([[R437]]). An Allure aimed at a hand card ([[R1140]]) is not on the field and stays. It is `remove_allures`, an effect that finds the waiting Allures by their mark, so any card that Allures (K-Pop Fanatic, Jade Beauty, Siva 1, Kwzka) is reached. Rejected: removing only the Allures Kwzka made, against the printed "every Allure on the field".
