Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VII: Flak v5, v4 and v3, "slightly reduce the chance to deal 3 damage". The weights [[R2521001]] lays over `least` to `most` from the low end are 60, 33 and 7, from 60, 30 and 10: the three points the top roll gives up go to the middle one, so a 3 comes about 7 times in 100 instead of 10 and a 1 as often as before, the average hit 1.47 instead of 1.5. Everything else [[R2521001]] says holds: one roll for each enemy Unit, Lucky on each roll, a value past the third weighted as the third. Replays of the versions through v0.6.12b fold under `script_v0_6_12b`, which rolls the old odds ([[R767000]]).
