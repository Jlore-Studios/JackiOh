Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event or message changes. Every Legendary or Mythic Unit summoned into a unit zone gets its rarity's entrance; a few marquee ones (#100 Ceaseless Void, BOOM! Big Max, Nature Titan, Spell Tyrant) get an entrance of their own instead, listed in the client's one per-card effects table ([[R502]]). It is keyed by the `summoned` event's own definition, which a summon into a unit zone shows both seats and a hidden summon never has ([[R202]]), so it plays the same on both seats, whoever cast the card, and never for a backrow set. It decorates the entry the animation table already timed and paces nothing: every delay lands inside the entry and everything is gone within the effects' tail ([[R200]], [[R201]]), and the effects off or reduced motion draws none of it.
