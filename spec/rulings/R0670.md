Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event or message changes. Every Legendary or Mythic Unit summoned into a unit zone gets its rarity's entrance; a few marquee ones (#100 Ceaseless Void, BOOM! Big Max, Nature Titan, Spell Tyrant) get an entrance of their own instead, each a `{ bespoke }` entry in its set's effects table ([[R2784000]]). It is keyed by the `summoned` event's own definition, which a summon into a unit zone shows both seats and a hidden summon never has ([[R202]]), so it plays the same on both seats, whoever cast the card, and never for a backrow set. It decorates the entry the animation table already timed and paces nothing: every delay lands inside the entry and everything is gone within the effects' tail ([[R200]], [[R201]]), and the effects off or reduced motion draws none of it. Since [[R2722001]] a readable Mythic's entrance also takes over the whole board, the marquee Mythics (#100 Ceaseless Void, #96 My Pawn, #97 Zephrys, Meditative #49 YileGPT) each with a look of its own and the rest with a shared one, inside one capped hold the animation queue gives its entry; a Legendary or Mythic Spell and a hidden card at its reveal take the rarity's rays too, and a Legendary's entrance lands harder, with a ring of its light. Since [[R2800000]] #100 Ceaseless Void's entry is its set piece, `{ setPiece: "voidFold" }`, which lands it with this same collapse and then folds the board around it. Since [[R3332000]] each entrance is its entry's `entrance` field, beside any entry of a Unit or a Field Spell or alone, and no longer a `{ bespoke }` entry.
