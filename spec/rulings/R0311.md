Each library card records what its owner was shown of it as it went in, its definition and its face (`knownAs`, [[§10.1]]), and the list reads that record, never the card. The record is written where a card goes in openly: the starting deck, a mulligan's returns, and a shuffle-in, which in Core is always done by a card both players watched resolve: CN-Virus's copies, Unstable Clone Machine's copies, and the opponent's CN-Viral Injection, whose play is public and whose text names the virus and, on its Radiant face, the virus's face. A change made inside a library, where nobody reads it, does not show: a card #28 picks or #42 rolls Radiant there is listed with the face it went in with until it leaves, and #95's discount and #98's roll never show
