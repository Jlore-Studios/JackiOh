A prompt opened for a random cast's caster while that cast resolves is answered at once at random ([[R452]]), as a `target` prompt is while a Mayor acts ([[R1200]]). When the prompt belongs to a card the other player controls (an opponent-held prompt, `by: "enemy"`, [[§10.6]]), the random answer is the holder's pick, but the step it answers runs as the asking card's controller, exactly as an answered prompt's does. So C #9 Income Tax, set by one player and fired inside the other's random cast on that player's own turn (where [[R1279000]] opens the keep at once), keeps one of the drawer's cards at random and gives the rest of the drawer's hand to the trap's controller, never the reverse. Hearthstone has no opponent-held prompt, and a random choice there never changes whose effect resolves.

Rejected: asking the drawer by hand, since [[R452]] rules that the caster is never asked while its random cast resolves; and deferring the keep to the drawer's next turn as [[R1279000]] defers an off-turn one, since the drawer is the active player.
