title: How a series is rated
cards: "§9.5, §9.12, R603, R604"
proven_in:
  - crates/server/tests/api/series.rs
---
A ranked series moves each rating once, when it ends: a win, a loss or a draw scored as one Glicko-2 game between its two sides ([[R603]]), from the ratings at the time it ends. Its games are recorded in `results` with both ratings unchanged. An abandoned or unranked series is unrated
