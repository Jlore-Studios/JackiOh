Issue #4154 took S #148 Alteran Reach from (0) to (1) and gave its base face "Your first travel each turn is free." and its Radiant face "Your first two travels each turn are free." As before the free travel is an ability `has` switches with the paid one ([[R752]], [[R1847002]], `starscape_abilities_free`). The first free travel is the player's first travel this turn, whatever made it, as [[R1847002]] read it. The second is free while the travels the player's cards acting on the field have made with their travel abilities this turn, each card's own count of its uses ([[R1751000]]), number at least one and fewer than two. A travel no ability makes (M+ #1.5 Star Chart's, [[R2521008]]) therefore spends both free travels when it is the turn's first, as its two nodes would, and counts toward neither after one. The engine keeps no count of a player's travels this turn, and adding one would change every replayed game that travels; the Activates' counts are already kept. Rejected: a new per-player travel count (it changes old games' state) and a free use counted on each Reach alone (two Reaches would give more). Through v0.6.6g the base face had no free travel and the Radiant face one (`script_v0_6_6g`, [[R767000]]).
