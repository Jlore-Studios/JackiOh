S+ #7 Churnwalker chains Units so that damage to one hits them all (shared) or is split between them (split, [[R3554007]]). The fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) found a Spell's hit on one chained Unit landing, as an echo or a share, on another that was Immune to Spells (S+ #67 Phase Wall).
**The carried hit deals nothing there.** [[§6.1]]: a Spell doesn't affect a Unit Immune to Spells. A Spell never aims at such a Unit and its walks of the field pass it by ([[R4453000]]), so its hit reaches one only when another card's rule carries it there. At [[§4.4]] step 0, beside Immune to Spell Damage ([[R1849000]]), a Spell's hit on a Unit Immune to Spells on the field is no damage instance at all: no Divine Shield pops, nothing triggers and no Trample excess goes on. A shared chain's echo deals the immune member nothing, and a split chain's share for it is lost, the other members keeping their shares.
Rejected: leaving the immune member out of the split, which would raise every other member's share because one of them is immune.
A replay this alters is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]).
