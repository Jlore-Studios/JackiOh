"Cry: Stack a permanent under this. Death: return that permanent to its controller" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #9 Ticketed) is Hold beneath ([[R1850003]]), not the Stack keyword, so its face prints that verb. Its target, declared with the play ([[R81]]), is another permanent acting face-up on the field, of either side: a face-down card is never offered, since the hold could not take it. "Death:" is Ticketed leaving the field any way — its Brittle 3 crumbling it ([[R638]]), a destroy, a Bounce, an exile — and the held permanent is then summoned back for its controller as it was taken, a Field Spell to the backrow, with no zone open exiled. The Radiant face has no Brittle, so it holds until it is removed. Rejected: release at Ticketed's Death alone, which would keep a permanent beneath a Ticketed bounced or exiled for the rest of the game; and an enemy permanent only, which the text does not say.
