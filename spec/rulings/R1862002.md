The Starship carriers' Cry, "Discover N Rare Weapons and Equip them into this", is `equip_picks` ([[R1841002]]): N Discovers of `DISCOVER_OPTIONS` in turn, each from the Weapon-tagged cards of the rarity the face names (Rare, or Epic on the Radiant face, for #81 to #83; Epic, or Legendary, for #85 and #86), the pool naming the Stinkyscape set so that it reaches the held set's Weapons ([[R1420]], [[R1857000]]). Each pick is a fresh copy fused into the carrier with its Activate, no Cry, no hand and never a Weapon it already holds, so the four Legendary Weapons offer three, then three, then the last two to Prototype-X2's Radiant face. A pick is not made Radiant: the carrier wears each on its own face, as every fusion wears its ingredients ([[R77]]), so a Radiant carrier wields its Weapons' Radiant faces. It stays a Starship Piece, the kept card's keyword ([[R1852001]]). As Hearthstone's "Discover a weapon" offers three of a pool, the picks are offered, not drawn. Rejected: the picks Radiant on the Radiant face (`radiant`), which the designer's text does not say; its Radiant face changes the rarity alone.
