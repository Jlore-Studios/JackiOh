[[§10.11]] pre-rendered every voice line with macOS `say` and capped the set at 3 MB for Core's 111 cards; Classic and Classic+ bring 206 more cards and tokens and 274 more lines. `gen-voice.mjs` has two backends, named per persona: macOS `say` (Core's personas, whose files and hashes are unchanged) and Windows SAPI (`System.Speech`, driven through `powershell.exe`, natively or from WSL) shaped by `ffmpeg` (silence trimmed, pitch shifted by the persona's semitones with the tempo kept, the persona's filter chain for character, peak-normalised) and encoded to the same mono AAC at 22050 Hz and about 32 kbps. A SAPI persona's hash covers its own fields (voice, rate, semitones, filter, text), and its lines take no per-card override. Generating renders only the stale keys whose backend the machine has and reports the others as needing theirs; `--check` stays OS-independent and gains `--catalog` to check against another catalog. The budget, `VOICE_BUDGET_BYTES`, is 6 MiB counted in 4 KiB blocks (the 429 files take about 4.8 MiB), and every line still stays within 4 s, flavour only, and speaks at [[R204]]'s moments. A missing file still falls back to the browser's speech synthesis
