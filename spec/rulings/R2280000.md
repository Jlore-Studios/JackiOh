A Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap becomes a Unit only when it meets both conditions: it has the Animated keyword ([[§6.1]]), and it has stats. A card has stats when its definition gives it an attack and health line: its own printed one, one from a fused definition ([[R77]], [[R102]]), a summon's `statsOverride` or X stats (`layers::has_stat_line`). Buffs, auras and `tuning` change a line the card already has but never make one. Any backrow card may come to have stats, a Fuse giving it a Unit's, and they do nothing until it is Animated.

Each of [[R383]]'s moments checks both. An Animated Field Spell with no stats enters the field and stays in its backrow zone; an Animated Trap or Field Trap with none fires and stays face-up in its zone, as one with no open unit zone does; an "Animated on your turn" card with none stays at its controller's start of turn. The `animate` verb an Animated Trap's list ends with moves an Animated card only, so a card with stats and no Animated, a Field Spell holding a fused Unit, stays a backrow card. A card that first has both on the field animates at that state check ([[R2280001]]). [[R386]]'s stats change reaches a Unit or a card with stats: a Nerf or a Buff no longer draws it for a stat-less Animated card, and draws it for a backrow card a Fuse gave stats. A card that has animated stays a Unit where it stands when it later loses Animated or its stats, as [[§6.3]]'s Vanilla and [[R1728000]] already have it.

A catalog face that prints Animated prints both `attack` and `health` or neither (`catalog check`). Since issue #2280 S #16 Rod of Ages and C+ #74 Twice Forward One Step Backwards print none on either face, so each becomes a Unit only once a fused Unit gives it stats ([[R1869000]]). Rejected: keeping [[R657]]'s 0/1, which would make an Animated card with no stats a Unit all the same.
