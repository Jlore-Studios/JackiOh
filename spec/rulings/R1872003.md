S #80 Galaxy (Tribute 2, [[R101]]) reads "Cry: Discover 2 Radiant Miners and Equip them into this". Its Radiant face reads "Cry: Equip a Radiant copy of every Miner into this", the designer's "Equip this with each Radiant Miner vX" (ST-S05-4).

- **Base.** Two Discovers of three in turn, from the five Miners (S #62 to #66), each pick Radiant ([[R469]]) and fused into Galaxy with its Activate, the second Discover leaving out the first pick ([[R1841002]]).
- **Radiant.** All five Miners, each a Radiant copy, fused one at a time from v1 to v5 with no prompt: five `equip_picks` of one named card each. One Galaxy holds each Miner once. Galaxy is the kept card, so it stays a Starship Piece ([[R1852001]]).
