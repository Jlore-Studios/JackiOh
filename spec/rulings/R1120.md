Meditative #52 Economic Anxiety's "while attacking" (MD-D4) covers the attacker's strikes in a combat
it started against a Unit: its strike, its Cleave and its Trample's excess. Every acting card's
`attack_mods` hook is summed over [[§10.4]]'s aura sources (`combat::attack_mod_for`), its attacks
added and its Poisonous or-ed, read once per combat beside [[R94]]'s reads. The strike back, a strike
on a hero and the attack a view shows get nothing. Tags compared are every printed tag, so an untagged
attacker always qualifies. A granted Poisonous marks the hit as `poisonous`, and My Pawn's lethal
projection reads the bonus.
