A challenge is a room, and the challenger's game starts when the friend joins it, wherever the challenger is. The shell's toast follows it: each read of the challenges, while one of the tab's own is open or was just sent, asks `/api/auth/me` for a live game and announces it as a pairing is announced ([[R765]]). [[R3205000]] keeps that poll running in a hidden tab for this reason.

[[R3214000]] stopped the poll while the social socket is live, so a join reached the challenger as one pushed read and one ask for the game. If that ask missed, nothing asked again until the challenge lapsed: the answer failed, it came from a read of `/api/auth/me` already in flight from before the join, or it was cancelled by the next render. The daily e2e run's spec 42 kept the challenger on `/replays` that way: its first attempt failed in nearly every run from 2026-11-10, when the socket arrived, and a retry hid it.

While a challenge of the tab's own is open or was just sent (`keepPolling`, the same test [[R3205000]] reads), the social poll now reads at `SOCIAL_POLL_MS` even while the socket is live, and applies its answer. Each read asks for the game again, so a missed ask costs one poll, as it did before the socket. With no challenge of its own open, a live tab still sends no social poll.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone settles nothing here.

Rejected: a timer in the toast that asks `/api/auth/me` on its own while it follows. It would be a second cadence beside the poll that [[R3205000]] already keeps for the follow, with its own hidden-tab rule to keep in step.
