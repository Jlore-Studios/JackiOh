Two of the sandbox's tools ([[R923000]]) clear a card away:

- **`destroy`** destroys a card on the field as a destroy effect does: the state check after the action moves it to the graveyard and runs its Death.
- **`remove`** exiles a card from the field, a hand, a library or a graveyard, its Death not run. A card already in exile or mid-resolution is no target.

The panel names only the cards the human can see: both fields' face-up cards, their own hand and both graveyards. A face-down enemy card and the AI's hand stay hidden ([[R97]]), so no tool can name them.
