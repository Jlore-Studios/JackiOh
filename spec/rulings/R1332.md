Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Wherever a portrait is drawn, on the board, in its inspect view ([[R1330]]) and in the deck builder's picker, its `CardArt` oval ([[R641]]) is given layers of its own: a ground, lit from the upper left and washed over the picture, so that the four Human portraits differ as well as the others; a rim light along the lit edge; a more saturated picture; a breathing light; and `PORTRAIT_MOTE_COUNT` (6) motes drifting up through the oval.

The colours are `PORTRAIT_LOOKS` (`apps/web/src/emotes/portraitLook.ts`), one per portrait. The motes' places, sizes and phases are seeded from the portrait's id, so a portrait looks the same everywhere, on every client and after every reload, and nothing about it reads a clock or a random draw. Reduce Motion, from the system or from the settings panel, stills the idle: the breath and the motes stop moving and the layers stay.
