[[§9.9]]'s lethal solver keyed a visited position on `search_signature` alone, which reads no Unit's lane (issue #1797). It tries a play in up to `AI_SEARCH.zone_variants` placements in move order; when the first lost and the second won, the second reached the first's signature and was dropped. Slashed at lane 3 ([[R3554003]]), Core #45 Deft Duelist (4/3 Charge) in lane 2 is walled by a 9/9 Taunt on its half and in lane 4 hits the hero at 4; beside the AI's S #157 The Gauntlet ([[R1849005]]) lane 4 may hit no hero and lane 5 wins. The solver gave up after 3 and 2 of its 300 nodes, and the lethal hint ([[R2102000]]) stayed dark on a sure win where nothing is hidden. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the search changes no game.

**What changes.** Both walks key a position on `position_key`, `search_signature` with every active Unit's lane, as the beam's duplicate check and the concession check ([[R2660000]]) already did; it moves unchanged from `unstoppable.rs` to `simulate.rs`, so the concession check's keys stay what they were. Placements that differ in lane are two positions; two orders of the same moves into the same lanes stay one. The allowance, the moves and their order are unchanged. The solver finds the two lethals above in 4 and 3 nodes. The boards `lethal.rs` pins cost what they did where they have a lethal (the wide board 96 nodes, Silence 6, Deft 4); its small board with none costs 12, not 10. The solver's nodes come out of the decision's budget: [[R4447000]]'s pinned decision ([[R4697000]]) keeps its action and line at 178 nodes, not 171.

**Rejected.** [[R4132000]]'s `lane_free_signature`, which merges more placements, not fewer. Raising `zone_variants`: both lanes were already among the solver's moves. Reading lanes only at a hero attack: a lane changes later steps too (Top Loser, Scorch, Bunker, a Cleave's neighbours). It adds no rule and no constant.
