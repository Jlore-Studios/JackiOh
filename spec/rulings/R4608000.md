`decide` runs its beam on the first sampled world (determinization 0) alone, and scores its finalists after the opponent's reply there and again on every other world ([[§9.9]], The opponent's reply; [[R3498000]], [[R4447000]]); the finalist with the best mean over the worlds scored is played, or drawn on a softmax over the means at a style's temperature ([[R1452]]). A won game scores `AI_EVAL.win − turn`, about a million, and no position comes near it, so a line that won on world 0 alone averaged about a third of a million and beat every line that won nowhere, however much it lost on the other worlds. A win on world 0 is no evidence of a win: the beam found that line because it wins there, where a random effect rolled lethal, the seat's own shuffled library dealt the card the line drew, or a face-down card was dealt as a trap that stops nothing. The other worlds are the only independent test, and the lethal solver already keeps a line only when it wins on every one of them.

**The rule.** `finalist_means` takes each finalist's row of scores, world 0's first. A row whose world-0 score is a won game (above `AI_WON_SCORE_SHARE`, a half, of `AI_EVAL.win`, so a win that comes during the opponent's reply counts) and whose scores on the other worlds, at least one, are none of them won takes the mean of those others in place of the mean of the whole row. Every other row keeps the plain mean of all its scores, added in world order as before. A line whose true chance to win is p was played whenever world 0's sample favoured it; it now also needs another world to win, which on the two other worlds of the shipped budget happens with chance 1 − (1 − p)²: three in four at an even chance, none for a win only world 0's own sample made possible.

**What stays.** A finalist that wins on world 0 and on at least one other world keeps its plain mean, about two thirds of a million, and is still played at once; a decision that scored world 0 alone (the counter or the clock stopped the rest, `worldsScored` 1) has nothing to confirm with and keeps today's order; a loss on any world stays in the mean, which errs toward caution. The lethal solver, the practice AI's lethal hint ([[R2102000]]) and a ladder bot's concession check ([[R2660000]]) never read the means. The ladder bots and the Duos AI team decide through `decide`, so their choices move with the practice AI's.

**Its value.** `AI_SEARCH.confirm_lone_win` ships on; off ranks every finalist by the plain mean of all its worlds, bit for bit as before. No fork screen was played, so the scene tests settle it, as an inconclusive screen's would ([[R4319000]]): on a turn whose knife throw is lethal on some worlds' rolls, the AI makes the board play on every seed whose throw is lethal on world 0 alone, and still throws on every seed whose throw is lethal on two worlds or three.

Rejected: capping a won world's score at the best position plus a margin (a scale nothing tunes, and a small margin would pass up an even lethal); dropping world 0 from every finalist's mean (it throws away the one world every finalist is replied on); and requiring a win on every world (the solver's rule already, so the AI would never take a likely lethal it cannot prove).

Hearthstone has no counterpart: it adds no rule of the game; its constants are `AI_SEARCH.confirm_lone_win` and `AI_WON_SCORE_SHARE`.
