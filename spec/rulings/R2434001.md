The owner asked for a simpler tutorial, each lesson about one thing (#2429), and this amends [[R2205003]] and [[R291]]:

| # | Id | The subject | The human's deck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `basics` | mana, playing units, attacking the hero, trading, winning | mostly vanilla units, a few Cries, Rush |
| 2 | `keywords` | Cries, Taunt, Armor, Divine Shield, Rush, Pierce | Cry and keyword units |
| 3 | `casting` | spells, removal | units and spells |
| 4 | `backrow` | Traps, Field Spells, Field Traps, the enemy's face-down cards | units, spells, Field Spells and Traps |
| 5 | `finale` | everything else, Legendary and Mythic cards among it | everything, TG #39 and #40 among it |

**The human's decks** are Training Grounds cards, `TUTORIAL_HUMAN`'s size ([[R1705005]]). Lessons 1 to 4 hold no Legendary or Mythic card; lesson 5 holds the set's four, #37 to #40, so #39 Call to Chaos (Training-Grounds Edition) and #40 Goetzmann Game Design are the player's alone.

- **Copies.** Training Grounds has three units with no text (TG #1, #8, #43) and one whose only text is Rush (TG #4), too few for twenty distinct cards that are mostly vanilla. So a lesson's deck may hold copies of such a vanilla unit, and of no other card. The engine allows them through the human's handicap, `deckCopies` ([[R2433001]], which amends §2.6 L3 for a handicapped seat); this ruling only says which cards a lesson copies. Lesson 1 holds four each of #1, #43 and #8 and two of #4, at most `TUTORIAL_HUMAN`'s `deckCopies` of each ([[R2433001]]), and six distinct Cry units.

**The AI's decks** are Core cards (`core-NNN`), [[R291]]'s twelve distinct, non-token, none on the shadow ban ([[R186]]) and none Legendary or Mythic. No snapshot (TG #41 to #60) and no Training Grounds card. The tutorial opponent never plays a card that is not Core, whatever reaches its hand: that is an engine rule on its handicap, `coreOnly` ([[R2433000]]), and every lesson's tests hold every policy's games to it.

**Nothing a later lesson teaches.** Neither seat's deck holds a type, keyword or ability a later lesson teaches: units from lesson 1, with a few Cries and Rush; Taunt, Armor, Divine Shield and Pierce from lesson 2; spells from lesson 3; Traps, Field Traps and Field Spells from lesson 4; every other keyword and ability (an Aura, Death, Tribute, Radiant, Quickdraw) in lesson 5. And the coach never shows a discovery bubble for a Field Guide entry a later lesson holds ([[R2205000]]): the entry is found without one.

**The pool hold.** Lessons 1 to 4 set `poolHold` on both seats ([[R2201001]]); lesson 5 on neither.

**Fewer moments.** Each lesson's coach keeps [[R2205000]]'s moments, fewer of them: three in lesson 1, five in lesson 2, two in lesson 3, three in lesson 4 and two in lesson 5, each followed by the last moment that ends with the game.

This supersedes [[R2206001]] (lesson 1's AI deck of Cry units): lesson 1 is now vanilla first, for both seats.

Hearthstone semantics: a scripted encounter deals from decks of its own choosing.

Rejected:

- Twenty distinct cards in lesson 1, which would be mostly Cry units, not mostly vanilla.
- Training Grounds cards for the AI, or the Core snapshots, which the owner ruled out ("cards from the Core set").

Amended by issue #2533 ([[R2533001]]): the AI's decks are Training Grounds cards too, none Legendary
or Mythic, in place of Core cards, and lesson 1's is mostly copies of the set's vanilla units, as the
human's is; the opponent plays nothing else ([[R2533000]], in place of [[R2433000]]).
