Any opponent's announced Spell springs it — its own controller's Spells never do ([[R448]]). The counter takes the Spell to the thief's hand (as Wormhole's does), and the cast aims it freely at once: a target prompt opens for the thief, with no `friendsOnly` fence. A full hand burns the countered card, and the cast still resolves from the graveyard. On the Radiant face a copy is read off the countered card before the counter moves it — the copy keeping its face ([[R57]]) — and added to the thief's hand.
