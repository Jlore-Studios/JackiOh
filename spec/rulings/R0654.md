A public, read-only statistics page (`/stats`) and card detail view (`CardDetail`) make card win-rate data and player statistics readable signed in or signed out. Card statistics aggregate finished live and development game records from [[§9.11]] ([[R376]]–[[R378]]). Until the active patch has logged `PUBLIC_STATS_MIN_LIVE_GAMES` (1000) live games (ranked and unranked combined, tutorial games excluded), AI development games pad the card statistics and `GET /api/stats/cards` labels the source "AI games + live games (provisional)"; the page shows only a "Provisional statistics" banner, never the source or the count towards the gate ([[R661]]). Once 1000 live games are reached on the patch, the gate flips: public stats use live games only, AI games are excluded from card statistics with no toggle to restore them, and the response labels the source "Live games". A card row or detail with fewer than `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` (20) games displays "not enough games" instead of a percentage. Card aggregates are served by `GET /api/stats/cards` and cached (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=300`), with card drill-down at `GET /api/stats/cards/:id`. Player statistics tracked per [[R639]] are synced to the account via `PUT /api/stats/player` and readable by the owner at `GET /api/stats/player`. Public player aggregates are served at `GET /api/stats/players` (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=60`), displaying games, wins, losses, win rate, favourite cards and fun stats (#125). Player stats are public by default; players may opt out in settings (`publicStats: false`), which hides them from the public players tab without affecting private viewing. Presentation and data serving only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
