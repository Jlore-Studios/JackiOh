[[R89]] says a death carries its attack and max health as the layers computed them at the moment it died, and auras are layer 5 ([[§10.4]]). S #49 Spacemine's Death ("deal damage equal to its Attack") and S #134 Felinor Containment Chamber's Death ("summon a Felinor Token with this Unit's Attack and Health") read their own stats, and read them with every aura left off: §4.5 step 1 takes each dying card off the field before any Death runs, and the field's own view leaves auras off a card that no longer tops its pile ([[R1884004]]). Under S #23 Warp Disruptor's +2 Attack a Spacemine showed 7, its `destroyed` event said 7, and its Death hit for 5; a Chamber showing 4/1 summoned a 2/1. Under an aura that lowers Attack the Death came out stronger than the card looked.

So both Deaths use the numbers the card's own `destroyed` event reports ([[R89]]), auras included:

- **The numbers.** Spacemine's hit equals the Attack of its latest `destroyed` event, and the Chamber's token takes that event's Attack and `maxHealth` (the Radiant face doubles both, as before). The event is read with every dying card still on the field, so an aura whose source dies in the same pass, such as Core #65.1 Spikey Pillow beside the card under Core #88 Twisting Nether, still counts, though that source is gone from the field as the Death runs ([[R463]]).
- **A later action.** A Death that runs after an earlier Death's prompt no longer holds the event, and reads the board's auras as they stand (`layers::unit_view_as_it_died`, [[R1884004]]).
- **A Starship.** The same holds for a launched Starship that carries either text ([[R4334000]]): its hit or its token reads the numbers it died with.
- **Replays.** The games of v0.6.9e and before fold under the scripts they ran, which left the auras off (`script_v0_6_9e`, [[R767000]]).

Rejected: `layers::unit_view_as_it_died` alone, which reads the board after the collected cards left ([[R463]]) and so drops the aura of a source that died in the same pass, while the event still counts it; carrying the numbers in the engine's death snapshot, an engine change to every Death hook that no card needs.
