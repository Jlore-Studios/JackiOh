A lesson cannot be lost by rule ([[R2201000]]); this ruling says how that is proved. Each lesson is
played through the real practice core ([[R293]]) under every policy the harness has (`LESSON_POLICIES`):

- the coach's line;
- the coach-passive player, who plays only what the coach names;
- the autopilot;
- random legal moves;
- the **reckless** player.

Under each, the lesson ends in a **human win** before the turn cap: never a loss, never a draw,
never a stall.

The reckless player:

- keeps its hand and answers each prompt with its first option;
- attacks first, with every unit that can, each attack into the enemy unit with the most Attack (the
  hero when no unit can be hit);
- then plays its cheapest card that guards nothing: never a Trap or a Field Trap, never a unit with
  Taunt, Divine Shield or Armor, never a play aimed at its own side;
- then ends the turn;
- and never switches a unit to Defense Position.

So it throws its units away and leaves its hero open, and the floor still holds.

Hearthstone semantics: a tutorial encounter cannot be lost however it is played.

Rejected: proving the coach's line alone, which shows a lesson can be won, not that it cannot be
lost.
