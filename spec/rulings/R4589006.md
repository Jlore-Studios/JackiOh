R4589006 (IA #119 Snapshot Lich): "your permanents" are the Lich's other permanents, counted the
way [[R3550007]] counts them: its start of turn resets only with fewer permanents on its side,
itself not counted, than its note holds. The reset is [[R3550007]]'s, with the Lich left standing
— its slot holds no snapshot card — and its note kept, so it may reset again later. The Radiant
face also draws a card.
