Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**Why.** A name in a card's text is a reference ([[R279]]): in the detail view, a resting mouse or focus on it opens a tooltip with the named card's face. On the Almanac those are a card's related cards, its tokens and the cards it names. A full-art card's face prints no words ([[R1017001]]), so its tooltip showed only its art. The owner asked in issue #3838 that the related cards' text can be read on hover too.

**The rule.** A reference's tooltip carries the named card's whole text in the Full text panel (`Extended.tsx`), as the hover preview does:

- Everywhere, beside a face that prints less than its whole text, a full-art card's among them.
- Below an `AlwaysFullText`, the Almanac's ([[R3838001]]), beside any face with words.

**Its size.** The face stays `REF_TOOLTIP_HEIGHT_PX` tall, never past the viewport (`refTooltipSize`, placement.ts):

- The panel stands on the face's left at the hover preview's width (`PREVIEW_EXTENDED_WIDTH_PX`), the face's height at most.
- On a phone (`isPhoneViewport`) it stacks above the face across its width, `PREVIEW_EXTENDED_STACKED_MAX_PX` tall at most, and the face shrinks so both fit the viewport's height.

The tooltip still takes no pointer, and its own references open nothing.

Rejected: the panel only on the Almanac, which would leave a full-art card's tooltip wordless in every other detail view.
