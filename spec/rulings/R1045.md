## R1045 — Radiant Paranoia's Echo is read as the Spell reveals

Built (Meditative #99; patch v0.3.7, #781). Radiant Paranoia prints "Spells you reveal have Echo
+{echo}." It is read as a Spell its controller reveals: while a Radiant Paranoia acts on their field
then, the Spell resolves with Echo +{echo} more (`alt_play::reveal_echo_bonus`), however it was set
(lent Cloaked by Paranoia or printing it), and however many Radiant Paranoias act (the largest one's
number, never their sum). A Paranoia that left before the reveal adds none. A set Spell stays set
after Paranoia leaves the field; bounced or destroyed first, it is its printed Spell again in hand or
graveyard.

Until patch v0.3.7 the Echo was stamped as the Spell was set, once, by a Radiant Paranoia acting
then.
