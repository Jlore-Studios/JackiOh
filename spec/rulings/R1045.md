## R1045 — A set Spell reveals on time after its permission leaves

Built (ME-ALTPLAY, Meditative #99). A set Spell stays set and reveals on time after Paranoia leaves
the field; bounced or destroyed first, it is its printed Spell again in hand or graveyard. One
Radiant Paranoia acting as it is set gives it Echo +1, however many permit the play.
