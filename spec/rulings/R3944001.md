On the Patch notes page ([[R724000]]) a micro that some major or patch lists in its `includes` ([[R3944000]]) sits inside that entry, oldest first, under the heading "Snapshots", and belongs to that entry's release line even when it comes before the major that opened it. A micro no entry includes sits under the nearest major or patch before it, as [[R3012000]] has it, unless it comes after the newest major or patch of the history: those micros, the ones the next daily patch will roll up, sit in a block of their own above the release lines, "Since the last patch", and in no entry. An entry with `includes` says how many snapshots it rolls up. An `includes` that names a micro after its entry is ignored, and the micro sits where its order puts it. The legend counts an entry's snapshots and leaves the waiting block out. Every reading is of `tier` and `includes`; no version is parsed ([[R105]]).
