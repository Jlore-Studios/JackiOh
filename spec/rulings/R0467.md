Runs the Cry of a Unit on top of its pile or in a graveyard, for the triggering controller. Declared targets and modes are asked as prompts in declaration order: modes first when a target belongs to a mode; an unsatisfiable declaration is skipped; a Tribute's slots are left `none` and nothing is paid. On the field the Unit is "this"; from a graveyard "this" finds nothing and X reads 0. It is not a play: no `cardPlayed` and no counters. A Unit that leaves before its choices are made triggers nothing. The Cry's own tail runs before the triggering card's ([[R113]]).
