Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: S+ #80 The Last Voyage's "Launch a random one at the start of each of your turns" (Radiant: "now and at the start of each of your turns") becomes "at the end of each of your turns". Its rest-of-game launch ([[R3539002]]) is [[R458]]'s effect with `atEnd` ([[R1861002]]): re-entered in the end of turn's delayed stage ([[R62]]), once at the end of each of its caster's turns and never at the other player's, a free launch ([[R1852006]]). Made by the Spell in its caster's main phase, it comes due first at the end of the turn it is cast on, as Hearthstone's "at the end of your turn" counts the turn it is played on: the base face launches its first Starship then, and the Radiant face, which launches one at once too, its second. Its badge reads "End of turn: Launch a random Terminus Starship." ([[R169]]). Through v0.6.9n the launch came at the start of each of its caster's turns from the next on, which `script_v0_6_9n` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
