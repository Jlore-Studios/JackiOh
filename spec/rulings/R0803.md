"Give one of your Units to your opponent" (Meditative #6 Me no Likey, `give_control`, [[R1423]]) moves the Unit to the opponent's side and pays its cost there in mana. The cost is the Unit's cost on the field, read before it moves ([[R65]]): an X Unit costs its X ([[R396]]), a token its printed cost. With no free zone on the opponent's side, nothing moves and no mana is gained, because the mana is the price of the gift. The owner does not change ([[§3.2]]), so a given Unit that dies goes to its owner's graveyard.
