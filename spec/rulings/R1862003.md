"Activate: Summon a Rush Token that attacks an enemy Unit, Taunt Units first" takes a declared enemy Unit ([[R81]]), never a hero. "Taunt Units first" reads [[§4.2]] step 3 for the choice: while the enemy has a Unit with Taunt that an attack may name, only those may be chosen; its Taunt is read through the layers, so an Indestructible Unit has none ([[R347]]), and a Taunt Unit that can't be attacked, or that a carrier holds, binds nobody, as a Taunt binds only the attackers that may attack it. The Rush Token ([[§7]]; Radiant on the Radiant face) is summoned into the leftmost open unit zone, else the leftmost empty Locked one ([[R64]], [[R688]]), and makes a forced attack on that Unit ([[R53]]), as Core #60 Bear Honeypot's tokens do: the Unit strikes back, and one the token may not attack (§4.2 step 2) is not attacked. The Activate is not offered or accepted (`canActivate`) while its controller has no zone for the token, none open and none Locked and empty, as #60 waits for one ([[R430]]) and Hearthstone's summoning Hero Powers wait for room (a Locked zone refuses only plays, [[R688]], so a summon with no named zone enters it once no unlocked zone is open; through v0.5.11p the Activate wanted an open one and was not offered while the only free unit zone was Locked, #3040), nor while the enemy has no Unit, as C #78 Mutate Spell's ability waits for a pick, since a declaration with no legal pick is passed and would summon a token that attacks nothing. Rejected: spending the use on a full board for nothing, refusing the Activate while only a Locked zone is free when the summon itself enters it, and letting the token attack a hero, which "an enemy Unit" rules out.
