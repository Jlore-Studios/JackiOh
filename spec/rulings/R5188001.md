Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The card parts of issue #5187 give the shipped cards' recurring hooks ([[R5188000]]) at most 1,331 new lines: one per card at Common and Rare, two at Epic, three at Legendary and four at Mythic, none for a token, over the 979 cards and tokens of the shipped sets. The set's 1,908 files take 20.49 MiB of [[R3596000]]'s 24 MiB today, counted in 4 KiB blocks (`voice-manifest.json`); the 1,331 lines, at the attack lines' mean of 8.43 KiB a file (four words, as theirs are), add about 10.96 MiB, 31.45 MiB in all, so they do not fit.

`VOICE_BUDGET_BYTES` (`apps/web/src/audio/constants.ts`, and `gen-voice.mjs`'s `BUDGET_BYTES` beside it) is 32 MiB, still counted in 4 KiB blocks, and every line still stays within 4 s ([[R501]]). The Infinite Assault set's 523 lines, owed when it ships ([[R1420]]), do not fit beside them and will need a raise of their own. The budget bounds what is deployed, not what one player downloads: the client fetches a file only when it needs the line.

Three checks keep the growth to that: **the word cap**, every line added since `apps/web/src/audio/census-baseline.json` (the lines and effects `main` had before [[R5188000]]) is four words at most (`VOICE_NEW_LINE_MAX_WORDS`, `voice-lines.test.ts`), as an attack line is ([[R1448]]); **the tables' gzip cap**, `card-audio.json5`, `voice-text.json` and `voice-manifest.json`, each gzipped and summed, stay within `SOUND_TABLES_GZIP_BUDGET_BYTES`, 144 KiB, set from their 90,954 bytes when it was written (92,801 once #5190's spell-verb effects landed) and a simulation of the card parts' 3,000 hook entries and 1,331 lines (about 142,000 bytes), with room for that and little more; and **the census**, for every card in the ranges `sound-space.test.ts` lists (`CENSUS_RANGES`, one range of catalog ids per line, none yet: each card part adds its own), the sounds added since the baseline (each line of a pool and each hook's effect counted) are at least its rarity's floor (`CENSUS_SOUND_FLOOR`) and its lines at most its rarity's ceiling (`CENSUS_VOICE_CEILING`). **Rejected:** a lower bitrate for every file, which would re-render and re-check every shipped line, as [[R1884000]] and [[R3596000]] rejected it.
