[[R1862006]] split the Book tag for Stinkyscape #143 Book Launcher by naming its harmful and helpful Books card by card, so a Book printed later joined neither list until someone added it. It rejected reading each Book's declared aim ([[R656]]) because most Books left it unset. Since issue #3869 the lists are read off the Books: a catalog query takes `aim`, the aim a card's base face declares outright on its first target, read off its registered script, and Book Launcher casts a random Book-tagged card whose first target declares `harm` at an enemy and one that declares `help` at an ally ([[R1862004]]). Classic #12 Book of Blood, #16 Book of Flame, #55 Book of Wildfire and Classic+ #72 Book of Nerf now declare `harm`, which a random cast already read their unset aim as, so no game plays differently; Classic #3 Book of Heal, Classic+ #57 Book of Stats and #71 Book of Buff already declared `help`. A target that leaves its aim unset matches neither, so Classic #29 Book of Vital Kill, a harm or a help by the hero it names, stays in neither, as does every Book with no declared target. The script kept for v0.5.9f keeps its two named lists ([[R767000]]). A Book printed with a target must declare its aim, or Book Launcher never casts it; a test holds every targeted Book but Vital Kill to that.
