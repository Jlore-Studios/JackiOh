title: A permanent's lasting effect lasts while it is on the field
cards: "#36, #49, #52, #79, #83, #85"
proven_in:

A lasting effect a permanent installs on its player — #79 Twinspell's "the next Spell you play gains Echo +1" through v0.5.11p, which [[§5.1]]'s "a permanent in the backrow with a lasting effect" described — belongs to that permanent's stay on the field. It is the permanent's and not a Cry's (that Twinspell printed no "Cry:", [[R169]]), so the permanent has it however it came to stand on the field — played, summoned (#22's copies, #95's backrow, #98's recruit), or carried onto another permanent by a Fuse (#85, [[R77]]), whose controller it then serves ([[§8]] Conventions). It ends the moment the permanent leaves, however it leaves (destroyed by #36 or #88, bounced by #52's radiant face or a Locked rotation, exiled, eaten by #22, replaced by #83, fused away by #85), and a permanent that comes back installs its own, so one such Twinspell on the field granted one Echo ([[R174]]). While it stands the effect follows the permanent: to its new controller when control changes ([[R171]], [[§8]] Conventions), and to its current face, so a Twinspell made Radiant on the field (#49 radiant) granted Echo +2 from then on without its Cry firing again ([[§5.2]]). [[R30]]'s consumption is unchanged: the Spell that takes the grant sends that permanent to its owner's graveyard. Since issue #3044 Twinspell is an Activate whose grant is its player's for the turn, a rider no permanent owns, so it outlives the Twinspell that gave it, and a Twinspell standing on the field gives nothing until it is activated ([[R3044000]]); the engine keeps the lasting grant for the replays of v0.5.11p and before ([[R767000]]). Hearthstone ends a minion's or a location's effect when it leaves play
