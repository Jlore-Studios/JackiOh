A hero's starting health ([[R1866002]], [[R4587004]]) in a Duos duel is the own HP its seat started on, its team's `own_health` ([[§13.2]], [[§13.4]]): 20 on every team's rules, the Hard AI team's included. It does not move when the seat is spent: the hero's health then reads the pool and is compared with the same start, so Stinkyscape #18.2 BALTHASAR votes at 9 or less, of own HP or, once spent, of the pool, and IA #71 Thermal Throttler counts what is missing from 20. A seat's handicap `heroHealth` ([[R290]], [[R180]]) is not read in Duos, since setup puts the hero on own HP whatever the handicap; a Glitch reset ([[R1168002]]) starts own HP over at the same value. The engine keeps the start on the seat's `DuosLife` as `ownStart`, written at setup and absent at `DUOS_OWN_HEALTH`, so a co-op game on the standard rules serializes and hashes as before. Rejected: the pool's cap once the seat is spent, which would move the threshold the moment the seat spends and make it vote at a pool of 14 on the Hard AI team's 30; the seat's whole life, own HP and pool together (40), which the AI's valuation anchors at ([[R964000]]) but which is no hero's health; and HERO_HEALTH (30), which no Duos hero starts on. This amends [[R1866002]] and [[R4587004]], written for 1v1 and the handicaps.
