[[R58]] caps the cast-on-draw chain one draw sets off at 20 casts, and [[R217]] makes every draw but a cast's own begin a chain of its own, a trigger's among them. The daily fuzz's seed 9688 found a loop between the chains: each #21 Hinder cast is a play #33 Unstable Clone Machine answers with three more Hinders in the library ([[R70]]), so the library never runs dry, and each Hinder's discard is answered by Classic #64 Malzahar's Recycler's draw, a chain of its own. Each chain cast 20 and sent its 21st to the hand, the next chain's first cast discarded that one, which drew again, and the action never ended. The spec bounded a chain and no action; this note bounds the action, as [[R28]] bounds a Call to Chaos chain. Hearthstone has no cast-on-draw loop to bound.

- **The cap.** One action (one `reduce`, with everything it settles, the turns it ends and the autopilot's actions it plays) casts at most `CAST_ON_DRAW_ACTION_CAP` (`LIBRARY_CAP`, 60) cast-on-draw cards on draw, over all its chains, so an action that does not refill its library as it draws never reaches it.
- **Past it.** The next cast-on-draw card drawn goes to the hand uncast (burned if the hand is full), as [[R58]]'s 21st does, and its draw does not repeat. [[R58]] still holds inside each chain.
- **The count.** It counts every cast a draw makes (`draw::complete_draw`), in any chain and on either side, and dies with the action: the action that answers a cast's prompt starts at 0, and each action of a turn #96 My Pawn plays for the AI is a `reduce` of its own. A card dealt in setup and cast at the start ([[R748]]) does not count.
