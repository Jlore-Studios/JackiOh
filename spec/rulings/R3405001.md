Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Meditative's 32 Legendaries and Mythics, its tokens by their printed rarity, come off the signature ratchet ([[R3332000]]), each with its own entry in `meditative.json5` and its recipe in `fx/cards/meditative/`, on the frame of [[R3400000]], [[R3389000]] and [[R3405000]], drawn with figures (`fx/figures.ts`: shapes in a tint over their anchor's box, never a card face). A Unit lands with an entrance of its own and plays its text's beat, a standing look on its own tile; a Spell plays a cast of its own; a Field Spell lands face up with an entrance of its own, plays its text's beat and stands a look on its zone or lane; each Mythic takes over in a look of its own with art, standing still under Reduce Motion.

Each sounds its own effect once (`audio/signatureCues.ts`): a Spell or a Field Spell as its play is read, a Unit as it lands face up in a unit zone, and The Cane as it lands face up in the backrow without a play ([[R3405000]]'s `landed`), each layering its card's three sounds within 1.6 s and gain 0.8: `bowlBreath`, `peakFlute`, `ticketHorn`, `forgeQuench`, `brushGuzheng`, `wellGush`, `minePick`, `jadeZither`, `windChime`, `marketGong`, `chalkHush`, `porcelainRoar`, `bronzeGulp`, `modemGuqin`, `serverChoir`, `dialupStutter`, `holoBow`, `synthPops`, `skullMutter`, `keyJangle`, `caneCrack`, `caneThunder`, `rootCreak`, `chalkGasp`, `frostPages`, `coinCheer`, `totemDrums`, `templeDie`, `golemStomp`, `klaxonSlam`, `cellSlam` and `hangarRev`.

Every recipe keys on its own play or hook and how many events of a kind it has seen, never on another card's definition. Every card flying to a pile is a card back; a beat only its owner may see (a Discover, a face added to the hand, a hand card's cost) shows as a sheen on its hand pile for the viewer who owns it alone, and nothing at all from the other seat; a card in a hand, a deck or face down has no place on the other seat, so a beat that would reach it draws at its pile, its zone or the board's centre ([[R202]]). Every beat returns `row: "replace"`, its shakes within its type's budget, every cue within its entry plus `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` and nothing with the effects off ([[R200]]), and each signature's gallery play within its rarity's and type's ceiling.
