Meditative #42 CN Flea Market's market offers every lot twice while the yuan covers it: on its base face at [[R1000]]'s price, and on its Radiant face at the Radiant price (`YUAN_RADIANT_MULTIPLIER` times the base). The Radiant option's key is the lot's with `:radiant` after it (`MARKET_RADIANT_SUFFIX`) and its `radiant` flag set, so a client draws it on its Radiant face; a bought Radiant lot arrives Radiant in the caster's hand at its printed cost, and the lot is spent whichever face was bought. Nothing else changes: the stall, the barters on the Radiant face ([[R1001]]) and Leave are as [[R1000]] has them. The issue's ten new CN and AI cards for the stall are left to the designer.
