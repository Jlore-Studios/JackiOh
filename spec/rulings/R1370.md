[[§9.9]]'s deck builder (`build_ai_deck`) takes one more option, `leanSet`: a set the deck leans on. At least `ceil(size × leanMinShare)` of the deck comes from that set (`AI_DECK.leanMinShare`, 0.5: 10 of 20, 13 of a Medium AI's 25, rounded up, and 15 of a Hard AI's 30). It is a hard floor, as the theme's `themeMinShare` and the units' `minUnitShare` are, so it holds whatever the rng does. While the deck is short of the set its cards weigh `leanBoost` (6) more, so the floor seldom has to force a card in at the end (fewer than one deck in ten, measured at every tier's size); once the floor is met the boost stops, and the rest is dealt as without the option: the curve, the unit and the theme weights, the uncastable weight and R390's `boost`. The shadow ban ([[R186]]) still keeps its cards out of the AI's own decks, and `banned: []` still deals a player's deck from every card.

The three floors are kept together. Each step works out the fewest cards that would still meet every floor from the cards left, and while that fits the slots left with one to spare any card may be drawn; once it needs every slot left, only a card that some fewest way of meeting them would take may be drawn. So a card that counts toward two floors is taken over one that counts toward one only when the slots need it, and every floor holds to the last card whenever the cards left can meet them all. A floor whose cards are fewer than it owes owes only those, so a set short of cards (a small set, a pool the bans have thinned) gives the deck every card it has, and the deck is still dealt whole. When the slots left cannot meet every floor, the deck keeps the leaned set's before the theme's before the units', the order a tie between the theme and the units always went.

Without `leanSet` the draw is the one it always was: the theme's narrowing, then the units', at the moment their slots run out, no extra rng draw, and every seed deals the deck it dealt before this ruling, so no golden trace, seed-pinned test or replay moves. The option names the set rather than switching on "the newest set", so a tool can lean on a set that has not shipped by previewing it ([[R1420]]); a set the pool holds none of gives none. "More cards from the newest set" passes the newest set that ships ([[R1371]]).
