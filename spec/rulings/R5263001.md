[[R276]] holds every card's Radiant face to differ from its base face, and a card whose base face prints a text to print one on its Radiant face too. The Felinors vs Humans list gives two cards faces it refuses: #47 Blazing Battlemage, a 1/1 whose Cry makes it Radiant, prints a 3/3 with no text as its Radiant face, and #66 Fuzzyfruit prints "Summon a random 1 cost Felinor and give it +1/+1" on both.

**The rule.** The smallest change that keeps each card's design:

- Blazing Battlemage's Radiant face prints its base face's Cry, "Cry: Make this Radiant.", and runs it. A Make Radiant of a card Radiant already does nothing ([[§6.3]], [[R22]]), so a Radiant Battlemage still plays as the designer's 3/3 with no ability, its stats the face's change.
- Fuzzyfruit's Radiant face gives the Felinor it summons +2/+2 (its declared `stats`, Radiant 2), the base face's +1/+1 doubled; the summon stays not Radiant, as the designer wrote.
