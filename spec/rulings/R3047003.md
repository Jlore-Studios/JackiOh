A buy of a Buffed or Nerfed lot ([[R3047002]]) lands each Buff and Nerf on the card it adds, as every Buff and Nerf is landed ([[R386]]): once the card is in its buyer's hand with its cost set to (0) ([[R3047001]]), each Buff is one Upgrade drawn from the card's menu of rows, as Book of Buff's is (`tune_once`, `effects::upgrade`), then each Nerf one Degrade. A lot has Buffs or Nerfs, never both, so the order between them is moot. Each Nerf takes the Degrade that spares the cost row (`degrade_sparing_cost`, [[R1845001]]), so the cost modifier never lifts the (0): a card in a hand is open to a Nerf of its cost, and the buyer would pay mana for a card they paid Credits for. A Buff's cost row has nothing to lower on a card that costs (0). The rows left (stats, keywords, numbers) are as [[§6.3]] draws them, from the match rng, and a card that is Immutable takes none.

They run in a context built as a played card's hidden Nerfs are (`resolve::make_context`, the buyer as controller, [[R1845001]]), and report as every Buff and Nerf does, after the buy's own events: `marketBought`, `marketCreditsChanged`, `addedToHand`, then one `upgraded` or `degraded` for each count, in that order, each hidden from the other seat, which may not read the card ([[R177]]). A lot with neither count, and every lot an effect added ([[R3047002]]), reports the three events and nothing more. The card is not Buffed or Nerfed on the offer before it is bought: the offer shows its count, and the card changes as it is bought.
