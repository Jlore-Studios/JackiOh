title: A lasting Echo grant waits for a Spell, then its permanent goes to the GY (Twinspell through v0.5.11p)
cards: "#79"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/rulings_a.rs
---
A permanent's lasting Echo grant (`staticFlags.echoGrant`, #79 Twinspell's "the next Spell you play gains Echo +1" through v0.5.11p) stays until a Spell is played, surviving cleanup, then goes to the GY with the permanent that gave it. Since issue #3044 Twinspell is an Activate whose grant lasts the turn and whose base face goes to the GY as it is used ([[R3044000]]); the engine keeps this grant for the replays of v0.5.11p and before ([[R767000]])
