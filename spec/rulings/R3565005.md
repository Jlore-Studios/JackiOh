It is read as [[R3549000]]'s Discover, without Equip or a discount: one Discover of three ([[§6.3]]) from the non-token Units of the Stinkyscape+ set carrying any of the six Automa tags ([[R3565001]]), the pool naming its set ([[R1420]]), never the card that made it ([[R387]]). A full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). The Radiant face adds the pick Radiant, at its printed price. The AI, the random policy and the timeout answer the prompt.
