S+ #74 Galaxia's "Aura: The first Unit you play each turn gets +3 Attack" is not a continuous layer but a standing answer to its controller's plays, as Double Header's is ([[R826]]): while Galaxia stands on the field, the first Unit her controller plays each turn gets a permanent +3 Attack (+6 on the Radiant face) once its play resolves, its Cry included, and keeps it when the turn ends or she leaves. "First" is the first Unit play she hears while she stands: a Unit played earlier in the turn than her arrival was played before she stood, so, as the designer's note says, the first Unit played after her on the turn she arrives counts as that turn's first, whether she was played or summoned. She does not answer her own play, nor a play that resolves as she arrives ([[R119]]), so she never buffs herself. A card set face-down is no Unit ([[R783004]]), a cast on draw is no play ([[R2179002]]), and only her controller's plays count. She keeps the turn she last gave the attack, so each turn is a new offer, and a second Galaxia gives its own.

Rejected: a layer-5 aura on whichever Unit is the turn's first, which would end with the turn or with her and would show the attack to the Unit's own Cry; counting the Units played before her arrival, so that nothing is the first after her on the turn she lands; and a count of Units played this turn, which a play made while another resolves would miscount.
