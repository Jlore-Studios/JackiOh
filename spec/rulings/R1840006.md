The asteroids are their controller's mining nodes. "Activate: Take N damage. Tribute this" ([[R384]]) is the controller's hero taking N damage, then a Sacrifice of the asteroid ([[§6.3]] Sacrifice), which is a death: its Death runs and gains its controller the Resources it names ("gain N random Resources"), into their tray ([[R1840000]]). The Radiant face drops the damage and is the Sacrifice alone; a Radiant asteroid's Death gains Radiant Resources where its text says so.
