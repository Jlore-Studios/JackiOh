The issue describes each version's look; this note fixes how each is drawn. Like every look, each is
a pure function of the card's id, name, tags and type, base and Radiant faces both, and wears only a
full face, as Full art does ([[R2725000]]). Each keeps every part of the normal face: the cost gem,
the type's panel colour, the crest, the name banner, the type line, the rules box and its words, the
tags and the stats.

**Extended art** (`version-extended-art`) is a borderless print. The card's own picture, the normal
one (a delivered file included), runs past its window across the whole panel, at the full-art shape
([[R1017001]]): a square `EXTENDED_ART_PICTURE_PCT` of the face's height standing on its foot, so its
middle, where a painter puts the subject, sits a quarter of the way down where the normal window's
does, clear of the name banner, and the rest reaches every edge. The face's frame lies over it
translucent: the panel itself at `EXTENDED_ART_FRAME_OPACITY` at the rim, fading to nothing toward the
middle as [[R2720000]]'s finish band does. A base face's rules box is its parchment at `EXTENDED_ART_BOX_PCT` over the picture, so
that over the darkest picture, its watermark on it, every ink reads at least as well as over the
normal box's edge ([[R2718000]]). It is not a full art: the face prints its words, and keeps them in
the viewer's own hand ([[R2723000]] is Full art's alone).

**Pixel** (`version-pixel`) draws the face's normal picture, whichever painter draws it (a Legacy
card's and a Starship's too, [[R2724000]], [[R2249000]]), under one SVG filter (`pixelSvg`,
`data-art-picture="pixel"`): sampled once in the middle of every `PIXEL_CELL` square of the picture's
100 units, each sample grown by half a cell to fill it over the picture blurred by as much (a browser
grows a sample by whole pixels, and the blur fills whatever seam a small face leaves), and on a base
face each colour channel cut to its nearest of `PIXEL_LEVELS`, 27 colours whose hues are each a
multiple of 30°. On a base face its frame is retro:
an edge of stepped slate bars, a slate bezel and a hard-stepped black ring round the window. Its name
banner is flat, and the name is set in a pixel face built in, a 5×7 bitmap of capitals, digits and
the signs card names use (`pixelName.ts`), white over a black drop shadow, as the banner's
background; the banner's words stay in the DOM, unseen, so a screen reader and a finder by name read
them. A name the face has no glyph for (a Chinese name) prints in the normal face, and so does one
too long to read in it: the pixel face draws on one line, so its cells shrink as a name grows, and
it takes a name of at most `PIXEL_NAME_MAX_GLYPHS` glyphs, the most that keeps every cell at
`PIXEL_NAME_CELL_MIN_PX` on the smallest full face (`FACE_TEXT_MIN_HEIGHT_PX` tall), across the
banner less its padding. A small face, a hand card's, prints the name in the normal face too, on the
two lines its banner gives it, since one line of cells there would draw them under a pixel.

**Comic** (`version-comic`) draws the normal picture under one SVG filter (`comicSvg`,
`data-art-picture="comic"`): smoothed (`COMIC_SMOOTH`) and brightened (`COMIC_LIFT`), its colour pushed
(`COMIC_SATURATE`) and flattened to the nearest of `COMIC_LEVELS`,
halftone dots of slate ink (`COMIC_INK`, `--pal-slate-900`) laid over its lighter parts, and a heavy
ink outline round every edge between its flat colours. On a base face its frame is a heavy black edge
with a white bezel inked round the window, and its name is set bold and italic in a burst, black on
cyan (`--pal-cyan-400`): the issue's pop art, cyan since yellow lies in Radiant's gold.

**A delivered file** (an artist's `public/art/` file) is the normal picture's: Extended art shows it,
and Pixel and Comic draw the painter's picture until an artist delivers theirs. Every restyled
picture is cached apart from the plain one, which keeps its key and bytes.

**Radiant.** [[R2725004]] holds under each: no colour of the three, frame, ring, banner, burst, name
face, pixel palette, flat colour, dot or ink, falls in the `RADIANT_GOLD_*` window, and none tints a
base face's picture gold. A Radiant face keeps its gold-foil frame (a gold rim round Extended art's
picture), its gilded panel and banner, its gold rules box, [[R277]]'s marks wherever its words show,
its gold stats and its picture's Radiant paint: a Radiant Pixel keeps its painted colours uncut, and a
Radiant Comic washes its painted picture back over the pop art (`COMIC_RADIANT_WASH`).
