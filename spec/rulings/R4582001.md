An Infinite Assault fight in progress is kept the way [[R668]] keeps a free game: its config, its
action log, the cursor of the AI's own stream and the state's hash, after every answer, in the
worker's own storage (IndexedDB), never sent to the page, whose log would name the AI's hidden
cards (rule 7). It is kept in a slot of its own, keyed by the fight (its seed names the day, the
level and the attempt, [[§15.6]]), so it never touches the free game's save or a dungeon fight's:
each slot holds at most one game, and no two overwrite each other in either direction.

When the attempt opens the fight again, the worker resumes the kept fight: the log is replayed
action by action under the same nonces and must reach the saved hash, and the AI picks its stream
up at the saved cursor, so the resumed fight plays on exactly as it would have, on its turn with
its board, hands and log, as a dungeon fight does ([[R3476000]]). A save that will not fold deals
the fight fresh from its seed. A finished fight clears the slot, and the next Assault deal
replaces it.

Menu asks before a fight in progress is left ([[R2972000]], [[R1754000]]), and Leave goes back
through the page's day screen, the attempt still on its fight, or to Play when the page gives no
way back ([[R3932000]]). Once the game is over, Menu sends the report to the attempt first and
leaves without asking.

Rejected: sharing the dungeon fight's slot, whose save another mode's fight would then refuse or,
worse, resume. This follows Hearthstone semantics: a Dungeon Run match in progress is resumed when
the player comes back, not dealt again.
