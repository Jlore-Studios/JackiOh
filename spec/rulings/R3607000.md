Part of #3604 (issue #3607, option (a)): a board minion's looping keyword treatments move only while its face holds one of the motion slots ([[R3340000]], [[R1295000]]), and otherwise show their still frame. Each face makes one ranked claim (`motionSlots.ts`, the hovered face first), and that claim now covers its keyword loops beside its art, foil and shipyard idle; the per-unit choice stays `keywordFxPlan`'s (`AMBIENT_MAX` loops at once, Charge and Rush only while the view's `canAct` is true), so a face past the budget stills every loop it planned. A face off screen, a hidden tab, the shed ladder at `SHED_FACES` and every reduced-motion setting hold all loops still — at `SHED_FACES` the hovered face keeps its loops, as it keeps its slot ([[R3351000]]) — while Animated foil off stills foils only, not keyword marks. Every treatment still renders its still frame with its `data-keyword-fx` attributes, which reads as its keyword ([[R438]]), under every motion setting ([[R200]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. Hearthstone semantics do not reach frame budgets.

Rejected: a budget of their own (`KEYWORD_LOOPS_ANIMATED_MAX`, ranked as the motion slots are), which would spend a second allocator, a second rank and a second shed rule on what the face's one ranked, shed-aware, visibility-aware claim already decides.
