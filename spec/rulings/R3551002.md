## Question

Every existing power costs (1) less, and End Times (10) Discovers a Terminus card with an End
of Turn cut. How far do the cuts stack, what pool does it Discover from, and which script
plays which edition?

## Decision

The thirteen cost (1) less than the kept v0.5.13g script's, never below (0) — the new face's
prices, while the kept script's are the old ones. End Times' cuts add up over its
controller's ends of turn — (1), (2) Radiant — floor at (0), and are taken back when it is
used, so the next use costs (10) again. Its pool is the shipped Terminus cards, tokens
included. The script's declarations pick the edition: a script declaring the `endTimes`
ability plays all fourteen at the new prices, any other the kept thirteen at the old ones.
The Cry, the arrival roll and Tank Up's refresh all read the card's edition, and the hero
panel shows the live price.

## Rejected alternative

A new `ActivationCost` field for the moving price: the declared number (`power: "endTimes"`,
[[R1430]]) reaches the cost through the existing `mana_param`, as a Buffed price does.
