An attempt starts at level 1 or at a checkpoint the day's record holds (6, 11 or 16, one higher
than each beaten Commander below the top), on the attempt after the record's last. The deck is
checked at create against the registered catalog at `HUMAN_HANDICAP`'s size with `MAX_COPIES`
([[R184]], [[§9.9]]): an attempt lasts one day, so [[R3496000]]'s filtering, which a run needs
because a patch may land mid-run, is refused here instead. A briefing fights, a fight's result
moves to the next level's briefing or ends the attempt lost, beating the top repels the Assault,
and abandoning from a briefing or a fight abandons it. Every other pair, a negative turn count,
another day's state and every step of an ended attempt are refused with the state unchanged.

The record folds a finished or running attempt idempotently: the larger attempt number, the
highest level beaten, each beaten Commander level below the top as its next level's checkpoint,
and the smaller repelling attempt. The file is the setup, the record at its start and the action
log, with the catalog version the caller folds under; the fold names the first refused step, as
a dungeon run's does. The fight's seed is `{day seed}-L{level}-A{attempt}`; the human plays its
deck at `HUMAN_HANDICAP` in P1, the enemy the level's deck, handicap and marks. The fight's
report is the dungeon run's, folded to a win and turns: a draw at the turn cap is a loss.
