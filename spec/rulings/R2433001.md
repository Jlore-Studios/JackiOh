[[§2.6]] L3 holds a deck to one copy of each card id (`MAX_COPIES`). The remade tutorial's first
lesson is mostly vanilla Units, and Training Grounds has only three with no text at all (#1, #8 and
#43) and one with Rush alone (#4), so a lesson's human deck needs copies of them. A handicap
([[R180]]) takes one more optional field, `deckCopies`: the most copies of one card id that seat's
deck may hold, in place of `MAX_COPIES`. `TUTORIAL_HUMAN` sets it to `TUTORIAL_DECK_COPIES` (4)
([[R1705005]]). No tier and not `AI_TUTORIAL` sets it, so every other seat still refuses a
duplicate with L3's own message. It must be a positive integer (`validate_handicap`). Like the
other optional fields, it is stored only when it differs from `MAX_COPIES`, so no other game's
state, hash or replay moves.

The deck is checked where L3 is checked when a game is made (`validate_setup`, so `createGame` and
the browser's deck check alike). A copy over the limit is refused, naming the seat's limit. The pair
rule ([[R2195000]]) is unchanged: a Core card and its Training Grounds snapshot are still one card
for a deck, so a deck holds copies of one or the other, never both. Two copies of the same id are
no pair. The deck builder's saved decks ([[§9.4]]) are no handicapped seat's and keep one copy.

Hearthstone semantics: a scripted encounter's decks are the encounter's own, and the constructed
rules do not bind them, as [[R1705005]] says of a lesson's deck size.

Amended by issue #2533 ([[R2533001]]): `AI_TUTORIAL` sets `deckCopies` to `TUTORIAL_DECK_COPIES`
too, so a lesson's opponent may hold copies of a vanilla unit as its human does.
