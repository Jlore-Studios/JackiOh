No effect reaches a Dormant card ([[§6.1]] Dormant, [[R1839000]]): no declaration or `target` prompt offers it, and every verb leaves it be. Four picks drawn at random from the board did not know it, since they drew from walks of the whole field that keep it:

- M #69 The Maestro's field exile, which drew from every permanent of the opponent's field and could exile a Dormant card, a Dormant Starship with every piece fused into it among them;
- C #40 MC Tech's random steal and S+ #25 Snom Bunny's Death ([[R3550009]]), which drew among the opponent's acting Units and every backrow card, so a Dormant Field Spell (S+ #56, #62 and #63, [[R3556000]]) could be drawn and the steal then did nothing;
- a Plague Counter placement drawn at random, inside a random cast ([[R452]]) or while a Mayor acts ([[R1200]]), which could put its counters on a Dormant card.

**Each pick now draws only among the permanents an effect reaches.** The Maestro's field pick and the random placement draw from `plague::reachable_permanents_on_field`, every permanent on the field in [[R68]]'s order less the Dormant cards (the Radiant picks' field pool reads it too); MC Tech and Snom Bunny draw from `query::enemy_permanents`, which leaves out a Dormant backrow card as `active_units_of` already leaves out a Dormant Unit. A placement its player chooses was already right, since its prompt never offers a Dormant card. With no Dormant card on the board every pool is the walk it was, in the same order, so the draws are the same.

**With only Dormant cards left, the pick finds nothing.** The Maestro skips the opponent's field as it skips an empty zone ([[R1110]]); the placements fizzle and draw nothing ([[R129]]); Snom Bunny's Death takes nothing and draws nothing.

**MC Tech does not count a Dormant card.** Its "5 or more permanents" counts what it could steal, on both faces and in its hand glow: a Dormant Field Spell counts for nothing, as a Dormant Unit already did not. Hearthstone's effects that count or pick minions pass a Dormant minion by. As a side effect, a Dormant Unit standing in a backrow zone ([[R1081000]]) no longer enters MC Tech's or Snom Bunny's pool through the backrow walk.

**The verbs are unchanged.** The guard is on the pools, not on exile or on a placement: a board sweep is no effect reaching for a card, so The Last Voyage's exile still reaches a Dormant card ([[R3539002]]).

**MC Tech is no longer complicated.** Its script loses its own copy of the walk, so its code lines fall from 63 to 50, under `COMPLICATED_CODE_LINES` (60): it leaves the complicated list ([[R1846000]]), and S #111 Shockwave447's random complicated cards no longer draw it ([[R2173001]]). It was never BUSTED, so no BUSTED pool changes.

A replay this change plays differently is refused as `rules_changed` rather than shown wrong ([[R767000]]); one folded under an older shipped catalog keeps that version's complicated list ([[R2173000]]).

Rejected:
- Counting a Dormant Field Spell toward MC Tech's 5 but never drawing it: the count would hold a sleeping Field Spell and not a sleeping Unit.
- A Dormant check in `exile_card` or `carries_plague`: it would also stop the board sweep [[R3539002]] lets through.
- Keeping MC Tech's old script for the replays of older catalogs: the three pools in the engine change for every catalog anyway, so a kept card script would keep nothing whole.
