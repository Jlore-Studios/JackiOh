The brief of #1651 (its §8) makes the account cosmetics, the playmats, backdrops and card backs
of [[R1297000]], a drop of their own ([[R1661001]]), Epic or better:

- **The re-rate.** The six below Epic become Epic: Wood, Embers and Lacquer (Common) and Felt,
  Rain and Starfield (Rare). Neon grid, Fireflies and Blueprint stay Epic, and each kind's default
  (Tavern table, No backdrop, Standard back) stays Common, what a board draws with nothing picked.
- **The check.** The registry's check refuses a `table`, `field` or `cardBack` entry that is not
  its kind's default and is below `GLOBAL_COSMETIC_FLOOR_RARITY`, Epic, the global roll's floor. A
  per-card cosmetic keeps any rarity.
- **Still starters.** Every one keeps its starter mark, so every account still owns all of them
  without a ledger row ([[§14.2]], [[§14.12]], [[R1310002]]). On the shipped registry no account
  cosmetic is left for anyone to own, so the global roll finds nothing and every duplicate rolls
  as before, until an account cosmetic that is not a starter is added.
- **Tokens.** A cosmetic token still buys at its tier or below ([[R1305003]], unchanged), so a Rare
  token now buys no account cosmetic: below Epic are only the defaults, which every account owns.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's card backs are rewards of their own, never a pack's filler;
the spec's account cosmetics sit at Epic or better for the same reason.

Rejected: lifting the best of each kind to Legendary, which would make them chases ([[R1333000]])
with no rule to say which; and taking their starter mark off so they can drop, which would take
them from new accounts and need a grant row for every existing one.
