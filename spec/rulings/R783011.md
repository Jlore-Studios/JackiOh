## R783011 — Crater: a Trap Locks its zone as it is revealed

Built (#783). A Trap or Field Trap with Crater Locks the backrow zone it stands in as it is revealed,
which is as it first fires face-down, before its effects (`crater::lock_on_firing`); a Field Trap firing
again is face-up already and Locks nothing more. A card set face-down by its Cloaked or a permission is
no Trap by its face: a Cloaked Crater Unit is a Unit by the time it reveals and Locks the unit zone it
comes out into ([[R783010]]), not the backrow zone it leaves. Crater on a Spell or a Field Spell does
nothing.
