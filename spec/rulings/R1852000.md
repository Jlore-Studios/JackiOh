The designer's Stinkyscape set builds a ship out of the Starship Pieces that die: "When a Starship Piece enters the GY a copy of it gets fused into a Dormant Starship. If you do not have a Dormant Starship summon a Dormant Starship; if your front row board is full, stack it on top of your Lane 3 front row (regardless if it's Locked or not)." Asked which ways into the graveyard count, the designer answered that only a piece that **dies** fuses. The mechanic ships ahead of the set, proved on fixture cards; no shipped card is a piece.

**The keyword.** Starship Piece ([[§6.1]]) is a keyword kind with no rule of its own until the card dies, read as the card stood (printed, granted or lent, through the layers, before it moved, [[R89]]) and only on a face-up card. Dying is [[§4.5]]'s death: the state check's collection or a Sacrifice, a token piece included, never a card a replacement sent elsewhere ([[R461]]). A discard, a mill, a burn or any other way into a graveyard fuses nothing.

**When.** Once the death pass that collected it has fired its Death hooks, returned its Reborn bodies and reported its graveyard ([[§4.5]] steps 3 and 4, then the pass's last step, `starship_step`), each collected piece fuses in [[R68]]'s order, so two pieces dying at once fuse one after the other into one Starship, the first summoning it. The original stays in its graveyard as usual. A pause in a Death hook carries the pieces in the parked pass (`DeathPass.pieces`), so the fusion waits for the answer.

**The copy.** What fuses is a copy of the card as it lies in its graveyard: a fresh card of its definition carrying what [[R57]]'s library copy carries (its Radiant face, its tuning, a token's X/X), on the face it was on ([[R469]]), with no buffs, damage or granted keywords, which [[R78]] took off as it died.

**The Dormant Starship.** The engine summons `DORMANT_STARSHIP_DEF_ID`, Stinkyscape's Dormant Starship, a 0/0 Unit that prints Dormant ([[R1839000]]) and the hull (`StaticFlags.starshipHull`). It enters its owner's backrow, the middle first, standing there, else their unit row the same way, since issue #2249 ([[R2249000]]; before it, the leftmost open unit zone; a Locked one takes a summon, [[R688]]), and when both rows are full it is stacked on top of their lane-3 unit zone, Locked or not; a zone held for a Reborn return ([[R64]]) takes nothing, which the pass's order makes rare, since the Reborn bodies are back by then. The copy fuses into it at once, with the Starship kept ([[R77]]: its zone, its Dormant status and its memory), so its stats are only what is fused into it and no state check ever reads it at 0/0. It holds the union of the pieces' tags and keywords, never Starship Piece ([[R1852001]]), and their scripts ([[R102]]). It is not a token, so it reaches a graveyard. Each player has one at a time, the first in lane order, beneath a Stack or not ([[R1839003]]); after a launch ([[R1852003]]) the next piece to die starts a new one. A fusion past `FUSED_CARDS_CAP` is not made ([[R874000]]), and until the set ships no catalog holds the Dormant Starship, so a piece's death summons nothing.

**Seen by both players.** The Fuse reports `fused` as any does, then `starshipFused` names the dead piece, its definition, the Starship and how many pieces it holds, public on both seats, each card judged by where it is now ([[R97]]): a piece since returned to a hand, or a Starship since bounced into one, is the sentinel to the other seat, the piece with its definition; the Starship's memory is on the view (`UnitView.starship`, [[R1852005]]), which the board's comet badge and its piece count read. The AI's dense features leave the new keyword kinds out until its layout's next refit, so the network it ships keeps its inputs.

Amended by [[R2521000]] (issue #2521): the copy is the dead piece's base version, the card it was printed as, never the cards fused into it and never its tuning; a Radiant piece still fuses Radiant and a token keeps its X/X.
