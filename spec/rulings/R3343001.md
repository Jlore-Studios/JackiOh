[[R3343000]] says which level a card's Default draws; this note says how it is drawn.

**The Default draws the default look.** A face wearing a Default level, the normal face or the
Default's Animated, on a card with a default look draws that look (`lookOn`): its `data-version`
names the look's level and it carries no `data-level`, since the level it wears is the Default's. Its
Animated level moves the look's picture as it moves the normal face's ([[R2715001]]). A level of any
other variant the card wears is drawn as before, so a Legendary full-art card ([[R2655001]]) still
wears Full art by the Full-art cards setting, and a listed token reads its parent's look
([[R2655002]]). A lesson board ([[R1108000]]) and the Glitch keep the normal face.

- **A Mythic** draws Full art ([[R1017001]]): its picture across the whole face under its name, cost
  and stats, its rules text left to the hover preview as Full art's is; in the viewer's own hand bare, as [[R2723000]] rules; and on the board
  full art, a Unit's art across the minion's face ([[R3046000]]), drawn art first as every Mythic in play is ([[R3339000]]). The
  Full-art cards setting promises a card's text when off, so with it off a Mythic shows its normal
  face, every copy alike, an opponent's too.
- **A Legendary** draws Extended art ([[R2942001]]): its picture past its window across the face,
  behind the translucent frame and rules box, its words kept, whatever the setting, since it hides
  no text. On the board it is no full art and names no version: it is drawn art first, as every
  Legendary in play is, its art edge to edge with no oval ([[R3339000]]).

**Prismatic is full art.** Prismatic's first level adds the full-art frame instead of the normal
frame's art, so both its levels draw at the full-art shape: the picture's mirror chrome and
iridescent sheen across the whole face ([[R2944001]]), its rules text left to the hover preview as
Full art's is, and its animated level moving it. Nothing else of its look changes: its chrome and sheen keep out of
Radiant's gold window, and a Radiant face keeps its gold-foil frame, its marks and its picture's
Radiant wash under it ([[R2725004]]).

**Radiant.** A Radiant face keeps every mark under each default look, as under every level
([[R2725004]], [[R3015002]]).

Rejected: Prismatic keeping its words, which the issue overrules; a full-art level of Prismatic's own
above its art level, which would shift its owners' animated level up a rank; and drawing a Mythic's
full art with the setting off, which would break the setting's promise of the card's text.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Amends [[R2725004]] (the default looks under its rule), [[R2944001]] (Prismatic is a full art at both
levels) and [[R3015002]] (the Default's levels draw the card's default look).
