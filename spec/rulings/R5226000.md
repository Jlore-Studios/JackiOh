[[R5113000]] keeps every keyword another card gives off an Immutable card, but `gain_keyword_for_good` naming a card by id ([[R4154202]]) still wrote its keyword into the card's tuning and reported a `keywordGranted`, so C #37 Last Hurrah gave an Immutable card in the hand Flashback and it was played again from the graveyard. Since issue #5226 a keyword another card gives for good passes an Immutable card by, in every zone, and reports nothing (`restrictions::immutable_keeps_off`), Immutable read where the card is now, an Indestructible Unit card's included ([[R4353000]]). A card's own keyword for good still lands, as its Radiant does ([[R23]]), so S #154 Dice of Doom's Lucky ([[R1870007]]) is unchanged.

Last Hurrah's cards are drawn from those of the hand that lack Flashback and are not Immutable, since a pick on a card that refuses the keyword wastes the gift as one on a card that has it does ([[R4154202]]): the base face draws its `flashback` among them, none when there are no more of them than that ([[R129]]), and the Radiant face gives it to every card of the hand but those. The other player still learns only how many cards gained it ([[R177]]). The fuzz monitor's I23 still allows a keyword kept for good on an Immutable card ([[R4609000]], [[R4609003]]), which is now only the card's own.

Rejected: letting the picks fall on an Immutable card and fail there, which gives fewer cards Flashback for nothing; and taking Brittle into this ruling (issue #5157's other half), which would amend [[R5113000]]'s statuses. A game recorded before the change folds under the script it ran, which draws among the Immutable cards of the hand too (`script_kept_5226`, and `script_v0_6_9n` for the versions through v0.6.9n, [[R767000]]): one whose picks passed every Immutable card by folds as it was played, and one in which Last Hurrah gave an Immutable card Flashback no longer folds to its recorded hash and is refused as `rules_changed`.
