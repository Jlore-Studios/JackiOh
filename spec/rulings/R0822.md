A Cry is a permanent's Cry (MD-A12): a Unit's, a Field Spell's or an Animated card's labelled
"Cry:", or a Field Spell's unlabelled one-time text ([[R408]]). A Spell's resolution is none, even
though the engine runs it from the same `cry` hook, so Double Counting never repeats a Spell:
Stockpile draws 2 and heals 2 under it, not 4 and 4. What counts is the Cry run at [[§10.5]] step 5
for a card played or cast ([[R1]], [[R70]]), or one C #54 Rewind triggers ([[R467]]).
