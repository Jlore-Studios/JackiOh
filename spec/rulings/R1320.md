Patch v0.3.4 (part MN01 of #496, `docs/meditative-set.md` M4): the two verbs [[R386]] defines are read as **Nerf** (Degrade) and **Buff** (Upgrade) wherever a player reads them, and this spec's rows name them so ([[§6.3]]). That is every card text that said either word (C+ #8 Withering Storm, #69 Buff Billy, #70 Chaos Machine, #71 Book of Buff, #72 Book of Nerf, #73 Call to Chaos (Classic+ Edition), T-AI-10 Fine-Tuning, and #98 Heroic Power's Radiant Steady Shot), every face with its numbers kept; the glossary's two terms, matched case-sensitively as every term is; the game log ("was nerfed", "was buffed"); C+ #73's rolled clauses as `chaosRolled` names them and the reel's short words ([[R436]]); the tuned mark, "Buffed", "Nerfed" or "Tuned" ([[R513]]); and the rules words a voice or flavour line may not use. The glossary's Radiant entry, and [[§6.3]]'s Make Radiant row, no longer say "Upgrade a card", and the tutorial's Radiant lesson calls the form stronger rather than upgraded.

The engine's identifiers keep the old names, as [[R373]] kept `library` and `Sacrifice`: `degrade`, `upgrade`, `tune_once`, `TuneDirection`, the events `degraded` and `upgraded`, the wire names, and the client's own machine words (the sounds `degrade` and `upgrade`, a tuned card's `data-tuned="upgraded"`). So the protocol does not change, and a recorded game plays the same: the golden traces were re-recorded with every action, legal action and ending as they were, only the hashes of 17 of the 240 games moving, since a card's text does reach the state and the views in two places, a fused card's transient definition, which keeps its ingredients' texts ([[R102]]), and `chaosRolled`, which names C+ #73's clauses as the card prints them ([[R436]]). A ruling or section written before the rename that says Degrade or Upgrade means Nerf or Buff.

"Buff a card" is one application, one draw ([[R386]]), and "buff it twice" is two. A "+2/+2" is a plain stat buff, and so is Steady Shot's "Buff this permanently by +2 damage": a raise of its own number ([[R754]]), not an application, renamed with the rest because the card wrote it "Upgrade". The patch notes keep the history as it shipped, so earlier patches' titles and notes say Degrade and Upgrade.

A test over the client's strings, the catalog's texts and names (every set, shipped or not), the flavour lines and the voice lines refuses either old word in any form, a machine word aside (lower-case with no space).
