The pick is on the series screen, between games, not on the board: no match exists until both decks are known, because a match starts with both decks frozen into it ([[§9.4]]) and replays from `(seed, decks, log)` ([[§9.3]]), so the engine never learns there was a choice and a pick survives a restart in the series row. The screen offers it as the board offers the mulligan: the player selects a deck that has not won, locks it in, and waits ("Waiting for your opponent…") with the other player's readiness and the one clock on screen; a deck that has won is shown locked and cannot be selected, and a player on the screen when both picks are in is taken to the game. It is presentation: the server decides what may be picked (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
