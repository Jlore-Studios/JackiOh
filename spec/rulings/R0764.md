[[R97]] judges a card an event names by where it sits now, and reads a card the state no longer holds as public, since it ceased to exist off the field or out of a hand ([[R11]], [[R86]]). A Glitch's reset ([[R676]]) and boards ([[R678]]) make cards cease to exist without a word: the old game's hands, libraries and face-down traps, and the face-down traps on both fields. So every event up to the end of the action that played the newest Glitch of those names such a card as the sentinel `"hidden"` in both seats' views (id and definition), where it would have read openly the moment the card vanished, and the other player would learn what an opponent drew or had set face-down. Cards the state still holds are judged where they sit, as ever, and a card a Transform or a Fuse replaced is judged by its replacement ([[R177]]). The animation of events after the Glitch is unchanged. Found by reading the fuzz gate's hidden-information invariant I6 against a Glitch the fuzz hardly ever plays
