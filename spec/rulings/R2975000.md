Presentation only, as [[R979000]] is (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no state, event, replay or AI decision changes, only what `viewFor` carries and what the fuzz monitor checks.

Two cards `legalActions` listed no play of said nothing. One offered choices, yet [[§10.5]] step 1 refused every one of them: for its targets' discards ([[R682]]), as two enemy C #89 Paul Allen's Ghosts the hand can pay for one at a time but not together, or for its Tribute pick ([[R123]]). It now says step 1's refusal of the first combination the face offers, on the listing's default play carrying that combination's picks ("targeting that costs 4 discards, and you hold 3 cards"); it says nothing when step 1 accepts any of them. The other was C #57 Echo copying an X-cost Spell with mana for an X but none left for it once its own price is paid ([[R545]]): an X the mana allows is now asked against the most X the play may choose, as step 1 asks it, so it says "X is above your mana after paying". Both hold for a graveyard card a permission admits too ([[R1571000]]), which reads the same order. Neither changes a reason a card already gave: a card that said something says it word for word, and only cards that said nothing now say why.

A single enemy Ghost that too few other cards pay for keeps "cannot be played without 1 legal target": by [[R450]] it is no legal target at all, so step 1 refuses that play for the missing target, not for the discard.

The fuzz monitor's I13 holds every sampled state to this: while a seat may act (R979000's window), each card in its hand, and each card in its graveyard a permission admits, carries `playReason` exactly when `legalActions` lists no play of it, and no other graveyard card carries one. It reads the view and listing I6 already builds, so it is sampled with I6. A seat that plays only the tutorial's cards ([[R2533000]]) is not asked of the others, which it lists no play of and says nothing about.

Rejected: naming the discard for the single Ghost, since step 1 refuses that play for its missing target and the reason is step 1's; a second view and listing for I13, which would double I6's cost; restating the discard and Tribute filters in a helper of the reason's own, which could drift from step 1's words, where putting each combination to step 1 cannot; and asking every copier's X against what its price leaves even with no mana at all, which would reword the "X is above your current mana" an Echo at 0 mana already says (the reducer's own words there are "X is above your mana after paying"; both are true) and so move the view of games whose play is unchanged.
