Classic+ #73.1 Classic Golem now plays as the designer's note has it: "When this attacks a unit, it transforms into a random Classic or Classic+ card. After this kills a unit this may attack again." When the Golem attacks a Unit, declared or forced, the attacked Unit is transformed before any damage ([[R2285000]]). It is transformed in place ([[§6.3]]) into a random non-token Classic or Classic+ Unit ([[R380]]), with no Cry ([[R1]]) and keeping its type ([[R35]]); an Immutable Unit is not transformed, and no random number is drawn when no Unit can take its place ([[R23]], [[R129]]). The base Golem gives the new Unit its Radiant face, and the Radiant Golem its base face, since Radiant faces carry the higher stats (the owner's call). The attack goes on against the new Unit. This holds for any "when this attacks" effect: when it transforms the attack's target, the attack is aimed at the instance that took the target's place, so the attacker fights it. If the Golem destroys the new Unit ([[R42]]) and is still alive after the combat ([[R2285001]]), it may attack again this turn, with a fresh exertion and the rest of [[§4.2]]'s restrictions applying, as Stinkyscape #94 Tmack Trav does ([[R1869002]]). An attack on the hero transforms nothing. This replaces [[R424]], under which the Golem transformed itself after a declared attack and passed "may attack again" to the Unit it became. Rejected: transforming the target after the combat, which leaves "If this destroys it" nothing to destroy; and canceling the attack because its target's instance ceased to exist, which would make the Golem's attack fight nothing.
