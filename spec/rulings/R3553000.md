S+ #21 Jlockeed Shredder-12 reads "End of turn: Deal {damage} damage to all enemies, twice", 2
on the base face and 5 on the Radiant face. "Twice" is two passes, each [[R51]]'s shape: one
`damage_all` of the declared number over the enemy units and the enemy hero, the hero last. Every
enemy takes two damage instances, two events per target. The two passes are two effects of one
hook, so no state check runs between them ([[R59]]): a unit the first pass reduces to 0 still
takes the second ([[R42]]; a later hit changes no kill), and all deaths are collected after both
([[§4.5]]). "Enemies" is the controller's enemies alone: nothing of its own side is hit, and the
hook runs at its controller's end of turn only ([[§2.5]]). Tribute 1 is the play validator's
([[R101]]), printed in the keyword line ([[R366]]); Pierce is catalog data ([[§6.1]]).

Rejected: one doubled instance per enemy; the second pass hitting survivors only; and the enemy
hero taking the damage once.
