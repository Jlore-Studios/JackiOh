A Unit whose text says "Any damage this takes is dealt to the enemy hero" (`staticFlags.reflectsDamage`) deals what a hit dealt it again at [[§4.4]] step 8a, after Lifesteal: a new damage instance from the Unit, of the amount dealt, on its controller's opponent's hero, through that hero's own pipeline. A hit that dealt nothing (a Divine Shield, the Armor, Indestructible, the zero rule, [[R63]]) reflects nothing, and a Unit the hit left at 0 or less still reflects it, the state check coming after. The Radiant face's "and the attacker" (`reflectsToAttacker`) also deals it to the attacker when the hit was that attacker's own strike, its hit on the defender or its Cleave's, while that attacker is still on the field. A strike back is the defender's and no attacker's, so a reflecting Unit that attacks sends what its target strikes back to the enemy hero alone; a Spell's or a card text's hit is no attacker's either; and a reflected hit is no strike, so two Radiant Platings fighting end after one reflection each. Rejected: moving the hit to the enemy hero instead of dealing it to the Unit as well, since the text says the Unit takes it; and reflecting every hit to its source, which would loop between two such Units.
