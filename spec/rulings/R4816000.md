S+ #103 Transcend, #104 Tireless Cultivation and #105 Spacersi ([[R3557004]]) made S #19 Imprisoned Cosmality and S+ #62 Overdrive Projector awake, S+ #157 Phantom Shipyard awake at 0/0, which the state check then killed, and #98 Heroic Power with no power and no way to get one: a Transform put its new card on the field through `zones::replace_in_zone`, which started its printed Brittle ([[R385]]) and nothing else. [[R1839000]] starts Dormant on a card arriving from off the field "any way it arrives", [[R3556000]] says so of a face-up Field Spell, [[R3039000]] calls a Transform's result a new card without the awakened mark, and [[R3134002]] rolls a power for a copy that reaches the field with no Cry. None excepts a Transform, and Hearthstone's transformed minion is a new minion, so the new card arrives.

**What it starts.** Every Transform onto the field (`transform`, `transformRandom`, `transformCostStep`, `transformBeneath`, `transformIntoCopy`) places its new card through `replace_in_zone`, which starts what every arrival from off the field starts (`zones::place_with`): its printed Brittle ([[R385]]); its printed Dormant on a Unit or a face-up Field Spell, its printed countdown tuned by its declared `dormant` ([[R1839000]], [[R3556000]]); and, on a hull arriving as a Unit, an empty Starship memory ([[R1852000]], [[R1852005]]). So a Dormant card a Transform makes sleeps out its countdown, an Imprisoned Unit answering its "When this awakens" as it wakes, and a Phantom Shipyard it makes is Dormant and judged by no health ([[R3539003]]). After its `transformed`, the new card's start-of-game clause runs for its controller, as a summoned card's does ([[R151]]): a Heroic Power rolls its power from the match rng, asking nothing ([[R3134002]]). A copy a Transform lays beneath a Stack pile (Classic+ #4) starts the same, its countdown waiting there ([[R1839003]]).

**What it does not.** It is still no summon ([[R1]]): no Cry, no `summoned`, and none of a summon's own steps. No Woozy stamp, which marks what its player newly plays, summons or draws ([[R3548001]]); no Crater Lock, a Transform being no arrival for Crater ([[R783010]], unchanged); and no entry animation of an Animated Field Spell, since a Replace keeps the new card in the old one's zone ([[§6.3]]): an "Animated on your turn" one steps out at its controller's next turn start as any card there does ([[R383]]). It is summoning sick ([[R659]]) and keeps the old card's size ([[R3546002]]), as before.

Rejected: leaving a Transform outside the arrival (a live Imprisoned Unit whose awakening never fires, a dead 0/0 shipyard, a Heroic Power with no power for good), and running every summon step on it, which would make it the summon [[R1]] and [[R783010]] say it is not.
