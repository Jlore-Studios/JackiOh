The issue asked that Animated and the card's foil, finish and border layer on each new version, and
that Signature go on none ([[R2949000]]).

**Animated** moves the version's own picture ([[R2715001]]): Alt art's and Alt full art's re-seeded
one, and the Sketch's pencilled picture, whose layers move under its filter. On Alt full art it also
lays the Full art band over the art, as on Full art ([[R2725003]]). It holds still under reduced
motion and with Animated foil off, and takes a motion slot, as on any face. It is offered by its own
offer, so it layers on a new version wherever both are offered.

**A foil, a finish and a border** wear on each as on the normal face ([[R1295000]]); on Alt full art
a finish paints its band round the art as on any full-art face ([[R2720000]]). On the Sketch a worn
border keeps its own edge and bezel: the pencilled outline is drawn only on a base face wearing no
border, and the window's pencil ring takes the border's bezel colour.

**Signature** goes on none of the three. It is worn only beside the Full art version and Animated
([[R2949000]]), so its own rule refuses it here and no mechanism of this part's is needed: a `PUT
/api/card-picks` naming it beside a new version is the 400 R2949000 gives, writing nothing; the
Collection marks it waiting until the card wears Full art and Animated; and a face wearing a new
version never signs. No other pairing is ruled out: every new version can show every other layer.

Amends [[R2725003]] (the layering on part 2's versions).
