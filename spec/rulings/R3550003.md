A cooldown of N sits out N−1 of its user's turns, and the view shows the turns left; the extra-turn cooldown is 2 in that count, sitting out exactly one; Refresh leaves a cooldown alone. Rejected: sitting out N turns, which would make "usable again on its user's next turn" false for a normal Activate.
