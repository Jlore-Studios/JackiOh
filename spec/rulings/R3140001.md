Meditative+ #22 Mountaintop Garden reads "Cry: Add a random Embiggen card to your hand." above its aura, and its Radiant face "Cry: Add a random Embiggen card to your hand. It costs (0)." (the designer wrote "a cards"). Through v0.5.11o it had no Cry.

- **The pool** is [[R1178001]]'s: the non-token embiggen cards of the sets that ship, one uniform pick, never the Garden itself ([[R387]]), repeats of an earlier Cry allowed. The card is added on its base face, not Radiant, to the hand, and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). The Cry runs as the Garden is played or cast ([[R1]]); a Garden put into a backrow zone by a summon or a fusion adds nothing.
- **The Radiant price** is a `costOverride` of (0) on the card added: it costs (0) before every modifier ([[R65]]), and [[R766]] takes it off if the card reaches a graveyard or an exile pile. It prices every play of the card, the normal one and the embiggen choice alike, so under a Garden the card plays Embiggened for (0) ([[R1181002]]), and the Garden's dearer choice, which plays it Radiant and Embiggened, costs (0) too, as [[R1181003]] has an override price it. The base face adds the card at its printed prices, which the aura then plays Embiggened at the normal one.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, with no Cry (`script_v0_5_11o`, [[R767000]]).

Rejected: an `embiggenCostOverride`, which would price only the Embiggened version while the normal one, the version a Garden plays, kept its price, where "it costs (0)" names the card; and charging the Garden's premium on top of the override, which would need an engine change to the price order for one card.
