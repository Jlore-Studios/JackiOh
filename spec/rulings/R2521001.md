Stinkyscape #53, #57 and #61 Flak's "Deal a random {least} to {most} damage to each enemy (60%, 30%, 10%, lowest first)" rolls once for each enemy, as Hearthstone's Lightning Storm rolls each minion apart, never once for the whole sweep: `damage_all` with `odds`, each target's amount its own weighted roll (`rng.int` of the summed weights, walked lowest first) made just before its hit, in the order the sweep hits ([[R1862001]]: every enemy Unit in lane order, then the enemy hero), all before the one state check ([[R59]]). The printed 60, 30 and 10 are laid over `least` to `most` from the low end; a value past the third, as an Upgrade of `most` makes one ([[R386]]), is weighted as the third, and a Nerf that crosses the two ends rolls between them, as S #145 Fortify Flesh does. "Twice" and "three times" are that many rounds, each its own sweep after the last one's check ([[R1862001]]), so every round rolls every target anew. Lucky X ([[§6.1]]) is on each roll: each target's roll is made X more times and the highest kept (`rng.lucky`), X being the card's Lucky now and its controller's Luck, as Fortify Flesh reads them ([[R1438]], [[R987]]); the Radiant faces print Lucky 1, so each enemy is rolled for twice. A sweep that names no odds deals its stated amount and draws nothing, so every other `damage_all` ([[§6.3]]) plays and replays as before. Rejected: one roll for the whole sweep, which is not "each enemy" taking "a random" amount, and Lucky once per activation, which would keep one target's better roll while its neighbors took theirs unimproved.
