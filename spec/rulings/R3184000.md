Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). No rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes, and the server and the wire types are untouched.

**The four columns go.** The Adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]], [[R3022000]]) reads a card's strength far better than the figures it was built from, so the Cards tab of `/stats` no longer shows these four:
- When drawn;
- Drawn vs not drawn ([[R2427000]]);
- Radiant and Normal ([[R1147000]]).

None of them has a column, a figure in the grid, a show/hide toggle or a sort any longer. An address whose `?sort=` names one of them opens on the default sort, the adjusted win rate descending, as [[R2446000]] has an old address's cost, rarity or keyword sort do. `GET /api/stats/cards` still answers every one of these figures. The card's stats block in the card detail view still shows When drawn and Drawn vs not drawn, with "not enough data" below the floor ([[R654]]). The drill-down's Show Radiant face toggle stays too, since it shows the card, not a rate.

**A tier's fit.** While Show 95% ranges is on ([[R2895000]]), each card with a tier and an Adjusted range shows, under its tier chip, how much of that range falls inside its tier's stretch of rates. It is a whole percent, styled as the ranges are:

```
fit = overlap([adjustedLow, adjustedHigh], [tierLow, tierHigh]) / (adjustedHigh − adjustedLow)
```

The stretches come from the response's band (`tiers.bandLow`, `tiers.bandHigh`):
- S is `bandHigh` and up;
- A, B, C and D split `bandLow` to `bandHigh` into quarters from the top;
- F is `bandLow` and down.

A range of no width is the rate itself, so its fit is 100% inside the tier and 0% outside it. A card below the floor, with no tier, no range or from a server with no band shows none. The line under the ranges says what the percent is.

The tier sort still puts S first ([[R3022000]]). Within a tier it now orders by the fit (higher first when descending), then by the adjusted rate. A card with no fit comes after the cards with one either way. The fit is read off figures the page already has, so it orders the sort whether or not the ranges show.

**The Created cards sort.** Each header of the Created cards table ([[R2089000]]) sorts it: Card, Games, Used, Not used, Used Radiant, Used normal and Used vs not used.
- A first click sorts a figure high to low and a name A to Z (as the page shows it), and a second click turns it round.
- The active header shows an arrow and its `aria-sort`.
- A row with no figure in the column (below the floor, or none) sorts after the rows with one either way, as in the deck table, and ties keep the server's order.
- Until a header is clicked the rows stay in the server's order.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
