Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes.

**Why.** The adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]) is the page's headline figure and its far better read of a card's strength. The drawn win rate, the drawn delta and the face rates beside it had become noise, and the owner asked in #3184 to clear them.

**What goes.** The Cards tab's table on `/stats` has no When drawn, no Drawn vs not drawn ([[R2427000]]) and no Radiant or Normal ([[R1147000]]) column, no show/hide toggle for them and no sort by them, and the grid shows no Radiant or Normal figure. With them goes the row's "not enough data" for a drawn delta below the floor. An address whose `?sort=` names one of them (`drawnWinRate`, `drawnDelta`, `radiantWinRate`, `normalWinRate`) opens on the default sort, as [[R2446000]]'s old sorts do. The table keeps Tier, Adjusted, Win rate, Games and Play rate.

**What stays.** `GET /api/stats/cards` still answers every figure, and the client's wire types keep them, since removing a served field breaks older clients. The card's stats block (`CardStatsBlock`) still shows When drawn and the drawn delta, with "not enough data" below its floor ([[R2427000]]). `stats report`, `--json`, the analysis loader and the server keep both too. The drill-down's Show Radiant shows the card's Radiant face, its art and not a win rate, and stays.

Rejected: hiding the four columns by default behind their toggles, which keeps the noise one click away and the code to carry it; and dropping the fields from the response, which an older client still reads. No Hearthstone rule is involved.
