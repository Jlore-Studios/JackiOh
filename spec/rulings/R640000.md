A ladder bot ([[R1452]]) holds a season row as a person does, which completes the replacement of [[R610]]'s "no season row" that issue #636 set out. A ranked game moves the bot's row by [[R605]]–[[R607]]'s rules, from its placements as a Raisin through pips, floors and peaks, toward the rank its own rating ([[R610]]) calls for, and the rated game's record carries the bot side's ranks before and after, as it carries a person's ([[R611]]). The leaderboard (`GET /api/leaderboard`) lists a placed bot in its Grape tier, and the match screen (`GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks`) shows its seat that same row, read exactly as a person's is ([[R612]]), so the two always agree and neither tells a bot from a person (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**People only.** Jlorious and the percentiles read people's rows alone. A bot is never one of the `JLORIOUS_SIZE` (100) Jlorious places ([[R608]]), so a bot whose rating would qualify stays Mythic Grape and is given no Jlorious peak, and so no Jlorious badge ([[R607]]); no person is kept out of Jlorious by a bot. A rank's target is its rating's percentile among the season's placed people ([[R605]], [[R606]]), a bot's target as much as a person's, so the bots on the ladder move no person's target, tier or badge. Counting the bots in the percentiles was rejected: the pool grows and shrinks with the people who play ([[R1454]]), so people's targets and badges would move with it.

**A bare rating.** A bot side whose id names no bot profile, a rating and nothing more ([[R610]]), holds no season row and carries no rank.

**No backfill.** A bot's row opens, as a Raisin, with its first ranked game after this ruling; the records of its earlier games keep their rankless bot sides.

**The tell this ruling knowingly leaves.** While Jlorious has room, every placed person in Mythic Grape is Jlorious, so a Mythic Grape opponent then is a bot. Putting bots in Jlorious was rejected, since issue #640 keeps Jlorious for people, and so was showing a Mythic bot one tier lower, which would make its match screen disagree with the leaderboard. This ruling closes two of [[R1452]]'s tells: a bot's username is now on the leaderboard as a placed person's is, and its rank no longer falls inside a season, since its tier floor holds it.
