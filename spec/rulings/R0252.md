A profile saves up to `MAX_SAVED_TRIOS` (5) named trios, each three ordered slots naming the profile's own saved decks, any of them empty: T1 a name as D1, T2 three slots, T3 no deck twice. A trio is a draft like a deck: decks that share cards may be saved together, and the builder marks every shared card with the deck that also holds it. Deleting a deck empties every slot that named it and keeps the trio
