Stage 2 of S #7 The Greatest Heist rewards "Add 3 random [Radiant] Anomalies or Resources to your hand", printed "Do this {finds} times: at random, add an Anomaly Found! to your hand or gain a random Stinkyscape Resource" (ST-S09-4).

- **The rolls.** `finds` draws (3, a declared number), one draw of the match rng each ([[R60]]), each an Anomaly Found! at even odds or else a Resource.
- **Where they go.** An Anomaly Found! goes to the hand, and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). A Resource is gained into the tray by [[R1840002]]'s roll, never the hand ([[R1840000]]), and a ninth Resource is sold at once.
- **Radiant.** On the Radiant face both the Anomaly Found! and the Resource are Radiant.
