Stinkyscape #13 Asphyxiants Cloud reads "This and your next Spell this turn have Poisonous. Deal 1 damage to all enemies. Draw 1." [[§4.4]] step 7 named only a Unit's Poisonous.

- **A Spell's Poisonous.** A Spell that has Poisonous — printed on its face, read off it while it resolves as a Spell's Pierce is ([[R346]]), or given to it — destroys each Unit one of its hits deals 1 or more to ([[§4.4]] step 7), as a Unit's does; a hit Divine Shield, Armor or Indestructible stopped deals nothing and destroys nothing. The Cloud's "this has Poisonous" is the keyword on its face.
- **"Your next Spell this turn has Poisonous."** `give_next_spell_keyword { keyword }` puts a `keywordNextSpell` player modifier on its player, expiry `thisTurn`. The next Spell that player plays face-up, a cast included ([[R70]]), takes the keyword at [[§10.5]] step 4, as Forever&'s "the next Spell you play gains" does, and spends the modifier; several are spent by that one Spell, and the keyword is had once. A Unit, a Field Spell or a Trap played meanwhile leaves it waiting, and so do a Spell set face-down (a Trap until it resolves, [[R783005]]) and a countered play, which never reach step 4. The cleanup that ends the turn ends it.
- **For one resolution.** The keyword is a granted one on the Spell, read through the layers wherever its hits read their source's keywords; [[R78]]'s reset takes it off as the Spell lands from the resolving zone, after its Echo repeats, so the card in the graveyard, and any later play of it, has no Poisonous from it. It is announced with `keywordGranted` on the Spell, public as the play is, and the modifier's badge says "Your next Spell this turn has Poisonous" on both seats.
- The modifier is absent from every game no Cloud is played in, so no shipped game changes.
