Three more cosmetic kinds join the registry of [[R1295000]], the slots of a **deck** rather than of a card, three items each beside a default that draws the board as it was before: a **table**, the playmat under the lanes (Wood, Felt, Neon grid; default Tavern table), a **field**, the ambient backdrop behind the board (Embers, Rain, Fireflies; default No backdrop) and a **card back** (Lacquer, Starfield, Blueprint; default Standard back). All nine are unlocked for every account. The playmat is called `table` in code, because "board" already names a gameplay concept (`last_boards`, `glitch_boards`). The registry's check holds one default per kind for all six kinds, and the generated `cosmetics.ts` carries them.

**Which pick wins.** Each saved deck carries its own three picks, each an id of its kind or none, beside its hero portrait ([[R641]], D5). On the viewer's side the deck's pick wins, then the account's pick in the `cosmetics` settings group ([[R633]], the same group as the card cosmetics, with a select for each of the three in the settings' visuals), then the kind's default. A pick the client does not know draws as its kind's default, and a deck's pick that is unknown does not hand the slot to the account's: the deck's word is final. The deck editor offers each slot as "Your settings' choice", which leaves the deck with none, and the registry's items.

**Whose side wears them.** Only the viewer's own half wears them, on a board the viewer plays (the same boards as [[R1295000]]): the backdrop behind the lower half of the board, the mat under the viewer's half of the field with its unit pads, backrow slots and the line between the sides, the backs of the viewer's face-down cards, their deck pile and the cards the deck sheet lists that the viewer was never shown. The opponent's half, their face-down cards, their hand and their deck pile draw as they always did, whatever the loadout. A card back depends on the seat alone, never on a card's id, cost or marks: every face-down card of one seat has the same back, a back carries no text, and `Card.test.tsx`'s B16 (a back says nothing of its card) holds for every item. A zone that is locked, reserved or glowing keeps the mark its state gave it over the playmat.

**The backdrop.** The backdrop is a layer of `BACKDROP_PARTICLE_MAX` (18) particles whose places and delays are a fixed function of their index, with nothing random, behind everything the board draws and over nothing: it takes no input (`pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden`) and a pointer meets what is on the board. Every backdrop has a still version, its tint and its particles at their places, which is what it draws under the system's reduced motion and the settings' Reduce motion; it moves only inside the motion gate, by transform and opacity alone.

**Saving them.** A deck's picks are saved through the deck upsert as `tableCosmetic`, `fieldCosmetic` and `cardBackCosmetic`, left out or `null` for none, and a save without them clears them. The server refuses an id that the shipped registry does not list under that kind (400 `bad_request`) or that is not a text; the database holds a slug's shape only. A trio import carries no picks, as it carries no portrait. The deckbuilder's mirror, snapshot and autosave carry them like the portrait, and a practice game carries the saved deck's picks to its board.

**What it does not do.** Online matches, hotseat games and Duos draw the account's choice, because the client is not told which deck a seat plays; carrying a deck's picks there waits for a frame that freezes them with the ticket and a ruling of its own. Nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log, a replay hash or a game record, and the opponent's cosmetics are not shown (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
