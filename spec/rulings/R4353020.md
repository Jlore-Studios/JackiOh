S+ #79 A Glimpse into the Endless Sea since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V: "You are immune to fatigue damage", on both faces, after the deck and every permanent are exiled and the Radiant face's Armor is gained. It is a player modifier on the caster (`fatigueImmune`, expiry `never`), so it lasts the rest of the game, outlives the Spell and every board, and is public on both seats as a badge ("You are immune to fatigue damage"); several are one. It follows Hearthstone's Immune hero meeting fatigue: a draw from the empty library is still a fatigue draw ([[§2.4]]), so the fatigue count moves, the draw counts toward a draw limit ([[R457]]) and `fatigue` announces it with its nominal hit, `FATIGUE_DAMAGE` of its count ([[R315]]), which the game summary's figures read as they read any fatigue's ([[R1256000]]); but the hit is not dealt: no `damage`, no `damageAbsorbed` and no report of one ([[R240]], [[R1362]]), and the hero's health and Armor are untouched. It reaches fatigue alone: every other hit lands as it would (unlike M #48 Tranquility's immunity to all damage, [[R1021]]), and the other player's fatigue still hits. Core #75 Infinite Reserves, which Bob's Plan pairs with it ([[R3569006]]), still replaces each such draw with a Rush Token card first, so no fatigue happens at all while it stands. Rejected: no fatigue draw at all (no count, no announcement), which "immune to fatigue damage" does not say; and a `fatigue` naming a hit of 0, which would make the event lie about the draw's count-given hit. Through v0.6.8b the card gave no immunity, which `script_v0_6_8b` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
