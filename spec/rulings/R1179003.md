Meditative+ #14 Potion Pouch and #15 Book of Potions ("Add N random Potion cards to your hand") pick from the Potion pool: the non-token cards tagged Potion of the sets that ship ([[R380]]). The pool names no set, so it is empty while Meditative+ is held, and M+ #11 to #13 under a preview ([[R1178000]]). A crafted potion is a transient definition and in no pool ([[R77]], [[R882]]).

Repeats are allowed: each pick is its own draw over the pool ([[R60]]).
