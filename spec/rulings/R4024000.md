Amends [[R2424000]] and [[R2687000]], whose Death price was keyed by a card's definition, face, granted Deaths and stat override, and [[R2477000]] and [[R2565000]], whose turn-hook price was keyed by its definition and face. Under them one side's copy of a card read the price the probes had set for the other side's, though the same text is worth very different amounts on different boards. Core #13 Jlockeed Shredder-10 ("End of turn: deal 2 damage to each enemy unit and the enemy hero") on the enemy's side, facing only the AI's Shredder, read the 11.0 the AI's copy earned against a Shredder and three Vanillas, where its own firing gives the enemy 5.0. Training Grounds #30 Floodgates ("Death: Destroy all your Units") in the AI's hand beside two Vanillas read the 0 of the enemy's copy alone on its board, since the probes list the enemy's field before the seat's hand, where its Death costs the AI 21.6. [[R2564000]]'s activation prices already keyed by the controller, for the same reason. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it keys.** Both prices now hold the card's controller, and a card reads only the entry of its own controller (`DeathPrice::is_for`, `TurnHookPrice::is_for`). The lists, their order, the probe world ([[R185]]) and the pricing are unchanged: each card is probed once for each controller, and the first copy listed for that controller sets the price. A card in a hand is its owner's (a card leaving the field returns to its owner's control), and the probes read only the seat's hand, so a hand card shares the price of the seat's own copy on the board and is probed only when the seat has none there. A copy that changes sides during the search reads no price until the next root probes it on its new side, as an activation price already does. This replaces [[R2565000]]'s "the enemy's copy of a card the seat holds reads the same price", and takes the probe of each side's copy that it rejected as spending a node for the same price: the price was not the same.

**Budget.** Both caps stay 24 nodes (`AI_DEATH_PROBE_NODES`, `AI_TURN_HOOK_PROBE_NODES`, [[R186]]). A card both sides hold spends its probe twice, and past a cap the cards listed later go unpriced, as before; the seat's own copies are still listed first, so a tight budget is spent where the AI acts. The AI's smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`, 20 games a matchup) passed with the change: 19 wins against the random policy (17 needed), 16 against the greedy baseline (10 needed) and 16 for Hard against Easy (16 needed), where the build before it won 20, 16 and 17 of the same games, and its timed decisions stayed where they were. The full gate (`--full`) passed too: 98 of 100 (91 needed), 43 of 50 (28 needed) and 45 of 50 (40 needed).

**Rejected.**

- Keying by the owner, which gives a stolen copy the price of the board it left.
- Probing the seat's hand before the enemy's board, which would change what a tight budget prices on every board, not only in a mirror.
- Raising either cap for the second probe.
