Issue #4293 prints Reignclipse's two fused Armors as one Armor of their stacked sum ([[R77]]): Armor 7 on the base face, Armor 10 on the Radiant face, in place of Armor 4 and Armor 3 (Radiant 4 and 6). Its two Burn lines print as the one `burnsOnDamage` rider ([[R3540002]]) of their sum ([[R102]]), 9 (Radiant 18), "Damage also Burns {burns}.". Its one declared number is that sum, `burns` 9 (18), which a tune moves ([[R386]]); the display-only `damage` number is gone. How it plays is unchanged: the script already dealt the sum, so a shipped catalog that printed the two lines folds as it was played ([[R767000]]). It amends [[R3565003]]'s Burns and Armor lines. Rejected: keeping the two Armor keywords and the two Burn lines on the face, which would print what the card no longer declares.
