A Spell may make Units attack: a forced attack ([[R53]]), S #120 Wild System's melee "until only one survives" ([[R1848005]]), M+ #18 Cognitive Offloading's AI turn ([[R44]]). An immune Unit then attacks, is attacked, takes combat damage and dies in them, and the fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) needs to know whether that is a Spell affecting it.
**It is an attack, not the Spell's effect.** [[§6.1]] says Immune to Spells is "no restriction on attacks" ([[§4.2]]): the attacking Unit reaches its target, and a Unit forced to attack attacks as it would by its controller's choice. So an immune Unit may be made to attack, may be a forced attack's target, and the melee and the AI's turn pair and play it as any other Unit.
**What I21 allows.** Anything after an `attackDeclared` in a Spell's run, the combat, its hooks and its deaths, until the next card's hook, trap or turn edge; and nothing after the AI's turn begins (`autopilotEngaged`), whose plays are its own.
Rejected: sparing immune Units in the forced-attack verbs (`effects/combat.rs`) and the melee's pairing. [[§6.1]] bars no attack, and the melee would no longer run until only one Unit survives.
