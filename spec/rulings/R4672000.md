**Why.** The owner balances a card by the choices it offers: each Choose One option, each X it is paid at, Embiggened or not. `cardPlayed` already named the X and the Embiggen and a play carried its declared modes ([[R81]]), but the game record ([[R376]]) dropped all three, and the raw record is purged a week after it is folded, so what a later figure needs must be in the record as it is written and in the totals ([[R4672003]]).

**The record.** Every play the record keeps carries what was chosen for it when its card offered a choice, `None` (absent) when it offered none: a dealt card's play from the hand in `playedVariants`, beside `played` as `playedRadiant` is ([[R1147000]]), absent from the record when no play had one; and a `castOrSummoned` entry ([[R4279000]]), a `createdUsed` entry and a `createdPlayed` entry ([[R2089000]]) in its own `variant`. A `createdUsed` entry keeps it only when the seat it is listed under, the one that made the card, also played it, so no seat is credited with the other's choice. A variant holds:

- `modes`: the options declared with the play ([[R81]]: `mode`, `direction` and `number` declarations, one per declaration in the card's order, which the play's check already holds them to), then the answer to every `mode` and `number` prompt the card's own script asks (`resume.hook` is `prompts::RESUME_HOOK`, §10.6) between its `cardPlayed` and its `cardResolved` that the seat which played it answers, each answer's options in the prompt's order. That is every such prompt, wherever its options come from, since the reader cannot tell a card-fixed list from any other; three cases are decided here. Classic #11 Mind Melt's Radiant cost prompt, a `number` prompt whose options are the costs in the opponent's hand, counts: the cost its player picks is the play's choice. Core #82 KY's Trial's "Discover a set", a `mode` prompt over three sets drawn at random, counts: the set taken is the variant (`Classic+`, say), and the sets offered are not recorded, for a `mode` offer names no card and so is no pick ([[R4672002]]); its second prompt, a Discover of numbers, names no card either and is neither. Classic #8 Pickle's three questions never count: its opponent answers them, which makes them the opponent's choice, not the play's, and they usually open at the start of the opponent's next turn ([[R1279000]]), after the play's `cardResolved`. A question asked at once, with the play still open (v0.4.0's kept script, [[R767000]]), is left out as well, because another seat answers it.
- `x`: the X paid ([[R65]], [[R348]]), as `cardPlayed` names it.
- `embiggened`: whether an Embiggen card was Embiggened ([[§6.3]]), as `cardPlayed` names it.

A cast an effect makes resolves with the modes the cast chose, which are its variant. Left out, as board choices rather than the card's: `target`, `zone`, `tribute`, `cell` and `hand` prompts; the play pipeline's own `mode` prompt for a half ([[R3554004]]), which picks a place; and an Echo repeat ([[R30]], §6.2), whose prompts ask the steps the first resolution answered again: each step of the play's script is read once. Left out as no choice of the card's: `mulligan`; `answer` (a question's answers are drawn at random); `craft` (an open-ended recipe); `market` (a random deal); `reward` (asked when a quest completes, not as a card is played); and an answer a timeout gave. `x` and `embiggen` are declared with the play and never prompted. A `discover` or `pick` prompt is an output of the card, a pick ([[R4672002]]).

Amended by [[R4707000]] (issue #4707): a variant also keeps what the play rolled (`rolled`), its table's entries, coins or dice, which is no part of its label.

**How.** `cardPlayed` gains `modes`, the play's declared options, as engine bookkeeping beside [[R2089000]]'s `creator`: no rule reads it (`as_rules_read` drops it, so no state hash moves), no view forwards it, and the golden traces' event text leaves it out. The record's reader takes each step's action with it (`SummaryReader::step`), and an `answer` to a card's own prompt (`resume.hook` is `prompts::RESUME_HOOK`) whose card still has a play open adds its options to that play's entries.

Rejected: reading `Play.modes` off the log alone, which misses a cast's choices; a field on `promptAnswered`, which every view and the golden traces carry; recording every prompt answered while a play resolves, which credits a trap's or another card's prompt to the play; crediting Pickle's questions to its play, which records the opponent's choice as the player's, and only when the play is still open; and a marker on the prompt that a random or out-of-card offer would carry to leave it out, which every such card's script would have to set and which takes KY's Trial's set choice out of its record.
