The owner asked in #3015 (2026-11-04) that nobody lose anything: every combination an account owned
before, a full art, the Full art version with the augments, or a version with Animated, is owned as the
levels it covers, and the 21 full-art cards and old ids keep working.

**Read.** A retired entry ([[R3015000]]) counts as owning, on its own card, the levels it maps to
(`cosmetics::implied_by`, read by `row_owns` under every ownership check, and by the client's
`impliedBy`), as [[R2725002]] read it for the versions and augments:

| Retired entry | Owns on its card |
|---|---|
| `full-art-<slug>` (Epic) | `version-full-art` |
| `full-art-<slug>-animated` (Legendary) | `version-full-art`, `version-full-art-animated`, `version-animated` |
| `full-art-<slug>-signed` (Mythic) | those three and `version-full-art-signed` |
| `animated-<slug>` (Epic) | `version-animated` |
| `augment-animated` | `version-animated` |
| `augment-signature` | nothing |

**Written.** Migration 0083 (`crates/server/migrations/0083_variant_levels.sql`) only inserts, so it is
safe to apply again:

1. The fifteen new levels' `public.cosmetics` rows, as the registry has them, since `release` runs the
   migrations before `seed-catalog` and the grants need their foreign keys.
2. Per account and card, three flags read off the rows held there: **full art**, `version-full-art` or
   any `full-art-…`; **animated**, `augment-animated`, an `animated-…`, or a Legendary or Mythic
   `full-art-…`; **signed**, `augment-signature` or a Mythic `full-art-…`. It grants the Default's
   `version-animated` where animated; `version-full-art` where full art, `version-full-art-animated` where
   full art and animated, and `version-full-art-signed` where all three; `version-alt-art` where
   `version-alt-full-art` is held, now the level above it ([[R2940000]]), and
   `version-alt-full-art-animated` where that and animated; and each one-art version's
   `version-<v>-animated` where `version-<v>` and animated. A level that moves is never granted on a Legacy
   card, whose picture never moves ([[R2724000]]).

Each row inserted gets its `cosmetic_grants` row: the card, +1, reason `launch`, ref `levels:0083`. A row
already held is passed over and gets none, so each ledger still sums to what is held ([[§14.11]]); nothing
is deleted or rewritten, so every retired row stays with its owner and still counts as its levels where
the server reads it. So Animated on a card owns the Default's Animated there and the animated level of
any one-art version held; Full art with Animated owns Full art's first two levels, and with Signature its
first three; Alt art or the Sketch with Animated keeps its first level beside the Default's Animated
([[R3015005]]). Every combination is owned gapless, as [[R3015003]] and [[R3015004]] need. The SQL
evidence is `crates/server/tests/sql/49_variant_levels.sql`, which gives one account a card per
combination and a Legacy card, applies the file twice and reads the levels and ledger rows it wrote.

**Signature alone.** An account owning Signature on a card without both Full art and Animated there owns
no level for it: Signature was only ever worn beside both ([[R2949000]]), and a signed level needs the
levels below it. Its row stays with its owner, retired, and nothing is taken; whether to make it up to its
owner (a token, say) is the owner's call, which this ruling does not make. Signature beside Alt full art
and Animated likewise owns no Alt art signed level, since it never fit there. Rejected: granting Full
art's signed level for it, which would grant two levels below it never owned; and granting the signed
level alone, a ladder with a gap.

Unlike [[R2725002]], whose implication no migration wrote, 0083 writes each combination's levels as rows
of their own, so the ledger and `card_cosmetics` hold them; the reading above still owns them for any row
the migration did not see. It rewrites ownership, so its pull request asks a person to merge it. Nobody owned a full art when the
economy went live ([[R1310002]]), so every combination it reads was earned since.

Amends [[R2655000]] (its three tiers own Full art's levels: the Epic the first, the Legendary the first
two and the Default's Animated, the Mythic all three) and [[R2725002]] (a retired entry maps to levels,
the Animated augment among them and Signature to none, and migration 0083 writes them as rows).
