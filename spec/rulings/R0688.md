Since balance patch 1, a Lock on a zone refuses only plays into it: a summon aimed at it lands, a move enters it, and a return lands there too — an animated card's return, a Reborn body, anything restored — while only the play action needs an empty, unlocked zone ([[R391]] reads the same rule, and a play's Stack entry still honors a Lock). Whole-board moves keep their own bounce: a rotation ([[R14]]) or a board swap ([[R88]]) still bounces a card whose destination is Locked to its controller's hand ([[R747]]). A summon with no named zone still prefers an unlocked zone ([[R64]]) and enters the leftmost empty Locked one only when no unlocked zone is open; a play and a cast still need an unlocked one. A card whose printed text names Locked zones keeps its own rule: Classic+ #19 League of Losers still skips an occupied or Locked unit zone (its text overriding this row, [[R47]]), while #67 Zoomerbin Oomen, whose text names no Lock, now summons into a Locked zone
