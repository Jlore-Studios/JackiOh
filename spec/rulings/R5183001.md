Core #98 Heroic Power's (0) Armor Up gave its hero 2 Armor until its next turn, a player modifier, and its Radiant face Tank Up 4 Armor the hero kept, then refreshed into another power ([[R757]]). Since issue #5183 both faces give Armor the hero keeps, which each hit it meets wears ([[R5183000]]): "Your hero gains {armor} Armor.", `armor` 1 on the base face and 2 on Tank Up (`ARMOR_UP_ARMOR`, `TANK_UP_ARMOR`), still (0) and once a turn. Tank Up refreshes no more: the card keeps the power, used for the turn. The Radiant face keeps the name Tank Up, so the Discover's labels a game stored stay as they were ([[R3134002]]), and `armorGained` reports each use's gain ([[R3857000]]).

Every kept script of the card (`script_v0_5_11o`, `script_v0_5_13g`, `script_v0_6_14`, and `script_kept_5183`, keyed by issue #5183's fragment, [[R4779000]]) runs the power as it was before, `armor_up_kept_5183`, with its words then, so the replays of those versions fold as they were played ([[R767000]]).
