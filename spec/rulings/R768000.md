A player reaches the replay of a finished game ([[R768]]) from three places.

The result dialog of a free practice game the device kept, and the result panel of a finished online match, offer "Watch the replay" and "Share the last turn". The first opens the replay at its first step. The second opens it at its last step with "Share as GIF" open and that turn's GIF already being made ([[R513000]]), once: a later move of the viewer's makes nothing again. Leaving the replay goes back to the result. A practice game the device did not keep (a tutorial lesson, a game Glitch voided) offers neither. The finished game's last snapshot names the number it was kept under and its step count, which is how the result dialog finds it.

The recent games (`/replays`) list the practice games kept on the device and, signed in, the account's finished online matches whose action log is kept, newest first and a page at a time. A row this build cannot show says why ([[R767000]]) and does not open. A row opens its replay, and leaving it goes back to the list.

A replay is a route a production build keeps. The GIF's drawing and encoding still load on demand, never in the main bundle.
