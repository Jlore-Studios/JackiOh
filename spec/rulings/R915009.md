A practice game whose human deck holds a custom card runs in the Luau module, a second WebAssembly build of the engine with the card kit, loaded the first time a practice game or the creator needs it and never by a normal game. Such a game records no card statistics ([[§9.11]]), no ladder progress and no tutorial progress. The normal module holds no Luau, and refuses a game with custom cards.
