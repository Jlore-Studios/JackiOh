Issue #3951 rewrote TG #31 Level Up! as "After one of your Units attacks, if it destroyed an enemy Unit, it becomes Radiant" (Radiant: "After one of your Units attacks, it becomes Radiant"). The `allyAttack` hook ([[R2199000]]) is handed `destroyedEnemy`: the state check that closed the combat destroyed a Unit of the attacker's opponent whose [[R42]] killer was the attacker, the defender or a Unit its Cleave hit ([[R63]]), read off that check's `destroyed` events. A Unit exiled instead is not destroyed, a Spell's kill credits no Unit, and a defender's kill of its attacker is no attack of the defender's side, so none of them count. The base face then makes the attacker Radiant if it still stands as the hook runs ([[R174]]: one that died in that combat, a Reborn body included, does not), a declared or a forced attack alike ([[R53]]); the Radiant face makes every surviving attacker Radiant; one already Radiant is unchanged ([[R22]], [[R74]]). It reads as Hearthstone reads "after this attacks and kills a minion": the kill is the attacker's own, in that attack. The counts of [[R2199007]] are what Level Up! did through v0.6.2b, whose games fold under the kept script ([[R767000]]).
