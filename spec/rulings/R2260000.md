A Dormant card is out of play: nothing targets it and no effect reaches it ([[R1839000]]). An Equip play ([[R1086]]), or a Unit played onto a host that lends Equip ([[R1869000]]), fuses into the card on top of the zone, so it reaches that card: a Dormant card on top of its zone hosts none. `play_choices::equip_host_at`, which both `legalActions` and the reducer's refusal read, finds no host there, so no Equip play onto its zone is offered or accepted, the Dormant Starship's included; once the card awakens it hosts as any Unit does. A Stack play onto a Dormant card is unchanged: it tops the card, which lies beneath it still Dormant ([[R1839003]]), and on a Dormant Starship it is exiled before it resolves ([[R1852007]]). The spec did not say whether an Equip play reaches a Dormant host; the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2260) says it does not, since a Dormant card is untargetable.
