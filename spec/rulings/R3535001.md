The set's texts print numbers and name cards in ways the cross tests' lists must read, so each reading is one entry of those lists and not a new rule:

- **Fixed numbers.** A number the set prints that no Buff, Nerf or X change moves is listed in `params.rs`'s `FIXED` with the reason it is no param ([[R482]], [[R1748003]]): a numbered keyword's count (Tipsy on #6.1, Lucky on #20, Armor on #42 and #55), a cost, threshold, Tribute count or launch price (#24's, #138's and #139's embiggen lines; the Tribute counts of #39, #52, #53, #57, #59, #68, #69.1, #75.1, #82, #97, #98, #103, #104, #128 to #131; #142's, #155's and #166's), a term of a formula (#87's 3X stats) and the numbers the set's AI generated cards print (T-AI-1, T-AI-2, T-AI-6). #162's Combo X gains sit on a Unit, which [[R1748003]] does not hold to this list.
- **Rules words.** "Fuse" is the name of #107 Fuse and the verb the set's texts use everywhere, so it joins the card names that are also rules words ([[R381]]): a text using it names the card only when its `refs` lists it.
- **Token pools.** #80 and #155 name the ten Terminus Starships as "Terminus Starship", and #133 its five Automa-ζ fusion tokens as "Automa-ζ": each phrase names the tag's tokens as a pool, and the card's `refs` lists every one ([[R279]]).
