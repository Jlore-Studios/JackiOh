The viewer's own hand card carries `counteredOnPlay: true` ([[§10.8]]) when a card acting on the field would counter it whatever price it is played at now: every X its player could choose and each embiggen choice, a price a ban forbids left out ([[R65]], [[R455]]), whether or not the mana is there, as the yellow glow beside it does not wait for mana ([[R195]]). The engine works it out and the client only draws it (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A counter is a card whose `wouldCounter` hook says so, the same predicate its counter trigger on `cardAnnounced` asks, so the warning and the counter are one rule; only a card that would answer the announce counts (on the field, the top of its pile, its trigger registered), never a Trap, which answers through [[§10.3]]'s trap window, and never a card the viewer may not read ([[R33]], [[R97]]). The key is absent otherwise, never `false`, and never on the opponent's cards. A fusion warns when any ingredient would ([[R102]]). C #87 Plague Chalice is the one counter that has it (patch v0.2.7): on its base face every player's cards whose cost equals its Plague Counters, on its Radiant face only its controller's opponent's; the mark moves with the count, and a card it would counter at one X but not another carries none
