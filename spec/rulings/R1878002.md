S #129 Jlockeed's Missile Pod reads "End of turn: Deal damage equal to its Attack divided by {divisor}, rounded up, to all enemies", divisor 4 (3 on its Radiant face), and its Embiggened version has Equip (ST-S19-4).

- **Its Attack now.** The Attack is the one the card has as its controller's turn ends ([[R62]]), through the layers ([[§10.4]]): buffs and auras count, and a Nerf or a Buff moves the divisor ([[R386]]), never below 1. The hit is ⌈Attack ÷ divisor⌉, one instance each on every enemy Unit and then the enemy hero, in one effect ([[R59]]); a hit of 0 is no hit ([[R63]]), which only an Attack of 0 makes, so a 2/1 Missile Pod deals 1 and a 4/2 Radiant one, ÷ 3, deals 2. Through v0.5.11p the quotient was rounded down, so a 2/1 Missile Pod dealt nothing until it had 4 Attack (`script_v0_5_11o`, kept, [[R767000]]).
- **Equipped.** Played as an Equip at (2) it fuses into its host ([[R1086]]), whose text it joins ([[R77]], [[R102]]): at the end of its controller's turn the host deals the host's Attack divided by the Missile Pod's divisor. The host is no Starship Piece by it ([[R1852001]], [[R1878001]]).
