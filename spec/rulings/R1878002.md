S #129 Jlockeed's Missile Pod reads "End of turn: Deal damage equal to its Attack divided by {divisor}, rounded down, to all enemies", divisor 4 (3 on its Radiant face), and its Embiggened version has Equip (ST-S19-4).

- **Its Attack now.** The Attack is the one the card has as its controller's turn ends ([[R62]]), through the layers ([[§10.4]]): buffs and auras count, and a Nerf or a Buff moves the divisor ([[R386]]), never below 1. The hit is ⌊Attack ÷ divisor⌋, one instance each on every enemy Unit and then the enemy hero, in one effect ([[R59]]); a hit of 0 is no hit ([[R63]]), so a 2/1 Missile Pod deals nothing until it has 4 Attack.
- **Equipped.** Played as an Equip at (2) it fuses into its host ([[R1086]]), whose text it joins ([[R77]], [[R102]]): at the end of its controller's turn the host deals the host's Attack divided by the Missile Pod's divisor. The host is no Starship Piece by it ([[R1852001]], [[R1878001]]).
