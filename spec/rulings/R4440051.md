Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI: S #158 Stratos Swarm's "Fill your board with Stratos" becomes "Summon {stratos} Stratos" (Radiant: "Summon {stratos} Radiant Stratos"), `stratos` a declared number, 7 on both faces ([[R386]]). It is seven summons of S #68 Stratos, one after another, as S #29 Drone Core III's Interceptors are: each goes into its caster's leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), a Dormant card's zone not one ([[R1839000]]), and once the unit row is full, as Stratos flies, into the leftmost open backrow zone, where it stands ([[R2248000]]); each is a summoned card of its own definition, no Cry ([[R1]]). A summon that finds no zone left on either row is lost, as a Hearthstone summon past a full board is: nothing waits for a zone to open, nothing goes to the hand, and the Spell still resolves. So an empty board takes five Stratos in its unit row and two in its backrow, and a board with room for fewer than seven takes what fits. Rejected: filling the board first and counting the seven against it, which the new words do not say. Through v0.6.9n it filled the board, up to ten ([[R1860008]]), which `script_v0_6_9n` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
