The rest of the list and of the action resolve and any prompt it opened is answered; then `turnCutShort` and every end-of-turn step. "N more actions" counts the player's main-phase actions (play, attack, switch, activation); the action that set it, answers and draw offers do not count. The turn ends once the Nth has resolved; ending the turn yourself uses it up; two riders keep the sooner end. Nothing happens off-turn.
