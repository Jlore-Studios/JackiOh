Since issue #4410 Stinkyscape #89 Spice no longer sells from the hand: it pays its quote in Credits as it is played or discarded, as a Resource does ([[R4410000]], [[R2250001]]). [[R4154401]]'s reading, which counted Spice in the AI's own hand as the Credits its sale would bring, is retired with the sale: `evaluate::hand_sale_worth` and `AI_EVAL.hand_sale_credits` go. Spice in the AI's own hand is priced as any (1) hand card (`AI_EVAL.hand_card` and `hand_per_cost` for its cost), as a Resource is priced as any (0) one, and the Credits its play or discard pays count once gained, at `AI_EVAL.market_credit` each, as every Credit held does. So a cast weighs the Discover it brings and the Credits it pays against the card spent, and the search tries it as any play. This is a judgement about the AI, not a rule of the game, and changes no game. Since issue #4440 the hand price is held at `AI_EVAL.credits_card_discount` off, so the AI sells Spice more often ([[R4440002]]).

Rejected: counting the quote on top of the hand price while Spice is held, which would make the Credits its cast brings move nothing and so hold it back.
