While Ground Truth stands active on the field, its controller reads the opponent's hand as revealed. The view presents the opponent's hand cards openly with no `hand_marked` redaction, the testkit invariant `hidden_from` does not flag them as private, and the AI's observation does not redact them from its search ([[§10.8]], [[R865]]).
