On a touch screen a card's sheet, a face-down card's sheet ([[R370]]) and a pile's sheet
([[R313]]) also close on a swipe down, through the same close as Close, Escape and the scrim,
so the focus goes back.

**What drags.** Touch only. A drag may start on the sheet's grip, on a pile sheet's header, or
anywhere on the sheet while nothing under the finger is scrolled down. Past
`SHEET_DRAG_SLOP_PX` the gesture commits: downward first drags the sheet, up or sideways first
is left alone, to the content's scroll or to nothing.

**On release.** Past `SHEET_DISMISS_DISTANCE` of the sheet's height, or moving down faster than
`SHEET_DISMISS_VELOCITY` within `SHEET_FLICK_WINDOW_MS` of the last move, the sheet closes as
Close does and the focus goes back. Otherwise it springs back, and the click from lifting the
finger is swallowed, so a drag that starts on Play or on a pile tile fires nothing.

**Reduce motion.** The sheet never follows the finger; a swipe past the threshold still closes it
at once, through the media query and the settings switch alike.

**Safe-area insets.** The sheets stay inside them, and a drag moves them only through the
`translate` property, never `transform`, so the centered pile dialog keeps its center.

**Never** a prompt or the mulligan ([[§10.6]]): a choice the game asks for never closes
unanswered, while a view opened to read does. That is Hearthstone semantics: a Discover or the
mulligan stays until it is answered; a card or pile sheet is only a look.

Rejected: mouse or pen drags, which fight text selection; a slide-out before the close, where
Close closes at once; a grip on the centered pile dialog, which still swipes from its header.
