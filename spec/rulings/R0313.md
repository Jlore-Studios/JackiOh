The client draws the list the view gives and nothing else (CLAUDE.md rule 7). On the viewer's own library pile a resting mouse opens a preview, and a click, a tap, a long-press or Enter opens a dialog, as for a graveyard ([[§10.10]]). The title gives the size and says the order is hidden ("Your deck — 14 cards (order hidden)", in players' words since [[R373]]). The faces are grouped with their counts in the view's order, each printed on the face it went in with and with no cost from play, and unknown cards are backs. The opponent's library pile opens nothing. It is the same board in hotseat (for the seat whose view is shown), online, practice and the tutorial
