A carrier holds a Unit its controller plays onto it ([[R446]]): C+ #33 Ivory Tower holds the first one while that play resolves, then fuses it in ([[R653]]). IA #138 Social Engineer, played onto a Tower in the daily fuzz's plain seed 1576, swapped control of itself with an enemy Unit in its Cry ([[R4590003]]). The swap places each card by [[R15]] in the row it left, so the Engineer went to the enemy's backrow zone in its lane, where no carrier held it, and it fell out of every pile. The fuzz monitor's I6 found its id in the view, naming a card that had ceased to exist.

**It crosses to the unit row.** A Unit a carrier held is a Unit for every rule ([[R446]]), and the carrier stays where it stood. So when a steal, a give or a swap moves the Unit, it goes to its new controller's unit row, the row its type takes ([[§5.1]]), placed there by [[R15]] as any Unit is: the same lane when it is free, else the first free zone. The carrier keeps its zone, and a fusing carrier whose Unit has left fuses nothing, as an Equip handed over does ([[R4834000]]).
