FvH #30 Big Book casts the declared `casts` ([[R386]]) random Books: the cards tagged Book of every set that ships ([[R380]]), as M #60 Eschews' pool reads its tags. FvH #23–#29 are Books of a set that is held, so they join the pool as it ships and are out of it until then ([[R1420]]); Big Book prints no Book tag, and a card never generates itself anyway ([[R387]]). Each cast resolves before the next ([[R113]]). The base face's casts make every choice at random ([[R452]]); the Radiant face's casts are Radiant and aim each target pick by its declaration, at enemies when it harms and at its caster's side when it helps ([[R656]]). FvH #23–#29 declare no targets, so they cast whole on either face.
