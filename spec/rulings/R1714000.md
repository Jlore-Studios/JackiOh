[[R222]]'s concealed copy, which [[§10.7]]'s dry run and the autopilot's tries play on ([[R1183003]]), is the whole state as its viewer may read it, so beside each hidden card's stand-in it keeps none of these: the decks and dealt seats the match began with ([[R676]]), so a Glitch reset tried there deals nothing; the Glitch boards ([[R678]]); the other seat's last board ([[R417]]), the viewer's own staying as their own last game; the other seat's unrevealed secret choices ([[R865]]), the viewer's own and a revealed one staying, so a guess tried there is judged against nothing; a multiple-choice problem's key, in the open prompt and in every parked continuation ([[R465]]), so no answer tried there is judged right; and the modes of the other seat's in-flight play of a `secretModes` card ([[R865]]). Each continuation keeps its shape, so a try still resumes it.

Until this ruling the copy blanked the hidden cards and cleared a handful of their fields and nothing else: a dry run or the autopilot answering a problem ranked its answers by the real key, and a guess tried on it was judged against the other seat's real secret, so a trial could peek at what the viewer may not read. A stand-in keeps its place and id and nothing else, as the AI's placeholder does ([[R185]]), except the Plague Counters of a face-down card that a backrow zone shows, which are public ([[R471]]). Rollback's snapshots ([[R419]]) show the other seat's face-down traps as the same stand-ins, so a try that restores one still restores a trap, and learns nothing of it. So two states that differ only in what the viewer may not read give the same copy, the same tries and the same scores.

The AI's redaction ([[R185]]) already dropped the same records for its seat, and is unchanged: the engine's copy ports those steps rather than the AI's redaction being extracted into the engine, since the AI's placeholder ids, its pile sorting and its [[R602]] stat carry-over differ from [[R222]]'s stand-ins. Definitions that only hidden cards name stay on the copy, since no try reads them and `def_of` must resolve every card a snapshot, a delayed effect or a memory still names. The viewer's own last board is kept, as the AI's redaction keeps it; clearing both was rejected. Rollback's snapshots have their hidden traps hidden rather than dropped, as the AI drops them, since dropping them would make every Rollback trial restore nothing. Queue entries that belong to hidden cards are not dropped, since removing a work item moves `work_cursor`.
