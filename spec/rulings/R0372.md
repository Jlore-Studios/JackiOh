#93's grade is a counter from 1 to 6 that the board drew as a bare number, while its text names grades E to S. The engine names the letter: `viewFor` carries `counters.gradeLetter` beside `counters.grade` on a backrow card, and #93's `preview` ([[R280]]) returns "Grade", with the grade as its value and its letter as `display`, and, below S, "N = the grades from E to the current one", with the plays the end of turn asks for, which is the number `gradeRises` compares the turn's plays with, so the value and the rise cannot disagree. A preview value may carry `display`, the word it prints as; a client prints it in the braces, "Grade {C}", and never derives one from the other. The preview is on the field only, since in hand the card has no grade and its text says it starts at E. The board's grade badge prints the letter ("C", titled "Grade C"); it sits on the compact backrow face, which prints no text, and the full face in hand and in the inspect overlays has no badge, so each shows the letter once, as [[R280]] asks. The printed text is rewritten short (the grade, the rule, then a line per step, E to S), and Combo-Fodder's faces are a keyword line and an effect line. No behaviour changes
