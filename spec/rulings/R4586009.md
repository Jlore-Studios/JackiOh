A random pick is drawn among the Units a Spell may reach ([[R4453000]]), and a single candidate draws no rng ([[R800]], [[R129]]); the copy lands for its controller ([[R57]]), and Brittle is given once it stands. Mass Hallucination reads the cost now ([[R65]]) and makes a plain Husk on both faces; Cognitive Dissonance reads the printed cost ([[R4577006]]) minus the step, draws from the shipped sets' Units ([[R380]]) and keeps the face.
