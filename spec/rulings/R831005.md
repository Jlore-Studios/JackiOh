Meditative #53 Prestige's "The next card you play this turn becomes Radiant", on both faces, is a player modifier (`ModifierKind::RadiantNextPlay`, expiring at the end of this turn, badged in the card's words, [[R169]]): the first card its controller plays while it stands is played Radiant, as a Gifted Program play is, and that play removes the modifier, so it makes one card Radiant. A cast is a play ([[R70]]), so it counts as the next one. Unused, it ends with the turn. The base face first De-Radiants a card on the field or in its controller's hand ([[R1103]]); the Radiant face De-Radiants every enemy card on the field, and no longer the opponent's hand and deck ([[R1104]]). It no longer adds an AI generated card.
