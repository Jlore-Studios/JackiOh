IA #71 Thermal Throttler's "for each 5 Health your hero is missing" (10 on the base face) counts the hero's Health missing from its starting health ([[R290]]): a handicap's `heroHealth` when the seat has one, else 30. A hero above its start — healed past it, or a seat whose start was lowered — counts 0 missing, floored, and gains nothing. Rejected: a fixed 30 for every seat, which a handicapped hero outgrows. Each full `health` missing is one tick of the declared `mana` ([[§2.2]], [[§2.3]]), gained this turn.
