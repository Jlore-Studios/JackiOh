No effect reaches a Dormant card ([[§6.1]] Dormant, [[R1839000]]), and the fuzz monitor's I19 now fails a step in which anything does (issue #4454). Two board moves did move Dormant cards, and no ruling said whether they may: Pocket Chaos's board swap ([[R73]]) and Silly Silas's rotation ([[R14]]).
**They may.** A swap and a rotation move every zone's contents, not a card an effect reached for, as [[R3539002]] says of a board exile: the Dormant card moves with its zone, still Dormant and with its countdown, as a Stack pile's buried cards already move with theirs. One that lands on the other side changes controller (`controlChanged`) and enters that side ([[R171]]), and its countdown then counts its new controller's turns ([[R1839001]]); one moving along its own side changes nothing but its lane.
**A bounce on the way.** A card whose destination is Locked, reserved or void ([[R3554002]]), or one leaving its caster's side under the Radiant rotation, goes to its controller's hand as any card there does, and [[R78]]'s reset takes its status off.
**What I19 allows.** A `controlChanged` or a `bounced` naming a Dormant card after a board `swapped` or a `rotated`, until the next card's hook, trap or turn edge; anything else in that window that names it is still a reach.
Rejected: passing it by as a board replacement does ([[R1855000]]). A swap or a rotation must empty every zone to fill it, so a Dormant card left standing would bounce the card bound for its zone instead, and the board would no longer be the mirror or the turn the card prints.
