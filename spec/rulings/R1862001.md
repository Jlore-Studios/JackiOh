The Flaks' "Deal N damage to all enemies" is `damage_all` over the enemy side, every enemy Unit and then the enemy hero, one hit each before one state check ([[R59]]), as #13 Jlockeed Shredder-10's "each enemy unit and the enemy hero". "Twice" and "three times" are that many such rounds, each its own effect after the last round's state check (`after_state_check`, as C #43 Plague Nuke's summons wait for its check), so a Unit one round destroys is not hit by the next, and a Unit on the field when a round begins is, as Hearthstone resolves deaths between the casts of Defile. The Flaks print no "half damage to heroes" ([[R1841001]]): the enemy hero takes each hit whole. Rejected: every round inside one check, which would hit a Unit already at 0 health again, and one sweep of the rounds' sum, which is not what "twice" says.
