[[R1523000]]'s seat ledger holds each profile to one game at a time, so no player is two places at once in live play. A daily game is played a move at a time over days, beside everything else: it takes no seat of the ledger and sets no in-match flag, so its players may queue, join a room, take a live challenge and play co-op while it runs. R1523000's one game per profile is for live games. What bounds daily games is `DAILY_MAX_ACTIVE_GAMES` (20): the daily games an account has running, its open seeks and the challenges it sent, together; one more challenge, seek or acceptance is refused (`daily_full`), so nobody holds hundreds of games or floods the queue ([[§9.8]]).
