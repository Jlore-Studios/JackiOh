Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7), as [[R979000]] is: no state, event, replay or AI decision changes, only what `viewFor` carries. Clicking a card in your own graveyard that some card lets you replay said nothing when the play was refused — the Play button simply was not there. The graveyard pile's cards now carry the same kind of reason where a replay is refused.

`playReason` is on the viewer's own graveyard card that a permission admits ([[R454]], Flashback [[R783012]]) while they may act — their turn, the main phase, no prompt open — and `graveyard_play_actions_for` lists no play of it. It uses R979000's order and words (`play_choices::why_graveyard_card_unplayable`): ban, price, zone, Tribute, choices. When no price can be paid, the price step gives `why_graveyard_play_refused`'s refusal at the cheapest price, for the payment the player would try: mana alone where a permission without `plague` admits the price, else the most Plague Counters the richest Plague permission could spend. Otherwise the cheapest payable price is the one read, and the rest of the order follows §10.5 step 1 as R979000's does.

No reason can come from a hidden fact alone ([[§9.1]]): the permissions come from the viewer's own face-up field — a face-down card's text sleeps ([[R1487000]]) — and the card's own Flashback. The counters sit on a public card. The price and the mana are public. The other refusals are R979000's.

The web shows the reason in the graveyard's sheet where "Play" would be, in the grid and with the face opened large. The face's inspect notes carry it. Nothing is clickable.

Rejected: a reason on every graveyard card ("you may not play that card…"), which is noise when no permission is out; the bare mana-only refusal under a Plague-only permission ("only by spending Plague Counters"), which doesn't say why the counters can't pay; a click notice, since the sheet has no control to click; the hover preview, whose faces take no pointer.
