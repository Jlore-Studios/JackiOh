The AI's shadow ban ([[R186]], `SHADOW_BAN` in `crates/ai/src/shadow_ban.rs`) holds cards of the Meditative set only, and at most half of that set's 102 non-token cards (51), so the AI deals itself at least half of the set. No card of an earlier set (Core, Classic, Classic+) is shadow-banned again: the unban lane taught the AI to play every one it had banned (generations 1 to 8), and a sweep's `timeout` on one of them, a decision over two seconds that the game's other cards may have caused, keeps it in the AI's decks. A sweep of record that would ban more than half the Meditative set does not go in; the AI is improved until it bans no more.

Issue #732's sweep of record, which lifted [[R1390]]'s gate, swept the 102 Meditative cards alone ([[R390]]'s two passes, each timed-out card run again with the machine to itself), so its report holds rows for no other card. It banned {BANNED} of them: {TIMEOUTS} for `timeout` and {OTHER} for the other flags. `SHADOW_WATCH` keeps the 2026-10-07 sweep's rows for the earlier sets and gains this sweep's Meditative rows. The table only shrinks from here: the unban lane removes entries ([[R732001]]), and no lane adds one.
