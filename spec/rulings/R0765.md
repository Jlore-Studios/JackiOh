Amends [[R668]]. Keeping a free practice game is the player's choice when they leave it. While a free game is on, "New game" and "Menu" ask "Leave this game?" with three answers: Keep playing (the default), **Save and leave**, and **Leave without saving**. Save and leave keeps the worker's save, which is already the game as of its last answer, and the page marks the setup it remembers as left on purpose; Leave without saving gives the game up as [[R668]]'s leaving did (the page forgets it, so nothing offers it again, and the next game's save replaces the worker's copy). A tutorial lesson is never kept ([[R668]]), so its question keeps its one way out. The page remembers where the player is: in the game, or out of it with Save and leave. Opening `/practice` with no parameter that starts a game picks a game the player was in up at once, as [[R668]] says (a reload, a closed tab or a Back in the middle of a game still resumes it); a game left with Save and leave is not picked up, and the setup shows with a banner at its top: the game is in progress, with its difficulty, and **Resume**. Resume folds the save exactly as a reload does and puts the player back in the game; a save that will not fold is dropped, and the setup says so. Starting any other game from that menu, a practice game or a tutorial lesson, ends the saved one and the banner goes; a URL that starts a game replaces it, as before. Back from the loading screen while a resume folds keeps the game saved for the banner. A setup kept before this ruling carries no mark and reads as one the player was in. Online, presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7; no endpoint changes): `GET /api/auth/me` already says whether the player is in a match (`currentMatchId`, [[§9.5]]) or between the games of a Conquest series (`currentSeriesId`, [[R259]]). A tab that queued on `/play` remembers it for as long as the ticket waits (this tab only, a reload included), so the player may leave the lobby: on any other screen the client reads that account every `SERIES_POLL_SECONDS`, and when the queue pairs them it says "Match found!" for `MATCH_FOUND_NAV_DELAY_MS`, as the lobby does, and takes them to `/match/<id>` (or the series screen); back on `/play` the lobby shows the wait again, with Leave. While the player's match or series is live, the main menu and the practice menu show the same banner at their top: they are in a game, with **Rejoin**, a link to the board (a reconnect, [[§9.5]]) or the series screen; it is read again every `SERIES_POLL_SECONDS` and goes once the game is over.
