An embiggen card ("A embiggen B", [[§2.3]]) has a normal price and an **Embiggen premium**, B − A, what its Embiggened version costs on top of the normal one. The catalog keeps printing `{base, embiggen}`, and the premium is read off it (`CardCost::embiggen_premium`). The Embiggened version costs the normal version's **finished** price plus the premium: the normal version is priced by every rung of [[R65]] and [[R455]] in their order (`costOverride`, `costMod`, hand auras, the player's discounts, the flat price rules, the threshold rules and Professor Curvature, the "costs (N)" sets, the card's own floor, then 0), and the premium is added to the number that comes out. So every price change moves both prices alike:

- **A `costOverride`** prices the normal version, and the Embiggened version is that price plus the premium: "this costs (1)" makes a 2 embiggen 4 card cost (1) and (3). This holds for [[R3140001]]'s (0) too: the Radiant Garden's added card costs (0) and, Embiggened, the premium.
- **A "costs (N)" price rule** ([[R455]]) sets the normal price to N, and the Embiggened one to N plus the premium.
- **Professor Curvature** (#77, [[R363]]) and every threshold rule read the normal price: a card whose normal price they reach is discounted at both prices, and one whose normal price they do not reach at neither.
- **The floor at 0** takes up a discount on the normal price, never the premium: a 2 embiggen 4 card under a discount of 3 costs (0) and (2).

Only a rule that names the premium changes it. Under Meditative+ #22 Mountaintop Garden the dearer choice's premium is the Garden's (2, Radiant 1), in place of the card's own, for an embiggen card ([[R1181002]]) and for a card of one price alike ([[R1563004]]), so the dearer choice costs the normal version's finished price plus the Garden's premium. A card with no Embiggen premium (`noEmbiggenPremium`, Meditative+ #23, [[R4350000]]) has a premium of 0, under a Garden too, so its two prices are always equal. A play at the normal price adds nothing, the Garden's Embiggened play at the normal price included.

Flashback's floor ([[R4285000]]) reads the sum, so a Flashback play of the Embiggened version costs at least (1) whatever its normal price came to, and a health price ([[R1180004]]) reads the finished price as it reads any. The `costRule` modifiers a play of the Embiggened version spends are the ones that changed its normal price. A card kept with an `embiggenCostOverride` (Meditative+ #23 in games of v0.6.9 and before, [[R1181003]]) has no premium: that override prices its Embiggened version on [[R65]]'s ladder as it did, so those games fold as they played.

The rule is the engine's and holds for every version, as [[R4285000]] does: a stored game in which one of these price changes met an Embiggened price no longer replays and retires as a rules change ([[R767000]]), which the patch's fragment claims. The AI reads prices through the engine and is unchanged.

Rejected: keeping the two printed prices and pricing each on its own ladder, which the designer found confusing since a discount, a set price or Curvature reached one price and not the other; pricing games before this patch the old way behind a version gate, which no price rule has and which would be kept forever; and storing `{base, premium}` in the catalog, which would rewrite every shipped snapshot and the wire for no change in play.
