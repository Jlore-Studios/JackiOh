## R783010 — Crater: a Unit Locks the zone it arrives in

Built (#783). A Unit with Crater, read through the layers, Locks its own unit zone as it arrives on
the field, before its Cry (`crater::lock_on_arrival`). "Arrives" is every way a Unit gets there (the
designer: a play from hand included): played or cast, summoned by an effect, Recruited, returned by
Reborn, summoned by Joro's replacement, Flickered, and revealed from Cloaked ([[R783006]]). A control
change, a board swap, a rotation, an animation, a Transform, a Glitch and a restored board move a card
already on the field and are no arrival. The Lock is [[R688]]'s: it refuses plays, a Stack play onto the
Crater Unit's zone included, never a summon or a move, and stays until an Unlock, so it outlasts the
Unit. A zone Locked already stays as it is. An Animated Crater Trap locks its backrow zone as it fires
([[R783011]]); its animation into a unit zone is a move and Locks nothing more.
