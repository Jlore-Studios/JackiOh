Issue #3583 bounds every route from a start to the final boss: `RUN_ROUTE_FIGHTS_MIN` to `RUN_ROUTE_FIGHTS_MAX` fights, the bosses counted, and at least `RUN_ROUTE_TILES_MIN` tiles, for every route rather than on average. It lets the deal either place the kinds so the bounds hold or deal again from the same `Rng`. This note places them, in one pass with no deal again.

- **The tiles bound is a fights bound.** Every route holds every row once, so its tiles are the rows less its fights, and the most fights a route may hold is the lesser of `RUN_ROUTE_FIGHTS_MAX` and the rows less `RUN_ROUTE_TILES_MIN`.
- **Fixed fights.** The bosses are fights, and so is every node of the first row, so a run opens on a fight, as it did before. Every other node is a fight at the chance that puts a route's fights at the middle of the bounds: the middle less the fixed fights, over the free rows (10 in 20 with the strawmen), so no new percent is needed.
- **The fix-up.** While some route breaks a bound, by the fewest and the most fights on any route (dynamic programming over the rows), a whole free row is made fights when a route holds too few, or tiles when one holds too many. While the spread between the fewest and the most fits the bounds, the row is one wholly of the other kind, which moves every route by one and so never overshoots; otherwise, or when no such row is left, it is a row of both kinds, which leaves one fewer such row. With no row of both kinds left every route holds the same count, so the loop ends. Its picks come from the run's own `Rng`, so a seed always deals the same map.
- **Kinds after.** A fight node's kind is then dealt as before (`RUN_ELITE_PERCENT`, then `RUN_GHOST_PERCENT`), a tile's by `RUN_TILE_WEIGHTS`. The lone campfire row before each boss is gone; a campfire is one of the ordinary tiles.
