"Gain the effects of your ascent level" (Meditative #8 Reach the Summit) is the one line for the
current level, not every line up to it. Ascent 2 draws; Ascent 3 is a random discard; Ascent 4 exiles
N distinct random enemy permanents ([[R60]]), tops of piles, face-down cards included and shown, a
pool holding N or fewer used as is with no shuffle ([[R129]]); Ascent 5 draws non-token cards of
every set ([[R380]]) but this one ([[R387]]), Radiant with `costOverride` 0; lines 6 to 9 do nothing.
