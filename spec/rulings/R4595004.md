**Why.** A Play of an `AI_SEARCH.expensive_def_ids` card pays `expensive_def_nodes` up front, and a cast that announces one pays after it runs ([[R4801000]]). A Zephrys set face-down as a Cloaked card ([[R783009]]) and revealed by its `activate` ran the same whole-catalog ranking, about a second of simulation, for one node, since a reveal announces nothing and is no Play: on the sweep's seed `sweep2:easy:infiniteassault-048:3` (turn 13) one decision took 4.2 s on the sweep's yardstick.

**The rule.** An `activate` of a card that stands for an expensive card pays `expensive_def_nodes` up front, and its announced charge counts it as prepaid, as a Play's does, so the reveal pays once.
