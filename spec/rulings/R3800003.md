C+ #37 Wardrum's "End of turn: Cast a copy of a random non-Unit you played this turn" (the Radiant face's "each") casts from the non-Units its controller played this turn before the turn's end began ([[§2.2]], [[R62]]). What the end of turn itself plays or casts, another Wardrum's copies among them, joins no Wardrum's pool, as [[R68]] makes the delayed effects due those that exist as their point begins. A cast still counts as a play for everything else ([[R70]]): Combo, a Wardrum's summoning threshold, and #39 Recycling Initiative's copies ([[R71]]). The engine notes, on each player's turn log, how many plays it held as the end began (`playedBeforeEnd`), and `startTurn` clears it with the rest of the log. Hearthstone's recasting cards (Zul'jin, Tess Greymane) recast what was played, never their own recasts. Before this, each Radiant Wardrum also cast the copies the ones before it had cast, doubling the pool once per Wardrum, and with Recycling Initiatives among the copies their end-of-turn adds burned 2,287 copies into the graveyard in one action (fuzz seed 5763, past the fuzz's instance bound). Rejected: a cap on a Wardrum's casts, a number the card does not print that would still let the doubling run up to it; and making a Wardrum's copies no plays at all, as a cast on draw is ([[R2179002]]), which would change Combo and every other count that reads them.
