An audit of how cards are played found S #9 Ticketed's play ([[R4665000]]) offered a GIGA permanent of either side, accepted and charged for it, and then lost the card to its graveyard when the placement failed; it also found Ticketed stacked onto C #84 Lockdown in its Locked zone. [[R4665000]]'s "any occupied zone" gives way to [[R3546000]] (nothing stacks onto a GIGA, covered zones included) and to [[R688]] (a play's Stack entry honors a Lock), as a Stack play's zone and an Equip play's host already do. A card that stacks on its target (`StaticFlags.stacks_on_target`) is offered, and may name, only a permanent that tops a zone a card played onto it may enter (`zones::accepts_stack_card`): never a GIGA, never a permanent in a Locked zone, never one in a zone held for a card's return or voided, and never a carrier holding a Unit or the Unit it carries ([[R446]]). The legal actions, the refusal of a play that names one anyway and the view's targets all read one list, and so does the target drawn while a Mayor acts ([[R1200]]). With no such permanent on the field the play is still legal and names nothing, and Ticketed lands in its graveyard ([[R90]], [[R4665000]]). Rejected: letting "any occupied zone" put Ticketed on a GIGA or into a Locked zone, which would make it the one play a GIGA's zones or a Lock take.
