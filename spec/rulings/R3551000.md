The designer's list gives Jay Fungus two Evolve lines: base at three zero-cost hand cards, Radiant at one. Which face reads which line, and what counts as costing (0)? Both faces gain the Swarm tag. The base face evolves when its controller's hand holds at least three cards costing (0); the Radiant face at least one — the list's first "Normal" line is the base face, its second the Radiant face. A hand card counts at its cost now ([[R65]]): discounts and cost changes apply, and an X card counts, at (0) out of play. The condition is read at the settled state check ([[R3536001]]), and the Evolve grants Poisonous once: later settled checks evolve it no more ([[R3536000]]).

Rejected: Reading the printed cost: a discounted card would never count, against [[R65]], which the Fungus's own discount already writes to.
