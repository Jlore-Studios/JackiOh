**Why.** The Cards tab shows each card's Decisiveness ([[R4552001]]), how far its play typically moves its player's chance of winning, either way. The Experimental tab's views ([[R4678001]], [[R4680000]], [[R5040000]]) showed none, and a sort by it read as Adjusted there. The owner asked for it on the Experimental tab (issue #5036, under issue #4670's deeper card stats).

**The figure.** Each play version's Decisiveness is [[R4552001]]'s, read by its code (`decisiveness_rows` beside `swing_report`), over the play version's span:
- Its card's turn tallies are summed over the versions it spans and the record sources its span counts, live alone once its live games clear [[R654]]'s gate and live and development together below it, as its games are ([[R4675001]]). Tallies are integer sums, so they add exactly over versions ([[R1044000]]). A card only a rule makes, the Dormant Starship, reads its Created copies' turns ([[R4552002]]), as its games are the seats that made one ([[R3070000]]).
- What an ordinary turn gains, `α` per bucket, and the turns' variance `σ²` are one baseline for every row: every turn of the records the table counts, each (version, source) some row's span counts taken once, in the modes the query reads and from every seat, as the Cards tab's baseline reads its window's. A v0.6.0 play version and today's are read against the same baseline, as every row is read against one field ([[R4675001]]).
- The play and board terms' prior spreads, the typical play version's root mean square `m` and the spread `τ_D` about it are [[R3022000]]'s estimate over the table's rows with `SWING_FIELD_MIN_ROWS` turns, the named priors below `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` such rows, as the Cards tab reads them over cards. A row a rule makes is read against them and counts toward neither, as on the Cards tab.
- A row below [[R654]]'s floor, or with no turns of the kind it is read off, has no figure.
- `?scope=major` ([[R5040000]]) reads the same over its cut rows: their own spans, the baseline of the versions they span and a field of the cut rows alone.

**The limit.** Only records written since [[R4552000]] carry turns, and none is backfilled. A live play version fills in as games arrive; most past play versions have their games and no Decisiveness, for good. Development runs (`cargo jackioh stats`) fill the same tallies, so the figures can be checked before live games arrive.

**The wire and the text.** Each row of `GET /api/stats/versions` sends `decisiveness`, a fraction or `null`, and the body sends `decisiveness`, the typical play version's `m`. The Hall of Fame's rows ([[R4681001]]) are the same rows and carry it too, unread. `jackioh-server stats-cards --versions` and `cargo jackioh stats report --versions` print each row's Decisiveness after its range and the typical value in the header line, as ±X% or "—".

**The column.** Major patch running, Historical and Versions show Decisiveness right after Adjusted, as the Cards tab does, in the table and in the Grid:
- It reads ±X% to one decimal (±14.2%), "not enough games" for a row below the floor or with no turns, and "—" from a server deployed before the field.
- Its header's tooltip is the Cards tab's, naming the typical play version's value, and adds "Versions played before games recorded their turns have none."
- It is sortable, highest first on the first press, and the Sort select offers it after Adjusted. A row with no figure sorts last either way. Decisiveness leaves [[R4678001]]'s list of sorts the running views read as Adjusted; with [[R5037000]]'s Win rate and Play rate gone from it too, the list is empty, and the select offers Name, Games, Tier, Adjusted, Decisiveness, Win rate, Play rate and Date ([[R5033000]]).

Rejected:
- Counting only the seats `?pilot=` names in the baseline (`swing_rows_of`'s pilot test): the Cards tab's baseline counts every seat of its window's records, and the figure is meant to read as the Cards tab's.
- A baseline per row, over its own span: two rows read against two baselines are on two scales, the problem one field solves for Adjusted.
- Gating the figure on the client alone: the server already gates the tier on the floor, and a row's figure should mean the same wherever it is read.
- Reading a row with games and no turns as "—", as the Cards tab does: it is the usual case for a past play version, and "not enough games" says why it has none; "—" stays for a server that sends no field.
- Sending the Cards tab's typical value (`tiers.decisiveness`) for the header: it belongs to another field, the cards of one window.
- The figure on `--json` alone: printing it in the text lets a development run check it before live games arrive.

Hearthstone has no counterpart.
