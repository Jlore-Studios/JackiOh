#32 draws for its attacks and nothing else: once a combat it attacked in — declared or forced ([[R53]]) — is over and the state check after it has run ([[R59]]), the player who controlled it in that combat draws 2 for each Unit that attack destroyed ([[R42]]): the attacked Unit and, on the Radiant face, each Unit its Cleave killed — whether or not the Panther is still on the field ([[R2396000]]). One that died in the combat draws as the snapshot it fought with, and one Reborn brings back draws once, its body a new stay that answers nothing more ([[R83]], [[R174]]). A Panther a Death in the check stole still draws, for the player who attacked with it ([[R212]]). It never draws while defending. Patch v0.2.0 tied the draw to its own attacks and required survival; balance patch 1 (issue #88) dropped the survival requirement, so under [[R42]] alone it drew when it traded itself away and when it killed an attacker defending; issue #2285 required survival again, for every "After this attacks" ([[R2285001]]); issue #2396 made its text "Whenever this attacks", which asks none, so it draws for a trade again ([[R2396000]])
