#32 draws for its attacks and nothing else: once a combat it attacked in — declared or forced ([[R53]]) — is over and the state check after it has run ([[R59]]), the player who controlled it in that combat draws 2 for each Unit that attack destroyed ([[R42]]): the attacked Unit and, on the Radiant face, each Unit its Cleave killed — even when the Panther died in the combat, even one Reborn brings back, because the hook is owed on the snapshot it fought with rather than answered by the stay it stands on now ([[R174]], [[R212]]). It never draws while defending. Patch v0.2.0 tied the draw to its own attacks and required survival; balance patch 1 (issue #88) drops the survival requirement: under [[R42]] alone it drew when it traded itself away and when it killed an attacker defending
