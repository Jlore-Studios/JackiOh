#32 draws for its attacks and nothing else: once a combat it attacked in — declared or forced ([[R53]]) — is over and the state check after it has run ([[R59]]), the player who controlled it in that combat draws 2 for each Unit that attack destroyed ([[R42]]): the attacked Unit and, on the Radiant face, each Unit its Cleave killed — as long as the Panther is still on the field on the stay it attacked from ([[R174]], [[R2285001]]). One that died in the combat draws nothing, and so does one Reborn brings back, a new stay. A Panther a Death in the check stole still draws, for the player who attacked with it ([[R212]]). It never draws while defending. Patch v0.2.0 tied the draw to its own attacks and required survival; balance patch 1 (issue #88) dropped the survival requirement, so under [[R42]] alone it drew when it traded itself away and when it killed an attacker defending; issue #2285 requires survival again, for every "After this attacks" ([[R2285001]])
