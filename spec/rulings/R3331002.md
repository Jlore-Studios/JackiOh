Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). [[R503]] pinned Core's pictures to the ones v0.1 gave them: a Core card
whose name calls up no motif draws exactly the picture it drew before. The owner decided on 2026-11-06 that
every card's picture is redrawn richer, Core's included (#3330), and the issue asked that this be ruled.

[[R503]] now reads: every catalog entry draws a distinct picture on each face, as before; a card with no scene
recipe ([[R3331000]]) keeps its picture byte for byte; and a card a set's picture pass gives a recipe draws
its scene, which no fixture pins. The three fixtures stay, as records of the old pictures: Core's v0.1
(`core-art-v0.1.json`), the Core and Training Grounds pictures before #2724 (`art-before-2724.json`,
[[R2724000]]) and Stinkyscape's as v0.5.0 shipped them (`stinkyscape-art-v0.5.0.json`, [[R2170000]]). Each
holds every card with no recipe to its recorded hash on both faces, and each card with one to a picture its
record does not hold. Stinkyscape's record is now held to the picture the face shows (`stinkyscapeArtUri`),
not to its painter's output, which a scene passes over; for a card with no recipe the two are the same bytes.
A Legacy card still freezes its Core card's old subject ([[R2724000]]).

Rejected: deleting the fixtures, which would let any card's picture drift unseen; re-recording them with the
proof cards' new hashes, which would pin pictures every set pass is meant to redraw; and lifting the pin for
the proof cards alone, which the owner's decision does not limit.

Amends [[R503]] (Core's pictures are a record, not a pin) and [[R2724000]] (a Core card with a scene draws it,
and no other picture is pinned beyond its record).
