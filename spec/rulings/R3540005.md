A Unit carries its Burn and Toxic in its view's `counters` (`burn`, `toxic`), a Unit standing in the backrow in its `BackrowCounters`, and a hero as `burn` and `toxic` beside its `armor`, each absent at none; both seats read them, as they read Plague Counters on the field. Neither ever rides a face-down card, which cannot carry them ([[R3540000]]). Their `counterChanged` and the `damage` of their ticks reach both seats unredacted, the hero's under `hero-<player>`. The client draws each as its own badge, ember for Burn and green for Toxic, beside a Unit's Plague Counters (`.counter-burn`, `.counter-toxic`) and on the hero's portrait (`.hero-burn`, `.hero-toxic`, shown above 0), titled "Burn counters" and "Toxic counters". The battle log says "{target} burn counters: {value}". Rejected: keyword chips, since neither is a keyword and a chip would read as text a Nerf could remove.
