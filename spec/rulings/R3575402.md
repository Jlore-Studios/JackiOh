Stinkyscape+ #157 Phantom Shipyard is a (4) 0/0 hull that lies Dormant until launched and whose text reads "Start of turn: Fuse a random Starship Piece into this": the engine runs that fuse itself, at the start of its controller's turn right after the Dormant countdowns (`yard_at_turn_start`, [[R3539004]]), not as a `startOfTurn` hook of the card's script, and a Dormant card's triggers are off in any case ([[R1839000]]). So [[R2477000]]'s turn-hook probes never saw it: the AI read the shipyard as [[R2380000]] reads any sleeper, the 0/0 it wakes as at the waiting share, which is nothing, and the one line of its text that pays every turn counted for nothing. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, among [[R2477000]]'s turn-hook probes and from the same nodes, each shipyard lying Dormant as a Starship on either field (`starshipYard` through its definition, Dormant and a hull) and each Unit in the seat's own hand whose definition is one ([[R923001]]'s spawn) is priced by one turn start: the engine's own yard step run on the sandbox copy, and its controller's evaluation after it less before, shared among that controller's shipyards, since one step fuses a piece into each of them. It rides in the card's `TurnHookPrice`, added to its start-of-turn and end-of-turn prices, so a card with both reads both; a shipyard's hooks are empty, so it reads the fuse alone.

`slumber_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.turn_hook` of it, outside the slumber share and after it: the probe's own evaluation already read the hull through that share, since it is a Dormant card on both sides of the subtraction, so discounting it again would charge the wait twice. The weight stays 0.5 and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. A piece fused into a Dormant hull is worth what [[R2380000]] says the hull is worth, so the price is a turn's growth and no more.

**What is not priced.** A shipyard that has launched or is no longer Dormant, whose fuse no longer runs. A step that leaves a prompt open or ends the game. The enemy's hand. A shipyard the nodes did not reach.

**Rejected.** Counting the fuse as an annuity over the turns the hull may still sleep, which no one-turn evaluation can weigh against the board it is played on, and which [[R2477000]] settled against for every other turn text; and making the fuse a `startOfTurn` hook of the card's script, which the engine's [[R3539004]] deliberately does not do, [[R1839000]] turning a Dormant card's triggers off.
