A hidden Nerf (`degrade_secretly`, reaching its cards as a Nerf does: one named card, a scope's cards or `random` different ones of them, [[R60]], each taking `times`) puts its Nerfs on a card in a hand or a deck as a count on the instance, `hiddenNerfs`, and does nothing else: no change is drawn and no event is emitted, so no seat learns that it happened, which card it reached or how many the card holds, the card's own owner included (Stinkyscape #123 Gokugains: "the opponent does not see the nerfs until they play the card"). A card anywhere else, and an Immutable card ([[R23]], [[R386]]), takes none; a random pick draws among the cards that can take them, so it never lands on nothing while another card could; counts add up. The card's cost, stats and text read as they did until the Nerfs land as [[R1845001]] says.

No view carries the count ([[§10.8]]), and the AI's redaction ([[R185]]) and the dry run's concealed copy ([[R222]]) drop it from every card, the seat's own hand's included, which neither makes a placeholder: so no decision of the AI's or of a scorer hangs on it, and `determinize` has none to sample ([[§9.9]]). The count rides the card in every zone and through a change of hands ([[R12]]); a copy or a Transform is a new card without it ([[R57]]).

Rejected: Nerfs applied at once and hidden from the owner, which needs a second tuning on every card and a card view that differs by seat for the owner's own hand; and a `degraded` event hidden from both seats, which still tells both that something was Nerfed, as [[R440]]'s count of cues does.
