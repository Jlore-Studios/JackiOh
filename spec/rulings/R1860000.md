Stinkyscape #38 Shield Amplifier's "Whenever this survives an attack, it gains +{buff}/+{buff}"
reads "survives an attack" as any combat it fights — as the attacker or the defender, declared or
forced — after which it is still on the field. The survival is read by two hooks: `afterAttack`,
after an attack it made, when its facts say `survived`, and `afterAttacked` ([[R1026]]), after an
attack made on it, when it still stands after that combat's state check (the absence of
`SELF_KEY` in the hook's data; present means the defender died and runs on its snapshot, [[R78]],
[[R89]]). A Cleave splash it survives is no attack on it, so it gains nothing. A Divine Shield
that took the hit counts: the card survives, so it gains. It takes R1860000, the first number of
issue #1860's block, and edits [[§6.2]]'s account of the two hooks.
