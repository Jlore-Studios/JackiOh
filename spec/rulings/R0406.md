C+ #7's "Right-House Protector" is #3 Right-house defender, the twin of C+ #6 Wrong-House Attacker, and ⅔ and ⅓ are the odds: its Cry and each start of its controller's turn summon a Right-house defender with chance 2 in 3, else a Wrong-House Attacker (the Radiant face summons both), generated from the catalog and summoned for its controller with no Cry; being cards and not tokens, they go to the graveyard when they die. Since issue #1704 ([[R1704002]]) its Cry places 3 Plague Counters on itself and its once-a-turn Activate spends one to summon the twin its controller names, Radiant on the Radiant face, the odds kept only for the replays of v0.4.0f and before ([[R767000]])
