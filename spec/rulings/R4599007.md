At a turn's end every Unit carrying Burn takes it as a sourceless hit, and its Burn is then removed whatever the hit did ([[R4154000]]). The fuzz monitor's I21 allowed the tick and a removal right after it ([[R4508005]]), but three shapes of the same tick were findings when a Spell's hook had run earlier at the turn's end. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 10075 the tick's echoes onto chained Units ([[R3554007]]) came between the tick and the removal; in seed 9644 the Unit was Indestructible, whose hit leaves no event at all ([[§4.4]] step 4); and in seed 4631 the tick took the Unit's Divine Shield, which leaves only `divineShieldLost`.
**What I21 allows.** A Burn's removal to 0 on an immune Unit that a tick hit earlier in the step, or that is Indestructible; and a `divineShieldLost` on it whose next event is its Burn's removal, which only the tick writes so. A shield lost with no removal after it, and a removal on a Unit no tick hit that is not Indestructible, are still reaches.
Rejected: allowing every Burn removal, which a Vanilla also writes after its own `transformed`, since the narrower reading keeps [[R4508005]]'s case of a removal with no tick a finding.
