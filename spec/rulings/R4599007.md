A turn ends in [[R62]]'s order: the end-of-turn hooks, the `turnEnded`, its trap window, the triggers it woke, then its tail with no marker of its own, the end-of-turn delayed effects, the Burn tick ([[R4154000]]), the Black Hole's pull and cleanup (Animated cards going home, [[R383]]). A Spell in hand whose trigger answers the turn's end, the Drone Cores' "Float reduces cost" and #29 GIGA Glowy Jelly Bean's among them, marks its `hookResolving` last, so I21 blamed the whole tail on it: a Burn tick's death, the Burn's removal, a shield the tick took, a delayed exile, an Animated card's return (fifteen of the daily fuzz's failing seeds on main, #4599).

**Its run ends with its own text.** From a `turnEnded` to the next turn's start, a `whenever` or end-of-turn hook of a Spell opens no run I21 reads; a cast there (a `cry`) still does, and so does a standing trigger before the turn's end. The trigger's own change, a cost or a transformation of itself, reaches no Unit on the field. [[R4508005]]'s allowances still hold everywhere else.
