Stinkyscape's two Legendary Quests, #7 The Greatest Heist and #112 Job Application, print their quests as stages: "Stage 1: Initiation. Quest: … Reward: …", then Stage 2 and Stage 3, Job Application's Interview drawing its quest at random among three. The engine already had a quest line, In Too Deep's tree ([[R404]]); a staged quest is the same line run through a list instead of a tree, as Hearthstone's Questlines run their three parts in order, the next shown once the last is done. No shipped card is staged, and the set's two scripts are its own (S09, S10).

- **The card.** A card declares its quest as `Script.stages` instead of `Script.quests`: each stage a name, its goals (one, or several to draw from) and its reward's text. The line runs on the book those compile to (`staged_book`), so every rule [[R404]] states of a quest holds of a stage's: it counts from the moment it opens, on either player's turn, and its completion is noticed at the state check after the event that completes it. The family is the **Quest** tag ([[R1837000]]), which "your Quests" reads ([[R1854001]]).
- **One stage at a time.** The first stage opens as the card enters the field. A stage of one goal opens it; a stage of several draws one from the match rng as it opens, one draw, and one goal makes no draw. The card's own trigger on its own `questCompleted` gives the stage's reward (`completed_stage_of` names the stage, 1 for the first): the reward is the card's effects ([[§6.3]], CLAUDE.md rule 5), with no prompt unless its text asks one, and its last effect moves the line on — `next_stage`, the stage after; `open_stage { n }`, any stage; or `hear_back` ([[R1854004]]). After the last stage's reward nothing is open and the line is done.
- **Opening a stage.** It closes the quests open on the line, unfinished, ends a wait to hear back, and forgets its own goals, so a stage opened again opens afresh (Job Application's "send in another application (return to Stage 1)"). It opens on the stay the effect's run began with, so nothing opens on a card that has left the field since ([[R174]]).
- **Leaving the field resets the line** ([[R78]]): played again, the card starts at Stage 1 (Job Application's "Death: Resummon this at Stage 1").
- **Seen by both players.** The card's view carries `quest.stages` beside its open quest, each stage with its name, whether it is done, open now or still to come, its goals (the one drawn once it has opened, every one it may draw before), its reward's text and, while the line waits to hear back with it, the turns left: the board's stage badge ("2/3") and the inspect overlay's list of stages read them, and nothing else. All of it is the card's own text and public counts, as [[R404]]'s line is.
- **No shipped game changes.** The line's new keys (`stage`, `hearBack`, `met`, `costs`) are absent while empty, so In Too Deep's `memory.quest` serializes and hashes as before, and a card that declares no stages has none.
