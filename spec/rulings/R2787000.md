Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. A card's entry in its set's effects table ([[R2784000]]) may carry, beside its hooks or alone, an `aura: { look, palette?, scope }`: one of eight looks (ember, frost, bloom, void, storm, holy, toxic and arcane, each a tint, a slow drift of motes and an edge glow), drawn in the card's flavor's palette unless the entry names a flavor or a sub-look (`"Tech/rocket"`, #2792), as a hook's palette may, over a scope, nearest first: the card's own `zone`, its `lane` (the lane's four zones, both seats), its `side` (that seat's ten zones) or the whole `board`. Only a Field Spell or a Field Trap stands in a zone, so only its entry may name `zone` or `lane`; any other card's aura covers a side or the board, and the table fails to load otherwise. The motif name `aura` on a hook still plays nothing: the standing look is the entry's `aura` field.

What wears one is read off the view the board is showing and nothing else ([[R202]]). Every Field Spell or Field Trap standing face-up in a backrow zone wears its entry's aura over its scope; a face-down card wears none on either seat, nor does the controller's own Field Trap that has not revealed (its view marks it `unrevealed`), nor a backrow zone whose top card is a Unit. A modifier the view lists ([[R169]]) wears the aura of the card whose readable play was resolving, from its `cardPlayed` to its `cardResolved` or `countered`, when its `modifierChanged` added it, on the side of the seat that holds it: none for a hidden play, for a play inside which a trap fired (the trap may be what added it), for a modifier no play added, and none for a Field card's own, which stands with the card. The view's modifier carries no source, and [[R169]] keeps it so, so the layer remembers the source of at most `AURA_SOURCES_LIMIT` modifiers from the newest view's events as they arrive, and forgets them all when the viewer changes; a modifier whose play left the event window before the layer saw it (a reload, a hand-over) wears none.

An aura begins and ends with the shown view, not with an entry, so it never paces the runner ([[R200]]): one the board starts to show fades in over `AURA_FADE_IN_MS` with a short burst of motes from its box's centre, one it stops showing fades out over `AURA_FADE_OUT_MS`, one that comes back while it fades begins again, and what the first view already shows stands without a begin. Where several cover one zone at most `AURA_MAX_PER_ZONE` show: those still holding before those fading out, then the nearest scope, then the newest, each kept only while every zone it covers holds fewer than that many already, and the nearest drawn on top. Every aura's motes share one canvas and one frame loop, which runs only while an aura shows and never while the page is hidden; each aura draws at most `AURA_MOTES_PER_AURA` times the effects intensity, and all of them together at most `AURA_PARTICLE_SHARE` of the particle cap. Under reduced motion, the media query or the viewer's setting, an aura is a still tint and edge with no motes and no fade, as the keyword visuals hold still ([[R438]]); with the effects intensity `off` there is none, and turning the effects on again begins none. No definition or name reaches the page. Ten Field Spells across the sets wear one as the pattern for the set passes.

Since [[R3402000]] a `{ bespoke }` entry may wear an aura too, and a Unit a `zone` one, which stands on its own unit zone while it stands there face up and readable (Silly Silas's); three signature looks join the eight.

Rejected: the newest aura first across scopes, which lets a side or board aura bury a card's own zone; drawing auras as the effects layer's particles, which [[R200]] binds to an entry and its tail; and a source on the view's modifier, which [[R169]] forbids and which would change the view and the replay hash.
