The client plays music on its own bus, under the master volume and mute. Every screen without a board plays the main menu theme. On a board, each viewer hears music driven by their own view ([[R203]]), by a priority stack: the result first (a victory, defeat or draw sting and its loop), then the station's low-health track (the viewer's hero at or under a quarter of HERO_HEALTH, rounded down), then the station's in-game track. While the opponent has the turn, the in-game track plays through a low-pass at a lower level; the mulligan is nobody's turn. A match opens on its station's sting into one of the station's in-game tracks, rotated per device from match to match, unless its first view is past the first turn (a reconnect). A station switch starts at the moment [[R204]] gives the card's cast line, and only for a card the viewer can read: a Trap's at its firing, never at its set. A card that switches its caster's station does so for the rest of the match, and the next match starts on the station the settings name. A hotseat hand-over plays the arriving seat's own music. A change waits for the playing track's next bar line (at most 3 s) and crossfades over 1.5 s. A track that opens on a sting comes in at once, and a station's tracks pick up within a match where they left off. With dynamic music off, only the station's in-game track plays. On top of the stack, a Legendary or Mythic card the viewer can read opens its play with an intro of its own, which ducks whatever the stack plays for its span ([[R1350]]–[[R1352]]); no card plays a theme of its own ([[R822000]]). When the page is hidden or the window loses focus the music fades out, and fades back in on return, unless the player turned on "Keep playing music when this tab is in the background" (off by default; effects and voice lines stay quiet in a hidden tab either way)
