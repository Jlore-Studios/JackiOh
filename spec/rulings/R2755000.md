A card back that only the top of the ladder wears: `card-back-jlorious`, the Jlorious card back, owned
by a person only once they have reached Jlorious ([[§9]], [[R608]]).

**What it is.** An account cosmetic of the card back kind, Mythic on the cosmetics' own scale
([[§14.2]]), marked `jlorious` in the registry. It is never a chase, whatever its rarity: the
registry's check refuses a Jlorious cosmetic marked as one, or one that is a default, a starter or a
per-card cosmetic, so [[R1333000]]'s chase mark is exactly the Legendary and Mythic cosmetics but this
one. It draws the logo's front card: on a card, the back's emblem gives way to the gold J and its
four-pointed star on a violet field, and breathes as a chase back's emblem moves ([[R1953001]]); on the
deck pile the J sits in the pile's look, darkened under its count as every back is.

**The one way to own it.** A rated game grants it, with the ledger reason `jlorious` and the game as
its reference, to each person the game leaves Jlorious: its own people who stand in Jlorious after it,
and every bystander whose Jlorious peak it writes ([[R607]]), in the transaction that writes the game.
A person who owns it already gets no second row. A ladder bot is never Jlorious ([[R640000]]) and so
never owns it. While the economy is off ([[§14.12]]) a game grants nothing. Migration 0074 grants it,
once, to every person whose season row holds a Jlorious peak, the ref `jlorious:0074`. Owned is kept:
falling out of Jlorious, or a new season, takes nothing back.

**Nothing else grants it.** No duplicate's global roll ([[§14.3]]) and no cosmetic pack ([[§14.6]])
drops it, no token buys it ([[§14.5]], as no token buys a Mythic), the Chase Shard bar refuses it and
the shop does not offer it there, a dev account's fill leaves it out ([[§14.10]], amending
[[R1300000]]), and a ladder bot's wardrobe never draws it however many people own it (amending
[[R1834005]]). [[R1833000]]'s global pool and the pack pool are the account cosmetics and the
cosmetics that are neither a default nor a starter, but this one. Practice, hotseat and the end-to-end
tests still treat every cosmetic as owned ([[R1310004]]), so it can be seen there.

Hearthstone semantics: a ranked season's top reward is a card back that only reaching the top earns.

Rejected: a chase it would be by its rarity, which the Chase Shard bar could buy; a grant at the
season's end alone, which a person who reaches Jlorious mid-season and falls out would miss; and taking
it back from a person who falls out, as a reward reached is a reward kept.
