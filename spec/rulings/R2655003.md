**The motion.** A Legendary full art ([[R2655000]]) moves: its art drifts slowly inside its crop,
scaled a little so no edge shows, and a light band sweeps across it. A Mythic full art does all of
that, and signs the card's name across the art: the name is written in a script face from left to
right, like a pen, and then held on the face. It is CSS over the face's own markup (an SVG text for
the signature), no new art file, and it runs wherever that copy prints its full art. It moves by
`transform` and `opacity` alone, so it paints nothing new each frame, and it takes no motion slot.
[[R2715001]]: the drift and the sweep still take none, and under them the art's own layers move,
each card's its own, which takes a moving foil's slot.

**Reduced motion.** Every animation of the two tiers sits inside
`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, as the chase cosmetics' do ([[R1953001]]), and
off under the app's Reduce Motion setting and with Animated foil off: then a Legendary shows a still
frame, the art at rest and the sweep still, and a Mythic shows its signature already whole.

**The signature.** [[R2715002]] makes it sign in under a second behind a pen tip, land with a burst
and a flourish and shine now and then, in its set's hand or its card's own, Dancing Script where a
set has none. It is set in Dancing Script, a script face under the SIL Open Font License 1.1,
self-hosted with the app's other faces (its woff2 and its licence in `apps/web/public/fonts/`, no
new CDN), as `--font-signature`, which falls back to the system's script faces. It signs the card's
**English** name, on a Chinese face too: the face has no Chinese glyphs, so a Chinese name would
fall back to a plain system face and read as print, not as a hand; and a signature keeps its
signer's name whatever language the card around it prints. It sits over the art between the crest
and the name banner, sized to the name's length, and overlaps no cost gem, rarity gem, crest, name
banner or Unit's numbers, on any card type ([[R1017001]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: a signature or diamond card's portrait moves, and the game's motion holds
still where the player asks for less of it.

Rejected: a pen stroke drawn along each glyph's outline (animating an SVG stroke's dash), which
repaints every frame and outlines the letters rather than inking them; one hand-drawn path per card,
fifteen and more files that drift from a renamed card; a Chinese script face, none of which is both
open-licensed and small enough to ship; and the signature's motion left on under reduced motion.
