S #149 Wildar reads "Add a random Call to Chaos to your opponent's hand. It costs (0). Then add a random Radiant Call to Chaos to your hand"; its Radiant face keeps only the second sentence (ST-S25-1).

- **The pool.** "A random Call to Chaos" is a pick of the cards tagged Call to Chaos from a pool that names no set, so it holds the editions of the sets that ship ([[R380]]): Core #95, C+ #73 and M #95 while the Stinkyscape set is held. S #150 and S #151 join it as their set ships ([[R1420]]).
- **Two picks.** Each is drawn from the match rng on its own ([[R60]]), so the two may be the same edition or different ones.
- **The opponent's.** A fresh card of the opponent's, owned by them, at `costOverride` 0, on its base face. Its edition is hidden from Wildar's caster, as every card added to a hand is ([[R97]]).
- **The caster's.** A fresh card on its Radiant face ([[§5.2]]) at its printed cost, (4).
- **A full hand** burns its card ([[§2.4]]); the other pick is made all the same.
