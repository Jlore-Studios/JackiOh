Amends [[R2564000]] and [[R2603000]], which price one use of the Activate of each card acting on a field at a decision's root, and price nothing while a prompt is open. #98 Heroic Power's Cry Discovers 3 of its powers, and the pick is the card's power for the game ([[R3134002]]): the one lasting choice on the card is made at the AI's own open prompt, where neither the root's probes nor [[R3575400]]'s hand probe ran, so every answer's line priced the power it gave at nothing and the search ranked the three answers by what this turn could do with them. With no mana left after the play, offered Armor Up (free), Die Insect and Witness Value, the AI kept Armor Up, the only power it could use at once, for the whole game; when none of the three could be used, all three scored alike and it took the first offered. And even a price would not have told two powers apart, since a price was keyed by the card's definition, face and controller alone, never the power it holds (`memory.power`, [[R43]]), which is what gates each of its abilities ([[R752]]). No game rule moves: as in Hearthstone, a choice that lasts the game is judged by what it keeps giving.

**What it counts.** At a decision whose root is the seat's own open prompt, in the seat's own main phase, whose card (the prompt's `resume.instance_id`) is one of the seat's cards the probes try ([[R2564000]]'s and [[R2603000]]'s `activate_sources`) and whose face declares an Activate, each answer `legal_actions` lists is played once on a copy of the probe world ([[R185]], [[R4070000]]), in `action_key` order, with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]). Where the answer leaves the seat's own main phase with no prompt open and that card holding a power it did not hold before, the seat ends its turn and then the enemy, and at the start of the seat's next turn the card's uses alone are tried and priced exactly as [[R3575400]] prices a hand card. The price is added to the decision's activation prices under the card as the answer leaves it, so a line that holds the card with that power reads it.

**The key.** An activation price is keyed by the card's definition, its face, its controller and the power it holds (`memory.power`; `None` for every card but Heroic Power, which is keyed as before). So two lines that hold one Heroic Power with two different powers read two different prices, and a power the root priced is never read for another.

**Caps.** The probe spends at most `AI_PROMPT_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's own nodes, a slice of its own since the root's probes price nothing while a prompt is open: 24, one answer, two turn ends and `AI_REPLY.visible_activations` uses for each of the three answers. `AI_DECISION_PRICE_NODES` adds it. Nothing is priced and no node spent at a zero `activation` weight, once the game is over, outside the seat's own main phase or at any other prompt. The weight stays `AI_EVAL.activation`, 0.5, and the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0.

**What is not priced.** Any other prompt: a target, a Discover of a card to hand, a prompt the enemy holds. An answer that fails, leaves a prompt open, leaves the card's power as it was (the Discover a power's own use opens), or that the nodes do not reach. A power whose use opens a Discover (Witness Value, Stitching, Terminus Tricks, End Times), whose use leaves a prompt open as [[R2564000]] holds. A Heroic Power still in hand, whose power is not offered yet. Steady Shot's growth and End Times' countdown past one use.

**Rejected.** Simulating every answer at every own prompt, as the issue first proposed, which would spend up to the cap at every Discover and target prompt for nothing and shrink those decisions' budgets; scanning every field card for a new power, since a card a pick summons rolls its power at random ([[R3134002]]) and the probe's copy would roll otherwise than the line; keying on the power's table entry rather than its stored name, which would scan the power table at every lookup; pricing the use this turn rather than at the seat's next, which is exactly the reading that kept the weak power; and raising `AI_EVAL.activation`, which would move every Activate's price to fit one card.
