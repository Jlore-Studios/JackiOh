A Dormant card that awakens ([[R1839002]]: its countdown's end at its controller's turn start, [[R1839001]], the Awaken verb, its own Activate, or a Dormant Starship's launch, [[R1852003]]) is summoning sick for the rest of that turn, as a card that entered the field that turn is ([[§4.1]]): its `summonedTurn` becomes the turn it awakened in. So it declares no attack that turn unless Rush or Charge lets a sick Unit attack, may switch position as any sick Unit may, and attacks from its controller's next turn on. `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal and the view's `canAct` read the one sickness check, so they agree. [[R1839002]] had an awakened card not summoning sick, as Hearthstone's awakened minions are not; the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2259) makes it sick, so a Starship launched for (3) does not swing the turn it launches.
