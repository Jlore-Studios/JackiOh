A Smoothie filled with two random Fruits fuses them into itself as it arrives, as one fusion with `count` picks drawn in turn — the picks first, the kept card last in the ingredients; the kept card is excluded from its own pool, and with `unfusedOnly` a card already a fusion is left alone, so a filled Smoothie is never filled again. Rejected: refilling on every arrival, which would pile Fusions onto a card that already is one.
