Quest 5 ("your cards deal 15 damage to enemies", 12 through v0.4.0f, issue #1704) counts a hit whose source its controller controlled and whose target was the opponent's hero or a Unit the opponent controlled, both read as the hit landed. Quest 2 ("2 enemy permanents destroyed") counts a permanent the opponent controlled as it died, by anything, a stolen card included: the `destroyed` event names the controller a card died under ([[R172]])
