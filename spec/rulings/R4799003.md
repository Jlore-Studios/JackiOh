[[R3575404]] counts a Dormant card's awakening Activate in full: the price was read from the sleeping card, so it already holds the wait, and `slumber_worth`, which counts the unit the card wakes as at the slumber share ([[R2380000]]), puts back the rest of it, on the ground that the awake copy's `unit_worth` counts the Activate inside the share. That holds for a shipyard, whose `wakesBy` ability is printed and stays on its awake copy. It does not for a Dormant Starship whose pieces print only keywords (S #100 AP Rounds): its one ability is the engine's launch ([[R1852003]]), which it has only while it slumbers, so its awake copy prices no Activate and the sleeper counted `1 − share` of its launch, not all of it.

**The rule.** The AI decides this; no game changes. `slumber_worth` puts back what the awake copy's own Activate does not count of the sleeper's: the sleeper's price less the slumber share of the awake copy's. A shipyard's reads exactly as before (both prices are one); a launch-only Starship's launch now counts in full.

**Measured.** On origin/main 5991e8da39 with issue #4798's commits, crates/tools' `r4583000_level_20_is_clearly_hard` reads level 20 won 1 of 6 fights without this read, the 1 its band allows, and 2 of 6 with it: the fight of 2026-11-27 flips, first diverging at the player's seat (p1's 40th action); the fight of 2026-11-24 diverges there too and is lost either way. So it waited for one re-test on the final main, the band moving only with a deliberate retune. On issue #4787's final main, after #5081's Assault and #4792, #4793, #4791 and #4797 (`5ed9f7fd55`), every band holds with it: `r4583000_level_20_is_clearly_hard` passes. Its fork screen on improve-dev (training/improve.md, A screen), against the control at `5ed9f7fd55`, played 200 trunk games, every one clean, with 39 forks, 17 live, forkEdge +0.033 (-0.031 to +0.098): inconclusive ([[R4319000]]), so the rule rests on its scene test.
