Since issue #1828 the token Core #51.1 KY's Empty Notebook shuffles `cards` random KY cards into its caster's deck, 3 on both faces and Radiant ones on the Radiant face, where it drew. The pool is the KY cards of every set that ships ([[R380]]) and none of the KY tokens, since a pool takes tokens only through the Fruit, Prime or AI tag or by saying so ([[§5.1]]): the Notebook itself, C+ #42.1 KY's Gift, M #45.1 Knowledge Breaker Prime and M+ #20.1 Lil' Kenny never come. Today that is fourteen cards: Core #31, #51, #57 and #82, C+ #41, #42 and #62, M #34, #38, #45, #79 and #87, and M+ #20 and #25.

Each pick is its own draw from the match rng, repeats allowed ([[R60]]), and each card goes in at a uniformly random place, as [[§6.3]] Shuffle into puts it; a full deck turns the rest away ([[R80]]). A card generated into a deck may be Glitch ([[R673]]), as with any such effect. Hearthstone shuffles random cards into a deck the same way.

Through v0.4.9 it drew 1 card, 2 on the Radiant face; `script_v0_4_9` keeps that for those versions' replays ([[R767000]]).
