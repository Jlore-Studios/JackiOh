title: Bounded fused ids

proven_in:

A fused id spells out its ingredients only while that list is at most `FUSED_ID_CAP` (120) characters. Past that it is `t-<n>:#<16 hex>`, a pure 64-bit digest of the list, with no crypto. Every fused def keeps `ingredients: {defId, radiant?}[]`. A digest's scripts are rebuilt from it on entry to reduce, legalActions and viewFor. Self-exclusion from pools ([[R387]]) still covers every ingredient.
