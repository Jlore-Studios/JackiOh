[[R403]] makes a face-down Trap or Field Trap with no reveal condition live, so a hidden card can change what both players see: C #88 Siphon Squad's aura shrinks the enemy's Attack while it stays face-down. [[R185]]'s redaction turns the card into a placeholder, and without more the AI's determinizations would hand its units their Attack back and it would declare attacks the engine refuses. So the redaction keeps every unit's Attack and Health as the board shows them (the difference the placeholder made goes on the unit's buffs), while the card itself stays hidden. The AI learns nothing a player does not read off the board, and a determinization agrees with the board it was dealt from: a hidden backrow card is never sampled as a Trap or Field Trap whose live aura would change a unit's shown Attack, Health or keywords
