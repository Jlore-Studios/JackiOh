Issue #814 asked that every keyword and term bold the same way wherever a card prints it. A word bolds when it is a [[§6.2]] trigger, a [[§6.3]] verb or Radiant used as that term, in every case and inflection the cards print: "exile it", "exiles", "exiled", "steal it", "fuse", "fused", "transform", "transforms", "Locked", "Tributes", "Buffs", "Nerfs", "bounced", "redirected", "activate" and "marked" bold as Exile, Steal, Fuse, Transform, Lock, Tribute, Buff, Nerf, Bounce, Redirect, Activate and Mark in a hand. Each such spelling is an alias of its glossary entry, so matching stays exact and case-sensitive, and [[R512]]'s plain lower-case "steal it" now bolds. A word spelled like a term that names something else stays plain: the exile pile ("your exile", "their exile", "from exile", "graveyard and exile", "graveyard or exile", and Lizard's Breath's "Exile," among its Deck and Graveyard), as the other piles do; the glossary lists these spellings. Corpse Plantation's "counter", one of its Plague Counters, was one too until patch v0.3.10 (#831) gave it a text that names them in full. A [[§6.1]] keyword bolds as the keyword a card has, so a verb spelled like one ("you stack onto this") stays plain. Card types, zones, stats, positions, rarities and the [[§6.3]] rows the glossary keeps rules-only (Summon, Draw, Discard, Destroy, Damage, Heal, Add to hand, Shuffle into and the like) stay plain, as Hearthstone prints them. A Chinese face bolds nothing, as before, since the tokenizer reads the English spellings, and its glossary lists the terms its English text names ([[R1301]]). A card that prints a trigger or verb that neither bolds nor is a listed plain spelling fails the web's tests. Presentation only: no card's text, and no rule, changes.
