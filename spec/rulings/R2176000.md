[[§8.10]] S #22 Gatinsir "summon[s] two Warp Disruptors, then two Phalanxes", each named. [[§6.2]] Cry and [[R411]] say a summon that names the card it summons fires that card's Cry, an exception to [[R1]]'s "copies, Recruit, Reborn and tokens do not", and nothing in the trap's text sets that exception aside. So each S #82 Phalanx Gatinsir summons runs its Cry ("Discover 2 Rare Weapons and Equip them into this", Radiant Epic) for Gatinsir's controller, in the order the Phalanxes arrived, after every summon of the ambush and before the combat, which waits on its Discovers ([[§4.2]] step 4, [[R1875002]]); a Phalanx that has left the field by then runs nothing. A Warp Disruptor (S #23) and S #174 Aerelret's Paragon Mk1s (S #72) print no Cry, so nothing changes for them. The other summons by name in [[§8.10]] that reach a card with a Cry already fire it as [[R411]] reads: S #6's Radiant face summons S #7, and the FO Anomaly board lays S #82 and S #83, its laid Cries fired as [[R1855001]] says. S #17's Fiery Waraxe, S #18's Brains, S #36's Layers and S #158's Startos print no Cry, and the rest of the set's "no Cry" summons name no card (a pool, a copy, the card a Sack or a Ticketed holds, a graveyard's). Rejected: stating an exception to [[R411]] for the trap, which the designer's text gives no reason for, and which would leave the Phalanx's only printed text dead on its way in.
