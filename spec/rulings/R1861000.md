A card whose face sets `handSale` ("Can be sold from your hand at the Marketplace, like a Resource", S #89 Spice) sells from its owner's hand as a tray Resource sells ([[R1853003]]): while the Marketplace is open, in its owner's main phase with no prompt open, a direct `marketSell` naming it, listed by `legal_actions` after the tray's Resources in hand order and shown in its owner's view alone, after them, at this turn's quote. The quote is drawn each turn as a Resource's is ([[R1853002]]), after every Resource's, so theirs draw as they did: each registered card whose base face sets `handSale`, in sorted order, its base face then its Radiant face. "Its price is determined like all other Resources" prints no range, so it takes a Rare Resource's (Gellium's), 3 to 10 Credits (`HAND_SALE_PRICE`), Radiant 6 to 20 (`HAND_SALE_RADIANT_PRICE`) (⚠ designer). The sale is public (`marketSold`), the card ceases to exist rather than reaching a graveyard ([[R1840004]]'s reading of a sold Resource) and its Credits are gained ([[R1840005]]); a sale on offer keeps the turn from ending as a tray sale does ([[R1853004]]). A closed Marketplace, the opponent's turn or a hand card that sets no `handSale` sells nothing, refused as a tray sale is. The card is no Resource: no tray takes it and no Resource roll draws it.
