Issue #4154 gave M #10 Double Counting "Activate: Pay ({price}) to Discover a Cry or Death card." (Radiant: "Activate: Discover a Cry or Death card."), once a turn ([[R384]]); `price` is the base face's declared number, 1 ([[R386]]). The pool is the non-token cards of the sets that ship whose base face's script has a Death, any card, or a Cry on a Unit or a Field Spell: a Spell's resolution runs on the same hook and is no Cry ([[R822]]). It is built as the Discover applies and never offers Double Counting itself ([[R387]]); the pick reaches the hand on its base face. Rejected: reading "Cry" and "Death" off the card text (a card naming another's Death would count). Through v0.6.6e it had no Activate (`script_v0_6_6e`, [[R767000]]).
