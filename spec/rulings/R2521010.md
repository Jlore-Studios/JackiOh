[[R732000]] keeps every card of a set older than Meditative out of `SHADOW_BAN`, and the unban lane's `SHADOW_UNBANNED` lifted only Meditative cards because only they had been banned. Since [[R1186000]], [[R1885000]] and [[R2203000]] the ban also takes Meditative+, Stinkyscape and Training Grounds cards (`BAN_SETS`), so `SHADOW_UNBANNED` may name a card of any of those four sets, and of no other: `sweep --write` still refuses an id no report of record bans ([[R186]]). Issue #2521 lifts S #149 Wildar and TG #25 Trigor, the two `neverPlayed` rows of #2358 and #2397, so `SHADOW_BAN` is empty and the AI deals itself every card of every set (#2452). A later sweep of record that bans either again drops it from `SHADOW_UNBANNED`, as any new sweep of a set does. Hearthstone has no counterpart.
