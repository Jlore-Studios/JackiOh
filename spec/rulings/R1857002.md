title: Which cards name the Stinkyscape set's tokens (R279, R480)
cards: "§7, R244, R279, R480, R1840002, Stinkyscape #4, #137, #154, its Resources"
proven_in:
  - crates/cards/tests/cross/references.rs

Every token is named by some card ([[R279]]). A Stinkyscape Terminus token (#4.1 to #4.6) is named by "Terminus card" (#4 Olivian Expanse discovers one, #154 Dice of Doom adds one), and a War Doctrine (#137.1 to #137.4) by "War Doctrine" (#137): they are pools a card names by their tag, as [[R480]]'s Pancake and AI pools are, so a text naming the pool names each of its tokens. A Resource is dealt by a rule, [[R1840002]]'s roll, whichever card or rule asks for one, so it needs no card to name it, as [[R244]]'s Coin needs none. Every other Stinkyscape token is named by its maker's text.
