Before the reaper writes [[R112]]'s ceiling draw for a `live` match past its ceiling, it folds the match's `(seed, log)` once ([[§9.5]]), as a rebuilt actor does, and reads the result off the folded state. A log that ends in a result is a game the actor finished but whose result write never landed ([[R1437]]), with no socket left to rebuild the actor and record it; the reaper records that result as the actor would have: the real winner and reason, the real turn count, each seat's last board, the final state's hash and the rating move, through the same write, with the series' record and the game record ([[§9.11]]) that follow it, and the seats credited by who plays each one as the log ends ([[R677]]). A log that does not end is the stalled match [[R112]] describes and still ends as a draw by `match-ceiling` with `turns = 0` and both ratings unchanged. So does a log that ends in a Glitch's void, which records nothing and is not the reaper's to record ([[R679]]), a log whose fold refuses an action it holds or panics (the log does not reconstruct the game, [[§9.3]], and no permanent result is written from it), a log the store fails to read, and a recovery whose write fails. Only a match past its ceiling is folded, and no actor, socket, clock or rematch plan is made for it. Rejected: rebuilding the actor to record and stop it, which arms sockets and alarms for a match being closed; routing a void into [[R679]]'s forgetting, which is a wider change that needs its own decision.
