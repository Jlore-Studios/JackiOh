**The change.** [[R1994000]] took the first seat's share and its 95% Wilson interval over every game, so a draw counted as a loss for the first seat: with even seats and 4% draws the figure read 48%, and a maintainer judging whether going first needs evening out saw a first player weaker than they are. `game_figures` now takes `firstShare`, `firstLow` and `firstHigh` over the decided games, `games - draws`. `games`, `draws`, `firstWins` and the stored totals stay as they are, so no migration is needed, and `GET /api/stats/games`, `windowed_game_figures`, `game_figures_of` and `cargo jackioh stats report --games` still read the one computation. A share read before this change rises by about the draw share.

**Every game drawn.** The share is unknown, not zero: `firstShare` is null and the interval is [0, 1], which is what `wilson_interval` gives for no games. The [[§9.11]] panel reads "First player wins: unknown, no game was won" and the report reads "first seat: unknown, no game was won".

**What the panel shows.** "First player wins X% of decided games (±m)", and no draw figure, as the owner asked on issue #2567, so `GameFigures` has no draw-share field. The report keeps its existing count of drawn games, and notebook 02's `first_win_rate` is taken over the same decided games.

Rejected: a `drawShare` field and a "Y% drawn" line, which the owner declined; counting a draw as half a win for each seat, which changes what a share of wins means; showing 0% when every game was drawn, which would read as the first seat never winning; and nulling `firstLow` and `firstHigh` as well, which would give the panel's margin and the report's interval a second shape for no game.

Amends [[R1994000]].
