[[R640001]]'s disclosure was one line of the Terms, "When no suitable opponent is available, ranked may pair you with an AI opponent.", which said that a bot may come and nothing of what one is. The ladder bots stay as they are, and nothing a client reads labels one or tells its seat from a person's ([[R1452]]); what changes is what the Terms say about them.

**Hearthstone semantics.** Hearthstone's patch 24.4 said in its patch notes which players may be matched against AI opponents, and why, and marked no seat in the match. Fortnite and Apex Legends say in public text that they use bots and mark no seat, and Marvel Snap marks none either. JackiOh's bots reach every rank and stand on its leaderboard, which Hearthstone's do not, so the Terms say that as well.

**What the Terms say.** Their "Ranked play" section becomes "AI opponents", which says:

- when: if no one near the player's rating is free after a short wait, ranked may pair them with an AI opponent the server plays, so they still get a game, at any rank and in every queue mode, Best of 1, All Random and Conquest ([[R1453]], [[R641000]], [[R1555000]]);
- what it looks like: a username, a portrait, cosmetics, a rank and a place on the leaderboard, as a player has, though never in Jlorious ([[R1454]], [[R1834005]], [[R640000]]);
- what it does: it plays at a person's pace, and may emote, concede, answer a draw offer, let a turn run out or offer a rematch ([[R1452]], [[R639000]]);
- that nothing in the game marks which opponents are AI ([[R1452]]);
- that it sees only what a player in its seat could see ([[R185]]), and that a game against one counts toward rank and rating like any other ranked game ([[R610]]);
- that a challenge sent to a friend, or accepted from one, is always played against that friend, since only the queue's fallback, a rematch of a bot's own game and the grinders' kickoff seat a bot ([[R1452]]).

The section names no seat and no match, so it still labels no bot (CLAUDE.md rule 7). It reads no number from the configuration: the fallback's 15 to 40 seconds are "a short wait" and `MAX_FAIR_GAP` is "near your rating", so tuning either changes none of its words. A change to what a bot is, wears or does that the section names changes the section too, and with it `TERMS_VERSION` ([[R757001]], [[R970001]]), so every account is asked to agree again.

Rejected: Hearthstone's own scope, bots only for new and low-rated players, with names that read as a character's, no leaderboard row and no friend request or report, which would leave the higher ratings with long waits while JackiOh's players are few; and marking a bot's seat in the match, which none of those games does and which would undo [[R1452]].
