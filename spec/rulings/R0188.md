The AI never concedes and never offers a draw (the action types [[R84]] skips), and it declines every draw offer at once: when the human offers, the AI answers `answerDraw` with `accept: false`, which blocks the human's next offers as [[R36]] says. An AI that let offers stand would leave the human with an unanswered offer and no way to tell a refusal from a hang. Ladder bots are the exception ([[R639000]]): this ruling holds for `decide` itself and for the practice AI, but a ladder bot's seat concedes and answers draw offers as a person does, and still never offers a draw
