The designer: "Starship Pieces Keyword does not transfer when equipped, i.e., if you equip a starship piece onto a non-starship piece the result is not a starship piece." An Equip is a Fuse onto its host ([[R1086]]), and [[R77]] unites the ingredients' keywords, so the rule is the Fuse's: **a fusion that keeps a card prints Starship Piece only when the kept card's own face does**, and keeps a granted Starship Piece only when the kept card had it granted itself. The fused face drops the keyword otherwise (`kept_piece_only`), and the kept instance's united grants drop it (`keep_instance`).

So a piece equipped onto a Unit that is no piece leaves it no piece, and its death fuses nothing; a Unit equipped onto a piece keeps the piece a piece; and the Dormant Starship, the card every piece's copy fuses into, is never made a piece ([[R1852000]]), or it would build a new ship of itself as it died. One rule serves every fusion that keeps a card, the Equip that the designer named among them ([[R470]]'s hand and deck cards and #85's kept permanent too); a fusion that keeps none (Craft a Card) unites the keyword as it unites every other.
