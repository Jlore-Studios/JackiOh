The AI's mulligan sent back every card costing more than `AI_MULLIGAN.keepMaxCost`, 3: a human's
turn-3 play. Under [[R181]] a seat's crystals are its turns started plus its `manaBonus`, up to its
`manaCap`, so Medium's and Hard's third turn holds 4, and a dungeon run's enemy can hold more
([[R2922001]]), yet their mulligans redrew those four-drops as if they had a human's mana. Following
Hearthstone, a seat keeps what its own mana plays early.

The bound is `keepMaxCost` plus the seat's `manaBonus`, never above its `manaCap` (no turn's mana
passes it) and never below 0, read from the handicap the seat holds ([[R180]]), never from its tier,
so any starting mana moves it:

- The shaped exception, the one best card one dearer than the bound whose shaped weight reaches
  `DEAL_MULLIGAN_KEEP` and that the cap lets the seat pay, moves up with the bound.
- `AI_MULLIGAN_CURVE.firstTurnMaxCost` is not raised; the check for an early play already counted
  the bonus and The Coin ([[R244]]).
- The greedy baseline's frozen `GREEDY_MULLIGAN` reads no bonus.
- A seat with no bonus (Easy, a human's resources, the tutorial's opponent, [[R290]]) keeps exactly
  what it kept before.

Hard keeps more four-drops, so the daily fixture smoke of hard-vs-easy, whose first 4 games had read
3 wins of the 3 needed, read 2 (games 2 and 3 lost) and would have blocked production on a sample
of four. It now plays the CLI smoke's `AI_GATE.smoke_seeds`, 20 games held to the same
`gateNeeded`, and passes; this build's `cargo jackioh gate` smoke read hard-vs-easy 20 wins of 20
(16 needed). Rejected: raising the bound in `decide`, which every caller of
`mulligan_keep_curved` would have to remember, and raising `firstTurnMaxCost` with the bonus.
