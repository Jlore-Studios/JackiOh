M #5 Death by 1000 cuts reads "Echo X, Pierce / Deal {damage} damage to a Unit. X is {echo} times your mana left after paying for this." (Radiant: Lifesteal, `echo` 4 against 2). Through v0.5.11o its X was `echo` times the caster's max mana ([[R802]]; 9 hits at 4 max mana, 17 on the Radiant face).

- **Mana left, read once.** `Script.echo_x` returns `echo` times `unspent_mana_of(controller)`, the mana its player holds unspent. It is asked at [[§10.5]] step 4, where the repeats are queued, after step 2 has paid the card's own price: cast with exactly 1 mana, Death by 1000 cuts leaves 0, so X is 0 and it cuts once. X stays fixed as [[R802]] fixes it: mana gained or spent while the card resolves changes nothing, since nothing reads it again.
- **A cast pays nothing.** A cast ([[R70]]) is free, so the mana left is the caster's mana as it stands: a random cast of it reads its caster's current mana, not their max.
- **Echo copying it.** An Echo that has its text ([[R399]]) is paid for first: the copied face's X is `echo` times the mana left after Echo's own (1) ([[R546]]).
- **The rest as before.** The larger of X and a fixed Echo holds, and the card's Echo tuning step and a Twinspell grant add on ([[R30]], [[R386]]). Its repeats keep their target ([[R3048002]]).
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under `script_v0_5_11o`, X from the max mana ([[R767000]]).
