A play gate ([[R1851000]]) is read where [[R703]]'s needed pick is read, and nowhere else. A cast is never refused ([[R70]]), so a cast of a card whose gate does not hold resolves as it would have, acting on what there is; an Echo repeat and a random cast ([[R452]]) skip the gate as they skip the pick, so a repeat resolves even when the first resolution took away what the gate asked for. A card set face-down by its Cloaked declares nothing as it is set, so it may be set with its gate shut; its Reveal is offered, and accepted, only while it can come out whole ([[R783006]]), which for a Spell is what playing it needs: a legal pick for each declaration it could not go without, and its gate, read as the reveal reads them. So a reveal never resolves with the gate shut ([[R1044]]).
