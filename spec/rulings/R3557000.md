S+ #20 B-Patch reads "Randomly either buff or nerf (50/50) every card on the field and in both hands", and the spec does not say whether the 50/50 is one roll or one per card. It is one roll for the whole effect: the Cry flips one coin, and every card of either side on the field and in both hands is then Buffed once, or every one Nerfed once, never a mix. Heads is the Buff, so the Radiant face's printed Lucky 1 is a second flip that keeps heads, as every coin's Lucky does ([[R1440]]): it Buffs about three times in four. A card that cannot be Buffed or Nerfed is skipped by the verb, and the hand and deck cards' changes stay silent to the other seat ([[R440]]).

Rejected: a coin per card, which would leave the board a mix and make the card's "50/50" a count of heads; and tails as the Lucky side.
