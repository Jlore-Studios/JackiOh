A card the viewer controls whose view lists `activations` wears one Activate control per ability on the card itself, since a click on the card stays its attack or its pick as a target: a lightning glyph and a badge with the uses left this turn ("∞" for Activate ♾️), and the ability's words, its numbers filled from the card's `params`, as its tooltip and accessible name. A control is live exactly when `legalActions` lists that card's `activate`; otherwise it is greyed, its tooltip gives the view's `reason`, and it sends nothing. A press builds the activation as a play's choices are built ([[R81]]): one listed activation is sent at once, and several wait for a target clicked on the board or dragged to from the control (or from a card of yours that has nothing to attack and one ability), a Tribute, or a mode in the inline picker. Heroic Power is built the same way from the hero panel, whichever of `activatePower` or `activate` is listed, and each power a player controls is its own control. The control flares while the `activated` row plays on its card, and under reduced motion shows a still ring instead. Presentation and intent only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
