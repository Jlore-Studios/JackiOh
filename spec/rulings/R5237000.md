`effects::delay::watch_summons { step, label, hook, data, unitsOnly }` gives its controller a `summonWatch` player modifier, expiry `never`, a badge in the card's own words on both seats ([[R169]]). For the rest of the game, after each card of that player's that enters one of their zones, it re-enters the card's `step` (under `hook`, the card's `resume` table by default) as that player, with the entering card's `instanceId` and the `watcher` in its data, in the order the watches were made. It answers the events Showdown's lane watch answers ([[R946]]) in every lane: a permanent's `cardResolved` on its own side, so a played card answers once, as its play resolves, and a countered play never; or a `summoned` that no play made (a token, a recruit, a Reborn body). With `unitsOnly` (FvH #4 KY's Unification Mandate's "whenever you summon a Unit") only a card whose type is Unit answers; absent, every card that enters, a Field Spell included. "Later" is from the moment the watch is made: what entered before it is not answered, while a permanent whose own Cry makes the watch is answered as its play resolves, since its `cardResolved` comes after the Cry. The watch outlasts the card that made it and runs with no `self` ([[R127]]), as Great Paperclip Theory's play watch does ([[R1863000]]). Several stack, each answering once.
