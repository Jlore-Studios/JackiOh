At the end of its controller's turn ([[R62]]) each Rush Token it summons ([[R64]]) makes one forced attack ([[R53]]) on a random enemy it may attack, the hero included, drawn as that attack begins, so two may pick the same one. "Each" is the tokens this end of turn summoned: a token with no zone to enter is not summoned and attacks nothing ([[R129]], [[§3.2]]), and a token an earlier end of turn summoned, still on the field, never attacks again by it, so the card clears each remembered token ([[R1126]]) before that token's summon.
