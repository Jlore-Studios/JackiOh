The Stinkyscape set (`SetName::Stinkyscape`, written "Stinkyscape") is the third set [[R1420]]'s gate holds, after Meditative and Meditative+ ([[R1178000]]): `CATALOG_SETS` orders it sixth, right after Meditative+, and `SHIPPED_SETS` does not list it until its release (issue #1884). Until then everything [[R1420]] says of a held set holds of it, unchanged, as [[R1178000]] said of Meditative+: no pool that names no set reaches it ([[R380]]), whatever tags it asks for, an AI pool's AI generated cards ([[R1421]]) and its own four tags included; a pool that names the set (`set: "Stinkyscape"`) or its cards by id does, so its cards find their own tokens and pools; no deck holds one ([[§9.4]]'s L3, "the Stinkyscape set has not shipped yet"); no lean names it and it is not the newest set ([[R1371]]); the Almanac and the deck builder show no card, chip or tag of it; no patch snapshot holds its entries and no fragment claims them ([[R646]]); the server neither seeds nor serves it ([[§9.11]]); no voice file is owed for its lines; and under the testkit `preview_sets(&[SetName::Stinkyscape])` opens it on one thread, as the fuzz tool's `preview_every_set` does on every seed. The golden traces deal from the shipped sets alone, so no card of it moves one.

Its entries are `stinkyscape-NNN` for card N (three digits), `stinkyscape-NNN-k` for the token N.k, and `stinkyscape-t-<name>` for an entry outside the numbers (the Dormant Starship, the shared tokens, and `stinkyscape-t-ai-01` to `-10` for the AI generated cards T-AI-1 to T-AI-10), and its scripts live in `crates/cards/src/scripts/stinkyscape/` (the sixth set folder, after `meditative_plus/`). `catalog check` and the cards crate's `STINKYSCAPE` fixture hold it to its shape alone while it is built ([[R1420]]): 175 cards, the Dormant Starship (a card at a `T-` index, `shared_cards`, [[R1857001]]) and 52 tokens, 42 Common, 50 Rare, 43 Epic, 29 Legendary and 12 Mythic at most over its 176 cards, every entry equal to its row and a row with no entry a card still being built; none counts toward the totals or the tag census until the release, when it is held to every count. While it is held, the census tests that pin a list of cards read the sets that ship (the Activate census, the params' base-only and Radiant-only lists, the AI and Pancake pools' makers) or the held set's entries that have landed (`landed()`: the Jlockeed, Catalyst, Prime, Acclaimed, Terminus, Quest, Otherworldly and Other-Origins pins, and R349's fallbacks); the release re-pins them.

The set adds four tags ([[§5]]): **Drone Core** on its #27 to #36, **Weapon** on #50 to #61 and #140 to #143, **War Doctrine** on the four tokens #137 makes (#137.1 to #137.4), and **Swarm** on its two Swarm Units, #91 and #92. They are family tags and name no tribe ([[R1424]]), no pool by one of them takes tokens ([[R382]]), and no rule reads them. They are in the engine's `Tag` union from now on, after Jelly Bean and before Token, but the server's `cards_tags_check` admits them only with the release's migration, as [[R1411]]'s did Wincon and [[R1178000]]'s Potion: until the set ships, the check lists the union less a tag only a held set's entries carry (`AWAITING_RELEASE`), and once it ships the check must list them.
