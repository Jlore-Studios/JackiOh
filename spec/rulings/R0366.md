Patch v0.1.1 asked for card text that is short, neatly formatted and consistent. A card's printed text calls the library the deck and a tribute a Tribute; writes a specific cost as the noun "(N) Cost" ("(4)+ Cost cards", "a (1) Cost or less card", "Discover a (2) Cost card") and a price as the verb "costs (N)" ("costs (1) less", "costs (0)"), never "Cost (N)", "N-cost" or "costing N", since patch v0.2.0 ([[R432]]); writes an embiggen card's bigger price as "Paid (4): …"; prints its keywords first, as one comma-separated list on a line of its own; starts each labelled ability ("Cry:", "Death:", "Start of turn:", "End of turn:", "Aura:", "Paid (4):", and since patch v0.2.0 the Activate labels "Activate:", "Activate 2:" and "Activate ♾️:", [[R384]]) on a line of its own, as the designer's own texts do; and writes every other line as sentences that start with a capital letter and end with a full stop. Patch v0.2.1 (issue #45) standardises terminology across all sets and retires variants via the vocabulary table: Deck (retires "library"), Tribute (retires "sacrifice"), Bounce (retires "Return … to hand" for a permanent returned from the field since balance patch 1, [[R692]], while a card returned from the graveyard or exile, or a Spell after it resolves, keeps "Return … to hand", [[R746]]; Bounce names no seat — it always reaches the controller's hand ([[R747]]) — while Add keeps "Add … to your hand"), labelled turn triggers "Start of turn:" / "End of turn:" (retires "Start of your turn", "End of your turn", "At the start of your turn", "Activates at the start of your turn"), "(N) Cost" as noun and "costs (N)" as verb (retires "Cost (N)", "that costs (N)", "costing (N)"), "backrow" (retires "backrow zone"), "all" / "each" / "every" kept as written (no two faces resolve them the same way), standard casing "Start of game" and "Once per turn" (retires "Start of Game" and "Once per Turn"), and prose "costs (1) less" (retires "cost 1 less"). Glossary aliases for retired variants are emptied so exact term labels are used, retaining grammatical plurals (such as Plague Counters) and specific card prompts (Look at your opponent's hand). Card text is presentation: no rule reads it (CLAUDE.md rule 7), [[§8]]'s cells stay the mechanics in their own shorthand, and the engine's identifiers (`library`, `LIBRARY_CAP`, `sacrifice`) keep their names
