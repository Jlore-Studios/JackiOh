Issue #3220 made a dialog one component, `Dialog` ([[R3220000]]), and moved the board's confirms onto
it; this moves the menus' dialogs and sheets. The settings panel, the friends panel, a conversation, the
challenge dialog, a card token's question, the shop's chase question, the question that deletes the
account, the statistics page's card breakdown, a dungeon run's details, the Field Guide, the patch
notes' card, the Collection's item preview, its card view and the Looks screen that opens over the
preview, the developer tools, the command menu and a lesson's result now draw through it. Each keeps
its testids and its Back layer ([[R2127000]]), and decides nothing.

**Sheet.** A panel that slid in from an edge is a sheet, `Dialog`'s `sheet`: centred on a wide screen
as any dialog, at the foot of an upright phone (600 px wide and under) and down the right edge of a
phone on its side (500 px tall and under). The settings panel, the friends panel and a conversation are
sheets, and the last two are now modal, as the settings panel was: the page under them waits.

**Close.** A dialog that asks a question with a Cancel is an `alertdialog`, and its Cancel, first in
the row and holding the focus, is its close: the challenge, a card token's, the chase's and the Looks
screen's questions, and deleting the account, whose act is a `danger` answer and which is now a layer
where Back, Escape and a click outside mean Cancel. Every other dialog closes by the ✕ at its top right:
the friends panel's ×, the run details', the Field Guide's and a lesson's bottom Close, the item
preview's unstyled Close, the command menu's Close beside its input and the pack scene's Close and Done
are the ✕ now, drawn the same on every screen: the shells' own button sizes (`index.css`, `tavern.css`)
pass it over. A lesson's result is a layer, where Back, Escape, its ✕ and a click outside mean View the
board, as the practice result's do.

**Keys.** Escape and Tab go to the top layer alone: a dialog opened over another (the Looks screen over
the preview, a card's detail over the card view) takes them, and closes first, one per Escape as one per
Back. Escape over an open reference's or term's tooltip closes the tooltip alone ([[R279]],
[[R2098000]]), in every dialog as in the inspect overlay. Tab skips a hidden tab's panel and a control
taken out of the order. No source reads Escape itself but the shared hooks and the exceptions below
([[R2126000]]).

**Layer.** Every dialog's scrim is the one at `--z-dialog`. The settings panel (at 1000), the command
menu (200), a conversation and the friends panel (96, 90) come down to it, so the challenge toasts, the
undo toast and the queue banner now draw over an open menu dialog. The inspect overlay's modal layers
(a card's detail, its sheets) take `--z-dialog` too, so a card's detail opened from the card view lies
over it, and their scrim takes the dialog's colour.

**Exceptions.** The inspect overlay's card detail keeps its own body, its scrim (which its swipe fades)
and `useModalOverlay`, for its closable flag and its long-press guard, but takes the ✕, the layer and
the scrim's colour. Its other sheets (a card's touch sheet, a pile's list, a face-down card, the stack
and hero inspect) keep their text Close: they were not on issue #3221's list. The pack scene keeps its
full-screen body, its layer and its hook, and takes the ✕, disabled while its open request is out. The
board's prompt and result scrims stay the board's. Escape stays with the tooltips and hover previews,
which close the tip alone; with the popovers and menus that hang off a control (the home screen's
Quests, the Marketplace, the deck builder's history and cosmetics, the portrait picker, the emote and
mute menus, the modifier list, the artifact tray), which are layers but not modal; with the board's
drags, aim and showcase; and with the dungeon's inline question. The inline questions (a friend's
Block, a series' Forfeit, a quest's Replace, a dungeon run's Abandon, removing a provider's new account
to move to one already held) stay inline, under no scrim, and
now put the safe choice first, as a dialog's row does.

**Rejected:** a conversation left non-modal beside the page; a ✕ beside a Cancel; a scrim class per
caller; `useModalOverlay` moved onto `useModalKeys`, which would lose the inspect overlay's long-press
guard and let its Escape stop short of the board's drag.
