[[R654]] let a player hide their statistics from the public players tab with a Settings switch (`publicStats: false`), and [[R1584000]] kept a note and a Clear under the landing's stats. Both are gone.

**No opt-out.** Settings has no Public player statistics switch and the store no `publicStats`; a value an older device stored is dropped on read, as any unknown key is. `PUT /api/stats/player` carries `stats` alone, never `isPrivate` ([[R1142000]]), and the unchanged server keeps a row's privacy and makes a new row public. Everyone is public from now on, and a player who opted out stays private: nobody is published without being asked.

**No Clear.** The landing's floor ([[R1584000]]) has no note and no Clear. The tally stays clearable as [[R639]] has it, through the store's reset and the browser's site data.

Hearthstone semantics: a player's record has no setting that hides it.

Rejected: making every stored row public, which publishes players who opted out without asking; sending `isPrivate: false`, which does the same at each next sync.
