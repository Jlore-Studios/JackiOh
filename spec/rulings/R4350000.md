Meditative+ #23 Enormous Elderberry's "Their Embiggened version costs no extra mana" leaves each card it adds with no Embiggen premium (`noEmbiggenPremium`): the card's Embiggened version costs its normal price. The price is read live each time a price is read, not fixed when the card is added: the Embiggened version starts from the normal printed price wherever the dearer one would stand, so a `costOverride`, a `costMod`, a discount, an aura and every other rung of [[R65]] move both prices alike and the two are always equal. The normal version keeps its price, and both versions are offered. Under Mountaintop Garden ([[R1181002]]) the mark zeroes the premium of the dearer choice too: that play costs the normal price and still plays the card Radiant and Embiggened, while a card added Radiant has only its normal price there, as any Radiant card does. The mark is price-like, so [[R766]] takes it off a card that reaches a graveyard or an exile pile, an exact copy keeps it as it keeps the card's other prices, and the AI's placeholder for a card its seat cannot read carries none ([[R185]]). The view's `embiggenCost` and the card face's second badge show the price, which now equals the gem ([[§10.8]]).

It supersedes [[R1181003]]'s (0) for the card. Games of v0.6.8d and before fold under the script kept for them, whose `embiggenCostOverride` the engine still prices as [[R1181003]] says ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: charging the Garden's own premium on top of the normal price, which would make the mark zero one premium and not the other; and replacing `embiggenCostOverride` with the mark in the engine, which would refuse every earlier replay that met the card.
