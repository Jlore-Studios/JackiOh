Ahead of the Stinkyscape set, issue #1837 tags shipped cards for the cards that will name them, a designer's decision ([[§5]]): **Terminus** on #100 Ceaseless Void, **Quest** on C #90 In Too Deep (beside its Quickdraw), **Otherworldly** on C+ #32 Otherworldly Removal, **Other-Origins** on its three tokens, C+ #32.1 Execute, #32.2 Brawl and #32.3 Blade Storm, and **Jelly Bean** on the four Glowy Jelly Beans, #26 to #29. The five follow Potion in the engine's `Tag` union and come before Token, and the server's `cards_tags_check` admits them from migration 0058.

What the spec did not say, this ruling does, as Hearthstone keeps a card family such as its Quests apart from its minion types. The five are family tags, not tribes ([[R1424]]): none is in `TRIBAL_TAGS`, so M #87 Tatches the Totem answers none of their plays and tribal tag based hate never reads them. None is in `POOL_TOKEN_TAGS` ([[R382]]): a pool by one of them holds that tag's non-token cards, as any tag's pool does, so an Other-Origins pool, whose cards are all tokens, holds nothing unless it asks for tokens, and the card that first names such a pool decides what it holds. No shipped rule or pool reads any of the five.

The same patch takes Felinor off C #32 Felinor Feelings and #62 Friend of Felinors, and Fruit off C+ #63 Fruit Tree and C+ #68 Organic Produce, the two Field Spells that make Fruit, so a random Fruit is always a Spell. No Fruit pool holds either Field Spell (C+ #58 Fruit Basket's, C+ #63's, C+ #68's Cry's, C+ #73's Fruit entry and #98 Heroic Power's Fruit power), C+ #64 Mulch Muncher's count of Fruit plays takes neither, C+ #68's aura makes neither Radiant (a second Organic Produce resolves on its base face), and Tatches the Totem answers neither Felinor Spell. The Felinor Tokens both Spells summon are still Felinors. A play records its card's tags as it is played (`playedByTag`, [[§10.1]]), so a game that plays one of these fourteen cards records the new tags and not the old, while a game played on an older version folds under that version's catalog ([[R767000]]).
