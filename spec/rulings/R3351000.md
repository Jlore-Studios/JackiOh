Part of #3330 (issue #3351, blocked by #3339, #3340, #3341, #3342, #3344, #3345, #3347, #3348, #3334, #3336, #3337 and #3338). The owner asked that all of #3330's looks hold together on a phone, not only one scene at a time. Each part checks itself at 390x844; this part plays them together on the board a real game produces and sets the one budget they share.

**The living board.** Five art-first Units (two Legendary and one Mythic among them) with tile auras ([[R3342000]]), an Anomaly's backdrop ([[R3337003]]), a launched Starship ([[R3334001]], [[R3334002]]), and a hand of ten with one tilted ([[R3344001]]) hold, at 390x844, 360x780 and 844x390 under 4x CPU throttling, a mean frame within `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` ([[R2803000]]) and a worst single frame within `FX_PHONE_FRAME_WORST_MS` through a Mythic takeover ([[R3345000]]), a Starship launch and broadside, a Drone Battleship arrival ([[R3336000]]), a Terminus set piece ([[R3338000]]), a hand-to-tile flight and a riffle.

**One shared budget.** Moving faces, tile auras, backdrops, particles and tilt share one budget on a phone, `PHONE_MOTION_BUDGET` (six concurrent motion units: a face counts 1, an aura 1, tilt 1), drawn through per-layer budgets that scale with the phone caps: the faces' share is the motion slots, `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX` ([[R3340000]]), which is this many, so the two stay equal; tile and aura motes scale with intensity against the phone particle cap (`auraBudget`, `tileMoteBudget` through `capacityFor` in the aura layers); tilt holds one claim. The adaptive cap (`FX_ADAPT_*`, the phone director) sheds in this named order when frames run long:

1. motes: particle emission halves, every burst still throwing at least one mote;
2. far auras: the auras and tiles past the pointer halve their mote budgets, the one under it keeping its whole;
3. the moving faces furthest from the pointer: every face past the pointer's own holds still — the hovered face is the nearest to the pointer, so it alone keeps its motion slot — and reclaims its old rank when the frames recover ([[R3340000]]);

never a set piece's main beats: a takeover, a fold, a launch, a rift and a title card still mount at the deepest stage, since they are DOM cues with no particle counts for emission scaling to touch ([[R2785000]]).

The mean frame stays under `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` (50 ms, [[R2803000]]), and the worst under `FX_PHONE_FRAME_WORST_MS` (120 ms, about one frame at 8 fps: past that a hitch reads as a stall, not motion).

**The one-screen rule ([[R1581000]]) and the tap areas ([[R2122000]])** hold with all of it on: specs 46 and 47 run unchanged, and a long press opens inspect from every board face and hand card and the full-screen view where [[R3345000]] allows it (an own Mythic or full-art face).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. Hearthstone semantics do not reach frame budgets; the numbers above extend [[R2803000]]'s phone budget to the whole living board.

Rejected: raising the phone budget instead of shedding the scene ([[R2803000]] rejected it first); per-layer budgets with no shared shed order between them, which lets faces, auras and particles each spend the whole frame; stilling the set pieces' beats, which would hide what the game just did; and a pointer-distance gradation among faces, which the slot allocator cannot measure — it ranks hovered first already, so the hovered face is the last to shed.
