IA #68 Short Circuit's "Reveals when an enemy Unit attacks" answers any declared attack by an enemy Unit, whatever it targets — a hero or a Unit — but never a forced attack and never its controller's own attack ([[R1881002]]). The Attack is read as the trap reveals, before the destroy, so later changes to the attacker do not move it. Then the attacker is destroyed and its controller's hero takes that Attack ([[§4.4]]), half of it rounded down on the Radiant face. The hero is hit even if the destroy fails (Indestructible): the hit is its own effect after the destroy. The destroy stops the fight ([[R220]]), so the declared attack deals nothing.
