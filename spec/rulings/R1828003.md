Since issue #1828 Meditative #94 Shrinking Felinor is 9/9, Radiant 18/18, and both faces' Death summons a base Shrinking Felinor whose base stats are the dying card's −3/−3 ([[R1181]]): the Radiant face's text is the base face's, and its `shrinkAttack` is 3, as its `shrinkHealth` and the base face's both are. The dying card's face sets the shrink, by its own declared numbers ([[R386]]), so a Radiant one shrinks by the Radiant face's numbers and every body after it, being base, by the base face's. The base chain runs 9/9, 6/6, 3/3 and then nothing, the next health being 0 ([[R1181]]); the Radiant chain runs 18/18 and then the base bodies 15/15, 12/12, 9/9, 6/6 and 3/3, six bodies against three. A Shrinking Felinor made Radiant on the field summons a base body too.

Through v0.4.9 the Radiant face summoned a Radiant body, on that version's 18/22 and −2/−3 a chain of eight; `script_v0_4_9` keeps that for those versions' replays ([[R767000]]). It amends [[R1181]].
