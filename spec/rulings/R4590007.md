IA #141 Logic Bomb's "In 3 turns" (Radiant: 2) counts its controller's turns ([[R1181000]]): a `delay` for the start of their Nth turn, watching the bomb itself, so killing it defuses the countdown ([[R78]]). The countdown shows as a badge. When it fires it destroys the Units of whoever does not control the bomb then ([[§4.4]]): the enemy side while the bomb is its caster's, its caster's own once stolen.
