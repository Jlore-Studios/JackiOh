The designer asked for card win rates backed by data (issue #48). Every finished game leaves one record: its match type ([[§9.5]]'s mode, read off what made the match: `bo3` for a game of a Conquest series, else its room's mode or its queue tickets'; the match row is unchanged), its patch (the newest version in [[R388]]'s `patches.json` of the build that played it, so a game is filed under the cards it was played with; since patch v0.2.0 that is also the catalog version, [[R388]]), each seat's pilot, `human` or `ai`, which seat took the first turn ([[§2.1]]'s Player 1), each seat's decklist, its opening hand (its hand as the first turn begins: the cards it kept, its replacements and, for the seat going second, The Coin; empty when the game ended before a turn began), every card it drew into its hand after that (a card burned or cast on its draw never reached the hand), every card it played from its hand (a cast, [[§2.4]] and [[R70]], is not a play from hand; a play #96 My Pawn makes for the player it springs on is that player's; since patch v0.2.0, a play countered in its announce window was never played, [[§10.5]] and [[R448]], a play from a graveyard is not from the hand, and a play [[§10.5]] step 3 replaced, C #23 Devil's Pact and [[R449]], is the card it resolves as), and the winner, the reason and the turn count. The engine reads it off `(seed, decks, log)` by `replay.fold`'s fold (`summarizeGame`), so the game state and the log are unchanged, and a log that leaves the game without a result makes no record. The server files a live match under its match id, with two `human` pilots, once the result's transaction has committed, and never at the result's expense: a failure is logged and the result stands, a second write of the same match files nothing, and the reaper's ceiling draws ([[R112]]), whose actor stopped answering, file none. A record names no account, outlives the match's action log, and only the server reads or writes it. A practice game records nothing and sends nothing ([[R187]]), so it leaves no record
