Until issue #3038 a Dormant Starship took the Starship keyword as it launched, a grant that [[R78]]'s reset took off again, and the Drone Battleship's layers printed it. Now a Starship is the card that holds the Starship tag ([[R3038000]]), and the launch gives no keyword: awakened, the Starship takes a name, its memory records the launch, `starshipLaunched` goes out, and its fused Cries and then its fused When launched fire, as before ([[R1852003]]). Its Flying and Ranged are its tag's, there before it launched, so a "When launched" that gives it random keywords (#90 After Burner) draws from the pool as before, less what it already has.

No current card prints or gains the keyword: the layers print Flying and Ranged in its place ([[R3038002]]). The kind stays in the wire's `Keyword` and `KeywordKind` and in the client's copies, outside the random keyword pool ([[R21]]) and uncounted by the AI's dense features, because the shipped catalog snapshots (`crates/cards/patches/*.json`) print it on the Drone Battleships and the replays played on those versions fold under them ([[R767000]]), #115 Scornboi's kept script reading it. Its §6.1 row and the client's term stay, both naming the tag.
