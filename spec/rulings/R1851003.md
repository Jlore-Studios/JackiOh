The designer asked that Meditative #53 Prestige be offered only while a Radiant card it could De-Radiant exists, where [[R1103]] let it be played and fizzle (MD-D7).

- **The base face**, "De-Radiant a Radiant card on the field or in your hand", marks its declared target needed ([[R703]]): a Radiant Unit or face-up backrow card on either side, or a Radiant card in its player's own hand. With none it is neither offered nor accepted, and `playReason` gives [[R703]]'s refusal. A face-down Trap is never a candidate, since its face is hidden ([[R440]]), and neither is a card in the opponent's hand ([[§9.1]]).
- **The Radiant face**, "De-Radiant every enemy card on the field", declares no target, so its play gate ([[R1851000]]) asks for a Radiant card the opponent controls on the field. A face-down one counts for nothing there: whether it is Radiant is hidden from its opponent ([[§9.1]], [[R440]]), and a gate that read it would tell the player through `legalActions` what their view hides. The player's own Radiant cards count for nothing either. Once played, the Cry still clears every Radiant enemy card on the field, face-down ones included (MD-D8).
- A cast of either face is never refused ([[R70]], [[R1851001]]): it clears what there is and still makes the next card its player plays this turn Radiant ([[R831005]]). A Trade is no play ([[R1838002]]), so either face may be Traded with nothing to clear ([[R1838000]]).
- A replay of a game played through v0.4.9, when either face could be played with nothing to clear, folds under the script those versions ran (`KEPT_SCRIPTS`, [[R767000]]).
