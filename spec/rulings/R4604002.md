S #150 Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition)'s fifth entry switches every enemy Unit to Attack Position, then Freezes them ([[R1882003]]). Its Freeze already passed a Unit Immune to Spells by, but its switch did not, so the fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) found the Spell switching immune Units out of Defense.
**The switch passes it by too.** The entry is a Spell's "every enemy Unit", and [[R4453000]] has every walk of the field skip a Unit Immune to Spells ([[§6.1]]), as `switchAllPositions` already does. An immune enemy Unit keeps its position and is not Frozen, and every other enemy Unit is switched and Frozen as before.
Rejected: switching it while sparing it the Freeze, which would leave the Spell affecting the Unit [[§6.1]] keeps it off.
A replay this alters is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]).
