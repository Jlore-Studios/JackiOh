**Size.** The pool holds `clamp(BOT_POOL_MIN, round(BOT_POOL_PER_WEEKLY_ACTIVE × weekly active people), BOT_POOL_MAX)` active bots: a quarter as many bots as the week's active people, never fewer than 8 and never more than 200. The weekly active people are the distinct people, bots left out, who finished a match in the last `BOT_POOL_ACTIVE_WINDOW_DAYS` (7) days. While `LADDER_BOTS` is `on` ([[R1452]]), the server sizes the pool as it boots and every `BOT_POOL_REFRESH_INTERVAL_SECONDS` (an hour) after. An excess retires the active bots with the youngest accounts (the latest `created_at`, the account age drawn for each below), as new accounts are the ones a ladder loses first. A retired bot finishes the match it is in, and the fallback never pairs it again while it is retired ([[R1453]]). A shortfall puts back the most recently retired first, so the ladder keeps meeting the same faces, and only then makes new ones.

**A new bot.** A new bot is drawn from the pool's seed, as a person might sign up:

- a strength, one of five (`BOT_STRENGTHS`), each seeding the bot's rating, from 800 for the weakest to 1200 for the strongest, beside a new player's deviation and volatility ([[R610]]);
- a speed, the multiple of its drawn think times, uniformly from 0.75 to 1.35 (`BOT_SPEED_MIN`, `BOT_SPEED_MAX`);
- a persona, by [[R645]]'s weights, which it keeps;
- a schedule of 8 to 16 consecutive hours a day (UTC) from an hour of its own, around midnight when it must (`BOT_ACTIVE_HOURS_MIN`, `BOT_ACTIVE_HOURS_MAX`);
- an account age, its profile's `created_at`, from 1 to 180 whole days before it was made (`BOT_ACCOUNT_AGE_MIN_DAYS`, `BOT_ACCOUNT_AGE_MAX_DAYS`), at a time of day drawn too;
- a username: for `BOT_DEFAULT_USERNAME_SHARE` (30%) of bots the `Player#n` every new account is given ([[R1434]]), as a person who skips the prompt keeps it ([[R1435]]); for the rest, a word from each of two lists (`BOT_NAME_FIRST`, `BOT_NAME_SECOND`), claimed as a person's pick is claimed, tag and filter included, and a name the claim refuses leaves the bot its `Player#n`.

A bot's profile is active and needs no auth user (migration 0032), and it owns no collection: the launch collection that every catalog stamp grants to active profiles ([[R481]]) goes to people's alone.

**Decks.** A bot holds two or three Best-of-1 decks (`BOT_DECKS_MIN`, `BOT_DECKS_MAX`). They are drawn from the current patch's distinct decklists that people took into live Best-of-1 games, shuffled from the bot's seed, then from random decks dealt as All Random deals a player's ([[R258]]), trying at most `BOT_DECK_DRAW_ATTEMPTS` in all. Those are not the decks the AI draws for itself: they keep the cards [[R186]]'s shadow ban keeps from the AI's own decks and the sets [[R1390]]'s soft gate holds back, since a bot whose decks never held one would tell its seat from a person's once a gated set ships, as [[R1453]] has it for All Random. Each is kept only if a player could queue it for Best of 1: [[§9.4]]'s L2, L3, L5 and L6, checked against a collection of the deck's own cards, since a bot owns none and needs none. Each deck gets a portrait dealt from the seed ([[R642]]). The decks are drawn for one catalog version: when it changes, every active bot is dealt new ones at the next sizing, as is a bot that holds none. Until then the fallback seats it in no Best-of-1 game ([[R1453]]). Each active bot also holds a Conquest trio, dealt after its decks for the same catalog version and dealt again with them, without which it plays no Conquest ([[R641000]]).

**A start cut off.** The fallback's claim puts the bot in its match before the match starts ([[R1453]]). A start that fails lets both seats go. The queue's sweeper lets go every bot whose claimed match was still unstarted `BOT_STALE_START_SECONDS` (60) after its claim, which is what a crash or a restart mid-start leaves, and does so whatever `LADDER_BOTS` says, since the restart may have turned it off. The reaper's sweep of open rows ([[R1450]]) would let them go too, but only after its five-minute grace. Its player is let go with it, so neither is held out of the queue for good.
