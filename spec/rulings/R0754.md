#98's `burn`, X 1: "Deal {shot} damage to the enemy hero." `shot` is one of the card's declared numbers (B3.4 rule 5; the other powers' numbers are declared too since patch v0.3.2, issue #493), printed 2, Radiant 4, step 2. The Radiant face, "… Upgrade this permanently by +2 damage", then sets `shot` to what it dealt plus 2 (`setNumber`, reported by `numberChanged`), which the card keeps in every zone as any tuning is ([[R386]]): a Radiant Steady Shot deals 4, 6, 8 and so on. It is a step of its own number, not [[§6.3]]'s Upgrade, whose change is drawn from a menu. A Degrade or an Upgrade may move `shot` too, on either face, as it may any declared number
