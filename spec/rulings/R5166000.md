The fuzz monitor's I21 ([[R4453000]]) blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, from the Spell's `hookResolving` marker to the next run's. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 14921, M+ #18 Cognitive Offloading, cast on draw, handed p1's turn to the autopilot, and after each card the autopilot played, M+ #17 Dogpile's standing trigger in p1's deck resolved with nothing to do. The autopilot then played C #45 Nature Titan, whose Tribute sacrificed p1's Core #11 Tempo Timmy, a Unit given Immune to Spells. [[§10.5]] pays a play's Tributes at step 2 and announces the play at step 3, so the sacrifice's killerless `destroyed` came before the play's `cardAnnounced`, with no marker of its own, and landed in Dogpile's run: I21 named the Spell.

**The Tribute is the play's.** A Tribute is a cost the play's own player pays ([[§6.3]]), and no Spell's effect: the engine played it as the rules say, and only the monitor misread whose run it was in.

**What I21 allows.** A `destroyed` that names no killer when the first event after it, past the tributed set's own `destroyed` and `enteredGraveyard` events, is a `cardAnnounced`, beside the casting card's collected marks of [[R4863000]]. A killerless death with any other event after it, another run's marker included, is still judged as the Spell's. A Spell's own killerless destroy that a play's announcement followed at once, with no event between, would pass too: I21 accepts that gap, the price of reading a Tribute from the events alone.

Rejected: releasing every standing Spell trigger while the autopilot plays, in the style of [[R4599007]]'s turn's end. It would leave unjudged the standing triggers that do act in the autopilot's turn, which a Unit Immune to Spells must still keep off.
