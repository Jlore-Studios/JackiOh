"The first time … each game" (S+ #120 Crawler's board arrival, S+ #121 Toxopid's hand arrival) is one mark per player per key, kept on the player ([[§6.3]]). Any arrival counts: a summon and the opening deal ([[R925]]) included. Both faces share the mark. The opponent's marks reach no view and no AI.

Rejected: a mark on the card, which [[R78]] wipes.
