Arc's (S+ #38) Activate deals 2 to a Unit, then jumps at random to an adjacent Unit — the next
lane or the backrow behind it — never the same Unit twice in one Activate, until no Unit is left
to jump to: each jump goes to an unhit Unit beside any Unit hit so far, so the whole connected
component is hit whatever the draws. Every jump draws from the game's Rng, and every hit is
ordinary damage. Rejected: a fixed order (nearest first), which the card's "randomly" forbids.
