A pointer, focus or touch on an in-app link or a navigating button starts loading that route's
code, once per page load, and the route renders from that same request, with no loading panel.
Screens behind EngineGate also start the engine and the scene data: the boards (Practice, Play, a match, a series, a Duos match, a watched game, the Dungeon run and its stats, the dailies, a lobby) and, now that the catalog is served by the engine module (issues #4180, #4222 and #4288), the Collection, the deck workshop, the Almanac, Statistics, the patch notes, Play together and the leaderboard. Nothing is prefetched while the browser asks
to save data, and a production build never prefetches `/dev/hotseat` or `/dev/duos`. It is
presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
