[[R1577000]] gave the landing a Collection door to `/collection`, which showed the owned cards,
the wallet, the tracks and the quests, while the decks, the card builder and the choice of what
to equip lived on three other screens. `/collection` is now the hub of everything the player owns
or makes, and the one place a cosmetic is equipped. It edits [[§9.1]] and [[§10.10]].

**The hub.** The Collection door of [[R1577000]] opens a hub of four doors: **Decks** leads to
`/decks`, **Card builder** to `/creator`, **Cards** opens in place at `/collection?section=cards`
and **Cosmetics** at `/collection?section=cosmetics`. The two in-place sections are a query, so
the deploy's route table needs no rewrite and the browser's Back and Forward move between a
section and the hub; an unknown `?section=` opens the hub. `/decks` and `/creator` stay where
they are, so no old address moves and no redirect is added (CLAUDE.md rule 13); their Back
leads to the hub, and the hub's Back to the landing. The page never scrolls, on a desktop or on
a phone: the board shrinks to the screen, and a section's body, the card list among them,
scrolls inside it.

**The selects.** The six selects of [[R1295000]] and [[R1297000]], the foil, finish and border of
your own cards and the playmat, backdrop and card back of your side, move from the settings'
visuals to Cosmetics. They keep the same store and the same `cosmetics` group ([[R633]]), so
every pick a player made is unchanged and no new request is made, and no settings reset touches
them: resetting the settings resets settings. No other screen equips a cosmetic: the settings
dialog and the account screen offer none, and a screen that wants one links to Cosmetics.

**The owned list.** Cosmetics also lists what the account owns, which the client reads and never
decides (CLAUDE.md rule 7): every starter but the defaults, which every account owns with no row
([[§14.2]]), once, plus whatever `GET /api/cosmetics` returns, a full art on the card it sits on
([[R1313000]]). An id this client does not know is not shown. A cosmetic pack's reveal links to
Cosmetics, where its prizes are equipped.

**The deck builder.** A deck's own playmat, backdrop and card back stay in the deck builder,
behind the Decks door, since they belong to the deck. Its option that leaves a slot to the
account's pick now reads "As equipped in Collection".

**Full-art cards.** It stays a setting: no full art is a pick ([[R1313000]]), and the switch also
decides the landing's fan ([[R1017001]]), so it is not an equip control.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's Collection holds the cards, the decks and the card backs
in one place.

Rejected: `/collection/…` addresses with redirects from `/decks` and `/creator`, since every old
address would move for a menu's sake; the deck builder and the card builder as tabs of the hub,
since each is a full screen; a link from the settings to Cosmetics instead of removing the
selects, since the settings open over a game and a way to leave it for another screen does not
belong there.
