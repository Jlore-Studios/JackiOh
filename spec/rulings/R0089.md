[[R78]] resets an instance as it leaves the field, and every trigger but the Death hook is queued and runs after that, so the `destroyed` event carries what the card was: its owner, the player who controlled it as it died ([[R172]]; C #14), its attack and max health as the layers computed them at the moment it died, `radiant` when it died on its Radiant face (C+ #12.8, [[R409]]), and `killerId`, the unit whose damage instance was lethal ([[R42]]), or null. A Death hook still reads the whole snapshot ([[R78]]); every other trigger reads the event
