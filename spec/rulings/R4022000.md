At [[§4.4]] step 3b each card with a pool works out what it would soak of the hit, and step 4a judges what is left of it as before ([[R3550004]], [[R44]]). The soak is written only when the pool takes the hit whole (the zero rule) or the hit goes on past step 4a. A hit a cancel stops ([[R1704001]]) spends none of the pool. A hit a guard catches ([[R1204]]) lands again from its full amount at the guard, and the pool soaks what it can of it there, at that leg's own step 3b. So a 2-health Unit hit for 7 with 5 left in the pool: the 2 the pool would leave is lethal, so Unan catches the 7, its Armor 2 leaves 5, the pool soaks those 5 and Unan takes 0, the pool spent once. It follows Hearthstone, where a protection is used up only by damage that reaches it: a Divine Shield pops only on a hit that lands on its minion. Rejected: spending the pool at step 3b whatever step 4a then does, the old way, where a pool was emptied by a hit that never landed and the moved hit met it empty; and judging step 4a on the hit before the pool, which would open the window on a hit the pool leaves harmless.
