## R1041 — A set Unit reveals at its controller's next start of turn

Built (ME-ALTPLAY, Meditative #45). At its controller's start of turn (the start-of-turn triggers,
traps first, [[§10.3]]) it fires: it turns face-up and animates ([[R383]]: its own lane, else
[[R64]]; `animated`, never `summoned`, [[R445]]). Then its Cry runs, with its declared choices asked
as prompts ([[R467]]'s way). It keeps the turn it was set as its `summonedTurn`, so it may attack
that turn. This is the one exception to [[R83]] and [[R171]]. With no open unit zone it stays
face-up in the backrow (a Field Trap, [[R383]]) and fires again at the next start of turn, and its
Cry waits for the animation.
