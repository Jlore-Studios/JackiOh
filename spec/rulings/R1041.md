## R1041 — A set Unit reveals by its Reveal and may attack

Built (Meditative #45; patch v0.3.7, #781). A Unit set by its Cloaked reveals when its controller
activates its Reveal ([[R783006]]), at any time they may act; no turn reveals it by itself. It turns
face-up and animates ([[R383]]: its own lane, else [[R64]]; `animated`, never `summoned`,
[[R445]]). Then its Cry runs, with its declared choices asked as prompts ([[R467]]'s way). It keeps the
turn it was set as its `summonedTurn`, so revealed on a later turn it may attack at once (the one
exception to [[R83]] and [[R171]]); revealed the turn it was set, it is sick as any Unit is. With no
open unit zone the Reveal is not offered and is refused, so the card waits, still set, until a zone
opens.

Until patch v0.3.7 a Unit set under Knowledge Breaker's permission revealed at its controller's next
start of turn by itself, and with no room stayed face-up in the backrow to try again at the next.
