"After this attacks" resolves after the attack has happened, once the state check that closes its combat has run, and only if the attacker is still alive: still on the field on the stay it attacked from ([[R174]]). An attacker that died in the combat, or left the field there in any other way, does nothing, and neither does one back through Reborn, which is a new arrival ([[R83]]). It is the attacker's `afterAttack` hook, as before, for declared and forced attacks alike, and its facts still carry `survived`, now always true. This replaces the reading under which the hook ran on the snapshot a dead attacker fought with ([[R78]], [[R89]]): Stinkyscape #91 The Propagation's Wasp and #92 The Propagation's Hornet no longer leave a copy when they die attacking ([[R1849001]]). Stinkyscape #94 Tmack Trav ([[R1869002]]) and #38 Shield Amplifier already asked for survival, so they play as before. This is the owner's ruling (issue #2285). Issue #2396 took #32 Prem Panther and Classic #13 Boots on the Ground out of it: the Panther's "Whenever this attacks" runs whether or not it survived, and Boots resolves "When this attacks", before the damage ([[R2396000]]). Classic+ #19.5 Bot Loser and Training Grounds #9 Ravager's "After this destroys a Unit" asks the same survival ([[R1245]]).
