A spawn ([[R923000]]) names a card or a token, its face (base or Radiant), a seat and a zone: the hand, a lane of the field, the graveyard or the top of the library. The panel finds the card by a search over every card and token of a set that ships: each word must match, a number the card's cost, any other word part of its name, either face's text, its id, set, type or rarity.

- **What can be spawned:** a card or a token of a set that ships ([[R1420]]); a set that has not shipped, or a definition a game made for itself (a Fuse's, a crafted card's), is no card to spawn.
- **Where it may stand:** a Unit in the unit row, any other non-Spell in the backrow, in a lane that exists and whose zone is empty and not reserved. A Spell is cast and never stands on the field (§5.1), so it may go anywhere but there.
- **Placed, not played.** Each zone is reached by the engine's own verb, as an effect puts a card there: on the field it is summoned, a Trap face-down and a Unit as summoning sick as any summon, its Cry not run; in the hand it is added, and a full hand burns it (§2.4); on top of the library it is put there, the next card drawn; in the graveyard it is a new card entering it. Nothing is paid, and no play is counted for anything that counts plays.
