Chain capped at 20 casts (`CALL_TO_CHAOS_CHAIN_CAP`), counting casts of every edition, a Call to Chaos a random cast makes (C+ #47, [[R452]]) included. Since patch v0.2.0 two cards share the Call to Chaos tag, #95 and C+ #73, and Meditative #95 is the third ([[R1240]]), so "cast a random Call to Chaos" finds any of them, Meditative #95 once its set ships ([[R380]], [[R1420]]), and C+ #73's "replace your deck with random Call to Chaos" names the same pool ([[R387]]). Each Radiant face casts three different random effects from its own list, the recursion where it falls in the list's order ([[R423]]), in place of #95's old pair of the recursion and one of the other 9 effects
