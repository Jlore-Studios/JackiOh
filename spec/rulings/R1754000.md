While the creator's draft holds unsaved work ([[R1484000]]), every way off the page within the client shows its note: its Back, the move a followed queue makes to the game it paired ([[R765]]), a challenge's Accept and the toast's move to a friend's game, any in-app link, and the browser's own Back or Forward. "Discard them" makes the move the player took; after the browser's Back or Forward, that is the page it went to. "Keep editing" stays, and the address is put back. A move kept from waits on nothing. The queue was left when it paired, and the menus' banner offers the game ([[R765]]). A challenge stays on its toast, to be accepted again. The note's Discard takes the latest way off the player tried. "Try it" saves first, so it goes at once. A saved card, or a new one nobody has touched, is left at once every way.
