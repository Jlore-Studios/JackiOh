Sign-up needs an unticked box, "I agree to the Terms and the Privacy Policy.", whose links open the two pages in a new tab; without it the form says so at the box and calls no provider. An OAuth sign-up meets the same box on the consent screen ([[R757000]]) before it can redeem a code. The version agreed to is `TERMS_VERSION` in `crates/server/src/config.rs`: the Terms' "Last updated" date, then the Privacy Policy's, which a web test holds to `TERMS_LAST_UPDATED` and `PRIVACY_LAST_UPDATED`, so a change to either page's text is a new version (a second change on one day gives its half a letter, [[R970001]]).

`POST /api/account/consent` records the agreement, one row per account and version with its time and its source (`terms_acceptances`, migration 0034): `signup` from a pending account, `reaccept` from an active one, `baseline` for every person's account that predates 0034, written by the migration under the version `baseline` at the account's own creation time so that no account is without a record. A baseline is not the version in force, so each of those accounts is asked once, at its next gated screen. A version other than the one in force is refused with 409 `update_required`, and the client says to reload the page. `GET /api/auth/me` says whether the account owes the version in force (`termsOwed`), and a server that sends no flag owes nothing. The consent screen asks an account that owes it for the box alone, saying that the Terms or the Privacy Policy changed. It is shown on the gated screens, never on `/account` (where a player who will not agree can still delete the account or sign out), never while the account is in a match or a series, and never over a screen the gate already showed, so a change of terms never interrupts play. Redemption refuses an account that owes the age check or the terms with 403 `consent_owed` before the attempt is logged, so it costs no try, and the code screen reads the account again to show the consent screen.

Hearthstone asks for its updated terms once, at the next sign-in, and records the agreement against the account. Rejected: refusing every active route while agreement is owed, which costs a read per request and could lock a player out mid-series; an implied agreement by a hint under the form, which is what the box replaces.
