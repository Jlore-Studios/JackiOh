Combo-Index's grade counter ([[R27]]) is field state, which [[R78]] resets as the card leaves the field. A fused card's Cry runs its ingredients' parts in turn, and the later parts still run after an earlier one took the card off the field ([[R77]], [[R102]]); a #93 part run then writes no grade and reports none, as [[R3693000]] skips the reveal of a card no longer there. Its other steps run as before. Rejected: stopping the remaining Cry parts once the card leaves the field, which would change what every fused Cry does (seed 6091, where C #44 Back from the GY's "Exile this" ran first).
