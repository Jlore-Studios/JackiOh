A keyword a fusion brings in applies at once (MD-D34). A fused "Animated on your turn" animates the kept card at your next start of turn with the summed stats, while a fused plain Animated waits until the card enters the field again ([[R383]]). The Tree's other fused lines work as C+ #74's do: a Cry never runs on a card already on the field, auras and start- and end-of-turn lines run, and fused Activates share the kept card's per-turn count. On the Radiant face the pick goes in on its Radiant face ([[R469]]).
