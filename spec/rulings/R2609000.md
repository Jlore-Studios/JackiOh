title: "A watcher's socket keeps a seat's limits on its later hellos and its junk frames (issue #2609)"

proven_in:

[[R1103000]] answered every later hello of a watcher's with the view it was last sent, with no budget, and after the hello [[R835002]] dropped a watcher's other frames unread. A player's socket has both guards: its hellos coalesce in the queue ([[R835001]]) and past `MALFORMED_FRAMES_PER_WINDOW` malformed frames it is closed with 1008 ([[R835002]]). `WS_OUTBOX_MAX_FRAMES` ([[R1441]]) bounds only a reader that falls behind, so a script that reads as fast as it sends had the actor send a view for every hello under the lock both players' moves wait on, and could send junk without end. A watcher's socket now keeps a `SeatGuard` of its own ([[§9.8]]):

- **A later hello is answered at most once per `WATCH_HELLO_INTERVAL_MS` (1 s).** One that comes sooner after the last answered one is dropped unanswered; a hello that finds no view to send is not an answer and starts no interval. A page probes only once its socket has been quiet past `WS_PING_INTERVAL_SECONDS`, and any answer ends the quiet ([[R1441]]), so a real page's probe never falls inside the interval.
- **Every other frame counts against the watcher's own `MALFORMED_FRAMES_PER_WINDOW` inside `REJECTION_ALERT_WINDOW_MS`**, still unanswered and read by nothing else. Past it the socket is closed with 1008; the close takes the watcher off as any watcher's leaving does, and both players are told the new count. The count starts when the actor takes the socket: the hello gate's count before it stays the socket's own ([[R835002]]).

Rejected: coalescing as a seat's hellos do, since a watcher's answer is a stored frame sent at once, never a queued task, so there is nothing to coalesce into; answering each junk frame `malformed` as a seat's are, since [[R1103000]] keeps a watcher's frames unanswered and the watch page sends nothing but the hello; counting a dropped hello as junk, which would close a socket that only says hello; carrying the gate's count over, at most `MALFORMED_FRAMES_PER_WINDOW` frames more per sign-in, which the account's socket budget ([[R2478000]]) already bounds.
