A Resource ([[R2250000]]) pays its player its Credits as it is played or discarded, its quote this turn while the Marketplace is open ([[R2250001]], [[R1853002]]). While it is open, a Resource in its owner's hand now shows that number: `view_for` gives the hand card an R280 preview value, labeled `Credits` (`preview::SELL_QUOTE_LABEL`), whose value is `marketplace::sell_quote` for the card's own face, the base face's quote for a base Resource and the Radiant face's for a Radiant one. The client's preview rendering already prints a value in braces after the card's words, `{N}`, its tooltip "Credits: currently N" ([[R280]]), and the label, which no Resource's text holds, puts it at the end of them. Nothing about the face model changes: it is the preview the view carries, and the client works out no price ([[§10.8]], CLAUDE.md rule 7).

It is added to the card's own preview, if its script declares one (the six Resource tokens' scripts declare none). It follows [[R280]]'s reader: only the owner's own hand ([[R280]]'s `may_preview`), so the opponent's view of that hand, a count, carries nothing; a Resource anywhere else, or a card that is no Resource, shows none. A closed Marketplace has no quote and the card shows none. The quote moves at every turn start, and so does the value. The range the face prints, `Credits: min–max` ([[R2521009]]), is the face's definition and stays as it was: the range of what it may sell for, with the braces what it sells for now.

A card that sells from the hand ([[R1861000]], `handSale`) shows none: it is sold at the Marketplace's Sell button, which already gives its quote, and it is no Resource.
