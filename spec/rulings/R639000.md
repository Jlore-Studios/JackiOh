A ladder bot ([[R1452]]) left tells a person could read: it never conceded, answered a draw offer and its mulligan at once, never let its clock run out, sent no aim arrow, and read as gone the moment the game ended. This ruling closes them with Hearthstone semantics: people concede when far behind, answer at a person's speed, sometimes let a turn run out, show their aim before they attack, and rematch only sometimes. Each habit is drawn as a person's is, at rates and in windows fitted from people's play ([[R1442]]), and nothing new reaches a client: the bot's moves are still server actions and its frames are those a person's client sends (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**The exception.** [[R188]] still holds for the practice AI and for `decide` itself: the search never concedes, never offers a draw and declines every offer, and practice is unchanged. A ladder bot's seat is the exception. It concedes, answers draw offers and lets a turn run out as below, from rolls of its own beside the search's decision. It never offers a draw, and the actor never plays a draw offer or an emote as one of its moves (`never_played`).

**Concession.** A bot concedes only at the start of its own turn: its first move of a turn it is active in, with no mulligan open and no prompt pending. Its temper is drawn once a match, on a stream of its own (`{seed}:bot-concede:{home}`): whether it is one who concedes at all (`BOT_CONCEDE_FIT.share`), and the deficit it gives up at, drawn from a normal of mean `deficit_mu` and standard deviation `deficit_sigma`. It concedes when the turn is at least `min_turn`, it trails its opponent, and its deficit is at least that threshold. Its deficit is the measure [[R1442]] records for a person's concession, the AI's evaluation of the state from its side, negated, which is the scale the threshold is fitted on. That measure is not centred on an even board: it counts the enemy hero's health against the seat, so an even opening reads about 30 behind, and the prior's threshold (`deficit_mu` 70, `deficit_sigma` 20) sits well past it. Whether it trails is a second reading, centred on the even board: half the gap between its opponent's evaluation of the state from its own side and the bot's. It must be above zero, so a bot never concedes while even or ahead, whatever its threshold drew. Both readings are taken on the state read through `redact` ([[R185]]) and filled in by one `determinize`d world on the stream `{seed}:bot-standing:{home}:{turn}`, as the search evaluates only determinized worlds, so no hidden card moves them and a face-down card cannot stop the read. The concession waits out a think time drawn from the `concede` prior (below) before it goes.

**Draw offers.** A bot never offers a draw. It answers a person's offer ([[R36]]) after a think time drawn from the `answerDraw` prior, not at once, and accepts only while it trails its opponent (read as for a concession), at `BOT_DRAW_ANSWER_FIT.accept_share`, on the stream `{seed}:bot-draw:{home}:{turn}`. Otherwise it declines, which blocks the person's next offers as [[R36]] says.

**Idle turns.** At the start of its own turn, while its turn clock runs, a bot lets the turn run out with the chance `BOT_TIMEOUT_FIT.chance_per_turn`, on the stream `{seed}:bot-idle:{home}:{turn}`: it plays nothing, and [[R79]]'s clock times it out as it times out a person who is away. The roll is the turn's own, so every later look at the same turn answers the same. With no clock running for it, it never idles, since nothing would end the turn.

**Pace.** A think time is [[R1452]]'s, with three changes. Its log-normal's mean grows by `THINK_COMPLEXITY_SLOPE` for each unit of `ln(legal actions)` above the row's own mean (`ThinkTimeFit.ln_legal_mean`, or `BOT_THINK_PRIOR_LN_LEGAL_MEAN` for a prior), since a board with more to do takes a person longer. It grows by `THINK_LETHAL_SHIFT` more on a move the AI decided as lethal, since people count before they swing. And a kind of move a person takes longer or shorter over has a prior of its own until a fit for it lands (`BOT_THINK_PRIORS`): a mulligan, about 9 s and well inside the mulligans' shared clock ([[R268]]), since answering it at once as the practice AI does ([[R266]]) was a tell; a draw answer; and a concession. Every other move keeps the first and later moves' priors. [[R1452]]'s bounds are unchanged: no sooner than `BOT_THINK_MIN_MS` (600 ms), no later than `BOT_THINK_MAX_MS` (30 s), and never with less than `BOT_CLOCK_MARGIN_MS` (5 s) left on its clock.

**Aim.** Before a play, an Activate, a hero power or an attack that declares a target, a bot shows its aim as a person's client would ([[R738]]). The aim is the server's mirror of the client's (`bots::aim::bot_aim`): its ends are public handles, a hero by its seat, a field card by its zone and a hand card by its position in the bot's own hand. A move a person's client shows no aim for shows none: an attack at a random target, a play with no target, and every move that only places a card. The aim goes through [[R738]]'s relay, judged against the person's view (`aim_is_public`) as a person's is, `aim_lead_ms` before the move: drawn uniformly from `BOT_AIM_LEAD_MIN_MS` (250 ms) to `BOT_AIM_LEAD_MAX_MS` (600 ms) on the stream `{seed}:bot-aim:{home}:{seq}`, `seq` the log's next, and taken out of the think time rather than added to it. It is taken down after the move. When less than `AIM_RELAY_INTERVAL_MS` (100 ms) of the think time is left, no aim goes, since the relay could not send it before the move.

**Emotes.** A bot emotes through [[R645]]'s personas, with rows of its own. A new bot is dealt its persona ([[R1454]]) by `BOT_PERSONA_WEIGHTS`, and it waits out its emotes inside `BOT_EMOTE_DELAY_FIT` after a trigger and `BOT_EMOTE_REPLY_DELAY_FIT` for a reply to a person's emote, windows refitted from people's emotes in place of the practice AI's 0.8–2.5 s; their priors are the practice AI's weights and window. [[R643]]'s rate limit, the persona's caps and the seat's dealt hand ([[R1344]]) still hold. The practice AI keeps its weights and its window.

**Rematch.** When a ranked match a bot played ends with a result, the bot draws its plan once, on the stream `{seed}:bot-rematch:{home}`: how long it stays, uniformly from `BOT_REMATCH_LINGER_MIN_MS` (4 s) to `BOT_REMATCH_LINGER_MAX_MS` (15 s); whether it offers a rematch itself (`BOT_REMATCH_FIT.offer_share`); and whether it meets a person's offer (`accept_share`). While it stays it reads as present on the match, exactly as a person still on the death screen does ([[R672]]), and after that as gone; it draws the plan as the game ends, so it reads as present without a break while the result is written. A bot that offers makes a normal-stakes offer `offer_after_ms` after the end, once the result has landed, drawn uniformly from `BOT_REMATCH_DELAY_MIN_MS` (1.5 s) to `BOT_REMATCH_DELAY_MAX_MS` (3.5 s). A bot that meets an offer meets it `reply_ms` later, drawn from the same window, at the stakes the person offered, if the offer arrives while it is still there, and stays on the match until that answer lands, past its linger if need be. A rematch with a bot needs `LADDER_BOTS` on, and claims the bot in the same step that seats it: free, in no match and not retired, whatever its schedule says, since a person still on the match plays again whatever the hour. This and the empty-queue fallback ([[R1453]]) are the only ways a bot is seated. A bot another match claimed meanwhile answers the rematch as a seat already in a match does. A rematch's offers are rendezvous state, not rows ([[R672]]), so a restart forgets the bot's plan as it forgets a person's offers.

**Fitting.** Every rate and window above is a named row in the server's config (CLAUDE.md rule 9): `THINK_COMPLEXITY_SLOPE`, `THINK_LETHAL_SHIFT`, `BOT_CONCEDE_FIT`, `BOT_DRAW_ANSWER_FIT`, `BOT_TIMEOUT_FIT`, `BOT_REMATCH_FIT`, `BOT_EMOTE_DELAY_FIT`, `BOT_EMOTE_REPLY_DELAY_FIT`, `BOT_PERSONA_WEIGHTS`, and each think-time fit's `ln_legal_mean`. Each holds a hand-set prior (`samples: 0`) until a person pastes over it what `jackioh-server timing-fit` prints once `TIMING_FIT_MIN_SAMPLES` (30) human observations back it. `timing-fit` reads people's rows only ([[R1442]]):

- the complexity slope is the pooled within-bucket least-squares slope of `ln(think)` on `ln(legal)`, and a bucket's `ln_legal_mean` the mean of `ln(legal)` over its moves;
- the lethal shift is the mean residual of the winner's moves on the last turn of the games between two people won by a hero's death;
- the concession's share is the conceders over the losers, its `min_turn` the `TIMING_FIT_CONCEDE_TURN_QUANTILE` (0.1) quantile of the turns conceded on, and its deficit's mean and standard deviation those over the conceders;
- the draw answer's share is taken over the seats offered a draw;
- the timeout chance is the timeouts per own turn;
- the rematch's offer and accept shares are taken over the matches a rematch could follow (ranked, and in no Conquest series);
- each emote window runs between the `TIMING_FIT_DELAY_QUANTILES` (0.1 and 0.9) of the delays observed, after a trigger or after the opponent's emote;
- the persona weights class each person's seat: one that sent no emote is Silent, one that sent mostly taunts BM, one that sent no taunt Polite, and any other Balanced.

The aim's lead, the linger and the rematch delays are hand-set, since [[R1442]] records none of them.

**Replay.** Every roll of this ruling draws on its own stream, seeded from the match seed and the seat the bot began in (so a Glitch's swap, [[R677]], moves none of them), always from cursor 0, apart from the match rng (`state.rng`), the search's stream and the think time's. Nothing it draws reaches the state, the log or a replay hash, so a match with a bot still replays from `(seed, log)` ([[§9.5]]), and an actor rebuilt after a restart draws the same temper, idle turns and answers. A rebuild reads off the log the turn each bot last moved in (`bot_moved_turns`), so a restart in the middle of the bot's turn does not begin that turn again: no second concession roll, idle roll or first move's think time. A concession or a draw answer reaches the log only as the move it is, a server action with the bot's nonce ([[R270]]), and an idle turn only as the clock's `timeout`.
