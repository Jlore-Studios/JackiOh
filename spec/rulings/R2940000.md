Part 2 of #2725's ten new card versions (tracker #2729) adds the three that draw the card's subject
anew, after Pokémon TCG Pocket's Art Rare and Special Art Rare. Each is a `version-…` entry of the
registry ([[R2725000]]), appended after part 1's three, per card, owned card by card, never a
default or a starter:

| Version | Id | Rarity | Chase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alt art | `version-alt-art` | Rare | no |
| Sketch | `version-sketch` | Rare | no |
| Alt full art | `version-alt-full-art` | Legendary | yes |

**Offers.** The owner asked (2026-11-03) that each version go to a varied set of cards of its own,
not to every card nor to Full art's cards. Each offer is data ([[R2725001]]), every clause sampling a
seeded share by the version's own id as its seed ([[R2949001]]), so no two versions take the same
cards:

- **Alt art**: 50 in 100 of every card, of any type and rarity, but the twenty Legacy cards
  ([[R2511000]]), whose one picture is their Core card's and has no Alt art ([[R2940001]]). 310 cards
  when it shipped, the widest of the three.
- **Sketch**: 45 in 100 of the Units and of the Legendary and Mythic cards, whose figure suits line
  work, Legacy cards included. 145 cards.
- **Alt full art**: 55 in 100 of the Epic Units and of the Legendary and Mythic cards, but the Legacy
  cards. 84 cards.

Each reaches `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_CARDS` in all and `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET` in every set that
ships (Alt full art 2 on Meditative+, its fewest), none is offered on every card, and no two of Full
art, Alt art, Sketch and Alt full art take the same set of cards. A version a card is not offered is
never dropped, sold, picked or shown for it: the Collection's strip does not list it, and a pick, a
token or the Chase Shard bar naming it there is a 400 that writes nothing ([[R2725001]]). No retired
full art implies one of them ([[R2725002]]), and Signature fits none of them ([[R2949000]]).

**Granted to no account.** Like every cosmetic added since #1833 (§14.12), the three start unowned
on every account, existing ones included; `POST /api/dev/fill` grants each on every card it is
offered, and `jackioh-server seed-catalog` upserts them into `public.cosmetics` as it does every
registry entry, so no migration is needed. Alt art and the Sketch are Rares, below the chases; Alt
full art is a Legendary chase a Legendary token or a full Chase Shard bar buys. The scale stays Common to Mythic ([[R2725000]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a version, and nothing enters `PlayerView`'s
rules, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725000]] (three more versions) and [[R2725001]] (three more offers, each a seeded share of
its own).
