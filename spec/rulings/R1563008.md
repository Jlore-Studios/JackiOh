The designer asked (#1563) that potions be "spells with no stats" with "a lower point total". The potion frame ([[R1182000]]) is now a **Spell** tagged Potion at its fixed cost, with no stats (attack 0, health 1, as every crafted Spell's recipe), the keywords a Spell takes (Lifesteal and Pierce, [[R883]]), and exactly one hat, **When cast**, holding at least one effect. It is played as any Spell is: its effects run as it is cast, a targeted block declaring its target with the play ([[R81]]), and it goes to its owner's graveyard. Its points are `CRAFT_POTION_POINTS` (4), no longer the crafter's `2 + 5 ×` cost (7 at cost 1): a Spell spends nothing on stats, so the frame's Unit of 7 points, which spent about half of them on its body, becomes 4 points of effect. A HUGE Potion keeps `CRAFT_HUGE_POTION_BUDGET_MULTIPLE` (5) times a Potion's points, 20 ([[R1182001]]). Lines (24), effects (8) and numbers (1 to 10) are the crafter's. The presets are one Potion per offered verb that fits ([[R1563007]]), at the largest N the points hold.

Any other answer to a potion prompt is refused with the prompt open: a Unit or another type ("a Potion is a Spell"), another hat or a second one ("a Potion has exactly one hat, its When cast"), another frame, or one over the points. The "Activate: Tribute this." hat is retired with the Unit frame: no type takes it and no prompt offers it, though a Potion crafted before #1563 still compiles from its recipe and activates as it did, so an older game folds unchanged ([[R882]]). It edits [[§6.3]], [[§8.9]], [[R883]], [[R1182000]] and [[R1182001]].
