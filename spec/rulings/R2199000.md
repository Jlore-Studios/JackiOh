A card's `allyAttack` hook ("After one of your Units attacks") answers each combat a Unit of its controller's fights as the attacker, declared ([[§4.2]] step 5) or forced ([[R53]]). Once the state check that closes the combat has run, and after the attacker's own `afterAttack` and the defender's `afterAttacked`, every card acting on the attacker's controller's side with the hook, the attacker itself included, runs it in [[R68]]'s order, each a whole effect followed by its state check ([[R59]]). The hook is handed `{ attackerId, targetId, forced, survived }` (`combat::ally_attack_of`), `survived` judged as the hook starts: the attacker still on the field and not gone from it since the check began ([[R174]]: a Reborn body is a new arrival). An attack called off before it fought ([[R44]]) wakes nothing, nor does a combat of the other side's Units. A question in a hook pauses the rest of the hooks until it is answered, owed on `state.work` as `@allyAttack` ([[R113]]). With no holder nothing runs and no event is added, so a game without one replays as before. Rejected: counting at `attackDeclared`, which would make TG #31's Unit Radiant before its own combat's damage, and a public after-combat event, which every shipped game's log would carry.
