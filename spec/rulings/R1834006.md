[[R1834005]] draws a ladder bot's cosmetics at the rates people of its account age own and wear
them, from rows a person pastes into `BOT_COSMETIC_FITS` (CLAUDE.md rule 9), as [[R1442]]'s
`timing-fit` prints the think times a bot copies. `jackioh-server cosmetics-fit`, a subcommand
beside `timing-fit` that reads `DATABASE_URL` and takes no option, prints those rows.

**What it reads.** The loadouts people's seats froze over the last `BOT_COSMETIC_FIT_WINDOW_DAYS`
(`matches.loadouts`, [[R1834002]]), a ladder bot's seat never among them, and what each of those
people owns now, from the ledger. It buckets by account age (`BOT_COSMETIC_AGE_BUCKETS_DAYS`: a
bucket up to each bound, inclusive, and one past the last): a seat by its account's age as its
match began, a person by their age at their latest match in the window.

**What it prints.** Each bucket of at least `BOT_COSMETIC_FIT_MIN_SAMPLES` seats prints one
`CosmeticFit` row; a bucket under it prints a comment, so its row keeps the prior. A row holds:

- each cosmetic's share of the seats wearing it in its slot, the six slots of a seat's loadout,
  defaults included;
- the share of seats with a card of their own picks (a foil, a finish or a border of its own), and
  the mean number of such cards those seats hold;
- for each full art, of the seats whose person owns it and whose deck holds its card, the share
  that printed it; ownership is read now, so a full art granted after a match counts that match as
  not printed;
- each non-starter's share of the bucket's people that own it, on its card for a per-card one.

The output is aggregates only: no account id and no username.

Nothing here is a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7): it reads no game state, and its rows reach only the
bots' cosmetics.

Hearthstone semantics: the spec is silent and so is Hearthstone; the rows are fitted from people's
own matches as [[R1442]]'s are, so a bot dresses as the people it plays among.

Rejected: fitting the rows in the server as it runs, which would move the bots' rates with no
person reviewing them, as `timing-fit` leaves its rows to a person too; and reading what people
equip in their settings rather than what their matches froze, which would count picks that were
never worn or never owned.
