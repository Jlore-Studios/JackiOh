IA #65 Overdrive's "At the end of your turn, deal 2 damage to your hero for each mana you didn't spend" reads the whole pool its controller holds unspent at its [[R62]] end-of-turn point: the mana the Cry gained ([[§2.3]]) and every other mana alike, including mana gained after the Cry resolved. Spending the whole pool costs nothing. The `damage` (2) is carried in the delayed effect's data, so each mana left counts that much.
