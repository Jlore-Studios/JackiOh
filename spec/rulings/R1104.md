M #53 Prestige's Radiant face De-Radiants "every enemy card": every Radiant card of the opponent's
field, hand and library — never the graveyard, whose cards De-Radiant cannot reach, and never its
controller's own cards. Each clear is reported by its own `deradianted`, hidden per zone ([[R440]]).
