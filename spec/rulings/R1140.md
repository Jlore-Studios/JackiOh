## R1140 — A hand watch lasts one stay per hand, then lets go

Built (ME-HANDMARK, Meditative #76). `delay` takes `handWatch`, the instance ids it watches in a
hand, and `mark`, the mark their owner sees on each watched card. Only ids still in a hand are
kept: an id the watch names that is not in a hand schedules nothing, and with none left nothing
is scheduled at all. A watched card is dropped the moment its stay in that hand ends — played,
discarded, shuffled away, stolen by something else, or otherwise leaving the hand ([[R174]]) — and
the delayed entry with it once its list empties, so a card that comes back to the hand later is a
new stay and unmarked. As the entry comes due, its step reads the ids still watched
(`HAND_WATCH_KEY`) in the order they were picked, in hand order. An entry with no `handWatch`
runs exactly as before.
