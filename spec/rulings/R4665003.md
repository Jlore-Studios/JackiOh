Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VII: "Bot loser loses its attack on kill". Both faces of C+ #19.5 lose "After this destroys a Unit, it gains +{attackGain} Attack" and the declared `attackGain` with it, so a Unit it destroys ([[R42]]), C+ #19.2 Jungle Loser's credited kill on the Radiant face among them ([[R412]]), gives it nothing. Replays of the versions through v0.6.12c fold under `script_v0_6_12c`, which gains as they did, never for a kill it died making ([[R2396000]], [[R767000]]).
