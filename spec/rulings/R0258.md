Both players are dealt a deck by the game's weighted random deck-builder — [[§9.9]]'s curve- and tag-aware draw, with nothing banned, the deck practice deals a human who asks for a random one — seeded from the match seed and the seat, and the dealt decks are frozen into the match, so `(seed, decks, log)` still replays it. The collection is not consulted and no saved deck is needed. A player who also asks for "More cards from the newest set" is dealt a deck that leans on the newest set that ships, at least half of it from that set, from the same seed and seat; the choice is each player's for their own seat, sent with the queue, room or rematch request, and the server deals both decks ([[R1372]])
