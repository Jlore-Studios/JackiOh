Both players are dealt a deck by the game's weighted random deck-builder — [[§9.9]]'s curve- and tag-aware draw, with nothing banned, the deck practice deals a human who asks for a random one — seeded from the match seed and the seat, and the dealt decks are frozen into the match, so `(seed, decks, log)` still replays it. The collection is not consulted and no saved deck is needed
