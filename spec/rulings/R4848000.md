The prompt clock of [[R79]] belongs to the question. Each prompt the player not on turn holds, told apart by its prompt id, gets a fresh `PROMPT_CLOCK_SECONDS` (30 s) when it opens. That holds whether the same seat's previous question was answered in time or timed out. S #22 Gatinsir's two S #82 Phalanxes ask the seat not on turn four Discovers in a row ([[R2176000]]), and each has its own 30 s; when none is left open, the turn clock resumes with what it had left ([[§9.5]]).

The same question keeps its clock. A sync that finds it still open re-arms nothing. Once its clock has run out, only [[R776000]]'s retry runs it out again, while that question stays open.

Nothing bounds a chain of questions for one seat beyond the cards that ask them: n questions can hold the active player's turn for up to n × 30 s (two minutes for Gatinsir's four), and the ceiling ([[R389]]) stays the backstop.

Hearthstone asks the player not on turn nothing, so R79's "its own prompt clock" decides.

Rejected: one deadline per seat across its questions (docs/implicit-states.md §13 T8 as it stood). It auto-answered a fast player's last question with time the earlier ones had spent. Worse, once that deadline was spent it left the next question with no clock at all, so a silent seat held the match until the ceiling.

Rejected: a budget per turn, under which a seat's later questions would be answered at once after one has timed out. R79 states no such budget, and it would answer for a player who was away for a single question.

**Proof:** `crates/shared/tests/clock.rs::match_clock` (a second prompt's own 30 s, the same prompt's kept deadline, a spent prompt waiting for the retry while the next gets a fresh one), `crates/shared/tests/duel.rs::the_prompt_clock` (Gatinsir's board on the host: a question after a timed-out one is timed and shown to both clients, a silent seat does not hold the turn, a question after one answered in time gets its own 30 s, a refused timeout's retry), `crates/shared/tests/host_fuzz.rs` (the silent probe meets the same board) and `crates/shared/src/host/snapshot.rs` (the clock reads the open prompt's id).
