Part 3 of #2725's ten new card versions (tracker #2729) adds the three that restyle the card's own
art. Each is a `version-…` entry of the registry ([[R2725000]]), appended after part 2's three
([[R2940000]]), per card, owned card by card, never a default or a starter, and Epic, beside the
Animated augment, so none is a chase:

| Version | Id | Rarity | Chase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extended art | `version-extended-art` | Epic | no |
| Pixel | `version-pixel` | Epic | no |
| Comic | `version-comic` | Epic | no |

**Offers.** The owner asked (2026-11-03) that each version go to a varied set of cards of its own,
not to every card nor to another version's cards. Each offer is data ([[R2725001]]), every clause
sampling a seeded share by the version's own id as its seed ([[R2949001]]), so no two versions take
the same cards, and each holds cards no other version takes:

- **Extended art**: 40 in 100 of the Spells and Field Spells, whose picture is a scene that reads
  past its window, and 40 in 100 of the Legendary and Mythic cards. 156 cards when it shipped, 9 of
  Training Grounds' its fewest in a set.
- **Pixel**: 40 in 100 of the Rare and Epic Units, Traps and Field Traps, and 40 in 100 of the
  Legendary and Mythic cards. 95 cards, 4 of Meditative+'s its fewest.
- **Comic**: 35 in 100 of the Rare and Epic Units, Spells and Traps, and 40 in 100 of the Legendary
  and Mythic cards. 138 cards, 5 of Meditative+'s its fewest.

Pixel and Comic take no Common card, and Extended art no Common Unit or Trap, so cards get different
mixes. Each reaches
`VERSION_OFFER_MIN_CARDS` in all and `VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET` in every set that ships, none is
offered on every card, and no two of the seven versions (Full art, part 2's three and these three)
take the same set of cards. A version a card is not offered is never dropped, sold, picked or shown
for it: the Collection's strip does not list it, and a pick, a token or the Chase Shard bar naming it
there is a 400 that writes nothing ([[R2725001]]); where it is offered, the Chase Shard bar refuses
it with a 409, as it does every cosmetic below Legendary. No retired full art implies one of them
([[R2725002]]), and Signature fits none of them ([[R2949000]]).

**Granted to no account.** Like every cosmetic added since #1833 (§14.12), the three start unowned
on every account, existing ones included; `POST /api/dev/fill` grants each on every card it is
offered, and `jackioh-server seed-catalog` upserts them into `public.cosmetics` as it does every
registry entry, so no migration is needed. An Epic cosmetic token buys one on a card it is offered.
The scale stays Common to Mythic ([[R2725000]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a version, and nothing enters `PlayerView`'s
rules, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725000]] (three more versions) and [[R2725001]] (three more offers, each a seeded share of
its own).
