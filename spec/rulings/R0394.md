C #33 answers from its owner's hand only: when an enemy Spell targets one of its owner's Units — the Spell's declared target ([[§10.5]] step 1) or a pick a Spell's prompt answer makes — Joro is summoned into its owner's leftmost open unit zone (with none, nothing happens), with no Cry and summoning sick, and the pick is redirected to it (Redirect, [[§6.3]]), a replacement at "a friendly unit is targeted" ([[§6.2]]). "Targeted" means chosen by a Spell: a Spell's declared target or a Spell's prompted pick; a random pick, an "all" effect, an attack or a forced attack ([[R121]]) targets nothing ([[R450]], [[R651]]). One Joro answers one targeting, and a Spell that names several of its owner's Units redirects the first. The Radiant face doubles to 2/2 and adds Indestructible, an endless decoy
