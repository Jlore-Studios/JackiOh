Issue #5048 asked that S+ #121 Toxopid's Legs "have Flexible, … be able to spawn anywhere on the board (including your opponents side) and CRUSH (destroy) the unit below them". S+ #121.1 Toxopid Leg prints Flexible on both faces. The Cry reads "Summon N Toxopid Legs in random zones on the field, destroying the Units there" (N the declared `legs`, 3; on the Radiant face 4 Radiant Legs "on your opponent's field").

As the Cry reaches it, the zones a Leg may land in are read once: every unit zone and every backrow zone of both sides (the Radiant face: the opponent's) that a summon may land in (the test [[R3554009]]'s Leg used: not void, not reserved, no Dormant card in it, on the Toxopid's half of a slashed board) and that holds nothing or only Units (a pile, a Unit standing in a backrow zone, a GIGA Unit covering it), never a zone the Toxopid itself fills. A zone holding a set Trap, a face-down card, a Field Spell or any other card that is no Unit is not drawn: a Leg crushes Units, nothing else. N of them are drawn together from the match rng ([[R60]]), no zone twice; with fewer than N, fewer are drawn ([[R129]]). No prompt opens.

Each drawn zone is crushed, a new verb, §6.3 Crush (`crush`), which a card's text calls and no keyword names: every Unit in the zone is destroyed as Destroy marks it, so one state check collects the crushes together ([[R59]]), their Deaths and Reborns firing as any destroy's do, and Indestructible ignores the mark ([[R46]]). Then a Leg is summoned into each drawn zone that is free, belonging to the side it lands on, standing in a backrow zone as a Flexible play does ([[R1081000]], [[R3543000]]; a summon's new `backrow`, which stands a Flexible Unit in a named backrow lane and lands any other card nowhere), where it Craters as ever. A zone an Indestructible Unit kept, or that a Death or a Reborn filled before the Legs land, takes no Leg. Indestructible while a Leg stands ([[R3560006]]) is unchanged, Legs of either side and row counting.

Before issue #5048 the Cry drew empty unit zones only and crushed nothing ([[R4154300]]), which `script_before_crushing_legs` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: stacking each Leg on the pile it lands on and destroying the cards beneath it, since the state check judges a pile's top card alone and would leave the cards beneath it marked and standing; landing each Leg before the next zone is crushed, which would let one Leg's crush wake Deaths that fill a zone drawn for another before it is crushed; and crushing whatever card is in the zone, a set Trap included, which the issue's "the unit below them" does not ask.
