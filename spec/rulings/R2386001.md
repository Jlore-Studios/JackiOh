While a game holds its pools ([[R2201001]]), the pick M #50 CN Tech and M+ #2 NA Tech make from the win-rate table ([[R1100]], [[R1183000]]) leaves out Legendary and Mythic cards, as a held pool does: the walk over the rows skips them, so the leader is the best of the rest and the trailer the worst of the rest, with the same eligibility and tie-breaks. With none left the pick names none, and the card falls back as it does for an empty table. An unheld game ranks every card. The tutorial's Legendary and Mythic cards must not show before its last lesson, and the table [[R2386000]] refills leads with a Legendary, which the old table's Rare leader and trailer never did. The cards sweep that proves [[R2201001]] over the real cards (`lesson_pools.rs`) met it at once. Hearthstone semantics: a scripted encounter's pools narrow, and so does a ranking drawn from the catalog. Rejected: listing CN Tech and NA Tech among the cards that name a held card, since their text names none and the table decides; and shaping the table around the tutorial, since it holds what the games showed.
