The owner asked in #3015 (2026-11-04) that a card's looks stop being versions with augments stacked on
them: a card's look is a **variant**, one picture, and each variant is a **ladder of levels**, each level
the one below it plus one more thing. First come the variant's art levels (its normal-frame form, then
its full-art form where it has one), then **animated**, always the next level after the art, then
**signed**, only where the level below is an animated full art. Owning a level means owning every level
below it, with no gaps ([[R3015003]]). Foils, finishes and borders stay apart and layer on every level.
Nobody loses anything ([[R3015006]]).

**The model.** Every `version` entry of the registry ([[R2725000]]) carries `level: { variant, rank,
adds }`: its variant's slug, its rank on the ladder from 1, and what it adds to the level below it, one
of `art` (the variant's picture in its normal frame), `fullArt` (that picture across the whole face,
[[R1017001]]), `animated` (the picture's own layers moving, [[R2715001]]) or `signed` (the card's name
signed across it, [[R2715002]]). No other kind carries a level. The **Default** variant is the one every
card has: its first level is the normal face, which no entry names and every account owns, so its
entries start at rank 2. #3015 appends fifteen levels after #2944's two, so the registry holds 26 levels
in eleven variants:

| Variant | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Default (`default`) | the normal face, no entry | `version-animated`, Animated | | |
| Full art (`full-art`) | `version-full-art`, Full art | `version-full-art-animated`, Full art, animated | `version-full-art-signed`, Full art, animated and signed | |
| Alt art (`alt-art`) | `version-alt-art`, Alt art | `version-alt-full-art`, Alt full art | `version-alt-full-art-animated`, Alt full art, animated | `version-alt-full-art-signed`, Alt full art, animated and signed |
| Sketch (`sketch`) | `version-sketch`, Sketch | `version-sketch-full-art`, Sketch full art | `version-sketch-full-art-animated`, Sketch full art, animated | `version-sketch-full-art-signed`, Sketch full art, animated and signed |
| Extended art, Pixel, Comic, Set showcase, Out of frame, Immersive, Prismatic (`extended-art`, …) | `version-<v>`, as before | `version-<v>-animated`, "<Version>, animated" | | |

The eleven versions that shipped before keep their ids and become first levels (Alt full art Alt art's
second); the fifteen new entries are the rest. Their rarities are [[R3015001]]'s, and every level is a
chase exactly when it is Legendary or Mythic ([[R1333000]]). Like every version before it, a level is
per card, owned card by card in `card_cosmetics`, never a default or a starter, and names no card.

**The rule every ladder follows.** The registry's check (`cosmetics::problems`, `level_problems`)
refuses a version with no level and a level on any other kind; a variant that is not a lower-case slug;
ranks that do not run gapless from 1 (from 2 in the Default variant); a ladder whose levels do not add,
in rank order, its art level, then its full-art level (at least one of the two, and neither in the
Default variant), then animated, then signed only above a full art (`Art? FullArt? Animated Signed?`); a
level not rarer than the one below it unless Mythic, and a signed level not Mythic ([[R3015001]]); a
level offered otherwise than its variant's first; and a Default offer that leaves out a non-token card.
So every ladder has exactly one animated level, right after its art.

**Offers.** Every level is offered where its variant's first level is ([[R2725001]]), so a card is
offered a variant's whole ladder or none of it, but for a Legacy card's moving levels below, and a variant
is on a card when its first level is. The
Default's offer is `[{}]`, every non-token card, so every card has at least the Default ladder. Two calls
the issue left open:

- **A Legacy card** ([[R2511000]]), whose picture never moves ([[R2724000]]), is never offered a level
  that moves, animated or signed (`cosmetics::offered`), so its ladders stop before animated. This takes
  R2725003's `except` of the four Legacy cards from the Animated augment's offer to every moving level on
  every Legacy card, with no list to keep.
- **Alt art and Alt full art** were two versions on different seeded shares ([[R2940000]]); one variant
  takes one offer. Alt art's offer became its former clause followed by Alt full art's former two (their
  union), on all four of its levels, so every card offered either before is offered the Alt art ladder
  and no pick worn before stops being offered. Rejected: Alt full art's offer alone, which would take
  Alt art from the cards that had only it; and Alt art's alone, which would take Alt full art from its.

**No full-art level for the seven one-art versions.** The issue left open which variants get a full-art
level. Full art is one, Alt full art was already Alt art's, and the Sketch gains `version-sketch-full-art`,
the sketch across the whole face ([[R3015002]]), so those three ladders reach a signed level. Extended
art, Pixel, Comic, Set showcase, Out of frame, Immersive and Prismatic get none, so their ladders are art
then animated and never signed: Extended art and Out of frame already break the frame, Immersive and
Prismatic already fill and move the face, and Pixel, Comic and Set showcase restyle the picture in a
frame of their own, which a full art would throw away. Rejected: a full-art level (and so a signed one)
for each of the seven, seven new looks each undoing what its variant is.

**The augments retire.** The Animated and Signature augments (`augment-animated`, `augment-signature`)
become levels: Animated is each ladder's animated level, the Default's on the normal face, and Signature
each signed level. Like the 84 full arts before them ([[R2725000]]) they are **retired**
(`Cosmetic::is_retired`, a full art or an augment): kept with their owners, never dropped, sold or picked
again (a dev account's fill still grants them, as it grants the full arts), each row still owning the levels
it maps to ([[R3015006]]). The `augment` kind stays in
the registry for them. Nothing layers on a level but a foil, a finish and a border, as on every face
([[R1295000]], [[R2725003]]), and Signature's own rule ([[R2949000]]) has nothing left to refuse: a
signed level stands above the animated full art it needs.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a level, and nothing enters `PlayerView`'s rules, an
action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725000]] (a card wears one level of a variant, not a version with augments, and the augments
retire), [[R2725001]] (each level is offered as its variant's first, the Default on every card, a moving
level never on a Legacy card), [[R2725003]] (Animated and Signature are levels, layering on nothing),
[[R2940000]] (Alt art and Alt full art are one variant with one offer, and the Sketch gains a full art),
[[R2940002]], [[R2942002]], [[R2943002]] and [[R2944002]] (Animated on each version is its animated
level, and Alt art and the Sketch reach a signed level), [[R2942000]], [[R2943000]] and [[R2944000]]
(each version is a first level with an animated level above it) and [[R2949001]] (Signature's offer is
gone with it, each signed level offered as its variant's first).

Amended by [[R3343000]] (issue #3343): a variant's first level may be a card rarity's default look (`defaultOn`), Full art's the Mythics' and Extended art's the Legendaries', so on those cards their ladders start at the level above it; and [[R3343001]]: Prismatic's one art level is a full art.
