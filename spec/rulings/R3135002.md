Each of C+ #40 Appropriations' four options drops one more point, as issues #1149 and #1704 each took one, the numbers being the `BASE` and `RADIANT` `Face` constants of the card's file. X is at least 1 ([[R348]]), and each number is at least 1:

| Option | Base through v0.5.11p | Base now | Radiant through v0.5.11p | Radiant now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Military: +Attack and Rush | 2X−1 | 2X−2 | 4X−2 | 4X−3 |
| Education: Radiant Books shuffled in | 2X−1 | 2X−2 | 4X | 4X−1 |
| Culture: % chance to become Radiant | 7X | 6X | 17X | 16X |
| Healthcare: +Health | 2X−1 | 2X−2 | 5X | 5X−1 |
| Healthcare: Armor | X | X | 2X | 2X |

At X = 4 that is 7 → 6, 7 → 6, 28% → 24% and 7 → 6 on the base face, and 14 → 13, 16 → 15, 68% → 64% and 20 → 19 on the Radiant. The Armor is not changed.

- **The floor.** A number below 1 reads 1: at X = 1 the base face's 2X−2 is 0, and an option that buffs by +0, shuffles in no Book or adds no Health would spend the card for nothing. So the Military and Healthcare buffs and the Education count are at least 1 whatever X, which keeps what they did at X = 1 (+1, 1 Book, +1 Health); every other value is at least 1 at X = 1 already. No kept face comes near the floor.
- **Culture** still rolls each card on its own ([[R581]]), a chance of 100% or more turning every one, which is now X = 17 on the base face and X = 7 on the Radiant, where it was X = 15 and X = 6.
- **The client** restates the option texts the Discover shows, X filled in on play (`modeText.ts`, their Chinese in the web's `zh-CN.json`), and the card's words ("Choose one: Military, Education, Culture, or Healthcare.") do not change.
- **Replays.** The script through v0.5.11p is kept as `script_v0_5_11o`, with the older `script_v0_4_0f` and `script_v0_3_7b` ([[R767000]]): a game played before this change folds under the numbers it ran.

It amends the paragraph of issue #1704 that tuned the four options (2X → 2X−1, 8X% → 7X%, Radiant 20X% → 17X%, 6X → 5X) and edits §8.7 row 40.
