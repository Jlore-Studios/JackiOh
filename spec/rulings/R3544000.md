The Activate of S+ #143 Fire Beam v3, #144 v4 and #145 v5, "Burn N an enemy Unit", and of S+ #146 Scourge Mortar v3, #147 v4 and #148 v5, "Toxic N an enemy Unit", declares one target ([[R81]]): an enemy Unit on the field, as the Stinkyscape weapons' "an enemy Unit" does ([[R1862000]]) and the Drone Assemblers' "an enemy" does ([[R3537000]]), never the enemy hero. The verb adds N to the count already there ([[R3540000]]) and deals no damage, so a second use adds to the first. A declaration with no legal pick is passed, so the Activate is not offered while the opponent has no Unit, and a pick of the hero, or of a Unit of its controller's own, is refused ([[R430]]). Rejected: allowing the enemy hero as a pick, and spending a use on nothing.
