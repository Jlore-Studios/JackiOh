Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. The cards that clear the board are the game's most dramatic plays and looked like a stack of ordinary deaths (issue #2800). Each now has a **set piece**, [[R2785000]]'s one held beat per action: a card's entry in its set's effects table may be `{ setPiece: "<key>" }` ([[R2784000]]), one of `nether` (#88 Twisting Nether), `voidFold` (#100 Ceaseless Void) and `vortex`, the shared one of every other card that clears both sides on its base face (#17 Flood, Classic #43 Plague Nuke, Classic+ #1 Doom Shroom, Classic+ #12.6 Frozen Wastes, Stinkyscape #4.1 Farewell, Stinkyscape #117 Core System), in the card's own colors.

**The wipe.** A set-piece card's hook opens at its marker, a `hookResolving` ([[R2783000]]) or, for a Trap, which carries none, its `trapFired`, and closes at its play's `cardResolved` or `countered`, the next marker, a `turnStarted`, an `attackDeclared` or the game's end. Its first run of removals in that window, consecutive `destroyed`, `exiled` and `bounced` events, each bounce with the `addedToHand` (or `burned`) the engine streams right after it, is one animation entry of `WIPE_MS` (0.6 s), pinned against the burst budget as a sweep is, and it closes the hook, so a hook makes one wipe; a later removal of the same hook plays as its own entry. The hook keys it only by a definition the seat reads: a marker R97 hides keys nothing, so the other seat of a hidden card's hook sees plain removals ([[R202]]). The queue claims the wipe as its card's set piece (`wipeClaim`): `SET_PIECE_HOLD_MS` (2 s) for `nether` and `vortex`, `VOID_FOLD_HOLD_MS` (2.6 s) for `voidFold`, held, capped, scaled, shortened and skipped exactly as [[R2785000]] holds every set piece.

**What it draws.** The effects layer draws it inside the held entry, in place of the removals' own rows. The `vortex` darkens the sides the wipe takes from and turns a spiral there, the board's zones on those sides bending into it until the hold ends and snapping flat; each card it takes is drawn in along the spiral, spun and swallowed at its centre as the hold ends, with an implosion, a ring and a hard shake. The `fold` closes black in from the board's edges, each card it takes sinks where it stands, and the light comes back where the Void stands. Every card it takes is hidden from the swallow until the board shows the next view ([[R200]]'s stage). It centres on the zone a Trap fired from, even one the view still shows face-down ([[R154]]), on a Unit's own zone, else on the middle of the one side it takes from, else on the board; a Radiant Twisting Nether's "enemy permanents" draws its vortex over that side alone, the caster's untouched. A card R97 hides (a bounce into a hidden hand) is taken as the next card of its side not yet taken, units in lane order, then face-up backrow: how many cards a side lost is public, and which hidden one is not guessed. No cue names a card's definition, everything it starts lands inside the entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]) and the speed scales it with the entry ([[R201]]); under Big moments' "off" it plays inside the unheld entry, swallowing at `SET_PIECE_SWALLOW_AT` of it, and Reduce Motion and the effects off draw none of it.

**The sound bed.** Each set piece has a bed, a short sequence of the existing `sfx.ts` recipes laid across its hold and sent as its entry starts (`SET_PIECE_BEDS`), and the wipe's removals sound once, condensed as a sweep's are, as the hold ends. This amends [[R2785000]]'s rejection of a set piece's sound bed, which it deferred to the first real set piece: the takeover keeps its intro and has no bed. A card's own cast sounds as ever (Twisting Nether's voice and its rumble).

**Ceaseless Void.** Its entry is `{ setPiece: "voidFold" }`, which keeps its bespoke `voidCollapse` entrance as it lands ([[R670]]). When its Cry exiles anything, its fold is the action's one hold: the Void's own entrance yields its takeover to it (`EntryEntrance.yielded`), drawing its landing and taking no hold, so the takeover and the collapse play inside one hold, the fold replacing the takeover's `void` look. With nothing to exile there is no wipe, and the Void takes over as [[R2722001]] has it. Its music and when and for whom its entrance plays are unchanged ([[R2722000]], [[R2722002]]). This amends [[R2722001]] and [[R2785000]]: a Mythic whose own wipe in the same action is a set piece takes no takeover hold.

**The rest.** A clear of one side only, or one too small to hold the game, takes the `wipe` motif on its hook: Brawl (Classic+ #32.2), which spares a Unit; both Calls to Chaos (Classic+ #73, Stinkyscape #151), one random effect of which clears one side; the Anomaly Found! token, which clears its caster's side; and Floodgates (Training Grounds #30), whose Death destroys its own side, on a `death` hook that plans nothing yet. Psychic Scream (Stinkyscape #12.3) takes it too and draws nothing until a motif reads `shuffledIn`, whose ids R97 hides on both seats. This amends [[R2784000]], whose table gains the `{ setPiece }` entry, and [[R670]], whose marquee Void now lands through it.

[[R3338000]] (issue #3338) grows two of these: Ceaseless Void's fold now draws each card it takes into the Void's chest in turn, as its removals come, and settles with its 10/10 burning in the dark, and Farewell takes a set piece of its own, a lamp lifting each card it exiles into its light; a Mythic's takeover yields to its own signature as to its own wipe.

Rejected: grouping every multi-removal into one entry, which would change how every other card plays; sequencing the Void's takeover before its fold, two beats where [[R2785000]] allows one; cancelling a skipped set piece's bed, which the sound sink cannot do once a sound is scheduled; and a set piece for Psychic Scream, whose shuffled cards no seat can place on the board.
