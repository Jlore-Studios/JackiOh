Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V: "Immutable -> spells can target this unit." Immutable loses the targeting half patch v0.3.7 gave it ([[R781001]]): no Spell, Field Spell or Field Trap is barred from choosing an Immutable card in play, its controller's or the opponent's. The designer's "spells" is read as the whole half, since it was one rule over the three sources, and the targeting it keeps out moves to Evasive ([[R4353002]]). What Immutable still does is its text half ([[R23]], [[R386]]): the Spell may be aimed at it and resolves, and its Transform, Vanilla, Fuse-onto, Nerf or Buff leaves the card as it is, while its damage, destroy and the rest land as on any card.

So a Field Trap whose trigger names an Immutable card acts on it like any other ([[R781002]] is gone): Classic #5 Tesla deals it its damage, and C+ #74 Twice Forward, One Step Backwards fuses in a copy of it, a copy being no change to the Immutable card. A Spell that Immutable leaves with nothing to do (C+ #9 Silence's Vanilla, Meditative #24 Polymorph's Transform) may now be aimed at an Immutable card in play and spends itself to no effect, as [[R17]] has Sheepish do. A random declared target ([[R1200]]) draws from the legal targets, Immutable ones among them.
