A random "becomes Radiant" pick chooses among non-Radiant cards and changes nothing if none are left, though a pick over cards the other player may not read is still cued ([[R177]]), and a pick whose zones mix cards everyone reads with cards someone may not is split between them first ([[R242]]); a random pick of N existing cards picks N different cards, or all of them if fewer exist (designer, 2026-09-17). Cards generated from the catalog may repeat unless the card says "different"; Discover options are always different
