S+ #76 Ayys LMAO reads "Summon a mix of 3 Cloaked Ayy tokens or Cloaked Small Asteroids" with the designer's note "The Ayys spawn in the backrow if possible". "Small Asteroids" are Stinkyscape's Tiny Asteroid (#169), the one asteroid there is. Each of the three is its own fair pick between the Ayy and the Tiny Asteroid, drawn in the Cry with repeats allowed ([[R60]]), and Cloaked. An Ayy goes to the leftmost open backrow zone where it stands as a Unit for every rule ([[R1081000]]), as a Flexible Unit does when the unit row is full ([[R2248000]]), and only with no backrow zone open takes the leftmost unit zone ([[R64]]); the verb's `backrowFirst` placement says so. An asteroid takes the unit row. A full board summons fewer. The Radiant face's adjective is the Ayy's: Radiant Ayys (4/4, Poisonous) and base asteroids. S+ #85 Body Snatch transforms a Unit into the base Ayy on both faces (its text says "an Ayy"); the base face's "in Defense Position" switches the new Ayy to Defense, and the Radiant face leaves the position the Unit had.

Rejected: Cloaked on the asteroids only; Radiant asteroids; the Ayys in the unit row first; and a Radiant Ayy from Body Snatch's Radiant face.
