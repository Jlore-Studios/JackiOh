The designer's "you buy in your main phase from a button, for Credits and no mana" is two actions, built as `trade` is ([[R1838000]]). `marketBuy { offer }` buys one copy of the lot at that index of the offering. `marketSell { instanceId }` sells a Resource from the sender's tray at its quote this turn ([[R1853002]]). The designer's Resources are "sold or bought on the Marketplace", so selling is in. One function decides each, `marketplace::why_market_buy_refused` and `why_market_sell_refused`. `legalActions` lists what they pass, the reducer refuses the rest in their words, and the view gives each lot the viewer may not buy that refusal as `reason`, while they may act, so a greyed button and the refusal agree ([[§9.3]]).

A buy asks, in order, for:

- a live game in the sender's own main phase with no prompt open, in a Trade's words;
- an open Marketplace;
- a lot at that index offered to them, which is not another player's own ([[R1853005]]);
- a copy of it left to them this cycle;
- Credits for its price this turn;
- room for the card: in their tray for a Resource ([[R1840000]]), else in their hand.

A sale asks the first two, then for the card in their tray and a quote for it.

The rejected design was a button that opens a `market` prompt, as the night market's stall does ([[R1000]]), with a Leave. Opening and leaving would change nothing, so the random policy, the fuzz and the AI could loop on them forever.

A buy into a full hand is refused, not burned ([[R4]]). A full tray is refused too, not sold at once ([[R1840001]]). Hearthstone's shops refuse a purchase they could not hand over, and burning a card just paid for would only waste the Credits. A buy pays the price from the buyer's Credits and no mana. Its card is a fresh card of the lot on the lot's face, counted against the buyer's stock. It arrives as `marketBought`, then `marketCreditsChanged`, then `addedToHand` or, for a Resource, `resourceGained`.

A purchase is hidden as a gain is ([[R97]]; the designer's answer that the opponent sees the tray's count only, ST9 Q5). `marketBought` names the card as `addedToHand` does, so the other seat reads a card back and neither its id, its definition nor its face. The view sends each player only their own stock (`left`) and their own sales, and the AI's redaction drops the opponent's purchases. Its price is public, because Credits are. The offering, its prices and the quotes are public too. So the other player may tell which lot it was when no other lot costs as much. A sale is public, as a Resource sold from a full tray is ([[R317]]): `marketSold` names the card and its Credits, the card ceases to exist, and `marketCreditsChanged` follows.

The client's shop ([[§10.10]]) renders only the view and `legalActions`. It is live exactly where a deal is listed and greyed with `reason` otherwise.
