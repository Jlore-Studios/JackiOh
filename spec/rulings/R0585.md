"A random other card … on your side of the field" never names Chaos Machine itself: the Upgrade's pick is drawn from every other card in its controller's hand and on their side of the field (tops of piles, face-down ones included). Alone on its side with an empty hand, the Upgrade finds nothing and changes nothing, while the Degrade still resolves. The Upgrade is drawn and applied before the Degrade
