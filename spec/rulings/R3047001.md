A card bought at the Marketplace is sold at (0): the Credits are its whole price ([[R1853003]], which took none of the buyer's mana), and the card then costs no mana to play either. The buy sets the new card's cost override ([[R65]]) to `MARKETPLACE_BOUGHT_COST`, 0, before it is added to the hand, so the hand keeps it as it keeps any price given on the way to it, and the card's price is that override plus its cost modifier ([[R65]]): a later discount or tax on the card stacks on it, and [[R3047003]] keeps the lot's Nerfs from lifting it. It holds for every lot, a Unit, a Spell or an X-cost card, whose override makes it free ([[R65]]), and for the lot an effect added for the rest of the game ([[R1853005]]).

The override goes where every override goes. A card that reaches a graveyard or exile costs its printed cost again ([[R766]], `zones::reset_price`), so a bought card discarded and played again from a graveyard that allows it is a full-price play. The cost the view shows on the card is (0) while it is in the hand. The rejected reading, a card that costs (0) the first time it is played only, would need a mark the card carries for as long as it stays unplayed, for no rule the designer's "sold at (0)" asks for.

Playing the card needs no mana, so it is something to do: a turn whose last Marketplace deal has been made does not end by itself ([[R82]]) while the card bought can still be played.
