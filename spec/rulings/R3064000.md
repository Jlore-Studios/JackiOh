[[R2380000]] priced a Dormant card ([[§6.1]], [[R1839000]]) as the unit it wakes as, a share of it for each turn left on its countdown, and nothing for what its "When this awakens" text ([[§6.2]], [[R1839002]]) does as it wakes. Stinkyscape #19 Imprisoned Cosmality, whose wake deals 10 damage randomly split among all enemies, read as a sleeping 10/6 Pierce, and #17 Imprisoned Ship Smith, whose wake summons a Fiery Waraxe, as a sleeping 2/2, so the AI seldom played either (`training/history/sweep-2026-10-29-4.md`: Ship Smith 3 times in 159 affordable turns at Easy, Cosmality twice in 208) and read an enemy one as its body alone. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** At a decision's root, once, on the same probe world as [[R2424000]]'s Death prices and [[R2688000]]'s attack-hook prices (a function of what the seat sees, [[R185]]), the AI prices each card whose face has a trigger on `awakened` (`has_wake_trigger`), each once, in this order: every Dormant card topping a zone on the seat's field, then every one on the enemy's, whose status and face are public, each as its controller's, then each Unit in the seat's own hand, placed first by the sandbox's spawn ([[R923001]]) as [[R2424000]]'s hand probe places it, priced only when it enters Dormant. On a sandbox copy ([[R923000]]) with both players' automatic turn end off ([[R345]]), it wakes the card as the engine does (`slumber::awaken_card`) and lets the resolution loop drain what that queued (`settled_after`), under the game's pool hold ([[R2201001]]) on the copy's own random stream. Beside it, from the same state, it wakes the card's hookless twin, as [[R2688000]] makes it: Vanilla ([[§6.3]]), so its trigger does not run, and granted the keywords it prints, its text given back before the twin's state is scored. The price is the controller's evaluation after the one wake less after the other, plus `AI_EVAL.next_turn_mana` for each crystal the one gained over the other, kept signed, 0 included. A card is keyed by its definition, its face and its controller, as [[R2688000]]'s are. `slumber_worth` adds `AI_EVAL.awaken` of the price inside the slumber share, so a card two turns from waking counts a quarter of its body and of its wake alike, for the seat's sleepers and the enemy's, and an enemy Cosmality counts against the AI for the damage it will deal. `AI_EVAL.awaken` is 1.0, not the untuned 0.5 the earlier prices started at: the share already discounts the wait, and no effect reaches a Dormant card ([[§6.1]]), so its wake comes; at 0.5, as at 0, the AI at Medium ends its turn rather than play a lone Ship Smith, and plays a Felinor Token over a Cosmality. At 1.0 the smoke gate read 20, 19 and 20 of 20, against random, against greedy and as Hard against Easy (floors 17, 10 and 16); at 0, 20, 19 and 20. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, and its shares are 0, so it reads no price. The probes run with no price passed and within `AI_AWAKEN_PROBE_NODES` of the decision's nodes ([[R186]]'s budget), a cap of their own, and spend none when no card has a wake trigger. Cosmality's split is one roll on the copy's random stream, so its price is that roll's. The prices travel with that one decision in `Prices`, beside the others, so a call outside a decision is passed none and prices nothing.

**What is not priced.**
- A card in the enemy's hand, and a face-down card.
- A card beneath a Stack pile, which does not wake there ([[R1839003]]).
- A Dormant card with no wake trigger, the Dormant Starship among them, whose launch is the engine's and no trigger ([[R1852003]]).
- A wake that leaves a prompt open or ends the game, which the probe neither answers nor scores.
- A card the probes' nodes did not reach, and every card while a prompt is open or once the game is over.

**Approximation.** The probe wakes every card on the seat's turn, so an enemy Ship Smith's Waraxe enters as it would on the AI's turn, not animated as it would be on its controller's. The twin switches off all of the card's text for its wake, not its trigger alone, so any other text of the card that answers the same `awakened` is counted in the price too; no shipped card prints such text.

**Rejected.** A weight above 1, which would count the wake for more than it gives; a flat premium per trigger, which cannot tell 10 damage from a weapon; any change to the engine or a card's script, which the evaluation does not need; drawing the probes from another probe's node cap, which would take the nodes the Deaths or the attack hooks drew before; and the issue's tests of Ship Smith over a 1-mana vanilla and Cosmality over a vanilla 2-drop: the 1- and 2-drops with no text are Mr. Vanilla (1) 4/4, Spikey Steven (1) 5/2 and Greaser (2) 7/7, each counted in full when played against a sleeper's quarter share, so beating one would take a weight of about 4, and a rival shrunk by a stat override skews its hand price. The tests prove the nearest forms: Ship Smith alone at one crystal is played at Medium and Hard only with the price, and Cosmality over a Felinor Token, a (1) 1/1 with no text, only with the price.
