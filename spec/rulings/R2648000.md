"Your table" ([[R639]], [[R1584000]], [[R1743000]]) named the wrong kind of card for several of its stats, because a counter means something else for each card type. A Spell never sends `destroyed` (it resolves into the graveyard), so a Spell or a sprung Trap always has `destroyed` 0 and was named Unbreakable; and a Unit fed to a Tribute or a Sacrifice ([[R42]], [[R119]]), or crumbled by Brittle ([[R638]]), does send `destroyed`, so "lost" counted cards the player gave up themselves. Presentation only, on the device (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no engine, event or endpoint change. Every card is still read from the viewer's seat ([[R97]]), and a stat with no data is still left out.

- **A death counts only when the other seat caused it.** `destroyed` (the viewer's card died) and `defeated` (the opponent's card died) are counted from the batch's events by `countDeaths` (`track.ts`), no longer one `destroyed` at a time. A death counts when either (a) its `killerId` names a card on the side across from its owner, looked up in the view before the batch and then the view after it, since the killer may have died in the same combat, or (b) the last `cardPlayed`, `activated`, `trapFired` or `attackDeclared` before it in the same batch was that seat's (a destroy Spell has no `killerId`, [[R89]]; an attack's seat is its attacker's, read where the views show it). Two kinds of death are never counted: one that follows a `crumbled` of the same instance in its batch (Brittle ran out, [[R638]]), and one whose batch's last actor was the card's own side with no killer from the other (a Tribute or a Sacrifice, [[R42]] and [[R119]], or the player's own sweeper). A card the sentinel hides names nothing ([[R97]]). Totals already stored keep their counts: nothing is migrated, and only games from now on use this rule. Fallen Hero, Most Toppled, Glass Cannon, Easy Target and Unbreakable all read these counters, so all five are fixed by it.
- **Unbreakable** (most played, `destroyed` 0) names only a Unit (catalog `type` Unit) that prints no Indestructible on either face (it cannot be destroyed, so "never destroyed" says nothing) and has at least `SHOWCASE_MIN_PLAYS` plays.
- **Glass Cannon** (`destroyed` per play) and **Workhorse** (`damageDealt`) name only a Unit; a burn Spell is not a workhorse. Heavy Hitter, Overkill and Last Laugh keep any card type.
- **Medic counts a Lifesteal hit.** A `healed` names no source ([[R1743000]]), so it counts toward the viewer's card that is the source when it comes, and two more events set the source: an `attackDeclared` whose attacker is on the viewer's side sets it to that card (the opponent's, or one the view does not show, sets it to none), and a `damage` with a source on the viewer's side sets it to that card, so a Lifesteal hit's `healed` after its `damage` is credited to it. A `damage` from anything else clears it.
- **Hot Streak** names the card with the most `playedInWin`, the line unchanged ("best streak: 6 wins"), and so no longer the card Old Faithful names whenever the most played card is not the one with the most wins.
- **The Record** names the card played in the most games (`playedInWin` plus `playedInLoss`), with the record line unchanged, where Familiar Face keeps the top `seen` card; they can now differ. The Record is left out while no card has a game played in.
- **Ride or Die and Survivor** read `survived`, which counts only in a game the player won. Ride or Die is `survived` per `playedInWin` (a card with at least `SHOWCASE_MIN_PLAYS` wins it was played in), "still standing at the end of 5 of the 6 wins you played it in", instead of per play, which counted every loss against the card. Survivor says "standing at the end of a win 7×".
- **A lunge fits the card's type.** `animationFor(defId, wanted)` (`showcase.ts`) is the one place the choice is made, and `showcaseStats` calls it for every stat. A stat whose animation is `lunge` (Arch-Nemesis, Last Laugh, Grudge Match, Never Tried It, The Closer, Heavy Hitter, Overkill, Brawler, and Workhorse, now Units only) plays it only on a Unit or a card that prints Animated or Animated on your turn. On a Spell or a Field Spell it plays the new stage animation `cast`, and on a Trap or a Field Trap it plays `trap`. Every other animation, and a card the catalog does not hold, keeps what the stat asked for. `cast` is added to `STAGE_ANIMATIONS` the way [[R1584000]] added the others, with no new keyframe or cue: the stage gives the board's planner a `cardPlayed` by the viewer's seat for the stage's card, and the board's entry for it (`jk-card-played`, the `cast` effects recipe) is what plays. That entry's target is the card where the view shows it (`locateInstance`), because the event names the viewer's seat, so it lands on the stage's card on either lane, never on a hand the stage does not draw: the stage card needs no second testid and `entrance` is not the fallback.
- **Comeback Kid is Lucky Charm** (key `luckyCharm`). It is the card's share of the games it was played in that were won, not a comeback. Its card and its line ("won 9 of the 12 games you played it in") are unchanged.

`zh-CN.json` has the new and changed words: "Lucky Charm", "standing at the end of a win {n}×" and "still standing at the end of {n} of the {m} wins you played it in".

Choices a person may want to review, each with the alternative rejected. A death with no killer and no actor counts for nobody (rejected: counting it for the seat whose turn it is, which would charge a Tribute to the player again). The seat that caused a death is the one across from the card's owner and the counter it goes to is the owner's too (rejected: the one across from its controller, [[R172]], which would judge a death by one seat and file it under the other). Any `damage` that is not from the viewer's card clears the heal's source (rejected: keeping the source, which credits an enemy Lifesteal's heal to the viewer's attacker). The Record is left out while no card has a game played in (rejected: falling back to the top `seen` card, which is Familiar Face's again). A lunge on the opponent's side casts too (rejected: `entrance` there). Totals stored before this ruling are not recounted (rejected: a migration, for a figure the device cannot rebuild from what it kept). It amends [[R1582000]], [[R1584000]] and [[R1743000]], and edits [[§9.11]].
