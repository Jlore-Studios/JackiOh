Cast a card that exists (hand, deck, graveyard, exile, never the field; it leaves its pile as the cast begins and lands in its owner's piles), or a new card of a named definition (caster-owned, to the caster's graveyard, [[R87]]). A non-random cast X card asks its caster for X (1..mana, at least 1) before its targets and modes, and pays nothing. "Then exile them" exiles the resolved Spell at step 7.
