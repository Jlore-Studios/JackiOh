A change made to cards someone may not read (a hand, a deck, a face-down trap) never tells the other player how many cards a filter let through. A Nerf, a Buff ([[R1320]]) or KY's Constant reports every application on such a card, with change `none` where nothing changed (Immutable, nothing applicable, or left out by the scope's filters). A scope or random pick reaches every hidden card of its piles whatever its filters say, and its events go out in [[R242]]'s group order. A Brittle tick, and a buff, keyword, count or enchantment given over a scope, is reported only on cards both players read; the rest change silently and their owners read the change off the view. A hidden card's Brittle count never appears in an event.
