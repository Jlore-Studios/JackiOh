[[R351]] shows both players a face-down Trap's or Field Trap's cost, and [[R185]]'s redaction keeps that number on its placeholder. A determinization fills each face-down card on top of a backrow zone from the Traps and Field Traps that would show that cost there (printed cost read as on the field, [[R65]]), still minus what the opponent has shown and any whose live aura the board rules out ([[R602]]), in lane order; with no unseen trap of that cost left it draws from the trap pool as before. A card dormant under a backrow pile ([[R447]]) or waiting to be set ([[R448]]) shows no cost and is drawn as before. So the AI reads a back as a player does: a (3) is one of the two Shrooms, and its lethal solver no longer swings at the face into one. The greedy baseline the quality gates measure against keeps the sampler the gates were fixed on, as it keeps its frozen weights
