At the start of every turn, before anything else happens, the state keeps a snapshot of the field — both sides' piles and backrow zones as whole instances, and their Locks — and holds the last four ([[§10.1]]). C+ #35's "N turns ago" is the snapshot taken at the start of the player-turn N before this one, and its Radiant face also chooses a side or both. Restoring a side: first each card on it now that the snapshot does not hold leaves the field for its controller's hand (a Bounce, [[R747]]; a token ceases to exist, [[R11]]); then each card the snapshot holds is put back in its zone as it was (damage, buffs, position, counters, controller), moved from wherever it is now or recreated from the snapshot if it no longer exists (a token, a card transformed or fused away); then that side's Locks become the snapshot's. Nothing else changes: health, mana, decks and graveyards stay, but for the cards that moved. No Cry and no Death fire, and one `rolledBack` event reports it beside the moves
