A Field Spell's unlabelled one-time text is its Cry: C+ #12.7 Legion of the Hungry resolves its text as a Cry when played and then stays in the backrow with no further text, a body for backrow effects to hit (C+ #12.6 Frozen Wastes did too, until balance patch 1 made it a Spell that resolves and goes to the graveyard). Frozen Wastes' "a card" is the top card of the deck, as Hearthstone's Doom Pact takes it, and "each one" counts the Units that died, not an Indestructible survivor; Legion's five cards are five different random ones ([[R60]]), and the Units among them are summoned out of exile with no Cry until the board is full, the rest staying exiled
