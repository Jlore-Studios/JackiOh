Issue #5038 asked that C+ #37 Wardrum read in game as M #87 Tatches the Totem does, and then that every card answering from its hand, deck or graveyard be worded alike. [[§6.2]] writes such a trigger "While this is in your hand / deck: …", and [[R3941000]]'s sweep retired the openings it knew ("In your hand or deck", "While in your deck"), but two printings still differed: S+ #89 Animosity's "When your exile has 5 cards, cast this, wherever in your deck it is." and Core #89 Corpse Eater's (and TG #56 Legacy: Corpse Eater's) "While this is in your hand, it gains the attack and health of each Unit that dies.". And Wardrum's whole text runs past the face's limit ([[R1017000]]), so it prints words written for it ([[R3941001]]), which reworded its trigger "In hand or deck, summoned after …".

Now a face whose script answers from where the card waits (a hand, deck or graveyard trigger, `castFromDeckWhen`, or a replacement read from the hand) prints that trigger as "While this is in your <zone>: …", the zones exactly those the script answers from, "hand or deck" for both. Animosity reads "While this is in your deck: When your exile has 5 cards, cast this.", Corpse Eater "While this is in your hand: When a Unit dies, this gains its attack and health." (Radiant: "… twice its attack and health."), a line shorter, so both its faces fit whole and print no written words. Written words keep any such line of the whole text word for word, `{key}`s and all, and take their saving from the face's other lines: Wardrum's end-of-turn line reads "Recast a random non-Unit played this turn." (Radiant: "Recast each non-Unit played this turn."), and Animosity's first "Starts last in your deck.". A "While this is in your hand, …" with no colon after its zone is an aura, no trigger.

Three printings name their moment without the zone, as they did: [[§6.2]]'s Float term ("Float reduces cost.", "Float reduces Tribute requirement by 1.", a hand trigger on its owner's Float, [[R2657002]]); a discard ("When you discard this", M #22.1; "Evolves when this is discarded", S+ #6.2), a graveyard trigger on the discard; and a Spell's "End of turn:" or "Start of turn:" (C #55 Book of Wildfire, S+ #6.1 Fine Fermented Grape), read from the hand, the one place a Spell waits. No rule reads card text, but a number a line declares is read when that line is: Animosity's exile count, now written on its deck line, is marked read from the hand or deck, so a Buff or Nerf reaches it only there ([[R3888000]]).
