C+ #37 Wardrum and M #87 Tatches the Totem are summoned from where they wait by a trigger whose whole text opens "While this is in your hand or deck:" and "While this is in your deck:". Tatches fits the face's limit ([[R1017000]]) and prints that line; Wardrum's whole text is a line over it, so it prints words written for it ([[R3941001]]), which had reworded the trigger ("In hand or deck, summoned after …"), and the two cards read differently in game for one mechanic. Written words now keep any such line of the whole text word for word, its `{key}`s and all (a trigger from a hand, deck or graveyard, "While this is in your <zone>: …"; Core #89's "While this is in your hand, it gains …" is an aura, not a trigger), and take their saving from the face's other lines: Wardrum's end-of-turn line reads "Recast a random non-Unit played this turn." (Radiant: "Recast each non-Unit played this turn."), and both faces print six lines. Presentation only: no rule reads card text, and the whole text stays where [[R3941001]] puts it.
