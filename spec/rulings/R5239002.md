FvH #100 Call to Chaos (Felinors vs. Humans Edition) summons "Felinors that have Death or Reborn" and "Humans that have Aura". Two filters join [[§5.1]]'s query. `hooks: [death | aura | cry]` keeps a definition whose base face has at least one of the hooks named, read off its registered script without state as `turnHook` is ([[R3134001]]): `death` its Death, `cry` its Cry (a Spell's resolution runs from the same hook, so a Spell with any text has one), and `aura` a board aura, a hand aura or a cost aura. A hook a card gains in play (a grant, a fusion) never puts it in a pool, and a definition with no registered script has none. `anyOf: [q, …]` keeps a definition the rest of the query keeps that also matches at least one of its queries; each narrows by the fields it names alone: one that names no set takes the definition's own, since the outer query chose the sets ([[R380]], [[R1420]]), and a token the outer query let in stays in ([[R382]]). An empty `anyOf` matches nothing. "Death or Reborn" is `anyOf: [{hooks: [death]}, {keywords: [Reborn]}]`.
