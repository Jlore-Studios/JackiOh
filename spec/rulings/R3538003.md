"Strongest" is chosen among a card's past versions only: the past Unit definition with the highest
attack + health on the face the unit wears, then the highest cost (`query_cost`, R65), then the
latest. A past unit's Past is its own earlier versions. Tokens are included, and a card with no past
version is untouched. Immutable refuses (R23), and no Cry runs on either side (R1). Rejected:
weighing the card's current version too, which the text's "from their Past" excludes — the Radiant
face would then often keep the present as the strongest and do nothing.
