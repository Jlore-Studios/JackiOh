Felinors vs Humans #5 Reanimator's Radiant face reads "Discover a Radiant Unit with a Death. Summon it and trigger its Death." Its base face's random summon draws nothing when no card of its pool has a zone to go to ([[R129]]), but the Discover is a choice of a card, not a summon, and the text does not say whether it is made when the summon cannot land.

**The rule.** As in Hearthstone, where a Discover that summons is still offered on a full board: the Discover ([[§6.3]]) is made whatever the board, three options of the pool, and its answer summons the pick Radiant into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]). With none open the summon lands nothing ([[R129]]), so no Unit was summoned and no Death is triggered ([[R4154205]] runs the Death of a Unit this Cry summoned, and there is none).
