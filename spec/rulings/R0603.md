Every player carries a hidden Glicko-2 rating — rating, deviation and volatility — starting at `RATING_START` with a new player's deviation (`RATING_DEVIATION_START`) and volatility (`RATING_VOLATILITY_START`). One rated game is one rating period for both sides, each rated against the other's rating from before the game, so neither side's update depends on which is computed first. Draws, offered or by the turn cap, score 0.5 for both; concedes and disconnects count as losses like any other ending. The update is deterministic: the same inputs give the same outputs on every machine, checked against Glickman's worked example and independently computed reference values, draws included
