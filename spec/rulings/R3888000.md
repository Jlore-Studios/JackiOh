A Nerf or a Buff ([[R386]]) draws only a change that can still change what the card does in the game, from the zone it is in or one it reaches in the normal course: a deck card reaches a hand, and a card on the field a graveyard, unless it is a unit token, which ceases to exist there ([[R11]]). A bounce, a resurrect or a "Trigger a Cry" is not the normal course, as [[R1748000]] reasoned about the cost, and as Hearthstone never reprices a minion in play nor runs its Battlecry again. [[§6.3]] holds the table: for each kind of change (the price; a keyword that acts from a hand, as the card is played or enters, on the turn it enters, while it stays, or from a graveyard; an X; a number by when it is read, [[R3888001]]; the Plague row), whether it is live in a deck, a hand, being played ([[§10.5]] step 3), on the field face-up, face-down, Dormant, in a graveyard and in an exile pile. The engine reads it from one place (`effects::tune_live`): every row of the menu, `applicable_changes`, and a hidden Nerf, which reads the being-played column ([[R1845001]]). Each row keeps its own conditions besides (types, bounds, [[R65]], Vanilla, Immutable), and the draw is as before: the row first, then the item ([[R442]]), with no draw where there is one choice ([[R129]]).

What the table changes. On the field a Nerf no longer takes off, nor a Buff gives, a Rush or a Charge after the turn the Unit entered, unless its own Reborn would bring it back sick ([[R83]]); nor an Echo, a Crater, an Equip, a Stack, a GIGA, a Tipsy or a Flexible, which acted as the card was played or entered; nor a Cloaked, a Tradable or a Temporary, which act from a hand ([[R2577000]]); nor a number only its Combo, its Cast on draw (on a Unit), its awakening once awake, or a hand or a deck reads; nor a number its Evolve reads once it has evolved ([[R3536000]]), nor one its awakening reads once it has awoken, in any zone ([[R3039000]]). A unit token never takes Flashback. A hidden Nerf never draws the Tribute step 2 has just paid ([[R2261000]]). A card face-down on the field, set by its Cloaked and not yet revealed, is read as the card it is ([[R783005]]): its Cry numbers, still to run as it reveals, are drawn, as are its stats and [[R21]]'s pool for a Unit, and its price is not. In a deck Cloaked and Crater are live as in a hand ([[R2727000]], [[R2727001]]), and a Nerf may give a face-down Trap Crater, which acts as it fires. In a graveyard only a card a play may take from there (a permission or its Flashback) has its stats, keywords, X and numbers drawn, its price aside ([[R766]]), and any card may take Flashback, which would let one; in an exile pile nothing is drawn. The Plague row is the field's, face-up or Dormant ([[R1748002]]).

Decisions the issue left open, each with what it rejected. A card that casts itself on draw keeps its price and its hand keywords in a deck, since its draw adds it to the hand uncast past [[R58]]'s cap or, for a Unit, with no unit zone open ([[R459]]); rejected: taking them off as never paid. A Spell's "Cast on draw:" line is its resolution, run wherever it is cast, so its numbers are read as it is played; a Unit's runs only as its draw casts it (`cast_on_draw_now`), so in a hand it is spent (C+ #26 Tommy Tempo). Rush and Charge are live on a Dormant card, whose awakening makes it summoning sick, and on a face-down one, which arrives as it reveals. An Evolve number is live in a graveyard while the card has not evolved, since an Evolve reaches a graveyard card where it lies (S+ #6.2 Stinky Cheese evolves there). Lucky stays a lasting keyword: a roll can come after the Cry (S #77 Yuma's offer at the next turn's start), and no mark says which rolls a card makes; rejected: a card-level mark for the cards whose only roll is their Cry. Combo X needs no row of its own: no Combo keyword is on the menu, and a Combo line's number is marked `combo` ([[R3888001]]). "When played face down:" writes no number, so it takes no mark. Targeting is unchanged: a random "Buff a card" may still land on a card with no live change and leave it alone with no draw ([[R129]]), and KY's Constant and its Discover are no Nerf or Buff and still reach any number ([[R1748000]]).

This amends [[R386]], [[R1748000]], [[R2261000]], [[R2261001]], [[R1845001]], [[R2727000]], [[R2727001]] and [[R3596001]]; a Marketplace lot's Nerfs still spare the cost row besides ([[R3047003]]). It moves rng draws: a replay folded under an earlier version that Buffed or Nerfed a card whose menu the table changed ends on another hash and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]).
