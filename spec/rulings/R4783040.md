Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII asked the AI to play Classic #37 Last Hurrah and Stinkyscape+ #79 A Glimpse into the Endless Sea far less: only with no other move to make or when times are dire, and A Glimpse also when it has a way to shuffle cards back into its deck. Its search priced both far above their cost: a fatigue draw counts nothing in its evaluation, so drawing the whole deck read as a full hand for free, and a countdown to a win is scored as a share of the win ([[R3696000]]), so a board-and-deck wipe with seven turns to survive read as nearly won. This is a judgement about the AI, not a rule of the game, and changes no game.

**The hold.** The cards are `AI_LAST_RESORT` (`crates/ai/src/config.rs`). A play of one, from the hand or the graveyard, is among the search's candidates (`candidates::hold_last_resorts`, inside the pool every caller of the move list reads, the lethal solver's and the opponent model's included) only while one of these holds:

- **dire:** the seat's life is at most `AI_LAST_RESORT_DIRE_LIFE` (10, a third of `HERO_HEALTH`), or the face damage the enemy's board could deal it next turn (`face_threat`, the reading the wall switches use, [[R2976000]]) reaches its life;
- **no other play:** it has played no card this turn and its legal actions play no card but last-resort ones. A turn that played its other cards first has had its plays, so the card is never the last action of every turn whose hand ran out;
- **a refill (A Glimpse alone, `AI_LAST_RESORT_REFILLED`):** a card of its own other than the Glimpse, in its hand, shuffles cards into its own deck by the text of the face it runs: "into your deck" (C #30 Recycle, S+ #24 Forced Propagation, …) or "into both decks" (M #54 Money Machine), never "into your opponent's deck".

Every other action stays, the Trade of a Tradable Last Hurrah among them ([[R1838000]]). All of it reads what the seat can see, its own hand, field and turn and the public board, so every world a determinization samples keeps or drops the same plays ([[R185]]).

Not modelled: a burn spell in the opponent's hidden hand (the dire life stands in for it), and a library small enough that Last Hurrah would burn nothing. Rejected: a penalty on the evaluation of a line that plays one, which a countdown's share of a win outweighs at any size short of one that warps every other line; and dropping the cards from the AI's decks, which is the shadow ban's job ([[R186]]) and would leave a deck that draws them unable to use them when they are its best move.
