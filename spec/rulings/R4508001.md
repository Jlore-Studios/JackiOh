The fuzz monitor's I21 fails a step in which a Spell changes a Unit Immune to Spells ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]; issue #4508). Core #87 Pocket Chaos's board swap ([[R73]]), a rotation ([[R14]]) and C+ #35 Rollback's restore ([[R419]]) move such a Unit, and no ruling said whether a Spell may.
**It may.** A swap, a rotation and a restore move every zone's contents, not a card an effect reached for, as [[R4454000]] and [[R4454002]] read them for a Dormant card: the immune Unit moves with its zone, changing controller when it lands on the other side, or goes back as the snapshot holds it, or to its hand when the snapshot did not hold it or its destination is Locked, reserved or void.
**What I21 allows.** A `controlChanged` or a `bounced` naming an immune Unit after a board `swapped`, a `rotated` or a `rolledBack`, and a restore's `radiantSet`, until the next card's hook, trap or turn edge. Anything else in that window that names it is still a reach.
Rejected: passing it by as a board replacement does ([[R1855000]]). A swap or a rotation must empty every zone to fill it, so an immune Unit left standing would bounce the card bound for its zone, and the board would no longer be the mirror, the turn or the snapshot the card prints.
