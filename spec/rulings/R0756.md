#98's `ping`, X 1: "Pierce. Deal {ping} damage." (1 as printed), to any unit or hero on either side, declared with the activation ([[R81]]); Pierce is the power's own, so the hit ignores Armor ([[§4.4]] step 2, [[R346]]). The Radiant face adds "If this kills a Unit, summon a Ghoul Token with its stats": after the state check that follows the hit ([[R59]]), if the Unit left the field in it — destroyed, Reborn's body put back included, judged against the activation's own mark ([[R174]]) — a Ghoul Token is summoned for you with that Unit's attack and max health as they stood before the hit (`statsOverride`, [[R353]]): its stats, not the health it had left (at its printed 1 damage Ping kills only a Unit on 1; the card's declared `ping` may be tuned higher while it has Ping, [[R386]], [[R1425]]). A hero hit, or a Unit the hit leaves standing, summons nothing
