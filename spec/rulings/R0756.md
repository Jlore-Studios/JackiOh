#98's `ping`, X 1: "Pierce. Deal 1 damage." to any unit or hero on either side, declared with the activation ([[R81]]); Pierce is the power's own, so the hit ignores Armor ([[§4.4]] step 2, [[R346]]). The Radiant face adds "If this kills a Unit, summon a Ghoul Token with its stats": after the state check that follows the hit ([[R59]]), if the Unit left the field in it — destroyed, Reborn's body put back included, judged against the activation's own mark ([[R174]]) — a Ghoul Token is summoned for you with that Unit's attack and max health as they stood before the hit (`statsOverride`, [[R353]]): its stats, not its 1 health left, since Ping kills only a Unit on 1. A hero hit, or a Unit the hit leaves standing, summons nothing
