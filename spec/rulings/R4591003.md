IA #156 Mirror Firewall's "the damage this took" is the sum of the `Damage` amounts that combat's window landed on it, after Armor — 0 when Divine Shield took the hit, which lands no `Damage` — read by `afterAttacked` (MD-C26, [[R1026]]) as the closing check closes. It applies even when the Unit died, on the snapshot it fought with ([[R89]]). It does nothing when the attacker is gone (the facts' `attackerSurvived` is false) or nothing landed. IA #150's "Deal {damage} damage to the attacker" is no such reading: it deals its declared number.
