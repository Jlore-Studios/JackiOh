The opponent's reply ([[§9.9]]) tries at most `AI_REPLY.visible_activations` (4) of the Activates the AI can see on the field (issue #1036, [[R384]]), to stay quick. The tries aimed at the AI's hero came first, so a busy board never hid a lethal; after them came the first ones `legal_actions` lists. That order is card by card and lane by lane: each source's abilities, each ability's Tribute sets, each set crossed with every target, the AI's Units in lane order. So the four tries were the first source's first targets. S #56 Cannon v4 facing a full row of five tried lanes 1 to 4, and the reply never destroyed the Unit in lane 5, whatever it was worth, so every line that put a Unit there read it as out of the Cannon's reach. While the first source had three or more targets among the AI's cards, a second source, or a second ability of the same card (a fused card keeps each ingredient's, [[R3291000]], a launched Starship's guns among them), was never tried. C #21 Turtinator's Tribute sets filled the cut with one hit per set on the same target. The root's activation price ([[R2564000]]) tries each card's uses through the same step, so a second ability, or any target past the fourth, went unpriced there too, for the AI's own cards as for the enemy's.

Now the tries are ordered before the cut:

1. Every try aimed at the AI's hero, in legal order, each of its Tribute sets included. This is unchanged, so no lethal the old order saw is lost.
2. Then the tries aimed at a card of the AI's, one per pick. A pick is the try's source, ability, modes and targets; a second Tribute set of the same pick waits. The picks are dealt round-robin over the visible sources and their abilities, in legal order: each source-and-ability's best target, then each one's second, and so on. Each source-and-ability ranks its targets by the AI's `unit_worth` of the Unit aimed at, highest first; ties keep legal order, and a target that is not one of the AI's Units (a backrow card of its) ranks after its Units, in legal order.
3. Then the Tribute sets that waited, in legal order.
4. Then every other try, an ability with no target among the AI's hero and cards, in legal order.

Then the cut to `AI_REPLY.visible_activations`, unchanged. So a step still tries at most four uses, one node each, and spends the same nodes as before on the same board; it reaches the Unit worth the most to the AI, and each gun a card or a board shows. `AI_REPLY.spread_activations` (on) switches it: off, the tries are sorted by aim alone, the order before it. The order reads only the tries the AI can see (their sources face-up on the field, none of the hidden ones) and the `unit_worth` of the AI's own Units under the decision's prices, so a card the AI cannot see changes no try and no decision ([[R185]]). In Hearthstone a player aims removal at the best target it has, and the reply now expects that shot.

Two limits are left as they are. A hit on a card keeps the first Tribute set of its pick among the first tries, so which of its Units a Turtinator tributes for it stays the first in legal order; its hero hits keep every set. And a target is ranked by its worth to the AI, not by whether the hit kills it.

Rejected:
- Ranking each target by whether the hit kills it. Telling takes a node per try, and the reply's cut exists to bound those.
- A fifth try, or more. It costs nodes on every step that shows an Activate, and the four reach every source once each before any source's second target.
- Choosing which Unit a hit on a card tributes. It would rank the Tribute sets by a worth of their own; the hero's hits already keep every set.
- A `spread` parameter on `visible_activation`, with its two callers passing the switch. The ordered and cut tries are split out (`visible_tries`), so a test passes the switch there and both callers read `AI_REPLY`.

No screen was played: the scene tests settle it, as [[R4433000]]'s switches were settled.
