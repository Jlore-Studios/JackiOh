Issue #4154 asked to make the AI better at using Classic #62 Living Bomb, "basically a 3 mana destroy a unit": its base face's Cry places a Plague Counter, its Radiant face's Activate does ([[R689]]), and its turn hooks destroy the plagued permanents of the player whose turn starts ([[R400]]). The search saw the destroy only where a line ended its turn and the next began, while the evaluation it ranks open lines by priced a Plague Counter as Fed Fauci's banked mana, a gain for the side it sits on, and the placement prompt lists the placer's own permanents first: so the beam kept the lines that put the counter on the AI's own permanents and dropped those that aimed it at the enemy. This is a judgement about the AI, not a rule of the game, and changes no game.

**Doomed.** A permanent is doomed while it carries a Plague Counter (public, [[R471]]), it is not Indestructible ([[R46]]), and a card of `AI_PLAGUE_BOMBS` (Living Bomb) holds the hook that fires at the start of the permanent's controller's next turn where the engine's trigger queue finds it (`trigger_holders_with_hook`: face-up, its text on): the start-of-turn hook of one its controller holds (the base face's) or the start-of-the-opponent's-turn hook of one the other side holds (either face's) (`evaluate::plague_doomed`).

**The price.** A doomed unit keeps Brittle 1's share of its body (`1 − brittle_discount`, the smaller of that and its Brittle and affliction shares counting), since a crumble comes at that same stage, and its Plague Counters bank nothing; its Death and turn hooks count as before. A doomed backrow card the AI can read keeps the same share of its backrow and Activate prices. So a counter on the enemy's best permanent is the best placement, one on the AI's own a loss, and an enemy unit already doomed is no target worth an attack. `GREEDY_EVAL`, whose `brittle_discount` is 0, reads none of it.

**The placement.** While a Plague Counter placement prompt is open under a Living Bomb, its answers aimed at the enemy's permanents (a face-down one named by its id alone, [[R177]]) come first in the move order, and an auto-answer of it tries every answer, the enemy's first, never a draw of `max_auto_answer_options` of them (`candidates::plague_aims_first`).

Not modelled: the enemy's units a doom removes from its face threat before they swing, which the line's end already plays, and a Living Bomb that leaves the field before the hook fires.
