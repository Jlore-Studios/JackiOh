A player's practice game had C #20 The Power to Punish's Radiant "Deal 4 Pierce damage" seem to do nothing. The rules were right. The opponent's S+ #123 Oct nullified the hit at [[§4.4]] step 3b ([[R3550004]]), and its `{5 left}` became `{1 left}`. But a pool's soak emitted no event, so the client showed no number, no animation and no log line. A partial soak was just as silent: a 4 that the pool trimmed to 3 showed a bare 3. [[§10.3]] says every visible state change emits an event.

**The rule.** At step 3b, each card whose pool soaks part of a hit reports it: `damageNullified { instanceId, sourceId, targetId, amount, combat }`. `instanceId` is the pool's card (S+ #42 Force Projector, S+ #123 Oct), `amount` is what that card's pool took, and the other fields are the hit's, as on `damage`. The report goes out when the pool is spent, as the hit lands ([[R4022000]]). That holds whether the pool took the hit whole or only part, so a hit that a lethal replacement cancels or moves reports nothing at its first target. Several pools on one side each report their share, in [[R68]]'s order, before the hit's `damage` (or before [[R1361]]'s `damageAbsorbed`, which a pool's soak never meets, since a pool soaks only what Armor left). An Indestructible Unit spends no pool, so its hit reports none.

**Nothing answers it.** Like [[R1361]]'s `damageAbsorbed`, it is a report withheld from the trigger loop. No rule, trap, trigger or quest reads it, it takes no number in [[R68]]'s order, and no state hash moves, so a recorded game replays to the same hash ([[R768]]). A fatigue draw that a pool soaks keeps to this too. [[R1362]]'s place in the order belongs to a draw that Armor took whole, so such a draw takes only its `fatigue` event's number, as it did before the report existed.

**Who sees it.** Each seat's view redacts it as it redacts `damage` ([[R97]]): its source and target are hidden from a viewer who may not read them, and so is the pool's card, judged as any card is. Its numbers are public. Like [[R3857000]]'s `armorGained`, it takes no room in [[R168]]'s floor. The golden traces' texts leave it out ([[R1956001]]'s way), so a trace recorded on either side of the change replays on the other, and no card plays differently.

**On the client.** The game log writes "Oct nullified 4 damage to the opponent's hero from The Power to Punish", plus a combat form ("… in combat") and a sourceless form; the line opens with the pool's card. The `ANIMATIONS` row pulses the pool's card for 400 ms (`jk-pool-pulse`) under the `nullify` recipe ([[§10.10]]). A cool ring leaves the card, a mote carries the soak to the hit's target, and a small shield blooms there with the nullified number in the absorb tone. Nothing shakes, since nothing got through. Where the view does not show the pool's card, the shield and the number land on the target alone. Its `SOUND_CUES` row plays `cancel`, the soft sound of a thing stopped, from the hit's lane. The bright ring (`armorRing`) stays the sound of a hit that Armor took whole ([[R1363]], [[R1365]]).

**Rejected:**
- Folding the soak into `damageAbsorbed`. That would report only a whole soak, would leave a partial one silent, and would make the log and the stats credit Armor that the target does not have.
- One report per hit with no card. The pool's card is what tells a player why, and the client could not find it from the view.
- Claiming Force Projector and Oct as playing differently with a fragment: the report changes how a game is shown, not how it is played.
