Since issue #2261 a Nerf or a Buff on a card on the field never draws its Tribute from the X row: the card paid that Tribute to be played, and nothing reads a card's own Tribute while it stays. The other numbered keywords on the X row are as [[R386]] has them, and an activation's Tribute cost (C #21 Turtinator's) was never on that row. This takes the paid Tribute off the field menu as [[R1748000]] took off the paid cost, and it holds for a Nerf as much as for a Buff, since the two verbs share one menu ([[R386]]).

In a hand or a deck the Tribute is on the row as before, and a play now asks for the Tribute a Nerf or a Buff left there, never below 1, less what Floats took off ([[R1841003]]). [[R386]] already said so, but the play read the printed Tribute, so a Buffed card showed Tribute 1 and still asked for 2.

A replay folded under an earlier version that Nerfed or Buffed a Tribute on the field, or played a Tribute card a Nerf or a Buff had moved, ends on another hash and is refused ([[R767000]]).
