The opening draw is [[§2.1]]'s table entry plus `extraOpeningCards`, so a Medium or Hard AI seated second draws 5 and seated first draws 4 (5 in either seat while both seats drew four, [[R1156000]], until [[R2368000]]). Quickdraw cards replace draws out of that total, and the mulligan works on the whole hand as [[§2.1]] says. Hard carries Medium's extra card rather than adding a second one
