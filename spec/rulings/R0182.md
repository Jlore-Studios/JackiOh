The opening draw is [[§2.1]]'s table entry plus `extraOpeningCards`, so a Medium or Hard AI draws 5 in either seat (seated first it drew 4 before patch v0.3.Y gave both seats four, [[R1156000]]). Quickdraw cards replace draws out of that total, and the mulligan works on the whole hand as [[§2.1]] says. Hard carries Medium's extra card rather than adding a second one
