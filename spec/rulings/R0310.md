title: A player's own library, without its order

[[§9.1]] hides a library's order from both players and its contents from the opponent; nothing requires hiding the contents from their owner, who built the deck, drew and returned their own opening hand, and watched every card go in that went in openly. So `viewFor` gives the viewer their own library as a list without order (`SideView.ownLibrary`): one entry per definition and face with a count, sorted by the definition's printed cost ([[R65]]: X is 0, an embiggen card its base price), then its name, then its id, the base face before the Radiant one. That order depends on the cards alone, so two libraries holding the same cards in any order give the same view, and the list carries no instance id, no position, no cost as it stands (#95's discount, [[R177]]) and no rolled power (#98, [[R43]]). The opponent's library stays a count. A deck the player was dealt rather than built (All Random, [[R258]]; practice's fresh random deck) lists, since patch v0.2.0, only what its owner has been shown of it ([[R433]]): its starting cards are unknown until they leave the library, and what goes in openly afterwards is listed as for any deck
