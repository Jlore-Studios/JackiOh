Meditative #95's first entry fuses its caster's hand into one card and adds 2 copies of it, all three costing (0) ([[R1242]]). A hand holding those three fuses again into a card three times as large, and a fused card plays every ingredient's text ([[R77]], [[R102]]), its Call to Chaos among them, so a chain of 0-cost plays can grow one card without end: the daily fuzz's seed 8286 reached 288 cards in one fusion and the next pushed the process past 7 GB. The spec set no bound on a fusion's size; this note sets one, as [[R28]], [[R452]] and [[R1241]] bound the other chains that multiply.

- **The count.** A card that is no fusion holds 1. A fusion holds the sum of its ingredients' counts, each fused ingredient read through its definition's ingredient list ([[R468]]), so a fusion of fusions counts every card they were made from.
- **The cap.** A fusion whose ingredients would hold more than `FUSED_CARDS_CAP` (64) cards is not made. Every Fuse verb goes through the same check, so each fizzles as it does for fewer than two ingredients ([[R77]]): nothing is fused and no definition is written. For Meditative #95 the entry then does nothing: the hand stays as it was and no copies are added.
- **At the cap.** A fusion of exactly 64 is made. A full hand fused once, then again with its two copies and seven more cards, holds 37, so a hand reaches the cap only through fusions of its own fusions.
