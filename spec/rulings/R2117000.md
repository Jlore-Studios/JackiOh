title: "A move between two menu screens cross-fades in the browser's view transition (issue #2117, part of #2036)"

A move from one menu screen to another runs inside `document.startViewTransition` with the browser's default fade of the whole page. That covers a link's or a redirect's `navigate`, and the browser's own Back or Forward. The new screen is drawn inside the update, and one move is one transition.

**Not on a board.** No move into or out of a board's route fades: a match, a watched game, a co-op match, practice (whose setup and game share `/practice`) and the two development hotseats. Nor does a move off a board on screen, such as a replay playing in place on the replays page, after a match or in Play together. The animation queue owns everything that moves there.

**Not within a screen.** A change of query or fragment alone (the Almanac's open card, a Collection section) starts none.

**Skipped.** Under Reduce motion (the system's, the settings panel's or the effects setting's), or without the API, the move is drawn at once, as before.

**The leave guard.** A move held by [[R1754000]] starts nothing until the player lets it go. Then it fades like any other move.

**Rejected:** knowing a board by its route alone, which would fade a replay out of the page it plays on; fading a change of query, which is not a new screen; named per-element transitions, which the task does not ask for.
