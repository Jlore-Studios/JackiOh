The owner asked that Radi, Halo and every card that Equips a Weapon into itself look as a Starship does, the gear not reading as fused into the card. An Equip, played or from a pick ([[R1841002]]), fuses the gear into the unit, and the face printed that fusion as the engine builds it: "Beam v3 + Repeater v3 + Radi", every ingredient's text joined; only its art was the host's already ([[R3045000]]).

- **What holds gear.** A fused definition whose leaves, walked as [[R3045000]] walks them, are gear but one: gear is a Stinkyscape Miner (Miner v1 to v5) or a Weapon-tagged card, as for the art, and the one card that is not is the host, the card the gear was equipped into. An Equip fuses onto its host as `[equip, host]`, again for each pick, so the gear comes oldest first by reading the leaves backwards. A Dormant Starship lists its own pieces ([[R2249000]]); gear fused onto gear has no host; a fusion holding two cards that are no gear (a host that was itself a fusion) prints as the Fuse joined it; and a fusion a digest hides is not walked ([[R468]]).
- **How it is drawn.** Its face is named for the host, wherever the face is drawn, and a log line names it so too, as a Starship is named for its hull ([[R3045000]]); a Chinese face reads the host's Chinese name ([[R1301]]). Its rules box prints the host's own text, its numbers its own ([[R5049000]]), and its keyword line the host's printed keywords, so a keyword the gear lends reads as gained. Below the text, under the Equip term, the gear is listed oldest first, each with its module icon and a reference that opens its printed face, on the face it reads on: Radiant on a Radiant unit or where it went in Radiant ([[R469]]). The gear's own texts are behind those references, as a Starship's pieces' are; its abilities are the unit's to use as before.

Presentation only ([[R2529000]]): the engine's definition keeps its joined name, its union of tags and every ingredient's text, which a rule can read. This ruling amends [[R3045000]], whose "any other fusion is named as it was" now leaves out a unit holding gear.
