Issue #4154: S #89 Spice "sells for ~25% more credits". Its quote range ([[R1861000]]), a Rare Resource's 3 to 10 Credits (Radiant 6 to 20), has each end raised a quarter and rounded to the nearest Credit, a half up: 4 to 13, Radiant 8 to 25 (`HAND_SALE_PRICE`, `HAND_SALE_RADIANT_PRICE`), drawn each turn as before ([[R1853002]]). Spice is the only card that sells from the hand, so the range is its own and no Resource's moves. Its text names no price and is unchanged; S #173 Abandoned Spice Tower names none either. The quotes are drawn in every game whose Marketplace is open, so every such game's quotes move: a rule change for replays. Rejected: raising Gellium's range (a Resource Spice never was). Since issue #4410 the range prices what Spice pays as it is played or discarded, the sale gone ([[R4410000]]). Since issue #4440 each end is 15% higher again, rounded the same way: 5 to 15, Radiant 9 to 29 ([[R4440001]]).
