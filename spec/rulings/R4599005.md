M #92 Unan's lethal guard ([[R1204]]) takes a lethal hit on its controller's hero or another of their Units on itself, and the Drone Battleship's Shield Layer guards the same way. No ruling said what a guard Immune to Spells does with a Spell's hit. The daily fuzz's plain seeds 3782, 5265, 6340 and 10294 each sent a Spell's lethal hit onto an immune guard: through its Armor, onto its Divine Shield, or into its health.
**The hit stays on its target.** A Spell passes an immune field Unit by, whatever its wording ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]), and the guard taking the hit is the Spell's damage reaching it. So a guard Immune to Spells does not answer a lethal hit from a Spell, which lands on the hero or Unit it was dealt to; the next guard in order may still answer it. A hit from a Unit, a Field Spell, a Trap or no card is caught as before.
Rejected: letting the guard take the hit and then dealing it nothing, which would turn Immune to Spells into a shield for every ally against every Spell, far past "doesn't affect it".
