A Field Trap that acts on the one card its trigger names targets that card for [[R781001]]: an Immutable card in play is no card it may act on. It still fires (it turns face-up and is not spent, being a Field Trap), does nothing to that card and does the rest of its text, as [[R17]] already has Sheepish fire and spend itself on an Immutable Unit. Two Field Traps act so today: Classic #5 Tesla, which still animates in Defense Position but deals the summoned Immutable Unit no damage (so its Lifesteal heals nothing), and Classic+ #74 Twice Forward One Step Backwards, which fuses in no copy of the Immutable card its count names (a Radiant one on its Radiant face, [[R425]], [[R831011]]) and still gains its Brittle, animating only when an earlier fusion gave it stats ([[R2280000]]). A card's own controller playing it onto a Field Spell (Classic+ #33 Ivory Tower, [[R653]]) is that player's play, not the Field Spell's targeting, and a Unit answering an attack (Meditative #49.3 AI Girlfriend) acts as a Unit; neither changes.

Superseded by Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V (issue #4353, [[R4353001]]): a Field Trap may target an Immutable card, so Tesla deals it its damage and Twice Forward, One Step Backwards fuses in a copy of it.
