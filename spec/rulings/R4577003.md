IA #18 Prompt Engineer's "Your Created cards cost (1) less, but not less than (1)" is a cost aura with two riders on its `CostRule`. `created: true` reaches only a Created card ([[R943]]); absent, a rule reaches dealt and Created cards alike. `floor: N` stops the rule's `amount` at (N), counted on the number every flat and threshold rung left ([[R65]]); a card already at or below (N) keeps its price, so a (1) Created card stays (1) and a (0) one stays (0), and the floor never raises a price. Floored rungs follow the flat and threshold rungs, each on the number the rung before it left, so two Prompt Engineers make a (3) Created card (1) and a (2) one (1), and a flat discount ahead of them leaves the floor where it is. A "costs (N)" still wins over every add, as it always has. A rule with a floor says it in its badge: "Your Created cards cost (1) less, but not less than (1)".
