Issue #4154 changed S #83 Halo's Discover to "choose from 1 of 2 [weapons] three times to fuse into this": "Cry: Choose one of 2 Rare Weapons and Equip it into this, 3 times." (Radiant: Epic Weapons). It is [[R1841002]]'s `equip_picks` as before, the declared `weapons` (3) picks in turn, each offering two where a Discover offered three (its `options`): the two are different cards drawn from the Weapon-tagged cards of the rarity ([[R1862002]]'s pool), never one Halo already holds, so a later choice never repeats an earlier pick, and a pool with one card left offers that one. The prompt is a Discover's, of two, so the chosen card is fused in as it is answered, its Activate usable that turn ([[R1751000]]), before the next choice opens. The two-card offer is the card's own number, printed and not declared, so no Buff moves it; the picks stay `weapons`. Through v0.6.6e each pick was a Discover of three (`script_v0_6_6e`, [[R767000]]). Rejected: one choice of two made three times over the same pair, which the issue's "three times" does not ask for and would let the second pick repeat the first.
