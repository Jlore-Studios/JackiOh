C+ #35 Rollback returns a side of the board to how a snapshot held it ([[R419]]). A Rush Token the step began with, Immune to Spells by a granted keyword, came back as the snapshot held it, without the grant, and C #25 Lag in the System then exiled it. The fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) judges a Unit gone from the field by the keywords it held as the step began, so it blamed the exile on a Spell reaching an immune Unit.
**The restore makes them new.** A Unit that a `rolledBack` restores is the snapshot's, so the keywords it held as the step began no longer describe it, as I19 reads a restored side's Dormant cards ([[R4524001]]). Now, for the rest of the step, I21 releases the Units of each side a `rolledBack` restores that are off the field as the step ends. One still on the field and immune as the step ends is judged by what it holds then, as before, so a restore's window still lets no Spell's buff through ([[R4508001]]).
Rejected: releasing every Unit of a restored side, as I19 does. That would stop judging a Unit the restore left immune, which [[R4508001]] judges.
