[[§8]] #55's base face: "Can use opposing Units as Tributes. If opposing Units are used, summon for your opponent." A play whose Tribute takes at least one unit the opponent controls — read as [[§10.5]] step 2 pays it, before those units die — puts the Golem, at step 4, into the opponent's zone in the lane the play named when that zone is open, else their leftmost open unit zone ([[R15]]'s placement for a card that changes sides), under the opponent's control. It is still the player's play, so the `cardPlayed` is theirs and the opponent's traps answer it as a play by the opponent, and still the player's card, which goes to its owner's piles when it leaves the field ([[§3.2]]); it has entered its controller's side on that turn, as [[R171]] has any change of side. With no open zone on the opponent's side once the Tribute is paid — a tributed Reborn unit's zone is reserved for its return ([[R64]]) — it lands where the play named, on the player's side, as a steal that finds no free zone leaves the card where it is ([[R15]]). The Radiant face keeps the permission and drops the clause, so it stays with the player whoever paid
