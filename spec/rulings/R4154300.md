Issue #4154 asked that S+ #121 Toxopid's Legs "are placed randomly", and took out its first-hand-arrival Crawler and the discount each friendly Crawler death gave it. Its Cry now reads "Summon N Toxopid Legs in random empty unit zones on the field" (N the declared `legs`, 3; on the Radiant face 4 Radiant Legs "on your opponent's field"). As the Cry reaches it, the empty unit zones are read once ([[§3.2]]: no card in the zone, no GIGA covering it, not void, and one a summon may land in, the test [[R3554009]]'s Leg used): both sides' on the base face, the opponent's on the Radiant face. N of them are drawn together from the match rng ([[R60]]), no zone twice, and a Leg is summoned into each, belonging to the side it lands on, where it Craters as ever. With fewer empty zones than N, fewer Legs land and nothing more is drawn ([[R129]]); the Toxopid's own zone is never empty, so it is never drawn. No prompt opens, so neither the AI nor a timeout has a Leg to place, and since only empty zones are drawn no Leg exiles anything: the Cry summons with no `exileBelow`. Indestructible while a Leg stands ([[R3560006]]) is unchanged, Legs of either side counting.

Its first hand arrival adds no Crawler (the once-a-game mark [[R3560000]] is no longer set by it), and a friendly Crawler's death takes nothing off it ([[R3560004]]'s trigger is gone); "Float reduces cost" stays. S+ #142's Toxopid entry therefore adds the Toxopid alone. Through v0.6.6g it ran the old text, the Mode prompt per Leg of [[R3560005]] included, which `script_v0_6_6g` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: drawing each Leg's zone as that Leg lands, which no rule in between can change (a summon's triggers wait for the step's end), and keeping `exileBelow`, which would exile a pile a zone gained after the draw, against "empty".
