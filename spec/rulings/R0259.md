Until 2026-09-25 a series was Best of 3: first to two game wins, each deck played once, in at most three games, with a pick that could be changed until the other player picked. Conquest replaced that ([[R330]]–[[R334]]). What stands of this row: a series is played from the trio each player froze when they queued, one deck per side per game; a pick is hidden from the other side until both have picked, and the game starts as soon as they have (sealed now, [[R331]]); game n's seed is the series seed and n, and the seats alternate ([[R335]]); neither side is shown the other's deck names or lists ([[R336]])
