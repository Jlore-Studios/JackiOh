R4589004 (IA #109 Revenant Process): its `Summoned` trigger fires only when the event names its
own id and the live card came back by Reborn (`returned_by_reborn`, since `Summoned.via` never
reaches a trigger, [[R2783001]]). The answer runs after the return set the body to 1 Health
(§4.5 step 4), so the buff lands on the returned body: a 4/2 comes back with 7 Attack and 4 of 5
Health, an 8/4 with 14 Attack and 7 of 10.
