Transforming a friendly unit into a copy of an enemy unit (Style Transfer) or copying the last played unit (Copycat Drone) applies standard transform rules ([[§6.3]], [[R23]], [[R57]]): stats, keywords, and face are replaced, R57 riders (such as Radiant or Chinese) ride the transformation, and an Immutable unit refuses the transform.
