S+ #81, #82 and #89 add "a random Terminus card" to their owner's hand, the Radiant faces a Radiant one: one draw from the pool [[R3552001]] reads for S+ #156, every Terminus card of the shipped sets, tokens included ([[R380]], [[R382]]), through the generator, so the card is never itself ([[R387]]) and [[R673]]'s Glitch odds apply. S+ #82's card costs (3) whatever it printed, `TERMINUS_COST`. S+ #81 Doomsayer, at the start of its controller's turn, exiles its controller's whole board first, itself with it ([[R3539002]]: Dormant cards too, no Death, no graveyard), and adds the card whether or not anything was there to exile. S+ #89's Cry adds the card as it is cast.

S+ #89's other texts, "Always starts on the bottom of your deck" and "When your exile has 5 cards cast this, wherever in your deck this is", belong to E8 (#3558), whose verbs this part was built before. Read when E8 lands: it casts when its owner's exile holds at least `card` cards, not exactly, so a card that reaches the count by exiling several at once is not missed.

Rejected: a pool of the Terminus cards of every set including the held one (the shipped sets only, as every pool is, [[R1420]]); and Doomsayer sparing itself (the text says "your entire board").
