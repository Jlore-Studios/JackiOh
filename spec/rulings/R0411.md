A summon that names the card it summons fires that card's Cry ([[R1]]): C+ #19.3 Mid Loser's Cry fires when C+ #19 League of Losers summons it. That is its only way onto the field but one — C+ #23 Dropshipping can hand a Loser out as a card, which fires its Cry when played from hand like any card — so under [[R1]] alone its Cry would be nearly dead text. League of Losers summons its five into its controller's unit zones 1 to 5 in that order, skipping an occupied or Locked zone ([[§3.2]])
