**Where.** Ctrl+K (Cmd+K) opens a command menu on every menu screen, and never over a board:
not on a match, a Conquest series (its pick clock runs), a watched game, a Duos game or the dev
boards, and not over a board sharing a menu's path (a practice game, a replay), which marks
itself instead. Ctrl and Meta both work on every platform, with no platform sniffing. It needs a
keyboard, so there is no touch entry point.

**Entries.** Three actions, then every menu page, then, once the player types, the Almanac's
shelf by name: New deck (which makes and opens one deck), Practice (which opens `/practice`) and
Play (which opens `/play`), not repeated as pages and starting no game by themselves; the menu
pages, but not the Collection's sections nor the sign-in, reset, invite and dev screens; and at
most `COMMAND_MENU_CARDS_SHOWN` (8) cards in catalog order, unranked, from `almanacShelf`
([[R1420]]: no unshipped card, [[R674]]'s Glitch aside), each opening its inspect detail
(`?card=`, [[R630]]). Every entry is the client's one move (`navigate`), so a page's leave guard
still holds it ([[R1754000]]).

**Filter.** Typing filters the entries by a case-insensitive match on their words in the player's
locale: every word typed must be a substring of the label. A blank query lists the actions and
the pages; cards list only once the player types. Card names are the reader's (`readerName`,
[[R711000]]).

**Keys.** Arrow keys move the selection, wrapping at either end; Enter runs it; Escape or a click
outside closes the menu. Ctrl/Cmd+K opens it even from a text field, but a plain letter typed
into another field never does. While another modal dialog is open the shortcut stays out of its
way.

**Focus.** The menu is a modal dialog. Focus moves to its input on open, Tab stays inside it, and
focus returns to what opened it on close.
