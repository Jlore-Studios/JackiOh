Journey Complete is cast on draw ([[§6.2]]; a cast, [[R40]], [[R70]]; its chains capped by [[R58]]). It returns, in the order its memory names them ([[R1246]]), every card its memory names that is still in exile, to its owner's hand ([[R746]], [[R12]]), made Radiant ([[§6.3]] Make Radiant, [[R74]]) and keeping its `tuning`; on the Radiant face each costs `discount` (1) less, a `costMod` that lands after [[R766]] has reset its price in exile, so it costs its printed cost less 1. The hand cap burns the rest ([[§2.4]]). It returns as many as went, two from a base Journey or five from a Radiant one, the designer's "two" on the base face being the base Journey's count and not a cap. A card that left that exile meanwhile does not come back. One that remembers nothing (made by any other card, a copy, [[R57]], or one that reached a graveyard or exile, [[R766]]) does nothing.
