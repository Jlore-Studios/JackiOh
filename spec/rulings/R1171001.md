`AI_MOVE_ORDER` (`crates/ai/src/config.rs`) is `MoveOrder::Heuristic` or `MoveOrder::Learned`, one line that turns the policy prior on or off, as `AI_EVAL_KIND` does the learned evaluation ([[R1010000]]). With `Heuristic`, the default, each open line of the beam expands the first `root_branching` (at the root) or `branching` of `candidate_actions` that are not End Turn, plus End Turn, as it always has. With `Learned`, those candidates are first sorted by the prior `POLICY_PRIOR` (`crates/ai/src/policy/weights.json`, compiled in by `crates/ai/build.rs`): a move scores the sum of its keys' weights ([[R1171000]]), a key the table lacks adding nothing, and the moves are sorted best first, equal scores keeping their heuristic order, before the prefix is taken.

The prior only re-sorts: the candidates are the same moves, each legal, and End Turn is always kept, after the prefix. Scoring a move reads the line's state and spends no node, so the search's node budget is unchanged. A table with no keys, the null one checked in until a fit replaces it, leaves every order as it is, and so does a Duos duel: the table never saw a team, as the learned network never did ([[R1010002]]).
