Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The owner asked on 2026-11-01 (issue #2724) that each Legacy card ([[R2511000]]) have a picture of its own that still reads as its Core card, replacing [[R2202000]]'s rule that the two look the same. It draws [[R2202000]]'s yard, its sky night or dusk when its Core card's [[R503]] picture is, and over it that picture's subject where it stands (a figure without its hills, a burst's every shape, no landscape ground) in the yard's wood and ink, its emblem on a stitched canvas patch, its motif and accent glyphs, and on the sand a practice-yard touch no other Legacy card has (#41 a rope cordon … #60 a scarecrow). Geometry comes from the two ids, so the Radiant face draws the same shapes, gilded, over gold rays. A file delivered for the Legacy card's own id wins; a Core card's stays its own. No other picture changes ([[R503]]); it ships as a micro ([[R2529000]]).

Amended by [[R3331002]] (issue #3331): the pictures recorded before #2724 are a record, not a pin. A Core or Training Grounds card with no scene recipe ([[R3331000]]) keeps its recorded picture byte for byte, one with a recipe draws its scene, and a Legacy card still draws its Core card's old subject, never the Core card's scene.
