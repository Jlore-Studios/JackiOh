Issue #5128: a player on a slow phone, an old laptop or battery saver had no one switch that makes the board cheaper to draw. Reduce motion ([[R200]]) stops every motion, the cards' flights among them, which is an accessibility setting and not a performance one, and the cheaper choices were spread over the Visuals tab and still left the ambient layers running. The settings panel's Visuals tab carries **Low graphics**, under Reduce motion, off by default and kept with the account's other panel switches ([[R633]]). While it is on:

- The effects layer runs as at the effects intensity off: no particles, light rays or board shake, no auras, no ripple of the arena under a press. The moving art, foils and dioramas of the card faces hold still and a depth face draws its flat picture ([[R3340000]], [[R3344000]]). The card motion stays, at the calm level the intensity off already gives: cards fly, attack and die, without the riffle, the deal or the drag's tilt.
- No set piece holds the board, as at Big moments off ([[R2785000]]).
- The board backdrop keeps its colors and draws none of its particles ([[R1297000]]), the chase tables' pads and the hero portrait's breathing light and motes stand still, and no surface blurs what is behind it.

It holds the effects intensity and Big moments without writing over them: the effects run on the held values, the panel shows the player's own choices, disabled, with a line saying Low graphics holds them, the account keeps the chosen values, and turning Low graphics off gives them back. The effects speed ([[R201]]) and the motion override are the player's as before, and the idle loops that mark what the player can act on ([[R5014000]]) keep running, since they are how the board says what is playable.
