Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The Scanning Probes played their table's shared `send` motif. Their entry is now the bespoke `probeScan` ([[R2784000]], [[R2797000]]), drawn by the seat's own events:

- At the probe's own `cardPlayed`, public to both seats, a radar sweep crosses the board from its caster's hand and fades within the effects' tail ([[R200]]).
- When its scan adds an Anomaly Found! ([[R1855003]]), its owner's `addedToHand` names the card, and that seat draws a ping on the board, the card flying from the ping into the hand, and, when the owner's next view shows it Radiant, a strong flash there: rays, a prismatic burst and a shake. That draws in place of the event's own row.
- The other seat's `addedToHand` names R97's sentinel ([[R97]]), so the recipe keys nothing on it and it keeps its own row, as any card added to a hand the seat cannot read does: the sweep, and nothing that says what was found ([[R202]]).

A scan that finds nothing adds nothing, so it draws the sweep alone.
