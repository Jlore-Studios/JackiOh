Radiant Force Projector's Armor 2 is an aura on its controller's Units, read off its own printed
Armor — inert on the Field Spell itself — and the pool soaks after it ([[R3550004]]): a hit past
Armor takes what the pool has left, the hero's Armor being [[R124]]'s own. Rejected: Armor resting
on the Field Spell, where it would soak nothing, and the hero borrowing the pool's Armor.
