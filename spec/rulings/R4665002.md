Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VII: Overdrive Projector and Overdrive Dome, "both forms only affect you". Awake ([[R3556008]]), each face of S+ #62 lends Rush and Windfury, and of S+ #63 Charge and Windfury, to its controller's Units alone, and its Echo ([[R3556001]]) reaches only the Spells its controller plays or casts (`SpellEchoAura.both` unset), where the base faces reached every Unit on both sides and either player's Spells. The same balance sets their Dormant countdowns ([[R3556000]]): Overdrive Projector 2 turns, Radiant 1, Overdrive Dome 2, Radiant 1, their declared `dormant`. Their texts say "Your Units" and "your Spells" on both faces. Replays of the versions through v0.6.12b fold under `script_v0_6_12b` ([[R767000]]).
