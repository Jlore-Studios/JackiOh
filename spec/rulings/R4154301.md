Issue #4154 rewrote S+ #120 Crawler: (1), 3/1 (Radiant 6/2), "May be played from your graveyard for (1). Death: Add a Toxopid to your hand. If you already have one there, reduce its cost by (N) instead.", N the declared `reduce`, 2 (Radiant 3, "a Radiant Toxopid"). It loses Can't Defend, "After this attacks, it dies" ([[R3560002]]), the first-arrival Toxopid ([[R3560000]]) and the Death that hit the last Unit it damaged ([[R3560003]], with its `damage`). The graveyard play is unchanged ([[R3560001]]).

The Death reads its controller's hand as it resolves. "One" is a Toxopid, S+ #121, of either face: a Radiant Crawler cheapens a base Toxopid held, and a base Crawler a Radiant one. With one or more held, the first in hand order takes the whole reduction (the hand keeps arrival order, so it is the one held longest) as a `costMod` of minus N ([[R65]]), a lasting change that goes with the card between zones until a graveyard or an exile ([[R78]], [[R766]]); the price never drops below 0. Nothing is added then. With none held a fresh Toxopid is added, Radiant on the Radiant face, and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). N is read off the dying card as it died, so a Buff or a Nerf on it moves it ([[R386]]). A Crawler's arrival does nothing, so S+ #134, #135 and #136 bring a Toxopid only through their Crawlers' deaths. Through v0.6.6h it ran the old text, which `script_v0_6_6h` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: cheapening every Toxopid held, which "its cost" in the singular does not say, and the newest one, which the designer's "if you already have one" points away from.
