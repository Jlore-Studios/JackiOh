#98 Heroic Power declares nine numbers since patch v0.3.2 (issue #493), one or two for each power that has a number, all on one Number row, while the card has only the power it rolled ([[R752]]): a Degrade, an Upgrade or KY's Constant could land on a number of a power the card does not have, and change nothing it does. Each number now names its power in the catalog (`power`: the id of the Activate ability that is the power, [[R103]]'s stored name): Steady Shot's `shot` is `burn`'s ([[R754]]), Life Tap's `tapDraw` and `tapDamage` are `draw`'s, `ping` is Ping's, `stitchCost` Stitching's, `armor` Armor Up's (Tank Up's on the Radiant face), `insect` Die Insect's, `discount` KY Brainstorm's and `fruitCost` Pluck's. A number that belongs to a power is in reach only while the card has that power, its ability one the card has now ([[R384]]'s `has`; a fused card's copy of it is `<id>#n`, [[R102]]): B3.4 rule 3's Number row, which [[R386]]'s Degrade and Upgrade draw from, offers only the numbers of the power the card has now and the numbers that belong to no power; KY's Constant (C+ #41) lists only those; and with no such number and nothing else to change, nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). A number of another power keeps whatever tuning it already has, in every zone, and reads it again once the card has that power back, by a reroll (Tank Up, [[R757]]) or otherwise; until then nothing moves it. A card that has not rolled a power ([[R43]]), or a Vanilla one, has no power's number in reach. `catalog check` holds `power` to a camelCase id, and a cross test holds it to an Activate ability the card declares on both faces. Only #98's numbers belong to a power.
