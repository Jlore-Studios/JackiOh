A player never drops below the Grape tier they have reached this season — the floor rises with them — though divisions inside a tier can still drop, and wins past the top hold it. The season keeps each player's best: the highest ladder reached and the best (lowest) Jlorious position held. The season's badge shows on the profile: the Jlorious position, or the Grape tier and division of the highest ladder; a season whose placements were never finished has none
