[[R82]] ends a turn by itself when only ending it, conceding and the draw offers are left. A buy or a sale at the Marketplace ([[R1853003]]) is none of those. So while one is listed the turn waits for the player, and once the last is made, with nothing else left, the turn ends by itself, as a Trade's does ([[R1838003]]). The three answerers of a turn each take it as they take a Trade:

- **The random policy** ([[§10.7]], [[R44]]) draws a buy or a sale from `legalActions` like any other action, so My Pawn's turn and the fuzz may deal.
- **The clock** ([[R79]]) answers prompts and ends the turn. It makes no move of its own, so a timeout never buys or sells.
- **The practice AI** ([[§9.9]]) tries a buy or a sale in its search, after its plays, answers and Trades and before its other attacks, and keeps it when the line scores best. Its position signature counts its Credits, its tray and its purchases while the Marketplace is open. What the opponent bought this cycle is not the seat's to read ([[R1853003]]), so its redaction zeroes it: two states that differ only in it give the same decision.
