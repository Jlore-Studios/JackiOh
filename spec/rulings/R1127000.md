[[§13.7]] has an AI seat pass "the best cards first" without saying what best means. This note
decides it as a Hearthstone player passes: the best card is the one that gives the teammate the
best next play.

- **Best means the highest teammate follow-up score**: the maximum of `evaluate` over at most
  `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` of the teammate's legal `Play` actions (the passed card's own plays
  first, then the rest in `legal_actions` order), each applied by one engine step to the teammate's
  determinized probe with the passed card added to its hand, scored from the teammate's seat with
  `NextSwing::Enemy` and `AI_EVAL`. A play that itself wins scores the win term, and a play that
  leaves lethal on the board scores `lethal_pressure`, so a cheaper card that wins or threatens now
  outranks a costlier one that only adds material. The seat's life is counted as [[R964000]] counts
  it.
- **Ties break by costliest first, then hand order**, and cards the teammate can only afford next
  turn (a free pass, [[§13.5]]) get no lookahead and keep that cost and hand order, so scoring
  stays bounded: at most one hand of cards times `DUOS_PASS_MAX_FOLLOWUPS` single `reduce` calls
  per `duos_passes` call, no search and no reply simulation.
- **Scoring reads only determinized probes under a fixed seed** (`DUOS_PASS_PROBE_SEED`), never a
  hidden opponent card ([[R185]], [[§13.6]]) and never the caller's stream, so hidden-opponent
  variants decide alike and the same state and seed reproduce the order. A follow-up that is
  refused or panics is skipped, and a scoring panic falls back to the cost order, so scoring can
  never empty or crash the pass list.
