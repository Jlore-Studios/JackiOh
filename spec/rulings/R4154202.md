"Give N random cards in your hand Flashback" (C #37 Last Hurrah's Radiant face) draws N different cards of its controller's hand as the clause is reached ([[R60]]), from the cards that lack Flashback now (read through the layers, a spent one, [[R783013]], counting as had), all of them with no draw when they are no more than N ([[R129]]). Each keeps the keyword for good, in its tuning as an Upgrade's keyword is (`gain_keyword_for_good` naming the card, [[R1870007]]): a keyword granted in a hand would be gone by [[R215]]'s reset as the card is discarded or lands from the resolving zone, and Flashback acts only in the graveyard ([[R783012]]), so a granted one would never act. So a Spell played from the hand, or a card discarded, is played once more from the graveyard. Each grant is a `keywordGranted` the view names to its owner alone ([[R177]]): the other player learns the keyword and the count, never which cards. Rejected: drawing among every hand card (a card already holding Flashback would waste the gift) and a granted keyword (it never reaches the graveyard).

Since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI (issue #4440) it is C #37's base face that gives `flashback` (4) random cards Flashback, and the Radiant face gives it to every card of the hand that lacks it, with no draw; through v0.6.9n the base face gave none (`script_v0_6_9n`, [[R767000]]).
