A series begun as [[R259]]'s Best of 3 goes on as a Conquest series from its next change: every game it has played counts as Conquest counts it — a win locks the deck it was won with — so a series at one win each after two games does not end after its third, and one that [[R259]] had already ended stays ended. Nothing in the row had to change for this ([[R330]])
