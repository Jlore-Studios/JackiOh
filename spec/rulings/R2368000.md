Each seat's opening draw ([[§2.1]] step 2) is three cards for Player 1, who goes first, and four for Player 2: `OPENING_DRAW` is `[3, 4]`. It is the split Hearthstone deals and the one the game had until issue #1156 gave Player 1 a fourth card for seat balance ([[R1156000]]); the owner asked for it back (issue #2368). It is still a table indexed by seat, not two constants.

Nothing else in setup moves. Player 2 is still dealt The Coin once both mulligans are answered ([[R244]]), so it opens on four cards and The Coin against Player 1's three and the first turn, on which Player 1 still draws ([[R10]]). A handicap's extra cards still come on top of the seat's entry ([[R182]]): a Medium or Hard AI opens on four seated first and five seated second. Quickdraw cards still replace draws out of that total ([[R640]]), so a deck holding five is dealt three of them seated first and four seated second. In Duos team A's seats, each its duel's Player 1, draw three, and team B's draw four ([[§13.2]]).

Fatigue: a 25-card deck ([[R1705000]]) holds 22 after Player 1's deal and 21 after Player 2's, so one draw a turn takes Player 1's last card on its 22nd turn and Player 2's on its 21st. The Nth empty draw deals N ([[§2.4]], [[R3]]): seven deal 28 and eight deal 36, so a hero at 30 health dies on the eighth, Player 1's 30th turn and Player 2's 29th. Player 2's 29th turn is player-turn 58, so two decks that do nothing end there, Player 1 winning, where under [[R1156000]] they ended at player-turn 57, Player 2 winning. The cap of 60 player-turns stays a backstop ([[§2.5]], [[R2]], [[R389]]).

A game made while both seats opened on four no longer folds to its recorded hash, since its opening deal differs, and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R768]], [[R767000]]), as issue #1156's own change retired the games before it. A game made at 20 cards still folds at the size it was made under ([[R1705003]]), but its deal is today's, so one made under [[R1156000]] is refused the same way.
