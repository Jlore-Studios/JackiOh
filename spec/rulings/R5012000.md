Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #5012 found that about half of `apps/web/src/palette.css` was
Tailwind's default palette, value for value, and that next to the wood those colours read as neon. It asked for
colours drawn from the table's materials, fewer of them, each name keeping the meaning its uses give it.

**The palette.** `palette.css` names 43 colours, from 50, and none is one of the 25 Tailwind defaults it held
(`palette.test.ts`'s `TAILWIND_DEFAULTS`). The 25 others stay as they were. Beside them stand jade for good and
ready, lacquer for damage and danger, ink for information, and verdigris, copper, amethyst and a pale brass for the
rest, with two brasses (`--pal-brass-300`, `--pal-brass-200`), `--pal-lacquer-400` and `--pal-verdigris-400` taken
from the tavern's own colours. Each removed name's uses move to one colour: `grey-500` to `stone-500`, `gold-200` to
`brass-200`, `yellow-400` to `gold-400`, `red-300` to `lacquer-300`, `red-400` to `lacquer-400`, `red-500` to
`red-600`, `red-900` to `lacquer-900`, `orange-400` to `copper-400`, `lime-200` and `green-200` to `jade-200`,
`lime-400` and `green-400` to `jade-400`, `green-500` to `jade-500`, `green-700` to `jade-700`, `green-900` to
`jade-900`, `cyan-400` to `verdigris-400`, `sky-300` to `ink-300`, `sky-400` to `ink-400`, `blue-200` to `ink-200`,
`blue-700` to `blue-800`, `blue-900` to `ink-900`, `violet-300` and `purple-400` to `amethyst-300`, `violet-500` to
`amethyst-500` and `violet-700` to `amethyst-700`.

**The meanings kept.** A name keeps what its uses mean, in its new material: the green glow of what is ready is
jade (`--glow-ready`, `--pal-jade-400`); the yellow glow of a met condition ([[R195]]) is `--pal-gold-400`; damage and
a lethal glow are lacquer; you and mana stay `--pal-blue-600`; plague is
amethyst; the opponent and danger stay `--pal-red-600`; the board's selection and a grade are ink. The text and the
dimmed text keep AA contrast on the three backgrounds, as before.

**The art.** The card art does not repaint. `SCENE_PALETTE` (`cards/art/scene.ts`) keeps the 50 colours
`palette.css` held before #5012 as the art's own palette, and agrees with `palette.css` on the 25 names both
declare; it no longer has to equal `palette.css`, which [[R3331000]] asked. An earlier ruling that names a
picture's colour by a `palette.css` name ([[R2725004]], [[R2942001]], [[R2943001]], [[R2944001]],
[[R3017002]], [[R3391000]]) now names `SCENE_PALETTE`'s colour of that name, so no recipe card's picture and no
version's picture changes.

**Owned looks.** A look a player owns, bought or paid by a track ([[R3335001]]), keeps its colours:
`cards/cosmetics.css`, the playmats in `game/table.css` ([[R1297000]]), and in `cards/cards.css` and
`collection/collection.css` the rules of a card version, a Set showcase, a finish, a frame, a rarity foil, a
Radiant face and the Mythic shimmer ([[R3345000]]). Where such a rule named a removed palette colour, it now
states that colour's old hex as a literal. A board stylesheet holds no literal (#2105), so it follows the palette,
`game/arena/arena.css`'s per-mat corner props and the starscape among them. The Infinite Assault set's accent
([[R4596000]], `cards/setmark.css`) is worn by every face of the set and owned by no one, so it follows the palette
too: its red and its cyan are `--pal-lacquer-400` and `--pal-verdigris-400`, still a red and a cyan as that ruling
asks.

**Literals.** The sheets with the most literals move onto the palette: a literal of a palette colour's exact value
takes its name everywhere, and outside an owned look and the art sheets a literal within an OKLab distance of
0.04 of a palette colour, or one of the removed Tailwind values, takes that colour, its alpha written as
`color-mix(in srgb, var(--pal-…) N%, transparent)`. A literal a test reads by its text stays as written.
`palette.test.ts`'s `UNMIGRATED` falls from 1,870 literals in 43 sheets to 871 in 28, and `UNMIGRATED_MAX` holds
it there.

Rejected: repainting the art and the owned looks from the new palette, since every recipe card's picture and every
owned version, finish and playmat would change under a player who bought or earned it; keeping Tailwind's values
under new names, which changes nothing a player sees; nudging a Tailwind value by a step to pass the test, which
keeps the neon; and moving the per-mat corner props into `table.css` to keep them exact, which would raise that
sheet's count where the issue lets no entry grow.
