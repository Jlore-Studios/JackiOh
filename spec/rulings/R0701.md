A landing is its `summoned` (or `animated`) animation entry: the queue gives the entry its slam, and the effects layer only decorates it and never paces the queue ([[R200]]–[[R202]]). By tier: Tiny nothing; Small a few grains of dust; Medium a dust puff; Large a dust cloud, a 40 ms hit-stop and the landing side's cards wobbling; Huge a 300 ms anticipation (the Unit hangs before its landing motion, inside the entry), a 90 ms hit-stop, a 6 px 250 ms shake, heavy dust, cracks in the sand around its zone and every board card wobbling; MASSIVE a 500 ms anticipation, a 160 ms hit-stop, a 12 px 400 ms shake, a crater in the sand, a shockwave ring, a vignette flash and every card wobbling. Several Units landing in one action play one entry each, one after another, and share one action's totals (`SLAM_BURST`: 200 ms of hit-stop, 12 px of shake, 500 ms of anticipation), so the first lands at full weight and the rest with what is left. Cracks and craters are sand marks under the cards: they never cover a card or take input, and fade. Under Reduce motion there is no shake, flash, wobble, cracks or anticipation; the hit-stop and the sound stay
