The Unit stacked onto the Tower is played in full first: it stands on the Tower as a carried Unit ([[R446]]) while its play resolves, its Cry and any prompt in it included, as #85 fuses a played permanent only after its Cry ([[R61]]) and Hearthstone's Magnetic keeps a minion's Battlecry. The Tower answers that play's `cardResolved` by fusing in whatever Unit then stands on it — the stacked card, or what an answer replaced it with where it stands (#41 Sheepish's Sheep Token, a Transform reaching a carried Unit as any Unit) — per [[R77]] with the Tower the kept card; with no Unit on it, or the Tower gone (its Unit then steps down, [[R446]]), nothing is fused. The Tower takes one Unit a stay on the field: the engine notes the first Unit to stand on it (`stackedOnto`), and no Unit may name its zone again, though that Unit left before it was fused, until the Tower leaves the field, which clears the note ([[R78]]); back on the field it is a new arrival ([[R174]]) and may take one again. An Immutable Tower takes none, since its text could not change to take a Unit in ([[R23]]). On the base face the Unit goes in as [[R77]] sums base faces, so a Radiant Unit shows its base face on a base Tower, as #85's played permanent does on a base target; the Radiant face makes the Unit Radiant as it lands, so its Cry runs on that face, and fuses it in on its Radiant face ([[R469]])
