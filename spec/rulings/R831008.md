A lane is empty when its four zones, both players' unit and backrow zones, hold no card (`query::lane_is_empty`); a face-down card occupies its zone like any card, which both players can see without reading it. Meditative #98 Showdown's "Choose an empty lane" offers only the empty lanes among its five modes ([[R81]], [[R945]]): a card's `mode_allowed` check, read alike by `legal_actions`, the play's refusal and a cast's own pick. With no empty lane Showdown is not offered and its play is refused; a random cast ([[R452]]) picks among the empty lanes, and with none it chooses no lane and does nothing.
