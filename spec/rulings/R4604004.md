Like those of [[R4508005]], some of the game's own rules run inside a Spell's run with no marker of their own, and the fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) blamed them on the Spell.
**A Burn tick a Divine Shield takes.** M+ #7 Life Mage's Divine Shield takes a turn-end Burn tick whole ([[§4.4]] step 1), so the tick shows as `divineShieldLost` and then the Burn's removal, with no `damage` for R4508005's reading to see ([[R4154000]]). Now I21 allows a `divineShieldLost` right before its Unit's Burn removal, and that removal.
**An exile aura that arrives by a transform.** S #115 Scornboi's aura exiles every Unit that is neither Human, a Starship Piece nor a Starship at each state check ([[R1850002]]), a rule standing on the board, not a Spell's effect, and [[R4599004]] allows an `exiled` once a card laying one arrives in the step by its `summoned`. In the wave on 823 cards, S+ #104 Tireless Cultivation turned a Drone into Scornboi, which arrives by a `transformed` and summons nothing. Now a `transformed` into a card laying an exile aura counts as its arrival too.
The note first also allowed any `destroyed` in a run after another marker of the step; issue #4599's [[R4599001]] and [[R4599008]] reached `main` first with narrower readings of the state check's deaths, which say the other cases are still a reach, so this note leaves the deaths to them.
Rejected: allowing every `exiled` after a `transformed`, which would pass a Spell's own exile once anything transformed.
