Classic+ #62 KY's Papaya asks each of its 1 to 4 cells as a Discover (a `mode` prompt drawn as the Discover pop-up), offering `PAPAYA_OFFERS` (3) cells drawn from the match rng among the cells of the lanes not used yet, any cell alike (empty, filled or Locked), shown in lane order and named by lane and row from the caster's seat ("Lane 3, enemy Units"); after the first pick the offer also holds "Done". So the curve stays the caster's, but which points they may pick is limited. Each option's key is the cell's `x,y` in [[R422]]'s grid. Once the last cell is picked, before any card is exiled, a public `curveDrawn` event names the curve's cells (every cell y = p(x) meets) in lane order, which both seats read ([[R97]]: board positions only) and a client animates as a line across the board. It amends [[R422]] (the cells were a `cell` prompt offering every cell of the unused lanes) and [[R514]] (the `cell` prompt answers on the board, and Papaya no longer opens one).
