Meditative #42 CN Flea Market (MD-C13) opens a night market (ME-MARKET, `subsystems/night_market.rs`).
Its stall is rolled once from the match rng as the Spell resolves: three different random non-token
CN cards of every set ([[R380]], [[R382]], [[R60]]), never the card itself ([[R387]]), two Auspicious
Rocks (M #39.1) and one random AI generated card (the ten C+ #78 names). Nothing else in a market draws
from the rng, so every simulation of it ([[R185]]) and every replay sees the same lots.

A lot's price in yuan is `YUAN_PER_COST` (10) times its printed cost out of play ([[R65]]: X counts 0)
plus `YUAN_PER_RARITY` (5) times its rarity rank (`YUAN_RARITY_RANKS`, Common 1 to Mythic 5; a token by
its printed rarity, an AI card, which prints none, as Common), times `YUAN_RADIANT_MULTIPLIER` (2) for a
Radiant card: a Rock is 10 yuan, a (1) AI card 15, a (2) Rare CN card 30.

A new prompt kind, `market`, held by the caster alone ([[§10.6]]) with its `budget` the yuan left,
offers each lot the yuan covers (none while the hand is full), the barters of [[R1001]] on the Radiant
face, and Leave. Each answer is one deal: a bought lot arrives in the caster's hand on its base face at
its printed cost, hidden per zone ([[R97]]), and the market reopens through `resume` ([[R113]]) with
its price taken off. Leave closes it and the yuan left is lost; with nothing left to buy or barter the
market closes on its own. Only answers the reducer accepts are offered, so neither `legal_actions`'
answer walk nor a random answer ([[R452]]) names a refused one. The stall, the yuan and whether barter
is open ride in the prompt's resume data, which no view sends ([[§10.8]]).
