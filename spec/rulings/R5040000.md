**Why.** Running ([[R4678001]]) pools each card's live play version ([[R4675000]]) over every patch since it last changed, across majors. A card unchanged since v0.5.12 pooled its v0.5.x and v0.6.x games into one figure, though a major ships a whole set and changes the field every card plays against. The owner asked for a running view that starts again at every major, and for today's to stay as Historical (issue #5040).

**The line.** The newest major's line is the shipped history (`jackioh_cards::patch_history`, `patches.json`'s order) from its last `major` step through its newest version. It is found by the steps' `tier` and compared by equality, never by parsing a version string ([[R105]]), so no constant names a version and a new major (v0.7.0) moves it by itself. A history with no major is one line.

**The route.** `GET /api/stats/versions?scope=major` sends the live play versions alone, each cut to its versions in the newest major's line (the engine's `major_line_versions`):
- A closed play version has no row. A live one begun before the major is cut to start at it; one begun after is unchanged.
- A cut row keeps its play version's id, card, name and `live`; its `first`, `last` and `patches` ([[R1973002]]'s windows its cut versions reach) are read off the cut span.
- Its games, Adjusted, range and tier are `play_version_figures` over the cut play versions ([[R4675001]]): its card's totals summed over the cut span, read against a field of the cut rows alone, with the same gate and [[R654]]'s floor. Every row reads on one scale, the line's.
- The table is kept once per `?scope=`, beside `?pilot=` and `?mode=`, cut from the snapshot every other table reads ([[R4675001]]). That snapshot keeps the newest line's totals as filed ([[R4965000]]: its reach is every patch of the newest `major.minor` line, which the major begins, and one before), so a cut span sums its own versions' totals.
- No `?scope=`, or any other value, answers as before: every play version over its whole span. The body is the same shape in both and never names the scope.

**The views.** The Experimental tab's Segmented reads Major patch running, Historical, Versions and the Hall of Fame ([[R4680000]], [[R4681001]]):
- **Major patch running** is the first view and the default, so `?experimental=running`, or none, opens it, and an old link to Running opens it. It is [[R4678001]]'s table, grid, sort, filters, ranges and drill-down over `?scope=major`'s rows. Since names the first version the figures count, the later of the play version's start and the major, then the patches it reaches in the line: "v0.6.0 across 9 patches", not "v0.5.12 across 25 patches". At each major every row starts again and reads "—" below [[R654]]'s floor until games accrue. The line above the tabs reads "Every card's current version, over this major's patches since it last changed"; its button's tooltip, "Each card's current version, over the patches since the newest major or since it last changed, whichever is later. Every row starts again at each major, so it has too few games for a tier until games accrue."
- **Historical** is [[R4678001]]'s view unchanged, second, at `?experimental=historical`. Its line reads "Every card's current version, over every patch since it last changed"; its tooltip, "Each card's current version, over every patch since it last changed, across majors."
- The client cuts nothing and reads no version (CLAUDE.md rules 7 and 9): it asks the route with `scope=major` on Major patch running and without it on Historical. Historical, Versions, the Hall of Fame and a version's drill-down ([[R4680001]]) read the whole table.

Rejected:
- Cutting the spans on the client: it would restate the version grammar and read the server's figures a second way (CLAUDE.md rules 7 and 9), and could not read the cut rows' own field.
- Keeping in the cut table the closed play versions that cross the major: the view lists live versions, and a closed row in its field alone would move every other row's scale.
- A new id for a cut row, `<card>~<major>`: it would name no play version, so a row could not be matched to its version elsewhere.
- Naming the scope in the body: the client knows what it asked, and the body keeps one shape.
- A `--scope` flag on `jackioh-server stats-cards --versions` and `cargo jackioh stats report --versions`: nobody reads the cut table there yet.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone settles nothing here.

Amended by [[R5036000]] (issue #5036): Major patch running shows each row's Decisiveness, read over its cut span against the cut rows' own field and the baseline of the versions they span.
