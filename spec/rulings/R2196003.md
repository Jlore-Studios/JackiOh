A lane is a column of four zones, two of each side's ([[§3]]), and both seats see lane 1 on the left, so "the rightmost lane" is the board's last lane (lane 5 of 5) and "the leftmost lane" is lane 1, the same column for both players. "All permanents" there are the top cards of its four zones, Units and backrow cards, face-down ones included ([[R13]]: a card beneath a pile resumes); an Indestructible one stays, a Unit standing on an Ivory Tower as its own play resolves is passed by ([[R446]], [[R653]]), and a destroyed card that prints Death fires it ([[§4.5]]). The base face includes the card itself when it was played into that lane, as Hearthstone's "destroy all minions" does; the Radiant face's "enemy permanents" never reach it or any card of its controller's. The brief's TG-P3-4.
