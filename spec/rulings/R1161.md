Meditative #85 Playtester (MD-E8) asks at its controller's start of turn ([[R62]]): a mode prompt
with two options, Book of Buff and Book of Nerf, and the answer puts that many (one, both faces)
of C+ #71 or C+ #72 into the controller's hand. The Books are real cards, named by `refs`, so the
prompt names no pool. The prompt is the controller's; a timeout answers it with the AI policy
([[R79]]), and the opponent sees the prompt open and the card arrive hidden. A full hand burns the
Book ([[R386]]'s hand cap). The option labels are the same on both faces; the Radiant face's Book
is Radiant.
