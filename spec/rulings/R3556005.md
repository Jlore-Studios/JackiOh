Thorium Reactor rolls `chance`% from the game's Rng at the end of its controller's turn, Lucky and
Luck rerolling toward a miss; on a hit it deals `damage` to its controller's hero, then destroys
itself ("blow up"). Its Activate discards a chosen #48 Thorium from hand — an existing
`choose_from_hand` prompt ([[R81]]) — to skip this turn's roll. Rejected: surviving the blow up,
which would leave a spent reactor humming, and discarding the first Thorium unasked.
