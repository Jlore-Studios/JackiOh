The owner asked (2026-11-04) for many more card art variants, so that every card has several. #3017
appends six variants on #3015's model ([[R3015000]]), each a ladder of two `version-…` levels, its art
in the normal frame, then animated, its picture's own layers moving:

| Variant | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `version-night`, Rare | `version-night-animated`, Epic |
| Neon sign | `version-neon`, Epic | `version-neon-animated`, Legendary, a chase |
| Watercolor | `version-watercolor`, Rare | `version-watercolor-animated`, Epic |
| Stained-glass window | `version-stained-glass`, Epic | `version-stained-glass-animated`, Legendary, a chase |
| Woodcut | `version-woodcut`, Rare | `version-woodcut-animated`, Epic |
| Tarot card | `version-tarot`, Epic | `version-tarot-animated`, Legendary, a chase |

Each level is a tier rarer than the one below it, as [[R3015001]]'s table rules. None has a full-art
level, so none a signed one: a full art would add a Mythic chase to each, and their looks are drawn in
the normal frame. The names keep apart from the Stained glass finish and the Tarot card back. Each
level is per card, owned card by card, never a default or a starter, and no retired entry owns one
([[R3015006]]). Their pulls, in order and out of order, and a token's or the Chase Shard bar's next
level work as for any level ([[R3015003]], [[R3015004]]); a Legendary token buys a Legendary one
([[R2728000]]).

Each variant's offer is data in the registry and is its two levels' alike, but a moving level is never
offered on a Legacy card, whose ladders stop at the art ([[R3015000]]); the offers are
[[R3017001]]'s. A level a card is not offered is never dropped, sold, picked or shown for it: a pick,
a token or the Chase Shard bar naming one there is a 400 that writes and spends nothing, and the
Collection does not list it ([[R2725001]]).

**Granted to no account.** Like every cosmetic added since #1833 (§14.12), the twelve start unowned on
every account, existing ones included. `POST /api/dev/fill` grants each on every card it is offered,
and `jackioh-server seed-catalog` upserts them into `public.cosmetics` as it does every registry
entry, so no migration is needed. Practice, hotseat and every end-to-end test but the one that walks
the economy treat them as owned, every level on the cards it is offered ([[R1310004]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a level, and nothing enters `PlayerView`'s rules,
an action, the log or a replay hash.

Amends [[R2725000]] (twelve more levels) and [[R2725001]] (six more offers).
