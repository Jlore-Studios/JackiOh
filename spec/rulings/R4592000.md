While the Infinite Assault set is held ([[R4577000]]), play history records nothing unless a card of the set is open in the match. When recorded, every face-up play is appended to the game log's play history in play order. Setting a Trap or Field Trap is face-down and never recorded in play history. `add_play_copies` takes the newest N face-up plays in chronological order (or plays from last turn), adding fresh copies to hand.
