The play history is the recursion log ([[R4589000]]): kept only in a game holding an Infinite Assault card, so no other game records anything, and in it every face-up play is appended in play order with its face, type, Chinese and player-turn. Rejected: a log of its own gated on the set being open, which a single-player set never is outside the testkit's preview ([[R4577000]]). Setting a Trap or Field Trap is face-down and never recorded in play history. `add_play_copies` takes the newest N face-up plays in chronological order (or plays from last turn), adding fresh copies to hand.
