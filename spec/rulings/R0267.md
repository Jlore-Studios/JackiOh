A player's answer is read when their turn to resolve comes ([[R265]]): the cards returned are the ones their prompt offered, that the answer did not keep, and that are still in that hand. Player 1's resolution can reach Player 2's hand before Player 2's resolves — a replacement draw's cast ([[§2.4]], [[R70]]) that discards from it or adds to it — so a card that has left the hand is not returned, and a card that arrived after the prompt opened was never offered and stays. No Core cast-on-draw card touches the other player's hand; the rule is there so an answer never names a card its hand no longer holds
