Classic #4 Palantir answers the opponent's play of any Spell whose cost per [[R65]] is (1) — an X Spell out of play counts as 0, so none is stolen — with no prompt: the play is countered into its controller's hand ([[R448]]: a countered card was never played) and Palantir's controller casts it ([[R70]]: free, a play of theirs for every play counter). The cast is random ([[R452]]): every choice the Spell asks, its declared targets, its modes, X and the prompts it opens for its caster, is drawn from the match rng, each target pick aimed by its declaration ([[R656]]: a harmful pick narrows to the caster's enemies, a helpful one to their friends, when one is offered), so "in your favour" never asks the caster anything. The base face Tributes itself as it steals; the Radiant face counts its steals (`memory`, a declared `threshold` of 3) and Tributes itself at the count. Neither seat's game summary counts the stolen card as its play ([[R376]]): it is the opponent's announced play countered, then a cast.
