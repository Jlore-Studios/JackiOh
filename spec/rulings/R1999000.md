Until #1999 only a spent slot filled the token bars ([[§14.5]]): a card pack's duplicate with
nothing of its rolled rarity left to own ([[R1301002]]) and a cosmetic pack's slot with nothing
unowned at all ([[R1306001]]). A slot that unlocked something new, a new card, a duplicate's new
cosmetic or a cosmetic pack's new cosmetic, added nothing, so a young collection, whose slots are
mostly new, barely moved the bars. The owner's decision in #1999 is that every slot counts:

- **The rule.** Every opened slot adds the points of the rarity it rolled to both token bars,
  whatever it grants: a new card, a duplicate's per-card cosmetic, an account cosmetic of a
  duplicate's global roll ([[R1661001]]), a cosmetic pack's cosmetic and the card it brings, or
  nothing. The slot still grants exactly what it granted before; the points come on top.
- **Which rarity.** A card pack's slot reads its card rarity, pity's force included, in
  `TOKEN_BAR_POINTS_BY_CARD_RARITY`. A cosmetic pack's slot reads the cosmetic rarity it rolled,
  not the one it fell to ([[R1306001]]), in `TOKEN_BAR_POINTS_BY_COSMETIC_RARITY`, as
  [[R1953000]] already read a spent one's.
- **The answer.** An opening's `bars.slotPoints` holds every slot's points, none of them 0, so
  every opening moves both bars. The bars settle as before, each paying for every size it holds
  and keeping the overflow in the opening's transaction ([[R1305000]]). The client flies each
  slot's points into the bars and fills them from that answer alone, counting none (CLAUDE.md
  rule 7).
- **Replay.** The rolls and their logs are unchanged: no points are logged, and `roll_opening` and
  `roll_cosmetic_pack` draw exactly as before, so every logged opening replays as it did
  ([[R1301003]], [[R1306003]]).
- **The sizes stay.** `CARD_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE` and `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE` stay 200 and both targets
  23 card packs. [[R1953000]]'s 8.65 points a pack already counted every slot, which then held
  only on a complete collection; it now holds on every collection, so the target does too. A
  cosmetic pack's points come on top of its card packs'.

This amends [[R1301002]] and [[R1306001]] (a spent slot grants nothing more and adds its points as
every slot does), [[R1661001]] (a global roll's hit adds its slot's points) and [[R1953000]]
(`slotPoints` is 0 for no slot, and its sizes hold on any collection), and edits [[§14.3]],
[[§14.5]] and [[§14.6]]. A dev account's fill ([[R1310003]]) is unchanged.

Amended by [[R2728000]]: the cosmetic token bar's size and target are now per step of its cycle;
the points every slot adds, and the card token bar's size and target, stand.

Hearthstone semantics: none applies directly, since Hearthstone's dust comes only from
disenchanting; the spec's bars already paid by the rarity a slot rolled ([[R1953000]]), and this
only widens which slots pay.

Rejected: paying spent slots alone, the rule this replaces; the rarity a cosmetic slot fell to,
which is not the one it rolled; new bar sizes, which the issue leaves out of scope; and logging
each slot's points, which a replay does not need, since they follow from the logged slots.
