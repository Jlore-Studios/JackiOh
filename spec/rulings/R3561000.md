Black Hole's (S+ #77) "closes after swallowing 10 permanents" is its declared `permanents`
([[R386]], [[R482]]). The hole keeps the number the Spell resolved with, so a Degraded or Upgraded
Black Hole closes after its own; a hole opened with none closes at the engine's 10. The Radiant
text leaves the closing out, but the face opens the same hole ([[R3554000]]) and closes at the
printed 10, which no Degrade or Upgrade of it moves ([[R1431]]). Rejected: the engine's 10 for
every hole (a tuned number that lies); a Radiant hole that never closes (a lasting midlane wipe).
