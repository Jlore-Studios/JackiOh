GIGA is a size taken as the card enters the field: printed, granted to a hand card, or an
embiggened conditional keyword ([[R1878001]]) alike. It is fixed on the field — no grant there
changes it — and [[R78]]'s reset ends it, so a card back after leaving the field enters at its
new size; Flicker and Transform keep it. Rejected: a size following field keywords, which would
resize a permanent under its cover; and a separate mark field, which the stamped `giga` already is.
