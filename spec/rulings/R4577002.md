IA #2 Scrap Prototype's "Shuffle a copy of this with +1/+1 into your deck" and IA #19 Infinite Scroll's Radiant "Shuffle a copy of this into your deck; it costs (1) less" put a rider on the copy. `shuffleInto` takes two: `buff`, added to each fresh card's buffs, and `costMod`, added to its `costMod` as `addToHand`'s is; absent, the card is as it was. The copy is fresh, of the face that died or was cast ([[R57]]), with R57's riders (its tuning, enchantments, granted tags and Chinese flag) and none of the card copied's buffs or discount, so the rider is the text's alone: a Scrap Prototype shuffled in by a Scrap Prototype that was itself a copy is 2/3 again, never 3/4, and a copy of a Radiant Infinite Scroll's copy costs (1) less again, never (2). Nothing compounds. As in Hearthstone, a card that dies loses what was put on it, and its Death makes a new card. A full library refuses a copy as it refuses any ([[R80]], [[R316]]).
