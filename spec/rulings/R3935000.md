[[R3555001]] read the Walls' size as `EMBIGGENED_SCALE` times the card's stats at the moment, so
anything that changed those stats was multiplied too: Harmony Spirit's 3/3 left an Embiggened Thorium
Wall at 12/12, Dino-size's 10/10 at 40/40, and a Buff or Nerf counted four times. This amends it.

- **Printed face.** The bonus is `EMBIGGENED_SCALE - 1` times the printed face of the face the card
  wears: its definition's stats, never `face_of`. It is lent at [[§10.4]] layer 2 as before.
- **Set stats.** After a Set stats ([[R1185002]]) the Wall has a `stats_override` and no bonus: it stands
  at exactly the stats set, as every Unit does ([[R781003]] clears the Buffs it had). It stays GIGA, and
  an Immutable Wall refuses the set as before.
- **Buff or Nerf.** A stat Buff or Nerf (tuning) counts once, at layer 1, a Buff the card took in hand
  before it was played included. Hearthstone semantics: a set replaces the stat line, and an
  enchantment after it adds its own amount once.
- **Unchanged.** The GIGA, the stamp and the Vanilla reading of [[R3555001]]: a Vanilla returns the
  Wall to its printed stats and keeps its size.

Rejected: keeping `face_of` and subtracting the double count (it would still multiply a set).
