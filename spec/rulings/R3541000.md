S+ #86 Astrophage's Evolve, "Place a Plague Counter on and steal a stat point from each Unit" (Radiant: 3 points, the declared `unit`), reads "each Unit" as every other Unit on both sides that a harmful walk reaches, the Unit whose play evolved it among them; a Dormant card ([[R1839000]]) and a card beneath a Stack ([[R13]]) are passed by, and so is one Immune to the walk ([[R940]]). Astrophage is never one of them: it places no Counter on itself and steals nothing from itself. Each such Unit receives one Plague Counter ([[R471]]), whether or not it has anything to give. From each, the face's points are taken as Attack while it has any and then as Health, but never the last point of Health, so the theft alone destroys nothing: a Unit with 2 Attack and 2 Health gives 2 Attack and 1 Health to a 3-point theft, a 0/1 gives nothing. Astrophage gains what it took, stat for stat: the Attack taken as Attack and the Health taken as Health, in one buff once the thefts are made. It evolves once ([[R3536000]]) when an opponent plays a Unit ([[R3536001]]), so this runs once. Rejected: giving every stolen point as Attack, since a stolen Health point is a Health point; placing the Counter only on a Unit that gave a point, since the text places it on each Unit; and taking Health before Attack, since Attack is the stat the text lists first.
