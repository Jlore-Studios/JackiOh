## R1224 — The repayment schedule: one instalment per refresh, forgiven when short

Built (ME-TURN's repayment schedule, Meditative #89). One turn's debt is split into instalments as
evenly as possible, larger first: 4 is 1, 1, 1, 1; 3 is 1, 1, 1, 0; 2 is 1, 1, 0, 0. One
instalment falls due at each of the borrower's next four refreshes ([[§2.3]]; their own turns, an
extra turn counting), and that refresh gives that much less mana, never below 0. The instalments
are locked in as soon as the mana is borrowed; the schedule is public, as Hinder's is, and the
view shows each instalment as locked crystals on the turn it falls due. When debts from different
turns fall due on the same refresh, their instalments add up. A missed instalment is forgiven:
mana never goes below 0, so an instalment that finds less mana takes what there is, and the rest
of it is dropped, never carried to a later turn. The debt stays if the lender leaves the field.
The AI counts what is owed against the side that owes it.
