After [[R65]]'s discounts: flat price rules (modifiers and auras), then threshold rules (each reads the number the flat rules left, as [[R363]] reads Curvature), then "costs (N)", which wins over every add, then the card's floor, then 0. X cards ignore every rule but the floor and a ban. A rule "until used" is spent by the next play it applies to, whether or not it moved that play's price (a Trap C #6 already sets to (0) spends C #2's), never by a cast. A ban reads the final price and refuses plays from hand and graveyard, never a cast. Forever&'s rider is taken at step 4 by the next Spell played or cast; that Spell returns to hand from where it landed
