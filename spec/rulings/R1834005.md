A person's seat now wears their loadout ([[R1834002]]). A bot's seat that wore only the defaults
would be the one seat of the ladder that never did, a tell that [[R1452]] forbids, and a bot owns no
collection to wear one from ([[R1454]]). So a bot draws what it wears as a person might own and
pick it. It amends [[R1452]] (*What a client sees*: a bot's cosmetics are drawn as a person's might
be, as its username and portrait are) and [[R1454]] (a bot holds a wardrobe, which is not a
collection).

**The wardrobe.** What a bot may wear past the starters is drawn from the pool's seed
(`{seed}:cosmetics:…`), so one seed draws one wardrobe: each eligible non-starter at the share of
people of the bot's account age that own it. Each holding is rolled once for the bot, on a stream of
its own from the seed of its first draw, which its row keeps: a later draw reads the same roll
against the share of its age then, so it owns each at that share and gains one only as the share
grows, never by being rolled again at each refresh, which would carry it past the people's. A cosmetic is eligible only when at least
`BOT_COSMETIC_MIN_OWNERS` people own it, on that card for a per-card one, and it was first granted
to a person at least `BOT_COSMETIC_MIN_AGE_DAYS` ago, so no bot wears what few people hold or what
has only just come out. A ladder bot's own profile is never counted, a cosmetic no grant row dates
is never eligible, and no portrait is: a bot's portraits are still dealt from its seed ([[R642]]).
The wardrobe is kept on the bot's row (`bots.cosmetics`, migration 0061) in the ledger's shape, and
never in the ledger, `collection_grants` or `GET /api/cosmetics`, so [[R1454]]'s "owns no
collection" holds and the economy never sees it.

**What it wears.** From the wardrobe and the same fit, the bot draws what a person's settings, decks
and cards would hold: its account's six picks, with its Full-art cards switch on; each deck's three
slots, its Best-of-1 decks' and its Conquest trio's ([[R641000]]); and its cards' own picks, a deck
at a time: each card that wears an earned foil, finish or border in its wardrobe wears it at the
fit's wear of it on that card; then, at the fit's share of seats that pick cards of their own, its
mean number of cards a deck, less those that just wore an earned one, are each given a pick other
than the account's, a default or a starter drawn at the fit's shares of people's picks on cards (a
kind at even odds under the prior); and each full art it owns is printed at the fit's wear rate. Each slot is
drawn among what the wardrobe owns there so that the bots wear each item at the fit's share of
people's seats wearing it, defaults included. That share counts the seats that do not own the item
too, so the kind's non-starters are offered first, each to a bot that owns it at its share over the
share of seats still undrawn and its share of owners, and a bot that takes none draws among the
starters and the default by their shares. An earned foil, finish or border is owned on one card
only, so its share of people's picks pooled over every card would hold on no card a bot dresses
(a bot of a fifteen-card deck dressing one card would wear it fifteen times too rarely): its wear
is fitted on its own card, of the people who own it there and play that card, and the wardrobe
holding it at its share of owners, the bots that play the card wear it at the share of people that
do, whatever else their deck holds.

**When it draws.** The pool's refresh draws a bot's cosmetics once it holds decks; again for its
decks and its cards' picks whenever its decks are dealt again, which leaves their slots unset; and
every `BOT_COSMETIC_REDRAW_DAYS` it ages as an account does: it keeps what it held that is still
eligible, may gain an item, and each slot changes at `BOT_COSMETIC_SLOT_CHANGE_SHARE`.

**The seat.** A bot's deck carries the slots it drew for it as a person's does, and its seat freezes
through the same `freeze_loadout`, its wardrobe as what it owns, onto the same match row, sent in the
same frames by the same rule ([[R1834002]], [[R1834003]]). Nothing a client reads tells its loadout
from a person's.

**The fit.** The shares come from `BOT_COSMETIC_FITS`, one row per account-age bucket
(`BOT_COSMETIC_AGE_BUCKETS_DAYS`), pasted from `jackioh-server cosmetics-fit` ([[R1834006]]). A
bucket with no row, or one under `BOT_COSMETIC_FIT_MIN_SAMPLES` seats, draws from
`BOT_COSMETIC_PRIOR`: each slot's default at `BOT_COSMETIC_PRIOR_DEFAULT_SHARE` and the rest split
among the kind's starters, cards of their own at `BOT_COSMETIC_PRIOR_PER_CARD_SHARE` and
`BOT_COSMETIC_PRIOR_CARDS_PER_DECK`, a full art worn at `BOT_COSMETIC_PRIOR_FULL_ART_WEAR`, and an
eligible item owned at `BOT_COSMETIC_PRIOR_OWNERSHIP`. `BOT_COSMETIC_FITS` is empty until enough
people's matches have frozen a loadout.

Nothing here is a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a bot's cosmetics reach no state, log, replay hash or
search, and none of its draws is the match rng.

Hearthstone semantics: an opponent's card back and versions say nothing of who is playing them.

Rejected: drawing every bot's cosmetics from the defaults, which tells its seat from a person's;
granting a bot its wardrobe through the ledger, which would give it a collection, its rows counted
in the economy and in the very ownership rates it draws from; weighing a slot's items by the fit's
shares among what one bot owns, which counts ownership twice and wears a non-starter at its share
times its owners' share; offering an earned per-card item at its share of people's picks pooled
over every card, which wears it too rarely by about the number of cards a deck holds; and dating a
cosmetic's age from a
ship date in `cosmetics.json`, which holds none: its age counts from its first grant to a person.
