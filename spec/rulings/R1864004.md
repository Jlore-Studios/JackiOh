"You may Trade up to {trades} cards each turn" (Radiant The Trade Union, [[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #165) gives its controller that many Trades a turn while it acts on their field (`Script.tradesPerTurn`), a pure read asked of the cards acting there as Radiant Paranoia's Echo bonus is ([[R1045]]), so a face-down card's text sleeps ([[R1487000]]). A player's Trades a turn ([[R1838000]], `trade::trades_per_turn_of`) are the larger of their own figure (`TRADES_PER_TURN`, or one an effect set) and the most any card acting on their field gives: two Unions give two, not four, as several Radiant Paranoias give one Echo bonus. Leaving the field, the card gives nothing, so the limit falls back at once, the Trades already made that turn still counted. The number is the Radiant face's alone ([[R824000]]), read through `param()`. The most holding is the card's row in [[§8.10]]; this note says where it is read. Rejected: adding the figures, which the text ("up to 2") does not say.
