The fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, up to the next card's marker. Two of the game's own rules run in such a run with no marker of their own. S+ #103 Transcend transformed a Unit into one whose aura bars Defense Position ([[R1849006]]), and [[§4.5]] step 1 switched an immune enemy Unit out of Defense. And S+ #97 Selective Enrollment's cost trigger ran at the end of a turn just before Burn ticked on an immune Unit ([[R4154000]]).
**These are rules, not the Spell.** [[§6.1]] keeps a Spell's effects off the Unit. The state check and the affliction ticks are the game's, and they reach every Unit the rule names, an immune one included: one barred from Defense is switched out of it, and a Unit carrying Burn or Toxic takes its tick and loses its Burn.
**What I21 allows.** A `positionSwitched` to Attack of an immune Unit that stands in Attack Position after the step while it is Indestructible, has Can't Defend or Evasive, or an aura bars Defense ([[R2179000]], [[R2657000]], [[R3543002]], [[R1849006]], I17's reading); and a sourceless `damage` or `damageAbsorbed` via Burn or Toxic on it ([[R4154000]], [[R3540001]]), with the Burn's removal right after its hit. A Spell's own switch, a hit with a source and a counter change with no tick before it are still reaches.
Rejected: ending a Spell's run at its last effect. The engine marks where a hook begins, never where it ends ([[R2783000]]), and a new marker for the rules' work would move nothing a player sees.
