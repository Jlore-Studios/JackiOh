Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash, and a set piece is claimed from the seat's own redacted entries alone ([[R202]]). The biggest moments may own the board for a beat: a wipe, a Starship's launch, a system's climax (issue #2782). [[R2722001]]'s takeover hold is made the one hold there is. **A set piece** is an animation entry something claims (`AnimationEntry.setPiece`): a readable Mythic's entrance claims the takeover set piece (`takeoverClaim`, its beat `TAKEOVER_HOLD_MS`, 1 s, as before), and a card or a system claims its own through the queue's `setPieceOf` (the board's `setPieceOf`), asked first, with its key and the beat it asks for at speed 1 (`SET_PIECE_HOLD_MS`, 2 s, unless it names another). Until issue #2782's set pieces land, the one such claim is the fixture set piece of the `/dev/fx` gallery ([[R2784000]]): "Play as a set piece" plays the card picked there with its play's entry claimed for `SET_PIECE_HOLD_MS`. The animation queue, never the effects layer, gives the claimed entry its hold ahead of its motion (`setPieceEntries`). The hold is scaled by the effects speed ([[R201]]), never below `MIN_ENTRY_MS` and never past `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS`, 3 s of real time whatever the beat asked and whatever the speed. It is pinned against the burst budget, so a burst never squeezes it, and the board's `data-animating` stays on through it, so `cy.settled()` and practice's pacing wait exactly as long and no longer. One action takes at most `SET_PIECES_PER_ACTION` (1): a second claim in the same action, whether a takeover's or another's, plays with no hold, as a plain entry. The effects layer draws a set piece inside its held entry, as [[R200]] has it draw every effect.

**Big moments.** The effects settings gain Big moments, `full`, `short` or `off`, in the settings panel's Visuals beside the effects speed and intensity, stored with them in `jackioh.fx.v1` and synced with the account's `fx` group ([[R633]]); a stored value it does not know reads as `full`. `full` holds the beat asked, `short` at most `SET_PIECE_SHORT_HOLD_MS` (0.6 s) of it, before the speed and the cap; `off` holds nothing, and the entry plays as any other, its effects still drawn.

**Skip.** A tap or a click anywhere on the board, or Space, ends the hold in flight at once: the queue ends the entry as its timer would have and starts the next (`skip`), and the effects layer cuts the set piece's effects to their end (`cut`): what it still had to land on the board's own elements lands now, every other effect of it goes, and the stand-ins and the cards hidden for the burst stay until the board shows the next view, as ever. The skip does nothing else: the click reaches no control, the Space presses no focused button (its keyup is swallowed too), and Space in a text field is the field's. A plain entry has nothing to skip, and a click or a key then is what it always was. "Tap to skip" is the first skip control there is: it fades in `SET_PIECE_SKIP_HINT_MS` (0.5 s) into the hold, takes no pointer event and is hidden from assistive tech. This amends [[R2126000]]: Space is the one key added to the board.

**Reduced motion.** Under Reduce Motion (the panel's or the system's) and with the effects off there is no hold, no hint, no shake, no flash and no sweep. The queue's reduced-motion drain is synchronous, so there is no still frame of the moment either (`SET_PIECE_STILL_MS` is not made); a Mythic's own still card ([[R2722001]]) is unchanged, holds nothing and is no set piece.

**Where it plays.** A live game, the practice and tutorial boards, both seats of a hotseat game (a set piece of either seat holds the board of the seat watching it), a replay and watching (both a `replay` board) play it. The Duos teammate's read-only board, silent by [[R2095000]], plays none. The engine's and the server's clocks are unchanged: a hold eats into the watching seat's real time, as every animation does, and `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS` bounds it to 3 s an action.

This amends [[R200]] and [[R2722001]]: the one beat that holds the game is a set piece's, and the takeover is the first. It plays as before at every speed the slider offers but a quarter, where the cap holds its 1 s, scaled, to 3 s instead of 4 s. Rejected: `SET_PIECE_STILL_MS`, a still frame under reduced motion, which would hold a queue that drains at once and buys nothing a still card does not; fast-forwarding a skipped set piece's cues to their tails, which the director cannot seek and which would draw a burst of light after the player asked it to stop; clearing every effect at a skip as a drain does, which would pop a carried card back into its hand until the burst ends; and a sound bed for a set piece, a sequence of existing `sfx.ts` recipes ducking the music as an intro does, deferred to the first real set piece of issue #2782, since the takeover has its intro already. [[R2800000]] lands the first of them, the board clears' set pieces, each with a bed laid across its hold; and a Mythic whose own wipe in its action is a set piece yields its takeover to it, so the action holds once. [[R2801000]] adds a Starship's launch (`starship`), whose bed lays its landing after the hold, and a still frame of it under Reduce Motion: the `SET_PIECE_STILL_MS` rejected here held the queue, while the launch's still frame holds nothing, as a Mythic's still card does.
