The Radiant face of every Call to Chaos card, #95, C+ #73 and Meditative #95 ([[R1240]]), casts three different random effects from its own list of ten, resolved in the list's order with the recursion ("cast a random Call to Chaos") where it falls, in place of #95's old Radiant pair of the recursion and one other, so one rule serves every edition ([[R28]], [[R87]]). The three are different entries ([[R60]]), each resolving as the base face's one does, and a recursion drawn at `CALL_TO_CHAOS_CHAIN_CAP` resolves into nothing and is not replaced ([[R87]])
