A unit-token card in a library — one of the copies #33 shuffles in when the Rush Token card #75 gave is played ([[R34]]) — leaves it only by being drawn ([[R11]]), so a Recruit (#69 Call to Arms, #71 Intern Stimmy, #98's Recruit power) passes over it to the next card its filter matches, as it passes over a Spell ([[§6.3]]), and finds nothing when no other card matches. Summoning it would take it out of the library some other way, and [[R11]] would have it cease to exist on the way The same holds for a reveal from the library that puts the pick in a hand (#51 KY's Private Tutor): it passes over a unit-token card, and the Tutor offers no type or bracket that only such a card matches.
