A card's winrate of record is the newest per-version table row at or before the version its id
names, past patches included, among rows with at least `WIN_RATE_RECORD_MIN_GAMES` (20) games: the
frozen tables the cards crate compiles in (`crates/cards/patches/win_rates/`, R1329000), walked from
the reference version back to `BROWSER_CARRIED_FROM`. It is the same under a fold, because the
tables and the snapshots they ship with never change (R3049000). A card with no recorded winrate —
no row, or only rows under the floor — counts as 50% (the owner, 2026-11-09), and so does KPIs
itself when it has none. Every comparison stays in integers, as R3049001's do. Rejected: the game's
own snapshot, which keeps 8 rows and is not handed to practice, AI or fuzz games.
