Issue #3951 gave C #28 Second Wind the text "Indestructible; Cry: Discard your hand and exile your deck; Aura: Every card in your deck and graveyard has Flashback", its Radiant face "Cry: Discard your hand and your deck". The Cry discards the whole hand first, as written ([[R16]]: no choice), then the base face exiles the library, each card its own exile ([[R135]]), and the Radiant face moves every card of it to its owner's graveyard, top first (`discard_library`): each lands as any card bound there does, through a "would go to a graveyard" replacement if one acts, a unit token ceasing to exist ([[R11]]). It is no draw, so no fatigue ([[§2.4]]), and no discard of a hand card, so it reports no `discarded`, no discard guard stops it ([[R800]]) and nothing that answers a discard answers it: Magic's mill, not Hearthstone's discard, which only ever reads a hand. The Aura is S #152 Sanctum's ([[R1869003]]): every card in its controller's deck and graveyard has Flashback ([[R783012]]), so the cards the Cry put in the graveyard may each be played once from there at its cost ([[R783013]]). Second Wind is Indestructible on both faces, so a destroy leaves its Aura standing. Its old Cry and play-from-the-graveyard permission ([[R393]]) are what it did through v0.6.4b, whose games fold under the kept script ([[R767000]]).
