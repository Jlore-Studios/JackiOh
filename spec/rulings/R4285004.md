M #87 Tatches the Totem since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes IV, which says it "no longer summons itself from your hand", read literally on both faces: "While this is in your deck: After you play a card with a tribal tag, summon this." It keeps its deck trigger ([[R464]]) and loses the hand trigger issue #3951 gave it, so a Tatches in its owner's hand stays there after a tribal play, and one in the deck is summoned as before. Through v0.6.7k it summoned itself from either (`script_v0_6_7k`, kept, [[R767000]]); were the designer to mean both zones gone, it is one script line and this ruling.
