#65 Masochism Mask reads, on both faces, "After you take {downsides} downsides from this, make your hand Radiant." after its Start-of-turn Choose, `downsides` being 6. A downside is any pick of the Mask's own Choose that is not "nothing": exile the bottom card of your deck, lose {loss} health ([[R18]]), summon a Spikey Pillow. The request's "facing punishment" is read as taking one of those three.

- **The count is the card's memory.** It rises by one for each downside, after the downside's own effect has resolved; at the sixth the whole hand becomes Radiant ([[§6.3]] Make Radiant, the cards in it at that moment) and the count restarts at 0, so the Mask does it again every sixth downside, with no limit. Its trigger runs before the turn's draw ([[R62]]), so the card that turn draws is not in the hand yet and is not Radiant, nor is a card drawn or added later, until the next sixth.
- **"Nothing" counts none.** The Radiant face's double pick counts each pick: a downside and a "nothing" are one, and when its first pick is the sixth, the hand is Radiant before the second prompt opens and the second pick, if it is a downside, is the first of the next six. Its two picks reach six in three turns, the base face's one in six.
- **Only its own turns count.** The Mask asks at the start of its controller's turn and not at the opponent's ([[R62]]), so the opponent's turns add nothing. The count lives on the instance and goes with it when it leaves the field ([[R78]]): a Mask played again starts at 0.
- **`downsides` is a declared number** ([[R386]]): a Buff takes it to 5, a Nerf to 7, at least 1.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, which counts nothing ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: the Mask doing it once and then stopping, which the request's words do not say; and counting the Radiant face's "nothing", which is no punishment.
