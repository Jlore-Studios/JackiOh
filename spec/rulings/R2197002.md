"Death: summon a Trigor with +3/+3" ([[§8.11]] TG #25 Trigor, printed "Death: Summon a Trigor with this one's base stats +3/+3.") summons a fresh base Trigor whose base stats are the dying one's plus 3/3, set as a stat override as [[R1181]] sets Meditative #94 Shrinking Felinor's, read off the card as it died ([[R78]]): 3/3, 6/6, 9/9 and on. It is not a copy ([[R57]]): no buff, granted keyword or tuning carries. It arrives in the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), a summon, so it fires no Cry and pays no Tribute ([[§6.3]]); a full unit row summons none. The Radiant face summons a base body too, at the dying Radiant one's 6/6 plus 6/6, which then grows by the base face's 3/3 ([[R1828003]]'s pattern). This is the terminator REVIEW A3.6 asks of a self-recursive Death: each body is strictly larger than the last, so an aura or a standing effect that takes N Health kills at most ⌈N/3⌉ of them before one survives, and the state check ([[§4.5]]) never loops. Rejected: a fresh 6/6 every time (Hearthstone's literal "summon a X with +3/+3"), which a stacked −6 Health aura would kill forever; and a cap, which cumulative growth makes unnecessary.
