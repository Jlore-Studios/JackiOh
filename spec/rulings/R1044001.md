Once every card figure reads the totals ([[R1044000]]), a game record's detail (both decklists, opening hands, draws, plays and their turns) serves only `stats-export` and the analysis notebooks. The retention purge empties it `GAME_RECORD_RAW_RETENTION_DAYS` (7) after the record was written, and only once the record is folded into the totals: a record the totals do not hold yet keeps its detail until it is folded.

The record's row stays with its id, source, mode, patch and time, so a second write of the same game is still refused and is never folded twice ([[R376]], [[R378]]). Every figure reads the same after the purge as before it. A development run's records also stay in the files the run wrote (the training box's `~/training-out/<lane>/`), so its detail can be read off those for as long as they are kept; a live game's detail older than a week is gone, as is its log after `MATCH_ACTION_RETENTION_DAYS`.

This amends [[R376]], under which a record outlived its match's action log: its counts in the totals do, and its id; its detail goes after a week. The privacy policy says a finished match's summary for card statistics is kept as long as the statistics are, which stays true.

Rejected: deleting the row with its detail, which would let the same game be written and folded again; and emptying a record not yet folded, which would lose its counts.
