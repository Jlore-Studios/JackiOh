C+ #62's player picks points, not coefficients: its curve over the board — x the lane (0 to 4 from its caster's lane 1), y the row (0 their backrow, 1 their units, 2 the enemy units, 3 the enemy backrow) — is the lowest-degree polynomial, at most cubic, through the 1 to 4 cells the caster picks in different lanes, in exact rationals (Lagrange), which is how a player "creates" one without typing it. The cells are asked one prompt at a time (a board-cell prompt, [[§10.6]]), each offering the 4 cells of every lane not yet used and, after the first, "done", so every curve stays reachable within the prompt answer bound. A card is on the curve when its cell satisfies y = p(x) exactly — cards are points at their cells, and between lanes the curve touches nothing — so four cells fix the curve, which may also cross the fifth lane. Every card on it is exiled (on the Radiant face, every enemy card), and a client may show the equation
