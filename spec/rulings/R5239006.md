FvH #112.1 Recursion Module's base face prints "Flashback (without its Cry)". A permanent whose running face carries `flashbackWithoutCry` runs no Cry at [[§10.5]] step 5 on a play that spends its Flashback ([[R783012]], [[R783013]]): the play is a play for every other rule, it is placed and counted, and the granted Combos of step 5 still run; only its own Cry does not, nor any extra run of it. Any other play of the card, from a hand or from a graveyard under a field permission, runs its Cry as ever ([[R1]]). A Spell's resolution is no Cry ([[R822]]), so a Spell with the flag still resolves. A card fused with such an ingredient carries the flag ([[R77]]), so FvH #112 Jilliax Deluxe fused with the base Recursion Module plays its Flashback without its Cry; the module's Radiant face prints plain Flashback and runs it.
