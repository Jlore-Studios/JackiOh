On every attack Windfast makes, declared (spending its exertion) or forced, before the attack is declared, its controller picks a Unit in their hand (a `hand` prompt, held on the opponent's turn under its own clock, [[R79]]; one Unit is no prompt, [[R129]]), which is summoned into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]], no Cry; a summon, so traps that answer one fire) and makes the attack on the same target. The substitute keeps the attack declared or forced, as Windfast's was: a declared one opens its trap window (always forcing it would let Windfast dodge every attack trap), and the `attackDeclared` event carries `insteadOf` naming Windfast. The substitute attacks only if [[§4.2]] step 2's restrictions allow it; Windfast neither strikes nor is struck. The base face bounces the summoned card after that combat's state check if it is still on the field, whether or not it fought. With no Unit in hand or no open zone the replacement cannot happen and Windfast attacks itself; a summoned Unit that leaves before its combat makes no attack, Windfast's attack being spent either way. Each of Windfury's attacks summons its own Unit. "A Unit from your hand" with no "random" is the controller's pick.
