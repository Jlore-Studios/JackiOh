The owner ruled that conceding counts as losing (#2429). The tutorial can't be lost except by conceding: [[R2201000]]'s floor still holds against everything the game does to the player, and a concede, which was already the player's own and outside it, now ends the lesson as a loss in so many words.

- **The result** is titled "Lesson lost" and says "You conceded. That counts as a loss." with the lesson's tip and Retry ([[§9.10]]).
- **Progress.** The lesson is not won, so nothing after it opens. This device keeps it as lost (`lost` in the progress record, [[R294]]), in path order, and the path says "Lost: you conceded" on it until the lesson is won, which clears it. Like the Field Guide's entries ([[R2205002]]) it is the device's alone: the account's copy ([[R320]]) keeps the lessons won and nothing else, and adopting it never clears the device's loss.
- A draw by the turn cap or a flight is still no loss: it offers a retry and records nothing.

Hearthstone semantics: conceding a match is losing it.

Rejected: keeping the loss on the account too, which would change the server's row and its RLS for a record no rule reads.
