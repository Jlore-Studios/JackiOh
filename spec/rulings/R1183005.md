A Discover may offer its cards as their code blocks alone ([[R1183004]]; `discover_from_catalog`'s `offer: code`, [[R247]]'s third kind): three different cards drawn from the pool as any Discover's are ([[R60]], [[R380]], never the asking card, [[R387]]), only cards that have a code block. Each option is its place in the offer (`code-0`, `code-1`, `code-2`), labelled with its card's code, and the view marks it `code` and names no definition ([[§10.8]]); which card stands behind each place rides the prompt's resume data, which no view sends, as [[R465]]'s answer key does, so the chooser reads no id, name or face of the cards until the pick, and the other player reads only that a prompt is open. The resume step reads the picked place's card (`discovered_code_card`). Meditative+ #25 adds that card to its hand Radiant (M #88's shape), a full hand burning it, the Radiant face at a `costOverride` of setCost (0). Like any prompt it is answered by the AI, by the random policy and by a timeout ([[R79]]); the AI, as with any Discover, may weigh the cards the engine knows stand behind the places.
