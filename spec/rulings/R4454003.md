No effect reaches a Dormant card ([[§6.1]] Dormant, [[R1839000]]). The fuzz monitor's I19 (issue #4454) found Core #85 Unlicensed Experimentation fusing an opponent's played permanent onto a Dormant Starship: the trap matches every permanent of its controller's of the played card's type, and `fuseCards`, the verb every card's Fuse runs (§6.3 Fuse, [[R77]]), took any instance it was named, as an ingredient or as a target.
**The verb passes it by.** A Dormant card named to `fuseCards`, as an ingredient or as a target, drops out as a card no longer there does, as a Transform passes a Dormant card by: with no other target the Fuse does nothing, and a random target is drawn among the others. Core #85's trap therefore still fires when its only match is Dormant and fuses nothing, as it does when its only match is Immutable ([[R23]], [[R61]]). A Starship's own fuse of a dead piece is no card's Fuse and is unchanged ([[R1852000]]), and an Equip already hosts on no Dormant card ([[R2260000]]).
A replay this change plays differently is refused as `rules_changed` rather than shown wrong ([[R767000]]).
Rejected: a Dormant check in Core #85's own match alone, which would leave every other card's Fuse able to reach one.
