## R1223 — A credit line: spend mana you don't have, owing up to the limit at once

Built (Meditative #89 Jlarna's Aura, MD-E13 rewritten). While a card with the `credit_line` static
flag acts on its controller's field, any mana they spend — a card's price, an X, an Activate's
cost — may go past the mana they have: their current mana goes first, and the shortfall is
borrowed. What they owe at once (every instalment not yet taken) never passes the limit (4), and
everything borrowed during one turn is one debt, re-split each time it grows. Two or more lenders
share one line: the limit is the largest acting one, never their sum. Mana spent on the
opponent's turn is borrowed the same way, repaid from the spender's own refreshes. One
affordability function, `spendable_mana` (current plus what the line still offers), decides what a
player can pay — `legal_actions`, the reducer's refusal and the AI all read it, so they agree.
Jlarna's own price is never borrowed: its Aura acts only once it is on the field.
