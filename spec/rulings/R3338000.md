Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. The Terminus cards are the game's end-of-the-world cards, and the owner asked that they feel it (issue #3338, part of #3330). Each of the nine, Stinkyscape #4 Olivian Expanse, its six Terminus tokens (#4.1 Farewell, #4.2 KY's Last Exam, #4.3 Event Horizon, #4.4 Last Word, #4.5 1v1 Final Destination, #4.6 The Big Crunch), Core #100 Ceaseless Void and Stinkyscape #154 Dice of Doom, has a set piece of its own ([[R2785000]]'s one held beat per action), named by its entry in its set's effects table (`{ setPiece: "<key>" }`): `terminusRift`, `farewell`, `lastExam`, `eventHorizon`, `lastWord`, `finalDestination`, `bigCrunch`, `voidFold` and `diceOfDoom`.

**Its moment.** Each set piece names the moment it plays over. Farewell's and Ceaseless Void's are board clears, a wipe as [[R2800000]] plays one. Every other is a **signature**: the run of its hook's consecutive events of the types it names, opened by the first type it lists, under its marker (a `hookResolving`, [[R2783000]]) or, for a Trap, its own `trapFired`, one animation entry of `SIGNATURE_RUN_MS` (1.2 s) pinned against the burst budget, after which the hook is closed, so a hook signs once; The Big Crunch's is its first cast's entry. Olivian Expanse's is its Discover's `addedToHand`; KY's Last Exam's its answer's `damage`; Event Horizon's its extra turn's `modifierChanged`; Last Word's its firing, its way to the graveyard and the turns it rearranges; 1v1 Final Destination's its firing, its `locked` lanes, its `healthSet`s and its discards; Dice of Doom's its `rolled`, its Lucky gain and the Terminus cards it adds or burns, each burned card's way to the graveyard among them. Another hook of the same card opens no signature: Dice of Doom's end-of-turn return, an `addedToHand` (or a `burned` into a full hand) with no `rolled` before it, plays its own rows on both seats. The hook keys it only by a definition the seat reads: a marker R97 hides signs nothing ([[R202]]). The queue claims it as its card's set piece (`signatureClaim`) at that set piece's beat, 2 to 2.8 s at speed 1, Dice of Doom's `DOOM_MISS_HOLD_MS` (1.2 s) when its run added and burned nothing, held, capped at `SET_PIECE_HOLD_CAP_MS`, scaled, shortened by Big moments' "short" and dropped by "off", and skipped by a tap, as [[R2785000]] holds every set piece. A Mythic's entrance whose own signature is the action's set piece yields its takeover to it, as one whose own wipe is does ([[R2800000]]), and an entry whose entrance yielded may still be claimed, so a Terminus Trap's reveal and its set piece are one entry held once. Olivian Expanse and KY's Last Exam wait on a prompt, so their takeover holds the play's action and their set piece the answer's.

**What it draws.** The effects layer draws each inside its held entry, in place of its run's own rows, its beats over the hold (unheld, up to `SET_PIECE_SWALLOW_AT` of the entry). Olivian Expanse: a violet rift opens, a star for each Terminus card circles in it (the pool, never the pick), and one falls into its caster's hand as a card back. Farewell: a lamp swings over the table, and every permanent and graveyard card it exiles lifts into its light and fades one by one, then a slow exhale and no shake. KY's Last Exam: the impossible problem chalks itself across the board, and the answer is a chalk bolt into the enemy hero, cracking the chalk and stamping its Echo. Event Horizon: the board smears toward its centre under a stretched clock and the turn banner shows twice. Last Word: everything freezes in mid-strike and the hit on its hero shatters. 1v1 Final Destination: every lane but one chains shut over the abyss, a beat a lane, both hero health counters spin down to what it set, and each hand's discarded Spells burn off its edge. The Big Crunch: the backdrop collapses to a bright point. Ceaseless Void: the fold of [[R2800000]], now drawing each other permanent into its chest in turn, as its removals come, and settling with its 10/10 burning in the dark. Dice of Doom: its die, a big d100 (two d80s, Radiant) on [[R2652001]]'s public faces, tumbles in from the board's edge and lands halfway through the hold; on a 67 the board goes dark, a star lights for each Terminus card and they rain into the caster's hand as card backs, one a full hand burns falling past it into its graveyard; on a miss the die crumbles; a die Lucky rolled again keeps a glint. The jackpot is read off what the run did, never off the face. Each set piece's sound bed opens with its own `organ` chord, a root of its own under [[R1883000]]'s Mythic choir, which closes it, and its run's own sounds play once, condensed, after the hold.

**What follows it.** Two play unheld when the turn they owe opens, read from the memory of the stream: Event Horizon's extra turn opens with the board snapping back, and Last Word's last turn with each of its controller's Units lit for Charge and a 3, 2, 1 on their hero that burns away inside that entry (a countdown standing through the turn would outlast its entry, [[R200]]). While The Big Crunch's play resolves, its point stands over each of its entries, and each Call to Chaos it casts flies out of it.

**The rules every one keeps.** No cue names a card's definition: a card added to a hand the seat cannot read is a card back flying to that hand, so the opponent sees how many Terminus cards rained, never which ([[R202]]). Everything it starts lands inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]), and the speed scales it with its entry ([[R201]]). Reduce Motion holds nothing and draws one still frame of each, standing `FX_TERMINUS_STILL_MS`; the effects off draw none.

This amends [[R2800000]], whose fold sank each card where it stood and whose Farewell took the shared vortex; [[R2797000]], whose Olivian Expanse played the bespoke `terminusRift` recipe, which this set piece replaces, and whose Event Horizon and The Big Crunch were `none`; and [[R2785000]], whose takeover now yields to a signature as to a wipe. Dice of Doom's end-of-turn return to the hand plays its own row, its table entry holding the set piece alone.

Rejected: one wipe-like entry per Terminus play, which would swallow its prompts' and its resolution's own rows; reading the jackpot off a face of 67, which would restate a card's number in the client; a countdown standing through Last Word's whole turn, which would outlast its entry; re-scoring the four Terminus cards' rendered intros, which needs FluidSynth, ffmpeg and a SoundFont the bot's machine lacks, so each sting is in its set piece's bed; and a second hold for Olivian Expanse's play and its pick in one action, where [[R2785000]] allows one.
