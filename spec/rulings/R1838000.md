Tradable is Hearthstone's Tradeable as the designer wrote it ([[§6.1]]): in their own main phase, with no prompt open, a player may Trade a Tradable card in their hand — the card goes into their deck at a uniformly random position drawn from the match rng, and they draw a card — **at no mana** (`TRADE_COST`, 0, where Hearthstone charges 1). The spec did not say how often, how the keyword is read, nor what the action looks like; this note decides it.

- **One Trade per player per turn, counted over all their Tradable cards**: `TRADES_PER_TURN` (1), or the player's own figure once an effect sets one (`PlayerState.tradesPerTurn`, read only by `trade::trades_per_turn_of`), so a later card can raise it to two without a literal anywhere. The count is `PlayerState.trades`, `{ turn, count }` as a draw count is ([[R457]]): absent until a player first trades, and a count kept for an earlier turn reads as 0, so a game nobody trades in serializes and hashes as it did before. A card acting on their field can give a figure too, the most of all holding ([[R1864004]]).
- **The keyword is read as Temporary's is** ([[R637]]): the set a card in a hand is made of, printed on its running face or given by an effect, so a card given Tradable in the hand may be Traded; since issue #1864 an aura over its controller's hand lends it too, read through the layers ([[R1864002]]). It does nothing anywhere else, and stays out of the random keyword pool ([[R21]]).
- **The action** is `trade { instanceId }` ([[§10.2]]). One function, `trade::why_trade_refused`, decides it: `legal_actions` lists a `trade` for each hand card it passes, the reducer refuses the rest in its words, and the view gives the viewer's own Tradable hand card that refusal as `tradeReason` while they may act, and the viewer their own Trades as `trades { cost, left }` while their hand holds a Tradable card ([[§10.8]]). In order it asks for a live game in the player's own main phase with no prompt open, the card in their hand, Tradable, a Trade left this turn, the mana for `TRADE_COST`, and room in their deck: a deck at [[R80]]'s cap would turn the card away, so no Trade is offered into it.
- Meditative #53 Prestige and #98 Showdown are Tradable on both faces; Stinkyscape's cards will use the same keyword.
