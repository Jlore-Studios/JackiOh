title: "'Damage also Burns N' and 'also applies Toxic N' add N after each hit their card deals that its target survives"
cards: "§4.4, §6.3, R42, R63, R386; Stinkyscape+ (issue #3534)"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/burn_toxic.rs
---
A card whose text says its damage also Burns N, or also applies Toxic N (`burnsOnDamage`, `toxicOnDamage`), adds N Burn and then N Toxic to its target after each damage instance it deals, at [[§4.4]] step 7, once Poisonous and the exile have had their say: every hit counts, a Cleave neighbour's and a Trample excess's included, and several hits add several times. The target must have survived the hit: a Unit still on the field and not killed ([[R42]]), a hero with life left. No rider follows a hit that dealt nothing — a Divine Shield that took it, Armor that took it whole, a cap, Indestructible — since [[R63]]'s zero rule stops such a hit before step 5, nor one that killed or Poisoned its target. N is the card's declared number `burns` or `toxic` where it declares one ([[R386]]), so a Buff or a Nerf moves it, and the flag's own otherwise. An Equip adds its N to its host's, as a Fuse sums the numbers of its ingredients. Rejected: adding to a target the hit killed, which no tick would ever reach; and after a hit that dealt nothing, which would let a shield be burned through.
