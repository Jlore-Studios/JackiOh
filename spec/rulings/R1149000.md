C #88 Siphon Squad's aura works only once the card is revealed: by its own "Start of turn: Reveal." ([[R686]]) or by anything else that reveals it, or once it is face-up. Set and unrevealed, it is dormant: enemy Units keep their Attack, and its base face's preview shows its controller no X ([[R280]], [[§10.8]]). This replaces [[R403]]'s reading that it is live from the moment it is set, which let a card the opponent could not read take their Attack away a turn before it showed itself. The rest of [[R403]] stands: its self-Tribute is a condition checked at every state check, face-down or not, the one right after it is set included, and the Radiant face's "0 Attack" sets attack last, after every other layer ([[§10.4]]). Since no shipped card is live face-down now, [[R602]]'s redaction has nothing of Siphon Squad's to keep while it is hidden, and it stays for any card that is. Through patch v0.3.7b the aura worked from the moment the card was set; a replay of a game from then folds under that script (`KEPT_SCRIPTS`, [[R767000]]).
