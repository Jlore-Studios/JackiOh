S #112 Job Application's "Death: Resummon this at Stage 1" ([[R1865002]]) brings the card back from the graveyard into its own zone. Its text fused onto a Unit ([[R102]]) brings that Unit back the same way, and a Unit the board holds at 0 max health, such as M #47 饕餮's 2/2 under an Embiggened Core #46 Suppressive Aura's −2/−2, came back to the same board and died again at the same state check. §4.5 never settled, and the release's handicapped fuzz wave panicked on it (seed 5555).

So the Death resummons nothing when the card is a Unit whose max health was 0 or less as it died, read on its snapshot ([[R89]]) with the board's auras still on it (`layers::unit_view_as_it_died`; the field's own view leaves auras off a card that no longer tops its pile). A Unit killed by damage, a destroy or a Tribute still comes back, its damage and buffs reset ([[R78]]), and so does Job Application on its own, a Field Spell.
