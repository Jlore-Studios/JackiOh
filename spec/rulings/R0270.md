---
id: R270
title: A client cannot send the server's nonce
cards: "§9.3, §9.5, §9.8, R79, R268"
proven_in:
  - crates/server/tests/actor/match_actor.rs
---
The actor answers a nonce it has seen with the ack it gave then and applies nothing ([[§9.3]]), and it mints the nonces of its own actions — a clock's `timeout`, `disconnectExpired`, `ceilingReached` — from the log seq they will take, which a player can predict. So a client frame whose nonce starts with the server's prefix (`srv-`) is refused as malformed: otherwise a player could send the nonce of the next expiry first and swallow it, and a turn, or the other player's mulligan, would never time out short of the ceiling ([[R79]], [[R268]])
