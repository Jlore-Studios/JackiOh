Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Core #100 Ceaseless Void costs 100 and falls with every card drawn, played,
destroyed or exiled over the game; the issue asked that its cost tumbling down show as the hand card's gem
draining (issue #3338, part of #3330). `gemDrainOf(defId, cost, printed)` (`gemDrain.ts`) gives the share of the
printed cost gone, `1 - cost / printed` clamped to 0 to 1, for a card `GEM_DRAIN_CARDS` lists (the Void alone),
and null for any other card, a printed cost of 0 or less, or a cost not known. `Card.tsx` asks for it only for a
card in its owner's own hand, from the cost the view gives that card and the catalog's printed one, and the face
(`CardFace.tsx`) draws it as the gem's falling level (`data-gem-drain`, `--cost-drain`), the number on the gem
reading as before. The level's transition is off under Reduce Motion.

No other seat's view draws it: not the board's Void, not one in a hand the view shows the opponent, so the drain
tells no seat more than the cost it already reads ([[R202]]).

Rejected: draining the gem on the board and in revealed hands too, which shows nothing a played Void's cost still
means; and a ring or a bar beside the gem, which the face has no room for on a phone.
