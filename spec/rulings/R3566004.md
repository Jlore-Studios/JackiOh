S+ #6.2 is a Field Spell with "Temporary 2" (Radiant: Temporary 1), "Evolves when this is discarded to Temporary" and "Evolve: gain Flashback, Animated and +5/+5" (Radiant +10/+10). Temporary discards it at the end of its controller's second turn holding it (the Radiant face's plain Temporary, which counts 1, at the first), and the `Discarded` event says so ([[R3536001]]); the card answers it in its graveyard with an `evolve` of itself. Its Evolve gives Flashback and Animated, then a 0/0 stat line and the buff on it, so its Flashback play makes it a 5/5 (10/10) Unit. A buff never makes a stat line ([[R2280000]]), so the new verb `give_stat_line` gives one to a card that has none, and leaves a card's own alone. Any other discard does not evolve it. A Temporary may print its count: the catalog check takes an integer `n` on it, which the engine's `Keyword::Temporary { n }` ([[R2179001]]) already reads.

Rejected: the buff alone, which makes no stat line and so no Unit.
