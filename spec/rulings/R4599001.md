The fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, up to the next card's marker. A Unit's death is the state check's, which may run inside such a run with no marker of its own. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 337 a Unit attacked an immune Core #54 Straaza, a trap cast M #36 CN Peptides in the attack, and the combat death came after the Spell's marker; in seed 267 a Burn tick ([[R4154000]], allowed by [[R4508005]]) killed S+ #112 Fortress at a turn's end just after S #34 Drone Core VIII's hand hook.
**The death is the hit's.** [[§4.5]] destroys a Unit at 0 health whatever hit it last; a death whose `destroyed` names a killer had its lethal hit credited to that card ([[R42]]), and a destroy effect clears the credit as it marks, so a Spell that destroys a Unit leaves no killer. The lethal hit itself is judged where it landed: a Spell's hit is still a reach there, an attacker's is lawful ([[R4508002]]).
**What I21 allows.** A `destroyed` of an immune Unit that names a killer, or that follows a sourceless Burn or Toxic tick on it earlier in the same step, in a Spell's run or not. A death with no killer and no tick on it is still a reach.
Rejected: reading the killer's card type to judge the death, which repeats the lethal hit's judgement and fails where the killer has left the game.
