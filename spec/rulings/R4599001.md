The fuzz monitor's I21 ([[R4453000]]) blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell. The state check that follows a Spell's effect collects every Unit whose damage now meets its health, with no marker of its own, so two Units that had struck each other died in M #36 CN Peptides' run and I21 named the Spell (the daily fuzz's plain seeds 2441, 2481 and 8949 and eight handicapped ones on main, #4599).

**The death is the hit's.** A `destroyed` that names a killer had its lethal hit credited to that card ([[R42]]; a destroy effect clears the credit), and the hit is judged where it landed: a Spell's own hit is its `damage` event, which I21 reads. So a death credited to any card but the one whose run is open is no reach of the running Spell. One credited to the running Spell is its own hit, and so is a death that names no killer, unless another ruling explains it ([[R4599008]], [[R4863000]]).
