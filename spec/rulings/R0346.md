A new keyword ([[§6.1]]): a hit whose source has Pierce skips [[§4.4]] step 2, on a unit and on a hero alike, and nothing else — Divine Shield, the hero cap and Indestructible still apply. A unit's Pierce is read through the layers like its Lifesteal and Trample, so a granted one counts; a spell's is printed on its face and read off the card while it resolves (#44 True Strike), and #44's effect states it as well, as [[R85]]'s `lifesteal` states Lifesteal, so an Echo repeat that has no source left still pierces. My Pawn's lethal projection ([[R44]]) and Zephyrs' scorer read it the same way. Pierce joins [[R21]]'s random-keyword pool, at the end
