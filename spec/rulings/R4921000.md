**What was wrong.** [[R4159000]] retired the learned evaluation on 2026-11-19 and moved the AI's decisions wherever it applied, but `crates/ai/generation.json` kept saying generation 13, the generation that evaluation made. Three things read that number and none moved: [[R1678000]]'s sweep of record (it starts on a push that moves `generation`), [[R1560000]]'s widening of a ladder bot's deviation once (it compares the server's generation with the one the bot was last rated on), and the build every record names ([[R1367000]]). The AI on `main` was not generation 13's, and the repository said it was.

**What holds now.** The AI without its learned evaluation is generation 14, in lane `hand`. A change that retires or replaces part of what the generation names (its evaluation or its search), as R4159000 did, moves the generation by hand under an issue and a ruling of its own; an ordinary fix to the AI keeps the generation, so no bump follows every AI pull request. The hand-written record is: `crates/ai/generation.json` takes the new generation, the lane `hand`, the `parent` (the last `main` commit before the change, here the first parent of R4159000's merge, `7d2cf6960b`), the `tree` of `crates/ai/src`, and `shadowBan` and `parentShadowBan`; the same line is appended to `training/history/hand.jsonl`. Generation 14's `shadowBan` is 0, the length of `SHADOW_BAN` ([[R732000]]). The sweep it starts rewrites `generation.json`'s `shadowBan` alone, so the history line keeps its 0, as `learn.jsonl` keeps generation 13's 17. Generation 9's one-off ([[R732001]], lane `meditative`) is the precedent for a generation no lane promoted.

**No `vsParent`.** `vsRandom` and `vsParent` are `null`. A build of generation 13's commit would play today's cards by its own older definitions, from before every balance patch since, so a screen against it would measure stale card text as much as the evaluation. The calibration reads a line without a `vsParent` as no promotion (`promotions_of` skips it), so `bot-calibrate`'s anchor moves by nothing and `BOT_CALIBRATION_GENERATION` stays 13; a new calibration stays the follow-on `docs/bot-calibration.md` prescribes.

**Consequences.** `cargo jackioh sweep --due origin/main` on the branch reports base generation 13 and generation 14 and names 189 watched cards of the sets that may hold bans (10 Meditative, 9 Meditative+, 85 Stinkyscape, 67 Stinkyscape+ and 18 Training Grounds), which the sweep of record plays on `main` after the merge. Each ladder bot widens once on its first rated game ([[R1560000]]), and new records name 14. No deck, decision or game hash reads the number. `WIN_PROBABILITY_AI_GENERATION` is 14 with its four weights as they were ([[R4552000]]): the fit's rows were written on 2026-11-24 by `evaluate`, the hand evaluation, which the retirement left as it was, so the generation only now names the AI that played them.

**Rejected.** Bumping the generation on every change to the AI: upkeep on every change, and each bump would start a sweep of several runner-hours. Leaving it at 13: while no lane promotes, no change to the AI would start the sweep or the re-settling again. Screening against generation 13's build: it plays stale card text (above). Hearthstone has no counterpart.
