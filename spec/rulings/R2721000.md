A Legendary or Mythic card stands out by its crest and its music, not by a shine: the owner's call,
the way Hearthstone tells a legendary apart by its dragon crest. A Legendary and a Mythic face wear
the crest at the top (the Mythic's tinted) and their rarity gem, and the Mythic's intro music
([[R1350]]) plays when it is played.

- **By default** no face wears a rarity foil: a Mythic face carries `data-foil="none"`, no rainbow
  sheen, no rainbow edge and no Mythic portrait ring. Its edge and ring take its rarity colour as
  every rarity's do, which still tells it from a Legendary at hand size. A Legendary face is
  unchanged: it never wore the sheen.
- **The setting.** Rarity foil is the viewer's own switch in the card settings store beside Hover
  previews, off by default, kept on the device and synced by the account's `cards` group as they are
  ([[R633]]). It never enters a view, an action, a log or another player's screen. On, a Mythic face
  wears today's look exactly: the rainbow sheen and rainbow edge, Animated foil deciding whether the
  sheen moves, as it does for every foil.
- **Not affected.** A Radiant face keeps its foil ([[R277]]), animated or still per Animated foil,
  whether Rarity foil is on or off. The cosmetic foils a player owns and equips (`data-cosmetic-foil`,
  [[§14.2]]) are cosmetics, not rarity foil: a Mythic face wearing no rarity foil wears its card's
  cosmetic foil, and with Rarity foil on it wears none. This amends [[R1295000]] (*On the face*),
  repeated by [[R1655000]], [[R1834004]] and [[R1956001]].

Rejected: keeping the rainbow on the edge while the sheen is gone, which would keep the shine by
another name; a gold sheen for a Radiant Mythic, which would blur Radiant's own mark; and ever a
cosmetic foil on a Mythic that wears its rarity foil.
