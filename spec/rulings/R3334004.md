Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The three were `"none: Starship part"` in Stinkyscape's table ([[R2784000]], [[R2801000]]); the issue asks for signature entries, per [[R3332000]].

- **The Dormant Starship** (`T-dormant-starship`, entrance `shipyardRollout`): it rolls out of its shipyard, weld sparks flashing over its hull as it comes down, then a ring, dust at its foot and a slam shake as it settles, in Tech's rocket look.
- **S #137 Starfleet Commander** (entrance `fleetCommand`): it lands and orders its side's Dormant Starship up, a rocket bolt from its zone to the ship and there a ring, a crown of gold and a shake; with no Dormant Starship (its Cry does nothing) the ring, the gold and the shake are its own. The launch and the War Doctrine that follow are the Starship's moment ([[R3334001]]).
- **S #176 Preemptive Strike** (recipe `preemptiveStrike`, `keep`): on its own play the alarm, rings of fire on its player's hero and a red spark strike with a shake; on the first `awakened` of its play, the Dormant Starship it wakes for its launch is branded red, with a ring and a softer shake (its hero when the board cannot place the ship). A Spell's signature ends within its ceiling ([[R3332000]]).

Each is its card's signature, keyed only on a readable event ([[R202]]): an entrance on its own `summoned`, the recipe on its own `cardPlayed` and the `awakened` after it; a hidden play keys none, the ship is read from the board's public `starship` status, and no cue names a card. Every cue starts inside its entry and ends within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of it ([[R200]]). The gallery plays Starfleet Commander against a Dormant Starship holding three pieces (`fleetBoard`), and Preemptive Strike's play with the `awakened` it causes.

This amends [[R2801000]]: the Dormant Starship's entry is its signature, no longer `none`.
