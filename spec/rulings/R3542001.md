S+ #3 High Evolutionary's Cry declares one friendly permanent, a Unit or a backrow card, with its play ([[R81]]), and only one that can evolve now is offered: it acts on the field, is neither face-down nor Dormant (its text is off, so it would run nothing), has not evolved ([[R3536000]]) and prints an Evolve text. Nothing else is offered and the play's validation refuses any other pick ([[R90]]), so with no such permanent the Cry declares nothing and does nothing, and the card is still played ([[R1]]). The `evolve` verb then runs the pick's Evolve text, and the Aura (`Script.evolve_extra`, [[R3536003]]) is in force during the card's own Cry: it has entered the field before its Cry resolves ([[R1]]), so the pick's text runs twice (Radiant: thrice). Rejected: offering any permanent that has not evolved, since evolving one with no Evolve text does nothing, and a card the player could not tell from the others would waste the Cry.
