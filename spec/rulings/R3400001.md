Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The eight Legendaries and Mythics of Meditative+ and Training Grounds come off the signature ratchet ([[R3332000]]), each with its own entry, built on [[R3400000]]'s frame and drawn with figures (`fx/figures.ts`: shapes in a tint covering their anchor's box, never a card face):

- **M+ #1 Bob's Plan** (Mythic Spell, bespoke `bobsPlan`, takeover `summit`): Bob's ink-wash range with a red brushstroke arrow climbing to the summit flag fills the screen, the same whatever it picks; ink splashes at the board's centre as the hold ends; each card it adds rolls up as a scroll there and flies to the caster's hand pile as a card back, the plan's scroll smaller.
- **M+ #24 Bob, Wisconsin's Strongest Soldier** (Legendary Unit, entrance `snowplowHeave`, bespoke `bobSoldier`, a `flurry` on his tile): he drops in a burst of snow, heaves a snowplow's outline overhead and slams it down, frost cracking into the enemy's zone of his lane. His Cry plays its mode: frost over every enemy Unit the board shows at the first freeze; a mitten plucks a stolen Unit and its card back flies to his hand; the other player's Coins fly from his hero; a Discovered card flies in, a sheen on the hand pile for its owner alone; a drawn card's copies fan in.
- **M+ #26 Scratch Pad** (Mythic Spell, bespoke `scratchPad`, takeover `canvas`): blank paper over faint ink peaks, a cyan cursor and ink rows laying a card's scaffold; pixels spark as the hold ends; while its prompts are open nothing plays; the crafted card's scaffold folds into a card back and flies to the hand pile, and the paper tears as it exiles itself.
- **TG #37 Seance** (Legendary Spell, bespoke `seance`): candle flames ring the chosen permanent's tile and lean in as the board dims to plum, embers flare and a pale spirit rises; its copy drifts from that tile to the hand pile as a card back.
- **TG #38 Tutorial Master** (Legendary Unit, entrance `chalkboardLesson`, bespoke `tutorialMaster`, `chalk` dust on his tile): a chalkboard swings down behind his zone, his pointer raps it twice and a chalk outline draws itself as he lands; each card his Cry or his Death adds slides to the hand pile as a card back, the first along a chalk arrow, his outline erasing first at his Death.
- **TG #39 Call to Chaos (Training-Grounds Edition)** (Legendary Spell, bespoke `chaosDice`): two carved wooden dice tumble in from the caster's side and clatter to a stop at the board's centre in magenta and teal sparks; at its roll the top face lights while [[R436]]'s panel names the clause; the outcome's own rows draw its beat, and a Call it casts rolls smaller dice.
- **TG #39.1 Training-Grounds Golem** (a token printed Legendary, entrance `golemLashing`, `timber` dust on its tile): training posts fly in from the yard's four edges and lash together in its zone at the slam, then its keywords lock on one by one: plank armor, a shield bubble, a palisade, its Rush, a splinter.
- **TG #40 Goetzmann Game Design** (Mythic Spell, bespoke `gameDesign`, takeover `napkin`): a napkin under canteen lamplight, a pencil stub sketching a card and card silhouettes spiralling up; pixels spark as the hold ends; the card it adds lifts off as a sketch and is dealt into the hand pile as a card back, in place of the hand's row.

Each sounds its own effect once (`audio/signatureCues.ts`), a Spell as its play is read and a Unit as it lands face up, each layering the card's three sounds within 1.6 s and gain 0.8: `inkSummit`, `plowHeave`, `cursorTear`, `seanceChant`, `slateRap`, `diceGong`, `timberLash`, `pencilArpeggio`.

Every recipe keys on its own play and how many events of a kind that play has seen, never on an added card's definition; every flight to a hand is a card back; a face-down target has no id in the view, so Seance's candles then stand at the board's centre ([[R202]]). Every beat returns `row: "replace"`, its bursts and shakes within its type's budget and every cue within its entry plus `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` ([[R200]]).

What moved to fit the budgets or the events the view carries: Call to Chaos's panel shrinks from 1.8 s to `FX_CHAOS_DICE_PANEL_MS` (760 ms), its reels landing within `FX_CHAOS_DICE_REEL_MS`, since the Legendary Spell's 1.2 s ceiling holds no more after its 0.4 s cast; the die's emblem is the panel's clause; the Golem's splinter for each Pierce hit is its Pierce lock-on, since no frame keys a later hit to its card; Tutorial Master's erase plays before his Death's first card, and his cards fly from his hero when the board no longer shows him; Bob's Plan's closed scroll in its Discover is dropped, the Discover being a prompt; the auras' "now and then" accents and their sounds are dropped, an aura being motes and an edge; the two dice are one figure; in the `/dev/fx` gallery, which plays no announce, Seance's tile is the centre. Rejected: raising the Spell share or leaving Call to Chaos without a signature, both a person's call.
