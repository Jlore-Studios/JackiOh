"Costs (1) less for each Fruit you've played this game" applies only where a play takes the card from: its player's hand, or a graveyard a permission lets them play it from ([[§6.3]] Play, [[R454]]). It counts that player's Fruit-tagged plays this game (`playedByTag`, casts included, [[R70]]). Anywhere else (a deck, a graveyard with no permission, the field, a pool or a filter) it costs its printed (10), as Hearthstone's self-discounts work in hand only ([[R65]]). The cost floors at 0
