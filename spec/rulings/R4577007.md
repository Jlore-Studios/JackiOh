IA #16 Benchmark Run's "Set a friendly Unit's Attack to the highest Attack and its Health to the highest Health among enemy Units" reads the enemy Units on the field as it resolves: the highest Attack, and apart from it the highest current Health, so a damaged Unit offers what it has left. The two may come from different Units. With no enemy Unit there is no highest and no stat changes; the Radiant face's "and give it Rush" is given all the same. Immutable refuses the set ([[R23]]).
