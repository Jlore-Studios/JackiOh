#52 Silly Silas's base face lands on the opponent's side at [[§10.5]] step 4 ([[R3837000]]), and his Cry runs after it at step 5, as every Cry of a play does. It runs for the player who played him, with the direction that player declared in the play ([[R81]]), so "left" and "right" are read from the player's seat ([[§3.1]], [[R14]]), wherever Silas stands. Silas rotates with every other card, so from the opponent's lane 1 a "right" carries him across to the player's lane 1, and he changes control back as any card crossing the center line does ([[R12]]).

Rejected: rotating first and then handing him over (step 4 places a card before its Cry, and the hand-over is step 4's placement), and reading the direction from the opponent's seat (the issue keeps the Cry's choice the player's).
