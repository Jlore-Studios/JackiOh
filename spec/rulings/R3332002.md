Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Every Legendary and Mythic, tokens printed so included, has an intro of its own ([[R1350]], [[R2722000]]), and every one of them shared one play sting, [[R669]]'s `entrance`. Now the intro is the card's sting. The sound director gives a readable Legendary or Mythic with an intro (`music-cards.json`) no shared `entrance` sting, on its summon, its play or its reveal ([[R2722002]]); a Legendary or Mythic with none keeps it, the fallback. The music director, at the moment [[R204]] gives the card's intro, plays the intro whole with dynamic music on and the music above zero, as before; otherwise it plays the intro's opening as the sting (`playSting`): the clip's first `MUSIC_STING_S` (1.4 s, the shared sting's span), held at `MUSIC_STING_GAIN` and faded out over the last `MUSIC_STING_FADE_S`, on the effects bus, so it follows the effects volume and the mute, not the music's, and ducks nothing. A newer sting cuts the last, the same card's still sounding is not restarted, one whose file is not decoded `MUSIC_INTRO_LATE_S` after its moment is dropped, and the board leaving stops it; the intros of the viewer's hand are fetched ahead whatever dynamic music is, as the sting needs them. It keys on what the intro keys on, the seat's own readable event, so it never names a hidden card ([[R202]], [[R203]]). No asset is added, so the music and voice budgets hold.

Rejected: playing the shared sting beside the intro, which doubles the opening every intro already has (its riser into the downbeat); a sting file of its own per card, which costs the music budget and a render for every card; and an offset into the intro per card ("a sting cut from it"), which `music-cards.json` can carry later without changing this.
