Every finished game the reward hook sees moves the held quests ([[§14.8]], [[§14.9]]), a ladder
result's and a paid practice claim's alike, but a quest can fall due while a game is under way,
and the spec does not say whether that game counts toward it. It does: the hook tops the account
up at the moment the game ended ([[R1304000]]) before it reads the game, so a quest that fell due
by then is held, and the whole game counts toward it, as toward every other quest held when the
game ended. A game counts toward no quest that falls due after it ended.

A quest's progress stops at its target: the part of a game past what completes it is not carried
anywhere. A quest that reaches its target completes in the same transaction, leaves the held
quests and pays its Jlore and its XP once, as ledger rows; the store lets a held quest go only
once, and only the write that let it go pays.

Hearthstone semantics: a Hearthstone quest counts what happens while it is held. The spec's reward
hook takes a finished game's summary, not its events as they happen ([[§14.8]]), so a JackiOh quest
is held or not when the game ends, and the game that ends as one arrives counts in full rather than
in part.

Rejected: counting only the actions after the arrival, which the hook cannot do without reading the
game's events against the clock, and which would make the moment an arrival falls due, which the
player cannot see, decide what a game was worth.
