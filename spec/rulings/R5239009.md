FvH #114 Kethspiracy reads "simple" cards. A card is simple when its script has at most `SIMPLE_CODE_LINES` (10) code lines, counted as [[R1846000]] counts them for BUSTED and complicated cards: its lines above its tests that hold code, comments and blank lines left out, off the metrics the cards' build compiles in. `is_simple(def_id)` answers it; a definition the metrics do not list (a fused or a crafted card, which has no script of its own, or a past id) is not simple. It is the definition's, never an instance's, so nothing a card gains in play makes it simple or unmakes it.
