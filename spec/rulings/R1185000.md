Meditative+ #1 Bob's Plan ("Add a random Wincon card to your hand, and its plan …") makes one pick from the Wincon pool: the non-token cards tagged Wincon of the sets that ship ([[R380]]), in catalog order, never Bob's Plan itself ([[R387]]), today Meditative #8 Reach the Summit and Meditative #20 Aestheticize the Game. The pick is one draw of the match rng as the card resolves, so it replays ([[R60]]), and it goes through the generator every card added to a hand from a pool goes through, so [[R673]]'s Glitch odds apply to it.

The plan is not random: it is the AI generated card the script's plan table names for the card picked, the easiest help to that win the design brief chose (`docs/meditative-plus-set.md` MP5 D3, [[R1178002]]): Meditative+ #1.1 Base Camp for Reach the Summit (Ascent levels and Coins, [[R1185001]]) and Meditative+ #1.2 Spa Day for Aestheticize the Game (its health line: a heal of a hero has no cap, and Love Bombs). It is added after the card. A card the table does not name — a later set's Wincon card, or a Glitch — is added alone. The Radiant face adds both Radiant. Each is an ordinary Add to hand, so a full hand burns what does not fit, the card first and its plan after ([[§2.4]]). The card is Quickdraw as printed ([[R640]]). The two plans are AI generated tokens printing no rarity ([[R1178002]]); unlike the ten T-AI cards, which print their numbers ([[R482]]), they tune theirs as their [[§8.9]] rows say (Base Camp's levels and Coins, Spa Day's heal and Love Bombs), and they join the pool of AI generated cards ([[R480]]) only once their set ships.
