"To the left of this" is the unit zone one lane lower on this card's side and "to the right of this" the one a lane higher, the lanes numbered left to right from its controller's seat ([[§3.1]]; [[R1060]]'s right, mirrored for the left). The lane is read as the Cry resolves; if the Caller has left the field by then, nothing is summoned. A Chosen by the Sun (TG #6) is summoned to the left first, then a Chosen by the Moon (TG #7) to the right, each an aimed summon that fails alone on an occupied or reserved zone ([[R47]], [[R64]]) and enters a Locked one ([[R688]]). Lane 1 has no left and the last lane no right, so that summon is not made and nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). The summoned cards are new cards of the set owned by the controller ([[R12]]), with no Cry ([[R1]]); the Radiant face summons them Radiant. The brief's TG-P3-2.
