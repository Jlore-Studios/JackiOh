For every lesson: a player who does what the coach asks, and the simplest sensible move where it asks nothing, wins; every step shows and completes on that line; and each mechanic the lesson lists actually comes up on it. A sensible player who ignores the coach also wins, and a player who ignores it with random legal moves neither breaks it nor stalls it. Each is a test that plays the lesson through the real practice core with the gates' budget and a frozen clock, where the AI's decisions are a pure function of the game ([[R187]])
