T-AI-7 Combat Subroutine's Cry ([[R1]]) walks the Units acting for its controller, in their order, itself left out, and gives each its own `grant_random_keywords`: one draw per keyword from [[R21]]'s combat keyword pool, never a keyword the Unit has and no Taunt beside Indestructible ([[R347]]). The keywords are granted, so [[R78]]'s reset takes them as the Unit leaves the field. Each Unit draws on its own, so two Units may get different keywords (ST-S21-6). A Dormant card is no Unit acting for its player ([[R1839000]]) and is passed by; the enemy's Units get nothing. Radiant: two different keywords each.
