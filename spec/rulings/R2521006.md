Stinkyscape #141 Fruit Launcher's "Cast a random Fruit on it" ([[R1862004]]) draws from every Fruit-tagged card of the sets that ship ([[R380]]), tokens included, as the owner's first Stinkyscape balance pass (issue #2521) states it. It amends [[R382]] for this card only: R382 names C+ #23 Dropshipping's "(including tokens)" as the one pool that states its tokens (`withTokens`), and Fruit Launcher's query now states them too, `{ tags: [Fruit], withTokens: true }`, so its pool takes the Fruit tokens by its own words and not only by the Fruit tag's place among the tags whose pools hold their tokens (`POOL_TOKEN_TAGS`).

- **What is in it.** The non-token Fruits and the five Grapes, C+ #65.1 to C+ #65.5, each one entry ([[§5.1]]); the Fruit tag keeps every other token out, and the card never offers itself ([[R387]]). Organic Produce and Fruit Tree stay out ([[R1837000]]).
- **A Grape picked.** It is rolled again on `GRAPE_ODDS` and the Grape that roll names is the one cast, as [[R382]] rolls every Grape a pool generates, so the Grape rarity pool holds here too. The cast is the random cast R1862004 rules: free, counted as played, aimed at the declared permanent where the Grape's target may be it, every other choice random, Radiant on the Radiant face.
- **No game plays differently.** The pool is the one R382's Fruit pool already held, in the same order, so every draw lands where it did; the change is that the card says so.
