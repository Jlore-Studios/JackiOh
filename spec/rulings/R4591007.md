IA #158 Access Denied's "when your opponent plays a Spell that targets" is an opponent's Spell — never a Field Spell — announced with at least one target (`cardAnnounced`, [[R448]]), on either side: a Spell aimed at its own side springs it as one aimed at yours does. An untargeted Spell, however far it reaches, never does.
