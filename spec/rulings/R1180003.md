"Healing your hero and Units is twice as effective" (Meditative+ #7 Life Mage's Radiant face, `doublesHealing`; [[R1160]]'s "twice as effective") multiplies every heal of its controller's hero and of the Units they control by `HEAL_MULTIPLIER` (2) while its card acts on their field, a Unit's heal still capped at its damage ([[§6.3]]). It reaches every heal of an amount, a Spell's, a Unit's text and Lifesteal's ([[R85]]) alike, and applies after the "would be healed" replacements ([[R413]], [[R462]]): a heal one of them turned into damage deals its stated amount, never doubled. "Heal up to N" sets a floor, not an amount, so it stops at N. Several multiply, as two Prophet Velens do in Hearthstone: two Radiant Life Mages make a heal four times as large. The opponent's heals are untouched.
