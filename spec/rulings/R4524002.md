Stinkyscape #20 Tnack Trav copies a Unit's text ([[R1850000]]), and copying a board sweep's, such as S+ #81 Doomsayer's "start of your turn, exile your board" ([[R3539002]]), it sweeps as that card does: a board sweep is no effect reaching for a card, so it exiles a Dormant card with the board. The fuzz monitor's I19 knew a copied Spell's sweep (C #57 Echo, `copies_a_sweep`) but not a copied Unit's, and it read the copier after the step, when its own sweep had exiled it and its copy with it ([[R78]]); the handicapped wave's seed 2334 failed on it. I19 now notes, as each step begins, every field card that runs a copied board sweep, a Spell's or a Unit's, and a hook that card resolves is a sweep. Nothing in the game changes. Rejected: listing Tnack Trav among the sweeps, which would let its copy of any text reach a Dormant card.
