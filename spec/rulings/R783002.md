## R783002 — Flying: attacked only by Flying or Ranged

Built (#783). A Unit with Flying in Attack Position may be attacked only by a unit with Flying or
Ranged: it is one of [[§4.2]] step 2's target restrictions (`restrictions::attack_restriction`, beside
"Only Units in this lane can attack this"), so `legal_actions` and the reducer's refusal read the same
function and cannot disagree, and a forced attack, which obeys step 2's restrictions, does not happen
on a Flying target its attacker may not attack (`force_attack`, and a random forced attack draws only
from the targets its attacker may attack). A Taunt binds only the attackers that may attack it (step 3),
so a Flying Unit with Taunt in Attack Position binds only Flying and Ranged attackers. It applies to
attacks only and is no immunity: Spells, Cry targets, Traps, "all" effects and Cleave's splash, which is
no attack on it ([[R1026]]), all reach it.
