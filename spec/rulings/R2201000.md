The tutorial must not be losable, and that is a rule of the game a lesson runs, not a habit of the
AI. So a handicap ([[R180]]) takes one more optional field, `heroFloor`: the least health that seat's
hero can fall to. `TUTORIAL_HUMAN` sets it to `TUTORIAL_HERO_FLOOR` (1) ([[R1705005]]); no tier and
not `AI_TUTORIAL` sets it. It must be at least 1 and at most the hero's starting health
(`validate_handicap`). Like `heroHealth` ([[R290]]) it is serialized only when set and stored only
then, so no other game's state, hash, replay or golden trace moves.

The floor holds against every way to lose:

- **Damage**, fatigue's included ([[R3]]): the floor is one more of [[§4.4]] step 3's hero caps, what
  the hero has above its floor, so every hit deals at most that and reports what it dealt. No hit on
  the seat is lethal, so step 4a's lethal-hit replacements ([[R1204]]) never answer for it, and a hit
  they redirect at it passes through the same cap.
- **Life loss** ([[R18]]) takes at most what the hero has above its floor; **set health** sets no
  lower than the floor; after **[[R73]]'s swap** each hero reads at least its floor. Every write to a
  hero's health (`duos_life`) puts a floored hero back at its floor, and the hero check never counts a
  floored seat as dead.
- **The other player's wins** ([[R850]]): at the game-end point a win by effect or a held win of the
  floored seat's opponent is not read, and its flight ([[R1843002]]) is read as one that leaves the
  floored seat a draw ([[R1843004]]): the opponent may still leave, and the human does not lose. A
  floored seat's vote tally never removes it ([[R1843000]]).

It never stops the floored seat from winning: its own lethal, its own win by effect and its held wins
stand, and against the opponent's claims, which are not read, it wins alone. Concede, a disconnect,
the turn cap and a Glitch void are the player's or the clock's, not a card's, and are unchanged. So the
tutorial can't be lost except by conceding, which ends a lesson as a loss ([[R2434002]]).

The AI plays every tier alike ([[R290]]), so it sees the floor through the engine's own judges: no
line is lethal on a seat that cannot lose (`can_lose`), so the lethal solver looks for none and
`ready_gap` reads infinite there, and a face hit on a hero at its floor changes nothing the evaluation
scores, so the AI trades instead. A lesson's human who does nothing at all still wins: the tutorial
opponent's 12 cards run out, and fatigue takes its 20 health before the turn cap. The tutorial tier's
frozen games ([[R290]]) seat their greedy stand-in at `TUTORIAL_HUMAN` without the floor, so they keep
measuring the tier by play.

Hearthstone semantics: a tutorial encounter cannot be lost, the player's hero stopping short of death,
while the player still wins it by the ordinary rules.

Rejected: an AI that holds back from lethal, which a random effect or the player's own cards would
still let through; and a hero made Immune, which would hide what the lessons show, a hero's health
falling as it is attacked, down to the floor.
