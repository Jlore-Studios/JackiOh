**Why.** Medium and Hard practice hand the AI more resources ([[R180]]): more mana, more cards, a bigger deck. A game against them measures the handicap as much as the player's cards. The owner asked in #2777 that no win rate count a game against Medium or Hard, only Easy and ladder games.

**The player's statistics.** The player's own tally ([[R639]]) is kept on the device, synced to the account and read by the public Players tab. It counted practice games at every tier. It now counts a practice game only against Easy, `PRACTICE_TALLY_DIFFICULTY`, where the AI plays on a person's resources. A game against Medium or Hard adds nothing to it: no game, no result, no card figure. Lessons, sandbox games and games with custom cards stay out as before. Online matches count as before.

**The card figures.** Public card win rates already count only live games and the AI's development runs:
- **Live games** are the ladder and room matches the server ran. A ladder bot plays them with no handicap ([[R1452]]).
- **Development runs** play both seats on a person's resources ([[R378]]), forced runs included; both sites' databases import them ([[R1933001]]).
- **Practice games** record nothing ([[R187]]).

So no figure on `/stats` reads a Medium or Hard game. This ruling holds the development run to that with a test, and changes nothing else there.

Rejected:
- Filing Easy practice games as card records. [[R187]] keeps practice off the server's figures, and the owner asked to remove games, not to add a source.
- Dropping the AI's development games from staging's card figures ([[R1933000]]). They are Easy-resource games, and below [[R654]]'s gate they are all the figures a fresh patch has there.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
