No damage or aura reaches a Dormant card, so no health judges it; only a destroy mark does.

A Dormant card fills its zone, and a hull with 0/0 printed stats would read at 0 health. But no
damage instance, aura or counter ever reaches a Dormant card ([[R1839000]]), so the state check's
death pass (§4.5 step 1) must not collect one for its health either: `is_dying` of a Dormant card
is only its destroy mark. A 0/0 Dormant card — S+ #157 Phantom Shipyard, every Terminus Starship
before its pieces fuse — stays on the field. Rejected: giving every Dormant hull health above 0,
which would print stats the card does not have and move every later Buff.
