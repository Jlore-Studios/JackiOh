At its controller's end of turn, Joon Jorker Buffs the acting Unit in the next lane on its own side, the lanes numbered from its controller's seat ([[§3.1]]): five separate Buffs (Upgrade, [[R386]], one draw each), an `upgraded` each. With none there, or an Immutable one ([[R23]]), nothing happens and nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). The brief's MD-D16.
