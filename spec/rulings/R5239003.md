FvH #104 Dark Skies deals its damage to random Units. `damageSplit` with `among: units` deals its amount as hits of `perHit`, each its own [[§4.4]] instance on a Unit drawn by the match rng among those still standing on either side, the caster's own Units included, in [[R68]]'s walk order, and never on a hero. The draw is made again for each hit, since the state check does not run between one effect's hits ([[R59]]), and the walk is a harmful one, so a card immune to it is passed by ([[R940]]). When no Unit stands, the rest of the damage is lost. A Spell's hits take Spell Damage each, as every split's do.
