Stinkyscape #145 Fortify Flesh's "Your hero gains a random {least} to {most} Armor" is the hero's Armor, [[§4.4]] step 2's reduction of every hit on it kept for the rest of the game and stacking with any Armor already there (C+ #46's, [[R124]]), not Hearthstone's pool. One luck-based roll from the match rng, uniform from the declared least to the declared most, both ends included; a Buff that lifts the low end past the high one rolls between the two. The Radiant face's Lucky 1 rolls once more and keeps the higher ([[§6.1]] Lucky X, [[R1438]]), and the controller's Luck rolls beside the card's own ([[R987]]).
