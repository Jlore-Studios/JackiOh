One application first draws the row uniformly among cost, stats, keyword, X and number rows that can change the card, then the item within the row, then the stats split k; a choice of one draws nothing ([[R129]]). A Degrade removes only a keyword the card has of its own (printed, Upgrade-added or granted; every entry of that kind), never one an aura or Defense Position lends. Upgrade-added keywords are card text: a Vanilla hides them, and nothing is added to a Vanilla unit. An X-cost card with no chosen X always takes its X step. Immutable refuses KY's Constant, and its "random" picks among numbers not already at the value.
