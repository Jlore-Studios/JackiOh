The designer's numbers #1–#40 stand, with no hole and no repeat; #39.1 Training-Grounds Golem is the one token, `traininggrounds-039-1`, `rarity: "Token"` with the designer's Legendary as `printedRarity`; #41–#60 are the twenty Core snapshots, in the designer's order (#41 Bigot to #60 Rush Token Farm). Rarity is the designer's by number, #1–#15 Common, #16–#27 Rare, #28–#36 Epic, #37–#39 Legendary and #40 Mythic, and each snapshot keeps its Core card's (none Legendary or Mythic): 26 Common, 19 Rare, 11 Epic, 3 Legendary and 1 Mythic over the set's sixty cards. #28 Occidental and #29 Oriental, whose header lines name no type, are Units, since each prints stats. The designer's casing and hyphens in names stand ("Group leader", "In a hurry", "Dino-size", "Training-Grounds Golem", "Call to Chaos (Training-Grounds Edition)"), as Core's "Right-house defender" does; no name of #1–#40 or #39.1 is another card's ([[R381]], [[R739]]), and a snapshot shares its Core card's name by the owner's decision, the one exception part 2 (#2195) adds. Every Unit face meets [[R275]]'s stat half as printed.

Amended by issue #2511 ([[R2511000]]): the snapshots #41–#60 are the Legacy cards, each named `Legacy: <its Core card's name>`, so no name of the set is another card's and the exception ends.
