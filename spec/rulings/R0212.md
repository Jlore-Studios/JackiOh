[[§10.3]]'s loop hands an event to the triggers some time after it happened — after the state check that follows a combat, an Echo repeat or a whole Cry ([[§4.5]]), and inside that check after the Death hooks and Reborn — so the board it is offered to can have moved on. The cards that answer it are the ones that stood where they are now when it happened: a card that has moved zones since — returned through Reborn, drawn or put in a hand, played or summoned — is on a stay that did not see the event and does not answer it ([[R174]]), so #91's Plague Counter for the hit that killed it never lands on its Reborn body, and a #89 Corpse Eater drawn after a death does not feed on it. A #32 Prem Panther that traded and came back through Reborn still draws for the kill of its last stay, but that is no answer to the event: its `afterAttack` hook is owed on the snapshot it fought with and runs even when the attacker died ([[R426]]). A card whose controller has changed since answers for the player who controlled it when the event happened, and a change of control is not a move ([[R171]]): a #32 that attacks and kills #86 "Miss" Mrow draws for its controller ([[R426]]), even though Mrow's Death steals it before the draw resolves. The events that happened after the one being dispatched are what say so — those still owed behind it, and those another loop has already dispatched, #96's AI turn among them: a #89 drawn during the AI turn My Pawn hands over does not feed on a death a trap dealt earlier in the same window. A card that resumes as its Stack pile's top when the card above it leaves ([[§3.2]]) lay dormant until then and registered nothing ([[R153]]): it does not answer the event whose removal of the top uncovered it — the top's death, bounce, exile or steal — nor any event before it, as a Reborn body does not answer the hit that killed its unit; no event reports a resume, so the engine keeps it against the card that left, for that removal alone: a later move of the card that left (exiled out of the graveyard it died into, discarded, shuffled back) uncovers nothing, and the card that resumed long before answers what happened before it. Traps are its first responders and answer the same way ([[§10.3]]): a trap that reached the field after the event — summoned by the rest of the list whose draw cast the card — is not offered it, and one whose controller has changed since answers for the player who controlled it then, whose tokens it makes ([[R52]]), in an immediate dispatch and in the end-of-turn window alike. Hearthstone makes a Reborn minion a new entity, and resolves a minion's kill trigger for its controller before a Deathrattle
