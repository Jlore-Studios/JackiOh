## R783012 — Flashback: a play from the graveyard, once

Built (#783). A card with Flashback, read through the layers, in its owner's graveyard may be played
from there at its current cost (X chosen at the play), with no permission on the field: Flashback is the
one play-from-the-graveyard permission ([[R454]]) a card grants itself, admitting any type at any price,
paid in mana (`graveyard_play::admitting`). It is a play for every rule — cost and [[R65]]'s prices,
Cry ([[R1]]), Combo, the counts — and needs what a play from hand needs: an open unit zone for a Unit, a
target for a Spell that requires one. `legal_actions` lists it only then, from the same check the
reducer refuses with.
