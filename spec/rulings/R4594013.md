The first start of its controller's turn deals `damage`, +`growth` at each later start ([[R62]]). The count rides on the card's own memory as `{turn, seen}`, bumped once a turn — an extra firing in the same turn (Meditative #9 Joint Filing) deals the same amount again — and a card that left and came back counts from its first start.
