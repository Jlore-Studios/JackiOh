The issue describes each version's look; this note fixes how each is drawn. Like every look, each is
a pure function of the card's id, name, tags and type, base and Radiant faces both, and wears only a
full face, as Full art does ([[R2725000]]).

**Alt art** (`version-alt-art`) is a new picture in the normal frame, the text kept: the card's own
painter, procedural ([[R503]]), Stinkyscape ([[R2170000]]) or Training Grounds ([[R2202000]]), seeded
apart from its normal picture by `artSeedOf(id, "alt")` (`data-art-picture="alt"`), as the Signature
look's is by its own name ([[R2949000]]). What the card's name, tags and type pick stays (a procedural
card's theme, composition and motif; a set's subject and family), and the scene, pose and palette
grow from the new seed, so its picture is neither the normal one nor the Signature look's. A Legacy
card's picture is its Core card's ([[R2724000]]) and has no other, so no Alt art offer takes one
([[R2940000]]); a Starship in play draws its pieces ([[R2249000]]) under any version.

**The Sketch** (`version-sketch`) is the art as an artist's pencil-and-ink sketch: the face's normal
picture, whichever painter draws it (a Legacy card's and a Starship's too), under one SVG filter
(`sketchSvg`, `data-art-picture="sketch"`). Its greys are dodged into line work, its dark hatched by
long diagonal strokes of slate ink (`--pal-slate-700`) seeded by the card's id, the whole multiplied
onto slate paper (`--pal-slate-100`) and grained. On a base face its frame is a pencilled outline:
the edge hatched in graphite on paper and the art window ringed by a pencil line with a smudge, the
bezel graphite (cosmetics.css). The normal frame, the type's panel, the name banner and the rules box
are kept.

**Alt full art** (`version-alt-full-art`) is Alt art's picture across the whole face as a full art,
with Full art's layout and no words ([[R1017001]]): the same picture as Alt art, never Full art's.

**A delivered file** (an artist's `public/art/` file) is the normal picture's: the normal face, Full
art and the Signature look show it, and Alt art and the Sketch draw the painter's picture until an
artist delivers theirs.

**Radiant.** [[R2725004]] holds under each: no Sketch colour, paper, ink or frame, falls in the
`RADIANT_GOLD_*` window, and neither Alt art's picture nor a base Sketch tints a base face gold. A
Radiant face keeps its gold-foil frame (a gold rim on Alt full art), [[R277]]'s marks wherever its
words show, its gold stats and its picture's Radiant paint: a Radiant Sketch washes its painted
Radiant picture back over the pencil (`SKETCH_RADIANT_WASH`), and its frame stays the Radiant face's,
never pencilled.
