The card statistics are farmed, not wished for. The gather training lane plays the current AI
against itself — best-AI versus best-AI random-deck games, one series per UTC day filed under the
named patch — and loads every finished day into Postgres (`stats-import`), where the figures read
them as development games ([[R378]]). A gather session changes nothing under version control: no
commits, no branches, no promotions, no landing; a checkout it changed is cleaned, not landed.
Live games keep filing their records as they always did, so the data exists whether or not it is
ever trained on, and no feedback loop exists: development and live sources stay separated.

The public figures split three ways internally and display merged. `GET /api/stats/cards` (and
the drill-down) count every counted seat, human and bot alike, unless `?pilot=` names a side:
`?pilot=human` reads the people's sides only, `?pilot=ai` the bots'. The response names its split
(`pilot`, `pilot_label`), and the compiled-in table (`cargo jackioh winrates`) counts record
files the same way. This amends [[R1451]]: the bot's side is no longer left out of the display,
and a bot game adds two decks like any game, so the per-game deck tell [[R1452]] recorded is
gone.
