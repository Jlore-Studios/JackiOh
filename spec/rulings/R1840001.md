The tray holds at most `RESOURCE_TRAY_CAP` (8) Resources. A Resource gained into a full tray is sold at once: it never enters the tray, it ceases to exist, and its player gains the Credits it fetched ([[R1840005]]), with a public `resourceSold` naming the card and the price, as a card a full hand burns is named to both players ([[R317]]), then `marketCreditsChanged`. Until the Marketplace (#1853) sets a Resource's current price, a sale fetches a whole number drawn uniformly from its face's printed sell range, the Radiant face's for a Radiant Resource, with one draw of the match rng. The spec did not say what "its current sell price" is before the Marketplace exists; this note decides it.
