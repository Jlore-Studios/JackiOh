[[§8]] #50's Radiant face makes the stolen permanent Radiant, and it does so only to a card the delayed steal took: a target that has left the field ([[R76]], [[R174]]), lies dormant under a Stack ([[R13]]), was already its controller's, or stays with the opponent because the row is full ([[R15]]) is not made Radiant, since the rider names "the stolen permanent" and there is none, and a Make Radiant on a card that has left the field could land in a hand the controller may not read. The rider is the face the Cry ran: the delayed steal carries it ([[§10.6]], [[R126]]), so a base K-Pop Fanatic made Radiant after its Cry steals without the rider, and a Radiant one that has died since still applies it
