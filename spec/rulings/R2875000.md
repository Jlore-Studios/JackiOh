Each news entry ([[§14.13]]) carries one reward, and an account claims it once.

**The claim.** `POST /api/news/:newsId/claim` is a spend of the economy ([[§14.11]]). It claims its account's turn ([[R2378000]]) and takes an optional `Idempotency-Key` ([[R2124000]]) whose kept hash covers the entry named in the path beside the body, so the same key sent to claim another entry is another request, 422. Under the account's economy lock ([[R1301003]]), before any check, a key the account committed answers what it answered then. In one transaction the claim then pays the entry's reward as ledger rows with the reason `news`, their reference the entry's id, and writes the account's claim of the entry to `news_claims` (migration 0077), whose key, the account and the entry, holds one row: a second claim, however it races the first, is refused with 409 and pays nothing. While `ECONOMY_LIVE` is off every claim is refused with 409 and writes nothing. A claimed entry stays listed, marked claimed, until it expires ([[R2875001]]).

**The table.** An account reads its own claims and no one else's; no client writes one, and a row goes only with its account. The entries themselves are data, not rows: a claim names an entry only by its id.

**The client.** A press of Claim mints a key and sends it again on that press's retries, as every spend does ([[R2124000]]), and the panel's Claim buttons wait while one of the tab's spends is out ([[R2378000]]).

Hearthstone semantics: a news or event reward is collected once per account, and collecting it again does nothing.

Rejected: a claim per session or device, which would pay one account many times; claiming by reading the panel, which would pay for an unroll the player never meant as a claim; and a claim that is no spend, which would run twice on a retry.
