Stinkyscape #4.5 1v1 Final Destination reads "Lock all but one random lane. Set each hero's health to 1. Both players discard all non-permanents", its Radiant face adding a hand filled with (0) Book of Buffs. The designer asked the build to infer its reveal.

- **The reveal.** It fires in the end-of-turn trap window of its controller's opponent's turn ([[R62]]), never its controller's own, and goes to the graveyard as it fires. Its controller's turn comes next, so they strike first at 1 health.
- **The lanes.** One lane is drawn uniformly from the five off the match rng and stays open; the other four are Locked whole, both sides' unit and backrow zones. A Lock evicts nothing ([[R688]]): Units in a Locked lane stay and fight.
- **Health.** Each hero's health is set to 1, no pipeline ([[R18]]).
- **The discards.** "Non-permanents" are Spells ([[§5.1]]): each player discards every Spell in their hand, its controller first, each hand in its order, no choice, and keeps its Units, Field Spells and Traps; a guarded hand keeps them ([[R800]]), and a Resource is in the tray, not the hand ([[R1840000]]).
- **Radiant.** Then its controller's hand is filled with C+ #71 Book of Buff at `costOverride` 0: their hand size less the cards left after the discards ([[§2.4]]).
