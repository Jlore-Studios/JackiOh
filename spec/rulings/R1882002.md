S #150's fourth entry reads "Add an Acclaimed Stinkyscape Unit to your hand. Refresh mana so you can play it" (ST-S25-3).

- **The card.** One pick from the match rng ([[R60]]) of the non-token Units tagged Acclaimed of the Stinkyscape set. The pool names the set, so it reaches its cards while the set is held ([[R1420]]).
- **The refresh.** Once the card is in its caster's hand, a Refresh ([[§6.3]]) of as much mana as its printed cost, never past max mana ([[R364]]). A card a full hand burns ([[§2.4]]) cannot be played, so nothing is refreshed.
