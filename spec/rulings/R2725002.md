The issue asked that #2715's combined cosmetics stay owned as they are and map onto the new model so
that nobody loses anything: owning Full art + animated on a card counts as owning Full art and the
Animated augment there. Each retired full art implies, on its own card alone:

| Retired full art | Implies |
|---|---|
| `full-art-<slug>` (Epic) | `version-full-art` |
| `full-art-<slug>-animated` (Legendary) | `version-full-art`, `augment-animated` |
| `full-art-<slug>-signed` (Mythic) | `version-full-art`, `augment-animated`, `augment-signature` |
| `animated-<slug>` (Epic) | `augment-animated` |

The implication is read, never written: no migration or grant writes a row for it, so no account
gains a cosmetic ([[§14.12]]). The server reads it in one place, `cosmetics::row_owns`, under every
ownership check (`owns`, a pick's [[R1834001]], a loadout's [[R1834002]], a duplicate's and a
pack's "already owned", a token's and the Chase Shard's); the client in one, `ownedCardCosmetics`.
So a duplicate never drops, and a token or the bar never sells, a version or augment an account's
retired full art already owns there. A retired pick keeps its look: `lookOf` reads the still Epic and
`true` as Full art, the Legendary as Full art and Animated, the Mythic as all three, and the Animated
art as the normal face and Animated, each the tier `data-full-art-tier` named before #2725.

Amended by [[R2949000]] (#2949): the Mythic, which still owns all three, now prints the Signature look, a full art and a motion of its own with the signature, in place of Full art with Animated and the signature.
