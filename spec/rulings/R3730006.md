A `/play?room=` link ([[R767]]), a `/together?coop=` link, a chat Join button ([[R2094000]]) and a friend's "Open in lobby" all open `/lobby/:code` now ([[R3730000]]). With no lobby under the code, the lobby screen offers the classic room still open under it, and it joins at once as before: a 1v1 room, a friend's challenge's among them ([[R1831000]]), with a pick for its mode, or an unjoined co-op room. Every `/api/rooms` and `/api/duos/rooms` route stays for them and for older clients.

A link opened before the account may play waits in this tab as before ([[R767]], [[R2094000]]): a signed-out visitor's through the sign-in, and a pending account's through the invite page. A `/lobby/` path waits the same way, signed in or not. The kept link opens its lobby once, on the first screen the account reaches active, and only then is it forgotten.

Rejected: turning a classic room into a lobby when it is opened, which would change a game its host made and left waiting.

The co-op deck the device remembers ([[R2094000]]) carries over: a co-op lobby's "Your deck", before the member sends a pick, and a classic co-op room's join card start on it, and a pick sent from either is remembered.

Amends [[R767]], [[R2094000]], [[R1075000]], [[R2031000]] and [[R3205000]], each of which says what it lost: `/play`'s room ticket, its Cancel room and its join form; the co-op panel's ticket, join form and chat invite; and the co-op host's room wait, whose place among the reads a hidden tab keeps sending the lobby's own read takes.
