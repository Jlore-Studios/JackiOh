The owner asked in #3015 (2026-11-04) that a card's pick name one owned level, and that old ids still
resolve so frozen loadouts and replays still draw.

**The pick.** A card wears one level of one variant ([[R3015000]]), named in its pick's `fullArt`
([[R2655000]]): `false` for the normal face, `true` for Full art's first level on a full-art card (as
`true` named its still Epic), or a level's `version-…` id. `PUT /api/card-picks` holds it to the offer and
to ownership on the card ([[R1834001]], [[R2725001]]), a retired row counting as the levels it maps to
([[R3015006]]), and answers 400 with nothing written otherwise: `"fullArt" is not offered on this card`,
`"fullArt" is a cosmetic this account does not own`, or, for `true` on a card with no full art, `"fullArt"
is not a cosmetic id of its kind`. A retired full art's id (`full-art-…`, `animated-…`) is a 400, `that
cosmetic is retired: "fullArt" names a level`, and so is a non-empty `augments`, `"augments" are retired: a
card wears one level, named in "fullArt"`; `null` or `[]` clears what a pick held from before. Every write
of `fullArt` clears the augments held, so no pick names a level and an augment both. Migration 0079's
column stays, read for the picks written before.

**Older shapes.** A pick stored, uploaded or frozen before #3015 is read as the level it drew
(`cosmetics::level_of_pick`, the client's `levelOfPick`):

- none or `false`: no level, the normal face;
- `true`: Full art's first level on a full-art card;
- a level's id: that level;
- a retired full art of the card: the highest level it maps to ([[R3015006]]), a level of another variant
  before the Default's: the still Epic Full art's first level, the Legendary its second, the Mythic its
  third, an Animated art the Default's Animated;
- then a listed Animated augment steps up one rank where the next level adds animated (from no level, to
  the Default's Animated), and after it a listed Signature one rank where the next adds signed.

So Full art with Animated is Full art's second level, and R2949000's Signature look (Full art, Animated
and Signature) its third; Animated on the normal face is the Default's Animated; Pixel, or any one-art
version, with Animated is its animated level. **Alt art or the Sketch with Animated** stays at its first
level: the level above each is a full art, so the motion of a normal-frame Alt art or Sketch no longer
exists, a call the issue left open. Rejected: the Default's Animated for them, which takes away their
picture; and their animated full-art level, two levels up, a full art the pick never wore and the account
need not own. Signature anywhere else was never worn ([[R2949000]]) and steps nothing.

**What is worn** (`cosmetics::worn_level`, the client's `wornLevel`): the level a pick names where the card
is offered it and the account owns it there, else the highest level below it that is, else the normal
face, so a pick of an older shape whose augment was not owned wears what it wore. A card with no pick of
its own wears Full art's first level on a full-art card while the Full-art cards setting is on, where it is
owned. A tutorial lesson's board wears none ([[R1108000]]).

**The loadout** (`freeze_loadout`) freezes each card's worn level as its id, `fullArt = "<level id>"`, and
no augments ([[R1834002]]); the other seat, a watcher and a replay's viewer wear it behind their own
Full-art cards setting as before ([[R1834003]], [[R1834004]]), and read a loadout frozen before #3015
(`true`, a retired id, augments) through `levelOfPick`, so an old replay still draws. The device's
one-time upload keeps the level a pick names, mapped, or the highest below it that the card is offered and
owns there, and no augment ([[R1834000]]). Practice, hotseat and every end-to-end test but the one that
walks the economy treat every level as owned on the cards it is offered ([[R1310004]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Amends [[R2655000]] (`true` names Full art's first level, a pick's id names a level, and its tiers' ids are
read as the levels they map to), [[R2725000]] (a pick names one level and no augments, and a match freezes
one level) and [[R2949000]] (its Signature look is Full art's signed level, and Signature names nothing in
a pick).
