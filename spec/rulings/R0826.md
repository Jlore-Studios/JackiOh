Double Header's own play is still the turn's first card, but it does not answer its own play
([[R119]]), so the turn it lands copies nothing (MD-A24). "First" is the head of the turn's plays
([[R213]]), played that once: a card played again later in the turn is the turn's first card no
more. A cast is a play ([[R70]]) and can be the first; a countered card was never played, so the
next card is the first. The copy is a fresh card of the definition the `cardResolved` event names
([[R71]]) — a fused or crafted card's transient definition included ([[R77]]) — so a card that has
ceased to exist since (Core #41 Sheepish's victim) still copies. The copy is a second card and
copies nothing; a full hand burns it ([[R4]]).
