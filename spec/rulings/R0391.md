title: A Tribute can pay for its own zone

With its unit row full a Tribute card may still be played, into a zone its own Tribute empties: a unit zone whose pile is exactly one tributed unit (tributing the top of a pile frees nothing, since the card beneath resumes, [[R13]]), whose tributed unit has no Reborn (its zone would be reserved for the return, [[R64]]), and which is not Locked. `legalActions` pairs each zone with the paying sets that leave it open, and the play's check reads the pair ([[R90]], [[R101]]); [[§10.5]] already pays at step 2 and places at step 4, holding the named zone between ([[R210]]), so only the check changes, and a play reads its own side once the Tribute is paid as [[R360]] reads the opponent's. It holds for a backrow card with a Tribute cost the same way, and never for an activation's Tribute cost, which needs no zone ([[R384]])
