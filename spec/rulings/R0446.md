A Unit on a carrier stands in its backrow zone, is a Unit for every rule, can neither attack nor be attacked (forced included) and binds no attacker with Taunt, may switch position; the zone takes nothing more; when the zone's acting card stops carrying (left the field, or Vanilla'd) the Unit steps down at the next state check to its lane's unit zone, else the leftmost open one, without leaving the field, not summoning sick (animated with carried); with none it is destroyed once, and an Indestructible one waits for a zone; a rotation or swap carries it with its carrier. Since patch v0.2.3 the one carrier, C+ #33 Ivory Tower, holds its Unit only until that Unit's play resolves, then fuses it in ([[R653]])
