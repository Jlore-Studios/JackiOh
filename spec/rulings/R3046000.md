The owner's balance pass (#3000) asked that a card which prints full art in a hand look full-art on the board too. [[R1017001]] gave a full-art card its art across the whole face and kept "a compact face and a board minion" to their windows: an oval portrait with the name plate over its rim for a Unit, a framed window for a face-up backrow card. Both board faces now print full art.

**Which cards.** The full face's decision, asked of the board face: a card prints it when its full face would, by the same setting, ownership, tiers and versions. That is a card with a `fullArt` entry in the cosmetics registry ([[R2655001]]), a listed token ([[R2655002]], through its parent) and a card wearing the Full art or Alt full art version ([[R2725000]], [[R2940001]]), while the Full-art cards setting is on, a tutorial lesson's board prints none ([[R1108000]]), and only the viewer's own copy whose full art is owned prints it once ownership decides ([[R1313000]]), the opponent's by its owner's frozen loadout ([[R1834004]]). A Unit's `MinionFace` and a compact `CardFace` call `boardLookOf` with the look the full face would wear (`useCardLook`). A card that prints no full art looks as before, its oval or its window.

**What it draws.** The art, the picture the version draws ([[R2940001]]: Alt full art's is Alt art's, else the card's normal one, delivered file first, [[R660]]), laid across the face inside the face's rarity rim, with a scrim darkening its top and a Unit's foot behind the gems. It is still. The tiers' motion, drift, sweep and signature, and the Animated and Signature augments ([[R2655003]], [[R2715001]], [[R2949000]]), stay on the full faces, as [[R2715000]] kept the Animated art off the board; a card wearing Full art with Animated has the same board face as one wearing Full art, and a card wearing Animated alone keeps its window. The face carries `data-full-art` and `data-version`, and no `data-full-art-tier` or `data-augments`.

**The name sits in a band below the art.** The art ends where the band begins; nothing of the art is under the name.
- *A Unit:* the name plate, as tall as before (`--mf-plate`), runs the whole width of the panel at its foot. The art fills the panel above it, to its top edge. The attack and health gems ride the art's lower corners, just over the band, so the band keeps the whole width for the name. The cost gem, the crest, the element chip, the keyword chip column (it stops above the gems, as before), the Vanilla stamp, the zzz mark, the counters, the state rail, the Dormant seal and the Starship hull stay where they were, over the art.
- *A face-up backrow card:* the band is the foot of the face, 24% of its height, and holds the name on two lines at most, as a small face does; the art fills the face above it. The cost gem keeps its corner, the rarity gem and the set mark take the top right as a small face's do, the crest stays, and the state rail runs along the art's lower edge. A full-art face prints no type line ([[R1017001]]), so a backrow card's type is its panel colour and its hover preview; it has no stats.

**What the art's size changes.** The keyword treatments ([[R438]]) are all still drawn. A veil fills the art, square. Divine Shield's bubble is a golden glow along the art's edge. Taunt's steel shield and the Stack's card edges stood behind the oval portrait, which the art now covers, so they are not drawn: a Taunt wears a steel ring over the art's edge, a Stack its chip and its buried count. The art takes a finish's band over its edge as a full face's does ([[R2720000]]), and a border shows round the art as the face's edge, so a full-art Unit draws no border ring over its panel.

**The other compact faces.** The compact face is also drawn in the Collection's cosmetics strip, the overflow notices and the practice sandbox's results: they print full art for a full-art card by the same decision. The hover preview, the sheet and the detail view are full faces and unchanged ([[R2723000]]).

This amends [[R1017001]]'s "a compact face and a board minion keep their windows" and the comment on `showsFullArt` that said so.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads it, and nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Hearthstone semantics: Signature and golden minions on the board keep their art and their frame; the art is the card's, shown larger.

Rejected: full art under the name, the banner over the art's foot as the full face does, which says "over", not "below", the owner's ask, and hides the foot of the art on a face 50 px wide; a prop that turns it on in `Card.tsx` alone, a second path that would let the compact face drawn in the Collection and on the board disagree; moving art on the board, a board's worth of it the motion rationing ([[R2715001]]) would starve; and an oval cut into the art, which keeps the window the owner asked to lose.

Amended by [[R3339000]]: art-first cards in play (full-art looks and Legendary/Mythic) remove the name band below the art and display edge-to-edge art with keyword icon marks, showing the name plate only on hover or keyboard focus. Where the player sets "Names on the board" to Always, no card is drawn art first and the name band below the art is as this ruling has it.
