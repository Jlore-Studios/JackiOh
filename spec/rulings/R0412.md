C+ #19.5 Bot Loser's Radiant "Tranquility makes this unit not be able to go Berserk" is the designer's name for the immunity: "This can't go Berserk." Berserk ([[§6.1]]) is a flag C+ #19.2 Jungle Loser sets, lost when the unit leaves the field; while it is set, at its controller's start and end of turn the unit makes a forced attack on its own controller's hero ([[§4.2]], [[R53]]), which never strikes back. Jungle Loser's "destroys" is [[R42]]'s killer, and its Radiant face credits the kill to Bot Loser, firing Bot Loser's "whenever this destroys a Unit" as if it had made the kill
