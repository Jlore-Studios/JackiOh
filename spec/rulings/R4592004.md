Pattern Recognition reveals the top three cards of the opponent's library. On base, the player chooses one to add as a copy to their hand, then chooses another among the remaining cards to exile; the second pick excludes the first pick (`except`). On Radiant, the player chooses two to copy, and if three cards were offered, the remaining card is exiled automatically without a second prompt.
