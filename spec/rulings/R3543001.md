A Field Spell with Flexible and stats (`layers::has_stat_line`) may also be played into an open
unit zone, and there it is a Unit: it lands in Attack Position, sick on the turn it lands, it
attacks, is attacked and defends, and its text keeps running. A summon still puts it in its
backrow, and a Flexible Field Spell without stats goes to the backrow only, since a card in a unit
zone is a Unit (`faces::card_type_of`, [[R2280000]]). This changes SP3's default, under which no
Field Spell took a unit zone. Rejected: a non-Unit in a unit zone, which about 115 code sites that
read the unit row would all have to handle.

Superseded by issue #3828 ([[R3828000]]): Flexible is a Unit's keyword, so no Field Spell prints
it and none takes a unit zone, stats or not.
