**Where it arrives.** A Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]) is summoned into its owner's backrow, the middle first: the first open backrow zone of lanes 3, 2, 4, 1, 5 (`STARSHIP_LANE_ORDER`), standing there as a Flying Unit played into the backrow stands ([[R1081000]]), a Unit for every rule once it launches; with the backrow full, the first open unit zone in the same order; then an empty Locked zone, backrow first, since a summon enters a Locked zone ([[R688]]); with every zone of both rows taken, it is stacked on top of the lane-3 unit zone, Locked or not, as before. A zone held for a Reborn return takes nothing ([[R64]]). The player's Dormant Starship is the first found in their unit zones, beneath a Stack or not, then in their backrow zones.

**What it prints.** Its printed text is short: "Dormant until it launches. Your Starship Pieces that die fuse into this. Activate: Pay (3) to launch this." The rest of what the engine does ([[R1859013]]: the Stack exile, the name, the fused Cries and When launched) is the Starship and Launch terms' rule, which the client's term tips read. In play a Starship, Dormant or launched, prints no rules text at all: its rules box holds the Starship term and the Starship Pieces fused into it, oldest first (`UnitView.starship.pieces`, [[R1852005]]), each a reference that opens the piece's printed face on hover or long press.

**Its art.** Every Starship wears the Starship's own art, a capital ship drawn from how many pieces it holds and whether it has launched, never from its definition's id or name, which a fusion changes. The owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2249) asked for less text, the pieces as a list, art that survives a fusion and a ship that sits in the middle of the backrow.
