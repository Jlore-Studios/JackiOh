Every AI opponent is dealt one persona at match creation — Balanced 40%, Polite 25%, BM 20%, Silent 15% — which decides its emotes and is never shown to the player; the tutorial's is always Silent and sends nothing ever. A persona's weights, trigger chances, pools, delays, thresholds and per-turn and per-match caps all live in one config keyed by persona, so one can be tuned or added without code. A trigger the issue's table gives a persona fires its chance once and, on a hit, sends one emote of the row's pool that the AI seat's dealt hand holds after a 0.8–2.5 s delay, nothing when the hand holds none of it ([[R1344]]); a dash fires nothing. Each reply rule fires at most once per match, and the AI never replies to an AI emote, so no loop exists. Every persona obeys the player's rate limit ([[R643]]) — an emote it cannot send yet is dropped, never queued — and the mulligan greeting and the end-of-match emotes are exempt from the per-match cap. The rolls draw on an injected RNG outside the engine and the AI's move search — never `state.rng`, a determinization or a play decision — read only from the views and event windows the AI already receives, and live in a module `crates/engine` and the move search never import
