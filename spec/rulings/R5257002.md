FvH #71 Felinor Reanimator's Radiant face reads "End of turn: Summon a random Felinor Unit from your graveyard and make it Radiant. If there is none, summon a random Radiant Felinor Unit instead."

**The rule.** At its controller's end of turn ([[R62]]) both faces pick one Unit card of its controller's graveyard whose tags hold Felinor ([[R923]]; a Felinor Trap is no pick), uniformly by the match rng as the effect resolves ([[R60]]), and summon it into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), running no Cry ([[R1]]); the Radiant face makes it Radiant as it lands. "If there is none" reads the graveyard: only when it holds no Felinor Unit as the end of turn comes does the Radiant face summon a random Radiant non-token Felinor Unit of the sets that ship instead. A full row is no reason to: the pick finds no zone, stays in the graveyard and nothing replaces it ([[R129]]), and with no Felinor Unit there and a full row the fallback draws nothing either.
