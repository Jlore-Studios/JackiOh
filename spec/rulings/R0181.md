title: Max mana under a handicap
cards: "§2.3, #6, #21, #24"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/handicap.rs
---
Max mana = min(turns started + `manaBonus`, `manaCap`), plus persistent modifiers, floored at 0. With no handicap that is [[§2.3]]'s min(turns, 4) plus persistent modifiers. A next-turn modifier moves that turn's refresh and never max mana, as for a human ([[§2.3]]: #24's next-turn mana is temporary mana, and #21 lowers one refresh). Temporary mana, Hinder and every cost rule apply on top exactly as for a human: Medium refreshes to 2 on its first turn and 5 from its fourth, and Hard to 7 from its sixth
