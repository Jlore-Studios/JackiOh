Each game ends as [[§2.5]] says, a concede or a disconnect loses that game and not the series, a drawn game counts for neither side, and between games a player may forfeit the series, which the other side then wins: [[R334]] keeps all of it. What [[R334]] replaced is the end: after three games more wins took the series and a drawn game still spent both decks; now a draw locks nothing and the cap is `SERIES_MAX_GAMES` (7)
