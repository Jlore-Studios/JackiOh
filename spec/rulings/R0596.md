A text that draws and then acts on "it" or "the drawn card" — prices it (C+ #65.2 Normal Grape's "It costs (1) less", C+ #65.3 Large Grape's "It costs (0)") or reads it (T-AI-4 Chain of Thought's "If it costs (1) or less, repeat this") — means the card that one draw itself put in its player's hand. A card cast on draw never reaches the hand ([[R58]]), and the card the cast-on-draw chain's repeat then brings is that repeat's draw, not this one's; a burned card is in the graveyard ([[R4]]), a fatigue draw brings none ([[§2.4]]), a draw a limit stops is no draw ([[R457]]): each of these leaves "it" nothing, so nothing is priced and Chain of Thought's chain ends. Hearthstone's reading of "draw a card; it costs less"
