The Coin (T-coin) is a Token-tagged Spell costing 0: "Gain 1 mana this turn", Radiant "Gain 2 mana this turn". The mana is [[§2.3]]'s temporary mana: it adds to current mana, may take it past the cap (4, or a handicapped seat's `manaCap`, [[R181]]), and the next refresh takes it away, as with #6 Mana Well's. Every card has a Radiant face ([[§5.2]]), and a hand card can be made Radiant (#23, #26, #27, #29, #93's B step) or become Radiant as it is played (#64), so the Coin's doubles its number as #51.1's does ([[§8]] Conventions). As a Token it is in no deck ([[§2.6]], [[§9.4]] L3, [[R184]]), an AI's included, and in no Discover, determinized hidden card or random pool but C+ #23 Dropshipping's, which takes every token ([[§5.1]], [[R54]], [[R185]], [[R382]]): [[§2.1]] deals one, C+ #42 KY's Test's Easy reward names three, and beyond those only Dropshipping or an effect that copies one (#33, #39, [[R34]]) makes one. Otherwise it is an ordinary spell token: playing it is a play for every rule that counts or answers plays (Combo, #38, #60, #64, #100, [[R70]]), it takes #79 Twinspell's Echo, and it goes to the graveyard when it resolves ([[R11]]), where #72 Reminisce can find it ([[R50]])
