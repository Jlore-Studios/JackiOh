AI Girlfriend is an Animated Field Spell. It animates as it enters the field ([[R383]]); with no open unit zone it stays a backrow card, and its forced attacks find no target.

This build's decision: the start-of-turn forced attacks run only while it stands animated in a unit zone, read as the hook resolves. The rejected alternative was forcing the attacks from the backrow, where it is no combat target.
