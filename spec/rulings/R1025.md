AI Girlfriend was an Animated Field Spell. It animated as it entered the field ([[R383]]); with no open unit zone it stayed a backrow card, and its forced attacks found no target.

This build's decision: the start-of-turn forced attacks ran only while it stood animated in a unit zone, read as the hook resolved. The rejected alternative was forcing the attacks from the backrow, where it is no combat target.

Superseded by issue #2279: AI Girlfriend is a Unit with no Animated keyword. YileGPT Prime's Cry summons it into a unit zone per [[R64]], and with no open unit zone the summon fizzles and nothing enters.
