The recursion log, what a game remembers of its plays and deaths for the Infinite Assault's Recursion cards to read back, was kept only in a game holding an Infinite Assault card ([[R4589000]]). Felinors vs Humans' Plagiarized reprints are frozen copies of Infinite Assault scripts, some of which read it, and they are played in games with no Assault card. So `recursion_log::kept` also holds once the game holds, anywhere, a card whose definition carries a tag of `RECURSION_LOG_TAGS` (`config.rs`): Plagiarized. A game with neither keeps no log, as before, and since no card of a shipped set carries the tag no recorded game's hash moves ([[R768]]).
