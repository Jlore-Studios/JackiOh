[[R2564000]] prices no Dormant card's Activate: `activate_sources` walks `active_units_of`, which leaves every Dormant card out ([[R1839000]]). But a Dormant card keeps exactly one ability, the one its text names for its awakening ([[R1839002]]): a Dormant Starship's launch ([[R1852003]]), and the `wakesBy` ability a hull's own text names in its place ([[R3539000]], Stinkyscape+ #157 Phantom Shipyard's "Activate 3: Exile the shipyard and launch as a Starship"). So the one thing a sleeper can do counted for nothing, and the AI read a shipyard it could launch for free next turn as the 0/0 it sleeps as. [[R3064000]] prices an awakening's "When this awakens" trigger, which a shipyard does not print; this prices the use that wakes it. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** `activate_sources` lists, after a player's active units and before its backrow, each Dormant card of that player's acting for its awakening alone (`acts_to_awaken`: Dormant, topping its zone and naming an ability). From there [[R2564000]]'s probe prices it exactly as it prices any other card's use, at the start of that player's turn.

The seat's own sleepers are priced in [[R3575400]]'s re-keyed pass, from the same `AI_HAND_ACTIVATION_PROBE_NODES` slice and for the same reason: a shipyard's definition changes under the probe as its yard fuses a piece in ([[R3539004]]), so the price must be keyed by the card as the seat holds it now, which is the card its lines play with. The enemy's are keyed as [[R2564000]] keys any card, by definition, face and controller.

One guard is lifted for such a use and only for it: [[R2564000]] drops a use that takes its own card off the field, so that a card which destroys itself is not counted as a gain, but an awakening's whole point is that the card changes — a shipyard's launch exiles the hull and stands a fresh Starship holding its pieces in its place ([[R3539004]]). For a Dormant card's own awakening the price is therefore its controller's evaluation after the use less before it, whatever instance stands at the end.

A sleeper read this way is worth what [[R2380000]] gives it, the unit it wakes as at the waiting share, plus `AI_EVAL.activation` of what waking it is worth, in full: the price was read from the sleeping card, its controller's evaluation after the launch less before it, and before it the hull already stood at the waiting share, so the price holds the wait once; `slumber_worth`, which counts the awake copy's Activate inside the share, puts the rest of it back, as [[R3575402]] adds the yard's fuse outside the share for the same reason. Nothing else changes: a Dormant card still acts in no line, threatens no face and is reached by no effect.

**What is not priced.** A Dormant card beneath a Stack, which acts for nothing ([[R1839003]]). A card whose awakening its own board refuses (a shipyard with no piece fused in, which its `canActivate` refuses). A use that leaves a prompt open or ends the game. The rest of [[R2564000]]'s exclusions, unchanged.

**Rejected.** Reading the awakening through [[R3064000]]'s wake probes, which price a "When this awakens" trigger against a hookless twin and so price nothing for a card that prints no such trigger; and lifting the "still on the field" guard for every use, which would pay a card for destroying itself.
