The release deals the Assault from the set's real cards ([[R4597000]]), and on them `cargo jackioh assault-sim` broke two of [[R4583000]]'s bands on its six band days at the tiny budget: the rank I Commanders fell at level 5 more easily than level 4's deck (a mean margin of 37 against 4), and level 20 was won 2 of 6. Hero health alone moved neither, since a level-5 Commander still died and level 20's wins did not hang on it. So a Commander level takes a card step and then `ASSAULT_COMMANDER_UPGRADES` (2) upgrades, three escalations where it took two, and `ASSAULT_COMMANDER_HEALTH` is 40 and five a level (65, 90, 115 and 140), where it was 30 and five. Nothing falls: the extra upgrade is kept by every later level, as every escalation is ([[R4579000]]). On the same days level 1 is won 6 of 6, level 5's margin is under level 4's (−22 against 26), level 20 is won 1 of 6, and each later Commander's margin is under the level's before it. A Commander-only bonus that the next level drops, such as a mana bonus, was rejected: only health and the fewer opening cards may fall ([[R4579000]]).
