The Machine's Cry prints "either The Coin or Warhero Tokens". When is the choice made, what happens when its Cry doesn't go off, and in which order do the printed cards land? The print is the Choose one declared with the play ([[R81]]), so no prompt opens: the AI, the random policy and the timeout answer it as a play choice. A Machine that reaches the field without its Cry prints The Coin. At its controller's end of turn the printed card is shuffled into the opponent's deck first, then the coin flips for its own ([[R1440]]): on heads into its hand, on tails into its deck. The Radiant face prints Radiant cards into both decks, with Lucky 1. A printed Warhero Token starts the game-wide worth ([[R3548002]]).

Rejected: A Cry prompt with a timeout default: the choice is a play declaration, never a prompt, so no prompt — and no default — exists to time out.
