`effects::player_mods::give_cards_keyword { keyword }` (FvH #95's Radiant face, "for the rest of the game") gives its controller a `cardsHaveKeyword` modifier, expiry `never`: every card the player controls on the field, in a unit zone or the backrow, and every card in their resolving zone, has the keyword (§10.4's lends), those that arrive later too. Unlike "your Units have" ([[R1859007]]) it reaches every type, a Field Spell, a Trap and a resolving Spell included, but not a Vanilla card, one Dormant ([[R1839000]]) or one buried beneath a pile ([[R13]]), and, when a Spell made it, not one Immune to Spells ([[R4508000]]). A card in a hand or a deck is held, not controlled in play, and has nothing. `layers::cards_have_aura(keyword)` is its "Aura:" form, reaching the same cards of the source's controller while the source acts, as "Your Spells have" does ([[R1180002]]).
