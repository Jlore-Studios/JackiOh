**Which keys.** The client's shortcuts are I on a board card or a deck tile, Ctrl/⌘+Z and
Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z on the deck editor's Undo and Redo, ← and → and Shift+← and Shift+→ on the replay's
step and turn buttons, and Ctrl/⌘+K, which has no control ([[R2118000]]). A widget's own keys get
no hint and no `aria-keyshortcuts`: Enter and Space on a button or a card, Escape in a dialog, the
arrows, Home and End inside a widget or the card builder's canvas, the context-menu key and
Shift+F10.

**How they show.** The Apple test reads the user agent: ⌘ and ⇧ on a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad,
Ctrl and Shift elsewhere. A tooltip ends with its keys, "Undo (Ctrl+Z)", and `aria-keyshortcuts`
writes Ctrl/⌘ as Control and Meta, "Control+Z Meta+Z". The hover preview of a board card or a deck
tile says "Press I to inspect" under the face, the command menu marks its selected entry with
Enter and names its arrows, Enter and Esc at its foot, and the site footer says that Ctrl/⌘+K opens
the command menu.

**Touch.** A device with no hover and a coarse pointer, `(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)`,
shows no hint, and follows the query as it changes. `aria-keyshortcuts` stays: it is not drawn.

**No new keys.** The board's key handlers are frozen: a test counts them, so no handler is added
and there is no End-turn key. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a hint reads the platform and a
media query, never the game.
