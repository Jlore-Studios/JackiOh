Issue #4154 made M #78 Occidentless Mandate's exile a destroy: "Destroy every non-CN permanent." (Radiant: "Destroy every enemy non-CN permanent."). It reaches what the exile reached, as Hearthstone's Twisting Nether reaches every minion: every non-CN permanent of both sides (the Radiant face's: its caster's opponent's alone), both rows, the top of each unit pile and every backrow card, face-down ones included, a card in Chinese being CN ([[R1176000]]) and a Unit immune to Spells spared ([[R940]]). Each is marked destroyed and the board is collected in one state check ([[R59]]), so no Death fires while the rest still stand; then each goes to its owner's graveyard, a unit token ceasing to exist ([[R11]]), and every Death fires. An Indestructible permanent keeps its place, its mark dropped ([[R46]]), where the exile took it; a card beneath a Stack is not on the field and resumes ([[R13]]). Through v0.6.6g it exiled them, beating Indestructible and firing no Death (`script_v0_6_6g`, [[R767000]]). Rejected: sparing face-down cards, which the exile never spared and "every permanent" does not.
