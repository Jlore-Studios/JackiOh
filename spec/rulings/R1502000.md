[[§14.8]] paid Jlore, XP and quest progress to ladder games and verified practice games alone, and
left to a sub-issue whether a game of a ranked Conquest series, an unranked game or a co-op game
earns. They earn, through the same reward hook, each in the transaction that writes its result and
under the economy lock of every account it pays ([[R1301003]]), and only while `ECONOMY_LIVE` is on
([[R1302006]]).

**Which game is which.** A finished 1v1 match is one of three, by its match and its series: a game
of a Conquest series, ranked or not, is a Conquest game; a ranked match outside a series is a ladder
game; any other match, a room challenge and its rematches ([[R604]]), is an unranked game. A co-op
game ([[§13]]) is a fourth mode, its two people each paid for their own seat. A practice game is
unchanged ([[R1303000]]).

**The rates.** An unranked game pays `JLORE_UNRANKED_WIN` for a win and `JLORE_UNRANKED_LOSS`
otherwise, and each game of a Conquest series `JLORE_CONQUEST_WIN` and `JLORE_CONQUEST_LOSS`, both
at the ladder's rates (20 and 10). A co-op game pays each person `JLORE_COOP_WIN` when the humans'
team wins and `JLORE_COOP_LOSS` otherwise, a draw included, at practice's rates (10 and 5), since the
team plays the AI. XP is the ladder's for an unranked game and a Conquest game, and a co-op game's
is the ladder's for the same moves times `XP_COOP_SCALE` (0.5), rounded down, as practice's is
([[R1302007]]). A draw pays the loss rate everywhere ([[R1302000]]). Each game of a Conquest series
pays as its own result, though the series moves the rating once, when it ends ([[R262]]): a win of
the series pays no more than its games did.

**What counts as a move.** A 1v1 seat's actions are counted as on the ladder ([[R1302004]]). A co-op
seat's are the rows of its seat in the match's log that its person sent: a duel action
`is_player_action` counts, a pass to the teammate and the team's concede. A row under the server's
own nonce ([[R270]]: the AI's moves for a seat, the ceiling) is no one's, and a row that does not
read as an action counts as none, so an unreadable log never stops a result being written. A co-op
log is not folded into what a seat played, so a co-op game moves its people's quests by its result
and its attacks alone: a quest for plays, Traps fired or damage dealt does not advance in a co-op
game.

**When it pays.** The write that lands the result pays; a repeat of it ([[R1047001]]'s sweep racing
the actor, a rebuilt actor) writes and pays nothing. A co-op game that a void ended
([[R1168002]], [[R679]]) pays nothing, as a voided 1v1 match writes nothing; the sweep's ceiling
draw ([[R1047001]]) and the actor's ([[R1253000]]) pay as a draw. A bot's side is paid nothing
([[R1454]]).

**The daily cap.** Every mode's games count toward `JLORE_GAMES_DAILY_CAP` together, in the UTC day
the game ended in ([[R1483000]]). The seats of a 1v1 match, ladder or not, are told on their `view`
frames when the cap held their Jlore back, as a ladder seat is; a co-op game's frames do not yet
say it.

Hearthstone semantics: casual games pay as ranked ones do, up to the same daily limit. Hearthstone
pays a friendly challenge nothing, to stop farming between one person's two accounts; the owner's
approval of #1502 sets that aside, and the daily cap bounds what such games can farm in Jlore. XP
and the track levels it buys have no daily bound, only each game's `XP_GAME_CAP`.

Rejected: no payout for these modes; reusing the ladder's and practice's constants, which would tie
their tuning together; a bonus for winning a series, which the series' own rating move already
rewards; paying co-op at the ladder's rates, when its opponent is the AI; folding the co-op log to
count plays and Traps for quests, which is engine work; paying a voided co-op game as a draw, which
would pay a game the server cannot vouch for; and a `jloreCapped` field on the co-op frames, which is
client work, left for a follow-up.
