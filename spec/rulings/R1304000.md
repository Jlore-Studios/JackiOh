[[§14.9]] has a quest arrive `QUEST_INTERVAL_HOURS` after the last one did while an account holds
fewer than `QUEST_MAX_HELD`, so a player away for a long while comes back to `QUEST_MAX_HELD`
waiting, without saying when the clock starts, when the arrivals of an absence are dated, or what
becomes of a quest that falls due with no room. The server's clock decides each, when the account
next shows up (its quests read, a game it finished, a dev tool it used):

- **The first visit starts the clock.** An account that has never been topped up holds nothing and
  gains nothing at its first visit; its first quest falls due one interval later.
- **One quest falls due per interval, on that rhythm.** A top-up brings every quest that fell due
  while there was room, oldest first, each dated when it fell due, so an absence of any length
  returns exactly `QUEST_MAX_HELD`. A quest that falls due with no room is skipped, never banked:
  the next falls due on the same rhythm, so completing a quest at the cap makes room for the next
  arrival, not an arrival at once.
- **The draw.** Each arrival is drawn uniformly among the pool's entries the account does not hold,
  so no quest is held twice, by a seed drawn afresh from the operating system, which its ledger row
  logs in `ref` ([[§14.11]]): no seed an account can read foretells its next quest.
- **Two at once.** A top-up reads the account's schedule locked to the end of its transaction, so
  a second top-up of the same account (its quests read in two tabs, or read as a game it played is
  recorded) waits for the first and reads what it wrote: a quest that fell due arrives once.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone gives one daily quest a day on a fixed daily rhythm and holds at
most three; a player whose log is full that day does not get it, and nothing is saved up for later.

Rejected: restarting the clock when a quest completes, which would make a player at the cap wait a
whole interval after every completion; and banking the arrivals a full log skipped, which would let
an account away for a week finish quest after quest on its return, each replaced at once.
