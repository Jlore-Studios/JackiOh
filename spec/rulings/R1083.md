`summon_random_from_hand` (MD-D18) summons one random Unit card from your hand — unit-token cards included — straight into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), a Flying one into the backrow when the unit row is full ([[R2248000]]), with no Cry ([[R1]]) and summoning sick. The pick is drawn only when a zone is open and a Unit is in hand, an open backrow zone counting when a Unit in hand flies ([[R129]]), and the opponent sees nothing until it lands ([[R177]]). The Radiant face makes it Radiant once it is on the field. Meditative #63 Skull of J'Nari runs it at its controller's end of turn since issue #3140; through v0.5.11o it ran at the start of the turn (`script_v0_5_11o`, [[R767000]]).
