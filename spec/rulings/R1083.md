`summon_random_from_hand` (MD-D18) summons one random Unit card from your hand — unit-token cards included — straight into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), with no Cry ([[R1]]) and summoning sick. The pick is drawn only when a zone is open and a Unit is in hand ([[R129]]), and the opponent sees nothing until it lands ([[R177]]). The Radiant face makes it Radiant once it is on the field.
