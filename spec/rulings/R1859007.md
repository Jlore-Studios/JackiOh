The War Doctrines: "For the rest of the game, your Units have Trample" (Deft, Rush, Flying). (ST-S02-7.) The verb is `give_units_keyword { keyword }`: a player modifier on the caster (`unitsHaveKeyword`), expiry `never`, a badge on both seats ("Your Units have Trample"). [[§10.4]]'s keywords take it beside the aura grants: every Unit its holder controls on the field, in a unit zone or standing in the backrow ([[R1081000]]), has the keyword, those that arrive later too, while it stays once the Doctrine is in the graveyard. It does not reach a card in a hand or in a pile, a Dormant card or one buried under a Stack pile ([[R1839000]], [[R13]]), nor a Vanilla Unit, as the Doctrines' rows of [[§8.10]] say, unlike an aura's grant. Each Doctrine's Starship line runs once, as it is cast, on its caster's launched Starship (`launched_starship_of`, [[R1852005]]), the last launched; with none, the line does nothing and the keyword is lent all the same.
