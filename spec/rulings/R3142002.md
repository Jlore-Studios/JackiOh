Two Stinkyscape cards change how they look, the client's alone ([[R2170000]]; presentation, CLAUDE.md rule 7; a visual claim, [[R2529000]]). `stinkyscape.ts` chooses a card's family by its tags and its name, and two of its choices were wrong for what the owner asked of the cards.

- **A Human Unit is a person.** The Drone Commander, a Human Unit, drew as a drone ship because its name holds the word "drone", which sent it to the `drone` family. A Unit tagged Human whose name holds "drone" is now the `crew` family's `commander` kind: the crew figure with epaulettes and a medal, and a drone hovering behind her shoulder. A card whose name holds "drone" and that is no Human Unit, such as the Drone Interceptor, the Drone Corvette, the Drone Battleship's layers and the Drone Hanger, stays the `drone` family. The Drone Commander is the only Human Unit of the set whose name holds the word, so no other card's picture moves.
- **The Golem is a hybrid.** The Stinkyscape Golem drew as a fighter hull because it printed Starship Piece, and the client's list of those cards named it ([[R1852000]]). It prints no Starship Piece now, so it leaves that list, and its name keeps it in the `fleet` family as the `golem` kind: a stone-plated humanoid body with engine nozzles at its heels, wing fins on its back and a cockpit visor for a face. The Training Grounds Golem has its own painter in `traininggrounds.ts` and is untouched.
- **Shipped pictures.** Both cards' pictures change, so their two entries in the recorded hashes of the v0.5.0 pictures are recorded again, as Starfleet Commander's were when it was renamed ([[R503]]); every other entry keeps the picture it shipped with.

Rejected: sending every Human Unit to the `crew` family ahead of every other rule, which would move the Acclaimed Humans' pictures; and a golem drawn from the fighter hull, which a hull with a torso only half answers.
