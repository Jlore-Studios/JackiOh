Felinors vs Humans #81 raises its player's Woozy counter. The counter ([[§6.1]] Woozy X, [[R3548000]]) rose only as a card with Woozy X was played.

**The rule.** `gain_woozy { player?, amount }` raises that player's Woozy counter (its controller's by default) by `amount`, exactly as a played card's Woozy X raises it: one `counterChanged` of the `woozy` kind on that hero with the amount added, a counter that was down starting on this turn, and the same decay at the start of its owner's turn. An amount of 0 or less does nothing. A played card's rise is the same function (`woozy::raise`), so it reports as before.
