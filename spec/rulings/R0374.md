The designer asked for the homescreen's cards to be randomized. The landing page deals a fresh hand on each visit from every non-token Core card, read from the catalog: four cards of four different rarities, chosen at random and in a random order, the middle one on its Radiant face, then a card back, which is the fixed hand's shape. The deal is drawn once per visit; since [[R704]] the page then swaps one card at a time while it is up. Its randomness is the page's own (`Math.random`), which CLAUDE.md rule 4 does not bind outside the engine, the cards and the AI, and the source is injected, so a test deals the same hand every run
