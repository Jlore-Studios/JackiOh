Since issue #2179 a card cast on draw ([[§2.4]], [[R58]]) is still a cast through [[§10.5]]'s steps,
free, its Cry or spell script run, its choices made as a cast's ([[R70]]), but it is no play: it is
not logged in its caster's turn log (`playedIds`, `cardsPlayed`, `costsPaid`), nor counted in the game
counters (`counters.played`), the per-game play counts or the last Spell and last card records, and
Gifted Program's hook passes it by ([[R213]]). Its `cardPlayed`, which still reports the cast
(`onDraw: true`, cost paid 0), wakes no trigger, trap or quest. Its `cardResolved` reports `onDraw` too
and still reaches every card, since some answer a card's resolution or a Unit's arrival rather than a
play (Tesla, Warp Disruptor, Dogpile), but a card whose text answers a play passes it by: Unstable
Clone Machine and Bear Honeypot ([[R17]]), Unlicensed Experimentation, EU Striker, Memory Leak,
Catnip, Tatches the Totem, Prison and Sacked. So nothing that counts or answers plays sees it: Combo
([[R40]]), Quickstriker's X, Jackiestan Auctioneer, Wardrum, Twice Forward One Step Backwards, Rate
Limit, Autocomplete, Jlockwork Machine, Double Header, Clip-Farming Critikal, Ceaseless Void, Echo's
last Spell (C #57). A cast on draw that its own text makes count (its own granted Combo) reads the
plays before it as ever. An Echo's repeat (Echo X, [[§6.2]]) re-resolves the card already played and
was never a play: the card is played once, which this ruling pins with a test. [[R155]]'s end-of-turn
return of a Spell cast on draw is cleared at that turn's cleanup from the graveyard, as the turn log no
longer holds it. A cast by any other effect still counts as a play ([[R70]]). The issue wrote the
rule; Hearthstone's Casts When Drawn cards are not played either
