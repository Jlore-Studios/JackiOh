[[R1297000]] carried a deck's playmat, backdrop and card back to practice alone, since the client
was not told which deck a seat plays, and left carrying them online to "a frame that freezes them
with the ticket and a ruling of its own". This is that ruling, for every seat of a 1v1 online match,
the board each seat plays, a watched game and an online replay: Best of 1, ranked and unranked,
Conquest, All Random, private rooms and friends' challenges, rematches and a ladder bot's seat
([[R1834005]]). Its frames are [[R1834003]]'s and the client's wear [[R1834004]]'s. It amends
[[R1297000]] (*What it does not do*).

**Frozen with the deck.** Whatever freezes a deck freezes its three slots with it, as [[R642]]
freezes its portrait: a ticket (`tickets.deck_cosmetics`), a room's host deck while the room waits
(`matches.room_deck_cosmetics`) and each deck of a frozen trio (inside `frozen_trio`). Editing the
saved deck after that changes nothing in the game it becomes ([[§9.8]]).

**Frozen with the match.** As a match starts, each seat's loadout is frozen onto its row
(`matches.loadouts`, both seats in seat order) from what the seat's account owns at that moment, its
picks in the `cosmetics` settings group and its Full-art cards setting, its cards' own picks
([[R1834000]]), its deck's slots and the cards its deck dealt. Every id frozen is a registry id of
its slot's kind that the account owns where it is worn:

- **The deck slots** (playmat, backdrop, card back): the deck's pick when the deck carries one, worn
  if the account owns it and the kind's default if not, since the deck's word is final ([[R1297000]]);
  else the account's pick, if owned; else the default.
- **The card slots** (foil, finish, border), which every card of the seat wears that has no pick of
  its own: the kind's default. A card with no pick of a kind wears its default, never the account's
  foil, finish or border, which nothing sets any more ([[R1953001]]), so whatever the `cosmetics`
  group still holds of them is frozen nowhere. The frame keeps the three, each its default, so its
  shape is the one it always had.
- **Each card's own**, for each distinct card the seat's deck dealt: of each kind, its own pick when
  that is owned on the card; when it is not owned, it has none or it is an id the registry has none
  of for that kind, the default, as its owner's client draws it ([[R1655000]], [[R1953001]]). Its
  full art prints when the account owns that card's full art and the card's own switch, else the
  account's Full-art cards setting, is on ([[R1313000]]); since [[R2655000]] the full art is the
  one its own pick names, else its Epic, frozen as `true` for the Epic and the id for another tier. A card is kept only where it differs from
  the seat, so only where it wears something but the defaults.

An id the registry does not know or the account does not own is never frozen, so none reaches
another client. The loadout is never read again from the account: a pick changed during a match
shows from the next one. A match row from before migration 0061 holds none, and every reader takes it
as every kind's default with no card of its own.

**Which deck.** An All Random seat plays no deck of its own ([[R258]]), so its deck slots are its
account's picks, its cards' own picks frozen over the deck it was dealt. A Conquest game wears the
slots of the deck each side picked for it ([[R330]]). A Best-of-1 rematch replays the finished
match's decks with the deck slots its seats wore, judged again as the rematch starts, and an All
Random rematch, dealt new decks, wears the accounts' picks ([[R672]]). A ladder bot's seat freezes
through the same function, its wardrobe as what it owns ([[R1834005]]).

**Where none is frozen.** Practice and hotseat run in the browser and freeze nothing: the viewer's
own side wears their own picks with every cosmetic counted as owned ([[R1310004]]), and the
practice AI's side draws the defaults. Out of scope, each for a follow-up: Duos, where every seat
but the viewer's own still draws the defaults; a downloaded replay file ([[R1141000]]), which holds
no loadout; and the shared GIF ([[R513000]]), which draws no cosmetic.

Nothing here is a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a loadout never enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log,
a replay hash or a game record, and the engine never reads one.

Hearthstone semantics: what a player had equipped as the game began, their card back and each
card's version, is what both players see for the whole game.

Rejected: freezing the account's foil, finish and border as the seat's, which would dress the other
side's view of a card in picks its owner's own client no longer draws on it ([[R1953001]]); an
unowned per-card pick freezes as its kind's default, as that client draws it. Reading the account's
picks afresh on every frame, which would change a seat's look mid-game and make a replay's depend on
when it is watched.
