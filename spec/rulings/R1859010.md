Stinkyscape #137.3 Air Doctrine: "Deal damage equal to 1× your Starship's Attack, split randomly among all enemies." (ST-S02-10.) [[§6.3]] Damage's split (`damage_split`): N hits of 1, N the Attack of its caster's launched Starship as it stands (twice it on the Radiant face), each to an enemy still standing, the hero or a Unit, drawn from the match rng ([[R60]]), each raised by the caster's Spell Damage. No launched Starship, nothing. The card is marked `dealsDamage` ([[R1748001]]).
