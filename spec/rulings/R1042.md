## R1042 — All other CN and KY cards means every other card with either tag

Built (ME-ALTPLAY, Meditative #45, #45.1). "All other CN & KY cards" is every card other than this
one with the CN tag or the KY tag, in every zone its verb reaches. A Nerf reaches both fields, both
hands and both decks; a Destroy reaches both fields. The Radiant Prime's Exile keeps the base face's
reach (both fields).
