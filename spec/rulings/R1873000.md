S #6 Tylannt reads "Cry: Complete the current stage on all your Quests". Its Radiant face first adds "Summon The Greatest Heist" (ST-S09-1, ST-S09-2).

- **The completion.** `complete_quests` ([[R1854001]]): every card with the Quest tag acting on its controller's side of the field, in lane order, has the quests open on it completed, each reward given at the next state check. The opponent's Quests and an untagged card with a quest line are passed by. A Tylannt recruited or summoned runs no Cry ([[R1]]), so it completes nothing.
- **The summon runs the Heist's Cry.** The summon names The Greatest Heist, so its Cry runs, as League of Losers' summon runs Mid Loser's ([[R411]]; the brief's ST-S09-1). The Heist enters its controller's leftmost open backrow zone ([[R64]]) and its Cry is triggered there, a face-up Field Spell's Cry being triggered as a Unit's is ([[R1873003]]): its Discover is asked of Tylannt's controller, and the completion waits behind it ([[R113]]). Once the pick is made, the Heist's first stage, opened as it entered ([[R1854000]]), is completed with the rest, so Stage 1's reward is given and Stage 2 opens. A full backrow summons nothing, so nothing is discovered, and the completion reaches the other Quests alone.
