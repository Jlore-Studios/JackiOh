S #6 Tylannt reads "Cry: Complete the current stage on all your Quests". Its Radiant face first adds "Summon The Greatest Heist" (ST-S09-1, ST-S09-2).

- **The completion.** `complete_quests` ([[R1854001]]): every card with the Quest tag acting on its controller's side of the field, in lane order, has the quests open on it completed, each reward given at the next state check. The opponent's Quests and an untagged card with a quest line are passed by. A Tylannt recruited or summoned runs no Cry ([[R1]]), so it completes nothing.
- **The summon has no Cry.** The brief proposed that the Radiant face's summon names The Greatest Heist, so its Cry (its Discover) runs, as [[R411]] has League of Losers fire Mid Loser's. The build decided otherwise. A summon is no play ([[R1]]), Hearthstone's summons fire no Battlecry, and the engine's triggered Cry reaches Units alone (`trigger_cry`, B2.7), while the Heist is a Field Spell. So the Heist enters its controller's leftmost open backrow zone ([[R64]]) with no Discover. Its first stage opens as it enters ([[R1854000]]) and is completed with the rest, so Stage 1's reward is given and Stage 2 opens. A full backrow summons nothing, and the completion reaches the other Quests alone.
