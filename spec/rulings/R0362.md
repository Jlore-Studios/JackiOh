[[§8]] #92's Radiant face: "Has twice the stats of all your Felinors, including those under Stack." [[R39]] reads "has the stats of" as the printed stats plus the combined Felinor stats, and the patch changed no stats, so the Radiant face is its printed 10/14 plus twice that sum; the floor at 0 ([[R39]]) and [[R131]]'s exclusion of itself apply to the sum before it is doubled
