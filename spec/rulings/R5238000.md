Felinors vs Humans #16 adds Travertine Stacks with Temporary 1 to 6, a count the plain `temporary` rider of [[§6.3]]'s Add to hand cannot give: it gives plain Temporary, which is Temporary 1 ([[R2179001]]).

**The rule.** `add_to_hand` and `add_random_from_catalog` take `temporary_count: N`. Once the card is in the hand it is Temporary N: it is given the keyword Temporary N, in place of any Temporary it was given before, and its count left starts at N (`CardInstance.temporary`, [[R2179001]]), so it is discarded at the Nth end of its owner's turn it spends in that hand ([[R637]]) whatever Temporary it prints. The view shows Temporary N, then the count left as each end of turn takes 1 off it.

**Riders.** It goes on with the cost riders, so a card a full hand burns ([[§2.4]]) takes none, and with `temporary` beside it the count wins. N below 1 gives nothing. As every keyword granted in a hand, it leaves with the card leaving the field ([[R637]]'s reset); the count left stays on the instance as [[R2179001]] keeps it.
