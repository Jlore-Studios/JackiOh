How does Tipsy X work: what raises it, when does it fall, and where does the client show it?

Tipsy X is a player counter like Jade or Credits, shown as a stein beside the player's hero on both
seats' views. Playing a card with Tipsy X adds X to its controller's counter, reported once per play
as a `tipsy` `counterChanged` on `hero-<player>` with the X it added, as Jade reports its rises. The
counter drops by `TIPSY_DECAY` (1) at the start of its owner's turn — never on the opponent's — and is
gone at 0: the state field and the view key are both absent then, so a game without one serializes
and hashes as before.

Rejected: decaying at the end of the owner's turn, like the Warhero worth's growth: the designer says it decays
"at the start of your turn", beside the turn's other start-of-turn ticks (§2.2).
