Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V: "Indestructible -> innately have immutable and can't defend and can't have taunt." Two of the three were so already: an Indestructible Unit is never in Defense Position ([[R2179000]]) and never has Taunt, whatever grants it ([[R347]]). The third is new: every Indestructible Unit card is Immutable, as Flying is Flexible ([[R3543000]]), without the keyword being added, so its keywords list Indestructible alone and a card that prints both (Core #66 The Rock's Radiant face) is no different for it. A Unit card is one whose running face prints the Unit type, wherever it stands. The engine asks one question wherever a rule reads Immutable (`layers::is_immutable`, `layers::immutable_by`): the keyword, or Indestructible on a Unit card.

Units only, as the designer's sentence reads: Can't Defend and Taunt are Units' things, and an Indestructible Field Spell's own text works by changing it. Rejected: every Indestructible card, which would stop C+ #33 Ivory Tower and S #16 Rod of Ages' Radiant face from taking the Units their text fuses in ([[R653]], [[R1869000]]), Core #98 Heroic Power's tuned numbers and End Times, and Classic #90 In Too Deep's fused Lockdowns.

So all that Immutable stops ([[R23]], [[R386]]) stops on an Indestructible Unit card too, in every zone Immutable holds in: no Transform, Vanilla, Fuse-onto, Nerf or Buff reaches it, and with them no Nerf ever takes its Indestructible away. A Unit Indestructible only for a while (an aura, a grant, S+ #121 Toxopid's while its Legs stand) is Immutable for as long as it is Indestructible. A Buff that makes a Field Spell Indestructible ([[R2436000]]) leaves it a Field Spell that later Buffs and Nerfs still reach. What Immutable never did, it does not do here: it bars no aim ([[R4353001]]), and damage, destroys, exiles, Sacrifices and steals reach the card as before, Indestructible's own refusals apart.
