M+ #5 Spirit Lash, "(0) embiggen (1)", gains Flashback on both faces (issue #3138, part 4 of #3128), the first card to have both an embiggen price and a graveyard play of its own. [[R783012]] has a card with Flashback played from its owner's graveyard "at its current cost (X chosen at the play)", a play for every rule, and [[R3039001]] keeps the keyword through each play; neither says which price an embiggen card pays there.

- **Both prices are offered.** An "A embiggen B" card ([[§2.3]]) lying in its owner's graveyard with Flashback is offered as two plays, one at A and one Embiggened at B, each an ordinary `play` action with `embiggen` false or true ([[R81]]), as the same card is offered in a hand. The graveyard play is a play for every rule ([[R783012]], [[R454]]), so the Embiggened one pays B ([[R65]]) and its Embiggened effects apply: each hit of Spirit Lash carries Lifesteal and Pierce, and the Radiant face's Echo 1 repeat is Embiggened too. The engine answers the Embiggened price (`embiggen_play_cost`, the number the view's `embiggenCost` carries) for a card in the graveyard as for one in a hand, so the client prices nothing itself (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- **Chosen again each time.** The choice belongs to the play, not to the card: a play Embiggened from the graveyard leaves nothing on it ([[R3039001]] writes no mark), so it lies there again with both prices on offer, the base one still (0).
- **Nothing in the engine changes.** The two prices came out of the one price list a hand play and a graveyard play share (`priced_play_actions`), so the ruling records what the engine already did.

Rejected: Flashback reading only the base price of an embiggen card, which would make Spirit Lash's Embiggened version a play from the hand alone, where [[R783012]] says a Flashback card may be played "at its cost" like any other play of it.
