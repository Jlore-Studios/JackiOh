Shrinking Felinor's Death reads the dying card's [[§10.4]] layer-1 stats off its last-known state ([[R78]]): its stat override as its face wears it ([[R349]]), else its printed stats. Buffs, tuning (layer 4), auras and damage are not base stats. It summons a fresh Shrinking Felinor on the same face, not a copy ([[R57]]), so no buff, granted keyword or tuning carries, in the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]); the new card's stat override is that attack less the attack shrink (at least 0) and that health less the health shrink. If the new health would be 0 or less nothing is summoned, which ends the chain. The base chain runs 9/11, 6/8, 3/5, 0/2 (four bodies); the Radiant chain runs 18/22, 16/19, 14/16, 12/13, 10/10, 8/7, 6/4, 4/1 (eight). Each shrink is a declared number that never tunes below 1 ([[R386]]), so the chain always ends. A Shrinking Felinor made Radiant on the field keeps its stat override and shrinks by the Radiant numbers from then on. The engine reads layer 1 as `base_stats_of(state, card)`.
