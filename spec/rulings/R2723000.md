The owner asked in #2723 for a full-art card ([[R1017001]], any tier of [[R2655000]]) in the viewer's own hand to be a small version of its art, since the player knows the card. On a board the viewer plays, their hand's full-art faces (the worn ones, [[R1295000]]) draw no name banner, Mythic signature, rarity gem, set mark or crest. They keep the cost gem with its live cost and tone, a Unit's attack and health, the hand's states, the scrim, the tier's motion ([[R2655003]]) and any finish, border or foil. The name stays in the face's words, visually hidden, for a screen reader and the tooltip. Every other copy prints the whole face: the hover preview, the sheet, the detail view, the board, a mulligan's and a Discover's options, the deck builder, the Collection, the Almanac, an opponent's revealed hand and a watched or replayed game. Presentation only: no rule reads it.
