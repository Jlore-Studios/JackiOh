C #42 Transmutable Toxins' aura gives its controller's Units +1/+1 for each Plague Counter on them, and enemy Units −1/−1 (+2/+2 and −2/−2 on its Radiant face). In the handicapped waves, two Spells changed a Toxins they could reach, and the change killed a Unit Immune to Spells:
- C #39 Outbreak destroyed an enemy Toxins whose aura's health alone held up a damaged S+ #112 Fortress.
- TG #27 Hopeful Jelly Bean made a Toxins Radiant, and its −2/−2 a counter brought an enemy S+ #67 Phase Wall to a max health of −2.

The state check destroyed each Unit in the Spell's first run of the step, which [[R4604004]]'s reading of the state check's deaths does not cover, so the fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) blamed the deaths on the Spell.
**The deaths are the state check's.** Neither Spell touched the immune Unit. Each changed a card it may reach, and [[§4.5]] destroyed the Unit because the aura's change ([[§10.4]] layer 5) left it at 0 health, the same rule that lets a −1/−1 aura kill ([[R69]]). [[§6.1]] keeps a Spell's effects off the Unit, not the board's rules.
**What I21 allows.** I21 allows a `destroyed` of an immune Unit in a Spell's run in two cases:
- as the step began, its damage was at least its health before the auras (layers 1 to 4), so only an aura's health held it up, whatever then took the aura away;
- it died at a max health of 0 or less, which only a stat change brings. A Spell's own Nerf or debuff on the Unit would show in an event naming it, which I21 judges by itself.

Any other `destroyed` in a Spell's first run is still a reach.
Rejected: allowing every `destroyed` after another card changed in the run. A Spell that destroys every Unit, the immune one wrongly among them, would pass that test too.
