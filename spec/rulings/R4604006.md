C #42 Transmutable Toxins' aura gives its controller's Units +1/+1 for each Plague Counter on them. In one handicapped game, C #39 Outbreak placed its counters on an enemy Toxins and destroyed it, since there was no free zone to steal it into. A damaged S+ #112 Fortress, Immune to Spells, had stood only on the aura's health, so the state check destroyed it. All of this happened in Outbreak's first run of the step, which [[R4604004]]'s reading of the state check's deaths does not cover, and the fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) blamed Fortress's death on the Spell.
**The death is the state check's.** Outbreak never touched Fortress. It destroyed a card it may reach, and [[§4.5]] destroyed Fortress because its health fell to 0 once the aura went ([[§10.4]] layer 5; the same rule that lets a −1/−1 aura kill, [[R69]]). [[§6.1]] keeps a Spell's effects off the Unit, not the board's rules.
**What I21 allows.** I21 notes a Unit whose damage is at least its health before the auras (layers 1 to 4) as the step begins, so that only an aura's health holds it up. A `destroyed` of such a Unit is allowed in a Spell's run, whatever took the aura away. Any other `destroyed` in a Spell's first run is still a reach.
Rejected: allowing every `destroyed` after another card left the field in the run. A Spell that destroys every Unit, the immune one wrongly among them, would pass that test too.
