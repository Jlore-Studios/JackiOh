The summon verb's `setCloaked` (S+ #76 Ayys LMAO) sets a fresh Unit that names no lane face-down by its Cloaked in its side's leftmost open backrow zone ([[R64]], [[R688]]), on its summoner's half of a slashed board ([[R3554003]]). The card is stamped as a Cloaked play stamps it ([[R783004]]) before it lands, so it stands as one set from a hand does: hidden from the opponent like any set Trap ([[R33]], [[R1046]]), its text inert, a Field Trap and a backrow card to every effect and no Unit ([[R3777000]]), with no Cry as it arrives ([[R1]]). It reveals only by its Activate: Reveal in its controller's main phase, and that reveal is the one [[R783006]] gives every card set by its Cloaked: it animates into a unit zone and its Cry runs, and it enters that zone on the turn it is revealed, so it is summoning sick then ([[R4237000]]). Destroyed or bounced unrevealed it is its printed card again, and a Unit token set so ceases to exist ([[R783008]], [[R2855000]]).

With no backrow zone open the summon lands where it would without the option, face-up ([[R64]]), and with no zone at all it fizzles ([[R129]]). The option sets only a fresh Unit: a summon naming a lane, a GIGA, a card that already exists (a Recruit, a return) or a card of another type lands as it would without it. The fresh card's id was never public, so it takes no second id ([[R227]]). Its `summoned` names a card the opponent cannot read, so `viewFor` hides its identity there as it hides any hidden card's. The summon's `keywords` rider lands on a card it set with no `keywordGranted`, which would tell the opponent which back took it (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The AI reveals such a card as it reveals a played one, through `legalActions`, and its opponent's AI sees a placeholder.

Hearthstone: a Secret an effect puts into play (Mad Scientist's) is a Secret as one played is. Rejected: a second fresh id, for an id no player has seen; announcing the rider; and a reveal that runs no Cry for a summoned card, which would make it the one card set by its Cloaked whose reveal skips its text.
