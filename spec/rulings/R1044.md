## R1044 — Paranoia's Aura gives your Spells Cloaked

Built (Meditative #99; patch v0.3.7, #781). "Aura: Your Spells have Cloaked." While Paranoia acts on
its controller's field, each Spell card in that player's hand has Cloaked ([[R783007]]); no Unit, Field
Spell or Trap is touched. A Spell set this way ([[R783004]]) is a Trap play for every rule that counts
or answers plays, and a Trap on the field until it reveals, but it is priced as the Spell it is
([[R783005]]), so Cloaked Toe Cracker's "Your Traps cost (0)" never makes it free. It declares no
targets when set; X and embiggen are chosen when set. It reveals only when its controller activates
its Reveal ([[R783006]]), which is not offered while a target its play needs ([[R703]]) has no legal
pick; a target it could go without lets it reveal and fizzle ([[R90]]). Its effects then come from a
Trap (no Spell Damage; a counter for Spells never sees it), but it is a Spell card however it was
played, so Immutable bars its aim ([[R781001]]). It resolves its Spell text with prompts, Echo
repeating with fresh prompts, and goes to the graveyard as the Spell. "This turn" in its text is the
turn it reveals in.

Until patch v0.3.7 the Aura was ME-ALTPLAY's permission: a Spell set under it was priced as a Trap
and revealed by itself at a time chosen as it was played (the end of this turn, the start of the next
turn or the end of the next), fizzling with no legal target. The wire keeps the three timings
(`RevealAt`) and the client's picker still reads whatever `faceDown` the engine offers.
