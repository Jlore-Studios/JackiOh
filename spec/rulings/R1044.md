## R1044 — A Spell may be played face-down as a Trap that reveals at a chosen time

Built (ME-ALTPLAY, Meditative #99). A Spell played this way is a Trap play for every rule that
reads plays and prices, and a Trap on the field until it reveals. Its effects come from a Trap. It
needs no legal target when set ([[R703]] is read as it reveals, where it fizzles); "this turn" in
its text is the turn it reveals in. X and embiggen are chosen when set. It reveals at the end of
this turn, the start of the next turn, or the end of the next turn, then resolves its Spell text with
prompts, Echo repeating with fresh prompts. Spells only; Field Spells are never set.
