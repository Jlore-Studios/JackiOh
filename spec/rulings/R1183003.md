The autopilot ([[R1183002]]) chooses each action with [[§10.7]]'s scorer, a pure function of the state and its player: every candidate is tried on that player's concealed copy of the board ([[R222]]: a card hidden from them is a stand-in with no text), on the dry run's own seed, so the match's rng is never touched, nothing hidden decides it, and the same state always makes the same choice. A try's outcome ranks a won game first, then the scorer's three priorities — lethal on the board after it, the enemy board cleared, a hero below `SCORER_LOW_HEALTH` healed — then the board's balance: each side's hero life and Armor plus its acting Units' Attack and Health, theirs taken from yours, risen most. A try that loses the game is never taken. In order:

1. A prompt of its player's is answered by the best of the first `AUTOPILOT_ANSWERS_TRIED` (8) answers `legal_actions` lists, ties to the earlier.
2. Otherwise a card is played: its player's playable cards in the scorer's order — each card's score as its face states it, the scorer's reading of a play as M #71's judge takes it ([[R1125]]) — each tried at the plays the scorer's dry run narrows it to, those tries being the dry run; the first card with a play that does no harm — a priority met, or no part of the balance lost — is played at its best such play. A card is never ranked by a dry run of its own before its tries, so a step costs a copy of the board per play tried, not per card held.
3. Otherwise an attack is made: the best of every attack `legal_actions` lists, when it meets a priority or raises the balance.
4. Otherwise the turn ends.

It never activates an ability, switches a position, emotes, offers or answers a draw, or concedes. It takes at most `AUTOPILOT_MAX_ACTIONS` (30) actions in a turn, an answer counted as one; past them, or should an action it chose be refused, the rest of the turn is a timeout's ([[R79]]): its player's open prompts answered by the random policy, then the turn ended.
