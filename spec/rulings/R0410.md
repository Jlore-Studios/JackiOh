C+ #14's "When this leaves your hand, add it right back to your hand" is read as "After this resolves, return it to hand": the next Spell its player plays (a player modifier that waits until used, not only this turn) gains an enchantment that rides the card in every zone ([[§10.1]]), so after each resolution it returns to its owner's hand, and it can't cost less than (2) ((1) on the Radiant face), a floor applied after every discount ([[R65]]). A discarded or countered copy does not come back: that would make it undiscardable and uncounterable, and the floor shows the aim was a repeatable Spell, not an indestructible one
