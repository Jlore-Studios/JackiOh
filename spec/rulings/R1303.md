Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7), shaped like [[R660]]'s flavour sidecar. Every card's Chinese lives in `crates/cards/chinese.json`, never in `catalog.json`: an edit to it is no card patch and claims no pending fragment, `catalog check` and `patches check` never read it, and the client reads it from the bundle as it reads the catalog. It is keyed by catalog id, in the catalog's order, and covers every card and token of every set, a set not yet shipped included; every card added later adds its entry in the same change. Each entry is `{ name, base, radiant }`, the name and both faces in Simplified Chinese, one Chinese line for each English line, a face empty where its English is. Each face writes exactly the `{key}`s its English face writes, and an English `{key|one|many}` becomes `{key}` and a measure word ("抽{draw}张牌"), since Chinese nouns do not agree. Names are unique, and a card the text names (`refs`, [[R279]]) appears in it by its Chinese name, an edition's without its "（…）". A card whose script declares `preview` ([[R280]]) also carries `previews`, one entry for each label its view can carry: the English label as a template, written with the face's `{key}`s where it prints a number, and the Chinese label written the same way, which the client fills with the numbers the face is filled with, so a tuned number ([[R386]]) moves in both; filled, each Chinese label is part of its Chinese face.

`crates/cards/chinese-terms.json` holds the frame's words: every card type, tag, rarity and keyword, every label that opens a line, "Radiant" (光辉) and "Created" (生成), and a Chinese label and rule for every glossary entry the client draws. Where Hearthstone's zh-CN client has a word, the tables use it: 战吼 Cry, 亡语 Death, 嘲讽 Taunt, 突袭 Rush, 冲锋 Charge, 圣盾 Divine Shield, 吸血 Lifesteal, 复生 Reborn, 剧毒 Poisonous, 风怒 Windfury, 发现 Discover, 抽到时施放 Cast on draw, 法术伤害 Spell Damage, 回响 Echo and 幸运币 The Coin. The set's own words are 光辉 Radiant, 献祭 Tribute, 牌库 Deck, 强化 Upgrade, 削弱 Degrade, 锁定 Lock, 瘟疫指示物 Plague Counter, 易碎 Brittle, 活化 Animated, 衍生物 Token, 中国 CN, 猫族 Felinor and 人类 Human. A match-made definition ([[R77]]'s Fuse, Craft a Card) has no entry; a fused card is drawn from its ingredients' entries ([[R1301]]).
