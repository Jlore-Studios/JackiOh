No effect reaches a Dormant card ([[§6.1]], [[R1839000]]), and a random pick never sees one ([[R4322000]]), but three picks still spent part of their effect on one, and a Spell's damage aimed at a Unit immune to Spells is dropped ([[§6.1]]):

- S #11.2 Arcane Missiles drew each missile from the match rng ([[R60]]) among the enemy hero and every acting enemy Unit, so a missile could land on a Unit immune to Spells and deal nothing, while C+ #25 Soul Shot, the shape its comment names, draws from `cards_in_scope`, which leaves that Unit out.
- C #85 Unlicensed Experimentation could match a Dormant card of yours, in `when` and in `run`, and fuse the played permanent onto it, which no effect may reach.
- An Embiggened C #83 Transmogulate walked a Dormant board card, drew a random Legendary through the run's unique tally ([[R3563000]]) for it, and then never replaced it, since `transform` passes a Dormant card by.

**Each card now puts its whole effect on what it can reach.** Arcane Missiles draws each missile from `cards_in_scope` with an enemy scope (Soul Shot's exact pool): a Unit immune to Spells is never drawn, and a Dormant card never is. With none left the hero takes every missile. Unlicensed Experimentation matches no Dormant card in `when` or `run`, so a Dormant-only match leaves the trap armed and face-down ([[R99]]): the base face fuses onto the acting match, and the Radiant face fuses onto acting matches only. An Embiggened Transmogulate leaves Dormant board cards out of its walk, as it leaves Immutable cards out ([[R129]]): a Dormant card stays as it is and draws nothing, and every other card is replaced by the draw it would get with the Dormant card absent.

A replay this change plays differently is refused as `rules_changed` rather than shown wrong ([[R767000]]): v0.6.9m, the newest version shipped as this was built, ran the pre-fix scripts of Transmogulate and Unlicensed Experimentation, which are kept at it (`script_v0_6_9l`, the script of v0.6.9l and v0.6.9m), and Arcane Missiles keeps both pools it drew from before: the unfiltered one of v0.6.9j and v0.6.9k (`script_v0_6_9j`, kept at v0.6.9k since #4496) and the slash-aware one of v0.6.9l and v0.6.9m (`script_v0_6_9l`), as #4396 kept its scripts.

Rejected:

- Guarding in the verbs instead of the pools: the draw would still land on the card and fizzle there, spending the missile, the fusion, or the Legendary draw against [[R129]].
- Filtering after the draw instead of drawing from the reachable pool: the same spent draw, and for Transmogulate the tally would still move through [[R3563000]].
