id: R3540003
title: "'Damage dealt Burns' and 'is Toxic' turn a hit into its target's counter after Armor, and it deals nothing"
cards: "§4.4, §6.3, R44, R63; Stinkyscape+ (issue #3534)"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/burn_toxic.rs
---
A card whose text says the damage it deals Burns, or is Toxic (`damageBurns`, `damageIsToxic`), turns each hit it deals into a counter at [[§4.4]] step 3a, after Divine Shield, Armor and the caps: the target gains Burn (Toxic) equal to the amount the hit would have dealt now, added to the count it has ([[R3540000]]), and the hit deals nothing. Nothing of steps 4 to 9 happens for it: no `damage` event, no Poisonous, Lifesteal, Trample, reflection or kill credit, no lethal replacement, and no rider ([[R3540002]]) either. A hit of 0 after step 3 converts nothing ([[R63]]); a Divine Shield took the hit whole at step 1, so none lands; an Indestructible Unit, which takes no damage at step 4, gains none; a source with both conversions Burns only. A converting attacker is still struck back and its Cleave still hits, since both belong to the attack and not to the hit. [[R44]]'s lethal projection and the combat forecast still count a converting hit as damage until S05 teaches them. Rejected: converting before Divine Shield and Armor, which would let a shield or Armor be burned through.
