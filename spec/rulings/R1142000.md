The device keeps every counter ([[R639]]), but what syncs to the account is pruned to what the public player summary reads ([[R654]]): the game totals (`games`, `wins`, `losses`, `draws`) and, for each card, only `played`, `playedAgainst`, `destroyed` and `defeated`. Counters with a value of zero, all-zero card entries and `seen` (which the summary never reads) are left out.

The server's body cap `PLAYER_STATS_BYTES_MAX` is 65,536 bytes, exported to `apps/web/src/wire/serverConfig.ts` so the client shares the bound. When a payload with all catalog entries and four-digit counters is pruned this way, it fits within the cap with room for future card sets. If a client's pruned payload exceeds the cap, `syncPayload` drops the lowest-count cards until the body fits, so the account copy keeps updating rather than failing silently on an oversized PUT.

This amends [[R654]]: the account copy carries the public summary's inputs rather than an exact mirror of every local device counter.
