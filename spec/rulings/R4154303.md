Issue #4154 asked of all three Call to Chaos editions it names: "Nerf each result slightly (that are easy to nerf)". The rule, the same for S+ #142, TG #39 ([[R4154304]]) and M #95 ([[R4154305]]): an entry whose strength is a number loses one step of it, about 15 to 25% and never more than a third, so a count of 3 or more drops by one (3 → 2, a third, is the one step a 3 has) and a 5 of Armor or mana by one; an entry with no number, or whose only number is 2 or less, where one step would halve it or take the clause away, is left as it is, as is a price, which a nerf would raise rather than cut. One number is cut an entry, the one the entry is for.

| Entry | Through v0.6.6e | Now | Declared |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1: Paperclip cards at (0), replenish 2 | 3 cards | 2 | `card` |
| 2: Evolved Swarm cards at (0) | 3 | 2 | `card2` |
| 4: summon or cast Jlockeed cards ([[R3570003]]) | 4 | 3 | `card3` |
| 5: Terminus cards, replenish 4 | 3 cards | 2 | `card4` |
| 7: Toxic and Burn all enemies | Toxic 3, Burn 2 | Toxic 3, Burn 1 | `burn` |

Left as they were: entry 3's two GIGA Units ([[R3570002]], a count of 2), entry 6's Toxopid at (0) (a price, and the Toxopid itself loses its Crawler, [[R4154300]]), the launch watch, the Golems and the recursion, which carry no number to cut. Entry 7 cuts its Burn and keeps its Toxic: Burn now hits once at the end of the turn and goes ([[R4154000]]), so its point is one point of damage on each enemy, a fifth of the counters it puts on and of its first turn's damage, while a point of Toxic is a point every turn, as [[R3996200]] reasoned. The replenishes stay: a card is the larger part of entries 1 and 5. The numbers are declared (catalog data, the same on both faces), so a replay of v0.6.6e or before resolves under its own catalog's and no script is kept ([[R767000]]); the clauses `chaosRolled` names ([[R436]]) are the engine table's, now "Add 2 random Paperclip cards to your hand, they cost (0), replenish 2 mana", "Add 2 random Evolved Swarm cards to your hand, they cost (0)", "Summon or cast 3 random Jlockeed cards", "Add 2 random Terminus cards to your hand, replenish 4 mana" and "Toxic 3 and Burn 1 all enemies".
