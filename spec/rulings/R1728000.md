A Unit standing in a backrow zone ([[R1081000]]: a Flying Unit played there) is a Unit already, as an animated card in a unit zone is. So whatever animates it (the `animate` verb an Animated Trap's text ends with, a fused-in card's included; an Animated Field Spell's entry; an "Animated on your turn" card's start of turn) moves and changes nothing: it stays in its backrow zone in the position it has, keeps its `summonedTurn` and its exertion, enters nothing ([[R83]], [[R171]]) and emits no `animated` ([[R383]], [[R445]]). It answers as a card that is a Unit already does ([[§6.1]] Animated: "one already a Unit when it fires again stays where it is"), so the text after the animation goes on. Hearthstone has no backrow; this follows R1081000's "a Unit for every rule".

Before issue #1728 the engine moved such a Unit into its lane's unit zone in Attack Position as an entry, readying it (fuzz seed 553: M #47 Taotie with C+ #74 Twice Forward One Step Backwards, an Animated Field Trap, fused into it). Keeping that move as an entry was rejected: it takes a Flying Unit out of the zone its controller chose and readies a spent one.
