FvH #24 Book of Starflight summons one Starship of its caster's: the generic Dormant Starship hull, the one a dying piece summons ([[R1852000]]), made the way S+ #155 The Far Vagabond makes its Terminus Starship ([[R3539001]]): it lands in its caster's first open backrow zone, middle first, then their unit row, and holds its pieces oldest first, fused into it as it is summoned. Its pieces are the declared `pieces` ([[R386]]), each drawn as the card resolves from the Starship Piece pool ([[R1878000]]: the non-token cards that print Starship Piece), one draw of the match rng apiece, the draws of one cast differing while `UNIQUE_RANDOM_DRAWS` holds ([[R3563000]]). With no open zone in either row nothing is summoned and nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). The Starship is a Dormant Starship like any other: a piece of its caster's that dies later fuses into it when it is their first, and it launches by hand at its usual price ([[R1852003]]), with no price of its own.

On the Radiant face the pieces are Radiant ("Radiant Starship Pieces"), and so is the hull, since the one summon makes both: the Dormant Starship's two faces are alike ([[R349]]'s fallback), so a Radiant hull plays as a plain one. Hearthstone has no Starship; the designer's "Starpieces" are the Starship Pieces.
