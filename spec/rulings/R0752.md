The designer's patch on #37: Heroic Power costs (0), and playing it uses nothing (it has no Cry). Each of its powers is an "Activate: Spend (X)" ability ([[R384]]): once per turn on your turn, its X paid in mana as it is activated, and the card has only the power it rolled (`ActivationDecl.has`), so `legalActions`, the refusal, the view and a resumed tail all read the one Activate subsystem. A power's target (Ping's) is declared with the activation ([[R81]]), so the client can drag the power onto it. Thirteen powers replace the eight, keeping [[R103]]'s names: Expedition Map (3, `recruit`), Life Tap (1, `draw`, [[R753]]), Steady Shot (1, `burn`, [[R754]]), Ranching (2, `rush`: a Rush Token, a Radiant one on the Radiant face), Cat Cafe (1, `felinor`, [[R755]]), Ping (1, `ping`, [[R756]]), Witness Value (2, `discover`), Stitching (2, [[R352]]), Armor Up (1, `armor`, [[R757]]), Die Insect (2, `insect`, [[R758]]), KY Brainstorm (2, `brainstorm`, [[R759]]), Pluck (2, `pluck`, [[R760]]) and Terminus Tricks (3, `tricks`, [[R761]]); the start-of-game roll picks from the thirteen, each as likely. The card's text says no more of playing it, and in play shows only the power it rolled ([[§10.10]])
