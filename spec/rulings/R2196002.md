The lanes run left to right from the owner's seat ([[§3]]) and no client mirrors them, so the Unit this card is to the right of is the top of the pile one lane lower on its own side and row ([[R13]]: never a card dormant beneath), read as its Cry resolves. It must act there: a Dormant Unit ([[R1839000]]) is not one, and beside a Felinor standing in the backrow only a standing Unit is ([[R1081000]]). An enemy Unit is never beside it. "That Unit" is that one; with none there, or in lane 1, nothing happens and the Felinor is not buffed either. Otherwise both gain the permanent buff, the Felinor first. A summon or a copy of it fires no Cry ([[R1]]). The brief's TG-P3-3.
