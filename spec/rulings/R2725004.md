The issue asked that Radiant stay easy to tell apart: no version or augment uses Radiant's gold on
its frame, edge, text marks or stats, none tints a base face's art gold, and every version keeps a
Radiant face's marks. The Radiant gold is the HSL window `RADIANT_GOLD_*` in `constants.ts` already
holds every color the Radiant face declares ([[R1295000]]). The Signature as #2715 drew it signed in
parchment (`#efe0bf`, inside the window) behind a pale gold pen glow and burst (`--pal-gold-200`):
part 1 recolors the ink and the flourish to `--pal-slate-100`, a cool silver, and the glow and the
burst to white. This is the one change of how an owned look prints, the Mythic signed full art among
them. The Full art band is white, and a card that borrows a motion for Animated borrows none whose
particles are drawn in gold (`GOLD_PARTICLES`: coins, sparks, stars, crumbs, confetti); the 21 keep
their own motions as #2715 drew them.

Under every look, augments on or off, a Radiant face still prints as Radiant: its gold-foil frame
(`data-radiant-face`), R277's gold marks wherever its text shows ([[R277]]), its gold stats and its
art's Radiant paint (`data-art-variant="radiant"`); a base face prints none of them.

Amended by [[R2940001]] (#2940): the rule covers part 2's looks: no Sketch colour is in the window, and a Radiant face keeps every mark under Alt art, the Sketch (its painted picture washed back over the pencil) and Alt full art.

Amended by [[R2942001]] (#2942): the rule covers part 3's looks: no colour of Extended art's, Pixel's or Comic's frame, name or picture is in the window, and a Radiant face keeps every mark under each (a Radiant Pixel its painted colours, a Radiant Comic its painted picture washed back over the pop art).

Amended by [[R3013002]] (#3013): the rule covers the proficiency stamps: no stamp colour is in the window, and a Radiant face keeps every mark under a stamp.
