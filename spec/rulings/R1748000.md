Since issue #1748 a Nerf or a Buff on a card on the field never draws its cost row: a permanent in play has paid its price, and a cost change there would only show on a card that leaves the field, which loses the rest of its price anyway on reaching a graveyard or an exile pile ([[R766]]). In a hand or a deck the cost row is as [[R386]] has it.

Nor does it draw a declared number that only the card's Cry reads: the Cry resolved as the card arrived, so the number moves nothing while the card stays. The catalog marks such a number `cry: true` in `params` ([[§5]]), and `cargo jackioh catalog check` holds the mark to the text: a number every line writing it begins "Cry:" is marked, and a marked number is written only on such lines. C #53 Plague Crawler's Cry `tokens` is out of reach on the field and its lasting `draw` is not. KY's Constant ([[R386]]'s last sentence) is no Nerf or Buff and still reaches either.

A replay folded under a version before this one that Nerfed or Buffed a card's cost or Cry number on the field ends on another hash and is refused ([[R767000]]). This amends [[R386]]'s cost and number clauses, as Hearthstone never reprices a minion in play nor re-runs its Battlecry.
