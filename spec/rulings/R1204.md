[[§4.4]] step 4a opens for a hit on a Unit as well as a hero (ME-LETHALGUARD): the window opens when the hit, judged after steps 0 to 3 for a hero and after steps 0 to 4 for a Unit, alone would leave its target at 0 or less ([[R44]]'s judge) — a Divine Shield takes the hit first and an Indestructible Unit takes nothing, so neither is lethal, and a Unit already killed ([[R42]]) opens no window. Unan's replacement, with `redirect: "self"`, sends a lethal hit on its controller's hero or another of its controller's Units to Unan as a new instance from the same source through its own pipeline (its Armor, shield and caps), with `redirected`, on either player's turn, in combat or by an effect; enemy Units are not guarded. The redirected hit is never caught again by the same Unan (each card once per event, [[R460]]), so a hit lethal to Unan itself kills it, a second guard catches what kills the first, and a cap bounds a chain of Unans; each hit of an "all" effect is judged alone before any lands ([[R59]]), Unan's Armor taking each one it catches. A Trample hit redirected to Unan tramples its excess into its controller's hero, keeping the hit's catchers.
