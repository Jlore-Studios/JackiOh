The owner asked in #2715 for a golden card's tier, after Hearthstone's card qualities: the card's
normal face, its frame and its words, with its portrait moving in its art window. Every full-art
card ([[R2655001]]) gains a fourth per-card cosmetic beside [[R2655000]]'s three:

| Rarity | Id | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Epic | `full-art-<slug>` | Full art, still (unchanged) |
| Epic | `animated-<slug>` | **Animated**: the normal face, its art moving ([[R2715001]]) |
| Legendary | `full-art-<slug>-animated` | Full art, its art moving, drifting and swept |
| Mythic | `full-art-<slug>-signed` | All the Legendary does, and signed |

**The registry.** The Animated art is a `fullArt` entry, Epic, no chase, no default and no starter,
naming its card; its name is the Epic's with "full art" read "animated art" ("Big D-fender animated
art"). #2715 appends the 21 after #2655's 57, in [[R2655001]]'s order, so the registry holds 84 full
arts, four to a card. The registry's check takes a full art whose id starts with `full-art-` or
`animated-`, refuses an `animated-` one that is not Epic, and refuses a card with two full arts of
one rarity among the `full-art-` ones or two `animated-` ones: the still Epic and the Animated art
stand side by side. `public.cosmetics` is seeded from the compiled registry as before.

**The pick.** A card's own pick keeps [[R2655000]]'s shape, `false`, `true` or an id, and the
Animated art is picked by its id. `true` still names the still Epic: the card's Epic is its
`full-art-` Epic, never its Animated art, so every pick written before #2715, and the bots' own
wardrobe, keeps wearing what it wore. Migration 0075 widens 0072's `card_cosmetic_picks_full_art_id_check`
to take `animated-` as well as `full-art-`, a slug of 40 characters at most ([[R633]]), still only
beside a switch that is on; no row is rewritten. `PUT /api/card-picks` and the device's upload take an
`animated-…` id where they took a `full-art-…` one, held to ownership on its card ([[R1834001]]):
another card's, or one the account does not own there, is 400 with nothing written. A match freezes
an owned Animated pick as its id, never as `true` ([[R1834002]]), and the opponent's, a watcher's and
a replay's views wear it from the owner's frozen loadout ([[R1834004]]), behind the viewer's own
Full-art cards setting as every tier of another's copy is. An old client or server refuses an
`animated-` pick, which then stays on the device ([[R1834000]]).

**How it is earned.** As the Epic full art is: it is no chase, so it drops wherever an Epic per-card
cosmetic drops on its card, a duplicate of that card whose roll lands on Epic ([[R1301002]]) and a
cosmetic pack slot that lands on Epic ([[R1306000]]); an Epic cosmetic token buys it on a card the
account owns, a Rare one does not ([[R1305003]]); the Chase Shard bar, which buys chases alone, does
not ([[R1306002]]). No account is granted it, existing ones included ([[§14.12]]), and
`POST /api/dev/fill` grants it with the rest.

**What is worn.** The Animated art prints the card's normal face: its frame, type line, rules box and
tags, no full art, so every check of whether a copy prints full art (the inspect views, the deck
builder's pool) says no for it; its art moves in the face's own art window ([[R2715001]]). It shows on
a full layout, the hand, the inspect views, the Collection, the deck builder and the Almanac, and
never on a compact face or a board minion, which [[R2655000]]'s tiers never reach either. A token
listed under its card wears it with its parent ([[R2655002]]). The gates of [[R2655000]] hold: the
pick's tier owned on the card, the Full-art cards setting for a card with no pick and for another's
copy, and a tutorial lesson's board ([[R1108000]]) turning it off.

**Where it is picked.** The Collection's card view offers No full art, then the Animated art, the
full art, the animated full art and the signed full art, by the registry's names, the unowned ones
locked ([[R1655000]]); `EquipButton` and the pack reveal's equip ([[R1955000]]) pick it by its id;
and the token offer on a locked Animated art offers an Epic token ([[R1953001]]).

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads it, and nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an
action, the log or a replay hash.

Hearthstone semantics: a golden card keeps its normal frame and words, and its portrait moves inside
it; a signature card is its own full art, moving and signed.

Rejected: a new cosmetic kind beside `fullArt`, which would need a second pick a card could hold at
once with its full art, where a card wears one of the four or none; a `full-art-<slug>-golden` id, a
full-art id on a face that prints no full art, which every reader of the prefix would take for one;
and moving minions, a board's worth of moving art the motion rationing ([[R2715001]]) would starve.
