A fused trap can carry several triggers that answer one event, and its firing runs them in turn ([[R425]]). When an earlier trigger takes the card itself off the field, by an attack that kills it or by an exile, the later triggers still run: the firing had begun. Twice Forward's turns its card face-up and starts its printed Brittle ([[R33]], [[R687]]), and a card that has left the field has no face to turn up: [[R78]] reset it as it left, so that trigger writes nothing to it there. Its Brittle gain, which names a card wherever it is, lands as before.
