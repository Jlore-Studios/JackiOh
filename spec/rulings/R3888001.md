[[R1748000]]'s `cry: true` marked a number every line writing it begins "Cry:", and [[R2261001]]'s `cry: "base"` or `"radiant"` one only that face's Cry reads. Since issue #3888 the mark says when the number is read, in general: `read` in `params` ([[§5]]) is `"cry"`, `"combo"` (a "Combo:" or "Combo N:" line), `"castOnDraw"`, `"awaken"` (a "When awoken:" line, or a sentence "When this awakens, …" inside one, S #19 Imprisoned Cosmality's), `"evolve"` (an "Evolve:" line or an "Evolves when" condition), `"handOrDeck"` ("While in your hand or deck:", "In your hand or deck:", "While this is in your hand:", "While in your deck:") or `"graveyard"` ("While this is in your graveyard:"). `readOn: "base"` or `"radiant"` names the one face whose such lines alone read it while the other face's lasting text reads it too, as `cry: "base"` did; on the other face the number counts as lasting text. [[R3888000]]'s table says where a Buff or a Nerf of each is live.

`cargo jackioh catalog check` holds the mark to the text face by face: a face's reading is the mark every line writing the number has, or none; the same mark on every face that writes it wants `read`, one face's mark beside the other face's lasting lines wants `read` with `readOn` naming that face, two faces with different marks fail ("the faces read … at different times"), and anything else wants no mark; a bad value, and `readOn` without `read`, fail. Every catalog card is marked so: the 89 `cry: true` numbers are `read: "cry"` and M #101 Gachaholic's `lucky` is `read: "cry"` with `readOn: "base"`, and the 38 new marks are 23 Cast on draw numbers on 15 cards (#21 Hinder's `mana` and `discards` among them, and C+ #26 Tommy Tempo's `actions`, the one on a Unit), three Combo numbers (#10 Rapid Replenish's `combo` and `draw`, S+ #102 Cosmic Bard's `summons`), nine Evolve numbers on seven cards (C+ #49 Jay Fungus's `zeroes`, read by its "Evolves when", among them), S #19 Imprisoned Cosmality's `damage` (`awaken`), C+ #37 Wardrum's `threshold` (`handOrDeck`) and C #47 Recurring Felinor's `returnCost` (`graveyard`). No card's faces read a number at different times. The Patch notes diff shows the mark ("read only by its Cry", "… as it is cast on draw", "… in a graveyard", " on the base face"), and still shows a shipped catalog's `cry` as it did.

KY's Constant is no Nerf or Buff and still reaches a marked number ([[R1748000]]). A catalog shipped before the change keeps its `cry`, which the engine no longer reads; a replay folded under it that Buffed or Nerfed a marked number ends on another hash and is refused ([[R767000]]).
