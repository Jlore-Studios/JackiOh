The **Field Guide** lists every mechanic the lessons set up, in lesson order. A met entry shows its
name and its rule. One not yet met shows "?" and the lesson that sets it up.

**Where it lives.** A button opens it from the lesson HUD and from the lesson path's header ("Field
Guide k/n"). It is a modal dialog: it takes focus, closes on Escape or Close, and gives focus back to
the button that opened it. A lesson's chips on the path are the entries it sets up, each marked found
once met.

**Storage.** The entries found are kept in the device's tutorial record ([[R294]]) as an optional
`found` list beside `completed`, in the guide's order. Unknown ids are dropped, and a record without
the list reads as none, so the record's version does not change. They are never sent to the account:
[[R320]] holds at most 32 lesson ids, the guide has more entries than that, and `completed` means
lessons won to every reader. [[R321]]'s merge keeps the device's list.

**What counts as met.**

- The guide shows the entries found on this device.
- It also shows every entry of a lesson won on any device. A win names each entry its game did not
  show found ([[R2205000]], [[R314]]) and adds it to the device's list, so a won lesson's entries
  are ones the player has met.
- So a player who signs in on a new device sees their won lessons' entries met, and the server
  needs no change.

Hearthstone semantics: a collection the player fills by playing, kept with the player's progress.

Rejected: putting the entries in the account's lesson list, which would break [[R320]]'s bound and
the meaning of `completed`; and a new server column, which this issue rules out.
