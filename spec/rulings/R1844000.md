- Decides: the autopilot as an aura (issue #1844, Stinkyscape's M10)
- Spec: §6.3 Autopilot aura, §10.1, §10.7

"Aura: the first 3 actions of your turn are played by an AI" (#46 Circadian Halfcycler) and "Aura: your turn is played by an AI" (#47 Circadian Fullcycler) are [[R1183002]]'s autopilot as an aura. While a card with `pilotsActions: N` or `pilotsTurn` acts on a player's field ([[§3.2]]: a Unit on top of its pile, a face-up backrow card), each of their turns engages the autopilot as it begins, right after `turnStarted` (announced to both players as `autopilotEngaged`), so a prompt the start of the turn opens is the autopilot's to answer too; before the main phase it takes no other action. Its status counts the actions the aura's count takes (`autopilot.auraActions`), and before every step the engine reads the aura again: a whole turn beats any count, among counts the largest holds and they never add up, and with no aura left the turn is the player's.

A count takes [[R456]]'s actions, which of the autopilot's are a play and an attack, and never an answer, which belongs to the action that asked. Once the count is spent, the autopilot answers the prompts its last counted action opened; when nothing is open, whoever's answer closed the last of them, it hands the turn back for good and its status goes, so a prompt the player's own action opens later is theirs. Like [[R1183003]]'s autopilot it ends the turn when it has no harmless play and no worthwhile attack, counted or not, since ending the turn uses the rest up by ending it ([[R456]]). A whole-turn aura plays as Cognitive Offloading does, `AUTOPILOT_MAX_ACTIONS` and the timeout ([[R79]]) included, and a turn left with nothing to do ends as [[R82]] ends it.

A card that arrives in the middle of a turn waits for its controller's next one. Cognitive Offloading cast while an aura's count holds the turn (the autopilot playing it, or a draw casting it) takes the rest of that turn whole and is announced again, which revises [[R1183002]]'s "once a turn" for that case; cast after the count handed the turn back, it engages the autopilot as [[R1183002]] says. The count is the printed one, never a declared number a Nerf or a Buff moves ([[R386]]). A search's step (`reduce_unpiloted`) leaves an aura's turn owed, as it leaves Cognitive Offloading's. A game in which no aura acts carries no field for it, so no recorded game moves.

Rejected: [[R1183003]]'s count of every action, answers included, which would spend a count on the pick of a Discover the counted play opened; engaging only as the main phase begins, which would leave the start of the turn's prompts to the player; handing the turn back where the autopilot would end it, which reads "the first 3 actions" as "up to 3 plays" and is not what [[R456]] says of ending a turn.
