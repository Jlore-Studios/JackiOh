The card creator ([[R915010]]) keeps its draft only in the page. The draft holds unsaved work when it differs from its saved card, or, for a card never saved, from a new card nobody has touched: the test opening another card already applies. While it does, the page's Back shows the note opening another card shows, whose "Discard them" leaves and "Keep editing" stays, and a `beforeunload` listener makes the browser ask before a reload or a closed tab, as a lesson in progress does ([[R668]]). A saved card, or a new one nobody has touched, is left at once, and the listener is gone once the work is saved or the page is left.
