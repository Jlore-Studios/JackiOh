**Why.** The card statistics' first read after a server wakes builds the kept snapshot ([[R3067000]]), and on staging it took about a minute on 2026-11-27, near the client's `API_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (75). The owner has seen it run past five minutes. Meanwhile `/stats` showed only a skeleton, and after the client's timeout "The card statistics didn't load." Worse, the load ran inside the read's own future. A read that gave up (the client's timeout, a proxy closing the connection) dropped the half-done load, and the next read began it again from nothing, so a load longer than the client waits could never finish while visitors kept timing out.

**The load.** The snapshot loads on a task of its own (`tokio::spawn`). Every read that misses the kept snapshot waits on that one load, as before ([[R3067000]]), but a read that gives up leaves it running, and the next read joins it or finds the snapshot it made. A load counts three steps: the records and game totals, then the card totals as they are filed by those records ([[R4965000]], [[R5174000]]), then the snapshot. The server keeps the step the load is on, when it began, and how long the last load that finished took.

**The route.** `GET /api/stats/loading` answers `loading`, `step` (from 1, or 0 when no load is under way), `steps`, `elapsedMs` (0 when none is) and `expectedMs`, how long the last load took (`null` before one has finished since the server started). It never waits on a load, and no cache keeps it (`Cache-Control: no-store`). `/api/stats/cards` answers as it did.

**The page.** While the Cards tab's read waits, `/stats` asks the route after `STATS_LOADING_AFTER_MS` and every `STATS_LOADING_POLL_MS` after, and draws, over its skeleton, a bar and a line: the step and what it is doing, the time so far, and the time to expect when it is known. The bar fills by the share of the time to expect gone by, or the share of the steps done, whichever is more, and never past `STATS_LOADING_SHARE_MAX` before the load ends. A read that finds no answer in time while the route still reports a load asks again rather than saying the statistics didn't load. A read that fails otherwise, or when no load is under way, says so as before.

**The deploy check.** `.github/actions/check-stats` follows the load as the page does: between its two attempts, while the route reports a load, it waits, for up to `LOAD_WAIT_SECONDS`, so its second attempt finds the statistics the load made. On 2026-11-27 both attempts timed out at 75 s on deploy-staging run 46074518310, the second begun again from nothing.

Amended by [[R5219000]] (#5219): the bar fills on the page's own clock on a curve that slows and never stops, from the page's first wait, never draws less than it drew, and says the load is almost done once the time to expect has passed.
