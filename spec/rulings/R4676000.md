**Why.** The owner balances a card by the choices it offers: which Choose One option, which X, Embiggened or not, is over- or under-performing. [[R4672003]] totals the seats that played each card with each variant, by face; this note says how a page reads them.

**The rows** (`variant_rows`). A variant's label ([[R4672003]]: its options, then `X=<x>`, then Embiggened or Not Embiggened) is read from its end into its parts, and each part is one row of its kind (`VariantKind`):

- `mode`: the options chosen with the play, as recorded, one row per set of options, so a "choose two" is one row per pair. Its key and label are the options' own words.
- `x`: one row per X paid, from `VARIANT_X_GROUP_FROM` (5) up one row, `X ≥ 5` (key `top`): few plays reach a high X, and each alone would read "not enough games".
- `embiggen`: Embiggened, or not.

A row adds every label that holds its part, so `deal, X=2` counts in `deal` and in `X = 2`. A seat that played two variants sharing a part in one game counts twice in that row, as [[R4672003]] already counts it once in each variant; no total says more.

**A row's figures** (`variant_figures`), read as [[R4673000]] reads a face:

- **games**: the row's seats on the face asked: both faces on All, the normal ones on Normal, the Radiant ones on Radiant ([[R4673001]]'s `face`). A row with none on that face is not sent, and a kind with no row left is not sent.
- **share**: its games over the card's played games on the face (on All its plays, on a face its `playedNormal` or `playedRadiant`): how often players choose it. A seat with two variants of one kind counts in both, so a kind's shares can sum past 1.
- **adjusted**: `50% + τ²/(τ² + SE²) · (delta − center)` with [[R2895000]]'s 95% range, the delta the row's win rate less its card's baseline: its never-drawn games ([[R2427000]]), or a Created card no deck held its copies made and never used ([[R2089000]]). With fewer baseline games than the floor it has no rate.
- **the field** (`variant_field`): `stats_field`'s random-effects estimate ([[R3022000]]) over every row of every card of the report the catalog holds on the same face, whatever the page filters, from the rows with `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games on both sides. Its centre is what a typical card's play with a choice is worth over the card never drawn, so the rows of one card sit on one scale, and a row at 50% plays as a typical choice does. Below `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` such rows it is `StatsField::PRIOR`'s.
- **tier**: in the face's band (`StatsFace::band`); none, and "not enough games", below `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games of its own ([[R654]]).

**The API.** `GET /api/stats/cards/:id` answers `variants`: one group per kind the card has, in `mode`, `x`, `embiggen` order, each `{ kind, rows }`, its rows by games, most chosen first, each `key`, `label`, `games`, `share`, `adjustedWinRate`, `adjustedLow`, `adjustedHigh`, `tier` and `hasEnoughGames`. It reads the page's `patch=` (the current patch when missing) over the window that patch's table reads, as `faces` does, so a patch past the caller's is 403 `patch_dev_only` before anything is read ([[R4677000]]), and the `face=` asked. The windowed report carries each card's choices over its family's window (`WindowedReport::choices`) for it. A card that offers no choice answers an empty list; an older client ignores the field.

**Rejected.**

- **The card's own played games as the baseline.** The rows would read only against each other, and a card whose every option is strong would read every option at 50%.
- **The card's adjusted rate as the field's centre.** One card's choices are too few to read a spread from, and their games overlap the card's.
- **[[R3022000]]'s card field.** It is centred on a card merely drawn; a choice is a play, which reads higher for every card, as [[R4673000]] found for a face.
- **Parsing the label on the client.** A rule the client would restate (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- **A share over the seats that played any variant.** A card's plays are the count a reader knows, and a play with no recorded choice (a Created copy used by the other seat, [[R4672000]]) is still a play.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
