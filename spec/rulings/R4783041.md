Issue #4783 asked a third time to make the AI better at using Meditative #40 Feng Shui. [[R3951100]] prices the verdict the seat's last element gives its next play and tries rewarded plays first, and [[R4154403]] takes its own Feng Shui's free Auspicious Rock (M #39.1, index 39.1: 水) before every play. Two things it still read wrong both come from that Rock, which the Activate adds once every turn ([[R384]]). This is a judgement about the AI, not a rule of the game, and changes no game.

**Next turn's Rock.** The outlook read the hand alone, so a hand the last element punished counted the hit though the seat's own Feng Shui would hand it a Rock to play first: ending a turn on 金 while holding a 木 card read as a 10-point punishment, where the Rock is rewarded after 金 (金 generates 水, [[R981]]) and leaves 水, which generates 木. While a Feng Shui of the seat's own acts, the outlook now counts the Rock its next turn's Activate adds (`evaluate::coming_rock`, `AI_FENG_SHUI_ROCK`, Radiant when one of its Feng Shuis runs its Radiant face) among the cards its next play may be, by the same verdict and prices as a hand card. So a hand the element punishes costs nothing while the Rock is not punished, and the Rock's own reward counts; the best still counts alone. The Rock a Feng Shui of the opponent's adds is no card of the seat's, and a Feng Shui leaving the field first is not modelled, as [[R3951100]] has it.

**The bridge.** Tier 2's move order took rewarded plays first, punished ones last and every other play by cost between, so the Rock, a (0) Spell, came last among the plain plays, and on a busy turn a level's `branching` cut could drop the one plain play that turns another card's play into a reward. While a Feng Shui of the seat's own acts, a play no Feng Shui judges now whose element generates the element of another card in the seat's hand that a Feng Shui would judge is now a bridge (`candidates::bridges`, `Auspice::Bridge`), tried after the rewarded plays and before the other plain ones: the Rock before a 木 card, a 金 card before the Rock. An own Feng Shui rewards its controller on either face ([[R984]]); a card whose play leaves no record ([[R982]]) bridges nothing, and under an opponent's Feng Shui alone nothing changes.

Both read only the seat's own hand and board and the public records. Rejected: counting the Rock and then the best card its 水 rewards as one outlook, which would price a two-play chain as a turn's first play and teach the AI to hold the card a plain play could take now; and taking the Rock's verdict into the move order of a hand that has no Rock yet, which the Activate already tries first.
