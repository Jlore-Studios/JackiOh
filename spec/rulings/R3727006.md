[[R36]] lets the active player offer a draw once a turn, and [[R269]]'s offer lapses as its turn ends. In a live match the other player is there to answer it; in a daily game they are not, and an offer that lapsed at End turn would never reach them, while the issue (#3727) wants a draw offer to stand until the other player's next move. So while the active player's offer stands it holds the move for the other player, with a fresh window ([[R3727001]]), who answers it (accept or decline) or loses on time; meanwhile its offerer may only concede, so it never lapses unanswered. A decline hands the move back to the offerer with a fresh window and their turn goes on; an accept ends the game in a draw. No engine change: the engine's `standing_draw_offer` is what the clock reads, and the host refuses the offerer's other actions (`daily_allows`).
