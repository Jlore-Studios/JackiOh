TG #31 Level Up! keeps each of its controller's Units' attacks in its own `memory` ([[R2199007]]), which no view carried, so no screen showed a Unit's progress toward its Radiant and the tutorial could not let a player discover it. A card may now declare `tallies`, a pure read like `preview` ([[R280]]): the counts it keeps on Units of the field, each `{ instanceId, value, goal }`. `viewFor` asks it of every card acting on the field that the viewer may read where it lies, and each Unit's view carries the counts naming it as `tallies`, `{ defId, value, goal }` with the keeping card's catalog id, in the keeping cards' trigger order ([[§10.8]]); absent when none counts it, never `[]`. A face-up Field Spell's count is public, as its plays and the attacks it counts are; a face-down card's reaches its controller alone ([[R33]]). Level Up!'s hook names a Unit of its controller's on the field whose count still holds: not one that left the field since its count began ([[R174]]), and none once Level Up! itself has left ([[R78]]); `goal` is its "attacks", 3 on the base face and 2 on the Radiant. A count that reached its goal stays shown. The client draws each count on the Unit and spells it out in the inspect overlay, and works none of it out (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
