[[R766]] makes a card that reaches a graveyard or an exile pile its printed card again, its price included, and [[R3569001]] lets a card scope reach those piles: Tears of the Kwzki Tree Buffs every card of both players there at the end of every turn. The release's fuzz wave found the two crossing: an Upgrade's cost step (B3.4 rule 3, [[R386]]) left a discount on a card in a graveyard. So an Upgrade or a Degrade of a card in a graveyard or an exile pile never takes the cost step, as one of a card on the field never does ([[R1748000]]): it picks among the card's other changes (its stats, keywords and numbers, which a pile card keeps as its `tuning`), and a card with none is left alone, as R3569001 leaves one no Buff can change. Rejected: letting the price stand in the pile, which would break R766's printed card, and clearing it again when the card leaves, which would make the step a change that does nothing.
