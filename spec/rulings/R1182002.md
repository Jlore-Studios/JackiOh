Meditative+ #26 Scratch Pad ("Code a card from scratch and add it to your hand. Exile this.") runs Meditative #17's chain ([[R880]]): a `number` prompt for the cost, 0 to 4, then the `craft` prompt at that cost's budget, `2 + 5 ×` the cost points and 24 lines, whose answer is any valid recipe of [[R883]]'s blocks, compiled into the very card Meditative #17 would make of it ([[R882]]: the same digest, the Meditative set, Mythic, no tags) and added to the hand, Radiant on the Radiant face, or burnt by a full one. "From scratch" is the canvas, not a smaller budget: the craft prompt carries `craftMode: "scratch"`, which the client reads to open the block editor on an empty canvas, a 0/1 Unit with no keyword or hat, and to show no suggestions, no prices on the blocks, no tooltips, no glossary on the card's face and no reasons for a refusal. What stays is the frame of the work: the blocks by name, the points and lines meters (the budget is the rule, not a hint), the face of the card being built, and a Craft button the engine's own verdict turns off while the recipe is refused (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

The engine still rolls Meditative #17's four presets as the prompt's options ([[R881]]), so the AI, the random policy and a timeout answer with one, and the clock runs as it does for #17 ([[R79]]); the presets travel only to the prompt's holder, whose client does not show them, and the opponent sees only that a prompt is open. A potion recipe is refused there ([[R1182000]]). After the card is minted, Scratch Pad goes to its owner's exile, not the graveyard ([[R178]]).
