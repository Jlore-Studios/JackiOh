Meditative #2 Rampaging Rhino discards a card whenever it takes damage. Each `damage` event aimed at it is one discard, and each hit of a sweep counts on its own ([[R59]]). A hit that Armor, a cap or a Divine Shield takes whole emits no `damage` and discards nothing ([[R63]]).

The discard is one random card ([[R682]]) from the hand of whoever controlled the Rhino as the hit landed, so a Rhino another player has taken (Core #49's Steal; Meditative #6, once it ships) discards from its new controller's hand ([[R212]]). An empty hand discards nothing and draws no random number ([[R129]]).

A hit that kills the Rhino discards nothing: the state check removes it before the trigger it queued pops, and a trigger whose source has left play ends with that stay ([[R153]], [[R174]]). Hearthstone's Acolyte of Pain still draws in this case; the Rhino does not, since reading it so would need the trigger to outlive its source.

On the opponent's turn the discard is forced, so Meditative #1 Disruptive Disruptor stops it (MD-A1 in `docs/meditative-set.md`).
