[[§2.1]]'s "any Quickdraw card in a deck replaces one of these draws" is read literally ([[R225]]): a card cannot replace a draw that does not exist, so a seat is dealt at most as many Quickdraw cards as its opening hand holds ([[§2.1]]'s table entry plus its handicap's extra cards, [[R182]]), the first ones in the shuffle's order, and the others stay in the library as ordinary cards, drawn later like any other card. So the opening hand is always exactly that size, whatever the deck holds, and the other seat can never count a seat's Quickdraw cards from it ([[§9.1]], [[R225]]), and a deal never overruns the hand cap ([[R4]]). A deck holds one copy of a card (L3), so five Quickdraw cards is the most it can hold (#65, #84, #98, C #90, C+ #37): the first seat's three draws take three of them, the second seat's four take four, and all five are dealt only to a seat with five draws, a Medium or Hard AI going second. The source's "add this to your starting hand" and BUILD M1-T5's "puts both in the opening hand" were silent on more Quickdraw cards than draws; the other reading, every Quickdraw card entering the hand with the random draws floored at 0, would have given a deck of five a hand larger than the table's.
