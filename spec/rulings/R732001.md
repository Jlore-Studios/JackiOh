The AI training lanes' gate (`cargo jackioh promote`, `training/README.md`) plays 100 games against the parent, the AI on `main`, and none against [[§10.7]]'s random policy. The `improve` lane promotes on 65 wins of 100 (`TRAINING_IMPROVE.vs_parent`, three standard deviations above an equal successor's 50), the `unban` lane on 60 (`TRAINING_UNBAN.vs_parent`, two deviations) together with strictly fewer shadow bans than the parent ([[R186]]). A draw is not a win, nor is a game without a result. A promotion's record (`crates/ai/generation.json` and `training/history/<lane>.jsonl`) says `"vsRandom": null`, and `promote --verify` holds that field like the rest.

Until generation 9 the gate also played 100 games against random and needed 90 of them, and its bars against the parent were 75 and 65. The owner dropped the random games once an AI with the Meditative set in its decks had beaten random in at least 90 of 100 for the first time: generation 9 did, on the sweep of record that lifted [[R1390]]'s gate ([[R732000]]). Beating random is still watched, by the daily quality gates (`cargo jackioh gate --full`, the random matchup at its floor), so an AI that forgets it fails the daily run, not a lane's promotion.

Since [[R850000]] the `improve` lane promotes on 220 of 400 paired games: 200 deals, each played twice with the seats swapped. The `unban` lane's gate is as above.
