---
id: R657
title: An Animated card with no printed stats fights as a 0/1
cards: "§6.1; R383"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/animated.rs
---
If a card with the Animated keyword, printed or granted, has no attack or health from any source (no printed stats, no statsOverride, no X stats), its layers read `ANIMATED_FALLBACK_ATTACK` (0) attack and `ANIMATED_FALLBACK_HEALTH` (1) health on either face, before buffs and auras, so it survives the state check as a 0/1 instead of dying as a 0/0
