S+ #41 Mend Projector since Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes V: "End of turn: Heal all Units to full and give them +{health} Health." (Radiant: "… +{health} Health and Divine Shield."). "Them" are the Units the heal reached, every Unit on both sides, its controller's first ([[R3556003]]), enemies too as the Radiant face's Divine Shield already was. Each Unit is healed to full and then given +`health` Health, the declared number (1, Radiant 2, [[R386]]): a permanent Buff of its max Health at [[§10.4]] layer 4, its current Health rising with it, as Hearthstone's "give +N Health" is, so each stands at full a point (or two) higher; the Radiant face's Divine Shield follows, Unit by Unit. It happens at each end of its controller's turn ([[R62]]), so the Health adds up turn after turn while the Projector stands, and a Unit that leaves the field loses it ([[R78]]'s reset). Rejected: healing after the Buff, which ends the same; and raising the max Health alone, leaving a healed Unit a point below full, which "give them +1 Health" does not read as. Through v0.6.8b it gave no Health, which `script_v0_6_8b` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]).
