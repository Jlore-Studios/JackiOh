A Figment of Echo-1's revealing is an ordinary trigger that runs after the combat ([[§10.3]]): this answers its `trapFired` — one of its controller's, the Figment's, no refire (`again`, [[R2657001]]) — and the firing names the attack it answered (`attackerId`, [[R4586007]]). The hit lands only on an attacker still on the field ([[R57]]); a firing with no attacker to hit, or an enemy Figment's, answers nothing.
