When a draw of yours takes a card from the opponent's deck (C #58 Common Resources, [[§6.3]] Steal's cards between piles) and that card has Cast on draw, it is cast as yours ([[R58]]) and the draw it owes is drawn from the top of your own deck, as Hearthstone's "Casts When Drawn: draw a card" replaces the card with an ordinary draw of yours: the draw that took the cast card was yours, and only the effect that named the opponent's deck reached into it
