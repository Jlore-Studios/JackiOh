A permanent with Stack base ([[§3.2]]) allows its controller to play any Unit into its zone as if the played Unit had Stack ([[R688]]), placing the Unit on top and putting the Stack base dormant beneath ([[R13]]). A summon or move never stacks onto a Stack base. A locked or reserved zone is not offered for stacking. The opponent cannot play onto your Stack base. When the top Unit leaves the zone, the underlying Stack base resumes control of the zone. On the Radiant face of Empty Plot, a Unit played onto it is Buffed once (one Upgrade, [[R386]]) as it is placed ([[§10.5]] step 4), before its Cry resolves.
