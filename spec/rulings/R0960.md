Meditative #39.1 Auspicious Rock makes one weighted roll (Dud 20, Jade 70, Red Jade 10) and adds
the result to its player's hand, uncast. The roll is `add_rolled_grapes`' table-and-order roll made
general (ME-WEIGHTED-ROLL): one draw of the match rng over the table `AUSPICIOUS_ROCK_ODDS` in
engine config — a distribution, so config and not params — walked in the table's order, which runs
from worst to best (Dud < Jade < Red Jade). Its printed Lucky rolls again that many times and keeps
the best: the base face rolls once, the Radiant face's Lucky 2 rolls three times and keeps the
latest table entry of the three. A player's Luck (ME-LUCK, M #40) joins in MB11, not here. "When
played" on a Spell is its resolution, the token rolled lands on its base face, and a roll the full
hand burns is lost ([[§2.4]]).
