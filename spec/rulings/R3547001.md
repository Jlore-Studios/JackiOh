S+ #44 Blast Compound's "lock the tile it is on" is [[§6.3]] Lock on the zone the chosen Unit stands
in as the Spell resolves, in the text's order after it has given the Unit its attack and Cleave: its
unit zone, or its backrow zone when it is a Flexible Unit standing there ([[R3543000]]). Only that one
zone is Locked, on the Unit's own side. The Unit is unaffected and keeps its attack and Cleave, and
the Lock stays when it leaves ([[§3.2]]). A zone already Locked stays so. Rejected: locking the zone
across the board from it, and locking every zone of its row.
