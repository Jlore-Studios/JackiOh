The designer: "When awoken gain one of these randomly (names with a tag gain preference and can only appear if the Starship would have that tag)": The Ninth Life (Felinor), Frontier's Promise (Human), The Celestial Archive (KY), The Lantern Ferry (CN), a Jlockeed name, and Ember of Departure and The Silent Choir for any ship. The Jlockeed name is the build's: The Jlockeed Skylark. The list is `STARSHIP_NAMES` (`config.rs`).

The draw is one pick from the match rng ([[§10.7]]) as the Starship launches ([[R1852003]]). Its pool is the tagged names whose tag the Starship holds (its definition's tags, the union of its pieces', and any granted to it) when there is at least one, which is the preference; otherwise the names that suit any ship. So a Starship holding a Human piece launches as Frontier's Promise, one holding a Felinor and a Human piece as one of those two, and one with no tagged piece as Ember of Departure or The Silent Choir. The names are names only: the Starship prints the name it took (`UnitView.starship.name`, which the client prints as its name), and no rule reads it.
