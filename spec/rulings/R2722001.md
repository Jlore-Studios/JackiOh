Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. After Hearthstone's Deathwing, a Mythic the viewer can read takes over the whole board as it enters, at the moment its entrance plays: its face-up play of a Unit or a Spell (a Unit's entry is its play with its `summoned`), a Unit an effect summons face-up without a play, a hidden card's reveal ([[R2722002]]). The animation queue, and never the effects layer, gives that entry a hold of `TAKEOVER_HOLD_MS` (1 s) ahead of its motion (`entranceEntries`), so the board can darken and a card-shaped silhouette in the card's colors sweep across it into the card's zone before the card lands, about when the intro's riser reaches its downbeat ([[R2722000]]); there light rays turn, two shockwaves cross both sides from the middle of the screen, the board shakes as hard as it does (`FX_TAKEOVER_TRAUMA`) and every other card on the board recoils. The silhouette is a shape, never a face ([[R202]]). The effects layer draws all of it inside the held entry and is gone within `FX_TAKEOVER_TAIL_MS` (0.7 s) of its end, inside `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS`, moving only by transform and opacity.

This amends [[R200]]: one readable Mythic entrance per action may hold the game, for one named and capped beat that the runner owns as it owns every duration. It is pinned against the burst budget, as a cast is (the beat is the action's, and squeezing it would cut the takeover short), and scaled by the effects speed ([[R201]]); the board's `data-animating` stays on through it, so `cy.settled()` and practice's pacing wait exactly as long and no longer. A second Mythic in the same action takes no hold, Reduce Motion (the panel's or the system's) and the effects off take none, and a card makes its entrance once (the queue remembers it until a reset). Rejected: fitting the takeover into a play's 400 ms and the 900 ms tail, which leaves no time for the board to darken and the card to sweep in before it lands, and holding from the effects layer, which [[R200]] forbids.

The marquee Mythics take a look of their own, as [[R670]]'s marquee entrances do (`TAKEOVER_LOOKS`): #100 Ceaseless Void collapses into the dark, #96 My Pawn in a pawn's ivory and red, #97 Zephrys blows in on a golden wind from the other side, Meditative #49 YileGPT in a terminal's green; every other Mythic takes the shared prismatic one. This amends [[R670]]: a Legendary or Mythic Spell, and a hidden card at its reveal, takes the rarity's rays too, with a gold or prismatic burst and ring where it lands; a Legendary's entrance lands harder (`FX_LEGENDARY_TRAUMA` 0.7, from 0.5) and throws a ring of its light, and takes no takeover. A slam's landing ([[R701]]) waits for the hold as for its anticipation.

Under Reduce Motion there is no hold, no shake, no flash and no sweep: the viewer's own Mythic is a still card centred over the board for `TAKEOVER_STILL_MS` (1.6 s), which takes no pointer event and carries no `data-animating`, so it holds nothing (the other seat's is up in the showcase already). The music still plays ([[R1350]]).
