`effects::player_mods::give_units_stats { player, attack, health, tags, expiry }` gives the player a `unitsStats` modifier: every Unit they control on the field whose tags (`query::tags_of`, granted tags included) hold one of `tags`, or every Unit when `tags` is absent, has `attack` more Attack and `health` more max Health, read in layer 5 beside the Attack lend ([[R4586006]], [[§10.4]]), so a Unit arriving later, or gaining the tag later, has it as it lands. It is gated as the keyword lend ([[R1859007]]): no Vanilla, Dormant or buried Unit takes it, nor, when a Spell made it, one Immune to Spells ([[R4508000]]). It holds from the moment it is made until its `expiry` (FvH #73 Bloodforged Crusader's is `never`, outlasting the card), and losing it lowers max Health, which may kill a damaged Unit at the state check. Several add up. A lend of 0/0 makes no modifier.
