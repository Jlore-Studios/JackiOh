Issue #4154 asked again to make the AI better at using Meditative #40 Feng Shui. [[R3951100]] already prices the verdict its next play gets and tries rewarded plays first; its Activate, a free Auspicious Rock (M #39.1) once a turn ([[R384]]), stood among the plain sources at cost 0, the last of them, so on a turn with many plays a level's `branching` cut could drop it and the Rock's element was in no line before the plays that could chain on it ([[R981]]). This is a judgement about the AI, not a rule of the game, and changes no game.

While a Feng Shui acts, the Activate of one of the AI's own (a card whose running face holds the `feng_shui` flag) comes before every play in tier 2 of the move order, rewarded plays included (`Auspice::Judge`). The plays keep [[R3951100]]'s order after it, and with no Feng Shui of the AI's own acting nothing changes.
