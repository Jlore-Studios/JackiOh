The AI counts an unconditional price hike on a player's coming turn in `coming_refreshes` as harm to that player's next turn. It reads each `PlayerModifier` of kind `CostRule` whose `amount` is above 0, with no card types, no `minCost`, no created-only filter, and expiry `NextTurnOf` before that turn has started. Each crystal of the hike is priced at `next_turn_mana` times `AI_TAXED_PLAYS` (1), weighted by `cost_hike` in `AI_EVAL` (1.0) and 0 in `GREEDY_EVAL`.
