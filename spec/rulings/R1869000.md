"The first Unit you play onto this is Equipped into it" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #16 Rod of Ages) is a host that lends Equip (`staticFlags.lendsEquip`). While Rod acts on its controller's field, its backrow zone or a unit zone ([[§6.1]] Animated), and its definition is no fusion, any Unit card its controller plays may be played onto it as an Equip play ([[R1086]]), an option beside the plain zones, though the card has no Equip: `legal_actions` lists it and the reducer accepts it from one function (`play_choices::equip_onto_at`). The Unit resolves on top, its Cry included, standing on Rod in its backrow zone, then fuses into Rod, which is the kept instance ([[R77]], [[R102]]); a Starship Piece fused in leaves it no piece ([[R1852001]]). Rod, which prints no stats, now has the Unit's beside its Animated, so it animates at that state check ([[R2280001]]), or stays a fused Field Spell in its zone when no unit zone is open. Once Rod holds a fusion it lends no more, so a second Unit plays as usual; a Unit played into another zone first leaves the offer open, since Rod has hosted none. Before issue #2280 Rod printed 1/1 and lent only while it was an animated Unit, so a Rod left in the backrow with no open unit zone hosted nothing ([[R2280000]]). Both faces lend. An Equip card may go onto Rod as onto any host, and that fusion ends the lending too. Animated and Flying count toward the 10 keyword kinds that make it Radiant ([[R1850005]]). Rejected: forcing the first Unit onto it, which the text's "you play onto this" does not say; and ending the lending at the first Unit played anywhere, which would make the order of two plays decide whether the text ever applies.
