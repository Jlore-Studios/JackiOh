"The first Unit you play onto this is Equipped into it" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #16 Rod of Ages) is a host that lends Equip (`staticFlags.lendsEquip`). While Rod acts on its controller's field as a Unit, animated into a unit zone ([[§6.1]] Animated), and its definition is no fusion, any Unit card its controller plays may be played onto it as an Equip play ([[R1086]]), an option beside the plain zones, though the card has no Equip: `legal_actions` lists it and the reducer accepts it from one function (`play_choices::equip_onto_at`). The Unit resolves on top, its Cry included, then fuses into Rod, which is the kept instance ([[R77]], [[R102]]); a Starship Piece fused in leaves it no piece ([[R1852001]]). Once Rod holds a fusion it lends no more, so a second Unit plays as usual; a Unit played into another zone first leaves the offer open, since Rod has hosted none. A Rod left in the backrow with no open unit zone is no Unit and hosts nothing. Both faces lend. An Equip card may go onto Rod as onto any host, and that fusion ends the lending too. Animated and Flying count toward the 10 keyword kinds that make it Radiant ([[R1850005]]). Rejected: forcing the first Unit onto it, which the text's "you play onto this" does not say; and ending the lending at the first Unit played anywhere, which would make the order of two plays decide whether the text ever applies.
