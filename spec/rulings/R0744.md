A seat is disconnected from the moment the first socket attaches to its match's actor if its own account has not had a socket on that actor: the actor was rebuilt after a restart ([[§9.5]]) and that player has not come back, or the player was matched and never opened the match. Its grace starts then, at the grace deadline stored on the match when the restart left one (kept, never later than a fresh window would end, and at once if it has already passed, [[R147]]), else a fresh `disconnectGraceSeconds`; its socket arriving in time cancels it, and running out is a loss through `disconnectExpired` ([[§2.5]], [[R146]]). Nothing starts at process boot, which rebuilds no actor: a match no socket returns to is left to the reaper's ceiling draw ([[R112]]), and when both seats are away nobody loses by seat order.
