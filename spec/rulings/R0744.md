A seat is disconnected from the moment the first socket attaches to its match's actor if its own account has not had a socket on that actor: the actor was rebuilt after a restart ([[§9.5]]) and that player has not come back, or the player was matched and never opened the match. A ladder bot's seat has no socket and is never disconnected, so it never has a grace; it counts as attached from the moment its match's actor arms, so against a bot a person who has not opened the match is disconnected from then ([[R1447]]). A disconnected seat's grace starts as it becomes disconnected, at the grace deadline stored on the match when the restart left one (kept, never later than a fresh window would end, and at once if it has already passed, [[R147]]), else a fresh `disconnectGraceSeconds`; its socket arriving in time cancels it, and running out is a loss through `disconnectExpired` ([[§2.5]], [[R146]]). Nothing starts at process boot, which rebuilds no actor: a match no socket returns to is left to the reaper's ceiling draw ([[R112]]), and when both seats are away nobody loses by seat order.
