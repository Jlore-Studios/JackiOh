Zone, X, embiggen, Tribute and the targets and modes a card's script declares travel in the `play` action, which `legalActions` enumerates; the client builds them with the prompt pickers. A declared `hand` or `zone` pick travels in `targets` and a declared `direction` pick in `modes`, so Glowy Jelly Bean's hand card and Silly Silas's direction are chosen with the play and never pause resolution. Every choice made during resolution (Discover, chained steps, Echo repeats, casts, triggers, mulligan) opens a `PendingChoice`
