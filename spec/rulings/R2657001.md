A Field Trap stays after it fires and can fire again ([[§5.1]]), but it reveals only the first time: the firing that turns it face-up ([[R33]]). Each later firing of a face-up Field Trap is a plain trigger. The engine still reports every firing as `trapFired`, and marks a later one with `again: true` (`traps::fires_again`, the same on a firing that replaces an event): the client plays the long reveal, its showcase and its stinger only on a firing without it, and a later firing gets the short cue a trigger gets. A text that answers a Trap revealing answers the reveal alone: Classic #47 Recurring Felinor's "When one of your Traps reveals" returns it for a Field Trap's first firing and not for its later ones. It still fires any number of times, and a face-up Field Trap still rolls no chance to reveal ([[R1849009]]). This is the owner's balance pass (issue #2657); through v0.5.9f Recurring Felinor answered every firing (`script_v0_5_9f`, [[R767000]]).
