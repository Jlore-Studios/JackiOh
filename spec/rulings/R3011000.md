The owner asked in #3011 to remove the art picker from the Statistics page's card breakdown ahead of the variant-ladder rebuild, which should not have to carry it over.

- **The choice is retired.** The breakdown of `/stats` no longer offers No full art, Animated, Epic, Legendary or Mythic under a full-art card's faces ([[R2756000]]), nor the Animated art ([[R2715000]]): it renders no art buttons at all. What the faces print is the old "Where it starts" rule as the whole behavior: the card's still Epic ([[R2655000]]) while the Full-art cards setting is on, its normal face while it is off, and the Radiant face the same while Show Radiant is on. A token listed under a full-art card prints its parent's ([[R2655002]]). A card without a full art draws its breakdown as before.
- **Whatever is owned.** The page is public and reads no ownership, so it prints the still Epic whatever the viewer owns, as the Almanac and the landing page's fan do ([[R1313000]]); and the setting's Epic prints where the setting says, as a card's own pick does ([[R1655000]]).
- **No pick written.** The breakdown writes no card pick, which would be sent to the account ([[R1834000]]), and changes no face anywhere else. It passes the setting down as the pick to show ([[R2655001]]), rather than leaving the pick out, so the device's stored Collection pick never leaks into this public preview.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a full art, and nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Hearthstone semantics: a stats page shows one canonical version of a card.

Rejected: keeping a picker for the variant ladders to carry over, which the owner asked to rebuild the looks as instead; carrying a choice in the address, as Show Radiant is not; and omitting the pick, which would print the viewer's own stored pick on a public page.
