## R1040 — Knowledge Breaker's Aura gives your Units Cloaked

Built (Meditative #45; patch v0.3.7, #781). "Aura: Your Units have Cloaked." While the card acts on
its controller's field, each Unit card in that player's hand has Cloaked ([[R783007]]), so
`legalActions` also offers it as a face-down play into an empty, unlocked backrow zone ([[R783004]],
`faceDown` `cloaked`). The price is the Unit's own: mana, X and Tribute ([[R783005]]). It declares no
targets or modes. It is announced and placed as a Trap ([[R448]], a fresh id per [[R227]]) and counted
as a Field Trap play. While face-down it is a Field Trap and none of its own text acts. The Aura ends
as the card leaves the field, and the Units left in hand lose Cloaked, but a Unit already set stays
set until it reveals or leaves the field ([[R78]]). The opponent's Units are not touched.

Until patch v0.3.7 the Aura was ME-ALTPLAY's face-down play permission ("You may play your Units
face-down as Animated Field Traps that reveal at the start of your next turn"), the same play with a
fixed timing.
