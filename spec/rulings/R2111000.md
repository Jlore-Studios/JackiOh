**Set.** Whatever lands in a backrow zone is laid down: a short drop that lands flat and heavy, with no bounce and no flash (`jk-trap-set` on the board's silhouette, `fx-hold-set` on the effects layer's stand-in), at the moment the arrival's thud sounds (`PAIR_OFFSET_MS`). What each seat hears is [[R203]]'s. A Field Spell or a Unit landing there lands the same way, so no motion tells a face-down arrival from a face-up one.

**Wait.** Every back on either board and the viewer's own face-down trap under its veil ([[R371]]) breathes one slow breath, so a trap waits as something alive. It comes from one rule keyed on nothing but being face-down, with no per-card value, inline style or stagger, just as every back looks alike ([[R1297000]]): nothing about a card can be read from its motion. A card that is mid-animation or glowing ready plays that instead.

**Flip.** A trap that fires holds a beat of anticipation through the first 15% of its motion, still face-down, sinking and dimming, then turns and is revealed at 30% together with the burst (`FX_TRAP_BURST_AT`, which moved from 20%). BUILD's 700 ms for `trapFired` is kept.

**Reduced motion.** None of it plays under the system's, the settings panel's or the effects setting's reduced motion.

**Rejected:** telling a set from a face-up arrival, which would let a motion say what a card is; a per-card stagger of the breath, which would make one back differ from another; moving the set sound, which is [[R203]]'s and [[§10.11]]'s; holding the trap's close-up back for the beat, since it names a trap only once it has fired, to each seat whose view names it ([[R2734000]]; its controller's alone before issue #2734, [[R154]]), and gives nothing away before it.
