Until #2728 the card and cosmetic token bars were both 200 points and took the same points from
every slot ([[R1953000]], [[R1999000]]), so they filled on the same openings, and the cosmetic bar
paid Rare, Epic, Rare, Epic, Rare, Cosmetic Pack ([[R1305001]]) with no token above Epic
([[R1305003]]). The owner's numbers in #2728 stagger the two bars and add a Legendary token:

- **The cycle.** `COSMETIC_TOKEN_CYCLE` is Rare, Epic, Legendary, Rare, Epic, Cosmetic Pack, then the
  same again. The account keeps its place, the `CosmeticTokenCycle` counter, and the step it stands
  at is still the cycle's entry at that position modulo six ([[R1305001]]). A Legendary step grants
  one cosmetic token counted under `Legendary`, which `0045_economy.sql` already allowed.
- **The sizes.** Each step takes its own points to pay: `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_RARE` 50,
  `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_EPIC` 75, `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_LEGENDARY` 100 and
  `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE_PACK` 100 (`CosmeticTokenStep::bar_size`), so one cycle is 450 points,
  down from 1,200. `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE` is gone. The card token bar, its size
  `CARD_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE`, its points and its payouts are unchanged, as are the points every slot adds
  to both bars.
- **Settling.** A bar that fills keeps its overflow ([[R1305000]]), and the next step has a size of
  its own, so the cosmetic bar settles step by step (`economy::tokens::settle_cosmetic_bar`): the
  points are compared with the size of the step the cycle stands at, a step that is reached pays
  and its size is taken off, and the rest is compared with the next step's size, until a step is not
  reached. One gain can pay several steps of different sizes, each in order; the steps paid, the
  points kept and the cycle's place moved on by the steps paid are written in the opening's
  transaction, one row each as before.
- **A bar already past its step's size.** A bar the 200-point bar left part filled keeps its points,
  and they settle whole at its next opening: 199 points at the Rare step pay Rare and Epic and keep
  74. Nothing is migrated or reset.
- **The answer.** An opening's `bars.cosmetic.size` is the size of the step the bar stands at after
  it, and `bars.cosmeticSizes` the size of each step its sweep crosses: each step paid, in order,
  then that resting step's, so `pays + 1` entries. `GET /api/tokens`'s cosmetic bar answers the size
  of the step it stands at, its `nextStep` may be `legendary`, and its tokens hold
  `cosmeticLegendary` beside `cosmeticRare` and `cosmeticEpic` ([[R1305004]]). An answer kept under
  an idempotency key before #2728 ([[R2124000]]) has no `cosmeticSizes`; the client then sizes every
  sweep at `size`.
- **The Legendary token's reach.** It buys any cosmetic at Legendary or below on the cosmetics' own
  scale that the account does not own, a per-card one for a card the account owns or an account
  one, a Legendary chase included: a chase is exactly a Legendary or Mythic cosmetic
  ([[R1333000]]), so the tier alone refuses every chase to a Rare or Epic token and a Mythic to every
  token, and the redemption's separate chase refusal is gone. It refuses with 409, spending nothing,
  a Mythic, a cosmetic already owned, a per-card one on a card the account does not own, and every
  refusal [[R1305003]] lists; a `Mythic` or `Common` token is no token at all, 400.
- **The dev fill** ([[R1310003]]) fills the cosmetic bar to its current step's size, so it pays that
  step: two fills from the cycle's start still pay a Rare token, then an Epic one. A bar already past
  its step's size pays every step it holds first, as an opening would, and the fill tops up the step
  it then stands at, so the bar ends at 0 as before.
- **The targets.** `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS` gives way to one target per step, each its
  size over [[R1953000]]'s 8.65 points a card pack, rounded:
  `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS_RARE` 6, `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS_EPIC` 9,
  `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS_LEGENDARY` 12 and `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS_PACK` 12, held
  by `economy/math.rs`; a whole cycle is about 52 card packs, so cosmetic rewards come about 2.7
  times as often as before. The card token bar still pays every `CARD_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS`, 23.
- **The client** sizes each sweep of the opening's cosmetic bar from `cosmeticSizes` and rests it at
  `size`, reveals a Legendary token on a Legendary step, shows the Legendary tokens held, and offers
  a held Legendary token on a locked cosmetic it covers in the Collection, a Legendary chase
  included and a Mythic never, the cheapest held token that covers it first ([[R1953001]]). It takes
  every size from the server's answer, none from `serverConfig.ts` (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

This amends [[R1305001]] (the cycle and its Legendary step), [[R1305003]], [[R1333000]] and
[[R1953001]] (a Legendary token reaches a Legendary chase; no token a Mythic), [[R1305004]]
(`legendary`, `cosmeticLegendary` and the cosmetic bar's size per step), [[R1310003]] (the dev fill
to the current step's size), and [[R1953000]] and [[R1999000]] (the cosmetic bar's size and target
per step), and edits [[§14.2]], [[§14.5]] and [[§14.10]].

Hearthstone semantics: none applies; Hearthstone has no token cycle, and the cycle, its sizes and
the Legendary token's reach are the owner's numbers in #2728.

Rejected: one size for the whole cycle, which would keep the two bars filling together, what the
issue undoes; the client reading the step sizes from `serverConfig.ts` and working out which step it
stands at, a rule the server owns (CLAUDE.md rule 7); resetting the bars the 200-point bar left part
filled, which would take points the players earned and need a migration; retiring the targets, when
one per step keeps `economy/math.rs` holding each size to the packs it should take; and a Mythic
token, which the issue keeps out of every token's reach.
