A sweep ([[R3554008]], Stinkyscape+ #107 Fuse) hits every enemy Unit it weighs as part of its attack, the attack on the enemy hero included, and a Trample attacker's excess over each of those Units goes on to the defending hero ([[§4.4]] step 9). The projection of an attack's damage to the defending hero (`subsystems::lethal::projected_damage`, which #96 My Pawn's lethal window reads under [[R44]] and the combat forecast's `dealt` on a hero, its `kills` and its seat's `lethal` read under [[R3100000]]) counted the blow and Cleave's hits ([[R176]]) but not the sweep's, so in fuzz seed 7606 a trampling Fuse's attack on a hero at 18 was forecast to deal 8 and leave it standing, and dealt 29. The projection now adds each of the sweep's hits after the blow and Cleave's, in `combat::strike`'s order, each Unit's Trample excess going on to the hero through the hero's own Armor, caps and pools, as the combat does. Each hit is read on the board as it stands, so a Unit both Cleave and the sweep strike is read at its health before either.

Hearthstone semantics: a projection of an attack is what the attack does.

Rejected: an I18 skip for a trampling sweeper, which would leave a forecast players see showing no kill on an attack that kills, and My Pawn missing the lethal attack R44 names.
