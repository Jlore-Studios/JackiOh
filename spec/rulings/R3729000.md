[[R1578000]] made `/play` the screen every way to play starts from, with the online panel first and the other
ways as small links beside it. A new player could not tell from it what each way is, how long a game takes
or what it takes to start. This amends [[R1578000]]: the hub is a catalog of cards in four groups, and the
online panel's three numbered steps become one panel an Online card opens.

**The cards.** `routes/playModes.ts` is the one catalog (CLAUDE.md rule 9): each card has a name, one line on
what it is, how long a game takes, what it needs, a testid and an action, and the screen restates none of
them. The groups come in this order:

- **Online**: Best of 1 (needs one saved deck), Conquest (a trio of decks) and All random (nothing to bring),
  the queue modes of [[R257]]. Each shows the rank of its ladder ([[R2778000]]: Best of 1 and Conquest share
  Constructed's), its expected wait ([[R640002]]) and a "Last used" mark when it is the mode the player queued
  or picked last.
- **Daily**: Daily games ([[§9.13]]), "Your move in N" while N games wait on the player, and a move every
  `DAILY_TIME_PER_MOVE_DAYS`'s shortest to longest days.
- **With friends**: Private lobby ([[R3730000]]) and Challenge a friend (Play together, where challenges are
  sent).
- **Solo**: Practice, the Dungeon run and the Tutorial (practice's first lesson).

A game's length is `GAME_MINUTES`, a pair of minutes per card: a presentation estimate to plan an evening by, no
rule (the spec fixes only the 75 s turn clock and the 120 minute ceiling, [[§9.5]]); the Daily card's time is
the shared constants'. Replays and Play together stay as two links under the groups.

**One panel.** An Online card is a button that opens a dialog titled with the mode: the rank of the mode's
ladder with its Leaderboard link, the deck or trio picker (All random's "More cards from" instead) with the
validator's verdict ([[R253]], UX only, CLAUDE.md rule 7), the server's refusal if there is one, and Find a
match, the one primary action. It is one step: the hub opens it on the last mode and, in it, the deck and trio
last used. Find a match sends what it sent before, and the panel closes once the server took the ticket. The
Private lobby card opens a dialog holding the private lobby's panels, a 1v1 in the last mode and a co-op one
([[R3730000]]), where "Play a friend" stood in the side column. The side column of links is the Solo group.

**No account.** A visitor sees the sign-in panel of [[R1578000]] and the Solo cards, and the hub reads and
calls nothing of the engine's module (`playCards` asks the daily constants only for a signed-in player).
Nothing is read from the server for them.

**Layout.** One screen ([[R1581000]]): the page never scrolls, and the hub's one region does, whatever the cards
need. Cards stand in one column up to 600 px, two to a row over it and three from 900 px, each at least `--hit-min` tall.

Nothing here is a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7): the server matches, validates and refuses, and a card only opens the
screen or the panel that sends the intent. **Proof.** `playModes.test.ts` holds the catalog's order, words and
numbers; `play.test.tsx` the four groups, a card opening its panel, Find a match's body, both ladders' ranks,
the last-used mark and the remembered deck, the lobby card, and the signed-out hub; spec 55 queues from the hub
at a desktop and an iPad size.

Rejected: keeping the numbered steps beside the cards, which is the three-step page this replaces; a deck picker
on each card, which would repeat the panel three times; and guessing a mode's length from the engine's clocks,
which fix a turn, not a game.
