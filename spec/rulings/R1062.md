This revises [[R383]]. An "Animated on your turn" card that has been on its controller's side of the field (either zone) since that turn began is not summoning sick when it animates at its controller's start of turn: it came under its controller's control before this turn (Hearthstone's reading). Still sick if the card entered or came to this side this turn, and the entry animation as it enters the field is still an entry; plain Animated is unchanged. So Fiery Waraxe, played on its controller's turn, animates sick that turn and attacks from its next start of turn, and C+ #12.8 Frostspatula's Rush reaches heroes from its second turn. The fuzz monitor enters nothing for such a re-animation, reading the shadow and the keywords, never `summonedTurn`. The revision comes with the Meditative set ([[R1420]]): until the set is open, R383 stands and the start-of-turn animation is an entry, so a game of the shipped sets plays as it did (D14). The brief's MD-D23.
