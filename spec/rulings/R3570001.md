"Random Evolved Swarm cards" (S+ #142's second entry) and "a Swarm card that is Evolved" (S+ #142.2) are
`entersEvolved` riders on `add_random_from_catalog` and `summon_random_with`: each card made is given
[[R3536002]]'s mark, which rides every zone and evolves the card as the state check after it enters the field
finds it, running its Evolve text once ([[R3536000]]). A card whose text prints no Evolve hook is given no mark
([[R3536000]]: a Vanilla card never evolves), so it is added or summoned as it is. The mark shows only on a card
the viewer may read (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Rejected: marking every card, which would leave a mark that can never be answered on a card with no Evolve text.
