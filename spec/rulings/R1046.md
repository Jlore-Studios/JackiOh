## R1046 — A face-down play is hidden like any set Trap

Built (ME-ALTPLAY; Cloaked since patch v0.3.7). The opponent sees a Trap set with its cost and no `x`
or `embiggened` ([[R33]], [[R351]]); how it reveals is the owner's alone. The owner's own backrow view
carries it (`revealAt`, skipped when absent), which their client badges on their own card; no other
seat ever receives it. `determinize` ([[R185]]) samples for a hidden backrow card a Trap, a card that
prints Cloaked ([[R783009]]), or a card an aura on that side lends Cloaked to in its hand now: every
Unit while Knowledge Breaker acts there, every Spell while Paranoia does.
