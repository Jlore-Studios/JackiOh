A face-down card in a backrow zone is a backrow card to every effect that targets, counts or hits
backrow cards, whatever its printed type: "target backrow card" (#36 Magic Jammed) may name it, and
"every backrow card" (C #51 Back Breaker, C+ #5 Guy Att) destroys it. A face-up Field Spell, a
face-up or revealed Trap and a Field Trap are backrow cards as before.

A card set face-down by its Cloaked ([[§6.1]] Cloaked, [[R783004]]) is one of them: set, it is
hidden like any Trap and its text is inert, so it is a backrow card and no Unit, whether it prints a
Unit (C #6 Cloaked Toe Cracker), a Spell (a Spell given Cloaked by M #99 Paranoia) or a Field Spell.
Destroyed before it reveals, it reaches its owner's graveyard as its printed card ([[R783008]]);
stolen (#36's Radiant face), it lands in the thief's backrow still set, and its owner sees only its
back ([[R33]]).

A Unit standing in a backrow zone is no backrow card ([[R1081000]]), and neither is a Cloaked Unit
once its Reveal has animated it into a unit zone ([[R783006]]): it is a Unit there, and no effect on
backrow cards reaches it.

The engine already read it so: `zones::is_standing_unit`, the one test every "backrow card" reader
uses to pass a Unit by, asks `card_type_of`, which reads a set card as the Trap or Field Trap it is
set as (`alt_play::set_type_of`), so #36, C #51 and C+ #5 agree. What the issue saw was the client's:
the targeter's view of the card is a back with no id ([[§10.8]]), so nothing on the board lit or took
the click, which [[R3777001]] answers.

This follows Hearthstone, where a Secret is a Secret to every effect that destroys or counts
Secrets, whatever it will do once it fires. Rejected: passing a set Cloaked Unit by as a Unit, which
would tell the other player what the face-down card is by what may target it ([[R33]]).
