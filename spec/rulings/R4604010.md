In the daily fuzz's plain seed 13680 a fused Starship attacked p1's hero. The forecast ([[R3100000]]) said 2 dealt and a kill. p1's backrow held Classic #52 Final Gambit fused onto a Unit, which stays on the field, so the Gambit's replacement ([[R1704001]]) acts there. The replacement cancelled the hit that would have brought the hero to 0, healed the hero 10 and drew 3, into fatigue that killed it. None of this fires a `trapFired` or a `hookResolving`, so I18 read the step as plain combat and the fatigue as the attack's 12. [[R4221000]] already leaves an attack out of I18 while a "would die" replacement acts for either side; a "lethal hit" one answers the blow itself, which the forecast does not project either, and is now left out the same way.

Hearthstone semantics: the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule, and an invariant that checks it may judge only what it can see.

Rejected: projecting the cancel in the forecast. The heal and draws that follow it reach the hidden library and its fatigue, which the forecast must not read ([[R292]]).
