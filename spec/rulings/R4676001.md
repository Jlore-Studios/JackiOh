**Why.** The owner wants every card on `/stats` to open its extended stats, but the page is already full, so it gains one button and one panel and nothing else.

**The toggle.** The drill-down's actions row gains **Extended stats**, a toggle (`aria-pressed`) right after Show Radiant, on every card. Pressed, the details column (Win rate by patch, by turn played, Co-played) gives way to the **Extended stats panel**; pressed again, they come back. Escape and Back still close the dialog ([[R2127000]], [[R3221000]]), and closing it closes the panel.

**The panel** opens on a Segmented of the sections the card has, in the Segmented's look ([[R3324003]]), and shows no segment for a section it lacks; with one section it shows no Segmented:

- **Choices**, when the card has a variant ([[R4676000]]): one small table per kind (Options chosen, X paid, Embiggened), its rows in the server's order, by share. Each row: the choice's words (a Choose One row's options each as the card names it in play, the client's `modeText`, its numbers filled from the face the page reads, a pair joined with " + "; the server's label for X and Embiggen), its share, the deck table's `TierChip`, its Adjusted ("not enough games" below the floor) and, with Show 95% ranges on, its range under it, and its games.
- **Radiant vs normal**: [[R4673001]]'s line under the faces moves here as a two-row table, Normal and Radiant, each with its tier, Adjusted (and its range with the ranges shown) and games, then the Radiant gap with its range. This amends [[R4673001]], which showed it under the faces on every face.

Amended by [[R4679001]] (issue #4679): the panel gains Outputs between Choices and Radiant vs normal, kept as `extended=outputs`.

**The address.** The panel is kept beside `?card=` as `extended=choices` or `extended=faces`, the section pressed: absent when the panel is closed or no card is open, and an unknown value reads closed. A link with it opens the card with its panel on that section, or on the first section the card has when it lacks that one. Pressing the toggle opens the first section the card has. A play version's drill-down ([[R4680001]]) reads no patch's details and has no toggle, so the address holds no `extended` while one is open, and opening or closing any drill-down closes the panel.

**Rejected.**

- **A second dialog.** A dialog over a dialog loses the card's face and its focus trap ([[R3221000]]).
- **Panels below the details.** On a phone the choices would sit under three sections of scrolling.
- **Keeping the face line under the faces as well.** The same figures twice.
- **The option as recorded ("damage").** A script's option is its interface, not its words; the prompt that offered it already names it in the card's words, and the Chinese reader reads those through the same dictionary.
- **Splitting the server's label on the client.** The label's join is the engine's format (CLAUDE.md rule 7); the server sends the options apart.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
