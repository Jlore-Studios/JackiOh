Felinors vs Humans #42's Cry "crushes the cards beneath it". [[§6.3]]'s Crush ([[R5048000]]) destroys every Unit in the zone, the card on top of the pile included.

**The rule.** `crush` with `beneathOnly` destroys, of the Units Crush would destroy in that zone, only those lying beneath the running card: the cards after it in the zone's pile ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]; a pile's top first). The running card stands, and so does any card above it. When the running card is not in that zone, nothing is beneath it and nothing is destroyed. A card beneath lies dormant, off the field for [[§4.5]] ([[R13]]), so no state check would collect a mark on it while the running card stands on top: each goes to its owner's graveyard at once, by a plain move, its text dormant so no Death runs. Everything else is Crush's: an Indestructible card stays, a card that is no Unit is left alone, and a Spell's crush passes a Unit Immune to Spells by.
