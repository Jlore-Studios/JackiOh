Felinors vs Humans #42's Cry "crushes the cards beneath it". [[§6.3]]'s Crush ([[R5048000]]) destroys every Unit in the zone, the card on top of the pile included.

**The rule.** `crush` with `beneathOnly` destroys, of the Units Crush would destroy in that zone, only those lying beneath the running card: the cards after it in the zone's pile ([[§3.2]], [[R13]]; a pile's top first). The running card stands, and so does any card above it. When the running card is not in that zone, nothing is beneath it and nothing is destroyed. Everything else is Crush's: a mark the next state check collects, Indestructible ignoring it, a card that is no Unit left alone, and a Spell's crush passing a Unit Immune to Spells by.
