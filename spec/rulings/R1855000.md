A board replacement (`replaceBoard { board }`, Stinkyscape's Anomalies) replaces its caster's side only, the designer's answer (`docs/stinkyscape-set.md` ST9 Q7). It begins with the public `boardReplaced`, naming the player alone, which the client plays over the whole board before the cards go and come. Then every card on that side an effect of the replacing card can reach is exiled, in [[R68]]'s walk: the unit zones left to right, then the backrow. A Stack pile goes whole: once its top has gone, the card beneath tops the zone and is exiled in turn ([[R13]]). Exile fires no Death ([[§6.3]]), and a unit token ceases to exist ([[R11]]). A Dormant card stays ([[R1839000]]), as does a card the replacement cannot reach (a Unit immune to Spells when the replacement is a Spell's), and since each still fills its zone, the preset lays nothing there.

Each entry of the preset is then laid in list order. An entry names a definition, its row and lane, and optionally Radiant, Defense Position and riders ([[R1855002]]). Each is a summon of a fresh card into that zone: no Cry of its own ([[R1]]; the Cries fire afterwards, [[R1855001]]), summoning sick, a Trap face-down and a Field Spell face-up, with the replacing card as its `sourceId` ([[R1088]]). Defense Position is an effect's switch, which spends no exertion ([[R20]]). An entry lays nothing in a zone that is taken or reserved ([[R47]], [[R64]]), on a lane off its row, or in a row its card does not live in (a Spell, or a Trap named in the unit row). A Locked zone takes its card, as every named summon's does ([[R688]]). A question a laid card asks as it arrives ([[R151]]) pauses the rest of the replacement, which resumes once it is answered ([[R113]]).
