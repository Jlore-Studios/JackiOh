"After this destroys a Unit, it may attack again" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #94 Tmack Trav) reads its own `afterAttack` facts: when the Unit it attacked is among that combat's destroyed ids and Tmack survived, it gets a fresh exertion this turn (`may_attack_again`), the rest of [[§4.2]]'s restrictions still applying. Each kill frees it again, with no cap: one more attack, or Windfury's two. A kill while it defends, a hit on the hero and a target that lives give nothing. A forced attack counts as a declared one does, as [[R1849001]]'s `afterAttack` fires for both; on the opponent's turn the fresh exertion is spent on nothing. The fuzz's invariant monitor reads such a kill as a fresh exertion, counting the turn's attacks again from there, and the sickness it lifts as readied, for an attacker whose own text answers its attacks, unless the state the action leaves shows its attack still spent. A kill that lands in a later action than the declaration, because the combat paused on a prompt (S #22 Gatinsir's Phalanxes' Discovers), is that attack's still: the monitor keeps the attack while the action that declared it leaves a prompt or work owed, as [[R424]]'s "after the combat" is after the whole of it (fuzz seed 4195 of the release, issue #1884). Rejected: [[R424]]'s declared-only reading, which is tied to the Classic Golem's transform, a change no forced attack should bring about, where Tmack's text only frees it to attack.
