"Adjacent units have +3 attack" ([[§8.11]] TG #21 Flame Tongue Totem, printed "Aura: Adjacent Units have +3 Attack.") is an aura of [[§10.4]]'s layer 5 over [[§3.1]]'s adjacency of the source's own zone: the Unit on top of the pile in lane N−1 and in lane N+1 of its side and row, never the source itself and never across sides, so an enemy Unit is never adjacent (Hearthstone's adjacent minions are the controller's). A source standing in the backrow ([[R1081000]]) reaches only the Units standing beside it there, never a Trap or a Field Spell. A source beneath a Stack pile projects nothing, and a Unit beneath one gets nothing ([[R13]], [[§3.2]]); a Dormant source projects nothing ([[R1839000]]). The aura reads where the source and its neighbours stand as each view is computed, so a move, a rotation ([[R14]]), a steal or a departure changes who has it at once, and nothing is stored on a neighbour. Rejected: both sides' Units in the neighbouring lanes, and the Units across from the source.
