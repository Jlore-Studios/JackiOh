The economy was built behind the server's `ECONOMY_LIVE` switch ([[§14.12]]), and its reward
hook's callers pay nothing while it is off ([[R1302006]]). Quests follow it: while the switch is off
no top-up runs, so no quest arrives and no clock starts, `GET /api/quests` answers the quests held
(none, but those a dev tool gave) and no time for the next, and a finished game moves no quest,
since the hook is not called. The going-live sub-issue turned it on ([[R1310000]]), and its
migration removed every quest held and every schedule ([[R1310002]]), so each account's clock
starts at its first visit after the launch ([[R1304000]]).

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone has no such switch; the spec's launch plan ([[§14.12]]) does,
and a decision it leaves open follows it.
