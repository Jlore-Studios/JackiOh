Since issue #2179 Temporary takes a number, as Brittle does: a Temporary X card is discarded at the
Xth end of its owner's turn it spends in their hand. At each cleanup of its owner's turn ([[R637]]) the count
drops by 1 and the card is discarded once it reaches 0, so a plain Temporary is Temporary 1, discarded
at the first, as before the number. A card holding several Temporary entries counts the largest. The
count left rides the instance (`CardInstance.temporary`) and stands in for the printed number from then
on, wherever the card goes, as a Brittle count is kept ([[R78]]), so the card shows the count as it
stands; a new card (a copy, a transform) starts from its printed number. A swap that keeps the card's
keywords (a Book of Books swap) keeps its count. C+ #23 Dropshipping gives Temporary 2 where it gave
Brittle 2
