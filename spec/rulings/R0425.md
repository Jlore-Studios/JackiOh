C+ #74 is a Field Trap (the designer wrote Trap), since a Trap is consumed when it fires, which would leave nothing to gain Brittle. It has no Brittle while it is face-down ([[R687]]): its printed Brittle starts when it first activates ([[R385]], [[R687]]). It counts the opponent's plays since it was set, and on each even count, after that card resolves, the card — if it still exists: a Unit on the field, a Spell in the graveyard, a trap in the backrow — is fused into this ([[R77]]: this is the kept instance and stays a Field Trap, and the opponent's card ceases to exist; on the Radiant face a Radiant copy of it is fused in instead), and then this gains +1 Brittle. The texts fused in work for its controller where they can (an end-of-turn line runs on their side, an aura covers it), a fused Cry never runs, since this is already on the field, and it turns face-up the first time it activates, whether or not anything is left to fuse ([[R33]], [[R687]])
