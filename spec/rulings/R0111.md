Becoming `active` grants one copy of each starter card ([[R1310000]]), written by a trigger on the `pending → active` transition and idempotent, so a repeated redemption cannot double a collection. Until the economy went live ([[§14.12]]) it granted one copy of every non-token card; migration 0048 narrowed it to the non-token cards of the current version that `app.settings.starter_cards` names
