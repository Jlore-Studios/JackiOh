Tipsy X is now **Woozy X**: only the name changes, and the counter, its decay, the stamp and the blur stay
[[R3548000]]'s and [[R3548001]]'s. Every card that printed Tipsy prints Woozy, in English and in Chinese
(晕乎 for 微醺), and the keyword kind, the counter's kind on `counterChanged`, the engine's module,
constants, types and view fields, the client and every test that named the old word name the new one.

What was written before the rename cannot change, as with Magnetic and Equip ([[R781000]]):

- The shipped snapshots print Tipsy. The keyword and its kind read `Tipsy` as an alias of `Woozy`, and
  the counter's kind reads `tipsy` as an alias of `woozy`; neither old name is ever written.
- The replay hash runs over the serialized state, and every game that played the keyword before the
  rename was filed with a final hash over the keys `tipsy` (the player's counter) and `tipsy` (a card's
  stamp). So the state keeps writing those two keys under the old name, while the engine calls the
  fields `woozy`: a stored replay folds to the hash it was filed with. The views, which no hash reads,
  call them `woozy` and `woozyBlur`.
- The blur's seed stream keeps its key, `:tipsy-blur`, so a replay of an older game blurs the same words
  it did.

The only traces of the old name are the shipped snapshots and their player notes, these aliases, the
two stored keys, the seed stream's key and one history line in [[§6.1]]'s Woozy row.
