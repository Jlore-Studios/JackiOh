Stinkyscape #10.1 Inner Fire's "Set a Unit's Attack equal to its Health" reads the Unit's stats now, auras included, through the [[§10.4]] layers, and is done as a buff of the difference, an existing primitive: Attack becomes Health plus the running face's shift, with a floor of 0, so a Unit whose Attack is higher loses the difference and a later aura leaving takes its part with it. A set of Attack alone at layer 1 would be a new verb; this is not one. The Radiant face's "plus {shift} if it's yours or minus {shift} if it's an enemy" shifts the Health read by the declared shift ([[R386]]), up for a Unit its caster controls and down for one it does not, before the floor. "If it's yours" is a Unit its caster controls.
