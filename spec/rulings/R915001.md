A custom card's frame ([[§12.2]]) is checked by one function, `custom::validate_custom_card`, which the server, the creator, the practice worker and a game's setup all call, and which lists every way a card breaks the limits, each with its field; a frame out of its limits is refused, never clamped.

- A name of 1 to `CUSTOM_NAME_MAX_LENGTH` characters, not only spaces; a Unit or a Spell; a cost from 0 to `CUSTOM_COST_MAX`; a printed rarity.
- A Unit has an attack from 0 to `CUSTOM_ATTACK_MAX` and a health from 1 to `CUSTOM_HEALTH_MAX`, and at most `CUSTOM_KEYWORDS_MAX` keywords, each once, from `CUSTOM_KEYWORDS`; a Spell has no stats and no keywords.
- A Radiant Unit's stats are the frame's Radiant stats, each up to `CUSTOM_RADIANT_STAT_MAX` (health at least 1), or twice the base stats when the frame gives none; its keywords and text are the base face's.
- Text of at most `CUSTOM_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH` characters, every `{key}` in it one of the card's numbers; at most `CUSTOM_PARAMS_MAX` numbers, each named by 1 to `CUSTOM_PARAM_KEY_MAX_LENGTH` lowercase letters, digits and underscores starting with a letter, once, with a base and a Radiant value from 0 to `CUSTOM_PARAM_MAX`.
- A script of at most `CUSTOM_SOURCE_MAX_BYTES` bytes and a builder's model of at most `CUSTOM_BUILDER_MAX_BYTES` bytes of JSON.
