When a play records another card's play (`records_play_as`, [[R451]]), the last face-up skip reads
the recorded card's printed tags as well as the played card's tags as played (granted tags
included, [[R923]]). So when C #57 Echo copies an AI generated Spell — a Book fused with T-AI-5
Autocomplete among them — the previous last face-up record stands, as [[§8.7]]'s T-AI-5 row promises
the record never takes an AI generated card, while `lastSpell` still takes what Echo copied
([[R399]]). Without this, the next Autocomplete would copy the fused card back, and the Coin and the
fused Book would replay without end in one turn (issue #1121).
