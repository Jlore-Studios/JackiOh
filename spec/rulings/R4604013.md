In the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 12125, p1 could play a fusion of Call to Chaos (Training-Grounds Edition) and Stinkyscape+ #79 A Glimpse into the Endless Sea. Its Cry first played Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition), which resolved, and then exiled every permanent, the Glimpse's text ([[R43]]), a Dormant Starship among them. I19 lets a board sweep's run exile a Dormant card ([[R3539002]]; the Glimpse and a fusion holding it are in its table). But it read every exile after the inner Spell's `hookResolving` as that Spell's, and judged the Starship's exile a reach ([[R1839000]]). I19 now keeps the runs opened in the step: when a card whose run opened after another's resolves (`cardResolved`), the run goes back to the card still resolving beneath it. A marker, a trap or a turn edge still ends a run as before.

Hearthstone semantics: a spell's text resumes once a spell it cast has resolved.
