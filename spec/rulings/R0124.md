[[§4.4]] step 2 says "hero uses Going Long's value" in the singular because one copy is the ordinary case, but [[§6.2]] already makes Armor stack (printed + Defense +1 + auras), so a second granting card contributes like any other layer: two Going Longs paid 2 give the hero Armor 4, plus any Armor written on the hero itself. This is the opposite of the Anti-oneshot cap of step 3, which takes the smallest cap any such card provides, because a cap is a ceiling and Armor is a reduction
