**Why.** A Pancake's row on the Tokens tab mixes every card that makes Pancakes, and the drill-down of the card that made it showed nothing about what it made: its "Tokens this card makes" lists the tokens its text names ([[R279]]) as faces with Almanac links and no figures, and The Mother Pancake's text names none.

**The segment.** The Extended stats panel ([[R4676001]]) gains **Outputs**, between Choices and Radiant vs normal, for a card the server sends an output row for ([[R4679000]]) or whose text names a token (the drill-down's own "Tokens this card makes"); a card with neither has no Outputs segment. With no row it says "No outputs recorded yet." A card's text naming a card it does not make, a token aside, earns no segment: Call to Chaos names itself and every other edition.

**The table**, its rows in the server's order, best adjusted first, each row: the deck table's Card cell (its cost on its rarity's badge), its name as the reader reads it, its share, its games, the deck table's `TierChip`, its Adjusted ("not enough games" below the floor) and, with Show 95% ranges on, its range under it; then, when any row was offered, Offered and Pick rate, "—" on a row with none. The table scrolls sideways in its own box on a phone, as every `/stats` table does.

**A row opens its output.** Pressing a row, or Enter or Space on it, opens that output's own drill-down in the same dialog, as a Created row does ([[R4155000]]): `?card=<output>` with no `extended`, its details shown. The row goes with the panel, so the focus moves to the Extended stats toggle, which the output's drill-down keeps, and comes back to whatever opened the dialog first once it closes; Escape and Back still close the dialog ([[R2127000]], [[R3221000]]).

**The tokens section gives way.** While the panel is open the drill-down's "Tokens this card makes" is hidden, as the details column is, and it comes back when the panel closes.

Amended by [[R4707002]] (issue #4707): the segment also shows for a card the server sends a rolled outcome for, its rolled outcomes in tables of their own under the outputs table, which a card that rolled and made nothing leaves out.

**The address.** `extended=outputs` opens the panel on Outputs, or on the first section the card has when it lacks Outputs, as [[R4676001]] reads every section.

**Rejected.**

- **The text's tokens as rows of their own, with no games**: two sources for one list, and "Tokens this card makes" already shows them, whose faces the table has no room for.
- **Every card the text names** as reason to show the segment: Call to Chaos names itself, and Wildar names the cards it makes only as collectible cards.
- **The output row as the opener kept for the focus**: the row is gone once its output's drill-down opens, so the focus would have nowhere to come back to when the dialog closes.
- **Leaving the focus where the row was**: it would fall out of the dialog to the page, and a screen reader would lose its place.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
