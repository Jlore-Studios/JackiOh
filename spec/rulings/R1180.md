Each Growing Felinor's Death summons the next size up: #93 Growing Felinor summons a Growing Felinor Sr (#93.1), the Sr a Growing Felinor Sr Sr (#93.2), the Sr Sr a Growing Felinor Super Senior (#93.3). The Super Senior has no Death and ends the chain. In the designer's text the base Sr and Sr Sr each summon a Sr; read those as copy slips, since a Sr summoning a Sr never ends, and the Radiant faces climb one size each. Each summon is a fresh card, Radiant when the dying card was on its Radiant face, placed in the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]); with no zone open nothing is summoned. A Sr, Sr Sr or Super Senior is a unit token: it vanishes when it leaves the field and still fires its Death ([[R11]]). No size can be in Defense Position ([[§6.1]]).
