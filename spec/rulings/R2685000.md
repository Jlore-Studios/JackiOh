A rematch offer, a person's or a ladder bot's ([[R639000]]), is taken only while no more than `REMATCH_OFFER_TTL_MS` has passed since the finished match's `finishedAt`: the window a seat's socket reopens in ([[R1285000]]), a co-op Play again's ([[R1398000]], [[R2092000]]) and the reaper's. It amends [[R672]], whose offer lived that long from its own time, kept by one clock per finished match that every offer from either seat restarted: A's offer at minute 0, B's mismatched one at minute 9 and B's matching one at minute 18 made a game from A's eighteen-minute-old offer, with A long gone and, in a ranked match, their rating at stake in a game they lose by disconnecting.

Past the window an offer lapses silently, as the co-op one does: it records nothing, not its stakes, its lean nor its telemetry, it tells no actor and makes no game, and any entry that has not made a game is dropped, so A's old offer cannot meet a new one. It answers `matchId` null, not an error. An entry that already made a game inside the window keeps its id, so a seat that offers or polls late still learns where to go. A finished row with no `finishedAt` is outside the window, as it is for its socket ([[R1285000]]). Two equal offers inside it make exactly one game as before, a ladder bot's included.

Hearthstone semantics: a rematch needs both players at the finished game, and a player who has left the end screen cannot be held to what they asked there.

Rejected: a clock per seat's offer, which also closes the gap but needs more state, and leaves the 1v1 window counted from each offer while co-op's is counted from the match's end; an error answer for a late offer, since the end screen's buttons are already gone and co-op's lapses silently; a new constant, since the window already is one.
