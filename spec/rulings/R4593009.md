Emergent Mind remembers, at each of its controller's ends of turn, their `cards_played_this_turn` (itself included on the turn it is played) and gains for it at their next start of turn; a Mind that arrived on the opponent's turn gains nothing at its first start. Rejected: a `TurnLog` field (which would move every golden trace); the count lives in card memory.
