[[R1875006]] makes Stinkyscape #138 Pocket Peptides a would-die replacement whose follow-up, a cast of M #36 CN Peptides, resolves before anything moves, and the state check that follows the cast judges the board it left. A Trap is spent as it fires, before that follow-up runs, so the check inside the cast never meets it. A card its firing does not spend can hold the same text: a fusion that is no Trap, which plays every ingredient's text ([[R77]], [[R102]]). The daily fuzz's seed 1322 had one, a Field Spell fused of Book of Buff, Pocket Peptides and Organic Produce, and ran out of stack: a CN Peptides that destroys the Unit it was cast at leaves a Unit still dying in combat, so the check inside the cast offered it to the same Field Spell, which cast again, and the check inside that cast did the same, without end. The spec did not say whether a card may answer the checks inside its own follow-up; this note says it may not, as a spent Trap cannot. Hearthstone has no fused cards, and a Secret, the Trap's model, is spent before its effect resolves.

- **Held out.** While a card's would-die follow-up resolves first, it answers no would-die check made inside it, the casts' own included. Every other card answers them as before, and a second Pocket Peptides set beside it fires and is spent as any Trap does.
- **After it.** Once the follow-up has resolved, the check that starts over passes the card by as [[R1875006]] says. A later check, after another death, may meet it again, since its firing never spent it.
- **What is unchanged.** A Pocket Peptides set as a Trap is spent before its follow-up runs, so nothing changes for it, and a check inside the follow-up still judges every Unit again on the board the cast left ([[R460]]).
