The designer's comment (MaxGoetzmann, 2026-10-01): a Trap "reveals" where text said it "activates", avoiding confusion with the Activate keyword ([[§6.2]], [[R384]]). Every trap condition reads "Reveals when", "this reveals" or "once this has revealed", and a face-down Trap with no reveal condition stays live ([[R403]]). The Activate keyword, its `activate` action, its `activated` event and `memory.activated` keep their names
