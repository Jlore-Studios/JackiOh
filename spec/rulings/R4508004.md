S+ #78 Slashed Dream Cries in Red exiles its lane with every permanent in it and voids it ([[R3554002]]), and S+ #77 Black Hole's state check swallows what stands in its lane ([[R3554000]]). Both took a Unit Immune to Spells, and no ruling said whether a Spell's may.
**They may.** The board loses the lane: the slash voids it for the rest of the game, a lane on neither half that nothing may hold ([[R3554003]]), and the open hole is the board's own rule, run by the state check whatever brings a card into its lane, not an effect of the Spell reaching for the card ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]). So the immune Unit goes with its lane, as every other permanent there does.
**What I21 allows.** An `exiled` naming an immune Unit in the run of a card of `LANE_TAKERS` (S+ #77, S+ #78), a fusion holding one, or a copier running its text, until the next card's hook, trap or turn edge. Another Spell's exile of it is still a reach.
Rejected: leaving the Unit in the slashed lane, which is on neither half and which nothing may hold ([[R3554002]]), or in the hole's lane, which the state check would then swallow at its next pass all the same.
