The series screen and the board's series banner show the score and which of each side's decks have won and are locked: your own decks by name, with their cards and how many games each has played; the other side's only by slot number and whether each has won, and whether their pick is in. A game's history shows the slot each side played once the game has begun. Neither side is shown the other's deck names, card lists or pending pick at any point ([[§9.1]])
