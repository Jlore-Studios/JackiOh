Amends [[R765]] for online 1v1 matches. While a match's board shows a view with no result, every way off it in the client is held by the page's leave guard ([[R1754000]]): the board's Back, the browser's Back or Android's back gesture, and any other move in the app, such as a link, the command menu, a friend's Watch or a challenge's Accept. Each asks "Leave the match? It goes on without you. Come back within 60 seconds or it counts as a loss." The number is `DISCONNECT_GRACE_SECONDS`, the server's disconnect grace ([[§9.5]], [[R79]]). Stay is the default. It has the focus, Escape, Back ([[R2127000]]) or a click outside the panel means the same, and it sends nothing. Leave makes the move the player took, and the menus' banner offers Rejoin ([[R765]]). A move the client makes for itself (`navigate`'s `force`) is never held. Once the view has a result, or the socket has closed for good (a voided match) or been refused, nothing is left to come back to: every way off goes at once, and a question still open closes. A reload or a closed tab asks nothing, since a reload rejoins the match at once. The co-op table asks nothing yet: there a disconnect hands the seat to the AI rather than losing ([[§13.8]]), which needs other words.

Rejected: holding the move before the first view, when the loading panel has no board to leave; and making the held move by itself when the result arrives with the question open, which would leave a board the player could now leave at once.
