IA #85 Merge Conflict needs two friendly Units: the play is refused while the board offers fewer ([[R703]]). The first one chosen is kept and the second fused into it; IA #93 Gestalt keeps itself and takes every other friendly Unit in lane order. Fused cards cease to exist with no Death ([[R77]]), and a fused-in Cry does not run ([[§6.2]]). The Radiant faces grant the result Rush and Divine Shield, Gestalt's even when nothing was fused.

Why: a Fuse keeps one ingredient ([[R77]]), and the text names no other keeper than the first pick, or itself. Rejected: keeping the second Unit chosen, which no line prefers.
