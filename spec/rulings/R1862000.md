A Stinkyscape weapon's "Activate: Deal N damage to an enemy" takes a declared target ([[R81]]), an enemy Unit or the enemy hero, as the weapon kit's fixture reads it ([[R1841001]]), and the hit is `damage` from the weapon, or from the Unit it is equipped into. "With Cleave" is Cleave's splash on that hit: the same hit, with the same riders, on each Unit beside a Unit target on its side and row ([[§3.1]], `adjacent_to`), the neighbors read before the first hit lands, as Hearthstone's Explosive Shot hits the minions beside its target; a hero target has none. "With Pierce" is `ignoreArmor` ([[R346]]): each hit skips [[§4.4]] step 2, as #44 True Strike states it. Neither keyword is given to the weapon: a Unit's Cleave and Pierce are its attack's ([[§6.1]]), and the Activate is no attack, so nothing strikes back and a host's own attacks are as they were. Rejected: granting the weapon or its host Cleave and Pierce, which would change its attacks and would not reach the Activate's hit.

Amended by issue #2521: Beam, Cannon and Repeater v3 to v5 now declare an enemy Unit only, so the enemy hero is never their target, and their half damage to heroes ([[R1841001]]) is gone with it; Jade Launcher (#142) still takes an enemy, hero included.
