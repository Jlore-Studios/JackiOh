"You have Spell Damage +N this turn" (Meditative+ #11 Living Mana Potion's Activate, [[R384]]) is a player modifier on the player whose effect gives it, added to their Spell Damage, the one sum [[§4.4]] step 0 reads ([[R1180000]]), from the moment it is gained until the cleanup of that turn ([[§2.2]]). It has no source, so it stays when the card that gave it leaves the field, its base face's own Tribute included; two stack, each its own modifier; the opponent's Spells are untouched. Both seats see it as a badge.
