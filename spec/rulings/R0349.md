A Unit that prints no Radiant form of its own is, made Radiant, its base face with its attack and health doubled (`RADIANT_FALLBACK_FACTOR`): the same keywords and text, and a summon's X/X (`statsOverride`) doubled with it, so a Radiant Ghoul Token summoned 3/3 is a 6/6. It is [[§10.4]] layer 1's reading, so it holds whenever the flag is set, on the field at once like any Radiant swap ([[§5.2]]) with buffs and damage kept, and the X on the instance is never rewritten. The catalog marks such a card `radiantFallback` and prints the fallback as its Radiant face, which `validate-catalog` holds it to; in Core only the Ghoul Token is one. A fused or crafted card is not: [[R77]] builds its Radiant form from its ingredients' Radiant forms, and an ingredient with none lends its fallback, its doubled X included
