[[R1882004]]'s death watch answers each Acclaimed death with a random Call to Chaos cast as a new chain, [[R28]]'s cap counting only within that chain, and a cast that summons an Acclaimed Unit and kills it starts another, so nothing bounded the chains a turn could start (REVIEW A3.6: every self-recursive effect states a terminator). The watch casts at most `CHAOS_STINKY_DEATH_WATCH_CASTS_PER_TURN` (5) Calls to Chaos for one player in one turn, whichever turn it is and however many watchers they hold: the count is the caster's, the player who controlled the dead card (`PlayerState.deathWatchCasts`, kept for its turn as `draws` is, a count from an earlier turn reading 0), and each death past it that turn casts nothing, with nothing rolled. The other player's count is their own. Five lets a player at the watcher cap (three) answer one death in full and part of a second; the figure is a rules number (CLAUDE.md rule 9), as [[R1241]]'s cap on M #95's rest-of-game cast is. Rejected: a cap on the deaths it answers, which a three-watcher player would spend on one death.
