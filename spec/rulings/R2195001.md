One name names one card ([[R381]], [[R739]]), with one exception the owner asked for on 2026-10-28 (#2195): a Training Grounds snapshot #41–#60 keeps the name of the Core card its `snapshotOf` names (TG #41 Bigot and Core #2 Bigot), in English and in Chinese ([[R1303]]). Exactly that pair may share a name: two snapshots, a snapshot and a card of another set or another Core card, a Training Grounds card outside #41–#60, or any two other cards still may not. `catalog check` holds a `snapshotOf` to a Training Grounds entry naming a Core card of its own name. What the client keys on a name keys on the Core card's alone: a hero portrait's full art ([[R1017001]]) is the Core card's cosmetic ([[R1313000]]), so a snapshot that shares a hero card's name prints its normal face.
