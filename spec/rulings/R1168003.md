[[§13.4]]'s lethal judge counts all of a seat's life, own HP and its team's pool together (the pool
alone once the seat is spent), and [[R964000]] reads the AI's life the same way. Three other judges
read the hero's health alone, so a Duos hero low on own HP looked killable with most of its team's
life left.

- **[[§10.7]]'s scorer** judges "lethal available" against own HP and the pool together, for Core #97
  Zephyrs, C+ #27 Zephrys Zealotism and Meditative #71 Pareto Optimality's judge.
- **The Radiant Mayor Medinamogger's Lucky** ([[R1201]], Meditative #86) ranks a hero as destroyed
  only by a hit that would take both.
- **My Pawn's projection** ([[R44]], [[R176]], Core #96): a First Strike defender's Lifesteal heals
  before the attacker's hit lands, and a spent hero's heal stops at its pool's cap, so the projection
  takes that heal at what it would heal. Any other heal lands with the hit, where the subtraction is
  exact.

A 1v1 game reads exactly as before: there a hero's health is all its life.
