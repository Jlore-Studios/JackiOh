A practice claim ([[§14.8]]) burns its seed before its check ([[R1303001]]), and its check and pay
run in a task of their own ([[R1688000]]). The spec did not say what becomes of a claim whose pay
never lands: every try failed, the server's shutdown closed the store under it, or a crash took it.
The claim is kept with its seed, its result and log written in the transaction that burns it, until
it settles, paid or refused, once. A claim that passes its check is paid in one transaction whose
first write marks the seed paid, and only where it has no mark yet, so a pay lands once whoever
runs it and a try that rolls back takes its mark with it; a claim its check refuses is marked
refused. A settled claim drops its log. A server that stops waits, inside `SHUTDOWN_DRAIN_SECONDS`,
for every claim it is settling, one whose caller has gone included, before it closes the store. The
retention loop settles every claim still unsettled `PRACTICE_SETTLE_GRACE_SECONDS` (600) after it
was made, as it files again a game record whose write failed ([[R1596000]]): one at a time, each
checked again under the same `PRACTICE_VERIFY_CONCURRENCY` permits, then paid at the sweep's own
time, or marked refused. A swept pay keeps no board ([[R1303003]]), since the account may have
played a newer game whose board the late one would replace. The sweep does nothing while the
economy is off; its claims wait. The account's prune deletes an old seed only once its claim has
settled; a seed claimed before the claim was kept holds none, so nothing settles it and the prune
deletes it as before. A live claim answers as before: its pay, its refusal, or an error when every
try fails, and `already_claimed` when the sweep settled it first.

Hearthstone semantics: a reward is granted once, and a server that failed to grant it grants it
later.

Rejected: a sweep with no grace, which would race every live settle for its seed and fold the log
again for a claim about to pay; writing a swept game's board, which could put an older game's board over
a newer one's; and paying on the claimed result without checking it again, which would pay a
forged claim whose check never ran.
