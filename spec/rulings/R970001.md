title: "A second change to the Terms or the Privacy Policy on one day is a new version, with a letter (issue #970)"

`TERMS_VERSION` is the Terms' "Last updated" date, then the Privacy Policy's ([[R757001]]), so a second change to a page on the day it already changed kept the version, and an account that agreed between the two was never asked about the second. A half of the version may now carry a letter after its date, `b` for the page's second change that day, then `c` and on (`2026-10-15b/2026-10-15b`); the page still shows the date alone. A web test holds each half to its page's date with at most that letter.

So that a change cannot keep the version by mistake, the same test keeps a row for each version: the SHA-256 of both pages' English text as rendered, the Terms then the Privacy Policy. A change to either page's words, a number they read from the configuration included, fails it until the version moves and its row is added; a row is never edited. Rejected: a counter kept beside the dates, which would be a second thing to move by hand with nothing to tell when it was missed.
