The fuzz monitor's I21 blames every event of a Spell's run on the Spell, from its marker ([[R2783000]]) to the next one. A play from the hand is nested, its run ended by its `cardResolved`, which hands the walk back the run the play interrupted. But a run the step began inside (the answer a prompt continues, [[§10.6]]) was ended by nothing, and when the prompt was the Spell's own, asked before its play (a cast on draw's target), the nested play's `cardResolved` handed the walk back the Spell's own run. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 10496 C #3 Book of Heal was cast as it was drawn and resolved, and another card's hit on an immune The Rock later in the step was named as Book of Heal's; plain seed 1140 named a hit after M #30.1 Love Bomb resolved alike.
**The run ends where the Spell resolves.** A `cardResolved` of the card a run belongs to (its marker's source, or its definition when no source is known) ends that run, whether the walk reached it through a nested play or not; what follows is no longer the Spell's.
**What I21 allows.** An event after the running card's own `cardResolved`, up to the next marker. A `cardResolved` of another card leaves the run as it was, and an event before the Spell resolves is still its.
Rejected: an engine marker of a run's end, which moves nothing a player sees ([[R4508005]] rejected it alike).
