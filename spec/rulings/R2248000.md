A summon that names no lane ([[R64]]) of a Unit whose face, as it stands, has Flying goes to the leftmost open unit zone of its side, as any summon does; when its unit row has none, it goes to the leftmost open backrow zone of that side, a Locked one after the unlocked ones as [[R688]] reads a row, and stands there as a Flying Unit played into the backrow stands ([[R1081000]]), a Unit for every rule. With both rows full it fizzles. The unit row stays its home, read first, as a Flying play's default zone reads it. A summon that names a lane is that lane, and a layered summon ([[R1842000]]) and a fill of random Units ([[R1080]]) take unit zones alone, as before. "Fill your board" with a Flying Unit fills every zone a summon of it may take: the unit row's as before ([[R1136000]]), then every empty, unreserved backrow zone, a Locked one included, left to right, each standing there, so S #34 Drone Core VIII and S #158 Stratos Swarm fill both rows ([[R1860008]]). It covers every summon of a card with no zone named: a fresh card (a token, "summon a random …"), a card moved onto the field from a hand, a library or a graveyard, and a copy, whatever summons it, one after another (S #29 to #35's drones, as many as both rows hold), a Recruit of every permanent, a random Unit from hand ([[R1083]]) and a Unit from hand or deck that makes or joins an attack ([[R1202]], [[R1203]]) among them; a random summon with its unit row full draws when its pool holds a Flying Unit and a backrow zone is open, since it may then land ([[R129]]). [[R1081000]] had no summon put a Unit in the backrow; the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2248) lets a Flying one land there, as the set's drones and Anomaly Found!'s boards already stand there ([[R1879001]]), and the owner's ask of 2026-10-30 (issue #2453) makes it every summon's, "fill your board" and the summons that checked only the unit row before they drew or moved included.
