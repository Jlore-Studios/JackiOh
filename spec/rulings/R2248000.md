A summon that names no lane ([[R64]]) of a Unit whose face, as it stands, has Flying goes to the leftmost open unit zone of its side, as any summon does; when its unit row has none, it goes to the leftmost open backrow zone of that side, a Locked one after the unlocked ones as [[R688]] reads a row, and stands there as a Flying Unit played into the backrow stands ([[R1081000]]), a Unit for every rule. With both rows full it fizzles. The unit row stays its home, read first, as a Flying play's default zone reads it. A summon that names a lane is that lane, and "fill your board" and a layered summon fill the unit row alone, as before. It covers every summon of a card with no zone named: a fresh card (a token, "summon a random …"), a card moved onto the field from a hand, a library or a graveyard, and a copy; a random summon with its unit row full draws when its pool holds a Flying Unit and a backrow zone is open, since it may then land ([[R129]]). [[R1081000]] had no summon put a Unit in the backrow; the owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2248) lets a Flying one land there, as the set's drones and Anomaly Found!'s boards already stand there ([[R1879001]]).
