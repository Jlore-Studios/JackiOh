A private lobby's settings ([[R3730000]]) are its host's to change, `POST /api/lobbies/:code/settings`, and every member sees them: a 1v1 lobby's mode, Best of 1, Conquest or All Random ([[R264]]), and a co-op lobby's difficulty. The format, 1v1 or co-op, is fixed when the lobby is made.

A change takes every member's Ready off, the convention of every party screen, since a Ready is agreement to the game as it was set ([[R3730001]]). A new 1v1 mode takes both picks off too, as no deck of one mode plays in another; a new difficulty keeps them. Setting what is already set changes nothing.

Rejected: a change that keeps the Readys, which would let a host turn a game into another after the guest agreed to it.

Amended by [[R3731000]] (issue #3731): a 2v2 has no settings; its team changes, Switch team and the host's Move, take every Ready off as a settings change does.
