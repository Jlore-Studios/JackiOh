FvH #64 Fiender Artist gives the card its Discover added the Felinor tag, and FvH #74.1 Yile, Acceleration Supremist Prime sets the price of the card its fusion put into the hand: each acts on "the card it just added". A script reads those cards with `added_to_hand_so_far(ctx)`: the instance ids of every `addedToHand` among the running script's own events, the window [[R136]] gives it (`events_from`), so another copy's adds and a trap's that fired mid-action are not its own. Each card is listed once, in the order it arrived, whichever hand it went to: a card an effect added (`add_to_hand`, a copy, a fusion's hand card) and a card the run drew, since a draw lands in the hand through the same add ([[§6.3]] Add to hand). A card a full hand burned never arrived ([[R4]]) and is not listed. A run that a prompt split reads from the step it resumed in, since the adds before the prompt are the earlier action's events; unlike [[R136]]'s summoned units, the continuation does not carry them, so a card that adds and then asks reads its adds before it asks. Hearthstone's reading of "the card added to your hand".
