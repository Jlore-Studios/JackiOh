S+ #28 Zombie-Box prints "Summon a Dormant 1 Gargantuar" and S+ #27 Gargantuar prints no Dormant, so Dormant is the summon's, not the card's: `summon` takes `dormantTurns`, and the fresh card it makes enters the field Dormant with that countdown ([[R1839000]]), counted down and awakened as any Dormant card is, at the start of its controller's Nth turn ([[R1839001]]). Absent or 0, or a card that is not fresh, enters as it would have: awake. A fresh card has never awakened, so [[R3039000]] bars nothing, and a card moved onto the field by `instance` is no fresh summon and keeps what it has. Rejected: a keyword granted to the summon, since Dormant is a status read off a card's text and not a keyword, and a Dormant 1 Gargantuar of its own definition, since the Radiant face summons the same Gargantuar awake.
