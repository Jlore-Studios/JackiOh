A `handAura` entry names which neighbour takes it; a lent card is Radiant only while the pair holds, and its price moves by the lend. A card that leaves the hand keeps its Radiance, and a card made Radiant on its own keeps it too, its lend forgotten — so a lent card played onto the field stays Radiant. Rejected: clearing Radiance with the lend, which would un-Radiant a card that earned it by being played.
