**Why.** In the deck workshop's split view ([[§9.4]]) the next deck opens in the same editor when a deck is deleted, with its own Delete deck button right under the pointer. A quick double click deleted the deck meant, and [[R2119000]]'s rule that a second deletion sends the first at once then held the neighbour for deletion too: unless the player noticed and pressed Undo in time, it was gone for good. Remove on a friend's row ([[§9.5]]) did the same, since the next friend moves up under the pointer. The toast only says "Deck deleted" or "Friend removed", so nothing told the player that a second item had gone.

**The rule.** A deletion that comes within `UNDO_REPEAT_MS` (500 ms, timed on the hold's own timer) of the last one taken is ignored. Nothing is sent, held or hidden, the first deletion stays held with its toast and its window, and the screen does not move on to the next item. `useUndoHold().hold` answers `false` and every caller (Delete deck and Delete trio in the workshop, Delete in the creator ([[§12.11]]), Remove on a friend) then leaves the item where it is. The guard ends with the hold: Undo, a send at once (`sendNow`), `pagehide`, the window's end and leaving the screen all end it, so a deletion right after any of them is taken. A refused press does not extend it, so a third press after the half second is taken. After the half second a second deletion sends the first at once and takes the toast, as [[R2119000]] has it ([[§10.10]]). A player clearing many decks waits half a second between them.

**Rejected.**
- **Disabling or debouncing each Delete button.** That is five call sites instead of one hook, and the next caller would have to remember it.
- **Not opening the neighbour after a delete.** It changes [[R2119000]]'s editor behaviour for every player, not only for a double click.
- **Reading `Date.now()`.** The hook's `UndoTimer` has no clock, and its tests drive it by a manual one.
- **Queueing the second deletion.** That is the unwanted delete.
- **A guard that outlives Undo.** Undo, then Delete, is a deliberate act.
- **A message for a refused press.** The press is a double click's echo, and a message would be noise.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no Hearthstone rule is involved, and the server decides every deletion as before.
