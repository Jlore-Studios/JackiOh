[[§14.6]] says a cosmetic pack is opened for cosmetics, and the issue gives its shape. An opening
of one `CosmeticPack` from the inventory ([[R1305001]]):

- **Its slots** are `COSMETIC_PACK_SLOTS`. Each rolls a cosmetic rarity on the cosmetics' own
  scale, Common to Mythic, not tied to the rarity of any card, a tiered roll on
  `COSMETIC_TIER_WEIGHTS` ([[R1339000]]): the same table as a duplicate's cosmetic ([[R1301002]]),
  Common 550, Rare 250, Epic 150, Legendary 45, Mythic 5 out of `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL`. The first slot,
  `PACK_FLOORED_SLOT`, is floored at `COSMETIC_PACK_FLOOR_RARITY`, so a Common or Rare becomes Epic
  (Epic 950, Legendary 45, Mythic 5) and every pack holds an Epic or better. There is no pity:
  nothing counts the packs since a rarity.
- **What it can drop** is the registry's `cosmetic_pack_cosmetics`: every cosmetic, per-card or
  account, that is not a default and not a starter, which [[§14.2]] gives every account. A chase,
  a Legendary or Mythic cosmetic, drops like any other since [[R1333000]], and the Chase Shard bar
  buys one too ([[R1306002]]).
- **What a slot grants** is one cosmetic of its rarity (or the one it falls to, [[R1306001]]) the
  account does not own, picked uniformly among the rarity's open ones. An account cosmetic is open
  while the account lacks it; a per-card one while some card of the shipped sets' non-token cards
  has it unowned, a full art on its own card alone ([[R1313000]]). A per-card cosmetic then takes
  its card, weighted by `COSMETIC_PACK_CARD_WEIGHTS` toward the cards the account owns: `owned`
  for a card it owns `MAX_COPIES` of, `unowned` for any other. An account cosmetic names no card.
- **A card the account does not own** is granted with the cosmetic, one copy, written to the
  collection with reason `pack` as a card pack's unlocks are. A slot's cosmetic for an owned card
  grants no card.
- **Ownership moves slot by slot**: a cosmetic or card a slot grants is owned by the next slot
  and cannot drop again in the same pack.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's cosmetic packs hold no duplicates it cannot grant either, and
guarantee one slot of a rarity or better; the owner's decisions in #1306 set every number, and the
weights are this ruling's, since the issue says only that the card leans toward owned ones.

Rejected: weighing every (cosmetic, card) pair at once, under which the five account cosmetics
would almost never drop beside hundreds of per-card pairs; and a second constant for the pack's Common share in place of extending
`COSMETIC_PACK_RATES`, which would leave the old four-share one with no reader. Since #1339 one
table, `COSMETIC_TIER_WEIGHTS`, serves both rolls, and `COSMETIC_PACK_RATES` and
`COSMETIC_PACK_GUARANTEED_RATES` are gone ([[R1339000]]).
