A ladder bot's rating moves only with its rated games ([[R610]]), and [[R1453]] gave a player rated above every bot no bot at all. So once the best people climbed past the best bot, the top of the ladder, who most need games, waited on an empty queue; the leaderboard ([[R640000]]) showed a ceiling where the bots stopped, every bot bunched below the people; and nothing in the pool could change it, since the bots under them met nobody stronger. While people outrank every bot, the highest-rated bots now grind: they play more, get better and climb toward the top people, so the top of the leaderboard keeps a bot or two near it and the fallback reaches the top players again.

**When.** Each sizing of the pool ([[R1454]]: as the server boots and every `BOT_POOL_REFRESH_INTERVAL_SECONDS` while `LADDER_BOTS` is `on`) ends by starting or stopping the grind. It starts when the `BOT_GRIND_REFERENCE_RANK`-th (3rd) highest-rated person placed this season is more than `BOT_GRIND_GAP` (200) above the highest-rated active bot: the third person, so that one person far ahead starts nothing. Once on, it stays on until a bot is within `BOT_GRIND_GAP − BOT_GRIND_HYSTERESIS` (100) of that person, so it does not start and stop at every sizing. With fewer placed people than that, or no active bot, it is off. A person's rating here is their hidden rating ([[R603]]), on the scale a bot's own is ([[R610]]); a bot's season row is no person's.

**Who.** The top `BOT_GRINDERS` (3) active bots by rating, ties by id, chosen again at every sizing, so a bot that falls out of the top hands its place on, and one that retires stops. Not the whole pool: a pool rising at once would look as false as the ceiling did.

**More hours.** A grinder is on for `BOT_GRIND_ACTIVE_HOURS` (20) hours a day from its own schedule's first hour, in place of its own 8 to 16 ([[R1454]]), as a person on a grind plays most of the day. Its own schedule is not changed, and is what it keeps to again once it stops.

**Getting better.** A grinder steps up one strength (`BOT_STRENGTHS`, [[R1454]]) every `BOT_GRIND_STEP_GAMES` (10) rated games it wins while grinding, against a person or a bot; a series counts as the one rated game it is ([[R262]]), and a draw or a loss counts nothing. It never steps past the strongest. The count starts again at each step and at each start and stop of its grind. A step is kept when the grind stops, and no bot steps down. The bot plays at its new strength from its next match, a match under way keeping the strength it began with, and its decks stay those it was dealt ([[R750000]]) until the catalog changes. Its rating is still its games' alone ([[R610]]): a step moves no rating, so a bot that steps up still has to win its way up, and the ladder shows no rating a game did not earn.

**Meeting the top players.** [[R1453]]'s "never given one" is relaxed for grinders alone. A player rated above every bot in the pool, free or not, may be paired with a grinder within `MAX_FAIR_GAP` (400, [[R640001]]) of their rating that meets every other condition of [[R1453]] (free, on its schedule, none of their recent opponents, holding what the mode needs), the closest such grinder, ties by id, and with no other bot. A player some bot is rated above is paired exactly as [[R1453]] says, a grinder being one bot among the rest.

**Games between two bots.** So that a grinder can climb when nobody is there to play it, the queue's sweeper ([[§9.5]]), once it has paired every ticket it can, kicks off a match between two bots when all of these hold at that sweep:

- `LADDER_BOTS` is `on`, and the queue holds no open ticket of any mode;
- fewer than `BOT_VS_BOT_MAX_LIVE` (1) matches between two bots are live;
- `BOT_VS_BOT_MIN_GAP_SECONDS` (300) have passed since the last rated game between two bots ended;
- a grinder is ready (in the pool, on its widened schedule, in no match, a side of no series that is not over, [[R641000]], and holding Best-of-1 decks dealt for this catalog, [[R1454]]) and has an opponent: the ready bot, grinding or not, closest in rating to it, ties by id, and none of its last `BOT_RECENT_OPPONENTS` (5) opponents. The lowest-rated such grinder plays, ties by id. With no such pair nothing starts.

The queue is read at the kickoff alone: a match under way plays to its end whoever queues meanwhile, and a person who queues then is paired as [[R1453]] says, with a person or with a bot that is not in it. The two bots are claimed together in one statement, single-shot, as a ticket and its bot are ([[R1453]]), and a start that fails, or never comes, lets both go ([[R1454]]).

The match is ranked and Best of 1, and its seed is the server's; no ticket made it, so its row states its mode, as a rematch's does ([[R672]]). The grinder is Player 1 and takes the first turn, and each bot plays one of its own Best-of-1 decks, picked by the seed and its seat, with that deck's portrait ([[R642]]). The actor plays both seats as it plays any bot's ([[R1452]]), each with its own think times, clock, persona and concessions ([[R639000]]). Neither seat is ever away, so neither loses by `disconnectExpired`; and neither offers the other a rematch or meets one, since a match between two bots draws neither of them a rematch plan ([[R639000]]). No socket comes back to it after a restart, so each sweep that finds a match two bots hold whose actor is not in memory folds it back from its log ([[§9.5]]), and it plays on. It is rated by [[R610]] for both sides: what one bot gains the other loses, which is accepted. Both season rows move by [[R640000]]'s rules, and the rated game records both sides with the `ai` pilot and their bot ids ([[R611]], [[R1451]]). Its game record is a live one whose seats are both `ai`. Nobody can watch it: a watcher watches a friend's match ([[§9.5]]), and a bot is no one's friend.

**What it never counts toward.** The weekly active people who size the pool, who are people alone ([[R1454]]); Jlorious and the percentiles, which read people's rows alone whatever a grinder's rating reaches ([[R640000]]); the human side of any statistics query, of which it has none ([[R1451]]); the people's decklists a bot's decks are drawn from ([[R750000]]); and the live read of the calibration's anchor, which reads the reference bot's games against people alone ([[R748000]]). Like any live game it counts toward the merged statistics ([[R807000]]), the publication gate of the card statistics ([[R654]]) and the queue readout's games of the last hour ([[R640002]]): leaving it out of those would leave a game that was played unrecorded.

**Nothing new for players to see.** No field, read or frame changes (CLAUDE.md rule 7, [[R1452]]): a grinder reads as a person who plays a lot, and a match between two bots is an ordinary ranked match to its record and its match screen.

**The ceiling.** A bot climbs only as far as it can win. The strongest strength is the full AI at a skill of 1, measured at 1200 (`SKILL_ELO_ANCHOR`, [[R748000]]); if the top people stand well above the AI's true strength, the grinders stall near it, and that is accepted. No strength is added here: a sixth strength for grinders alone, with a larger search budget, is a possible follow-up.

**What this leaves.** The calibration reads each bot's games by the strength it holds now ([[R748000]]), so a grinder's games from before a step count toward the strength it stepped into. A match between two bots cut off by a restart after which `LADDER_BOTS` is `off` is folded back by nothing, and stays the reaper's draw at the ceiling ([[R112]]), as a bot's match whose person never comes back does ([[R1452]]).

Hearthstone has no rule for this: its ladder has no bots. The design is the issue's owner's, and where it leaves a choice open the ruling takes what a person on a grind would do.

Rejected: the whole pool grinding, which would move every bot at once; a column on `bots` for when the last match between two bots ended, which the rated games already hold; a coin for the seats, when the grinder is the side the match is played for; a step undone when the grind stops, which would take back a strength the bot won; checking the queue again while a match between two bots is under way, which would end a game for a reason nobody playing could see.
