The designer's Dormant Starship is awakened by an Activate ("pay (3) to awaken"), so awakening is a verb as well as a countdown's end ([[R1839001]]), and "When this awakens" is a new trigger.

**Awaken** ([[§6.3]]) is `awaken { target }`: the one verb that reaches a Dormant card, so it names the card as "this" or by its id without the reach every other verb is held to ([[R1839000]]). The card's status comes off, it is not summoning sick (as Hearthstone's awakened minions are not, so one awakened by its own Activate on the turn it was played may attack at once), and `awakened` reports it, public on both seats. Nothing happens to a card that is not Dormant, nor to one beneath a Stack, which acts for nothing ([[R1839003]]).

**The one ability it keeps.** A Dormant card's text names the Activate it keeps while it sleeps (`StaticFlags.slumber.wakesBy`, [[R384]]): that ability is offered and accepted while the card is Dormant, and every other is neither, as a set Cloaked card keeps its Reveal alone. A card that keeps none has no ability until it awakens.

**When this awakens** ([[§6.2]]) is a trigger on the card's own `awakened` and never another card's (`when_this_awakens`), answered as any field trigger is, in [[R68]]'s order, since the card is awake when the event is dispatched. It answers once per awakening, at the countdown's end or at an effect's.

The new event, `awakened`, has its row in the client's animation table (a brand pulse on the card, 400 ms) and its sound (the Radiant glint), and the log says "{card} awakened".
