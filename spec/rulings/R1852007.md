The Dormant Starship's text: "While Dormant cards cannot be stacked on top of this, if they do the stacked card is exiled before its effect resolves." [[R1839003]] lets a Stack card top a Dormant card; the Dormant Starship is the exception. A Stack card may still be played onto it, and the play is made: it is paid for, it lands on top at [[§10.5]] step 4, it is counted and reported (`cardPlayed`, `summoned`), and then, before anything of it resolves, it is exiled ([[§6.3]] Exile: the exile pile, the exile counter, `exiled`). Step 5 finds it gone, so its Cry is lost as a card a trap took at step 4 loses it ([[R17]]), and it never fused, armed or animated anything. The Starship tops its zone again, still Dormant ([[R13]]). An exile is no death, so a Stack piece exiled so fuses nothing.
