Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event or message changes. Every card the viewer can read stings as it is played (its `cardPlayed`), sized by its rarity: a Common, Rare or Epic Unit or Spell its own small, middling or full sting just after the card whoosh, and a Legendary or Mythic Spell the entrance a Legendary or Mythic Unit already gets on its `summoned`, where that Unit's sting stays. A token stings by its printed rarity, and one with none does not. Under [[R203]] a card behind the sentinel never stings and a Trap's set never does; a card cast as it is drawn keeps its cast-on-draw sting in place of this one. Every sound effect about a Unit on the field (its arrival, a hit on it, its attack, its death, a buff, a lost Divine Shield and the like) comes from that Unit's lane: the middle lane centred, the outer lanes panned part-way to either side, the same for both seats since both rows share the lanes' order on screen; anything about a hero, a hand or a player stays centred, and voice lines are never panned. Every voice line, emote lines included, dips the sound effects for its span, whatever the music's duck setting says, and the effects and voice lines share one light reverb, the music not.
