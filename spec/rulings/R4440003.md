Issue #4440, Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VI, reworks S+ #4 Profe Yates: cost (2), from (4); 3/7, Radiant 6/14, from 6/6 and 12/12; and "Tribute 1; Cry: Summon a random %radiant% trap; Death: shuffle %radiant% Profe Yates Prime into your deck", the designer's `%radiant%` marking the word only the Radiant face prints. So the base face reads "Tribute 1. Cry: Summon a random Trap. Death: Shuffle a Profe Yates Prime into your deck." and the Radiant face "… a random Radiant Trap … a Radiant Profe Yates Prime …", as M #45 Knowledge Breaker and S #147 IN33DH3, the other Catalysts, print their Deaths. It loses its exile, which its Prime carries ([[R4440004]]).

Tribute 1 is #66 The Rock's ([[§6.3]]): its play sacrifices one of its controller's Units, or one Sheep Token worth two ([[§3.2]]), and with none to give it is not playable. The Cry ([[R1]]) is one `summon_random` of a non-token Trap or Field Trap of the sets that ship ([[R380]]), face down into its controller's leftmost open backrow zone ([[R64]]), nothing with none open ([[R129]]), as [[R3567001]]'s draws were; on the Radiant face the Trap arrives Radiant. Since the Cry targets nothing, the play declares no target. The Death is Hearthstone's Deathrattle: any death of it, a destroy, a Tribute or a Sacrifice among them, shuffles one fresh Profe Yates Prime into its controller's deck, Radiant from the Radiant face (`shuffle_into`, Knowledge Breaker's shape); an exile or a Bounce is no death and shuffles none, and a full deck takes none ([[R80]]). Replays of v0.6.9n and before fold under the script that exiled (`script_v0_6_9n`, [[R4154100]], [[R767000]]). Rejected: reading `%radiant%` as Radiant on both faces, which would leave the faces' only difference the stats; and shuffling the Prime into its owner's deck when another player controls Yates, where "your deck" is its controller's as every Death reads it.
