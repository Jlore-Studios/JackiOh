## R1222 — The True Sheep's Cry puts the pick in the hand, and the Sheep stays

Decided (MD-E12, Meditative #88 The True Sheep). "Discover a card with Tribute to replace this
with" Discovers over the non-token cards of every set with a Tribute X play cost ([[R380]],
[[R1221]]), three different options shown to the chooser only ([[§6.3]] Discover), Radiant on the
Radiant face — and the pick goes to its controller's hand. "To replace this with" says what the
card is for: the Sheep, worth 5, pays any printed Tribute alone (the largest is 3, and a set of
one Unit is minimal, [[R101]]), so its Tribute replaces this. A Transform reading would leave
"Worth 5" with nothing to do, so this reading keeps both lines live.
