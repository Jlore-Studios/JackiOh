A Trade ([[R1838000]]) puts the traded card into its owner's deck at a random position under the top card, one rng draw over the positions below it, and then draws, so the draw always brings another card while the deck holds one (`draw::shuffle_into_library_below_top`). Into an empty deck the card is the top and only card, and the draw brings it straight back: the one way a Trade draws its own card. The Syndicate's roll still skips a card drawn straight back ([[R1864003]]). Through v0.5.9f the card went in at any position, the top included, so a Trade could draw it back from a full deck. This is the owner's balance pass (issue #2657).
