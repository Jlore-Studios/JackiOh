A round is one team turn ([[§13.3]]), so both enemy seats swing in the same round, and the damage each deals past its target's own HP comes off the one pool both duels share ([[§13.4]]). [[R964000]] has every AI seat count its whole life, own HP and pool, and left the other duel out: an AI seat at 5 own HP with a pool of 8 read 13 and shrugged off an enemy board of 10, while its spent teammate's enemy board of 4 was about to drain the same pool. Next round the first takes 5 off its own HP and 5 off the pool, the second takes 4, and the team loses with neither seat having defended.

- **The pool's coming loss** (`duos_pool_loss`) is what the enemy board in the teammate's duel could take off the pool next round: the face damage that board could deal the teammate (`face_threat`, read on that duel redacted for the teammate, [[R185]], as the team sees it, [[§13.6]]), less the teammate's own HP while the teammate is not spent, at least 0 and at most the pool. It is 0 when that duel's next turn is not the enemy's: when the duel is not on the teammate's turn, or an extra turn is owed to the teammate ([[R846]]). No swing already under way there is estimated.
- **Where it counts**: where the AI judges whether the enemy can kill the team next round, the seat's life less that loss. That is the evaluation's threat term (`lethal_threat` in place of `threat_per_damage` per point once the threat reaches it, [[§9.9]]) and the gate of the switches to Defense Position the beam keeps past its cut ([[R2976000]]). It counts for the deciding seat only, never for its opponent, whom the decision also scores.
- **Where it does not**: the lethal solver, the hero value, the race, the closing term and the reply's lethal hit keep [[R964000]]'s whole life, since none of them judges the coming round's threat. A 1v1 game has no teammate, so every 1v1 decision, gate, fuzz seed and golden trace is unchanged.
- **It is read again at every decision.** The teammate acts in the same round and may still lower its duel's threat, so early in the round the seat may defend against a loss that does not come: it errs toward defending, never toward losing.

Rejected: the attack side, whether the two seats together can empty the enemy pool, which needs the seats to agree an order across the duels; and playing the teammate's duel through, a second search per decision at a Duos budget already half the practice AI's ([[R2575000]]).
