Meditative+ #19 Grubby Gloves ("Steal the top 2 cards of your opponent's deck. Embiggened (2): 2 random cards of their hand instead") takes the opponent's top `cards` cards in order, with no random number (as [[R801]]). Fewer cards give fewer; an empty deck gives nothing and deals no fatigue. A unit-token card is passed over ([[R218]]).

Embiggened, it takes `cards` random cards of their hand instead ([[R60]]; an empty hand draws nothing, [[R129]]). Either way the cards become the taker's ([[R12]], [[§6.3]] Steal), hidden from the opponent but for the count ([[R97]]), the hand cap burning the overflow ([[§2.4]]).
