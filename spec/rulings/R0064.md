A summon with no named zone takes the leftmost empty, unlocked zone of its row, and "fill your board" summons into every empty unit zone left to right, a Locked one included since patch v0.3.Y ([[R1136000]]; it skipped a Locked one before). A unit collected with Reborn reserves its zone until it returns, and nothing else may enter that zone meanwhile
