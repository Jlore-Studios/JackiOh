Stinkyscape #158 Stratos Swarm's "Fill your board with Stratos" is Stinkyscape #68, the two cards
spelled alike since issue #2327 corrected #68's swapped letters, which S03 had kept as the
list printed them: `fill_board` with that definition — every open unit zone of its caster's, a Dormant
card's zone not one ([[R1839000]]), then, as Stratos flies, every empty backrow zone, each standing
there ([[R2248000]]), each a summoned card of its own definition, no Cry ([[R1]]),
no token; Radiant `radiant: true`. A full board, both rows, summons none. Each is a Starship Piece, so each
that dies fuses ([[R1852000]]). It takes R1860008 of issue #1860's block.
