S+ #82's "For the rest of the game lose 1 mana" is the verb `lose_max_mana`: it lowers the caster's persistent modifier to max mana by `mana` (§2.3's "persistent modifiers"), so max mana is that much lower at every refresh from now on, and floors at 0 as §2.3's formula does. Current mana falls as far as max does, but an empty crystal is the one lost first: a caster at 2 of 4 who loses 1 is at 2 of 3, one at 4 of 4 at 3 of 3, and temporary mana above max keeps its excess. A loss that changes neither number says nothing, as a loss of 0 or less does not. The card is Quickdraw ([[R640]]) and costs (1).

Rejected: next turn only (the text says the rest of the game, and Hinder's `nextTurnMod` is the verb for the one turn); and taking the lost crystal from the full ones before the empty (it would make the mana the caster can still spend this turn smaller for no reason).
