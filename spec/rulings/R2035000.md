The tavern landing shows the account's held quests behind a bookmark on its doors' box.

**Who.** An active account while `ECONOMY_LIVE` is on. Nobody else gets a bookmark or a request:
not a visitor, not a loading or failed account read, not a pending account, and not anyone while
the economy is off, including when nothing is held or due ([[R1304002]]).

**Where.** Under [[R1577000]]'s doors, as no fourth door ([[R1578000]]): a narrow ribbon hung off the
box's bottom-left edge, with the held count, opening a parchment panel that unrolls downward over
the floor without moving the hero ([[R1584000]]). It closes on a second click, on Escape (which
gives the focus back to the bookmark) and on a press outside, and at once under reduced motion.

**What.** `/collection`'s rows: each held quest's title, progress and reward, the time until the
next, and the same "You hold {max} quests" and "No quests yet." lines. The quests are read once on
mount (CLAUDE.md rule 7: the client shows what `GET /api/quests` answers) and again, once per due
time, when the next falls due while the panel is open; a failed read offers Try again in the panel.

Hearthstone semantics: the quest-log icon opens the log in place.

Rejected: a fourth door beside Play, Collection and Shop; a bookmark for pending accounts, whose
read the server would refuse; and a read on every opening instead of on arrival.
