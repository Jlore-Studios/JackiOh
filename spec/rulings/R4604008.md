At the end of each turn every Burn ticks once and is then removed ([[R4154000]]). The tick runs after the turn's `turnEnded`, its trap window and its delayed effects ([[R62]]), and has no marker of its own, so it lands inside whatever run is going, such as a hand Spell's "whenever" hook answering the `turnEnded`. [[R4508005]] had the fuzz monitor's I21 (issue #4508) read the Burn's removal as the tick's only right after the tick's own hit. In plain shard 4's wave the removal came apart from that hit twice:
- the tick's hit on a shared-chained Unit echoed onto the chain's other members first ([[R3554007]]);
- the tick's hit dealt nothing and showed no event at all ([[§4.4]]: a hit of 0 is no damage instance).

**The removal is the tick's.** Between a `turnEnded` and the next turn's `turnStarted`, or in a step that resumes a turn's end (`Phase::End`), I21 allows a Burn's removal (`counterChanged`, Burn, 0) on a Unit Immune to Spells, whatever came between it and its tick's hit. Outside a turn's end, a Burn removed with no tick right before it is still a reach.
Rejected: following each tick's hit through its echoes to its removal. The events do not say which hit a removal closes, and a tick of 0 leaves nothing to follow.
