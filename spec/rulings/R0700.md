Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A Unit the viewer can see that enters a unit zone (a `summoned` into the units row, played, summoned, from a graveyard or a token, or an `animated` card) lands with a weight read off its total stats: Attack + Health + 2 × Armor + 4 if it has Indestructible + 4 × each Tribute the card requires. The numbers are the Unit's as it stands in the newest view the client has when the landing is queued (its Radiant stats when Radiant; Armor as the view shows it, printed plus aura, never Defense Position's +1); the Tributes are the face's printed "Tribute N", which the view does not carry; a Unit already gone from that view is read off its printed face. The tiers are Tiny ≤ 5, Small ≤ 11, Medium ≤ 17, Large ≤ 27, Huge ≤ 39 and MASSIVE from 40 (`UNIT_SLAM` in apps/web `game/damageFeel.ts`). A Unit is weighed once, as it lands: a later buff that would change its tier does not slam it again. A Unit behind the sentinel never slams ([[R97]]), and the opponent's Units, the AI's included, slam exactly as the viewer's do
