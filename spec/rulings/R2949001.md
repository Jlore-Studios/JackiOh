The owner's answer to #2725's third question: the versions are to be spread so that cards differ, not the same
cards for every look. Animated does not change: an Epic, offered on 239 cards as [[R2725001]] has it.

**A sample.** A clause of an offer ([[R2725001]]) may carry a `sample`, `{ seed, percent }`: the clause then
takes a card its other fields take only when the FNV-1a hash (over UTF-16, the replay's) of `<seed>/<card id>`,
modulo `OFFER_SAMPLE_PERCENT_SCALE` (100), falls below `percent`. It is data, a pure function of the card's id,
so the server and every client read the same cards, and the client still reads the server's reading by id
(`COSMETIC_OFFERS`), never the clauses. The registry's check refuses a percent of 0 or of the whole scale (a
sample takes some cards and not all) and a seed that is not a lower-case slug. Two entries with the same clauses
and different seeds land on different cards, so each later version (#2940, #2942, #2943, #2944) samples by a seed
of its own.

**Full art.** The Full art version's Rare and Epic Unit clause and its Legendary and Mythic clause each sample 60
in 100 by the seed `version-full-art`; its clause of the 21 full-art cards ([[R2655001]]) samples nothing, so
every one of them stays offered and no pick worn today stops working. It is offered on 157 cards, at least
`VERSION_OFFER_MIN_PER_SET` of every set that ships (Meditative+ on 4, its fewest) and at least
`VERSION_OFFER_MIN_CARDS` in all, and a Legacy card the sample takes keeps it as any Rare or Epic Unit
([[R2725003]]).

**Signature** is offered exactly where both the Full art version and Animated are ([[R2949000]]): Full art's
clauses with Animated's `except` of the four Legacy cards, 155 cards. A test holds its offer to the intersection
of the other two, and Full art's to a strict share of what its clauses take whole, the 21 among it.

Nobody owned the Full art version on a card it now leaves out when this shipped: the economy's production build
had no version yet. A ledger row of one there would stay owned and become unwearable, as an offer narrowed
always leaves it ([[R2725001]]).
