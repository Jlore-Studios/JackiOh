Immutable blocks Transform, Vanilla, Fuse-onto, Nerf and Buff ([[R23]], [[R386]]) in every zone it holds in, since [[R4353000]] on every Indestructible Unit card too, and since [[R4353001]] a Spell may be aimed at an Immutable card and must leave its text alone as it resolves. No check of the fuzz monitor read it (issue #4609). The monitor's I23 now fails a step that changes an Immutable card's text, and this is what it judges.
**Which cards.** A card is Immutable for I23 by the Immutable keyword, or by Indestructible on a card whose running face prints the Unit type in the catalog ([[R4353000]]): read on the field face-up through the layers ([[§10.4]]), in a hand or a library by the card's own keywords, which a Nerf reads there. A Dormant card ([[R1839000]]), a face-down Trap or Field Trap (its text sleeps), a card in any other zone and an Indestructible Field Spell, which is no Unit card, are not judged. A card is judged when it is Immutable after the step with the text it began with put back on it, so a grant or an aura that lapses inside the step lets it go ("Immutable for as long as it is Indestructible"), while a Vanilla or a Nerf that took its own Indestructible away does not; a card gone from those zones is judged by its own keywords.
**What fails.** A `transformed` of it (a Vanilla's, which names its own definition on both sides, and a copied or fused text's included), a `degraded` or an `upgraded` of it, and a `fused` whose kept card is it, each one finding naming the card; and the card's record holding a different definition, Vanilla flag, tuning, hidden Nerf count or copied text after the step with no such event. Radiant is the card's own text ([[R23]]) and is never read. The fused ingredients are not judged: Immutable stops a Fuse onto the card, and an ingredient is taken whole. What is lawful besides is [[R4609001]]'s, [[R4609002]]'s and [[R4609003]]'s.
**How it reads.** Off the events, the state and the catalog, never through `layers::is_immutable`, `layers::immutable_by` or `restrictions`, on the played path, on I14's branches and in each Duos duel. A sweep plays every shipped card whose script calls a verb that rewrites a card's text, on both faces, at a board of Immutable stand-ins, aimed at each one it may, and holds each stand-in's text as it was.
