FvH #100 is an edition of Core #95: Core's subsystem rolls one entry of its table (`CHAOS_FVH_EFFECTS`), three different ones on the Radiant face resolved in table order ([[R423]]), and both players read each rolled entry's label, its printed clause at the printed numbers ([[R436]]); a tuned number changes what an entry does, never its label. Every pool is the non-token cards of every set, held ones included, as [[R3570000]]'s; each summon is its own draw ([[R60]]), placed per [[R64]], fewer with fewer open zones ([[R129]]), and runs no Cry ([[R1]]).

1. `felinors` random Felinor Units whose base face has a Death or prints Reborn ([[R5239002]]'s `anyOf`).
2. `humans` random Human Units whose base face has an Aura: a board, hand or cost aura.
3. Every enemy Unit card on the field, in its owner's hand and in their deck gains Fragile ([[R5236010]]); a Field Spell gains none.
4. `plagiarized` random casts of cards tagged Plagiarized ([[R5235002]], [[R452]]): a Spell resolves, a permanent is played free into a zone ([[R70]]), every choice random ([[R656]]).
5. and 6. Each empty unit zone of the caster's filled with a Radiant Unit of the first tribe (Felinors, then Humans), then each of the enemy's with a base Unit of the other ([[R1080]]).
7. Every enemy Unit and Field Spell card in its owner's hand, deck and graveyard is made GIGA, entering the field GIGA ([[R3546002]]); the field's cards are not.
8. A Radiant FvH #115 Call to Chaos Vending Machine into the caster's backrow; a full backrow takes none.
9. FvH #100.1 Felinor Golem, then #100.2 Human Golem, on their base faces.
10. Core's recursion, a link of the chain [[R28]] caps, that never draws this edition's own card, as [[R3570005]]'s.
