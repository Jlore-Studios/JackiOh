Since patch v0.2.0 ([[R429]]) the Fib index is the times the card has been played, this play included, + 1 (Radiant + 3, [[R275]]), read off the instance ([[§10.1]]), and its cost plays no part: `costMod`, player discounts and the cost paid change its price, never its damage, so a 1st play deals Fib(2) = 1 on the base face and Fib(4) = 3 on the Radiant whatever it cost. The index clamps at 11 ([[R25]]). The end-of-turn return raises its own cost (printed plus `costMod`, [[R65]]) by 1 and never above (4), so at (4) or more it adds nothing
