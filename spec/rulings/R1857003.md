The Stinkyscape set prints five labels no shipped card does: `Activate 3:` to `Activate 6:`, an Activate of that many uses a turn ([[R384]], [[R1751000]]), on its weapons and its #65 and #66, and `Embiggened (5):`, the Embiggened version of #79 Orca, which costs 4 embiggen 5 ([[R1175000]]). Each starts a line of its own as [[R366]]'s labels do, in English and in Chinese ([[R1302]]), where they read 激活3： to 激活6： and 加码(5)： (`chinese-terms.json`).
