C+ #29 Portal to the Past falls back when its caster's last board gives no card: there is none (hotseat, the AI's seat), it is empty (a first game), every entry was dropped as the match was created because the match's catalog lacks it (an older patch's card, [[R564]]), or it holds only Portal itself, which it never gives ([[R387]]). Then the base face Discovers one of 3 different cards (fewer if the pool holds fewer) of the pool [[R3837004]] defines, and the Radiant face adds `cards` (3) different ones of it to the hand. Each is a new card its caster owns, on its base face, costing `setCost` (0), as the last board's cards do. This replaces [[R417]]'s fizzle, which drew no random number ([[R129]]); a board that gives at least one card plays as it did, and draws as it did. Through v0.6.0d such a board gave nothing (`script_v0_6_0d`, kept, [[R767000]]).

Rejected: the fallback's cards on the Radiant face for the Radiant Portal (the last board's cards keep their own face, and the owner named none), and the opponent's last board in place of the caster's (it is never the caster's "last game").
