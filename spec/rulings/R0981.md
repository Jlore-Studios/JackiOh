MD-C6: the relation runs from your last played card to the card now played. It is positive when the last card's element generates the new one (木→火→土→金→水→木) and negative when it overcomes it (木→土→水→火→金→木). The same element, the two reverse relations and a player's first play are neutral, so a random play is positive one time in five and negative one time in five.
