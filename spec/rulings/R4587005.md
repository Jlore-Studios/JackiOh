IA #55 Heat Sink's "Whenever your hero takes damage on your turn" counts a hit only when Health gets through: the `Damage` event's `amount` above 0, after Armor ([[§4.4]], [[§6.1]]), so a hit its Armor takes whole counts nothing. It must land on its controller's turn ([[R1875000]]): a hit on the opponent's turn counts nothing, even to its controller's hero. Losing Health is no damage ([[R18]]) and counts nothing.
