A Vanilla takes a Unit's Burn and then its Toxic off with its text, each reported by `counterChanged` at value 0 with no `placed`, as it removes Frozen ([[R1174000]]); a Vanilla that Immutable refuses, or on a Unit already Vanilla, clears nothing. A Nerf does not: Burn and Toxic are counters, not keywords a Nerf removes, and Degrade's menu holds neither, so a Nerf leaves them as it leaves Plague Counters and the Brittle of a Unit. [[R78]]'s reset takes both off as the card leaves the field. Rejected: keeping them through a Vanilla, as it keeps a Unit's damage.
