The issue asked for a Jlorious Starship granted exactly as the Jlorious card back is ([[R2755000]]),
and backfilled to everyone who already reached Jlorious. This rules how.

**What it is.** `starship-hull-jlorious`, a hull design ([[R3335000]]), Mythic, marked `jlorious` in the
registry, so earned only ([[R3335002]]): no chase, default, starter or per-card cosmetic. Its design
carries its own livery, violet and ivory under the star emblem, never Radiant's gold.

**The one way to own it.** The rated game that grants a person the Jlorious card back grants them the
Jlorious Starship beside it, in the same transaction, with the reason `jlorious` and the game as its
reference: each of its people who stands in Jlorious after it and every bystander whose Jlorious peak it
writes ([[R607]]). A person who owns it already gets no second row; a ladder bot, never Jlorious, never
owns it ([[R640000]]); falling out of Jlorious takes nothing back.

**The backfill.** Migration 0086 grants it once to every person whose season row holds a Jlorious peak,
as 0074 granted the back, a ledger row each with the ref `jlorious:0086`; one who owns it already is
passed over, so each ledger still sums to what is held ([[§14.11]]).

Hearthstone semantics: the top of the ladder earns its cosmetic reward, and keeps it.

Rejected: granting it at a season's end alone, which [[R2755000]] rejected for the card back too.
