Setting a Figment is a fresh summon, face-down, into the leftmost open backrow zone; with no open zone, nothing happens ([[§3.2]]). Its `startOfGame` clause rolls `costOverride` uniformly from its declared `low` to `high` as it arrives — 1 to 4, 2 to 4 on a Radiant Figment — through the match rng, one draw per arrival. It reveals on a declared enemy attack (a forced attack declares nothing, [[R53]]), does nothing ([[R61]]), then ceases to exist ([[R11]]).
