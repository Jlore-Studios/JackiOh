C #37 Last Hurrah reads "Draw your deck. At the end of this turn, discard {discard} cards." and its Radiant face "Draw your deck. At the end of your next turn, discard {discard} cards." (`discard` 6 on both faces, fewer being better and never below 1, [[R386]]), where through v0.5.11o it discarded the whole hand. The delayed discard (`discardHandAtTurnEnd`, [[R458]]) takes an optional `count`, kept in the delayed entry's data as the numbers of every delayed continuation are ([[§10.1]]), so the entry survives a JSON round trip. An entry with no count discards the whole hand as before, in hand order and drawing no random number.

- **A counted entry discards through the path of every "discard N".** It takes that many cards from the hand one at a time through the match rng ([[R682]]: a discard is random cards unless the card says "of your choice", and Last Hurrah says neither), each card a `discarded` event, so "whenever you discard" and C #64 Malzahar's Recycler see each card, one effect discarding 6 ([[R458]]). A hand of fewer cards than the count loses all of them, a count of 0 nothing, and a discard guard stops it as it stops any effect's discard ([[R800]]). It opens no prompt and runs in the end-of-turn delayed-effect step ([[R62]]), as the whole-hand form always did.
- **The count is read as the Spell is played.** `discard` is read through `param` as the Cry resolves, so a Buff or Nerf made before the play moves it ([[R386]]) and one made after moves nothing: the entry already holds its number. A Spell declares every number it prints ([[R1748003]]).
- **The hand is the one held at the end of the turn.** A card played between the Spell and the end of the turn is not in it, and a card drawn between is, so 6 of 8 cards held go and 2 stay.
- **Radiant.** The same count at the end of the controller's next turn, the turn it was played on passing it by ([[R458]]); it keeps its later timing, which is its upgrade ([[R275]]).
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11o folds under the script it ran, `script_v0_5_11o`, whose entry carries no count and discards the whole hand ([[R767000]]).

Rejected: discarding the whole hand without a random draw whenever it holds no more than the count, which would leave the rng cursor elsewhere than every other "discard N" does for the same hand; a discard "of your choice", which the card does not say and which would open a prompt at the end of a turn the opponent may be waiting on ([[R682]]).
