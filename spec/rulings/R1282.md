A card with wide tribute ([[§4.1]]) specifies a tribute cost and a maximum cost threshold (`cheap`, typically 1). The required sacrifices may be paid by any permanent on either side of the board whose cost is at most the threshold, in addition to the player's own Units. Dormant or buried cards cannot pay tribute. Each permanent sacrificed is sent to its owner's graveyard. In the AI search, when the number of valid tribute combinations exceeds `AI_SEARCH.tribute_sets` (120), the candidate moves are capped to the best 120 sets, prioritizing sets that sacrifice the fewest of the AI's own cards ([[R90]]).
