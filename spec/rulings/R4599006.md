S+ #7 Churnwalker chains a side's Units: what a shared-chained Unit is dealt is dealt again to every other member, and a hit on a split-chained Unit is divided among every member ([[R3554007]]). Each echo and share is a hit from the first hit's source. The daily fuzz's plain seeds 4247, 6093, 6762, 7190, 7728, 11563 and 12279 each had a Spell's hit echo or split onto a member Immune to Spells, through its Armor, onto its Divine Shield or into its health.
**The Spell passes it by.** A Spell passes an immune field Unit by, whatever its wording ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]), and an echo or a share of its hit is still its damage. So a Spell's echo skips an immune member, and a Spell's hit on a split-chained Unit is divided among the members it may reach, the target among them: when the target is the only one, it takes the hit whole. A hit from any other source reaches every member as before.
Rejected: dividing a Spell's hit among every member and dropping the immune member's share, which would make the immunity soak damage the Spell aimed elsewhere.
