`GET /api/admin/leaderboard?ladder=` answers a dev account ([[R3074000]]) every season row of the ladder this season, Constructed when none is named: people and ladder bots, placed or still placing, highest rating first, a tie by profile id. Each row carries its `profileId` and `username`, its `kind`, `person` or `bot`, its hidden rating, its record, its visible rank read exactly as the public leaderboard and the match screen read it ([[R612]], [[R640000]]), whether the rating is hand-set ([[R3074001]]) and whether it is the caller's own; a bot's row carries its strength, its persona and whether it is retired, read-only, from its `bots` row ([[R1454]]).

This is the one read that tells a bot from a person, and the one that shows a rating that is not the caller's own: it amends [[R1452]]'s "nothing a client reads labels a bot" and [[R612]]'s "no read carries the hidden rating" for the dev dashboard alone. `GET /api/leaderboard`, `GET /api/ranked`, the match screen's ranks and every other player route are unchanged, for a dev account as for anyone: none carries `kind`, a rating, a deviation, a persona or a strength.
