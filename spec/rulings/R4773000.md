---
id: R4773000
title: On a phone a mounted game board locks the page against scroll, bounce and pull-to-refresh
cards: "§10.10, R1581000, issue #4773"
proven_in:
  - e2e/cypress/component/board-scroll-lock.cy.tsx
  - apps/web/src/game/Game.test.tsx
---
While a `Game` is mounted, the document never scrolls or overscrolls on touch: no vertical or
horizontal page scroll from a swipe anywhere on the board, the hands, the seats or the controls,
no rubber-band bounce and no pull-to-refresh, and no page movement at all during a drag or an aim
(`:root[data-dragging]`), wherever the press started and wherever the finger travels. Taps,
long presses, card drags and drag-to-attack work as before.

`Game` sets `data-game-scroll-lock` on the document element while it is hosted (beside
[[R1581000]]'s menus, which this ruling does not cover: that ruling is about menu screens, and
explicitly not the boards) and clears it on unmount, so leaving the game restores the page's
normal scrolling. Off that attribute the document carries `overscroll-behavior: none` and the
body `overflow: hidden`, and the board carries `touch-action: none`, which stops the browser from
starting a pan under a finger that is aiming: once a pan has started, no `preventDefault` can
take it back.

The lock stays on while a selection menu is open. Every menu (a Discover and the other card
pickers, mulligan, `LanePick`, the Marketplace, the pile browser, the match menu, a `Dialog`,
the settings) and the game log keep scrolling inside their own regions with
`overscroll-behavior: contain` on their own scrollers. A Discover with more options than fit
still scrolls to the rest, and [[R658]]'s rule stands: a card option lifts on touch while list
options keep the panel scrolling. See [[§10.10]] for the client's rendering contract.

Hearthstone semantics: its board is an app-like surface with no bounce, and its sheets scroll
within themselves.

**Proof.** `board-scroll-lock.cy.tsx` drags an aim from an attacker and from the hero's power to
an enemy minion at 390x844 and 844x390 with `scrollX`/`scrollY` at 0 throughout and the action
landing, swipes empty field space with the page unscrolled, and scrolls an overflowing Discover,
the match menu and the log inside themselves. `Game.test.tsx` proves the effect sets the lock
attribute while mounted and clears it on unmount.

Rejected: lifting the lock while a menu is open, which needs menu-open state plumbed from
`Board`, `Prompt`, `Dialog` and the settings into `Game`; fragile beside one attribute the
host effect already owns.
