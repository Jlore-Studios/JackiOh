Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads it, no state hash moves for it. [[R5188000]] gave a card sounds for the hooks [[R2783000]] marks, and [[R5189000]] marks nine more, so `card-audio.json5` gains nine hooks (`CARD_HOOKS` in `apps/web/src/audio/constants.ts`), each named as `hookResolving` names it and taking what [[R655]]'s hooks take, a voice, an effect or both, its line's words under the same name in the card's `voice` in `crates/cards/card-text.yaml`:

- `onPlayHook` (the card answers a card's play), `allyAttacked` and `allyAttack` (one of its controller's Units is attacked, or has attacked), `startOfGame` (its start-of-game clause), `entersHand` (it arrives in a hand) and `delayed` (one of its delayed effects resolves), on any kind of card;
- `whenAttacks`, `afterAttack` and `afterAttacked`, on a Unit alone, the only card that attacks or is attacked.

Each sounds as its marker does, the card named by the marker's `sourceId` (by its `defId` for a rest-of-game effect, which names no card), and all nine are recurring hooks under [[R5188000]]'s rule: the effect every time, the line the first time that card's hook fires in a game and then at most once a turn per card, by chance, at the lowest priority. Both seats hear the hooks of a card they can read, and a marker naming the sentinel plays nothing ([[R203]]). `whenAttacks` and `afterAttack` sound for both seats as the attack happens; neither is `attack`, which stays the viewer's own pick-up ([[R655]]). Their lines are four words at most, as every recurring hook's is ([[R5188001]]). No card carries one yet: each card part of issue #5187 that gives one adds its range to the census (`CENSUS_RANGES`), which counts them as it counts the others.

The effects layer opens no entry at these markers (`HOOK_OF` in `apps/web/src/fx/memory.ts` maps them to none, [[R2797000]]), as no effects table keys them yet. **Rejected:** opening a `trigger` entry for them, which would play a turn hook's motif on an attack or a draw. This amends [[R5188000]] (the hooks a card's sounds may sit on).
