IA #135 Mana Leech's "Steal N of your opponent's mana crystals" counts from the capped maximum (`mana::move_some_mana_crystals`, `stealSomeManaCrystals`): N against the victim's count inside the cap first, then against crystals they had stolen. The victim's count drops to what is left inside the cap, so their own crystals come back one a turn ([[§2.3]]); the thief holds what the victim lost past the cap ([[R4331001]]). Each side whose mana changed is announced, and a steal that moves nothing announces nothing.
