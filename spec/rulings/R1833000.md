The endgame of the economy is a collection of cosmetics ([[§14]]), but past the six full arts and
the five portraits ([[R1313000]], [[R1827000]]) every cosmetic was a starter that every account
owns, so the global roll ([[R1661001]]), cosmetic packs ([[R1306000]]), Epic tokens ([[R1305003]])
and the Chase Shard bar ([[R1306002]]) had little or nothing to give, and no chase could be earned
([[R1333000]]). Issue #1833 roughly doubles the registry, from 36 entries to 72:

- **The 36.** Appended after every earlier entry, so none moves and every opening's log replays as
  it did ([[R1301003]]), six of each kind a player picks, each neither its kind's default nor a
  starter:

  | Kind | Common | Rare | Epic | Legendary | Mythic |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | foil | Glitter | Sunburst, Aurora | Galaxy | Molten gold | Void shimmer |
  | finish | Parchment | Brushed steel, Velvet | Stained glass | Obsidian | Starlit |
  | border | Stone | Copper, Vines | Runes | Filigree | Celestial |
  | table | | | Marble, Chessboard, Desert sand | Obsidian slab, Ancient map | Astral |
  | field | | | Snow, Cherry blossoms, Autumn leaves | Aurora, Lightning storm | Cosmic drift |
  | cardBack | | | Crimson damask, Tidal, Tarot | Dragon scale, Clockwork | Nebula |

  Their ids are the kind's prefix and the name in lower case, hyphenated (`foil-void-shimmer`,
  `card-back-nebula`). The per-card kinds have no rarity floor; the account kinds sit at
  `GLOBAL_COSMETIC_FLOOR_RARITY`, Epic, or better ([[R1661000]]). The chase mark is set exactly on
  the fifteen Legendary and Mythic ones, as the registry's check holds every entry to
  ([[R1333000]]).
- **The first earnable chases and Mythics.** They are the registry's first Mythics and its first
  chases no account owns. A duplicate's cosmetic roll ([[R1301002]]), its global roll
  ([[R1661001]]) and a cosmetic pack's slot ([[R1306000]]) that land on Legendary or Mythic now
  grant one of that tier while one is unowned, instead of falling ([[R1306001]], [[R1339000]]); a
  full Chase Shard bar buys one, on a card the account owns or on the account ([[R1306002]]); and no
  token reaches one: a Rare token buys a Common or Rare per-card one, an Epic token an Epic one, a
  playmat, backdrop or card back among them ([[R1305003]]). No economy number changes.
- **The global pool.** A duplicate's global roll draws from the five portraits but vanilla and the
  18 new account cosmetics, in registry order, all Epic or better; once an account owns all of them
  it takes no global draw, as before ([[R1661001]]).
- **No grant.** Nothing grants any of the 36 to an account, those that exist when it ships
  included: every account starts with none and earns them ([[§14.12]]: "every cosmetic added later
  must be owned"). `seed-catalog` upserts the rows into `public.cosmetics`, so no migration is needed.
- **Ownership gates the account picks.** The client reads what the account owns from `GET
  /api/cosmetics` and decides nothing (CLAUDE.md rule 7). While ownership decides, as it does for
  full art ([[R1310004]]: a production build), the Collection's playmat, backdrop and card-back tiles
  ([[R1579000]]) lock what the account does not own, and the deck builder's playmat, backdrop and
  card-back selects offer only the defaults, the starters and what it owns. The Collection's
  account-wide foil, finish and border (the defaults every card wore, [[R1655000]]) offered only the
  defaults and the starters, since a per-card cosmetic is owned on one card, until [[R1953001]]
  took them away; a card wears an earned one by its own pick, as before. Where nothing decides
  (practice, hotseat, a development or end-to-end build) every cosmetic counts as owned.
- **An unowned stored pick draws as the default.** A pick the account does not own, a playmat
  synced from another device or a deck's slot say, is drawn on the viewer's side as its kind's
  default and shown so in the tiles and the deck picker; an account-wide foil, finish or border was
  worn on a card only where the account owned it on that card, and since [[R1953001]] is not worn.
  The store keeps the pick, as it keeps an id it does not know ([[R1295000]]).
- **The deck's save.** On top of [[R1297000]]'s registry check, the server refuses a save whose
  `tableCosmetic`, `fieldCosmetic` or `cardBackCosmetic` names an account cosmetic the account does
  not own, a default and a starter always owned: a 400 `bad_request`, `"<field>" is a cosmetic this
  account does not own`, as it refuses a malformed pick, and writes nothing. This is the check
  [[R1310002]] left to the issue that adds the first such cosmetic, as [[R1827000]] brought the
  portrait's.
- **Their looks.** Each is drawn by the client's stylesheets alone, with no image: the rarer, the
  more it does, every Legendary and Mythic one animated and holding a still frame of its own under
  reduced motion. A Legendary or Mythic finish or border moves only on a face holding one of the
  `COSMETIC_FOIL_ANIMATED_MAX` motion slots a foil takes, sharing the foil's slot. The card-pack and
  cosmetic-pack reveals, the token redeem choice and the Chase Shard purchase show a cosmetic's name,
  its own rarity and its look: a per-card one on its card's face, a playmat or backdrop as the
  Collection's mini board, a card back as a game draws it. Since [[R1953001]] the token is spent on
  the locked cosmetic's own preview in the Collection, and each Legendary and Mythic one moves
  wherever it is drawn, the Collection's samples and mini boards included.

This amends [[R1297000]] (the deck editor offers, and the deck's save takes, only the playmats,
backdrops and card backs the account owns), [[R1301002]] (a duplicate's cosmetic roll now drops a foil, finish or border of every
tier on any card, a Mythic one included, so far fewer duplicates are spent), [[R1305003]] (the
registry holds Mythics, which no token buys), [[R1827000]] (the global pool is no longer the five
portraits alone), [[R1333000]] (the shipped chases are no longer only starters), [[R1306001]],
[[R1306002]] and [[R1339000]] (a Legendary or Mythic roll can land, and the Chase Shard bar has
something to buy), [[R1661000]] and [[R1661001]] (the account cosmetics past the starters include
playmats, backdrops and card backs, and the global pool is 23 strong) and [[R1310002]] (the check on
a picker it left to a later issue is here).

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's game boards, card backs and hero skins are account-wide
cosmetics a player earns or buys, the defaults everyone's, and a player can set only what they own;
a cosmetic added to the game is earned from then on, not handed to every existing account.

Rejected: granting existing accounts the 36, which would leave them nothing to earn and break
[[§14.12]]; listing unowned entries in the selects as locked options, which a select cannot show as
a tile does; a client-only gate, under which any client could save a deck wearing what its account
does not own; and changing `COSMETIC_TIER_WEIGHTS` or the token cycle for the larger pool, which the
issue leaves to a person.
