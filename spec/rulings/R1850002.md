"Aura: all … are exiled" is an exile aura (`Script.exile_auras`): a board scope and, when the text names them, a cost filter and the keyword kinds that spare a card. While its card acts on the field face-up and awake (a face-down card's text sleeps, [[R1487000]], and a Dormant card's is off, [[R1839000]]), every state check ([[§4.5]]) exiles each card on the field an entry reaches: the scope read for the card's controller as a harmful walk, so a card immune to it is passed by ([[R940]]), and the cost the card has now, as a sweep reads it ([[R66]]). A Very Dandy Lad's "permanents that cost (2) or less" is both rows with `maxCost` 2; Scornboi's "neither Human, Starship Piece nor Starship" is the Units with `notTags` Human and `notKeywords` Starship Piece and Starship, the keyword kinds the Starship brought ([[R1852000]], [[R1852003]]), read through the layers ([[§10.4]]) as `unit_has` reads them, so a granted one spares a card too; each Radiant face's "enemy" is the scope's enemy side. The card itself is never reached, nor a face-down card, whose cost and tags are hidden, nor a Dormant one, which no walk of the board reaches ([[R1839000]]). The step runs before deaths are collected and the check starts over after it, so a card arriving under the aura resolves its Cry first and is exiled at the check after. The auras are read in board order, the active player's side first ([[R68]]), each exiling what it reaches as the walk comes to it: a card an earlier aura exiled reaches nothing. It ends with its card: a card that leaves the field, is Vanilla'd or is set face-down exiles nothing more.

Rejected: exiling what every aura reaches at once, which would have two exilers that reach each other exile both.
