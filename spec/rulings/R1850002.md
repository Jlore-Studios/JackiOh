"Aura: all … are exiled" is an exile aura (`Script.exile_auras`): a board scope and, when the text names one, a cost filter. While its card acts on the field face-up (a face-down card's text sleeps, [[R1487000]]), every state check ([[§4.5]]) exiles each card on the field an entry reaches: the scope read for the card's controller as a harmful walk, so a card immune to it is passed by ([[R940]]), and the cost the card has now, as a sweep reads it ([[R66]]). A Very Dandy Lad's "permanents that cost (2) or less" is both rows with `maxCost` 2; Scornboi's "non-Human" is the Units with `notTags`, the Starship tags joining when the set adds them; each Radiant face's "enemy" is the scope's enemy side. The card itself is never reached, nor a face-down card, whose cost and tags are hidden. The step runs before deaths are collected and the check starts over after it, so a card arriving under the aura resolves its Cry first and is exiled at the check after. The auras are read in board order, the active player's side first ([[R68]]), each exiling what it reaches as the walk comes to it: a card an earlier aura exiled reaches nothing. It ends with its card: a card that leaves the field, is Vanilla'd or is set face-down exiles nothing more.

Rejected: exiling what every aura reaches at once, which would have two exilers that reach each other exile both.
