"Random 1/1s" (MD-D3) are Units whose printed base face is 1/1, tokens included, with one independent pick per zone filled. A catalog query may ask for printed base stats (`stats`, on the base face's attack and health; a face that prints none never matches), and `fill_board_random` is `fill_board` where every empty unit zone, left to right, a Locked one included ([[R64]], [[R1136000]]), draws its own pick from the match rng, repeats allowed ([[R60]]), from every set ([[R380]]) but the card that generated it ([[R387]]). With no zone to fill nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). Summons fire no Cry ([[R1]]).
