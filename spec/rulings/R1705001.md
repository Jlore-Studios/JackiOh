When `DECK_SIZE` became 25 ([[R1705000]]) every complete deck a player had saved held 20 cards.
Nothing rewrites one. A save checks structure alone, D2 asking at most `DECK_SIZE` cards
([[R250]]), so a deck of 20 is a draft as any incomplete deck is: it saves, the deck builder shows
it incomplete, and practice lists it disabled with the validator's reason ([[§9.9]]). Queueing it,
alone or in a trio, or bringing it to a room, fails L2 and names the deck ([[§9.4]], [[R253]])
until the player adds five cards. A trio's slots keep the decks they held.

A ladder bot's decks are dealt, not saved ([[R1454]]). The pool's refresh deals a bot again when a
deck it holds is not of `DECK_SIZE` cards, as it does when the catalog version changes, and one
that holds such a deck is never chosen, its Best-of-1 decks (`holds_decks`) or its Conquest trio
(`holds_trio`, [[R641000]]) alike, so no bot is seated with a deck L2 would refuse a person. The
human decklists a bot's decks are drawn from first leave out every list not of `DECK_SIZE` cards:
until the catalog version moves, the patch's totals still hold the 20-card lists played before,
and left in they could take every one of the draw's `BOT_DECK_DRAW_ATTEMPTS` and deal no deck.

Hearthstone semantics: a deck that no longer meets the rules stays in the player's collection,
marked incomplete, and cannot be played until the player finishes it; nothing fills or deletes it
for them.

Rejected: a migration that filled every 20-card deck with five cards, or deleted it, which would
change a player's deck behind their back.
