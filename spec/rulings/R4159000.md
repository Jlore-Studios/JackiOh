**Why.** The owner decided on 2026-11-18, after reviewing issue #4142, to retire the learned evaluation ([[R1010000]]) and the policy prior ([[R1171001]]). The learn lane ([[R1012000]]) started 70 runs on GitHub's runners and promoted once, generation 13 (2026-10-21, 220 of 400 against its parent), where it was expected to promote every three or four days. A run's export takes about a day, and patches ship daily, so most runs were cancelled before they could gate. The shipped network was fitted on catalog v0.3.17, and 499 of the 978 cards (all of Stinkyscape, Stinkyscape+ and Training Grounds) had no row in it, so a decision whose view named mostly those cards already kept the hand evaluation ([[R3479000]]). What the network added most, a learned worth per card id, is what each patch makes stale, and what survives a patch is what `AI_EVAL` already scores. The prior was never switched on: `AI_MOVE_ORDER` stayed `Heuristic`. No game rule changes.

**What holds now.**

- Every score the search gives, an open line's at each depth, a complete line's where the beam closes it and a finalist's after the opponent's reply, is the hand evaluation priced by the decision's root (`evaluate_priced` over `AI_EVAL` with its `Prices`, [[§9.9]]), at every tier, the tutorial opponent's included, in 1v1 and in Duos.
- The beam expands each line's moves in `candidate_actions`' order, with [[R1759000]]'s stand-downs and [[R2976000]]'s walls after the prefix as before.
- `crates/ai` has no build script and compiles no weights: no `learned` or `policy` module, no `weights.json`, no `EvalKind`, `LEARNED_*`, `MoveOrder`, `POLICY_RANK_CAP` or `FEATURES_READ_SINCE`, and `Prices` carries no root flags (`starship_root`, `unknown_root`). The WebAssembly modules export no `learned_parity`.
- `cargo jackioh positions` writes no policy label and prints no count of cards a network has no row for.

The notes that existed for the network or the prior are retired, each saying what is true now: [[R1010000]], [[R1010001]], [[R1010002]], [[R1011000]], [[R1012001]], [[R1012002]], [[R1012003]], [[R1171000]], [[R1171001]], [[R3038003]], [[R3112001]], [[R3112002]], [[R3479000]] and [[R1012005]], whose two decisions are held again.

**What stays until a person removes the learn lane's workflows** (CLAUDE.md rule 13): `.github/workflows/learn.yml`, `learn-set.yml`, `selfplay.yml` and `selfplay-shards.yml` still run, so the commands they run stay: `cargo jackioh selfplay`, `positions` (and `features.rs`, which it reads), `promote --lane learn` and `stats from-selfplay`, with `training/ml/retrain.sh` and `learn-promote.sh`. Neither script can ship a network any more: each stops where it would switch one on. The self-play games keep reaching the card statistics through `stats from-selfplay` while the lane runs, and `gather.yml` files development records on its own after it ([[R1976000]]). The sweep of record ([[R1678000]]) and the quality gates are unchanged.

**The decisions that moved.** With the correction gone, the AI's decisions move wherever it applied. Of the AI's tests, three pinned decisions moved, each the same on every seed and stream tried, and each is pinned again rather than `AI_EVAL` tuned, which this issue keeps:

- [[R844]]'s Expedition12 decisions, which [[R1012005]] left unchecked while the network was on, run again: beside two K-Pop Fanatics the AI now casts the Expedition, as it does beside Silly Silas (`lost_mana.rs`).
- [[R3695001]]: on an empty board Hard on 7 crystals plays Genn before Drone Core X; facing a unit, and beside Greaser, it still casts X first (`float_discount.rs`).
- [[R3696000]]: beside two castable Vanillas the AI plays a Vanilla before Supply Drop; the payoff line still keeps its place among the replied lines (`meditative_plus.rs`).

The practice-claim fixture ([[R1303002]]) did not move: the worker plays it move for move as before.

**Rejected:** keeping the network compiled in but off behind `AI_EVAL_KIND`, since it compiles weights for nothing while the lane keeps spending runners and upkeep issues; refitting more often, since an export takes a day and patches ship daily.
