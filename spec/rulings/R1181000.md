"In N turns" (Meditative+ #4 Supply Drop's "in 3 turns") is a delayed effect at the start of its owner's Nth turn from now, counted in the turns that player starts: made on the owner's own turn T, it passes the starts of their next N − 1 turns by and resolves at the start of the one after, at [[R62]]'s start-of-turn delayed effects, after the refresh and before the start-of-turn triggers and the draw, as "at the start of your next turn" does ([[R458]]). The count is the owner's own turns started, so an extra turn counts as one ([[R846]]), and one made on the other player's turn counts from the owner's next turn, the first of the N. It is the owner's effect, not the card's: it waits and resolves whatever becomes of the card that made it, a Spell in the graveyard, exiled or gone ([[R76]], [[R127]]). Hearthstone shows a Dormant count on the minion; a Spell is in no zone a mark could ride ([[R437]] marks a card in play), so the countdown is a badge on its owner's side, in both views, as a player modifier is ([[R169]]): it reads "… in 3 turns", "… in 2 turns", "… in 1 turn", and goes as the effect resolves, its arrival and its going each announced as a modifier change. An effect with no count, or a game that makes none, stores nothing new (`DelayedEffect.dueOwnTurn` and `label` are absent).
