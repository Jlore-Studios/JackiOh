A card whose script computes its Echo X (`Script.echo_x`, Meditative #5 Death by 1000 cuts) prints the larger of that and its fixed `staticFlags.echo`. X is fixed when the play is made ([[§10.5]] step 4), from the caster's max mana at that moment: mana gained while the card resolves does not change X. A random cast uses its caster's max mana. The card's "Echo" tuning step ([[R386]]) and a Twinspell grant ([[R30]]) then add on, as they do to a fixed Echo.
