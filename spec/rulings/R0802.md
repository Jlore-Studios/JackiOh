A card whose script computes its Echo X (`Script.echo_x`, Meditative #5 Death by 1000 cuts) prints the larger of that and its fixed `staticFlags.echo`. X is fixed when the play is made ([[§10.5]] step 4): mana gained while the card resolves does not change X. Through v0.5.11o it was computed from the caster's max mana at that moment; since issue #3048 Death by 1000 cuts reads the mana its caster has left once the card is paid for ([[R3048001]]), and keeps one target through its repeats ([[R3048002]]). The card's "Echo" tuning step ([[R386]]) and a Twinspell grant ([[R30]]) then add on, as they do to a fixed Echo.
