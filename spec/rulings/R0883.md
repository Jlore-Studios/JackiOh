The block table (`docs/meditative-set.md` M6 #17, all numbers named constants under `CRAFT_*`):

- **Type and stats.** A Unit pays `attack + health − 1`; nothing else holds stats. The name is two
  words from the two lists (the designer may replace them).
- **Keywords.** R21's pool plus Armor and Echo. A Unit takes any; a Spell takes Lifesteal and
  Pierce only; a Field Spell and a Trap take none. Armor costs 2 per N; Echo costs 4 per N and
  only a Spell echoes.
- **Hats.** A Unit takes Cry, Death, Start of turn and End of turn; a Field Spell takes Cry,
  Start and End of turn; a Spell takes exactly one When cast; a Trap takes exactly one reveal
  hat. Start and End of turn double their effects' points; a reveal hat costs 4 lines. A Spell
  with no effect and a Field Spell with no hat are refused.
- **Verbs.** Each block costs `points + per-N × N`, halved (rounded up) for healing; a targeted
  block's row counts its `TargetDecl` line. Only Cry and When cast take targeted blocks: each
  declares its own play-time target (damage and heal aim at any unit or hero, the rest at
  another unit), read as the n-th selection the play carried (R81). A reveal hat fires on the
  opponent's play of the named type (`CardPlayed`, def type Unit or Spell) or on their attack
  (`AttackDeclared` by an enemy), in Core #41's shape.

Stockpile's recipe — a cost-1 Spell drawing 2 and healing its hero 2 — costs 7 points (`3 × 2`
plus `⌈2 / 2⌉`) and 7 lines (the frame, When cast, and the two verbs) against budgets of 7 and
24.
