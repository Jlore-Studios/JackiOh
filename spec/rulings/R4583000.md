`cargo jackioh assault-sim` plays Infinite Assault days on the content the browser deals, the
practice AI in the player's seat at [[R747000]]'s skill with the day's Assault deck and the AI at full
skill in the enemy's, as `run-sim` plays a dungeon run's fights ([[R3586000]]). The plan (IA10) asks
for level 1 clearly winnable, level 20 clearly hard and each Commander harder than the level before
it, and leaves the measures and the numbers open. They are:

- **The sample.** A whole attempt ends at its first loss, so the bands play each level they read
  from its own fight, as `assault-sim --levels` does: levels 1 and 20, and each Commander level with
  the level before it, on the `BAND_DAYS` (6) days from 2026-11-23, at the tests' small search budget.
  The deal is seeded, so the sample is the same on every run.
- **Clearly winnable** is level 1 won in at least `LEVEL_ONE_WIN_PERCENT_MIN` (25) percent of its
  fights. Both seats are the same AI, so an even fight is about half: the floor fails a level 1 that
  has become a wall, not one that is merely a fair fight (measured: 3 of 6, 4 of 12 and 7 of 16 at the
  small budget, 5 of 8 at the full one).
- **Clearly hard** is level 20 won in at most `LEVEL_TWENTY_WIN_PERCENT_MAX` (20) percent (measured:
  none of 12, none of 8 at the full budget).
- **Harder** is a lower mean margin: the player's hero health left less the enemy's, each floored at
  0, over the level's fights. A win rate alone ties when both levels are lost every time, as levels 19
  and 20 are; the margin still tells how far the player fell short, and it counts the Commander's
  extra health as the player meets it.

`assault-sim` also prints, for each upgrade, the fights at the levels where some day holds it, split by
whether the fight holds it: upgrades pile up with the level, so a rate over all levels would measure
the level, not the upgrade.

A deliberate retune of the escalation (`crates/run/src/config.rs`'s `ASSAULT_*`) moves the bands in
the same patch. Rejected: playing whole attempts per pull request (most end by level 5 at the small
budget, so the high levels would go unread) and a win-rate comparison for the Commanders (it ties at
0). It takes R4583000, the first number of #4583's block.
