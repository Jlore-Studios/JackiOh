- Spec: §5.2, §6.3, §10.3

De-Radiant (`clearRadiant`) is Make Radiant reversed, and the one exception to §5.2's "nothing
un-sets Radiant". A card that is not Radiant is untouched. Otherwise the flag is cleared and
`deradianted` reports it, hidden per zone like `radiantSet` ([[R440]]): a hand or deck card reads
redacted, a face-up card openly. The layers read the flag, so the face swaps back while damage,
buffs and tuning stay; keywords only the Radiant face printed are lost, ongoing triggers read the
base text from then on, and no Cry fires. A fused card returns to its fused base form ([[R77]]), since
the face is the flag.
