The designer's keyword (#1563): a player Floats when their turn ends with mana unspent, and the Float is that much mana. It is the `turnEnded` event's `unspentMana` ([[§2.2]]'s end step), the number #18 Bread and Butter and M #49.1 already read; a turn that spends everything is no Float, so nothing that answers a Float happens. "Reveals when any player Floats" fires in the end-of-turn trap window ([[R62]]).

"Float reduces cost" is [[R1022]]'s rule made a keyword: whenever the card's owner Floats, the card costs that much less. It is a hand trigger and a deck trigger ([[R464]]) on its owner's `turnEnded`, a `costMod` of minus the Float on the card itself, kept in hand and deck until a graveyard or exile resets it ([[R766]]), the price never below 0 ([[§2.3]]). The opponent's Float lowers nothing, as Hearthstone's "costs less for each mana you didn't spend" reads only its owner's turns. #29 GIGA Glowy Jelly Bean and M #49.1 YileGPT Prime print it, the engine's `float_reduces_cost`. C #90 In Too Deep's quests 4 and 7, "Float 3 or more mana" and "Float 5 or more mana", are the designer's own use of the word, and count a Float of at least that much. It edits [[§6.2]] and [[§8]].
