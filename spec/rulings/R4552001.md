**Why.** The adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]) reads a card's average lift cleanly, win-more included, since the shuffle decides which decks never draw it. It cannot read spread: a card at 50% may be mild, or may win one game by 40 points and lose the next by 40. The owner wants to find cheap, game-altering cards that should cost more. Decisiveness reads that spread.

**A turn's change.** For each row of a seat ([[R4552000]]), `Δ` is the seat's win probability at the start of its next turn less at the start of this one; the last row ends at the result (a win 1, a draw ½, a loss 0). The window holds the opponent's reply (a card answered at once decided nothing) and one end-of-turn and start-of-turn cycle, and since it starts before the play, a card is never credited for being played from ahead. Each `Δ` is tallied in whole thousandths (`SWING_PERMILLE`), so every total is an integer sum that adds the same in any order ([[R1044000]]).

**The tallies.** Under its key, a card's totals keep the turns it was played on (from the hand, or cast or summoned by an effect from its seat's deck or hand, R4279000, each card once a turn), the turns it acted on its seat's field, and the turns it acted on the other field (that seat's `Δ`), each as turns and `Σq` per bucket and `Σq²`, `Σq³`, `Σq⁴` and the games' turns squared. A play counts with the seat's last turn begun by the play's turn. The records key keeps every turn's `Σq` and `Σq²` per bucket: the seat's own turn number, capped at `SWING_TURN_BUCKETS`, the seat that went first and the other apart.

**The figures.** `α`, what an ordinary turn gains, is each bucket's mean `Δ` over the records the window counts, and `σ²` the turns' variance about it, pooled.

- The **play term** `b` is the mean of `y = Δ − α` over the card's play turns, shrunk toward 0 as [[R2777000]] shrinks a lift: kept in the share `τ²/(τ² + σ²/n)`, `τ` the spread of real cards' terms, [[R3022000]]'s estimate over the cards with `SWING_FIELD_MIN_ROWS` turns, or `SWING_PRIOR_PLAY_SPREAD` below `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` such cards.
- **Board swing** `a` is the same over its acting turns, a turn on the other field counted with its sign turned (`SWING_PRIOR_BOARD_SPREAD`).
- **Swing** is `b` plus, per play, the turns it then acted times `a` and the turns each Created card it made acted times that card's `a` ([[R4552002]]).
- **Decisiveness**: the card's raw RMS is `√(Var y + b²)` over its play turns, the root mean square of `r = y − ȳ + b`, the part of each turn that what an ordinary turn and the card's own mean explain leaves, plus its play term. Its sampling variance is the delta method's, `Var(r²) / (4 · RMS² · n_eff)`, with `n_eff = n² / Σ(turns a game gave)²` as the games cluster the turns, and `Var(r²)` at least a normal distribution's, `2 · RMS⁴`, so a few turns that happen to swing alike are not read as certain. Decisiveness is `m + w · (RMS − m)`, `w = τ_D² / (τ_D² + SE²)`, `m` and `τ_D` the typical card's RMS and the spread of real cards' about it, [[R3022000]]'s estimate over the cards with `SWING_FIELD_MIN_ROWS` play turns, or `DECISIVENESS_PRIOR_CENTER` and `DECISIVENESS_PRIOR_SPREAD` below `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` such cards. A card with no play turn has no Decisiveness, but for a Created card an effect put onto the field, which reads its acting turns ([[R4552002]]). It is an RMS around 0, not a spread around the card's own mean: a card that reliably swings 30 points is as decisive as one that swings 30 either way.

Every figure is read off finished totals in a fixed order, so the same records give the same figures bit for bit, in any order of folding.

**What shows.** Only Decisiveness, as its plain value in points to a tenth, `±14.2%`: "playing it typically moves your chance of winning by 14.2%, one way or the other", never as a difference from the field. On the Cards tab of `/stats` it is a column right after Adjusted and before Win rate, sortable by its value (highest first; the default sort stays Adjusted), reading "not enough games" below [[R654]]'s floor as Adjusted and Win rate do; the Grid shows it after Adjusted. Its header's tooltip says it is how far playing a card typically moves your chance of winning, that ± is the swing itself and not a margin of error, and names the typical card's value. Tooltips only: no note on the page ([[R4155000]]). The play term, Board swing, Swing, the raw RMS and the reliability checks are printed by `cargo jackioh stats report --swing` and `jackioh-server stats-cards --swing`, and shown nowhere else.

**Rejected.**

- **The full regularized adjusted plus-minus**, every card's terms solved together from `XᵀX`. Pairs of cards acting in the same turn make the matrix nearly dense under All Random (some 1,200 cards), and the totals are the only store once a record's detail is purged. Each term is instead its own shrunk mean of what `α` leaves (a diagonal ridge); the calibrated probability already reads the board, so what other cards did is mostly in `α` and in `P` itself. A person may still ask for the full solve, which needs storage of its own.
- **Showing Swing.** For a deck card it should repeat the adjusted win rate through a model, the weaker read.
- **The per-row residual less every other card's term.** It needs third-order sums per pair of cards; `y − ȳ + b` needs only the card's own.
- **"WAR" as the name**: it reads as a season total against a replacement level.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
