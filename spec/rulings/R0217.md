[[R58]] caps the cast-on-draw chain one draw sets off at 20 casts (`CAST_ON_DRAW_CHAIN_CAP`), and a cast can draw: radiant /fullsend's "Combo: draw 1" rides on every card played that turn, a cast included ([[R70]]), and a cast Spell's own text can draw. A draw a cast-on-draw cast makes is part of that cast, so it continues the chain's count instead of beginning a chain of its own, and the cap bounds everything the first draw set off, however deeply its casts draw: once the chain has cast 20 cards, the next cast-on-draw card any draw of it meets goes to the hand uncast. The draw that began the chain ends it, when its own repeat is done. Every other draw — a played card's, a trigger's, each of "draw N"'s N — begins a chain of its own, as [[R58]] says. Without this a CN-Virus cast under /fullsend drew the next CN-Virus before its script shuffled two more in, and the nested chains never ended. Hearthstone has no cast-on-draw loop to bound, and this keeps the designer's cap whole
