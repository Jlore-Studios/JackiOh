Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes. The device-size
screenshots of the account screen ([[R3733000]]) showed four things at the sizes of [[R3728000]], and each
is settled here.

**"This season" heading.** Overview's "This season" heading was drawn whenever the Constructed ladder was
read, and each ladder's line under it is left out while that ladder has no season games ([[R2255000]],
[[R2778000]]), so over 600 px a fresh season showed the heading with nothing under it and "All time"
following at once. The heading is now drawn only over at least one record: the Constructed line, the All
random line or both. With no season game on either ladder, or with neither ladder read, there is no heading,
as the header's ranked line is already left out at 0 games. The badges, the Leaderboard link and "All time"
are unchanged.

**The rank crest on the dark ground.** The crest is drawn on the account header's panel (`#341e10` at its
lightest) and on the Play hub's Online cards (`--pal-wood-800`), and Raisin's outline (a dim brown at 60%
opacity, 1.7:1) and Rotten's (2.7:1) all but vanished there. Every tier's outline now reads at 3:1 or better
on both grounds, and the berries of Raisin and Rotten, the two tiers with the darkest art, on what they sit
on (bare ground for Raisin, its frame for Rotten). Raisin is drawn at full opacity; its dashed, frameless
outline still marks it as unplaced ([[R607]]) and its drawing still differs from every other crest
([[R605]]). The crest is one component, so the Play hub, the ladder, the leaderboard and the match board's
rank rail take the change with the account screen. The other tiers' outlines were already 6:1 or better and
are unchanged.

**"All replays" does not cover "Watch replay".** In Match history the "All replays" link was a text link
under the last row, and on a touch screen or a phone its tap band ([[R2122000]]) reached up over the last
row's "Watch replay". It is now a `button-secondary` link, 44 px tall like "Watch replay" and Overview's
Leaderboard link, so the stylesheet draws it no band and none of its taps can land on another control. Spec
47 measures it at every `MENU_SIZES` entry.

**A history row's date stays whole.** A row's date is its own element and does not break inside: at 820 and
744 px it moved to the next line whole instead of wrapping across two.

Rejected: keeping "All replays" a text link and widening the gap above it, since the band's reach depends on
the link's line height, so the gap would hold at one size and not another; a row layout that always puts the
date on a line of its own, which spends a line on every row at the sizes where one line fits; a drop shadow
or a backing disc behind the crest, which would make every crest heavier at 16 px to mend two tiers; and
lifting Normal's, Large's and Golden's berries, whose outlines already read and whose berries sit on their
frames at 2.2–2.9:1 (a person may ask for it).
