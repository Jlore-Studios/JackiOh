"Your table" ([[R639]], [[R1584000]]) is made to feel as if it could go on for ever, so it never names a token, shows 35 stats instead of ten, tells them in a random order and has no arrows or dots to count them by. Presentation only, on the device (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

- **No token is a stat's card.** The tally ([[R639]], `model.ts`, `track.ts`, `store.ts`, `sync.ts`) still counts tokens, as it must (a token is played, destroyed and seen like any card). Only the showcase's choice of card passes over a card whose catalog entry is a token (`def.token`), taking the next one, as it already passes over a card the catalog no longer holds. That holds for every stat, The Record's card, and the Pocket Pick, which is dropped when it names a token, as when it names a card the catalog lacks, and for every stat below. A token in the longest game's plays is passed over for the next card of that game.
- **Twenty-five stats more.** Each has a title, a card, a line and one of the board's stage animations ([[R1584000]]; nothing new is animated), and a stat the device has no data for is left out. A stat that is a share of plays, "per play", needs a card with at least `SHOWCASE_MIN_PLAYS` (5) of the plays it is a share of, so one game does not win it. In the order of the table:
  - *From the counters the device already kept.* Glass Cannon (the card with the highest `destroyed` per play, "destroyed 8 of the 10 times you played it", Death, yours), Unbreakable (the most played card with `destroyed` 0, "played 12×, never destroyed", Divine Shield), Grudge Match (the highest lesser of `played` and `playedAgainst`, "you 7×, them 9×", lunge), Never Tried It (the most `playedAgainst` of a card never played, "faced 11×, never played it yourself", lunge, the opponent's), Easy Target (the highest `defeated` per `playedAgainst`, "taken down 9 of the 10 times you faced it", Death, the opponent's), The Closer (the highest `lethal` per play, "the winning blow in 3 of 5 plays", lunge), Hair Trigger (the highest `sprung` per play, "sprung 6 of the 7 times you set it", a Trap's reveal), Ride or Die (the highest `survived` per play, "still standing 5 of the 6 times you played it", Divine Shield), Familiar Face (the highest `seen`, "in 34 of your 50 games", Radiant), One-Hit Wonder (a card with `played` exactly 1, drawn at random, "played once. Just once.", entrance), Second Fiddle and Bronze Medal (the #2 and #3 most played cards, "your #2, played 7×" and "your #3, played 5×", entrance), Explorer (a card played, drawn at random, "87 different cards played", entrance) and Collector (a card seen, drawn at random, "seen 212 of 480 cards", Radiant).
  - *From new per-card counters.* Workhorse (`damageDealt`, "dealt 312 damage", lunge), Heavy Hitter (`biggestHit`, "hit for 14 once", lunge), Overkill (`overkill`, "hit 9 harder than it needed to", lunge), Brawler (`attacks`, "attacked 41×", lunge), Medic (`healing`, "healed 96", Divine Shield), Card Shark (`drawn`, "drawn 19×", entrance), Shine On (`radiant`, "made Radiant 6×", Radiant), Comeback Kid (`playedInWin` per game won or lost with the card played, "won 9 of the 11 games you played it in", Radiant) and Jinx (`playedInLoss` per the same, "lost 7 of the 9 games you played it in", Death, yours).
  - *From new game-level totals.* Hot Streak (the most played card, "best streak: 6 wins", Radiant; left out while `bestStreak` is 0) and Marathon (the card played most in the longest game, else the most played overall, "longest game: 23 turns", entrance; left out while `longestGame` is 0).
  A line whose number is 1 says it in the singular where it has a word to bend ("in 1 of your 1 game", "1 different card played", "best streak: 1 win", "longest game: 1 turn"). Ties go to the lower id, as in [[R1584000]]. All 25 can be read from what the views show; none was dropped.
- **The new counters, as the viewer's seat reads them** ([[R97]], [[R1582000]]'s way: off the view and the events new with it, once per game, naming only a card the view shows). `damageDealt`, `biggestHit`, `overkill` and `attacks` name the card on the viewer's side (its hand, piles, exile, resolving, units, carried units and face-up backrow) that a `damage`'s `sourceId` or an `attackDeclared`'s `attackerId` names; a null, hidden or opposing source, or one the view no longer shows, counts nothing, as does a `damage` of 0. `damageDealt` sums the amounts, and `biggestHit` and `overkill` keep the largest of any game (the totals take the larger of the stored and the game's, not a sum). A hit's overkill is its amount less its target's health, when the target (a hero, or a Unit on the field or carried) is shown with a health in the view before the batch, less the earlier hits of the same batch on it, and nothing when the view before is missing (a seat's first view) or the target is not in it. `healing`: a `healed` names no source, so it counts toward the viewer's card whose `cardPlayed`, `activated` or `trapFired` came last before it in the same batch, and counts nothing with none, or when that last one was the opponent's or hidden. `drawn` counts each of the viewer's own `drawn` that names the card (the opponent's reads as the sentinel). `radiant` counts each `radiantSet` on the viewer's own zone. `playedInWin` and `playedInLoss` count, for a game the player won or lost, one for each card the player played in it, once however often; a drawn game counts neither.
- **The new totals.** `streak` is the games won in a row now, and `bestStreak` the most: a win adds 1, a loss or a draw sets `streak` to 0. `longestGame` is the most turns of any finished game, the highest `turn` a `turnStarted` of the game named (the first view of a seat has no earlier one to compare, so its events are not counted, and the turn number does not need them), and `longestGamePlayed` the player's plays by card in that game, replaced only by a longer one.
- **Storage.** Under the same key and version as [[R639]]'s ([[R1582000]]'s way): a counter or total stored before them, or one that is not a whole positive number, reads as 0 (a card with only a new counter is kept, and `longestGamePlayed` keeps only its whole positive entries), and Clear empties them. None syncs ([[R1142000]]); `sync.ts`'s pruning is as it was.
- **A random order.** The showcase draws its stats from the page's injected random source, the fan's (`LandingRoute`'s `random`, [[R374]]), by a shuffled bag: every stat shown once before any repeats, and a new round never starts with the stat just shown. A stat that joins (the Pocket Pick, once its request answers) is shown from the next round; a stat that goes is dropped from the round. The three random stats draw from the same source, once each and only when they have a candidate, so a tally and a seed give the same stats. The first stat shown is the bag's first.
- **No arrows, no dots.** The previous and next arrows and the dot for each stat are gone, with their test ids and styles: the dots would give away how many stats there are. The showcase is itself the control, with the role of a button and in the tab order, once there are two stats or more: a click, a tap, Enter or Space moves it to the next stat of the bag, and under reduced motion too, where it still never moves on by itself. A hand step gives the stat it lands on its whole interval, and the holds ([[R1584000]]: a pointer, focus, a hidden page) stay.

Choices a person may want to review, each with the alternative rejected. The floor of 5 plays: a per-play stat needs a sample of its own, and [[R1583000]]'s 3 is for a rate against the player base (rejected: reusing `POCKET_PICK_MIN_PLAYS`). The share's part is capped at its whole (a card can be destroyed more often than it was played: rejected, "destroyed 9 of the 5 times"), compared by cross-multiplication and a tie going to the lower id (rejected: the larger sample, which would make the card depend on counts the line does not show). Comeback Kid and Jinx divide by the games won or lost with the card played, so a draw is neither and the device keeps no draw counter (rejected: a third counter, for a stat that shows none). Hair Trigger asks for no Trap type: only a Trap's `trapFired` counts `sprung`. A heal's source is the last play, activation or Trap before it in its batch (rejected: the batch's first, and any card of the viewer's in the batch, which would give a heal to a card that did not make it). Overkill reads the view before the batch (rejected: the view after, where a target that died is gone, and a new field on `damage`, which no engine event carries). Collector counts, and draws from, the non-token cards of the shipped sets that the player saw, the rotation's pool ([[R639]]; rejected: the whole catalog, whose tokens and unshipped cards would make "of N" a number no player can reach). Marathon keeps the plays of the longest game, not one card's id, because the card played most in it may be a token, which must pass to the next. The turns are the highest `turn` (rejected: counting `turnStarted` events, which misses the turns before the first view is compared). The Pocket Pick is added after the random stats are drawn, so its answer redraws nothing and moves nothing on screen (rejected: drawing everything again when the pick arrives). It amends [[R1582000]] and [[R1584000]], whose list of ten stats, order and Controls it replaces, and edits [[§9.11]] and [[§10.10]].

Amended by [[R2648000]]: Unbreakable names only a Unit that prints no Indestructible and has at least `SHOWCASE_MIN_PLAYS` plays; Glass Cannon and Workhorse name only a Unit; Ride or Die is `survived` per `playedInWin`, "still standing at the end of 5 of the 6 wins you played it in"; Hot Streak names the card with the most `playedInWin`; Comeback Kid is Lucky Charm (the same card and line); and Medic credits a Lifesteal hit's heal to the attacker, an `attackDeclared` or a `damage` of the viewer's card setting a heal's source.
