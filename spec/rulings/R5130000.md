Issue #5130 reverts issue #5014's motion and glow budget ([[R5014000]]), which landed as #5073, and the follow-up from #5073's after-merge spot-check (issue #5091, #5097). The client's stylesheets stand as they did before #5073. Changes later pull requests made to the same lines stay: #5060's palette tokens, with each restored color written in the token #5060 gave it, and #5057's radius tokens.

**The title screen.** The JackiOh wordmark is gold leaf again: a gradient of six stops, not one flat color. The hearth's glow (`landing-hearth`), the rising embers (`landing-ember`) and the card fan's float (`landing-float`) loop for as long as the screen is open. The table keeps its inset shadow, and the fan keeps its pool of hearth light (`.landing-fan::before`).

**The rest of the client.** An `infinite` loop no longer has to be named in `IDLE_LOOPS`, and the list and its ceiling are gone. A card face's decoration loops again: the keyword treatments, a Mythic's hand shimmer, the full-screen view's camera, the zzz, the Dormant seal, the shipyard idle, the marks, Glitch and the animated rarity foil. So do the menus' ornaments and loaders. The primary button's face (`--primary-face*` in `buttons.css`) is its gradient under its shadows again, and the menus' chrome keeps its gradients and blurred shadows. The amendments [[R5014000]] made to [[R2111000]], [[R3334000]], [[R3345000]], [[R3607000]] and [[R3608000]] are taken back, and §10.10 loses the paragraph it gained. State-change motion keeps BUILD's event durations, as it did throughout.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing here reaches the engine, an action, `PlayerView` or the log.

**Rejected:** reverting the title screen's part alone. Issue #5130 asks for #5014 whole, and the title screen's loops and the rest of the client's are one budget, held by one test.
