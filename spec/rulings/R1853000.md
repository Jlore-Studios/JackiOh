The Marketplace is one shop for both players, `GameState.marketplace` ([[§10.1]]), built on the night market's ([[R1000]]). It opens the first time either player gains a Resource ([[R1840000]]) or Credits ([[R1840005]]): a gain into the tray, a Resource a full tray sells at once, a Credits gain above 0, or a lot an effect adds ([[R1853005]]). From then on both seats' views carry it ([[§10.8]]). Before then nothing has it. A new game, a Resource laid in the tray by a test's setup, a gain of 0 Credits and every game with no Stinkyscape card all leave the key out of the state, its hash and both views, so no shipped game changes (D14).

The designer did not say whether opening or re-rolling is announced. Neither is: no event reports them, as Hearthstone's Tavern refreshes without a word. The client reads the offering off the view, and the turn it renews on is `rerollsOn` ([[R1853001]]). Opening draws the first offering from the match rng in the middle of the gain that opens it ([[§10.7]]).
