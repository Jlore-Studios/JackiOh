FvH #112 Jilliax Deluxe is an X Unit printed 0/1 on both faces (X at least 1, [[R348]]): the designer's 0/0 body with 1 Health, so it lives to its Cry (below). Its Cry is two chained Discovers ([[R352]]), each of the four dearest ([[R5239005]]) of the thirteen module tokens #112.1 to #112.13 printed at X or less. X is the X the play paid, read once as the Cry starts and carried to the second offer, the designer's "initial mana snapshot"; spending more mana makes the offer dearer, never a module's numbers bigger. No offer holds a module this Cry has picked or one already fused into the card, [[R387]]'s rule carried to the card's own ingredients, so one module never goes in twice.

After the second pick, one `fuse_cards` of both into this card ([[R77]]), each on the Radiant face when Jilliax is Radiant ([[R469]]). It is one fusion, so a Radiant Viral Module's Immutable ([[R23]]) lands only once both are in and never blocks the second. When nothing is left for the second offer, the one pick is fused alone.

The body stands while the Discovers are open: a play paused on its Cry's prompt runs no state check until the prompt is answered (the check [[R156]] describes is one already under way when a prompt opens). Before the Cry, [[§10.5]]'s step-4 window holds [[§4.5]]'s check unless a trigger resolves in it; one that does (another card's "when a Unit is played or summoned") runs the check, which would collect a 0/0 body before its Cry, as it would any Unit at 0 Health. So the body prints 1 Health on both faces (the owner's call, 2026-11-28, FV2 #112), and the check finds it alive: the Cry always runs, and the modules' stats add to the 0/1 as a fusion's do ([[R77]]). Its Radiant 0/1 is below [[R275]]'s stat half, a named exception ([[R5263000]]): the Radiant modules carry the face.

Then, from the picks alone: a Starpiece Module's Starship Piece is granted to the fused card, which [[R1852001]] keeps as the kept card's own grant; a Twin Module summons copies of the whole fused card, one on the base face and two on the Radiant, each a summon with no Cry ([[R1]]) into an open unit zone ([[R64]]), none with none ([[R129]]). A copy of an Immutable fused card is a copy, which [[R23]] does not refuse. The Twin Module has no hook of its own, so alone it copies nothing.
