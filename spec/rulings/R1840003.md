A Resource in its player's own Resource tray is played with the same `play` action as a hand card, naming it ([[§10.2]]): `legalActions` lists it by the same function it lists a hand card's plays by, in the player's own main phase, and [[§10.5]] takes it out of the tray at step 4, as [[R454]] takes a graveyard card out of the graveyard. Its price, its choices ([[R90]]), its counting and its Cry are a hand card's, and `cardPlayed` carries `from: "tray"`; as a Spell it then goes to its owner's graveyard. A card in the other player's tray is in no pile the player may play from, and a tray card is played on its player's turn only: the listing and the reducer's refusal agree on both. No Plague Counter pays for it. The client starts the play from the Resource in the viewer's tray.
