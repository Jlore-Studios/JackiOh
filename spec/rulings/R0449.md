A live `replacePlays` replaces each play or cast by a new card. The old card ceases to exist, the price paid stays, the new card's choices are asked before the announce, and it takes its zone by [[R64]]. A card already identical to the named one is not replaced. A tag rule makes every play carrying that tag Radiant, read at step 1 as [[R214]] reads Gifted Program
