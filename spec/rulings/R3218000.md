Issue #3189 gave the bar one skin (`routes/top-bar.css`) and no layout: the push of the right-hand group
to the right edge was a chain of `margin-left: auto` and `margin-left: 0` sibling rules in four
stylesheets, which worked only while `.screen-nav` was full width. On the Almanac, Statistics and the deck
workshop the gear sat beside Back and the title came after it.

BackLink's nav (`routes/nav.tsx`) now has fixed slots, in this order, and `routes/top-bar.css` is the only
sheet that places anything in them:

1. start: Back, always BackLink's own pill (`top-bar-button--text`), on the replay player as on every
   menu; only the landing, whose corner holds no Back, has nothing here;
2. the line (`.screen-nav__line`): the screen's title, if the screen puts one in the bar (its `title`
   prop), then its own extras (the children: the workshop's Undo, Redo, cosmetics button and save status,
   the invite screen's who is signed in and Sign out), pushed to the line's end;
3. a flexible spacer: the line takes the room left between Back and the end group, and the end group's
   `margin-left: auto` does the same on a screen with no line;
4. the end group (`.screen-nav__end`), in a fixed order: the Jlore pill, Friends, the language button, the
   settings gear. A control that is absent drops out and the others keep their order, so the gear is always
   the last item at the right edge ([[R1651000]]: no pill on a screen that guards its way out;
   [[R1916000]]: the language button may be gone; signed out, no pill and no Friends). A Back-only bar
   (`showSettings={false}`, the board's and the replays') has no end group.

No stylesheet but `top-bar.css` sets `margin`, `margin-left`, `margin-right` or `order` on the gear, the
language button, the Jlore pill or Friends inside `.screen-nav`, and none names a `.screen-nav__…` class: a
screen that needs room gives it through its own content (a child of the bar) and never a margin on a bar
control. The bar's phone breakpoint is the touch rule's, `(max-width: 600px)` (index.css's `(pointer:
coarse), (max-width: 600px)`, [[R2122000]]): there the line moves to a row of its own under Back and the
end group, so Back stays top left and the gear top right, the language button steps aside when Friends is
in the bar (the gear's Gameplay tab holds Language), and the gaps and Back's side padding close. The skin
itself is on the client's tokens (`--radius-pill`, `--radius-round`, `--motion-fast`, `--ease-standard`,
`--hit-min` and the palette): the bar's raw radii, durations, hit size and colors are gone.

In-match placement (the gear in the board's control bar, `placement="game"`) and the landing's corner
(whose markup already ends in the gear) are as before. Each shell keeps its own gutter, so Back's inset
from the window's edge still differs between the tavern's column and the wide deck screens; the rule is
that Back is the leftmost control of its bar and the gear the rightmost.

Hearthstone semantics do not reach a page's chrome; this follows [[R1581000]]'s menus that fit one screen
and keeps #3189's skin. **Proof.** `top-bar.test.ts` reads every stylesheet and fails a margin or an
`order` on one of the four controls under `.screen-nav` outside `top-bar.css`, fixes the slots' rules and
the single 600 px breakpoint, and holds the sheet to the tokens; `nav.test.tsx` proves BackLink's DOM order
(Back, the line with its title then its extras, the end group ending in the gear) and the replay tests (and spec 13's replay) prove
the replay's Back is the pill, in the player and on the failure screen. Spec 46 visits every menu at
1280x720 and 390x844 and measures the drawn bar: Back's left edge is the leftmost of the bar's controls
and the gear's right edge the rightmost, the landing's corner included.

Rejected: another margin chain per sibling, which breaks whenever a screen's control is absent or the bar
is not full width; a flex `<header>` wrapper per screen (the almanac's, Statistics', the workshop's), which
is what let the gear drift beside Back; a title or extras inside the end group, which would put the title
after the gear; and one gutter for every shell's Back, which moves layouts no spec measures.
