A drawn game counts for neither side and locks neither deck ([[R330]]). Without a draw a series is decided by its fifth game at the latest (2 × `SERIES_WINS_NEEDED` − 1), and a series plays at most `SERIES_MAX_GAMES` (7) games, which leaves room for two drawn games in the longest series and stops a run of draws from holding both players for ever: when the cap is reached with neither side at `SERIES_WINS_NEEDED`, more wins takes the series and equal wins is a series draw (`exhausted`). Each game ends as [[§2.5]] says: a concede or a disconnect loses that game, not the series. Between games a player may forfeit the series, which the other side then wins; during a game the way out is to concede the game. It replaces [[R261]], whose cap was three games
