A card on the field that is no Unit — a Field Spell, or a face-up Trap or Field Trap — whose Durability
left is 0 ([[R5236000]]) goes to its owner's graveyard at the **settled state check** ([[§4.5]]): once
nothing is dying and the heroes and wins are judged, the check moves every such card and starts over
on the board it left. Since the use spends its Durability before its effect runs and the effect's own
state check comes after it, the card stays on the field while its last activation resolves, its effect
reading it there, and leaves after. An activation whose cost paused is owed on `state.work` ([[R113]]);
its card waits until that effect has run.

The move is `send_to_graveyard`'s plain move ([[R3044000]]): no `destroyed`, no Death trigger, no
Reborn, no count of deaths ([[R55]]) and no credit for a kill, and Indestructible does not hold it,
since nothing destroys it. It lands in the graveyard of the player it stood for ([[R2618000]]), or
wherever a replacement sends a card bound for one ([[R460]]); a unit token ceases to exist ([[R11]]).

A Unit at 0 stays ([[R5236001]]). Hearthstone destroys a weapon at 0 Durability; a JackiOh Field Spell
"enters the graveyard" instead, as Core #79 Twinspell always has, so the Field Spell's Death and an
Indestructible Buff change nothing.
