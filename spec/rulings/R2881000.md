[[§9.5]]'s watching a friend took a live 1v1 match only, and [[§13]] left watching a co-op game out of scope ([[R2615000]] repeated it). A co-op game is now watched the way a 1v1 match is, over the same `/ws/watch` socket and the same hello: the server tells the two apart by the match the hello names.

**Who may watch.** A friend of either person of the human team, with neither blocking the other, while the game is live and fewer than `SPECTATORS_PER_MATCH` watch it. The cap counts every watcher of the game, both seats' together, as a 1v1 match's counts both players'. The friend's own teammate holds a seat in the game and is refused, as a 1v1 player watching their own match is. A player who is not a friend, a blocked pair and a full game are refused as for a 1v1 match, with the same one `forbidden` frame and close, and an unfriend, a block either way or the watcher deleting their account lets a watcher already in go at once. The seat's view holds its teammate's duel, their hand and face-down cards among it, so a block between the watcher and the friend's teammate, either way, refuses the hello and lets a watcher already in go at once, as [[§9.4]]'s block ends a watch of either by the other, and the friends list offers no Watch then; the teammate ending a friendship with the watcher ends nothing, as no friendship with the teammate was asked.

**What a watcher sees.** The friend's seat's view ([[§13.6]]): its own duel, its teammate's duel as the teammate sees it, both teams' life and its own team's turn, each view `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` after the state it shows, with the clock's time left as the seat was shown it and no legal action, whether the person or the AI ([[§13.7]]) plays the seat at the time. Nothing else the seat is sent reaches a watcher: no ping ([[§13.10]]), no `clock` or `teammate` frame, no `jloreCapped` and no `economy`. Nothing of the AI team's hidden cards is in that view, so none is in a watcher's.

**What a watcher sends.** Nothing reaches the game: a later hello is answered with the view last sent ([[R1103000]]), and every other frame is dropped unanswered within [[R2609000]]'s limits.

**The count.** Both human seats are told how many watch whenever the count changes, and a seat that reattaches is told it again while anyone watches.

**The end.** As [[R1107000]]: once a watcher has been sent the view that shows the result, its socket is closed and both seats are told the new count. A finished game's actor is kept until each of its watchers has had that view, even once both people have left, so the sweep never drops a watch before its last view. After that the game is the replay [[§13.9]] gives its two people, and a watcher gets no replay.

Rejected: refusing only a block with the friend, which would show someone the teammate blocked, or who blocked the teammate, that teammate's hand; asking the watcher to be a friend of both people, which no block needs and which would make a 1v1 watch and a co-op one differ in whom they let in; a second socket path for co-op watching, which would double the hello gate, the sign-in and the refusals for no difference a watcher can see; showing a watcher only the friend's own duel, which hides the teammate's duel the seat itself is shown and so draws a board the co-op client does not have; and folding nothing back for a watcher, which would refuse every co-op watch after a server restart until the sweep folded the game back.

Amended by issue #2990 ([[R2990000]], [[R2990001]]): a co-op watcher is sent its own emote hand before any view, and sends emotes, relayed live to both people and the other watchers; everything else it sends still reaches nothing.
