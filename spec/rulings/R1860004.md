Stinkyscape #49 Spacemine's "Death: Deal damage equal to its Attack to {targets|random enemy|
random enemies}" reads "attack" as the Attack it died with, buffs included ([[R89]]), dealt as one
[[§4.4]] instance each to `count` different random enemies — the enemy hero or an enemy Unit —
drawn from the match rng ([[R60]]) among those still standing, fewer when fewer stand, through the
new `damageRandomEnemies` verb (`damageSplit` re-draws per hit and can hit one enemy twice). At 0
Attack, nothing ([[R63]]). Then its copy fuses into the Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]). It takes
R1860004 of issue #1860's block, and edits [[§6.3]]'s account of random-enemy damage.
