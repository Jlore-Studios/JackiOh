S+ #166 Mindustrial Revolution reads "For the rest of the game at the start of its owner's turn add a
random Mindustrial card to your hand", its Radiant face "… a random Radiant Mindustrial card …, it costs
(1) less". It is Classic+ #52's effect ([[R458]]): a player effect for the rest of the game on the
caster, "its owner" being the player who played it, at the start of their own turns only ([[R62]]),
before the turn's draw, never at the opponent's. Each turn it adds one card, picked as
[[R3549003]] picks, from the non-token cards of the Stinkyscape+ set tagged Mindustrial ([[R1420]]),
never this card, which is Mindustrial itself ([[R387]]: the re-entry carries its definition, [[R127]]).
It does not end: no board wipe touches it, a player effect with no card on the field. Two Revolutions
add two cards a turn. The Radiant face adds the card Radiant and costing 1 less, never below (0)
([[R65]]), once it has reached the hand ([[§2.4]]). Its badge, public to both seats, is the face's
text as the catalog the game runs under prints it. The numbers, 1 card and 1 less, are printed and
declared nowhere ([[§10.1]]).

Rejected: an effect that ends when the Revolution's caster loses the board, or after a number of
turns; "owner" as the card's printed owner when a card changes hands; and the discount on the base
face, which the text does not print.
