[[§13.1]] has team A go first in both duels. In a 2v2 from a private lobby ([[R3731000]]) both teams are people, so the match's seed decides which lobby team plays as A: [[R1989000]]'s coin, `seat_coin_p1_first(seed)` on `{seed}:seats` at cursor 0, puts the host's team at A when it lands true and the other team there when it lands false. The coin reads only the seed, so the same seed always lines the teams up the same way, and a replay folds unchanged.

Each team keeps its order: its first seat (the host's team's `host`, the other team's `guest2`) is A1 or B1 and plays duel 0, its second (`guest` or `guest3`) A2 or B2 and plays duel 1. So the host's team's host and the other team's `guest2` always face each other in duel 0. An end-to-end server's Start takes a `seed` as a room's join does ([[R143]]), which a networked spec uses to know the lineup.

Where the spec gives the first turn to team A but does not say which people make it, this follows Hearthstone, which tosses for who goes first.

Amends [[R1989000]], whose coin now also seats a 2v2's teams.
