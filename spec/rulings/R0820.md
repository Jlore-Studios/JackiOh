Several trigger multipliers never add (MD-A10): the highest acting one holds, as with Hearthstone's
Drakkari Enchanter for turn triggers and Brann Bronzebeard for Cries. `multipliers::extra_runs`
folds every acting multiplier's number with `max` from `TRIGGER_EXTRA_NONE`, so two Joint Filings
give one extra run, not two, and the same holds for two Double Countings. A fused card gives its
ingredients' highest (`fused_highest_extra`), not their product. With no multiplier the count is
zero and every multiplied path stays off, so a game that never uses one serializes, hashes and
replays as before (D14).
