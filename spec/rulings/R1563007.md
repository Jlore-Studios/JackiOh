The designer asked (#1563) that crafting work as Kazakus's does: the options limited to "4 keywords, 2 hats, 3 effects". As a `craft` prompt opens, the engine draws its **offer** from the match rng and keeps it on the prompt (`craftOffer`): `CRAFT_OFFER_KEYWORDS` (4) keywords, `CRAFT_OFFER_HATS` (2) hats and `CRAFT_OFFER_VERBS` (3) effect verbs, each list in its table's order, a pool no larger than its count offered whole. The keywords come from the whole keyword table ([[R883]]), since a keyword serves the card or a "Give a unit a keyword" block. Meditative #17's hats come from the eight a type takes (Cry, Death, Start of turn, End of turn, When cast and the three reveal hats) and its verbs from those that fit the budget alone under a hat of multiplier 1 that may aim, "Give a unit a keyword" when the cheapest keyword does; a potion prompt's one hat is When cast ([[R1563008]]) and its verbs are those that fit the frame's points. A retired verb is never offered ([[R1563009]]). The offer is drawn after the cost is chosen and before the presets.

A recipe may use only what the offer holds: its keywords and the keyword a block grants, its hats and its verbs. One that uses anything else is refused ("… was not offered") with the state unchanged and the prompt open, as any refused recipe is ([[R880]]). The presets are built from the offer ([[R881]]): Meditative #17's Unit, which needs no hat, always, with a 2-point keyword of the offer when the budget holds one; then its Spell, Field Spell and Trap, each only when the offer holds one of that type's hats and a verb that fits alone under it, so a prompt has one to four; a potion prompt's one Potion per offered verb that fits ([[R1182000]], [[R1182001]]). The AI, the random policy and a timeout still answer with a preset.

Meditative+ #26 Scratch Pad codes from scratch ([[R1182002]]): its prompt has no offer and takes any block but a retired one. The offer travels only to the prompt's holder, whose editor shows only its blocks; the opponent sees that a prompt is open and nothing of it ([[R97]]). It edits [[§6.3]], [[§8.8]], [[§8.9]], [[R881]] and [[R883]].
