The players see a watcher's emotes ([[R2990000]]) apart from the portrait emotes they send each other ([[R643]]), in a sideline strip at the board's edge, and so do the other watchers:

- **The strip.** Each emote shows with its sender's name, the watcher's own as You, held as a player's emoji is and then gone; each watcher has at most one on screen, a new one replacing their old one, and the newest is last. It shows on a 1v1 match's board, on each person's own boards in a co-op game and on the watch screen, which also holds the watcher's emote menu: its hand and nothing else ([[R2990001]]), each emote greyed out while the watcher's limit holds it ([[R2990002]]). On a phone the strip runs along the board's top edge and wraps.
- **Muting.** A player mutes every spectator emote for the rest of the match from the strip; a device setting, "Mute spectator emotes", beside [[R643]]'s "Mute opponent emotes", mutes them in every match. A muted spectator emote is neither shown nor heard. A watcher's own strip still shows its own emotes when it mutes the others'. A block between a seat and a watcher keeps that watcher's emotes from the seat already ([[R2990003]]).

Rejected: showing a watcher's emote on a portrait, where it would read as a player's; and a mute per watcher, which a strip of at most eight names does not need while the match lasts.
