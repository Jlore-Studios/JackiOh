## R783001 — Ranged and Cleave: nothing more

Built (#783). The designer's "Ranged applies to Cleave" adds no rule of its own: Cleave's splash is
plain damage to the units beside the target ([[§4.4]] step 10, [[R63]]) and none of them strikes back
([[R1026]]), so a Ranged Cleaver takes nothing from them, as any Cleaver does, and nothing from the
target it attacked, by [[R783000]]. Its splash lands as ever. The open question this answers (#783,
"is 'applies to Cleave' anything beyond the above?") is read this way, after Hearthstone, where a
splash never deals damage back; the designer may flip it.
