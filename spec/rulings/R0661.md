Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes. The landing page's calls to action carry no Stats link: the site footer's "Stats", beside Patch notes and Card almanac, is the landing page's one way to `/stats`, and a card's detail view keeps its "View full stats" link; the device's own "Your table" ([[R639]]) stays under the fold. A player is never shown where public statistics come from or how they are gated: the statistics page shows no data source, no count of live games towards `PUBLIC_STATS_MIN_LIVE_GAMES`, no progress bar, and no words about AI games padding the figures or a publication gate, in its banner, its "How to read these statistics" notes or its card drill-down; the card detail view's statistics block shows no source badge. `GET /api/stats/cards` still sends `source`, `sourceLabel` and `gate` ([[R654]]); the client shows none of them and reads `source` only to know whether to show the banner. The page keeps the number of games counted on the patch, in one tile named for the patch, never the live count beside it, and, while the patch's figures are provisional, a "Provisional statistics" banner with its way to the previous patch. The Play online lobby (`/play`) shows the player's own visible rank, read once from `GET /api/ranked`, in [[R612]]'s words with the season (Raisin with the placements played, a Grape tier with its division and pips, or a Jlorious position, never the hidden rating), and links the leaderboard (`/leaderboard`).
