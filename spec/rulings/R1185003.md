Meditative+ #24 Bob's second mode puts an enemy Unit into your hand. The spec moved a card between the players' piles only off the field (Steal off the field, [[R12]]), so the field half follows a Bounce ([[R747]]) with the taker in place of the controller: `take_to_hand { target }` (and `ownership::take_into_hand`) changes the card's owner and controller to the taker, reported by `stolen` from the field (public, as the card was, [[R466]]), then moves it to the taker's hand. It leaves the field as a Bounce takes it: [[R78]]'s reset (damage, buffs, statuses and a set's stats go), no Death and no destroy, and the card beneath it in a pile resumes on top ([[R13]]). It is the taker's card from then on, so its later piles are the taker's ([[§3.2]]). A unit token taken ceases to exist on the way ([[R11]]): no hand and no graveyard sees it. The taker's hand cap applies, so a full hand burns it into the taker's graveyard ([[§2.4]]). A rider (`costOverride` and the rest of `TakenRiders`) lands only once it is in the hand. A target gone, or already in the taker's hand, takes nothing.
