A Spell cast on a slashed board ([[R3554002]]) resolves on one half ([[R3554003]]): an untargeted
Spell asks its caster through a `mode` prompt at [[§10.5]] step 5, a targeted Spell takes its
target's half unasked, a random cast draws its half from the game's Rng, and a permanent lands on
its zone's half. A card whose half is already stamped keeps it. Rejected: asking at announcement,
which would prompt for Spells that fizzle before resolving.
