An audit of the AI found that [[R4154400]]'s reading of the next refresh, "this turn's maximum, one more while under `MAX_MANA`", is wrong once a maximum is lowered for the rest of the game. S+ #95 JC Backed ([[R3538006]]) and S+ #82 Endless Expedition ([[R3569008]]) lower it through `mana.perm_mod`. The engine's refresh gives the crystal count capped at `MAX_MANA`, plus the crystals stolen past the cap, plus `perm_mod` ([[§2.3]], [[R1854003]], [[R4331001]]). So at the cap, a maximum of 3 stays 3, while the AI read 4. A (4) Tradable card (M #11 Double Header) then kept its full price and its Trade's place for the rest of the game. A thief at `MAX_MANA` whose own count was still under the cap read no growth where the engine gives one more. This is a judgement about the AI and changes no game.

**The reading.** `next_turn_crystals` adds one crystal while the count under the cap is under `MAX_MANA`, not while the maximum is. That count is this turn's maximum less `perm_mod` and less the crystals stolen past the cap, all public counts. On a seat with no handicap it equals the engine's next maximum whenever this turn's maximum was not floored at 0.

**Not modelled.**

- The handicap ([[R180]]): its mana bonus and cap stay unread, so a seat whose cap is above `MAX_MANA` still reads no growth from `MAX_MANA` on.
- A maximum floored at 0: the reading may say one crystal where the engine gives none. It errs only toward a live card.
- A lost refresh ([[R844]]): the reading counts the next refresh as if it were given, since the loss is of a turn's crystals and not of the maximum, and `lost_crystals` prices it. It errs only toward a live card. Reading 0 would mark every Tradable card dead, even one this turn's crystals pay for.

**Rejected.** An engine read helper that answers what `max_mana_for` gives at the next refresh. It reads the seat's handicap (`mana_bonus`, `mana_cap`), which the evaluation never reads ([[R180]]). Instead, a test holds the AI's reading to the engine's over a grid of counts.
