title: "A slot whose rarity the pack's pool lacks keeps it and draws from the nearest one held (issue #1301)"

A slot that rolls a rarity its pack's pool ([[§14.3]]) holds no card of keeps that rarity, pity's
force included ([[R1339001]]): the pack still holds it, so its pity counter still resets
([[§14.4]]). Its card comes from the nearest rarity the pool does hold: each lower rarity first,
from the nearest down, then each higher one, from the nearest up (`fallback_order` in
`crates/server/src/economy/roll.rs`), drawn as any slot's card is, weighted by
`PACK_UNOWNED_WEIGHTS`. The floored slot ([[R1339000]]) reads every rarity at or above its floor,
`PACK_FLOOR_RARITY`, first, in that order, so it falls to Rare, then up, and below Rare only when
the pool holds nothing at or above it. A pool with no card at all rolls nothing, and the
opening is refused before it writes. Every set that ships holds all five rarities today, so this
waits for a set that does not.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone gives no such case, since its sets print every rarity; stepping
down first keeps a pack's value closest to what its slot rolled without granting more than it.

Rejected: rerolling the rarity until the pool holds it, which would bias the table's odds
([[R1339000]]) and could reset a counter without its rarity dropping; and leaving the slot empty,
which would grant less than `PACK_SLOTS`.
