id: R1301001
title: "A slot whose rarity the pack's pool lacks keeps it and draws from the nearest one held (issue #1301)"
cards: "§14.3, §14.4"
proven_in:
  - crates/server/src/economy/roll.rs
---
A slot that rolls a rarity its pack's pool ([[§14.3]]) holds no card of keeps that rarity: the
pack still holds it, so its pity counter still resets ([[§14.4]]). Its card comes from the nearest
rarity the pool does hold: each lower rarity first, from the nearest down, then each higher one,
from the nearest up (`fallback_order` in `crates/server/src/economy/roll.rs`), drawn as any slot's
card is, weighted by `PACK_UNOWNED_WEIGHTS`. A pool with no card at all rolls nothing, and the
opening is refused before it writes. Every set that ships holds all five rarities today, so this
waits for a set that does not.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone gives no such case, since its sets print every rarity; stepping
down first keeps a pack's value closest to what its slot rolled without granting more than it.

Rejected: rerolling the rarity until the pool holds it, which would bias the rates the pity math
is tuned to and could reset a counter without its rarity dropping; and leaving the slot empty,
which would grant less than `PACK_SLOTS`.
