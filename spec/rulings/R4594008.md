Any declared enemy attack springs it, but the cancel lands only when the attacker's current Attack — read through the layers at declaration — meets the declared threshold: a buffed 4-Attack attacker counts as much as a printed 5. A forced attack declares nothing and never fires ([[R53]]); its own controller's attacks never do. It fires every time ([[R2657001]]).
