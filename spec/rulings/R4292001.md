Issue #4292 takes Armor 2 off Radiant Force Projector (S+ #42), which keeps its doubled pool (10, against the base face's 5) as its one upgrade ([[R275]]). Its aura reads the face's own printed Armor ([[R3556004]]), so it now lends its controller's Units none, and the pool soaks the whole hit. A shipped catalog that printed Armor 2 folds as it was played, the aura lending 2 under the same script, with no kept version ([[R767000]]). Rejected: removing the aura from the script, which would take a kept script for old games and change nothing in a game on this catalog.
