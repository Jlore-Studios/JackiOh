I21 judges the Units Immune to Spells as the step begins, by instance id. In the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 6323 a Trap destroyed C+ #6 Wrong-House Attacker, given Immune to Spells, and its Reborn brought it back under the same id ([[R175]]) with nothing given; S #11.3 Flame Strike then hit it, and I21 named the Spell. In plain seed 11748 #94 Genn's Greed exiled from a deck a card the step had already moved off the field.

**A Unit that has left is not the one that stood.** Once an event in the step destroys, exiles, bounces or shuffles away a Unit, I21 judges that event as before and no later one naming its id: a card returned under it is a new object, and a card in a hand or a deck is no Unit on the field, where Immune to Spells keeps a Spell's effects off ([[§6.1]]).
