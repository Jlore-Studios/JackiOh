A patch opens a new statistics window for every card ([[R1367000]]), so every card's figures start again, while a micro opens one only for the families it plays. [[R1365000]]'s bars made patches far too easily. Ten families, about one family in seventy of the shipped pool, restarted every card's figures after balance passes of 10 to 33 cards. A single divergence that named no card, in one of the 200 unforced games, made v0.4.7 (4 families), v0.4.9 (5), v0.5.8 (9) and v0.5.10 (6) patches. A patch should mean the change reaches enough of the game that every card's statistics need to restart. Anything smaller is a micro.

- **Families.** `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` is 50 (it was 10). A change in which 50 or more families play differently is a patch. One of 49 is a micro, as is one of wording alone, however many cards it touches. Amended by [[R2499000]]: 15 reprinted families (`PATCH_MIN_REPRINTS`) make a patch too.
- **A rule every game meets.** Stage 2 still replays `UNFORCED_GAMES` (200) unforced games against the base build after a change to play code. The divergences that touch no card are a rule every game meets, which makes a patch, only once they reach `RULE_MIN_GAMES` of those games: a quarter of them, 50. A base build that refuses the head's decks is still a rule ([[R1705004]]), since it can play none of them. Divergences without a card in fewer games are a rule few games meet. They count as no card, as stage 1 counts play code it could not confirm, and the change is tiered by the families its divergences do name. `classify` prints how many games held one.
- **Claims.** `--rule` ([[R1366000]], [[R1370000]]) claims the rule every game meets alone. A fragment claiming one fails when too few games diverge without a card, and divergences in fewer games ask for no claim.
- **History.** Every entry of `patches.json` records its tier, so no shipped version is renamed or re-tiered. The shipped tiers stand as named. Amended by [[R2532000]]: the owner had the v0.5 line renamed by these tiers. The reading of an entry with no tier ([[R1367000]], [[R1369000]]) follows `PATCH_MIN_CARDS`, and no entry lacks one.

Rejected: a share of the shipped families, which would move the bar each time a set ships with no change to what a patch means; and a micro tier for every rule, which would make a deck-size change ([[R1705004]]) or the first player's opening hand a micro.
