[[R1020]] says of 饕餮 that a Trap trigger it eats never fires from a unit zone. The same holds for
every Fuse ([[§6.3]]): a Trap's or Field Trap's text fused into a card that stands in a unit zone
and is no trap there never fires from it, whether an Equip fused it ([[R1086]]), a Starship Piece's
copy carried it into its Dormant Starship ([[R1852000]]), or any other Fuse put it onto a Unit
([[R77]], [[R102]]). An ingredient fused inside an ingredient counts too.

The card's other texts work as before: its auras, its start- and end-of-turn lines, its Death and its
Activates. A card that is still a trap where it stands, an animated Field Trap in a unit zone
([[R383]], C+ #74), fires the text as a trap, as it always did. Hearthstone's Secrets act only as
Secrets, and a minion that somehow held a Secret's text would not spring it.

The engine leaves such a trigger out of the card's holder (`triggers::holder_of`) and out of what
wakes it, read by `fuse::is_trap_ingredient_trigger` off the trigger's ingredient. A trap's condition,
its `when` ([[R99]]), is asked only where traps fire ([[§10.3]]), so a Trap's trigger queued as a
unit's answered every event it named, its own side's included: 饕餮 holding Wormhole countered its own
controller's Spell and cast it again, without end.

Rejected: firing the text as a trap from the unit zone, which nothing arms or spends there, so it
would answer every event its condition admits for as long as the card stands.
