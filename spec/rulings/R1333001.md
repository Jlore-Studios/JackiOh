[[§14.8]] says a game's XP stands in for time played, `XP_PER_ACTION` per action the player took
and `XP_WIN_BONUS` more for a win, capped at `XP_GAME_CAP`, and #1333 asks for the three to be set
from measured action counts so a ladder game averages `XP_LADDER_TARGET_MEAN`, 1,000, and a
practice game half of it.

- **The games** are `crates/server/tests/fixtures/xp-selfplay.jsonl`: 40 games of this build's AI
  playing itself at the gate's budget, written by `cargo jackioh selfplay --games 40 --seed
  xp-1333`, one line each with its log and result (the same build writes the same bytes,
  [[R1006001]]). Each of the 80 seats counts its own actions as the reward hook does
  ([[R1302004]]): a mean of 35.4375 a seat, 38.525 for a winner and 32.35 for a loser, 114 at most.
- **The numbers**: 20 XP an action, 600 for a win and 2,000 at most average 999.75 XP a ladder
  game, and 499.875 a practice game at `XP_PRACTICE_SCALE` with its rounding down ([[R1302007]]).
  The win bonus is the large share #1333 asks for; the cap is twice the target, which two seats
  reach, so a long game pays more than a short one without a loop of empty actions farming it.
- **The test** folds nothing: it reads each game's log and result, prices every seat with
  `game_xp`, and holds each mean within 5% of its target, so a change to the numbers or to what
  counts as an action that moves the mean that far fails it. A later AI plays other games; the
  fixture keeps these until the numbers are measured again.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone's reward track pays XP by time played and wins; the spec's
proxy for time is the action count, and these only fix its numbers.

Rejected: the golden traces, which the fuzz's random policy plays (51.5 actions a seat, a quarter
of them position switches), so they measure no real game; and live games, of which none is recorded
while `ECONOMY_LIVE` is off. The numbers should be measured again on live games once #1310 turns
the economy on.
