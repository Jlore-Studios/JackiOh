S #151's sixth entry reads "Fill your board with Drone Interceptors, they have stack" (ST-S25-8). The Stack keyword alone would do nothing to a Unit summoned onto the field, so "they have stack" is read as stacking.

- **Each zone.** A Drone Interceptor is summoned into each of its caster's five unit zones, in lane order. An empty zone takes it as any summon's does; an occupied one takes it on top of the pile there ([[§3.2]]), the Unit beneath it buried, as a Stack play's would be. A Locked zone takes it as a summon's does ([[R688]]); a zone held for a Reborn return takes none ([[R64]]).
- **Its caster's side alone.** The enemy's zones are not touched.
