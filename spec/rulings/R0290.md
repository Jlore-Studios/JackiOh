[[§9.9]]'s tiers raise the AI seat's resources; the tutorial needs one that lowers them. `AI_TUTORIAL` is a handicap like the three tiers ([[R180]]) — deck size 12, no mana bonus, mana cap 3, no extra opening cards and no extra draws — plus one new, optional handicap field, `heroHealth`: the health the seat's hero starts the game with, in place of [[§2]]'s 30. Absent means 30, and `createGame` stores it on the seat only when it moves the hero off 30, so no tier's state, hash or replay changes. A handicap's `heroHealth` is a positive integer; the tutorial's is 20. Nothing else about the hero changes: its health has no upper cap ([[§3]]), fatigue and every damage rule apply, and an effect that sets health to a number (#53 Reno's 30) does exactly that. The tutorial handicap is not one of `DIFFICULTIES`: no player picks it, the tutorial deals it. The AI's search, evaluation and budget stay every tier's ([[§9.9]]), so the opponent is easier by its resources alone, and the lesson decks ([[R291]]) do the rest
