[[§9.9]]'s tiers raise the AI seat's resources; the tutorial needs one that lowers them. `AI_TUTORIAL` is a handicap like the three tiers ([[R180]]) — deck size 12, no mana bonus, mana cap 3, no extra opening cards and no extra draws — plus one new, optional handicap field, `heroHealth`: the health the seat's hero starts the game with, in place of [[§2]]'s 30. Absent means 30, and `createGame` stores it on the seat only when it moves the hero off 30, so no tier's state, hash or replay changes. A handicap's `heroHealth` is a positive integer; the tutorial's is 20. Nothing else about the hero changes: its health has no upper cap ([[§3]]), fatigue and every damage rule apply, and an effect that sets health to a number (#53 Reno's 30) does exactly that. The tutorial handicap is not one of `DIFFICULTIES`: no player picks it, the tutorial deals it. The AI's search, evaluation and budget stay every tier's ([[§9.9]]), so the opponent is easier by its resources alone, and the lesson decks ([[R291]]) do the rest. With the learned evaluation on, the evaluation is the hand eval here, where the tiers take the network's correction, as the lessons are scripts written for this opponent ([[R1012003]]). A lesson's human cannot lose ([[R2201000]]): the floor is a field of the human's handicap, not of `AI_TUTORIAL`, whose seat can lose as every tier's can; against it the AI finds no lethal and trades instead of hitting a hero at its floor. `heroFloor` and `poolHold` ([[R2201001]]) join `heroHealth` as handicap fields stored only when set, so no tier's state, hash or replay changes. The tier's frozen games (`tutorial_tier`) seat their greedy stand-in without the floor, so they still measure the tier by play. Since issue #2433 `AI_TUTORIAL` also carried a bar on what it plays ([[R2433000]]), and since issue #2533 that bar is `tutorialCardsOnly`: the tutorial opponent plays only Training Grounds cards that are not Legendary or Mythic, whatever reaches its hand ([[R2533000]]), and its deck may hold copies of a card ([[R2533001]]); the frozen tier games seat it without the bar or the copies, as they deal distinct cards from every set.
