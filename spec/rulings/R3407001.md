Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Each of [[R3407000]]'s 23 cards sounds its own effect once (`audio/signatureCues.ts`, `SIGNATURE_SFX`), a synthesized recipe of its own (`audio/sfx.ts`) layering the issue's sound cell, as [[R3400001]]'s eight do, each within 1.6 s and gain 0.8 at a routine card's level:

- MELCHIOR `ballotBeep` (a sterile beep count, a ballot clack, a rising tone); BALTHASAR `lullabySigh` (a soft monitor blip, a lullaby hum, a worried sigh); CASPER `compactTut` (a compact snapping shut, a sultry chime, a tut);
- xxFreshJack `glitchDrop` (a glitch stutter, a crowd roar, a bass drop); iKwzki `harpGliss` (a harp glissando, a warm two-note chime); Shockwave447 `markerThunder` (fast-forwarded chatter, a marker squeak, an electric thunderclap);
- Job Application `stampTrill` (a pneumatic thunk, a desk-phone trill, a rubber stamp); Paycheck `tillRattle` (a paper feed, a roulette rattle, a till drawer); Scornboi `gavelSlam` (a gavel crack, a scoff, a heavy hatch slam); Megabase `dockingChime` (a deep docking clank, a lobby chime, a coin cascade);
- IN33DH3 `keyboardPing` (a keyboard flurry, a chirpy AI ping, a smug click); IN33DH3 Prime `synthSnap` (a low synth swell, a confident snap, a drum tap); the Wardrum Blob `blobDrum` (a squishy plop, a two-beat hand drum, a happy hum);
- Alteran Reach `warpSparkle` (a warp whoosh, a sparkling chime per node); Edge of the Universe `tearChoir` (paper tearing, a long held choir note, then silence); Wildar `cackleThunder` (a cackle, a crack of thunder, two coin flicks);
- Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition) `kazooRatchet` (a wet squelch, a spinning ratchet, a kazoo fanfare); the Stinkyscape Golem `thrusterSniff` (a grinding rock thud, a thruster roar, a loud sniff); Call to Chaos (Scape Edition) `marbleGong` (a deep tilting groan, rolling marbles, a cosmic gong);
- Sanctum `sanctumBell` (a hushed choir breath, a page turn, a soft bell); Stimmycheck `cashWhistle` (a cash-drawer ping, then a slowly rising whistle); Narcor `whisperClink` (a whispered breath, a shimmer, a soft clink); Red Narcor `crystalRise` (a deep resonant hum rising to a bright crystal ring).

A Spell sounds as its play is read (`cardPlayed`), a Unit as it lands face up in a unit zone and, new, a Field Spell as it lands face up in the backrow (`summoned` into `backrow`), the moment its signature's entrance starts; a Field Spell never sounds on its play, which would sound it twice. Each sounds for the `defId` its event carries alone, so a hidden play (R97's sentinel) and a card set face down (a `summoned` with its `formerId`, R227) sound nothing more ([[R203]]). Rejected: each card's sound as a sequence of the existing recipes, which would sound like the other cards that play them.
