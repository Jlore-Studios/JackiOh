An Echo repeat opens a fresh pick for each target the card declares ([[§6.2]] Echo, [[§10.6]], [[R81]]). A script can say otherwise: `Script.echoKeepsTargets`, which only M #5 Death by 1000 cuts sets. Its repeats hit the one Unit its first resolution hit, with no new prompt; the card's text is "Echo X … Deal {damage} damage to a Unit", read as one aim repeated ([[R3048001]]).

- **What a repeat takes.** For each declaration, in order, the picks the first resolution took: the play's `targets` or a cast's choices, the flat list read per declaration as [[R90]] reads it. A pick is kept while the declaration still offers it and its card has not left the field since the choices were made ([[R174]]): a Unit that died and came back by Reborn is a new arrival ([[R83]]) and is not hit again.
- **A pick that is gone fizzles the repeat.** With no pick left a declaration takes none, as one the board cannot satisfy takes none ([[R90]]), and the repeat resolves with nothing to hit: it fizzles ([[R76]]) and asks nothing. It never falls back to another Unit, so once its target has died or left the field the remaining repeats of Death by 1000 cuts do nothing. Dropping the repeats from the queue outright was the other reading; it was rejected because a repeat is a whole resolution of the text, and the fizzle is the rule the spec already has for a pick that has gone.
- **No new targeting.** A kept pick is the first resolution's, not a new aim: it is neither charged nor redirected again ([[R450]]), as the pick of a random repeat is not, so a Unit that cost a discard to target costs it once.
- **Modes are still asked.** Only targets are kept; a mode declaration still opens its prompt in each repeat.
- **Random, copied, fused.** A random cast ([[R452]]) picks once, at random, and every repeat keeps the pick. An Echo that has the text of such a card ([[R399]]) keeps its picks, since the text it runs is the card's. A fusion keeps them when an ingredient does ([[R102]]), reading the picks per declaration as it reads its ingredients' choices.
- **Every other card** asks afresh in each repeat, as before.
- **Replays.** A game played through v0.5.11p folds under `script_v0_5_11o`, whose repeats ask ([[R767000]]).
