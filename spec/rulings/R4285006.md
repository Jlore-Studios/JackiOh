Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes IV prints S #83 Halo at 3/1 with a Radiant face of 6/1. [[R275]]'s stat half holds a Radiant Unit's health to at least twice its base face's, which a 1 → 1 breaks, where the same patch gives Orca and Phalanx 3/1 → 6/2. The designer's printed numbers stand, as [[R275]] lets them for a named card: Halo is the one entry of `STAT_EXCEPTIONS`. Should the designer mean 6/2, the catalog's Radiant health is the one change and this exception goes.
