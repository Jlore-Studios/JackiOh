[[R11]] ends a unit token that leaves the field or the resolving zone, and a unit-token card that leaves a hand other than by being played, whether the deck it is sent into is full or not. A full deck already reported it as [[R316]]'s `libraryOverflow` with outcome `ceased`; a deck with room reported it `shuffledIn` at a position, a card in no library, and ran [[R151]]'s arrival roll on it. Now a shuffle-in or a placement at a named end that ends such a card reports it as a full deck does: one `libraryOverflow` `ceased`, no `shuffledIn` (which names a card now in a library, [[R1838001]]), no arrival and no slot taken, and the funnel answers that the card was dropped. The position is still drawn from the match rng before the move, as it was, so every later draw of the game stays where it was and only the events change. S #12.3 Psychic Scream's unit tokens from either field are the plainest case ([[§6.3]]). A copy shuffled into the library it was made in never leaves that library, so it lives as before ([[R34]]).
