R4589001 (the Recursion cards' time and copies, [[R4589000]]): "this turn" is this player-turn
(`GameState.turn`), and "since your last turn" is this player-turn and the one before
(`SINCE_LAST_TURN` 1). A random pick weighs each death record once ([[R60]]); IA #99, #110 and
#116 each pick one record and copy it once or twice. A copy is fresh, on the face the card died
with ([[R4577002]]), and two copies come from the one pick.
