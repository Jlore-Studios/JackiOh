TG #13 springs on an enemy Unit's declared attack on its controller's hero, in [[§4.2]] step 4's trap window, after the exertion is spent ([[R44]]); a forced attack opens no window ([[R121]]), and an attack on one of your Units leaves it set. "All enemies" are the enemy hero and every enemy Unit, the attacker among them, one hit each before one state check ([[R59]]). An attacker that check collects no longer stands as declared, so no combat resolves and the hero takes nothing ([[R220]]): the designer's note, "if the unit attacked your hero with <=3 life and no armor it would die and your hero wouldn't take damage". One that survives (more Health, Armor, a Divine Shield) fights on.
