After each action, each whole effect or trigger, each cast-on-draw cast and each combat (each forced attack counts as one, [[R53]]); never between the hits of one effect. Two heroes at 0 or less in that check is a draw (designer, 2026-09-17). A Sacrifice is one effect of the list that makes it ([[§6.3]]), so when the sacrificed unit's Death asks, finishing that Death pass after the answer runs no check: the check waits for the rest of the list, as it does when nothing asks ([[R113]]), and a heal later in the same Cry still saves a unit the Cry brought to 0 first. Only the state check's own pass goes round again once a Death question is answered ([[§4.5]] step 5, [[R156]]); a Tribute paid at [[§10.5]] step 2 leaves the check to step 4's loop ([[R118]]). Two effect lists also check inside themselves, each for a reason of its own: Radiant My Pawn's, between its destroy and the AI turn it hands over ([[R283]]), and, since patch v0.2.0, C+ #32.3 Blade Storm's, whose every round is an effect list with its own check — the base face's round casts Whirlwind (C+ #21), the Radiant face's deals 1 damage to all enemy Units — so the storm stops after a round in which any unit died (a Reborn death included), after its round cap, `BLADE_STORM_ROUNDS` (30) (printed on the Radiant face only, [[R652]]), which only a Degrade or Upgrade of that card moves ([[R386]]), or when no target is left
