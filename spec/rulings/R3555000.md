S+ #23 GIGA-ify reads "Make a permanent in your hand GIGA and Indestructible" (two on its Radiant
face) and S+ #100 Plesiosize "Give a unit in your hand +6/+6 and make it GIGA" (+12/+12 Radiant).
The spec has no such pick, so it is read as follows.

- **The pick.** "A permanent / a Unit in your hand" names no random, so it is a pick declared with
  the play ([[R81]]) from the player's own hand: a Unit, Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap for #23, a
  Unit for #100. A Spell is never offered for #23.
- **The grants.** GIGA, Indestructible and the +N/+N are given in the hand and kept onto the field
  ([[R1878003]]), where the card enters GIGA ([[R3546002]]).
- **Immutable.** An Immutable hand card may be picked: [[R781001]] bars a Spell's aim only on the
  field, and a grant or a stat buff is none of [[R23]]'s.
- **Fewer.** Radiant #23 picks two, and fewer resolve on what there is ([[R90]]).

Rejected: a random pick.
