[[§4.5]] step 4 returns a collected Reborn unit to the zone it reserved in two cases the step did not spell out. A unit token that has Reborn — granted by #63 Plastic Surgery, or rolled by #80 Zao Gao, since [[§6.1]]'s pool keeps Reborn for tokens — ceases to exist as it leaves the field ([[R11]]), and Reborn brings it back all the same, as a reset token at 1 health without Reborn: one of the two ways back past [[R11]]'s "nothing can reach it again" — C+ #35 Rollback's restore, which recreates a token from its snapshot, is the other ([[R419]]) — as Hearthstone resummons a token with Reborn. A token summoned X/X comes back as that X/X: [[§7]]'s `statsOverride` (and the Bread Token's `armorOverride`) is its printed face, [[§10.4]]'s layer 1, so the reset keeps it, where [[R78]] takes it off a card that leaves the field for good — a Bread Token back as a printed 0/0 at 0 health would only die again. And a Reborn unit that died on top of a Stack pile returns on top of that pile: the card beneath resumed in the zone as the top left ([[§3.2]]), which is not entering it, so the zone is still the one [[R64]] reserved, and the return lands on the pile (a Lock on the zone stops nothing since balance patch 1, [[R688]]). The resumed card goes dormant again
