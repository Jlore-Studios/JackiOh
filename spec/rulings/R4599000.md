S #150 Call to Chaos (Stinky Edition)'s fifth entry switches every enemy Unit to Attack Position and Freezes them ([[R1882003]]). Its freeze already passed a Unit Immune to Spells by, but its switch did not: the daily fuzz's plain seed 2071 switched an enemy S+ #111 Mace, immune, out of Defense.
**The switch passes it by too.** A Spell passes an immune field Unit by, whatever its wording ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]), so the entry switches and Freezes every other enemy Unit and leaves the immune one in the position it stood in, unfrozen. Each other "every Unit" switch already asks the same question (`switch_all_positions`).
Rejected: switching the immune Unit and leaving only the Freeze off, which splits one entry's text over the same Units.
