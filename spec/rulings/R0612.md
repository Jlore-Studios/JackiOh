The client reads its own season, username, rank, streak, record and season badges (`GET /api/ranked`), the leaderboard (`GET /api/leaderboard`: Jlorious #1–#100, then every other placed player grouped by Grape tier, highest first, then how many are still placing), and both seats' ranks for the match screen (`GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks`). Each leaderboard row and both seats carry the player's `profileId` and `username` ([[R1436]]). The profile shows its tier, division and pips with its badges. No read carries the hidden rating, deviation or volatility — not even to its owner — and a caller learns nothing about a match it is not in

Amended by [[R2101000]]: both seats of `GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks` carry their win `streak` too, for a ranked match's flame.

Amended by [[R2388000]]: once a ranked match's rated record is written, both seats of `GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks` carry their visible `rankBefore` and `rankAfter` too, never a rating.
