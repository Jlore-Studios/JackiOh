The client reads its own season, tag, rank, streak, record and season badges (`GET /api/ranked`), the leaderboard (`GET /api/leaderboard`: Jlorious #1–#100, then every other placed player grouped by Grape tier, highest first, then how many are still placing), and both seats' ranks for the match screen. The profile shows its tier, division and pips with its badges. No read carries the hidden rating, deviation or volatility — not even to its owner — and a caller learns nothing about a match it is not in
