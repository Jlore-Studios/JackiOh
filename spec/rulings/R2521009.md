A card whose definition carries `resource` ([[R1840000]]: the six Stinkyscape Resources, Reknite, Korrelite, Gellium, Axnit, Narcor and Red Narcor) prints one line under its text on its full face, `Credits: <min>–<max>`, the `sellMin` and `sellMax` of the face shown: the base face its `resource.base` prices, the Radiant face its `resource.radiant` ones, so Korrelite reads `Credits: 2–5` on its base face and `Credits: 4–10` on its Radiant face. It is the same face wherever one is drawn ([[§10.10]]): in hand, in the inspect views, in the Almanac and in the deck builder, in play or out, and the whole text a clamped face's preview shows carries it too. The words go through the reader's language ([[R711000]]), the numbers as the definition prints them. A card with no `resource` prints no such line. The client reads the definition alone and works out no price ([[§10.8]], CLAUDE.md rule 7): what a sale actually pays is the engine's roll ([[R1840001]]), never this line. Rejected: writing the line into the catalog's face text, which would make it a rule's text, change the Radiant diff ([[R277]]) and every text sweep, and drift from `resource` the day a price is patched.
