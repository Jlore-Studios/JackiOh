Showdown's Radiant face sets a turn watcher (MD-B22): a `thisTurn` player modifier (`ModifierKind::LaneWatch`, badged in the card's own words, [[R169]]) that answers, through the trigger queue (`@laneWatch`), the caster's resolution of a permanent played into one of their zones in the chosen lane, and any summon onto their side in that lane — a move (an animate, a steal, a rotation) is not putting a card there. Each answer re-enters the card's `book` step as its owner ([[R127]]), casting a new C+ #71 Book of Buff (its base face) with an aimed cast: `aimAt` takes the named legal pick for the cast's first target declaration, falling back to the normal choice when it is not legal. The Book is a play ([[R70]]) that goes to the caster's graveyard ([[R87]]).
