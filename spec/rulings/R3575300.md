The AI's evaluation read no Burn or Toxic count ([[R3540000]]), on a Unit or on a hero, so a count was worth nothing until it ticked. The shadow-ban sweep flagged S+ #144 and #145 Fire Beam, #146 and #148 Scourge Mortar, #151 Volcanic Rounds and #153 Noxious Rounds as never played at Hard: a Fire Beam's or a Scourge Mortar's Activate ("Burn N an enemy Unit", "Toxic N an enemy Unit") changed nothing the evaluation read, so its price ([[R2564000]]) was no price and the piece read as its 0/3 or 0/2 body; and a hit that Volcanic or Noxious Rounds turns into its target's count ([[R3540003]]) read as no hit. No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** A count is damage already on its way. Its coming ticks are walked as [[R3540001]] deals them, one at the start of each of its carrier's owner's turns: Burn first, unpierced, so the carrier's Armor takes its Armor off each tick (a hero's, `hero_armor_of`), then down by `BURN_DECAY`; then Toxic, piercing and lasting; an unspent Divine Shield takes the first tick whole; until the ticks have dealt the carrier's life. Each tick counts `AI_EVAL.affliction_turn_share` to the power of the owner's turns until it lands, the next tick the first power. On a Unit, a tick that leaves it alive takes `AI_EVAL.health` a point it deals, and the tick that kills it takes the rest of what its death would take (its body inside its Brittle share, its Plague Counters and its turn hooks, but never what its Death leaves, [[R2424000]]); an Indestructible Unit takes no damage and loses nothing. `unit_worth` takes that off, so a kill of a burning Unit is worth less, as it is dying anyway. On a hero, each tick takes what it deals off `hero_value`'s curve, read down to 0 life with no `win` in it, since a tick that would end the game is the reply's and the lethal search's to see, and the enemy hero's discounted points shorten the race (`enemy_health`) as damage does.

**The share.** `AI_EVAL.affliction_turn_share` is 0.5, as `slumber_turn_share` holds a turn's wait ([[R2380000]]) and Brittle 1 is half "the next tick away": a tick is a loss its owner still has a turn to answer, with a heal, a Vanilla or a bounce. It was set before any measurement, not to the sweep. The frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0, so its yardstick is unchanged.

**What is not priced.** The "Damage also Burns N / applies Toxic N" riders as keywords of the Unit that carries them (`KeywordWeights` names neither); a tick's own chain of effects, such as a Death it sets off; and the static trade model's reading of a converted hit (`blow`), which the opponent's reply and the threat terms still read as damage.
