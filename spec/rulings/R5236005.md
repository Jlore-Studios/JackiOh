Durability X is a numbered keyword ([[R386]]), `NumberedKey::Durability`, more being better, tuned in
the layers like Armor and Lucky: a Buff's X row raises it by 1 (by its multiplier's steps), a Nerf's
lowers it by 1, never below 1, as Activate X never drops below 1 ([[R386]]). Its row acts while the
card lasts, so it is live wherever R3888000's table calls a lasting number live ([[R3888000]]).

The card's Durability left moves with X ([[R5236000]]): a Buff on a card that spent 1 of Durability 2
leaves it 2 of 3. A Nerf on a card with none left keeps it at 0, X never dropping below 1 and the
spent count staying.

A Nerf's keyword row never removes Durability, which would lift the card's cap on uses: Durability is
one of the harmful keywords (`TUNE_HARMFUL_KEYWORDS`), beside Brittle. A Buff does not remove it either.
