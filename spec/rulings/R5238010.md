Felinors vs Humans #61 is Hearthstone's Devolve: "a random Unit that costs (1) less". `transform_cost_step` ([[R3557004]]) asks for prices at or above `start + step` and takes the cheapest, right for a step up and for an exact step down, wrong across a price hole: an (8) asked for a (7), when no Unit costs (7), became another (8).

**The rule.** With a negative step the asked price is `start + step`, floored at 0, and the Transform takes the dearest price at or below it that its type's pool holds: the exact price when there is one, else the closest cheaper one. A (0) asked for less becomes a random (0), as Hearthstone's Devolve does. With nothing at or below it, the card stays as it is ([[R129]]). A step of 0 or more reads as before.
