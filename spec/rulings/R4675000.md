Every card has its **play versions**: the runs of shipped versions, in `patches.json`'s order ([[R105]]), over which it plays the same. `jackioh_cards::play_versions` reads them at compile time off the history every build carries, the same reading of `patches.json` a card's window takes ([[R1367000]]); `deck_play_versions` keeps the cards a deck can hold, those of a set that ships and no token, in catalog order. A play version has an id, `<card>~<first version>`; its versions, oldest first, so its first and its last; its card's name at its last version; and whether it is live, the card's version in the newest shipped catalog, whose last version moves at every ship. Its id never moves: its first version is fixed once it starts. `@` is a past version's mark (`<card>@<version>`, [[R3538002]]), so a play version's is `~`.

A card's first play version starts where a change line adds it, and a new one starts where:
- a change line changes one of its fields that plays: any field but its wording ([[R1365000]]'s `WORDING_FIELDS`: a rename, a face's text, its references) and `loc` (`PLAY_VERSION_QUIET_FIELDS`), its script's line count, which other cards read but which never moves its own play; or
- the version's own `plays` claim it: its family (`card_family`), or its id for a set's own token, which the classifier reads as a family of its own ([[R1365000]]), so a script change claimed through `--plays` starts one ([[R1366000]]) and a token's change starts its card's. A claim whose every change line there moves `loc` alone is no claim: #1371's retcon read the plays of the versions before #1366 from their `changes`, counting `loc` as play, and 94 such claims, all of v0.2.x, would otherwise restart cards that played the same.

A day's cut, a patch with `includes` ([[R3944000]]), restates its micros' plays, which started their play versions when they shipped, and starts none; a major's `includes` names the micros it rolls up, but its `plays` are its own. A tier starts none: a major or a patch opens a window for every card ([[R1367000]]) but ends no play version, and a rule every game meets claims no family, so it starts none (a system's patch claims only its own families, [[R2529000]]). A line removing a card ends its last play version at the version before. The history holds 3,856 play versions of the 800 deck cards as this was built, from one (60 cards) to 28.

The name keeps them apart from a card's cosmetic versions ([[R2725000]]) and from Legacy, a card name and tag in Training Grounds ([[R2511000]]): the tab that will show them is called Experimental.

Built on `patch_history.rs`'s reading of `patches.json`, which every target compiles in and whose change lines reproduce every snapshot's playing fields. Rejected: PAST's play-alike runs ([[R3538002]]), which start at `BROWSER_CARRIED_FROM` (v0.3.0c), compare face text and see no script claimed through `plays`; and `card_eras` ([[R4382000]]), which split on wording and on kept scripts and leave out the newest version. Both stay as they are, for the replays and pools they serve. Hearthstone has no counterpart.
