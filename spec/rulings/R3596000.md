Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A held set's lines are written with its cards and rendered only when it ships ([[R1420]]). So the Stinkyscape+ release (issue #3596, [[R3535000]], [[R3572000]]) owes the files of all 440 of its lines at once: a play, a death and an attack line for each of its Units, tokens included, and a cast line for each of its other entries. The shipped sets' 1,465 files already took about 15.7 MiB of [[R1884000]]'s 16 MiB, counted in 4 KiB blocks. The set's lines take about 4.8 MiB more, 20.5 MiB in all, so they do not fit.

`VOICE_BUDGET_BYTES` (`apps/web/src/audio/constants.ts`, and `gen-voice.mjs`'s `BUDGET_BYTES` beside it) is 24 MiB from the release, still counted in 4 KiB blocks, and every line still stays within 4 s ([[R501]]). That leaves about 3.5 MiB for the lines fullsends 2 and 3 may reword. Nothing else changes: the renderers, the voices, the per-line caps, and the client's fetch of a file only when it needs that line, so the budget bounds what is deployed, not what one player downloads. Rejected, as [[R1884000]] rejected them: a lower bitrate for every file, which would re-render and re-check every shipped line for one set's sake; and dropping lines from the set, since every Unit has its three lines and every other card its cast line ([[R1448]]).
