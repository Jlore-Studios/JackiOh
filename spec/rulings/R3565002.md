S+ #132 and #133 read "the first time you play each Automa-α/β (ζ) cost card, add the subsequent cost Automa-α/β (ζ) to your hand". [[R1863000]]'s watcher (S #44) answers the first play of each card it names, so the text is read as that watcher over a ladder of five cards by cost, (0) to (4).

- **The ladders.** α: Dagger, Mace, Fortress, Scepter, Reign (S+ #110 to #114). β: Flare, Horizon, Zenith, Antumbra, Eclipse (#115 to #119). ζ: Daglare, Macizon, Fortnith, Sceptumbra, Reignclipse (#133.1 to #133.5).
- **The choice** of α or β is a Mode choice made with the play ([[R81]]) and travels with the play; no mode reads as α. α adds Dagger, β adds Flare, and the watch follows that ladder alone.
- **One watch** for each cast, over the rungs (0) to (3): the first play of each after it resolves adds the next rung to its caster's hand, once per rung and per cast, and the top rung adds none. Two casts keep two records. Its badge is the face's text with "(α)" or "(β)" after it, in both views ([[R169]]); the ζ card's is its face's text.
- **The Radiant faces.** Every card the Radiant face adds is Radiant. #133's "a Radiant Dagger and Flare" is the Radiant fusion of the two, a Radiant Daglare. "The first time you play each Radiant … cost card" does not narrow the plays that count: any play of the rung counts.

Rejected: a watch only a Radiant play of a rung answers, which would make the Radiant face the weaker one ([[R275]]); and two Radiant cards for #133's Radiant face, the fusion being the one card the list names.
