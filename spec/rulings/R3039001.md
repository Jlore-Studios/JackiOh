The owner's pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #3000, section 1) keeps Flashback, superseding [[R783013]], which had a card lose the keyword for good as its Flashback played it ("the card loses the keyword after it is used the one time").

**Nothing is spent.** A play by the card's own Flashback ([[R783012]]) is a play from the graveyard as before, at its cost, but [[§10.5]] step 1 no longer reads whether the play is Flashback's, and step 4 writes nothing on the card as it leaves the graveyard. The card keeps its Flashback, printed or lent by an aura (Stinkyscape #152 Sanctum, [[R1869003]]), so whenever it lies in its owner's graveyard it is offered again at its cost: a Spell goes back to its graveyard as any Spell does and a Unit that dies later does too, each to be played from there again. A play a field permission allows was never Flashback's and plays as before ([[R454]]).

**The retired mark.** `CardInstance.flashbackSpent`, which [[R783013]] wrote, stays on the instance and the layers still drop the keyword while it is set, but nothing writes it any more, so no card loses Flashback by playing.

**The cards.** Stinkyscape #21 Moonberry ([[R1867007]]) and #144 Prepare For Liftoff are offered from their graveyard each time they land there, Prepare For Liftoff set again face-down by it; a card #152 Sanctum lends Flashback to is offered each time while Sanctum stands. No card's words change.
