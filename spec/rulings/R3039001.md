Issue #3000's section 1 asked that "a card played through Flashback keeps Flashback, so it can be flashed back again", and issue #3039 built it: [[§10.5]] wrote nothing on the card as its Flashback played it, so a card was offered from its graveyard again each time it lay there. With Meditative+ #5 Spirit Lash, a (0) Spell that deals 1 damage to all Units, given Flashback by issue #3138, that made a board wipe a player could repeat for nothing as often as they liked in one turn. Asked, the owner ruled on 2026-11-05 that a card played by Flashback is meant to lose it, so a card is flashed back once.

**Spent, as before.** [[R783013]] stands: a play only the card's own Flashback allows ([[R783012]]) spends it as the card leaves the graveyard (`flashbackSpent`, [[§10.5]] step 4), whether the card printed the keyword or an aura lent it (Stinkyscape #152 Sanctum, [[R1869003]]), and a play a field permission allows was never Flashback's and spends nothing ([[R454]]). Stinkyscape #21 Moonberry ([[R1867007]]), #121 System Schematic and #144 Prepare For Liftoff are each played from the graveyard once by it, and Spirit Lash is offered there at both its prices the one time ([[R3138000]]).

**What stays of #3039.** Its Dormant half, an awakened card never Dormant again ([[R3039000]]), stands.
