Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Every card and token of Stinkyscape+ draws art of the set's own, routed by its id prefix as [[R2202000]] routes Training Grounds', while the set is held ([[R1420]]).

**The painter.** The ten Terminus Starship tokens each draw a ship of their own (a hull and a livery) and the five Automa-ζ fusions each a look of their own, on the space backdrop, and no two alike. Every other card draws Stinkyscape's picture ([[R1883000]]) under a Mindustrial overlay of girders, gears and a conveyor seeded by the hash of its id: at least 30 shapes, so every picture holds at least 60 and stays under `ART_DETAIL_MAX_BYTES`. A picture is a pure function of the card's id; the overlay is skipped when only the subject is drawn, and no base face paints in Radiant's gold window.

**The scenes.** The set's 40 Legendaries and Mythics, which must clear their rarity's detail floor with all seven layers, draw placeholder scene recipes: the template of the first of Terminus, Starship, Swarm, Mindustrial and Human the card carries (else a spell's), built from the space, craft, gear and cosmos kits and varied by the hash of its id; a Mythic doubles its near and particle layers. No part turns, none is gold, none is one of Stinkyscape's Mythic parts or an Alt art's. A later brief pass replaces them (`ART.md`).

**The variety draw** ([[R503]]). The set's procedural spec, which a face drawn by the painter never shows but R503's sweep reads, takes the set's own salt, and the 15 cards whose draw lands on another card's layout, backdrop, sky, emblem and motif draw again (`STINKYSCAPE_PLUS_REDRAWS`), one AI token until its layout fills the four B40 asks of the group.
