Both seats of `GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks` carry `rankBefore` and `rankAfter`, the visible ranks the match's rated record holds for the seat's side ([[R611]]), found by profile, a ladder bot's side by its bot id ([[R640000]]); a ranked series' last game carries the series' one record, its earlier games none ([[R604]]). An unranked match never carries them and a ranked one not until its record is written, so absent means "not recorded yet", never "no change"; never a rating, deviation or volatility ([[R612]]). Once the result arrives the match screen reads the ranks again, `MATCH_RANKS_READ_ATTEMPTS` (5) reads in all, `MATCH_RANKS_RETRY_MS` (1000 ms) apart, while a ranked read carries no move; its result panel shows each seat's rank before → after (a Jlorious seat by position, a bot's as a person's), and the bar the rank after, as Hearthstone's post-game screen shows the rank a game moved. Rejected: the viewer's move alone, since the bar shows both seats.
