## R783007 — An aura may lend Cloaked to the cards in a hand

Built (#783), so that "Aura: Your Units have Cloaked." (part 2's Knowledge Breaker Prime) and "Aura:
Your Spells have Cloaked." can exist. [[§10.4]]'s aura layer is read for any card the aura's own test
admits, a card in a hand included, so an aura whose entry admits the cards of a type in its controller's
hand lends them the keyword, and a card of it reads as Cloaked there while the granting card acts on its
controller's field. It ends as that card leaves the field, but a card already set stays set ([[R1040]])
and keeps its Reveal. Rejected: a new hook for hand keywords, which would duplicate the aura layer, and
reading such grants into `card_keywords`, which would let a Nerf strip a lent keyword ([[R442]]).
`determinize` reads the same grants ([[R783009]]).
