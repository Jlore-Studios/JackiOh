Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes.

**Why.** The adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]) is the page's headline figure, its default sort and what the tiers read ([[R3022000]], [[R3184000]]), but the little bar in the Cards table's Win rate cell tracked the raw win rate, which mostly shows the deck's strength. The bar drew the eye to the less useful number.

**The change.** The existing bar markup and CSS move to the Adjusted cell: the fill is `Math.round(adjustedWinRate * 100)%` wide on the same 0–100% scale the Win rate bar used, shown only with enough games and a numeric rate, and the cell reads exactly "—" otherwise. The Win rate cell keeps its percentage and its 95% range ([[R2895000]]), and the adjusted range still shows under the figure when ranges are on. No new styles; the grid has no bar and is untouched.

Rejected: rescaling the bar (for example, centered on 50%, since the adjusted rate is shrunk toward 50% by construction). The ask keeps the current 0–100% scale, and a new scale needs a person to decide. No Hearthstone rule is involved.
