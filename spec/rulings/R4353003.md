Stinkyscape+ T-Supernova summons Alnilam and fuses every Unit on both boards into it ([[R3569002]]). Alnilam is an Indestructible Unit, so since [[R4353000]] it is Immutable, and Immutable refuses a Fuse onto it ([[R23]]). The fusion is how Supernova makes its card, though, not a change to a card that stood in play: Alnilam exists only for it, summoned by the same effect a moment before. So a Fuse whose kept card the same effect has just made for it (`FuseArgs::fresh`) is not refused by that card's Immutable, and Supernova plays as it did. Every other Fuse onto an Immutable card, an Indestructible Unit's included, is refused as before ([[R77]], [[R23]]).
