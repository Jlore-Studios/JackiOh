`fill_board` takes `row` (default units), `side` and `radiant_for` (MD-B3). Meditative #26's end of turn fills with `row: backrow, side: any`: every empty, unlocked backrow zone of both players, the active player's side first ([[R68]]), each summoned for the player whose zone it is, Locked zones skipped. The Radiant face passes `radiantFor: self`, so only its controller's Wells are Radiant. Summoned, so no Cry ([[R1]]).
