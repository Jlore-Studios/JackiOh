**The panel.** A card's Statistics panel (`CardStatsBlock`, opened from the Almanac and the collection, [[R3185000]]) shows Games, Tier, Adjusted win rate, Decisiveness, Win rate and Play rate; a card no deck holds shows Games, Tier, Adjusted win rate and Decisiveness ([[R4083000]]). Decisiveness reads `±X.X%`, or "not enough games" below [[R654]]'s floor as its neighbours do. Its label's tooltip names the card's value and its field's typical value ([[R4552001]], [[R4552002]]): "playing this card typically moves your chance of winning by X%, one way or the other", and says that ± is the swing itself, not a margin of error.

**The response.** Each `cards` row and each `created` row of `GET /api/stats/cards` gains `decisiveness`, a fraction of win chance like the other rates, or null when the card has no turn to read; `tiers` gains `decisiveness`, the typical deck card's value, and `tiers.created` the typical Created card's. Nothing else of [[R4552001]]'s is sent: no Swing, no Board swing, no play term and no raw RMS, which only the reports print. A card a rule makes reads its Created copies' figure, as its adjusted win rate does.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
