MD-C12: "Luck X" is a player's Lucky X (ME-LUCK): while Feng Shui is on its controller's field, every luck-based roll that player's cards make rolls X more times and keeps the best. A roll with no "best" is unaffected ([[R32]]).

Luck sums the acting "Luck X" texts through the `luck` param, at `radiantChance`, the Grapes' roll, Die Insect, Soul Shot's pick, Book of Tokens' count, Mid Loser's coin and Reoccurring Dream. It is shown on the card and beside its controller's hero, as Armor is: each side's view carries its Luck (`SideView.luck`, absent at 0), and the board draws a Luck badge by the hero's portrait.
