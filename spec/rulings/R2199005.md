TG #24 is a Field Trap, which stays after firing and fires again ([[§5.1]], [[R33]]): each Unit its controller's opponent plays fires it at the announce, face-down the first time and face-up after, and every firing is the designer's "On reveal". "That Unit's cost" is the cost paid ([[R56]]: a discounted Unit at its price, a free cast at 0, [[R70]]); the Radiant face fires only on a Unit paid (2) or more (`minCost`). Each card then in its controller's hand that costs less than that (Radiant: no more), its cost as it stands ([[R65]], [[R396]]: an X card in hand costs 0), gets a cost override of (0): a later discount never takes it below (0), a raise still adds ([[R65]]). A card added to the hand later is untouched, and the opponent reads none of the new costs ([[R97]]). A countered Unit still fired it: the announce happened. Rejected: the first reveal alone, which would spend a (1) Field Trap on one play.
