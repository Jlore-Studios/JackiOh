---
id: R114
title: Trample into a unit with no health left
cards: "#21, #32"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/rulings_c.rs
---
A Trample source's hit on a unit already at 0 or less health deals nothing to that unit: no `damage` event, no on-damage trigger, no Poisonous mark and no Lifesteal off it, exactly as [[R63]]'s zero rule. The whole amount becomes the step 9 Trample instance on the target's controller's hero, where Lifesteal heals it once, so the total healed is unchanged ([[§4.4]] step 5, [[R63]])
