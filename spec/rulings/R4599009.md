The fuzz monitor's I21 judges a Unit gone from the field after the step by the keywords it held as the step began, since no aura reaches it off the field. In the daily fuzz's plain seed 4960 a stolen Rush Token held Immune to Spells under its thief; a steal's end handed it back to its owner without it, and C #25 Lag in the System then exiled it, rightly, as the engine read it no longer immune. I21 named the exile as a reach.
**What I21 allows.** Once a `controlChanged` names a Unit that is gone from the field by the step's end, I21 no longer judges it for the rest of the step: its keywords as the step began no longer say whether it was immune when the Spell reached it. A Unit still on the field after the step is judged by its keywords then, as before, and one that never changed hands is judged as before.
Rejected: rebuilding the board at each event to read its keywords then, which the monitor cannot do from the events alone.
