"Discard a Narcor" (`discard_resource { defIds }`) takes the oldest Resource in its player's tray of one of those names, or the oldest of any with none named, with no prompt; the card ceases to exist rather than reaching a graveyard, with a public `resourceDiscarded`, and a tray that empties is absent again. It is no hand discard: no discard guard, discard count or hand trigger reads it ([[§6.3]] Discard, [[R682]]), and a tray with no match discards nothing. "Fill your hand with Resources" (`gain_resources { fill }`) gains as many Resources as the tray has room for, so it sells none, and a full tray gains nothing. The spec did not say which Narcor goes, nor where a discarded Resource lands; this note decides both.
