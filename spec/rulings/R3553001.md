S+ #29 Grinder Veteran reads "Activate: Discard a Felinor to add {summons} Warhero Tokens to
your hand", Radiant ones on the Radiant face. "A Felinor" is a Felinor-tagged card of its
controller's own hand. The discard is no `ActivationCost` (which pays a random discard, mana or
Tribute, [[R384]]): its controller picks the Felinor as the activation resolves, a `chooseFromHand`
prompt of the Felinor-tagged hand cards alone — the only prompt the card opens — which the
`resume` step answers (C #81's shape, [[R81]]), discarding the chosen card first and then adding
the declared `summons` Warhero Tokens — Radiant ones on the Radiant face — as fresh cards
(`add_to_hand` with the token's `defId`, C+ #60's shape), which mints them and starts the worth
([[R3548002]]). With no Felinor in hand the ability is not offered, the cost-like gate. Deft is
catalog data ([[§6.1]]).

Rejected: a random discard cost (which could take any card); a declared hand target (which opens
no prompt, so the AI, the random policy and the timeout could not answer it); and gaining the
tokens before the discard.
