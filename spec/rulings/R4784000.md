[[§9.9]]'s deck builder (`build_ai_deck`) keeps a theme's floor (`AI_DECK.themeMinShare`) and the units' (`AI_DECK.minUnitShare`) together for a deck that leans on no set, as [[R1370]] keeps three together for one that leans. Each pick is drawn as it always was, from one roll: the theme's narrowing, then the units', each once its own slots run out. When the card that roll draws would leave the two floors out of reach, the pick is drawn again from the same roll and the same weights. It is drawn from the cards of that narrowing that [[R1370]]'s fewest way of meeting the floors (the leaned set owing nothing) would take, or from every card left that it would take when the narrowing holds none. When the slots left cannot meet both, the theme's floor is kept first, R1370's order. The units' narrowing never counted the theme cards still owed. So a theme with few or no Units (Book, Fruit, Drone Core, Jelly Bean, Mineral and Call to Chaos hold none, KY and Terminus too few) took the last slots and lost the units' floor: before this ruling, 260 of 9,000 of the AI's own decks (3,000 seeds at each tier, the theme rolled) held fewer Units than it, some of the quality gates' decks among them.

Only a card that would cost a floor is drawn again, and a deck dealt that card missed a floor before. So every deck that held both is dealt card for card as it was, and the rng stops where it always did, since the second draw reuses the roll. The decks measured were 24,600 that lean on no set: the AI's own and a player's at each tier, every theme at 25, 30 and 35 cards, and the quality gates', their fixture's and the tuning series' at 100 games a matchup. The 1,308 of them that held fewer Units than the floor are dealt again and hold it, and no other deck or rng cursor moves. A theme with fewer cards than its floor still gives the deck every card it has. 16 decks of the `gate:v3` full run are dealt again: ai-vs-random games 4, 7, 52, 78 and 82; ai-vs-greedy 9, 12, 19, 22 (both seats), 24, 37 and 50; hard-vs-easy 5, 13 and 33. So is one deck in each of 12 of the 150 tuning games [[R4513000]] measured its rates on, and those rates stand until the gates are next measured. This amends [[R1370]]'s last paragraph, which said no deck that leans on no set would move.
