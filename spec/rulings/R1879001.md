[[R1855000]] lays nothing for a preset entry named in a row its card does not live in. A Unit whose face prints Flying is the exception: an entry naming one in the backrow summons it standing in that backrow zone, as a Flying play may stand there ([[R1081000]]), where it is a Unit for every rule. The zone must be open and unreserved, else nothing is laid; a Unit without Flying named in the backrow still lays nothing. Its Radiant and Defense Position are as the entry says, and once the board is laid its Cry fires with the rest, in the preset's order, its caster's choices random ([[R1855001]]). Anomaly Found!'s SP board names three Drone Interceptors in its backrow (`docs/stinkyscape-set.md`, the designer's list); laying none of them was rejected, since the designer named a card that can stand there.
