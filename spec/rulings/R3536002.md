"Evolve all cards in your deck" marks each library card that prints an Evolve text (`CardInstance.enters_evolved`); a card with no Evolve text takes no mark. The mark rides every zone until the state check after the card enters the field ([[§4.5]], [[R68]]'s order), so after its Cry, and the card then evolves there if it still can: its Evolve text runs as it enters. [[R78]]'s reset leaves the mark alone, and a marked card with no Evolve text loses it silently. Marking is silent, since the library is hidden ([[R97]]), and a hidden card carries neither mark. Rejected: evolving inside the play pipeline, before the card's Cry.
