**Why.** [[R3734001]] meant the workshop's rail, pool and deck to sit side by side from 601 px, and the rail and the sidebar did: but the deck editor's own grid (`deckbuilder.css`) stacked the sidebar over the pool at 760 px and under, so an iPad mini held upright (744x1133) showed the deck behind "Show list" above a pool, beside a rail whose names were cut to four letters. The device-size screenshots of #3734 (pull request #3775) also showed the title "Decks" on a row of its own above Back at 744 and 820, a phone's name field cut to "Pyre Contro", the editor's last action row cut at a 1280x720 window's foot, and the Cards tab's set row cutting set names ("Stinkysc…", "Training Gr…") and running off the right edge with nothing to show that it scrolls. Issue #3956 fixes each, at every size of [[R3728000]]'s list.

**The rule.**

- **The editor splits from 601 px.** The editor's grid stacks (the sidebar first, the pool under it, the deck's list behind "Show list") at 600 px and under only, the phone; from 601 px the pool and the deck sit side by side. From 601 to 760 px the sidebar is 232 px: the shell's 80 px icon rail, the workshop's 148 px rail and the page's padding leave 478 px of an iPad mini's 744, so the pool keeps about 232 px, two columns of cards about 100 px wide. The same two breakpoints move in the Almanac's and the dungeon browser's sheets, which pair with the editor's, and the Collection's Cards tab and the dungeon's deck builder share the editor's filters and pool: from 601 to 760 px they take the layout they already had from 761 to 1024 px (the pool in its frame, the Cost and Sort labels shown). The status toast centers on the pool between the rail and that sidebar.
- **The rail shows a name whole.** A deck's name in the rail wraps, to three lines at most, and breaks inside a word that is too long for the column, in place of one line cut by an ellipsis; the row's padding is tighter from 601 to 1024 px so the name column is 60 px at 744 and 76 px at 820 (it was 44 and 60), and the full name is the row's tooltip.
- **The title sits beside Back.** From 601 to 1024 px the bar's line dissolves into the bar: Back, the title and the end group share the first row and the extras (Undo, Redo, the cosmetics button, the save status) take the second, right-aligned. `routes/top-bar.css` stays the one sheet that places the bar's slots ([[R3218000]]): it reads three custom properties (`--bar-line-display`, `--bar-extras-order`, `--bar-extras-flex`, the phone's layout their default) that the workshop's sheet sets from 601 to 1024 px. At 600 px and under the title stays under Back ([[R3218000]]).
- **A phone's name field shows the name.** On a phone the editor's "Back to decks" button keeps its 44 px height and shows its arrow only, its words kept for a screen reader, so the name field gains about 85 px (about 147 px of text on a 390 px phone). The trio's and the import's Back keep their words.
- **The editor's actions are in view at 1280x720.** On a desktop one screen tall the deck keeps room for a tile or two (200 px, it was 360) however much sits under it, so the sidebar's last action row is on screen and the sidebar does not scroll.
- **The Cards tab's set row.** Each set's item is as wide as its name and count (`flex: 1 0 auto`), the name is never cut, and the row's right edge fades out (a mask the width of `--space-10`, with that much padding so the last item clears it at the scroll's end) wherever it scrolls, its scrollbar shown again as a second cue.

Amends [[R3734001]]'s last sentence, which said the editor is one column from 601 to 760 px.

Decisions the issue did not settle, and what each rejected:

- Dropping the rail at 744 (a "Decks" button over the editor), which the issue's wish for the names in the rail rules out.
- A 280 px sidebar at 601 to 760 px, as the 761 to 1024 px block has: it leaves the pool 184 px at 744, under two readable columns.
- One column of larger cards in the pool at 744: two columns of about 100 px keep more of the pool on a screen the width of the deck beside it.
- Moving a phone's title beside Back: [[R3218000]]'s two-row bar keeps the extras beside the title on a phone.
- A 601 to 1024 px query in `top-bar.css`, or the workshop's sheet placing the bar's line itself: `top-bar.test.ts` pins that sheet's queries to the phone's 600 px and forbids any other sheet from naming the bar's slots, so the workshop asks by custom properties instead. `align-items: flex-start` on the bar, which keeps the extras wrapped under the title inside the line and takes only the title's row beside Back, was rejected for the extras' narrower, taller block.
- A shorter name column only (ellipsis kept, a tooltip): the issue asks for the name whole or wrapped.

No Hearthstone rule is involved; this is layout only. **Proof.** `layout.test.ts` proves that the sheets stack at 600 px and under and split from 601 px, with the 232 px sidebar between, that the rail's name wraps with no ellipsis, that the workshop sets the bar's custom properties from 601 to 1024 px and the bar's sheet reads them, and that the Cards tab's set name is not cut and its row has a mask and its scrollbar. The Cypress specs measure the drawn layout: `46-menus-fit-one-screen.cy.ts` at every size of the list (the pool beside the deck, the names whole, the title beside Back, the actions in view, a phone's name field, the set row), and `deckbuilder-layout.cy.tsx` on the bare workshop at 744x1133, 820x1180, 390x844 and 1280x720.
