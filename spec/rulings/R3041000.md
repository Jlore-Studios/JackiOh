Stinkyscape #13 Asphyxiants Cloud reads "Flashback / Deal {damage} damage to all enemies. Your next Spell this turn has Poisonous." ([[§8.10]]; damage 1, 2 on its Radiant face), where it read "Poisonous / Your next Spell this turn has Poisonous. Deal {damage} damage to all enemies. Draw {draw}." and its Radiant face had Echo 1. Its two effects run in the order the words give them: one hit of `damage` on the enemy hero and every enemy Unit (`damage_all`, one effect, [[R59]]), then `give_next_spell_keyword` ([[R1848002]]). The log shows it: the `damage` events, then the modifier's `modifierChanged`.

- **Nothing sits between them.** The state check, with every Death it brings and every trigger those queue, waits for the whole effect list ([[R59]]), so the modifier is in place before any of it runs; the order moves no card in play. It is ruled so the words and the log agree, and a replay's final hash is the one place it shows, which is why the older script, which gave the modifier first, is kept (`script_v0_5_11o`, [[R767000]]).
- **No Poisonous of its own.** The Cloud prints Flashback in place of Poisonous, so its own hits destroy nothing by being Poisonous ([[R346]]); the modifier it gives is for the next Spell its caster plays, never itself, as it is spent at [[§10.5]] step 4 before the Cloud resolves.
- **Flashback.** From its owner's graveyard it is offered as a play at its cost ([[R783012]]), and a play by Flashback is a play for every rule, so it deals its damage and gives the modifier again.

Rejected: giving the modifier first, as the old text read and the old script ran, which would leave the words and the order apart; and keeping Poisonous on its own face, which the card's new words no longer print.
