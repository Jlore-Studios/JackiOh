The Cane (#71.1) is a Field Spell token that is Animated on your turn. When Meditative #71 Pareto
Optimality's trigger sends it to attack (MD-D30), it animates from its backrow zone into an open unit
zone and makes one forced attack on a random enemy ([[R53]]), then returns home to its backrow zone
after that combat's state check, unless it is now its controller's turn. With no open unit zone it
stays in the backrow and no attack is made.
