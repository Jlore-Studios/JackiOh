A random cast (C+ #47, C+ #38.1) makes every choice its caster would make at random, uniformly: declared targets and modes, Echo repeats, and every prompt opened for its caster while it resolves (Discover, its text, nested casts), answered at once, so the caster is never asked; a cast made inside it is random too. The other player's prompts are theirs and asked as usual. Its X is the caster's mana, at least 1. "Target enemies when possible" (and the `targetEnemies` enchantment, [[§10.1]]) aims each target pick by its declaration ([[R656]]) — a pick that harms narrows to the enemies among the options, a pick that helps to the friends — random or chosen, and the card's own prompts too, when there are enough. A random cast and the casts inside it make at most RANDOM_CAST_CHAIN_CAP (20) casts; one past that resolves into nothing
