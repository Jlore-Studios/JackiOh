Both readouts are public values and are shown through the card's preview, so the client restates no number (CLAUDE.md rules 7 and 9).

**The worth.** The engine's `warhero_worth` reads the game-wide value a Warhero Token pays in Credits, zero until a Token is printed. A Warhero Token previews "the Warhero Tokens' worth" in every zone, and its Radiant face "twice the Warhero Tokens' worth", the worth read at that moment.

**The pool.** Oct, on the field, previews what its per-turn damage pool has left ([[R3550004]]), displayed as "{N} left": "5 left" on a fresh turn, "3 left" after it has soaked 2.
