A preset entry may be marked `stack`: it is then laid on top of the Unit an earlier entry of the same replacement laid in its unit zone, the new top of a [[§3.2]] pile, as a layered summon's later cards are ([[R1842000]]). In an empty zone it is laid as any entry. On a card the replacement did not lay, a Dormant card ([[R1839000]]) or one the replacement could not reach, it lays nothing, as an unmarked entry would ([[R47]], [[R1855000]]): the replacement builds piles of its own cards only. A laid card beneath the new top has no Cry to fire ([[R1855001]]). Anomaly Found!'s BF board lays the Drone Battleship so: Meltdown, then Propagation, then the Shield on top, the pile S #36 summons, so "the Drone Battleship" is its three layers and the Shield is fought first. Laying the Shield Layer alone was rejected, since "the Drone Battleship" names all three ([[R1857001]]).
