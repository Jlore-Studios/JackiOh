## R1142 — The Mandate counts with the exile's own scope, by controller

Built (Meditative #78). "Each of yours exiled this way" counts the permanents its controller
controlled that this exile took off the field: the count and the exile use the same filter, so
whatever the exile spares is uncounted too. A stolen card its controller controls counts, and a
card of theirs the opponent controls does not. A unit token counts, though it ceases to exist
instead of reaching exile ([[R11]]). A card dormant under a Stack is not exiled and does not
count ([[R13]]). A unit Immune to Spells is neither exiled nor counted. The hate is tribal tag
based (non-CN), so a card immune to it stays ([[R1424]]).
