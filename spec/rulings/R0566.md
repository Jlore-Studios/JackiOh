It takes the snapshot's instance whole (definition, face, damage, buffs, keywords, position, counters, memory, Brittle, owner, face-up or face-down) except turn state: one that stood on the same side of the field just before and keeps its id keeps its present sickness and exertion ([[R171]]) and is reported only by `rolledBack`; any other entered on this turn — summoning sick, fresh exertion ([[R83]], [[R171]]), a face-up trap re-set face-down on its own side included — and is named by `controlChanged`, with `formerId` when it took a fresh id ([[R227]]); a card mid-play in the resolving zone is not moved, and a carried Unit left with no carrier goes to its controller's hand (a Bounce, [[R747]])
