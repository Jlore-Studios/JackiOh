On a slashed board ([[R3554003]]) the fresh card Supernova makes lands on the half the Spell resolves on ([[R3554004]]), so [[R3569002]]'s room is a unit zone on that half, free now or freed by a Unit of the caster's there that goes. With none there, nothing happens and no Unit leaves. The ingredients are still taken from both halves, since [[R3554003]] narrows no verb that walks acting Units.

Before this fix the room was read across the slash: a Unit of the caster's on the other half counted as freeing a zone, so every ingredient ceased to exist and the summon on the Spell's half then found no zone. In fuzz seeds 11469 and 10167 Units ceased to exist with no event and no Alnilam was made.

Rejected: narrowing the ingredients to the Spell's half, which [[R3554003]] does not make; summoning Alnilam on the other half, against [[R3554004]].
