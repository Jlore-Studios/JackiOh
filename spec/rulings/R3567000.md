S+ #4 Profe Yates's Cry exiles every card on both fields, in both libraries, hands and
graveyards whose name holds M, O, U, S or E in either case: first its controller's field,
then the opponent's, each walked as `exile_board` walks it, then each side's off-field zones
in [[R135]]'s order, each card its own exile ([[R55]]). Spared are Yates itself, a card carrying
the CN tag (read through `tags_of`, so a card in Chinese, [[R1300]]), and a field card no effect
reaches ([[R940]]); Dormant cards go ([[R3539002]]).

Rejected: one side only.
