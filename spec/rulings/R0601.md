title: The numbers a judgement ban reads
cards: "§9.9, R186, R390"
proven_in:
  - crates/ai/tests/ai/shadow_ban.rs
---
A `neverPlayed` or `selfHarm` ban reads pass 2's numbers at one tier and nothing else: the card's games of that tier in pass 2, the ones it was forced into and the ones it was dealt as at-risk filler, summed. `neverPlayed` needs `banAffordableTurns` (6) affordable turns and no play in any of them; `selfHarm` needs `banHarmPlays` (8) plays whose mean evaluation change is below `selfHarmDelta`. Pass 1's numbers are never added in: they only put a card at risk, so a card at risk that pass 2 did not sweep is not banned for either. `error` and `timeout` count the card's own forced games of both passes, as [[R186]] always did
