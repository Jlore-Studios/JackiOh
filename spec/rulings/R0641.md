A deck carries one hero portrait — a cosmetic picked in the deck builder from the launch roster of six, `vanilla`, `gary`, `timmy`, `dfender`, `felinors` and `shredder`, each the art of the Core card its name names (looked up by name, never by number) drawn in `CardArt`'s `oval` shape on the hero, with health badged on the oval's lower right and armor on its lower left. All six are unlocked for every account; there is no collection for them. A save checks the portrait as **D5**: the deck's `portrait` is `null` or a known portrait id, an unknown id failing the save with a validation error naming the field, and `null` reads as `vanilla` everywhere the deck is shown — which is also what every deck saved before the column existed holds, and what a deck or trio import's decks arrive with, since the codes carry no portrait ([[R255]], [[R339]]). A portrait never enters `PlayerView`, the action log, the replay hash or a game record, and picking one never changes a rule
