**Unlucky X**, `{ "kind": "Unlucky", "n": X }`, is Lucky's opposite. A card's **net luck** is its Lucky
less its Unlucky (`query::lucky_on`), each read as Lucky is ([[R1438]]): what the face prints as tuning
leaves it, plus what was given, entries of the kind summing across their sources. Its controller's
Luck adds to it as before ([[R987]]). Lucky 2 and Unlucky 1 roll as Lucky 1; Lucky 1 and Unlucky 1 as
no luck at all.

`Rng::lucky(x, roll, better)` rolls a net luck below 0 as **worst-of**: |x| extra rolls, each pair kept
as the one `better` did not keep, so Unlucky 1 rolls twice and keeps the worse. A coin flip, whose better
side is heads ([[R1440]]), keeps **tails if any** lands tails (`lucky_coin`). At a net of 0 it draws once,
as a roll with no Lucky always has.

Every roll that read Lucky as "above 0, else one plain draw" (the shortcuts at a Trade's offer, a weighted
damage roll, a Radiant chance, a weighted table, a Trap's reveal chance, Die Insect, and the cards' own)
now asks whether the net is other than 0, so an Unlucky card rolls worst-of there too; the odds a view
shows read a net below 0 as every one of the 1 + |x| rolls having to land ([[R4707001]]).

Hearthstone has no Unlucky; the arithmetic reading is the plain one. Where a Nerf and a Buff move it, and
where it is live, is [[R5236021]]'s.
