Issue #4154 gave S #165 The Trade Union's base face "You may Trade up to {trades} cards each turn." with `trades` 2, and its Radiant face 3, the same text on both, so the number is no longer the Radiant face's alone. While it acts its controller's Trades a turn are that number, the most of several holding, never their sum ([[R1864004]], [[R1838000]]). Through v0.6.6h the base face gave none and the Radiant face 2 (`script_v0_6_6h`, [[R767000]]).
