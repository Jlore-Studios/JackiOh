`/almanac` is public: it renders signed out, with no redirect to sign-in, and calls no endpoint (not the catalog's, the collection's, the decks' or the account's), reading the catalog the bundle ships. It shows every catalog card, tokens included, since L3's reason for leaving tokens out of the deck builder's pool is a deck's and the almanac builds none; with no filter, cost ascending, base faces. Its filter bar, card grid, empty state and detail view are the deck builder's own components, through one browse pane both screens render, so the two cannot drift apart: the filter bar without its "owned only" control and with Prime, AI and Token after the deck builder's tag chips, the grid read-only (no "+", no drag, no deck or ownership marks; a card's name is its name, type, cost and rarity), and the detail view with no action but Close and a mic dropdown previewing the card's voice lines, its line under the glossary the rarity, and for a token its printed rarity if any, then "Token · not deckable". The deck builder otherwise renders exactly as before. Linked from the site footer beside Patch notes, listed in the sitemap, and served by vercel.json. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
