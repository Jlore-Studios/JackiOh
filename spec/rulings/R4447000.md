`decide` runs its beam on the first sampled world alone and replays each finalist's line, found there, on every other world ([[§9.9]], The opponent's reply). That replay (`replay_line`) is cut at the first action the world's candidate set does not list, the root's set for the line's first action (issue #1635), unless the seat's own prompt is open there: that prompt is answered with the seat's best answer, at most `AI_SEARCH.max_auto_answers` of them, and the line plays on (issue #1636), which is no cut. A line's own earlier step is the commonest cause of a cut: each world shuffles the seat's own library, so a card the line drew is another instance there and the line's play of it names a card that world never drew, and each world rolls its random effects on a seed of its own, so a unit the line attacks can be gone.

**The count.** `SearchStats.replaysCut` counts the finalist replays a step their world does not offer cut, over the worlds every finalist was scored on, as `worldsScored` counts those worlds ([[R3498000]]); a world the counter could not finish counts none. It is 0 for a decision that never reached its finalists. `cargo jackioh think` prints it beside `worldsScored`.

**The fill.** While `AI_SEARCH.replay_fill_steps` is above 0, a cut replay drops the rest of its line and plays that world's turn on (`fill_turn`) before it ends: up to that many steps, each the best by `static_score` (the score the beam ranks its lines by) of the first `branching` candidates that are not endTurn, in move order, taken only when it scores strictly above standing still, ties to the earlier candidate. A candidate that leaves the seat's own prompt open is not taken. Every node the fill spends comes from a slice of `steps × (branching + 1)` nodes, so a step whose surcharge does not fit the slice is refused as `simulate` refuses any, and `decide` keeps that slice back beside each other world's reserve only while the switch is above 0, so the beam's slice is what shrinks. A counter that runs out mid-fill leaves the replay with no score, as a replay that runs out anywhere does, and the world goes unscored for every finalist alike. `score_line` is `replay_line` with the fill off, whatever the switch. The fill knows its world's draw, as the beam knows the first world's: the bias is the same on every world instead of a cut on all but one.

**Its value.** The switch ships at 0, its fork screen ([[R4397000]]) recorded in its comment in `crates/ai/src/config.rs`: a cut replay ends its turn where it stands, and every decision is node for node what it was before this ruling.

Rejected: skipping the missing step and playing the line's later ones (a drawn card's play still names the first world's instance, so the commonest cut stays); one shuffle of the seat's own library shared by every world (every world would score the same guess of its next draw as certain); the beam on every world (ISMCTS, a far larger change); endTurn simulated as one more candidate of each fill step (a passed turn's score takes off the unspent mana and plays the other side's turn start, which an open move's score does not, so nearly any move would beat it and the stop would rarely come); answering an own prompt the fill opens (up to `max_auto_answers × max_auto_answer_options` nodes per candidate); and the beam's extra stand-downs and wall switches in the fill's candidates (`expansion`). Not modelled: a listed step that `reduce` refuses ends the line where it stands, uncounted and unfilled, as before.

Hearthstone has no counterpart: it adds no rule of the game; its one constant is `AI_SEARCH.replay_fill_steps`.
