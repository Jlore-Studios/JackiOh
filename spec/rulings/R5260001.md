Felinors vs Humans #99 says "Whenever a player casts a Spell, shuffle a copy of it with Cast on draw into your deck" (Radiant: "a Radiant copy").

**The rule.** Every play of a Spell by either player wakes it, a Cast included ([[R70]]); a cast on draw is no play and wakes nothing ([[R2179002]]), so a copy it shuffled never feeds it when drawn. It shuffles into its controller's deck one fresh copy of the Spell's definition, carrying what a copy shuffled into a library carries ([[R57]]: the Radiant flag and the tuning, never a buff), with Cast on draw put on it ([[R5238004]]): on the base face the copy is Radiant when the Spell cast was, and on the Radiant face it is always Radiant. A full deck refuses it as it refuses any copy ([[R316]]). Two Chos shuffle two copies.
