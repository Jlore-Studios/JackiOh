[[§14.5]] says a Rare or Epic step is a cosmetic token of that rarity, and the issue says it
buys any cosmetic **at its tier or below** on the cosmetic rarity scale (Common to Mythic),
which is not tied to card rarity: a Rare token buys a Common or Rare cosmetic, an Epic token a
Common, Rare or Epic one. No token reaches Legendary or Mythic.

- **The tiers** are the cosmetics' own scale, commonest first: Common 0, Rare 1, Epic 2,
  Legendary 3, Mythic 4 (`CosmeticRarity::tier`). The registry holds no Mythic and no chase
  yet; both are refused by construction now, so a later registry addition is closed without a
  second change.
- **What it buys** is one cosmetic the account does not own: a per-card cosmetic (a foil, a
  finish, a border, a full-art face) for a card it owns, or an account cosmetic (a board, a
  field, a card back). A full art is owned on the one card it names alone ([[R1313000]]): only
  that card, owned, takes it.
- **What it refuses**, each with 409 and spending nothing: no such token held; a chase, which
  no token pays for ([[§14.2]]); a cosmetic above the token's tier; a per-card cosmetic with no
  card or for a card not owned, or an account cosmetic given one; and one already owned, a
  ledger row or a default or starter, which [[§14.2]] gives every account. A token outside Rare
  and Epic, and an unknown cosmetic id, are 400.
- **The transaction** is the cosmetics grant, then one `CosmeticToken` of the token's rarity
  spent (reason `token_redeem`), all with the same `at`; one commit under the account's lock
  ([[R1301003]]). Every refusal returns before the commit.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone sells cosmetics for money or gold at fixed prices; the spec
spends a tiered token instead, so at-or-below is the owner's decision in #1305 over [[§14.5]]'s
"of that rarity", which #1333 brings §14's text in line with.

Rejected: a token buying only its own rarity, which was [[§14.5]]'s text until the owner's
decision; reaching Legendary or Mythic, which no cycle step pays a token for; and a full art
for any owned card, which would own a cosmetic on a card the roll never drops it on.

§14's text is #1333's to change.
