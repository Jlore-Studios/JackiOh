Stinkyscape #2 Absolute Zero reads "Freeze a Unit forever" (its Radiant face adds "You may give it Indestructible"). [[R1174000]]'s Freeze stamps a thaw as it lands; a forever freeze is the same status with a thaw no cleanup reaches.

- **The status.** `freeze { target, forever: true }` makes the Unit Frozen ([[§6.1]]) with `CardInstance.frozen` stamped `FROZEN_FOREVER`, a turn number no game reaches (R2's turn cap is far below it), so `freeze::thaw_at_cleanup` never thaws it. Everything [[R1174000]] says a Frozen Unit cannot do it cannot do, for as long as it stays: no declared attack, no switch of its own, while an effect's switch and a forced attack still happen.
- **Two freezes.** A Unit keeps the later of two thaws, so an ordinary Freeze on a Unit Frozen forever changes nothing, and a forever Freeze on a Unit Frozen until its next cleanup makes it Frozen forever.
- **What ends it.** Only what ends any freeze besides its thaw: leaving the field ([[R78]]'s reset, a Bounce, a death, an exile; a Transform makes a new card) and a Vanilla, as Hearthstone's Silence removes Freeze. A change of control ([[R171]]) keeps it, and it is not stamped again on the new side, since there is no thaw to stamp.
- **Seen by both players.** It lands with [[R1174000]]'s `marked` "frozen" like any freeze, and both views carry `UnitView.frozenForever`, so the face's frost badge says it never thaws. No new event.
- Absent on every card no forever freeze reached, so no shipped game changes.
