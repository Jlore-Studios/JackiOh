"That Unit" of S+ #93 Recessive Replicators is the first friendly card whose definition is a Unit, a token included, and whose death evolved it, remembered with the face it died on (a card whose definition is no Unit, a Field Spell dying in the backrow or an Animated one dying in a unit zone, is none: the `destroyed` event names the card's definition and not the zone it died in). Another card than itself, and only before it has evolved ([[R3536000]]), so a later death is not remembered. Each run of its Evolve text gains one Death, so under High Evolutionary's Aura ([[R3536003]]) it gains two or three and resummons that Unit two or three times. Its Death is read as it dies ([[R78]], [[§4.5]]) and summons a fresh copy of that Unit for each Death gained: each copy is a new card that enters R64's zone and has no Cry ([[R1]], [[R64]]). A copy is Radiant if that Unit died Radiant, and always on the Radiant face. A Replicators evolved with no such death (High Evolutionary's Cry) gains no Death and resummons nothing; one that dies in the same pass as the friendly Unit never evolved, since a card that has left the field answers no trigger. It names that Unit after "that Unit" on the field, to both seats, and shows the dash before it has evolved ([[R2476000]]). Rejected: returning the dead instance itself, which [[R78]]'s reset makes a new card in any case; and counting an Animated card that dies standing as a Unit, which the event cannot tell from a backrow death.
