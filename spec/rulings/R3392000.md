Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a scene reads a card's id alone, never anything hidden. On 2026-11-06 the
owner asked that every card's art be more unique and richer, in its set's style: simpler for small cards,
elaborate for Mythics, the Spells and Field Spells real scenes rather than a few glyphs over a sky (#3330). The
issue gave each Core entry but Ceaseless Void (#100, whose picture #3338 owns) a brief, and asked that each draw
from a scene recipe ([[R3331000]]) of its own, from that brief.

**Every Core entry has a scene.** Core #1 to #99 and the eleven Core tokens draw from recipes
(`sceneRecipesCore1.ts` to `sceneRecipesCore3.ts`, in Core's family, the glass orb of ART.md's art bible), each
from its brief: the subject, the setting and the card's idea stay, and a detail the kits could not hold was
changed to one they could, the recipe's doc comment saying so. A part a brief named that the kits lacked joined
them. Each scene clears its rarity's floor ([[R3331001]]): a Common one subject in a simple setting, a Rare its
props, an Epic its far, middle and near ground, a Legendary every layer with its light and its moment of action,
a Mythic every layer and a part no other scene draws; their median element count rises rarity by rarity, and
every one of them is off `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`, which now names 453 cards at most. The Radiant face draws the same
shapes, repainted toward each layer's gold, as every scene's does.

**Characters.** A Unit, a token Unit included, draws its character from the kits: a figure, its look built on
its tags' (`figureLookFor`) with the parts its brief names, or, when its character does not stand like a person
(a scarab, a gelatin cube, a panther, a dragon, a pillow, a boulder, a sheep, a loaf, a Rush Token), a creature
part drawn for it in its subject layer. KY, on every Core card that draws him, wears one look
(`KY_TUTOR_LOOK`), the chalk-dusted tutor in a cardigan.

**The cast.** A recipe may stand more figures than its one (`cast`), each in a layer of its own choosing, drawn in
front of that layer's parts and, in the subject layer, after the figure; a recipe with no cast draws from its
stream exactly as before, so the scenes this issue does not own keep their pictures.

**Two proofs redrawn.** Speciesist (#2) and Heroic Power (#98) drew [[R3331000]]'s proof scenes; the issue gave
both new briefs, so each draws its new brief's scene, and the proofs' bar is held by every Core scene.

**Moving art.** A scene draws no motif glyph and no accent, so a full-art card's props beat ([[R2715001]]) had
nothing to move: the seven full-art Core cards that moved their props (#1, #4, #8, #11, #12, #18, #86) move
their near layer on the same beat instead.

Rejected: a figure body for beasts and objects (a "body" kind in the figure kit), which would build a scarab or a
pillow round a person's neck, hip and hand; keeping Speciesist's and Heroic Power's proof scenes, which would
leave two entries off their briefs; keeping the motif glyphs in a scene so the props beat had something to move,
which [[R3331000]] removed; and leaving `ART_DETAIL_SHORT_MAX` at 561, which would let 108 new shortfalls in.

Amends [[R3331000]] (every Core entry but Ceaseless Void draws a scene; Speciesist's and Heroic Power's are their
new briefs'; a scene may stand a cast).
