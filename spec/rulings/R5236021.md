Unlucky X ([[R5236020]]) is a numbered keyword, `NumberedKey::Unlucky`, less being better, moved on a
Nerf's and a Buff's **X row** ([[R386]]) like any other, but from 0: a card with a luck roll that prints
no Unlucky is at Unlucky 0, so

- **a Nerf adds Unlucky 1** to it, or raises the Unlucky it has by 1 (by its multiplier's steps);
- **a Buff lowers it by 1**, and the last one comes off (`UNLUCKY_TUNED_FLOOR` is 0, where every other
  number floors at 1); a Buff on a card at 0 has nothing to lower;
- **a Nerf never removes it** on its keyword row (`TUNE_HARMFUL_KEYWORDS`).

**Live only on a card with a luck roll.** The X row offers Unlucky only on a Luck-based card
(`catalog::is_luck_based`, [[R1444]]: it prints Lucky on a face or flips a coin) or on one that prints
Unlucky, so a Nerf never spends its draw on a card that rolls nothing; on such a card it is live wherever
a lasting number is ([[R3888000]]). A Vanilla card has no text, and no roll. KY's Constant lists a card's
Unlucky among its numbers only while it is above 0.
