A card that buffs a card in its caster's hand, or grants it a keyword there, writes the hand card's `buffs` and `granted_keywords`, which the card keeps onto the field ([[R3555000]], [[R1878003]]): S+ #100 Plesiosize gives a Unit in hand +8/+8 and GIGA, S+ #23 GIGA-ify makes a permanent in hand GIGA and Indestructible, and other cards write them too, M #18 Expedition 12's Buff of the hand card it makes Radiant among them. `search_signature` signed a hand card's id, definition, Radiant face, cost changes, memory, tuning and enchantments, but neither of those two fields, so "Plesiosize on the 7-drop" and "Plesiosize on the 2-drop" read as one position. Three readers keep the first of two positions that sign alike and drop the second: the beam ([[§9.9]], at its root too, so the second could never be the action played), the lethal solver's visited set ([[R4806000]]) and the concession check's walk ([[R2660000]], [[§9.12]]). With 4 mana, Plesiosize on the 2-drop and then the 2-drop played as a 10/10 GIGA was never searched, and a Plesiosize on the second hand unit, a 1/1 Taunt made a 9/9 that takes both of the enemy's attacks and lives, was an escape the concession check's walk never tried, so a bot could concede a game it could still save. (The audit's own escape, a GIGA-ify on a hand unit played as an Indestructible wall, is none: an Indestructible Unit never has Taunt and cannot be in Defense Position, [[R347]], [[R2179000]].)

**The rule.** The AI decides this. A card off the field signs its buffs and its granted keywords, each only when it holds some, the keywords sorted so two orders of the same grants are one position, as [[R4808000]]'s parts and a card's memory and tuning are signed (`grants_signature`). A card with neither signs exactly as before, so a position with no such card signs as it did. The part is in `search_signature`, so the beam, the lethal solver and the concession check's walk all keep the two positions apart; the lane-free signature the beam's lane variants read carries it too.

**What it costs.** More distinct positions means a beam that holds fewer copies of what is really one board, but only a board with a buffed or granted card off the field has more of them. `AI_SEARCH.hand_grants_signed` off signs neither part, as before.

**Rejected.** Signing the grants of hand cards only: `card_signature` signs every card off the field (the hand, the graveyard, the exile, the backrow and its piles), and a card that carries neither part anywhere else signs there as before, so the one rule is the smaller change and splits no position that holds no such card.
