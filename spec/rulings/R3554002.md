Slashed Dream Cries in Red (S+ #78) exiles the middle lane (lane 3) with every permanent in it,
Dormant cards included and a covering GIGA by its anchor, and voids it for the rest of the game:
no play, summon or GIGA can enter, and a rotation or swap into it bounces the card instead of
panicking. A second slash does nothing, and neither does a lane outside lanes 2–4. The Radiant
face picks the slash at lane 2, 3 or 4 through a `mode` prompt, which the AI, the random policy
and the timeout answer as any prompt. Rejected: voiding without exiling, which would strand
permanents in a lane no rule can read.
