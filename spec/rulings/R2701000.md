A dungeon run's deck grows after every won fight ([[§15.7]]), and shops and events add to it ([[§15.8]]), while each fight starts the run deck at the human's seat with a handicap whose deck size is the deck's length, which the engine refuses past `LIBRARY_CAP` ([[R184]]). A run that grew its deck past it would reach a fight it cannot start. The plan made no decision; this note makes it, as a Hearthstone deck that is full gains no more cards ([[R80]]).

- **The cap:** a run deck holds at most `RUN_DECK_MAX` cards, the engine's `LIBRARY_CAP`.
- **A pack** keeps no more than the deck has room for: its keep count is also at most `RUN_DECK_MAX` less the deck's size, so a full deck keeps none of an opened pack (and with a Skip passive still takes its Coins by keeping none).
- **A shop** refuses to sell a card to a full deck. It still stocks cards, and sells artifacts as before.
- **An event's** option that adds a card adds none to a full deck; the rest of the option is paid as written.
- Removing and extracting cards make room again.
