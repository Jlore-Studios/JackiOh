T-AI-5 Launch Sequencer's "Your Starship launch costs (1)" is `starship_launch_costs { cost: 1 }` ([[R1852006]]): its caster's next launch by the Activate costs (1) in place of (3), a public badge that launch spends; a launch by an effect spends none. With no Starship yet the price waits for the next launch, whenever a piece's death makes one (ST-S21-5). Then it draws one ([[§2.4]]), so the card is never dead without a Starship. Radiant: the launch costs (0) and it draws two.
