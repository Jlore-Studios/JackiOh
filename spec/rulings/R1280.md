The Cry ([[R1]]), when Blueprints is played, Discovers ([[§6.3]]) three different buildings drawn uniformly without replacement ([[R60]]) from the named pool of nine tokens (M #97.1 to #97.9, [[R382]]). The offer is shown to the chooser only ([[§10.8]]), while the opponent sees only the prompt kind with hidden options. Choosing an option adds that card to hand, plain on the base face and Radiant on the Radiant face. If the hand is full at the maximum hand size ([[§2.4]]), the card burns and ceases to exist ([[R11]]).
