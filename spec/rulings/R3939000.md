Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**Why.** [[R3838000]] put the Show card text button in the header of the Almanac ([[R630]]) and the Statistics page ([[R654]]). Both pages show one card at a time in a modal dialog that covers the header: the Statistics breakdown ([[R3011000]]) and the Almanac's detail view. So a reader looking at a full-art card there saw its art and no words, and could not reach the button. The owner reported it in issue #3939 from The Last Voyage's breakdown.

**The rule.** Each dialog holds the same button, on the pages' one choice:

- The Statistics breakdown, in its action row beside Show Radiant, drawn as that row's other buttons are.
- The Almanac's detail view, in its action row, in the `Segmented` look the header's has.

Pressing either prints the faces with their words at once, the dialog's and the page's behind it. The header's button reads the same, and the device keeps the choice as [[R3838000]] has it.

Rejected: closing the dialog to reach the header, which loses the card the reader was looking at.
