Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**Why.** [[R3838000]] put the Show card text button in the Statistics page's header ([[R654]]). The page shows one card at a time in a modal breakdown ([[R3011000]]) that covers the header. So a reader looking at a full-art card there saw its art and no words, and could not reach the button. The owner reported it in issue #3939 from The Last Voyage's breakdown.

**The rule.** The breakdown holds the same button, on the pages' one choice:

- It stands below View in Almanac, the last of its buttons, drawn as Show Radiant is.
- Pressing it prints the faces with their words at once, the breakdown's and the page's behind it.
- The header's button reads the same, and the device keeps the choice as [[R3838000]] has it.

The Almanac's card detail view holds none: the owner kept its action row as it was, so the Almanac's button stays in its header alone.

Rejected:

- Closing the dialog to reach the header, which loses the card the reader was looking at.
- The button between Show Radiant and View in Almanac, which the owner moved below.
- A button in the Almanac detail view's action row, which the owner removed.
