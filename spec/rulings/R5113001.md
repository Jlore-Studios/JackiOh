`grant_keywords` gives one card a list of keywords, in order, each as `grant_keyword` gives it, with the target resolved and Immutable read once, before the first keyword lands ([[R5113000]]). S+ #46 Phase Fabric gives its keywords with it, Immune to Spells still last ([[R3547002]]). One grant at a time no longer works for it: once its Immutable stands, the Spell's later grants are another card's and Immutable turns them away, and once Immune to Spells stands, the Spell no longer affects the Unit. So the Unit ends with every keyword the face lists, as before, in the same order of `keywordGranted` events. An Immutable target takes none of the list. This amends [[R3547002]], which rejected a verb that grants a list at once while no other rule needed one.
