An audit of the engine's public surface found the items only a test named. Those that were a second way to do what the engine already does, or that nothing could reach, are removed:

- the verbs no card calls: `convert_healing` (Blood Moon converts through its replacement, which leaves the same this-turn modifier), `lock_own_zone` (Doom Shroom's zone is Locked by Crater), `grant_keywords_random_unit` ([[R1085]], off Keenus since patch v0.3.7) and `swap_health` (`swap` with `what: health` is the same swap, [[R73]]);
- the reads and wrappers that only restated one line: `consume_modifier`, `drop_delayed`, `peek_work`, `is_owed`, `unpark_work`, `enchantments_of_kind`, `trigger_holders_for_event`, `grade_name_of`, `chaos_effect_by_name`, `card_drills_of`, and the hand reads `hand_leftmost`, `hand_rightmost`, `hand_neighbor` and `is_rightmost_in_hand` ([[R3558000]]'s order is read through `hand_index` and `is_leftmost_in_hand`, which a card calls);
- the tables the client keeps as its own: `PORTRAITS` with `PortraitEntry` ([[R641]]'s roster is `PORTRAIT_IDS`; the names and voice directions are the client's), the `VoiceEmoteId` alias and the `PARAM_PLACEHOLDER` text.

No card plays differently and no state or wire shape changes: each rule the removed verbs' tests proved is still proven through the path a card takes. A verb a later card needs comes back from git with that card.

Kept, because another rule's proof stands on them or many tests read through them: `steal_all` (the mass steal [[R68]], [[R171]] and [[R4205000]] rule on has no other implementation), `shuffle_copies_of_self` (the copies six rule tests carry enchantments and tuned numbers onto), and the probes the rule tests are written in (`owed_work`, `clone_state`, `due_delayed`, `fire_traps_for`, `dispatch_event`, `registered_scripts`).

Rejected: removing every item only a test names, which would move the same lines into the tests of each rule that reads through them.
