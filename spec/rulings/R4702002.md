An audit of the engine's public surface found the items only a test named. Those that were a second way to do what the engine already does, or that nothing could reach, are removed:

- the verbs no card calls: `convert_healing` (Blood Moon converts through its replacement, which leaves the same this-turn modifier), `lock_own_zone` (Doom Shroom's zone is Locked by Crater), `grant_keywords_random_unit` ([[R1085]], off Keenus since patch v0.3.7), `swap_health` and `swap_board` (`swap` with `what: health` or `what: board` is the same swap, [[R73]]), `steal_all` (a steal of each card is `steal`, which destroys the one its taker has no room for, [[R4205000]]) and `shuffle_copies_of_self` (a card's own copies are `shuffle_into` with `copy_of`, as Super-Virus writes them, [[R57]]);
- the reads and wrappers that only restated one line: `consume_modifier`, `drop_delayed`, `peek_work`, `is_owed`, `unpark_work`, `enchantments_of_kind`, `trigger_holders_for_event`, `grade_name_of`, `chaos_effect_by_name`, `card_drills_of`, and the hand reads `hand_leftmost`, `hand_rightmost`, `hand_neighbor` and `is_rightmost_in_hand` ([[R3558000]]'s order is read through `hand_index` and `is_leftmost_in_hand`, which a card calls); `clone_state` (a state's own `clone()`), `stays::left_field_since` (a scan of the events for a card's exit, which [[R174]]'s readers replaced with the exit marks `left_field_after` reads), and `ky_test_script`, `papaya_begin` and `papaya_answered` (the forms that filled in today's rules for `ky_test_script_with`, `papaya_begin_with` and `papaya_answered_with`, which the cards call);
- the methods nothing called at all, a test included: `ScriptRef::into_owned`, `WorkPlan::resume`, `SwingReport::of`, `ActingCard::card`, and the `as_str` of `TuneRow`, `EnchantmentKind` and `MoveResult`;
- the tables the client keeps as its own: `PORTRAITS` with `PortraitEntry` ([[R641]]'s roster is `PORTRAIT_IDS`; the names and voice directions are the client's), the `VoiceEmoteId` alias and the `PARAM_PLACEHOLDER` text.

No card plays differently and no state or wire shape changes: each rule the removed verbs' tests proved is still proven through the path a card takes. The rule tests that used `steal_all` or `shuffle_copies_of_self` as setup now steal or shuffle through `steal` and `shuffle_into`. A verb a later card needs comes back from git with that card.

Kept, because many tests read through them: the probes the rule tests are written in (`owed_work`, `fire_traps_for`, `dispatch_event`, `registered_scripts`). Two of them were a second copy of what the engine runs and are now the one copy: the turn's own list of due delayed effects is `due_delayed` ([[R68]]), and `cards_in_trigger_order` is the walk the dispatch filters (`holders_where`), so `trigger_holders_of` is gone. Kept too, though only tests call them, are the forms a ruling, a card's header or a README names as the engine's word for a thing (`why_cannot_declare` in [[R849000]], `created_field` in [[R4082000]], `next_inflation_chance` in Stimmycheck's header, `counter` as §6.3's Counter) and the reads the cards' own tests assert through (`roll_resource`, `is_launched_starship`, `building_a_ship`, `resources_in_hand_of`, `market_credits_of`, `last_spell_played`).

Rejected: removing every item only a test names, which would move the same lines into the tests of each rule that reads through them.
