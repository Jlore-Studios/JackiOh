Issue #3951's S #46 Circadian Halfcycler summons "1 to 2 Drone Interceptors" at the end of its controller's turn ([[R62]]), the designer's note giving two thirds for 1 and one third for 2. Each end of turn draws once from the match rng ([[R60]]) over three outcomes, one of which summons 2 and the other two 1, and summons that many, each into its controller's leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]); with no open zone a summon does nothing, and the roll is drawn all the same, so a full row moves the rng as an open one does. The Radiant face's Interceptors are Radiant. The printed text names no odds: "1 to 2" is the count, the spec row the odds. Through v0.6.2b it summoned one, which its kept script keeps for those games ([[R767000]]).
