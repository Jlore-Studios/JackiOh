The designer's Dormant Starship: "Activate: Pay (3) to Awaken and trigger all Cries", and, asked, launching it and awakening it are the same thing. Its pieces print "When launched", a new trigger.

**The ability.** While it is Dormant, a Dormant Starship has one ability, the engine's and not its script's (`starship::launch_decl`): Activate, once a turn, for `STARSHIP_LAUNCH_COST` (3) mana, moved by "your Starship launch costs (N)" ([[R1852006]]), offered to its controller in their main phase as any Activate is ([[R384]], [[R1839002]]). The ability's label names its price ("Launch (3)"), which the view shows. `legalActions` lists it exactly when the reducer takes it, and the view's `usable` and `reason` say the same: a launch the player cannot pay for is refused "that ability costs 3, more than your mana", and the other seat may not launch it. A launched Starship has no launch left.

**Awakening is launching.** Paid for, the launch awakens the Starship (`slumber::awaken_card`, `awakened`), and a Starship's awakening is its launch whatever awakened it, so an effect that awakens it (Awaken, #137's "Launch it") launches it too, for nothing. As it launches it takes the Starship keyword ([[§6.1]]) and a name ([[R1852004]]), its memory records the launch ([[R1852005]]), and `starshipLaunched` goes out, public, naming the Starship (judged by where it is now, [[R97]]), its name and its pieces.

**Every fused Cry, then every fused When launched.** Its fused Cries fire next as a triggered Cry does ([[R467]], `cry_trigger::trigger_cry_of`): for its controller, who makes their choices, each declared target and mode asked as a prompt, each ingredient's Cry in its own place ([[R102]]). Its fused "When launched" triggers answer `starshipLaunched` ([[§6.2]], `when_launched`: the event of the Starship the piece is fused into and no other), queued as the event is dispatched and run once the Cries are done, since work drains before the trigger queue moves ([[R113]]); they resolve in [[R68]]'s order, in the order the pieces fused. A Trap may reveal on the launch of either player's Starship (#131 Spacerace Stimulus): it answers the event as any trap answers its event.

**Answered by every seat.** A launch and the prompts it opens are ordinary actions and prompts, so the practice AI considers the launch among its candidates and answers its prompts, the random policy draws them, and a timeout answers them by the random policy ([[R79]]). The state check follows the whole launch ([[R59]]).
