Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event or message changes. Hearthstone's silent audio costs nothing: a sound turned all the way down neither waits for anything nor makes anything else quieter.

- A voice line is unheard when the game is muted, Voice lines are off, or master × voice is 0. [[R669]] and [[R204]] gave every line the voice channel and a dip of the music and the effects whatever its level, so a line at volume 0 held the practice AI's next step (§9.9, up to four seconds), made speech nobody heard and kept the whole voice library downloading. An unheard line now takes no channel (no `data-speaking`, no practice hold), makes no speech and no duck, and nothing is preloaded or prefetched for it (B58), exactly as with Voice lines off. A line already playing, speech included, stops when the level drops to 0, and the prefetch resumes when it rises again. A pick-up (R655) at that level plays its effect and no line.
- An effects-bus sound is unheard when the game is muted or master × sfx is 0. A card's effect and the `MUSIC_DUCK_SFX` sounds (`trapSting`, `victory`, `defeat`) then no longer dip the music ([[R631]]). The cues still run at gain 0 and are logged as before.

Levels above 0 behave as before. This extends B58 (`docs/polish/2-sound.md`), [[R631]] and [[R669]], and edits [[§10.11]]. **Rejected:** letting the line play at gain 0, today's behaviour, which held the practice AI for nothing; a new setting for it; reading the levels inside `duck()` itself, which does not know who asked for the dip.
