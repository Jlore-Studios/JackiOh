The reaper finishes a stuck match itself rather than flagging it for a server that may be the very component that crashed. The consequence is deliberate and visible: a reaper-resolved ceiling draw records `turns = 0` and leaves both ratings unchanged, where the same draw resolved by a live match actor records the real turn count and applies the ordinary Glicko-2 move
