"Give a unit you control to an enemy" (Meditative #6 Me no Likey) and "they gain control of this" (#30 Fickle E-Kitten) are a steal the other way: the card's controller now loses it to their opponent (`give_control`). It lands in the same lane on the receiver's side when that zone is free, else in that row's first free zone ([[R15]]), keeps its owner ([[R12]]), damage, buffs, counters and position, and enters its new side this turn, so it is summoning sick there and its exertion is fresh ([[R171]]); the event is `controlChanged`. As with a steal, a card off the field or beneath a Stack ([[R13]]) stays where it is; unlike a steal, which destroys a card it has no room for ([[R4205000]]), so does a card the receiver has no free zone for ([[R803]]), and nothing is reported.
