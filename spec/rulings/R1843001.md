At the state check's game-end point the hero check runs first, as [[R850]] orders it: a hero at 0 or less loses, and a full vote tally is not read then. Next the tallies: a player whose tally is full ([[R1843000]]) is removed, a loss for them, before any held win is read, theirs or the other player's, so a player whose list both fills their tally and wins them the game outright is removed. When both players' tallies are full at the same point, as when one effect list made every Brain on both sides vote, both are removed together, a draw (`both-voted-out`), as two heroes at 0 in the same check are; `votedOut` names each side's cards before the `gameOver`. Hearthstone's semantics decide what the designer left open: losses before wins, and two players losing at once a draw.
