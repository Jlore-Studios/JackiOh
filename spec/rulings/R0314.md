The user asked on 2026-09-25 for the tutorial's Skip step to go, from the HUD and from the coach's bubble. Nothing needs it to keep a player moving. Only a tip or an `info` step may hold the AI, and each shows Got it; an `act` step never holds it, whatever its script says. A step still current after `TUTORIAL_STEP_TURNS_MAX` of the player's own turn starts retires by itself, except the last, which ends with the game. A step never blocks an action, since the engine takes whatever the board sends, and Exit tutorial is in the HUD throughout
