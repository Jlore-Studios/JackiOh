I23 fails a `transformed` naming an Immutable card ([[R4609000]]). Off the field a Transform is a Replace ([[R35]], [[R365]], [[R921]]): the hand or library card ceases to exist and a new card takes its place, so a library keeps its count ([[§9.1]]). No ruling said whether that is a change to the Immutable card's text.
**It is not.** Nothing rewrites the card: it is gone, and the card in its place is another one, as Hearthstone's transform in hand replaces a card outright. [[R23]] says Immutable blocks a Transform, which on the field is the card's own text rewritten in place; off it, [[R35]] has always replaced an Immutable card. So I23 allows a `transformed` whose new instance is another one and that is hidden from a player, as every hand or library card's is ([[R177]]); a Replace on the field is readable to both players and still a finding, and so is a `transformed` in place in a hand (a Vanilla's, a copied or fused text's), which rewrites the card it names.
**And a `none` cue.** A Nerf or a Buff on a card someone may not read is reported every time, with change `none` where nothing changed, so no count leaks ([[R440]]). An Immutable card in a hand is one such card: the cue changes nothing, and I23 allows a `degraded` or `upgraded` whose change is `none`.
Rejected: refusing the Replace on an Immutable hand or library card, which would reverse [[R35]] and change how Transmogulate plays, a rules change only a person may ask for.
