[[§4.4]] step 3a ([[R3540003]]) turns a hit from a source that states "Damage dealt Burns" (S+ #151 Volcanic Rounds) or "Damage dealt is Toxic" (S+ #153 Noxious Rounds), past Armor and the caps, into that counter on its target: nothing of steps 4 to 9 happens, so no damage, no kill and no Lifesteal heal. The AI's quick arithmetic ([[§9.9]]) counted such a hit as damage, so it could read lethal on a hero a Rounds-equipped unit would only Burn.

No rule of the game covers what the AI reads, so its reads follow the engine (`query::converts_to`, which reads Burn where a source states both). With the flag `pool_and_conversion_read`:

- a converted hit deals a hero no face damage, and in the Taunt walk neither wears a Taunt down nor breaks it. Its attacker waits at the Taunt, as one whose hit the Taunt's Armor takes whole does ([[R3937000]]), and goes on when the Taunt falls to another;
- a converting First Strike Taunt destroys no attacker first;
- a Divine Shield still takes a converting hit, since step 1 comes before step 3a, and a converted hit spends no damage pool;
- no kill, no death and no heal follow from a converted hit, in `blow`, `kills_and_survives`, `fight` and `trade_value` alike.

A converted hit is still worth something to its striker. On a Unit, its counter is priced by `counter_price`: the worth the unit loses with that much more Burn or Toxic on it, as `afflicted` prices its counters ([[R3822000]]). The reply adds it for its blow, `cleave_share` of it for each splash, and takes off the attacker's own for a strike back that converts, unless the attacker destroys the target first. On a hero, the counter is worth `face_per_damage` × `hero_affliction` for each point of life its coming ticks add (`hero_ticks_added`).

Rejected:

- the issue's "pops no Divine Shield": [[§4.4]] step 1 takes the hit before step 3a reads it, as the engine and [[R4032000]]'s projection play it;
- giving the counter no worth, under which the reply would never swing a Rounds unit at a hero or at a unit it cannot kill.

It reads only public state, and it adds no rule of the game and no constant.
