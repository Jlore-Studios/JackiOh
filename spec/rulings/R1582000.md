[[R639]]'s device tally keeps four more counts per card. Like the others, they are read off the views and the events new with each, from the viewer's seat, once per game, and they name only a card the view shows:

- `allured` (Most Coveted): a card of the player's taken by the opponent's Allure ([[R963]], [[R1045000]]): a `controlChanged` steal to the other seat in the same batch as the `allure` mark ([[R437]]) leaving that card. A face-down Trap taken reads as the sentinel ([[R33]], [[R97]]) and is not counted. Neither is a card Allure destroys for want of room, nor any other steal.
- `lethal` (Last Laugh): in a game the player won by `hero-death`, the card on the player's side that dealt the opponent's hero its last damage before `gameOver`, in that batch. Fatigue and a hidden or opposing source count nothing.
- `sprung` (Springloaded): each `trapFired` of a Trap the player controlled.
- `survived` (Survivor): in a game the player won, each Unit on top of a pile on the player's side at the end, one per copy.

They are stored with the rest under the same key and version: a value stored before them reads them as 0, as does a count that is not a whole positive number, and the clear empties them. None syncs ([[R1142000]]); the landing's showcase shows them ([[R1584000]]). Rejected: remembering marked cards across views to count a face-down Trap (the steal's event hides it), and walking the hero's health back to find the blow (heals and overkill blur it).
