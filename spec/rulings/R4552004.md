Both sections of the Tokens tab, the Tokens and the Other created cards, show Card, Name, Tier, Adjusted and **Decisiveness** ([[R4552001]], [[R4552002]]), amending [[R4083000]]'s "Tier and Adjusted alone". Decisiveness reads `±X.X%` from [[R654]]'s floor and "not enough games" below it; its header's tooltip names the typical Created card's value. It sorts by its value, highest first, through the tab's shared sort ([[R3184002]]), and the sort select offers it; each section still opens on Adjusted. In the Grid a Created card shows it after its Adjusted. Each `created` row of `GET /api/stats/cards` sends `decisiveness` beside its adjusted win rate ([[R4083000]]'s list gains it, [[R4552003]]).

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
