A card's script may declare `stays_sick`, a read of the card: while it holds, the Unit on the field is summoning sick whatever turn it entered (`combat::is_sick`, [[§4.1]]), so it cannot declare an attack ([[§4.2]] step 1) and its view says it is sick, as on the turn it entered; Rush and Charge lift it as they lift any summoning sickness. FvH #37 Old Grum "stays summoning-sick until it has come back by Reborn": its read holds while the card has not returned by Reborn ([[R83]], `returned_by_reborn`), so its Reborn body may attack from the turn after it returns, as any arrival. Only a card on the field is read, and a Vanilla has no text. A fusion stays sick while any ingredient's read holds.
