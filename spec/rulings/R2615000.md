[[§9.5]]'s watching a friend had a switch, "Let friends watch my games" (`allowSpectators`, kept on the account and on by default), which refused a friend's watch and let go of one already in. It is gone, the owner's call: watching is part of having friends, so the way to stop a person watching is to stop being their friend.

**Who may watch.** A friend of either player may watch a live 1v1 match, ranked or unranked, from that friend's seat, `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` behind and with no legal action, while fewer than `SPECTATORS_PER_MATCH` watch it, as before but for the switch. A player who is not a friend, a blocked pair, a full match and a co-op game are refused as before, and a watch still ends with its match ([[R1107000]]).

**The way out.** Removing a friend, or a block either way, refuses the next watch and lets go of one already running at once (`revoke_between`), as before.

**The server.** It reads `profiles.allow_spectators` no longer: the watch gate (`social::watch::watchable_in`) and the friends list's `watchable` (`presence_watchable`) drop the check, and `GET` and `PUT /api/settings` answer `{ settings: { groups } }`. A body that still carries `allowSpectators` beside its groups, from a page loaded before this, merges its groups and nothing else, as any other key past `groups` is read; one that carries it alone has no groups and is refused as such a body is. The column stays, so a server still on the code before this during a deploy reads it safely; a later migration may drop it.

**The client.** Settings and Play together lose the switch. Play together's Watching panel says instead that friends can always watch a player's online games, a few seconds behind, and that removing or blocking a friend stops one. The Privacy Policy says the same of a player's friends and of their opponent's ([[R2615001]]).

Rejected: keeping the switch with watching off by default, which reads privacy-by-default rules more strictly than the owner wants for friends; and dropping the column in the same change, which would fail every read of it by a server still on the old code while the deploy overlaps.
