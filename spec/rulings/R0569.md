C+ #8's "Degrade 4 random cards in your opponent's deck" draws its 4 different cards ([[R60]]) among the deck cards a Degrade can change now ([[§8.7]]), all of them when there are no more, drawing no random number when there are no more than 4 ([[R129]]). So that the number of `degraded` cues never tells either player how many deck cards a Degrade could change ([[R440]]), the pick is padded up to 4 (or the deck's size) with cards no change reaches, each cued with the change `none`. The Radiant face reaches every card of the deck once, as [[R440]]'s sweep does
