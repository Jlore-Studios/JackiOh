---
id: R1301000
title: "A card pack's slots take their rarities from the pity math's pack (issue #1301)"
cards: "§14.3, §14.4"
proven_in:
  - crates/server/src/economy/roll.rs
---
[[§14.4]]'s pity math rolls per card pack, not per slot: it says which of Epic, Legendary and
Mythic a pack holds ([[R1298000]]), while [[§14.3]] says every slot rolls a rarity and one of them,
the Rare-or-better slot, never rolls Common. `crates/server/src/economy/roll.rs`'s `slot_rarities`
turns the one into the other:

- The first slot, the Rare-or-better slot, holds the best tracked rarity the pack holds, or Rare
  when it holds none.
- Each other tracked rarity the pack holds takes one more slot, highest first: a pack holding a
  Mythic and an Epic opens Mythic, Epic, then the rest.
- Every other slot is a filler: Rare at `PACK_FILLER_RARE_RATE`, else Common, one draw each.

So a pack holds a tracked rarity exactly when the pity math says it does, and each counter reads
the pack as [[§14.4]] counts it. `PACK_SLOTS` is at least the three tracked rarities, which the
module asserts when it compiles. `PACK_FILLER_RARE_RATE` is this issue's pick, since #1294's table
gives none: 25%, about the share of Rares among Hearthstone's slots past its guaranteed one.

Hearthstone semantics: a Hearthstone pack guarantees one Rare or better and rolls its other slots
by their own odds, with pity on its Epics and Legendaries. Here the odds and the pity are the
spec's, per pack, so the slots are dealt from them.

Rejected: rolling each slot's rarity on its own, by per-slot odds. [[§14.4]]'s rates and counters
are per pack, so a per-slot roll could neither land on `PACK_TARGET_RATES` nor reset a counter on
the pack that holds its rarity without a second rule that could disagree with the first.
