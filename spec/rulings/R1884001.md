§4.5 step 1 asks each `wouldDie` replacement once a check, and one that resolves its follow-up at once (`resolveFirst`, [[R1875006]]) starts the check over with the cards that did so earlier in it left out ([[R460]]). A follow-up can run a state check of its own: Stinkyscape #138 Pocket Peptides casts CN Peptides, and the cast's own steps check the board. That inner check met the same death and asked the same replacement again, and a card no firing spends (only a Trap in the backrow is spent, so a fused Field Spell that holds #138's text is not) answered it again inside its own follow-up, without end: the release's fuzz wave found it on seed 1322, a fused Book of Buff, Pocket Peptides and Organic Produce, and the recursion overflowed the stack, which ends the process a match runs in.

So while a card's `resolveFirst` replacement is resolving, a check that its follow-up runs does not ask that card again (`EngineSink::dying_replaced_by`); every other replacement is asked as before, and the check the follow-up returns to starts over as [[R1875006]] says. A spent Trap was never asked twice, so no game that ended before plays differently; only the inner checks of a follow-up still in progress change, which ended in the overflow or rolled CN Peptides' coin again until it Buffed. Hearthstone likewise resolves a triggered effect fully before the same trigger may fire for the death it caused.

The guard lives on the sink for the one reduce that runs the follow-up. A follow-up that waits on a player's pick (a cast its controller aims) ends that reduce, and the next one resumes it without the guard; the card is still not asked again, since the check the follow-up returns to starts over without the cards that resolved first ([[R1875006]]): the replacement answers once and the Unit dies, whichever target is picked. No shipped card's follow-up waits on a pick today: Pocket Peptides' casts are aimed only.

The flicker is held to the same once a check since issue #2280. A card that flickers its controller's dying Units instead (Classic #14's Radiant face) answered §4.5 step 1 again on every later pass of the check when no firing spends it, a Field Trap or a fused Unit holding #14's text, so a Unit that came back dying, under an aura that takes its last health, was flickered without end: the fuzz wave found it on that issue's seed 55, a fused Unit holding Shadowstep's text beside Suppressive Aura. Such a card is now among those that answered earlier in the check ([[R460]]), and the Unit dies at the next pass. A spent Trap was never asked twice, so no game that ended before plays differently.
