An account may make `REPLAY_SHARES_PER_HOUR` (30) replay-share attempts an hour, counted per account on every attempt and before any lookup, as friend requests are ([[§9.8]]). The next is 429 `rate_limited` with `retryAfterMs`; it writes no share and tells nobody, and another account's allowance is its own.

A share of a match with a friend that is already held still answers 204, but writes no row and tells nobody: no feed row, no social frame, no toast, so the friend's feed and unread count stay as they were, even after the first row was read ([[R4233000]]). `shares_put` reports whether it wrote a row, in both stores, and the route notifies only when it did. Unsharing and sharing again writes a new row and tells again, bounded by the limit. This amends [[R4039000]], whose "a re-shared replay never stacks" now holds because a held share is not told at all.

Rejected: counting shares against `CHALLENGES_PER_HOUR`, as lobby invites do ([[R3730005]]), since a batch of shares would block the player's unrelated challenges; and answering a re-share with an error, which would show an honest double-click as a failure.
