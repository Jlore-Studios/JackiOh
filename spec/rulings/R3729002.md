A challenge, a friend request, a daily game on the player's move or a lobby invite arrived as a toast or as the
Friends button's badge, and one missed while looking away was gone once its toast closed. The inbox keeps them.

**The bell.** A bell in the top bar's end group, after the wallet, on every menu screen inside the shell and in the
landing's corner, signed in only, and absent while the social reads are refused or not yet answered once. Its badge counts
what needs the player: every item below but a shared replay already seen (a replay is news, not a debt). It opens
a sheet, the inbox, which reads its lists again as it opens.

**What it holds, in this order**, and each with the action it needs, inline:

1. **Challenges in** that have not lapsed, the soonest to lapse first, with the time left ticking (issue #1268):
   Accept joins the room with the deck or trio the player last used for that mode (their remembered choice), or
   Open in lobby when none fits, and Decline.
2. **Lobby and co-op invites**, newest first, as long as the room they name lives ([[R3730005]]): Join and Dismiss.
3. **Daily games on the player's move**, in the Daily screen's order ([[§9.13]]): Play.
4. **Daily challenges in**: Open (the Daily screen, where accepting asks for a deck) and Decline.
5. **Friend requests in**, oldest first: Accept and Decline.
6. **Replays a friend shared**, newest first: Watch, which opens Play together on that replay.

**Sources, and no new server state.** Everything is what the client already reads: the social snapshot
(`GET /api/social`, the `/ws/social` frame, [[R3214000]]) for challenges, and the daily list, the friends read
and the shared replays read, once on mounting the bell, again every `INBOX_POLL_MS` while the tab is shown, when
a friends frame arrives, and after every answer, never oftener than `INBOX_MIN_GAP_MS` unless the player acted.
A read that fails keeps the list it had. Invites are what the `lobbyInvite` frames carried, kept in the tab: a
reload loses them, the lobby's own reads ([[R3730004]]) not. "Seen" is the Friends badge's "as last read": a
replay counts until the panel has listed it once, kept in this browser (`INBOX_SEEN_KEY`, at most
`INBOX_SEEN_MAX` marks).

**The toasts.** A live challenge still pops its toast. The toast gains Later, which sends it away alone; the
challenge stays in the inbox until it is answered or lapses. A lobby invite's toast likewise hides on Open or
Dismiss and the invite stays until the player joins or dismisses it in the inbox.

**Rule 7.** The inbox sends intent and shows what the server answered: every refusal is the server's words, in
the panel, and the lists are the server's. The client drops a challenge only when its own clock passes the
expiry the server named. **Proof.** `order.test.ts` holds the order, the lapse and the badge count;
`InboxBell.test.tsx` the six kinds answered and refreshed, a refusal shown and the bell hidden signed out; the toast
tests Later and the invites kept; `TogetherRoute.test.tsx` a shared replay opened from its link; spec 56 sends
a challenge's toast away and accepts it from the bell, and accepts a friend request there.

Rejected: closing the toast on its own, which hides the very thing the inbox is for; a deck picker in each row
(a challenge joins with the remembered choice, and Open in lobby is the way to pick); and a count kept by the
server, which this issue is not to add.
