"Ascent level" (Meditative #8 Reach the Summit) is the number of times this instance has been
played before ([[R429]]). `counts_plays` counts each play at [[§10.5]] step 4, casts included and
countered plays never, and the count rides the card through every zone; while it resolves the level
is the count less one, and anywhere else the level is the count. A copy starts at 0 ([[R57]] resets
the count). An Echo repeat is not a play, so it resolves the same level again. Made Radiant
mid-climb, the card keeps its level.
