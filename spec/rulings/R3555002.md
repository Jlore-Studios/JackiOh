S+ #99 Big Time Recruits reads "Draw the 3 most expensive permanents and make them GIGA" (and
Radiant on its Radiant face). The spec has no such draw, so it is read as follows.

- **The pool.** The library's non-Spell cards by [[R24]]'s costs — a Spell is no permanent — dearest
  first. Fewer permanents draw what there is.
- **The tie.** A tie at the cut is broken by the match rng, which draws nothing when there is no
  choice ([[R129]]): only a tie straddling the cut rolls.
- **The draws.** Each is its own named draw, dearest first ([[R135]]): the hand cap burns ([[R4]]),
  cast on draw casts ([[R58]]). Each one that reaches the hand is given GIGA there by
  `drawFromLibrary`'s `keywords` rider, and the Radiant face also makes it Radiant ([[R1061]]). Each
  keeps GIGA onto the field ([[R1878003]]).

Rejected: [[R24]]'s nearest-the-top tie (the issue's reading is random), and a Spell counted as a
permanent.
