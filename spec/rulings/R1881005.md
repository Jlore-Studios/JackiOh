T-AI-8 Drone Uplink's End of turn ([[R62]]) summons a Drone Interceptor into its controller's leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), nothing with none, and "It gains Starship Piece" is the summon's `keywords` rider: the keyword is granted as the drone lands, so no other effect can come between (no `TargetSpec` names a card just made), and kept until it leaves the field ([[R78]]). A granted piece fuses as a printed one does: when the drone dies a copy of it fuses into its owner's Dormant Starship, one summoned first if they have none ([[R1852000]]). The copy is a plain Drone Interceptor, its definition's, the granted keyword not carried (ST-S21-7). Radiant: a Radiant Interceptor.

T-AI-9 Honeypot's "Give it Taunt" is the same rider: each Drone Interceptor it summons lands with Taunt, and one that rolled Taunt as its combat keyword keeps it.
