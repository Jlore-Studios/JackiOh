A finished game's replay ([[R768]]) draws the prompt open in each step's view, and nothing it draws sends anything. The replay's seat's own prompt shows its title, options and count as they were offered, opened folded to a bar on the top edge with "View the board"'s way back (issue #1204), clear of the replay's controls. Unfolded, every option is greyed (`aria-disabled`) and a click picks nothing; there is no Confirm, Cancel or countdown. A prompt that is only an answer's form (a craft, [[R880]]; a night market, [[R1000]]; a placement answered on the board, [[R821000]]) is the waiting panel instead, under its own words. The other seat's prompt is the waiting panel with no option, and the seat's answered mulligan its stamped hand, as live play shows them ([[§10.6]], [[§10.8]], [[R265]]).

Nothing hidden is added: the options are the step's view, which the seat saw. A watched live game and the co-op boards still draw no prompt, and the option picked is not marked (that needs the next step's view). Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
