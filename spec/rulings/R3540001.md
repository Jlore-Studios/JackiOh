At the start of a player's turn, in the same stage as the Brittle tick and after the Dormant countdown and the quests' waits ([[§2.2]]), their hero and then each Unit acting on their side in [[R68]]'s order that carries a count takes its Burn and then its Toxic, before the delayed effects, the start-of-turn triggers and the draw. Each tick is one [[§4.4]] hit from no source with every step applying: Burn's is unpierced, so Armor and a Divine Shield meet it, and Toxic's pierces Armor only ([[R346]]; a Divine Shield and a hero's caps still meet it). Burn's count drops by 3 (`BURN_DECAY`; 8, 5, 2, none) after its hit, whatever the hit did, a hit Armor or a shield took whole included; Toxic never drops. The two `damage` events carry `via` Burn and Toxic ([[R2783001]]). The targets are read before the first hit and no state check runs inside the tick ([[R59]]): the stage's own check afterwards collects what it killed, before the draw, and a hero it brought to 0 loses the game at its owner's turn. Rejected: ticking among the start-of-turn triggers, where a prompt could pause it; and letting Toxic pass a Divine Shield, which Pierce never does.
