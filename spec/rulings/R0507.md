Words and numbers a patch added to a face's text are marked bold, with a double underline, in a teal of their own, distinct from [[R277]]'s gold and [[R279]]'s dotted line; screen readers hear "new:". Where the patch took words away, the old text follows under "Was", with them struck through (read as "removed:"). Values read "(2) Cost → (1) Cost", "2/3 → 3/3". On the Patch notes page only the newest patch shows its cards on arrival; the others load their snapshot and the one before when opened. A patch's cards can be filtered by name. A changed card whose change shows on a face appears as one face from that patch's snapshot: the Radiant face if every printed change is the Radiant face's, else the base face, with its changes beside it. A card changed only in data no face prints (lines of code, cards named, tunable-number limits) is listed by name with what changed. Added cards are listed by name, grouped by set in catalog order, with each set's tokens as their own group; removed cards by name. A card opens as it stands now (the newest snapshot holding it, or the last before its removal) in the collection's detail view with its History open. Old faces in History mark referenced names but never make them controls. Every text is filled in (`fillParams`) before it is drawn or diffed, so no placeholder shows.
