#98's `armor`, X 1: "Your hero gains 2 Armor until your next turn" is a player modifier (`heroArmor`) that `heroArmorOf` adds to the hero's Armor ([[R124]]) from the moment it is gained until the cleanup that ends your opponent's next turn takes it off, so it is gone when your next turn begins. The Radiant face is named Tank Up (the designer's own words: "Power name becomes Tank Up"): "Your hero gains 4 Armor, then this power refreshes." The 4 Armor is the hero's own and stays (C+ #46's `gainHeroArmor`), as the designer's Radiant text names no end to it; "refreshes" is, as the designer asked it written short, "refreshes into a different Radiant one": the card rolls again among the other twelve powers (the match rng), on its Radiant face as it is, and gets back the use Tank Up spent, so the new power may be activated this turn too, paying its own X
