title: "A play is judged against the opponent's best-scored playable card, before it resolves"

While a card that judges plays acts (Meditative #71 Pareto Optimality, MD-D28,
`StaticFlags.judges_plays`), each card its opponent plays is judged as the play begins
(`subsystems::pareto`): optimal when it was the scorer's best-scored playable card for that player,
read from their own view of the pre-play state, so nothing hidden from them counts; a tie counts as
optimal. Only plays are judged, never a cast or an activation, and nothing is stored while no card
judges. The verdict (`GameState.play_judgement`) is never in a view ([[§10.8]]); the card's trigger
reads it after the play resolves, and a face-down Trap keeps its verdict until it reveals. A game with
a judge replays from its log to the same hash.
