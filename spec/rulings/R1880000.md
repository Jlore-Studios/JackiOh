The five Stinkyscape bundles each add a list of named cards to their caster's hand (ST-S24-1). Each adds a new card of every name, in the order printed, owned by its caster, on that card's own base face and at its printed price: no `costOverride`, no cost change. The Radiant face's "a Radiant X, Y and Z" reaches the whole list, as C+ #42.1 KY's Gift's and [[R1867001]]'s do, so every card it adds is Radiant. Vanguard Coalition's Radiant face keeps Vxyl and Star-jumper Core's adds its five Radiant, the plan's fixes (ST8) to the designer's lines. The cards arrive through [[§2.4]]'s pipeline one at a time, so a hand with room for fewer keeps the first ones and burns the rest, in that order. The cards are named, so they reach the hand while the set is held ([[R1420]]).
