A card that begins the game in a player's library has its origin noted (`started_in`) before its first change of owner, in a game that keeps the recursion log ([[R4589000]]: one holding an Infinite Assault card), so no other game's state moves. Stealing a card from a deck or swapping libraries transfers ownership while preserving that the card started in the other player's deck. A copy made of a card in a deck, hand or graveyard is a fresh card created in hand or deck and never counts as having started in the opponent's deck ([[R57]]).
