Classic+ #74 Twice Forward One Step Backwards fuses into itself, on each even count of the opponent's plays, a copy of the played card's definition: on the base face its base definition, whatever face was played, and on the Radiant face a Radiant copy ([[R469]]). The played card is never consumed and stays where it is, so wherever it has gone there is a copy to fuse and every even count fires the trap ([[R589]]'s "if it still exists" goes). It is Animated and, since issue #2280, prints no stats ([[R2280000]]; a 0/1, Radiant 0/2, before): as the last step of each firing it animates into its own lane's unit zone (§6 Animated) once what it has fused gives it stats, a copy of a Unit, with the stats of what it has fused ([[R77]]); with only stat-less copies (Spells, Traps) it stays a face-up Field Trap in its zone. It stays a Field Trap for every rule that reads its type. An Immutable card in play is still passed by ([[R781002]]). It amends [[R425]], [[R589]] and [[R781002]].
