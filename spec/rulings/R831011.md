Classic+ #74 Twice Forward One Step Backwards fuses into itself, on each even count of the opponent's plays, a copy of the played card's definition: on the base face its base definition, whatever face was played, and on the Radiant face a Radiant copy ([[R469]]). The played card is never consumed and stays where it is, so wherever it has gone there is a copy to fuse and every even count fires the trap ([[R589]]'s "if it still exists" goes). It is Animated, a 0/1 (Radiant 0/2) as the issue gives no stats: as the last step of each firing it animates into its own lane's unit zone (§6 Animated), a 0/1 plus what it has fused ([[R77]]), and stays a Field Trap for every rule that reads its type. An Immutable card in play is still passed by ([[R781002]]). It amends [[R425]], [[R589]] and [[R781002]].
