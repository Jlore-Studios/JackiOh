C+ #26's "one more action" is one main-phase action — a play, an attack, a position switch or an activation — and ending the turn by hand also uses it; once it resolves the turn ends ([[§6.3]] End the turn). The base face ends its controller's turn when it is cast on draw on their turn, the rest of the effect list resolving first, so drawn at the start of their turn it ends that turn before the main phase; cast on the opponent's turn, there is no turn of its controller's to end and only the summon happens
