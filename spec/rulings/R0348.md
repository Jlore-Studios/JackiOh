An X-cost card whose X the player chooses — #24 Efficiency Dividend and #74 Adaptive UI, the only Core cards that cost X (#98 costs (0) since [[R752]]) — is played for 1 ≤ X ≤ current mana (`MIN_CHOSEN_X`). One check (`playChoices.whyXRefused`) is both what `legalActions` offers and what the play refuses, so a play naming X = 0, or naming no X, is refused ("X must be at least 1"), and with no mana the card cannot be played at all, even when a `costOverride` makes it free ([[R65]])
