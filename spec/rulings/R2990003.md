[[§9.4]]'s block ends at once a watch of either by the other, and in a co-op game a block between the watcher and the friend's teammate does too ([[R2881000]]). A watcher may still share a match with someone a block stands between: the 1v1 friend's rival, or another watcher. Its emotes ([[R2990000]]) never reach that one: before each emote let through goes out, one read of the store asks of every recipient, a seat's person or a watcher, whether either blocks the other, and the emote goes to those with no block between them, as [[R1351000]] keeps a blocked pair's emotes apart in a match. The watch itself goes on, and so does everything else the watcher is sent. A read that fails sends the emote to no one: an emote is worth less than a block.

Rejected: closing a watch whenever its watcher and anyone in the match stand blocked, which would let a rival end a friend's watch by blocking the watcher; and reading the blocks once as the watcher is let in, which would miss a block made during the watch unless every block also reached into each live actor.
