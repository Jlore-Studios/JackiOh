On the Radiant face only (MD-C14), the night market's `market` prompt offers one barter per card in
the caster's hand. A barter exiles that card, in public, and adds its price to the yuan, by
[[R1000]]'s rule and doubled if the card is Radiant; the market then reopens. Any number of hand cards
may be bartered, one an answer. The base face never offers a barter.
