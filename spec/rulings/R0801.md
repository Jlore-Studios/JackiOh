"Draw a [kind of] card from your deck" takes the topmost matching card, as Recruit's top-down scan does ([[§6.3]]), and draws no random number: the deck is shuffled, so that is still a uniform pick. Costs are read as the library filter reads them ([[R65]]): an X card counts as 0 and an embiggen card at its base price. No match draws nothing and causes no fatigue.

Meditative #4's six cards are chosen as the effect begins and noted on the resolving card, so a cast-on-draw pause cannot lose them; each is then a real draw ([[R58]]'s chain, the hand cap and [[R457]]'s limit all apply). Meditative #14 reads the same way, one memoized sweep.
