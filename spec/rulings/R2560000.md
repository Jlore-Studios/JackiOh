A practice claim ([[§14.8]]) pays when its log folds from its seed's setup to a finished, unvoided game with the claimed result. Each action is under its player's next nonce, from the seat that player plays then ([[R677]]), and accepted by the engine. The AI's seat is held to exactly that and nothing more. So a legal move the AI would not make pays, including a move a clock cut short and an AI concession. An action the engine refuses is `does_not_fold`, and no refusal is `ai_mismatch`. Games that earn decide under `PRACTICE_AI_CLOCK_MS`, and nothing marks a cut decision.

The owner's reasons: a re-run refused games the player really played, since a move the browser's clock cut short, or any drift between the browser's AI and the server's, differs from the re-run, and [[R2208000]]'s long cap only narrowed that, still failing on slow devices; every claim cost a full-`AI_BUDGET` search per AI move on the server's blocking pool; and practice pay is already held to the day's Jlore cap ([[R1483000]]), so a forged AI side earns no more than an honest game. `PRACTICE_VERIFY_CONCURRENCY` still bounds the folds. Supersedes [[R1303002]] and [[R2208000]].

Rejected: keeping the long clock, since slow devices still failed; and still refusing what [[R188]] keeps from the AI (Concede, a draw offer or its acceptance, an Emote) as a cheap guard, since the cap bounds what it could earn.

Hearthstone semantics: a game against the computer earns a small, capped reward, and the check is that it reached its result, not how the computer played.
