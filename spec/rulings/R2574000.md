The view marks every hand card of the viewer's that the Trade rule passes as Tradable (`trade::is_tradable`, [[§6.1]], [[R1838000]], [[R1864002]]) with `tradable: true`, whether by its printed face, a granted keyword or an aura over its controller's hand (The Syndicate, The Trade Union); the card's keyword list still shows only what the card is made of ([[R1864002]], [[R243]]). The client draws the Tradable pip from `card.tradable` alone and works out no aura itself (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The key is absent otherwise, never `false`, and never on the opponent's hand or post-game revealed hands ([[R434]]). Rejected: having the client inspect the field for hand auras, which violates CLAUDE.md rule 7, and adding Tradable to the hand card's `keywords`, which would contradict R1864002.
