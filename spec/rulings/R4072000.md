An unstarted private lobby ([[R3730000]]) moves onto the running catalog the first time anyone opens or touches it after a card patch: its read compares its stored catalog with the running one ([[§9.4]]), and when they differ writes the lobby onto the running catalog through the revision compare-and-set, running the TTL from now and telling every member over `/ws/social` ([[R3214000]]). Every seat's pick is removed and every Ready turned off, since each pick was frozen under the old cards; seats, teams and settings stay. A lobby whose game has started is left alone, since its game already pinned its catalog ([[R767000]]). `enter` and `start` keep refusing a stale catalog ([[R3730002]]), which then catches only a deploy landing between that read and its write. When two members' reads race to move it, both get the lobby: the loser of the compare-and-set reads once more rather than answering `changed`.

Rejected: re-freezing each member's saved deck behind their backs, since they may have edited it since they picked, so their Ready would agree to a deck they never chose, and a random pick was dealt from the old card pools. Keeping the picks whose cards still pass the deck checks: the same objection applies to random decks, and it is more code for a once-a-day case. Closing the lobby when the catalog changes: in effect what happens today, and the group has to gather again.

Amends [[R3730002]], whose Start check it narrows, and [[R3730003]], whose list of what takes picks and Readies off it joins.
