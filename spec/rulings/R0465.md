The key stays in the `answer` prompt's resume data. `viewFor` never sends it, and the AI's redaction ([[R185]]) strips it from its own prompt and from parked work, so the AI answers from what the prompt shows. Without the key, no answer is judged right. Options appear under letters in an rng-shuffled order.
