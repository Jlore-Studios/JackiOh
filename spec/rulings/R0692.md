Balance patch 1 (#88) made "Bounce" the keyword for "return to hand" and asked for it on every card where it fits; since #354 it fits only a permanent the return takes from the field ([[R746]]). A card's text says Bounce wherever it returns a permanent from the field to its controller's hand (#17 Flood, C #22 Mid Runner, C #66 EU Striker), the Units it answers as they die (C #14 Shadowstep), or a card a rotation turns away (#52's Radiant face, "Bounced"). The glossary explains the word with [[§6.3]]'s Bounce row. The word changes nothing a card does: each keeps its own semantics (a unit-token card still vanishes, the hand cap still applies)
