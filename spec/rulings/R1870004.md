Stinkyscape #4.4 Last Word reads "Reveals when your hero would take lethal damage: Prevent it and end the turn. The next turn is yours: your Units have Charge during it, and you lose the game at its end"; its Radiant face adds "you have 100 mana" in that turn. The spec has no turn whose Units all have Charge and no loss timed to a turn's end.

- **The hit.** A face-down Trap on its controller's hero's lethal replacement ([[§4.4]] step 4a, [[R1204]]'s judge, after Armor, multipliers and caps), on either player's turn, in combat or by an effect: it fires, goes to the graveyard, and the hit is cancelled, dealing nothing ([[R1704001]]). A hit that is not lethal leaves it set.
- **The next turn.** The turn running ends once what is resolving has resolved ([[R456]]). On its controller's own turn they are owed an extra turn ([[R845]]); on the opponent's, their own next turn follows and no extra one is owed. Extra turns the opponent is already owed come first ([[R846]]), and "the next turn" is then the controller's next one after them.
- **Charge.** Through that turn of theirs each Unit its controller has on the field has Charge, a Unit that lands during it too: a `unitsKeyword` player modifier, expiry `nextTurnOf` (`give_units_keyword_next_turn`), read by [[§10.4]] beside the auras' keywords, live on that turn only and gone at its cleanup. Its badge reads "Your Units have Charge", with "during your next turn" until that turn comes.
- **The loss.** A delayed step at that turn's end-of-turn stage ([[§2.2]], one turn of theirs on, [[R1181000]]) makes the opponent win the game (`winGame`, [[R850]]), a hero at 0 still losing first.
- **Radiant.** A delayed step at the start of that turn, after its mana refresh, tops their mana up to 100 (the declared `mana`), past `MAX_MANA` ([[§6.3]] Mana).
