[[§8]] #86's Death: "Take control of the Unit that destroyed this." The unit that destroyed her is [[R42]]'s killer: the unit whose damage instance took her to 0 or less health, or whose Poisonous hit marked her — in combat, as attacker or defender, or through its own text's damage (#68's Cry). Her Death reads it off her last-known state ([[R78]]) and takes that unit with [[§6.3]]'s Steal while it is a Unit acting on the field, placed per [[R15]]. Nothing is taken when no unit destroyed her — a destroy effect, a Tribute or a Spell's damage leaves no killer ([[R42]]) — when the killer has since left the field (a mutual combat death) or lies dormant under a Stack ([[R13]]), or when her controller already controls it
