Your portrait's emote menu offers your hand and nothing else ([[R1341]]): the hand's three voice lines on the arc above the portrait and its five emoji in the row below, each in the pool's order. The arc's curve is unchanged: an item `d` places from the middle dips 3.5 × d² pixels below the crown, rounded, so five lines would drop 14, 4, 0, 4 and 14 as they always did and a hand's three drop 4, 0 and 4. In hotseat the menu that opens is the seat on move's and offers that seat's hand. Until a hand reaches the client — a bare board, a frame from a server that deals none — a seat shows the default hand, the first three voice lines and patch v0.2.X's five emoji (`greetings`, `wellPlayed`, `oops`, `sob`, `yawn`, `laugh`, `angry`, `wahWah`), which has a dealt hand's shape and holds only ids every server relays. The grey-out, the wait, the lifetime and the mute menu are [[R643]]'s. The deck builder's portrait picker previews the whole pool in the previewed portrait's voice, every emote a game may deal ([[R644]]).
