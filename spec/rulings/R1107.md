M #58 and #59 grant the Unit they summon a Death ability (`meditative-058#bookDeath`,
`meditative-059#fusedBookDeath`). The grant is a hook the granting card registers beside its faces
(`Script.grants`, keyed `<defId>#<key>`); the instance carries only the name, the face and the
numbers as plain data (`CardInstance.grants`). A closure never enters state, so state stays JSON
and replays exactly, and a resume rebuilds the same Death list from the snapshot alone: the card's
own Death first, then each granted Death in the order granted, each running with its JSON staged
under `__grant`. A grant behaves like a granted keyword (§10.4): it shows on the Unit, survives a
copy ([[R57]]) and a Vanilla, and is lost when the card leaves the field ([[R78]]). It fires for the
controller at the death — a stolen unit's controller gets the card. A fused card merges its
ingredients' grant maps and unites the grants they carry ([[R102]]).
