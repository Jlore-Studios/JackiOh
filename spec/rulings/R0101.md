The tributed set must meet its X — printed, or as a Degrade or Upgrade left it ([[R386]]) — and be minimal — no unit could be dropped from it and still pay — because the Sheep Token counts 2, or 3 Radiant ([[§3.2]], [[§7]]) makes overshooting unavoidable while wasted sacrifices are not what "sacrifice X" asks for. Unlike a target, a Tribute the board cannot pay makes the play illegal rather than fizzling on resolution (message `<name> needs Tribute N`), and only a card that says so may tribute the opponent's units
