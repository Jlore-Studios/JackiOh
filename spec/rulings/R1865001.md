Stinkyscape #112 Job Application's "Hear back in 2 to 6 turns" is the card's own evaluation of the resume sent in ([[R1854004]] leaves the number to the card). The designer asked for "some evaluation algorithm based on the card uploaded" with "a little bit of RNG", and for an iKwzki upload that always hears back in 6.

- **Merit.** A resume's merit is its rarity's rank, Common 0, Rare 1, Epic 2, Legendary 3 and Mythic 4 (a Token's, which is none of them, 0), plus its cost as it stands, 0 to `MAX_MANA`, plus 1 on its Radiant face.
- **The tables.** A merit below 3 is a weak resume, from 3 a solid one and from 6 a strong one. Each table weighs the waits 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, in that order: weak 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; solid 2, 3, 4, 3, 2; strong 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. The wait is one draw of the match rng over the weights' sum, made as the Activate resolves.
- **An iKwzki** (S #110) always waits 6, and nothing is drawn.
- **Lucky.** The wait is a luck-based roll whose better side is the shorter wait ([[§6.1]]): the card's Lucky, printed (the Radiant face's 1) or given ([[R1438]]), and its controller's Luck ([[R987]]) each draw it again, and the shortest is kept.
- **No resume.** A stage completed by another card's "complete the current stage" ([[R1854001]]) has nothing sent in: its wait is drawn on the weak table, and the Interview's first goal asks for a play that costs (0), the goal's own cost while no resume's is noted ([[R1854002]]).

Rejected: a flat draw from 2 to 6, which evaluates nothing; reading the resume's stats, which a Spell or a Field Spell has none of, where its rarity and its cost price every card.
