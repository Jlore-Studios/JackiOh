The owner's pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #3000, its first part issue #3038): Starship becomes a tag, and every Starship has Flying and Ranged.

**The tag.** `Tag::Starship` follows Faction in the union, Token still last: a family tag, no tribe ([[R1424]]), whose pool takes no token unless it asks for tokens ([[R382]]). The Dormant Starship carries it, so every Starship does, Dormant or launched, whatever is fused into it, since a fusion keeps the hull's tags. No other card carries it: the Drone Battleship's layers are no Starships ([[R3038002]]). The server admits it from migration 0080.

**Flying and Ranged.** [[§10.4]]'s keyword layer gives a card whose tags hold Starship, read through the layers as `query::tags_of` reads them (printed or granted), Flying and Ranged, each listed once beside whatever else it has, while it is Dormant too. `view_for` lists them on both seats ([[§10.8]]), and the board draws their marks as it does any Unit's. The Dormant Starship's entry prints the two as well, on both faces, its text leading "Flying, Ranged" as a keyword line does, so every view that reads the catalog shows them. So a Starship in Attack Position is attacked only by Units with Flying or Ranged, and its Taunt, if a piece gave it one, binds only them ([[§6.1]] Flying, [[R783002]]); it takes no strike back from a defender without Ranged and may attack a Flying Unit ([[R783000]]). It is still in no pool and no deck ([[R1857001]]): no pool that names Flying, Ranged or the tag reaches it, as no query reaches a card only a rule makes.

**The hull is the same.** `StaticFlags.starshipHull`, `CardInstance.starship`, `dormant_starship_of`, `launched_starship_of` and `building_a_ship` do not change: Starship Pieces still fuse into it ([[R1852000]]), and every card that names "your Starship" still finds it by the hull, Dormant or launched, never by the tag ([[R1852005]]). `STARSHIP_NAMES` draws by the Starship's tags ([[R1852004]]), and Starship is no name's tag: a Starship holding Human and Starship launches as Frontier's Promise, one holding Starship alone takes a name that suits any ship.

**What read the keyword reads the tag.** Stinkyscape #115 Scornboi's "a Starship" is the tag now: its aura spares `notTags` Human and Starship and `notKeywords` Starship Piece ([[R1850002]]), so a Unit given the tag is spared, and a Drone Battleship is exiled ([[R3038002]]). The launch gives no keyword ([[R3038001]]).
