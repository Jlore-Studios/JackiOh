Per seat, not per match. [[§9.8]] asks for "a per-match rate limit" and [[R109]] names 5 actions per second, but a single shared counter lets a flooding player spend the **opponent's** budget so the victim's legitimate clicks are refused — turning an anti-abuse limit into the abuse. Each socket therefore gets [[R109]]'s allowance, the match's aggregate ceiling is twice it, and neither player can consume the other's
