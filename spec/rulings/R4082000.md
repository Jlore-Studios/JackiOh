**Why.** A deck card has a tier to balance by ([[R3022000]]). A Created card ([[R2089000]]) had only raw rates, so a token or a generated card could not be called a nerf or a buff candidate. Its figures cannot be read the way a deck card's are, for two reasons:

- **There is no drawn delta.** [[R2777000]]'s adjusted rate compares the decks that saw a card with the decks that held it and never drew it, and a shuffle chose which. Nothing chooses a Created card's seats at random. A seat that never made one is not a seat that could have.
- **Made is not even.** A seat makes a Created card only once a card it played made it. A seat that is playing its cards tends to be winning, so the seats that make a Created card win more than the average deck, whatever the card. Read against the average deck as [[R3070000]] reads the Dormant Starship, most Created cards would sit above 50% for that alone, and the tiers would flag the play that made them, not the card.

A development run of 308 AI games bore both out. It held 28 Created cards made by a card's text with at least 20 games made, and their seats won 4.0 points more than the average deck. Read against the average deck, they averaged 51.1%, with 18 of the 28 in A or B. Read against each other, they averaged 50%, with 14 in A or B. Cochran's Q was 45 on 27 degrees of freedom, so they also really differ, by about 6 points. In the same run The Coin's seats won 0.8 points fewer than the average deck and the Dormant Starship's 3.9 fewer. Neither shares that selection.

**The Created field.** A Created card is read against the other Created cards, as a deck card is read against the deck cards. The field (`created_field`) is read from every Created card of the report the catalog holds, whatever the page's filters show, with `CARD_STATS_MIN_SAMPLE` games made ([[R654]]'s floor). The cards a rule makes are left out (below). Each card gives its lift `d`, the win rate of the seats that made one, used or not ([[R2370000]]), less the average deck's (`baseline`, [[R3070000]]), and that lift's sampling variance `v = ¼ / made games`. DerSimonian and Laird's estimate, [[R3022000]]'s, then reads:

- `center`: the lift an average Created card shows. It is the play selection above, measured rather than assumed.
- `spread`: τ, how far real Created cards differ.
- `baseline`: the average deck's win rate plus `center`, the win rate of the seats that make an average Created card.

With fewer than `ADJUSTED_FIELD_MIN_CARDS` (30) such cards, the field is the deck field. Its `cards` and `center` are 0, and each Created card is read against the average deck, as the Dormant Starship always was.

**The adjusted win rate** is [[R3070000]]'s `held_posterior` of the made games, read against the Created field:

```
adjusted = 50% + τ²/(τ² + SE²) · (made win rate − Created baseline),   SE² = ¼ / made games
```

An average Created card reads 50%. A card's distance from the field is kept as far as its own games back it. Its 95% range is [[R2895000]]'s, and its tier is [[R3022000]]'s `tier_of`, with the same band, from the sample floor. A Created card's tier therefore means what a deck card's means: S for a nerf candidate, F for a buff candidate.

**Cards a rule makes** are read against the average deck (`made_by_the_rules`) and left out of the field. These are The Coin, which the rules deal to every second seat before either seat has played a card ([[R244]]), and the cards only a rule makes, Glitch and the Dormant Starship ([[R1857001]]). A rule makes one whenever its moment comes, so no play of the seat's chooses the seats that made one. The Coin's tier says whether the second seat, with its Coin, wins as the average deck does. The Dormant Starship's Created row reads exactly as its row in the deck table ([[R3070000]]).

**Where it shows.**

- **`GET /api/stats/cards`**: each row of `created` adds `adjustedWinRate`, `adjustedLow`, `adjustedHigh` and `tier`. The tier is null below the floor. `tiers.created` gives the Created field's `center`, `spread`, `cards` and `baseline`.
- **`/stats`' Tokens and Other created cards tables** show Tier and Adjusted after Games, as the deck table does. Below the floor, both read "—". Tier sorts S first and orders by Adjusted within a tier. Each table now opens on Adjusted, best first, as the deck table opens on its own headline rate (amending [[R3324002]]'s Win rate). Each section's line says what Tier and Adjusted are read against: the Created cards, what their seats win against the average deck, and how far they differ. It also says that The Coin and the cards only a rule makes are read against the average deck, or, when too few Created cards were read, that every card is.

Amended by [[R4155000]]: both tables show the deck table's columns — Card, Name, Games, Tier, Adjusted, Win rate — honouring the Columns toggle, and a row opens the card's drill-down. Each section's line is gone.
- **The card detail's Statistics panel** ([[R3185000]]) shows a card no deck holds, a token, by its Created figures. Games and Win rate are the seats that made one. Play rate is how many of them used one, as [[R3950000]] reads the Dormant Starship's. Tier and Adjusted are the server's. A card a deck holds keeps its deck figures.

**What it still measures.** A Created card's seats are the seats that played what makes it. A token's rate therefore carries its maker's play as well as the token, as the Dormant Starship's carries its pieces ([[R3070000]]). When a card always makes the same token, no figure can tell the two apart. The field takes out the selection every Created card shares, but not the part of it one card has more of than the rest. Each card's figures are still R2089000's, read off the same totals, so there is no new tally and no migration.

**Rejected.**

- **Read against the average deck** ([[R3070000]]'s reading) for every Created card. It reads the shared play selection as strength.
- **Used against not used** ([[R2089000]]'s Created delta) as the lift. A summoned token is used as it is made, so it has no unused side. A card left unused is often one its seat died holding, which is [[R377]]'s played-delta bias.
- **The maker's seats as the baseline.** It needs the generator of each Created card in the record, which the record does not keep ([[R1956001]]'s is presentation only). A card that always makes one token would also read exactly even.
- **Two fields, one for the Tokens and one for the Other created cards.** Each would hold half the cards for the same selection. The two sections are presentation ([[R3324002]]).
- **Leaving The Coin in the field.** Its seats are chosen by seat order, not by a play, so the field's centre would mark it down by a selection it never had.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.

Amended by [[R4083000]]: Tier and Adjusted are the Created tables' only figures, Tier ordering a tier's cards by tier fit before Adjusted; a card no deck holds shows its Games, Tier and Adjusted win rate alone in its Statistics panel, with no Win rate or Play rate; and each `created` row sends only its games and these figures, `tiers.created` staying.

Amended by [[R4673000]]: a Created card is also read on its Normal and Radiant faces, the seats that used a copy on each, against that face's Created field (`created_face_field`); the cards a rule makes are read against the average deck on every face.
