Stinkyscape #40 Poison Capsule's "Cry: Place {counters|Plague Counter|Plague Counters}" names no
card, so [[R689]]'s single prompt places them: the counters all go on the one permanent that one
prompt names, either side, face-down cards included, this card a choice among the rest. On another
permanent they do nothing for the Capsule. It takes R1860001 of issue #1860's block.
