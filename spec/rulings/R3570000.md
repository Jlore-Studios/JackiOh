S+ #142's ten entries are rolled, announced and resolved by Core #95's subsystem ([[R28]], [[R87]],
[[R423]], [[R436]]): one on the base face, three different ones in list order on the Radiant face. Each
label is its clause at the printed numbers ("Add 3 random Paperclip cards to your hand, they cost (0),
replenish 2 mana"), so a tuned `param` does not change the label `chaosRolled` names. The tag pools
(Paperclip, Swarm, Jlockeed, Terminus, Weapon) name every set, so the held set's cards reach them
([[R1420]]), as S #150's quest pool does; the Terminus pool takes tokens ([[R382]]). The pools of the two
Units and of the recursion name no set, so they draw the sets that ship ([[R380]]). "Refresh" reads
Replenish: each is `refresh_mana` ([[R364]]), applied once the cards are added, whatever a full hand
burned. "All enemies" is every enemy Unit and the enemy hero, Toxic before Burn ([[R3540000]]). The
Paperclip, Swarm and Toxopid cards arrive at (0) (`costOverride`); the Terminus cards keep their prices.

Rejected: tag pools of the sets that ship alone, which would leave the held set's Paperclip, Mindustrial
and Weapon cards out of the card until the release.
