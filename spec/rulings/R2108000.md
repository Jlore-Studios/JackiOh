A menu panel that waits on a read no longer flashes its wait: the skeleton ([[R2115000]]) and the shell's waiting panel stay hidden until the wait outlasts a moment, and an empty panel draws one empty state in the tavern's look.

**No flash.** The skeleton's blocks ([[R2115000]]) and the shell's waiting panel stay hidden until `SKELETON_DELAY_MS` (300 ms). Their room is kept, and the hidden status line is there at once. It is a timer because Reduce motion ([[R200]]) zeroes CSS delays. A kept answer shows no wait at all ([[R2125000]]).

**The tavern's look.** Faint brass blocks with a hairline and a gold sheen, still under Reduce motion. The shell's waiting panel, as every shell panel, stands on the tavern's wood under the stacked JackiOh logo (`public/brand`), whose name is the screen's heading. The wood and the logo appear with the panel, so a wait too short to show flashes neither.

**One empty state.** A brass tankard (`aria-hidden`) beside the words, in a dashed brass frame, never moving.

**The next step.** The wallet offers the Shop for unopened packs, the workshop offers Make a deck, Play offers Decks for a missing deck or trio, and Replays offers Practice for a missing practice game and Play for a missing online one. They show only while the list is empty, so [[R1646000]]'s standing links are unchanged. None is offered where the way on already sits beside the list (the add-friend form, the shop's buttons).

**Where.** The wallet, the quests, the card packs, the cosmetic packs, the workshop, Play, Replays, Play together, the patch notes and the statistics, and Replays' three waits.

**What stays text.** Dialogs, popovers, overlays and `<select>` options; the boards; the ladder's rows; the account's record line; the statistics' drill-down, token and shard lines; the rail's hint; the deck builder's pool; a card's patch history.

**One screen.** It holds [[R1581000]].

Hearthstone semantics: a menu keeps its frame while it loads, and an empty shelf says so in the game's own look.

Rejected: a CSS delay, a minimum time on screen, a mark per screen, and a next step on every empty state.
