[[R2455000]]'s one bar of 50 families made a deliberate balance pass a micro. Meditative balance changes IV (v0.5.4) reprints 19 families (cost, attack, health, keywords and numbers, the text of 22), and its cards' stats move on purpose. An engine change that only reaches cards, such as the 43 families of a Buff that may give a Field Spell Immutable, changes no printed card. So the classifier ([[R1365000]]) now counts the two apart.

- **Reprinted.** A family is reprinted when it plays differently and its catalog entry changed: a field that plays, or its wording beside a change to its play (a rework rewrites its text and its script). `PATCH_MIN_REPRINTS` (15) or more reprinted families make a patch. Wording alone still reprints nothing and is a micro however many cards it touches.
- **Reached.** `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` (50) or more families that play differently by any route, the engine's included, make a patch, as [[R2455000]] has it.
- **A rule every game meets** still makes a patch, as [[R2455000]] has it.
- **Micro.** Anything smaller.

`patches ship` reads the same rule from the two snapshots and the fragment's `plays` ([[R1366000]]), so a fragment needs no new field, and `classify` names the reprinted families (`reprinted` in its JSON). Stage 2 still stops at `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` families or a rule. On the v0.5 line, v0.5.4 stays a patch, and v0.5.1 (1 reprinted of 24), v0.5.6 (0 of 20) and v0.5.8 (2 of 9) would be micros but for their rule claims, which the planned retcon measures.

Rejected: one bar for both, which cannot keep v0.5.4 a patch and v0.5.10c (0 reprinted of 43) a micro; and counting catalog ids rather than families, which would count a card's tokens apart from it.
