The `improve` lane's gate (`cargo jackioh promote --lane improve`, `training/README.md`) plays 400 games against the parent, the AI on `main`: 200 deals, each played twice with the seats swapped (`TRAINING_IMPROVE`: `games` 400, `paired`, `vs_parent` 220). Deal `d`'s seed is `improve:<tree>:<d>`, the lane's hold-out deals ([[R850001]]). Game `2d − 1` seats the candidate on p1 and game `2d` seats it on p2, both on deal `d`'s seed, so the two games deal the same decks (each seat's deck is built from the seat's seed and its AI's shadow ban, [[R186]], one list in this lane), the same shuffles and the same seat to go first; only who sits where changes. It promotes on 220 wins of the 400. A draw is not a win, nor is a game without a result ([[R732001]]). CI's `promote --verify` plays the same games again.

An equal successor wins about 200 of 400. Were the games independent, the standard deviation would be 10 games and 220 two of them out: such a successor would pass 2.55% of the time, where 65 of 100, the bar before, let 0.18% through. Pairing makes the games less than independent in the gate's favour: a deal that favours a seat or a hand tends to give the candidate one of its games and the parent the other, so the deal's luck cancels and an equal successor passes less often than 2.55%, and one AI against itself plays both games of a deal alike and wins exactly 200. A successor whose true win rate is 52%, 55%, 57%, 58% or 60% passes about 12%, 52%, 80%, 90% or 98% of the time on independent games (more with pairing's cancelled luck), where 65 of 100 passed those successors 0.6%, 2.7%, 6.4%, 9.3% and 18% of the time. The bar is set for the gains the lane's sessions measured, a few points at a time, which the old one let through almost never: a gain of five points banks about half the time, one of eight nearly always, and each promotion is the next session's parent, so gains compound.

The gate's report and the latest pass (`last-pass.md`) add the paired edge: the mean over deals of the candidate's wins less the parent's, over the deal's two games, as points of the games, with its 95% interval (the mean plus or minus 1.96 standard errors of the deals' spread). The edge is information; the gate's rule is the 220 wins. `cargo jackioh arena --paired` and `--set` play and report a series the same way.

The `unban` lane's gate is unchanged: 60 of 100 games, each on its own seed, together with strictly fewer shadow bans than the parent ([[R732001]]). The owner asked for the lower, paired bar in issue #850, the change of the gate that #808 left to a person.
