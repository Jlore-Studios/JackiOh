---
id: R150
title: Where a stat floor lives in a summing read
cards: "#92, R39, R116, R132"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/rulings_c.rs
---
A read that sums other units' stats must not floor each contributor at 0 before adding, or a negative buff contributes nothing instead of pulling the total down, which is what [[R132]] requires. The layer-4 reading has no per-unit floor, so the summing read must not invent one; a floor belongs where the value is finally used — on the combined total for [[R132]], and at the point of display for a card's own stats
