Felinors vs Humans #46 summons two Units and has its player choose one in secret to gain Fragile, while the opponent sees Fragile on both. Nothing in the engine showed a seat a keyword a card lacks: Hidden Nerfs ([[R1845000]]) only hide.

**The grant.** `grant_keyword_secretly { target, decoys, keyword }` grants `keyword` to the card `target` names, as `grant_keyword` does ([[§6.3]]; another card's grant passes an Immutable card by, [[R5113000]]), and reports nothing: a `keywordGranted` naming the target would tell the other seat which card is real ([[R97]]), so, as a hidden Nerf, it has no event. Then the target and every decoy are marked with it (`CardInstance.shownToOpponent`). A target that names no card fizzles the whole verb. The choice of the target is the card's own prompt, made before the verb runs, so the verb opens none.

**The views** ([[§10.8]]). The seat that controls a marked card sees its keywords as they are: the true target with the keyword, each decoy without it. The other seat sees, on each marked Unit, its keywords with each marked keyword it lacks added after them, so the target and every decoy read alike. The AI's copy of the state for a seat ([[R185]]), the dry run's ([[R222]]) and the combat forecast ([[R3100000]]) believe what that seat is shown: each marked card that seat does not control is given the marked keywords, and no copy keeps the marks, so no seat's decision hangs on which card is real.

**How long.** A mark is a status of the card's stay on the field: [[R78]]'s reset takes it off as the card leaves. The truth shows by what follows from it: a decoy that survives what Fragile would have killed, or the target's `destroyed`, which names Fragile as its cause.
