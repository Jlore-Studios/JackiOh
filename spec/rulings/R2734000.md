Hearthstone shows a revealed Secret to both players. Through issue #2734 [[R154]] keyed a `trapFired` event's `instanceId` and `defId` to the trap's controller ([[R97]]'s one exception, which [[R763]] narrowed to the zone rule for the controller), so the other seat read the sentinel wherever the trap went: its reveal showcase (issue #1493) and its flip played a card back, and the card was named a moment later by the graveyard it lay in, which [[R97]] already conceded hid nothing.

`trapFired` now follows [[R97]]'s zone rule alone, the same on both seats: it names the trap to each player who may read it where it is now. That is both players while it lies in a graveyard or an exile pile, or stands face-up on the field (a Field Trap, or a Trap whose firing a prompt pauses, [[R113]]); neither while it is in a library ([[R763]]'s Counterspell Trap shuffled back into its owner's deck); and only that hand's player while it is in a hand. `row`, `lane` and `controller` travel as before, and so does `again`: a face-up Field Trap firing again ([[R2657001]]) reveals nothing on either seat and is named to both, as the card already stands face-up before them.

No view names a trap face-down by it. A trap is face-up from the moment it fires (`traps::fire_trap`, `replacements::fire`, [[R33]]), and a trap set face-down again takes a fresh id ([[R227]]), by which `view_for` judges the old one, so its `trapFired` reads as the sentinel to the other seat once more. [[R2652000]]'s flip, which reaches the other seat only when the trap's own `trapFired` follows in the same action, already named the card as the card itself is named, and is unchanged.

Sound follows the view ([[R203]]), so the other seat now hears a fired Trap's sting and its cast line wherever the view names it, and a Legendary or Mythic Trap's first firing opens its intro on both seats ([[R1350]]), as Hearthstone plays a Secret's reveal to both players. **Rejected:** keeping the line and the intro on the controller's seat with a check of the event's `controller` in the client, which would make the audio say less than the screen shows for no hidden fact, and keying the identity to the seat for a Field Trap firing again, which the other seat already reads on the field.
