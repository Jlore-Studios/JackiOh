[[R154]] keys a `trapFired` event's identity to the seat, since firing moves a trap to a public pile: the other player always reads the sentinel. This row edits [[R154]] and [[R97]]'s `trapFired` exception: to its controller the event follows [[R97]]'s zone rule like every other event naming the card, so it shows the sentinel once the card is somewhere the controller may not read: a library (C #17 Counterspell Trap fired, went to the graveyard and was shuffled back into its owner's deck, and its controller's view still named that library card's instance id, which [[§10.8]] and [[R310]] never send) or the other player's hand.
