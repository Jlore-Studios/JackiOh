The homescreen's hand ([[R374]]) swaps on every device, not only once it has logged `ROTATION_MIN_GAMES` games ([[R639]]): every `ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS` (7 s) one slot swaps, left to right, for a card of the same rarity the hand is not showing, drawn from the pool the hand is dealt from — [[R374]]'s non-token Core cards, every one equally likely, below the threshold, and [[R639]]'s every shipped set, weighted, at it; a device that reaches the threshold mid-visit swaps from the larger pool from then on, and its hand is not dealt again. A swap is a crossfade of `ROTATION_SWAP_MS` (1.2 s) in the slot: the card going out stays over the new one as a ghost that fizzles away (it greys, brightens and swells as it fades, the board's refused-card fizzle of [[R318]]) while the new card fades in out of the same smoke and then floats with the others, never dealt again. Only the new card is a control: the ghost is hidden from assistive tech and takes no pointer. [[R639]]'s holds stand for every device: no swap under reduced motion, under a pointer or focus on the hand, with a card open, or while the page is hidden. The draws come from the page's injected random source, like [[R374]]'s deal and [[R639]]'s weighting. Presentation and bookkeeping only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
