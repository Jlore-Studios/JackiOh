A dry run, the scorer's copy of a play that Zephyrs' Discover, M #71 Pareto Optimality's judge ([[R1125]]) and the autopilot's tries ([[R1183003]]) read, makes at most `DRY_RUN_SETTLE_PASSES` passes of the resolution loop in all, every loop nested in it counted together (`LIBRARY_CAP` times `CAST_ON_DRAW_CHAIN_CAP`, [[R58]]'s bound on the triggers of one play). Once they are spent it stops where it stands and is read as it stands. A pair of cards that feeds itself never settles: M #12 Fear Mongerer's Cry triggers End of turn effects ([[R824]]), and C+ #37 Wardrum's End of turn casts a copy of a non-Unit its owner played, so a fused Spell with the one's Cry and the other's text on the board casts itself again for ever. The play itself ends at the loop's cap with a panic, [[§10.3]]'s "a board that never settles is a bug, not a draw", but a dry run only guesses at a play, so the judge that tried it neither hangs nor ends the game for it. The play it was a guess at is judged and resolved as before, and a dry run that settles within its passes is read exactly as before.
