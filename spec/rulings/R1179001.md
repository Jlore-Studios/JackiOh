Meditative+ #10 Medina Grifter ("Cry: Steal a random card from your opponent's hand") takes `cards` distinct random cards of the opponent's hand ([[R60]]), which become the stealer's ([[R12]], [[§6.3]] Steal). The opponent's hand is hidden, so the opponent sees only that a card left ([[R97]]). A full hand burns the taken card into the stealer's graveyard ([[§2.4]]).

An empty hand gives nothing and draws no random number ([[R129]]). The Cry runs when the Grifter is played or cast ([[R70]]).
