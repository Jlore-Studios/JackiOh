TG #15 In a hurry's Rush Tokens wore the Created and Token marks only, so nothing on screen said they die at the end of the turn ([[R2198002]]). Each of its delayed destroys now marks the token it watches with [[R437]]'s reusable mark, `endOfTurn` in `orange` (`effects::END_OF_TURN_MARK`), in both views and announced with `marked`, as Classic #20's pending destroy marks its Units: the play is public, so the mark tells neither player anything it did not. The mark lasts exactly as long as the delayed destroy: it goes as the destroy resolves at the end of the turn, or as a token that left the field first takes its watch with it ([[R174]]). The client draws it on the card and its badge's words, "Destroyed at the end of this turn", in the inspect overlay. The play is unchanged: the same tokens die at the same point.
