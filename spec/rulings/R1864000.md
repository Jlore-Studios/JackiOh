"Cry: Allure a random enemy Acclaimed Unit" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #87) draws one pick from the match rng, uniformly ([[R60]]), as the Cry resolves rather than as the card is played, over the enemy Units Allure reaches ([[R963]]): the top of each enemy unit pile in lane order, then the enemy Units standing in the backrow ([[R1081000]]). A Unit a carrier holds ([[R446]]), a card buried under a Stack pile ([[R13]]) and a Dormant card ([[R1839000]]) are never in it, since no Allure takes them. A candidate is one whose tags, granted ones included, hold Acclaimed, read as every instance tag is (`tags_of`). The pick is Allured as K-Pop Fanatic's and Jade Beauty's targets are ([[R1045000]]): the engine's delayed steal at the start of Siva 1's controller's next turn, into the same row or destroyed with no open zone, aimed at the Unit's stay on the field and carrying the public pink `allure` mark while it waits ([[R437]]). With no candidate nothing happens and nothing is drawn ([[R129]]). This is Hearthstone's "a random enemy minion": picked among those that qualify as the effect resolves, nothing with none. Rejected: picking among every acting enemy Unit, carried ones included, which Allure could then not steal; and a pick declared at play time ([[R1]]'s Cry is no target the player chooses).
