Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. A signature permanent plays its arrival, its text's beat and a standing aura of its own on its tile ([[R3399000]]), worn on the effects table's grammar ([[R2787000]], [[R3332000]]) as [[R3400000]] and [[R3389000]] widen it:

- **An aura beside a `{ bespoke }` entry.** A `{ bespoke: "<recipe>" }` entry carries `aura: { look, palette?, scope }` beside its recipe and its frame, as a hooks entry does ([[R3400000]]); a `{ setPiece }` entry still holds nothing but its key and frame. `entryAura` reads either kind's, and every reader of an entry's aura (the aura layer, the tile auras of [[R3342000]], the coverage and budget tests) reads it there.
- **A Unit's aura stands on its own tile.** Each of Classic+'s thirteen signature Units wears a `tile` aura ([[R3400000]]), covering its own unit zone while it stands face up there, keyed `unit-<instanceId>`; a Unit's `zone` or `lane` aura fails the loader. A Unit is one by its card's type: a Field Spell animated onto the unit row keeps its aura to its backrow, as before. A Unit the seat may not read (its definition `hidden`) wears none ([[R202]]), and a modifier its own play adds wears no second copy, since the Unit's stands with the Unit, as a Field card's does. Its four signature Field Spells and its Field Trap wear theirs from their backrow zone, Anti-Waffle Shell's over its side.
- **A signature's own look.** `AURA_SIGNATURE_LOOK_NAMES` ([[R3389000]]) gains one look per Classic+ signature permanent (syrup, waffleDome, frostMist, ovenSteam, laneWall, snoreLeaves, coinGlint, staffBreath, bowNotch, warHeartbeat, appleRibbon, bannerBreeze, greatBannerShade, laserDot, stoneFaces, stairCracks, jungleLeaves, serverLights), each used by its card alone, its motion in `AURA_SIGNATURE_LOOKS` (read through `auraLookSpec`) and its alpha, tint and edge glow within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`; the shared looks stay `AURA_LOOK_NAMES`.

Such an aura stands in place of the default tile aura of [[R3342000]], as every card's own aura does since [[R3400000]], so a tile wears one aura, never two. The `/dev/fx` gallery stands a signature Unit in its entrance's zone so its tile aura shows.

Rejected: a separate tile-aura table for Units, which would split one card's animation across files; a Unit's `zone` aura standing on its own unit zone, beside [[R3400000]]'s `tile`, which already does; reading a Unit's aura from the Cry's modifier, which would draw it only while a modifier lasts and twice beside the Unit; and shared looks with palettes, which the issue's "an aura of its own" rules out.
