Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. A signature permanent plays its arrival, its text's beat and a standing aura of its own on its tile ([[R3399000]]), so the effects table's grammar ([[R2787000]], [[R3332000]]) widens in three ways:

- **An aura beside a `{ bespoke }` entry.** A `{ bespoke: "<recipe>" }` entry may carry `aura: { look, palette?, scope }` beside its recipe and its frame, as a hooks entry does; a `{ setPiece }` entry still holds nothing but its key and frame. `auraOfEntry` reads either kind's, and every reader of an entry's aura (the aura layer, the tile auras of [[R3342000]], the coverage and budget tests) reads it there.
- **A Unit's `zone` aura stands on its own unit zone.** A face-up Unit whose entry wears an aura wears it while it stands on top of its zone: a `zone` aura covers that unit zone (`row: "units"`), a `side` or `board` aura its side or the board, keyed `unit-<instanceId>`. A Unit's `lane` aura fails the loader. A Unit is one by its card's type: a Field Spell animated onto the unit row keeps its aura to its backrow, as before. A Unit the seat may not read (its definition `hidden`) wears none ([[R202]]), and a modifier its own play adds wears no second copy, since the Unit's stands with the Unit, as a Field card's does.
- **A signature's own look.** `AURA_LOOKS` gains one look per Classic+ signature permanent (syrup, ovenSteam, waffleDome, frostMist, laneWall, snoreLeaves, coinGlint, staffBreath, bowNotch, warHeartbeat, appleRibbon, bannerBreeze, greatBannerShade, laserDot, stoneFaces, jungleLeaves, serverLights, stairCracks), each used by its card alone, its alpha, tint and edge glow within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`; the eight shared looks stay `AURA_SHARED_LOOK_NAMES`.

Since this ruling, a card whose entry wears an aura beside a bespoke recipe stands that aura in place of the default tile aura of [[R3342000]], as one beside hooks already did, so a tile wears one aura, never two. The `/dev/fx` gallery stands a signature Unit in its entrance's zone so its tile aura shows.

Rejected: a separate tile-aura table for Units, which would split one card's animation across files; reading a Unit's aura from the Cry's modifier, which would draw it only while a modifier lasts and twice beside the Unit; and shared looks with palettes, which the issue's "an aura of its own" rules out.
