[[§9.5]] keeps a shared replay readable "until the sharer withdraws it (only the sharer can)". "Share with a friend" knew only the shares made while it was open, and no route answered the shares a player had made, so after a reload it offered Share to friends who already had the replay and never offered Stop sharing again. The sharer could withdraw a share only during the visit that made it.

`GET /api/replays/:matchId/shares` answers `{ friendIds }`: the profile ids of the friends the caller shared that replay with, first shared first. A share is listed exactly while its friend may read it, by the rule the friend's own read uses ([[R768]]'s replay route and Shared with me): the match is over, the caller held a seat in it, its log is kept, and the two are still friends with neither blocking the other. Another seat holder's shares are that seat's and never listed. A caller who held no seat in a finished match of that id, the friends it was shared with among them, gets the same 404 a share of it would get, so the route tells them nothing about the match.

Opened, the control reads the friend list and this answer together and shows a friend in it as shared, with Stop sharing, on any visit. A refused read shows the server's sentence and offers no Share, since a list that does not know what is shared would offer Share for a replay that already is.

Hearthstone has no replay sharing, so this follows what the spec says the sharer may do.

Rejected: keeping the shares in the browser, which another device or a cleared tab would not see.
