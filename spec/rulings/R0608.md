Jlorious is not a ladder position but the season's placed Mythic Grape players by hidden rating, highest first, ties broken by profile id: the first `JLORIOUS_SIZE` (100) of them, shown as a numbered leaderboard position (#1–#100) instead of divisions. When fewer than 100 Mythic players qualify, every one of them is Jlorious. A player who falls out of the top 100 is Mythic Grape again, where the tier floor holds them
