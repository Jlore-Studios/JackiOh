A Resource ([[R2250000]]) pays its player Credits when they play it and when it is discarded from their hand. The Credits are its quote: the Marketplace's quote this turn for its face while the Marketplace is open ([[R1853002]]), else one draw of the match rng uniformly in its face's printed sell range, as [[R1840001]] priced a sale (`resources::sell_price`), the Radiant face's for a Radiant Resource. They are gained as `gain_market_credits` gains them ([[R1840005]], `marketCreditsChanged`). A play pays as it is made, right after `cardPlayed` and before its Cry ([[§10.5]] step 4); a cast by an effect is no play of its player's and pays nothing. A discard pays after the card reaches its graveyard (`discarded`), whatever made the discard: a discard effect, a random discard, a cost or "discard a Narcor". A Resource that leaves the hand any other way (burned, stolen, traded, shuffled, exiled) pays nothing. The owner's first pass over the Stinkyscape set (issue #2250) folded the sale into the play and the discard: the Marketplace no longer buys Resources ([[R2250002]]).
