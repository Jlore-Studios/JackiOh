The owner asked that the tutorial be remade from the Training Grounds set, with its Legendary and
Mythic cards showing up only in the last game, and chose five lessons. They follow [[R291]], which
this ruling amends:

| # | Id | The human | What the player finds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `basics` | goes first | mana, playing units, summoning sickness, attacking the hero, trading, hero health, winning |
| 2 | `spells` | goes first | spells, removal, Cry, Taunt, Armor, Divine Shield, Rush, Pierce, Defense Position |
| 3 | `traps` | goes first | lanes, neighbours, Auras, Traps, the enemy's face-down cards, Field Spells, Field Traps, tokens |
| 4 | `advanced` | goes second | the mulligan, The Coin, Radiant, tribes, Tribute, Death, tokens that last one turn |
| 5 | `finale` | goes first | Quickdraw, Legendary and Mythic cards, random effects, copying |

**The ids.** The four ids of the Core tutorial stay, so a player who won those lessons sees them won
and the fifth open ([[R294]]). The server needs no change: it keeps any slug, at most 32 ([[R320]]).

**The decks.** Every card in both decks of every lesson is a Training Grounds card.

- **The human's deck** is 20 distinct cards under `TUTORIAL_HUMAN` ([[R1705005]]).
  - Lessons 1 to 4 hold no Legendary or Mythic card.
  - Lesson 5 holds all four: TG #37 Seance, #38 Tutorial Master, #39 Call to Chaos (Training-Grounds
    Edition) and #40 Goetzmann Game Design.
  - This replaces [[R291]]'s limit of at most two.
- **The AI's deck** keeps [[R291]]'s rules: 12 distinct non-token cards, none on the shadow ban
  unless named ([[R186]]), and none Legendary or Mythic. It brings spells from lesson 2 and the
  backrow from lesson 3, and in lesson 1 it holds units with no keyword, no text but a Cry ([[R2206001]]).

**The pool hold.** Lessons 1 to 4 set `poolHold` on the human's handicap ([[R2201001]]), so no card a
lesson generates is Legendary or Mythic either. Lesson 5 sets none. Every lesson's human plays under
[[R2201000]]'s floor.

Lesson 4 alone seats the human second, for The Coin ([[R244]]). Each lesson keeps a fixed seed, chosen
so that the coach's line shows every moment ([[R2205000]]).

Hearthstone semantics: a scripted encounter deals from decks of its own choosing.

Rejected:

- New ids for the five lessons, which would have reopened lessons a returning player has already won.
- A Legendary card in an earlier lesson, which the designer ruled out.
Amended by issue #2434 ([[R2434000]], [[R2434001]]): each lesson is now about one subject, lessons 2 to 4 take the ids `keywords`, `casting` and `backrow` (the ids above reading as the lesson in their place), the AI's decks are Core cards, the pool hold is on both seats in lessons 1 to 4, and a human deck may hold copies of a vanilla unit.
