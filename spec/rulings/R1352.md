Every Legendary and Mythic card of the sets that ship (`SHIPPED_SETS`, [[R1420]]), and every token printed Legendary or Mythic among them, has an intro of its own, and no other card has one: `music-cards.json`'s `intro` names each card's track, no two cards share one, and none is a theme. The shipped sets hold 58: Core's 7 Legendaries and 5 Mythics, Classic's 10 and 1, Classic+'s 13 and 3, and 19 Classic+ tokens printed Legendary (the eight Pancakes, the five Losers, KY's Gift, Felinor Flagbearer Prime, Golden Grape, the Classic Golem and the EX-plorer Pack) or Mythic (Mythic Grape). The totality is checked over a list of sets, so the Meditative set's 22 come with the release that ships it and no card part waits on audio.

Each intro is a score in `apps/web/scripts/music/tracks.mjs`, on the toolkit every track is composed with ([[R631]]): the JackiOh motif, varied, and an answer that cadences on the tonic, two bars of 4/4 or three of 3/4, whose music runs 3 to 5 seconds (`MUSIC_INTRO_MIN_S`) and then rings out for a second whose end is faded, so the file runs at most `MUSIC_INTRO_MAX_S` (6 s). It is derived from its card: its id seeds the key, the tempo, the meter, the motif's variant and its answer; its family picks the instruments, the accompaniment and the modes (its station where `music-cards.json` gives one, else its tribe, the first of its tags the card art reads, then the Jlockeed and Acclaimed tags, else its type: pizzicato for a Felinor, chimes for KY, bright brass for a Pancake, a calliope for Call to Chaos, synths for AI, a march for Jlockeed); its set lends a glint into the cadence (Core a harp, Classic a dulcimer, Classic+ a celesta), and a Mythic adds a prismatic shimmer over a soft choir. The Mythics are hand-tuned from their themes, each in its theme's key and forces with the motif as its theme quotes it, and so are the best-known Legendaries. A card's facts are written into the score as the catalog printed them when the intro was composed, so a later catalog change moves no rendered file until it is rendered again.

`gen:music` renders the intros like every track, with FluidSynth and the FluidR3 GM SoundFont into the same AAC-LC at 44.1 kHz stereo and 80 kbps, each as a sting whose `handoff` is its music's end. An intro's loudness is measured over the file it keeps, so it sits at every other track's level, and its last 0.6 s fade to silence. The 58 take about 3.1 MB, so `MUSIC_BUDGET_BYTES` is raised from 24 MiB to 28 MiB, which leaves room for the Meditative set's 22 at the same encoding. Every rendered file is committed with its `music-manifest.json` row, and `gen:music --check` proves them.
