Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: S+ #80 The Last Voyage, "Normal version & Radiant version: launches one now & at the end of turn; Normal version: skips launching at the end of turn on the turn its played". Both faces launch a random Terminus Starship as the Spell resolves, a free launch as the Radiant face's always was ([[R3539002]]), and keep [[R4440053]]'s rest-of-game launch at the end of each of their caster's turns. The base face prints "Launch a random one now and at the end of each of your turns after this one.": its rest-of-game step ([[R458]] with `atEnd`, [[R1861002]]) carries the number of the turn it was cast on, and comes due as before at the end of that turn, where it launches nothing; it launches at every later end of its caster's turns. "The turn it is played" is the turn the Spell resolved on, whoever's it was, so a copy cast on the opponent's turn (by a card that casts it there) launches at its caster's first end of turn, since no end of its caster's turn came on the turn it was cast. The Radiant face still launches at the end of the turn it is cast on, so it stays a launch ahead of the base face. The badge is the same on both faces, "End of turn: Launch a random Terminus Starship.". Replays of v0.6.6i to v0.6.13c, whose base face launched none at once and one at the end of the turn it was cast on, fold under `script_v0_6_13c` ([[R767000]]).
