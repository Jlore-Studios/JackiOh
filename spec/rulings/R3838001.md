Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

**Why.** In the deck builder's card grid, and so on the Almanac ([[R630]]), a resting mouse opened a hover preview only on a card whose face prints less than its whole text, a full-art card or a short form (#1017), with that text beside it at reading size (the Full text panel, `Extended.tsx`). A card whose face prints its words whole opened nothing, though a grid card is small and its words, fitted to its box, can be too small to read. The owner asked in issue #3838 for a hover text box on the Almanac's cards, so people can read the text.

**The rule.** Below an `AlwaysFullText`, which the Almanac puts around its page:

- A mouse or pen resting on any grid card with words opens the hover preview, and the preview carries the card's whole text in its Full text panel beside the enlarged face, as it does for a face that prints less.
- A card with no words (its text, a Resource's Credits line, gained keywords and grants all empty, `printsText`) opens nothing new.

The deck builder's grid and every board keep #1017's rule: there the panel is only for a face that prints less.

Rejected: opening the preview on every card everywhere, which would cover the deck editor's grid as a pointer crosses it.
