A Starship with its own price launches for it, or for "launch costs (N)" when that is lower.
A `no_hand_launch` Starship has no launch by hand. A Starship whose text names a `wakesBy`
Activate offers that Activate instead of the launch.

**Its own price.** A hull the card that made it priced (`StarshipMemory.price`, S+ #155 The Far
Vagabond's (3), (0) on its Radiant face) launches for that price, or for the least "your Starship
launch costs (N)" its controller holds when that is lower ([[R1852006]]), never below 0. The
modifier is spent by the launch as any launch spends it. The label names the price it launches for
now ("Launch (3)"), which `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal and the view agree on, as for any
launch ([[R1852003]]).

**No launch by hand.** A hull that prints `no_hand_launch` (every Terminus Starship, S+ #80 The Last
Voyage's) has no launch by hand: `legalActions` holds no Activate for it, `abilities_of` is empty,
and activating it is refused as a card with no Activate ability is ([[R384]]). Its awakening by an
effect still launches it, for nothing ([[R1852006]]), since an awakening is a launch whatever
awakened it ([[R1852003]]); only the launch by hand is sealed.

**A hull whose text names its Activate.** A Dormant Starship whose text names a `wakesBy` Activate
(S+ #157 Phantom Shipyard's "shipyard") offers that ability alone, never the engine's launch: it
falls through to the Dormant rule's own filter, which keeps the named ability ([[R1839002]]).
Rejected: offering the launch beside it, which would let the shipyard launch empty, before it holds
anything.
