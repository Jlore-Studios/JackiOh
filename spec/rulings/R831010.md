Classic+ #26 Tommy Tempo's "When this enters the Field" is a Cry ([[R1]]), so it runs both when Tommy is played and when it is cast on draw ([[R70]]). Its controller loses all the mana they have left as the Cry resolves — after a play, what its price left; after a cast on draw, which pays nothing, all of it — and Tommy gains +1/+1 (Radiant +2/+2) for each mana lost. With none left nothing is lost and it stays as printed. The issue's "enters the Field" is read as the Cry rather than a new on-summon hook.
