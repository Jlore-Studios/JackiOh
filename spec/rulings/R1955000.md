[[R1579000]] made the Collection the one place a cosmetic is equipped, and a pack's reveal only
linked there. A pack opening's reveal now also equips the cosmetic it just granted, so a player
can wear it without leaving the opening. It amends [[R1579000]] (*The selects*) and [[R1655000]]
(*Where it is equipped*).

**Which slots.** Every slot granting a new cosmetic offers **Equip**: in a card pack
([[R1301002]], [[R1827000]], [[R1833000]]) once the slot is revealed, so at once under reduced
motion or skip-all; in a cosmetic pack ([[R1306000]], [[R1306001]]) at once. A portrait is a
deck's pick ([[R641]], [[R1827000]]), so it offers none, and neither does a card, bars or a
cosmetic this client does not know.

**What it writes.** The Collection's stores, with no new store and no request. A foil, finish or
border becomes the pick of the card it was granted on, and a full art that card's full-art pick
([[R1655000]]); a playmat, backdrop or card back becomes the account's pick in the `cosmetics`
group ([[R633]]). A deck's own pick still beats the account's ([[R1297000]]). While the store
holds the pick, the button reads **Equipped** and is disabled. The cosmetic pack's link to
Cosmetics stays.

**Ownership.** The client decides none ([[R1313000]], [[R1833000]]). After an opening, the open
screen and the next read `GET /api/cosmetics` again, so the cosmetic is worn once the ledger lists
it, with no reload; nothing is marked owned from the opening's answer. A pick never enters
`PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Hearthstone semantics: a deck's own card back beats the account's favourite, as a deck's pick
beats the account's here.

Rejected: an Equip for a portrait, which has no deck to go on; marking the cosmetic owned from
the opening's answer; leaving the re-read to the next screen's mount (`Gated key={path}`), which
keeps the open screen on the pre-opening answer.
