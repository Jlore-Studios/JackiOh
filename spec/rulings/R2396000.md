A card's own attack and kill triggers have three timings, one word each. **When** this attacks resolves before any damage of the attack ([[R2285000]]): Classic #13 Boots on the Ground, Classic #45 Nature Titan, Classic+ #73.1 Classic Golem, Stinkyscape #75 Badger and #76 Honey Badger, and Meditative #91.1 Windfurious Prime, whose joiners attack its target first ([[R1203]]). **Whenever** this attacks, or this destroys a Unit, resolves after the attack or the kill, once its combat's state check has run, and does not ask that the card is still on the field: #32 Prem Panther and Meditative #95.1 CN Golem. **After** this attacks, or this destroys a Unit, resolves at that same point only for a card still on the field on the stay it attacked from ([[R174]], [[R2285001]]): Classic+ #73.1 Classic Golem, Stinkyscape #91, #92 and #94, Classic+ #19.5 Bot Loser and TG #9 Ravager ([[R1245]]).

"Whenever this attacks" is the attacker's `afterAttack` hook with `afterAttackEvenIfGone`: it runs at [[§4.2]] step 5 with the same facts (`targetId`, `destroyedIds`, `forced`), for declared and forced attacks alike ([[R53]]), never for an attack called off in its trap window ([[R44]]) nor for a defending card; an attacker that died or left the field in that combat runs it as the snapshot it fought with, `survived` false, as a Death hook reads its card ([[R89]]). "Whenever this destroys a Unit" is a kill trigger with `evenIfGone`: a `destroyed` whose killer is the card ([[R42]]), Cleave, Poisonous and defending kills included, is answered by the card on the field or, when it is gone, by the snapshot it killed with, which the state check keeps for that `destroyed` and which answers after the board's own answers to it. Either answers for the player who controlled the card when it fought ([[R212]]), and either is parkable like any hook or trigger ([[R113]]). A body Reborn brings back in that check is a new arrival ([[R83]]): the attack or the kill is answered once, by the card as it fought, never again by the Reborn body, as Hearthstone owes such a trigger to the event, not to the minion that comes back.

So Prem Panther draws 2 for each Unit its attack destroyed even when it trades itself away, once when it comes back through Reborn, and nothing for a kill made defending ([[R426]]); Boots on the Ground draws or Recruits before the damage, and so though it dies in that combat; and CN Golem shuffles its CN-Virus for each Unit it kills even when it dies in that combat. Badger, Honey Badger, Windfurious Prime and Nature Titan change their word from "Whenever" to "When", and Bot Loser and Ravager from "Whenever" to "After", and play as before. This is the owner's ruling (issue #2396): it ends [[R2285000]]'s reading of "When" and "Whenever this attacks" as one timing, takes Prem Panther and Boots on the Ground out of [[R2285001]], and drops [[R1245]]'s survival clause for CN Golem. Rejected: a second hook field beside `afterAttack`, which would leave two owed hooks to order in each combat, and one flag on the whole script for the kill trigger, which a fusion would carry to an ingredient's "After this destroys a Unit".
