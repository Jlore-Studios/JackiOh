A hit the target's Armor takes whole at [[§4.4]] step 2 (Spell Damage counted, a hit that pierces never) still stops at the zero rule ([[R63]]), and since patch v0.3.X it is reported: `damageAbsorbed { sourceId, targetId, absorbed, combat }`, `absorbed` being the whole hit and the other fields `damage`'s. The zero rule stands where §4.4 puts it, after step 3 and before step 4, so an Indestructible unit whose Armor takes a hit whole reports it like any other; a hit the Armor only shrank meets Indestructible at step 4 and is stopped unreported, as before. A hit Divine Shield takes (step 1) has its own event, and a hit of 0 before step 1 is no instance at all.

It is a report and not damage, and R63's zero rule holds for every rule that reads damage: no on-damage trigger (step 6), no trap and no quest answers it ([[R404]]), no Poisonous mark (step 7), no Lifesteal heal (step 8, [[R85]]), no Trample excess (step 9) and no kill credit ([[R42]]) come of it, and every card that reads `damage` events reads none. To the rules it is a hit of 0 (`GameEvent::as_rules_read`). Since nothing answers it, it never joins the trigger loop's frontier and takes no number in [[R68]]'s order, so a game where Armor took a hit whole folds to the state it always did ([[R768]]).

Each seat's view redacts it exactly as it redacts `damage` ([[R97]]): its source and target are hidden from a viewer who may not read them, and its numbers are public.
