[[§9.5]]'s watcher could only look: after its hello the actor read nothing it sent but a later hello ([[R1103000]]), and dropped every other frame unanswered ([[R2609000]]). A watcher of a live 1v1 match or a co-op game ([[R2881000]]) now reacts from the sidelines, as a seat does, with emotes:

- **The message.** A watcher sends `{ type: "emote", emote }` on its watch socket, the very frame a seat sends ([[R643]]). Like a seat's, it is never an `ActionBody`: it never reaches `reduce`, the action log, the replay hash, a game record or the play telemetry, it is never minted as an `Emote` action, so a card that hears emotes never hears it ([[R1127]]), and a ladder bot's persona is never offered it, so no trigger or reply of [[R645]]'s fires on one. An emote frame whose `emote` is no pool id is answered `malformed`, as on `/ws/match`, and counts against the watcher's malformed budget ([[R2609000]]); one of the pool the watcher's hand does not hold, a voice line among them, is dropped silently ([[R2990001]]), and so is one past the watcher's limit ([[R2990002]]). Every other frame stays as unanswered as it was.
- **Who hears it.** An emote let through goes out at once, never `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` late, as `{ type: "spectatorEmote", watcher, emote }` to each person's own socket (a 1v1 match's two players, a co-op game's two people; a ladder bot's seat and a co-op game's AI seats have none) and to every other watcher of the match, whichever seat it watches, and never back to its sender, whose page shows its own at once. A block between the watcher and one of them keeps it from that one alone ([[R2990003]]). `watcher` is the watcher's username as every friends list shows it, `name#tag` ([[R1436]]), never an account id.
- **The accepted cost.** A watcher sees only its friend's seat, `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` late, so an emote can carry to the opponent nothing the watcher could not already tell them outside the game, and less: one of at most `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_HAND_SIZE` emoji, under a limit stricter than a seat's, about a board already five seconds old. That residue is accepted as the price of the feature, as [[§9.8]]'s ghosting row accepts a friend watching at all.

Rejected: delaying the relay by `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS`, which would make a reaction to the board arrive after the moment the players reacted to and hide nothing the delayed view does not already hide; naming the watcher by an account id, which no page may see; letting a watcher send a voice line, which speaks in a portrait's voice and would sound like a player; and a frame of a new type, which would need a second parser where the seat's already says what an emote is.
