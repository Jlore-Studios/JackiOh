R4589005 (IA #106 Checkpoint Daemon): its end of turn remembers its controller's hero Health, and
each end of turn overwrites the last, so the Death restores the snapshot from the latest one.
With no snapshot the Death sets nothing, though the Radiant face still gains its Armor. Setting
Health may lower it, as `set_health` does.
