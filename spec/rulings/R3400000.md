Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. The frame the signature tier ([[R3332000]]) gives an effects table entry grows four ways, so Meditative+'s and Training Grounds' signatures can be built ([[R3400001]]):

- **A bespoke entry may wear an aura.** `{ bespoke, aura }` is parsed as a hooks entry's aura is (`parseAura`), beside the frame; the aura layer reads it as it reads a hooks entry's ([[R2787000]]). Until now a bespoke entry could take none.
- **A Unit's aura may stand on its own tile.** `scope: "tile"`, the nearest scope (`AURA_SCOPES` is tile, zone, lane, side, board), is a Unit's alone: a face-up Unit the shown view lists whose entry has a tile aura wears it over its own unit zone (`unit-<instanceId>`), a face-down or hidden Unit none ([[R202]]), and a modifier its play installs wears no tile aura, which stands with the Unit. A tile aura counts toward `AURA_MAX_PER_ZONE` like any other. Three looks join the eight, each within `AURA_SIGNATURE_MAX_INTENSITY`: `flurry` (snow), `chalk` (chalk dust) and `timber` (sawdust). A card whose entry wears its own aura, a bespoke entry's among them, wears it in place of [[R3342000]]'s default tile aura.
- **Three takeover looks with art.** A Mythic may name `summit`, `canvas` or `napkin` as its own (`OWN_TAKEOVER_LOOKS`), each with its own tints (`TAKEOVER_TINTS`) and art (`TAKEOVER_ART_PARTS`, `fx/takeoverArt.ts`): its parts mount over the takeover's dark and under its sweep, rays and wave, the card-shaped sweep hidden, and they move only under the live takeover, timed to its landing. Under Reduce Motion the still card ([[R2722001]]) stands over the same parts at rest, so the Mythic's own art shows still; a look with no art shows the still card alone. Every part is a shape, never a card face.
- **A signature Spell's own cast.** A signature entry of a card that lands neither as a Unit nor as a Field Spell (`ownCast`) draws its play with its recipe: the entrance draws none of the shared rays, gold or prismatic burst, ring, echo ring or shake, and a Mythic's takeover keeps only its own look, centred on the screen (its recipe draws at the board's centre, and the card its play leaves may no longer be drawn), lasting its entry and `FX_SIGNATURE_TAKEOVER_TAIL_MS` (400 ms) rather than `FX_TAKEOVER_TAIL_MS`, with no shared rings or shake, the recipe's own shake standing in. So a Mythic Spell's held cast (the 1000 ms hold and the 400 ms cast) and its takeover end at 1800 ms, inside its ceiling (`SIGNATURE_MYTHIC_MAX_MS` × `SIGNATURE_SPELL_SHARE`, 1950 ms), where the shared takeover's 2100 ms and its burst's 2140 ms did not fit.

Every new cue starts inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of its end ([[R200]]). Rejected: reusing `zone` for a Unit's own tile, which `specOf` turns into its side for a card not in the backrow; a separate table of Unit auras beside the effects tables; and raising the signature budgets to fit the shared takeover's tail.
