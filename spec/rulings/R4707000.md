**Why.** The owner balances a card whose outcome is drawn from a known list by the match's seed, so that no outcome is a dud or a game-winner by itself. Those outcomes are already told to both players, Call to Chaos's as `chaosRolled` ([[R436]]) and a coin's or a die's as `rolled` ([[R2652001]]), but the game record ([[R376]]) dropped them, and [[R4679000]] noted that Call to Chaos's effects, being no cards, have no row.

**The record.** A play's variant ([[R4672000]]) gains `rolled`, every roll the play made in the order made, absent when it made none. Each is one of three kinds, keyed so that a rewording splits nothing:

- `effect`: a table's roll, the places in the card's table of the entries rolled (`picked`, 0 first), their clauses as the announcement named them (`labels`) and the table's size (`of`). A Call to Chaos of any edition (#95, C+ #73, M #95, S #150, S #151, TG #39, S+ #142) rolls one entry, its Radiant face three different ones ([[R423]]); any other pick-N list drawn as it resolves (`doRandomEntries`, M #49.1 YileGPT Prime's two of five) rolls its N. A list done whole (YileGPT Prime's Radiant five of five) draws nothing and records nothing.
- `coin`: a `rolled` batch of coins, `coins` of them flipped at Lucky `lucky` ([[R1438]]), `heads` of them heads, the kept side after Lucky's flips, heads being a coin's better side ([[R1440]]). One batch is one roll: Gary the Gambler's (#4) six coins are one roll of "3 of 6 heads", say; Ace in the Hole's (C #65), Conjure Rush Token+'s (C+ #16), Mid Loser's (C+ #19.3) and Money Machine's (M #54) single coins each one of heads or tails.
- `die`: a `rolled` batch of dice, `sides` faces at Lucky `lucky`, the face each die kept (`kept`), and the faces the card checks for (`hit`, `from` to `to`) when its script names them: Dice of Doom (S #154) checks 67 to 67, Unreal Estate (IA #47) 1 to its `chance`. A die whose card checks no face is recorded by its faces only at `ROLL_SPLIT_MAX_SIDES` (6) sides or fewer, each face an outcome; a larger one is no outcome of a short list and is left out. No card rolls such a die yet.

**Which play a roll joins.** A roll joins the play of the card that rolled it, read off the instance id its report names, whenever it comes: a Cry's as the play resolves, a Trap's coin as it fires at the end of a later turn (C #65), a unit's later flip. It joins the play's last entry only (its `createdPlayed` entry for a Created card played from the hand, otherwise the one entry), so no roll is counted twice. A card that has no play in the record (one an effect summoned or set) records none. A Created copy's rolls are recorded under its own catalog id, so a Call to Chaos that Wildar (S #149) added and its seat played rolls as that edition.

**A Recursion's chain** ([[R28]]): a Call to Chaos cast by a Call to Chaos's "Cast a random Call to Chaos", up to `CALL_TO_CHAOS_CHAIN_CAP` casts, credits its roll to the play that began the chain: the innermost play of the card that made it still resolving, and that play's own chain's first when it is a cast itself. The roll keeps its own edition's id and table (an `effect` of C+ #73's table inside a #95 play), and the cast's own `createdUsed` entry holds none. A Call to Chaos cast by any other card (The Big Crunch, S #4.6; Chaos Accelerator, S+ #154; Jogg's Box, C+ #47) begins its own chain and records its rolls on its own entry, under its own id.

**How.** `chaosRolled` gains `indices` and `tableSize`, and `rolled` gains `hit`, as engine bookkeeping beside [[R4672000]]'s `modes`: `as_rules_read` drops `indices` and `tableSize`, so no state hash moves (the trigger loop never holds a `rolled`), no view forwards any of the three, and the golden traces' event text leaves them out. A card that checks its dice reports them with `report_die_hits`.

**Left out**, and why:

- Rolls no event announces, which the record cannot read: B-Patch (S+ #20) and CN Peptides (M #36), which flip a coin without a `rolled` report; The Syndicate's (S #164) printed table; a drawn count or amount in a range (Book of Knowledge, C #24; Book of Tokens, C+ #53; Circadian Halfcycler, S #46; Fortify Flesh, S #145; Catboy Maid SSR+, M #102; Paycheck, S #112.1, and Job Application, S #112); and a single printed chance with no roll reported (Reoccurring Dream, #23 and TG #49). Once any of them announces its roll, it joins this record.
- Glitch (Glitch in the System's, C #18), which a rule makes ([[R4082000]]) and two of whose four outcomes restart or void the game the record is of.
- A card made at random, which [[R4679000]] already reads as an output, and board randomness: a random target, lane, discard or draw, a Flak or Scatter roll per target, an Anomaly's board.

Rejected: recording a clause rather than its place, which a rewording would split; a field of its own beside `playedVariants`, which a cast and a Created card's entries would need again; crediting a Recursion's cast with its own rolls, which reads a chain as two plays of two cards and leaves the play that began it with "Cast a random Call to Chaos" alone; and a die's raw face for every die, a hundred rows for Dice of Doom where its card asks one question.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
