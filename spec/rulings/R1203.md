On each attack Windfurious Prime makes, declared or forced, before its own combat: two different Units drawn uniformly from the Unit cards in its controller's hand and deck together ([[R60]]; unit tokens in the deck are in the pool), each summoned into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]], no Cry), a deck card seen first as it arrives ([[R97]]); each joiner, in lane order, attacks the Prime's target as a forced attack ([[R53]], each its own combat and state check, the run stopping once the target is gone); then the Prime's own combat. Joiners stay on the field. A declared attack whose target has left the field before its combat is cancelled (as [[R44]] cancels; the exertion stays spent, so Windfury's second attack is still there, and `attackCancelled` is emitted only on this path); fewer joiners arrive when zones or Units run out, and with no pool or no open zone nothing is drawn. A joiner that is itself a Windfurious Prime brings its own, bounded by the five zones.
