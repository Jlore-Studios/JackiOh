"A Starship Piece" on T-AI-3 Route Optimizer and T-AI-10 Salvage Protocol is [[R1878000]]'s pool, `query::starship_pieces()`: every non-token card that prints Starship Piece on its base face, named by its set so that it reaches the pieces while the set is held (ST-S21-3). A token that prints the keyword (Stinkyscape Golem, Salvage Protocol itself) is never one, and neither is the Dormant Starship.

Route Optimizer's Discover ([[§6.3]]) offers three different pieces to its caster alone; the pick is added to their hand with a `costMod` of −1, which travels with the card ([[R65]], [[R78]]) and never takes its cost below 0; a full hand burns it. Its Radiant face adds the pick Radiant ([[§5.2]]). Salvage Protocol's Death adds one piece drawn from the same pool, Radiant on its Radiant face.
