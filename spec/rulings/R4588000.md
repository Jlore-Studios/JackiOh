IA #83 Swarm Logic's "Your Units have +{attack} Attack and Rush this turn" is two `thisTurn` lends over its caster's Units: `giveUnitsAttackThisTurn` of the declared `attack` beside the auras' grants ([[§10.4]] layer 5), and `giveUnitsKeywordThisTurn` of Rush beside their keywords. Each reaches every Unit its caster controls this turn, those that arrive later too, with [[R1859007]]'s gates — never a Vanilla, Dormant or buried one, and never, when a Spell gave it, one Immune to Spells ([[R4508000]]) — and both end at cleanup. An aura that sets Attack still wins over the lend.

Why: the text has no "Give", which would buff the Units there now once; "have … this turn" is the War Doctrines' lend ([[R1859007]]) on Stinkyscape #4.4 Last Word's turn ([[R1870004]]), for this turn rather than the next. Rejected: a one-shot buff on the Units there now, which leaves a Unit played after it at its printed Attack.
