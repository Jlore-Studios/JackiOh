Since balance patch 1, a summon aimed at a specific zone fails when that zone is occupied or reserved ([[R64]]); a Locked zone takes it ([[R688]]) unless the card's own text says otherwise — Classic+ #19 League of Losers prints that it skips an occupied or Locked zone, while #67 Zoomerbin Oomen, which says nothing of Locks, summons into a Locked zone and fizzles only on an occupied one. A dying Reborn unit's zone is reserved for it ([[R64]]), and the return is no play, so it lands there Locked meanwhile or not
