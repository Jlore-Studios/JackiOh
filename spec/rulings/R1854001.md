Stinkyscape #6 Tylannt's Cry reads "Complete the current stage on all your Quests", and its Radiant face "Summon The Greatest Heist. Complete the current stage on all your Quests". The issue says it reaches In Too Deep's quests as well as the staged ones, which is why In Too Deep carries the Quest tag ([[R1837000]]). Hearthstone has no card that completes a quest outright; this follows its own rule for a quest's completion, which is the reward's moment, nothing earlier.

- **Your Quests.** `complete_quests`: every card with the Quest tag acting on its controller's side of the field (face-up, topping its zone, not Dormant), in lane order, whose text holds a quest — a staged one ([[R1854000]]) or a tree such as In Too Deep's ([[R404]]). The opponent's Quests and an untagged card with a quest line are passed by.
- **The current stage.** Every quest open on the card is marked completed: a staged quest's current stage, In Too Deep's one open quest on its base face, and each of the quests its Radiant face has open at once. Each completion is noticed at the next state check as any completion is, and its reward follows then: In Too Deep's base face asks for its reward as ever, its Radiant face grants every reward ([[R543]]), a staged quest's card gives its stage's reward and moves on. A quest those rewards open was not open when the effect ran and is not completed.
- **A quest card with no line yet.** A card summoned by the same effect list, before any event or state check has opened its line (Tylannt's Radiant face), opens its first quest or stage first, as it would on its own, and then completes it.
- **Nothing open.** A line waiting to hear back ([[R1854004]]) has no stage open, and a finished line none left, so both are untouched.
- **A goal only an effect meets.** `byEffect` goals (Job Application's "Send a card from your hand in as your resume", "Activate to claim") count no event and read no board; they are completed by "complete the current stage" or by the card's own `complete_this_quest`, its Activate's last effect, which works whatever the card's tags.
- In Too Deep plays exactly as before: no shipped card completes a quest.
