[[§4.4]] step 8 reads Lifesteal off the source's keywords, but an effect whose own text says its damage has Lifesteal heals its controller's hero all the same, without granting the source the keyword. The heal is the amount actually dealt, so Armor, the Anti-oneshot cap and [[R63]]'s zero rule apply to it exactly as they do to the hit
