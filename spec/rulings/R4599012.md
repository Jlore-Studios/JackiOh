S+ #94 PIP gives a Unit some turns to get a kill or be destroyed ([[R3550006]]): an engine delayed effect, marked `deadline` on the Unit, which comes due at a turn's end with no marker of its own. In the daily fuzz's handicapped seed 10624 a deadline set on C #27 Pestilent Slime came due just after Core #49 Snom Bunny Mind Control's hand hook at that turn's end; the Slime had been made Immune to Spells since, and I21 named its destroy as Snom Bunny's.
**The destroy is the deadline's.** A delayed effect is made when its card resolves and runs as it was made, with no marker of the card that made it; I21 judges none (its walk sees no Spell's run there), and the card whose hook ran just before had no part in it. Whether a delayed effect a Spell made should pass by a Unit that became immune after it was made is not decided here: the engine runs it as Hearthstone runs an effect already on a minion, and this ruling changes no play.
**What I21 allows.** A `destroyed` of an immune Unit right after its `deadline` mark is taken off. Another mark's removal, or none, leaves a destroy in a Spell's run a reach.
Rejected: naming the deadline's maker as the running card, which the walk cannot read off the events.
