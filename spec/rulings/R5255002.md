Felinors vs Humans #57.2 Tackling reads "Look at the top three cards of your deck. Add one of them to your hand and discard the others" (Radiant: "and make it Radiant"). Hearthstone's Tracking, whose text it echoes, draws the card it keeps; Tackling's text adds it instead.

**The rule.** The caster alone is offered the top three cards of their library in library order, no rng drawn ([[R4586005]]); a library of fewer offers what it holds, an empty one asks nothing. The pick moves to the caster's hand as an added card, not a draw: no `drawn`, no fatigue, no cast on draw, and a full hand burns it ([[§2.4]]). On the Radiant face it is made Radiant first, so it stays Radiant wherever it lands ([[R74]]). The cards offered and not picked go to the graveyard by `discardLibrary` with their ids, in library order ([[R5238007]]): no discard from a hand, so nothing that answers a discard answers them ([[R3951001]], [[§6.3]]). The cards offered are the ones the play looked at, carried with the prompt, so the answer's step sends exactly those.
