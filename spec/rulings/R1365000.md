A patch number moves exactly when the cards do (#1360). `cargo jackioh classify --base <rev> [--head <rev>] [--parent-bin <path>]` reads a change between two commits and names its **kind**, the highest it reaches of Cards, AI, App and Internal, and for Cards its **tier** and the card families that play differently. A family is a card with its tokens (`meditative-097-6` counts as `meditative-097`).

- **Major**: the head's `SHIPPED_SETS` lists a set the base's does not ([[R1420]]). Nothing else makes a major.
- **Patch**: `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` (10) or more families play differently, or a divergence touches no card (a rule every game meets).
- **Micro**: anything smaller that changes a card, wording alone included, however many cards it touches.

No flag, title or label overrides a tier or a kind.

A card plays differently when its catalog entry changes a field other than its wording (`name`, a face's `text`, `refs`, `printedRarity`, `index`), `loc` included since cards read it, when it is added or removed, or when its script's code changes: the lines `cargo jackioh catalog loc` counts, so its tests, comments, `use` lines and layout are not code. A card of a set that has not shipped is no change. A change to the AI's decisions (`crates/ai/src` but its gate, sweep, development-run and baseline tooling, and `crates/ai/generation.json`) is AI; to the client, the server, the custom-card host, the engine's wire types and view, or the patch history is App; to anything else is Internal.

With `--parent-bin`, the base's build, and the command run from the head's own build, the base build is asked what the diff cannot say. A card claimed only through its script keeps the claim only when `FORCED_GAMES` (40) games with it forced into both decks diverge on the base build, so a refactor claims nothing. A change to code a card's play can rest on (the engine outside its wire types and view, the cards crate outside the scripts) replays `UNFORCED_GAMES` (200) unforced games on the base build: the cards each divergence touches play differently, read from the instances whose state differs between the two builds' texts at that step and else from the diverging action's cards, and a divergence that names no card makes a patch. A change to the AI plays `SELFPLAY_GAMES` (8) self-play games on both builds, and identical logs (seed, actions, final hash and result) make it Internal. The check stops once the tier is a patch. Without `--parent-bin`, script claims count unconfirmed, a change to play code counts as no card, and the output names both.

The tier was a guess when an issue opened (`v0.3.X` or `v0.3.Y`, [[R650]], [[R743]]) and `patches ship` named it from that guess ([[R646]]); later parts of #1360 name versions from this tier.
