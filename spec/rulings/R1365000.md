A patch number moves exactly when the cards do (#1360). `cargo jackioh classify --base <rev> [--head <rev>] [--parent-bin <path>]` reads a change between two commits and names its **kind**, the highest it reaches of Cards, AI, App and Internal, and for Cards its **tier** and the card families that play differently. A family is a card with its tokens (`meditative-097-6` counts as `meditative-097`); a set's own token, which no one card owns (`core-t-rush`, `stinkyscape-t-dormant-starship`), is a family of its own (issue #2271).

- **Major**: the head's `SHIPPED_SETS` lists a set the base's does not ([[R1420]]), unless the owner wraps that set into the current line (`WRAPPED_SETS`, [[R2204000]]), when it earns the tier its cards do, every card of it playing differently. Nothing else makes a major.
- **Patch**: `PATCH_MIN_REPRINTS` (15, [[R2499000]]) or more families are reprinted (their catalog entry changed and they play differently), `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` (50, [[R2455000]]) or more families play differently, or divergences that touch no card hold `RULE_MIN_GAMES` of the unforced games, a quarter of them (a rule every game meets, [[R2455000]]).
- **Micro**: anything smaller that changes a card, wording alone included, however many cards it touches, and a change to how cards look (`CARD_VISUALS`, [[R2529000]]).

No flag, title or label overrides a tier or a kind.

A card plays differently when its catalog entry changes a field other than its wording (`name`, a face's `text`, `refs`, `printedRarity`, `index`), `loc` included since cards read it, when it is added or removed, or when its script's code changes: the lines `cargo jackioh catalog loc` counts, so its tests, comments, `use` lines and layout are not code. A card of a set that has not shipped is no change. A change to the AI's decisions (`crates/ai/src` but its gate, sweep, development-run and baseline tooling, and `crates/ai/generation.json`) is AI; to the client, the server, the custom-card host, the engine's wire types and view, or the patch history is App; to anything else is Internal.

With `--parent-bin`, the base's build, and the command run from the head's own build, the base build is asked what the diff cannot say. A card claimed only through its script keeps the claim only when, in `FORCED_GAMES` (40) games with it forced into both decks, a divergence on the base build touches the card or touches no card (the games' other cards are random, so a divergence one of them explains says nothing of it); a refactor claims nothing. A change to code a card's play can rest on (the engine outside its wire types and view, the cards crate outside the scripts) replays `UNFORCED_GAMES` (200) unforced games on the base build: the cards each divergence touches play differently, and divergences that name no card make a patch once they hold `RULE_MIN_GAMES` of the games, while fewer count as no card ([[R2455000]]). A divergence touches, most direct first, the cards whose own legal actions differ, else the card the diverging action plays, activates, switches or attacks with, else the cards whose instances differ between the two builds' texts at that step. A change to the AI plays `SELFPLAY_GAMES` (8) self-play games on both builds, and identical logs (seed, actions, final hash and result) make it Internal. The check stops once the tier is a patch. Without `--parent-bin`, script claims count unconfirmed, a change to play code counts as no card, and the output names both.

The tier was a guess when an issue opened (`v0.3.X` or `v0.3.Y`, [[R650]], [[R743]]) and `patches ship` named it from that guess ([[R646]]); later parts of #1360 name versions from this tier.
