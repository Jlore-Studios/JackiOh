Every won fight, of any kind and bosses included, adds `RUN_WIN_MAX_HP` to the run's own
permanent max-HP bonus (`max_hp_bonus`, kept in the state so `run_fold` replays it) and then
refills HP to the new max, whatever the hero's final health was, even 1. HP outside a fight
always equals the max, and every fight starts at full health. A lost fight still drops HP to
zero and ends the run, bonus or none.

`damage_taken` counts the max before the win minus the report's final health, floored at zero:
a hero that ended past its starting health (in-fight healing is uncapped for heroes, §6.3, so
the report may top the max) counts no damage. The refill is not counted as `healed`, and a
campfire's rise is not either: healing is what a fight's report and an event's HP move say, and
a higher max is not a heal. The fight's setup carries the run's max HP as the human's hero
health ([[R290]] asks only for a hero that starts alive), past `HERO_HEALTH` included, which the
engine takes; the engine is unchanged here. Rejected: setting HP to the final health and healing
the rise, which would pay score for the refill ([[§15.10]] counts `healed`), and clamping an
overhealed report to the max, which would hide damage the accounting already floors. It takes
R3584000, the first number of #3584's block.
