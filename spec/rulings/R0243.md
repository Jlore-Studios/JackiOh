[[§10.8]] gives each viewer what they may read, and the client renders the view alone ([[§10.10]], CLAUDE.md rule 7), so what a viewer may read about a card that its printed definition does not say is in the view, not only in an event that scrolls out ([[R169]]). A Unit card in its owner's hand shows its owner its stats as they stand — its face (the radiant one when it is Radiant, a fused card's summed one) plus the permanent buffs it has gained there ([[§10.4]] layers 1, 3 and 4: #89 Corpse Eater's meals), attack floored at 0 — and a #98 Heroic Power in hand names the power it rolled as it arrived ([[R43]], [[R151]]), which its cost does not (four of the seven powers cost 1). A unit whose text a Vanilla took ([[§6.3]], [[R115]]) is marked Vanilla in both seats' views, since its definition still names the keywords and scripts it no longer has (#61's copy of #91). And a definition made during the match — a Fuse's ([[R77]]) or a crafted card's ([[R102]], [[R179]]) — travels in the view beside it, by id, whenever the view names a card of it that the viewer may read; a card the viewer may not read is the sentinel by then ([[R97]]), so its definition stays in the match
