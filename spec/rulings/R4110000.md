Amends [[R2977000]] and [[R3936000]]. Beside the cards within its bound, the AI's mulligan kept at
most one card printed one dearer whose `shaped_weight` reached `DEAL_MULLIGAN_KEEP` (2.0), and where
issue #993's curve chose among its cheap cards (at most two one-drops, only the cheapest of a hand
with no early play) it ranked them by the same `shaped_weight`. That weight is the unban lane's
`DEALT_Q`, each card's win share in the decks it was dealt into, fitted in October on an AI since
replaced and on Core, Classic and Classic+ alone, so every later card read as average, an old card
the table rated low always went back and a new one never outranked an old one it rated high. The
exception's reason, a deck the lane's shaping dealt, is gone: no deck is shaped since [[R976000]].
What it kept was the table's: #52 Silly Silas, #54 Straaza, #94 Genn's Greed (through the lane's own
`UNBANNED_PREFER`), C #68 Small Card Lobbyist and C+ #19 League of Losers on Easy, and #49 Snom Bunny
Mind Control on Medium and Hard.

The mulligan now keeps exactly the cards whose mulligan cost ([[R3936000]]) is within
`mulligan_keep_bound` ([[R2977000]]), and nothing dearer. Where the curve chooses among them,
`mulligan_keep_curved` ranks them by `hand_card_value` under `AI_EVAL`, the price the evaluation
already gives a card in its own hand (its cost, Radiant, Temporary, extra-turn and cost-change
terms), best first, ties in hand order. So of three one-drops the two it prices highest stay,
whatever set they come from: a Radiant copy of TG #1 Spikey Steven, which the table never rated,
stays beside one of two plain copies of #15 Me and Mr Token, which it rated high. A hand of M #25
Blue-Eyes White Felinor (1 mana, Tribute 2, a mulligan cost of 3) and two plain three-drops has no
early play and digs, keeping a three, which the evaluation prices above Felinor's printed 1. It
reads only the seat's own hand and never the rng.

Unchanged: the one-drop cap and the no-early-play cut; `DEALT_Q` and `shaped_weight`, which stay
for `AiDeckOptions::shape` ([[R976000]]); the tutorial's opponent, whose cap of 3 ([[R290]]) the
exception never passed; and the greedy baseline's frozen mulligan. Following Hearthstone, a
mulligan keeps what the seat's mana plays early. Rejected: refitting `DEALT_Q` over every set, which
only the stopped lane fits and which says whether to put a card in a deck, not whether to keep it in
an opening hand; and a weights parameter on `mulligan_keep_curved`, whose one caller, `decide`,
plays at `AI_EVAL`, while the greedy baseline keeps its own mulligan.
