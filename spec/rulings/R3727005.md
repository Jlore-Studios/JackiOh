A daily game can outlive a card patch, or several. Changing its cards in the middle would change the game both players planned for, and its log would no longer fold to what they saw. So a daily game is played, folded and replayed under the catalog version it started on, for its whole life, as a live match is pinned across a rebuild ([[R388]]) and the replay fold folds under older shipped catalogs ([[R767000]]); its replay hashes on that version. Its read names the version and says when it is older than the server's (`olderCatalog`), which the board tells its players. A version this build no longer carries falls back to its own, logged, as a live rebuild does.
