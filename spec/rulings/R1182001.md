Meditative+ #16.1 Jazakus Prime's Cry ("Craft a HUGE custom Potion. It costs (3).") opens the potion crafter ([[R1182000]]) in its HUGE frame (`craftMode` and `frame` `"hugePotion"`): the same frame, a Unit tagged Potion whose one hat is "Activate: Tribute this.", at the fixed cost `CRAFT_HUGE_POTION_COST` (3), with `CRAFT_HUGE_POTION_BUDGET_MULTIPLE` (5) times a Potion's points, 35 against 7. The designer's "about 5 times better than the normal custom potion" is read as five times the points budget; the frame is otherwise a Potion's, so its lines (24), effects (8) and numbers (1 to 10) are the crafter's. A recipe within the 35 points is crafted and one over them refused, as is a recipe in the plain Potion frame. The HUGE Potion is named "HUGE <words> Potion"; it is a transient definition of the Meditative+ set, tagged Potion, as a Potion is ([[R882]]), and its Radiant face doubles every number and stat. Its four presets are HUGE Potions rolled as a Potion's are, within half of the 35 points ([[R881]]). The Prime's Cry runs when it is played or cast ([[R70]]); only Jazakus's Death puts one in a deck. Since #1563 a HUGE Potion is a Spell under When cast with 20 points, five times a Potion's 4 ([[R1563008]]).
