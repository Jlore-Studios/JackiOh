A replay clip shared as a GIF is a turn, or a range picked on the scrubber, of at most `REPLAY_GIF_MAX_STEPS` steps and `REPLAY_GIF_MAX_SECONDS` seconds. Its frames draw only its steps' views ([[R768]]) and nothing the viewer's seat did not see: a card is drawn only where the view names it, a face-down card as its back, and each hand as a count, the opponent's and the viewer's own alike.
The seats are drawn as their portraits ([[R641]]) labelled "You" and "Opponent", with a small JackiOh mark and the catalog version in a corner. No account name appears in a frame, whoever the seats were.
The frame plan is a pure function of the clip and its options (the size, the frame rate, the portraits and the catalog version) on a fixed frame clock: a hold per step and a pulse per animation entry, counted in frames. The wall clock, the device's settings and its language play no part, so the same clip and options give the same plan, and the encoder turns the same frames into the same bytes.
A GIF over `REPLAY_GIF_MAX_BYTES` is drawn again in the smaller shape, then at the slower frame rate, and a clip that is still over is refused ("Pick a shorter clip"). Nothing is uploaded: the GIF is handed to the device's share sheet where it takes a file, and downloaded everywhere else.
