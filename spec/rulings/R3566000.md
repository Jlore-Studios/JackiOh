S+ #1 is a Spell with the keywords Cleave and Pierce and the text "Deal 1 damage to a Unit" (Radiant: Lifesteal, 2). A Spell's Cleave on a damage target gives the same hit to the Units beside the target, in its row and on its side ([[R2198003]]), every Unit read before the first hit; Pierce is `ignoreArmor` ([[R346]]) on every hit, and the Radiant face's Lifesteal is on every hit ([[R85]]), so its caster's hero heals for each Unit hit. The target is a declared Unit of either side, never a hero ([[R81]]), and the number is the declared `damage` ([[R386]]).

Rejected: hitting only the target, which leaves Cleave printed and doing nothing.
