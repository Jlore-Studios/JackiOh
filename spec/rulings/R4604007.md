[[R4577008]] has the fuzz monitor's I21 allow the Black Hole's pull at a turn's end when the step began with a hole that pulls then. In plain seed 8247 of the wave on 823 cards, a player cast S+ #77 Black Hole and ended their turn in the same step. The hole opened in that step, so I21 saw no hole as the step began. C #16 Book of Flame's "whenever" hook answered the `turnEnded` from the hand, and the hole's pull then swallowed an immune S #104 Reflective Plating one lane from it, inside Book of Flame's run. I21 blamed the exile on the Spell.
**The pull is the hole's however new the hole is.** A hole opened earlier in the step pulls at the turn's end exactly as one that was open as the step began ([[R4440050]]), and no Spell reaches for the card it swallows ([[§6.1]], [[R4453000]]). So I21 reads [[R4577008]]'s test against the hole the step began with or, when there was none, against the hole still open as the step ends, if that hole pulls at a turn's end. The rest of [[R4577008]] holds: the Unit stood on the side of the player whose turn ended, in a lane next to the hole's ([[R3554001]]). An exile of an immune Unit two lanes from such a hole is still a reach.
Rejected: allowing any `exiled` while a Black Hole is open, which would also pass a Spell's own exile of an immune Unit the pull cannot reach.
