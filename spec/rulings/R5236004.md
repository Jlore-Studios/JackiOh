What a card's uses spent of its Durability (`CardInstance.durability_spent`, [[R5236000]]) is a status of
its stay on the field: [[R78]]'s reset takes it off as the card leaves the field (`zones::reset_instance`),
as it takes the per-turn uses in `memory`. A Field Spell bounced to its owner's hand and played again, or
a Unit returned from a graveyard, comes back with its whole Durability, as a Hearthstone weapon returned
to the hand comes back whole. The Durability X itself is printed, so it rides every zone as it is, a
Buff's or a Nerf's steps on it included ([[R5236005]]).

A card that never left the field keeps what it spent: control changing hands, a move between zones of
the field or a position switch is no leaving.
