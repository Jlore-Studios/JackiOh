The designer takes Charge and Indestructible out of the combat keyword pool ([[R21]], [[R781005]], [[R1025000]]): handed out at random they swing a game too far, as Immutable would, which the pool never held, and without them the cards can give keywords out more freely. The pool is Taunt, Rush, First Strike, Flying, Ranged, Poisonous, Reborn, Divine Shield, Windfury, Cleave, Deft, Lifesteal, Pierce and Trample, in that order, fourteen entries. The survivors keep their relative order, so a seeded draw still picks by the same list with two entries fewer, which moved the golden traces.

No grant that draws from the pool can give Charge or Indestructible: Plastic Surgery, Zao Gao, the Conjure Rush Tokens, a Buff's keyword row and Keymaster Keenus's Aura all read the same list, so they stop at once. Both stay keywords of [[§6.1]], printed on cards and given by the cards that name them; only the random draw no longer reaches them. A unit is still never offered a keyword kind it holds, nor Taunt while it is Indestructible by its own text or another card's ([[R347]], [[R781004]]); since no draw gives Indestructible, no Taunt drawn from the pool is held down by a later draw.
