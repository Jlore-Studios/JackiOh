The token's "at the end of any turn buff all cards" is `forRestOfGame` ([[R458]]) once for each player, at the end of that player's turns ([[R1861002]]), so it runs at the end of every turn of the game, the opponent's included. Its step is one `upgrade` over every card of both sides, once each ([[R386]]): the field (both rows), the hands, the libraries, the graveyards and the exile piles. A card scope reaches a graveyard or an exile pile now (`CardZone`), both public. A hand and a library are read whole, the cards a Buff changes and the cards it cannot alike cued, so the cues count nothing ([[R440]]). A card a scope reaches that no Buff can change is left alone, and a Buff of a card in a graveyard or an exile pile never takes the cost step ([[R3596001]]). Each cast of the token adds its own pair, so two casts Buff every card twice at each end of turn. The Radiant face adds `mana` temporary mana as it resolves ([[§2.3]]), above the maximum if it must. Each side carries one badge naming it ([[R169]]).

Rejected: the field and the hands only (the text says all cards, and the graveyard and exile are where the other zones' cards go); and one entry for the caster alone (it would stop at the opponent's end of turn, which is "any turn").
