A practice AI's persona ([[R645]]) sends only emotes of the AI seat's own hand, dealt from the game's seed like the player's ([[R1341]]). Each row of the trigger and reply tables keeps its pool from the issue's table, verbatim and first, then the MN03 emoji of the same sense: a wave or a salute where the table greets, a clap, a thumbs up, a heart or a salute where it compliments, a flame, a skull, shades or a party popper where it gloats, a sweat drop, a gasp, a facepalm or a shrug where it winces, a thinking face beside a yawn at a long turn. A roll keeps its order of draws — the chance first, then the pick, then the delay — and picks uniformly from the part of the row's pool the hand holds; a hit on a row the hand holds none of sends nothing. So a persona keeps its manner whatever eight it was dealt, and stays quiet where its hand has nothing to say. Polite's pools still hold no taunt. The reply table sorts the player's new emoji too: a wave greets; a clap, a thumbs up, a heart and a salute compliment; a flame, a skull, shades and a party popper taunt; a facepalm and a sweat drop apologise; a shrug, a thinking face and a gasp earn no reply, as a yawn earns none. The caps, the delays and the rate limit are [[R645]]'s and [[R643]]'s, unchanged, and a persona made without a hand (a test) draws from the whole pool.
