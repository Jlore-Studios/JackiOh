FvH #112 Jilliax Deluxe offers "the 4 dearest affordable" modules. A Discover from the catalog, and an Equip pick's Discover ([[R1841002]]), may name `order: dearest`: its pool is shuffled by the match rng as a `random` offer's is ([[R60]]) and then ordered by each card's printed cost, read out of play ([[R65]]), dearest first; the sort keeps the shuffle's order among cards of one cost, so a tie at the cut is drawn at random. The first `count` are offered in that order, dearest first, a Core-or-snapshot pair shown once as ever ([[R2204001]]). `random`, the default, is the uniform draw it always was. A random Equip pick ignores the order.
