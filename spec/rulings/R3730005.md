A member of a private lobby ([[R3730000]]) invites a friend into its empty seat, `POST /api/lobbies/:code/invite`: a friend neither side blocked, as a challenge's ([[R1689000]]); one already in the lobby, or a full lobby, is refused. The invite is a live toast, a `{type: "lobbyInvite", code, format, from}` frame over the friend's `/ws/social` ([[R3214000]]), with Open and Dismiss; nothing keeps it, so a friend with no socket open misses it. Invites count against a challenger's `CHALLENGES_PER_HOUR`.

Rejected: sending the lobby's link by chat, which fills a conversation with codes that lapse; keeping invites in an inbox, which is a later issue's.

Amends [[R3214000]], whose list of the social socket's frames gains `lobbyInvite`.
