"For each other friendly Unit" on IA #80 Hive Ganger and IA #95 The Botnet counts the other Units acting on their controller's side, read live as an aura ([[§10.4]] layer 5): each arrival lifts the Attack and Health it gives, each departure lowers them, so Health falls as Units leave. The Botnet's Radiant face lends Rush beside the stats.

Why: the text counts bodies, not a number remembered once, so an aura reread on every layering is what it says. Rejected: a buff of the count as the card lands, which would outlive the bodies.
