Both the 2-cost draw and the odd-cost exile read each card's cost per [[R65]] at resolution; X-cost cards are exempt from both. Each clause reads its set once, as it begins: a card the draw clause drew that is cast on draw and asks its caster something has left the library by the answer, and the draws still owed after it are the ones the clause began with ([[R113]]), as the exile's set is fixed once ([[R135]])
