The deaths are the recursion log's (`deaths_this_game`, [[R4589000]]): one record per death under its controller ([[R172]]), tokens in; a discard is no death. The three most expensive are read by printed cost (`query_cost`), the list stable-sorted highest first; a tie at the cut is drawn from the match rng, and with no tie no number is drawn ([[R129]]). Each is a fresh copy on its printed face, most expensive first, with Rush on the Radiant face. The log is kept only in a game holding an Infinite Assault card ([[R4589000]]), so no recorded game's hash moves for it. Rejected: a second, game-wide history written in every game and left out of the hash.
