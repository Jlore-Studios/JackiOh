The deaths are the game history's (`deaths_of`): one record per death under its controller ([[R172]]), tokens in; a discard is no death. The three most expensive are read by printed cost (`query_cost`), the list stable-sorted highest first; a tie at the cut is drawn from the match rng, and with no tie no number is drawn ([[R129]]). Each is a fresh copy on its printed face, most expensive first, with Rush on the Radiant face. The history is written in every game and `replay::hash_state` leaves it out, so no recorded game's hash moves for it.
