Echo is played for its own printed (1) Cost, never for X or an embiggen price. When the text it has is an X-cost Spell's, the play chooses that X as a play's X is chosen ([[R81]]): at least 1, at most the player's current mana once Echo's price is paid ([[R348]]), so with nothing left after paying it cannot be played; a cast of it asks its caster for X as any cast X card's does. An embiggen Spell's text resolves at its base price
