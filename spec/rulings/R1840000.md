A Resource is a (0) Spell token whose definition carries `resource` (its rarity and its prices, [[§5]]): the catalog check admits the field, and the roll reads its rarity, never `printedRarity`, which stays the card frame's. It takes no hand slot. Each player has a Resource tray beside the hand ([[§3]]), `PlayerState.resourceTray`, oldest first and absent until they first gain one, so a game without a Resource serializes and hashes as before; a Resource is gained into it and never into a hand, with `resourceGained`.

No hand rule reaches the tray: not the hand cap (a Resource is gained with the hand full, and nothing burns), a discard, a steal, a give, a "fill your hand", a draw, the mulligan or a pass. A card that names Resources ("discard a Narcor", "fill your hand with Resources") means the tray ([[R1840004]]).

Its owner reads it, and the other player only its count: the view's `resourceTray` is a hand's shape, the viewer's own in full and the opponent's `{ count }` ([[§10.8]]), and `resourceGained` is hidden from the other player as `addedToHand` is ([[R97]]), its `radiant` with the card. Once the game is over both trays read in full, as both hands do ([[R434]]). The AI never reads the opponent's tray: its redaction hides it as it hides their hand, and its worlds fill it with Resources sampled from the catalog.
