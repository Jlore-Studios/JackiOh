Meditative+ #1.5 Star Chart, a (0) AI Spell token new in the owner's first Stinkyscape balance pass (issue #2521), reads "Travel {nodes} along your starscape map, gaining each node's reward" (2, Radiant 4, declared, [[R386]]). Meditative+ #1 Bob's Plan adds one after Stinkyscape #148 Alteran Reach, its plan ([[R1185000]]), Radiant after a Radiant Alteran Reach on the Radiant face.

- **A travel is Alteran Reach's travel.** Each node is one `travel_starscape`, the verb Alteran Reach's Activate runs: its caster's map, the player's and no card's, charted from the match rng at their first travel when they have none ([[R1847000]]); one node, reported `starscapeTravelled`, and its reward; at the twentieth node, the edge, Stinkyscape #148.1 Edge of the Universe to the hand on its base face, a full hand burning it ([[R1847001]], [[§2.4]]).
- **One after another, stopping at the edge.** The travels resolve in order, each a step of its own. Nothing travels past the edge, so once a travel reaches it the travels left do nothing, and a Star Chart cast by a player standing on the edge does nothing at all. The edge's card is added once, by the travel that reaches it.
- **Free, and needing no Alteran Reach.** No travel price is paid ([[R1847002]]'s (1) is the Activate's, not the travel's), and the caster need not have an Alteran Reach on the field.
- **Each is a travel.** A Radiant Alteran Reach's "Your first travel each turn is free" reads whether its player has traveled this turn ([[R1847002]]): after a Star Chart they have, so the Reach's next travel that turn is the paid one.
