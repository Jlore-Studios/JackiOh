The owner asked in #2756 to choose, on the Statistics page, whether a card such as Gary the Gambler is seen in its full art or its normal face.

- **The choice.** Under the faces of a full-art card ([[R2655001]]; a token listed under one reads its parent's, [[R2655002]]), the card breakdown of `/stats` offers four buttons: No full art, Epic, Legendary and Mythic, the tiers of [[R2655000]]. The one pressed is the art its faces print, the Radiant face's too while Show Radiant is on. A card without a full art offers none.
- **Where it starts.** Until the viewer presses one, the pressed button is what the face printed before: the Epic while the Full-art cards setting is on, No full art while it is off. A choice holds from card to card, and over closing and opening the breakdown, as Show Radiant does, while the page is open; the address does not carry it.
- **Whatever is owned.** The page is public and reads no ownership, so it prints every full art, each tier included, whatever the viewer owns, as the Almanac and the landing page's fan do ([[R1313000]]); and a tier picked prints whatever the setting says, as a card's own pick does ([[R1655000]]).
- **No pick written.** The choice is a preview: it writes no card pick, which would be sent to the account ([[R1834000]]), and changes no face anywhere else.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule reads a full art, and nothing here enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash.

Hearthstone semantics: a card's crafting view in the collection shows its normal and golden versions whether or not the player owns them.

Rejected: a two-way normal-or-full-art switch, which hides the Legendary and Mythic a player chases; carrying the choice in the address, as Show Radiant is not; offering only the tiers the viewer owns, which needs an ownership read this public page does not make; and writing the card's pick, which [[R1834000]] sends to the account.
