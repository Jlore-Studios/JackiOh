Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to "go harder on alt art": keep the theme but move
it to another style or place, so each Alt art is clearly its own. [[R3396000]] did so for Classic+; Core's Alt art
still redrew the card's own scene re-seeded ([[R2940001]], [[R3392000]]), which changed a median 54% of a Core
picture's pixels, nearly all of it the sky behind the same subject, and as little as 18%.

**An alt recipe per card.** Each of the 61 Core cards offered Alt art (`version-alt-art`) draws an alt recipe of its
own (`coreAltRecipes1.ts` to `coreAltRecipes3.ts`, spread into `ALT_SCENE_RECIPES` beside Classic+'s), written from
#3398's brief for it: the same subject and idea in another place, time or story moment, in Core's family.
`sceneRecipeOf` reads it under the card's Alt art seed (`<id>#alt`) alone, as [[R3396000]] set; the card's id, every
repaint's seed ([[R3017002]]) and any other seed keep the normal recipe. Every level of Alt art draws the one alt
picture ([[R3015002]]), base and Radiant.

**The same subject.** A figure both pictures draw is the normal one's person, its head and outfit unchanged (only its
cloth, headgear or held item may change: the sorcerer's horns go in the years before he was bent, the assassin holds
the stopwatch as the pocket watch, the Fiender has put his binoculars down); a Unit drawn as a figure keeps one;
Duplicating Felinors keeps its three Felinors, Carnivorous Cube its two knights and True Strike its archer, each the
normal picture's; and a subject that is a part of the kit stays in the subject layer. Where a brief changes who is in
the picture, an extra figure stands for the new person and the kept part for the subject: Conjure KY's students watch
the portal take KY, Masochism Mask's wanderer wears the mask, Unlicensed Experimentation's clamps seize a delivery
golem, and Combo-Index's cleaner sweeps the empty stadium; Collateral Damage gains an inspector.

**Ceaseless Void before it wakes.** Its brief is "a tiny black pearl in a sunlit tide pool, the water around it
bending inward", so its alt recipe draws no giant: [[R3338001]]'s void composition and its giant stay its normal
picture's, and the alt recipe draws the void's swirl and a black pearl (an `orb` painted black) from the kits, with an
emblem of its own, like every other alt recipe (`ownSubject` stays the normal recipe's alone).

**Measurably elsewhere.** An alt recipe's sky shares no palette colour with its normal recipe's, and its subject
moves at least 8 units, or grows or shrinks by at least a quarter, or a figure comes or goes: a close-up, a wide or
distant view, one from above or from below. In a real browser each Alt art window changes at least
`ALT_PICTURE_CHANGE_MIN` (0.6) of the normal window's points sampled on card-versions.cy.tsx's grid, counted with its
threshold for a point that differs, base and Radiant.

**What still holds.** Each alt picture clears its rarity's detail floor and the byte ceiling ([[R3331001]]), on
both faces; prints no text; draws one geometry on both faces, the Radiant one gilded under its rays, with no base
colour in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]); is apart from every other picture ([[R503]]); stands its emblem small
inside the band every face shows; keeps props out of its subject layer, and Tempo Timmy, Duplicating Felinors, Bread
and Butter and "Miss" Mrow mark the placements that move on their props beat ([[R2715001]]). A Radiant face keeps its
marks at every Alt art level. Ten parts join the scene kit for the briefs' places: a palm and a camel
(`sceneKitNature.ts`), a crater (`crater-field`, named apart from the smoking `crater` of [[R3397000]]) and a van
(`sceneKitPlaces.ts`), and a hay bale, a picnic blanket (`gingham-blanket`, named apart from the `picnic-blanket` of
[[R3397000]]), a rug, a hammock (`beach-hammock`, named apart from the Legacy touches' `hammock`, [[R3393001]]), a sleeping bag and a teapot
pouring tea (`pouring-teapot`, named apart from the Meditative ink kit's iron `teapot`, [[R3401000]])
(`sceneKitObjects.ts`). No existing part changed, so every normal picture draws as before.

**Stand-ins.** A brief's smaller details are drawn with parts the kits already hold, each named in its recipe's doc
comment: white trees for Moths to the Flame's birches, a bench for Duplicating Felinors' window seat, the stopwatch for
Hit Job's pocket watch, the basket and bread for Bread and Butter's rolls, the steel helmet for Collateral Damage's
hard hat, fruit trees for Gravedigger's orchard and, painted pink, Quickstriker's cherry trees, crates under bushes
for Recycling Initiative's planters, sparks for Selective Breeding's glinting pods, house cats for Big Felinor's tiny
Felinors, a vine for the fig's branch, a vortex at the visor for Snom Bunny's reflected eyes and flowers for its
clover, a ramp for Silly Silas's tilting planks, the market awning and a cog for Jilliax's tarp and bolt, shards for
Conjure KY's chalk, an arch in a battlement for the caravanserai's gate, the rotunda for the university and the
soldier for Prejudiced Postdoc's clone, a long table for Gifted Program's jetty, a dead tree and cacti for Masochism
Mask's thorns, the held card and an ice floor for Transmogulate's tongs and salt flat, the hatch for Unlicensed
Experimentation's manhole, a jar for Craft a Card's glue pot, and ferns and bushes in coral paint for Ceaseless Void's
coral. The sleepers sit (pose-sit): the figure kit has no lying pose.

Rejected: keeping the giant in Ceaseless Void's Alt art, which its brief's "before it wakes" leaves out; a lying pose
for the sleepers, a new pose the figure kit would need for six pictures; new parts for the small details the
stand-ins draw; splitting the 61 cards into pull requests of about 40, which the three recipe files map onto should a
person want them split; changing a figure's head or outfit, which would make it someone else; and alt recipes for the
repaints, which the issue does not ask.
