`combat::strike` deals an attack's blow, then its Cleave hits ([[§4.4]] step 10), then a sweep's hits on every other enemy Unit it weighs ([[R3554008]]), so a Cleave attacker that sweeps (Stinkyscape+ #107 Fuse granted Cleave, as Stinkyscape+ #44 Blast Compound grants it) strikes each unit beside its target twice. [[R4290000]]'s splash read Cleave's hit alone, so its `kills` said a neighbour stood that the sweep's hit after it destroyed, and the projection of the sweep ([[R4221003]]) read each such unit at its health and Divine Shield before Cleave's hit, so a trampling sweeper's overflow over it was short.

The sweep's hit on a unit an earlier hit of the same attack struck is now read as that hit left it: its damage taken and, when the earlier hit was 1 or more, its Divine Shield gone, as the combat deals the sweep after Cleave. A splash hit's `kills` counts the sweep's hit on the same unit after it: Cleave's and the sweep's dealt together meet its health, or either lands from a Poisonous or exiling attacker. Its `dealt` stays Cleave's hit alone, the Cleave-routed damage the fuzz's I18 reads ([[R2783001]]). The projection [[R44]]'s trap window reads changes with it, for a trampling sweeper with Cleave alone.

Hearthstone semantics: a projection of an attack is what the attack does, each hit landing on the board the hit before it left.

Rejected: an I18 skip for a sweeping Cleaver, which would leave the preview showing a neighbour standing that the attack destroys, as [[R4221003]] rejected one for a trampling sweeper; folding the sweep's hit into the splash's `dealt`, which would misstate Cleave's hit.
