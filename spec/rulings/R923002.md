Three of the sandbox's tools ([[R923000]]) set numbers, each within a bound in the engine's config:

- **`setHero`** sets a hero's health, from 1 to `SANDBOX_HERO_HEALTH_MAX`, its Armor, from 0 to `SANDBOX_ARMOR_MAX`, or both; one of them must be given. Health is set, not healed or damaged, so nothing that watches damage or healing fires.
- **`setMana`** sets a player's current mana, its maximum, or both, each from 0 to `SANDBOX_MANA_MAX`.
- **`draw`** draws 1 to `SANDBOX_DRAW_MAX` cards for a seat, as any draw: a full hand burns the card, and an empty library deals fatigue.

A number out of its bound is refused with the bound named, and nothing changes.
