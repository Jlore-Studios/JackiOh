Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Issue #3393 gave each Training Grounds Legacy card (#41–#60,
[[R2511000]]) a brief of its own, set in the yard, and asked that it keep its link to its Core card's subject
in a scene of its own, where [[R2724000]] drew the Core card's frozen subject in the yard.

**A scene of its own.** Each Legacy card draws its own scene recipe ([[R3331000]]) from its brief in the
Training Grounds family's look, its subject the brief's (the Speciesist at the viewing stand's cordon, the
Rock lifting a training stone), and places once the yard touch [[R2724000]] gave it, as a kit part of the
same thing (#41 the rope cordon … #60 the scarecrow). Its face wears its Core card's frozen emblem: the glyph
and paint of the picture its Core card drew before any recipe, as `legacySvg` reads it, so the two still read
as one card across the sets. It is still one picture, as [[R2724000]] made it: no motion, no Out of frame
parts and no seed of its own. It never draws its Core card's scene, which every Legacy card's Core card has since
[[R3392000]]; a file delivered for the Legacy card's id still wins. `legacySvg` draws only a Legacy card with no recipe.

Rejected: drawing the Core card's frozen subject in the scene, which for a Unit is the generic silhouette
this issue retires; giving a Legacy card Alt art, motion or parts, which no Legacy card has had; and deleting
`legacySvg`, the fallback for a Legacy card with no recipe.
