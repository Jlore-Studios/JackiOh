title: "Pity counters are shared across every card pack an account opens (issue #1298)"
cards: "§14.3, §14.4"
proven_in:
  - crates/server/src/economy/math.rs

An account keeps one set of pity counters ([[§14.4]]): the three counters in
`crates/server/src/economy/math.rs`'s `PityCounters`, one per tracked rarity. Every card pack the
account opens ([[§14.3]]), of its set's kind or the global kind, increments all three counters,
and a pack holding a tracked rarity resets only that rarity's counter: a Mythic leaves the Epic
and Legendary counters running, and a Legendary the Epic one. The counters are the account's to
see, with each rarity's odds.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone keeps pity per pack kind, but an account here opens packs of
several kinds toward one collection, and per-kind counters would pity each kind apart while the
generous earn of [[§14.3]]'s dupe chain means most packs are opened wherever the account fancies.
One shared set is also less storage and less plumbing for the pack roll, which passes one
counter set with every opening.

Rejected: one counter set per kind of pack, Hearthstone-like, but more storage and more plumbing
for the pack-roll sub-issue, and less generous than [[§14]]'s "earned generously": packs of a
kind opened rarely would pity rarely too.
