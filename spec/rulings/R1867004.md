Stinkyscape #11.1 Frostbolt's "a target" is any Unit or hero on either side ([[R81]]): one hit of the declared damage, then a Freeze on the target, a hero passed by since heroes are never Frozen ([[§6.1]]) and a Unit the hit destroyed passed by with it. The Radiant face's "Deal {damage} damage to the Units adjacent to it" is the Spell's own hit landing on each Unit beside the target in its row on its side, Cleave's adjacency ([[§3.1]]) and not the combat keyword; every hit is its own [[§4.4]] instance, Spell Damage raising each, all landing before one state check ([[R59]]). "Freeze it" still Freezes the target alone: the neighbours are never Frozen, and none stand beside a hero.
