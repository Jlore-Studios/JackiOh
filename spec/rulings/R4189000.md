[[R195]] lights a card yellow when playing it now would take its special branch, and no card
printed after Meditative declared the hook: the closed list held 20 cards, the newest Meditative
#57. Five newer cards print the same kind of condition, so they glow now, each hook a pure read of
the same fact its own resolution reads, so the glow and the branch cannot disagree.

S+ #102 Cosmic Bard and S+ #162 Edwin Van'Spacechief print Combo ([[§6.2]]): one card played
earlier this turn. Their hooks ask `played_earlier` from the hand against the same `COMBO` count
their Cries read, as Core #10 Rapid Replenish reads it; their [[§8]] rows already promised this glow.

S #137 Starfleet Commander and S #176 Preemptive Strike print "If you have a Dormant Starship,
launch it, then Discover a War Doctrine and cast it" ([[§6.3]]). Their hooks ask
`dormant_starship_of`, the same fact their Cries branch on: with none both do nothing, and dark.

S #4 Olivian Expanse prints "If your deck is empty, it costs (0)", and its Radiant face wins the
game when it was also the last card in hand. Its base hook asks whether the controller's library
is empty, the same fact the Discover's `FREE` reads; its Radiant hook asks that and whether the
hand holds only itself, since at resolution the card has left the hand, where the Discover's
`WINS` reads an empty one, while a glow read from the hand still holds the card.

Hearthstone lights such cards too, and these are the same kind of condition [[R195]] lights on
Core #53, #68 and #71: each reads only its controller's own board and piles ([[§10.8]],
[[§10.9]]), so the opponent's view never carries the flag.

Rejected:
- Lighting every conditional card of the later sets in one pass. The rest are cost reductions,
which are [[R662]]'s other kind, or depend on the zone the player picks, which is no condition.
- Lighting only the two Stinkyscape+ cards. That fixes the mismatch with the spec but leaves
Preemptive Strike dark, though it does nothing at all without a Dormant Starship.
