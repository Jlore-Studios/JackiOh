The client's labels, page names, headings and buttons drifted across sentence case and Title Case, and one action was referred to with several different words:

- Staying was called "Keep playing" or "Stay".
- Leaving was called "Leave", "Leave game", "Leave fight", or "Leave without saving".
- Back was called "← Back", "← Play together", "← All decks", "Back to Play together", "Back to lessons", with and without arrows.
- Closing was called "Close", "Done", "Got it", or "Dismiss", the same notice sometimes one and sometimes another.
- "Create an account" was used for the title while "Create account" was used for the submit button.
- A page's name differed between the command menu, footer, `<title>`, and page heading (e.g. "Card almanac" vs "Almanac", "Stats" vs "Statistics", "Privacy" vs "Privacy policy", "Terms" vs "Terms of service").

Sentence case now governs every label, heading, button, page and mode name (All random, Dungeon run, Field guide). Only the first word is capital, except for card and keyword catalog spellings, the game's terms as cards print them (Unit, Field Spell), currencies, sets, JackiOh, Jlore and acronyms. Each page has exactly one name across its heading, the command menu, the footer and `<title>`: Almanac, Statistics, Privacy policy, Terms of service, Account, How JackiOh uses AI, Dungeon run, and Play.

One verb is used per action:
- Staying is "Stay".
- Leaving is "Leave" (with "Leave without saving" retained where Save and leave sits beside it).
- Closing a dialog or sheet is its ✕, named "Close" ([[R3221000]] keeps a text "Close" off every button); taking an inline notice or panel away is "Dismiss" (the practice game's earning notice, and the Conquest explainer, whose "Got it" becomes "Dismiss"); the coach's "Got it" acknowledging a tip and "Done" finishing picks remain.
- Creating an account is "Create account".
- Back is "Back" alone (using `ArrowIcon direction="back"`), or "Back to <place>" to name a place ("Back to Play together", "Back to decks", "Back to the lobby", "Back to lessons", "Back to sign in").
- Translated strings never start or end with arrow characters (`← → ↑ ↓`): directional arrows are rendered by `ArrowIcon`.

Hearthstone semantics do not reach the client's chrome and wording.

Rejected: short menu/footer page names (Statistics, Almanac, Privacy policy and Terms of service were preferred over Stats, Card almanac, Privacy and Terms); renaming "Leave without saving" when a loss or save is explicitly named beside it; translating arrow keys with newly invented machine Chinese instead of stripping decorative arrows and keeping existing vetted translations.
