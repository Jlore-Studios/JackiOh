"Aura: Cards in your hand have Tradable" (The Syndicate, The Trade Union, [[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #164 and #165) lends Tradable to every card in its controller's hand while the card acts on their field, a keyword lent at [[§10.4]]'s layer 5 as Knowledge Breaker's and Paranoia's auras lend Cloaked to a hand ([[R1040]], [[R783007]]); the other player's hand is lent nothing, and the lending ends the moment the card leaves the field. So Tradable is now read through the layers, as Cloaked is: a card is Tradable when its running face prints the keyword, an effect gave it, or such an aura lends it, which extends [[R1838000]]'s reading (printed or given) and changes nothing where no aura lends it. `legal_actions`, the reducer and the view read it the same way (`trade::why_trade_refused`), so the Trade control, `tradeReason` and the viewer's `trades` follow the aura; a hand card's own keyword list shows what it is made of and no aura's, as for Cloaked ([[R243]]). Hearthstone's auras over a hand (a cost aura, "your cards have…") hold only while their source is in play, which this follows. Rejected: giving each card the keyword as it reaches the hand, which would outlive the aura.
