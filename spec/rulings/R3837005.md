The designer's list for issue #3837 reads, where it does not match the cards as they stand:

- Meditative #44 CN Jade Well, "End of turn → activate": its trigger was "Start of turn", so its Start of turn becomes an Activate, "Activate: Add an Auspicious Rock to your hand." (Radiant: a Radiant one), once a turn and free on its controller's turn, as Meditative #40 Feng Shui's Rock is ([[R384]], [[R3140000]]). It stays a Field Spell ([[R964]]).
- TG #35 Corpse Recycler, "For its opponent → For you": its text said "for its controller", so the Rush Token goes to the Recycler's controller, whoever controlled the Unit that died: "After a non-Token Unit dies, summon a Rush Token for you." This amends [[R2199006]]'s side; the rest of it stands.
- The list names "Miner v1" twice; the second is S #66 Miner v5: it follows Miner v4, it is the only Miner left, and its numbers are Miner v5's halved, as the others' are.
- #98 Heroic Power's "reduce the cost of each power by (1)" already shipped with v0.6.0, in issue #3551, so the card is not changed again.

Rejected: an end-of-turn trigger for the Well (the list's arrow ends in an Activate), and the token for the dead Unit's opponent (the list's "For you" is the card's own controller).
