id: R57
title: Copy semantics
cards: "#12, #22, #33; R385, R386, R429"
proven_in:
  - crates/engine/tests/rules/rulings_b.rs
---
A copy of a unit on the field keeps its radiant flag, buffs, granted keywords, Vanilla state, `statsOverride` and `tuning` ([[R386]]) and resets damage, exertion and counters, so it never inherits a Brittle count ([[R385]]) or a times-played count ([[R429]]); copies shuffled into a library are fresh instances carrying only the radiant flag, `statsOverride` and `tuning`
