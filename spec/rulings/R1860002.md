Stinkyscape #40 Poison Capsule's "End of turn: Deal {damage} damage to all enemies for each Plague
Counter on this" is one hit of damage times the counters on it — the counters times its declared
`damage` — to the enemy hero and to every enemy Unit (`damage_all`, [[R59]]), not that many hits.
With no counter on it, nothing; counters on another permanent add nothing. It takes R1860002 of
issue #1860's block.
