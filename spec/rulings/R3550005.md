Declared Ranged attacks and `activate` actions are countered: the attack deals no damage, the Activate does nothing, and the Activate's use and costs stay spent. One budget a turn is shared by both, and a forced attack is never countered ([[R121]]). Rejected: a trap window for Activates, which would retime shipped Wishing Well and Tommy Tempo.
