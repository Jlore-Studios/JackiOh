title: "The crowd stays quiet while the page is hidden, as effects and voices are (issue #4512)"
cards: "§10.11, R631, R669, issue #4512"
proven_in:
  - apps/web/src/audio/crowd.test.ts
---
A match in a background tab plays no crowd sound. While the page is hidden the engine's `crowdOutput()` returns null, so the crowd director's `live()` reads no output and a murmur or reaction that comes due is dropped, exactly as one due on a suspended, interrupted or closed context is dropped: nothing is saved up to burst out on return, and the murmur loop keeps its rhythm, rescheduling as it goes. The gate closes whatever the audio settings say, following the effects' rule rather than the music's "Keep playing music when this tab is in the background" setting ([[§10.11]], [[R631]]): sound effects and voice lines stay quiet in a hidden tab either way, and the crowd follows them, on the crowd bus under the same mute ([[R669]]). A window that is visible but not focused keeps its crowd, as it keeps its effects. Rejected: gating the crowd on the music background setting, which stays music-only; and suspending or closing the audio context for a hidden page, which would need a resume the gate avoids.
