Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to "go harder on alt art": keep the theme but move
it to another style or place, so each Alt art is clearly its own. Until this issue a Meditative card's Alt art redrew
its own ink-wash scene re-seeded ([[R2940001]], [[R3401000]]): the same parts with another seed, which changed a
median 42% of the picture's pixels and as little as 7% (饕餮).

**An alt recipe per card.** Each of the 54 Meditative cards offered Alt art (`version-alt-art`) draws an alt recipe
of its own (`meditativeAltRecipes1.ts` to `meditativeAltRecipes3.ts`, #1 to #38, #40 to #71 and #72 to #100, spread
into `ALT_SCENE_RECIPES` after Classic+'s, [[R3396000]]), written from #3404's brief for it: the same subject and idea
in another place, time or story moment, in Meditative's ink-wash family. `sceneRecipeOf` reads it under the card's
Alt art seed (`<id>#alt`) alone; the card's id, every repaint's seed ([[R3017002]]) and any other seed keep the
normal recipe, and the 78 Meditative entries offered no Alt art still grow their normal recipe from that seed. Every
level of Alt art draws the one alt picture ([[R3015002]]), base and Radiant.

**The same subject.** A Unit drawn as a figure keeps its figure; a figure both pictures draw is the normal one's
person, its head, outfit and skin unchanged (only its cloth, headgear or held item may change: the smuggler's straw
hat, the explorer's map, the vendor's card, the scholar's book); and a subject that is a part of the kit (the council
table and its bound hands, the melon, the abacus, the pea vine, the sheep in its helmet, the lion dancer, the bottle,
the printer, the rune circle, the jade, the well, the taotie, the card ring, the round robot, the dragon fruit, the
house, the parcels, the skull, the cane, the giant, the great tree, the gust, the lantern and its books, the deck on
its table, the totem, the basket, the jade die, the blueprint) stays in the subject layer, placed first.

**Measurably elsewhere.** An alt recipe's sky shares no palette colour with its normal recipe's, and its subject
moves at least 8 units, or grows or shrinks by at least a quarter, or a figure comes or goes: a close-up (a figure
at 1.6 to 1.9 standing below the box, so its head and shoulders fill the window), a wide or distant view, or one from
above or below. In a real browser each Alt art window changes at least `ALT_PICTURE_CHANGE_MIN` (0.6) of the normal
window's points sampled on card-versions.cy.tsx's grid, counted with its threshold for a point that differs, base and
Radiant.

**What still holds.** Each alt picture stamps one red seal in its near layer, inside the band every face shows
([[R3401000]]); clears its rarity's detail floor and the byte ceiling ([[R3331001]]), on both faces; prints no text;
draws one geometry on both faces, the Radiant one gilded under its rays, with no base colour in Radiant's gold window
([[R2725004]]); is apart from every other picture ([[R503]]); stands its emblem small inside the band; and keeps props
out of its subject layer, API Key Fishing and Tatches the Totem marking the placements that move on their props beat
([[R2715001]]). A Radiant face keeps its marks at every Alt art level. Three parts join the scene kit for the briefs:
a fisherman's oar and a sofa (`sceneKitInkObjects.ts`) and a tower crane (`sceneKitInkMachines.ts`).

Rejected: one pull request per part of about 27 cards, which the three recipe files map onto should a person want it
split; standing in a bench for the sofa or a figure's oar for the oar; another family for the Alt pictures, which
would lose Meditative's seal and brush line; holding each alt picture only to "hue or composition", where every brief
names a new palette and the skies can share none; measuring the pixels in vitest, where jsdom has no rasterizer;
dropping the detail floor or the byte ceiling for alt pictures; and changing a figure's head, outfit or skin, which
would make it someone else.
