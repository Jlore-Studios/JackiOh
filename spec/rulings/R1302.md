A face printed in Chinese (Meditative #32's Radiant face) keeps its Chinese in the catalog as its `text`, and its entry in the Chinese table is the same text; no English form of it exists. [[R366]]'s house style reads a face written in Chinese characters by Chinese punctuation: the printed keywords lead on a line of their own, joined with "，" and with no full stop, each written as the terms file writes it with its number after it ("护甲7", "法术伤害+2"); a labelled ability ("战吼：", "亡语：") opens a line, or follows another label's words ("战吼和回合开始时：", C+ #7's "Cry and start of turn:"), and never follows a sentence ended on the same line; every other line ends with "。"; a cost stays "(N)" ([[R432]]), so no full-width bracket is written; and no ASCII ".", ",", ";" or ":" appears. Every face of the Chinese table ([[R1303]]) is held to the same style. [[R277]]'s gold diff compares such a face with the other face's Chinese character by character, each Chinese character and punctuation mark a word of its own, so English faces are marked exactly as before.
