The tag chips of the Card Almanac and of the deck builder list the tags a card of a shipped set carries, in the order of `FILTER_TAGS` and `ALMANAC_TAGS`, read off the bundled catalog (`shippedTags`); a tag no shipped card carries has no chip, and Glitch lends none ([[R674]]). Wincon, the tag of Meditative #8 Reach the Summit and #20 Aestheticize the Game, is in `FILTER_TAGS` and gets its chip the day a card of a shipped set carries it ([[R1420]]), and not before. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
