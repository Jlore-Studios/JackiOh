[[R732000]] kept the AI's shadow ban ([[R186]], `SHADOW_BAN` in `crates/ai/src/shadow_ban.rs`) to the Meditative set, at most half of it. The Meditative+ set joins it on the same terms: the ban holds cards of those two sets only, and at most half of each set's non-token cards, 51 of Meditative's 102 and 13 of Meditative+'s 26, each set held to its own half. No card of an earlier set is shadow-banned again.

Issue #1186's sweep of record swept the Meditative+ cards alone ([[R390]]'s two passes, every set previewed as `fuzz` previews them, [[R1420]]), so its report holds rows for no other card. `cargo jackioh sweep --write` reads the reports of record oldest first, and a card takes the row of the latest report that lists it: a ban takes it off `SHADOW_WATCH`, a watch row off `SHADOW_BAN`. So the 2026-10-12 sweep's Meditative rows stand beside #1186's Meditative+ rows, and `SHADOW_UNBANNED`, the unban lane's removals ([[R732001]]), is checked against every report's bans. [[R732000]]'s "no lane adds one" holds for the training lanes: a sweep of record of a set that had no rows adds that set's.
