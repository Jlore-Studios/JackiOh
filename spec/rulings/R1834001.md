[[R1295000]]'s `cosmetics` settings group took any short text for a pick, and [[R1297000]]'s deck
slots any id of their kind in the registry: the server checked a deck's pick against the registry
and the settings for shape only, and ownership was judged by the client, where it drew the viewer's
own side ([[R1655000]]). A pick is now shown to another player ([[R1834002]]), so the server judges
every pick as it is written. It amends [[R1295000]] (*The picks*: the group's six picks are checked)
and [[R1297000]] (*Saving them*: a deck's pick must also be owned).

**The check.** A pick of an id for a slot must be an entry of the shipped registry
(`cosmetics.json`) of that slot's kind, and one the account owns where it is worn: a kind's default
and a starter, which every account owns on every card ([[§14.2]]), or a ledger row, on that card for
a per-card kind and on the account for an account kind. A per-card pick made for every card, as the
settings group's foil, finish and border are, is owned only when it is a default or a starter,
since no ledger row names every card. A full art names its card, so another card's full art is the
wrong kind for it ([[R1313000]]). One check, `cosmetics::check_pick`, serves every write:

- the deck upsert's `tableCosmetic`, `fieldCosmetic` and `cardBackCosmetic` ([[R1297000]]), beside
  its portrait's ([[R1827000]]), whose ownership [[R1833000]] checked first and which now go through
  the same check as every other pick, in its words;
- the `cosmetics` group of `PUT /api/settings`: each of its six keys (`foil`, `finish`, `border`,
  `table`, `field`, `cardBack`) that the body names must be a text that passes; any other key of the
  group, and every other group, is still checked for shape only ([[R633]]);
- `PUT /api/card-picks` ([[R1834000]]): each of `foil`, `finish` and `border`, owned on that card,
  and `fullArt: true` only for a card whose full art the account owns (since [[R2655000]], its Epic;
  `fullArt` may name one of the card's full arts by id, owned on that card).

A pick that fails is 400 `bad_request`, and nothing in the request is written. The message names
the field: `"foil" is not a cosmetic id of its kind` for an id the registry does not list under that
kind, `"foil" is a cosmetic this account does not own` for one it does not own (`"cosmetics.foil"` in a
settings body). A clear, `null` or a key left out, is never refused. The upload of [[R1834000]] is
the one write that refuses nothing for a pick: it leaves out what it could never wear and keeps an
id it does not know.

**What still keeps any text.** The device's stores keep what they are given and a face draws an id
the client does not know as its kind's default ([[R1295000]], [[R1655000]]), and the client keeps
its own ownership check for drawing the viewer's own side, and the Collection's for what it offers to
equip. A pick saved before this check, in a deck or a settings group, is judged again when a match
freezes it ([[R1834002]]), so none reaches another client unowned.

Hearthstone semantics: a player can equip only what they own, and the server, which holds what they
own, is the one that says so (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Rejected: trusting the client's check, which a hand-made request passes by; and judging a pick only
when a match freezes it, which would let a deck or a settings group hold a pick that is never worn
without its player being told.
