A private lobby ([[R3730000]]) has a third format, `{format: "2v2"}`: four people play Duos ([[§13]]) in two teams of two, team B played by people instead of the AI team ([[R3731002]]). It is made on Play together beside a co-op lobby and is unranked; there is no 2v2 queue.

- **Seats.** Four, in two teams: the host's team, `host` and `guest`, and the other team, `guest2` and `guest3` (migration 0091). `GET /api/lobbies/:code` lists them in that order, each with its `role`, and `you` is the caller's role. An entrant takes the first empty seat in that order; a lobby with none is `409 full` to an entrant and a 404 to a visitor's read, as a full 1v1 lobby is.
- **Teams.** `POST /api/lobbies/:code/team` moves its caller, any member but the host, to the other team's first empty seat, keeping their pick (`409 team_full` when that team has none; the host's team is the host's, `409 host_stays`). `POST /api/lobbies/:code/move` `{profileId, seat}` is the host's alone (`not_host`): it seats a member other than the host at `guest`, `guest2` or `guest3`, swapping them with whoever sits there. Either takes every member's Ready off, as a settings change does in the other formats ([[R3730003]]). A 2v2 has no settings: no mode and no difficulty, and `settings` is `400` for it.
- **Decks.** Each member picks as in a co-op lobby, a saved deck or a random one ([[R3730001]]). A teammate's deck is shown by name, as a co-op teammate's is; the other team's seats show their deck state alone, never a name, as a 1v1 opponent's do (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- **Blocks.** A block between any two members counts as one with the host does in the other formats ([[R1689000]]'s reading): a visitor blocked with any member reads the lobby as an unknown code and cannot enter it, a friend blocked with any member cannot be invited ([[R3730005]]), and of two members a block now separates, the one seated later in seat order is dropped as the lobby is next read.
- **Life.** It closes `DUOS_ROOM_TTL_MS` after its last change, as a co-op lobby does, and a host holds at most `MAX_OPEN_ROOMS_PER_HOST` not started of this format, counted apart from the other two ([[R3730004]]).
- **Start.** The host's Start needs all four seats filled, picked and ready (`not_ready`), checks every person's games and the seat ledger again, and starts the Duos match whose teams the seed's coin lines up ([[R3731001]]); a refusal for someone other than the host reads "A player is playing another game right now." ([[R3730002]]).

Rejected: seating an entrant on the team with fewer people, which would move a friend the host invited onto the other team; letting a member who is not the host move others, which would let anyone undo the host's teams; showing the other team's deck names, which a 1v1 lobby already keeps from an opponent.

Amends [[R3730000]] (a lobby's seats and who reads them), [[R3730002]] (a 2v2's Start) and [[R3730003]] (a 2v2 has no settings, and a change of teams takes the Readys off).
