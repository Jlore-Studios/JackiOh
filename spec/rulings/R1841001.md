Damage that "deals half damage to heroes" (`halfToHeroes`, a rider of `damage` and `damageAll` beside `trample`) is halved on a hero at [[§4.4]] step 2, rounded down, after Spell Damage (step 0) and before the Armor, the hero's multipliers and its caps; a Unit takes it whole. `HALF_DAMAGE_TO_HEROES_DIVISOR` is 2, so 7 deals 3 and 1 deals nothing. A hit halved to 0 is no hit (the zero rule, [[R63]]): no `damage` event and no `damageAbsorbed`. The Armor takes the halved hit, and one it takes whole is reported absorbed at the halved amount ([[R1360]], [[R1361]]). A hit a lethal replacement re-aims (step 4a, [[R1204]]) and a Trample excess (step 9) are new instances from the same source with the same rider, so each starts whole and is halved again only if it lands on a hero. Rejected: halving after the Armor, as C #75 Argusland's divisor does (rounded up, [[R463]]): the designer wrote "rounded down", and the rider is the source's, not the hero's.
