[[§13.5]] keeps a passed card's buffs, cost modifiers and face and gives it a new instance id in
the teammate's duel, and said nothing of what the card remembers. What a card can remember in a hand
names something of its own duel: a Fortify Mind's secret ([[R862]]), a Journey Complete's journey
([[R1246]]). Both duels number their cards and secrets from the same start, so the same id names
something else in the teammate's duel, which [[§13.1]] forbids any card to reach. An audit found a
passed Fortify Mind revealing, judging and cancelling a secret of the other duel, and a passed Journey
Complete returning a card of the teammate's own from exile.

- **A passed card keeps of its memory only what is the card itself**: its climbing return price
  ([[R153]]) and Heroic Power's rolled power ([[R43]]). Everything else stays behind. A passed Fortify
  Mind has no link and guesses nothing, as a copy guesses nothing ([[R862]]); a passed Journey Complete
  returns nothing.
- **A pass ends the card's stay in the passer's hand** as any other way out of a hand does
  ([[R1140]]): a Do or Die watching it stops watching it.
- **The AI's pass model ([[§13.7]]) makes the same copy**, and prices a card it may pass in the
  teammate's duel, where it would be played: a card whose price reads its own duel, such as Core #100
  Ceaseless Void's counts, is priced by the duel it goes to.
