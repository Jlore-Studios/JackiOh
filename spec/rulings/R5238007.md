Felinors vs Humans #57.2 puts named library cards into the graveyard. `discard_library` ([[R3951001]]) took the whole library.

**The rule.** `discard_library` with `instanceIds` takes only those cards of the named player's library, in the library's order, top first; an id that names no card of that library is passed by. Each goes as the whole library's cards go: to its owner's graveyard, reported as it lands, a unit token ceasing to exist instead ([[R11]]). It is no draw and no discard from a hand ([[§6.3]]), so nothing that answers a discard answers it. Absent, the whole library goes, as before.
