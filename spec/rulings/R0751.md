The designer's answer on #355: the Core faces drop the target wording, as #88 did for Classic and Classic+. A face says what it does to the card or hero it reaches ("Deal 3 damage.", "Destroy a Unit.", "Steal an enemy permanent."), not "to a target", "to any target" or "target <noun>": who picks it is [[§8]]'s convention (a choice the player makes at play time, [[R81]]). "Heal a target N" stays (Book of Heal kept it in #88), and "target" as a verb ([[R394]]'s "targets one of its owner's Units") is no pick. The [[§8.1]] rows keep their wording under [[§8]]'s convention, and the catalog's faces, the hero power's labels and the client's mode texts say the new words
