[[§13.9]] says nothing of a co-op match when one of its people deletes their account ([[§9.4]]). Migration 0039 took the match, its result and its log with either person, so a deleted account erased every finished co-op game its teammate had played with it, and a match that started between `DELETE /api/account`'s refusal and its delete vanished mid-game. A co-op seat now follows the rule migration 0012 gives a 1v1 seat, under which a finished match stays in the other player's history. Once a co-op match has ended, a deleted account's seat on it empties: it reads back as the empty id in both stores, which no caller holds, while the match, its result and its log stay, so the teammate still opens its replay from their own seat ([[R768]]). While the match is live, the delete is refused: `DELETE /api/account` answers 409 before anything is deleted, and should a match start in between, migration 0051's `duos_matches_seats_filled_unless_over_check` refuses the delete in Postgres, as the in-memory store refuses it too. A co-op room still goes with either of its people.

Rejected: cascading a finished co-op match away and refusing only a live one, which still erases the teammate's games; keeping the deleted id on the seat in the in-memory store, as its 1v1 matches do, which would let the two stores disagree on what a finished co-op match reads back.
