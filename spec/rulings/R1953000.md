Issue #1953 is the owner's next pass over the economy ([[§14]]), after #1651's. Its numbers are in
`crates/server/src/config.rs` and exported to `apps/web/src/wire/serverConfig.ts` (CLAUDE.md rule 9):

- **Points by rarity.** A spent slot ([[§14.3]], [[R1301002]]) no longer adds one point to each
  token bar: it adds the points of the rarity it rolled, pity's force included, Common 1, Rare 2,
  Epic 3, Legendary 5 and Mythic 10. A card pack's spent duplicate reads its slot's card rarity in
  `TOKEN_BAR_POINTS_BY_CARD_RARITY`; a cosmetic pack's spent slot, one with nothing left unowned
  ([[R1306001]]), reads the cosmetic rarity it rolled, not the one it fell to, in
  `TOKEN_BAR_POINTS_BY_COSMETIC_RARITY`. The two rarities are separate types ([[R1333000]]), so each
  has a table of its own, today holding the same numbers.
- **Both bars, the same points.** Each spent slot's points go to the card token bar and to the
  cosmetic token bar alike, and each bar still pays for every size it holds and keeps the
  overflow, in the opening's transaction ([[R1305000]], [[R1305001]]).
- **The sizes.** `CARD_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE` and `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_SIZE` are 200. On a complete
  collection, every slot spent and before pity, a card pack's mean points are its slots' rarity
  odds ([[R1339000]]) weighted by those points: about 1.61 for each of the four open slots and
  2.21 for the floored one, 8.65 a pack, so each bar fills once every 200 / 8.65, about 23, packs
  on average. `CARD_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS` and `COSMETIC_TOKEN_BAR_TARGET_PACKS` are that 23, and
  `economy/math.rs` holds the computation to them. A bar that already holds points keeps them:
  nothing is migrated.
- **The answer.** An opening's `bars` answers `slotPoints` beside `card`, `cosmetic` and
  `cosmeticPaid`: each slot's points to each bar, in slot order, 0 for a slot that was not spent
  until [[R1999000]] gave every slot its points.
  The opening's log carries no points and `roll_opening` is unchanged, so every logged opening
  replays as it did ([[R1301003]]). The client draws each spent slot's points flying into the
  bars, pinned at the top of the screen while the opening shows, from that answer alone, and
  computes none (CLAUDE.md rule 7); under reduced motion, or with effects off, it shows only the
  final fill.
- **The global roll** of a duplicate ([[R1661001]]) is 20 of `TIER_WEIGHT_TOTAL`'s 1,000, so 2%,
  down from 10%; `GET /api/packs` publishes it so.

This amends [[R1301002]] and [[R1306001]] (a spent slot adds its rarity's points, not a point per
duplicate) and [[R1661001]] (the rate), and changes [[§14.5]]'s sizes and targets. [[R1999000]]
widens it: every slot, not only a spent one, adds the points of the rarity it rolled, so the
sizes' mean, computed with every slot counted, holds on any collection, not only a complete one.

Amended by [[R2728000]]: the cosmetic token bar no longer holds 200 but takes a size of its own at
each step of its cycle, 50, 75, 100 and 100, with a target per step, 6, 9, 12 and 12 card packs;
the card token bar's 200 and 23 stand.

Hearthstone semantics: what a duplicate is worth scales with its rarity, as a disenchanted card's
dust does.

Rejected: a points table per bar, which the issue does not ask for and would let the two bars drift;
the rarity a cosmetic slot fell to, which is not the one it rolled; and counting the points in the
client from the slots' rarities, which would put a rules number on the client's side of the wire.
