IA #61 Meltdown Charge's "Repeat once for each mana you have left" counts 1 round plus one round for each mana its controller holds as the Cry resolves, after the Charge's own price. The count is read once and the mana is not spent: holding 1 mana means 2 rounds, holding none means 1. The rounds are one list with no state check between them ([[R59]]): every round resolves in the order it was listed, even what an earlier round destroyed. The base face sweeps every Unit and both heroes ([[§4.4]]); the Radiant face sweeps the enemy side alone, sparing its controller's side and hero.
