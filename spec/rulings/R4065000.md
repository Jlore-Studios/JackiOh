Burn and Toxic ([[§6.3]]) tick at the start of their controller's turn, before that turn's attacks ([[R3540001]]): Burn's hit, which Armor meets and an unspent Divine Shield negates, then Toxic's, which pierces Armor. A unit that first tick destroys never swings. The AI's face threat (`face_threat`, [[§9.9]]) and `could_attack_next_turn` already left out a unit its own Brittle tick destroys ([[R3793000]]), but still counted one its Burn or Toxic destroys: an enemy 4/4 on Burn 5 (S+ #37 Scorch's damage leaves Burn 5) beside a 3/3 read as 7 damage against a hero at 7, when only 3 can come, which fed the walls ([[R2976000]], `wall_switches`) and the stand-downs ([[R2021000]], `stands_down`). The Burn and Toxic come from cards in the AI's decks: S+ #37 Scorch, S+ #114 Reign, S+ #117 Zenith, S+ #118 Antumbra, S+ #119 Eclipse and S+ #121 Toxopid.

A unit now counts no attack when the first tick of its own Burn and Toxic, read as the evaluation reads it (`tick_damage`, the reading `afflicted` uses for [[R3822000]]), deals it its health or more (`afflicted_before_swing`):

- Burn's hit meets the unit's Armor and Toxic's pierces it, and its own cap holds each ([[R2179005]], [[§4.4]]);
- an unspent Divine Shield negates the first hit, so a shield alone keeps it counted, while Toxic after a Burn the shield took still lands;
- an Indestructible unit takes none ([[R46]]), so it still counts;
- with `pool_and_conversion_read` ([[R4064000]]), the side's damage pools ([[§4.4]] step 3b, S+ #42 Force Projector) meet the tick refilled, since they refill before it, and soak what they can of each hit, as `afflicted` reads them, so a unit its pool keeps standing still counts.

Only the first tick counts. Every start of the controller's turns ticks, so that tick is the one at its controller's next start, and no turn number is asked. The timing is [[R3793000]]'s: the player on turn has made this turn's attacks, as `face_threat` reads every position, unless the evaluation reads it swinging first (`NextSwing`), when its attacks come this turn, after this turn's tick, and none of its units is left out. That tick reads the counters as they stand; Burn's drop after it and the other player's turn in between change nothing it reads.

An unspent Reborn returns the unit's body summoning sick ([[R83]]), so it keeps the unit out: the body cannot attack on that turn. Since issue #4154 a Burn ticks at the end of the turn under way ([[R4154000]]), so a body a Burn alone brings back is back before its controller's turn starts and counts; the rest of this paragraph is a Toxic tick's. The exception is a body that may attack at once: one with Charge still counts, and one with Rush still counts where `could_attack_next_turn` asks about a Unit, though never for the face threat (C+ #6 Wrong-House Attacker's Radiant face is such a body). A unit that still counts is read at its own attack, as before.

It applies to both sides: an enemy unit adds no threat and the seat's own adds no pressure. It reaches the threat and pressure terms, `wall_switches` and the learned features through `face_threat`, and `stands_down` through `could_attack_next_turn`. Threat and worth stay separate terms, as Brittle's are: `afflicted` still prices the unit (its damage and its doomed share, [[R3822000]]), and `can_swing` does not change, so `unit_worth` does not read the tick twice. The flag `affliction_kills_before_swing` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, so the greedy yardstick keeps the old reading; `stands_down`, which takes no weights, reads `AI_EVAL`'s, as for [[R3793000]], so the move order the greedy baseline takes its candidates from reads it too.

It amends [[R3822000]], which left a doomed unit's attacks in `face_threat` and rejected discounting them, since `face_threat` feeds the learned features and moving it moves the network's inputs without a refit. [[R3793000]] already moved that input for Brittle, and this issue asks the same of the tick: the features still read no counter, and only a unit the first tick destroys stops counting in the threat they read. So [[R3822000]]'s "nothing is read" with its three weights at 0 now holds with this flag off too: with the flag on, a board whose unit the first tick destroys reads its threat without that unit.

Not modelled:

- counters the other player's turn adds or clears before the tick;
- the hero's own tick, which comes first and can spend the side's pools before the unit's: each unit is read alone with its side's pools refilled, as `afflicted` reads it ([[R4064000]]);
- a Reborn body's attack, which can be smaller than the unit's, where the body still counts.

Rejected: the test inside `can_swing`, since `unit_worth` reads it too and would discount the unit twice; a second copy of the tick's loop for the threat, which would let the two readings drift apart; reading every unit with an unspent Reborn as still counted, since its body cannot swing on that turn without Rush or Charge.

**Gate.** The smoke gate (`cargo jackioh gate`, fixed seeds), with both of issue #4065's flags (`affliction_kills_before_swing`, `exile_on_damage_read`) on: ai-vs-random 19 of 20 (17 needed), ai-vs-greedy 16 (10), hard-vs-easy 16 (16), with no search panic, on base `f94a31821f` and again merged onto `699914740f` ([[R4064000]]'s pools read); with both off on the first base, and on `699914740f` itself, the same three counts.

It reads only public state (the counters on a field unit, its keywords and the player on turn, which the view shows), and it adds no rule of the game.
