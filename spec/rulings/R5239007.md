FvH #112.12 Debuff Module's Radiant face says "Damage dealt also applies Toxic". A card whose text says so (`damageAlsoToxic`) is [[R4783001]]'s "Damage dealt also Burns" with Toxic for Burn: each hit it deals goes through [[§4.4]] as any hit, and then, at step 7 beside "also applies Toxic N" ([[R3540002]]), adds Toxic equal to the damage the hit dealt at step 5 to a target that survived it, a Unit still on the field and not killed or a hero with life left. A hit that dealt nothing adds none, nor does one that killed its target, and a Unit Immune to Toxic takes none ([[R4154001]]). A source that also says "also applies Toxic N" adds N and the damage dealt as one gain. It is a flag, so a card fused from two such cards adds the damage dealt once, and FvH #112 Jilliax Deluxe fused with the Radiant module carries it.
