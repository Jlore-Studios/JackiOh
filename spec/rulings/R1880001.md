S #163 Swear Loyalities adds the bundle its caster chose, then exiles "every card you own of the four you didn't choose, wherever it is" (ST-S24-2, the plan's E9). That is every card its caster holds in their own library, hand and graveyard whose definition is one of the four other bundles, base or Radiant: a Spell never stands on the field, and a card is its owner's off the field. The opponent's copies are not its caster's and stay. The piles are walked in Core #94 Genn's Greed's order, library, then hand, then graveyard, each card its own exile ([[R135]]). The cards are read as the Cry resolves, after the chosen bundle is added: copies of the chosen bundle stay, and a chosen bundle a full hand burns ([[§2.4]]) reaches the graveyard and stays there.

Narrowed by [[R2521004]] (issue #2521): Swear Loyalities now reads only its caster's hand and library, and exiles the cards of the other four factions, their Faction cards and the cards those add; the graveyard is no longer reached.
