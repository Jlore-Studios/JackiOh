Friends never takes the player off the screen (issue #3728's fifth convention, `docs/polish/10-menus.md`).
The friends panel ([[R3221000]]: the one Dialog's, a sheet, modal) opened from the shell's navigation
([[R3728000]]) is the Dialog's drawer instead, `drawer` (`data-drawer` on its scrim): down the right
edge of the window at every width, the full height of the window, its body scrolling inside it and
inside the safe-area insets it touches. Over 1024 px it sits beside the screen: its scrim is clear and
the shell's screen column narrows by the drawer's width (`--shell-drawer-w`, 360 px), so the screen
stays in sight. From 601 to 1024 px it is over the screen, the scrim dimming it as a dialog's does,
and at 600 px and under it takes the whole width, a full sheet.

It is still the one Dialog, and still modal (the page under it waits, as [[R3221000]] made the panel):
Escape closes it, the browser's Back closes it and leaves the address and the screen as they were
([[R2127000]]: a layer of its own, or the close watcher where the browser has one), a tap on the scrim
outside it closes it (one whose press began in the panel does not), its ✕ closes it, Tab stays inside,
and the focus goes back to the Friends item. What it holds is the panel as it was (the friends list,
read only while it is open and when the social socket says it changed, [[R3214000]]); the friends issue
of #3726 redesigns its inside. The board's control bar, a phone's match menu and the top bar off the
shell (practice's setup) still open it as the sheet.

Hearthstone opens its friends list over whatever screen is up, without leaving it; this follows that
and the issue (Steam's and Discord's friends lists beside the window). **Proof.**
`FriendsButton.test.tsx` opens the drawer from the shell's Friends on a menu screen and proves it is the
Dialog's drawer and not its sheet, that the path and the screen stay, and that Escape, Back and the
scrim each close it; `MenuShell.test.tsx` reads its width, its clear scrim and the screen's room over
1024 px, and its full width on a phone, from the sheets; spec 51 closes it each way at every size of
`MENU_SIZES` (Back where the browser keeps a history layer for it), and spec 38 runs axe with it open.

Rejected: a drawer that is not modal, which would leave two places to type and Tab through at once and
break [[R3221000]]'s one top layer; and the centred sheet at every width, which covers the screen the
player opened it from.
