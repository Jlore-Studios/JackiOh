An offer ([[R36]]) stands until the other player answers it or its offerer's turn ends. Nothing clears it at the turn's end, it simply stops standing, and one that lapses unanswered is not a declined one, so it blocks nothing ([[R36]]'s block is a decline's). Both players' views carry the standing offer (`drawOffer: { by }`, [[§10.8]]): it was a public action (`drawOffered`), and a reconnect shows it ([[§9.5]]). How often a player may offer is [[R36]]'s — once per turn, and not for three of their own turns after a decline — whose numbers are engine config values (`DRAW_OFFERS_PER_TURN`, `DRAW_OFFER_BLOCK_TURNS`), because hotseat and practice have no server to hold them. The answer comes only from the player who did not offer, and only while no prompt is open ([[§9.3]]). Practice's AI declines at once ([[R188]]); a hotseat hands the device to the other player to answer and back to the active player after, which is presentation and no rule
