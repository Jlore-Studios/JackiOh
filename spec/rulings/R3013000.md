The issue asked for a card "proficiency": playing the same card levels it up, and each level unlocks
a stamp to wear on it ([[R3013001]], [[R3013002]]). It left open which games count, what a play is,
the numbers of the curve and several defaults. This rules them.

**What a play is.** One entry of the seat's `played`, as the engine's fold of the log reads it
(`summarize_game`): a card dealt from the seat's own deck, played from its hand, a Unit summoned, a
Spell cast or a Trap set, its base and Radiant faces alike, since `played` names the card and keeps
its Radiant mark apart. A play of a card the seat's deck does not hold (one taken from the other
seat's hand) is left out, and so is a token. A Created card ([[R2089000]]) counts for nothing: its
plays are in `created_played`, which the quests count and proficiency never reads, so a card that
makes copies of itself cannot level itself. Hearthstone semantics: what a player is credited for is
what they chose to play from their deck.

**Which games.** Those the server ran and pays for, where it holds the log: a ladder game, an
unranked game and each game of a Conquest series ([[§14.8]]). The server counts from its own fold,
as it counts the actions XP pays for ([[R1502000]]), and the client reports nothing. The count goes
through the one reward hook, `pay_game_rewards`, in the transaction that records the result, so a
failure rolls the result and the plays back together; a play id a row could not take is dropped
before, so a count never fails a result. A practice game counts nothing: it runs in the browser
against the AI, so proficiency could be farmed on Easy. A co-op game counts nothing either: its pay
counts each person's moves without folding the log ([[R1502000]]), so the server has no `played` to
read. A ladder bot's side counts nothing ([[R1454]]), as it is paid nothing.

**From when.** Nothing is backfilled: every account starts at zero when this ships, and a game
recorded before counts nothing.

**The curve.** `PROFICIENCY_LEVEL_PLAYS` is 3, 15, 40, 100 and 200 plays: the first level comes
within a game or two of a card a deck runs twice, the last after a long commitment to that card.
`PROFICIENCY_LEVELS` is its length, 5. A card's level is how many thresholds its plays have
reached, and it never falls: a curve retuned later lowers no level a card holds.

**No ledger.** A row holds the plays, the level and the stamp worn, current state with no ledger
([[§14.11]]): a level is progress, no holding, and what it unlocks is read off it where it is worn.

Rejected: counting `created_played` too, which would let a card that copies itself, or one a
Created card stands for, climb on plays its owner never drew; counting co-op through a fold of its
log, a `SummaryReader` the co-op pay does not run, left to a later issue; counting practice, against
the issue's own default; backfilling from the stored game records, which the default rules out;
and writing each level reached as a cosmetic grant to the ledger, which would collide with the
cosmetics registry's rework of versions and augments that a separate issue is making.
