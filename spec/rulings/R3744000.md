Issue #3744: the daily super run's `e2e component` job failed on the living game ([[R3351000]]) in runs 88 and 89, after three rounds of performance work (#3633, and #3691 and #3725 for #3674, [[R3674000]]). The component server runs React's development build, whose commit of each new view under 4x CPU throttling takes 350–580 ms (#3633), past `FX_PHONE_FRAME_WORST_MS` alone. In run 89 the Mythic takeover's best play at 390x844 meant 74.3 ms a frame with a worst of 273 ms, and the Anomaly cast's best worst frame at 844x390 was 146 ms. The owner chose to stop gating on it.

**What the living-game spec still proves.** At 390x844, 360x780 and 844x390 under 4x throttling the living board plays every one of [[R3351000]]'s moments, and each one's beats mount: the Anomaly's rift, the Mythic takeover's planes, the Starship's cinematic, the broadside's attack, the Drone Battleship's layers, the Ceaseless Void's fold, the hand-to-tile flight's hold and the riffle, with the tilted hand card tilting throughout and back level after. Every play samples requestAnimationFrame for 2 s, and a window of 10 frames or fewer (`MIN_FRAMES`) is played again with events of its own, up to `FRAME_ATTEMPTS` plays, the last of which must sample more. The forced shed still sheds motes, then far auras, then faces, and a set piece's beats never.

**What it no longer judges.** Each sample's mean and worst frame are logged with whether they held `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` (50 ms) and `FX_PHONE_FRAME_WORST_MS` (120 ms), and fail nothing. Those two numbers stay [[R3351000]]'s target for the living board and the numbers #3604's work is measured by. What still gates frames: [[R2803000]]'s five phone scenes (`fx-phone-frame.cy.tsx`) keep their mean within `FX_PHONE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` in the same suite, and the busy-board frame probe (spec 50, #3605) holds the production build's p95 and long tasks to its budgets in the same daily run. Neither budget, the throttle nor a moment changes, and the director's shedding ([[R3606000]], [[R3674000]]) is untouched.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): frame times, never game state, an action, `PlayerView` or the log. Hearthstone semantics do not reach frame budgets.

**Rejected:** keeping the gate while the board is made faster (the choice on #3674), which keeps `_staging` frozen for as long as that takes; throttling at 2x, under which the Drone Battleship's arrival and the takeover still missed the worst frame in #3744's measurements, and judging only the mean there, whose thin margins on a shared runner would flake; and raising the budget, which [[R2803000]] and [[R3351000]] rejected, and which this keeps rejected: the numbers are unchanged and reported.
