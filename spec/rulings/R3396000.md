Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, name, tags and type, never anything hidden, and
nothing plays differently. The owner asked on 2026-11-06 (#3330) to "go harder on alt art": keep the theme but move
it to another style or place, so each Alt art is clearly its own. Until this issue Alt art redrew the card's own
scene re-seeded ([[R2940001]], [[R3390000]]): the same parts with another seed, which changed a median 52% of a
Classic+ picture's pixels and as little as 16%.

**An alt recipe per card.** Each of the 40 Classic+ cards offered Alt art (`version-alt-art`) draws an alt recipe
of its own (`classicPlusAltRecipes1.ts` and `classicPlusAltRecipes2.ts`, spread into `ALT_SCENE_RECIPES`), written
from #3396's brief for it: the same subject and idea in another place, time or story moment, in Classic's family.
`sceneRecipeOf` reads it under the card's Alt art seed (`<id>#alt`) alone; the card's id, every repaint's seed
([[R3017002]]: Night, Watercolor, Stained-glass window, Tarot card) and any other seed keep the normal recipe, and a
card with no alt recipe still grows its normal recipe from its Alt art seed. Every level of Alt art draws the one
alt picture ([[R3015002]]), base and Radiant.

**The same subject.** A Unit drawn as a figure keeps its figure; a figure both pictures draw is the normal one's
person, its head and outfit unchanged (only its cloth, headgear or held item may change); the J15 Fighter stays the
jet; League of Losers keeps its five Losers, each the normal picture's; and a subject that is a part of the kit (the
mushroom, the scroll, the padlocks, the jet, the tomes, the fruit) stays in the subject layer.

**Measurably elsewhere.** An alt recipe's sky shares no palette colour with its normal recipe's, and its subject
moves at least 8 units, or grows or shrinks by at least a quarter, or a figure comes or goes: a close-up, a wide or
distant view or one from above. In a real browser each Alt art window changes at least `ALT_PICTURE_CHANGE_MIN`
(0.6) of the normal window's points sampled on card-versions.cy.tsx's grid, counted with its threshold for a point
that differs, base and Radiant.

**What still holds.** Each alt picture clears its rarity's detail floor and the byte ceiling ([[R3331001]]), on
both faces; prints no text; draws one geometry on both faces, the Radiant one gilded under its rays, with no base
colour in Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]); is apart from every other picture ([[R503]]); stands its emblem small
inside the band every face shows; keeps props out of its subject layer, and League of Losers, Felinor Flagbearer and
J-lease J-Jungle EX-plorer mark the placements that move on their props beat ([[R2715001]]). A Radiant face keeps
its marks at every Alt art level. Six parts join the scene kit for the briefs' places: a boat, a tent and a waterfall
(`sceneKitPlaces.ts`), and bamboo, a cactus and a fox (`sceneKitNature.ts`).

Rejected: alt recipes as `#alt` keys inside `SCENE_RECIPES`, which would count them among the cards with recipes and
reach the repaints' seeds too; giving the repaints alt recipes, which the issue does not ask; measuring the pixels in
vitest, where jsdom has no rasterizer; holding each alt picture only to "hue or composition", where every brief names
a new palette and the skies can share none; dropping the detail floor or the byte ceiling for alt pictures; changing a
figure's head or outfit, which would make it someone else; standing in kit parts the briefs name with others; and
splitting the 40 cards into smaller pull requests.

Amended by [[R3398000]] (issue #3398): the alt recipes hold the 61 Core cards offered Alt art too, under the same rules, and a Core alt recipe draws its subject from the kits, never its painter's own.
