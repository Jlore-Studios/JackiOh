Damage Meditative #72 The Banisher deals to a Unit marks it (MD-D31, `marked_exiled`), and the next
state check exiles every marked Unit ahead of deaths ([[§4.4]] step 7, [[§4.5]] step 1): the Unit has
not died, so no Death fires, Reborn does not return it and nothing counts it destroyed. Its Cleave hits
mark too. Damage a Divine Shield, Indestructible or the zero rule stopped marks nothing. A source that
dies in the same combat still dies.
