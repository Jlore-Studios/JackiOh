---
id: R3060000
title: One press of End turn, a one-tap choice or Ready is sent once for the view it was pressed on
cards: "§10.10, R846"
proven_in:
  - apps/web/src/game/Game.test.tsx
---
A board waits for the game's answer after a press, and until a new view arrives its moves stay live, so a double click on End turn, a double tap on a one-tap choice or a double press of the mulligan's Ready sent twice, each with a fresh nonce that the engine's nonce check could not tell from a second intent. Usually the second was refused and left a red "it is not your turn" line on the board for the whole of the opponent's turn; but when the card played gave an extra turn ([[R846]]) the same player is still active, so the second End turn was legal and ended the extra turn before it was seen. The board now remembers the last `endTurn`, `answer` or `mulligan` it sent and the newest view it was sent against, and drops an equal body sent against that same view. A new view releases it, as does an error line that gains text, whether a refusal or an offline send, so a retry goes through; the line clearing does not, since the client clears it on every send. Concede, auto end turn, plays, attacks, activations, buys, trades and the draw offer and its answers are never dropped, and hotseat, whose views are new objects on every render, never blocks. This only collapses a duplicate intent against one view and enforces no rule: the client sends intent (CLAUDE.md rule 7) and the engine still refuses what it must ([[§10.10]]).
