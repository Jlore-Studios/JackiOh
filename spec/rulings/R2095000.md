On a screen wide enough for two boards, a player may show the teammate's duel beside their own, live, instead of flipping with the portal. The spec said only that a portal on the board flips it to the teammate's duel, read-only ([[§13.6]]); it left open whether both duels may show at once, and Hearthstone has no co-op board to borrow the answer from, so this note decides it.

- **The toggle.** The board rail holds "Both boards" (`duos-both`, `aria-pressed`) beside the portal. The choice is kept per device in `localStorage` under `DUOS_BOTH_KEY`, read and written in `try`/`catch`; it starts off, so a player who never presses it sees today's board.
- **The second board** is the portal's board: `<Game view={view.teammate} legal={NOTHING} onAction={ignore} replay>`, plus silence (`silent`: it feeds no audio and mounts no effects layer or showcase, which find their anchors on the whole page and would draw the teammate's duel onto the own board). The own board's drag never drops on it: a replay board is no drop target of another board's drag, so a card dropped there does nothing. Its cards can be hovered and inspected, and the ping wheel works on it ([[§13.10]]); a ping marks whichever board shows its duel. Each board keeps its own `key`, so neither animates the other duel's events.
- **Passing** ([[§13.5]]): a hand card dragged onto the teammate's board, or onto the portal, passes nothing; the pass control on an inspected hand card is the only way to pass. This note first had the drop pass the card, as a drop on the portal then did; issue #2619 removed both drags, since they ignored "Drag to play" and a pass is final and costs mana.
- **The portal** stays mounted while both boards show: CSS hides it on a wide screen, and it comes back when the toggle is off. Below the width two boards fit, the toggle is hidden, CSS draws only the showing board, and the portal flips between them, so the board works as today: the rail (the portal, the life strip, the concede offer) rides whichever board the portal names, as the single board's does, so the board drawn always carries the way back. Pressing the toggle opens both boards on the own one.
