A card the view reports changed by Degrade, Upgrade or KY's Constant is marked wherever the viewer may read it. Each change is better or worse for its controller: a stat, a numbered keyword or an X going up is better, except Tribute, which is better going down; a keyword added is better and one removed worse; a declared number follows its `better`; a value set outright that is not a declared number is neither. A moved number in the text sits in a dotted box with ▲ or ▼, a tuned stat carries the same glyph, an added keyword is a "+" chip and a removed one a struck "−" chip, and the card is marked Upgraded (▲, every change better), Degraded (▼, every change worse) or Tuned (◆). The inspect overlays list each change in words beside the printed text. A Brittle count shows as a cracked-glass badge with its number, an enchantment as a badge with its words, a backrow pile as its depth, and a card's lines of code in its detail line.
