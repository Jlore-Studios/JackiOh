S #150's eighth entry reads "For the rest of the game, all Acclaimed cards have 'Death: Cast a random Call to Chaos'" (ST-S25-5).

- **What it covers.** Every card that prints the Acclaimed tag, either player's, on the field now or arriving later, for the rest of the game. It is no granted Death written on each card ([[R1107]]): it is the engine's watcher, a `never` modifier on its caster (`ModifierKind::DeathWatch`), which outlasts the card that made it ([[R76]]). It prints no quoted label of its own on the cards ([[R1445]]); its badge is the entry's words ([[R169]]).
- **When it answers.** After each `destroyed` of an Acclaimed card ([[§4.5]]), queued with the event's other triggers in [[R68]]'s order. The cast is the player's who controlled the card as it died ([[R172]]), whoever holds the watcher.
- **The cast.** A random Call to Chaos of the pool that names no set, cast as a play ([[R70]]) with no `self`, so it is a new chain with [[R28]]'s cap of its own, its card a base Call.
- **The bound.** A player holds at most `CHAOS_STINKY_DEATH_WATCH_CAP` (3) of these watchers, as M #95's rest-of-game cast is bounded ([[R1241]]); a roll of the entry for a player at the cap resolves into nothing, with no re-roll.
