The owner asked in issue #4037 to remove the public How JackiOh uses AI page, so [[R2631000]]'s
`/ai` is gone entirely: no route, no chunk, no document title, no site-footer link, no sitemap or
`static-pages.ts` entry, no `_redirects` rule, no Chinese translation entry and no
`docs/compliance.md` section. `/ai` now draws the client's `NotFound` with the unknown-path title,
exactly like any other path nothing serves.

`assets/voice/SOURCES.md` for the voice lines, whose opening line no longer points at the page. What
the page described is unchanged: the practice AI, the ranked AI opponents ([[R2614000]]) and the
co-op team, and the Terms' "AI opponents" section.

Rejected: keeping a stub page or a redirect at `/ai`, which would leave a public address answering
for a statement the owner asked to take down.
