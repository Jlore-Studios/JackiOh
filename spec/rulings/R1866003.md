Stinkyscape #18.3 CASPER's "if you Floated on your last turn" reads the Float ([[§6.2]], [[R1563000]]) of the last turn end of its controller's that it saw: a trigger on its controller's `turnEnded` remembers on the card that player and the event's `unspentMana`, and at their next start of turn ([[R62]]) it votes ([[R1843000]]) when the turn end it remembers is its controller's and held a Float. A turn that spends everything is no Float, and the opponent's Float never counts. A CASPER that saw none of its controller's turns end does not vote: one that arrived since that turn ended, one stolen since, whose memory names the other player, or one back on the field after leaving it, which forgets what it saw ([[R78]]). Hearthstone would read the player's last turn wherever the card stood; the engine keeps no player's Float past the start of their next turn (the turn log's `unspentAtEnd` is rebuilt before the start-of-turn triggers run), and a per-player record of it would ride in every shipped game's state and move the golden traces (`docs/stinkyscape-set.md` D3). Rejected: that per-player record. The ruling departs from Hearthstone only for a CASPER that arrived after its controller's last turn ended.
