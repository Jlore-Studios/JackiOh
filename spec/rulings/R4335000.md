Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, endpoint or response changes.

**The tabs show their names alone.** Cards, Tokens, Sets and Players stay raised buttons with a border, the open one filled and ringed in the selected colour ([[R4279001]]), but no tab has the line of what it shows under its name any more ("Win rate and tier of every card", "Tokens and other cards made in games", "How each set does", "Every player's games and win rate"), on any screen. The owner asked for them gone: the names say enough, and the row reads as one line of buttons on a desktop as it already did on a phone.

**The tab row never shrinks.** On `/stats` the tabs were hidden: the page is one screen tall ([[R1581000]]), its panel scrolls inside itself and may shrink, and the tab row above it could shrink too. The row scrolls sideways when its tabs do not fit, which makes its automatic minimum height 0, so a long panel (the Cards table) squeezed it to a sliver of 9 to 14 pixels that cut off every tab. The shared tab row (`tabs.css`, every page's `Tabs`) now never shrinks: it keeps its tabs' height and the panel takes the rest. The Collection and Together screens had kept their own rows from shrinking (`flex: none`), and lose those copies to it (issue #4339).

**A menu fits without clipping a tab row.** Spec 46 held `/stats` to one screen ([[R1581000]]) and passed while its tab row was crushed, since crushing it is how the page fit. Its one-screen check now also fails a page, at every menu size, on which any tab row is shorter than its tabs (issue #4339).

Rejected:
- Keeping the hints on a desktop alone: the owner asked for them gone.
- A minimum height on `/stats`' row alone: every page whose `Tabs` sits on a one-screen page has the same row, and a fixed height would clip a tab whose text wraps or whose font is larger.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
