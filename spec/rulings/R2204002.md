[[R2201001]] holds Legendary and Mythic cards out of every random pool a game with `poolHold` draws from, and its weighted tables leave a held entry out. Anomaly Found!'s random board ([[R1879000]]) is such a pool, of boards rather than cards, and one of the ten boards, MT Monument, lays S #152 Sanctum, a Legendary. So a held game draws its Anomaly from the boards that lay no held card, each still equally likely, whether the card draws as it arrives ([[R2247000]]) or as it resolves, and S #151 Call to Chaos (Scape Edition)'s Anomaly roll does the same. A board the card already holds is laid as it is. An unheld game leaves every board in, so its draws, and every replay and golden trace, are unchanged.

The Training Grounds release found the gap: its cards joined `lesson_pools.rs`'s random decks, and game 8 then dealt S #167 Scanning Probe Mk2, whose find laid Monument under the hold.

Hearthstone semantics: as [[R2201001]], the hold narrows each pool and every other rule of the pool stands.
