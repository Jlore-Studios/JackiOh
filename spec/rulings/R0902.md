CN Peptides' Lucky roll keeps the result better for the caster: the Buffs when the target is a Unit they control, the destroy when it is an enemy one. The target's controller as the Spell resolves decides which. With no target nothing is flipped ([[R129]]), and the Lucky is the face's Lucky X as Nerf and Buff have moved it, read as a Fruit reads a Spell's. A Lucky given to the card counts too: the Lucky is the printed one plus the given one ([[R1438]]).
