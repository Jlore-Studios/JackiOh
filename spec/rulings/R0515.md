title: A budgeted pick shows its total

A `pick` prompt with a budget (Classic #44's "total cost of (5) or less") draws each option as its card's face at the cost the option carries and keeps a running total in [[R432]]'s words, "(3) of (5) spent"; every card not yet picked whose cost would take the total past the budget is greyed, says "Over the budget" and is not added when pressed, while a picked card can always be taken back. Confirm waits until the picks are within the prompt's `min` and `max` and the total within the budget. All of it is read off the prompt's `cost`s, `budget`, `min` and `max`; the engine still refuses an answer over the budget. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7)
