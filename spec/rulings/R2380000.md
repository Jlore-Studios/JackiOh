[[R1839000]] made a Dormant card no Unit for every rule that reads Units, the AI's evaluation among them, before any shipped card printed Dormant, so the AI counted one as an empty zone until it woke and almost never played Stinkyscape's Imprisoned Units (`training/history/sweep-2026-10-29-2.md`). No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** The evaluation (`material`) walks `units_on_field_of` and counts each Dormant card at the `unit_worth` of the same card awake, times `slumber_turn_share` for each turn left on its countdown ([[R1839001]]) or `slumber_waiting_share` when it waits for an effect (the Dormant Starship's launch). `AI_EVAL` starts both at 0.5, untuned; the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL` keeps 0. Its status and countdown are on both seats' views ([[R185]]), so the enemy's counts alike, on its side of the ledger.

**What does not change.** It is still no Unit for every other rule: `legal_actions` offers nothing through or onto it, and the face-threat reads and the lethal solver (`active_units_of`) ignore it until it wakes. A Dormant Starship standing in the backrow ([[R2249000]]) counts its slumber price alone, never a backrow price on top, as every Unit standing there counts its own price once ([[R2490000]]).

**Rejected.** Counting it in full, though it can neither attack nor block until it wakes; pricing it by its cost, (2) for a 10/6.
