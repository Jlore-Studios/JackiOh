The owner asked in #2778 for the ranks to be split into Constructed and All Random: a deck built to win and
a deck dealt at random are different skills, and one rank over both measured neither ([[§9.12]]).

**Two ladders.** A season holds two ladders:
- **Constructed**, which the Best of 1 and Conquest queues play for;
- **All Random**.

Each ladder has all of these of its own:
- a hidden Glicko-2 rating ([[R603]]) for every person and every ladder bot ([[R610]]);
- placements and a Grape rank ([[R605]]), a tier floor and the season's peaks ([[R607]]);
- a win streak ([[R606]]) and a season record ([[R2255000]]);
- Jlorious, the top 100 of its own Mythic Grape ([[R608]]);
- a leaderboard.

**What a game moves.** A ranked game moves only its mode's ladder, read off what made the match (`matches_mode_of`):
- Best of 1 and Conquest move Constructed, a Conquest series once as it ends ([[R604]]).
- All Random moves its own ladder.
- A ranked match nothing names a mode for reads as Constructed.

The game rates from both sides' ratings on that ladder and ranks against that ladder's season. A person new to one ladder starts it as a new player there, whatever they hold on the other: R603's new rating, deviation and volatility, and R605's placements. The record of the game names its ladder ([[R611]]).

**Matchmaking.** A ticket freezes its player's rating on the ladder its mode plays for. So R640001's rating window, the bot fallback's closest bot and the rule that a player above every bot meets only a grinder ([[R1453]], [[R1555000]]) all compare on that ladder.
- The top bots' grind is planned on each ladder against that ladder's people, and a bot either plan names grinds.
- The five recent opponents a player is not paired with again stay counted over every match, whichever ladder it moved, so a bot met on one ladder is not met again at once on the other.

**Seasons.** A new season's soft reset ([[R609]]) pulls each ladder's ratings toward that ladder's own mean. A rated game on either ladder makes a rated player of both.

**Reads.** Every read names its ladder, and no read carries a rating ([[R612]]):
- `GET /api/ranked` and `GET /api/leaderboard` answer the ladder `?ladder=` names (`constructed`, the default, or `random`).
- `GET /api/matches/:matchId/ranks` and the play telemetry's rank bucket ([[R1442]]) answer the match's own ladder.
- The account screen shows the Constructed rank, record and badges, then the All Random ones.
- The Play online lobby shows the rank of the ladder the picked mode plays for, and links that ladder's board ([[R661]]).
- The leaderboard has a Constructed / All Random switch, with All Random in the address as `?ladder=random`.

**The Jlorious card back** ([[R2755000]]) is granted by reaching Jlorious on either ladder.

**Migration 0076.** Nobody loses the rank they held when the ladders split:
- Each person's hidden rating is copied onto the All Random ladder; their current one stays as Constructed's.
- Each bot's rating is copied onto both ladders.
- Each row of the newest season is copied onto All Random. Older seasons' badges stay on Constructed, the one ladder they were played on.
- The new rating columns are on no client's column grant, as the Constructed rating's are not ([[R612]]).

Hearthstone semantics: Standard and Wild keep separate ranks and separate leaderboards, each moved only by
its own format's ranked games.

Rejected:
- One rating with two visible ranks, which would let a deck built to win rate a player's random games.
- Starting All Random from nothing at the split, which would take a rank from every player who earned one in All Random.
- Copying older seasons' badges onto All Random, which would credit a badge to a ladder it was never played on.
