**Why.** [[R4082000]] gives every Created card a Tier and an Adjusted win rate, read against the other Created cards. Those two are the only figures a balance decision can rest on. The others say little, for the reasons R4082000 gives:

- **Win rate** is the made seats' raw rate, which carries the play selection every Created card shares.
- **Used, Not used and Used vs not used** carry [[R377]]'s played-delta bias, and a summoned token has no unused side at all.
- **Used Radiant and Used normal** split that same biased side.

The deck table was cleared the same way ([[R3184000]], [[R3185000]]). The client computes no new figure (CLAUDE.md rule 7): every one is the server's, and the tier fit below only measures the server's range against the server's band, as [[R3184001]]'s does.

**Columns.** Both sections on the Tokens tab ([[R4279001]]), the Tokens and the Other created cards, stay apart ([[R3324002]]) and show only Card (the thumbnail), Name, Tier and Adjusted. Games stays behind the tab's Columns toggle, as in the deck table, since it is the sample behind the tier: the seats that made one, used or not ([[R2370000]]). Win rate is gone from both tables, with its sort key. The Columns group on the Tokens tab offers Games alone, since its tables have no Play rate.

**Sort.** The Tokens tab sorts by the Cards tab's one sort: the same sort select and direction button, kept in the address as `sort` and `dir`, and every header of either section sets it, as a deck header does ([[R3184002]]). Changing it on one tab changes it on the other.

- The select offers Name, Games, Tier and Adjusted, the columns the Created tables have.
- Tier puts S first, then orders the cards of a tier by tier fit ([[R3184001]]), a card with no fit after those with one either way, then by Adjusted.
- Adjusted, Name and Games sort as in the deck table: a row below [[R654]]'s floor, with no Adjusted, last either way.
- Each section opens on Adjusted, best first, rows below the floor last ([[R4082000]]).
- A Win rate or Play rate sort, which the Cards tab offers and the Created tables lack, reads as Adjusted there, keeping its direction, so no sort leaves the Tokens tab unsorted.
- Rows that tie keep the server's order ([[R3184002]]).

**Views and ranges.** The Tokens tab has the Cards tab's View (Table or Grid) and Show 95% ranges, one state for both tabs, kept in the address as `view` and `ranges`.

- In Grid, each section is a grid of its own under its title, and a Created card shows as a deck card does: its face (or its plain text, [[R3887000]]), then its tier chip, its Adjusted and its games, and no Win rate. Pressing the card opens its drill-down, as a row does ([[R4155000]]).
- With the ranges shown, the Adjusted range ([[R2895000]]) shows under the Adjusted, and the tier fit under the tier, in the Table and in the Grid alike, from the floor only.

**Filters.** The Tokens tab has every filter of the Cards tab: the search, the set, rarity and cost, the match type and now the keyword ([[R4279001]] left it off). One keyword state serves both tabs, and its list stays the deck cards' keywords, so a keyword no deck card has is not offered.

**Statistics panel.** The card detail's Statistics panel (`CardStatsBlock`, [[R3185000]]) shows a card no deck holds, a token among them, by its Games, Tier and Adjusted win rate alone, with no Win rate or Play rate. Below the floor its Tier and Adjusted read "—". A card a deck holds keeps its five figures.

**Response.** Each `created` row of `GET /api/stats/cards` sends only `id`, `name`, `cost`, `rarity`, `set`, `games`, `hasEnoughGames`, `adjustedWinRate`, `adjustedLow`, `adjustedHigh`, `tier` and `window`. The `createdPlayed*` and `createdNotPlayed*` figures, their Radiant and normal splits and `createdPlayedDelta` are no longer sent, as [[R3185000]] trimmed `cards`. `tiers.created` stays, and so does the route, which deploy jobs poll (CLAUDE.md rule 13). The records, the totals and the store are unchanged, with no migration: `jackioh-server stats-cards`, `cargo jackioh stats report` and `stats compare` keep the full Created table as development tools ([[R2089000]]).

**Rejected.**

- **A sort of the Tokens tab's own.** The tabs already share one set of filters ([[R4279001]]); a second sort would need address keys of its own, and the same select would mean two things.
- **Win rate in the Grid**, as the deck grid shows it: it is the figure this ruling takes away.
- **Keywords of the tokens' own in the list.** One state serves both tabs, so a token's keyword would empty the Cards tab.
- **Keeping the fields for an older client.** Only a page open across the deploy reads them, and only until it reloads; meanwhile its Win rate reads "NaN%", as [[R3185000]] accepted for its own fields.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
