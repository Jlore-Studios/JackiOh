`DECK_SIZE` is 25, where it was 20. A deck a player brings holds exactly 25 cards ([[§2.6]],
[[§9.4]] L2), and so does every seat that plays with this spec's resources (`HUMAN_HANDICAP`,
[[R180]]): a 1v1 seat, a Duos seat, a ladder bot's and an Easy AI's. A Conquest trio shares no card,
so it needs 75 distinct cards, and `seed-accounts` builds its accounts three 25-card decks for one.
The database's own copy, `app.settings.deck_size`, which the deck save reads for D2, is 25 from
migration 0055.

The practice tiers keep the edge they had over a human: a Medium AI's deck holds
`MEDIUM_EXTRA_DECK_CARDS` (5) more than `DECK_SIZE` and a Hard AI's `HARD_EXTRA_DECK_CARDS` (10)
more, 30 and 35 where they were 25 and 30 ([[§9.9]], [[R184]]). Easy is still a human's resources
exactly. The tutorial opponent's `AI_TUTORIAL` keeps its 12-card list ([[R290]]).

Fatigue comes later. A 25-card deck holds 21 after the four-card deal ([[R1156000]]), so one draw a
turn takes its last card on the seat's 21st turn, and the eighth empty draw, the 29th turn, kills a
hero at 30 health ([[§2.4]]). Two decks that do nothing end at player-turn 57, Player 1's 29th turn,
where they ended at 47. `TURN_CAP_PLAYER_TURNS` stays 60, still a backstop, and the match ceiling
stays 120 minutes ([[§2.5]], [[R389]]).

A deck saved at 20 stays a draft ([[R1705001]]), and a game made at 20 folds as it was made
([[R1705003]]).

Hearthstone semantics: there is one deck size, and every deck a player brings meets it exactly
(Hearthstone's is 30). The tiers are this spec's own, and an edge counted in cards past a player's
deck moves with that deck.

Rejected: leaving Medium and Hard at 25 and 30 cards, which would shrink their edge over a human
from five and ten cards to none and five, so Medium would play a human's deck size.
