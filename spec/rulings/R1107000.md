[[§9.5]] lets a player watch a friend's live match, each view `SPECTATE_DELAY_MS` after the state it shows, and did not say when the watch ends. The server sent the view with the result and then kept the socket open, the watcher in the players' count and the watch screen reading Connected, until the reaper dropped the actor `REMATCH_OFFER_TTL_MS` later. As a Hearthstone spectator leaves the game once it ends:

- **Once the match is over, a watcher that has been sent its friend's seat's last view, the one with the result, is let go**: its socket is closed with 1000 and the reason "the match is over", and both players are told the new count, as when a watcher leaves.
- **A watcher behind is sent every view it is owed first**, oldest first, the last one included; nothing is skipped.
- **A watcher let in after the last view came due** (only before the result lands, since the hello refuses a finished match) is sent that view and closed at once.
- **A void is unchanged**: [[R679]] closes every watcher at once with `MATCH_VOIDED_CLOSE_CODE`, and an actor that goes closes them with 1001.

Rejected: leaving the socket open until the reaper drops the actor; clearing each seat's record once every watcher has the last view, which saves one view a seat (the record already holds only that one once it is due) and would leave a watcher let in afterward with nothing to be sent and nothing to close it.
