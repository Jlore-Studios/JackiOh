The release (issue #4597) turns the browser's content, the WebAssembly module's `assault_content()`, from `assault_placeholder_content` to `assault_catalog_content` over the build's catalog ([[R4579001]]): the set's cards by tag and the twelve Commanders of `assault.json`. `assault_content_at` ([[R3496000]]) answers a carried version with `assault_catalog_content` over that version's shipped catalog, never the placeholder: until the release no production build served `/assault` (`ASSAULT_OPEN`), so no player holds an attempt dealt from the placeholder, and every version carried from the release on deals the set's cards. A carried catalog from before the release whose set lacks a Commander's cards deals no such Commander, and a day it cannot deal is refused, as any other.
