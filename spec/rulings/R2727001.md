While a card is in a hand, a Buff's keyword row ([[§6.3]], [[R386]]) also removes a Crater the card has of its own, from a card of any type (`TUNE_BUFF_HAND_REMOVES`, read beside `TUNE_BUFF_REMOVES`): printed, as on C #80 BOOM! Big Max, C+ #1 Doom Shroom, C+ #34 Memory Leak and S #155 Comet Crash, given, or added by a Nerf in a hand ([[R2657005]]), so a Crater a Nerf gave there may come off again. It never removes one an aura lends ([[R442]]). In a deck or on the field a Buff leaves Crater alone, as before, and Crater stays among the harmful keywords (`TUNE_HARMFUL_KEYWORDS`), so a Nerf still never removes it. Removals come first in the row's one uniform draw, in the order the card lists its keywords ([[R442]], [[R2657005]]), and a card a multiplier tunes more than once keeps to its first draw's way ([[R1160]]). The change is `tuning`, part of the card in every zone ([[R78]]): a Unit Buffed free of Crater in a hand Locks no zone as it arrives ([[R783010]]), a Trap none as it fires face-down ([[R783011]]), and a Field Spell none as it enters ([[R1850006]]). The owner asked for it (issue #2727) as the other half of [[R2727000]]; the cards whose Buffs reach a hand include C+ #70 Chaos Machine, Radiant C+ #71 Book of Buff, T-AI-10 Fine-Tuning, M #82 Medina Outfitter, M #83 Medina Enforcer and M #87 Tatches the Totem. A game recorded before the change in which a Buff reached a card in a hand that carried Crater no longer folds to its recorded hash and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). This amends [[R386]]'s and [[R2657005]]'s keyword clauses.
