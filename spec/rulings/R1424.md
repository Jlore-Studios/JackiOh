The tribal tags are the ones that name a people or a faction: Human, Felinor, KY, CN and Jlockeed (`TRIBAL_TAGS`). "A card with a Tribal tag" (Meditative #87 Tatches the Totem) has at least one of them; a card that has all five is "All Tribes", which #87's tags list and the card frame prints; and "tribal tag based hate" (#25 Blue-Eyes White Felinor is immune to it) is an effect that picks its cards by having or lacking a tribal tag, as Core #2 Bigot's "non-Human" and #43 Big Felinor's "non-Felinor" do. Book, Fruit, Pancake, AI, Plague, Catalyst, Prime, Acclaimed, Call to Chaos, Quickdraw, Wincon and Token name card families and mechanics, and no rule reads them as tribes.
