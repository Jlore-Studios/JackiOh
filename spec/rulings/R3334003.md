Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7). A Starship died with the generic Unit's rows.

**The wreck.** When a Starship's `destroyed` plays (Dormant or launched, as the board shows it), outside a wipe:

- its newest modules, at most `STARSHIP_WRECK.modulesMax`, blow off one at a time from `blowFrom` to `blowTo` of the row, each flung `fling` of its picture away from the hull's middle and spinning, with sparks;
- at `breakAt` the hull breaks in two, each half of its picture turning `halfTurnDeg` away and falling apart, in smoke and a shake (`trauma`), as its card is hidden;
- at `debrisAt` debris drifts across its side's backrow behind it;
- at `scorchAt` a brief scorch settles on its zone, gone `scorchTailMs` after the row.

It is quieter than the launch, with no hold. Inside a wipe the board clear's set piece ([[R2800000]]) swallows it, as it does every death.

**The sound.** In the death sting's place a Starship groans as its hull breaks (`starshipWreck`, at `breakAt`); its line and its effect stay.

**Hidden information.** It reads only the `destroyed` and the ship's public `starship` list ([[R202]]); a Starship R97 hides ([[R97]]) dies with the generic rows. Every cue starts inside its row and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` of it ([[R200]]), scaled with the speed ([[R201]]).
