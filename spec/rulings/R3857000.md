Hearthstone announces a gain of Armor, and [[§10.3]] says every visible state change emits an event. Armor a hero keeps for the rest of the game ([[R124]]: Tank Up's, [[R757]], and the cards' that give it) changed the badge on its portrait in silence: no event, no log line, no cue.

**The rule.** `effects::gain_hero_armor`, the only write to a hero's Armor after setup, emits `armorGained { player, amount, armor }` when it gives more than 0, and nothing for 0 or less. `player` is the hero's seat, `amount` the Armor gained and `armor` the hero's Armor after the gain as the view shows it ([[§10.8]], `damage::hero_armor_of`: the kept Armor with Armor Up's modifier and any Going Long text's added), so the number on the event is the number on the badge. Armor Up's temporary Armor stays a player modifier, announced by `modifierChanged` as before, and reports no gain.

**Nothing answers it.** Like [[R1361]]'s `damageAbsorbed` it is a report withheld from the trigger loop: no rule, trap, trigger or quest reads it, it takes no number in [[R68]]'s order, and no state hash moves, so a recorded game replays to the same hash ([[R768]]).

**Who sees it.** Both seats, as it is: a player and two numbers, and the Armor was public already. It takes no room in [[R168]]'s floor, so the view's window holds what it held before the report existed, the reports among it; and the golden traces' texts leave it out, as they leave out [[R2783000]]'s markers ([[R1956001]]'s way), so a trace recorded on either side of the change replays on the other and no card plays differently.

**On the client.** The game log writes "Your hero gained 2 Armor" or "The opponent's hero gained 2 Armor"; the hero's `ANIMATIONS` row ticks its Armor badge for 300 ms under the `armor` recipe, the full shield [[R1361]]'s whole hit blooms but with no shake, since nothing struck the hero ([[§10.10]]); and its `SOUND_CUES` row plays `buff`, the sweep of a stat gained that a Buff and a gained keyword play, centered on the hero. The bright ring (`armorRing`) stays the sound of a hit Armor took whole alone ([[R1363]]), one of [[R1365]]'s sounds of their own, so a player who hears it knows a hit was absorbed.

**Rejected:** `counterChanged` with a new `CounterKind::Armor`: Armor is not a counter, nothing ticks it down or removes it as one, and the client's counter badges would misread it. The stored field alone as `armor`, which differs from the badge under Armor Up or a Going Long text. `armorRing` as its sound: a gain would ring as a hit absorbed does. A new synthesized sound of its own: sound design beyond an announcement, for a later change if the sweep proves too plain. Claiming the cards that give Armor as playing differently with a fragment, [[R2652001]]'s way: the report changes how a game is shown, not how it is played.
