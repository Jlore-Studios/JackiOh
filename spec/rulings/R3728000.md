No screen had a navigation of its own: each had BackLink's top bar ([[R3218000]]), and the landing's
three doors were the only map of the game, so going from the Collection to Play to Friends was Back,
then a door. Issue #3728 gives the menus one frame, the same on every screen
(`docs/polish/10-menus.md` holds its conventions and wireframes, which the other UI issues of #3726
build to).

**Which screens.** `MenuShell` (`apps/web/src/shell/MenuShell.tsx`) wraps every route of `paths`
(`net/navigate.ts`) in `main.tsx`, and a path nothing is at, but three kinds: a board's path
(`isBoardPath`: a match, a watched game, a co-op match, a daily game ([[§9.13]]), practice and its setup,
which share the game's path), the Conquest series screen (part of a game's flow), and the dev screens (the hotseat, the
four-seat co-op board, the motif gallery). Sign-in, the password reset and the invite-code screen are
inside. A board drawn on a menu path (a replay, a dungeon fight, `game/boardShown.ts`) takes the
navigation away while it is up; the screen is not remounted, since the shell draws the screen's column
first and the navigation after it, which is also why Tab reaches the screen's Back first. The shell
stays mounted from one menu to the next, and the screen's boundary, keyed by its path, still remounts
inside it.

**The navigation.** Home, Play, Collection, Shop and Friends, in that order, each a link that moves in
place (the current screen's marked `aria-current="page"`), but Friends, which is the friends panel's
button ([[R3728001]]) once the friends reads have answered and a link to Play together before that
(signed out, or an account the server refuses). Friends leaves the top bar on every screen inside the
shell, and the landing's corner: it is in the navigation instead. This amends [[R3218000]]'s end group,
which off the shell (practice's setup) keeps it. The width decides the layout, never the device, as an
iPad's window can be any width in Split View or Stage Manager, on the named breakpoints of issue
#3227 (each the widest window of the smaller layout): over `tablet` (1025 px and wider) a rail down the
left, each item an icon and its label; from 601 to 1024 px (an upright iPad) the same rail with the
icons alone, the label still the item's name; at `phone` and under (600 px) a tab bar at the foot of
the window. The issue said "at least 1024" and "under 600"; no listed device sits at 1024 or 600 px, and
the named breakpoints keep every sheet on the same two edges. Every item is at least `--hit-min`
(44 px) square.

**The insets.** The screen's column is padded by the top inset, the rail by the top, bottom and left
ones, the screen's column by the right one beside the rail, and on a phone the tab bar by the bottom
and side ones and the column by its height and the side insets. A menu that is one screen tall
([[R1581000]]: index.css's `.one-screen`, the tavern, the hub, the collection, the shop, the deck
screens, the landing) fills `--shell-screen-h`, the window less the top inset and the tab bar, instead
of `100dvh` (its fallback outside the shell), so it still fits one screen with the tab bar under it:
this amends [[R1581000]]'s one screen to the room the shell leaves. A toast fixed at the foot of the
screen's column keeps clear of the navigation the same way: the deck builder's status toast, drawn under
the tab bar's layer, and the undo toast ([[R2119000]], on the deck workshop and Play together), drawn
over it, sit 16 px above a phone's tab bar and the bottom inset under it (`--shell-bottom`), and the
status toast centers on the screen's column beside the rail (`--shell-start`, the rail's width), as it
centered on the pool before, its widths leaving the rail out.

**The workshop's split.** The deck workshop's list, pool and sidebar sat side by side from 1101 px; beside
the labelled rail's 176 px that needs a 1277 px window (`WORKSHOP_SPLIT_MIN_WIDTH_PX`), so an iPad in
landscape (1180 px) takes the halves in turns and a 1280 px desktop still splits. Issue #3734 took this
back: from 601 px the halves sit side by side ([[R3734001]]), and only a phone takes turns.

**Sizes.** Every menu layout spec runs one list, `MENU_SIZES` (`e2e/support/config.ts`): desktop 1440x900
and 1280x720, iPad landscape 1180x820 and 1366x1024, iPad portrait 820x1180 and 744x1133 (iPad mini),
phone 390x844. Spec 46 holds every menu to one screen at each, spec 47 every control's tap area
([[R2122000]]'s bands, drawn under a coarse pointer at any width, so over 600 px the spec emulates
touch), spec 51 clicks through the five destinations from Home at each, and spec 38 runs axe over the
shell. Two controls that fell short under a finger at the new sizes take their room in their own
sheets: a row of the statistics page's card table, 39 px tall on an iPad's width, is `--hit-min` tall
under a finger (`routes/stats.css`), and the dev build's hotseat link on Home, whose band took the taps
of the doors above it at 1280x720, is too (`routes/landing.css`).

Hearthstone semantics do not reach a page's chrome; this follows the issue's conventions (one navigation
in one place, as Marvel Snap and Legends of Runeterra keep it, and Apple's iPadOS guidelines for the
frame). **Proof.** `MenuShell.test.tsx` walks every entry of `paths` through the route table and fails on
a menu path outside the shell or a board's inside it, proves the navigation's order, its current item
and its moves, that a board takes it away and keeps the screen mounted, and reads the sheet's widths,
insets and the workshop's split from `menu-shell.css`, and the two toasts' places from their sheets;
`FriendsButton.test.tsx` proves Friends is in the navigation and in no top bar on every signed-in menu,
in the bar off the shell and on a board, and a link signed out; specs 46, 47, 51 and 38 measure the
drawn shell at every size of the list.

Rejected: new 599 and 1023 px breakpoints to match the issue's words, which no device needs and which
would put a third pair of edges beside #3227's; keeping Friends in the top bar too, two doors to one
panel on every screen; the series screen inside the shell, whose navigation would lead out of a running
series; and leaving the toasts at the window's foot, where the tab bar hid the deck builder's status
and the undo toast lay over the middle tabs for its 8 seconds, taking their taps.
