A card in the viewer's own hand whose play asks a Tribute ([[§6.3]] "Tribute X") carries `tribute` in the view: how many Tributes a play of it asks now, read by the function `legalActions` and the reducer hold the play to (`play_choices::tribute_cost_of`), so the printed X as a Nerf or a Buff left it ([[R386]]) less what its owner's Floats took ([[R1841003]]). It counts the Tributes asked, never what the units that could pay them are worth: a Sheep Token's 2 or 3 ([[§3.2]], [[§7]]) leaves it as it is. It is absent at 0 and on every other card: the opponent's hand, a count until the game ends and shown without it then ([[R434]]), the field, where the Tribute was paid ([[R2261000]]), and the piles; a meal a Cry eats, Carnivorous Cube's, asks no Tribute of the play and shows none ([[R428]]). The client draws it as a small hand-hint pip labeled Tribute ([[R373]]) and works out no number itself, as with `embiggenCost` ([[R81]]). Rejected: reading the printed Tribute off the catalog in the client, which a Float or a Nerf leaves wrong (CLAUDE.md rule 7), and sending 0, which would put a pip on a card that asks nothing.
