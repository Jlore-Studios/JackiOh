The Black Hole (S+ #77) occupies the middle lane ([[§3.1]], lane 3 of 5) on both sides with a
lasting animation, and any permanent that enters it is exiled ([[R97]]): no play is offered there,
and whatever a move, rotation or summon leaves standing in the lane the state check swallows. A
pile counts as one swallowed permanent, a Dormant top is passed by, and a GIGA is met once. It
closes after swallowing 10 permanents; a new hole replaces an open one, restarting the count. The
Radiant face pulls only the opponent's permanents, at the start of the opponent's turn. Rejected:
closing at once when full mid-sweep, which would leave the second card of the lane standing in a
closed hole.
