Classic #74 Corpse Plantation's Activate (once a turn, [[R384]]) arms its graveyard permission ([[R454]]) for one play: while armed, this turn, its controller may play a Unit from their graveyard paying with Plague Counters as mana, at least 1. The play that uses it disarms it, as does the end of the turn, so one Activate is one play. Unarmed, the permission offers nothing. Its Cry places its `tokens` on itself.
