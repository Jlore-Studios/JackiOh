A watcher ([[R2990000]]) is dealt a hand as a seat is ([[R1341]]), and held to it as a seat is ([[R1342]]):

- **What it holds.** `SPECTATOR_EMOTE_HAND_SIZE` (5) of the nineteen emoji, distinct, and no voice line: a voice line speaks in a portrait's voice, and from the sidelines it would sound like a player speaking. It is listed in the pool's order, as a seat's is, so the menu reads the same whatever order the draw took.
- **How it is dealt.** A uniform draw without repeats on a stream of its own, `{seed}:spectator-emotes:{profile id}`, from the match seed and the watcher's own account: never the match rng, so no state, log, replay hash or golden trace moves, and the same account holds the same hand on every socket it watches the match with, after a restart too. Two watchers of one match almost never hold the same five.
- **Where it travels.** As the watcher is let in, after the seats' `loadouts` ([[R1834002]]) and before any view, the actor sends that watcher alone `{ type: "spectatorHand", emotes }`; no seat and no other watcher is sent it, and it is never part of a view. An emote of the pool the hand does not hold, a voice line among them, is dropped silently, before the limit and spending none of it ([[R2990002]]), as a seat's is ([[R1342]]).

Rejected: dealing a watcher a seat's hand of eight with its three voice lines, for the reason above; one hand for every watcher of a match, which would let one watcher's menu say what another's holds and make the strip read alike; and dealing from the seat the watcher watches, which would give two watchers of one friend the same hand.
