[[R3100000]]'s forecast entry stated an attack's blow on its target and the strike back onto the attacker, and nothing else the same attack deals: a Cleave attack's hits on the units beside the target ([[§4.4]] step 10) and a Trample attack's excess onto the target's hero (step 9) were in no entry, so the aimed preview understated the attack and no gate held those hits to anything.

Each entry now names both. `splash` holds one hit for each unit a Cleave attack on a unit strikes beside its target, in the combat's order, each with its `dealt`, `kills` and `pops` as the target's: the bare attack (its combat-only bonus included, no lane multiplier) through the damage pipeline's own steps (a Divine Shield, Armor unless the attacker pierces, the unit's own cap, a conversion, the damage pool, Indestructible). Cleave is the attack's, not the blow's, so the splash lands when a Divine Shield, Indestructible or the zero rule stops the blow ([[R63]]) and from a Ranged attacker that takes nothing back ([[R783001]]); a GIGA beside the target takes it once ([[R3546001]]); a Dormant unit beside it, which nothing reaches ([[§3.2]], [[R1839000]]), takes none, and neither does a card dormant under a Stack; and an attacker a First Strike defender destroys or exiles first strikes nothing, so its splash is empty ([[R176]], [[R4111000]]). `overflow` is what an attack on a unit tramples on to that unit's hero: each hit's excess past its unit's health as its own instance through the hero's Armor and the Anti-oneshot cap, the blow's and each Cleave hit's added up ([[R176]]), [[R44]]'s projection, with `kills` when it ends the game net of a Lifesteal strike back. A hero target has none (its `dealt` holds what reaches it already), and a strike back never tramples ([[R1750000]]), so no defender's entry reports one.

The blow and the splash are one projection read in the combat's order, so a damage pool the blow spent is spent before the first Cleave hit ([[R4032000]]). It is the projection [[R44]]'s trap window reads, which so passes a Dormant unit beside the target by as well, as the combat does. Both fields are left off the wire when empty, so a plain attack's entry reads as it did.

While the viewer aims an attack, the preview marks each unit the hovered attack's splash names with its own tentative hit, and the hero its overflow reaches with the overflow, each with the skull and the struck-through shield mark the target's marker wears ([[R3615000]]). A hovered attack on the hero itself shows that hero's own blow, never another attack's overflow. The client still computes none of it (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

The fuzz's I18 holds both fields through the testkit's `PlayedAttack`, the reading the forecast's rules tests share: each Cleave hit is read off the `damage` events its route tags ([[R2783001]]), the units the forecast's splash names first, since a hit a Divine Shield, Armor or Indestructible stops leaves no event, and the overflow is the Trample-routed damage onto a hero. It skips an attack whose Cleave strikes a unit in a damage chain or one with cards beneath it or held, as it skips a target in one.

Hearthstone semantics: Cleave's and Trample's hits are separate damage instances, each through the pipeline; the forecast is a derived fact, not a rule.

Rejected: folding the splash into the target's `dealt`, which would misstate the blow; simulating each attack with `reduce`, which [[R3100000]] rejected since it would run face-down traps; re-pricing the practice AI's own splash counting here, which issue #4257 owns.

Amended by [[R4290001]] (#4290): a splash hit's `kills` counts a sweep's hit on the same unit after it.

Amended by [[R4379000]] (issue #4379): a splash hit's `kills` counts every pass of the state check after the combat, so a neighbour an aura no longer holds up is destroyed; its `dealt` and the overflow stay the standing board's.
