A season is named by the live version's minor ([[R609]]), and a build whose season has no row opens it with a soft reset of every rated player. The version retcon ([[R1371000]]) moves the live version's minor with no new expansion shipping (the old v0.3.17b becomes v0.4.0d), so its migration renames the season in play, the old live version's, to the new live version's season, with the ranks and rated games that name it, when no season holds that name yet: no build opens a new season, nor resets a rank, for a rename. An older season keeps its name, a label of its time, and its patch version is renamed through the map with every other.
