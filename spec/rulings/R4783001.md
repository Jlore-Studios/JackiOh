Stinkyscape+ Balance Changes VIII: Volcanic Rounds, "damage dealt Burns" becomes "damage dealt also Burns". A card whose text says the damage it deals also Burns (`damageAlsoBurns`) converts nothing at [[§4.4]] step 3a: each hit it deals goes through the pipeline as any hit, with its `damage` event, Poisonous, Lifesteal, Trample, reflection and kill credit, and then, at step 7 beside "also Burns N" ([[R3540002]]), adds Burn equal to the damage the hit dealt at step 5, after Armor, the caps, the damage pool and Trample's cap, to a target that survived it, judged as [[R3540002]] judges it: a Unit still on the field and not killed ([[R42]]), a hero with life left. So, as [[R3540002]]'s rider, every hit counts, a Cleave neighbour's and a Trample excess's included; a hit that dealt nothing (a Divine Shield, Armor that took it whole, Indestructible, a cancelled lethal hit, [[R63]]) adds none, and nor does one that killed its target. A source that also says "also Burns N" (a Starship holding Scorching Rounds too) adds N and the damage dealt as one gain. An Equip carries it to its host and a launched Starship carries it; it is a flag, so a card fused from two such cards adds the damage dealt once. Rejected: adding the Burn at step 3a as [[R3540003]]'s conversion did, before the hit lands, which would Burn a target the hit then kills, one no tick would reach; and Burning by the hit's amount before Armor, which would let Armor be burned through. Replays of the versions through v0.6.13h fold under `script_v0_6_13h`, which converts each hit as [[R3540003]] says ([[R767000]]).
