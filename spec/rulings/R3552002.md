S+ #19 Normalization Module reads "Activate: Vanilla a unit", and its Radiant face "Activate 2: Vanilla a
unit". The spec leaves the target's side and the base face's number of uses open, so it is read as follows.

- **The target.** One declared Unit on either side, as Silence's base face ([[R81]], §8.7 C+ #9): the
  Module's own Unit, or the one it is Equipped into, included. Never a hero. With no legal pick the Activate
  is passed and Vanillas nothing.
- **The effect.** `vanilla` ([[§6.3]]): the Unit's printed keywords and scripts stop applying and it is back
  to its printed stats keeping its damage, a Unit the lost stats would kill left at 1 health ([[R781003]]);
  an Immutable Unit is untouched ([[R23]]).
- **The uses.** The base face's "Activate" is Activate 1, once a turn; the Radiant face's "Activate 2" is
  twice a turn ([[R384]], [[R1857003]]). The counts are the script's literals, as for every Activate of the
  set, since a use count has no param. Equipped, the host takes the Activate ([[R1086]], [[R1751000]]).

Rejected: enemy-only targets; a hero as a target; and the base face's "Activate" read as unlimited.
