[[R1859007]] lends a `unitsHaveKeyword` modifier's keyword to every Unit its holder controls, now and later, and says nothing of Immune to Spells. [[§6.1]] says a Spell can't affect a Unit with it, and [[R4453000]] that every Spell's effect list skips it, so a War Doctrine gave Trample, Deft, Rush or Flying to an immune Unit, and so did The Big Crunch casting one and TG #39's army.
**A Spell's lend passes it by.** The modifier records that a Spell made it (`fromSpell`, set when `restrictions::effect_is_from_spell` holds as the verb runs, on `give_units_keyword` and `give_units_keyword_next_turn` alike), and [[§10.4]]'s keywords lend its keyword to no Unit whose other keywords (printed, conditional, granted or an aura's) hold Immune to Spells. The test is read live, as the lend is: a Unit that loses its immunity takes the keyword from then on, and one that gains it drops it. Every other Unit its holder controls takes the keyword as before, those that arrive later included.
**A Trap's lend still reaches it.** S #4.4 Last Word is a Trap, so its Charge reaches an immune Unit, as a Field Spell's or a Unit's would.
**Replays.** A game in which a Spell's lend met an immune Unit plays differently and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). Only a Spell's lend writes the field, so every other state reads and hashes as before.
Rejected: fixing the lend as the Spell resolved, so that a Unit immune then never takes it and one immune only later keeps it. A lend reaches each Unit while it stands, as an aura's grant does, and the immunity is read the same way.
