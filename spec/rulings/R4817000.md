[[R385]] starts a printed Brittle as its card enters the field and says nothing of a face swapped where the card stands. [[§5.2]] has a card made Radiant on the field take its new face at once ("newly gained keywords apply at once"), and R385 makes the count public on the field, so the count follows the face. After Make Radiant, or De-Radiant ([[R1102]]):

- **A count the old face's printed Brittle started goes when the new face prints no Brittle**, in any zone, since that count is the card's text. S #9 Ticketed made Radiant (S #110 iKwzki's Duo, C+ #65.4 Golden Grape) wears a face with no Brittle, so it holds until it is removed ([[R1875001]]), in a hand too, where the count is kept ([[R766]]). A Vanilla only switches such a count off while the card stays on the field, and the card's Vanilla ends as it leaves, so a Vanilla card's count goes the same way.
- **On the field, a card with no count starts the one its new face prints**, as an arrival does, its turn cycle from that turn ([[R638]]): S #135.1 Sack made Radiant starts Brittle 1 and crumbles a turn cycle later, and a Ticketed De-Radianted by M #53 Prestige starts Brittle 3. A face-down Trap starts none until it reveals ([[R687]]), and a Vanilla card, which prints nothing, starts none. Off the field nothing starts.
- **A count that stays keeps its number and its cycle**: one an effect gave, and one printed on both faces, whatever the number on each (Core #89 Corpse Eater's Brittle 1 against 3, B3.3 rule 5).

No `counterChanged` goes out for a count that starts or goes this way, as at an arrival; the view carries the count. A count that went does not come back: a later swap back starts the printed count afresh on the field. Core #28 Knockoff Temu Glowy Jelly Bean and Core #81 Radiant Saintess's Death reach this through the same Make Radiant.

Rejected: a count that starts only as the card enters the field, which would have a Sack made Radiant show Brittle 1 to both seats and never crumble; and reading the running face's Brittle each time the count is read (`active_brittle_count`, beside its Vanilla test), which would stop a Ticketed's count without a write but start no Sack's, and would leave the stored count, which some 50 places read, out of step with the face.
