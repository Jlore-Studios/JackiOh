[[R1295000]] gave a player one pick per kind for all their cards, and [[R1313000]] made a full art
a setting gated by ownership. A player now picks, card by card, which owned version of a card they
play: the **foil**, **finish** and **border** of [[R1295000]] and the **full-art face** of
[[R1313000]] are each equipped per card. It amends [[R1295000]] (*The picks*, and *Whose cards wear
them*) and [[R1313000]] (a full art becomes a per-card pick).

**Where the picks live.** Each card's picks are a store on the device, in localStorage under
`jackioh.cosmetics.cards.v1`: a card's catalog id, to its foil, finish and border ids and whether
its full art prints. Like [[R1295000]]'s store it keeps any short text it is given, so an id a
newer client wrote is kept and never overwritten, and a face draws an id the client does not know
as its kind's default. The `cosmetics` settings group that syncs to the account ([[R633]],
[[R634]]) keeps exactly its six keys and its shape, and no card's pick ever enters it, a settings
request or the log, so an old client and a new one never overwrite each other's picks, and no
settings reset touches them ([[R1579000]]). The account's picks, and the account cosmetics (the
playmat, backdrop and card back of [[R1297000]]), still roam. Per-card picks stay on the device
that made them.

**The fallback.** A card with no pick of a kind wears the account's: today's pick for the foil,
finish and border, and the Full-art cards setting for its full art. A pick of a kind's default
(No foil, Plain finish, Standard trim, no full art) is a pick: it beats an account pick that is not
the default. A card's pick the client does not know is read as its default, and the account's does
not step in, as a deck's does not ([[R1297000]]). The rarity rules of [[R1295000]] hold whatever a
card picks: a Mythic face wears no cosmetic foil, and a Radiant face no foil, finish or border.

**Ownership.** A per-card pick is worn only where the viewer owns that cosmetic on that card, or
where every cosmetic counts as owned: practice, hotseat, and the builds [[R1310004]] opens. A
default and a starter are owned on every card ([[§14.2]]), and so is whatever the ledger lists for
the card, which the client reads from `GET /api/cosmetics` once per session ([[R1313000]]) and
never infers. A full art, which no account owns at the start, is worn on its own copy only once the
ledger lists it, where ownership decides. A pick the viewer does not own is not worn: the card
wears the account's pick, and the Collection offers no way to equip it. The device store keeps what
it is given; the wear path and the equip check apply ownership.

**Who wears them.** Only the viewer's own copy, as full art already is ([[R1313000]]): a face that
is the viewer's own card on a board they play, and the faces of that card's hover preview, touch
sheet and detail view, which sit below an `OwnCopy` of that card (the deck builder's detail view
among them, below its own, [[R1313000]]; its pool and deck list tiles draw the defaults); and the
Collection's card view, which is the viewer's own copy too. An opponent's copy of the same card, a watched game, a replay,
a prompt's options, the showcase of a card played, the piles, the Almanac, the homescreen and every
face not marked as the viewer's own draw the defaults ([[§14.2]]: the client never infers
another's cosmetics). A card wears its own picks while another card keeps the account's.

**Where it is equipped.** The Collection stays the one place a cosmetic is equipped ([[R1579000]]):
its card view sets a card's picks, with the card previewing them live, and the account cosmetics
are tiles that preview what they set. The settings and the account screen offer none, and the
Full-art cards setting stays a setting, the default of a card with no pick. The Collection is built
by a sibling part of #1651, whose tests are named after this ruling.

Nothing here is a rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a pick never enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log, a
replay hash or a game record, and nothing the engine does reads one.

Hearthstone semantics: a player picks, card by card in the collection, which owned version of a
card they play.

Rejected: a synced per-card settings group, which the server's limits (`PLAYER_SETTINGS_KEYS_MAX`
keys of 32 and `PLAYER_SETTINGS_BYTES_MAX` of 4096 bytes a group, mirrored in migration 0018) hold
to a handful of cards; raising those limits, or a server equip table, each a migration, for a look
that is the device's own; an ownership check in the store, which would drop a pick the ledger has
not yet answered for, where the wear path reads the answer when it arrives; and cosmetic picks
worn by every face under an `OwnCopy`, the deck builder's pool among them, which would change the
deck builder for every account pick.
