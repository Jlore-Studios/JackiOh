`effects::turn_buff::grant_for_turns { target, keywords, attack, turns }` gives one Unit on the field `attack` more Attack and `keywords` until its caster has started `turns` more turns: the grant records the caster and the caster's `turnsStarted` count plus `turns` (`CardInstance.timedBuffs`, one `TimedBuff` each), and holds while the caster's count is below it. So "for a turn" (FvH #7 Whimsy!, #8 Joy!, `turns` 1) lasts until the caster's next turn starts, and "for two turns" (2) until the caster's second turn from now starts, whoever's turn it was cast on, as Firewall's containment ends at its caster's next turn ([[R4591001]]) and Armor Up's at the opponent's ([[R757]]). The layers read it beside the "this turn" buff ([[R4587000]]): its Attack in layer 4, its keywords beside the granted keywords ([[§10.4]]). Two grants stack, each its own record. It is a status of the stay on the field: R78's reset takes it off ([[R78]]), a copy keeps it with the same caster's count ([[R57]]), a Vanilla takes its Attack while its keywords stay, and another card's grant passes an Immutable Unit by ([[R5113000]]). A grant of no Attack and no keyword, or of 0 turns or fewer, does nothing.
