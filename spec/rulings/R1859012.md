Stinkyscape #144 Prepare For Liftoff: "Reveals when one of your Starship Pieces dies: Your Starship launch costs (0)" (Radiant: first "Summon it from your graveyard"). Decided against the brief's ST-S02-12, which read "your" as the piece's controller as it died: a stolen piece dies into its owner's graveyard and fuses into its owner's Starship ([[R1852002]]), where neither "your graveyard" nor "your Starship" of the thief could reach it. So "one of your Starship Pieces" is a piece the trap's controller owns whose copy fuses into their Dormant Starship: the trap answers `starshipFused` for its controller ([[R1852000]]), wherever the piece died and whoever controlled it, and an enemy piece leaves it set. A death that fuses nothing (no Starship to be had) reveals nothing. It fires as any Trap does ([[R17]]): `starship_launch_costs { cost: 0 }` ([[R1852006]]). The Radiant face first summons that card out of its owner's graveyard into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), a summon with no Cry ([[R1]]), its copy staying fused, so its next death fuses again.
