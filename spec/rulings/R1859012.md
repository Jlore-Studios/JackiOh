Stinkyscape #144 Prepare For Liftoff: "Reveals when one of your Starship Pieces dies: Your Starship launch costs (0)" (Radiant: first "Summon it from your graveyard"). "One of your Starship Pieces" is a piece whose copy fuses into the trap's controller's Dormant Starship: the trap answers `starshipFused` for its controller ([[R1852000]]), and an enemy piece leaves it set. Since [[R2618000]] a piece dies into the graveyard of the player who controlled it and fuses into their Starship ([[R1852002]]), so that is the brief's ST-S02-12 reading, "your" as the piece's controller as it died: a piece the trap's controller stole and that dies on their side reveals it, and a piece of theirs the opponent stole does not. Until then a stolen piece died into its owner's graveyard and fused into its owner's Starship, and the trap answered for the piece's owner wherever it died. A death that fuses nothing (no Starship to be had) reveals nothing. It fires as any Trap does ([[R17]]): `starship_launch_costs { cost: 0 }` ([[R1852006]]). The Radiant face first summons that card out of its owner's graveyard into the leftmost open unit zone ([[R64]]), a summon with no Cry ([[R1]]), its copy staying fused, so its next death fuses again.
