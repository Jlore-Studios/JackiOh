**Why.** The owner asked how the adjusted win rate ([[R2777000]]) reads a card that casts itself on draw, and a card that goes into play from the deck without being drawn, C+ #26 Tommy Tempo and M #87 Tatches the Totem among them. The record ([[R376]]) followed the hand alone: `drawn` lists the cards a draw put in the hand and `played` the cards played from it. A card cast on its draw never reaches the hand ([[§2.4]]) and its cast is no play ([[R2179002]]), and a card an effect summons from the deck never passes through the hand, so neither list held them. Their games fell on the never-drawn side of the drawn delta ([[R2427000]]), the very games the card shaped, and its seen side held only the games a cast was refused ([[R58]]'s cap, or a Unit with no open zone). A card that always casts on draw had almost no seen games, so almost never an adjusted win rate or a tier, and its decks that cast it read as decks that never drew it; under All Random its own figures dropped those games altogether ([[R2777002]]), and its play rate ([[R3950000]]) read close to 0.

**The record.** Each seat's summary gains `castOrSummoned`: every dealt card of the seat's that an effect cast or summoned from the seat's own deck or hand rather than its play from the hand, each time, with the turn ([[§2.5]]'s counter) and whether it was Radiant then:
- cast on its draw ([[§2.4]]; the draw's `cardPlayed` says `onDraw`), from whichever deck it was drawn, as `drawn` credits the drawer. A card setup dealt uncast and cast at the start of the game ([[R748]]) is cast from the hand, and is listed here and no longer as a play;
- cast from the deck, by its own standing condition ([[R3558007]]) or another card's effect;
- summoned from the deck or the hand, by its own trigger (M #87) or another card's effect (a Recruit).

A play's own summon follows its `cardPlayed` and is that play, so a Unit cast on its draw is listed once. A Created card is still its maker's ([[R2089000]]), and a summon from a graveyard or a field lists nothing. The list is absent when empty, and from every record written before it. `opening` and `drawn` are unchanged, and so is `played` but for that cast at the start of the game, which no legal deck reaches ([[R748]]); so are the quests, the proficiency and the fleet, which count plays from the hand ([[§14.9]]).

**The statistics.** The totals count a card in `castOrSummoned` as played, since it went into play. Its game is a seen game, never a never-drawn one; it is split by the face it went in on ([[R1147000]]) and counted at its turn in the drill-down. So the drawn delta, the adjusted win rate and tier ([[R2777000]], [[R3022000]], [[R3070000]]), All Random's own figures ([[R2777002]]) and the play rate ([[R3950000]]) all read a card's cast and summoned games on its seen side. The stored totals already hold `played`, so nothing is stored differently and there is no migration. A record's summary is read once, when the record is filed, so the records and totals filed before the change keep their counts.

Rejected:
- A breakdown of its own beside played and drawn but not played: a stored tally and a column no page reads, which every figure would add into the seen games anyway.
- Counting them as drawn but not played: a card that went into play is no card held and kept, and its play rate would read 0.
- Leaving out the summons from the hand: M #87's game from the hand is seen already, but its play rate would read a card that went into play as never played.
- Reading the records filed before the change again: that needs each game's log, which the retention loop purges, so it would correct only some of them.

The statistics' own counting: no game rule, state or event changes, and no Hearthstone rule speaks to it.
