Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): a picture reads a card's id, never anything hidden. The owner asked on
2026-11-06 (issue #3330) that every card's picture be more its own and richer, simpler for a small card and
elaborate for a Mythic, its Spells and Field Spells real scenes; this issue is Stinkyscape's pass over cards 1
to 90, on the art bible, the kits and the detail floor of [[R3331000]] and [[R3331001]].

**Every card of the table draws its brief.** Each of the 64 cards draws from a scene recipe of its own
(`apps/web/src/cards/art/stinkyscapeRecipes.ts`), in the Stinkyscape bible's deep space: Arg Arg Defender's
proof redrawn to its brief (a stocky defender in dented orange plate flinging arms and shield wide over two
crewmates on a cargo ramp) and 63 new. Each brief is the target, its subject, setting and idea kept; a part a
brief names that the kit lacked joined the kit. The cards the table leaves out keep their pictures byte for
byte ([[R3331002]]): every Starship Piece and the Dormant Starship (issue #3333 draws them) and Olivian Expanse
(issue #3338). No other Stinkyscape card draws a scene but those of [[R3403000]]'s table (the later cards and
the early cards' tokens it names), and each card that draws one still draws its own picture on each face
([[R503]]).

**A Unit draws its character.** A person is the figure kit's (a pose, a head, headgear, an outfit and an item,
starting from the look of its tag), and the others a brief names (a crew, a squad, a rival on a leash) stand as
[[R3390000]]'s further figures (`extras`); a ship Unit is its own craft part in the subject layer (Stratos' boxy
fighter, Paragon-X's blade, Ozark's barge); Imprisoned Cosmality is its creature. None keeps the painter's
silhouette or its own subject.

**Richer the rarer.** Each card clears its rarity's floor ([[R3331001]]) and leaves `ART_DETAIL_SHORT`, whose
maximum this pass leaves where the passes before it set it ([[R3390000]], [[R3392000]], [[R3393000]], then
[[R3401000]] and [[R3403000]]); an Epic draws a foreground, a middle and a background, a Legendary or Mythic every layer,
and the median element count rises strictly from Common to Mythic. Each Mythic places a part no other recipe
does: Tylannt's getaway skiff, Rod of Ages' rod, Drone Core X's battleship, Great Paperclip Theory's wire,
Paragon-X's blade fighter and Prototype-X2's fused hull. Both faces share one geometry, the Radiant face
repainted toward its layers' golds, every picture under `ART_DETAIL_MAX_BYTES`, and no base face's colour in
Radiant's gold window ([[R2725004]]). A scene splits for Out of frame as every scene does ([[R2943001]]).

**The kit's new reach.** The scene kit gains a seventh kind, craft (ships, drones and vehicles, each facing
right about its anchor), and the parts the briefs name in every kind: stations, docks, hangars, vaults, server
cores, cockpits, portholes and the panes of open space they frame, decks, corridors, regolith, lava flats and floating platforms; ray guns, drone cores, credit
chips, an ice block, a boot clamp, an ancient rod, a red-hot axe head, a containment sphere, a wire, a rope and a coffee
bulb; a gloved hand, starstuff and a sleeper; a silver vine and a rosemary sprig; beams, light streams,
meteors, smoke, comets, star glare, gas giants, shattered space, light grids, rifts, frost, missiles, flame and hex shields. The figure
kit gains a reaching pose, two heads (a horn-mawed herald, a one-eyed lantern wraith), four headgears (a space
helmet, a headset, shades, silver hair), three outfits (academic robes, a hydraulic arm, a body of wreckage) and
seven held items. Where a brief names what [[R3390000]]'s pass already added, the card wears that pass's part:
its spread and seated poses, its goggles, its lectern, its shockwave, its drum (the galley's war drum), its
playing card (the blank card a drone core gulps) and its scales (the merchant's); and where it names what
[[R3392000]]'s pass already added, that pass's: its lab coat, its work apron, its anvil (the smith's, the axe head
lying on it) and its conveyor (the factory's line). Where that pass had taken a name for something else, this
pass's part took another: `head-wraith` beside its lantern head, `cracked-helmet` beside its knight's helmet and
`light-cone` beside its hanging spotlight. So did each of the eighteen whose names a pass that landed first had
taken for a drawing of its own, so no kit module's spread replaces it: beside [[R3403000]]'s, `wedge-fighter`,
`eye-drone`, `retro-rocket`, `welding-arm`, `reaching-hand`, `control-desk`, `herb-cracker`, `ship-deck`,
`moon-surface`, `riveted-porthole`, `reactor-core`, `ice-cube`, `comet-streak`, `cracked-helmet` (its bubble
helmet is `space-helmet`) and `hair-professor`; beside [[R3401000]]'s, `ring-station` and `ringed-rift`; and
beside [[R3397000]]'s rope, `swing-rope`. A placement may turn a pointing part (`rotate`, in degrees clockwise:
a beam, a light stream, a light cone, a meteor, a smoke trail, a comet). It is opt-in: a placement with none draws
from its stream exactly as before, so every other recipe, none of which places a turn (the proofs left in
`sceneRecipes.ts`, the other sets' passes' and the Alt art's), draws byte for byte as it did.

Decisions, each with the alternative rejected: a ship Unit's character is its craft part, not a ship in the
figure kit, whose parts dress a standing body; a thing a brief names that [[R3390000]]'s or [[R3392000]]'s pass
already drew is that pass's part, repainted and scaled, not a second drawing of the same thing under another
name; a part of this pass's that looks like one of theirs stays because it draws something else or turns where
that one does not (a light stream and a smoke trail that point and turn, where the light ribbon loops in place and smoke
rises; a meteor's burning rock, not a fireball; a column of flame, not a campfire or a small standing flame; a towering
computer core, not a crystal server obelisk; hexagonal credit chips, not coins; lava rivers running to the
horizon below, not a crusted lava field; a gloved hand reaching up, not a bare palm held out; a robot arm whose
tool bends down onto its work, a weld or a console's panel, not a clamp gripping upward); `rotate` is opt-in and turns only the parts that point, rather than every part,
whose look would change with no gain; craft is a kind of its own rather than ships filed under objects, so the
kind says what a Unit's character is ([[R3403000]]'s ships and drones, which landed first, stay objects and
creatures); a set's recipes live in a file of their own rather than one growing
`sceneRecipes.ts`, which every concurrent pass would conflict on; the short list's maximum stays rather than
falling with this pass, which would conflict with every pass in flight; and a brief's colours outside the bible's
palette (a pink idol, a crimson pulsar, a rusty barge) are drawn in palette.css's nearest names rather than
recoloured to the bible's few.
