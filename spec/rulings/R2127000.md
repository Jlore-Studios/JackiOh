The browser's Back, and Android's back gesture, close the topmost open sheet or dialog before
they leave the screen. One Back closes one, the one opened last first, and leaves the address and
the screen as they were.

**Which.** A card's sheet and its detail, a face-down card's sheet ([[R370]]), a pile's sheet
([[R313]]), the stack sheet, hero inspect and the pack scene; the command menu ([[R2118000]]); the
settings panel; the Collection's card view ([[R1655000]]) and the statistics page's card breakdown
([[R654]]), both since issue #2563; the Marketplace's shop in a match ([[R1853003]]) and the deck
builder's history ([[R2100000]]), cosmetics ([[R1297000]]) and portrait picker ([[R641]]), all since
issue #2604; the home screen's Quests scroll, the board's active-modifier list (which practice, the
lessons, the online match, Duos, watching and replays share) and the emote picker and opponent mute
menu that hang off a hero's portrait, all since issue #2682; the friends panel, a conversation and the
challenge dialog; and the Concede and "Leave this game?" questions, where Back means "Keep playing".
Each closes through its own close, as its Close button does, so the focus goes back; the Quests scroll
gives it to its bookmark, as its Escape does. The emote menus drawn inline in hero inspect add no
layer: that view is the layer, and owns them.

**Never by Back.** A prompt, a play still being built and the mulligan ([[§10.6]]): a choice the
game asks for never closes unanswered, which is Hearthstone semantics, as [[R2121000]] has it.
With one of them on top Back works as it did, and it leaves the screen. An overlay left open under
one is still a layer, so Back closes it first and the choice stays unanswered: the Marketplace's shop
with a prompt drawn over it (issue #2604). The hotseat hand-over is a button and draws no screen of
its own. The inline questions (Block, Forfeit, a quest's Replace, deleting the account, the creator's
notes) and the result dialogs keep Back as it was. The pack scene ignores Back while its open request
is out, as it ignores Close.

**How.** Where the browser has `CloseWatcher`, the top overlay holds one, which Android's back
gesture closes; Escape keeps to each overlay's own keys there, so one Escape never closes two.
Elsewhere the first overlay pushes one history entry that copies the address, and a Back off it
closes the top overlay; the next overlay down gets the entry again. An overlay closed some other
way leaves that entry spent: the next overlay reuses it, a move takes its place, Back steps on past
it, and Forward onto it steps back, so nothing reopens, not even an overlay the address opens (the
Almanac's card). A replacing move keeps the entry, and Back then skips the address it replaced.

**Leave guard.** Closing an overlay is never a leave: it never reaches the leave guard
([[R1754000]]) nor a view transition ([[R2117000]]), and a Back with no overlay open meets the
guard as before.

Rejected: one entry per overlay, which piles up history; calling `history.back()` on every close,
which races a move made in the same click; canceling Escape in each overlay's own keys, which
leaves an Escape on the board under a conversation to close it; treating every `role="dialog"` as
an overlay; for the statistics breakdown, one layer held by the whole page and not closable while the
breakdown is shut, which would leave Back closing nothing there; for the Marketplace, giving the focus
back to its toggle on every close, which would pull it out of a prompt drawn over the shop.

Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
