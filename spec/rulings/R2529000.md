The owner's call, after [[R2499000]]. A change to how cards look took no version: the classifier ([[R1365000]]) called it App, and it reached players only as a technical change under the live patch ([[R1369000]]). A huge system, such as the card packs, took none either, though players meet it as a release.

- **How cards look.** The client's `apps/web/src/cards/` holds the cards' faces, art, frames, foils and marks and the CSS that draws them (`CARD_VISUALS`). A change to its code, tests aside, is a Cards **micro** of no family, however much it redraws, since no card plays differently. `--claims` ([[R1370000]]) asks for a pending fragment claiming `visual` (`cargo jackioh patches "<title>" --visual`), and refuses a fragment that newly claims it when nothing there changed. `patches ship` names such a fragment the next letter ([[R1366000]]). A change that also plays cards differently takes the tier its cards earn. The rest of the client (the board, its animations, the screens) stays App.
- **A system the owner names.** The classifier cannot tell a huge system from a big refactor, so the owner names each one in `SYSTEM_RELEASES` (`crates/tools/src/classify.rs`) by its tracker issue, as they name a wrapped set ([[R2204000]]). Naming one is never a builder's call. A fragment claims it with `--system <issue>`, and `patches check` refuses an issue the list does not name. `patches ship` names the fragment a **patch** whatever its cards earn, and records `system: true` on it when the system alone made it a patch.
- **Statistics.** A patch recorded `system: true` moves no card but the families it plays, so every card statistics window reads it as a micro over them ([[R1367000]], `patch_history`). Its version keeps its name and its tier everywhere else: the patch notes, the release lines and the technical log ([[R1369000]]).

Rejected: measuring a system by its size, which a large refactor or a pile of tests would trip; a new screen or route, which small pages would trip too; and leaving the cards' look versionless, which hides a redrawn card from the patch notes.
