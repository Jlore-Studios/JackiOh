**Why.** [[R4673000]] reads each card on three faces. This note says how the server answers them and how `/stats` shows them.

**The API.**

- `GET /api/stats/cards` and `GET /api/stats/cards/:id` take `face=all|normal|radiant`, trimmed and in any case. A missing or unknown face is All, so an older client reads today's answer unchanged, and `GET /api/stats/cards`' answer gains no field.
- On Normal or Radiant, each row's `games`, `winRate` and its range, `adjustedWinRate` and its range, `tier` and `hasEnoughGames` are that face's, and so are each Created row's. `playRate` stays All's. `tiers` is that face's field and band, and `tiers.created` that face's Created field.
- The drill-down's `patches`, `byTurn` and `coPlayed` follow the face. Its answer gains `face`, the face it read, and `faces`: on every face, the card's Normal and Radiant `games`, adjusted rate and range, `tier` and `hasEnoughGames`, and the Radiant `gap` with its `low` and `high`, null unless both faces are past the floor. `faces` reads the page's `patch=` (the current patch when missing). A card no deck holds, a token, reads its Created faces there; any other card its deck row's.
- [[R3185000]] stopped sending the face figures because no client read them. They are sent again, under `face=` only and in the drill-down's `faces`.
- The routes stay (CLAUDE.md rule 13).

**The drills by face.** From now on each card's turn and co-played counts are also folded by the seat's face, as [[R1147000]] splits its tallies: Radiant at least once, or never (`CardTotal::radiant`, `CardTotal::normal`). A seat that did not play the card, or whose plays carry no face flags, counts in neither. A face keeps counts only: on a face, co-played cards that tie are listed in the order the card first met them on any face. Totals folded before this hold no face drills, so a face's `byTurn` and `coPlayed` read empty for them, never All's figures shown as the face's. The patch history reads the face's tallies, which every total holds.

**Storage.** The face counts ride in the existing `card_totals.turns` and `.co` entries, after the card's own: a turn is `[games, wins]`, or `[games, wins, radiant games, radiant wins, normal games, normal wins]` when a face counted it; a co-played card is `[games, wins, record, seat, index]`, or the same with the four face counts after it. An entry no face counted keeps its old form, so a total folded before reads and writes as before, and a retcon's rewrite of the record id (the third number) is untouched. A server older than this cannot read an entry with face counts.

**Keeping.** The current patch's kept report holds all three faces' drills from its one load ([[R978000]]). The drill-down's history is kept once per pilot, match type and face, cut from the snapshot ([[R2491000]], [[R3067000]]), and the `faces` line reads the window its patch's table reads, cut from the same snapshot. A face costs no load of its own.

**The page.** The Cards and Tokens tabs' options row gains one **Face** control, `All | Normal | Radiant`, beside View, in the Segmented's look ([[R3324003]]). It is kept in the address as `face` (absent means All, an unknown one reads All), one state for both tabs, as `view` and `ranges` are ([[R4083000]]). So that the row stays one row at 1280 wide, its groups now sit `--space-6` apart and its buttons' sides are `--space-5`, the tavern's bare-button height and face kept; on a phone it wraps, a row taller. The client leaves `face` out on All, so All's requests and the reads kept for them stay as they were. Everything the tabs show follows it: table, grid, sort, tiers, ranges, tier fit and the Sets tab's sums. Pressing a row opens the drill-down on the same face and patch. There, on Normal or Radiant, the details say "Normal faces only" or "Radiant faces only". On every face one line under the faces gives the card's Normal and Radiant adjusted rates and the Radiant gap with its range, each "not enough games" below the floor. Show Radiant still shows the Radiant face's art. This amends [[R3184000]], which showed no Radiant or Normal figure: the faces come back as the Face control's views and the drill-down's line, not as columns.

**Rejected.**

- **Folding by each play's face.** A seat that played a card once Radiant and once normal would count on both faces, and the drills would disagree with the face's games.
- **Backfilling older totals.** A record's detail is kept a week ([[R1044001]]), so no fold can split what was folded before.
- **New columns for the face counts.** A migration, and every retcon would have to rewrite them too.
- **The current patch only for the `faces` line.** It would disagree with the table the reader opened it from.
- **Sending `face=all` from the client.** All's address and kept reads would change for nothing.
- **Only narrowing the row's gaps.** It saves 40 of the 150 px the Face control adds at 1280, so the row would still wrap. Dropping the control's visible label instead would set it apart from View's.

No Hearthstone rule is involved.
