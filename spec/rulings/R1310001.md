[[§14.12]]: once the economy is live, a new catalog version grants no account its cards, and a new
set's cards come from its track ([[§14.7]]) and its packs ([[§14.3]]). Migration 0050 drops
[[R481]]'s grant whole: the trigger `settings_grant_catalog_growth` on the catalog stamp,
`app.on_catalog_version_stamped()` and `app.grant_launch_collection_all()` (migration 0016, over
people only since 0032, passing over the dev accounts since 0046). A stamp now writes the version
and grants nobody: not a person's active account, not a dev account, not a ladder bot
([[R1454]]), not a pending account, which waits for its own activation's starter cards
([[R111]], [[R1310000]]).

- **What was granted stays.** Every card [[R481]]'s grant gave before the launch is still owned
  ([[R1310002]]); nothing is taken back.
- **Every new card alike.** The cards a patch adds to a set that already shipped are not granted
  either: each joins its set's pack pool and the card token's choices ([[§14.5]]) like the rest of
  the set.
- **`seed-catalog`** no longer reads `DEV_PROFILE_IDS` nor sets `jackioh.dev_profile_ids` before
  its stamp: with no grant on the stamp there is no one to pass over, and a malformed list no
  longer refuses a seed. The server's boot still reads the list ([[§14.10]]). [[R1300001]] ends
  with it.

Hearthstone semantics: a new Hearthstone expansion gives no player its cards; they come from its
packs and its rewards.

Rejected: keeping the trigger with an empty grant, which would leave a function every stamp runs
to do nothing and `seed-catalog`'s dev-account plumbing with it; and granting the cards a patch
adds to a set already shipped, which would make a card's way into a collection depend on the patch
that brought it rather than its set.
