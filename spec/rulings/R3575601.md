[[R2564000]]'s probe tried a card's uses as the opponent's reply tries them (`visible_activation`), judging each at the state the use leaves. An Activate whose text asks its controller a question after the use (Stinkyscape+ #126 Vela's "Choose an enemy tile") leaves that question open: the cost is paid and nothing has happened yet, so the use never beat standing still and the card's Activate was priced at nothing, on the field as in hand ([[R3575600]]). No Hearthstone rule is involved: the evaluation changes no game.

**What it counts.** A use the probe picks that leaves a prompt of its controller's own open has that prompt answered by the controller's best auto-answer before it is priced (`answered_own`: one node an answer tried, at most `AI_SEARCH.max_auto_answers` prompts, as the beam answers the seat's own prompt ahead). When the reply's way finds no use worth making, the uses that open such a prompt, at most `AI_REPLY.visible_activations` of them, are tried again with their prompts answered, and the best positive price is kept (`prompted_use_price`). A use that opens no prompt is judged as before and not tried again, so every price [[R2564000]] and [[R2603000]] gave stands.

**What is not priced.** A use whose prompts outlast the cap, whose answer fails, or that the probe's nodes did not reach. The opponent's reply itself still judges an Activate at its open prompt: this probe alone answers it.

**Rejected.** Answering the prompt inside `visible_activation`, which would change the opponent's reply and every decision that reads it, beyond what the probe needs.
