Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): no rule, state, event, action, log line or replay hash changes. After Hearthstone, where every Legendary has an animation of its own, Classic+'s pass of [[R3332000]]'s signature tier gives each of its 16 Legendaries and Mythics and 19 tokens printed Legendary or Mythic a `signature: true` entry in `fx/cards/classicplus.json5`, its recipe in `fx/cards/classicplus/`, built from the card's concept in issue #3399, and takes them all off `SIGNATURE_PENDING`:

- **A Unit** lands with an `entrance` of its own (the pancakes of The Mother Pancake stacking one by one, Wardrum's three drumbeats, the Losers each in their own way), then plays its text's beat in its `{ bespoke }` recipe on the hook its marker opens ([[R2797000]]: an end-of-turn trigger, a Whenever, a Death, its Cry), and wears an aura of its own on its tile ([[R3399001]]).
- **A Field Spell** lands face up with its entrance, plays its cast or Activate in its recipe, and wears its aura.
- **A Spell** casts in a recipe of its own, which claims its own `cardPlayed` for the cast, then the events its text emits; Rollback's Flourish ([[R2795000]]) grows a cast of its own, a stone clock face rewinding over a violet wash.
- **A Mythic** takes a takeover of its own whose picture fills the screen ([[R3399002]]); Twice Forward One Step Backwards, a Field Trap, takes its at its firing.

A concept is the target, not a script: a beat moves where the events the view carries or the budgets demand, and `fx/cards/classicplus.md`'s Signatures table records each card's first beat, text's beat, tile, sound and verdict (`as concept`, or `moved:` and why). The moves are of three kinds. A text that emits no event of its own stands in the card's tile (Top Loser's lane wall, Jungle Loser's snore, Classic Golem's carved faces). A summon of a card with an entrance of its own plays that entrance before any recipe, so League of Losers' Losers land each with their own and Felinor Flagbearer Prime's copies with a smaller variant of his. A beat outside the card's play or hook (Jogg's Box's lid at the turn's end) is dropped, and a Spell's beats are pulled into its burst window. Frozen Wastes leaves the shared `vortex` set piece for a white-out of its own with no hold, to fit a Legendary Spell's ceiling.

Every signature keeps [[R200]]'s tail, the effects speed, intensity, Big moments and Reduce Motion ([[R201]]), one set piece per action ([[R2785000]]) and [[R202]]: no cue names a card, and [[R3399003]] holds what the other seat sees. Each card's own cues (its entrance, its recipe, its takeover) end within its signature ceiling of the entry they play in, its shakes within its type's (`classicplus/signatures.test.ts`). [[R3332000]]'s budget test (`tiers.test.ts`) counts every cue of the whole gallery play, the shared event rows and the shared takeover included, from the play's start; a Mythic Spell's shared takeover alone (its held entry plus `FX_TAKEOVER_TAIL_MS`) passes its ceiling, so that test fails on this pass until a person settles the measure (issue #3399, Open question 1). Each card plays a sound of its own ([[R3399004]]). The `/dev/fx` gallery plays every one of them, and a component spec plays each at desktop size and on a phone and holds the two heaviest to the phone frame budget.

Rejected: shared motifs with new palettes, which the issue rules out ("one concept per card"); a set piece for every Spell, which would hold the game on every Legendary cast and break one set piece per action; and redrawing a summoned card's entrance in the play's recipe, which would draw it twice.
