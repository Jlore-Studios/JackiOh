Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7): nothing enters `PlayerView`, an action, the log or a replay hash. Casting an Anomaly played a 500 ms board swap, a ring and a whoosh, the same for all ten (issue #3337).

**The entry.** A `boardReplaced` that names an Anomaly ([[R3337000]]) and every event after it up to the next hook, question, Trap, play's end, replacement or turn's or game's end (`riftAt`) play as one entry, its cards' `exiled` and `summoned` among them; the Cries the board fires ([[R1855001]]) each play their own entries after it. The entry is pinned against the burst budget, lands no slam, and gives the board no swap motion of its own. It claims itself as the action's set piece, `anomaly` (`anomalyClaim`, after the Starship's): `ANOMALY_RIFT_HOLD_MS` (2.8 s) at speed 1, held, capped, scaled and skipped as [[R2785000]] holds every set piece, then `ANOMALY_LAND_MS` (0.9 s) of landing. A replacement naming no Anomaly plays as before.

**The set piece** (`planAnomaly`), whichever card laid it (Call to Chaos (Scape Edition)'s motif yields to it):

- through the hold, a rift tears down the middle of the caster's side, every card the replacement takes is drawn into it and gone as it flares white (rays, a prismatic burst, a void ring and a shake), and the Anomaly's title card stamps across the screen: its name in the display face over its picture ([[R3337001]]), drawn in place from the art painter's own SVG as the layer's icons are, so the effects layer loads no image (docs/polish/1-animations.md, B27);
- as the hold ends, each card the board lays falls out of the rift onto its zone in turn, as its board lands: asteroids tumble with dust, drones and hulls power on with a spark ring, the outpost's lights come up; the stage's own landing for each yields to it.

Big moments "short" draws the title card over its short hold and the landing, "off" the title card alone, the old cards gone at once and the new ones landing as any summon does. Under Reduce Motion nothing holds: the layer draws one still title card for `ANOMALY_STILL_MS` (`planAnomalyStill`), as the Starship's still launch does ([[R2801000]]). The set piece draws the replacement's, the exiles' and the landings' rows in their place. Every cue lands inside its entry and is gone within `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS` ([[R200]]), the effects speed scaling it ([[R201]]). The board, its name and its zones are public, so no cue tells a seat anything it does not read ([[R202]]).

**The backdrop.** While an Anomaly's board stands, the caster's half of the field wears a faint standing look of it, an aura of the side's scope ([[R2787000]]): the board's picture (the card art's own, in place of the aura's tint) screened faintly over it, and its motes (the Wormhole's void, asteroid `drift`, the outpost's holy lights, and so on). The client remembers it from the events it has played, not from state: a later `boardReplaced` for that side that names another Anomaly changes it, one that names none ends it, and so does the game's end. It is quiet, and none of it is drawn under Reduce Motion.

**The sting.** An Anomaly's `boardReplaced` sounds a short sting of its own made in code (`sfx.ts`'s `anomaly`), in place of the whoosh: a hum for the Wormhole, rumbling rock for the Asteroid Fields (Narcour's with a chime, Axnit's with a ring), a clank for the Battlefields, a falling hiss for the Spice Platform, a deep bell for the Monument, a buzzing swarm for the Drone Hideouts and a chime for the Frontier Outpost.

**The takeover.** Anomaly Found! prints Mythic, so its play makes a Mythic's entrance, whose takeover ([[R2722001]]) would claim the action's one set piece before the rift could. As a Mythic yields its takeover to its own wipe ([[R2800000]]), the play that lays the rift (the play open as it lands, `AnomalyRift.sourceId`) yields its takeover to it: its entrance draws no takeover and claims no hold, and the rift holds; under Reduce Motion it stands no still Mythic card either, the Anomaly's still title card standing in its place.

**The table.** Anomaly Found!'s entry is the bespoke `anomalyCall`, a void ring and stars gathering where the rift tears as its `boardReplaced` opens the entry; the Scanning Probes' is [[R3337002]]'s `probeScan` ([[R2784000]]). None of them is a signature ([[R3332000]]): the probes are Common and Rare, which the tier leaves out, and a Mythic Spell's signature ends within three quarters of `SIGNATURE_MYTHIC_MAX_MS` (1.95 s), which the rift's hold alone passes, so Anomaly Found! stays on `SIGNATURE_PENDING` for Stinkyscape's signature pass. Rejected: shortening the set piece to that budget, which the issue sets within the set-piece hold cap instead.
