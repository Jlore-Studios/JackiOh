Stinkyscape #4.2 KY's Last Exam reads "Solve an impossible problem. If you get it right, deal 30 damage to the enemy hero, and this gains Echo +1"; its Radiant face adds "You snuck in a cheat sheet: the right answer is marked." The spec has no impossible problem, no Echo a card gives itself and no marked option.

- **The problem.** One problem is drawn from the match rng out of a bank of impossible ones (`crates/cards/src/ky_last_exam_bank.rs`), statements whose unknowns stay open, so none gives its answer away. Its right option is drawn as it is asked, then the four are shown under letters in an rng order, C+ #42's `answer` prompt ([[R420]]), the key kept in the engine ([[R465]]). Every answerer guesses one in four: the player, the AI, the random policy and a timeout ([[R79]]).
- **Right.** One hit of 30 on the enemy hero ([[§4.4]], its caster's Spell Damage raising it), then `gain_echo`: the resolving card is owed one more resolution ([[§6.2]] Echo, one more repeat on `state.echoQueue`), which asks a new problem. A play gives itself at most `SELF_ECHO_GAIN_CAP` repeats, counted in the card's memory, which [[R215]]'s reset clears as it lands, so a later play counts from nothing. **Wrong:** nothing more; the Spell was still played and lands in the graveyard.
- **The cheat sheet.** The Radiant face writes a ✓ after the right option's label. Only its chooser's prompt carries the labels; the other seat's view says only that a prompt is open, and the key stays out of every view and of the AI's redaction ([[R465]], [[R185]]). The AI reads no label, so it still guesses.
