The owner ruled on issue #5113 that Immutable Units can't be buffed or nerfed, after S #15 Arg Arg Defender's Cry gave S #116 Megabase beside it +1/+1 and Taunt in a practice game. Immutable stopped a change to a card's text ([[R23]], [[R386]]) and a Set stats ([[R1185002]]). Since issue #5113 it also stops every change another card makes to its stats or keywords, in every zone it holds in, read where the card is now (`layers::is_immutable`, so every Indestructible Unit card too, [[R4353000]]):

- **Layer 4** ([[§10.4]]): a buff or a debuff (`+X/+Y`, `-X/-Y`, a doubling, a random Unit's), for good or this turn; a set Attack this turn, which [[R4591000]] let through as a status and which is a change to its stats; a keyword given, whether named, random (none drawn for it, [[R129]]) or given by a scope to a hand or deck card. Each leaves the card as it is and reports nothing (`restrictions::immutable_keeps_off`). A keyword a summon gives the fresh card it brings (its `keywords` and random keywords) or a draw gives the card it draws is how that card arrives, and lands on an Immutable one too: S+ #142.1 Mindustrial Golem's summoned permanent "that can't attack" can't, whichever it is.
- **Layer 5**: no other card's aura reaches it (stats, keywords or a set Attack), nor a combat-only Attack bonus ([[R1120]]), nor its controller's "your Units have" keyword or Attack lend. Layer 5 reads Immutable off layers 1 to 4 (the printed, conditional, granted and this turn's keywords), so an aura that makes a card Immutable (Indestructible on a Unit) holds beside the others.

A card's own text still changes it, as its Radiant does ([[R23]]): its own buff, grant or aura. Since every Indestructible Unit is Immutable, no other card's aura or debuff takes its max health to 0 any more (core #46 Suppressive Aura, C #42 Transmutable Toxins, C+ #50 Adaptive Growth): it dies at 0 only by its own text or a debuff it took before it was Indestructible ([[R69]]), the owner's call on the issue. Damage, healing, destroys, statuses (Freeze, Berserk), steals and moves land as before, and Immutable still bars no aim ([[R4353001]]): an effect may be aimed at the card and leaves it as it is. A list of keywords one effect gives a card is given together ([[R5113001]]). The fuzz monitor's I23 ([[R4609000]]) still judges the card's text alone.

Rejected: stopping buffs and grants but letting auras through, which leaves the reported case open to an aura of Taunt; and stopping the card's own text too, which would print a self-buffing Immutable card for nothing. A game recorded before the change in which another card buffed, granted or lent to an Immutable card no longer folds to its recorded hash and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]).
