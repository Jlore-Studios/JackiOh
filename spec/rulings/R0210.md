[[§10.5]] step 1 accepts the zone a play names and step 4 puts the card in it, and between them step 2 pays the Tribute — whose units die ([[§6.3]]), so a Death can land cards in the very row the play is going to: #3 radiant's base copy and #22's copies by [[R64]], #86's steals by [[R15]]. The named zone is held for the play from step 2 to step 4 exactly as a zone is held for a dying Reborn unit ([[R64]]), so those cards take another zone and the played card lands where the player put it; a played card is never left in no zone ([[§10.1]]). A tributed unit with Reborn comes back to its own zone at 1 health like any other first death ([[§6.1]], [[§4.5]] step 4), since a sacrifice counts as a death
