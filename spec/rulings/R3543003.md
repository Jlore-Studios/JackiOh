After the Unit's attack destroys the Unit it attacked and it survives, judged once as the check
closes, Frenzy answers with "this may attack again" (`mayAttackAgain` with sickness kept), first
in [[§4.2]] step 5 before the card's own "After this attacks". It is a fresh exertion, so a Unit
that entered this turn stays summoning sick ([[R2657004]]). One more attack per kill, each kill
freeing the next; forced attacks count, and Cleave's kills of neighbouring Units do not, since the
attacked Unit must be among its kills. A Nerf may remove it. S01 moves Tmack Trav and Classic Golem onto the keyword ([[R3551004]]), and Frenzy joins the random-keyword pool as its fifteenth entry, so a granted Frenzy answers the same kill. Rejected: counting Cleave's kills; and a generic "Frenzy: [effect]" hook that no card needs.
