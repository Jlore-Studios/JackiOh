"30% chance to Reveal when an opponent attacks" ([[§8.10]] Stinkyscape #22 Gatinsir, #174 Aerelret) is an attack an enemy Unit declares, never a forced one, which no one declares ([[R121]]), nor its own side's; only such an attack rolls the trap's chance ([[R1849009]]). "AMBUSH" is a label: on a hit the trap fires in [[§4.2]] step 4's window, and its summons — the Warp Disruptors into its controller's open backrow zones, then the Phalanxes or Paragon Mk1s into open unit zones ([[R64]]), no Cry ([[R1]]), one that finds no zone doing nothing — arrive before the combat, which then goes on to the target it was declared at, the Disruptors' aura already acting on it. The Radiant face, which the designer printed without its reveal line, keeps the trigger and the chance, and summons every card Radiant. Rejected: cancelling the attack, which the designer's "AMBUSH" does not say, and redirecting it to an arrival.
