Animated is a keyword a Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap may print together with attack and health, its unit face; "Animated on your turn" is its variant ([[§6.1]]). To animate is to move the card from its backrow zone to a unit zone of its controller's — the one in its lane when that is open, else the leftmost open, unlocked, unreserved one ([[R64]]) — in Attack Position unless its text says otherwise (C #5 Tesla: Defense); with no such zone it does not animate and stays where it is, and a Trap that has fired then stays face-up in its zone, as a Field Trap does. An Animated Trap or Field Trap animates as the last step of its firing, and one already a Unit when it fires again neither moves nor changes position; an Animated Field Spell animates as it enters the field; an "Animated on your turn" card animates at the start of its controller's turn, after the mana refresh and the Brittle tick and before start-of-turn delayed effects and triggers ([[§2.2]]), and as it enters the field on its controller's turn, and it returns to its backrow zone at the end of its controller's cleanup, after every end-of-turn step, so its own end-of-turn text runs while it is a Unit. While animated it is a Unit for every rule: it attacks and is attacked, takes damage, counts among "your Units", is hit by "all Units" effects, is face-up, and keeps all of its text (a Field Trap's trigger still fires, an aura still applies); when it dies it goes to its owner's graveyard and fires Death. Moving between the two zones is not leaving the field: it keeps its damage, buffs, counters, memory and granted keywords ([[R78]] does not apply), but entering the unit zone is entering it on that turn, so it is summoning sick ([[R83]], [[R171]]). While an "on your turn" card is animated its backrow zone is reserved for its return, as a dying Reborn unit's is ([[R64]]). At a cleanup it does not return, and stays a Unit until its next one, when it lies dormant under a Stack (since balance patch 1 a Lock on its home zone stops nothing: the return is a move, [[R688]]); one whose controller has changed has no home on its new side, and returns to its new controller's leftmost open backrow zone, or stays a Unit when there is none. In the backrow it is not a Unit: it cannot attack or be attacked, "all Units" effects skip it, and backrow effects reach it. A face-down Animated Trap is hidden like any trap until it fires ([[R33]]). The moves are reported by `animated` and `deanimated` ([[§10.3]])
