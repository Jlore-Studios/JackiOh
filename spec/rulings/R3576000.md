**Why.** The game server's container sleeps once no request has reached it for `SLEEP_AFTER` (`apps/edge/src/index.ts`, five minutes), and an open WebSocket counts as a request in flight. A signed-in tab kept it awake for as long as the tab existed, seen or not: its presence beat ([[R3210000]]) went every `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` while hidden, and its social socket ([[R3214000]]) stayed open. One tab left in the background anywhere kept a site's server, and its bill, awake all day.

**The rule.** Once a tab has stayed hidden for `HIDDEN_QUIET_MS` (two minutes, the client's own number in `together/config.ts`), it goes quiet:
- It closes its social socket. To the social poll that is a drop like any other ([[R3214000]]): the poll stays paused while the tab is hidden ([[R3205000]]), except while a challenge of the player's own is open or just sent, when it reads at once and goes on at its pace, so the challenger's follow still finds the game.
- It sends no presence beat. Its friends see it offline `PRESENCE_TTL_MS` after its last signed-in request, as they would after a closed tab.

A tab that shows again sooner keeps its socket and its beat, and the count starts over the next time it hides; a page that starts hidden counts from its mount. When a quiet tab shows, it opens its social socket again, its paused polls read once as [[R3205000]] has them (the social poll's read is a signed-in request, so presence is back at once), and its beat is a full `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS` after its last signed-in request again. One helper, `quietWhileHidden` in `game/net.ts` beside `pollWhileVisible`, tells both. What a quiet tab still sends is what the server expires or a game needs: the queue's reads, the series pick, the co-op room wait, the poll with a challenge open ([[R3205000]]), and a live match's, a watch's and a co-op room's sockets, which keep the server awake as they must.

**Presentation only.** CLAUDE.md rule 7: the client asks for less and decides nothing; the server's presence, its sockets' checks and every read are as before.

**Hearthstone semantics.** A client nobody has looked at for a while stops telling friends it is there, as Hearthstone's friends list shows a player away, and catches up when the player comes back.

Rejected: going quiet the moment the tab hides, which would close and reopen the socket on every glance at another tab, each reopen one upgrade against the account's budget ([[R2478000]]); keeping the beat while closing the socket, which still keeps the server awake every `PRESENCE_HEARTBEAT_MS`; a server-side cut of idle social sockets, which cannot tell a tab nobody sees from one somebody reads; and keeping the socket while a challenge of the player's own is open, since the poll already follows it while hidden.
