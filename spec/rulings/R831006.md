Meditative #91 Windfast's "summon a random Unit from your hand" draws the Unit that makes each of its attacks from the match rng among the Units in its controller's hand: no prompt is asked, on either player's turn, and a hand with one Unit draws no random number. Everything else is [[R1202]]'s (the leftmost open zone, no Cry, the same target, declared or forced as Windfast's attack was). The issue's "random Unit" is read as one from the hand the text already names, not any Unit. Windfast also gains Rush.
