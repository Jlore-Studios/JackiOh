A host holds at most `MAX_OPEN_ROOMS_PER_HOST` co-op rooms nobody has joined and whose expiry has not passed, for the reason [[R890000]] gives for a 1v1 host's rooms: they cannot amplify a seat race, or fill the table, without bound. A fourth is `409 conflict` before a code is minted. A joined, deleted or expired room frees its place. The count is advisory, as R890000's is: the claim in the join is what stops a second match. It is counted apart from the host's 1v1 rooms, so a host may hold three of each.

Rejected: one count over both kinds of room, which changes what a 1v1 host is refused.
