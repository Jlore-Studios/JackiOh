At the state check's game-end point, a player whose hero is at 0 or less loses even if a win of
theirs holds. Otherwise a player with a held win wins — an alternative condition or Meditative #8's
Ascent 10 (`GameOverReason::AltWin`, `WonByEffect`) — and two winners draw. A win effect takes hold
at the check that closes its effect list ([[R59]]).
