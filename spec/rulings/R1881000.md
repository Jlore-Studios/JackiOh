T-AI-2 Mining Subroutine's "a random Common or Rare Stinkyscape Resource" is `gain_resources` ([[R1840002]]) over a pool that names the Resources by id: Reknite, Korrelite and Gellium. The roll is the usual one over the rarities that pool holds, Common 55 against Rare 25, then one of the rolled rarity, into its caster's tray, never the hand, a gain past the tray's cap sold at once ([[R1840001]]). Radiant: the Resource is Radiant.

The pool leaves Axnit, Narcor and Red Narcor out (ST-S21-2). The ten AI cards are offered at the Marketplace, priced by their cost ([[R1853002]]): a (0) card at 5 Credits, 10 on its Radiant face. A Resource from the whole pool sells for about 5.8 Credits on average, more than the card's price, so buying the card to sell its Resource would print Credits in a Marketplace both players share. From the Common and Rare Resources the average sale is about 4 (Radiant 8), below the price. The card declares no number ([[R482]]).
