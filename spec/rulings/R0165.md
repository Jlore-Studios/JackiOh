A profile that has never saved a loadout fails the queue-time check as a **loadout** failure, not a missing resource. [[§9.4]]'s L1 assumes a loadout exists and so cannot state the case, but the player is in exactly the position L1 describes — they do not have three decks — and the remedy is the same: go and build one. A 404 would say the endpoint found nothing, sending a client looking for a route that is working correctly, and it is not a staleness problem either. The rule generalises: a queue-time refusal the player fixes in the deckbuilder reports as a loadout failure, and only a catalog mismatch reports as stale. Since [[R253]] it covers a profile with no saved deck to queue, and a deck or trio that has since been deleted, the same way
