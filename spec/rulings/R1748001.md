Since issue #1748 a Buff's keyword row reaches cards that are not Units. It adds, among the keywords the card lacks, Indestructible to a Field Spell (`TUNE_FIELD_SPELL_KEYWORDS`), and Pierce or Lifesteal to a Spell or a Field Spell that deals damage (`TUNE_DAMAGE_KEYWORDS`), one drawn uniformly when both are open ([[R442]]); a Field Spell that deals damage may draw any of the three. A Unit still draws from [[R21]]'s pool, and a Trap, a Field Trap, a Vanilla card and every other card gain none. The keyword is `tuning`, kept in every zone as [[R386]] keeps it, so a Lifesteal Spell heals its caster for the damage it deals and a Pierce one pierces, as a printed keyword does ([[§6.1]]).

The catalog marks a Spell or a Field Spell that deals damage `dealsDamage: true` ([[§5]]), and `cargo jackioh catalog check` refuses the mark on any other type or on a card whose text says no "damage". A fused or crafted definition carries no mark. A Nerf's keyword row is unchanged: it removes a keyword the card has, a Buff's included. This amends [[R386]]'s keyword clause.
