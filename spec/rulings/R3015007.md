The owner asked in #3015 (2026-11-04) that each variant read as a ladder, owning a level meaning owning every
level below it, and left the Collection's layout to the plan.

**The ladders.** A card's Collection view (`CardCosmeticsView`) shows its variants as ladders
(`collection/VariantLadders.tsx`, from `cardLadders` in `collection.ts`): the Default first, then each
variant whose first level the card is offered ([[R3015000]]), in the registry's order. Each is a
`collection-ladder` section (`data-variant`) holding an ordered list of `collection-rung` buttons, one per
level the card is offered, lowest first (a Legacy card's stopping before animated), the Default's first
rung the normal face, "Normal", always owned. A rung shows its level's name, its rarity and its variant's
picture ([[R3015002]]) and says `data-level`, `data-owned`, `data-next` (not owned, every level below it
owned) and `data-equipped`. A locked rung that is not the next shows "Needs {name}", the highest level
below it the viewer does not own.

**What a rung does.** Hovering or focusing a rung shows it on the card; a click on an owned rung wears it
on this card alone, writing the card's pick of that level ([[R3015005]]); a click on a locked rung, holding
any, or the inspect key opens its fullscreen preview ([[R2730000]]), where a held token that covers it is
offered only when it is the next ([[R1953001]], [[R3015004]]), and no rung past the next is offered one.
The strip below keeps the foil, the finish and the border; its versions and augments are gone, and no
retired full art or augment is listed anywhere ([[R2725000]]). Practice, hotseat and the end-to-end builds
count every level as owned on the cards it is offered ([[R1310004]]).

The Collection decides nothing (CLAUDE.md rule 7): ownership and the offer are the server's (`GET
/api/cosmetics`, `COSMETIC_OFFERS`), and the server refuses any level past the next ([[R3015004]]).
Rejected: the flat strip of versions with augment toggles, which no longer matches the model; and hiding
the levels past the next, where showing each with what it needs lays out the path the owner described.

Amends [[R2725000]] (the Collection lists a card's variants as ladders, not its versions and augments) and
[[R2949000]] (no rung waits for Full art and Animated: a signed level is a rung of its own above them).

Amended by [[R3016001]] (#3016): hovering a rung, or its keyboard focus, also opens that level's hover preview, the card, in hand and on the board, beside it.

Amended by [[R3343002]] (issue #3343): a Mythic's and a Legendary's Default rung is named and pictured by its default look, and a variant whose first level that is is listed from the level above, next with nothing owned.
