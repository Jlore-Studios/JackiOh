The designer asked (#1563) that the option to give your hero Armor be removed from crafting. `gainArmor` ("Gain Armor") is in `CRAFT_RETIRED_VERBS`: no prompt offers it ([[R1563007]]), Scratch Pad's palette leaves it out, and a recipe that uses it is refused ("gaining Armor is no craft block since #1563") with the prompt open. The Armor keyword a crafted Unit may take is a keyword, not this verb, and stays. The verb keeps its row in the block table and its compiled effect, so a card crafted before #1563 still compiles from its recipe and plays as it did, and an older game folds unchanged ([[R882]]). It edits [[§6.3]] and [[R883]].
