A count that has crumbled its card is spent: [[R78]]'s and [[R215]]'s reset drop a count of 0, and a card that re-enters the field starts its printed Brittle afresh. "Gain +N" on a card with no count starts one now at N more than it prints; that count is a given one, so a Vanilla keeps it. A card dormant under a Stack does not tick ([[R13]]), and no card in a hand or a deck does ([[R638]]).
