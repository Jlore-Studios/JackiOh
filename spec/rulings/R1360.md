A `damage` event says what got through ([[§4.4]] step 5) and, since patch v0.3.X, what the target's Armor took off the hit at step 2: `absorbed`, the target's Armor up to the whole hit, which is a unit's total through the layers or a hero's total ([[R124]]). Nothing after step 2 is Armor: a hero's divisors and its cap shrink the hit further and are not counted, and neither is Divine Shield (step 1) or Indestructible (step 4). A hit that pierces ([[R346]]) has no Armor taken off it, so its `absorbed` is 0. A hit a replacement re-aims at the other hero ([[R460]]) is a new instance through that hero's Armor, and its `absorbed` is that Armor's part; the hero it left took nothing that landed and reports nothing. A Trample excess is an instance of its own, through its own hero's Armor ([[R63]]).

`absorbed` is left off the wire at 0, so a hit no Armor touched serialises as it did before the field existed (D14). It is a report to the client, which no rule reads: the trigger loop holds every event it dispatches as the rules read it, a `damage` without its `absorbed` (`GameEvent::as_rules_read`), so the state a game passes through, a frontier paused on a prompt or left by a game's end included, hashes as it did before, and a recorded game's replay keeps its final hash ([[R768]]). Public, as `amount` is: the view redacts the event's ids and never its numbers.
