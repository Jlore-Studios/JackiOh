**Why.** A second socket of an account for a seat it already holds (the match open in a second tab, or picked up on a phone while the laptop still has it) took the seat, and the server closed the older socket with 1000. Both clients reconnect on 1000 after `RECONNECT_DELAYS_MS`' first step, 250 ms, so the replaced tab opened a socket that took the seat back and closed the other tab in turn, and the two went on taking it from each other for as long as both stayed open, each screen falling back to reconnecting again and again.

**The rule.** A newer socket for a seat the account already holds takes the seat, 1v1 and co-op alike, and the older is closed with `SEAT_TAKEN_CLOSE_CODE` (4412, "this game is open somewhere else"). The seat was never empty, so no grace starts, as before ([[R744]]). On that code the client goes to a new connection state, `elsewhere`: no backoff, no wake probe, no socket however long the page waits, its last view kept and no error. The match page says "This game is open in another tab or on another device" (the connection line on a live board, a panel of its own before the first view, where nothing is loading) beside a **Play here** button. A press opens one socket, which is the page's own new socket and takes the seat back on purpose; the tab it replaced then lands in `elsewhere` the same way. Only a socket another tab or device took over ever receives the code: a client that lets a stale socket go itself ([[R1441]]'s replace) has already unhooked its close handler, so that tab's own replacement never reaches `elsewhere`. A watcher's replacement is unchanged (1000, a newer socket of the account replaces its older one). The code is the server's, exported to the client through `serverConfig.ts` (CLAUDE.md rule 9). A client older than the server reads 4412 as a drop and reconnects, as it did on 1000, and a newer client against an older server sees 1000 and reconnects: nothing gets worse during a deploy.

**Hearthstone semantics.** An account plays a game from one client; a client replaced by a login elsewhere is told so and does not reconnect by itself.

**Rejected.** Keeping 1000 and spotting the loop on the client (a count of recent replacements, a longer backoff): the client cannot tell its own replacement from another tab's, and the two would still trade the seat, only more slowly. Closing the new socket and keeping the old: the newest client is the one the player is looking at, and a stale tab left open would keep the game from a phone for good. A server-side grace for the replaced seat: the seat is never empty ([[R744]]). Reusing 4410 or 4411 ([[R679]], [[R3694000]]): they mean the match is gone, and the page would say so and offer the lobby. Changing the watchers: a watch socket takes no seat, and one tab of a friend's watch replacing another costs nothing. Dropping the leave confirmation ([[R2479000]], [[R2973000]]) while the page is in `elsewhere`: left asking, the smaller change.
