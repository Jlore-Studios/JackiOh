Sound reads the viewer's `PlayerView` and nothing else, so it follows [[R97]]'s and [[R154]]'s redaction exactly: an event whose card is the sentinel plays a generic sound and never that card's voice line. Setting a Trap never speaks on either seat, because a line on the set would name a face-down card to anyone within earshot of the device: its owner hears the set sound every Trap shares, and the other seat, whose event carries the sentinel, hears the plain play sound of any card it cannot name. A Trap speaks when it fires, and only on its controller's seat, because [[R154]] withholds its identity from the other seat at that moment. A hotseat hand-over plays nothing the arriving seat's view did not produce. A sound may vary with the public catalog's facts about a card the viewer can read (its type, tags and rarity): a Unit's arrival on the field and a cast Spell do, a card behind the sentinel never does, and a Trap never does, so its set and its arrival in the backrow sound like every Trap's. Since [[R2722002]] a Trap's first firing, on the seat that can read it, may vary with its rarity (a Legendary's or a Mythic's entrance sting beside the trap sting), and a Cloaked card's reveal stings as its own play would, on its controller's seat at its firing and on the other seat at its Activate: Reveal; its set still sounds like every Trap's
