FvH #97 Paltry Felinor summons a random Unit that is Dormant. `summonRandomWith` takes `dormantTurns: N`, and the card its pool draws enters Dormant for N of its controller's turns though its own text prints no Dormant, exactly as a fresh `summon` naming `dormantTurns` does ([[R3541002]]): it is asleep before it lands, its countdown counts its controller's turns and it awakens at the start of the Nth ([[R1839001]]), and a fresh card has never awakened, so [[R3039000]] does not stop it. Absent or 0, it enters awake. The draw itself is unchanged: one pick over the pool, made only when some card of the pool has a zone to go to ([[R129]]), and the rider combines with the others the summon may carry (GIGA, marked to evolve, keywords).
