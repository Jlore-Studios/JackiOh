[[§13.7]] has each AI seat play its duel "as the practice AI plays a 1v1 game" ([[§9.9]]), and [[§13.4]] gives a Duos seat two layers of life, its own HP and then its team's pool. The spec did not say which one the AI's judgment reads; this note decides it as a Hearthstone player counts a hero's life: all of it.

- **Every life the AI reads is the seat's whole life**, the number [[§4.4]] step 4a's lethal judge counts: own HP and the pool while own HP is not spent, the pool once it is. The evaluation's hero value, threat, pressure, race and closing terms, the lethal solver's distance to lethal, the reply's face value (a hit wins only when it reaches that number) and the search's charge for a would-be-lethal attack into an armed My Pawn all read it.
- **The hero value and the closing term are anchored at the seat's starting life**: 30 in a 1v1 game, `DUOS_OWN_HEALTH` and the team's pool cap in a Duos duel (40, or 50 for a seat of the Hard AI team, whose pool is `DUOS_POOL_HEALTH_HARD`), so a full seat is worth its life and an untouched opponent is no damage down.
- **Nothing reaches a 1v1 game**: a 1v1 seat's life is its hero's health and its starting life `HERO_HEALTH`, so every 1v1 decision, gate, fuzz seed and golden trace is unchanged.
- **Only public life is read** ([[§13.6]]), never a hidden card ([[R185]]). The other duel is not modelled: damage the pool takes there counts once it has happened.
