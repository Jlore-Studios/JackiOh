A card's figures come from the records a query reads and, in them, the seats it counts, each seat's deck once per game: a game in which both decks held the card counts once for each, and a card is its catalog id, so a copy made in play counts as the card for a deck that held it, and a card no counted deck held has no figures. Its breakdowns are four: in deck (every such deck); in the opening hand; going first and going second (the deck's seat took the first turn, or did not); and played (the seat played it from its hand at least once) against its baseline, drawn but not played (the card reached the seat's hand, in its opening hand or by a later draw, and the seat never played it; a card neither played nor drawn is in neither). Each is a win rate, wins over games with a draw a game not won, as the AI's quality gates count them ([[§9.9]]), shown with its number of games beside it, and with no games it shows no rate. The played delta is the played win rate minus the drawn-but-not-played win rate, in percentage points, and shows nothing while either has no games. A query names any combination of match type (`bo1`, `bo3`, `random`, or every mode), patch (one, or every) and pilot (`human`, `ai`, or `unified`, every seat), and one card or all; `stats:cards` prints the four breakdowns for each card with its games beside every rate, or the same as JSON
