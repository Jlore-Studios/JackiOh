The ingredient is drawn uniformly from the enemy Units on the field (tops only, the unit row and a Flying Unit standing in the backrow, [[R1081000]]; never a Field Spell or a Trap, face-up or face-down), never an Immutable one. Through v0.5.11o it was drawn from the enemy permanents (tops only, both rows, face-down included), which `script_v0_5_11o` keeps for those replays ([[R767000]]); since #3140 its card text reads "Fuse a random enemy Unit into this". The Radiant face's deck card is drawn uniformly from the opponent's library, Immutable cards excluded.

It fuses per [[R77]] with 饕餮 kept. The fused texts act as on C+ #74 (auras, start- and end-of-turn lines, Death and Activate work; a Cry never runs again; a Trap trigger never fires from a unit zone).

This build's decision: a deck ingredient is fused as a fresh phantom of its definition — radiant flag, tuning and enchantments carried — and the library card ceases to exist, so its id never reaches a view. The rejected alternative was fusing the library card itself, which would have moved a hidden id through the fusion's reports.
