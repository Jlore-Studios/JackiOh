A player spam-clicking the pack panel saw the server fail. Every spend took a database connection
first and then waited on its account's economy lock ([[R1301003]]), and every press was a new key
([[R2124000]]), so a burst of presses held the pool's connections on one lock until every other
checkout timed out and answered 500. So an account runs one spend at a time.

**The turn.** Each economy spend, `POST /api/packs/buy`, `/api/packs/open`,
`/api/tokens/redeem-card`, `/api/tokens/redeem-cosmetic`, `/api/cosmetic-packs/open` and
`/api/cosmetic-packs/chase`, claims its account's turn once its request has been read (a malformed
one is still 400) and before it reads the store, and holds it until it answers.
`ECONOMY_SPENDS_RUNNING_MAX` run at once. A spend sent while they run waits its turn in the server,
in the order sent, holding no database connection, then runs as it would have: it is blocked, not
refused, so a second tab or a retry is served. Up to `ECONOMY_SPENDS_WAITING_MAX` wait; one sent
past them is refused at once with 429 `rate_limited`, its `retryAfterMs`
`SPEND_BUSY_RETRY_AFTER_MS` ([[R192]]), and writes and keeps nothing. Every ending, an answer, a
refusal, a failure or a request dropped while it waits, gives its place back.

Since issue #2610 every other route that writes under the account's economy lock for a press takes
the same turn: `PUT /api/card-picks` and `POST /api/card-picks/upload` ([[R1834000]]),
`POST /api/practice/seeds` ([[R1483000]]), `DELETE /api/practice/seeds/:seed` ([[R2379000]]) and
`POST /api/quests/:questId/replace` ([[R2210000]]). Each took a database connection and then waited
on the lock as the spends did, so a burst of cosmetic picks could hold the pool as the pack panel
did; each now waits in the same queue, behind the spends and they behind it, and is refused the same
way, writing nothing.

A read takes no turn, and neither does an invite code's redemption ([[§9.4]]), which spends no
economy, nor a practice claim, whose check and pay run in a task of their own, apart from its
request ([[R2422000]]).

**Its keys.** A retry racing its first try waits behind it and answers what it answered, as
[[R2124000]] has it. A 429 ran nothing, so the client keeps that press's key for its retry.

**The client.** A spend of the tab's is out from its press until its answer or its failure. While
one is out, the Shop's Buy, the pack panel's Open and Open all, the sealed pack, Reveal all on it
and Next pack wait, disabled, Buy, Open and Open all turning a loading wheel, so a press that would
be refused or queued is never sent and a person sees a wait, not an error. A refusal that still
comes, from another tab past the waiting ones or [[R109]]'s limit, shows as the server's words.
A quest replace or a practice seed refused so shows the server's words too: the quest panel under
its quests, and the practice game, which then starts earning nothing, as its reason ([[R2379000]]).
A card's picks and a seed given back go in the background, one at a time from a tab, and a refusal of
one stays off the screen as any failure of theirs does: the pick stays worn on the device
([[R1834000]]) and the seed lapses ([[R2379000]]).

**What a player gets.** Openings commit one at a time, each from its own fresh seed
([[§14.11]]), so how fast or in what order a player presses buys nothing they could not get pressing
slowly.

Hearthstone semantics: Hearthstone opens one pack at a time, each tap waiting on the one before.

Rejected: refusing every spend sent while one runs, which hands a person an error for a second tab
or a quick second press, where the owner asked for a wait; seeding each pack when it is bought, which
needs one row per pack where the inventory keeps a count, a migration, a backfill and a write on
every grant of a pack, while the openings already commit one at a time from fresh seeds, so it would
change no result; an unbounded queue, which a script fills; `pg_try_advisory_xact_lock`, which
still spends a connection on every refusal and refuses while a game's payout holds the lock; a
turn for an invite code's redemption or a practice claim, as above; and, for issue #2610, a refusal
worded apart for the routes that are no spend, a second message and its translation for a refusal one
tab does not meet alone, since it sends its spends and its picks one at a time, and renaming the
turn's type and constants, which changes no behaviour.
