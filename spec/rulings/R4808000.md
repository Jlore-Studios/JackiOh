An audit of the AI crate found three pieces of state that change what a position offers or what it is worth, which neither `search_signature` nor `unit_lanes` signed. The beam's duplicate check reads the two together, and the lethal solver's visited set reads `search_signature`, so both took two different positions for one and dropped the later line ([[§9.9]]):

- **The last Spell.** C #57 Echo has the text of the last Spell either player played ([[R399]], [[R3048000]]), read live while it is in hand. With C #55 Book of Wildfire, Echo and The Coin at 1 mana against a hero at 8, Coin then Wildfire at the face leaves Echo as Wildfire and wins; Wildfire then Coin leaves Echo as The Coin. The two orders signed alike (the turn log is sorted), the solver reached the second first and pruned the first as visited, and found no lethal after 19 of 1,200 nodes.
- **Damage chains.** S+ #7 Churnwalker's Cry chains every enemy Unit or every friendly one ([[R3554007]]). The two modes signed alike and scored alike, so the beam kept "enemy", first listed, and never searched "friendly", though the same attack then plays out differently from each.
- **Where a card stands that is no active unit.** `unit_lanes` walked the active units, which leave out a Dormant card ([[R1839000]]), and the signature's Dormant part holds its countdown but not its lane, so S #17 Imprisoned Ship Smith played in lane 1 and in lane 3 were one position and the second was dropped outright rather than kept as a lane variant ([[R4132000]]). An animated Field card (M #66 Fiery Waraxe, played on its own turn) off two backrow lanes into one unit lane differed only in its home ([[R383]]), which nothing signed.

None of these changes a game rule; they are judgements about the search.

**What changes.** Each part is said only when there is one, as [[R3951100]] does for the last Feng Shui element and [[R3772000]] for a Mind Games' secrets, so a position without them signs exactly as it did:

- `search_signature` adds the last Spell (its definition and whether it was Radiant, or none) only while a card that copies it (`subsystems::copies_text`) is in the seat's hand or graveyard, the zones `legal_actions` plays from.
- A unit's part of `search_signature` adds the chain it is in, shared or split, only while it is in one.
- `unit_lanes` walks every unit on the field, Dormant cards included, and writes an animated card's home zone beside its lane.

The last Spell and the chains are in `search_signature`, so the beam and the lethal solver both keep such positions apart. The Dormant lane and the home zone are only in `unit_lanes`, so they make lane variants ([[R4132000]]), and the lethal solver still reads no lane (issue #1797); `lane_free_signature` is unchanged.

**Hidden information.** The last Spell, the chains and the homes are public. The copier is looked for in the seat's own hand and graveyard only.

**Rejected:**

- signing the last Spell always: it would split two orders of any two Spells into two positions for nothing;
- counting a copier in the library: it is not playable until drawn, and the library's order is hidden;
- signing which Units share a chain rather than the chain's id, which the engine mints from a running count: two Cries in swapped order then sign apart though they end alike, which is rare and costs only a duplicate line;
- putting the Dormant lane in `search_signature`: the lethal solver reads no lanes by design;
- changing `lane_free_signature`.

Not in scope: a backrow card's `vanilla` flag and a graveyard card's spent Flashback, which the audit saw but did not isolate. It adds no constant.
