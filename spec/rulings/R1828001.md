Since issue #1828 Classic+ #43 AI Slop fuses random (1) Cost AI generated cards. Its pool is the AI generated cards, which an AI pool holds though they are tokens ([[§5.1]], [[R1421]]), narrowed to those whose cost is (1). "(1) Cost" is the printed cost, read out of play as [[R65]] reads it and as every cost-bracket pool does, so no discount, override or tuning moves a card in or out. Today that is six cards: T-AI-1 Helpful Assistant, T-AI-4 Chain of Thought, T-AI-7 Alignment Tax, T-AI-8 Rate Limit, T-AI-9 Refusal and M+ #1.3 Garage Sale. The picks are independent, repeats allowed ([[R60]]), and Radiant on the Radiant face; the fusion, the (0) and the hand cap are as before. Tuned down to one card, the one card comes from the same pool ([[R582]]).

Through v0.4.9 the pool was every AI generated card; `script_v0_4_9` keeps that for those versions' replays ([[R767000]]). It amends [[R582]].
