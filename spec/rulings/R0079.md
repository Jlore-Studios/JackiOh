Server config: turn clock 75 s, disconnect grace 60 s, hard match ceiling 120 minutes (60 before patch v0.2.0, [[R389]]), room codes of 6 characters from the invite-code alphabet. Ranked games move the hidden Glicko-2 rating ([[§9.12]], [[R603]]). The turn clock belongs to the active player and pauses while a prompt is open for the non-active player (a trap firing on the opponent's turn), which runs its own prompt clock of 30 s. `timeout` answers only the prompts of the player whose clock ran out, using the AI policy, and ends the turn only when that is the active player. One `timeout` answers at most `TIMEOUT_ANSWER_CAP` (500) prompts, so a card that kept asking could not hold it forever: if a prompt is still open after that many, the timeout stops with the turn still running and the next expiry carries on (no Core card asks that often). `disconnectExpired` is a loss; reaching the ceiling is a draw
