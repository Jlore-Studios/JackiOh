[[R634]] had a request to the account that fails or never answers change nothing on screen and raise nothing, so a change that never reached the account went unsaid until coming back online, the tab being shown again, the next change or the next load sent it.

Now, while the account sync's last request has failed, the settings dialog shows one quiet line above its footer, on every tab: "Settings didn’t save to your account.", with Try again, which at once loads the account's copy again when the first load never answered and otherwise sends what is owed, as [[R634]]'s own retries do. The line goes as soon as a request is out and stays gone once one succeeds; with nothing syncing (signed out, pending or banned) it never shows. It is a status, not an alert: the settings on the device stay in force, nothing else on screen changes, and [[R634]]'s automatic retries are unchanged. Rejected: a toast or a mark on the gear outside the dialog (the failure matters to a player looking at their settings, and the device's copy is the one the game reads), and a sentence of its own for a failed load (either way the player's settings have not reached the account).
