Animosity casts itself the moment its owner's exile holds 5 cards, wherever in the deck it is: the settled state check queues it, hidden, and the cast is its owner's own — they make its choices. It fires whenever the condition holds at a settled check, not only as the exile goes from 4 to 5, and a card that leaves the deck casts nothing further; one action queues at most `DECK_CAST_ACTION_CAP` casts. A fusion drops the hook, as `evolvesWhen`. Rejected: firing only on the 4-to-5 transition, which would miss an exile that jumps past it.
