Stinkyscape #137.1 Land Doctrine: "Your Starship attacks a random enemy Unit" (Radiant: "each enemy Unit"). (ST-S02-8.) A forced attack ([[R53]]) by its caster's launched Starship on an enemy Unit drawn from the match rng among those it may attack ([[§4.2]] step 2, Flying's rule holding, [[R60]]), the target striking back. The Radiant face's Starship attacks each enemy Unit in lane order, as the Doctrine resolves, each its own combat and state check, the run stopping once the Starship has left the field. With no enemy Unit, no attack.
