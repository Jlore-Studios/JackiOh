Issue #3996 made TG #52 Legacy: Snom Bunny Mind Control cost (3) and read "Steal an enemy Unit. Next turn, lose (2) mana." on its base face and the same with (1) on its Radiant face. The loss is `next_turn_mana` on its caster ([[R1848004]]): their next refresh gives them their max mana less it, never below 0, a badge both seats read until then, and the refresh after it is whole again. Once the Spell resolves with its target, the loss is paid whatever the steal does: the two run in one list, so a steal that finds no free zone to land in ([[R15]]) still costs the mana, as Hearthstone's Overload is locked in as its card is played, whatever the card then does. With no enemy Unit to target, the play stays legal and the Spell fizzles whole, as [[§8]]'s Conventions have every Spell whose target set is empty: it counts as played and nothing is lost, the case Hearthstone never meets since it refuses a targeted spell with no target. The Radiant face's target is an enemy Unit again; the any-permanent reading of [[R3139002]], and the base face's 2/2, are kept for the replays of the versions through v0.6.5b (`script_v0_6_5b`, [[R767000]]). The number is the declared `loss`.
