**[[R4391000]] Replay shares count against an hourly limit, and a replay already shared tells the friend nothing again (issue #4391, 2026-11-21)**: sharing a replay had no limit, and each share, a repeat of one already held included, wrote a new unread feed row and pushed a toast to the friend, so a loop could re-light the bell at will or push every other row out of the friend's feed. An account may now make `REPLAY_SHARES_PER_HOUR` (30) share attempts an hour, every attempt counted and before any lookup; the next is 429 `rate_limited` with `retryAfterMs`. A share already held answers 204 as before but writes no row and tells nobody, `shares_put` now reporting whether it wrote one. Rejected: counting shares against `CHALLENGES_PER_HOUR` ([[R3730005]]), which would let a batch of shares block unrelated challenges. It takes R4391000 and edits [[§9.5]] and [[§9.8]].
