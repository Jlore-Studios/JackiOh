**[[R4257000]] The AI's static Cleave splash meets a GIGA in both its lanes (issue #4257, 2026-11-20):** the AI's static Cleave splash (`cleaved_neighbours`, [[§9.9]]), which the opponent's reply and the count of the damage past the AI's Taunts read, is now the engine's own walk, the board fact `query::cleave_struck` less the target: a GIGA beside the target from either of its lanes is met once, a GIGA target splashes the units beside both its lanes ([[R3546001]]), and beside a Unit standing in the backrow only a Unit standing there is struck ([[R1081000]]). Where such a splash through a GIGA meets a later Taunt, the Taunt walk tries a third order, that Cleave attacker first ([[R3937000]]), so a CN Golem and a 6 against a GIGA Taunt beside a Thorium Wall count the 6 that goes face. The flag `giga_cleave_read` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`. Rejected: a second copy of the walk in the AI, and the Cleave-first order on boards with no GIGA. It adds no rule of the game and no constant.
