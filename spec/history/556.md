**[[R1430]] and [[R1431]] are patch v0.3.3 (issue #556, 2026-10-08)**, two follow-ups to patch v0.3.2's numbers (issue #493) the owner decided: a number of #98 Heroic Power belongs to its power and is tuned only while the card has that power, keeping its tuning meanwhile ([[R1430]]); and a number only the base face prints is tuned on the base face only, [[R749]]'s mirror ([[R1431]]). They take two numbers of the foundation's block (R1420–R1429), assigned to them while several branches were in flight. They edit the Tunes notes of the thirteen cards they change in [[§8.2]], [[§8.3]], [[§8.5]], [[§8.6]] and [[§8.7]], [[R386]], [[R482]], [[R749]], [[R754]] and [[R756]].
