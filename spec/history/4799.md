**[[R4799000]] The greedy baseline and the skill miss give each candidate the nodes its own simulation may take, its surcharge among them (issue #4799, 2026-11-26)**: an audit of the AI crate (issue #4787) found the greedy baseline and the skill miss sizing one counter at 9 nodes a candidate, so a play `simulate` surcharges (a Zephrys, a play Pareto Optimality judges) was refused or took the nodes of the candidates after it. The ruling gives each candidate a counter of its own and the sentence of [[§9.9]]'s Quality gates that names it; it takes R4799000 of the issue's block.

**[[R4799001]] A play's placement reads a zone a GIGA covers as the GIGA, and the half of a slashed board its lane is on (issue #4799, 2026-11-26)**: the same audit found the AI's placement key reading a zone a GIGA covers as open and nothing of a slashed board, so a lane that plays differently was merged with another and never tried. The ruling adds both to the key and a sentence to [[§9.9]]'s Search; it takes R4799001.

**[[R4799002]] Frenzy is no attack hook to the AI's attack-hook probe, which cannot measure it (issue #4799, 2026-11-26)**: the same audit found the attack-hook probe spending nodes on every Frenzy card to read 0, its twin frenzying as the unit does. The ruling takes Frenzy out of the hooked test, adds a clause to [[§9.9]]'s Evaluation and takes R4799002. The audit's fifth item, `play_ai_turn`'s nonces, was fixed by issue #4786 before this branch and takes no ruling.

**[[R4799003]] A launch-only Dormant Starship counts its launch in full, as a shipyard does (issue #4799, 2026-11-27)**: the same audit found `slumber_worth` putting back only `1 − share` of a sleeper's waking price, on the ground that the awake copy counts the rest inside the slumber share; a Dormant Starship whose pieces print only keywords has its one ability, the engine's launch, only while it slumbers, so its awake copy counted none of it. The AI now puts back the sleeper's price less the share of the awake copy's own: a shipyard reads as before and a launch-only Starship's launch counts whole. It amends [[R3575404]] and takes no number.
