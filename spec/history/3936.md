**[[R3936000]] The AI's mulligan counts a Tribute card's units (issue #3936, 2026-11-15)**: amends [[R2977000]]. The AI's mulligan priced every card by its mana cost alone, so a card that also asks for units to sacrifice ([[§6.3]]) looked cheap: holding Blue-Eyes White Felinor (1 mana, Tribute 2), Lava Golem and Trigor (3 mana, Tribute 3), it kept all three, none playable before it had built a board, and counted Felinor as its early play. A card's mulligan cost is now its mana cost or, while it has Tribute X as Nerfs, Buffs and Floats left it ([[R386]], [[R1841003]]), the larger of that and X plus `AI_MULLIGAN_TRIBUTE_ALLOWANCE` (1), the turn after one unit a turn has laid X down. The keep's bound, the one-drop cap and the early-play check read it; the shaped exception, read off the printed cost, never takes a card its Tribute made dearer; a Tribute brought to 0 costs mana alone. It reads only the AI's own hand. The bound, the early-play limit and The Coin still move with the seat's crystals ([[R244]]), so a Hard seat keeps Lava Golem and the seat going second counts Felinor as an early play. Lava Golem may tribute enemy units, but no enemy holds one that early, so its Tribute counts as any other's. The greedy baseline's frozen mulligan reads mana alone. Rejected: Tribute times an allowance, which would send back the Tribute 2 three-drops a seat plays on turn 3; a field on `AiMulligan`, which `GREEDY_MULLIGAN` shares; an exception read off the mulligan cost, which would keep Lava Golem (shaped weight 3.0) at the bound of 3. It takes R3936000, the first number of issue #3936's block, and edits [[§9.9]].
