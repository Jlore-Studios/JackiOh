**[[R1833000]] doubles the cosmetics registry with 36 cosmetics accounts earn, the first earnable chases and Mythics among them, and gates the account picks by ownership (issue #1833, 2026-10-25)**: past the full arts and the portraits every cosmetic was a starter, so the global roll ([[R1661001]]), cosmetic packs ([[R1306000]]), Epic tokens ([[R1305003]]) and the Chase Shard bar ([[R1306002]]) had little or nothing to give, and no chase could be earned ([[R1333000]]). The registry appends six foils, six finishes, six borders, six playmats, six backdrops and six card backs, none a default or a starter, their Legendary and Mythic ones chases; the global roll's pool is the five portraits and the 18 account ones. No account is granted any of them, existing ones included ([[§14.12]]). While ownership decides, the playmat, backdrop and card-back pickers and the account-wide foil, finish and border offer only what the account owns, a stored pick it does not own draws as its kind's default, and a deck's save refuses an unowned playmat, backdrop or card back; practice, hotseat and [[R1310004]]'s builds still own every one. Granting the 36 to existing accounts, locked entries in the selects and a client-only gate were rejected.
