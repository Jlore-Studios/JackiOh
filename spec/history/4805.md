**[[R4805000]] The AI's Taunt walk lands a Cleave splash on every unit it strikes, and a Trample attacker's tramples over each onto the hero (issue #4805, 2026-11-25)**: an audit of the AI crate found the walk behind `face_threat` and `ready_gap` reading 0 for a Cleave and Trample attack that kills the hero through two splashes on units without Taunt, the engine dealing 8. The ruling adds the flag `splash_trample_read` (on in `AI_EVAL`, off in `GREEDY_EVAL`) and the clause of [[§9.9]]'s Evaluation that names it, extending [[R4402000]]; it takes R4805000 of the issue's block.
