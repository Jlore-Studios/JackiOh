Now, while `wall_switches` ([[R2976000]]) offers a wall, the step tries the best ranked attack first and up to `AI_REPLY.defense_switches` of those walls through `best_try`, one node each, by the opponent's own evaluation, and keeps a wall only when it beats both standing still and that attack; an attack that wins is still taken. It walls nothing while the opponent's attacks left reach the seat's hero together (`ready_gap`), and a Deft unit that may still attack switches only after its attack ([[R49]]). Off lethal nothing changes and no node is spent; it adds no constant and no weight. It takes R4187000 and edits [[§9.9]].
