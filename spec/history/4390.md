**[[R4390000]] The daily sweep deletes every daily invite past its lapse, whatever became of it (issue #4390, 2026-11-21)**: nothing deleted a daily invite, although every read skips a closed or lapsed one, so a challenge or a seek stayed for good with the sender's frozen deck and who challenged whom. §9.13's sweep, in its own transaction, now deletes every invite whose `expires_at` has passed, in any state (open, accepted, declined, cancelled, expired or paired), as §9.5 does a live challenge, and counts them in its answer and log (`purged`). An invite not yet lapsed stays. The game an accepted challenge or a paired seek started is not touched, since nothing reads `daily_invites.match_id`; the `dailyChallenge` and `dailyAnswered` feed rows carry only the invite's id and stay as [[R4233000]] has them; the cap of [[R3727004]] is unchanged. No index, no migration.

**[[R4390001]] A replay share is deleted once the retention purge has taken its replay's log (issue #4390, 2026-11-21)**: §9.5 says a share lasts until the purge takes the log, but only an unshare, an unfriend or a block deleted its row. The retention loop now deletes, in a step of its own after the purge (`shares.purge`, its own failing streak), every share whose match holds no action-log row; a share of a held log is untouched and reads answer as before. No index, no migration. See also [[R4390000]].
