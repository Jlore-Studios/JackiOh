**[[R4813000]] A Duos AI seat's stale pass or end of turn is decided again (issue #4813, 2026-11-25)**: an audit of the AI's Duos play found the host playing an AI seat's answer whenever the engine still took it, however far the game had moved since the seat was asked, so two AI seats asked on one state at the end of team B's round wasted the pass between them: a card passed to a teammate that had ended, or a turn ended with a card just passed to it unplayed. The Duos host now drops a pass, or an end of turn once the seat's hand has changed, decided on a state since moved past, and asks the seat again. It edits [[§13.7]] (a bullet after **Passing.**) and takes R4813000.
