**[[R4319000]] A screen that decided too few deals says it measured nothing (issue #4319, 2026-11-20):** a screen's paired tally counts `decided` (the deals whose edge is not 0, split into `wonBoth` and `lostBoth`) and `diverged` (the deals whose two games end on different hashes). A screen with fewer than `SCREEN_MIN_DECIDED` (20) decided deals prints `inconclusive: true` and neither keeps nor drops the change; `--extend` plays on in 100-deal batches to 20 decided deals or `SCREEN_MAX_GAMES` (2,000) games, reading the edge only at the end. The screen comments in `crates/ai/src/config.rs` record each past screen's decided count; no knob changes value.
