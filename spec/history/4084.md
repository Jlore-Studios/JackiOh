**[[R4084000]] The /stats Cards table's bar reads the adjusted win rate (issue #4084, 2026-11-16)**: the little bar (`.stats-confidence-bar` / `.stats-confidence-fill`) moves from the Win rate cell to the Adjusted cell, sized by the adjusted rate on the same 0–100% scale, shown only with enough games and a numeric rate. The Win rate cell keeps its percentage and its 95% range, and a row below the floor or without an adjusted rate reads "—" in the Adjusted cell with no bar.
