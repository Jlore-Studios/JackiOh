**[[R1849000]] to [[R1849009]] are Stinkyscape's combat and replacement verbs, built on `main` before the set (issue #1849, part of #1836, 2026-10-25)**: nine engine primitives, proved on fixture scripts, which no card reaches until the set's cards do. Immune to Spell Damage makes a Spell's hit on the Unit no damage instance, before its Divine Shield, while Spells still target it ([[R1849000]]). Evolve runs the Unit's own Evolve text when an effect evolves it, and "summon a copy of this, then evolve" summons the copy first ([[R1849001]]). Destroy becomes exile: while the aura acts, what the state check collects is exiled instead, judged before anything moves, the Radiant face's for the enemy's cards only, and a Sacrifice still reaches the graveyard ([[R1849002]]). A reflecting Unit deals what it takes again from itself to the enemy hero, and on the Radiant face to the attacker whose own strike it was ([[R1849003]]). The own-turn redirect moves a hit on its controller's hero, on their turn and before their Armor, to the opponent's hero, up to a cap a turn on what is taken, the Radiant face doubling what is dealt ([[R1849004]]). The Gauntlet's middle three lanes attack only each other, attacks and forced attacks alike, and a Unit's text still targets across it ([[R1849005]]). "Units cannot be in Defense Position" as an aura reaches both sides and flips a Unit already in Defense at the state check ([[R1849006]]). A forced attack by "a random Unit of yours" is drawn among the Units that may attack the target, with no draw when none can ([[R1849007]]). "When one of your Units is attacked" runs after a declared attack's trap window and before its combat, never for a hero or a forced attack ([[R1849008]]). A trap's chance to reveal is one roll each time its triggers admit an event, Luck and Lucky rolling again, and a miss leaves it set ([[R1849009]]). Hearthstone settles each where the designer's text does not; the readings a person may overrule before the cards are built are the Gauntlet covering attacks alone, the redirect acting before the Armor and the copy coming before the evolve. A `KeywordKind` for the two printed keywords was rejected, as it would widen the learned network's input ([[R1010000]]): both are static flags. The new state is optional and absent from every shipped game. It takes R1849000 to R1849009, the first numbers of issue #1849's block, and edits [[§4.1]], [[§4.2]], [[§4.4]], [[§4.5]], [[§5.1]], [[§6.1]], [[§6.2]], [[§6.3]] and [[§10.9]].
