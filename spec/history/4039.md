**[[R4039000]] The server keeps a notification feed for each account, written where each event is handled and kept until read (issue #4039, 2026-11-16; amends [[R3214000]], [[R3729002]] and [[R3730005]])**: the owners chose not to send player email for now, so JackiOh tells a player what happened itself and keeps telling them until they have seen it. Each event writes one row for one account to `public.notifications` (migration 0093, the server's alone), by the code that already handles it and after its commit, in a transaction of its own whose failure is logged and loses the row: `dailyMove` when a daily game begins waiting on its recipient (the waiting seeker of a paired seek too, the challenger of an accept being told by `dailyAnswered` alone), `dailyEnded` to both players with the result each sees (on time and by concession included, a void telling nobody), `dailyChallenge`, `dailyAnswered` (accepted or declined; a cancel or a lapse tells nobody), `friendRequest`, `friendAccepted` (an accept or a mutual request), `lobbyInvite` (listed while its lobby is open and unstarted) and `replayShared`. A new row replaces its recipient's unread one of the same kind, actor and subject; nobody is told of their own act and a block either way writes nothing; an account keeps its newest `NOTIFICATIONS_KEEP` (100) and the daily sweep deletes rows older than `NOTIFICATIONS_KEEP_DAYS` (30). `GET /api/notifications?before&limit` pages the caller's own rows newest first, `POST /api/notifications/read {upTo?}` marks them read up to an id inclusive or all, each new row reaches its recipient's `/ws/social` sockets as a `notification` frame, the social read counts the unread as `notificationsUnread`, and `GET /api/daily/settings` says `emailAvailable`. Rejected: writing inside each event's own transaction, replacing only `dailyMove` rows, a feed kept in memory.
