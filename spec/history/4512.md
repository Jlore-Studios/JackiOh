**[[R4512000]] The crowd stays quiet while the page is hidden, as effects and voices are (issue #4512, 2026-11-22)**: a match in a background tab plays no crowd sound: while the page is hidden the engine's `crowdOutput()` returns null, so a murmur or reaction that comes due is dropped as one due on a suspended context is, nothing is saved up to burst out on return, and the murmur loop keeps its rhythm and murmurs again on return; the gate closes whatever the audio settings say, following the effects' rule rather than the music's "Keep playing music when this tab is in the background" setting ([[§10.11]], [[R631]], [[R669]]), and a window that is visible but not focused keeps its crowd.
