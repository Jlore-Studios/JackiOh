**[[R831000]] to [[R831016]] are the Meditative patch notes' first round (issue #831, patch v0.3.10, 2026-10-15)**: the designer's balance, text and behavior changes for some fifty cards, most of them Meditative. Where the notes leave a decision open the change follows Hearthstone and a ruling says so: Silly Silas rotates into Locked zones ([[R831000]]); Palantir casts the (1) Spell it steals, every choice random and aimed ([[R831001]]); the night market sells each lot on its Radiant face too ([[R831002]]); KY's Papaya Discovers each cell among 3 and announces its curve in a public `curveDrawn` event ([[R831003]]); KY's Trial Discovers a set, then a number in it ([[R831004]]); Prestige makes the next card played this turn Radiant ([[R831005]]); Windfast's substitute is a random Unit from hand ([[R831006]]); Tesla zaps only the Unit that reveals it ([[R831007]]); Showdown asks for an empty lane, all four zones empty ([[R831008]]); The Power to Thrive's card is chosen as it resolves ([[R831009]]); Tommy Tempo loses the mana left once its price is paid ([[R831010]]); Twice Forward is Animated and fuses a copy, never the card ([[R831011]]); Ever Growing Tree offers 2 Field Spells ([[R831012]]); Jilliax is Equip only at its (3) price ([[R831013]]); Selective Breeding exiles every deck card costing (2) or less per [[R65]] ([[R831014]]); one Corpse Plantation Activate arms one graveyard play ([[R831015]]); and Plague Crawler may place on itself ([[R831016]]). It edits [[§8.3]], [[§8.6]], [[§8.7]] and [[§8.8]]'s rows of those cards, [[§5.1]]'s tag note, [[§6.1]]'s Equip row, [[R14]], [[R54]], [[R88]], [[R247]], [[R422]], [[R425]], [[R514]], [[R589]], [[R688]], [[R945]], [[R1000]], [[R1086]], [[R1103]], [[R1104]], [[R1202]], [[R1421]], [[R1444]] and [[R781002]]. CN Flea Market's ten new shop cards are left to the designer.
