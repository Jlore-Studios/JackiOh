**[[R3944000]] One patch a day: `patches ship` names only majors and micros, and `patches cut` rolls the micros since the newest major or patch into the day's patch (issue #3944, 2026-11-15)**: a set that ships is a major, `v0.(X+1).0`, at once; every other fragment is a micro, the next letters, however many cards it reprints or plays, whatever rule it meets and whatever system it claims; and a patch, `v0.X.(Y+1)`, comes only from `patches cut`, which `patches-ship.yml` runs once a day after the ship (waiting while production does not hold the newest major or patch) and by hand with `cut` ticked (the fast-forward, no wait). The cut lists the micros it rolls up in `includes`, unites their plays, merges their player notes and takes the newest one's snapshot; a major rolls up the micros before it the same way. A pull request's tier label is the tier it ships as, never `tier:patch`. Card statistics are unchanged ([[R1367000]]), and no shipped version is renamed. Amends [[R1366000]] and [[R2529000]].

**[[R3944001]] The Patch notes page nests the micros a patch rolls up under it as its snapshots, and shows the micros no patch has rolled up yet above the lines (issue #3944, 2026-11-15)**: a micro an entry's `includes` names sits inside that entry under "Snapshots", in that entry's release line; a micro after the newest major or patch sits in a "Since the last patch" block above the lines; every other micro sits under the major or patch before it, as before. Amends [[R3012000]].

**[[R3944002]] A daily patch's cards, art and GitHub release are its micros', and a micro a patch rolls up has no release of its own (issue #3944, 2026-11-15)**: a patch with `includes` reads its cards from its micros, against the snapshot before the first of them; its release art is drawn from them, and its GitHub release lists its snapshots and their cards. A micro a patch includes, or the next one will, gets no release. Amends [[R3012001]].
