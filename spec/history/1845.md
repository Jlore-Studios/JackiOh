**[[R1845000]] to [[R1845002]] are hidden Nerfs and trade offers, the mechanics Stinkyscape #123 Gokugains and #77 Yuma are written with, built on `main` before the set (issue #1845, 2026-10-25)**: two engine primitives, proved on fixture scripts, which no card reaches until the set's cards do. A hidden Nerf is a count on a card in a hand or a deck that no seat reads, its owner included: nothing is reported, no view shows it, and the AI's redaction and the dry run's concealed copy drop it ([[R1845000]]). It lands at [[§10.5]] step 3 of the card's next play or cast as that many Nerfs, each drawn from every row but the cost's and reported as every Nerf is ([[R1845001]]). A trade offer waits for the start of the opponent's next turn; then its controller gives a card from their hand and the opponent Discovers one of their own to give back, through two ordinary prompts ([[R1845002]]). Hearthstone has neither, so each follows its nearest kin here: a Nerf's own rows, and C #9's change of hands asked as [[R1279000]] asks. Nerfs applied at once and hidden, which need a second tuning on every card, and a simultaneous swap kept hidden until both have chosen were rejected.
