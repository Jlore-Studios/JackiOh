**[[R4013000]] A 1v1 nonce copied by the other seat is taken, and the owner's action stands (issue #4013, 2026-11-17)**: a 1v1 action nonce is only its own seat's duplicate check ([[§9.3]]): the seat the server stamps on the row ([[§9.1]]) owns the nonce, its resend answers its original ack with no second row, and another seat's copy is answered `taken` while the owner's action still applies, as the co-op host's is. Rejected: global dedup, which let a copy read as the owner's ack, and per-seat-independent nonces, which the store's global per-match nonce key ([[R776000]]) cannot hold without a migration.
