**[[R4082000]] A Created card's adjusted win rate and tier, read against the other Created cards (issue #4082, 2026-11-16; amends [[R3324002]])**: a seat makes a Created card only once a card it played made it, so the seats that made one win more than the average deck whatever the card. Each Created card a card's text makes is read against the Created field instead: [[R3022000]]'s DerSimonian and Laird estimate over the Created cards' made lifts (made win rate less the average deck's, sampling variance ¼ over made games), whose centre is that shared selection, measured. Its adjusted win rate is [[R3070000]]'s held posterior of the made games against the field's baseline (the average deck plus the centre), so an average Created card reads 50%. Its 95% range is [[R2895000]]'s and its tier [[R3022000]]'s. With fewer than 30 Created cards at the floor, every Created card is read against the average deck. The Coin, Glitch and the Dormant Starship, which a rule makes, are read against the average deck and are no part of the field. `GET /api/stats/cards` answers each Created row's `adjustedWinRate`, range and `tier`, and `tiers.created`. `/stats`' Tokens and Other created cards tables show Tier and Adjusted, open on Adjusted, and say what the cards are read against. A token's Statistics panel shows its Created figures.
