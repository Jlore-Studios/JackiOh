**[[R4145000]] Stage 2 forces script-claimed cards in groups of five, falling back card by card (issue #4145, 2026-11-18)**: `classify` played `FORCED_GAMES` (40) forced games on both builds for each script-claimed card one at a time, so a refactor across many scripts cost one twenty-second run a card and outgrew CI's time limit. Stage 2 now forces those cards in groups of `FORCED_GROUP_CARDS` (5), each card still in 40 games of the group's one recorded run and one parent check. A group with no divergence touching one of its cards, or touching no card, clears every card in it; a group with one, or one the recorder or the parent cannot play, falls back to each card's own forced games exactly as before, a card that cannot be forced still counting as changed. [[R3935001]]'s steered subjects never join a group, and the `PATCH_MIN_CARDS` stop is checked between groups as between cards. Rejected: groups of ten (two-fifths of a deck) and halving a diverged group.
