**[[R4773000]] On a phone a mounted game board locks the page against scroll, bounce and pull-to-refresh (issue #4773, 2026-11-24)**: while a `Game` is mounted the document never scrolls or overscrolls on touch — no page scroll from a board swipe, no rubber-band bounce, no pull-to-refresh, and no page movement during a drag or an aim — and unmounting restores normal page scrolling. `Game` marks the document element with `data-game-scroll-lock`; the board carries `touch-action: none` while every menu and the log scroll inside their own regions (`overscroll-behavior: contain`). Extends [[R1581000]], which rules the same for menu screens and explicitly excludes boards.
