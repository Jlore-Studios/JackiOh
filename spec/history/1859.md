**[[R1859000]] to [[R1859013]] are the Starship cards of the Stinkyscape set (issue #1859, its part S02, part of #1856, 2026-10-27)**: the Dormant Starship and the eleven cards built on [[R1852000]]'s Starship, held by [[R1420]]'s gate with the rest of the set. A fused piece's numbers are read on the face it fused on, so a Radiant piece's When launched reads its Radiant numbers in a Starship that is not ([[R1859001]]); After Burner's keywords, Warpfield Amplifier's switch to Attack and its lasting Attack, and Ultra Capacitor's count of the pieces the Starship remembers are read off the Starship it fused into ([[R1859000]], [[R1859002]], [[R1859004]]); Spacerace Stimulus reveals once, on any launch or, Radiant, on a Starship Piece played face-up, a face-down play being no piece ([[R1859003]]); Magic Module's Spells are the ones the catalog marks `dealsDamage`, which a query now names ([[R1859005]]); Firststrike Capability's Doctrine resolves inside its Cry, before the Starship's When launched ([[R1859006]]); "for the rest of the game, your Units have X" is a player modifier the layers read beside the auras, not on a Vanilla Unit ([[R1859007]]), and each War Doctrine's Starship line runs once, as it is cast ([[R1859008]] to [[R1859011]]); Prepare For Liftoff answers its owner's piece fusing into their Starship, decided against the brief's controller reading ([[R1859012]]); and the Dormant Starship's script is the hull alone ([[R1859013]]). The part also fixes [[R1852003]]'s When launched, which a piece on the field, or a Starship launched before, answered on every later launch. They take the first numbers of issue #1859's block: [[R1859000]] is the brief's ST-S02-1, [[R1859002]] to [[R1859013]] its ST-S02-2 to ST-S02-13, and [[R1859001]] the build's own.
