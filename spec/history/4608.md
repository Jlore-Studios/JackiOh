**[[R4608000]] A finalist that wins only on the sampled world the beam searched is ranked by its mean on the others (issue #4608, 2026-11-23)**: `decide` searched its turn on the first sampled world alone and played the finalist with the best mean over every world it scored, so a line that won on that world alone averaged a third of a won game and beat every line that won nowhere, though the beam had found it because it won there: a random effect's lucky roll, a draw from the seat's own shuffled library or a face-down card dealt as harmless. A finalist whose first world's score is a won game and whose other worlds, at least one, won none now takes its mean on those others (`finalist_means`, a win above `AI_WON_SCORE_SHARE` of `AI_EVAL.win`). A win on two worlds keeps the plain mean and is still played at once, a decision that scored one world keeps today's order, and losses stay in the mean; the lethal solver, the lethal hint ([[R2102000]]) and the concession check ([[R2660000]]) are untouched, and the ladder bots and the Duos AI team change with the practice AI. `AI_SEARCH.confirm_lone_win` ships on; off gives the old means bit for bit.
