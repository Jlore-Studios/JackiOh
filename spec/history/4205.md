**[[R4205000]] A steal with no room destroys the card, and nothing prevents that destroy (issue #4205, 2026-11-18)**: amends [[R15]], [[R963]], [[R1045000]], [[R1423]] and [[R3546003]]. A steal that reaches a card on the field with no free zone in its row on the thief's side, or no fit for a GIGA, destroyed only under Allure and otherwise left it where it stood; now every such steal marks it destroyed, and the state check collects it past Indestructible, Untributable and any "would die" replacement, while a destroy-to-exile aura still exiles it and its Death and Reborn apply on its own side. A give the other way ([[R803]]) and a card no steal reaches (Dormant, beneath a Stack, already the thief's) are unchanged, and the Radiant riders of #49 and M #97.4 land only on a card the steal took ([[R282]]). It edits [[§4.5]] and [[§6.3]].
