# Pending: a Unit token into a deck, and the Trade of a Unit-token card (issue #4821)

The §11 paragraphs of issue #4821, an audit of the engine's turns, drawing and zones, 2026-11-25.

**[[R4821000]] A unit token sent into a deck with room ceases to exist and is reported `libraryOverflow` `ceased`, never `shuffledIn` (issue #4821, 2026-11-25)**: The audit found a token that S #12.3 Psychic Scream sent into a deck with room reported `shuffledIn` though [[R11]] had ended it. Edits [[§6.3]]'s Shuffle into row and adds a sentence to [[R316]].

**[[R4821001]] A unit-token card cannot be Traded, since it would cease to exist on the way into the deck (issue #4821, 2026-11-25)**: The same audit found a Rush Token card lent Tradable by S #164 The Syndicate traded into an empty deck, vanishing and fatiguing its player. Edits [[§6.1]]'s Tradable row and adds a sentence to [[R1838000]].

**[[R4821002]] A ceased card reads as ceased, not as a full deck (issue #4821, 2026-11-25)**: Follows from [[R4821000]], which lets a `ceased` refusal come from a deck with room. Edits [[§10.10]]'s `libraryOverflow` clause and adds a clause to [[R318]].
