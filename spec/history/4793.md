**[[R4793000]] The AI's search tells apart which hand card a buff or a granted keyword went on (issue #4793, 2026-11-26)**: the audit of issue #4787 found that `search_signature` signed neither a hand card's buffs nor its granted keywords, so Plesiosize or GIGA-ify on one hand unit and on another read as one position, and the beam, the lethal solver and the concession check kept only the first. A card off the field now signs both, each only when it holds some. It edits [[§9.9]]'s Search and adds the switch `AI_SEARCH.hand_grants_signed`.
