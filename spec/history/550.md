**[[R1380]] to [[R1384]] are the Almanac and the set mark (issue #550, part MN07 of #496, 2026-10-09)**: the set chips, the sets that ship, in the Almanac and the deck builder ([[R1380]]); the tag chips, the tags a shipped card carries, Wincon's among them ([[R1381]]); "All Tribes" on a card with every tribal tag ([[R1382]]); the newest set's "New" ribbon for its patch and the next ([[R1383]]); and the glossary's rows for the Meditative cards' words ([[R1384]]). Meditative's mark, an ensō with a red seal, is [[R503]]'s. They take the first numbers of MN07's block (R1380–R1389, `docs/meditative-set.md` M10). They edit [[§10.10]], [[R503]] and [[R630]].
