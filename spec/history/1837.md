**[[R1837000]] puts five family tags on shipped cards and takes Felinor and Fruit off four (issue #1837, 2026-10-25)**: the Stinkyscape set's cards discover Terminus cards, complete Quests, add Other-Origins cards and discover a Jelly Bean, and its Fruit Launcher casts a random Fruit, so ahead of the set the designer tagged #100 Ceaseless Void Terminus, C #90 In Too Deep Quest, C+ #32 Otherworldly Removal Otherworldly, its three tokens Other-Origins and the four Glowy Jelly Beans Jelly Bean, and took Felinor off C #32 Felinor Feelings and #62 Friend of Felinors and Fruit off C+ #63 Fruit Tree and C+ #68 Organic Produce, the two Field Spells that make Fruit. The spec did not say what kind of tag the five are; following Hearthstone, which keeps its Quests apart from its minion types, they are family tags, not tribes ([[R1424]]), and no pool by one of them takes tokens ([[R382]]) until a card that names one decides otherwise.
