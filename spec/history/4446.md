**[[R4446000]] The hourly retention purge deletes cancelled queue tickets once they are an hour old (issue #4446, 2026-11-22)**: a ticket, holding a copy of its player's chosen deck, was only marked cancelled when its player left the queue, went unheard from for `QUEUE_TICKET_STALE_MS` ([[R897000]]) or was cleared at a match end, and nothing read it again, so every enqueue left a row for good. The retention loop now deletes, in a step of its own after the share step (`tickets.purge`, its own failing streak), every cancelled ticket enqueued more than `QUEUE_TICKET_CANCELLED_KEEP_SECONDS` (1 hour) ago and logs `tickets.purged`. A queued ticket and a claimed one stay, the latter because a queue match's mode is read from it (`matches_mode_of`). No index, no migration. See also [[R4390000]], [[R4390001]].
