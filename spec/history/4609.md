**[[R4609000]] What the fuzz monitor's I23 judges: the text of a card Immutable as a step begins and after it (issue #4609, 2026-11-24)**: since [[R4353000]] every Indestructible Unit card is Immutable, and since [[R4353001]] a Spell may be aimed at one and must leave its text alone, but no check of the fuzz monitor read Immutable. I23 now fails a step that changes the text of a card Immutable by the keyword, or by Indestructible on a card whose running face prints the Unit type: a `transformed` of it, a `degraded` or `upgraded` of it, a `fused` onto it, or its record (definition, Vanilla flag, tuning, hidden Nerfs, Set stats' `statsOverride` ([[R1185002]]), copied text) changed with no such event. It reads a field card face-up through the layers and a hand or library card by its own keywords, never a Dormant, face-down or Indestructible Field Spell card nor a fused ingredient, never Radiant ([[R23]]), and judges a card only while it is Immutable after the step with its starting text put back, or, gone, by its own keywords. It reads the events, the state and the catalog, never `layers::is_immutable` or `restrictions`, on the played path, on I14's branches and in each Duos duel; a sweep plays every card that calls a text-rewriting verb, on both faces, at Immutable stand-ins.

**[[R4609001]] A Replace off the field and a Nerf's `none` cue change no Immutable card's text, and I23 allows them (issue #4609, 2026-11-24)**: a Transform off the field replaces the card with another ([[R35]], [[R921]]), which rewrites nothing, so a `transformed` to a new instance hidden from a player, as a hand or library card's is ([[R177]]), is lawful; a Replace on the field and a `transformed` in place in a hand are findings. A `degraded` or `upgraded` whose change is `none`, [[R440]]'s cue on a card someone may not read, is lawful too. Rejected: refusing the Replace of an Immutable hand or library card, which would reverse [[R35]].

**[[R4609002]] A Fuse onto a card the step made, a card reset in place and a board restore or Glitch are lawful for I23 (issue #4609, 2026-11-24)**: a Fuse onto a card that did not exist as the step began, as S+ T-Supernova's onto its fresh Alnilam ([[R4353003]]), is not judged; a card `flickered` or `summoned` back through Reborn is reset as it leaves the field ([[R78]]) and its record is not held from then on; and a step with a `rolledBack` ([[R419]], [[R4454002]]) or a Glitch's reset or boards ([[R676]], [[R678]]) holds no record to its start. Their `transformed`, `degraded`, `upgraded` and `fused` are still judged.

**[[R4609003]] A keyword a card keeps for good is a grant, not a Buff, and I23 allows it on an Immutable card (issue #4609, 2026-11-24)**: a keyword kept for good ([[R1870007]], [[R4154202]]) rides the card's tuning and is reported by a `keywordGranted`; it is a grant, which Immutable never stopped ([[R23]]), not a Buff from [[R386]]'s menu. A tuning whose added keywords grew by exactly the keywords the step's `keywordGranted` names on the card, with nothing else changed, is no finding.
