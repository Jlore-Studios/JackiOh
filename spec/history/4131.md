**[[R4131000]] Wrong invite codes block one profile or address for its hour, never everyone (issue #4131, 2026-11-18)**: redemption has no global circuit breaker. [[§9.4]] steps 2 and 3 are its only blocks, each holding one profile or one address off until its oldest counted attempt leaves the hour. A guess spread over many addresses cannot find an 80-bit code, and a breaker it could trip would shut sign-ups for everyone, so this replaces [[R106]]'s breaker (100 failures system-wide in 600 seconds) and its alert. The operator's switch (`moderate redemption off`) is the one global pause, mirrored by the server for `REDEMPTION_PAUSE_MIRROR_SECONDS`
