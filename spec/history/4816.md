**[[R4816000]] A card a Transform makes arrives on the field: its printed Dormant, a hull's memory and its start-of-game clause start, though it is no summon (issue #4816, 2026-11-25)**: An audit of the engine's continuous effects found that S+ #103, #104 and #105 left the Dormant cards they made awake, killed the 0/0 Phantom Shipyard they made and left the Heroic Power they made with no power, since a Transform's placement started only Brittle. The ruling edits [[§6.1]]'s Dormant row, [[§6.2]]'s Start of Game row and [[§6.3]]'s Transform row, and takes R4816000.
