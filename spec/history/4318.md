**[[R4318000]] The fuzz's I18 does not judge an attack whose other hits or deaths can lower a combatant (issue #4318, 2026-11-20):** the combat forecast ([[R3100000]]) reads auras as the board shows them, so it does not foretell what the deaths of an attack's other hits (a Cleave's, a sweep's, [[R3554008]]) or of a combatant take away at the state check after the combat (fuzz seed 6180: a sweep killed the two Units whose auras held its target up).
