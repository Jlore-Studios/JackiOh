**[[R4781000]] An open target prompt is answered by a click on the board (issue #4781, 2026-11-24)**: a `target` prompt that offers cards or heroes is answered on the board as well as in its list — a click on an offered card or hero, or on the zone of an offered face-down card, picks that option, at once for a prompt of one pick and into the picker's selection for a prompt of several; the list stays.
