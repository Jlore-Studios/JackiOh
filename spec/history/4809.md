**[[R4809000]] A step of the opponent's reply that the AI's node budget cannot pay for cuts the reply, as a spent budget does, instead of being skipped (issue #4809, 2026-11-25)**: an audit of the AI crate found that the opponent's reply, short of the nodes for an attack's surcharge, ended the opponent's turn and came back whole, and held back a known play it could not pay for, so lines scored late in a decision were scored against an opponent that passed. The reply now cuts there (`simulate_reply` answers `None`) without draining the counter; [[§9.9]]'s Budgets bullet says so. Takes R4809000.

**[[R4809001]] The AI charges the My Pawn surcharge only for an attack the trap would answer (issue #4809, 2026-11-25)**: the same issue found the surcharge charged for any attack by a unit whose Attack was at least the defender's life, an attack on a Defense Position Unit included. It is now charged only when the trap's own predicate (`subsystems::defending_hero` and `is_lethal`) holds; [[§9.9]]'s Budgets bullet says so. Takes R4809001.
