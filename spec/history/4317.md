**[[R4317000]] An issue refuses a practice game's deck its claim's deal would refuse (issue #4317, 2026-11-21)**: a seed request ([[§14.8]]) was kept with the setup the browser sent and its deck checked only when the claim settled, so a forged request could store a deck of the API's whole body cap, claim an empty game at once to free its place ([[R3500001]]) and go again. The request now deals the game its claim would deal (`economy::practice::config_of` and the practice session's `deal`, under the setup's pinned catalog ([[R767000]]) and off the runtime) before it opens a transaction, and a deck the deal refuses is a 400 `bad_request` naming the engine's reason, writing no seed and taking no place among the open ones. A `cards` deck on a seed is at most `DECK_SIZE` catalog ids; a `random` deck passes as before; the claim's check is unchanged. It amends [[R1303001]] and [[R1483000]].

**[[R4317001]] The hourly retention purge deletes every account's lapsed practice seeds (issue #4317, 2026-11-21)**: the account's prune ran only at that account's next seed request ([[R1303001]]), so a seed outlived an account that stopped asking. Each pass of the retention loop, after the sweep of unsettled claims, now deletes every account's seeds issued over `PRACTICE_SEED_TTL_SECONDS` ago that are unclaimed or settled, under a failing streak of its own (`practice.prune`), economy on or off. A claim not yet settled stays for the sweep ([[R2422000]]); the account's prune at its next request stays; a purged seed's claim answers `unknown_seed` where it answered `expired`. It amends [[R1303001]] and [[R2422000]].
