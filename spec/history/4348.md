**[[R4348000]] A block deletes the notifications between the two accounts (issue #4348, 2026-11-21)**: blocking deletes every notification row whose recipient is one of the two accounts and whose actor is the other, either way, read or not, so neither feed lists the other and both unread counts drop; third parties' rows and rows with no actor stay, unblocking restores nothing, and rows written after an unblock arrive as before. Rejected: hiding the rows at list time, which would still light the unread badge and shorten pages.
