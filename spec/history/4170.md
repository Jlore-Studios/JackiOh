**[[R4170000]] The AI's evaluation counts no attack this turn from a Frozen unit, and none on a hero from a unit summoning sick without Charge, for a side whose attacks it reads as this turn's (issue #4170, 2026-11-18)**: extends [[R1174000]] and [[R3793000]]. Where the evaluation reads a side's attacks as this turn's (the AI at its turn start after the opponent's reply, the enemy on turn in a line the AI has passed), the face damage it counts leaves out every Frozen unit, which the engine lets attack nothing until its cleanup thaws it, and every unit summoning sick without Rush or Charge, and lands nothing on the hero from one with Rush alone, as Hearthstone's frozen and newly summoned minions. The flag `frozen_sick_before_swing` is on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`; `face_threat`'s end-of-turn read, `can_swing`, the engine's `frozen_next_turn` and `unit_worth` do not change.
