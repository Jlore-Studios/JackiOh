**[[R5125000]] A finished Infinite Assault fight is kept for replay with the practice games (issue #5125, 2026-11-26)**: an Assault level's fight was a mode fight the practice host never kept, like a dungeon fight ([[R2706000]]), so a player beaten by a Commander could not watch the game again. Issue #5125 asked for Assault replays; the ruling keeps a finished Assault fight with the free games ([[R768]]), carries the enemy's marks and sets in a kept game's setup ([[R767000]]) so the replay folds to its hash, names the level and enemy in its summary, and adds Watch the replay to the fight's result. It edits [[§9.9]], [[§16.6]] and [[§16.7]], and takes R5125000.
