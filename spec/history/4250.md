**[[R4250000]] A match or co-op socket's backoff starts over on its first view, not on open (issue #4250, 2026-11-20)**: `onopen` still sends `hello` and still shows the connection as open, but the reconnect backoff starts over only once the socket has delivered a view. A socket refused after the upgrade (4429, 1011) climbs the steps, as one that never opened does, instead of retrying at the first step about four times a second.
