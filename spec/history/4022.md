**[[R4022000]] A damage pool is spent only by a hit that lands, not by one a lethal replacement cancels or moves (issue #4022, 2026-11-16; amends [[R3550004]])**: the issue found that `land()` wrote a damage pool's soak at [[§4.4]] step 3b, before step 4a's lethal-hit replacements had their say, so a hit they canceled or moved still emptied the pool. A 2-health Unit hit for 7 with 5 left in Force Projector's pool spent the 5, Unan caught the hit from its full 7 and took 5 past its Armor 2, and the pool stood empty for the rest of the turn having stopped nothing; a hit Final Gambit canceled ([[R1704001]]) spent the pool on a hit that dealt nothing. Step 3b now only works out each pool's soak, step 4a judges what is left as before, and the soak is written when the pool takes the hit whole or the hit goes on past step 4a: a canceled hit spends nothing, and a moved hit ([[R1204]]) spends only what the pool soaks of it at its new target, so Unan takes 0 and the pool shows 0. Before the change `land()`'s two copies of step 4a, the hero's and the Unit's, became one helper, changing no game. A game recorded where one hit met both a pool and a lethal replacement no longer reaches its recorded hash and is refused as `rules_changed` ([[R767000]]). It takes R4022000, the first number of issue #4022's block, edits [[§4.4]] and amends [[R3550004]].
