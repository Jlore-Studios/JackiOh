### 8.11 Training Grounds

Sixty cards, `traininggrounds-001` to `traininggrounds-060`, and one token, from the designer's list of 2026-10-28 (tracker #2193, `JackiOh_TrainingGrounds_Cards.md`) and the brief that reads it (`docs/training-grounds-set.md`, "TG #N" Training Grounds card N): 61 rows. The set was held by [[R1420]]'s gate until its release (issue #2204, [[R2194000]]), which ships it as a patch of the v0.5 line ([[R2204000]]). #1–#40 are the designer's, rarity by number: #1–#15 Common, #16–#27 Rare, #28–#36 Epic, #37–#39 Legendary, #40 Mythic. #39.1 Training-Grounds Golem is the token #39 defines (`traininggrounds-039-1`), `rarity: "Token"` with the designer's Legendary as `printedRarity`, shown in parentheses. #41–#60 are the Legacy cards, snapshots of twenty Core cards: separate entries with their Core cards' data and scripts as they stand when the foundation lands, frozen against later Core patches, at their Core rarities, none Legendary or Mythic; part 2 (#2195) added them ([[R2195003]]). Since issue #2511 each is named `Legacy: ` and its Core card's name and tagged Legacy beside its Core card's tags ([[R2511000]]), and each is its own card: a deck may hold it beside its Core card and a Discover may offer both ([[R2511001]], superseding [[R2195000]] and [[R2195001]]). The set: 26 Common, 19 Rare, 11 Epic, 3 Legendary and 1 Mythic. The designer's types and tags stand, #28 Occidental and #29 Easterner read as Units (each prints stats); the designer's casing and hyphens in names stand; a card's "Training-grounds" or "Tutorial-Grounds" is the set, written Training Grounds ([[R2194001]], [[R2194002]]). Each row's Engine cell gives the brief's reading and the part that builds the card; TG5 has each card's full reading and BUILD M13 its must-pass cases.
