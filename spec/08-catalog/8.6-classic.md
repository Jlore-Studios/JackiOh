### 8.6 Classic

Ninety cards, `classic-001` to `classic-090`, from the designer's list of 2026-09-30 (patch v0.2.0, [[R380]] on). Classic generates only cards that exist elsewhere (the Felinor Token, C #16 Book of Flame, C #34 Ancient Acquisition), so it defines no token. The Engine column ends with "Tunes:", the numbers a Nerf, a Buff and C+ #41 KY's Constant may change (`params`, [[§5]], [[R386]]; a numbered keyword's number, such as C+ #19.1's Armor 3 or C+ #38's Spell Damage +2, is listed too, though the catalog keeps it on the keyword, where [[R386]]'s X change moves it, and not in `params`): ↑ means more is better for the card's controller, ↓ less; a step is 1 for a number up to 5, 2 for 6 to 12 and a quarter (rounded) above that, read off the base face's value (one step serves both faces), unless the cell names one; an amount never drops below 1, and a threshold moves toward harder for a Nerf. A number only the Radiant face's text prints is tuned on the Radiant face only: on the base face a Nerf or a Buff never draws it and it reads its printed value ([[R749]], [[R824000]]); one only the base face's text prints is tuned on the base face only ([[R1431]]). A number only the card's Cry reads (`read: "cry"` in `params`) is out of a Nerf's and a Buff's reach while the card is face-up on the field, as its cost and its Tribute are: the Cry has resolved and the price is paid ([[R1748000]], [[R2261000]]); one only one face's Cry reads, the other face's lasting text reading it too, is out of reach there while the card shows that face (`readOn`, [[R2261001]]). Since issue #3888 every number is marked by when it is read, its Combo's, its Cast on draw's, its awakening's or its Evolve's, or in a hand or a deck, or in a graveyard, and [[§6.3]]'s table says where each is live ([[R3888000]], [[R3888001]]). Issue #1748 audited every Spell and Field Spell of every set: a number one prints is declared, unless it is a numbered keyword's, a price, an engine table's, a formula's term, the card's structure, a declared target count, one whose better way hangs on the target, an AI generated card's or an Immutable card's ([[R1748003]]).
