### 8.12 Stinkyscape+

The Stinkyscape+ set: 166 cards, `stinkyscapeplus-001` to `stinkyscapeplus-166`, and 38 tokens, from the designer's list (tracker #3533, `JackiOh_StinkyscapePlus_Cards.md`) and the brief that reads it (`docs/stinkyscape-plus-set.md`, "SP #N"). The set was held by [[R1420]]'s gate until its release (issue #3573, [[R3535000]]), which shipped it as the newest set, gated for the AI's own decks ([[R1390]]); its rarity split is 36 Common, 47 Rare, 43 Epic, 28 Legendary and 12 Mythic. Each part of the build writes the rows of its own cards, and the Engine cell names that part; the weapon rack (S06, #3537) built these six.
