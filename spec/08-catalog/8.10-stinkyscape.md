### 8.10 Stinkyscape

One hundred and seventy-seven cards, `stinkyscape-001` to `stinkyscape-177`, from the designer's list of 2026-10-25 (issue #1836, `JackiOh_Stinkyscape_Cards.md`), each token a card defines right after it (index N.k, id `stinkyscape-NNN-k`), thirty in all; then the set's shared entries, at T- indices (id `stinkyscape-t-<name>`): the Dormant Starship, the twelve shared tokens (Drone Interceptor, Drone Corvette, the Drone Battleship's three layers, the six Resources and Anomaly Found!) and the ten AI generated cards S T-AI-1 to S T-AI-10 (`stinkyscape-t-ai-01` to `-10`): 230 rows (228 until #2521 added #176 Preemptive Strike and #177 Cloaking Module). The designer used #21 and #122 twice; his second #21 is #174 Aerelret and his second #122 #175 Frost Module, and every other number stands ([[R1857001]]). A token carries `rarity: "Token"` and the designer's rarity as `printedRarity`, shown in parentheses, as in [[§8.7]]; the four War Doctrines, whose headers print no Token, are tokens, and the ten AI cards, which the build designs (`docs/stinkyscape-set.md` ST15), print none, as Classic+'s ten. The Dormant Starship is the set's one entry that is neither a numbered card nor a token: a (3) Legendary Unit, 0/0, that the engine summons when a Starship Piece dies ([[R1852000]]), its Radiant face [[R349]]'s fallback, in no pool and no deck unless a query names it ([[R1857001]]). The designer's types and tags stand, with the brief's fixes: #125 and #164 are Field Spells and #125 is Jlockeed, #91 and #92 are Units tagged Swarm, #40 is Plague, #16 Rod of Ages prints no stats ([[R2280000]]; 1/1 until issue #2280), #120 Wild System's Radiant face gains 2 mana next turn ([[R276]]), Falcon's Radiant face is 8/2, Prototype-X2's 16/16 and xxFreshJack's 12/22 ([[R275]]), and the Drone Battleship's layers are named "Drone Battleship (… Layer)" so that "the Drone Battleship" names all three ([[R1857001]]). The source's header tags Starship and Dormant Starship (on the Drone Battleship's three layers and the Dormant Starship) are read as keywords, Starship and Dormant ([[§6.1]], [[R1839000]], [[R1852004]]), not as tags, so those rows print "Unit, Token" or "Unit" with the keyword in their text. The set adds four family tags, Drone Core, Weapon, War Doctrine and Swarm ([[§5]], [[R1857000]]), and since #2521 two more, Resource on the six Resources and Faction on the five Faction cards (#95, #159 to #162). Stinkyscape: 41 Common, 51 Rare, 43 Epic, 31 Legendary (the Dormant Starship among them), 12 Mythic (178); 42, 50, 43, 29 and 12 (176) until #2521, the owner's first balance patch for the set, made #137 Starfleet Commander Legendary and added the Legendary #176 and the Rare #177. The set was held by [[R1420]]'s gate until its release (issue #1884, [[R1857000]]), which ships it; the AI's own random decks still hold none of it behind [[R1390]]'s soft gate until it is measured on the set (issue #1885). It is built in the catalog card by card, part by part (BUILD M12, S01 to S26, issues #1858 to #1883), from the brief `docs/stinkyscape-set.md`, whose proposed readings (ST-S13-1 and the like) take R numbers from each part's issue block as the part that proves them lands; until then a card's script is a stub. Its cards are written in the terms of the mechanics that landed before it (the brief's ST3): Dormant, Starship Pieces and the launch, Tradable, Resources and Credits, the weapon kit, layered summons and the lethal guard for Units, staged Quests, the two new endings, the AI at the wheel, hidden Nerfs and trade offers, the card metrics, the starscape map and the spell, combat and card-on-card verbs. Two mechanics landed after the rows were first written, #1853's Marketplace ([[R1853000]] to [[R1853005]]) and #1855's board swap and Anomaly boards ([[R1855000]] to [[R1855003]]), and the rows that use them name them and their rulings. "Tunes:" is as in [[§8.6]].
