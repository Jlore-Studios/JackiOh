### 8.9 Meditative+

Twenty-six cards, `meditativeplus-001` to `meditativeplus-026`, from the designer's list of 2026-10-18 (issue #1173, `JackiOh_MeditativePlus_Cards.md`), each token a card defines right after it (index N.k, id `meditativeplus-NNN-k`): seven tokens, 33 rows (four until #1563 added M+ #1.3 and #1.4, six until #2521 added M+ #1.5). The designer's numbers stand, but for the Radiant faces of #9 and #17, which patch v0.3.14 (#1220) lowered, as their rows say. Two tokens are the designer's, M+ #16.1 Jazakus Prime and M+ #20.1 Lil' Kenny, each carrying `rarity: "Token"` and the designer's rarity as `printedRarity`, shown in parentheses, as in [[§8.7]]; five are the AI generated cards M+ #1 Bob's Plan adds, M+ #1.1 Base Camp, M+ #1.2 Spa Day, M+ #1.3 Garage Sale, M+ #1.4 Leg Day and M+ #1.5 Star Chart (#1.3 and #1.4 since #1563, #1.5 since #2521), which print none, as the T-AI cards ([[R1178002]]). Classic #60 holds the name Pile On, so the designer's #17 Pile On is Dogpile ([[R381]], [[R1178002]]); #24, listed as "Unit, Spell", is a Unit; #26 Scratch Pad, given no rarity, is Mythic, as M #17 True Craft a Card. The designer's "Jlockheed" is the tag Jlockeed ([[R278]]), and the set adds one tag, Potion ([[§5]], [[R1178000]]). Meditative+: 5 Common, 9 Rare, 9 Epic, 1 Legendary, 2 Mythic (26). The set was held by [[R1420]]'s gate until the last part of its patch, which shipped it in v0.3.15 (issue #1187, [[R1178000]]); its deck and trio code numbers are 4000 + n ([[R1410]]). It was built in the catalog card by card, batch by batch (BUILD M11), from the design brief `docs/meditative-plus-set.md`, whose proposed rulings (MP-B01-1 and the like) took R numbers from each part's issue block as the part that proved them landed. Its cards are written in the four patches' terms that landed beside it: an embiggen card's dearer price is its Embiggened version, labeled "Embiggened (N): …" (#1175; "Embiggened (+N): …", N its premium, since #5109, [[R5109001]]), Freeze is #1174's, and a card in Chinese is CN (#1176). In this section "M+ #N" is Meditative+ card N, "M #N" a Meditative card, and "#N", "C #N" and "C+ #N" are Core, Classic and Classic+ cards as elsewhere. "Tunes:" is as in [[§8.6]].
