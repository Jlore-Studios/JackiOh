   **`mergeStage(stage, cap)`**
   - Split the n items into `min(n, cap)` contiguous groups whose sizes differ by at most one, with the larger groups nearest: 7 → [2,2,3], 8 → [2,3,3], 4 → [1,1,2].
   - A slice group becomes `{ image: its images nearest first, joined with ", ", plane: group[0].plane, depth: group[0].depth }`.
   - Planes keep their length: a group's first index holds the joined images, and the rest of its indexes hold `""`.
   - When n ≤ cap, return the stage unchanged.
