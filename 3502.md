## Goal

A dungeon run's time becomes the time it was played, not the calendar's.

- `KeptRun` gains `elapsedMs`.
- The store adds the running session to it on every save, when the page hides or closes (`visibilitychange`, `pagehide`), and when the Dungeon page unmounts (the in-app "Back to Play" leave).
- A reload starts a new session from the kept total, not from `startedAt`. Hidden time never counts.
- A run kept before this change has no `elapsedMs` and starts counting at load.
- `RunTimer` shows the kept total plus the running session.
- The settled `timeMs` is that total, still floored by `DUNGEON_ELAPSED_MIN_MS`.
