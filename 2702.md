## Goal

`createGame` takes `deckMarks`, a per-seat list of `DeckMark { index, radiant, upgrades }`. At setup, before §2.1's shuffle, the card at `index` of that seat's deck list is dealt on its Radiant face when `radiant` is set (B1).
