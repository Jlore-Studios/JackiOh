## Risks

- **Turn dropped too early.** `let _ = app.spends.claim(..)` drops the turn at once and `#[must_use]` fires. Always write `let _turn`.
- **Clock read before the wait.** Claim before `now_ms()` in `post_seed` and `post_replace_quest`, so a request that waited reads the clock when its turn comes (the seed TTL and the UTC day of a replace).
- **New 429s.** A request that used to wait can now be refused during a burst.
  - One tab alone stays under the limit: it sends spends one at a time, card picks one at a time, one quest replace and one seed request (which waits for its release), so at most 4 are in flight against the limit of 5.
  - The quest panel and the practice route show the server's words; the two new web tests prove it.
  - The card picks sync and the seed release swallow a 429 like any failure, by R1834000 and R2379000 (see Open questions).
- **No other contracts affected.** No migration, no change to the wire or to `serverConfig.ts` (the `ECONOMY_SPENDS_*` constants are not exported), no hidden information, no determinism concern (server only).
- **Wait test sending too much.** Sending all five routes at once overfills `ECONOMY_SPENDS_WAITING_MAX`, so the wait test must send one route at a time.

Decisions this plan made that the issue left open:
