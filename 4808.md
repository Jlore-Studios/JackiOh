## Goal

Stop the AI's search from treating two different positions as one. Three changes:

1. `search_signature` adds the last Spell played (`state.last_spell`: its definition and its Radiant face). It does this only while a card that copies the last Spell (C #57 Echo, `subsystems::copies_text`) sits in the seat's hand or graveyard, which is where `legal_actions` offers plays from.
2. A unit's part of the signature adds its `shared_chain` / `split_chain` (S+ #7 Churnwalker), only when it has one.
3. `unit_lanes` also lists Dormant cards' lanes (it now walks `units_on_field_of`) and an animated card's home zone.

A position with none of these signs byte-for-byte as before. The issue's five failing cases, plus two more (an Echo in the graveyard; M #66 Fiery Waraxe's home lane), become tests named after a new ruling, R4808000.
