# Implicit states

Some of the code's states have no name: several fields read together (an `Option`, a flag, a counter, a
"pending" field beside what it guards) hold them, where one enum would. This file maps them: for each, its
fields, every combination of their values, what moves it between them, and whether each combination can
happen. It was started by #3678. The code audit's fourth part (`.github/hygiene/code.md` section 4, #3719)
extends it on each run: #3899's mapped states 4 to 6 and checked states 1 to 3 again; #4114's mapped states 7 to 9 and checked 1 to 6 again; #4291's mapped states 10 to 12 and checked 1, 2, 6 and 8 again; #4550's mapped states 13 to 15 and checked 1 to 3, 6 to 8 and 10 to 12 again; #5005's mapped states 16 to 18 and checked 1 to 3 and 6 to 14 again.

How to read and extend it:

- **One table per state, one row per combination.** The columns are `#`, the combination, whether it is
  reachable (and the shortest sequence that reaches it) or not (and why), what it should do (and the
  source: a spec section, a ruling, or the README that is the code's contract, or **unspecified**), and
  what it does (the test that shows it, as `path::module::fn`).
- **Only the real machinery counts.** A combination is reachable when a test reaches it through the
  machinery: the testkit's `scenario()` and `reduce`, the server suite's actor, the run's `run_reduce`.
  A combination that only fields set by hand reach is unreachable, however a test builds it.
- **Versions are not states.** A field that is `None` or a default for a record older than it (a match
  before portraits, an older replay, checkpoint or shipped catalog) is a version. It is listed under
  [Not states](#not-states-versions) and checked to still read.
- **Re-checking.** Each state names the commit it was checked at. `git log <commit>.. -- <its files>`
  lists what has changed since; a row whose code changed is checked again.
- **Representable when serialized.** A row marked so cannot be reached, but a value of the type (a game
  state, a run, a wire frame, a checkpoint or replay read back) can still hold it, which one enum would
  rule out. It is named for a person; no shape is changed here.

- `sockets[home]`: none, an open `Socket`, or a closed one the actor has not yet heard close;
- `hello_pending[home]` (P): none, or the socket generation whose Hello is queued and unrun;
- `socket_generation[home]` (G): 0 until the first `attach`, then bumped by each;
- and, read with them, the machine's `connected[home]` (C: set by `attach`'s `Connected`, cleared by `on_socket_gone`'s `Left`; the co-op machine keeps one per person, read through `here`; since #4717 the Disconnect task starts no grace while C is set, and the Attach task clears the grace and greets only while it is), `stopped` (after `halt`) and, for a 1v1 match, `voided` (R679, which sets `stopped` too).

## 18. A room code and its challenge

**Checked at** `4b412e1f33`, plus #5005's pull request.

**Files:** `crates/server/src/actor/rooms.rs` (`open_room`, `joinable_room`, `join`, `claim_and_start`, `cancel`, `mine`,
`release_room`; `seeds()`, with `remember_seed` and `take_seed_for_room`), `crates/server/src/social/challenges.rs`
(`create`, `close`, `assert_may_join`, `sweep`), `crates/server/src/social/friends.rs` (`remove_friend`, `post_block`),
`crates/server/src/api/auth.rs` (`remove_account`), and the two stores' `rooms_*`, `challenges_*`, `matches_discard_open`
and `matches_reap_open`: `crates/server/src/db/fake.rs` (with `remove_profile_rows`) and `crates/server/src/db/pg.rs`
(with its KNOWN DIVERGENCES list).

**Fields,** per room code: the room row (none, unjoined, or claimed: `guest_profile_id` and `match_id`) and its
`expires_at`, with the match the claim names (none yet, live or finished; in Postgres the row itself, `open`, `live` or
`over`); the challenge row that names the code (none, or its `state`: `open`, `accepted`, `declined`, `cancelled` or
`expired`) and its `expires_at`, the room's own instant; and the e2e seed `seeds()` holds under the code (R143, with
R1989000's seat pin), in memory. Read with them: now, the friendship and its blocks, and the sweeper's and the reaper's
passes.

**What moves it:** `POST /api/rooms` (`open_room`: the row, then the seed, held once the create won); `POST
/api/challenges` (`open_room` for `CHALLENGE_TTL_MS`, then, in a second transaction, the friendship read again and the
challenge row written, R1689000); `POST /api/rooms/:code/join` (`joinable_room`'s read and `assert_may_join`'s, then
`claim_and_start`: the claim and the challenge's accept in one transaction, the seed taken, both seats'
compare-and-set, the start; a failure after the claim runs `matches_discard_open`, which deletes the claimed row,
R890000); a decline or a cancel (`close`: the challenge closed and, before its lapse, its room freed with its seed, in
one transaction); an unfriend or a block (`challenges_close_between`, the same); `DELETE /api/rooms/:code` (an unjoined
room no open challenge holds, R1075000); `DELETE /api/account` (each open challenge's unjoined room freed, #5005, then
the profile with its challenges and its unjoined rooms); the clock (a room and its challenge lapse at their one
`expires_at`); the challenge sweeper (`sweep`, every `CHALLENGE_SWEEP_INTERVAL_SECONDS`: every row past its
`expires_at`, whatever its state); the reaper (`matches_reap_open`, R1450: a row still unstarted
`OPEN_MATCH_REAP_GRACE_SECONDS` past its expiry); the match's end (Postgres frees the code, R110); a restart (the seeds
and the lapse's push timer are in memory). Its rows are Q1 to Q26 (Q for quarrel), never `R<n>`.
