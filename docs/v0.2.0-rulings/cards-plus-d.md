# Patch v0.2.0 rulings: the cards-plus-d workstream, Classic+ #40–#78 and the AI cards (R580–R599)

Rows in SPEC §11's format, for the docs workstream to port into §11. Each is proved by a test named
after it and indexed in `packages/engine/test/rulings.test.ts`.

| R584 | C+ #64 Mulch Muncher's discount prices a play | "Costs (1) less for each Fruit you've played this game" applies only where a play takes the card from: its player's hand, or a graveyard a permission lets them play it from (E11, R454). It counts that player's Fruit-tagged plays this game (`playedByTag`, casts included, R70). Anywhere else (a deck, a graveyard with no permission, the field, a pool or a filter) it costs its printed (10), as Hearthstone's self-discounts work in hand only (R65). The cost floors at 0 | C+ #64; R55, R65, R70, R454 |
| R585 | C+ #70 Chaos Machine may pick itself | "A random card … on your side of the field" includes Chaos Machine itself, since the text does not say "other" (Hearthstone). Alone on its side with an empty hand, it Upgrades itself: its cost or its declared `cards`. The Upgrade is drawn and applied before the Degrade | C+ #70; R60, R386 |
| R586 | T-AI-3 Hallucination's copies and the deck's order | The copies reach the caster's hand in the order drawn, never the deck's, so the hand says nothing of where the originals lay (§9.1). A deck holding no more cards than it copies is copied whole with no draw (R129), in definition-id order. A copy the hand cap burns goes to the caster's graveyard and is given no Brittle | T-AI-3; §9.1, R57, R60, R129, R385 |
| R596 | "It" after a draw is the card that draw put in the hand | A text that draws and then acts on "it" or "the drawn card" — prices it (C+ #65.2 Normal Grape's "It costs (1) less", C+ #65.3 Large Grape's "It costs (0)") or reads it (T-AI-4 Chain of Thought's "If it costs (1) or less, repeat this") — means the card that one draw itself put in its player's hand. A card cast on draw never reaches the hand (R58), and the card the cast-on-draw chain's repeat then brings is that repeat's draw, not this one's; a burned card is in the graveyard (R4), a fatigue draw brings none (§2.4), a draw a limit stops is no draw (R457): each of these leaves "it" nothing, so nothing is priced and Chain of Thought's chain ends. Hearthstone's reading of "draw a card; it costs less" | C+ #65.2, C+ #65.3, T-AI-4; §2.4, R4, R58, R457 |
