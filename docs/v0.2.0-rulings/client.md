# Client rulings for patch v0.2.0 (R500–R519)

The client workstream's block (PLAN §5). The docs workstream ports these rows into SPEC §11 at
integration, in SPEC §11's format. Each row's proving tests are named `it("R<n> …")` and indexed in
`packages/engine/test/rulings.test.ts`.

| R | Topic | Ruling | Cards affected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R510 | The Activate control | A card the viewer controls whose view lists `activations` wears one Activate control per ability on the card itself, since a click on the card stays its attack or its pick as a target: a lightning glyph and a badge with the uses left this turn ("∞" for Activate ♾️), and the ability's words, its numbers filled from the card's `params`, as its tooltip and accessible name. A control is live exactly when `legalActions` lists that card's `activate`; otherwise it is greyed, its tooltip gives the view's `reason`, and it sends nothing. A press builds the activation as a play's choices are built (R81): one listed activation is sent at once, and several wait for a target clicked on the board or dragged to from the control (or from a card of yours that has nothing to attack and one ability), a Tribute, or a mode in the inline picker. Heroic Power is built the same way from the hero panel, whichever of `activatePower` or `activate` is listed, and each power a player controls is its own control. The control flares while the `activated` row plays on its card, and under reduced motion shows a still ring instead. Presentation and intent only (CLAUDE.md rule 7) | Classic #7, #15, #20, #21, #23, #42, #78, #81, #84; Classic+ #76.1; Core #98; §6.2, §10.10, R43, R81, R384 |
| R511 | C #57 Echo's face shows the text it copies | Where the view gives a copier the Spell text it has (R399), its face prints that text, on the face it was played on and filled with the numbers the view gives it (R386), in place of its own copying sentence. Whatever else its face prints stays: a Radiant Echo reads "Echo 1" over the copied text. It keeps its own name, (1) Cost, type and art; its inspect notes say "Copying: <name>" ("(Radiant)" on a Radiant face) and its own printed text stands beside it. With no copy it prints its own text | C #57; R243, R386, R399, R546 |
| R512 | Glossary rows for patch v0.2.0's terms | The glossary has a row, in players' words, for every §6 term a v0.2.0 card prints: the §6.1 statuses Can't be in Defense Position (SPEC's "Cannot be…" an alias), Can't be attacked, Only Units in this lane can attack this and Berserk; §6.2's Activate (one term for "Activate", "Activate X" and "Activate ♾️"); §6.3's Counter, Steal, Unlock, Flicker, Plague Token, Set health, Redirect, End the turn, Trigger a Cry and Look at a hand, each matched by the words the cards print; and Degrade and Upgrade, which split the one "Degrade / Upgrade" row by pairing the words either side of its slash with the rest of its sentence. A rule is printed without the ruling numbers SPEC cites. Matching stays case-sensitive, so a lower-case "steal it" or "can't attack or be attacked" stays plain words. The glossary beside a face in play also explains the states it wears | §6, §10.10, R373, R500 |
| R513 | How a client marks a tuned card | A card the view reports changed by Degrade, Upgrade or KY's Constant is marked wherever the viewer may read it. Each change is better or worse for its controller: a stat, a numbered keyword or an X going up is better, except Tribute, which is better going down; a keyword added is better and one removed worse; a declared number follows its `better`; a value set outright that is not a declared number is neither. A moved number in the text sits in a dotted box with ▲ or ▼, a tuned stat carries the same glyph, an added keyword is a "+" chip and a removed one a struck "−" chip, and the card is marked Upgraded (▲, every change better), Degraded (▼, every change worse) or Tuned (◆). The inspect overlays list each change in words beside the printed text. A Brittle count shows as a cracked-glass badge with its number, an enchantment as a badge with its words, a backrow pile as its depth, and a card's lines of code in its detail line. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7) | Classic+ #8, #14, #23, #40, #41, #69–#74, T-AI-3, T-AI-10; §10.8, §10.10, R383, R385, R386 |
| R514 | Board cells answer a cell prompt | A `cell` prompt (Classic+ #62, one cell a prompt, R422) is answered on the board: the zones its options name glow green on both sides, filled or empty, as a play's zones do, and wear a crosshair while it is open; a click or a tap on one or on what stands in it, or Enter or Space on a focused one, sends that option. The picker lists the same cells, grouped by side and row and named by lane, with any option that names no cell ("Done") among them, for the keyboard and small screens. Which zones answer is read off the options alone; a zone not offered answers nothing. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7) | Classic+ #62; §10.6, R422 |
| R515 | A budgeted pick shows its total | A `pick` prompt with a budget (Classic #44's "total cost of (5) or less") draws each option as its card's face at the cost the option carries and keeps a running total in R432's words, "(3) of (5) spent"; every card not yet picked whose cost would take the total past the budget is greyed, says "Over the budget" and is not added when pressed, while a picked card can always be taken back. Confirm waits until the picks are within the prompt's `min` and `max` and the total within the budget. All of it is read off the prompt's `cost`s, `budget`, `min` and `max`; the engine still refuses an answer over the budget. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7) | Classic #34, #44; §10.6, R432 |
