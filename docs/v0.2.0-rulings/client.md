# Client rulings for patch v0.2.0 (R500–R519)

The client workstream's block (PLAN §5). The docs workstream ports these rows into SPEC §11 at
integration, in SPEC §11's format. Each row's proving tests are named `it("R<n> …")` and indexed in
`packages/engine/test/rulings.test.ts`.

| R500 | The glossary's Cry and Tribute rows are short reminders | Patch v0.2.0 asked for shorter Cry and Tribute reminder text. The keyword glossary the inspect views print beside a card copies SPEC §6's Rule column verbatim (R373 puts it into players' words), except two rows, which it writes as one short line each that is still true to the rule: Cry, "When you play this card or an effect casts it. Never when it enters play otherwise" (§6.2's ruling, which it already stated in place of the Rule column), and Tribute, "Playing this also costs X of your Units, which go to the graveyard" (§6.3's cost, without R41's backrow clause, which R428 removed). The glossary names the two rows as its short reminders, and a test pins their words, keeps each to one line of at most 16 words, and checks every other row against SPEC as before. Presentation only: no rule reads the glossary (CLAUDE.md rule 7) | §6.2, §6.3, §10.10, R373, R428 |
| R501 | The voice set renders on macOS or Windows, and may take 6 MiB | §10.11 pre-rendered every voice line with macOS `say` and capped the set at 3 MB for Core's 111 cards; Classic and Classic+ bring 206 more cards and tokens and 274 more lines. `gen-voice.mjs` has two backends, named per persona: macOS `say` (Core's personas, whose files and hashes are unchanged) and Windows SAPI (`System.Speech`, driven through `powershell.exe`, natively or from WSL) shaped by `ffmpeg` (silence trimmed, pitch shifted by the persona's semitones with the tempo kept, the persona's filter chain for character, peak-normalised) and encoded to the same mono AAC at 22050 Hz and about 32 kbps. A SAPI persona's hash covers its own fields (voice, rate, semitones, filter, text), and its lines take no per-card override. Generating renders only the stale keys whose backend the machine has and reports the others as needing theirs; `--check` stays OS-independent and gains `--catalog` to check against another catalog. The budget, `VOICE_BUDGET_BYTES`, is 6 MiB counted in 4 KiB blocks (the 429 files take about 4.8 MiB), and every line still stays within 4 s, flavour only, and speaks at R204's moments. A missing file still falls back to the browser's speech synthesis | every card; §10.11, R203, R204 |
| R507 | How the patch history marks and lists changes | Words and numbers a patch added to a face's text are marked bold, with a double underline, in a teal of their own, distinct from R277's gold and R279's dotted line; screen readers hear "new:". Where the patch took words away, the old text follows under "Was", with them struck through (read as "removed:"). Values read "(2) Cost → (1) Cost", "2/3 → 3/3". On the Patch notes page only the newest patch shows its cards on arrival; the others load their snapshot and the one before when opened. A patch's cards can be filtered by name. A changed card whose change shows on a face appears as one face from that patch's snapshot: the Radiant face if every printed change is the Radiant face's, else the base face, with its changes beside it. A card changed only in data no face prints (lines of code, cards named, tunable-number limits) is listed by name with what changed. Added cards are listed by name, grouped by set in catalog order, with each set's tokens as their own group; removed cards by name. A card opens as it stands now (the newest snapshot holding it, or the last before its removal) in the collection's detail view with its History open. Old faces in History mark referenced names but never make them controls. Every text is filled in (`fillParams`) before it is drawn or diffed, so no placeholder shows. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7) | §10.10, R105, R277, R279, R388, R432 |
| R504 | An empty hand keeps its place | Patch v0.2.0 asked that empty hands not minimise during a battle. A hand with no cards, either seat's, keeps its row: the client draws one card-sized dashed outline worded "Hand empty" in it, sized in every layout (desktop, a tablet either way, a phone upright or on its side) exactly as that seat's cards are, so the row keeps a card's height and the field does not move when the last card leaves or the first arrives. The outline is not a card: nothing plays, drags or targets it, and the hand count reads 0. Presentation only (CLAUDE.md rule 7) | §10.8, §10.10 |
| R505 | The Find a match box repeats no queue count | Patch v0.2.0 asked to remove the queue counts from the Find a match box, which repeated the mode tiles above it. The lobby shows how many players are waiting (R257) once, on each mode's tile ("N waiting"). The box says only what the screen is doing (Find a match, Leave the queue, "Looking for a … opponent…"), and the notice after queueing names the queue joined, not its size. The room code is unchanged. Presentation only | §9.5, R257 |
| R502 | A cast on draw is shown on both seats | A card cast the moment it is drawn (§2.4, R58) is known in the redacted stream by its `cardPlayed` coming right after its own `drawn` (a `cardAnnounced` of the same card may stand between; the sentinel matches either id; a card set face-down matches by `formerId`, R227), never by its definition. On both seats, the drawer's own too, it bursts out of the drawer's Deck pile and the showcase holds it up under a "Cast on draw!" ribbon, longer than a play (`SHOWCASE_CAST_HOLD_MS` over the effects speed, at most `SHOWCASE_CAST_HOLD_CAP_MS`, under `PRACTICE_SHOWCASE_HOLD_MAX_MS`), as the runner reaches its `cardPlayed`; a hidden card is a back and is never named. `CARD_FX` gives a card its own recipe over its resolution: #21 Hinder cracks the crystals of the victim's tray that its next refresh will not fill, as many as the view's rider badge says (R169), and they stay frosted and cracked while the view lists the rider, drawn in every mode and still under reduced motion; #27 Blood Ridden Glowy Jelly Bean runs a crimson stream from the caster's hero into the card made Radiant, on the other seat into a back picked by the play's own event count, so it never says which card. It paces nothing (R200) | #21, #27; §2.4, §10.10, R58, R97, R169, R200, R202, R227 |
| R503 | Card art and set marks | Every face shows its set as a small mark on its frame: a ringed orb for Core, a columned temple for Classic, the temple with a plus for Classic+, and a plain diamond for a set the client has no glyph for. The mark is named by its title and label ("Classic+ set"), sits clear of the cost gem, name, rarity gem, rules box, stats, crest and Radiant marks, and is hidden only on faces under 100 px. A token that prints a rarity (B2.5) wears that rarity's frame, gem, crest and foil, on the board too; its rarity stays Token everywhere else. The procedural art gains the Book, Pancake and AI families (tag themes: AI after Call to Chaos, Book after KY, Pancake before Fruit, so a family's token reads as its family) and a motif read from each card's printed name. A plain card or Token wears its motif as its emblem; a tribe or family carries it beside its own emblem and skips the motif its own picture already draws. Every catalog entry draws a distinct picture on each face, and a Core card whose name calls up no motif draws exactly the picture it drew before. Presentation only: no rule reads the art, the mark or the printed rarity | every card; the tokens with a printed rarity (Classic+ #12.1–#12.8, #19.1–#19.5, #32.1–#32.3, #36.1, #38.1, #42.1, #46.1, #65.1–#65.5, #73.1, #75.1, #76.1) |
| R506 | Card moments in sound | A sound may answer the readable play an event happens inside: the innermost `cardPlayed` whose `cardResolved` has not come, forgotten when a turn starts, never a play whose card is the sentinel. #21 Hinder's rider landing on its victim's next refresh (`modifierChanged` "nextTurnMana", whether it adds a rider or cancels one) is a glassy mana crack in place of the plain notice, on both seats, since that is where the victim's mana visibly changes. A card #27 Blood Ridden Glowy Jelly Bean turns Radiant is a blood drain and then a gold burst in place of the plain glint, the same on the opponent's seat, where the `radiantSet` carries the sentinel, because the sound reads the public cast and never the card it hit. A readable `drawn` directly followed by that card's `cardPlayed` is a card cast as it is drawn, and stings in place of the play whoosh, except a Trap's. A token's printed rarity (B2.5) gives its summon the Legendary or Mythic sting as a card's rarity does. Call to Chaos's roll (R436) spins like a slot machine and dings once for each effect it names, at most three. A mark (R437) brands its card as it lands and lets go softly as it lifts, whatever the mark, its colour or the card. Book, Pancake and AI are sound families like the other tags, following the card art's theme (R503). The last 30 seconds of the viewer's own running turn clock (R439) beat once a second through the effects bus, a heartbeat and then, in the last 10, a tick sharper each second; never the other seat's clock, and nothing while muted, at master or effects volume 0, or before the first gesture. None of this is a rule, and R203 bounds all of it | #21, #27, #50, #95, Classic+ #73, the Book, Pancake and AI cards, tokens with a printed rarity; §10.11, R202, R203, R436, R437, R439 |
