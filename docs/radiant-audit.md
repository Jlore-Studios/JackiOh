# The Radiant audit

2026-09-24 · the Radiant pass (SPEC §5.2, §11 R275–R283) · patch v0.2.0 of 2026-09-30 (Classic, Classic+, the Core patches) · patch v0.2.10 of 2026-10-03 (the Animated pass and Ivory Tower; last section)

Every card's Radiant face held against one standard, and the ones below it raised. SPEC §8 (and §7
for the tokens) is where the result lives; this document is the record of the judgement, one row
per catalog entry, which `packages/cards/test/radiant-standard.test.ts` holds to the catalog.

## The standard (R275)

A Radiant face is about twice its base face.

- **A Unit's stats**: attack and health each at least double the base face's. A 0 stays 0 (#1 Big
  D-fender, #65.1 Spikey Pillow), and a token summoned X/X takes its X from the card that summons
  it (#18's Bread Token, #74's Rush Token), which scales X itself. This half is mechanical and a
  catalog-wide test holds every Unit to it; it names no exception.
- **A Unit's effect**: 100–150% stronger — 2 to 2.5 times the amount, a broader scope (one target to
  all, one side to a choice of sides), or an added rider. A unit whose text is only keywords gets a
  keyword more, or a stronger one (Rush to Charge). Doubling the stats alone is not enough.
- **A Spell, Field Spell, Trap or Field Trap**: its effect scales the same way, and it may add a
  draw or a tangential rider instead.
- **Edge cases.** A card whose base effect has no number is raised in scope or by a rider. An X
  card's X is scaled (#24's 2X, #74's 2X and 3X). A formula is scaled as a formula (#31 moves its
  Fibonacci index two places, about 2.6×; #40 and #70 double their exile term). A drawback card
  (#65, #65.1) counts a lighter drawback as the upgrade.
- **Every card has a Radiant face (R276).** The five the source left without one (#38, #80, #93.1,
  #95.1, #96) were below the standard by definition, since becoming Radiant changed nothing.

The Radiant face is also printed whole now (R277): the catalog's `radiant.text` is the §8 cell read
by §8's Conventions and written out, and the client marks in gold what the base face does not say.
"Radiant after" below is that printed text.

## Summary

- 110 entries audited: 100 Core cards and 10 tokens. Patch v0.1.1 (issue #27) added the Ghoul Token,
  whose Radiant face is R349's fallback, and its designer changes to #44, #74, #80, #90, #90.1 and
  #98 are recorded on their rows.
- **27 raised** to the standard: #8, #12, #24, #25, #31, #33, #38, #39, #40, #50, #54, #67, #68,
  #70, #73, #80, #81, #86, #87, #90.1, #93.1, #95.1, #96, #99, #100, Rush Token, Felinor Token.
- 83 already met it and keep their Radiant rules; their Radiant text is only written out in full
  (#61's "choose a unit" became "choose any unit" so the change it always had reads as one).
- New rulings: R275 (the standard), R276 (every card has a Radiant face), R281 (Radiant
  Quickstriker's 2X is one hit), R282 (Radiant K-Pop Fanatic's rider rides its steal), R283 (Radiant
  My Pawn destroys the attacker). R67's Fib index moved with #31.

## Patch v0.1.1 (issue #27, 2026-09-26)

The designer's patch changed sixteen cards' numbers or rules: #1, #8, #20, #25, #42, #46, #55, #56,
#77, #78, #81, #83, #84, #86, #87 and #92 (#30, #50, #62, #85 and #99 changed only a tag, a name or a
cost, and #51.1 already read "Draw 2"). Their rows below are re-audited: "Radiant before" is the face
the Radiant pass printed, "Radiant after" the one the catalog prints now, and the verdict is the
designer's. Every Unit still meets the stat half (the catalog-wide test lists no exception), and
where a Radiant face no longer adds as much as R275's effect half asks — #8's tripled vanilla body,
#87's gift made optional with its draw gone, #55's Radiant text, which is its base text less the
drawback — the designer's word stands. The patch's wording pass (R366) rewrote every face's printed
text without changing what it does; the other rows keep the pass's 2026-09-24 wording.

## Patch v0.2.0 (issue #40, 2026-09-30)

Patch v0.2.0 added the Classic and Classic+ sets, and every card of them is held to R275 below:
the 90 Classic cards, the 78 Classic+ cards with the 28 tokens they define (#12.1 to #76.1, each
right after its card) and the ten AI generated cards (T-AI-1 to T-AI-10), 206 new entries in all.
The fourteen Core cards the patch changed are re-audited first: #16, #17, #21, #22, #31, #32, #34,
#41, #43, #49, #60, #65, #88 and #95. Seven of them changed only their cost and keep their
verdicts; #21, #22, #31, #32, #41, #60 and #95 changed rules (R423, R426–R431) and are judged
again, and all fourteen still meet the standard. Every face below is printed in R432's house style
("(N) Cost" the noun, "costs (N)" the verb), stats first for a Unit, " / " between printed lines as
R372 lays them out.

- **The stat half.** Every new Unit face at least doubles its base face's attack and health, a 0
  staying 0 (C+ #19.4 Support Loser, C+ #48 Jlockheed's Lobbyist). An Animated card's unit face is
  held to it as a Unit is (C #5 Tesla, C #38 Jackiestan Auctioneer, C+ #12.8 Frostspatula, R383),
  and C+ #69 Buff Billy's X stats are held as multipliers (3X → 7X). Two Units carry a named
  exception, both the designer's printed numbers:
  - **C #33 Joro** stays 1/1. Its worth is in being targeted in another Unit's place (R394), not in
    fighting, and its Radiant face's Indestructible makes its health moot: a decoy that takes every
    hit and stays. A 2/2 would add a point of attack and nothing a decoy uses, so the designer's
    number stands; the effect half is the Indestructible it gains.
  - **C #80 BOOM! Big Max** keeps its 26 attack, and its health doubles (8 → 16). 26 is the card's
    number on both faces: with Trample it already outsizes nearly every Unit and takes most of a
    30-health hero in one hit, so 52 would change the printed number and little of the game, and
    the designer's number stands. Its effect half is Charge for Rush, the patch's rider (R276 gave
    #95.1 Chaos Golem the same).

  R275 says the catalog-wide test of the stat half names no exception ("there is none"). It now
  names these two, by name, and R275's row says so; every other Unit face in the three sets still
  meets the stat half with no exception.
- **The effect half is the designer's word** on three cards whose Radiant face adds less than R275
  asks, recorded as v0.1.1 recorded #55 and #87: C #22 Mid Runner (its Radiant text is its base
  text word for word; only the stats double), C #60 Pile On (its Radiant face is the base face less
  the clause that returns it to the deck, which the designer reads as a drawback) and C+ #3 Second
  Amendment Snake (2 Plague Tokens a turn → 3, 1.5×).
- **Faces the patch wrote.** Where the designer's Radiant face was missing, identical to the base
  face or short of the standard, the patch prints one that meets it: C #63 Crop Dusting had none
  (R276) and doubles both numbers; C #86 Genn's two 14/14 faces become 14/14 → 42/42, tripled as
  #8 Mr. Vanilla's; C+ #63 Fruit Tree's identical faces become a Radiant Fruit; C #41 State of the
  Game gains Lifesteal; C #80 BOOM! Big Max trades Rush for Charge; C+ #75 J-lease J-Jungle
  EX-plorer shuffles a Radiant Pack; C+ #66 Vine of Grapes adds 5 Radiant Grapes, not 3; C+ #17
  Conjure Rush Token++ gives 6 keywords on both faces; C #1 Curse of the Forgotten Classic keeps its
  Draw 1 and C #7 InfiniScepter its "from your hand". C #55 Book of Wildfire and C #72 Grand
  Counterspell keep the designer's faces under their own names (R381). So every card of the three
  sets has a Radiant face that differs from its base face (R276).

C+ #33 Ivory Tower's text (R418, R653). Each Animated card's Radiant face doubles its unit face, as
Tesla's, Jackiestan Auctioneer's and Frostspatula's do, which the stat half holds an Animated card to,
and keeps the Radiant text it had: the effect half is the one the rows above judged, and every verdict
stands. Ivory Tower's Radiant face is the designer's: the Unit it fuses in goes in on its Radiant face.
