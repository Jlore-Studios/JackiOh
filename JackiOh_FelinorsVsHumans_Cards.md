## Felinors vs Humans Mechanics 

* **Mechanics**  
* **Durability X (New Mechanic)**  
  * Attached to Field Spells or Units, their **Activate Y** can be used **Y** times a turn but in total **X** times across all turns. After activating **Durability** goes down 1 and when **Durability** reaches zero if the card is a field spell it enters the GY, if its a unit it stays alive but can no longer use its activate  
  * For cards with multiple activates one **Durability** counter controls all its activates  
  * **Refresh** *{activates}* does not increase **Durability**   
  * For example if I had a field spell **Activate 1: Draw 1, Durability 3** I could use it once three times in a row then **Durability** would be 0 and the card would enter the GY  
  * Reword all past cards to have this  
* **Fragile (New Mechanic)**  
  * When units receive any damage *{that lowers their hp}* they die. Essentially all incoming damage has **poisonous**   
  * Reword all past cards \[if there are any\] to have this  
  * A unit can receive or lose **Fragile** when nerfed or buffed respectively  
* **Unlucky X (New Mechanic)**  
  * The opposite of **Lucky X**  
  * For now, mostly just a Nerf option to throw on things.
