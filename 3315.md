A DEF unit on a phone must never lose its keyword marks without a sign. Today `Stack` in `FieldBadges.tsx` steps `fit.room` down to -1 when even the `+N` overflows the stack's `max-height`, and then draws no `.keywords` at all. Over a DEF card that cap is about 3.7, 21.5, 17.7 and 28.1px at the four phones, and the control plus a `+N` needs 30px, so the marks vanish.
