## Goal

Both bots get a **fullsend** mode, with the labels `bot:fullsend` and `squishy:fullsend` (the "fullsend tag") and the verbs `/harness fullsend` and `/squishy fullsend`.

1. A strong model splits the issue into parts, sub-issues that own disjoint files.

4. When every part has closed, a different strong run reconciles the branch, following `.claude/skills/fullsend/` phases 3–6. It makes every check green, passes review, and opens one PR into `main`, under the usual review rule and auto-merge.
