## Goal

`offer_as` in `crates/server/src/api/rematch.rs` refuses a rematch offer on a finished match whose `catalog_version` is not the server's `app.catalog.version`, before the offer is recorded. This covers a person's offer and a ladder bot's offer (`offer_for_bot`). The refusal is `409 update_required` with details `{ "expected": <server version>, "received": <match version> }` and the message `The cards changed since this game. Start a new one.` No offer is kept and no match is made, for both Best of 1 and All Random matches. A rematch of a match on the current catalog works as before. The decision is ruling R1638000, the first number of issue #1638's block. It comes with its note, its tests, edits to SPEC §9.4 and §9.5, and `spec/INDEX.md` rewritten.
