# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

"Share as GIF" in the replay player on `/dev/replays` exports the shown turn (the default) or a range marked with the scrubber, within `REPLAY_GIF_MAX_STEPS` steps and `REPLAY_GIF_MAX_SECONDS` seconds.

- **Frame plan (D5).** A pure function of the steps' views, timed by a fixed frame clock.
- **Raster.** Draws on an `OffscreenCanvas` in a worker, or on the main thread one frame per macrotask where a worker can't.
- **Encoder.** A hand-written GIF encoder with a fixed palette. The raster and the encoder load only through `import()`.
- **Byte cap.** Over `REPLAY_GIF_MAX_BYTES`: the smaller preset, then fewer fps, then "Pick a shorter clip". Progress and Cancel are shown.
- **Share.** `navigator.share` where `canShare({ files })` accepts; otherwise download.
- **No account names.** Portraits labelled "You" and "Opponent", the JackiOh mark and the catalog version.
