   **Decision, and the alternative rejected.** The issue suggests `unclamped_attack(state, shown) - unclamped_attack(&next, unit)`, raw against raw. The chosen formula gives the same result as raw against raw for every Unit no hidden card touches. For every Unit whose shown Attack a hidden card changed, it gives the same result as today's code.
