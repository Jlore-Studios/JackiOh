## Rules every recipe keeps

- First read the card's script, `crates/cards/src/scripts/meditative/c<NNN>_*.rs` (token `c039_5_…` etc.),
  for the events it really emits; a concept beat no event carries is dropped or moved, and you say so.
- Header comment: the card, its keys, its beats, `R3405001`. Local `TUNING`/`TIMING` constants (rule 9).
- A recipe returns `{ cues, row: "replace" }` for the events it claims, `null` otherwise; keys on `play`
  (`play.hook`, `play.player`, `play.instanceId`) and `play.seen` counts, never on another card's `defId`.
  A Field Spell recipe keys on event type, not on `play.hook`.
- Every card flying to a pile is a `ghost` (a card back). An owner-only beat (`costChanged`, a Discover, a
  face added to hand) shows as a `sheen` only when `p.view.viewer === play.player`.
- A hidden target (defId/instanceId `"hidden"`) anchors to its zone testid or `testid.board` at `FX_CENTER`.
- An entrance: `lead = leadMs ?? 0`, `slam = lead + frac(FX_SLAM_AT, D - lead)`; no cue before `lead`;
  exactly one shake, `...shakeCues(i, ENTRANCE_TRAUMA.<key>, slam)`; no gold area burst; every non-Mythic
  signature draws at least one figure (`figureCue`, `fx/figures.ts`).
- Every cue starts in [0, D] and ends by D + `FX_MAX_TAIL_MS`; a ghost ends by D. Intensity 0 draws nothing
  (use `tunedBurst`, which scales by intensity; figures are dropped by the layer at intensity 0).
- A gallery fixture (`RecipeFixture`): one to three events showing the text's beat; no `hookResolving`; no
  `summoned` of a card that has an entrance of its own (Virus, AI Girlfriend, The Cane, CN Golem, Jade Beauty)
  — use `classicplus-078`, `core-006` or a plain Common instead.
- The budgets: `fx/cards/tiers.test.ts` (R3332000's loudness budget: each signature's gallery play must end
  within its rarity's ceiling, its shakes within its type's trauma). A scratch test prints each card's
  entries and end against its ceiling: `cd apps/web && M3405_IDS=meditative-063,meditative-075 pnpm exec
  vitest run src/fx/cards/meditative/zz-budget.scratch.test.ts` (it is deleted before delivery; don't edit).
