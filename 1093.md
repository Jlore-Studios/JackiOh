## Goal

`loops::spawn_supervised` treats a loop that returns normally as a failure. It logs an alert `loop.returned` that names the loop, waits `LOOP_RESTART_DELAY_SECONDS`, logs the existing `loop.restarted` and starts the loop again, just as it already does after a panic (`loop.crashed`). Aborting the handle still ends the supervisor for good: an abort while the loop runs, or during the restart wait, starts no replacement, so `app::stop_loops`'s shutdown is unchanged. The tests prove four things: a returning loop restarts after the delay, its alert comes before `loop.restarted`, panic-and-restart is still covered, and an abort starts nothing new.
