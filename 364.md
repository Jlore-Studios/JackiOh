## Goal

I audited all 13 cards with Activate abilities: `classic-007`, `-015`, `-020`, `-021`, `-023`, `-042`, `-074`, `-078`, `-081`, `-084`, `classicplus-031`, `-060` and `-076-1`. On a fused card, an ingredient's Activate ability does not run in that ingredient's place, which breaks R102 ("each ingredient's text runs in its own place … what it remembers it remembers apart"). I saw two effects:

- **InfiniScepter loses its Spell.** When a Fuse keeps InfiniScepter, its remembered Spell sits under `scepter@1`. `canActivate` and `run` both read `scepter`, so the ability is never listed, and even if it were it would cast nothing.
- **Numbers come from the wrong ingredient.** A fused ability's `param` reads the first ingredient that declares the key. Fused The Power to Punish dealt 1 damage instead of 2. Radiant Mutate Spell fused under The Power to Thrive drew 2 instead of 4.

Done means `fuse.ts` wraps each ingredient's abilities in its place, with tests that fail before the change and pass after. Card scripts, `catalog.json` and SPEC stay unchanged. No patch fragment is needed, because there is no card data change (`docs/issues-and-patches.md`).
