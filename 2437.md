## Goal

A face-up card in a hand shows how many of its owner's ends of turn it has left, client only:

1. **Last-count aura.** When its count is 1, it wears the Allure mark's aura in a new fuchsia, the aura alone. This reuses `CardMarks`' `bareAura`, the way #2270 drew Brittle's last count in gray.
2. **Turns-left badge.** Every Temporary card in a hand gets a `"temporary"` state-rail badge, `⌛{n}`, with its words translated.

One helper, `temporaryLeft`, reads the count for both. Only a card in a hand gets it: never the field, the resolving strip or a card back. No engine, card, spec, wire or ruling change, and no patches fragment (no card plays differently).
