- **Mounted in two places.** The 1v1 toolbar sits inside `Game`'s `boardNotice` and remounts with each fresh board; the co-op one sits outside. Do not move the 1v1 toolbar out of `boardNotice`: that changes the layout.
- **Focus.** Giving focus back after a fresh board stays in each replay (`refocus`, `focusedControl` / `focusedDuosControl`), which looks the control up by test id inside its root. The toolbar must keep every `data-testid` exactly as it is.
