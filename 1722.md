# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

`evaluate()` gives credit for the public promises each side has banked, for both seats:

- a rest-of-game `StartOfTurnEffect` counts as an annuity (`start_of_turn_effect`);
- a held `AltWin` counts by how close the side is to its threshold, as a plan for its holder and a danger to the holder's enemy (`alt_win`);
- the one unmarked delayed hook the engine owns that has a known sign, `DELAYED_DISCARD_HAND_HOOK` (Classic #37 Last Hurrah), discounts the hand it will discard (`doomed_hand_discount`).

The marked hooks (destroy, allure) are already priced through `marks` (`marked_enemy_worth`). A card's own delayed steps (`delayed`/`resume` hooks) and the other 15 modifier kinds stay unpriced. All three weights are on in `AI_EVAL` and are 0 in the frozen `GREEDY_EVAL`. Scenario tests pin each term's sign, and `cargo jackioh gate` still passes.

5. **The `StartOfTurnEffect` term is flat per effect.** Rejected: scaling it by the turns left.
