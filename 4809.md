# Plan (opus, strong)

**Goal.** The opponent model (`crates/ai/src/reply.rs`) stops returning a whole reply when it ran out of nodes for a step's surcharge. The reply is cut instead: `simulate_reply` returns `None`, which is what its contract promises when the counter is spent. This holds for an attack (today the opponent's turn just ends) and for a known play such as Zephrys or a play Pareto Optimality judges (today the play is skipped). An attack the engine itself refuses still ends the turn, as now. The issue's second defect is fixed by narrowing the code, not by rewording the README: `surcharge_nodes` charges the My Pawn surcharge only for an attack that My Pawn's own check (`subsystems::is_lethal`) would call lethal.
