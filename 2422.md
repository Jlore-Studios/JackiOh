## Goal

A practice claim's pay lands exactly once, even across a restart:
- **Kept claim:** when a claim burns its seed, it keeps the claim (result and log) with the seed, in migration **0071**. The issue says 0068, but 0068–0070 already exist.
- **Marks:** a pay marks the seed `paid` in its own transaction, and only while the seed has no mark. A refusal marks it `refused`.
- **Drain:** the shutdown drain waits for every settle still running before the store closes.
- **Sweep:** the retention loop's sweep settles any claim still unsettled `PRACTICE_SETTLE_GRACE_SECONDS` after its burn. The prune keeps such a claim until then.
- **Ruling:** R2422000 amends R1688000 and R1303001.
