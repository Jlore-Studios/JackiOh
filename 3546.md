## Goal

A GIGA permanent stands in its **anchor**, lane L of its own row, and **covers** the other row's zone in lane L and both rows' zones in lane L+1. Nothing else enters those four zones, it is played only where all four fit, a summon that can't fit fails, and every lane rule reads it in both its lanes.

It is GIGA if it has the keyword as it enters the field: printed, granted in a hand (the mark), or from an embiggen `conditional_keywords` hook. Its size is then fixed until it leaves.

The view carries `giga` and the client draws the card across all four zones, with rulings R3546000–R3546004, fixture tests and no card change.
