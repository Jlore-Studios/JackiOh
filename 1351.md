## Goal

A block between two accounts, either way, mutes their in-match emotes toward each other. After the block, an emote either seat sends still goes through the hand check (R1342) and the rate gate (R643), and still spends the limit. Then it is relayed to nobody. The sender gets no error, no bot persona answers it, and the play telemetry (R1442) does not record it. A card that hears emotes still hears it (R1127). Emotes between unblocked seats relay exactly as they do today. The mute applies to a block that stands when the match's actor arms, and to one made mid-match through `POST /api/blocks`. SPEC §9.4 gains the clause, and the new ruling R1351000 has actor-level tests.
