# Plan (sonnet, medium)

## Goal

`damage_past_taunts_with` now counts what the opponent's board could deal past the AI's Taunts under a better order of attackers. An attacker a Taunt would stop whole (its Armor takes the hit whole, or its First Strike destroys the attacker) waits for the next Taunt or the hero instead of being thrown away. The smallest attacker that destroys a Taunt alone goes at it first. A flag, on in `AI_EVAL` and off in `GREEDY_EVAL`, gates both changes. The pinned Armor test then counts 2, a 3-health Defense guard against 3, 3 and 4 counts 6, and `wall_switches` offers the second unit's switch at 6 life. A ruling and the README record it.

**Deviation from the issue.** The issue says "one greedy pass, send the smallest lone breaker first". I prototyped that literal rule in a scratch copy and it undercounts. A Defense Mr. Vanilla at full health (4 health, Armor 1) against attackers 2, 2, 2, 2 and 9: the literal rule counts 8, while the true best is 9 (four 2s soak it, the 9 goes face). The same happens when attackers that cannot reach the hero are free soakers. So the count is the larger of two orders, smallest first and breaker first, each one a line the opponent can play. That is two cheap passes with no search. Decision recorded under Risks.
