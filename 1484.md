## Goal

On `/creator`, a card with changes not yet saved is no longer dropped without a word:
- **Back** shows the existing unsaved-changes note ("This card has changes you haven't saved.", **Discard them** / **Keep editing**). Discard them goes to the landing page. Keep editing keeps the draft.
- **Reload or closed tab:** while unsaved work is held, a cancelable `beforeunload` is default-prevented, so the browser asks first.
- **Nothing to lose:** a saved card, or a new card nobody has touched, leaves at once with no listener. The listener is removed after a save and on unmount.

The behaviour becomes a new ruling, R1484000, written into SPEC §12.11. The issue says §12.9, but the creator is §12.11; §12.9 is "Saved on the account".
