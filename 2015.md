# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

Two fixes on the board's field, both in `apps/web` only.

1. **Each half taps in its own playmat.** A tap on the field takes its tap family from the half it lands on, and that family picks both the mark (`tapMarks`) and the sound (`TAP_SFX`). The upper half (`y < 50`) uses the opponent's playmat (`theirTable`); the lower half uses the viewer's (`table`). An absent or unknown playmat taps as sand. Slams and the shared build counter stay as they are.
2. **A zone that can't be picked shows no pressed feedback.** A zone that is not legal right now shows no browser tap or pressed feedback. A legal zone keeps its role, `aria-*`, `tabIndex` and click. The sand tap on an empty zone still happens.
