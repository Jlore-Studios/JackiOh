## Risks

- **The existing tests tapped the upper half.** `press` defaulted to `y = 40`. Once the halves split, `"#1954 every playmat taps in its family's sound and mark"` and the Board-wiring test would play sand. Moving the default to `y = 70` (step 8) fixes that. Do not weaken their assertions instead.

- **Inheritance:** without the `> *` reset, a legal card standing in a non-legal zone would lose its own tap feedback. Keep both rules.
- **#757:** do not touch `Zone.tsx`'s role, `aria-disabled`, `tabIndex` or handlers. The Zone tests pin them.
- **Hotseat and watched games:** the opponent is always the upper half (`FIELD_ROWS` in `Board.tsx`), and the landscape phone layout keeps the lanes' four rows top to bottom. So `y < 50` holds in every layout.

## Open questions

Nothing blocks the build. These are the decisions taken, each with the alternative rejected:

- **The half is chosen by `y < 50` against the field**, as the issue says. Rejected: reading `closest(".zone")?.dataset.side`, which misses taps in the gaps and the mat's margins.
- **The selector is `.zone:not([data-legal="true"])`.** Rejected: the issue's `:not([data-legal])`, which matches nothing because `data-legal="false"` is always written.
- **The fix is CSS only.** Rejected: dropping `onClick` from a non-legal zone, which the issue rules out ("don't change … behaves"). It would also not hold: React keeps the `onclick` it set on an element after the prop goes away, so a zone that had once been legal would still highlight.
- **Children of a non-legal zone keep the browser's tap highlight (`initial`).** Rejected: letting them inherit `transparent`, which would change a legal card's feedback and is outside the task.
