# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

A feed kept on the server (migration 0093, `public.notifications`). Each of the eight events writes to it where that event is already handled. It is read by `GET /api/notifications` and `POST /api/notifications/read`, pushed as a `notification` frame on `/ws/social`, and counted as `notificationsUnread` in the social read. Each profile keeps at most `NOTIFICATIONS_KEEP` rows, and rows older than `NOTIFICATIONS_KEEP_DAYS` are purged.

The web shows the feed as the inbox's Recent section, a dot on the bell, one toast at a time, a `(n)` tab title while the tab is hidden, and desktop notifications. It hides the email toggle when `emailAvailable` is false. §9.4, §9.13, rulings R4039000 (server) and R4039001 (client) and the READMEs record all of this.
