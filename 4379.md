## Goal

`combat_forecast` should say what an attack's own state check does. It will settle the deaths of the attack's other hits on a scratch copy of the board. That covers the Cleave neighbours, the sweep hits and the strike back. Each death that lowers another unit (an aura-bearer dying) then counts. As a result, `kills`, `survives` and each splash `kills` match the combat on the seed-6180 shape (a sweep or Cleave kills the units whose aura holds the target up). I18 then judges those attacks again.

I18's remaining skips are a card beneath or held, a Reborn unit that goes before a later pass, and the unchanged hooks, traps, chains, reflections and replacements. The change also needs a new ruling with `proven_in` tests, and `cargo jackioh spec check` must pass.

**Decision the issue left open (this departs from its wording).** The issue says to re-project the main blow, splash `dealt`, overflow and lethal sum from the settled board. I would not do that.

- Deaths happen in `state_check` passes *after* the whole combat (§4.5, R59), so every hit lands on the standing board.
- A Trample 3 into a 4-health target held up by a dying neighbour deals 3 with no overflow. Re-projecting from the settled board would say 2 dealt and 1 overflow.
- So `dealt`, `taken`, `pops`, splash `dealt`, overflow and the lethal sum stay as they are.
- Only `kills`, `survives` and splash `kills` change, and only from false to true (`kills`) or true to false (`survives`).
- The lethal sum needs no code change, because hero damage is dealt before any death. A test proves it agrees.
