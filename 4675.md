        - Sum the `CardStats` of `version.card` over (version, source) with `add_stats`, giving an `Option<CardStats>`.

     5. For each row:
        - The counted tally is `seen(stats)` when `mode == ModeFilter::Only(GameMode::Random)` (R2777002), otherwise `stats.in_deck`; with no sum it is the default tally.
        - `games` is that tally's games; `has_enough_games = games >= min_sample`.
        - The adjusted rate and range come from `adjusted_win_rate` and `adjusted_interval` against `field`.
        - `tier` is `tier_of(..)` only when `has_enough_games`, copying `get_cards`' lines 1239–1252.

     - A header naming the field's centre and spread, its cards, the band and the floor.
     - One line per row: `id name first..last [live] games N adjusted R% (L%–H%) tier T`, with `NO_FIGURE` where a value is missing.
     - A closing line: `"{total} play versions of {cards} cards ({live} live); {with} with games; {enough} with {min_sample} games or more ({enough_live} live, {enough_closed} closed)."`
   - Add `pub mod play_versions; pub use play_versions::*;` in `stats.rs` after `pub use window::*;`.
   - Do not derive `ts_rs` on these types.

3. **Build the play versions** in `crates/cards/src/patch_history.rs`.
