# Plan issue #1142: Patch v0.3.Y: player statistics keep syncing to the account once a player has seen 200 cards

## Goal

Prune the player statistics payload synced from the browser to the account so that it carries only the inputs read by the public summary (game totals, and non-zero counts of `played`, `playedAgainst`, `destroyed`, and `defeated`, omitting `seen` and all-zero entries). Raise the server's `PLAYER_STATS_BYTES_MAX` cap from 16,384 to 65,536 bytes, export it to `apps/web/src/wire/serverConfig.ts`, and gracefully drop lowest-count cards if a payload ever exceeds the cap. Document the account copy semantics in new ruling `spec/rulings/R1142000.md` amending `R654`.
