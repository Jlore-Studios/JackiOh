## Goal

Practice setup gains a **Sandbox** choice: a normal game against the AI plus a dev-tools panel that searches the catalog (name, text, id, set, type, cost, rarity; Radiant toggle) and spawns a card, base or Radiant, for either seat into a hand, a field lane, a graveyard or the library top; sets hero health and Armor and current and max mana; draws; destroys or removes a card; and holds the AI's turn.
