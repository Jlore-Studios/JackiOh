**Goal.** Stinkyscape's Marketplace becomes an engine primitive on fixture scripts, reached by no shipped card. It opens for both players on the first Resource or Credits gained (`GameState.marketplace`, absent until then, so no golden moves). It holds one offering rolled from a table of lots (re-rolled every 6 turns), a stock per player, prices by the designer's rule (20% Radiant at 2×, moving every turn by up to a tenth; Resources' buy prices fixed, their sell quotes redrawn each turn in range) and fixed-price lots added for the rest of the game. A player buys (and sells tray Resources) in their own main phase with two new actions, for Credits and no mana. `legalActions`, the reducer's refusal and `view_for` read one function each. The AI, the random policy and the timeout handle the actions, the client's shop renders the view alone, and the spec, the rulings R1853000–R1853005 and the events' rows come in the same change.

**Decisions the issue left open** (each goes into a ruling note; reject the alternative named):
- D-a. **Direct actions, no prompt**: `marketBuy { offer }` and `marketSell { instanceId }`, each listed while its refusal passes, as `trade` is (R1838000). Rejected: a button action opening a `market` prompt with Leave. An open-then-Leave pair changes nothing, and the random policy, the fuzz and the AI could repeat it without end. "Built on the night market" is kept for the prices' shape and the shop's look (`market.css`, `MarketPrompt.tsx`'s card face).
- D-b. Selling is in: the designer's list says Resources "can be sold or bought on the Marketplace". A sale fetches the Resource's quote this turn. Once the Marketplace is open, the full-tray sale (R1840001) fetches the quote too and draws nothing.
- D-c. "Every 6 turns" counts game turns (`state.turn`), so 3 of each player's. Rejected: six of each player's.
- D-d. The spread is a uniform whole-number draw in [p − ⌊p/10⌋, p + ⌊p/10⌋], with no draw when ⌊p/10⌋ is 0. Rejected: a normal draw (floats).
- D-e. A Radiant Resource lot costs its Radiant face's printed `buy`, not 2× the base one.
- D-f. A buy is refused when the hand (or, for a Resource, the tray) is full, as the night market offers no lot to a full hand. Rejected: burn or auto-sell.
- D-g. Everything at the Marketplace is public: the offering, prices and a public `marketBought` naming the card. Credits are already public (R1840005) and the prices tell the offers apart. The card itself arrives through `addedToHand`/`resourceGained`, hidden per R97. The view sends only the viewer's own stock and sales.
- D-h. A fixed lot (`add_market_lot`) is offered to its caster alone. Its price never moves, it survives every re-roll, and its stock is `MARKETPLACE_FIXED_LOT_STOCK` (1) a cycle. Adding one opens the Marketplace if it is closed.
- D-i. The production table `MARKETPLACE_TABLE` stays empty until S21 fills it. Tests set a fixture table through a testkit override. No fold reads the table (no carried version gains Resources). The patch that ships the set's table owes each version its own (as R1846003).
- D-j. Opening emits no event. The client shows the shop from `view.marketplace`, so #1840's exact-event tests stay green.
