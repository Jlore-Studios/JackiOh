# Plan (opus, strong)

## Goal

`POST /api/cosmetic-packs/open` opens an earned cosmetic pack (`InventoryItem::CosmeticPack`): `COSMETIC_PACK_SLOTS` slots from a fresh seed, the first at `COSMETIC_PACK_GUARANTEED_RATES` (Epic or better), the rest at `COSMETIC_PACK_RATES` (Common to Mythic), no pity. A slot grants an unowned cosmetic for a card or the account, leaning toward owned cards, plus the card when unowned. A tier with nothing unowned falls to the nearest tier below, then above; nothing unowned at all pays bar points (R1305000). Each opening adds one to `Counter::ChaseShardBar`, which stops at `CHASE_SHARD_PACKS`. `POST /api/cosmetic-packs/chase` spends a full bar on a chosen Legendary or Mythic cosmetic and empties it; `GET /api/cosmetic-packs` reads the bar and unopened packs. Each opening is logged in its rows' `ref`, replays from its seed, and runs under the account lock in one transaction. Rulings R1306000–R1306003.
