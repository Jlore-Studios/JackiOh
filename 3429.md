## Risks

- **Flaky gate.** Production waits on the daily run, so it must not flake. The mitigations:
  - budgets bound medians over thousands of samples, with 5× headroom;
  - each daily test is alone in its binary's `--ignored` run;
  - timing is serial (rayon only builds the corpus).
- **Drifting corpus.** New cards move the deals of seeds 1–40; a crossed budget names the constant to re-measure.
- **Determinism.** Everything comes from seeds and fixed streams, and the fold's hash is checked against the live game's. No clock enters a pure crate.
- **`spec check`.** No test name may hold an `r<digits>` token, and new code cites no R-id.
- **CI time.** The smoke tests add seconds to CI; keep `PERF_SEED_LAST` at 40.
