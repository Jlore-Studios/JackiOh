`cargo jackioh perf [--smoke] [--json]` times `reduce`, `view_for`, `legal_actions`, `hash_state` and the replay fold on a frozen corpus of states. The corpus is the hotseat fixture's states, the gate's two wide boards and the heaviest states of fuzz seeds 1–40.
